# EcoFusion RPM (Rapid Planning Method) System

## What is RPM?

RPM stands for **R**esults-focused, **P**urpose-driven, **M**assive Action Plan.

Tony Robbins developed this system because:
> "Most people overestimate what they can do in a year and underestimate what they can do in a decade."

The key insight: Focus on RESULTS first, then figure out the actions.

---

## The RPM Process

### Traditional Planning (Ineffective)
1. List all tasks
2. Prioritize by urgency
3. Work through list
4. Get overwhelmed
5. Lose focus on what matters

### RPM Planning (Effective)
1. Define the RESULT you want
2. Clarify the PURPOSE (why it matters)
3. Create a MASSIVE ACTION PLAN
4. Execute with focus
5. Review and adjust

## RPM Weekly Review

Every week, review these questions:

### What did I ACCOMPLISH this week?
- [ ] Celebrate wins
- [ ] Document progress
- [ ] Share with team

### What did I LEARN this week?
- [ ] What worked well?
- [ ] What didn't work?
- [ ] What would I do differently?

### What are my RESULTS for next week?
- [ ] Top 3 outcomes
- [ ] Why do they matter?
- [ ] What actions will I take?

### What PATTERNS am I noticing?
- [ ] Recurring obstacles
- [ ] Energy management
- [ ] Time allocation

### Am I on track for my 90-day goals?
- [ ] Yes: Keep going
- [ ] No: Adjust or get help

## The Psychology of Achievement

Tony Robbins teaches that success is 80% psychology, 20% mechanics.

### Limiting Beliefs to Address

**Belief:** "We're just a small course company"
**Reframe:** "We're building the future of food education"

**Belief:** "There's too much competition"
**Reframe:** "The market is huge and we serve a unique niche"

**Belief:** "We can't charge premium prices"
**Reframe:** "Our transformation is worth 10x what we charge"

**Belief:** "We need to be perfect before launching"
**Reframe:** "Done is better than perfect; we improve continuously"

### Empowering Questions

Ask these daily:
1. "What am I most excited about today?"
2. "What would I do if I couldn't fail?"
3. "Who needs to hear from us today?"
4. "How can we deliver 10x more value?"
5. "What's the ONE thing that would change everything?"
