## 1. Introduction to Aquaculture Integration

### 1.1 The Role of Fish in Aquaponics

In aquaponic systems, fish serve dual functions: as primary protein producers and as the biological engine driving nutrient cycling. The fish metabolize feed into forms usable by plants, closing the loop between animal and plant production.

**Nutrient Production by Fish**:
```
Feed Input → Fish Metabolism → Waste Products → Bacterial Conversion → Plant Nutrients

100 kg feed → 30-40 kg fish + 60-70 kg waste
                              │
                              ▼
                    NH₃ (ammonia) → NO₂⁻ (nitrite) → NO₃⁻ (nitrate)
                              │
                              ▼
                    Available nitrogen for plant uptake
```

## 6. Fish Health Management

### 6.1 Common Diseases

**Bacterial Diseases**:

| Disease | Pathogen | Symptoms | Treatment |
|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Columnaris | *Flavobacterium columnare* | White lesions, fin rot | Salt, temperature reduction |
| Streptococcosis | *Streptococcus* spp. | Erratic swimming, eye opacity | Antibiotics (with oversight) |
| Aeromonas | *Aeromonas hydrophila* | Hemorrhaging, ulcers | Water quality, probiotics |
| Edwardsiellosis | *Edwardsiella ictaluri* | Hole in head, spiral swimming | Prevention, vaccination |

**Parasitic Diseases**:

| Parasite | Type | Symptoms | Treatment |
|----------|------|----------|-----------|
| Ich (*Ichthyophthirius*) | Protozoan | White spots | Salt, temperature |
| *Trichodina* | Protozoan | Flashing, lethargy | Formalin, salt |
| *Gyrodactylus* | Monogenean | Excess mucus, flashing | Formalin, praziquantel |
| Anchor worm (*Lernaea*) | Copepod | Visible parasites | Manual removal, salt |

### 6.2 Biosecurity Protocols

**Prevention Strategies**:
1. Quarantine new fish (21-30 days)
2. Prophylactic salt treatment (0.3-0.5% for warm water)
3. Maintain optimal water quality
4. Avoid overcrowding
5. Proper nutrition
6. Minimize stress
7. Footbaths, hand sanitizers
8. Dedicated equipment per system

### 9.3 Fish-to-Plant Ratio

**Balancing Fish and Plant Production**:

```
FISH-TO-PLANT RATIO CALCULATION

Daily Fish Feed (lb) × 0.06 = Daily Nitrogen Production (lb N)
Daily N Production × Plant Area = Required Growing Area

RULE OF THUMB:
1 lb fish feed/day supports approximately 50-100 sq ft of growing area

For a 500-gallon system with 100 lbs of fish @ 2% feeding:
- Daily feed: 2 lbs
- N production: ~0.12 lbs
- Supported area: 100-200 sq ft leafy greens
```
