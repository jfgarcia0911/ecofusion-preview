## Executive Summary

This Master Implementation Timeline provides the definitive roadmap for EcoFusion's journey from startup to industry-leading sustainable agriculture company. Organized across five phases over 10 years, this timeline coordinates all operational, financial, and strategic initiatives required to achieve our vision of transforming urban food systems while building a Fortune 500-caliber enterprise.
