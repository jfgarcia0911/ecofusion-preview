# EcoFusion Job Descriptions & Organizational Design
## Complete Role Architecture and Position Documentation

---

## Executive Summary

This document provides EcoFusion's comprehensive organizational design and job description library. Built on WorldatWork job evaluation methodology and SHRM best practices, this framework ensures clear role definition, career path visibility, and scalable organizational structure as EcoFusion grows from startup to industry leader.

---

## Part 1: Organizational Design Philosophy

### 1.1 Design Principles

| Principle | Description | Application |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Span of Control** | Optimal ratio of reports to managers | 5-8 direct reports per manager |
| **Flat Where Possible** | Minimize hierarchy to speed decisions | 4-6 levels maximum |
| **Clear Accountability** | Every function has clear ownership | RACI for key processes |
| **Scalability** | Structure that grows with the company | Design for 10x, implement for current |
| **Cross-Functional** | Collaboration across teams | Matrix where needed |
| **Mission Alignment** | Structure supports mission delivery | Sustainability integrated throughout |
