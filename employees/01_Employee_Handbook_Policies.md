## Section 2: Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Employee Categories

**Full-Time Employees**
- Regularly scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week
- Eligible for full benefits package
- May be exempt or non-exempt under FLSA

**Part-Time Employees**
- Regularly scheduled to work fewer than 30 hours per week
- Eligible for limited benefits (see Benefits section)
- Typically non-exempt under FLSA

**Temporary/Seasonal Employees**
- Hired for a specific period or project
- Benefits eligibility varies by duration and hours
- Employment ends upon completion of assignment

**Interns**
- Educational program participants
- May be paid or unpaid per applicable law
- Limited term, developmental focus

### 2.2 Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Classification

**Exempt Employees**
- Not eligible for overtime pay
- Paid on a salary basis
- Meet FLSA duties tests (executive, administrative, professional, etc.)
- Expected to work hours necessary to complete responsibilities

**Non-Exempt Employees**
- Eligible for overtime pay (1.5x for hours over 40/week)
- Must accurately record all hours worked
- Entitled to meal and rest breaks per state law
- May be paid hourly or salary

### 2.3 Introductory Period

All new employees serve a 90-day introductory period. During this time:
- Performance is closely monitored and feedback provided
- Either party may end employment with minimal notice
- Benefits eligibility begins as scheduled (some on day 1, others after 30/60/90 days)
- Successful completion leads to "regular" employee status

Completion of the introductory period does not change the at-will nature of employment.

## Section 3: Compensation & Payroll

### 3.1 Pay Philosophy

EcoFusion is committed to fair, competitive compensation that:
- Attracts and retains talented team members
- Reflects individual performance and contribution
- Maintains internal equity across positions
- Aligns with our financial sustainability
- Complies with all applicable wage and hour laws

### 3.2 Pay Periods and Paydays

- **Pay Frequency**: Bi-weekly (every two weeks)
- **Pay Day**: Friday
- **Pay Method**: Direct deposit (required) or physical check upon request
- **Pay Statements**: Available electronically via payroll portal

### 3.3 Timekeeping Requirements

**Non-Exempt Employees Must:**
- Accurately record all hours worked
- Clock in/out for each shift
- Record meal periods
- Report any missed punches to supervisor immediately
- Never work "off the clock"

**Falsification of time records is grounds for immediate termination.**

### 3.4 Overtime Policy

- Non-exempt employees receive overtime pay at 1.5x regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek
- Overtime must be pre-approved by supervisor
- Working unauthorized overtime may result in discipline
- Workweek runs Sunday 12:00 AM through Saturday 11:59 PM

### 3.5 Deductions

**Required Deductions:**
- Federal income tax
- State and local income taxes (where applicable)
- Social Security (FICA)
- Medicare
- Court-ordered garnishments

**Voluntary Deductions (with authorization):**
- Health insurance premiums
- Retirement contributions
- Flexible spending accounts
- Supplemental insurance
- Other benefit premiums

### 3.6 Pay Corrections

If you believe there is an error in your pay, report it immediately to your supervisor and HR. EcoFusion will investigate and make corrections promptly. Improper deductions from exempt employee pay will be reimbursed.

## Section 4: Work Hours & Attendance

### 4.1 Standard Work Hours

- **Office/Administrative**: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (1-hour lunch)
- **Farm Operations**: Varies by role; schedules posted weekly
- **Flexibility**: Some positions eligible for flexible scheduling with manager approval

### 4.2 Attendance Expectations

Regular, punctual attendance is essential. Employees are expected to:
- Arrive on time for scheduled shifts
- Be ready to work at the start of shift
- Provide advance notice for absences when possible
- Call in at least 30 minutes before shift start for unexpected absences
- Contact supervisor directly (text/call), not just a coworker

### 4.3 Absence Reporting Procedure

1. Contact your direct supervisor as soon as possible
2. If unable to reach supervisor, contact HR
3. Provide expected duration of absence
4. Keep supervisor updated on extended absences
5. Provide documentation if required (e.g., doctor's note for 3+ days)

### 4.4 Tardiness and Absenteeism

Excessive tardiness or absenteeism may result in progressive discipline:
- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Final written warning
- Termination

**No Call/No Show**: Failure to report to work without notification for 3 consecutive scheduled workdays may be considered job abandonment and voluntary resignation.

### 4.5 Meal and Rest Periods

**Meal Periods:**
- 30-minute unpaid meal break for shifts over 6 hours
- Must be relieved of all duties
- Should be taken approximately mid-shift

**Rest Periods:**
- One 10-minute paid break per 4-hour work period
- May not be combined with meal break
- May not be used to arrive late or leave early

### 4.6 Remote Work Policy

Select positions may be eligible for remote work arrangements:
- Must be approved by supervisor and HR
- Requires signed Remote Work Agreement
- Employee must maintain suitable workspace
- Must be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM)
- May be modified or revoked based on business needs or performance

## Section 5: Leave Policies

### 5.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)

EcoFusion provides a combined PTO bank for vacation, personal time, and sick leave:

| Tenure | Annual PTO Accrual | Maximum Accrual |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 0-2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 180 hours |
| 2-5 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 240 hours |
| 5-10 years | 25 days (200 hours) | 300 hours |
| 10+ years | 30 days (240 hours) | 360 hours |

**PTO Guidelines:**
- Accrues each pay period
- Available for use after 90-day introductory period
- Vacation requests require 2 weeks advance notice when possible
- Approval based on operational needs
- PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave
- Unused PTO paid out upon separation (up to accrued balance)

### 5.2 Paid Holidays

EcoFusion observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day After Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve (half day)

**Holiday Pay:**
- Full-time employees receive regular pay
- Part-time employees: pro-rated based on average hours
- If required to work: 1.5x pay plus comp time

### 5.3 Sick Leave

Included in PTO bank, but employees may also use unpaid sick leave if PTO is exhausted. Sick leave may be used for:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Care for ill family member
- Mental health days

Documentation may be required for absences of 3+ consecutive days.

### 5.4 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per 12-month period for:
- Birth and care of newborn child
- Placement of child for adoption or foster care
- Care for spouse, child, or parent with serious health condition
- Employee's own serious health condition
- Qualifying military exigency

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- 12 months of employment
- 1,250 hours worked in preceding 12 months
- Work at location with 50+ employees within 75 miles

**EcoFusion Enhancement:** First 2 weeks of FMLA leave paid at 100% for employees with 1+ years of service.

### 5.5 Parental Leave

In addition to FMLA:
- **Birth Parents**: 8 weeks paid leave at 100% (includes recovery and bonding)
- **Non-Birth Parents/Adoptive**: 4 weeks paid leave at 100%
- May be taken consecutively with FMLA for extended leave
- Available to employees with 1+ years of service

### 5.6 Bereavement Leave

Paid bereavement leave:
- **Immediate Family** (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days
- **Extended Family** (grandparent, grandchild, in-law): 3 days
- **Close Friend/Other**: 1 day

Additional unpaid time may be approved based on circumstances.

### 5.7 Jury Duty

- Employees receive regular pay for jury service (up to 10 days)
- Must provide jury summons documentation
- Expected to work when not actively serving
- Any jury pay received may be retained by employee

### 5.8 Voting Leave

- Up to 2 hours paid leave to vote on Election Day
- Must be scheduled at beginning or end of shift
- Advance notice required

### 5.9 Military Leave

EcoFusion complies with USERRA requirements:
- Unpaid leave for military service
- Job protection for qualifying service
- Continuation of benefits as required by law
- Enhanced benefits for deployed employees (see HR for details)

### 6.5 Benefits During Leave

Benefits continuation during various leave types:
- **FMLA**: Benefits continue; employee pays their portion

## Section 7: Workplace Conduct

### 7.1 Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:
- Act with honesty and integrity
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Follow all company policies and procedures
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- Report concerns through appropriate channels
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Use company resources responsibly

### 7.2 Professional Appearance

EcoFusion maintains a business casual dress code:
- Clean, neat, professional appearance
- Closed-toe shoes required in farm operations
- Safety equipment (PPE) required where specified
- Company-branded apparel encouraged
- Accommodations for religious or medical needs

### 7.3 Anti-Harassment Policy

EcoFusion is committed to a workplace free from harassment. Harassment based on any protected characteristic is prohibited, including:
- Verbal harassment (slurs, jokes, epithets, threats)
- Physical harassment (assault, blocking, unwanted touching)
- Visual harassment (offensive images, gestures, displays)
- Sexual harassment (unwanted advances, requests for favors, inappropriate comments)

**Reporting Harassment:**
1. Report to supervisor, HR, or any member of management
2. Reports may be made anonymously via ethics hotline
3. All reports will be investigated promptly
4. No retaliation for good-faith reports
5. Corrective action taken as appropriate

### 7.4 Anti-Discrimination Policy

Discrimination in any employment decision based on protected characteristics is prohibited. This includes decisions regarding:
- Hiring and firing
- Compensation and benefits
- Promotions and transfers
- Training and development
- Any other terms of employment

### 7.5 Workplace Violence Prevention

EcoFusion has zero tolerance for workplace violence:
- Threats (direct or implied)
- Physical altercations
- Intimidation or bullying
- Possession of weapons on premises
- Damage to property

**Report any concerning behavior immediately.** Violations result in immediate termination and potential legal action.

### 7.6 Substance Abuse Policy

EcoFusion maintains a drug-free workplace:
- Prohibited: Use, possession, sale, or being under the influence of illegal drugs
- Prohibited: Being under the influence of alcohol during work hours
- Pre-employment drug screening required
- Random and for-cause testing may occur
- Prescription medication must not impair job performance

**Employees seeking help for substance abuse are encouraged to contact EAP. Voluntary self-referral before a policy violation is not grounds for discipline.**

### 7.7 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests:
- Outside employment that conflicts with EcoFusion duties
- Financial interests in competitors or vendors
- Personal relationships affecting business decisions
- Use of company position for personal gain

**Disclosure:** Report potential conflicts to HR. Many can be managed with proper disclosure.

### 7.8 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential information including:
- Trade secrets and proprietary processes
- Customer and employee personal data
- Financial information
- Business strategies and plans
- Any information marked "Confidential"

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.

### 7.9 Social Media Policy

When using social media:
- Do not speak on behalf of EcoFusion unless authorized
- Clearly identify personal opinions as your own
- Do not disclose confidential information
- Be respectful; do not disparage company, colleagues, customers
- Do not use social media excessively during work hours

Positive posts about EcoFusion are encouraged but must be truthful.

### 8.1 Acceptable Use of Technology

Company technology (computers, phones, email, internet) is provided for business purposes:
- Limited personal use is permitted if it doesn't interfere with work
- No expectation of privacy on company systems
- Company may monitor usage
- Prohibited: illegal activity, harassment, excessive personal use, unauthorized software

- Use professional language and tone
- Include clear subject lines
- Respond promptly (within 24 business hours)
- Do not send confidential information insecurely
- Use BCC for large distribution lists

### 8.3 Data Security

All employees must:
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Lock computers when away from desk
- Report suspected security incidents immediately

### 8.4 Personal Devices (BYOD)

If using personal devices for work:
- Must be approved by IT

## Section 10: Discipline & Termination

### 10.1 Progressive Discipline

EcoFusion generally follows progressive discipline:
1. **Verbal Warning**: Documented conversation
2. **Written Warning**: Formal written notice
3. **Final Written Warning**: Last chance notice
4. **Termination**: Employment ends

**Note:** Steps may be skipped based on severity. Serious misconduct may result in immediate termination.

### 10.2 Grounds for Immediate Termination

- Theft or dishonesty
- Violence or threats
- Harassment or discrimination
- Possession of weapons
- Illegal drug use/possession
- Gross insubordination
- Serious safety violations
- Breach of confidentiality
- Falsification of records

### 10.3 Resignation

Employees wishing to resign should:
- Provide at least 2 weeks written notice
- Continue performing duties during notice period
- Return all company property
- Participate in exit interview (optional)

### 10.4 Final Pay

- Final paycheck provided per state law
- Includes all earned wages and accrued PTO
- Deductions for unreturned property may apply where legal

### 10.5 Exit Process

Upon separation:
- Return all company property (keys, badges, equipment)
- Complete exit interview with HR
- Provide forwarding address for tax documents
- COBRA information provided for benefits continuation
- References: HR verifies employment dates and title only
