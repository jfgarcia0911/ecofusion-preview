# EcoFusion HR Compliance & Legal Framework
## Comprehensive Employment Law Compliance and Risk Management

---

## Executive Summary

Compliance with employment laws is non-negotiable. This document provides a comprehensive framework for HR legal compliance, covering federal, state, and local employment laws, required postings, documentation requirements, and audit procedures. Built on SHRM and HRCI compliance standards, this framework protects EcoFusion and its employees while maintaining our reputation as an ethical employer.

```
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Understand limitations

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Brainstorm possible accommodations
• Consider employee's preferences
• Research accommodation resources (JAN)
• Evaluate effectiveness of options

STEP 4: EVALUATE UNDUE HARDSHIP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Impact on operations
• Safety considerations
• Nature and structure of operation

STEP 5: IMPLEMENT ACCOMMODATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Select and implement accommodation
• Document the accommodation
• Communicate to relevant parties
• Protect confidentiality

STEP 6: MONITOR AND ADJUST
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Check if accommodation is effective
• Adjust as needed
• Reassess if circumstances change
```

### 3.2 Exempt Classification Tests

**FLSA Exemption Analysis**

```
EXEMPT CLASSIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

To be exempt from overtime, employee must meet ALL three tests:

1. SALARY BASIS TEST
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Paid a predetermined salary
   • Salary not reduced for variations in quality/quantity of work
   • Minimum: $684/week ($35,568/year) - check for updates

2. SALARY LEVEL TEST
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • Salary meets or exceeds minimum threshold
   • Highly compensated employee threshold: $107,432/year

3. DUTIES TEST (one of the following)
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   EXECUTIVE EXEMPTION
   • Primary duty: Managing enterprise or department
   • Customarily directs 2+ employees
   • Authority to hire/fire or significant input

   ADMINISTRATIVE EXEMPTION
   • Primary duty: Office/non-manual work related to management
   • Exercise discretion and independent judgment on significant matters

   PROFESSIONAL EXEMPTION
   • Learned: Advanced knowledge in field of science/learning
   • Creative: Invention, imagination, originality in recognized field

   COMPUTER EMPLOYEE EXEMPTION
   • Systems analyst, programmer, software engineer
   • Specific computer-related duties

   OUTSIDE SALES EXEMPTION
   • Primary duty: Making sales or obtaining contracts
   • Customarily engaged away from employer's place of business
```

### 3.4 Pay Equity Compliance

**Equal Pay Act Requirements**

```
EQUAL PAY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

PROHIBITED: Paying different wages based on sex for equal work

EQUAL WORK DEFINED:
• Equal skill (experience, training, education, ability)
• Equal effort (physical or mental exertion)
• Equal responsibility (accountability, supervision)
• Similar working conditions (physical surroundings, hazards)

PERMITTED DIFFERENTIALS (Affirmative Defenses):
• Seniority system
• Merit system
• System measuring quantity/quality of production
• Differential based on any factor other than sex

COMPLIANCE ACTIONS:
□ Conduct annual pay equity analysis
□ Document legitimate reasons for pay differences
□ Remediate unexplained gaps
□ Train managers on pay decisions
□ Maintain pay decision documentation
```

### 5.2 Injury and Illness Recordkeeping

**OSHA 300 Log Requirements**

```
OSHA RECORDKEEPING DECISION TREE

Work-related injury or illness?
         │
         ├── NO → Not recordable
         │
         └── YES → Did it result in:
                        │
                        ├── Death? → RECORD (report within 8 hrs)
                        │
                        ├── Days away from work? → RECORD
                        │
                        ├── Restricted work/transfer? → RECORD
                        │
                        ├── Medical treatment beyond first aid? → RECORD
                        │
                        ├── Loss of consciousness? → RECORD
                        │
                        ├── Significant injury/illness diagnosed? → RECORD
                        │
                        └── None of the above → NOT RECORDABLE

FIRST AID vs. MEDICAL TREATMENT:
First aid (not recordable): Bandages, cold packs, non-prescription meds, etc.
Medical treatment (recordable): Sutures, prescription meds, physical therapy, etc.
```

## Part 6: Immigration Compliance

### 6.1 I-9 Compliance

**Form I-9 Requirements**

```
I-9 COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

SECTION 1 (Employee - Day 1)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Employee completes Section 1 no later than first day of work
□ All fields completed (N/A if not applicable)
□ Employee signs and dates

SECTION 2 (Employer - Within 3 business days of start)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Examine original documents from Lists A, B, and C
□ Documents appear genuine and relate to employee
□ Record document information in Section 2
□ Sign and date Section 2

DOCUMENT RULES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Accept any valid document(s) from the lists
• Cannot specify which documents to provide
• Cannot request more documents than required
• Cannot reject valid documents
• Make copies (recommended but not required unless E-Verify)

REVERIFICATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Track document expiration dates (employment authorization only)
□ Reverify before expiration (Section 3)
□ Do not reverify: US Citizens, permanent residents, List B documents

RETENTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Retain I-9 for 3 years after hire OR 1 year after termination
   (whichever is later)
□ Store separately from personnel files (recommended)
□ Make available for inspection upon request
```

```
□ Pull list of all current employees

□ Create audit spreadsheet

□ Section 1 complete?
□ Section 1 signed and dated?
□ Section 2 complete within 3 days of hire?
□ Documents from correct list(s)?
□ Document information complete?
□ Employer signed Section 2?

□ Do NOT backdate
```
