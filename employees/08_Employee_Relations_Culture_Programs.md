# EcoFusion Employee Relations & Culture Programs
## Building an Engaged, Mission-Driven Workforce

---

## Executive Summary

Culture is the foundation of everything we do at EcoFusion. This document outlines our comprehensive approach to employee relations, engagement, wellness, and culture-building. A strong culture attracts talent, drives retention, improves performance, and ultimately enables us to achieve our mission of revolutionizing sustainable agriculture. These programs are designed to create an environment where every employee feels valued, connected, and inspired to do their best work.

### 3.3 Conflict Resolution

**Conflict Resolution Approaches**

| Approach | When to Use | Process |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct Conversation** | Minor interpersonal issues | Parties discuss directly |
| **Facilitated Discussion** | Moderate conflicts | Manager/HR facilitates |
| **Mediation** | Significant conflicts | Neutral third party mediates |
| **Management Decision** | When parties can't agree | Manager makes decision |
| **Investigation** | Allegations of misconduct | Formal investigation |

**Facilitated Discussion Guide**

```
CONFLICT RESOLUTION MEETING STRUCTURE

OPENING (5 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Set ground rules (respect, listening, no interrupting)
• State purpose: "We're here to understand each other and find resolution"
• Clarify confidentiality

EACH PARTY'S PERSPECTIVE (10-15 min each)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Each person shares their perspective uninterrupted
• Facilitator summarizes what they heard
• Clarifying questions only

IDENTIFY COMMON GROUND (10 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• What do both parties agree on?
• What shared interests exist?
• What do both want for the working relationship?

GENERATE OPTIONS (15 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Brainstorm possible solutions
• No evaluation during brainstorming
• Build on each other's ideas

AGREE ON RESOLUTION (10 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Evaluate options together
• Agree on specific actions
• Clarify who does what by when

CLOSE (5 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Summarize agreements
• Schedule follow-up
• Thank participants for engagement
```

### 3.4 Grievance Procedure

**Formal Grievance Process**

| Step | Timeline | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Step 1: Written Complaint** | Within 30 days of issue | Employee submits written grievance to HR |
| **Step 2: HR Review** | 5 business days | HR acknowledges, begins review |
| **Step 3: Investigation** | 10 business days | HR investigates as needed |
| **Step 4: Response** | 5 business days after investigation | Written response provided |
| **Step 5: Appeal** | 10 business days after response | Employee may appeal to CEO |
| **Step 6: Final Decision** | 10 business days | CEO's decision is final |

### 4.4 Recognition Best Practices

**For Managers - Recognition Guidelines**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Be specific about what you're recognizing | Give generic "good job" feedback |
| Recognize publicly when appropriate | Embarrass introverts with unwanted spotlight |
| Be timely - recognize soon after the action | Wait until annual review |
| Personalize recognition to the individual | Give same recognition to everyone |
| Recognize effort, not just results | Only recognize "winners" |
| Include peer recognition, not just top-down | Hoard recognition for yourself |
| Make it genuine and heartfelt | Go through the motions |

### 9.2 Exit Interview
