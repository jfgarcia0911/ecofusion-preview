# EcoFusion Performance Management Framework
## Comprehensive Performance Development and Evaluation System

---

## Executive Summary

Performance management at EcoFusion is a continuous process focused on developing employees, aligning individual contributions with company goals, and creating a high-performance culture. This framework moves beyond traditional annual reviews to embrace ongoing feedback, goal alignment, and developmental conversations. Built on SHRM best practices and performance management research, this system drives both business results and employee growth.

### 2.2 SMART Goal Framework

**SMART Criteria**

| Element | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **S**pecific | Clear and well-defined | "Increase lettuce yield per square foot" |
| **M**easurable | Quantifiable metrics | "from 8 lbs to 10 lbs per sq ft" |
| **A**chievable | Realistic and attainable | "based on new LED system implementation" |
| **R**elevant | Aligned with role and company | "supports revenue and efficiency goals" |
| **T**ime-bound | Clear deadline | "by end of Q3" |

**Goal Categories**

| Category | Description | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Performance Goals** | Results and deliverables for the role | 60% |
| **Behavioral Goals** | How work is done (values, competencies) | 20% |
| **Development Goals** | Skills and capabilities to build | 20% |

### 3.2 Feedback Framework

**SBI-I Feedback Model**

```
FEEDBACK MODEL: SBI-I

S - SITUATION
│   When and where did the behavior occur?
│   "In yesterday's team meeting..."
│
B - BEHAVIOR
│   What specific behavior was observed?
│   "...when you presented the production data..."
│
I - IMPACT
│   What was the effect of the behavior?
│   "...it helped the team clearly understand our progress,
│    and led to a productive discussion."
│
I - INVITATION
    What should happen next?
    "I'd like to see you take on more presentations in the future.
     How do you feel about that?"

EXAMPLE - CONSTRUCTIVE:
────────────────────────
S: "In this morning's customer call..."
B: "...I noticed you interrupted the customer several times
    before they finished speaking..."
I: "...which may have made them feel unheard and could
    impact our relationship."
I: "How do you think we could approach these calls differently
    next time?"

EXAMPLE - POSITIVE:
───────────────────
S: "During the harvest this week..."
B: "...you noticed the quality issue with the basil batch
    and immediately flagged it..."
I: "...which prevented us from shipping substandard product
    and protected our reputation."
I: "This kind of quality focus is exactly what we need.
    Keep it up!"
```

**Feedback Frequency Guidelines**

| Constructive guidance | As needed, timely | Informal |
| 1:1 feedback | Weekly | Semi-formal |

### 3.3 Performance Coaching

**GROW Coaching Model**

```
GROW COACHING FRAMEWORK

G - GOAL
│   What do you want to achieve?
│   • What is the outcome you're looking for?
│   • What would success look like?
│   • How will you know when you've achieved it?
│
R - REALITY
│   What is the current situation?
│   • Where are you now relative to the goal?
│   • What have you tried so far?
│   • What's working? What's not?
│
O - OPTIONS
│   What could you do?
│   • What options do you have?
│   • What else could you try?
│   • What would happen if...?
│   • Who could help?
│
W - WAY FORWARD
    What will you do?
    • What's your next step?
    • When will you do it?
    • What support do you need?
    • How will you stay accountable?
```

### 4.6 Calibration Process

**Calibration Session Guidelines**

```
CALIBRATION SESSION PROCESS

PURPOSE:
• Ensure consistent rating standards across managers
• Identify high performers and development needs
• Reduce bias in ratings
• Make informed compensation decisions

PREPARATION:
• Each manager brings proposed ratings with justification
• HR prepares ratings distribution analysis
• Leadership reviews company performance context

SESSION FORMAT (90-120 min):
────────────────────────────

1. CONTEXT SETTING (10 min)
   • Company/department performance overview
   • Calibration guidelines review
   • Target distribution reminder

2. EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMERS (20 min)
   • Discuss proposed 5-ratings
   • Verify criteria met with specific examples
   • Agree on final 5-ratings

3. EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS (20 min)
   • Discuss proposed 4-ratings
   • Differentiate from 3s and 5s
   • Agree on final 4-ratings

4. DEVELOPING/DOES NOT MEET (20 min)
   • Discuss proposed 1s and 2s
   • Ensure documentation and action plans
   • Agree on final 1s and 2s

5. MEETS EXPECTATIONS (15 min)
   • Review remaining 3-ratings
   • Identify any to move up or down
   • Finalize 3-ratings

6. FINAL DISTRIBUTION CHECK (10 min)
   • Compare to guidelines
   • Make final adjustments
   • Agree on final ratings

7. COMPENSATION RECOMMENDATIONS (15 min)
   • Review merit increase pool
   • Discuss recommendations
   • Identify promotions

OUTPUT:
• Finalized ratings
• Compensation recommendations
• Documented rationale for 1s, 2s, 5s
```

## Part 7: Manager Training

### 7.1 Performance Management Training Curriculum

| Module | Topics | Duration | Audience |
|--------|--------|----------|----------|
| **PM Fundamentals** | Philosophy, cycle, tools | 2 hours | All managers |
| **Goal Setting** | SMART goals, alignment, documentation | 1.5 hours | All managers |
| **Feedback & Coaching** | SBI-I, GROW, 1:1s | 2 hours | All managers |
| **Conducting Reviews** | Preparation, conversation, documentation | 2 hours | All managers |
| **Difficult Conversations** | Addressing issues, PIPs, terminations | 2 hours | All managers |
| **Calibration Training** | Rating consistency, bias reduction | 1 hour | All managers |
| **Legal Compliance** | Documentation, discrimination, retaliation | 1 hour | All managers |

### 7.2 Manager Competencies for Performance Management

| Competency | Behaviors |
|------------|-----------|
| **Sets Clear Expectations** | Communicates goals clearly; ensures understanding |
| **Provides Ongoing Feedback** | Gives regular, specific, actionable feedback |
| **Coaches for Development** | Helps employees grow and develop skills |
| **Recognizes Contributions** | Acknowledges good work regularly |
| **Addresses Issues Promptly** | Does not avoid difficult conversations |
| **Evaluates Fairly** | Uses objective criteria; reduces bias |
| **Documents Appropriately** | Keeps records; follows processes |
