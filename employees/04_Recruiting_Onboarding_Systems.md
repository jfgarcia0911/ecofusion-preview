# EcoFusion Recruiting & Onboarding Systems
## Talent Acquisition Strategy and New Hire Integration Framework

---

## Executive Summary

World-class talent acquisition and onboarding are critical to EcoFusion's success. This document provides comprehensive recruiting strategies, hiring processes, and onboarding systems designed to attract top talent, ensure hiring excellence, and rapidly integrate new employees into our culture and operations. Built on SHRM best practices and ATD talent development principles, these systems position EcoFusion as an employer of choice in the sustainable agriculture sector.

### 1.4 Employee Referral Program

**Program Structure**

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Referral Bonus | $1,000 for professional roles; $500 for hourly roles |
| Payment Timing | 50% at hire, 50% after 90 days |
| Eligibility | All employees except those involved in hiring decision |
| No Cap | Unlimited referrals per employee |
| Special Bonuses | $1,500 for hard-to-fill or critical roles |
| Recognition | "Talent Scout" recognition for top referrers |

**Referral Process**

1. Employee submits referral via HR portal
2. HR acknowledges within 24 hours
3. Referred candidate receives priority screening
4. Employee updated on status at key milestones
5. Bonus processed after eligibility met

### 2.3 Interview Process

**Standard Interview Process**

```
INTERVIEW STAGES

STAGE 1: PHONE SCREEN (HR)
├── Duration: 20-30 minutes
├── Purpose: Basic qualification, fit, logistics
├── Decision: Advance / Decline
└── Timeline: Within 48 hours of application

STAGE 2: HIRING MANAGER INTERVIEW
├── Duration: 45-60 minutes
├── Purpose: Technical skills, experience depth
├── Format: Video or in-person
├── Decision: Advance / Decline
└── Timeline: Within 1 week of phone screen

STAGE 3: PANEL/TEAM INTERVIEW
├── Duration: 60-90 minutes
├── Purpose: Culture fit, cross-functional assessment
├── Panel: 2-3 team members
├── Decision: Advance / Decline
└── Timeline: Within 1 week of manager interview

STAGE 4: FINAL INTERVIEW (Leadership)
├── Duration: 30-45 minutes
├── Purpose: Values alignment, final assessment
├── Interviewer: Department head or executive
├── Decision: Hire / Decline
└── Timeline: Within 1 week of panel interview

STAGE 5: OFFER
├── Reference checks completed
├── Background check initiated
├── Offer extended within 48 hours of final interview
└── Decision deadline: 3-5 business days
```

### 2.5 Reference and Background Checks

**Reference Check Process**

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Request 3 professional references from candidate | With final interview |
| 2 | Obtain signed reference check authorization | With final interview |
| 3 | HR conducts reference calls | Within 48 hours |
| 4 | Document responses on standard form | Same day |
| 5 | Share summary with hiring manager | Same day |
| 6 | Make final determination | Within 24 hours |

**Reference Check Questions**

1. How do you know [candidate] and in what capacity did you work together?
2. What were their main responsibilities?
3. How would you describe their work quality and reliability?
4. How did they handle challenges or pressure?
5. How did they work with others on the team?
6. What are their strengths? Areas for development?
7. Would you rehire them? Why or why not?
8. Is there anything else I should know?

**Background Check Components**

| Check Type | Positions | Timeline |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| SSN Verification | All | 1-2 days |
| Criminal History | All | 2-5 days |
| Employment Verification | All | 3-5 days |
| Education Verification | Professional+ | 3-5 days |
| Credit Check | Finance roles only | 2-3 days |
| Driving Record | Delivery/driving roles | 1-2 days |
| Drug Screening | All | 1-3 days |

### 2.6 Offer Process

**Offer Development Checklist**

- [ ] Confirm budget approval
- [ ] Determine salary based on range, experience, internal equity
- [ ] Confirm start date availability
- [ ] Prepare offer letter
- [ ] HR review for compliance
- [ ] Manager approval
- [ ] Final approval (VP/CEO for senior roles)

**Offer Letter Components**

1. Position title and reporting relationship
2. Start date
3. Compensation (salary, bonus target, commission if applicable)
4. Equity grant (if applicable)
5. Benefits summary
6. At-will statement
7. Contingencies (background check, I-9)
8. Response deadline
9. Signature lines

**Offer Negotiation Guidelines**

| Element | Flexibility | Approval |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Base Salary | ±10% of initial offer | Manager/HR |
| Signing Bonus | Case by case | VP approval |
| Start Date | Generally flexible | Manager |
| Title | Limited (must match level) | HR |
| Equity | ±20% of guidelines | VP/CEO |
| PTO | Generally not negotiable | VP approval for exceptions |
