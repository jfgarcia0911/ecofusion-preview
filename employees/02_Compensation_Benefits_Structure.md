# EcoFusion Compensation & Benefits Structure
## Total Rewards Philosophy and Framework

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines EcoFusion's comprehensive total rewards strategy, designed to attract, retain, and motivate top talent while maintaining fiscal responsibility. Our compensation philosophy balances market competitiveness with internal equity, performance recognition, and alignment with company values. This framework incorporates best practices from WorldatWork's Total Rewards model and is designed to scale as EcoFusion grows.

### 1.2 Compensation Philosophy Statement

EcoFusion's compensation philosophy is built on five pillars:

| Pillar | Description | Application |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Market Competitive** | Pay at or above market median | Target 50th-60th percentile for base, 75th for total compensation |
| **Performance Driven** | Reward high performers | Variable pay tied to individual and company performance |
| **Internally Equitable** | Fair pay across similar roles | Job evaluation, pay grades, transparent ranges |
| **Fiscally Responsible** | Sustainable compensation costs | Target 30-35% of revenue for total labor costs |
| **Mission Aligned** | Reward what matters | Incentives tied to sustainability and quality metrics |

### 1.4 Pay Transparency Commitment

EcoFusion is committed to pay transparency:
- All job postings include salary ranges
- Employees may discuss compensation freely (NLRA protected)
- Pay ranges available for all positions
- Annual pay equity audits conducted
- Promotion and adjustment criteria clearly communicated

### 2.4 Salary Administration

**New Hire Offers**
- External candidates: Generally 85-100% of midpoint

- Exceptional candidates: Up to 110% of midpoint with VP approval

**Annual Increase Process**
- Review cycle: January 1st effective date
- Performance reviews completed by November 15th
- Manager recommendations due December 1st
- Leadership calibration: December 10-15
- Communication to employees: December 20th
- Increases effective: January 1st

**Increase Guidelines**

## Part 3: Variable Compensation

### 3.1 Annual Incentive Plan (AIP)

**Eligibility and Target Bonuses**

| Level | Position Type | Target Bonus (% of Base) |
|-------|---------------|--------------------------|
| 1-2 | Individual contributors | 5% |
| 3-4 | Senior individual contributors | 10% |
| 5 | Managers | 15% |
| 6 | Directors | 20% |
| 7 | Vice Presidents | 25% |
| 8 | Executives | 30-50% |

**Bonus Structure**

```
ANNUAL INCENTIVE CALCULATION

Total Bonus = Base Salary × Target % × (Company Factor × 60% + Individual Factor × 40%)

COMPANY PERFORMANCE FACTOR (60% weight):
────────────────────────────────────────
Revenue Achievement      (30%)
EBITDA Achievement       (20%)
Sustainability Goals     (10%)

Performance Level        Factor
─────────────────────────────────
Below Threshold (<80%)   0.0x
Threshold (80%)          0.5x
Target (100%)            1.0x
Maximum (120%+)          2.0x

INDIVIDUAL PERFORMANCE FACTOR (40% weight):
────────────────────────────────────────────
Based on performance review rating

Rating                   Factor
─────────────────────────────────
Exceeds Expectations     1.25x-1.5x
Meets Expectations       1.0x
Developing               0.5x
Does Not Meet            0.0x

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
Base: $70,000 | Target: 15% | Company: 110% (1.2x) | Individual: Exceeds (1.3x)
Bonus = $70,000 × 15% × (1.2 × 60% + 1.3 × 40%) = $10,500 × (0.72 + 0.52) = $13,020
```

### 3.4 Profit Sharing

- Pool funded at 10% of profits above plan
- Distributed to all employees based on:
  - 50% equally
  - 50% proportional to salary
- Paid annually in Q1 for prior year
- Minimum 1 year employment to participate

## Part 4: Equity Compensation

### 4.1 Equity Philosophy

EcoFusion believes employees should share in the value they create. Equity compensation:
- Creates ownership mentality
- Aligns employee and shareholder interests
- Rewards long-term commitment
- Attracts talent who believe in the mission

### 4.2 Equity Grant Guidelines

**New Hire Grants (Stock Options or RSUs)**

| Level | Title | Equity Value (% of base) | Vesting |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|---------|
| 1-2 | Entry/Associate | 10-15% | 4 years |
| 3-4 | Specialist/Senior | 15-25% | 4 years |
| 5 | Manager | 25-40% | 4 years |
| 6 | Director | 40-60% | 4 years |
| 7 | VP | 60-100% | 4 years |
| 8 | Executive | 100-200%+ | 4 years |

**Vesting Schedule**
- Standard: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- Year 1: 25% vests at 1-year anniversary
- Years 2-4: Monthly vesting (2.08%/month)

### 4.3 Refresh Grants

Annual equity refresh for strong performers:
- Eligibility: 1+ year tenure, Meets Expectations or higher
- Typical value: 25-50% of new hire grant size
- Performance modifier: 0.5x to 1.5x based on rating
- Vesting: 4 years, monthly

### 4.4 Equity Administration

**Exercise and Settlement**
- Options: 10-year exercise window (typical)
- Post-termination exercise: 90 days (most departures)
- Early exercise: Available for options (with 83(b) election)
- RSU settlement: Shares delivered upon vesting

**Tax Considerations**
- ISO vs. NSO treatment explained during grant
- 83(b) election guidance provided
- Company does not provide tax advice; consult professional

## Part 6: Pay Equity & Compliance

### 6.1 Pay Equity Analysis

**Annual Pay Equity Audit Process**

```
PAY EQUITY REVIEW CYCLE

Q1: Data Collection
├── Pull compensation data for all employees
├── Standardize job levels and titles
└── Identify comparison groups

Q2: Statistical Analysis
├── Regression analysis controlling for:
│   ├── Job level
│   ├── Experience/tenure
│   ├── Performance history
│   ├── Education/certifications
│   └── Geographic location
├── Identify unexplained gaps by:
│   ├── Gender
│   ├── Race/ethnicity
│   └── Age
└── Document findings

Q3: Remediation
├── Investigate individual cases
├── Develop adjustment recommendations
├── Budget for adjustments
└── Implement approved adjustments

Q4: Monitoring & Reporting
├── Track metrics over time
├── Report to leadership
├── Refine processes
└── Prepare for next cycle
```

**Pay Equity Targets**
- Unexplained gap <3% for any protected group
- Zero tolerance for statistically significant disparities
- Remediation within 90 days of identification
