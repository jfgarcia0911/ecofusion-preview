### Module 2: Hydraulic System Design (1 hour)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Apply fluid dynamics principles
- Calculate head loss and pump sizing
- Design efficient piping systems

**Content Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Type | Materials |
|------|----------|------|-----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Water flow demonstration | Demo | Physical demo |
| 0:05-0:25 | Lecture: Hydraulic principles | Presentation | Slides 21-45 |
| 0:25-0:40 | Calculation workshop: Head loss | Math | Worksheets |
| 0:40-0:50 | Pump selection exercise | Activity | Pump curves |
| 0:50-0:55 | Q&A | Interactive | Discussion |
| 0:55-1:00 | Module summary | Wrap-up | Formulas |

**Key Concepts:**

**Total Dynamic Head (TDH) Calculation:**

```
TDH = Static Head + Friction Head + Pressure Head

Where:
- Static Head = Vertical lift (ft or m)
- Friction Head = Energy lost to pipe friction
- Pressure Head = Operating pressure requirements

Example Calculation:
System Parameters:
- Fish tank water level to grow bed: 4 ft vertical
- Piping: 100 ft of 2" PVC, 6 elbows, 2 tees
- Required flow: 50 GPM
- Biofilter pressure drop: 2 ft

Static Head = 4 ft
Friction Head (from charts) = 3.2 ft per 100 ft at 50 GPM in 2" PVC
Fitting losses: (6 × 0.6) + (2 × 0.8) = 5.2 ft equivalent
Total friction = 3.2 + (5.2 × 0.032) = 3.37 ft
Pressure Head = 2 ft

TDH = 4 + 3.37 + 2 = 9.37 ft

Pump Selection:
Select pump delivering 50 GPM at 9.37 ft head (add 20% safety factor)
Target: 50 GPM @ 11.2 ft TDH
```

**Pipe Sizing Guidelines:**

| Flow Rate (GPM) | Recommended Pipe Size | Max Velocity (ft/s) |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| 5-15 | 1" | 4-5 |
| 15-30 | 1.5" | 4-5 |
| 30-60 | 2" | 5-6 |
| 60-100 | 3" | 5-6 |
| 100-200 | 4" | 6-7 |

**Pump Types for Aquaponics:**

| Type | Best For | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|------|----------|------------|---------------|
| **Submersible** | Small-medium systems | Quiet, easy install | Heat transfer, access |
| **External/Inline** | Large systems | Efficient, serviceable | Priming, noise |
| **Air Lift** | Low head applications | No moving parts, aeration | Low efficiency |

**Key Concepts:**

**Hydrostatic Pressure:**

```
P = ρgh

Where:
P = Pressure (Pa or lb/ft²)
ρ = Fluid density (kg/m³ or lb/ft³)
g = Gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s² or 32.2 ft/s²)
h = Depth (m or ft)

Example:
Water depth = 4 ft
Water density = 62.4 lb/ft³
P = 62.4 × 4 = 249.6 lb/ft² = 1.73 PSI at bottom
```

**Tank Material Comparison:**

### Module 4: Biofilter Engineering (1 hour)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Size biofilters for ammonia load
- Design for optimal bacterial performance
- Integrate biofilters into system hydraulics

**Content Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Type | Materials |
|------|----------|------|-----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Biofilter types comparison | Interactive | Images |
| 0:05-0:25 | Lecture: Biofilter engineering | Presentation | Slides 66-85 |
| 0:25-0:40 | Sizing calculations | Math | Worksheets |
| 0:40-0:50 | Design comparison activity | Analysis | Case studies |
| 0:50-0:55 | Q&A | Interactive | Discussion |
| 0:55-1:00 | Module summary | Wrap-up | Reference |

**Key Concepts:**

**Biofilter Sizing Methodology:**

```
Step 1: Calculate Ammonia Load
Daily Feed = 10 kg
Protein Content = 35%
TAN Production = Feed × Protein × 0.092
TAN = 10 × 0.35 × 0.092 = 0.322 kg TAN/day

Step 2: Determine Nitrification Rate
Typical rate = 0.5-1.0 g TAN/m²/day (depends on media, conditions)
Use conservative: 0.5 g TAN/m²/day

Step 3: Calculate Required Surface Area
Required Area = (0.322 kg × 1000 g/kg) / 0.5 g/m²/day
Required Area = 644 m²

Step 4: Select Media and Calculate Volume
Kaldnes K1 = 500 m²/m³
Required Volume = 644 / 500 = 1.29 m³ of media

Step 5: Add Safety Factor (50%)
Design Volume = 1.29 × 1.5 = 1.93 m³ of media
```

**Biofilter Types & Applications:**

| Type | Surface Area | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|------|--------------|------|------|----------|
| **Moving Bed (MBBR)** | 500-800 m²/m³ | Self-cleaning, efficient | Requires air, media cost | Commercial |
| **Trickling Filter** | 100-200 m²/m³ | Simple, passive | Large footprint | Greenhouse |
| **Fluidized Bed** | 1000+ m²/m³ | Compact, efficient | Complex, high energy | High-density |
| **Media Bed** | 100-300 m²/m³ | Dual purpose, simple | Size limitations | Home/small |
| **Bead Filter** | 200-400 m²/m³ | Mechanical + bio | Cost, backwash | Medium-large |

### Module 5: Environmental Systems Integration (1 hour)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Design integrated HVAC systems
- Size heating and cooling equipment
- Calculate ventilation requirements

**Content Outline:**

| Time | Activity | Type | Materials |
|------|----------|------|-----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Climate control challenge | Interactive | Scenario |
| 0:05-0:25 | Lecture: Environmental engineering | Presentation | Slides 86-110 |
| 0:25-0:40 | Heat load calculations | Math | Worksheets |
| 0:40-0:50 | System integration exercise | Activity | Diagrams |
| 0:50-0:55 | Q&A | Interactive | Discussion |
| 0:55-1:00 | Module summary | Wrap-up | Formulas |

**Key Concepts:**

**Heat Load Calculation:**

```
Total Heat Load = Qsolar + Qconduction + Qinfiltration + Qinternal + Qmetabolic

Qsolar = Solar radiation through glazing
Qconduction = Heat transfer through walls/roof
Qinfiltration = Air exchange heat
Qinternal = Lights, equipment, pumps
Qmetabolic = Plant and fish respiration

Example: 5,000 sq ft greenhouse, winter
Qconduction = U × A × ΔT
U (double poly) = 0.7 BTU/hr/ft²/°F
A (surface area) = 8,000 ft²
ΔT (inside 70°F, outside 20°F) = 50°F

Qconduction = 0.7 × 8,000 × 50 = 280,000 BTU/hr

Heating System Size = 280,000 × 1.25 (safety) = 350,000 BTU/hr
```

**Ventilation Requirements:**

| Condition | Air Changes per Hour | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Minimum winter | 0.5-1 | Humidity control |
| Normal operations | 1-2 | Temperature, CO2 |
| Summer cooling | 40-60 | When evaporative cooling |
| Emergency | 1 per minute | Fire, chemical spill |
