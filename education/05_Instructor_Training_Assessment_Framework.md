### Developing World-Class Educators

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Related Courses:** 74 courses across 5 series (100-500)

## Executive Summary

The quality of EcoFusion Academy education depends entirely on the excellence of our instructors. This comprehensive framework establishes the standards, training programs, and assessment methods that ensure every EcoFusion instructor delivers transformative learning experiences. Built upon research from the world's most effective educators, this framework creates a pathway for instructor development from novice to master level.

## Part 2: Instructor Training Programs

### 2.1 Assistant Instructor Training (16 hours)

**Program Description:**
Entry-level training for those beginning their journey as EcoFusion Academy instructors. Covers fundamental teaching skills and EcoFusion standards.

**Curriculum:**

| Module | Hours | Topics |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **Orientation** | 2 | EcoFusion mission, educational philosophy, instructor role |
| **Learning Fundamentals** | 2 | How adults learn, attention, retention |
| **Classroom Presence** | 2 | Voice, body language, presence, confidence |
| **Assisting Techniques** | 2 | Supporting lead instructor, small group facilitation |
| **Assessment Basics** | 2 | Grading, feedback, consistency |
| **Technology** | 2 | LMS, video conferencing, digital tools |
| **Safety & Compliance** | 2 | Food safety, lab safety, liability |
| **Practicum** | 2 | Observed micro-teaching and feedback |

**Assessment:**
- Knowledge exam (50 questions, ≥80%)
- Micro-teaching observation (pass/remediate)
- Professionalism evaluation

### 2.2 Instructor Certification Program (40 hours)

**Program Description:**
Comprehensive certification program for independent instructors. Based on research from Doug Lemov (Teach Like a Champion), John Hattie (Visible Learning), and Eric Mazur (Peer Instruction).

**Curriculum:**

#### Week 1: Foundations of Effective Teaching (10 hours)

| Module | Hours | Topics |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 1.1 Learning Science | 2 | Memory, cognitive load, retrieval practice |
| 1.2 Adult Learning Principles | 2 | Andragogy, motivation, self-direction |
| 1.3 Instructional Design | 2 | Backward design, objectives, alignment |
| 1.4 EcoFusion Standards | 2 | Course structure, materials, quality |
| 1.5 Knowing Your Learners | 2 | Needs assessment, differentiation |

#### Week 2: Instructional Delivery (10 hours)

| Module | Hours | Topics |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 2.1 Presentation Skills | 2 | Voice, pacing, visual aids, storytelling |
| 2.2 Engagement Techniques | 2 | Active learning, questioning, discussions |
| 2.3 Demonstration Mastery | 2 | Live demos, hands-on activities |
| 2.4 Classroom Management | 2 | Time, participation, disruptions |
| 2.5 Virtual Teaching | 2 | Online engagement, technology, presence |

#### Week 3: Assessment & Feedback (10 hours)

| Module | Hours | Topics |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 3.1 Assessment Design | 2 | Types, validity, reliability |
| 3.2 Formative Assessment | 2 | Checking for understanding, adjustment |
| 3.3 Providing Feedback | 2 | Effective feedback, growth mindset |
| 3.4 Grading & Rubrics | 2 | Consistency, calibration, records |
| 3.5 Using Data | 2 | Analytics, improvement, reporting |

#### Week 4: Practice & Certification (10 hours)

| Module | Hours | Topics |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 4.1 Teaching Practice I | 2 | 30-minute teaching with peer feedback |
| 4.2 Teaching Practice II | 2 | 30-minute teaching with expert feedback |

| 4.5 Certification Assessment | 2 | Panel evaluation, certification decision |

**Assessment Components:**

| Component | Weight | Criteria |
|-----------|--------|----------|

## Part 3: Teaching Observation & Evaluation

### 3.1 Observation Framework

**The EcoFusion Teaching Observation Protocol**

Observations are developmental, not punitive. The goal is continuous improvement toward teaching excellence.

**Observation Schedule:**

| Instructor Level | Frequency | Observers |
|------------------|-----------|-----------|
| L1 Assistant | Quarterly | L2+ Instructor |
| L2 Instructor | Bi-annually | L3+ Senior |
| L3 Senior | Annually | L4 Master |
| L4 Master | Bi-annually | Peer L4/L5 |
| L5 Director | Annually | External/peer |

**Pre-Observation Conference:**
- Review lesson plan and objectives
- Discuss areas of focus
- Clarify expectations

**Observation Conduct:**
- Observer remains unobtrusive
- Complete observation form
- Document specific examples
- Note both strengths and growth areas

**Post-Observation Conference:**
- Schedule within 48 hours
- Begin with instructor self-reflection
- Share observations with evidence
- Collaborate on improvement actions
- Document agreements

---

### 3.2 Teaching Observation Rubric

**EcoFusion Teaching Excellence Rubric**

Each dimension is scored on a 4-point scale:
- **4 = Exemplary:** Consistently exceeds expectations, model for others
- **3 = Proficient:** Meets expectations reliably
- **2 = Developing:** Shows progress, inconsistent
- **1 = Emerging:** Significant improvement needed

---

#### Dimension 1: Content Mastery (20%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Deep expertise evident; incorporates current research/industry; answers complex questions with authority; provides additional depth when appropriate |
| **3** | Solid content knowledge; accurate information; answers most questions effectively |
| **2** | Adequate knowledge; occasional inaccuracies; struggles with complex questions |
| **1** | Content gaps evident; inaccuracies; unable to extend beyond materials |

---

#### Dimension 2: Instructional Planning (15%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Clear, measurable objectives aligned with outcomes; excellent pacing; seamless transitions; adaptive to learner needs |
| **3** | Clear objectives; good pacing; logical flow; appropriate for audience |
| **2** | Objectives present but unclear; pacing issues; some disconnected elements |
| **1** | Missing or unclear objectives; poor pacing; disorganized |

---

#### Dimension 3: Engagement & Interaction (25%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Highly interactive throughout; all learners actively engaged; excellent questioning; effective discussion facilitation; enthusiasm infectious |
| **3** | Regular interaction; good participation levels; effective questioning; maintains interest |
| **2** | Some interaction; uneven participation; limited questioning; some disengagement |
| **1** | Primarily lecture; limited interaction; poor participation; bored learners |

---

#### Dimension 4: Clarity of Communication (15%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Crystal clear explanations; excellent use of examples and analogies; checks for understanding continuously; adapts explanations as needed |
| **3** | Clear explanations; good examples; checks understanding |
| **2** | Generally understandable; limited examples; minimal comprehension checking |
| **1** | Confusing explanations; lack of examples; doesn't check understanding |

---

#### Dimension 5: Assessment & Feedback (10%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Continuous formative assessment; immediate, specific feedback; uses data to adjust instruction; promotes reflection |
| **3** | Regular assessment; helpful feedback; responds to results |
| **2** | Occasional assessment; general feedback; limited adjustment |
| **1** | Minimal assessment; no meaningful feedback |

---

#### Dimension 6: Learning Environment (10%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Exceptionally positive, inclusive atmosphere; strong rapport; celebrates all learners; psychologically safe |
| **3** | Positive atmosphere; good rapport; respectful environment |
| **2** | Generally positive; some rapport; occasional tension |
| **1** | Negative or tense atmosphere; poor rapport; disrespectful |

---

#### Dimension 7: Professionalism (5%)

| Score | Indicators |
|-------|------------|
| **4** | Exemplary professional conduct; prepared; punctual; responsive; represents EcoFusion excellently |
| **3** | Professional conduct; prepared; punctual; appropriate |
| **2** | Generally professional; occasionally unprepared or late |
| **1** | Unprofessional conduct; frequently unprepared or late |

## Part 4: Learner Feedback Systems

### 4.1 End-of-Course Evaluations

**Standard Evaluation Form:**

All courses collect learner feedback using a standardized instrument.

**Section A: Course Evaluation (1-5 scale)**

| Question | Rating |
|----------|--------|
| The course met my learning expectations | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The content was relevant to my needs | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The course was well-organized | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The pace was appropriate | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The hands-on activities were valuable | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The materials supported my learning | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| I would recommend this course to others | 1 2 3 4 5 |

**Section B: Instructor Evaluation (1-5 scale)**

| Question | Rating |
|----------|--------|
| The instructor demonstrated expertise in the subject | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor explained concepts clearly | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor was engaging and held my attention | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor encouraged participation | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor answered questions effectively | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor treated all learners respectfully | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| The instructor was well-prepared | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| Overall, this instructor was effective | 1 2 3 4 5 |

**Section C: Open Response**

1. What were the strengths of this course/instructor?
2. What suggestions do you have for improvement?
3. What additional topics would you like covered?
4. Any other comments?

**Section D: Net Promoter Score**

"On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend EcoFusion Academy to a colleague?"

## Part 7: Instructor Code of Conduct

### 7.1 Professional Standards

**EcoFusion Academy Instructor Code of Conduct**

All instructors agree to uphold these standards:

**Integrity:**
- Represent information honestly and accurately
- Acknowledge limitations in knowledge
- Give proper credit to sources and contributors
- Maintain confidentiality of learner information
- Avoid conflicts of interest

**Excellence:**
- Prepare thoroughly for every teaching engagement
- Continuously improve teaching skills
- Stay current in subject matter
- Meet all professional responsibilities
- Respond promptly to learner needs

**Respect:**
- Treat all learners with dignity and respect
- Create inclusive learning environments
- Value diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- Provide equitable attention and support
- Accept feedback gracefully

**Safety:**
- Prioritize physical safety in all activities
- Create psychologically safe learning environments
- Report safety concerns immediately
- Follow all safety protocols and procedures

**Professionalism:**
- Represent EcoFusion Academy positively
- Maintain appropriate boundaries with learners
- Dress and conduct appropriately
- Be punctual and reliable
- Communicate professionally

---

### 7.2 Violations & Consequences

**Categories of Violations:**

| Category | Examples | Typical Consequence |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| **Minor** | Late to class, incomplete preparation | Verbal coaching |
| **Moderate** | Multiple minor issues, evaluation decline | Written warning, PIP |
| **Serious** | Content inaccuracy, professionalism breach | Suspension, remediation |
| **Severe** | Safety violation, harassment, dishonesty | Immediate termination |

**Due Process:**
1. Documentation of concern
2. Investigation as appropriate
3. Opportunity to respond
4. Decision by appropriate authority
