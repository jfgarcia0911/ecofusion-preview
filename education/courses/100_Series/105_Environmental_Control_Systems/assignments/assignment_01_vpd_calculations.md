## Objective
Calculate Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) for different environmental conditions and match to appropriate plant growth stages.

- VPD = _______ kPa

**Scenario 2:** Temp=82°F, RH=50%
- VPD = _______ kPa

**Scenario 3:** Temp=68°F, RH=80%

## Part 2: Growth Stage Matching (3 points)

Match each VPD to optimal growth stage:
- Propagation: 0.4-0.8 kPa
- Vegetative: 0.8-1.2 kPa
- Flowering: 1.0-1.5 kPa
