# Assignment 2: HVAC Load Calculation

## Course 105: Environmental Control Systems

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 20-30 min

## Objective
Calculate heating and cooling loads for a growing space and select appropriate HVAC equipment.

## Given Parameters
- Room: 20ft × 30ft × 10ft ceiling
- Lighting: 40,000W LED
- Outdoor temp: 90°F (summer) / 30°F (winter)
- Target: 75°F constant
- Insulation: R-13 walls, R-30 ceiling

## Calculations Required

**1. Heat Load from Lighting (3 pts)**
- BTU/hr from lights: _______

**2. Heat Loss/Gain Through Walls (6 pts)**
- Summer cooling need: _______ BTU/hr
- Winter heating need: _______ BTU/hr

**3. Equipment Selection (6 pts)**
- AC tonnage needed: _______
- Heater BTU rating: _______
- Estimated costs: _______

## Formulas Provided
- 1 Watt = 3.41 BTU/hr
- 12,000 BTU/hr = 1 ton AC
- Heat transfer = Area × ΔT / R-value
