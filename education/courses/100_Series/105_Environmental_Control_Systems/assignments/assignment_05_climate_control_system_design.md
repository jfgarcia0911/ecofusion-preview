# Assignment 5: Climate Control System Design Project

## Course 105: Environmental Control Systems

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Design a complete environmental control system for a commercial greenhouse or indoor growing facility.

## Specifications

**Choose one facility type:**

**Option A: Greenhouse (5,000 sq ft)**
- Mixed light (natural + supplemental)
- Vine crop production (tomatoes/peppers)
- Located in your climate zone

**Option B: Indoor Vertical Farm (2,000 sq ft)**
- Sole-source LED lighting
- Leafy greens production
- Climate-controlled warehouse

## Required Deliverables

### 1. Facility Overview (2 pts)
- Dimensions and layout
- Crop selection and production goals
- Climate zone considerations

### 2. HVAC System Design (4 pts)
- Heating load calculations
- Cooling load calculations
- Equipment selection and specifications
- Ductwork/distribution plan

### 3. Lighting System Design (3 pts)
- Fixture selection and layout
- PPFD mapping
- Control strategy (photoperiod, dimming)

### 4. Environmental Monitoring & Control (3 pts)
- Sensor specifications and placement
- Controller selection
- Set points and control logic
- Data logging and alerts

### 5. Budget and Energy Analysis (3 pts)
- Complete equipment list with costs
- Installation cost estimates
- Annual energy consumption
- Operating cost projections

## Grading Rubric

| Component | Excellent (13-15) | Good (10-12) | Satisfactory (7-9) |
|-----------|------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Technical Accuracy** | All calculations correct, appropriate equipment | Minor errors | Some errors |
| **Design Quality** | Comprehensive, efficient design | Good design | Basic design |
| **Documentation** | Professional, complete | Well-organized | Adequate |
| **Feasibility** | Highly practical and buildable | Generally feasible | Some concerns |
