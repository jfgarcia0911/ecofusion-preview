# Assignment 4: Environmental Control Strategy

## Course 105: Environmental Control Systems

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Develop comprehensive environmental control strategy for specific crop production scenario.

## Assignment

Choose one crop type:
- Tomatoes (fruiting, high-light)
- Lettuce (leafy greens, moderate-light)
- Cannabis (high-value, demanding)

Create a 3-4 page environmental control plan including:

### 1. Target Environmental Parameters (4 pts)
- Temperature (day/night)
- Humidity/VPD by growth stage
- Light intensity and photoperiod
- CO₂ levels
- Air circulation requirements

### 2. Equipment Specifications (4 pts)
- HVAC system sizing and type
- Lighting system specifications
- Humidification/dehumidification
- CO₂ delivery system
- Monitoring and control equipment

### 3. Energy Management (3 pts)
- Daily energy consumption estimate
- Peak demand calculations
- Energy-saving strategies
- Annual operating cost projection

### 4. Automation Strategy (2 pts)
- Controller requirements
- Sensor placement
- Set points and control logic
- Backup systems

### 5. Seasonal Adjustments (2 pts)
- Summer vs. winter operations
- Natural ventilation opportunities
- Supplemental vs. sole-source lighting

## Submission
PDF, 3-4 pages, professional formatting
