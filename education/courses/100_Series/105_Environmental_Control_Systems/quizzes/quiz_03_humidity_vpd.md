## Answer Key

| Question | Answer | Explanation |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | B | VPD = Vapor Pressure Deficit |
| 2 | B | Warm air can hold more water, so same absolute moisture = lower RH |
| 3 | C | 0.8-1.2 kPa is optimal for vegetative growth |
| 4 | B | VPD tells you what the plant experiences, accounting for temperature |
| 5 | B | At 75°F/70% RH, VPD is approximately 0.80 kPa (see VPD chart) |
| 6 | A | True - dehumidifiers release heat as a byproduct of condensing moisture |
| 7 | C | Clones need high humidity (low VPD 0.4-0.8) because they lack roots |
| 8 | C | Botrytis thrives above 85% RH with free water present |
| 9 | B | 2-4 pints/day per 100 sq ft of canopy is the guideline |
| 10 | B | All three - susceptible host, pathogen present, favorable environment |
