## Questions

### Question 1
What is the ideal day temperature for leafy greens?

a) 50-60°F (10-15°C)
b) 68-75°F (20-24°C) ✓
c) 85-95°F (29-35°C)
d) 100°F+ (38°C+)

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Leafy greens like lettuce, kale, and herbs thrive at 68-75°F during the day, with slightly cooler nights for optimal growth.*

---

### Question 2
What heating system provides most uniform temperature?

a) Single space heater in corner
b) Radiant floor or distributed forced air ✓
c) Open flame
d) Passive solar only

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Radiant floor heating or properly distributed forced air provides even temperature throughout the grow space, preventing hot/cold spots.*

---

### Question 3
What is thermal mass?

a) Weight of heating equipment
b) Materials that store and slowly release heat ✓
c) Air temperature
d) Insulation thickness

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Thermal mass (water barrels, concrete, brick) absorbs heat when warm and releases it when cool, stabilizing temperature swings.*

---

### Question 4
What is DIF in environmental control?

a) Difficulty level
b) Day-night temperature difference ✓
c) Dehumidification intensity factor
d) Direct irrigation flow

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: DIF is the difference between day and night temperatures. Positive DIF (warmer days) elongates stems; negative DIF creates compact growth.*

---

### Question 5
What cooling method uses water evaporation?

a) Air conditioning
b) Swamp coolers (evaporative cooling) ✓
c) Ice baths
d) Dehumidifiers

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Evaporative coolers (swamp coolers) cool air by water evaporation. They're energy-efficient but add humidity, so work best in dry climates.*

---

### Question 6
Why is insulation important in CEA facilities?

a) For soundproofing only
b) Reduces heat loss/gain, stabilizes temps, lowers energy costs ✓
c) Makes buildings look better
d) Insulation has no purpose

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Proper insulation (walls, roof, foundation) reduces temperature fluctuations, lowers heating/cooling costs, and improves environmental control.*

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of temperature sensors?

a) Decoration
b) Monitor and trigger heating/cooling systems ✓
c) Measure light only
d) Water quality testing

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Temperature sensors provide real-time monitoring and feedback to controllers that automatically activate heating/cooling to maintain set points.*

---

### Question 8
Where should temperature sensors be placed?

a) In direct sunlight
b) At plant canopy level, away from heat sources ✓
c) On the floor in corners
d) Outside the grow space

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Sensors should be at canopy height where plants experience temperature, shaded from direct light, and away from heaters/vents for accurate readings.*

---

### Question 9
What happens to plants above 90°F (32°C)?

a) They grow perfectly
b) Heat stress, reduced photosynthesis, potential damage ✓
c) Faster growth always
d) Nothing changes

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Temperatures above 90°F cause heat stress in most crops, reducing photosynthesis, increasing respiration, and potentially denaturing proteins.*

---

### Question 10
What is the benefit of night temperature setback?

a) No benefit
b) Saves energy and reduces respiration losses ✓
c) Increases pest problems
d) Makes plants grow slower always

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Lowering night temperature by 5-10°F saves heating energy and reduces plant respiration, allowing more photosynthate to go into growth.*
