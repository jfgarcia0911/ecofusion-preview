## Answer Key

| Question | Answer | Explanation |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | B | CEA = Controlled Environment Agriculture |
| 2 | C | The six factors are: temperature, humidity, light, CO₂, airflow, and nutrition. Soil type is not one of them. |
| 3 | C | Indoor farms offer maximum control as all conditions are artificially managed |
| 4 | C | Energy (especially lighting) is typically 50%+ of operating costs in indoor farms |
| 5 | C | Structure & Enclosure forms the base - you need a structure before controlling anything else |
| 6 | B | False - factors are highly interconnected; changing temperature affects humidity/VPD, etc. |
| 7 | C | Commercial indoor farms typically cost $100-400 per sq ft depending on sophistication |
| 8 | A | True - greenhouses rely primarily on natural sunlight with possible supplementation |
| 9 | B | CEA enables consistent production regardless of season or weather |
| 10 | C | Hybrid greenhouses use both natural light and supplemental artificial lighting |
