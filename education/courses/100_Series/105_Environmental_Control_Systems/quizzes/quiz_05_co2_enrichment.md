## Questions

### Question 1
What is the atmospheric CO₂ concentration?

a) 100 ppm
b) 400 ppm ✓
c) 1500 ppm
d) 5000 ppm

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Ambient atmospheric CO₂ is approximately 400-420 ppm. Plants evolved to use this baseline level for photosynthesis.*

---

### Question 2
What CO₂ level is typically targeted for enrichment?

a) 200-400 ppm
b) 600-800 ppm
c) 1000-1500 ppm ✓
d) 3000+ ppm

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Enriching to 1000-1500 ppm can increase photosynthesis and growth by 20-30% if light and nutrients are adequate.*

---

### Question 3
When should CO₂ enrichment be used?

a) Only at night
b) During lights-on period when plants photosynthesize ✓
c) 24 hours continuously
d) Never use CO₂

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ enrichment during photosynthesis (lights-on) boosts carbon fixation. It's wasted at night when photosynthesis doesn't occur.*

---

### Question 4
What is the most common CO₂ source for small operations?

a) Breathing on plants
b) Compressed CO₂ tanks ✓
c) Nuclear reactors
d) Ocean water

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Compressed CO₂ cylinders with regulators are safe, clean, and practical for small/medium facilities. Larger operations may use generators.*

---

### Question 5
Why must you stop CO₂ enrichment during ventilation?

a) To save money and prevent waste ✓
b) CO₂ harms exhaust fans
c) It's illegal to vent CO₂
d) Plants don't need ventilation

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: Venting exhausts enriched CO₂ outside, wasting money and defeating the purpose. Coordinate CO₂ injection with closed/recirculating ventilation.*

---

### Question 6
What safety concern exists with CO₂?

a) It's poisonous at all levels
b) High concentrations (>5000 ppm) can displace oxygen and harm humans ✓
c) It causes explosions
d) No safety concerns exist

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ above 5000 ppm causes health issues (headaches, drowsiness). Above 40,000 ppm can be life-threatening. Use monitors and ventilation.*

---

### Question 7
What is required for CO₂ enrichment to be effective?

a) Only high CO₂ levels
b) Adequate light, nutrients, and water ✓
c) Low temperatures
d) No other factors matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ enrichment only helps if light, water, and nutrients aren't limiting. It's the "Law of the Minimum"—weakest link limits growth.*

---

### Question 8
What device measures CO₂ levels?

a) pH meter
b) CO₂ monitor/controller ✓
c) Thermometer
d) Light meter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ monitors measure ppm levels and controllers automate injection to maintain target ranges, preventing over or under-dosing.*

---

### Question 9
How do CO₂ generators produce CO₂?

a) By magic
b) By burning propane or natural gas ✓
c) By freezing air
d) By compressing oxygen

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ generators burn propane/natural gas, producing CO₂ and water vapor. They also generate significant heat, requiring cooling considerations.*

---

### Question 10
What is the economic consideration for CO₂ enrichment?

a) It's always profitable
b) Cost must be justified by increased yield/quality ✓
c) It's always too expensive
d) Economics don't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: CO₂ enrichment costs money (gas, equipment, reduced ventilation). Calculate ROI based on expected yield increase and crop value.*
