## Questions

### Question 1
What does PPFD measure?

a) Total light output from a fixture
b) Light intensity at a specific point (μmol/m²/s) ✓
c) Electricity consumption
d) Color temperature

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density) measures PAR light intensity hitting a surface area, expressed in μmol/m²/s.*

---

### Question 2
What is the main advantage of LED grow lights?

a) Lowest initial cost
b) Energy efficiency and customizable spectrum ✓
c) Longest lifespan for all types
d) Highest heat output

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: LEDs are energy-efficient (2-3x more than HPS), produce less heat, and allow spectrum customization for specific growth stages.*

---

### Question 3
What PPFD range is ideal for leafy greens?

a) 50-100 μmol/m²/s
b) 200-400 μmol/m²/s ✓
c) 800-1200 μmol/m²/s
d) 2000+ μmol/m²/s

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Leafy greens thrive at 200-400 PPFD. Higher levels can work but provide diminishing returns and increase energy costs.*

---

### Question 4
What is supplemental lighting?

a) Lights used only at night
b) Additional lighting to boost natural sunlight ✓
c) Backup emergency lights
d) Decorative lighting

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Supplemental lighting complements natural daylight in greenhouses, extending photoperiod or boosting intensity during cloudy/winter conditions.*

---

### Question 5
What is sole-source lighting?

a) Lights from one manufacturer
b) Artificial lighting providing 100% of plant light needs ✓
c) One light fixture only
d) Solar power only

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Sole-source lighting provides all light needed for plant growth, with no natural sunlight (used in indoor vertical farms and warehouses).*

---

### Question 6
What does "light uniformity" mean?

a) All lights are the same brand
b) Even PPFD distribution across growing area ✓
c) Constant photoperiod
d) Uniform electrical draw

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Uniform light distribution ensures all plants receive similar PPFD, preventing uneven growth. Measure with PAR meter across canopy.*

---

### Question 7
What is the typical photoperiod for vegetative growth?

a) 6-8 hours
b) 12 hours
c) 16-18 hours ✓
d) 24 hours continuous

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Most crops prefer 16-18 hours of light during vegetative growth, providing ample photosynthesis time while allowing dark period for metabolic processes.*

---

### Question 8
Why do HPS lights produce yellow-orange light?

a) They're defective
b) High red spectrum output with some green/yellow ✓
c) To save energy
d) Random design choice

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: High Pressure Sodium (HPS) lights emit primarily red/orange spectrum (600-700nm), which promotes flowering but lacks blue for compact growth.*

---

### Question 9
What is light degradation?

a) Plants blocking each other's light
b) Gradual decline in fixture output over time ✓
c) Light quality getting worse
d) Dirty lenses

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: All lights degrade—LEDs lose 20-30% output over 50,000 hours; HPS degrades faster. Plan replacement schedules to maintain PPFD targets.*

---

### Question 10
What is the purpose of light movers?

a) To save electricity
b) Distribute light more evenly over larger area ✓
c) Move plants instead of lights
d) They serve no purpose

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Light movers (rail systems) move fixtures back and forth, providing more even light distribution and allowing fewer fixtures to cover larger areas.*
