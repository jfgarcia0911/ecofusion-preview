## Questions

### Question 1
What does HVAC stand for?

a) Heavy Ventilation And Cooling
b) Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning ✓
c) Hydraulic Valve Air Compressor
d) High Voltage Alternating Current

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: HVAC encompasses all systems for heating, ventilating, and air conditioning to control environment in buildings and grow spaces.*

---

### Question 2
What is a heat load calculation?

a) Weight of heating equipment
b) Estimating total heat that must be removed (cooling) or added (heating) ✓
c) Electricity bill
d) Number of heaters needed

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Heat load calculations account for all heat sources (lights, equipment, solar gain) and losses (walls, ventilation) to size HVAC equipment properly.*

---

### Question 3
What is the largest heat source in indoor grows?

a) Heaters
b) Grow lights ✓
c) Dehumidifiers
d) People

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Grow lights, especially HPS, generate massive heat. LEDs are cooler but still significant. Lights typically account for 60-90% of cooling load.*

---

### Question 4
What is a mini-split system?

a) Small room divider
b) Ductless heat pump with indoor and outdoor units ✓
c) Miniature HVAC for tiny spaces
d) Split personality controller

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Mini-split systems are ductless heat pumps with an outdoor compressor and indoor air handler(s), offering efficient heating and cooling.*

---

### Question 5
What is a dehumidifier's function?

a) Add moisture to air
b) Remove excess moisture from air ✓
c) Heat the space
d) Measure humidity only

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Dehumidifiers remove water vapor by cooling air below dew point, condensing moisture. Essential in sealed grow rooms where transpiration accumulates.*

---

### Question 6
Why do dehumidifiers produce heat?

a) They're broken
b) Heat is a byproduct of the refrigeration cycle ✓
c) To warm the room intentionally
d) They don't produce heat

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Dehumidifiers use refrigeration which generates heat. Every gallon of water removed adds ~8700 BTU of heat to the space, requiring additional cooling.*

---

### Question 7
What size HVAC system is needed?

a) Biggest available is always best
b) Sized based on accurate heat load calculations ✓
c) Smallest to save money
d) Size doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Under-sized systems can't maintain conditions; over-sized systems short-cycle (inefficient, poor dehumidification). Proper sizing is critical.*

---

### Question 8
What is an ERV (Energy Recovery Ventilator)?

a) Electric Resistance Valve
b) System that recovers heat/cooling from exhaust air ✓
c) Extra Redundant Vent
d) Emergency Relief Vent

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: ERVs transfer heat and moisture between outgoing and incoming air streams, reducing energy waste while providing fresh air exchange.*

---

### Question 9
What is the benefit of zoned climate control?

a) No benefit
b) Different areas can have different temperature/humidity settings ✓
c) Only aesthetic purposes
d) Makes systems more complex for no reason

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Zoning allows different conditions in different areas (e.g., warmer propagation zone, cooler flowering zone) improving efficiency and plant quality.*

---

### Question 10
What backup should HVAC systems have?

a) No backup needed
b) Redundant systems or alerts for critical equipment failure ✓
c) Just buy insurance
d) Backup is too expensive always

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Critical systems should have backup/redundant units, alarms, and emergency protocols. HVAC failure can destroy an entire crop within hours.*
