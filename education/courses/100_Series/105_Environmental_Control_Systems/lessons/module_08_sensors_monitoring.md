## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Sensor accuracy matters** - invest in quality sensors
2. **Placement is critical** - measure where plants experience conditions
3. **Data logging** enables trend analysis and troubleshooting
4. **Calibration** maintains accuracy over time
5. **Redundancy** protects against sensor failure
