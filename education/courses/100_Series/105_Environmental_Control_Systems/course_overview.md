## Course Description

Master the art and science of creating optimal growing environments. This comprehensive course covers climate control, lighting systems, atmospheric management, and automation technologies used in modern controlled environment agriculture (CEA). Learn to design, implement, and optimize environmental systems for greenhouses, indoor farms, and grow rooms of any scale.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Design** appropriate HVAC and climate control systems for growing spaces
2. **Calculate** lighting requirements and select appropriate fixtures
3. **Manage** temperature, humidity, and VPD for optimal plant growth
4. **Implement** CO₂ enrichment strategies safely and effectively
5. **Integrate** sensors and automation for environmental monitoring
6. **Troubleshoot** common environmental control problems
7. **Optimize** energy efficiency while maintaining ideal conditions

## Key Topics

### Temperature Management
- Heating systems: radiant, forced air, hot water
- Cooling systems: evaporative, mechanical, passive
- Heat load calculations
- Thermal mass and insulation
- Day/night temperature differentials (DIF)

### Humidity & VPD
- Understanding relative humidity
- Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) science
- Humidification methods
- Dehumidification systems
- Disease prevention through humidity management

### Lighting Systems
- HID (HPS, MH) vs. LED technology
- Light spectrum and plant response
- PPFD mapping and uniformity
- Photoperiod control
- Supplemental vs. sole-source lighting
- Light fixture selection and layout

### CO₂ Enrichment
- Photosynthesis and CO₂ relationship
- CO₂ sources: tanks, generators, burners
- Safe handling and monitoring
- Integration with ventilation
- Cost-benefit analysis

### Automation & Control
- Environmental controllers
- Sensor types and placement
- Data logging and analysis
- Remote monitoring
- Integrated pest management (IPM) tie-ins

## Environmental Targets Quick Reference

### Temperature Guidelines

| Crop Type | Day Temp | Night Temp | DIF |
|-----------|----------|------------|-----|
| Leafy Greens | 68-75°F (20-24°C) | 60-65°F (16-18°C) | +5-10°F |
| Tomatoes | 75-85°F (24-29°C) | 62-68°F (17-20°C) | +10-15°F |
| Peppers | 75-85°F (24-29°C) | 65-70°F (18-21°C) | +10-15°F |
| Cucumbers | 75-82°F (24-28°C) | 68-72°F (20-22°C) | +5-10°F |
| Strawberries | 68-77°F (20-25°C) | 55-60°F (13-16°C) | +10-15°F |

### VPD Targets by Growth Stage

| Growth Stage | Target VPD (kPa) | RH at 75°F |
|--------------|------------------|------------|
| Propagation/Clones | 0.4-0.8 | 80-90% |
| Seedlings | 0.5-0.8 | 70-80% |
| Vegetative | 0.8-1.2 | 60-70% |
| Flowering/Fruiting | 1.0-1.5 | 50-65% |
| Ripening | 1.2-1.6 | 45-55% |

### Light Intensity Recommendations

| Application | PPFD (μmol/m²/s) | DLI (mol/m²/day) |
|-------------|------------------|------------------|
| Propagation | 100-200 | 6-10 |
| Leafy Greens | 200-400 | 12-17 |
| Herbs | 300-500 | 15-22 |
| Vine Crops | 400-600 | 20-30 |
| Fruiting Crops | 500-800 | 25-40 |

## Equipment Covered

### Climate Control
- Mini-split AC systems
- Evaporative coolers (swamp coolers)
- Dehumidifiers (portable and commercial)

- Heaters (electric, gas, hot water)
- Heat exchangers

### Lighting
- LED fixtures (bars, panels, boards)
- HPS and CMH fixtures
- T5 fluorescents
- Light movers
- Reflectors and hoods

### Monitoring & Control
- Thermostats and humidistats
- Environmental controllers
- CO₂ monitors and controllers
- Light sensors (PAR meters)
- Data loggers
- Smart sensors and IoT devices
