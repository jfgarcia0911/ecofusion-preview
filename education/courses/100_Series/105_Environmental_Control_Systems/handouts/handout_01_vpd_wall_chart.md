## Discussion Questions

1. Why is VPD a better metric than relative humidity alone for managing plant environments?

2. If your VPD is 0.3 kPa (too low), what two adjustments could you make?

3. A grower has 85°F temperature and 60% RH. Looking at the chart, what is the approximate VPD? Is this appropriate for vegetative growth?

4. Why do clones and cuttings need lower VPD than mature vegetative plants?

5. During late flowering, growers often target higher VPD. What benefit does this provide?
