# Activity: Production Planning Exercise
## Course 106: Crop Production & Management

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 60-90 minutes
**Type:** Individual or Team Project
**Materials Needed:** Calculator, spreadsheet software (optional), crop timing data

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will be able to:
1. Calculate seed requirements for continuous production
2. Develop a weekly seeding schedule
3. Determine system capacity requirements
4. Account for seasonal variations in production

---

## Scenario

You have been hired as the production manager for a new urban farm operation. Your facility has:
- **Growing area:** 4,000 square feet of Deep Water Culture (DWC) systems
- **Propagation area:** 500 square feet
- **Target market:** 3 local restaurants with weekly standing orders

### Customer Orders (Weekly)

| Customer | Product | Quantity |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Farm Table Restaurant | Butterhead Lettuce | 150 heads |
| Green Leaf Bistro | Mixed Baby Greens | 40 lbs |
| Harvest Kitchen | Genovese Basil | 30 lbs |
| All Customers | Specialty Mix (your choice) | 25 lbs |

## Part 2: Seed Requirements Calculation (20 minutes)

Calculate the seeds needed weekly for each crop.

### Calculation Template

**Butterhead Lettuce:**
```
Target: 150 heads per week

Step 1: Account for crop loss
150 ÷ (1 - 0.05) = 150 ÷ 0.95 = _____ plants needed at transplant

Step 2: Account for transplant success
_____ ÷ 0.95 = _____ seedlings needed

Step 3: Account for germination
_____ ÷ 0.90 = _____ seeds per week

Final answer: _____ seeds per week
```

**Baby Greens Mix:**
```
Target: 40 lbs per week
Yield: 0.25 lb per sq ft

Step 1: Calculate area needed
40 lbs ÷ 0.25 lb/sq ft = _____ sq ft per week

Step 2: Calculate seeds needed (at 2" × 2" = 36 plants/sq ft)
_____ sq ft × 36 = _____ seeds theoretically

Step 3: Account for losses (10% crop loss, 85% germination)
_____ ÷ 0.90 ÷ 0.85 = _____ seeds per week

Final answer: _____ seeds per week
```

**Genovese Basil:**
```
Target: 30 lbs per week
Yield: 0.5 lb per plant

Step 1: Calculate plants needed at harvest
30 lbs ÷ 0.5 lb/plant = _____ plants

Step 2: Account for crop loss (8%)
_____ ÷ 0.92 = _____ plants needed at transplant

Step 3: Account for transplant success (90%)
_____ ÷ 0.90 = _____ seedlings needed

Step 4: Account for germination (85%)
_____ ÷ 0.85 = _____ seeds per week

Final answer: _____ seeds per week
```

## Part 3: System Capacity Analysis (20 minutes)

Determine how much growing space each crop requires.

### Space Calculation Template

**Butterhead Lettuce:**
```
Spacing: 8" × 8" = 64 sq in per plant = 0.44 sq ft per plant

Plants in production system at any time:
DTM = 45 days in production (after transplant)
Harvest frequency = weekly (7 days)
Weeks in system = 45 ÷ 7 = 6.4 weeks ≈ 7 batches

Plants per batch (from Part 2, after transplant): _____
Total plants in system: _____ × 7 = _____
Space required: _____ plants × 0.44 sq ft = _____ sq ft
```

**Baby Greens Mix:**
```
Weekly area needed (from Part 2): _____ sq ft
DTM = 21 days
Batches in system = 21 ÷ 7 = 3 batches

Total space required: _____ sq ft × 3 = _____ sq ft
```

**Genovese Basil:**
```
Spacing: 6" × 6" = 36 sq in per plant = 0.25 sq ft per plant

DTM = 60 days
Batches in system = 60 ÷ 7 = 8.6 ≈ 9 batches

Plants per batch (from Part 2, after transplant): _____
Total plants in system: _____ × 9 = _____
Space required: _____ plants × 0.25 sq ft = _____ sq ft
```

## Answer Key (Instructor Reference)

### Part 2: Seed Requirements

**Butterhead Lettuce:**
- 150 ÷ 0.95 = 158 plants at transplant
- 158 ÷ 0.95 = 166 seedlings
- 166 ÷ 0.90 = 185 seeds/week

**Baby Greens:**
- 40 ÷ 0.25 = 160 sq ft
- 160 × 36 = 5,760 seeds theoretical
- 5,760 ÷ 0.90 ÷ 0.85 = 7,529 seeds/week

**Basil:**
- 30 ÷ 0.5 = 60 plants
- 60 ÷ 0.92 = 65 plants at transplant
- 65 ÷ 0.90 = 73 seedlings
- 73 ÷ 0.85 = 86 seeds/week

### Part 3: Space Analysis

**Lettuce:** 158 × 7 batches × 0.44 sq ft = 487 sq ft
**Greens:** 160 × 3 batches = 480 sq ft
**Basil:** 65 × 9 batches × 0.25 sq ft = 146 sq ft
**Total:** ~1,113 sq ft (28% of capacity - feasible with room for specialty crops)
