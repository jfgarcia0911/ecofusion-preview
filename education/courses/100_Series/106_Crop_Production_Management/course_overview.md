## Course Description

Master the practical skills needed to grow crops successfully in controlled environments. This comprehensive course covers seed selection, propagation techniques, crop scheduling, pest and disease management, and harvest timing. Learn industry best practices for producing high-quality vegetables, herbs, and leafy greens year-round.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Select** appropriate varieties for controlled environment production
2. **Propagate** plants from seed, cuttings, and tissue culture
3. **Develop** production schedules for continuous harvest
4. **Implement** integrated pest management (IPM) strategies
5. **Diagnose** and treat common plant diseases
6. **Determine** optimal harvest timing and post-harvest handling
7. **Calculate** yield projections and track production metrics

## Key Topics

### Variety Selection
- Determinate vs indeterminate varieties
- Days to maturity (DTM) considerations
- Disease resistance traits
- Compact varieties for vertical farms
- Flavor vs yield trade-offs

### Propagation
- Seed germination requirements
- Cutting propagation
- Grafting for disease resistance
- Hardening off protocols
- Transplant timing

### Crop Scheduling
- Staggered planting for continuous harvest
- Production planning software
- Bay rotation systems
- Succession planting calculations

### Pest Management
- Biological controls (beneficial insects)
- Cultural practices for prevention
- Physical barriers and traps
- Least-toxic chemical interventions
- Scouting and monitoring protocols

### Disease Management
- Environmental conditions that favor disease
- Fungal, bacterial, and viral diseases
- Integrated disease management
- Sanitation protocols
- Resistant variety selection

## Common Pests Reference

| Pest | Crops Affected | Signs | Biological Control |
|------|----------------|-------|-------------------|
| Aphids | Most crops | Sticky residue, curled leaves | Ladybugs, lacewings, parasitic wasps |
| Fungus gnats | All | Larvae in media, weak seedlings | Hypoaspis mites, Bt, sticky traps |
| Spider mites | Tomatoes, peppers | Fine webbing, stippled leaves | Predatory mites (Phytoseiulus) |
| Thrips | Most crops | Silver streaks, distorted growth | Predatory mites, minute pirate bugs |
| Whiteflies | Tomatoes, peppers | White insects, sooty mold | Encarsia wasps, sticky traps |

---

## Common Diseases Reference

| Disease | Type | Crops Affected | Conditions | Prevention |
|---------|------|----------------|------------|------------|
| Powdery mildew | Fungal | Most crops | High RH, temp swings | Airflow, resistant varieties |
| Botrytis | Fungal | Dense crops, flowers | High RH, still air | Lower humidity, remove debris |
| Pythium | Oomycete | Root zone | Waterlogged, high temp | Good drainage, sterile media |
| Fusarium | Fungal | Tomatoes, lettuce | Warm, wet | Resistant varieties, sanitation |
| Downy mildew | Oomycete | Lettuce, basil | Cool, wet | Airflow, avoid leaf wetness |
