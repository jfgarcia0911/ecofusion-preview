## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. Which environmental condition most commonly promotes fungal disease development?**
- A) Low humidity and good airflow
- B) High humidity and poor airflow
- C) Cool temperatures only
- D) High light levels

**2. Powdery mildew appears as:**
- A) Brown spots with yellow halos
- B) White/gray powdery coating on leaf surfaces
- C) Black, water-soaked lesions
- D) Wilting without visible symptoms

**3. What is the primary difference between fungal and bacterial diseases?**
- A) Bacterial diseases are always more serious
- B) Fungal diseases often produce visible spores; bacterial diseases cause water-soaked lesions
- C) Fungal diseases only affect roots
- D) Bacterial diseases cannot spread through water

**4. Pythium root rot is caused by:**
- A) A true fungus
- B) A water mold (oomycete)
- C) Bacteria
- D) A virus

**5. Which practice is MOST important for preventing disease spread?**
- A) Increasing fertilizer application
- B) Sanitation and removing infected plant material
- C) Raising temperature
- D) Reducing watering frequency only

## Identification (2 points)

**9. Match the disease symptom to the most likely disease type:**

| Symptom | Disease Type |
|---------|--------------|
| A. White fuzzy growth on leaves | 1. Bacterial |
| B. Water-soaked spots with bacterial ooze | 2. Fungal |

9. A matches with: ___
10. B matches with: ___

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - High humidity and poor airflow
2. **B** - White/gray powdery coating on leaf surfaces
3. **B** - Fungal diseases often produce visible spores; bacterial diseases cause water-soaked lesions
4. **B** - A water mold (oomycete)
5. **B** - Sanitation and removing infected plant material
6. **False** - Viral diseases have no cure; infected plants must be removed
7. **True** - Resistance reduces but does not eliminate susceptibility
8. **True** - Water splash is a major vector for many pathogens

**Identification:**
9. A matches with **2** (Fungal - white fuzzy growth indicates fungal mycelium)
10. B matches with **1** (Bacterial - water-soaked lesions and ooze are bacterial indicators)
