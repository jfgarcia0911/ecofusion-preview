## Calculation Problem (2 points)

**9-10. Calculate the following metrics:**

Your 2,000 sq ft production area produces:
- 800 lbs of lettuce per week
- Labor: 60 hours per week
- Growing cycle: 5 weeks

**9.** What is the yield per square foot per week?

Show calculation: _______________________________________

**10.** What is the labor efficiency (lbs per labor hour)?

Show calculation: _______________________________________

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Harvested weight ÷ growing area
2. **B** - How many crop cycles are completed in a growing space annually
3. **B** - 15% of planted crops don't reach harvest quality
4. **B** - Labor hours per unit produced (lb or head)
5. **B** - Comparing your metrics to industry standards or top performers
6. **True** - Data without action doesn't drive improvement
7. **False** - Cost matters; profitability considers both yield AND cost to achieve it
8. **True** - Regular review enables timely adjustments

**9. Yield per square foot per week:**
800 lbs ÷ 2,000 sq ft = **0.4 lbs per sq ft per week**

(Annual yield per sq ft would be: 0.4 × 52 = 20.8 lbs/sq ft/year)

**10. Labor efficiency:**
800 lbs ÷ 60 hours = **13.3 lbs per labor hour**
