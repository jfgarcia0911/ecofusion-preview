## Calculation Problem (2 points)

**9-10. Production Planning Calculation:**

You want to produce 200 heads of lettuce per week for farmers market sales.
- Germination rate: 90%
- Transplant success rate: 95%
- Expected crop loss: 5%

**9.** How many seeds should you start each week to achieve 200 harvestable heads?
Show your work:

_______________________________________

**10.** If the crop cycle is 42 days total (14 days propagation + 28 days production), how many total plant sites do you need in your production system for continuous weekly harvest?

_______________________________________

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Achieving continuous harvest rather than all-at-once harvest
2. **C** - 500 plus a buffer for losses (e.g., 550-600)
3. **B** - A defined production section for crop rotation
4. **D** - All of the above
5. **B** - Time spent in propagation before transplanting to production area
6. **False** - Even in controlled environments, seasonal changes in natural light and temperature affect growth rates
7. **True** - Buffers (typically 10-15%) account for losses
8. **False** - Comprehensive crop calendars track all critical dates

**9. Calculation:**
Working backwards from 200 heads:
- Account for crop loss: 200 ÷ 0.95 = 211 plants needed at transplant
- Account for transplant success: 211 ÷ 0.95 = 222 seedlings needed
- Account for germination: 222 ÷ 0.90 = 247 seeds
- **Answer: Start approximately 250 seeds per week**

**10. Calculation:**
- 28 days in production system ÷ 7 days between harvests = 4 weeks of crops in system
- 200 plants per week × 4 weeks = 800 plant sites
- Add buffer: approximately **850-900 plant sites needed**
