## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. The optimal germination temperature for most leafy greens is:**
- A) 50-55°F (10-13°C)
- B) 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- C) 85-95°F (29-35°C)
- D) 100-110°F (38-43°C)

**2. What does the term "seed vigor" refer to?**
- A) The physical size of the seed
- B) The ability to germinate and establish strong seedlings
- C) The color intensity of the seed coat
- D) Resistance to pest damage

**3. Which factor does NOT typically affect seed germination?**
- A) Temperature
- B) Moisture
- C) Light spectrum during germination
- D) Wind speed

**4. Seed priming is a technique that:**
- A) Removes the seed coat
- B) Initiates germination then halts it for storage
- C) Doubles the seed size
- D) Sterilizes seeds for tissue culture

**5. What is the purpose of stratification for certain seeds?**
- A) To increase seed size
- B) To simulate winter cold for breaking dormancy
- C) To add nutrients to the seed
- D) To remove pathogens

## True/False (1 point each)

**6. All seeds require light for germination.**
- True
- False

**7. Seeds stored in cool, dry conditions typically remain viable longer than seeds stored in warm, humid conditions.**
- True
- False

**8. Seed germination percentage is guaranteed to remain the same regardless of storage time.**
- True
- False

## Matching (2 points)

**9-10. Match the seed characteristic to its definition:**

| Characteristic | Definition |
|----------------|------------|
| A. Viability | 1. Energy storage tissue in the seed |
| B. Endosperm | 2. Percentage of seeds capable of germinating |

9. A matches with: ___
10. B matches with: ___

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - 65-75°F (18-24°C)
2. **B** - The ability to germinate and establish strong seedlings
3. **D** - Wind speed (does not affect germination)
4. **B** - Initiates germination then halts it for storage
5. **B** - To simulate winter cold for breaking dormancy
6. **False** - Many seeds germinate in darkness; some require light, others are inhibited by light
7. **True** - Cool, dry storage extends seed viability
8. **False** - Germination rates decline over time
9. A matches with **2** (Viability = percentage capable of germinating)
10. B matches with **1** (Endosperm = energy storage tissue)
