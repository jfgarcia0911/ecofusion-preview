**3. The ideal relative humidity for propagating cuttings is typically:**

**4. What is "hardening off"?**

- B) Gradually acclimating plants to harsher conditions

**5. Grafting is primarily used in controlled environment agriculture to:**
- A) Create new plant varieties
- B) Combine disease-resistant rootstock with productive scion
- C) Speed up germination
- D) Reduce water requirements

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Minimizes root disturbance during transplanting
2. **B** - Auxins (such as IBA or NAA)
3. **C** - 80-95%
4. **B** - Gradually acclimating plants to harsher conditions
5. **B** - Combine disease-resistant rootstock with productive scion
6. **True** - Bottom heat (70-75°F typically) accelerates both processes
7. **False** - Propagation success varies greatly by species
8. **True** - Rockwool has naturally high pH and requires soaking in pH-adjusted water
9. **Acceptable answers:** Rockwool, peat moss, coco coir, perlite, vermiculite, oasis cubes, phenolic foam, seed starting mix
10. **Answer:** Without roots, cuttings cannot uptake water to replace moisture lost through transpiration. High humidity reduces transpiration rate, preventing wilting and desiccation while roots develop.
