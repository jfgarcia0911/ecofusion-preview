- A) Temperature
- B) Day length (photoperiod)

**3. Which nutrient ratio is typically higher during vegetative growth?**
- A) Higher phosphorus (P)

- A) Rapid root growth
- B) Premature flowering due to stress or long days

**5. What is the optimal approach when a crop is growing more slowly than expected?**
- A) Immediately increase all nutrients
- B) Diagnose the cause before making changes

- D) Add more plants to compensate

_______________________________________

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Vegetative
2. **B** - Day length (photoperiod)
3. **C** - Higher nitrogen (N) for leaf and stem growth
4. **B** - Premature flowering due to stress or long days
5. **B** - Diagnose the cause before making changes
6. **False** - Optimal conditions vary by growth stage
7. **True** - Larger plants with more leaf area can utilize more light
8. **True** - Mild stress can enhance flavor compounds, color, or essential oil production in some crops

**9. Growth Stages:**
1. Germination/Emergence
2. Vegetative (or Seedling/Establishment)
3. Reproductive/Flowering
4. Maturation/Senescence (or Harvest)

**10. Answer:** DIF (difference between day and night temperature) affects stem elongation and plant height. A positive DIF (warmer days than nights) promotes taller growth, while a negative DIF (cooler days than nights) produces more compact plants. Growers manipulate DIF to control plant architecture without growth regulators.
