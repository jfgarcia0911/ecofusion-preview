## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. What is the FIRST step in an effective IPM program?**
- A) Apply pesticides preventatively
- B) Prevention through cultural practices
- C) Release beneficial insects
- D) Wait for visible damage before acting

**2. Which beneficial insect is commonly used to control aphids?**
- A) Praying mantis
- B) Ladybugs (lady beetles)
- C) Dragonflies
- D) Butterflies

**3. What is a "threshold" in IPM terminology?**
- A) The door to the greenhouse
- B) The pest population level at which action should be taken
- C) The maximum temperature for pest survival
- D) The cost of pesticide application

**4. Yellow sticky traps are primarily used to monitor and catch:**
- A) Spider mites
- B) Flying insects like fungus gnats and whiteflies
- C) Slugs
- D) Soil-dwelling pests

**5. Which cultural practice helps PREVENT pest problems?**
- A) Overcrowding plants for maximum yield
- B) Maintaining proper sanitation and removing plant debris
- C) Using the same growing media indefinitely
- D) Keeping doors and vents open at all times

## Matching (2 points)

**9-10. Match the pest to its most effective biological control:**

| Pest | Biological Control |
|------|-------------------|
| A. Spider mites | 1. Hypoaspis miles (predatory mites) |
| B. Fungus gnats | 2. Phytoseiulus persimilis (predatory mites) |

9. A matches with: ___
10. B matches with: ___

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Prevention through cultural practices
2. **B** - Ladybugs (lady beetles)
3. **B** - The pest population level at which action should be taken
4. **B** - Flying insects like fungus gnats and whiteflies
5. **B** - Maintaining proper sanitation and removing plant debris
6. **False** - IPM integrates all methods including approved chemical treatments when necessary as a last resort
7. **True** - Regular scouting catches problems early when they're easier to control
8. **False** - Beneficials work best when released at low pest populations; high infestations may require additional interventions

**Matching:**
9. A matches with **2** (Spider mites controlled by Phytoseiulus persimilis)
10. B matches with **1** (Fungus gnats controlled by Hypoaspis miles)
