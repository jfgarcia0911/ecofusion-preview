## Answer Key

1. **B** - Early morning when plants are fully turgid
2. **B** - Heat retained in produce after harvest that must be removed
3. **C** - 34-38°F (1-3°C)
4. **B** - Rapid cooling (pre-cooling) after harvest
5. **B** - To prevent spread of diseases between plants
6. **True** - Cold water maintains crispness and slows metabolism
7. **False** - Different crops have different optimal storage temperatures (e.g., tomatoes shouldn't be refrigerated below 50°F)
8. **False** - While harvest timing matters, proper post-harvest handling can still significantly improve outcomes

**9. Acceptable indicators for lettuce harvest:**
- Reached target size/weight
- Proper head formation (for head types)
- Mature leaf development
- Target number of true leaves
- Days to maturity reached
- Firm but not overmature
- Before bolting begins

**10. Answer:** Respiration rate is the speed at which harvested produce consumes sugars and releases CO2 and heat. It matters because higher respiration leads to faster quality degradation, sugar loss, and heat buildup. Cooling reduces respiration rate, extending shelf life.
