## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. What does "DTM" stand for in crop production?**
- A) Daily Temperature Mean
- B) Days to Maturity
- C) Determinate Trait Marker
- D) Direct Transfer Method

**2. Which type of tomato variety continues to grow and produce fruit throughout the season?**
- A) Determinate
- B) Semi-determinate
- C) Indeterminate
- D) Bush type

**3. When selecting varieties for vertical farming, which trait is MOST important?**
- A) Maximum fruit size
- B) Compact growth habit
- C) Long harvest window
- D) High water requirements

**4. Disease resistance codes on seed packets (e.g., "TMV, F2") indicate:**
- A) Seed treatment chemicals applied
- B) Genetic resistance to specific pathogens
- C) Required fertilizer formulations
- D) Certified organic status

**5. What is the primary trade-off when selecting high-yielding commercial varieties?**
- A) Higher seed costs
- B) Often reduced flavor
- C) Increased disease resistance
- D) Faster germination

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Days to Maturity
2. **C** - Indeterminate
3. **B** - Compact growth habit (critical for space efficiency in vertical systems)
4. **B** - Genetic resistance to specific pathogens
5. **B** - Often reduced flavor (yield and flavor often have inverse relationship)
6. **False** - F1 hybrids do not breed true; offspring show variation
7. **True** - Open-pollinated varieties maintain traits when isolated
8. **False** - Different lettuce types have varying optimal temperatures
9. **Acceptable answers include:** Market demand, system compatibility, DTM, yield potential, disease resistance, climate requirements, labor requirements, profitability, buyer preferences, post-harvest handling requirements
10. **Acceptable answers:** Reduces pesticide costs, prevents crop loss, more reliable production, lower labor for disease management, potentially higher net yield when disease pressure exists
