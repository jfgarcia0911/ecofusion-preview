## Succession Planting Calculator

```
Weekly Production Target: _____ units

Step 1: Account for losses
├── Germination rate: ____%
├── Transplant success: ____%
└── Crop loss: ____%

Calculation:
Target ÷ (Germ% × Transplant% × (1 - Loss%)) = Seeds needed

Step 2: Determine planting frequency
├── Crop cycle: _____ days
└── Harvest frequency: Weekly / Bi-weekly / Other

Step 3: Calculate system capacity
├── Days in production system: _____ days
├── Harvest frequency: _____ days
└── Batches in system: Production days ÷ Harvest frequency

Total sites needed: Weekly plants × Batches in system
```

## Seasonal Adjustment Factors

### Winter (Low Light)
- Add 10-20% to DTM for most crops
- Reduce production targets by 15-25%
- Consider supplemental lighting
- Monitor for reduced yields

### Summer (High Light/Heat)
- Reduce DTM by 5-15%
- Watch for bolting in cool-season crops
- Increase harvesting frequency
- Manage heat stress

## Notes:
- **Germ** = Germination time
- **DTM** = Days to Maturity (from transplant unless noted)
- **Turns/Year** = Number of crop cycles possible annually
- All temperatures in Fahrenheit
- Times are approximate; adjust based on variety and conditions
