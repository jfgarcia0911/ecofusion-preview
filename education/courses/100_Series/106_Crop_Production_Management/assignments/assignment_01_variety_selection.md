# Assignment 1: Crop Variety Selection

## Course 106: Crop Production & Management

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 10 | **Time:** 15-20 min

## Objective
Compare crop varieties and select optimal cultivars for specific production scenarios.

## Assignment

**Scenario:** Selecting lettuce varieties for year-round indoor production

**Requirements:**
1. Compare 4 lettuce varieties across criteria:
   - Days to maturity
   - Yield potential
   - Disease resistance
   - Market appeal
   - Growing characteristics

2. Rank varieties for:
   - Summer production
   - Winter production
   - Farmers market sales
   - Restaurant sales

3. Justify final selections (2-3 sentences each)
