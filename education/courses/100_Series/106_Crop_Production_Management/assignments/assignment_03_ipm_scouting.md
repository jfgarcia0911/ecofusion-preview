# Assignment 3: IPM Scouting Exercise

## Course 106: Crop Production & Management

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 30-45 min

## Objective
Practice Integrated Pest Management scouting and develop action plans.

## Activity

**Part 1: Pest Identification (6 pts)**
Identify 6 common pests from samples/photos:
- Aphids
- Fungus gnats
- Spider mites
- Thrips
- Whiteflies
- Caterpillars

**Part 2: Damage Assessment (5 pts)**
For each pest, describe:
- Type of damage caused
- Where to look for pest
- Life cycle considerations

**Part 3: Control Strategy (4 pts)**
Select IPM response for 2 scenarios:
- Prevention methods
- Monitoring threshold
- Control options (biological, cultural, chemical)
- Treatment timing

## Deliverable
Complete scouting worksheet with action plans
