# Assignment 5: Production Optimization Project

## Course 106: Crop Production & Management

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Analyze and optimize crop production for maximum yield and quality.

## Choose One Crop
- Tomatoes (fruiting)
- Lettuce (leafy greens)
- Basil (herbs)

## Project Components

### 1. Growth Analysis (4 pts)
- Growth stages and duration
- Environmental requirements by stage
- Critical control points
- Common production challenges

### 2. Optimization Strategy (5 pts)
- Variety selection rationale
- Environmental optimization
- Nutrient management
- Training/pruning techniques
- Harvest timing

### 3. Production Economics (3 pts)
- Cost per plant/pound calculation
- Yield projections
- Market pricing research
- Profitability analysis

### 4. Quality Control (3 pts)
- Quality standards
- Harvest criteria
- Post-harvest handling
- Common defects and prevention

## Deliverables
- 4-5 page analysis
- Production data sheets
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Grower's quick reference guide

## Grading
Technical accuracy, practical application, and professional presentation
