# Assignment 4: Comprehensive Crop Planning

## Course 106: Crop Production & Management

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Develop a complete crop production plan for a small commercial operation.

## Scenario
200 sq ft growing space, mixed crop production for local sales

## Requirements

### 1. Crop Selection (4 pts)
- Select 5-7 crop types
- Justify each selection
- Include fast and slow crops
- Consider market demand

### 2. Production Schedule (5 pts)
- 12-week planting calendar
- Succession plantings
- Harvest projections
- Space utilization plan

### 3. Nutrient Management (3 pts)
- Crop-specific nutrition needs
- EC/pH targets by crop
- Fertilization schedule

### 4. Pest & Disease Prevention (3 pts)
- Crop rotation plan
- Sanitation protocols
- Monitoring schedule
- IPM strategy

## Submission
3-4 page plan with schedules and justifications
