### Section C: Short Answer

**31.** Acceptable answers include:
- Market demand/customer preferences
- System compatibility
- Days to maturity (DTM)
- Yield potential
- Disease resistance
- Temperature requirements
- Light requirements
- Labor requirements
- Profitability
- Post-harvest handling/shelf life
- Space efficiency

**32.** Hardening off is gradually acclimating plants to harsher conditions (lower humidity, more light, temperature variation) before transplanting. It's important because it reduces transplant shock, strengthens cell walls, and improves survival and establishment.

**33.** Acceptable cultural practices:
- Proper sanitation/removing debris
- Adequate plant spacing for airflow
- Proper irrigation (avoid overwatering)
- Inspecting incoming plants/materials
- Maintaining optimal temperature and humidity
- Using disease-free seed/transplants
- Rotating crops
- Removing infected plants promptly

**34.** Without roots, cuttings cannot absorb water to replace moisture lost through leaf transpiration. High humidity (80-95%) reduces the transpiration rate, preventing wilting and desiccation while new roots develop.

**35.** Traceability records should include:
- Lot/batch number
- Seed source and lot
- Planting date and location
- Harvest date
- Handling/processing records
- Distribution/customer information
- Any inputs applied (nutrients, treatments)

### Section D: Calculations

**36a.** Working backwards:
- Need 500 heads at harvest
- Account for 5% crop loss: 500 ÷ 0.95 = 527 plants needed after transplant
- Account for 95% transplant survival: 527 ÷ 0.95 = 555 seedlings needed
- Account for 85% germination: 555 ÷ 0.85 = 653 seeds
- **Answer: Plant approximately 655-660 seeds per week**

**36b.**
- 35 days in production ÷ 7 days per harvest = 5 batches in system
- 500 plants × 5 = 2,500 plant sites (plus buffer)
- **Answer: Approximately 2,500-2,750 plant sites**

**37a.** 1,500 lbs ÷ 3,000 sq ft = **0.5 lbs/sq ft/week**

**37b.** 1,500 lbs ÷ 80 hours = **18.75 lbs/labor hour**

**37c.** $4,500 ÷ 1,500 lbs = **$3.00/lb**

**37d.**
- Revenue: 1,500 × $4.50 = $6,750
- Cost: $4,500
- Profit: $6,750 - $4,500 = $2,250
- Margin: $2,250 ÷ $6,750 = **33.3% profit margin**
