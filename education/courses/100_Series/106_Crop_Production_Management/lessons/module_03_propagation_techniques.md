## Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Match method to crop** - Choose propagation technique based on crop requirements
2. **Control environment** - Temperature, humidity, and light are critical
3. **Timing matters** - Transplant at the right stage for best results
4. **Plan ahead** - Calculate propagation needs for continuous production
5. **Maintain cleanliness** - Sterile conditions prevent disease
