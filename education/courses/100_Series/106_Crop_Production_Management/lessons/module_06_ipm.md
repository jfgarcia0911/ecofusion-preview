## Prevention Strategies

```
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                                             │
    │                    PEST PREVENTION CHECKLIST                               │
    │                                                                             │
    │   EXCLUSION:                                                               │
    │   ══════════                                                               │
    │   □ Screen all intakes (minimum 50 mesh for thrips)                       │
    │   □ Seal gaps around doors, vents, utilities                              │
    │   □ Use air curtains or double-door entry                                 │
    │   □ Inspect all incoming plant material                                   │
    │   □ Quarantine new plants before introduction                             │
    │                                                                             │
    │   SANITATION:                                                              │
    │   ═══════════                                                              │
    │   □ Remove plant debris promptly                                          │
    │   □ Disinfect tools between crops/areas                                   │
    │   □ Clean and sanitize between crop cycles                                │
    │   □ Remove weeds around exterior                                          │
    │   □ Dispose of culled plants properly (sealed bags)                       │
    │                                                                             │
    │   ENVIRONMENTAL:                                                           │
    │   ══════════════                                                           │
    │   □ Maintain optimal VPD (pest stress reduces plant defense)              │
    │   □ Proper airflow (reduces fungus gnat breeding)                         │
    │   □ Avoid overwatering (reduces fungus gnats, root disease)               │
    │   □ Don't over-fertilize (soft growth attracts pests)                     │
    │                                                                             │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Common CEA Pests

### Pest Identification Guide

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    MAJOR CEA PESTS                                        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   APHIDS                                                                  ║
║   ══════                                                                  ║
║   Size: 1-3mm    Color: Green, black, or peach                           ║
║   Signs: Sticky honeydew, curled leaves, sooty mold                      ║
║   Reproduction: Extremely rapid (clonal in summer)                       ║
║   Crops: All leafy greens, herbs, peppers                                ║
║   Biologicals: Aphidius wasps, lacewings, ladybugs                       ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FUNGUS GNATS                                                           ║
║   ════════════                                                            ║
║   Size: 2-3mm adult, larvae in soil                                      ║
║   Signs: Adults flying near media, larvae damage roots                   ║
║   Conditions: Wet media, organic matter                                  ║
║   Crops: All - especially seedlings                                      ║
║   Biologicals: Hypoaspis mites, Bt dunks, nematodes                     ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SPIDER MITES                                                           ║
║   ════════════                                                            ║
║   Size: 0.5mm (barely visible)                                           ║
║   Signs: Fine webbing, stippled/bronze leaves                            ║
║   Conditions: Hot, dry, low humidity                                     ║
║   Crops: Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, strawberries                     ║
║   Biologicals: Phytoseiulus persimilis, Neoseiulus californicus         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   THRIPS                                                                  ║
║   ══════                                                                  ║
║   Size: 1-2mm, slender                                                   ║
║   Signs: Silver streaks, distorted growth, black feces                   ║
║   Vector for: Tomato spotted wilt virus (TSWV)                          ║
║   Crops: Most crops, especially peppers and tomatoes                    ║
║   Biologicals: Orius (pirate bugs), predatory mites, Amblyseius        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   WHITEFLIES                                                              ║
║   ══════════                                                              ║
║   Size: 1-2mm, white winged adults                                       ║
║   Signs: Clouds when disturbed, honeydew, sooty mold                    ║
║   Crops: Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers                                   ║
║   Biologicals: Encarsia formosa, Eretmocerus eremicus                   ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Scouting Protocol

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    WEEKLY SCOUTING PROTOCOL                               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FREQUENCY: Minimum 2× per week, daily during outbreaks                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   EQUIPMENT NEEDED:                                                      ║
║   □ Hand lens (10-20×)                                                   ║
║   □ Yellow sticky cards (monitor flying insects)                         ║
║   □ Blue sticky cards (thrips prefer blue)                              ║
║   □ Scouting data sheet                                                  ║
║   □ Flagging tape (mark problem areas)                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SCOUTING PATTERN:                                                      ║
║   ─────────────────                                                       ║
║   • Check 10-20% of plants each session                                 ║
║   • Focus on: Entry points, edges, hot spots, under leaves              ║
║   • Check both upper and lower leaf surfaces                            ║
║   • Look at growing tips and new growth                                 ║
║   • Inspect root zone/media surface                                     ║
║                                                                           ║
║   RECORD:                                                                ║
║   ────────                                                               ║
║   • Date and time                                                        ║
║   • Location (map zones)                                                 ║
║   • Pest type and life stage observed                                   ║
║   • Number seen (estimate: none, low, moderate, high)                   ║
║   • Damage severity                                                      ║
║   • Action taken                                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ACTION THRESHOLDS:                                                     ║
║   ═══════════════════                                                    ║
║   Sticky card counts that trigger intervention:                         ║
║   • Fungus gnats: >10 per card per week → increase biological          ║
║   • Thrips: >5 per card per week → release predators                   ║
║   • Whitefly: >5 per card per week → deploy parasitoids                ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **IPM prioritizes prevention** over intervention
2. **Exclusion and sanitation** are the foundation
3. **Biological controls** work best when started early
4. **Regular scouting** catches problems before they spread
5. **Chemical interventions** are last resort and may harm beneficials
