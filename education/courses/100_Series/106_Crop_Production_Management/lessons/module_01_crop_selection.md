## Understanding Variety Characteristics

### Key Traits to Evaluate

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    VARIETY SELECTION CRITERIA                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DAYS TO MATURITY (DTM)                                                 ║
║   ══════════════════════                                                  ║
║   • Time from transplant (or seed) to harvest                            ║
║   • Shorter = faster turnover = more cycles/year                         ║
║   • Account for germination time if seed-to-harvest listed               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   GROWTH HABIT                                                            ║
║   ════════════                                                            ║
║   Determinate: Compact, uniform harvest window                           ║
║   Indeterminate: Continuous growth/harvest, needs pruning                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   BOLT RESISTANCE                                                         ║
║   ═══════════════                                                         ║
║   • Resistance to premature flowering (critical for leafy greens)        ║
║   • Heat tolerance often correlates with bolt resistance                 ║
║   • Important for warm growing conditions                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DISEASE RESISTANCE CODES                                               ║
║   ═════════════════════════                                               ║
║   Common codes in seed catalogs:                                         ║
║   • V = Verticillium wilt         • F = Fusarium wilt                   ║
║   • T = Tobacco mosaic virus      • N = Nematodes                       ║
║   • PM = Powdery mildew           • DM = Downy mildew                   ║
║   • TSWV = Tomato spotted wilt    • BLS = Bacterial leaf spot           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Example: "VFT" = Resistant to Verticillium, Fusarium, TMV             ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Trial and Evaluation

### Setting Up Variety Trials

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    VARIETY TRIAL PROTOCOL                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TRIAL DESIGN:                                                          ║
║   ═════════════                                                          ║
║   1. Test minimum 3-5 varieties per crop                                ║
║   2. Include current "control" variety                                  ║
║   3. Run at least 2 production cycles                                   ║
║   4. Use identical conditions for all varieties                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DATA TO COLLECT:                                                       ║
║   ════════════════                                                       ║
║   □ Germination rate (%)                                                ║
║   □ Days to transplant size                                             ║
║   □ Days to harvest                                                     ║
║   □ Yield (weight per plant or per sq ft)                              ║
║   □ Quality observations                                                ║
║   □ Disease/pest incidence                                              ║
║   □ Labor requirements                                                  ║
║   □ Post-harvest shelf life                                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   EVALUATION SCORECARD:                                                  ║
║   ═════════════════════                                                  ║
║   Rate 1-5 on each factor, multiply by importance weight               ║
║   Total score determines variety selection                              ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Match crops to your system** capabilities and market opportunities
2. **Tier 1 crops** (greens, herbs, microgreens) offer best CEA economics
3. **Variety selection** significantly impacts yield, quality, and disease resistance
4. **Disease resistance codes** help prevent problems before they start
5. **Trial new varieties** systematically before committing to production
