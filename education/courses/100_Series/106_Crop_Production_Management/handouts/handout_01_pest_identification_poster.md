## Discussion Questions

1. You find sticky honeydew on leaves and see small green insects clustered on new growth. What pest is this, and what biological control would you deploy?

2. Your seedlings are weak and wilting despite moist media. Small dark flies are visible near the surface. What's the likely pest and cause?

3. You notice fine webbing on tomato plants and tiny specks on leaf undersides. What environmental change could help reduce this pest?

4. Why are thrips considered especially dangerous beyond direct feeding damage?
