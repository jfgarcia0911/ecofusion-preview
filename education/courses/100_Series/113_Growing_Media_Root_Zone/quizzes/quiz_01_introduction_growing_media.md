# Quiz 01: Introduction to Growing Media

**Course:** 113 - Growing Media and Root Zone Management
**Module:** 01 - Introduction to Growing Media
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary function of growing media in soilless cultivation?
- a) To provide nutrients exclusively
- b) To anchor plants and support root development
- c) To hold water only
- d) To attract insects

### Question 2
What are the three key properties of good growing media?
- a) Color, smell, and taste
- b) Water retention, aeration, and drainage
- c) Weight, texture, and temperature
- d) Cost, availability, and color

### Question 3
Which of the following is NOT a common type of growing media?
- a) Clay pebbles
- b) Coconut coir
- c) Concrete
- d) Perlite

### Question 4
What does "inert" mean when describing growing media?
- a) Very heavy
- b) Does not contribute nutrients or significantly alter pH
- c) Contains many nutrients
- d) Changes pH dramatically

### Question 5
Why is aeration important in growing media?
- a) It makes media look better
- b) It allows oxygen to reach roots and prevents waterlogging
- c) It reduces media weight only
- d) It has no importance

### Question 6
What is the ideal growing media for deep water culture (DWC)?
- a) Heavy soil
- b) Net pots with minimal media like clay pebbles
- c) Sand only
- d) Wood chips

### Question 7
Which property determines how well growing media holds moisture?
- a) Color
- b) Water retention capacity
- c) Electrical conductivity
- d) Age

### Question 8
What is a benefit of using expanded clay pebbles?
- a) They decompose quickly
- b) They are reusable, pH neutral, and provide good drainage
- c) They retain too much water
- d) They are not suitable for hydroponics

### Question 9
Why is particle size important in growing media?
- a) It only affects appearance
- b) It affects drainage, aeration, and water retention
- c) It has no effect on plant growth
- d) Smaller is always better

### Question 10
What should you consider when selecting growing media?
- a) Only the color
- b) System type, plant requirements, and cost
- c) Personal preference only
- d) Media should never be selected carefully

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To anchor plants and support root development
2. **b** - Water retention, aeration, and drainage
3. **c** - Concrete
4. **b** - Does not contribute nutrients or significantly alter pH
5. **b** - It allows oxygen to reach roots and prevents waterlogging
6. **b** - Net pots with minimal media like clay pebbles
7. **b** - Water retention capacity
8. **b** - They are reusable, pH neutral, and provide good drainage
9. **b** - It affects drainage, aeration, and water retention
10. **b** - System type, plant requirements, and cost
