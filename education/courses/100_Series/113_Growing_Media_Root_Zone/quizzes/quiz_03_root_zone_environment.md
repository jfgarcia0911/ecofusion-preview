## Questions

### Question 1
What is the root zone?
- a) The area above the plant
- b) The region where roots grow and interact with media, water, and nutrients
- c) The fish tank
- d) The lighting area

### Question 2
Why is oxygen critical in the root zone?
- a) Roots need oxygen for respiration and nutrient uptake
- b) Oxygen makes roots green
- c) Oxygen is not needed by roots
- d) Only leaves need oxygen

### Question 3
What happens when the root zone lacks sufficient oxygen?
- a) Plants grow faster
- b) Root rot and poor nutrient uptake occur
- c) Roots turn blue
- d) Nothing happens

### Question 4
What is the ideal root zone temperature for most plants?
- a) 32-40°F (0-4°C)
- b) 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- c) 90-100°F (32-38°C)
- d) 110-120°F (43-49°C)

### Question 5
How does root zone temperature affect plant growth?
- a) Temperature has no effect
- b) It influences nutrient uptake, oxygen availability, and metabolic processes
- c) Only air temperature matters
- d) Roots don't respond to temperature

### Question 6
What is rhizosphere?
- a) A type of growing media
- b) The zone of soil or media influenced by root secretions and microorganisms
- c) A brand of fertilizer
- d) A watering system

### Question 7
Why is proper drainage important for the root zone?
- a) It prevents oxygen from reaching roots
- b) It prevents waterlogging and allows oxygen exchange
- c) Drainage has no importance
- d) It removes all nutrients

### Question 8
What role do beneficial microorganisms play in the root zone?
- a) They harm plant roots
- b) They help with nutrient cycling and disease suppression
- c) They have no role
- d) They only consume oxygen

### Question 9
How can you optimize root zone conditions?
- a) Ignore temperature and aeration
- b) Maintain proper temperature, aeration, moisture, and pH
- c) Keep roots in complete darkness only
- d) Never check root health

### Question 10
What indicates a healthy root zone?
- a) Brown, mushy roots
- b) White or light-colored, firm roots with good branching
- c) Foul odor
- d) No roots visible

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The region where roots grow and interact with media, water, and nutrients
2. **a** - Roots need oxygen for respiration and nutrient uptake
3. **b** - Root rot and poor nutrient uptake occur
4. **b** - 65-75°F (18-24°C)
5. **b** - It influences nutrient uptake, oxygen availability, and metabolic processes
6. **b** - The zone of soil or media influenced by root secretions and microorganisms
7. **b** - It prevents waterlogging and allows oxygen exchange
8. **b** - They help with nutrient cycling and disease suppression
9. **b** - Maintain proper temperature, aeration, moisture, and pH
10. **b** - White or light-colored, firm roots with good branching
