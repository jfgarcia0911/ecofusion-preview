**Module:** 05 - pH and Media Interactions
**Duration:** 20 minutes

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
How can growing media affect pH?
- a) Media never affects pH
- b) Some media can raise or lower pH of the nutrient solution
- c) All media makes water neutral
- d) Media only affects temperature

### Question 2
What should you do with new rockwool before use?
- a) Use it immediately without preparation
- b) Soak and rinse it to adjust pH to acceptable range
- c) Freeze it
- d) Burn it

### Question 3
Which media is considered pH neutral?
- a) Untreated rockwool
- b) Expanded clay pebbles (LECA)
- c) Limestone
- d) Acidic peat

### Question 4
Why is media pH important for nutrient availability?
- a) pH doesn't affect nutrients
- b) pH influences which nutrients are soluble and available to plants
- c) All nutrients are available at any pH
- d) Only water pH matters

### Question 5
What happens if media pH is too high (alkaline)?
- a) All nutrients become more available
- b) Micronutrient availability decreases, causing deficiencies
- c) Plants grow faster
- d) Nothing happens

### Question 6
How can you test the pH of growing media?
- a) Taste it
- b) Test runoff water or create a media slurry to test
- c) Look at its color
- d) Media pH cannot be tested

### Question 7
What is a buffer in the context of growing media?
- a) A cleaning product
- b) A substance that resists pH changes
- c) A type of fish
- d) A watering schedule

### Question 8
Which media may need pH adjustment before initial use?
- a) Pre-washed expanded clay
- b) Rockwool and some coir products
- c) Pure water
- d) None ever need adjustment

### Question 9
What is the target pH range for most hydroponic growing media?
- a) 3.0-4.0
- b) 5.5-6.5
- c) 8.0-9.0
- d) 10.0-11.0

### Question 10
How does decomposing organic media affect pH over time?
- a) No effect
- b) Can lower pH as organic acids are released
- c) Always raises pH significantly
- d) Makes pH unstable in both directions equally

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Some media can raise or lower pH of the nutrient solution
2. **b** - Soak and rinse it to adjust pH to acceptable range
3. **b** - Expanded clay pebbles (LECA)
4. **b** - pH influences which nutrients are soluble and available to plants
5. **b** - Micronutrient availability decreases, causing deficiencies
6. **b** - Test runoff water or create a media slurry to test
7. **b** - A substance that resists pH changes
8. **b** - Rockwool and some coir products
9. **b** - 5.5-6.5
10. **b** - Can lower pH as organic acids are released
