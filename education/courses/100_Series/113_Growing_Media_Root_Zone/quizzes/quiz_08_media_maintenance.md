**Course:** 113 - Growing Media and Root Zone Management
**Module:** 08 - Media Maintenance and Troubleshooting
**Duration:** 20 minutes

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is a sign that growing media needs attention?
- a) Healthy white roots
- b) Salt buildup, algae growth, or foul odors
- c) Even moisture distribution
- d) Good drainage

### Question 2
What causes salt buildup in growing media?
- a) Too much rain
- b) Accumulation of mineral nutrients from fertilizers over time
- c) Underfeeding
- d) Excessive aeration

### Question 3
How can you prevent algae growth on media surfaces?
- a) Increase light exposure to media
- b) Keep media surface covered and reduce light penetration
- c) Add more nutrients
- d) Stop watering completely

### Question 4
What should you do if you notice root discoloration and slime?
- a) Ignore it
- b) Check for root rot, improve aeration, and consider media replacement
- c) Add more water
- d) Increase temperature to 100°F

### Question 5
How does compacted media affect plant growth?
- a) Improves growth
- b) Reduces oxygen availability and restricts root growth
- c) Has no effect
- d) Increases nutrient uptake

### Question 6
What indicates proper media moisture levels?
- a) Media is bone dry
- b) Media is soggy and waterlogged
- c) Media is moist but not saturated, with good drainage
- d) Media is frozen

### Question 7
Why is regular inspection of growing media important?
- a) To waste time
- b) To identify issues early before they affect plant health
- c) Inspection is never needed
- d) To increase costs

### Question 8
How can you address media compaction?
- a) Add more media on top
- b) Replace media or add amendments to improve structure
- c) Water more frequently
- d) Ignore the problem

### Question 9
What causes foul odors in growing media?
- a) Healthy root activity
- b) Anaerobic conditions and decomposing organic matter
- c) Proper drainage
- d) Good aeration

### Question 10
How often should growing media be inspected?
- a) Every 5 years
- b) Never
- c) Regularly (weekly to monthly depending on system)
- d) Only when plants die

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Salt buildup, algae growth, or foul odors
2. **b** - Accumulation of mineral nutrients from fertilizers over time
3. **b** - Keep media surface covered and reduce light penetration
4. **b** - Check for root rot, improve aeration, and consider media replacement
5. **b** - Reduces oxygen availability and restricts root growth
6. **c** - Media is moist but not saturated, with good drainage
7. **b** - To identify issues early before they affect plant health
8. **b** - Replace media or add amendments to improve structure
9. **b** - Anaerobic conditions and decomposing organic matter
10. **c** - Regularly (weekly to monthly depending on system)
