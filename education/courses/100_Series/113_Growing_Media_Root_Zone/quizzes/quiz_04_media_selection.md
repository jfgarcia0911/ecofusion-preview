## Questions

### Question 1
Which growing media is best suited for NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) systems?
- a) Heavy clay soil
- b) Rockwool cubes or minimal media in net pots
- c) Thick peat moss
- d) Compacted sand

### Question 2
What media works well in media bed aquaponics systems?
- a) Water only
- b) Expanded clay pebbles or gravel
- c) Fine silt
- d) Plastic beads

### Question 3
Why is media selection important for system performance?
- a) It only affects appearance
- b) It influences water retention, drainage, and root health
- c) All media perform identically
- d) Media selection doesn't matter

### Question 4
What type of media is preferred for drip irrigation systems?
- a) Media that retains moisture well like coir or perlite mixes
- b) Non-absorbent materials only
- c) Pure water
- d) Solid blocks

### Question 5
Which factor should you consider when choosing media for vertical systems?
- a) Only cost
- b) Weight, water retention, and structural support
- c) Color only
- d) Media is not needed in vertical systems

### Question 6
What is the advantage of using inert media in recirculating systems?
- a) It adds nutrients continuously
- b) It doesn't alter water chemistry or contribute to nutrient imbalances
- c) It requires daily replacement
- d) It blocks water flow

### Question 7
For starting seedlings in any system, what media characteristic is most important?
- a) Maximum drainage
- b) Good moisture retention and fine texture for seed contact
- c) Extreme pH levels
- d) High salt content

### Question 8
Which media is NOT recommended for flood and drain (ebb and flow) systems?
- a) Expanded clay pebbles
- b) Gravel
- c) Fine powder or dust-like media that clogs drains
- d) Grow rocks

### Question 9
What should you consider about media weight in rooftop or elevated systems?
- a) Weight is never a concern
- b) Lighter media reduces structural load requirements
- c) Heavier is always better
- d) Weight has no impact on structure

### Question 10
How does plant type influence media selection?
- a) All plants need the same media
- b) Different plants have different water and aeration requirements
- c) Plants don't interact with media
- d) Media type has no effect on plants

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Rockwool cubes or minimal media in net pots
2. **b** - Expanded clay pebbles or gravel
3. **b** - It influences water retention, drainage, and root health
4. **a** - Media that retains moisture well like coir or perlite mixes
5. **b** - Weight, water retention, and structural support
6. **b** - It doesn't alter water chemistry or contribute to nutrient imbalances
7. **b** - Good moisture retention and fine texture for seed contact
8. **c** - Fine powder or dust-like media that clogs drains
9. **b** - Lighter media reduces structural load requirements
10. **b** - Different plants have different water and aeration requirements
