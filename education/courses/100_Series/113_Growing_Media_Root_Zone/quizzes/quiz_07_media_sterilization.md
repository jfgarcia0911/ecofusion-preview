## Questions

### Question 1
Why is media sterilization important?
- a) To make media look cleaner
- b) To eliminate pathogens, pests, and disease organisms
- c) To change media color
- d) Sterilization is never necessary

### Question 2
Which media can be effectively sterilized and reused?
- a) Expanded clay pebbles
- b) Perlite
- c) Gravel
- d) All of the above

### Question 3
What is a common method for sterilizing growing media?
- a) Painting it
- b) Heat treatment, hydrogen peroxide solution, or bleach solution
- c) Adding sugar
- d) Freezing for one hour

### Question 4
What should you do before reusing growing media?
- a) Use it immediately without cleaning
- b) Remove plant debris and sterilize to eliminate pathogens
- c) Add more old plant material
- d) Never reuse media

### Question 5
How does hydrogen peroxide help in media sterilization?
- a) It adds color
- b) It kills microorganisms through oxidation
- c) It makes media heavier
- d) It has no sterilizing effect

### Question 6
What concentration of bleach solution is typically used for media sterilization?
- a) 100% bleach undiluted
- b) Approximately 10% bleach solution (1 part bleach to 9 parts water)
- c) 0.01% bleach
- d) Bleach should never be used

### Question 7
Why must media be thoroughly rinsed after bleach treatment?
- a) To remove chlorine residue that can harm plants
- b) Rinsing is not necessary
- c) To add nutrients
- d) To change the pH to acidic

### Question 8
What is the benefit of reusing growing media?
- a) No benefits exist
- b) Reduces costs and environmental waste
- c) Always decreases plant health
- d) Makes systems more complicated

### Question 9
Which media is generally NOT recommended for reuse?
- a) Expanded clay pebbles after proper cleaning
- b) Gravel after sterilization
- c) Heavily decomposed organic media with disease history
- d) Perlite after treatment

### Question 10
What should you inspect for when considering media reuse?
- a) Media color only
- b) Structural integrity, contamination, and salt buildup
- c) Age in years only
- d) Brand name

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To eliminate pathogens, pests, and disease organisms
2. **d** - All of the above
3. **b** - Heat treatment, hydrogen peroxide solution, or bleach solution
4. **b** - Remove plant debris and sterilize to eliminate pathogens
5. **b** - It kills microorganisms through oxidation
6. **b** - Approximately 10% bleach solution (1 part bleach to 9 parts water)
7. **a** - To remove chlorine residue that can harm plants
8. **b** - Reduces costs and environmental waste
9. **c** - Heavily decomposed organic media with disease history
10. **b** - Structural integrity, contamination, and salt buildup
