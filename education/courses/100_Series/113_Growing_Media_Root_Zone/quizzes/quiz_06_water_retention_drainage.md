**Module:** 06 - Water Retention and Drainage

## Instructions

## Questions

### Question 1
What is water retention capacity?
- a) The weight of the container
- b) The ability of media to hold water after drainage
- c) The color of water
- d) The temperature of water

### Question 2
Why is proper drainage essential for healthy roots?
- a) It removes all water instantly
- b) It prevents waterlogging and allows oxygen to reach roots
- c) Drainage is not important
- d) It keeps media completely dry

### Question 3
Which media has the highest water retention?
- a) Gravel
- b) Large clay pebbles
- c) Coconut coir or peat
- d) Coarse sand

### Question 4
What is the risk of media with poor drainage?
- a) Roots get too much oxygen
- b) Root rot and oxygen deprivation
- c) Plants grow too fast
- d) No risks exist

### Question 5
How can you improve drainage in growing media?
- a) Compact it more tightly
- b) Mix in perlite or larger-particle materials
- c) Add more water
- d) Use finer particles

### Question 6
What is "field capacity" in growing media?
- a) The maximum size of a growing field
- b) The amount of water media holds after excess drains away
- c) The number of plants per area
- d) The media temperature

### Question 7
Which system requires media with excellent drainage?
- a) Systems with continuous flooding
- b) Ebb and flow or flood and drain systems
- c) Static water culture
- d) Systems without any water

### Question 8
What happens if media retains too much water?
- a) Improved root health
- b) Reduced oxygen in root zone and potential root disease
- c) Faster growth
- d) Nothing negative

### Question 9
How does particle size affect water retention?
- a) Particle size has no effect
- b) Smaller particles generally retain more water
- c) Larger particles retain more water
- d) All particles retain water equally

### Question 10
What is the benefit of balanced water retention and drainage?
- a) Media stays completely dry
- b) Roots have access to both water and oxygen for optimal growth
- c) Plants never need watering
- d) Nutrients are washed away

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The ability of media to hold water after drainage
2. **b** - It prevents waterlogging and allows oxygen to reach roots
3. **c** - Coconut coir or peat
4. **b** - Root rot and oxygen deprivation
5. **b** - Mix in perlite or larger-particle materials
6. **b** - The amount of water media holds after excess drains away
7. **b** - Ebb and flow or flood and drain systems
8. **b** - Reduced oxygen in root zone and potential root disease
9. **b** - Smaller particles generally retain more water
10. **b** - Roots have access to both water and oxygen for optimal growth
