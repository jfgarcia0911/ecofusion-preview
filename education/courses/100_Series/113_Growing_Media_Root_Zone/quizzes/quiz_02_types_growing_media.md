# Quiz 02: Types of Growing Media

**Course:** 113 - Growing Media and Root Zone Management
**Module:** 02 - Types of Growing Media
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is coconut coir made from?
- a) Plastic fibers
- b) The fibrous husk of coconuts
- c) Tree bark
- d) Recycled paper

### Question 2
Which growing media is known for being lightweight and improving aeration?
- a) Sand
- b) Clay soil
- c) Perlite
- d) Gravel

### Question 3
What is a primary characteristic of rockwool?
- a) It is organic and biodegradable
- b) It is made from spun volcanic rock and excellent for water retention
- c) It is extremely heavy
- d) It naturally contains many nutrients

### Question 4
Which media is commonly used in ebb and flow systems?
- a) Water only
- b) Expanded clay pebbles or grow rocks
- c) Pure sand
- d) Newspaper

### Question 5
What is an advantage of using coconut coir?
- a) It is non-renewable
- b) It is a renewable resource with good water retention and aeration
- c) It rapidly decomposes
- d) It increases pH dramatically

### Question 6
What is vermiculite primarily used for?
- a) As a standalone growing media in all systems
- b) To improve water retention when mixed with other media
- c) As fish food
- d) To increase system pH only

### Question 7
Which media requires pH adjustment before use?
- a) Expanded clay pebbles
- b) Perlite
- c) Rockwool (often needs to be pH balanced)
- d) All media always need adjustment

### Question 8
What type of media is best for starting seeds?
- a) Large gravel
- b) Fine, moisture-retentive media like rockwool cubes or coir
- c) Dry sand
- d) Wood chips

### Question 9
Which media can be sterilized and reused multiple times?
- a) Coconut coir
- b) Perlite
- c) Expanded clay pebbles
- d) All of the above with proper treatment

### Question 10
What is the main disadvantage of using sand as growing media?
- a) Too much aeration
- b) Poor drainage and can become compacted
- c) Too expensive
- d) It floats

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The fibrous husk of coconuts
2. **c** - Perlite
3. **b** - It is made from spun volcanic rock and excellent for water retention
4. **b** - Expanded clay pebbles or grow rocks
5. **b** - It is a renewable resource with good water retention and aeration
6. **b** - To improve water retention when mixed with other media
7. **c** - Rockwool (often needs to be pH balanced)
8. **b** - Fine, moisture-retentive media like rockwool cubes or coir
9. **d** - All of the above with proper treatment
10. **b** - Poor drainage and can become compacted
