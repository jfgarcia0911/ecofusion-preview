## Course Description

This foundational course explores the science and application of growing media for hydroponic and aquaponic systems. Learners will understand media properties, selection criteria, and root zone management techniques essential for optimizing plant health and productivity in soilless growing environments.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Describe** the physical and chemical properties of common growing media
2. **Compare** different media types and their applications
3. **Test** media characteristics using standard methods
4. **Select** appropriate media for specific crops and systems
5. **Manage** the root zone environment for optimal plant health
6. **Apply** media preparation and maintenance best practices
