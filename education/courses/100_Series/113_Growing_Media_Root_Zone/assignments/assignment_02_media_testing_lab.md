# Assignment 2: Growing Media Testing Lab

## Course 107: Growing Media & Root Zone

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 20-30 min

## Objective
Test physical properties of growing media samples.

## Tests Performed

**1. Water Holding Capacity**
- Saturate media
- Drain and weigh
- Calculate % water retention

**2. Drainage Rate**
- Timed drainage test
- Compare media types

**3. pH Testing**
- Test media pH
- Test runoff pH

**4. Air-Filled Porosity**
- Volume displacement method
