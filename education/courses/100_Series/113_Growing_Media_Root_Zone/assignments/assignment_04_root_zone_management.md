# Assignment 4: Root Zone Management Plan

## Course 107: Growing Media & Root Zone

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Develop comprehensive root zone management strategy.

## Requirements

### 1. Media Selection & Preparation (4 pts)
- Media type and justification
- Pre-treatment procedures
- Buffering/conditioning
- Sterilization if needed

### 2. Environmental Control (5 pts)
- Root zone temperature management
- Oxygen levels and aeration
- Moisture management
- EC/pH monitoring in root zone

### 3. Problem Prevention (3 pts)
- Root disease prevention
- Algae control
- Salt buildup management
- Media degradation

### 4. Maintenance & Replacement (3 pts)
- Cleaning protocols
- Media lifespan
- Replacement schedule
- Disposal/recycling
