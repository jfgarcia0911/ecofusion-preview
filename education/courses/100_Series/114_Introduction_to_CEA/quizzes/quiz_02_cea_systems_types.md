# Quiz 02: CEA System Types

**Course:** 114 - Introduction to Controlled Environment Agriculture
**Module:** 02 - CEA System Types
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the difference between a greenhouse and an indoor vertical farm?
- a) No difference exists
- b) Greenhouses use natural sunlight while vertical farms typically use artificial lighting
- c) Greenhouses are always smaller
- d) Vertical farms don't control temperature

### Question 2
Which CEA system relies primarily on natural sunlight?
- a) Warehouse vertical farms
- b) Plant factories
- c) Greenhouses
- d) Basement growing operations

### Question 3
What is a key feature of hydroponic systems?
- a) Growing plants in traditional soil
- b) Growing plants in water-based nutrient solutions without soil
- c) Growing only aquatic plants
- d) Requiring no nutrients

### Question 4
What makes aquaponic systems unique?
- a) They use only chemical fertilizers
- b) They integrate fish farming with plant cultivation
- c) They require no water
- d) They work only outdoors

### Question 5
Which system type offers the most precise environmental control?
- a) Open field farming
- b) Hoop houses
- c) Fully enclosed indoor growing facilities
- d) Rain-dependent systems

### Question 6
What is the primary purpose of a high tunnel or hoop house?
- a) To provide complete climate control
- b) To extend growing seasons with basic protection
- c) To eliminate all sunlight
- d) To increase pesticide use

### Question 7
Which CEA system is best suited for leafy greens and herbs?
- a) Traditional orchards
- b) Hydroponic or aeroponic systems
- c) Dry land farming
- d) Rain-fed agriculture

### Question 8
What is aeroponic growing?
- a) Growing plants in soil
- b) Growing plants with roots suspended in air and misted with nutrients
- c) Growing plants underwater
- d) Growing plants without any water

### Question 9
Which system typically has the highest production per square foot?
- a) Traditional field farming
- b) Vertical farming systems
- c) Pasture systems
- d) Broadcast seeding

### Question 10
What is a plant factory?
- a) A traditional greenhouse
- b) A fully controlled indoor facility with artificial lighting and automation
- c) An outdoor farm with machinery
- d) A facility that manufactures plastic plants

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Greenhouses use natural sunlight while vertical farms typically use artificial lighting
2. **c** - Greenhouses
3. **b** - Growing plants in water-based nutrient solutions without soil
4. **b** - They integrate fish farming with plant cultivation
5. **c** - Fully enclosed indoor growing facilities
6. **b** - To extend growing seasons with basic protection
7. **b** - Hydroponic or aeroponic systems
8. **b** - Growing plants with roots suspended in air and misted with nutrients
9. **b** - Vertical farming systems
10. **b** - A fully controlled indoor facility with artificial lighting and automation
