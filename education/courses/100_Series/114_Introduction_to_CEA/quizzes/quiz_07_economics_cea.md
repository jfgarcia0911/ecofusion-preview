## Questions

### Question 1
What is typically the largest operational expense in CEA facilities?
- a) Marketing
- b) Energy costs for lighting and climate control
- c) Insurance
- d) Packaging materials

### Question 2
What is a significant barrier to entry for CEA operations?
- a) Too much available funding
- b) High initial capital investment for infrastructure and equipment
- c) Lack of crop options
- d) Excessive government support

### Question 3
How can CEA operations maximize profitability?
- a) Minimize crop quality
- b) Optimize yield per square foot and select high-value crops
- c) Reduce production frequency
- d) Increase waste

### Question 4
What is vertical integration in CEA business models?
- a) Stacking plants vertically only
- b) Controlling multiple stages from production to distribution
- c) Building tall structures
- d) Growing climbing plants

### Question 5
Which market strategy can increase revenue for CEA operations?
- a) Selling only wholesale at low prices
- b) Direct-to-consumer sales and specialty crop production
- c) Avoiding all marketing
- d) Producing only commodity crops

### Question 6
What role does location play in CEA economics?
- a) Location doesn't matter
- b) Proximity to markets reduces transportation costs and improves freshness
- c) Remote locations are always better
- d) Urban locations are always unprofitable

### Question 7
How does year-round production benefit CEA economics?
- a) It doesn't provide any benefit
- b) Consistent revenue stream and ability to meet demand during off-seasons
- c) Increases downtime
- d) Reduces total annual output

### Question 8
What is the importance of economies of scale in CEA?
- a) Smaller is always more profitable
- b) Larger operations can reduce per-unit costs through efficiency
- c) Scale has no impact on profitability
- d) CEA doesn't follow economic principles

### Question 9
Which factor can reduce energy costs in CEA?
- a) Running equipment inefficiently
- b) Using energy-efficient LED lighting and renewable energy sources
- c) Leaving systems on unnecessarily
- d) Avoiding all technology

### Question 10
What financial metric is critical for evaluating CEA profitability?
- a) Number of plants only
- b) Return on investment (ROI) and operating margins
- c) Facility color scheme
- d) Number of employees
