**Module:** 05 - Crop Selection for CEA

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
Which crops are best suited for CEA systems?
- a) Large fruit trees
- b) Leafy greens, herbs, and vine crops
- c) Wheat and corn exclusively
- d) Root vegetables only

### Question 2
What makes lettuce ideal for CEA production?
- a) It requires years to mature
- b) Fast growth cycle and high market demand
- c) It needs extreme temperatures
- d) It requires large amounts of space

### Question 3
Which factor is most important when selecting crops for CEA?
- a) The color of the crop
- b) Market value and system compatibility
- c) How the crop sounds when harvested
- d) The country of origin

### Question 4
Why are herbs popular in CEA operations?
- a) They require no light
- b) High value per pound and consistent market demand
- c) They grow slowly
- d) They need no nutrients

### Question 5
What characteristic makes a crop suitable for vertical farming?
- a) Very tall growth habit
- b) Compact size and short growth cycle
- c) Requires outdoor conditions
- d) Needs deep soil

### Question 6
Which crop category typically has the shortest time to harvest in CEA?
- a) Tree fruits
- b) Microgreens
- c) Pumpkins
- d) Sweet corn

### Question 7
What is a challenge of growing fruiting crops like tomatoes in CEA?
- a) They are too easy to grow
- b) Higher energy requirements for lighting and longer growth cycles
- c) They require no pollination
- d) They have no market value

### Question 8
Why is crop diversity important in CEA operations?
- a) It's not important
- b) Spreads market risk and can maximize facility utilization
- c) To confuse customers
- d) Single crops are always better

### Question 9
Which crop is considered a high-value specialty item for CEA?
- a) Generic lettuce only
- b) Specialty greens like arugula or unique basil varieties
- c) Common weeds
- d) Field corn

### Question 10
What should you consider regarding crop rotation in CEA?
- a) Rotation is impossible in CEA
- b) Planning successive crops to maximize production and revenue
- c) Growing only one crop forever
- d) Waiting years between crops

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Leafy greens, herbs, and vine crops
2. **b** - Fast growth cycle and high market demand
3. **b** - Market value and system compatibility
4. **b** - High value per pound and consistent market demand
5. **b** - Compact size and short growth cycle
6. **b** - Microgreens
7. **b** - Higher energy requirements for lighting and longer growth cycles
8. **b** - Spreads market risk and can maximize facility utilization
9. **b** - Specialty greens like arugula or unique basil varieties
10. **b** - Planning successive crops to maximize production and revenue
