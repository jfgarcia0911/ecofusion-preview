**Module:** 03 - Environmental Controls

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
Which environmental factor has the greatest impact on plant photosynthesis?
- a) Noise level
- b) Light intensity and spectrum
- c) Floor color
- d) Wall material

### Question 2
What is the purpose of HVAC systems in CEA facilities?
- a) To grow fish
- b) To control temperature and humidity
- c) To harvest crops
- d) To package produce

### Question 3
What is VPD in the context of CEA?
- a) Very Poor Design
- b) Vapor Pressure Deficit (relationship between temperature and humidity)
- c) Vertical Plant Distribution
- d) Variable Power Device

### Question 4
Why is CO2 supplementation used in some CEA operations?
- a) To harm plants
- b) To enhance photosynthesis and increase yields
- c) To reduce plant growth
- d) It serves no purpose

### Question 5
What is the optimal temperature range for most leafy greens?
- a) 32-40°F (0-4°C)
- b) 60-75°F (15-24°C)
- c) 90-100°F (32-38°C)
- d) 110-120°F (43-49°C)

### Question 6
How does humidity affect plant growth?
- a) Humidity has no effect on plants
- b) It influences transpiration, disease pressure, and nutrient uptake
- c) Only temperature matters
- d) High humidity always improves growth

### Question 7
What is the purpose of air circulation in CEA facilities?
- a) To create wind damage
- b) To distribute temperature, humidity, and CO2 evenly
- c) To remove all oxygen
- d) Circulation is not needed

### Question 8
Which control system allows remote monitoring and adjustment of environmental parameters?
- a) Manual switches only
- b) Automated climate control systems with sensors
- c) Mechanical timers from 1950s
- d) No technology is used in CEA

### Question 9
What happens when temperature fluctuates dramatically in CEA systems?
- a) Plants grow better
- b) Plants experience stress affecting growth and quality
- c) No effect on plants
- d) Yields increase significantly

### Question 10
Why is precise environmental control important in CEA?
- a) It's not important
- b) It optimizes growth, quality, and resource efficiency
- c) To make facilities more expensive
- d) To complicate operations

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Light intensity and spectrum
2. **b** - To control temperature and humidity
3. **b** - Vapor Pressure Deficit (relationship between temperature and humidity)
4. **b** - To enhance photosynthesis and increase yields
5. **b** - 60-75°F (15-24°C)
6. **b** - It influences transpiration, disease pressure, and nutrient uptake
7. **b** - To distribute temperature, humidity, and CO2 evenly
8. **b** - Automated climate control systems with sensors

10. **b** - It optimizes growth, quality, and resource efficiency
