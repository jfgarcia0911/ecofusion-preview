# Quiz 01: What is CEA?

**Course:** 114 - Introduction to Controlled Environment Agriculture
**Module:** 01 - What is CEA?
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What does CEA stand for?
- a) Central Environmental Authority
- b) Controlled Environment Agriculture
- c) Continuous Economic Analysis
- d) Climate Education Alliance

### Question 2
What is the primary goal of Controlled Environment Agriculture?
- a) To grow plants outdoors in natural conditions
- b) To optimize growing conditions by controlling environmental factors
- c) To reduce crop yields
- d) To eliminate all technology from farming

### Question 3
Which of the following is NOT a component typically controlled in CEA?
- a) Temperature
- b) Light
- c) Rainfall patterns
- d) Humidity

### Question 4
What is a major advantage of CEA systems?
- a) Complete dependence on weather
- b) Year-round production regardless of climate
- c) Lower yields than traditional farming
- d) Increased water usage

### Question 5
Which type of facility is considered a CEA system?
- a) Open field farming
- b) Greenhouses with climate control
- c) Pasture land
- d) Orchards

### Question 6
How does CEA impact water usage compared to traditional agriculture?
- a) Uses significantly more water
- b) Uses the same amount of water
- c) Can reduce water usage by 70-90%
- d) Eliminates the need for water completely

### Question 7
What is vertical farming?
- a) Growing crops on steep hillsides
- b) Stacking growing layers vertically to maximize space
- c) Traditional row farming
- d) Growing only climbing plants

### Question 8
Which factor makes CEA particularly valuable in urban areas?
- a) Requires large amounts of land
- b) Efficient space utilization and proximity to consumers
- c) Produces lower quality crops
- d) Requires outdoor space only

### Question 9
What is a challenge of CEA systems?
- a) Too much reliance on natural sunlight
- b) High initial capital investment and energy costs
- c) Inability to control any variables
- d) Can only grow in summer

### Question 10
How does CEA contribute to food security?
- a) By reducing food production
- b) By enabling local production in areas with challenging climates
- c) By increasing transportation distances
- d) By limiting crop variety

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Controlled Environment Agriculture
2. **b** - To optimize growing conditions by controlling environmental factors
3. **c** - Rainfall patterns
4. **b** - Year-round production regardless of climate
5. **b** - Greenhouses with climate control
6. **c** - Can reduce water usage by 70-90%
7. **b** - Stacking growing layers vertically to maximize space
8. **b** - Efficient space utilization and proximity to consumers
9. **b** - High initial capital investment and energy costs
10. **b** - By enabling local production in areas with challenging climates
