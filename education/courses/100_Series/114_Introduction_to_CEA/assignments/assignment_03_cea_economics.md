# Assignment 3: CEA Economics Analysis

## Course 108: Introduction to CEA

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 30-45 min

## Objective
Analyze economic factors in CEA operations.

## Analysis Components

**1. Capital Costs (5 pts)**
Compare startup costs for different CEA systems:
- Greenhouse vs. indoor farm
- Small scale vs. commercial
- Low-tech vs. high-tech

**2. Operating Costs (5 pts)**
- Energy consumption
- Labor requirements
- Maintenance
- Inputs (seeds, nutrients, etc.)

**3. Revenue Potential (5 pts)**
- Yield per square foot
- Crop pricing
- Market opportunities
- Break-even analysis
