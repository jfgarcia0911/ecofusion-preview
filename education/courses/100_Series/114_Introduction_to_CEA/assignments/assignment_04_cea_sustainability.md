# Assignment 4: CEA Sustainability Analysis

## Course 108: Introduction to CEA

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Evaluate sustainability aspects of Controlled Environment Agriculture.

## Requirements

### 1. Environmental Impact (5 pts)
- Water use efficiency
- Energy consumption
- Carbon footprint
- Waste reduction
- Land use comparison

### 2. Resource Efficiency (5 pts)
- Water recirculation and savings
- Nutrient cycling
- Year-round production
- Space optimization
- Transportation reduction

### 3. Sustainability Metrics (3 pts)
- Calculate water savings vs. field agriculture
- Energy use per pound produced
- Food miles reduction
- Overall sustainability score

### 4. Improvements (2 pts)
- Renewable energy integration
- Waste valorization
- Circular economy approaches
