# Assignment 5: CEA Facility Proposal Project

## Course 108: Introduction to CEA

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Propose a CEA facility appropriate for your community or market.

## Project Components

### 1. Facility Concept (4 pts)
- Type of CEA system
- Scale and capacity
- Location rationale
- Target market

### 2. Technology Plan (4 pts)
- Growing system selection
- Environmental control approach
- Automation level
- Technology justification

### 3. Economic Analysis (4 pts)
- Capital budget
- Operating costs projection
- Revenue model
- Financial feasibility

### 4. Implementation Plan (3 pts)
- Phased development
- Timeline
- Key milestones
- Risk mitigation

## Deliverable
Professional proposal document (5-7 pages) with visuals and financial projections
