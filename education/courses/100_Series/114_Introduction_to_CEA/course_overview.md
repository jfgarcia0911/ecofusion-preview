## Course Description

This foundational course provides a comprehensive introduction to Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA), exploring the technologies, systems, and practices that enable year-round food production independent of external conditions. Learners will examine greenhouse, vertical farm, and indoor growing operations while evaluating the economic and sustainability considerations of CEA.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Define** CEA and explain its role in modern food production
2. **Identify** major CEA system types and their characteristics
3. **Assess** technology components including lighting, climate control, and automation
4. **Evaluate** the economics of CEA operations
5. **Analyze** sustainability benefits and challenges of CEA
6. **Develop** a basic CEA facility concept proposal
