### 6.3 Revenue Models

```
GREENHOUSE REVENUE SCENARIOS (10,000 sq ft)

SCENARIO 1: TOMATO PRODUCTION
├─ Growing area: 9,000 sq ft
├─ Yield: 60 lbs/sq ft/year
├─ Production: 540,000 lbs/year
├─ Price: $2.50/lb (wholesale)
├─ Revenue: $1,350,000/year
├─ Operating costs: $1,010,000
├─ EBITDA: $340,000 (25% margin)
└─ Net profit: ~$240,000 (18%)

SCENARIO 2: LETTUCE/GREENS
├─ Growing area: 9,000 sq ft
├─ Yield: 24 heads/sq ft/year
├─ Production: 216,000 heads/year
├─ Price: $1.75/head (wholesale)
├─ Revenue: $378,000/year
├─ Operating costs: $850,000
└─ Net loss: -$472,000 ✗
     (Not viable without higher volume/price)

SCENARIO 3: HERBS (Basil)
├─ Growing area: 9,000 sq ft
├─ Yield: 20 lbs/sq ft/year
├─ Production: 180,000 lbs/year
├─ Price: $8/lb (wholesale)
├─ Revenue: $1,440,000/year
├─ Operating costs: $1,050,000
├─ EBITDA: $390,000 (27% margin)
└─ Net profit: ~$290,000 (20%)

KEY INSIGHT: Crop selection drives profitability!
```

### 6.4 Break-Even Analysis

```
BREAK-EVEN CALCULATION

Formula:
Break-even (units) = Fixed Costs / (Price - Variable Cost per unit)

EXAMPLE: TOMATO GREENHOUSE
├─ Fixed costs: $290,000/year
├─ Price per lb: $2.50
├─ Variable cost per lb: $0.68
├─ Contribution margin: $1.82/lb
└─ Break-even: $290,000 / $1.82 = 159,341 lbs/year

If capacity = 540,000 lbs/year:
└─ Break-even at 30% of capacity

BREAK-EVEN TIMELINE
Year 1: -$100,000 (startup inefficiencies)
Year 2: -$50,000 (improving)
Year 3: +$150,000 (profitable)
Year 4: +$240,000 (full operation)
Year 5: +$300,000 (optimized)

Cumulative: Break-even in Year 4
```

### 6.5 Pricing Strategies

```
PRICING APPROACHES

COST-PLUS PRICING
├─ Calculate total cost per unit
├─ Add desired margin (20-40%)
├─ Example: $1.50 cost + 40% = $2.10 price
└─ Pro: Ensures profitability
    Con: May not be market competitive

MARKET-BASED PRICING
├─ Research competitor prices
├─ Position slightly below/at/above
├─ Example: Market basil at $8/lb, price at $7.50
└─ Pro: Competitive
    Con: May not cover your costs

VALUE-BASED PRICING
├─ Price based on customer perceived value
├─ Premium for: local, organic, freshness, story
├─ Example: $12/lb for "ultra-fresh urban basil"
└─ Pro: Maximum margins
    Con: Limited market size

HYBRID APPROACH (Recommended)
├─ Know your costs (floor price)
├─ Research market (competitive range)
├─ Identify value differentiators
├─ Price competitively with premium where justified
└─ Adjust by customer segment (retail vs wholesale)

TYPICAL PRICE POINTS:
Wholesale (B2B):      $2-8/lb (varies by crop)
Retail (direct):      2-3× wholesale
Restaurants (fresh):  Premium 20-50% over wholesale
```

### 6.6 Funding Sources

```
CEA FINANCING OPTIONS

EQUITY FINANCING (No repayment, but ownership dilution)
┌────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Personal Savings/Family            │
│ • Pros: No debt, flexible          │
│ • Cons: Risk personal wealth       │
│ • Typical: $50K-500K               │
├────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Angel Investors                    │
│ • Pros: Expertise, connections     │
│ • Cons: Equity dilution (10-30%)   │
│ • Typical: $100K-1M                │
├────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Venture Capital                    │
│ • Pros: Large amounts, resources   │
│ • Cons: Control loss, pressure     │
│ • Typical: $1M-50M+                │
└────────────────────────────────────┘

DEBT FINANCING (Repayment required)
┌────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Bank Loans/SBA                     │
│ • Pros: No equity dilution         │
│ • Cons: Repayment, collateral      │
│ • Typical: $100K-5M, 4-8% interest │
├────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Equipment Financing                │
│ • Pros: Equipment is collateral    │
│ • Cons: Higher rates (6-12%)       │
│ • Typical: 60-80% of equipment cost│
├────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Crowdfunding                       │
│ • Pros: Marketing + capital        │
│ • Cons: Time intensive, uncertain  │
│ • Typical: $10K-500K               │
└────────────────────────────────────┘

GRANTS & INCENTIVES
┌────────────────────────────────────┐
│ USDA/Agriculture Grants            │
│ Economic Development Incentives    │
│ University Research Partnerships   │
│ Green Energy/Sustainability Grants │
│ • Typical: $10K-500K (non-dilutive)│
└────────────────────────────────────┘

RECOMMENDED MIX:
├─ 20-40% Equity (personal + investors)
├─ 40-60% Debt (bank + equipment loans)
└─ 10-20% Grants/incentives (opportunistic)
```

### 6.8 Financial Metrics for CEA

```
KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

PRODUCTION METRICS
├─ Yield per sq ft per year
├─ Crops per year (turnover)
├─ First quality percentage (95%+ target)
├─ Waste percentage (<5% target)
└─ Labor hours per lb produced

FINANCIAL METRICS
├─ Revenue per sq ft ($100-200+ target)
├─ Gross margin (30-50% target)
├─ EBITDA margin (20-35% target)
├─ Net profit margin (10-25% target)
├─ Return on assets (ROA): 15%+ target
└─ Cash conversion cycle (days)

EFFICIENCY METRICS
├─ Energy cost per lb ($0.10-0.30)
├─ Water use per lb (1-5 gallons)
├─ Labor cost as % of revenue (15-30%)
├─ Customer acquisition cost
└─ Customer lifetime value

BENCHMARKS BY SYSTEM TYPE:
Greenhouse (tomatoes):  $120-180/sq ft revenue
Vertical farm (lettuce): $150-250/sq ft revenue
Hoop house (greens):     $30-60/sq ft revenue
```

### 6.9 Risk Management

```
CEA BUSINESS RISKS & MITIGATION

PRODUCTION RISKS
├─ Disease outbreak → Sanitation, IPM, insurance
├─ Equipment failure → Maintenance, redundancy
├─ Crop quality issues → QC, training, testing
└─ Weather (greenhouse) → Backup systems, insurance

MARKET RISKS
├─ Price volatility → Diversification, contracts
├─ Customer loss → Multiple customers, relationships
├─ New competition → Differentiation, innovation
└─ Demand fluctuations → Market research, agility

FINANCIAL RISKS
├─ Cash flow gaps → Reserves, credit line
├─ Input cost increases → Suppliers, efficiency
├─ Interest rate changes → Fixed-rate loans
└─ Under-capitalization → Realistic planning, reserves

OPERATIONAL RISKS
├─ Labor shortage → Training, culture, automation
├─ Supply chain → Multiple suppliers, inventory
├─ Regulatory changes → Compliance, associations
└─ Technology obsolescence → Modular, upgradable

RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES:
├─ Insurance (property, crop, liability)
├─ Contingency funds (10-20% of costs)
├─ Diversification (crops, customers)
├─ Contracts (supply & sales agreements)
└─ Continuous improvement & learning
```

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **EBITDA** | Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization |
| **Gross Margin** | Revenue minus cost of goods sold, as percentage |
| **Break-Even** | Point where total revenue equals total costs |
| **Working Capital** | Cash needed for day-to-day operations |
| **Capex** | Capital Expenditures - money spent on assets |
| **Opex** | Operating Expenditures - ongoing costs |
| **Cash Flow** | Movement of money in and out of business |
| **ROA** | Return on Assets - profit relative to total assets |
| **Contribution Margin** | Price minus variable cost per unit |
| **Sensitivity Analysis** | Testing how changes in assumptions affect outcomes |
