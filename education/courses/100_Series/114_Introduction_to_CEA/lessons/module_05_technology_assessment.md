### 5.2 ROI and Financial Analysis

#### Return on Investment Calculation

```
ROI ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

ROI = (Net Profit / Investment) × 100%

Example: LED Lighting Upgrade

INVESTMENT
├─ LED fixtures: $50,000
├─ Installation: $5,000
├─ Electrical: $3,000
└─ TOTAL: $58,000

ANNUAL BENEFITS
├─ Energy savings: $12,000/year
├─ Increased yield: $8,000/year
├─ Reduced replacement: $2,000/year
└─ TOTAL: $22,000/year

ANNUAL COSTS
├─ Maintenance: $1,000/year
└─ NET BENEFIT: $21,000/year

SIMPLE PAYBACK = $58,000 / $21,000 = 2.8 years

ROI (Year 1) = ($21,000 / $58,000) × 100 = 36%

Over 5-year LED life: ($105,000 / $58,000) × 100 = 181%
```

#### Payback Period Targets

```
ACCEPTABLE PAYBACK BY TECHNOLOGY

Technology          Target Payback    Justification
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LED lights          2-4 years         Energy savings
Automation          1-3 years         Labor savings
Climate control     3-5 years         Yield improvement
Sensors/monitoring  1-2 years         Loss prevention
Advanced controls   2-4 years         Efficiency gains
Robotics            3-7 years         Labor + consistency

Shorter = Better (but consider intangibles)
```

### 5.4 Build vs. Buy Decisions

```
BUILD VS. BUY ANALYSIS

BUILD IN-HOUSE
Pros:
✓ Customization
✓ Lower cost (maybe)
✓ Full control
✓ Learning/IP development

Cons:
✗ Time investment
✗ Technical expertise needed
✗ No warranty/support
✗ Ongoing maintenance burden

BUY TURNKEY/COMMERCIAL
Pros:
✓ Faster deployment
✓ Proven technology
✓ Support & warranty
✓ Financing options

Cons:
✗ Higher upfront cost
✗ Less flexibility
✗ Vendor lock-in
✗ May include unneeded features

RECOMMENDATION BY COMPONENT:

BUILD: Benches, simple structures, irrigation
BUY: Climate controls, LEDs, automation systems
HYBRID: Greenhouse (structure DIY, equipment commercial)
```

### 5.5 Common Technology Mistakes

```
PITFALLS TO AVOID

1. OVER-AUTOMATION TOO SOON
   └─ Start simple, automate proven processes
   └─ Learn manually before automating

2. UNDER-SIZING CRITICAL SYSTEMS
   └─ Don't skimp on HVAC, power, water
   └─ Build in 20-30% excess capacity

3. IGNORING OPERATING COSTS
   └─ Cheap equipment may cost more long-term
   └─ Energy efficiency pays back

4. VENDOR LOCK-IN
   └─ Avoid proprietary systems
   └─ Demand open protocols, data portability

5. LATEST/GREATEST SYNDROME
   └─ Proven technology > cutting edge
   └─ Let others beta test

6. FORGETTING SCALABILITY
   └─ Can system grow with you?
   └─ Modular > monolithic

7. NEGLECTING TRAINING
   └─ Budget 10-15% for training
   └─ Technology useless if staff can't operate

8. NO BACKUP PLANS
   └─ Redundancy for critical systems
   └─ Manual overrides essential
```

### 5.6 Vendor Selection Criteria

```
VENDOR EVALUATION CHECKLIST

COMPANY STABILITY
□ Years in business (5+ preferred)
□ Financial health
□ Customer references (call them!)
□ Industry reputation

PRODUCT QUALITY
□ Certifications (UL, CE, etc.)
□ Warranty terms (2+ years)
□ MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)
□ Performance data (not just claims)

SUPPORT & SERVICE
□ Technical support hours
□ Response time commitments
□ Spare parts availability
□ On-site service (if needed)
□ Training programs

BUSINESS TERMS
□ Payment terms
□ Lead times
□ Return/refund policy
□ Contract flexibility
□ Exit clauses

COMPATIBILITY
□ Works with existing systems?
□ Open protocols (Modbus, BACnet)?
□ Third-party integration?
□ Data export capabilities?
```

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **ROI** | Return on Investment - profitability measure |
| **Payback Period** | Time required for investment to pay for itself |
| **TCO** | Total Cost of Ownership - includes operating costs over life |
| **Turnkey Solution** | Complete system ready to operate |
| **Vendor Lock-in** | Dependence on single supplier for proprietary system |
| **MTBF** | Mean Time Between Failures - reliability metric |
| **Scalability** | Ability of system to grow/expand |
| **Modular** | Designed in independent units that work together |
| **Capex** | Capital Expenditure - upfront investment |
| **Opex** | Operating Expenditure - ongoing costs |
