# Quiz 05: Water Testing Methods

**Course:** 112 - Water Chemistry Foundations
**Module:** 05 - Water Testing Methods
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the purpose of regular water testing?
- a) To waste test kits
- b) To monitor water quality parameters and prevent problems
- c) To increase costs
- d) Testing is unnecessary

### Question 2
Which type of test kit uses color changes to indicate parameter levels?
- a) Digital meter
- b) Colorimetric test kit
- c) Thermometer
- d) Scale

### Question 3
What is an advantage of digital water quality meters?
- a) They are always less accurate
- b) They provide quick, precise readings
- c) They never need calibration
- d) They are disposable

### Question 4
How often should ammonia be tested in a new system during cycling?
- a) Once per year
- b) Once per month
- c) Daily or every other day
- d) Never

### Question 5
What should you do before using a pH meter?
- a) Shake it vigorously
- b) Calibrate it with standard buffer solutions
- c) Heat it up
- d) Freeze it

### Question 6
Which parameter should be tested immediately if fish show signs of stress?
- a) Water color
- b) Ammonia, nitrite, dissolved oxygen, and pH
- c) Tank dimensions
- d) Feed quality only

### Question 7
What is the benefit of keeping a water quality log?
- a) No benefit
- b) To track trends, identify issues early, and improve management
- c) To create extra paperwork
- d) To replace actual testing

### Question 8
How should test kit reagents be stored?
- a) In direct sunlight
- b) In extreme heat
- c) In a cool, dark place according to manufacturer instructions
- d) Storage doesn't matter

### Question 9
What indicates that a test kit may be expired or compromised?
- a) Results that don't make sense or reagents that have changed color
- b) The kit is one day old
- c) The kit is stored properly
- d) Test results are consistent

### Question 10
Which testing method is most accurate for critical parameters?
- a) Guessing
- b) Laboratory-grade equipment or certified testing services
- c) Visual inspection only
- d) Tasting the water

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To monitor water quality parameters and prevent problems
2. **b** - Colorimetric test kit
3. **b** - They provide quick, precise readings
4. **c** - Daily or every other day
5. **b** - Calibrate it with standard buffer solutions
6. **b** - Ammonia, nitrite, dissolved oxygen, and pH
7. **b** - To track trends, identify issues early, and improve management
8. **c** - In a cool, dark place according to manufacturer instructions
9. **a** - Results that don't make sense or reagents that have changed color
10. **b** - Laboratory-grade equipment or certified testing services
