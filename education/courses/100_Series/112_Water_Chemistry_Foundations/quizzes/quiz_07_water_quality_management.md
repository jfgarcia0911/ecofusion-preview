# Quiz 07: Water Quality Management

**Course:** 112 - Water Chemistry Foundations
**Module:** 07 - Water Quality Management
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary goal of water quality management?
- a) To make water look clear
- b) To maintain optimal conditions for fish, plants, and beneficial bacteria
- c) To reduce water temperature
- d) To eliminate all minerals

### Question 2
Which practice helps maintain stable water quality?
- a) Making frequent large water changes
- b) Regular monitoring and gradual adjustments
- c) Ignoring water parameters
- d) Randomly adding chemicals

### Question 3
What is biofilm in aquaculture systems?
- a) A type of fish disease
- b) A layer of beneficial bacteria on surfaces
- c) Dirty water
- d) Dead plant material

### Question 4
How often should water be partially changed in most systems?
- a) Never
- b) Hourly
- c) Depends on system type, but often weekly or as needed based on testing
- d) Every minute

### Question 5
What is the purpose of mechanical filtration?
- a) To remove dissolved gases
- b) To remove solid waste particles
- c) To add nutrients
- d) To heat the water

### Question 6
What does biological filtration provide?
- a) A home for beneficial bacteria that convert ammonia
- b) Color to the water
- c) Extra fish food
- d) Light for plants

### Question 7
What is the benefit of proper water circulation?
- a) It wastes energy
- b) It distributes oxygen, nutrients, and maintains temperature uniformity
- c) It stresses fish
- d) It has no benefit

### Question 8
How can you reduce ammonia spikes in an established system?
- a) Overfeed fish
- b) Avoid water testing
- c) Proper feeding practices and adequate biofiltration
- d) Remove all bacteria

### Question 9
What should you do if multiple water quality parameters are out of range?
- a) Make all corrections at once rapidly
- b) Prioritize critical issues and make gradual, monitored adjustments
- c) Ignore the problem
- d) Drain the entire system

### Question 10
Which factor can stress beneficial bacteria populations?
- a) Stable conditions
- b) Sudden pH changes, temperature fluctuations, or chlorine exposure
- c) Proper aeration
- d) Adequate surface area

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To maintain optimal conditions for fish, plants, and beneficial bacteria
2. **b** - Regular monitoring and gradual adjustments
3. **b** - A layer of beneficial bacteria on surfaces
4. **c** - Depends on system type, but often weekly or as needed based on testing
5. **b** - To remove solid waste particles
6. **a** - A home for beneficial bacteria that convert ammonia
7. **b** - It distributes oxygen, nutrients, and maintains temperature uniformity
8. **c** - Proper feeding practices and adequate biofiltration
9. **b** - Prioritize critical issues and make gradual, monitored adjustments
10. **b** - Sudden pH changes, temperature fluctuations, or chlorine exposure
