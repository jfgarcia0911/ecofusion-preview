# Quiz 02: The Nitrogen Cycle

**Course:** 112 - Water Chemistry Foundations
**Module:** 02 - The Nitrogen Cycle
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the nitrogen cycle in aquaculture?
- a) The process of adding nitrogen fertilizer
- b) The biological conversion of toxic ammonia to less harmful compounds
- c) The removal of all nitrogen from water
- d) The cycling of water through pumps

### Question 2
What is the most toxic form of nitrogen to fish?
- a) Nitrate
- b) Nitrite
- c) Ammonia (NH3)
- d) Nitrogen gas

### Question 3
Which beneficial bacteria convert ammonia to nitrite?
- a) E. coli
- b) Nitrosomonas
- c) Lactobacillus
- d) Salmonella

### Question 4
Which bacteria convert nitrite to nitrate?
- a) Nitrosomonas
- b) Nitrobacter
- c) Streptococcus
- d) Bacillus

### Question 5
What is the primary source of ammonia in aquaculture systems?
- a) Rain water
- b) Fish waste and uneaten food
- c) Air pollution
- d) Plant roots

### Question 6
What does "cycling" a new aquaculture system mean?
- a) Running the pumps continuously
- b) Establishing beneficial bacteria populations before adding fish
- c) Draining and refilling the tank daily
- d) Testing water color

### Question 7
What is the ideal ammonia level in an established aquaculture system?
- a) 10-20 ppm
- b) 5-10 ppm
- c) 1-2 ppm
- d) 0 ppm or undetectable

### Question 8
How long does it typically take to cycle a new aquaculture system?
- a) 1-2 days
- b) 4-8 weeks
- c) 6-12 months
- d) 1-2 years

### Question 9
What is nitrite's effect on fish?
- a) It provides nutrition
- b) It interferes with oxygen transport in blood
- c) It improves fish color
- d) It has no effect

### Question 10
What can be used to speed up the cycling process?
- a) Adding more fish immediately
- b) Adding established filter media or bacterial starter cultures
- c) Raising water temperature to 100°F
- d) Stopping all water flow

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The biological conversion of toxic ammonia to less harmful compounds
2. **c** - Ammonia (NH3)
3. **b** - Nitrosomonas
4. **b** - Nitrobacter
5. **b** - Fish waste and uneaten food
6. **b** - Establishing beneficial bacteria populations before adding fish
7. **d** - 0 ppm or undetectable
8. **b** - 4-8 weeks
9. **b** - It interferes with oxygen transport in blood
10. **b** - Adding established filter media or bacterial starter cultures
