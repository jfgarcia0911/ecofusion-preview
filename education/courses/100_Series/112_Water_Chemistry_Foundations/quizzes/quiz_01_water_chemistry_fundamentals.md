**Module:** 01 - Water Chemistry Fundamentals

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is pH a measure of?
- a) Water temperature
- b) The concentration of hydrogen ions (acidity or alkalinity)
- c) Oxygen content
- d) Salt concentration

### Question 2
What pH range is considered neutral?
- a) 0-3
- b) 4-6
- c) 7
- d) 8-10

### Question 3
What does the term "hardness" refer to in water chemistry?
- a) The physical state of frozen water
- b) The concentration of calcium and magnesium ions
- c) The pressure of water
- d) The clarity of water

### Question 4
What is dissolved oxygen (DO)?
- a) Oxygen that has been removed from water
- b) The amount of oxygen gas dissolved in water
- c) Oxygen in the air above water
- d) Solid oxygen particles

### Question 5
What is the ideal dissolved oxygen level for most aquatic organisms?
- a) 0-2 mg/L
- b) 2-4 mg/L
- c) 5-8 mg/L or higher
- d) 15-20 mg/L

### Question 6
What does TDS stand for?
- a) Total Dissolved Solids
- b) Temperature Dissolved System
- c) Thermal Distribution Scale
- d) Tank Depth Sensor

### Question 7
How does temperature affect dissolved oxygen in water?
- a) Temperature has no effect
- b) Warmer water holds more oxygen
- c) Colder water holds more oxygen
- d) Only pressure affects oxygen levels

### Question 8
What is alkalinity in water chemistry?
- a) The same as pH
- b) The water's capacity to resist pH changes (buffering capacity)
- c) The amount of salt in water
- d) The water's temperature

### Question 9
What is the primary reason to monitor electrical conductivity (EC)?
- a) To measure water color
- b) To estimate total dissolved solids and nutrient levels
- c) To determine water depth
- d) To check water pressure

### Question 10
Which water property is most affected by the presence of dissolved minerals?
- a) Color
- b) Odor
- c) Electrical conductivity
- d) Viscosity

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The concentration of hydrogen ions (acidity or alkalinity)
2. **c** - 7
3. **b** - The concentration of calcium and magnesium ions
4. **b** - The amount of oxygen gas dissolved in water
5. **c** - 5-8 mg/L or higher
6. **a** - Total Dissolved Solids
7. **c** - Colder water holds more oxygen
8. **b** - The water's capacity to resist pH changes (buffering capacity)
9. **b** - To estimate total dissolved solids and nutrient levels
10. **c** - Electrical conductivity
