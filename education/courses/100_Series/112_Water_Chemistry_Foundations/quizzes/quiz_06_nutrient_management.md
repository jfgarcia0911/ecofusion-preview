# Quiz 06: Nutrient Management

**Course:** 112 - Water Chemistry Foundations
**Module:** 06 - Nutrient Management
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three primary macronutrients needed by plants?
- a) Iron, zinc, and copper
- b) Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium (N-P-K)
- c) Carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen only
- d) Calcium, sulfur, and chlorine

### Question 2
In aquaponics, where does nitrogen primarily come from?
- a) The air
- b) Fish waste converted by bacteria
- c) Plant roots
- d) Soil additions

### Question 3
What is a common micronutrient deficiency in aquaponic systems?
- a) Nitrogen
- b) Carbon
- c) Iron
- d) Water

### Question 4
How can you supplement iron in an aquaponic system?
- a) Add rust to the water
- b) Add chelated iron supplements
- c) Increase fish feeding
- d) Iron supplementation is never needed

### Question 5
What does NPK ratio refer to?
- a) Water temperature ranges
- b) The relative proportions of nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium
- c) Fish stocking density
- d) Light intensity levels

### Question 6
What is eutrophication?
- a) Healthy plant growth
- b) Excessive nutrient enrichment leading to algae blooms and oxygen depletion
- c) Lack of nutrients
- d) Normal water cycling

### Question 7
Which nutrient is essential for chlorophyll production and photosynthesis?
- a) Phosphorus
- b) Calcium
- c) Nitrogen and magnesium
- d) Sodium

### Question 8
What role does phosphorus play in plant growth?
- a) No role
- b) Energy transfer and root development
- c) Only leaf color
- d) Water absorption only

### Question 9
How can you identify nutrient deficiencies in plants?
- a) All deficiencies look identical
- b) Visual symptoms like chlorosis, necrosis, and stunted growth
- c) Plants never show deficiency symptoms
- d) Only through expensive laboratory tests

### Question 10
What is the best approach to nutrient management in aquaponics?
- a) Add as many nutrients as possible
- b) Regular monitoring and balanced supplementation as needed
- c) Never add any nutrients
- d) Change all water weekly

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium (N-P-K)
2. **b** - Fish waste converted by bacteria
3. **c** - Iron
4. **b** - Add chelated iron supplements
5. **b** - The relative proportions of nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium
6. **b** - Excessive nutrient enrichment leading to algae blooms and oxygen depletion
7. **c** - Nitrogen and magnesium
8. **b** - Energy transfer and root development
9. **b** - Visual symptoms like chlorosis, necrosis, and stunted growth
10. **b** - Regular monitoring and balanced supplementation as needed
