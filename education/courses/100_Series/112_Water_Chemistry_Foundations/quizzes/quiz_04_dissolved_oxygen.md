# Quiz 04: Dissolved Oxygen Management

**Course:** 112 - Water Chemistry Foundations
**Module:** 04 - Dissolved Oxygen Management
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
Why is dissolved oxygen critical in aquaculture?
- a) It makes water look clearer
- b) Fish and beneficial bacteria require oxygen for respiration
- c) It increases water weight
- d) It is not critical

### Question 2
What factors decrease dissolved oxygen levels?
- a) High temperature and high organic matter
- b) Low temperature and aeration
- c) Low stocking density
- d) Reduced feeding

### Question 3
What is the minimum dissolved oxygen level for most fish species?
- a) 0-1 mg/L
- b) 1-3 mg/L
- c) 4-5 mg/L
- d) 10-12 mg/L

### Question 4
Which method is commonly used to increase dissolved oxygen?
- a) Reducing water flow
- b) Aeration using air stones or diffusers
- c) Heating the water
- d) Adding salt

### Question 5
What happens to fish when dissolved oxygen is too low?
- a) They become more active
- b) They experience stress, gasping at surface, and potential death
- c) They grow faster
- d) Nothing happens

### Question 6
How does photosynthesis affect dissolved oxygen?
- a) It removes oxygen from water
- b) It produces oxygen during daylight hours
- c) It has no effect
- d) It only consumes oxygen

### Question 7
What is the oxygen demand at night in planted systems?
- a) Oxygen demand decreases because plants rest
- b) Oxygen demand increases because plants consume oxygen without producing it
- c) Oxygen levels stay constant
- d) Plants produce more oxygen at night

### Question 8
Which device measures dissolved oxygen?
- a) Thermometer
- b) DO meter or dissolved oxygen probe
- c) pH meter only
- d) Scale

### Question 9
What is supersaturation of dissolved oxygen?
- a) Water containing less oxygen than normal
- b) Water containing more oxygen than the saturation point
- c) Water with no oxygen
- d) Water at freezing point

### Question 10
How does water movement affect dissolved oxygen?
- a) Water movement decreases oxygen levels
- b) Water movement increases gas exchange and oxygen levels
- c) Movement has no effect
- d) Still water has highest oxygen

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Fish and beneficial bacteria require oxygen for respiration
2. **a** - High temperature and high organic matter
3. **c** - 4-5 mg/L
4. **b** - Aeration using air stones or diffusers
5. **b** - They experience stress, gasping at surface, and potential death
6. **b** - It produces oxygen during daylight hours
7. **b** - Oxygen demand increases because plants consume oxygen without producing it
8. **b** - DO meter or dissolved oxygen probe
9. **b** - Water containing more oxygen than the saturation point
10. **b** - Water movement increases gas exchange and oxygen levels
