## Course Description

This foundational course introduces the essential water chemistry concepts critical for successful hydroponic and aquaponic system management. Learners will master pH, alkalinity, dissolved oxygen, and nutrient testing while developing practical skills for water quality monitoring and problem-solving.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Explain** the fundamental chemistry of water and its properties
2. **Measure** and interpret pH, alkalinity, and hardness readings
3. **Test** water quality parameters using standard testing methods
4. **Identify** water treatment options and when to apply them
5. **Manage** water quality within optimal ranges for plant and fish health
6. **Troubleshoot** common water chemistry problems in growing systems
