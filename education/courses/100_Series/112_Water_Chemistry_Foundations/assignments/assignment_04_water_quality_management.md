# Assignment 4: Water Quality Management Plan

## Course 106: Water Chemistry Foundations

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Develop comprehensive water quality management plan.

## Requirements

### 1. Source Water Analysis (4 pts)
- Test and document source water
- Identify problematic parameters
- Compare to target ranges

### 2. Treatment Protocol (5 pts)
- Required treatments
- Equipment specifications
- Operating procedures
- Cost analysis

### 3. Monitoring Schedule (3 pts)
- Testing frequency
- Parameters to monitor
- Record keeping
- Troubleshooting protocols

### 4. Maintenance Plan (3 pts)
- Filter changes
- Equipment calibration
- Preventive maintenance
