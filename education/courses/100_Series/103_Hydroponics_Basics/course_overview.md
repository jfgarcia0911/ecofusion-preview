## Course Description

Master the art and science of growing plants without soil. This comprehensive course covers hydroponic system types, nutrient management, environmental controls, and hands-on system operation. Learners will understand how hydroponics works, compare different system configurations, and gain practical skills for successful soilless cultivation.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Explain** the principles of hydroponic plant cultivation
2. **Compare** and contrast hydroponic system types (DWC, NFT, Ebb & Flow, Drip, Aeroponics)
3. **Select** appropriate growing media for different applications
4. **Mix** and manage nutrient solutions correctly
5. **Monitor** and adjust pH and EC for optimal plant health
6. **Set up** and operate a basic hydroponic system
7. **Troubleshoot** common hydroponic problems

## Module Summaries

### Module 1: Introduction to Hydroponics
- Definition and history of hydroponics
- How plants grow without soil
- Benefits and challenges
- Comparison to soil-based growing
- Commercial and home applications

### Module 2: Hydroponic System Types
- Deep Water Culture (DWC)
- Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)
- Ebb and Flow (Flood and Drain)
- Drip Systems (Recovery and Non-Recovery)
- Aeroponics
- Wick Systems
- Choosing the right system

### Module 3: Growing Media Comparison
- Purpose of growing media
- Inert media: Clay pebbles, perlite, growstone
- Organic media: Coco coir, peat moss
- Engineered media: Rockwool, oasis cubes
- Media-free systems
- Selection criteria

### Module 4: Nutrient Solution Science
- Essential plant nutrients (macro and micro)
- Nutrient function in plants
- Understanding fertilizer labels (N-P-K)
- Complete vs. incomplete formulas
- Organic hydroponic options

### Module 5: Mixing Nutrient Solutions
- Reading EC/TDS and understanding concentration
- Two-part and three-part nutrient systems
- Mixing order and procedures
- Adjusting for growth stages
- Common mixing mistakes

### Module 6: Water Quality for Hydroponics
- Source water analysis
- Understanding hardness and alkalinity
- Chlorine and chloramine removal
- Reverse osmosis basics
- pH adjustment methods

### Module 7: Environmental Management
- Light requirements (PAR, DLI)
- Temperature effects on growth
- Humidity management
- Root zone temperature
- CO2 enrichment basics

### Module 8: Crop Selection & Management
- Best crops for hydroponics
- Spacing and plant density
- Germination and transplanting
- Growth stage management
- Harvest timing

### Module 9: Pest & Disease Management
- Common hydroponic pests
- Root diseases (Pythium, Fusarium)

- Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

### Module 10: Building Your Hydroponic System
- DIY DWC system construction
- Material selection and sourcing
- Step-by-step assembly
- Startup and testing

## Key Topics Covered

### Nutrient Elements

**Macronutrients (needed in large quantities):**
- Nitrogen (N) - Leaf growth
- Phosphorus (P) - Root and flower development
- Potassium (K) - Overall plant health
- Calcium (Ca) - Cell wall strength
- Magnesium (Mg) - Chlorophyll production
- Sulfur (S) - Protein synthesis

**Micronutrients (needed in small quantities):**
- Iron (Fe), Manganese (Mn), Zinc (Zn)
- Copper (Cu), Boron (B), Molybdenum (Mo)
- Chlorine (Cl)

### Target Ranges

| Parameter | Vegetative | Flowering/Fruiting |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|
| EC | 1.2-2.0 mS/cm | 2.0-3.0 mS/cm |
| pH | 5.5-6.0 | 5.8-6.2 |
| Temperature | 65-75°F | 60-70°F |
