# Course 103: Hydroponics Basics - Grading Rubric

## Course Assessment Overview

This rubric defines the evaluation criteria for all graded components in Course 103: Hydroponics Basics. All assessments align with the course learning outcomes and measure both theoretical understanding and practical application skills.

---

## Assessment Components

### Total Points: 150

| Assessment Type | Quantity | Points Each | Total Points |
|----------------|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Module Quizzes | 10 | 5 | 50 |
| Lab Activities | 3 | 15 | 45 |
| Discussion Posts | 5 | 5 | 25 |
| In-Class Assignments | 3 | 10-15 | 35 |
| Take-Home Assignment | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| Project | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| Final Exam | 1 | 30 | 30 |

---

## Grading Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Points | Description |
|-------|------------|--------|-------------|
| A | 90-100% | 135-150 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |
| B | 80-89% | 120-134 | Good - Meets all expectations |
| C | 70-79% | 105-119 | Satisfactory - Meets most expectations |
| D | 60-69% | 90-104 | Needs Improvement |
| F | Below 60% | Below 90 | Does not meet minimum standards |

**Passing Threshold:** 80% (Grade B or higher, 120 points minimum)

## Assignment Rubrics

### In-Class Assignments

#### Assignment 1: System Type Comparison (10 points)

| Criteria | Excellent (9-10) | Good (7-8) | Satisfactory (5-6) | Needs Improvement (0-4) |
|----------|------------------|------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **System Analysis** | Comprehensive comparison of all system types with detailed pros/cons | Good comparison with minor gaps | Basic comparison, some details missing | Incomplete or inaccurate comparison |
| **Application Matching** | Excellent justification for system selection based on scenario | Good justification | Limited justification | Poor or no justification |

#### Assignment 2: Nutrient Mixing Lab (15 points)

| Criteria | Excellent (13-15) | Good (10-12) | Satisfactory (7-9) | Needs Improvement (0-6) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Mixing Accuracy** | Precise measurements, correct procedure, accurate EC/pH | Minor measurement errors | Some procedural errors | Major errors in mixing |
| **Calculations** | All calculations correct with units | Minor calculation errors | Some calculation errors | Major calculation errors |
| **Safety & Technique** | Exemplary lab safety and technique | Good safety practices | Some safety concerns | Unsafe practices |

#### Assignment 3: System Assembly (15 points)

| Criteria | Excellent (13-15) | Good (10-12) | Satisfactory (7-9) | Needs Improvement (0-6) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Assembly Quality** | System properly assembled, all connections secure, no leaks | Minor assembly issues | Some connection problems | Major assembly problems |
| **Understanding** | Demonstrates deep understanding of component functions | Good understanding | Basic understanding | Limited understanding |

---

### Take-Home Assignment (15 points)

#### Assignment 4: Hydroponic Crop Planning

## Module Quiz Rubric (5 points each)

Quizzes are auto-graded based on correctness. Each quiz contains 5 questions worth 1 point each.

---

## Final Exam Rubric (30 points)

- Multiple choice/True-False: 15 points (30 questions)
- Short answer: 10 points (5 questions)
- Applied problems: 5 points (2 scenarios)

- 24-48 hours: 25% deduction
- More than 48 hours: 50% deduction
- More than 7 days: Not accepted

## Academic Integrity

All work must be original. Violations result in zero on assignment and possible course failure.
