## Assessment Categories Explained

### 1. Module Quizzes (50 points total)

**Purpose:** Assess understanding of key concepts from each module

- **Quantity:** 10 quizzes
- **Points Each:** 5 points
- **Format:** Multiple choice, true/false, matching
- **Time Limit:** 15 minutes each
- **Attempts:** 2 attempts (highest score counts)
- **Open Resources:** No (closed-book)

**Topics Covered:**
1. Introduction to Hydroponics
2. System Types
3. Growing Media
4. Nutrient Science
5. Mixing Nutrients
6. Water Quality
7. Environmental Management
8. Crop Management
9. Pest & Disease
10. Building Systems

---

### 2. Lab Activities (45 points total)

**Purpose:** Develop hands-on skills in hydroponic production

- **Quantity:** 3 labs
- **Points Each:** 15 points
- **Format:** Practical lab work with written reports

**Lab Topics:**
1. **Nutrient Mixing Lab** - Practice mixing nutrient solutions, measuring EC and pH
2. **System Assembly Lab** - Assemble a small DWC system
3. **Crop Planning Activity** - Develop production schedule for continuous harvest

---

### 3. Discussion Participation (25 points total)

**Purpose:** Foster community learning and peer interaction

- **Quantity:** 5 discussions
- **Points Each:** 5 points
- **Requirements:**
  - Initial post: 150-200 words (3 points)
  - Response to 2 peers: 50+ words each (2 points)

---

### 4. In-Class Assignments (35 points total)

#### Assignment 1: System Type Comparison (10 points)
- **Time:** 15-20 minutes
- **Task:** Compare hydroponic system types and match to applications

#### Assignment 2: Nutrient Mixing Lab (15 points)
- **Time:** 20-30 minutes
- **Task:** Mix nutrient solutions and adjust EC and pH

#### Assignment 3: System Assembly (15 points)
- **Time:** 30-45 minutes
- **Task:** Assemble small hydroponic system components

---

### 5. Take-Home Assignment (15 points)

#### Assignment 4: Hydroponic Crop Planning
- **Time:** 1-2 hours
- **Format:** Written plan with schedule
- **Components:**
  - Crop variety selection
  - Production schedule
  - Nutrient management plan
  - Pest management strategy

---

### 6. Project Assignment (15 points)

#### Assignment 5: DIY Hydroponic System Build
- **Time:** 2-4 hours
- **Format:** Build documentation package
- **Deliverables:**
  - System design
  - Build photos
  - Materials list
  - Lessons learned reflection

---

### 7. Final Exam (30 points)

**Purpose:** Comprehensive assessment of course knowledge

- **Format:** Mixed question types
- **Time Limit:** 90 minutes
- **Structure:**
  - Multiple Choice/True-False: 15 points
  - Short Answer: 10 points
  - Applied Problems: 5 points

**Content Coverage:**
- System types and selection (20%)
- Nutrient management (25%)
- Growing media (15%)
- Water quality (20%)
- Crop management (15%)
- Troubleshooting (5%)
