## pH Problems

### Quick pH Reference

```
pH SCALE FOR HYDROPONICS
════════════════════════

        ACIDIC              NEUTRAL            ALKALINE
           │                    │                   │
    4.0────5.0────5.5────6.0────6.5────7.0────7.5────8.0
           │      └─────────┬─────────┘      │
           │          OPTIMAL RANGE          │
           │           (5.5 - 6.5)           │
           │                                 │
           ▼                                 ▼
    Nutrient lockout              Nutrient lockout
    (Ca, Mg, Fe, etc.)            (Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu)
```

### pH Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| pH too high (>7.0) | High alkalinity source water | Use RO water, pH Down |
| pH too high | Algae growth | Block light, clean system |
| pH too high | Calcium buildup | Clean system, change water |
| pH too low (<5.5) | Too much pH Down | Add pH Up slowly |
| pH too low | Root decay | Check oxygen, remove dead roots |
| pH too low | Bacterial growth | Clean system, sterilize |
| pH swings wildly | Small reservoir | Use larger reservoir |
| pH swings wildly | Weak buffering | Use pH buffer products |
| pH won't adjust | Expired pH products | Use fresh products |

## EC/TDS Problems

### Quick EC Reference

```
EC TROUBLESHOOTING
══════════════════

EC TOO LOW (<1.0 for mature plants)
───────────────────────────────────
• Plants are hungry
• Add more nutrients
• Increase feeding frequency

EC TOO HIGH (>3.0 for most crops)
─────────────────────────────────
• Nutrient burn possible
• Dilute with fresh water
• Flush system

EC RISING OVER TIME
───────────────────
• Plants drinking more water than nutrients
• Top up with plain water
• Check for heat stress

EC FALLING OVER TIME
────────────────────
• Plants consuming nutrients heavily
• Add nutrients when topping up
• Consider reservoir change
```

### Root Health Assessment

```

══════════════             ═════════════

   ┌─────┐                    ┌─────┐
   │█████│ White/Cream        │░░░░░│ Brown
   │█████│ Firm               │▒▒▒▒▒│ Slimy
   │█████│ Extensive          │  ▒  │ Sparse
   │█████│ Fresh smell        │  ▒  │ Bad smell
   └─────┘                    └─────┘

    GOOD!                     ROOT ROT!
```

### Root Problem Solutions

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Brown, slimy roots | Root rot (Pythium) | H2O2 treatment, increase oxygen |
| Brown roots | High temperature | Cool nutrient solution (<75°F) |
| Sparse root growth | Low oxygen | Add more air stones |
| Mushy roots | Overwatering | Check drainage, reduce flood cycles |
| Root bound | Container too small | Transplant to larger container |
| Algae on roots | Light exposure | Block light from root zone |

### Root Rot Treatment Protocol

```
ROOT ROT EMERGENCY TREATMENT
════════════════════════════

1. REMOVE affected plants from system

2. TRIM brown/dead roots with sterile scissors

3. RINSE roots in clean water

4. DIP roots in H2O2 solution (3%, 1:10 dilution)

5. CLEAN entire system:
   • Drain completely
   • Scrub with H2O2
   • Rinse thoroughly

6. REFILL with fresh nutrient solution

7. ADD beneficial bacteria (Hydroguard, etc.)

8. INCREASE aeration

9. LOWER water temperature if above 72°F

10. MONITOR closely for recurrence
```

## Environmental Problems

### Temperature Issues

```
TEMPERATURE TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════

AIR TEMPERATURE
───────────────
Too Hot (>85°F):           Too Cold (<60°F):
• Wilting                  • Slow growth
• Rapid transpiration      • Purple leaves (P lockout)
• Nutrient uptake issues   • Root function reduced
→ Increase ventilation     → Add heating
→ Add shade/cooling        → Insulate containers

WATER TEMPERATURE
─────────────────
Too Warm (>75°F):          Too Cold (<55°F):
• Root rot risk HIGH       • Nutrient uptake slows
• Dissolved O2 drops       • Root growth stops
• Pathogens thrive         • Shock to plants
→ Use water chiller        → Use aquarium heater
→ Add frozen bottles       → Insulate reservoir
→ Increase air pumping     → Warm water before adding
```

### Light Issues

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Leggy/stretched plants | Not enough light | Move lights closer, add lights |
| Bleached leaves | Too much light | Raise lights, reduce hours |
| Slow growth | Insufficient DLI | Increase light intensity/hours |
| Algae in reservoir | Light reaching water | Cover reservoir, opaque tubing |
| Flowering too early | Wrong photoperiod | Adjust light schedule |

## Pest Problems

### Common Hydroponic Pests

```
PEST IDENTIFICATION
═══════════════════

APHIDS
──────
• Small, soft-bodied, green/black
• Found on undersides of leaves
• Leave sticky "honeydew"
Treatment: Neem oil, insecticidal soap

SPIDER MITES
────────────
• Tiny, barely visible
• Fine webbing on leaves
• Yellow stippling on leaves
Treatment: Increase humidity, miticide

FUNGUS GNATS
────────────
• Small black flies near media
• Larvae damage roots
• Thrive in wet conditions
Treatment: Yellow sticky traps, BTi

WHITEFLIES
──────────
• Small white flying insects
• Congregate under leaves
• Plants become sticky
Treatment: Yellow sticky traps, neem

THRIPS
──────
• Tiny, slender insects
• Silver/bronze leaf damage
• Distorted growth
Treatment: Blue sticky traps, spinosad
```

## Quick Fixes Reference Table

| Symptom | Quick Diagnosis | Immediate Action |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| Yellow old leaves | N deficiency | Increase nutrients |
| Yellow new leaves | Fe deficiency | Lower pH, add iron |
| Brown leaf edges | K deficiency or burn | Check EC, adjust K |
| Purple leaves | P deficiency or cold | Check temp, pH |
| Wilting despite wet roots | Root rot | Check roots, clean system |
| Wilting with dry roots | Pump failure | Check pump, refill |
| Slow growth | Low light/nutrients | Check DLI, EC levels |
| Leggy growth | Insufficient light | Move lights closer |
| Curled leaves | Heat stress or Ca | Lower temp, add calcium |
| Brown spots | Ca deficiency or burn | Check pH, EC |
