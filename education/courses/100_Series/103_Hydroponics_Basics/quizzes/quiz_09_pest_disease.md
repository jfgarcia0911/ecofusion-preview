## Questions

### Question 1
What is the best approach to pest management in hydroponics?

a) Wait until infestation then spray
b) Prevention through biosecurity and monitoring ✓
c) Never worry about pests indoors
d) Use only chemical pesticides

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Prevention is key—screen intakes, inspect new plants, maintain cleanliness, and monitor regularly to catch problems early before they spread.*

---

### Question 2
Which pest is most common in hydroponic systems?

a) Gophers
b) Aphids ✓
c) Deer
d) Rabbits

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Aphids are small sap-sucking insects that reproduce rapidly and are common in protected environments. They cluster on new growth and undersides of leaves.*

---

### Question 3
What are beneficial insects?

a) All insects are beneficial
b) Predatory insects that eat pest insects ✓
c) Insects that pollinate only
d) Harmful insects

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Beneficial insects like ladybugs (eat aphids), lacewings, and predatory mites help control pest populations naturally without chemicals.*

---

### Question 4
What is pythium?

a) A beneficial bacteria
b) A root rot fungus that thrives in warm, wet conditions ✓
c) A type of nutrient
d) A fish species

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Pythium is a water mold causing root rot. Symptoms include brown, mushy roots and wilting. It thrives in warm water (>75°F) with low oxygen.*

---

### Question 5
How can you prevent root diseases?

a) Keep solution temperature high (85°F+)
b) Maintain cool root zone (65-72°F), good aeration, and clean system ✓
c) Never change water
d) Add soil to the system

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Prevent root disease by keeping water cool, maximizing dissolved oxygen, maintaining cleanliness, and avoiding contamination from outside sources.*

---

### Question 6
What is powdery mildew?

a) A white fungal growth on leaves ✓
b) A nutrient deficiency
c) A beneficial mold
d) A type of algae

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: Powdery mildew appears as white powdery spots on leaves. It's favored by high humidity, poor air circulation, and moderate temperatures.*

---

### Question 7
What is IPM (Integrated Pest Management)?

a) Using only chemical pesticides
b) Combining multiple strategies: prevention, monitoring, biological, and chemical controls ✓
c) Ignoring all pests
d) Only using organic methods

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: IPM uses a hierarchical approach: prevent pests, monitor regularly, use cultural/biological controls first, and use targeted chemicals only when necessary.*

---

### Question 8
What should you do when introducing new plants?

a) Add them directly to the system
b) Quarantine and inspect for pests/disease first ✓
c) Wash with bleach
d) Nothing special needed

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Quarantine new plants for 1-2 weeks, inspecting for pests and disease before introducing them to your system to prevent contamination.*

---

### Question 9
What are sticky traps used for?

a) Catching large animals
b) Monitoring and catching flying insects like fungus gnats and whiteflies ✓
c) Holding plants upright
d) Measuring humidity

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Yellow sticky traps attract and catch flying insects, helping monitor pest levels and providing some control for light infestations.*

---

### Question 10
How do you treat a severe aphid infestation organically?

a) Do nothing
b) Use insecticidal soap or neem oil spray ✓
c) Add more water
d) Increase temperature to 120°F

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Insecticidal soap and neem oil are organic options that kill soft-bodied insects like aphids on contact. Spray thoroughly, including leaf undersides.*
