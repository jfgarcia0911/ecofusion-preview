## Questions

### Question 1
What is the simplest hydroponic system for beginners to build?

a) Aeroponics
b) Deep Water Culture (DWC) ✓
c) Dutch bucket system
d) Commercial NFT

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: DWC (Kratky method) is simplest—just a container, net pot, air stone (optional), and nutrient solution. Perfect for learning basics.*

---

### Question 2
What is the Kratky method?

a) A complicated automated system
b) Passive DWC with no pump or aeration ✓
c) A type of soil gardening
d) Commercial farming only

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: The Kratky method is passive hydroponics—plants in net pots above solution, air gap forms as plants drink, providing oxygen. No electricity needed.*

---

### Question 3
What should you use for a DIY reservoir?

a) Metal containers
b) Food-grade, light-blocking containers ✓
c) Clear glass jars
d) Cardboard boxes

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Use food-safe, opaque containers (typically plastic) to prevent algae growth from light. Avoid metal which can react with nutrients.*

---

### Question 4
Why should reservoirs be opaque/light-blocking?

a) For aesthetics only
b) To prevent algae growth ✓
c) Plants prefer darkness
d) Light doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Light + nutrients = algae growth. Algae competes with plants for nutrients and oxygen. Dark reservoirs prevent this problem.*

---

### Question 5
What size air pump do you need for DWC?

a) No air pump needed ever
b) 2-4 watts per gallon of solution ✓
c) The smallest available
d) Size doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: General rule: 2-4 watts of air pump per gallon, or use an air stone rated for your reservoir volume. More aeration is generally better.*

---

### Question 6
Where should you place your hydroponic system?

a) Dark basement with no light
b) Near light source (window or grow lights), with level surface and power access ✓
c) Outside in direct rain
d) Location doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Systems need light (natural or artificial), level surface to prevent uneven water distribution, and access to power for pumps/lights.*

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of a net pot?

a) To catch fish
b) To hold plant and growing media while allowing root access to solution ✓
c) To filter nutrients
d) To measure water level

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Net pots hold plants and growing media while allowing roots to grow through openings into the nutrient solution below.*

---

### Question 8
How full should you fill a DWC reservoir initially?

a) Completely full to the top
b) 2/3 full, leaving air space for roots ✓
c) Only 10% full
d) Empty—no water needed

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Fill 2/3 full initially. As plants mature and roots grow into solution, lower water level to create an air gap for oxygen exposure.*

---

### Question 9
What should you do before adding plants to a new system?

a) Nothing—add plants immediately
b) Run system with water to check for leaks and test equipment ✓
c) Add all nutrients at 10x strength
d) Keep system dry

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Test-run with plain water to check for leaks, verify pump/timer function, and ensure proper water flow before adding expensive nutrients and plants.*

---

### Question 10
What is the first crop you should grow in your new system?

a) The most expensive crop
b) Fast-growing, forgiving crops like lettuce or herbs ✓
c) Slow-growing exotic fruit
d) Don't grow anything for 6 months

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Start with fast, forgiving crops (lettuce, basil) that tolerate mistakes and provide quick results to build confidence and learn system management.*
