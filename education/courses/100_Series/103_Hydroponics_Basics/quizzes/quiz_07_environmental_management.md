## Questions

### Question 1
What does PAR stand for in lighting?

a) Plants And Roots
b) Photosynthetically Active Radiation ✓
c) Power And Resistance
d) Purple And Red

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: PAR is the wavelength range (400-700nm) of light that plants use for photosynthesis. Not all light is PAR (e.g., green light is less useful).*

---

### Question 2
What is DLI (Daily Light Integral)?

a) Daily temperature range
b) Total amount of PAR received in 24 hours ✓
c) Duration of daylight
d) Distance between lights

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: DLI measures total photons (mol/m²/day) plants receive daily. Lettuce needs 12-17 DLI; tomatoes need 25-35 DLI.*

---

### Question 3
What is the ideal temperature range for leafy greens?

a) 32-40°F (0-4°C)
b) 60-75°F (15-24°C) ✓
c) 85-95°F (29-35°C)
d) 100°F+ (38°C+)

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Leafy greens grow best at 60-75°F. Too cold slows growth; too hot causes bolting (premature flowering) and bitter taste.*

---

### Question 4
What does VPD stand for?

a) Very Perfect Day
b) Vapor Pressure Deficit ✓
c) Vertical Plant Design
d) Vitamin Production Daily

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: VPD is the difference between moisture in air and moisture in leaf. It drives transpiration and affects nutrient uptake.*

---

### Question 5
What happens if humidity is too high?

a) Plants transpire faster
b) Risk of fungal disease increases, transpiration slows ✓
c) Plants always grow better
d) Nothing changes

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: High humidity (>80%) slows transpiration, reducing nutrient uptake, and creates conditions for powdery mildew, botrytis, and other fungal diseases.*

---

### Question 6
What is the benefit of CO₂ enrichment?

a) It cools the grow room
b) Can increase photosynthesis and growth rates ✓
c) It replaces oxygen
d) No benefit

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Elevating CO₂ to 1000-1500 ppm (from ambient 400 ppm) can increase photosynthesis rates by 20-30% if light and nutrients are adequate.*

---

### Question 7
What photoperiod do most leafy greens prefer?

a) 6-8 hours
b) 10-12 hours
c) 14-18 hours ✓
d) 24 hours continuous

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Most leafy greens thrive with 14-18 hours of light daily, providing adequate photosynthesis while allowing some dark period for respiration.*

---

### Question 8
Why is air circulation important?

a) Plants like wind
b) Strengthens stems, prevents stagnant air and disease ✓
c) It's not important
d) Only for cooling

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Air circulation strengthens stems, prevents hot/cold spots, reduces humidity pockets where disease develops, and ensures even CO₂ distribution.*

---

### Question 9
What is the ideal root zone temperature for most crops?

a) 32-40°F (0-4°C)
b) 65-75°F (18-24°C) ✓
c) 85-95°F (29-35°C)
d) 100°F+ (38°C+)

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Root zone temperature of 65-75°F optimizes nutrient uptake and prevents root diseases. Too warm increases pythium risk; too cool slows growth.*

---

### Question 10
Which color LED light spectrum promotes vegetative growth?

a) Pure red
b) Blue ✓
c) Green
d) Yellow

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Blue light (400-500nm) promotes compact, bushy vegetative growth. Red light (600-700nm) promotes flowering and stem elongation.*
