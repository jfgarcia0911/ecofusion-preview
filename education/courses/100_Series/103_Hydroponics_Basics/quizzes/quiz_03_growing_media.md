## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of growing media in hydroponics?

a) To provide nutrients to plants
b) To support plant roots and provide aeration ✓
c) To add flavor to vegetables
d) To prevent plant growth

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Growing media provides physical support for roots and ensures proper aeration and moisture retention. Nutrients come from the solution, not the media.*

---

### Question 2
Which growing media is made from volcanic rock?

a) Rockwool
b) Coco coir
c) Perlite ✓
d) Oasis cubes

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Perlite is made from expanded volcanic glass. It's lightweight, provides excellent drainage and aeration, and is pH neutral.*

---

### Question 3
What is a key advantage of clay pebbles (LECA)?

a) They dissolve in water
b) They're reusable and pH neutral ✓
c) They're the cheapest option
d) They compact over time

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Lightweight Expanded Clay Aggregate (LECA) is reusable, pH neutral, provides excellent drainage and aeration, and lasts for years with proper cleaning.*

---

### Question 4
What is rockwool made from?

a) Cotton fibers
b) Plastic
c) Melted rock spun into fibers ✓
d) Compressed wood

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Rockwool is made by melting basaltic rock and spinning it into fine fibers, similar to cotton candy. It retains moisture while providing air pockets.*

---

### Question 5
Which media is organic and renewable?

a) Clay pebbles
b) Coco coir ✓
c) Growstones
d) Perlite

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Coco coir is made from coconut husks, making it organic, renewable, and biodegradable. It has excellent water retention and aeration properties.*

---

### Question 6
What is a disadvantage of rockwool?

a) Too expensive
b) Not reusable and can be itchy to handle ✓
c) Compacts quickly
d) Absorbs no water

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Rockwool is typically single-use, must be disposed of properly, and can irritate skin and lungs during handling without proper protective equipment.*

---

### Question 7
Which medium provides the BEST drainage?

a) Vermiculite
b) Clay pebbles ✓
c) Coco coir
d) Peat moss

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Clay pebbles drain extremely well due to their round shape and large air spaces, making them ideal for systems requiring fast drainage like ebb and flow.*

---

### Question 8
What should you do before using coco coir?

a) Nothing—use it straight from the bag
b) Rinse to remove salts and buffer pH ✓
c) Bake it in the oven
d) Mix it with soil

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Coco coir often contains excess salts and needs rinsing. It may also need buffering with calcium to prevent calcium deficiency in plants.*

---

### Question 9
Which media is best for seed starting?

a) Large clay pebbles
b) Gravel
c) Rockwool cubes or Oasis foam ✓
d) Sand

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Rockwool cubes and Oasis foam provide ideal moisture retention and support for delicate seedlings, making transplanting easy without disturbing roots.*

---

### Question 10
Can you reuse coco coir?

a) No, it must be disposed after one use
b) Yes, with proper cleaning and sterilization ✓
c) Only for ornamental plants
d) Never reuse any growing media

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Coco coir can be reused if properly cleaned, sterilized (with hydrogen peroxide or steam), and amended. However, it degrades over time.*
