## Questions

### Question 1
What does EC stand for in hydroponics?

a) Electrical Current
b) Electrical Conductivity ✓
c) Element Count
d) Energy Capacity

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: EC (Electrical Conductivity) measures the concentration of dissolved nutrients in solution. Higher EC means more dissolved salts/nutrients.*

---

### Question 2
What is the typical EC range for lettuce?

a) 0.1-0.5 mS/cm
b) 1.2-2.0 mS/cm ✓
c) 5.0-7.0 mS/cm
d) 10+ mS/cm

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Leafy greens like lettuce typically thrive at EC 1.2-2.0 mS/cm. Lower values may cause nutrient deficiency; higher values can burn plants.*

---

### Question 3
What is TDS?

a) Total Daily Sunlight
b) Total Dissolved Solids ✓
c) Temperature Distribution System
d) Textured Dry Surface

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) measures dissolved substances in ppm. It's related to EC: roughly EC (mS/cm) × 500-700 = TDS (ppm).*

---

### Question 4
When mixing a two-part nutrient solution, what order should you add components?

a) Add both parts together at once
b) Add Part A, stir, then add Part B ✓
c) Add Part B first
d) Order doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Always add components separately to prevent precipitation. Add Part A to water, stir well, then add Part B. Never mix concentrates directly.*

---

### Question 5
Why are nutrients sold as two or three separate bottles?

a) To make them more expensive
b) Certain nutrients precipitate if mixed concentrated together ✓
c) For color-coding
d) No reason

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Calcium can precipitate with sulfates and phosphates when concentrated. Separating prevents precipitation and extends shelf life.*

---

### Question 6
Should you mix nutrients before adding to water?

a) Yes, always mix concentrates first
b) No, always dilute in water separately ✓
c) Either way is fine
d) Never use water

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: NEVER mix concentrated nutrients together. Always dilute each component in water separately to prevent chemical reactions and precipitation.*

---

### Question 7
How do you adjust nutrient concentration for seedlings?

a) Use full strength immediately
b) Use 1/4 to 1/2 strength solution ✓
c) Double the concentration
d) Don't feed seedlings

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Seedlings have delicate roots and can't handle full-strength nutrients. Start at 1/4-1/2 strength (EC 0.5-0.8) and gradually increase as plants mature.*

---

### Question 8
What should you do if EC is too high?

a) Add more nutrients
b) Add plain water to dilute ✓
c) Change the pH
d) Remove all plants

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: If EC is too high, dilute the solution by adding plain (pH-adjusted) water. Monitor EC and adjust until it reaches the target range.*

---

### Question 9
When should you completely change nutrient solution?

a) Daily
b) Never
c) Every 1-2 weeks ✓
d) Only when plants die

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Most growers change solution every 1-2 weeks to prevent nutrient imbalances, salt buildup, and pathogen accumulation, though this varies by system size.*

---

### Question 10
What temperature should nutrient solution be?

a) Ice cold (32°F / 0°C)
b) Room temperature (65-75°F / 18-24°C) ✓
c) Very hot (100°F+ / 38°C+)
d) Temperature doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: 65-75°F is ideal. Too cold slows nutrient uptake; too warm reduces dissolved oxygen and can promote pathogen growth.*
