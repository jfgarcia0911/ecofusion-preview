## Questions

### Question 1
Which crops are easiest for hydroponic beginners?

a) Strawberries and melons
b) Lettuce and herbs ✓
c) Corn and wheat
d) Large fruit trees

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Lettuce and herbs grow quickly, tolerate varying conditions, have low nutrient demands, and provide fast results for beginners.*

---

### Question 2
What spacing is recommended for butterhead lettuce?

a) 1 inch apart
b) 6-8 inches apart ✓
c) 24 inches apart
d) They can touch

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Butterhead lettuce needs 6-8 inches to develop full heads with good air circulation. Closer spacing causes small heads and disease risk.*

---

### Question 3
How long does it take to grow lettuce from seed to harvest?

a) 3-5 days
b) 2-3 weeks
c) 4-6 weeks ✓
d) 6 months

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Most lettuce varieties mature in 4-6 weeks hydroponically, faster than soil (6-8 weeks). Microgreens harvest in just 7-14 days.*

---

### Question 4
What is "bolting" in lettuce?

a) Rapid leaf growth
b) Premature flowering and seed production ✓
c) Root rot disease
d) Nutrient deficiency

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Bolting is when lettuce flowers prematurely due to stress (heat, long days). Leaves become bitter and plant quality declines.*

---

### Question 5
Which herb is NOT well-suited for hydroponics?

a) Basil
b) Cilantro
c) Rosemary (prefers dry conditions) ✓
d) Mint

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Rosemary prefers dry Mediterranean conditions and struggles in constantly moist hydroponic systems. Basil, cilantro, and mint thrive.*

---

### Question 6
When should you harvest leafy greens?

a) When roots are 12 inches long
b) When leaves reach desired size, before bolting ✓
c) After plants flower
d) Only in the morning

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Harvest when leaves are at desired size (4-6 weeks typically) and before bolting. Morning harvest is ideal when leaves are most turgid.*

---

### Question 7
What is succession planting?

a) Planting the same crop repeatedly
b) Starting new plants at intervals for continuous harvest ✓
c) Planting in alphabetical order
d) Growing plants taller each time

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Succession planting starts new crops every 1-2 weeks, ensuring continuous harvest rather than one big harvest then empty space.*

---

### Question 8
How many days does basil typically take to harvest?

a) 7-10 days
b) 28-35 days ✓
c) 90 days
d) 6 months

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Basil is ready for first harvest in 28-35 days. Pinch tips regularly to promote bushy growth and prevent flowering.*

---

### Question 9
What should you do with fruiting plants like tomatoes?

a) Never prune them
b) Prune suckers and provide support stakes ✓
c) Let them grow on the ground
d) Harvest all leaves

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Indeterminate tomatoes need pruning (remove suckers) and support (stakes, trellises) to direct energy to fruit and maintain airflow.*

---

### Question 10
Which crops have the highest profit potential per square foot?

a) Wheat and corn
b) Microgreens and specialty herbs ✓
c) Potatoes
d) Pumpkins

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Microgreens and herbs like basil have quick turnover (1-4 weeks), high market value, and maximum space efficiency for profitability.*
