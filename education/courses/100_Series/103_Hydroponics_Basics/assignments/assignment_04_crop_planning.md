## Assignment Purpose

Develop a comprehensive crop production plan for a small-scale hydroponic operation, demonstrating your ability to select appropriate crops, create harvest schedules, and manage nutrient requirements for year-round production.

---

## Scenario

You have a hydroponic growing space with these specifications:
- **System Type:** NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)
- **Growing Area:** 100 square feet (10 channels, each 10 ft long)
- **Location:** Climate-controlled indoor space
- **Goal:** Continuous weekly harvest for local farmers market sales
- **Target:** Produce $200-300 worth of crops weekly

- When to start seeds/transplants

**Requirements:**

---

### Part 3: Nutrient Management Plan (3 points)

**Question 1:** Your crops have different nutrient needs. How will you manage this in a single recirculating system?

**Question 2:** Create a nutrient monitoring schedule:
- How often will you test EC and pH?
- What are target ranges for your crop mix?
- When will you change nutrient solution completely?

**Question 3:** Calculate weekly nutrient costs:
- System volume: 50 gallons
- Nutrient cost: $0.50 per gallon of prepared solution
- Weekly top-off: 10 gallons
- Complete change frequency: every 2 weeks

---

### Part 4: Pest and Disease Management (3 points)

Develop an Integrated Pest Management (IPM) strategy:

1. **Prevention measures** (what you'll do to prevent problems)
2. **Monitoring protocol** (how often, what to look for)
3. **Response plan** (actions if pests/disease detected)
4. **Acceptable control methods** (specific to hydroponics)
