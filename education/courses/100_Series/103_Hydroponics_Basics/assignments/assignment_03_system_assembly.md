## Learning Objectives

---

## System Components Provided

- 5-gallon bucket (reservoir)
- Net pot lid
- Air pump with air stone and tubing
- Clay pebbles (growing media)
- Seedling (lettuce or herb)
- Nutrient solution (pre-mixed)

---

## Assembly Procedure

### Step 1: Prepare Reservoir (5 minutes)
- [ ] Clean bucket thoroughly
- [ ] Fill with 4 gallons of nutrient solution
- [ ] Check that lid seals properly

### Step 2: Install Aeration (5 minutes)
- [ ] Connect air stone to tubing
- [ ] Place air stone at bottom of bucket
- [ ] Route tubing through hole in lid
- [ ] Connect to air pump
- [ ] Test pump operation (should produce steady bubbles)

### Step 3: Prepare Net Pot (10 minutes)
- [ ] Rinse clay pebbles
- [ ] Fill net pot bottom 1/3 with pebbles
- [ ] Carefully remove seedling from starter cube/plug
- [ ] Place seedling in center of net pot
- [ ] Fill around seedling with pebbles (leave top exposed)
- [ ] Ensure roots can reach water

### Step 4: Final Assembly (5 minutes)
- [ ] Place net pot in lid opening
- [ ] Ensure water level is just touching bottom of net pot
- [ ] Verify all connections are secure
- [ ] Turn on air pump

### Step 5: System Check (5 minutes)
- [ ] Verify bubbles are vigorous
- [ ] Check for leaks
- [ ] Ensure water level is appropriate
- [ ] Confirm seedling is stable
- [ ] Test pump with power on/off

## Success Criteria

Your system is successful if:
- ✓ Air pump produces vigorous bubbles throughout reservoir
- ✓ No leaks detected after 5 minutes of operation
- ✓ Water level touches bottom of net pot
- ✓ Seedling is stable and upright
- ✓ Roots have access to nutrient solution
- ✓ All components properly seated/connected

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Overfilling reservoir (should be 4" from top)
- ❌ Burying seedling too deep in pebbles
- ❌ Kinked air tubing blocking airflow
- ❌ Not rinsing clay pebbles (causes cloudy water)
- ❌ Loose connections causing air leaks
- ❌ Water level too low to reach roots

---

## Post-Assembly Care

If you're taking this system home:
- Check water level daily (top off as needed)
- Monitor plant health
- Keep air pump running 24/7
- Provide adequate light (12-16 hours/day)
- Change nutrient solution weekly
