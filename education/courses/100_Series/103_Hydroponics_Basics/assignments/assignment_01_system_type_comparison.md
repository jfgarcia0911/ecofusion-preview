## Background

Choosing the right hydroponic system is critical for success. Each system type has unique characteristics that make it better suited for certain crops, scales, and growing conditions. This assignment helps you understand these differences and apply that knowledge to real-world scenarios.

### Six Major System Types:

1. **Deep Water Culture (DWC)** - Roots suspended in oxygenated nutrient solution
2. **Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)** - Thin film of nutrients flows over roots in channels
3. **Ebb and Flow (Flood and Drain)** - Periodic flooding and draining of grow beds
4. **Drip Systems** - Nutrient solution dripped onto growing media
5. **Aeroponics** - Roots misted with nutrient solution in air
6. **Wick Systems** - Passive nutrient delivery via capillary action

## Reference Guide

### Quick Comparison

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)**
- **Complexity:** Low-Medium
- **Cost:** $ (Low)
- **Best for:** Lettuce, leafy greens, herbs
- **Pros:** Simple, effective, visible roots for learning
- **Cons:** Temperature sensitive, power outage risk
- **Maintenance:** Daily monitoring, weekly water changes

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)**
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Cost:** $$ (Medium)
- **Best for:** Leafy greens, herbs, strawberries
- **Pros:** Water efficient, commercial-scale capable, clean harvest
- **Cons:** Pump failure = rapid death, slope critical, root clogging
- **Maintenance:** Daily checks, channel cleaning

**Ebb and Flow**
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Cost:** $$ (Medium)
- **Best for:** Wide variety of crops
- **Pros:** Versatile, forgiving, works with many media types
- **Cons:** More complex plumbing, timer dependent
- **Maintenance:** Weekly reservoir changes, occasional media cleaning

**Drip Systems**
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Cost:** $$ (Medium)
- **Best for:** Vine crops (tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers)
- **Pros:** Scalable, works for large plants, flexible
- **Cons:** Emitter clogging, runoff management needed
- **Maintenance:** Clean emitters regularly, monitor runoff EC

**Aeroponics**
- **Complexity:** High
- **Cost:** $$$ (High)
- **Best for:** Research, fast-growing greens, propagation
- **Pros:** Maximum oxygenation, fastest growth, water efficient
- **Cons:** Expensive, mister clogging, power failure = quick death
- **Maintenance:** Daily mister checks, frequent cleaning

**Wick Systems**
- **Complexity:** Very Low
- **Cost:** $ (Very Low)
- **Best for:** Herbs, small plants, demonstrations
- **Pros:** No power needed, simple, quiet
- **Cons:** Limited to small plants, slow nutrient delivery
- **Maintenance:** Minimal, refill reservoir
