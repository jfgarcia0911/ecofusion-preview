## Example DIY Systems

### Simple DWC (Deep Water Culture)
- **Cost:** $30-50
- **Components:** Bucket, net pots, air pump, clay pebbles
- **Best for:** Beginners, lettuce/herbs
- **Build time:** 1-2 hours

### Kratky Method (Passive)
- **Cost:** $20-40
- **Components:** Containers, net pots, media (no pump!)
- **Best for:** Ultra simple, lettuce, limited plants
- **Build time:** 30 minutes

### Small NFT System
- **Cost:** $75-125
- **Components:** PVC/gutter channels, reservoir, pump, timer
- **Best for:** Multiple plants, leafy greens
- **Build time:** 3-4 hours

### Dutch Bucket System
- **Cost:** $80-150
- **Components:** Buckets, drip emitters, pump, media
- **Best for:** Tomatoes, peppers, fruiting crops
- **Build time:** 2-3 hours

## Tips for Success

**Planning:**
- Start with simple, proven designs
- Research thoroughly before purchasing
- Check component compatibility
- Allow budget buffer (10% extra)

**Building:**
- Follow food-safe material guidelines
- Test components before final assembly
- Take photos at every step
- Don't rush - quality over speed

**Documentation:**
- Clear, well-lit photos
- Measure and record everything
- Note any deviations from plan
- Be honest about challenges
