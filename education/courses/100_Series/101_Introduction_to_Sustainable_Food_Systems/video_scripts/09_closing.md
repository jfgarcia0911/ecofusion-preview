## What the final exam contains in the platform

The source exam (`tests/final_exam_option_a.md`) has 50 items. The platform holds the **35 that can be marked automatically**: 25 multiple choice and 10 true/false. The matching section and the 5 short-answer questions need a person to mark them, and there is nowhere in the platform to do that yet.

The source also says *one attempt, 60 minutes*. The platform doesn't enforce either: learners can retake it and there's no timer. That's why the script mentions only the 80% pass mark. Don't add attempt or time limits to the script unless the platform starts enforcing them.

## Figures to verify before rendering

- No statistics are spoken in this video.
- **"Earned your certificate"** - the platform issues a completion certificate when every lesson, including the final exam, is passed. Keep the line only while that stays true.
- **Assignment 5** can't be submitted through the platform; the script offers it as something to keep, not something to hand in.
