# Video 0 - Welcome to the Course

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Plays** | First thing in the course, before Module 1 |
| **Target length** | ~2 minutes |
| **Tone** | Warm, unhurried. This is the learner meeting their teacher. |

---

## Script

[ON SCREEN: Course title card - "Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems" - EcoFusion Academy]

Hello, and welcome. I'm [Instructor name], and I'll be your guide through this course.

[ON SCREEN: Instructor, full frame]

Here's a question to start us off. Think about the last meal you ate. Where did it actually come from? Not the store, or the restaurant - I mean where was it grown? Who grew it? How far did it travel to reach you?

[PAUSE 2 seconds]

For most of us, the honest answer is: we have no idea. And that's not a failing on your part. The system that feeds us was built to be invisible. This course is about making it visible - and then asking whether it could work better.

[ON SCREEN: The eight module titles, appearing one by one]

Over eight modules, we'll follow food from the field to your fork. We'll look hard at what the current system gets wrong - the water, the soil, the climate. Then we'll look at the people and methods doing it differently: organic and regenerative farms, city rooftops, greenhouses that grow all year, and systems where fish and plants feed each other. And in the last module, you'll plan your own first step.

[ON SCREEN: "Watch - Read - Check" with an icon for each]

Here's how each module works. First, a short video like this one, where I walk you through the big ideas. Then the full lesson to read - that's where the detail lives. Then a quick quiz. You'll need 80 percent to pass each one, and if you don't get there first time, that's fine - review the lesson and try again.

[ON SCREEN: The "Course resources" button in the course sidebar, highlighted]

Along the way, keep an eye on the **Course resources** button in the sidebar. That's where you'll find the handouts, a glossary, a quick-reference sheet, and the activities we'll do together.

[ON SCREEN: Instructor, full frame]

No experience needed. Just curiosity. Let's begin with the system itself.

[ON SCREEN: "Up next - Module 1: The Global Food System"]

## Production notes

- The "Watch - Read - Check" slide sets expectations for the whole course. Keep it simple enough to read in 3 seconds.
