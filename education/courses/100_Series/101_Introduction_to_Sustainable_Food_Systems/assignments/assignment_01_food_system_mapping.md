# Assignment 1: Food System Mapping
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

**Type:** In-Class Activity
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Points:** 10 points
**Due:** During Module 1

---

## Overview

Trace the journey of a meal from farm to fork, analyzing the environmental, social, and economic impacts at each stage of the food system.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will:
1. Identify the key stages in a food supply chain
2. Understand the concept of "food miles" and transportation impacts
3. Recognize connections between food choices and sustainability

## Instructions

### Step 1: Select a Meal (2 minutes)
Choose a meal you ate recently (breakfast, lunch, or dinner). List 3-5 main ingredients.

### Step 2: Trace Origins (5 minutes)
For each ingredient, research or estimate:
- Where it was likely grown/produced
- Distance traveled to reach you
- Mode of transportation (truck, ship, plane)

### Step 3: Analyze Impacts (5 minutes)
For each ingredient, identify:
- Environmental impact (water use, carbon footprint)
- Social considerations (labor practices, community impact)
- Economic factors (price, local vs. global economy)

### Step 4: Reflection (5 minutes)
Answer these questions:
1. Which ingredient traveled the farthest?
2. What surprised you about your food's journey?
3. How could you make this meal more sustainable?

## Deliverables

Submit a completed worksheet with:
- List of meal ingredients
- Origin and distance for each
- Impact analysis summary
- Reflection responses
