# Assignment 4: Personal Sustainability Action Plan
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

**Type:** Take-Home Assignment
**Duration:** 1-2 hours
**Points:** 15 points
**Due:** End of Module 6

---

## Overview

Develop a personal action plan to integrate sustainable food practices into your daily life, with measurable goals and concrete action steps.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will:
1. Translate knowledge into personal behavioral change
2. Set SMART goals for sustainable food choices
3. Create accountability structures for ongoing improvement

## Instructions

### Part 1: Personal Assessment (20 minutes)

Reflect on your current food-related practices:

1. **Food Sourcing**: Where do you buy food? How much is local/organic?
2. **Food Waste**: Estimate how much food you waste weekly
3. **Dietary Choices**: Assess environmental impact of your diet
4. **Food Preparation**: How often do you cook vs. eat processed foods?
5. **Food Knowledge**: Rate your understanding of food systems (1-10)

### Part 3: Action Steps (30 minutes)

For each goal, identify:
1. **Week 1 actions**: What will you do this week?
2. **Month 1 milestones**: What will you achieve in 30 days?
3. **Resources needed**: What tools, information, or support do you need?
4. **Potential obstacles**: What might prevent success?
5. **Strategies to overcome obstacles**: How will you address challenges?

### Part 4: Reflection (15 minutes)

Write a 1-page reflection addressing:
1. What did you learn about yourself through this assessment?
2. Which changes will be easiest? Most challenging?
3. How does this connect to the broader food system?
4. How will you hold yourself accountable?

## Deliverables

Submit a complete action plan document including:
- Personal assessment summary
- Three SMART goals with justification
- Detailed action steps for each goal
- Written reflection (1 page minimum)
