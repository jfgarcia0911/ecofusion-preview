# Assignment 2: Sustainability Case Study Analysis
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

**Type:** In-Class Activity
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Points:** 15 points
**Due:** During Module 3

---

## Overview

Analyze a real-world food system challenge and develop sustainable solutions by applying concepts learned in the course.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will:
1. Apply critical thinking to food system problems
2. Identify root causes of sustainability challenges
3. Propose evidence-based solutions

## Instructions

### Case Scenario: Urban Food Desert

Riverside Community is a neighborhood of 15,000 residents with:
- No full-service grocery store within 3 miles
- High rates of diet-related health issues
- Several vacant lots and abandoned buildings
- Strong community organizations and churches
- Limited public transportation

### Part 1: Problem Analysis (10 minutes)
1. Identify 3-5 key problems this community faces regarding food access
2. For each problem, identify potential root causes
3. Map the connections between problems

### Part 2: Solution Development (10 minutes)
Develop solutions in these categories:
1. **Short-term** (0-6 months): What can be done immediately?
2. **Medium-term** (6-18 months): What requires planning?
3. **Long-term** (18+ months): What systemic changes are needed?

### Part 3: Sustainability Assessment (5 minutes)
Evaluate your solutions against the "triple bottom line":
- Environmental sustainability
- Social equity
- Economic viability

## Deliverables

Submit a written analysis including:
- Problem identification and root cause analysis
- Three-tiered solution proposal
- Sustainability assessment matrix
