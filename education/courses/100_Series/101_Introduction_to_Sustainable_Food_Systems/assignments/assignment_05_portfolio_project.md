# Assignment 5: Sustainable Food Systems Portfolio
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

**Type:** Comprehensive Project
**Duration:** 2-4 hours
**Points:** 15 points
**Due:** End of Course

---

## Overview

Create a comprehensive portfolio demonstrating your understanding of sustainable food systems through research, analysis, and practical application of course concepts.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this project, you will:
1. Synthesize knowledge from all course modules
2. Demonstrate understanding through multiple formats
3. Apply concepts to a real-world context
4. Communicate effectively about food systems

## Project Components

### Component 1: Food System Research Report (4 points)

Research and write a 2-3 page report on a local sustainable food initiative:

**Requirements:**
- Select a local farm, farmers market, food co-op, urban garden, or food justice organization
- Research their mission, methods, and impact
- Interview a representative if possible
- Analyze how they address sustainability (environmental, social, economic)
- Identify challenges they face and opportunities for growth

### Component 2: Comparative Analysis (4 points)

Create a comparison of two food production methods:

**Compare one of these pairs:**
- Conventional vs. organic farming
- Hydroponics vs. soil-based growing
- Aquaponics vs. traditional aquaculture
- Industrial vs. local food distribution

**Include:**
- Environmental impact comparison
- Economic considerations
- Social implications
- Resource efficiency
- Visual comparison chart or infographic

### Component 3: Food Systems Concept Map (3 points)

Create a visual concept map showing:
- Key components of sustainable food systems
- Relationships between components
- Feedback loops and dependencies
- Where you (the consumer) fit in the system

**Format:** Digital or hand-drawn, full page minimum

### Component 4: Personal Reflection Essay (4 points)

Write a 1-2 page essay reflecting on:

1. **Knowledge Growth**: What did you learn that changed your perspective?
2. **Connections**: How does food connect to climate, health, justice, and economy?
3. **Future Actions**: How will this knowledge influence your future choices?
4. **Advocacy**: How might you share this knowledge with others?

## Deliverables

Submit a complete portfolio containing:
- Food system research report (2-3 pages)
- Comparative analysis with visual element
- Concept map (full page)
- Reflection essay (1-2 pages)

**Total length:** 5-8 pages plus visuals

## Grading Criteria

| Component | Points | Criteria |
|-----------|--------|----------|

| Concept Map | 3 | Completeness, clarity, connections |
