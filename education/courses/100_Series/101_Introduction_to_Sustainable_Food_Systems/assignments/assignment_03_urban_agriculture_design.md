# Assignment 3: Urban Agriculture Design
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

**Type:** In-Class Activity
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Points:** 15 points
**Due:** During Module 5

---

## Overview

Design a small-scale urban agriculture project that addresses food security while incorporating sustainable practices.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will:
1. Apply sustainable agriculture principles to urban settings
2. Consider community needs in food system design
3. Integrate multiple sustainability features into a cohesive plan

## Instructions

### Scenario

You have been given a 5,000 sq ft vacant lot in an urban neighborhood to develop into a community food production site. The lot has:
- Full sun exposure
- Access to city water
- $10,000 startup budget
- 10 volunteer hours per week available

### Part 1: Site Design (15 minutes)
Create a rough site plan including:
- Growing areas (what methods? raised beds, containers, etc.)
- Water management (irrigation, rainwater collection)
- Composting area
- Community gathering space
- Accessibility features

### Part 2: Crop Planning (10 minutes)
Select crops based on:
- Nutritional value for community
- Growing season and succession planting
- Cultural preferences of neighborhood
- Ease of maintenance with volunteer labor

### Part 3: Sustainability Features (10 minutes)
Incorporate at least 4 of these elements:
- Rainwater harvesting
- Composting system
- Pollinator habitat
- Solar power for tools/irrigation
- Native plantings
- Recycled/upcycled materials
- Educational signage
- Youth programming

### Part 4: Community Engagement (5 minutes)
Describe how you will:
- Involve community in decision-making
- Share the harvest equitably
- Provide education and skill-building
- Ensure long-term sustainability

## Deliverables

Submit:
- Site plan sketch with labeled areas
- Crop plan with seasonal timing
- List of sustainability features with justification
- Community engagement strategy (1 paragraph)
