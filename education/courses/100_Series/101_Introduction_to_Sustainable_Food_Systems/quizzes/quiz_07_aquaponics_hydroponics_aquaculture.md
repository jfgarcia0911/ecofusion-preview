## Questions

### Question 1
**What is hydroponics?**

A) Growing fish in tanks

B) Growing plants in water-based nutrient solutions without soil ✓

C) Farming using only rainwater

D) Growing plants in dry sand

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Hydroponics is a method of growing plants without soil, using mineral nutrient solutions dissolved in water.*

---

### Question 2
**What is aquaculture?**

A) Growing plants in water

B) Farming aquatic organisms like fish, shellfish, and aquatic plants ✓

C) Studying ocean currents

D) Water conservation techniques

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaculture is the farming of aquatic organisms including fish, crustaceans, mollusks, and aquatic plants in controlled environments.*

---

### Question 3
**What makes aquaponics unique?**

A) It uses only fish waste as fertilizer in soil

B) It combines fish farming with soilless plant growing in one system ✓

C) It grows only aquatic plants

D) It requires no water changes

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics integrates aquaculture and hydroponics, using fish waste to provide nutrients for plants, while plants filter water for fish—a symbiotic system.*

---

### Question 4
**In aquaponics, what role do bacteria play?**

A) They feed the fish

B) They convert fish waste into plant-usable nutrients ✓

C) They harm plant roots

D) They have no role

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Beneficial bacteria (Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter) convert toxic ammonia from fish waste into nitrites and then nitrates, which plants absorb as nutrients.*

---

### Question 5
**Which system uses NO fish?**

A) Aquaponics

B) Aquaculture

C) Hydroponics ✓

D) Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Hydroponics grows only plants using dissolved mineral nutrients. It does not include fish, unlike aquaponics.*

---

### Question 6
**What is a major advantage of aquaponics over hydroponics?**

A) Faster plant growth

B) Produces both plants and protein (fish) ✓

C) Requires less monitoring

D) Uses no water

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics produces two revenue streams—vegetables and fish—while also reducing the need for synthetic fertilizers since fish waste provides nutrients.*

---

### Question 7
**Which statement about tilapia is TRUE?**

A) They require saltwater

B) They are popular aquaponics fish due to hardiness and fast growth ✓

C) They cannot survive in tanks

D) They are carnivorous only

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia are ideal for aquaponics because they tolerate varying water conditions, grow quickly, and are herbivorous/omnivorous.*

---

### Question 8
**What is the nitrogen cycle in aquaponics?**

A) Fish → ammonia → bacteria → nitrite → nitrate → plants ✓

B) Plants → oxygen → fish → waste → bacteria

C) Water → fish → plants → soil

D) Light → plants → fish → water

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: The nitrogen cycle starts with fish producing ammonia waste, which bacteria convert to nitrite then nitrate, which plants absorb, cleaning the water.*

---

### Question 9
**Which crops grow well in hydroponic systems?**

A) Wheat and corn

B) Root vegetables like carrots

C) Leafy greens, herbs, tomatoes, peppers ✓

D) Large trees

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Leafy greens (lettuce, kale), herbs (basil, cilantro), and fruiting plants (tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers) thrive in hydroponic systems.*

---

### Question 10
**What is a common challenge in aquaponics?**

A) Too many nutrients for plants

B) Balancing the needs of fish, plants, and bacteria ✓

C) Plants growing too slowly

D) Excessive space requirements

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics requires balancing pH, temperature, and nutrient levels to meet the sometimes-competing needs of fish, plants, and beneficial bacteria.*

After passing this quiz, remember these key points:

3. **Aquaponics** = hydroponics + aquaculture integrated

4. **Bacteria** convert fish waste (ammonia) into plant nutrients (nitrate)

5. **Dual harvest**: Aquaponics produces both vegetables and protein
