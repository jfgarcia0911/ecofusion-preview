## Questions

### Question 1
**What is permaculture?**

A) A type of organic farming certification

B) Design system based on natural ecosystem patterns ✓

C) Growing only perennial crops

D) Farming without using any technology

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Permaculture is a design philosophy that creates agricultural systems modeled on natural ecosystems, emphasizing sustainability, self-sufficiency, and working with nature.*

---

### Question 2
**Which farming method uses no synthetic pesticides or fertilizers?**

A) Conventional farming

B) Industrial agriculture

C) Organic farming ✓

D) Precision agriculture

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Organic farming prohibits synthetic pesticides, fertilizers, GMOs, and growth hormones, relying instead on natural inputs and practices.*

---

### Question 3
**What is agroforestry?**

A) Farming in forests without clearing trees

B) Intentionally integrating trees with crops or livestock ✓

C) Growing only tree fruits

D) Forestry managed by agricultural departments

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Agroforestry combines agriculture and forestry, deliberately integrating woody perennials (trees, shrubs) with crops and/or animals on the same land.*

---

### Question 4
**What distinguishes biodynamic farming from organic farming?**

A) It's cheaper to certify

B) It incorporates spiritual and cosmic principles ✓

C) It uses more synthetic inputs

D) It requires less record-keeping

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Biodynamic farming follows organic principles but adds spiritual, cosmic, and astrological considerations, viewing the farm as a living organism.*

---

### Question 5
**In a polyculture system, what is grown?**

A) Only one crop variety

B) Multiple crops together in the same space ✓

C) Crops in separate fields that are rotated

D) Genetically modified crops only

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Polyculture grows multiple crop species simultaneously in the same space, mimicking natural diversity and reducing pest and disease pressure.*

---

### Question 6
**What is the primary benefit of a farmers market compared to conventional retail?**

A) Lower prices for all products

B) Direct connection between producer and consumer ✓

C) More processed food options

D) Longer food storage times

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Farmers markets eliminate intermediaries, allowing direct relationships between farmers and consumers, fresher products, and more money going to the producer.*

---

### Question 7
**What does "farm-to-table" mean?**

A) Restaurants that own farmland

B) Direct sourcing of food from local farms to restaurants ✓

C) Tables made from farm wood

D) Eating meals at farms

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Farm-to-table restaurants source ingredients directly from local farms, emphasizing fresh, seasonal, and locally produced food.*

---

### Question 8
**Which statement about food cooperatives (co-ops) is TRUE?**

A) They are owned by large corporations

B) They focus only on organic products

C) They are member-owned and democratically controlled ✓

D) They cannot sell fresh produce

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Food cooperatives are owned and governed by their members, who share in decision-making and often receive benefits based on their participation.*

---

### Question 9
**What is no-till farming?**

A) Farming on flat land only

B) Growing crops without plowing or tilling the soil ✓

C) Farming without farm equipment

D) Planting crops at an angle

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: No-till farming avoids plowing or tilling, leaving soil structure intact, which reduces erosion, builds organic matter, and supports soil organisms.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the main goal of the Slow Food movement?**

A) Eating meals very slowly

B) Preserving traditional food cultures and sustainable practices ✓

C) Cooking food at low temperatures

D) Reducing restaurant service times

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Slow Food promotes traditional food preparation, local ingredients, sustainable agriculture, and food culture preservation as alternatives to fast food and industrial agriculture.*
