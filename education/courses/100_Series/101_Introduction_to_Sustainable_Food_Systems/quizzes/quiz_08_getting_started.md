## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the FIRST step in starting a small-scale sustainable food project?**

A) Purchase all equipment immediately

B) Assess your goals, resources, space, and time ✓

C) Quit your job to farm full-time

D) Build the largest system possible

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Successful projects start with honest assessment of your goals, available resources (money, time, space, skills), and constraints before making any purchases.*

---

### Question 2
**Which growing method is best for beginners with limited space?**

A) Commercial greenhouse operation

B) 50-acre farm

C) Container gardens or small hydroponic system ✓

D) Large aquaponics facility

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Beginners should start small with manageable systems like container gardens, countertop hydroponics, or small raised beds to learn without overwhelming investment.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the benefit of starting with a pilot project?**

A) It guarantees immediate profit

B) It requires no learning

C) It allows learning and testing before scaling up ✓

D) It eliminates all risks

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Pilot projects let you learn systems, test markets, identify challenges, and refine processes before committing to larger, more expensive operations.*

---

### Question 4
**Which factor is most important when choosing crops to grow?**

A) What looks prettiest

B) What your climate, skills, and market demand support ✓

C) Only the most expensive crops

D) Whatever requires the most work

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Successful crop selection considers your growing conditions, skill level, available resources, and whether you have a market (or personal use) for the harvest.*

---

### Question 5
**What is a key consideration for urban growers?**

A) Unlimited land availability

B) Zoning regulations and space constraints ✓

C) No need to consider neighbors

D) Free water and electricity

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Urban growers must navigate zoning laws, HOA restrictions, limited space, and neighbor relations while finding creative solutions like rooftops, balconies, or community gardens.*

---

### Question 6
**How should beginners approach learning sustainable food production?**

A) Learn everything before starting anything

B) Start practicing immediately with small experiments while learning ✓

C) Only read books, never practice

D) Copy exactly what commercial farms do

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The best learning combines study with hands-on practice. Start small projects while reading and learning, allowing theory and practice to reinforce each other.*

---

### Question 7
**What is the "start small, think big" principle?**

A) Never expand your operation

B) Dream big but only plan for small scale

C) Begin with manageable projects while planning for future growth ✓

D) Think only about large-scale commercial operations

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: "Start small, think big" means beginning with achievable pilot projects to build skills and confidence while keeping long-term expansion goals in mind.*

---

### Question 8
**Which online resource is valuable for sustainable agriculture learners?**

A) Social media only

B) Reputable extension services, research universities, and specialized forums ✓

C) Unverified blog posts exclusively

D) Commercial advertisements only

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: University extension services, research institutions (like Cornell CEA, Auburn Aquaponics), and moderated forums provide science-based, reliable information.*

---

### Question 9
**What should you do when facing challenges in your sustainable food project?**

A) Give up immediately

B) Ignore the problems and hope they resolve

C) Troubleshoot systematically and seek advice from experienced growers ✓

D) Start completely over from scratch

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Challenges are learning opportunities. Systematically diagnose issues, consult resources, connect with mentors, and adjust your approach based on evidence.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the value of connecting with local growers and networks?**

A) No value, work alone always

B) Sharing knowledge, resources, and mutual support ✓

C) Only to compete with them

D) To copy their business exactly

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Local growing communities provide mentorship, share resources, troubleshoot problems together, and create networks for knowledge exchange and mutual support.*
