## Questions

### Question 1
**What percentage of global soil is currently degraded?**

A) 10%

B) 22%

C) 33% ✓

D) 50%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Approximately 33% (one-third) of global soil is degraded due to erosion, compaction, nutrient depletion, and other factors.*

---

### Question 2
**How long does it take to form one inch of topsoil naturally?**

A) 10-50 years

B) 100-200 years

C) 500-1,000 years ✓

D) 5,000-10,000 years

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Topsoil formation is an extremely slow process, taking 500-1,000 years to create just one inch, making soil loss essentially irreversible on human timescales.*

---

### Question 3
**What percentage of global greenhouse gas emissions comes from the food system?**

A) 10%

B) 18%

C) 26% ✓

D) 40%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The food system accounts for approximately 26% of global greenhouse gas emissions, including production, processing, transportation, and land use change.*

---

### Question 4
**What is "eutrophication"?**

A) A method of organic farming

B) Excessive nutrients causing algae blooms and oxygen depletion ✓

C) The process of soil improvement

D) A type of pesticide application

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Eutrophication occurs when excessive nutrients (usually nitrogen and phosphorus from fertilizer runoff) enter water bodies, causing algae blooms that deplete oxygen and create dead zones.*

---

### Question 5
**Approximately how much crop diversity has been lost in the last 100 years?**

A) 25%

B) 50%

C) 75% ✓

D) 90%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: 75% of crop diversity has been lost in the last century due to industrial agriculture's focus on a narrow range of high-yielding varieties.*

---

### Question 6
**In industrial agriculture, for every 10 calories of fossil fuel energy invested, how many calories of food energy are produced?**

A) 1 calorie ✓

B) 5 calories

C) 10 calories

D) 20 calories

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Modern industrial agriculture has a 10:1 energy ratio—it takes 10 calories of fossil fuel energy to produce just 1 calorie of food energy, the opposite of traditional agriculture.*

---

### Question 7
**What caused the Aral Sea disaster?**

A) Climate change causing drought

B) River diversion for cotton irrigation ✓

C) Industrial pollution from factories

D) Overfishing depleting the ecosystem

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The Aral Sea, once the 4th largest lake in the world, was destroyed by Soviet-era diversion of rivers for cotton irrigation, reducing it to 10% of its original size.*

---

### Question 8
**What percentage of the food dollar do farmers receive today compared to 1950s?**

A) $0.41 in 1950s vs $0.14 today ✓

B) $0.50 in 1950s vs $0.35 today

C) $0.30 in 1950s vs $0.20 today

D) $0.60 in 1950s vs $0.40 today

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Farmers' share of the food dollar has dropped dramatically from $0.41 in the 1950s to just $0.14 today, with most value captured by processors, distributors, and retailers.*

---

### Question 9
**Which greenhouse gas from agriculture has 265 times the warming potential of CO2?**

A) Methane (CH4)

B) Nitrous Oxide (N2O) ✓

C) Carbon dioxide (CO2)

D) Ammonia (NH3)

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Nitrous oxide (N2O), primarily from fertilizer use and manure, has 265 times the global warming potential of CO2, making it a significant contributor to climate change.*

---

### Question 10
**What is a "dead zone" in the context of water pollution?**

A) An area where fishing is prohibited

B) An area of water with insufficient oxygen to support life ✓

C) A section of a river that has been dammed

D) Land that cannot be used for agriculture

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Dead zones are areas of water (usually in oceans or lakes) where oxygen levels are too low to support most marine life, typically caused by nutrient pollution from agricultural runoff.*
