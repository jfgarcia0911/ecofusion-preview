## Questions

### Question 1
**What are the three pillars of sustainability?**

A) Economic, political, technological

B) Environmental, social, economic ✓

C) Agriculture, industry, services

D) Production, distribution, consumption

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The three pillars of sustainability are environmental (planet), social (people), and economic (profit), often referred to as the "triple bottom line."*

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following BEST defines regenerative agriculture?**

A) Maintaining current soil conditions

B) Using only organic fertilizers

C) Improving and restoring soil health over time ✓

D) Rotating crops annually

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Regenerative agriculture goes beyond sustainability by actively improving and restoring ecosystem health, particularly soil health, biodiversity, and water cycles.*

---

### Question 3
**What does the term "circular economy" mean in food systems?**

A) Growing food in circular greenhouses

B) Waste becomes input for another process, minimizing disposal ✓

C) Rotating crops in circles around a farm

D) Transporting food in circular routes

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A circular economy eliminates waste by designing systems where outputs become inputs, such as composting food waste to create fertilizer.*

---

### Question 4
**Which farming practice helps prevent soil erosion?**

A) Deep tilling before every crop

B) Removing all crop residue after harvest

C) Cover cropping and no-till farming ✓

D) Monoculture planting

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Cover crops protect soil from erosion, and no-till farming maintains soil structure. Both practices keep the soil covered and intact.*

---

### Question 5
**What is biodiversity in agriculture?**

A) Using biological pesticides only

B) Growing diverse species and varieties ✓

C) Diversity of farming equipment

D) Number of farmers in an area

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Biodiversity refers to the variety of plant and animal species in an agricultural system, which supports resilience and ecosystem health.*

---

### Question 6
**How does crop rotation benefit sustainable farming?**

A) It confuses pests and reduces disease, improves soil nutrients ✓

B) It looks more attractive to consumers

C) It requires less water

D) It eliminates the need for fertilizer completely

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Crop rotation breaks pest and disease cycles, balances nutrient demands, and can reduce reliance on chemical inputs.*

---

### Question 7
**What is the primary goal of integrated pest management (IPM)?**

A) Eliminate all insects from the farm

B) Use only organic pesticides

C) Minimize pest damage using multiple strategies with least harm ✓

D) Rely solely on beneficial insects

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: IPM uses a combination of biological, cultural, physical, and chemical methods to manage pests effectively while minimizing environmental and health risks.*

---

### Question 8
**Which practice is characteristic of sustainable water management?**

A) Flood irrigation for all crops

B) Drawing from groundwater without limits

C) Drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting ✓

D) Watering only when plants show stress

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Efficient irrigation methods like drip systems and collecting rainwater reduce water waste and dependence on groundwater or municipal sources.*

---

### Question 9
**What does "food sovereignty" mean?**

A) Government control of all food production

B) People's right to define their own food systems ✓

C) Corporations owning seed patents

D) International trade agreements for food

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Food sovereignty is the right of peoples to healthy, culturally appropriate food produced through ecologically sound methods, and their right to define their own food and agriculture systems.*

---

### Question 10
**Which statement BEST describes sustainable agriculture?**

A) Maximizing yields regardless of environmental cost

B) Meeting present needs without compromising future generations ✓

C) Growing only organic crops

D) Using traditional farming methods exclusively

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Sustainable agriculture meets current food needs while preserving resources and environmental health for future generations, balancing productivity with stewardship.*
