## Questions

### Question 1
**What are the five main components of a food system?**

A) Growing, selling, eating, composting, recycling

B) Production, processing, distribution, consumption, waste management ✓

C) Farming, trucking, shopping, cooking, disposing

D) Seeds, water, land, labor, markets

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A food system encompasses production (growing), processing (transforming), distribution (moving), consumption (eating), and waste management (disposal/recycling).*

---

### Question 2
**Approximately what percentage of global freshwater is used for agriculture?**

A) 30%

B) 50%

C) 70% ✓

D) 90%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Agriculture consumes approximately 70% of global freshwater withdrawals, making it by far the largest water user.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the definition of "food miles"?**

A) The total weight of food transported

B) The distance food travels from production to consumption ✓

C) The number of miles a truck travels per day

D) The fuel efficiency of food transport vehicles

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Food miles refers to the distance food travels from where it is grown or produced to where it is ultimately consumed.*

---

### Question 4
**On average, how far does food travel in the conventional US food system?**

A) 100 miles

B) 500 miles

C) 1,500 miles ✓

D) 5,000 miles

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The average food item in the US travels approximately 1,500 miles from farm to consumer.*

---

### Question 5
**Which food has the HIGHEST water footprint per pound?**

A) Rice

B) Chicken

C) Beef ✓

D) Wheat

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Beef requires approximately 1,800 gallons of water per pound, far more than other common foods.*

---

### Question 6
**What percentage of all food produced globally is wasted?**

A) About 10%

B) About 20%

C) About 33% ✓

D) About 50%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Approximately one-third (33%) of all food produced for human consumption is lost or wasted globally—about 1.3 billion tons per year.*

---

### Question 7
**Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of local food systems compared to global food systems?**

A) Food travels shorter distances

B) Food is typically fresher

C) Greater year-round variety of all foods ✓

D) Stronger connection between producer and consumer

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Local food systems typically offer LESS variety year-round because they are limited to seasonal production, unlike global systems that can source from anywhere.*

---

### Question 8
**What does CSA stand for in the context of local food?**

A) Certified Sustainable Agriculture

B) Community Supported Agriculture ✓

C) Consumer Shopping Association

D) Cooperative Sustainable Alliance

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) is a model where consumers purchase shares directly from a farm and receive regular deliveries of produce throughout the growing season.*

---

### Question 9
**Which food type generally has the LOWEST carbon footprint?**

A) Beef (27 kg CO2/kg)

B) Cheese (13.5 kg CO2/kg)

C) Chicken (6.9 kg CO2/kg)

D) Vegetables (2 kg CO2/kg) ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: Vegetables have the lowest carbon footprint at approximately 2 kg CO2 per kg, compared to animal products which are significantly higher.*

---

### Question 10
**What percentage of world calories come from just three crops (wheat, rice, and corn)?**

A) 30%

B) 45%

C) 60% ✓

D) 75%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Approximately 60% of global caloric intake comes from just three crops: wheat, rice, and corn, showing the narrow base of the global food supply.*
