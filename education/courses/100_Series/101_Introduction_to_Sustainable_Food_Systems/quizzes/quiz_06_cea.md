## Questions

### Question 1
**What does CEA stand for?**

A) Certified Environmental Agriculture

B) Controlled Environment Agriculture ✓

C) Community Economic Alliance

D) Crop Enhancement Analysis

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: CEA stands for Controlled Environment Agriculture, which involves growing crops in structures where environmental factors are precisely managed.*

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following is an example of CEA?**

A) Open field farming

B) Greenhouses with climate control ✓

C) Rain-fed agriculture

D) Pasture grazing

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Greenhouses with climate control systems exemplify CEA by managing temperature, humidity, light, and other environmental factors for optimal plant growth.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the primary advantage of CEA over traditional outdoor farming?**

A) Lower initial investment costs

B) No need for skilled labor

C) Year-round production and environmental control ✓

D) Larger growing areas available

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: CEA enables year-round production regardless of outdoor weather, with precise control over growing conditions for consistent, high-quality yields.*

---

### Question 4
**Which environmental factor can be controlled in CEA systems?**

A) Temperature, light, humidity, CO2 ✓

B) Rainfall only

C) Wind direction only

D) Outdoor pest populations

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: CEA systems can precisely control temperature, lighting (intensity, spectrum, duration), humidity, CO2 levels, and nutrients for optimal plant performance.*

---

### Question 5
**What is a key water advantage of CEA?**

A) Uses more water than field agriculture

B) Requires connection to rivers

C) Recirculates water, reducing consumption by 90%+ ✓

D) Depends entirely on rainwater

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: CEA systems typically recirculate water, reducing consumption by 90-95% compared to conventional field agriculture.*

---

### Question 6
**Why is vertical farming considered a form of CEA?**

A) It uses vertical transportation only

B) It controls environment while stacking crops to maximize space ✓

C) It grows only climbing plants

D) It requires no electricity

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Vertical farming is CEA because it provides complete environmental control while stacking growing layers vertically to maximize production per square foot.*

---

### Question 7
**What challenge does CEA face?**

A) Too much natural sunlight

B) High energy costs for lighting and climate control ✓

C) Inability to control pests

D) Cannot grow leafy greens

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: CEA's main challenge is high energy consumption for supplemental lighting, heating, cooling, and dehumidification, which can impact profitability.*

---

### Question 8
**Which crops are most economically viable in CEA?**

A) Wheat and corn

B) Leafy greens, herbs, and specialty crops ✓

C) Soybeans and cotton

D) Pasture grasses

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: High-value, fast-growing crops like lettuce, herbs, microgreens, and specialty produce provide the best economic returns in CEA due to faster turnover and premium prices.*

---

### Question 9
**How does CEA reduce pesticide use?**

A) By using stronger pesticides

B) Plants in controlled environments naturally resist all pests

C) Isolated environment minimizes pest exposure ✓

D) Pesticides are always required in CEA

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: CEA's enclosed, controlled environment dramatically reduces pest pressure, often eliminating the need for pesticides entirely through exclusion and biosecurity.*

---

### Question 10
**What technology is commonly used for lighting in modern CEA?**

A) Incandescent bulbs

B) Candles

C) LED grow lights ✓

D) Oil lamps

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: LED grow lights are the standard in modern CEA due to their energy efficiency, customizable light spectrum, and low heat output.*
