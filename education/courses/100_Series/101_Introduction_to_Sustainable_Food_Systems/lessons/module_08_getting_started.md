## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. **Assess** your current knowledge, skills, and resources
2. **Identify** your personal goals and motivations in sustainable food systems
3. **Create** a personalized action plan for next steps
4. **Locate** resources and support for your sustainable food journey
5. **Connect** with communities and networks in sustainable agriculture
6. **Commit** to specific, achievable actions in the next 30, 60, and 90 days

### 8.5 First Steps Guide

#### The 30-60-90 Day Plan

**DAYS 1-30: FOUNDATION**

```
MONTH 1 CHECKLIST

LEARN
☐ Complete this course!
☐ Read 2-3 recommended books
☐ Watch documentaries
☐ Follow 5-10 accounts on social media
☐ Join online community/forum

CONNECT
☐ Find local food systems organizations
☐ Attend 1 farmers market
☐ Visit 1 community garden
☐ Reach out to 3 people doing what interests you
☐ Join relevant Facebook groups

EXPERIMENT
☐ Start herb garden (windowsill or balcony)
☐ Visit local farm or garden
☐ Try one new local food per week
☐ Start composting
☐ Keep food journal
```

**DAYS 31-60: EXPLORATION**

```
MONTH 2 CHECKLIST

DEEPEN KNOWLEDGE
☐ Take next course in series (102, 103, or other)

☐ Subscribe to relevant publications
☐ Start a learning journal

BUILD CONNECTIONS
☐ Volunteer at community garden (4+ hours)
☐ Schedule informational interviews
☐ Join working group or committee
☐ Attend networking event

EXPAND PRACTICE
☐ Expand growing space/variety
☐ Try new technique (hydroponics, composting, etc.)
☐ Document your experiments
☐ Share learnings with others
```

**DAYS 61-90: COMMITMENT**

```

☐ Complete full self-assessment
```

#### Finding Your People

**Online Communities:**
- Aquaponics forums and Facebook groups
- r/aquaponics, r/hydro, r/gardening (Reddit)
- Discord servers for growers
- Instagram sustainable food community
- YouTube channels and comment communities

**In-Person:**
- Farmers markets (talk to vendors!)
- Community garden workdays
- Extension office events
- Farm tours and open houses
- Conferences and trade shows
- Local food meetups

**Mentorship:**

```
FINDING A MENTOR

1. IDENTIFY potential mentors
   → Who's doing what you want to do?

2. RESEARCH their work
   → Follow, learn, understand

3. OFFER value first
   → Volunteer, help, contribute

4. ASK thoughtfully
   → Specific questions, not "tell me everything"

5. MAINTAIN relationship
   → Update them, show gratitude, pay it forward
```
