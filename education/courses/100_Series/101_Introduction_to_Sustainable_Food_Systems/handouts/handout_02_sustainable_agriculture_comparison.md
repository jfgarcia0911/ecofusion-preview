## Key Sustainable Agriculture Methods

### 1. Crop Rotation
```
    YEAR 1        YEAR 2        YEAR 3        YEAR 4
    ┌─────┐       ┌─────┐       ┌─────┐       ┌─────┐
    │Corn │  ──►  │Beans│  ──►  │Wheat│  ──►  │Cover│
    │🌽   │       │🫘   │       │🌾   │       │🌿   │
    └─────┘       └─────┘       └─────┘       └─────┘

    Benefits: Breaks pest cycles, balances nutrients, improves soil
```

### 2. Cover Cropping
```
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                              │
    │  Main crop harvested                         │
    │          ↓                                   │
    │  Plant cover crop (clover, rye, vetch)      │
    │          ↓                                   │
    │  Roots hold soil, fix nitrogen              │
    │          ↓                                   │
    │  Terminate and incorporate                   │
    │          ↓                                   │
    │  Plant next main crop in enriched soil      │
    │                                              │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Sustainable Food Labels Decoded

| Label | What It Means | What It DOESN'T Mean |
|-------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Organic** | No synthetic chemicals | Not necessarily local or fair trade |
| **Local** | Grown nearby (<100 mi) | Not necessarily organic |
| **Fair Trade** | Fair wages for farmers | Not necessarily organic or local |
| **Non-GMO** | No genetic modification | Nothing about chemical use |
| **Natural** | Minimal processing | Largely unregulated term |
| **Regenerative** | Builds soil health | Emerging, various standards |
