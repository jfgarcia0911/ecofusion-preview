## Quick Facts for Discussion

### Food Production Capacity
- The world currently produces enough food to feed 10 billion people
- Yet hunger persists due to distribution, waste, and access issues
- 80% of the world's hungry live in rural areas

### Resource Intensive
- It takes 1,800 gallons of water to produce 1 lb of beef
- It takes 39 gallons of water to produce 1 lb of vegetables
- Agriculture uses 10x more land than urban areas globally

### The Local Food Movement
- Local food travels < 100 miles vs. conventional > 1,500 miles
- Fresh food loses 45% of nutrients within one week of harvest
- Urban agriculture is growing 5-7% annually worldwide
