# Grading Rubric
## Course 101: Introduction to Sustainable Food Systems

---

| Component | Points | Percentage |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Module Quizzes (8) | 80 | 40% |

| **Total** | **200** | **100%** |

| Grade | Percentage | Points |

## Assignment Rubrics

### Assignment 1: Food System Mapping (10 points)
**In-Class Activity - 15-20 minutes**

| Criteria | Excellent (9-10) | Good (7-8) | Fair (5-6) | Needs Improvement (0-4) |
|----------|-----------------|------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Completeness | All meal components traced | Most components traced | Some components traced | Few components traced |
| Analysis | Detailed impact analysis | Good analysis | Basic analysis | Minimal analysis |
| Participation | Actively engaged | Good engagement | Some engagement | Limited engagement |

### Assignment 2: Sustainability Case Study (15 points)
**In-Class Activity - 20-30 minutes**

| Criteria | Excellent (14-15) | Good (11-13) | Fair (8-10) | Needs Improvement (0-7) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Problem ID | All key issues identified | Most issues identified | Some issues identified | Few issues identified |
| Analysis | Excellent cause/effect | Good analysis | Basic analysis | Limited analysis |
| Solutions | Multiple viable solutions | Some good solutions | Basic solutions | Weak solutions |

### Assignment 3: Urban Agriculture Design (15 points)
**In-Class Activity - 30-45 minutes**

| Criteria | Excellent (14-15) | Good (11-13) | Fair (8-10) | Needs Improvement (0-7) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Design Quality | Creative and practical | Good design | Basic design | Incomplete |
| Sustainability | Innovative features | Several features | Some features | Few features |
| Presentation | Clear and organized | Well organized | Somewhat organized | Disorganized |

### Assignment 4: Personal Action Plan (15 points)
**Take-Home Assignment - 1-2 hours**

| Criteria | Excellent (14-15) | Good (11-13) | Fair (8-10) | Needs Improvement (0-7) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Goal Setting | SMART goals | Good goals | Basic goals | Vague goals |
| Action Steps | Detailed steps | Good steps | Basic steps | Unclear steps |
| Reflection | Deep reflection | Good reflection | Basic reflection | Minimal reflection |

### Assignment 5: Sustainable Food Systems Portfolio (15 points)
**Comprehensive Project - 2-4 hours**

| Criteria | Excellent (14-15) | Good (11-13) | Fair (8-10) | Needs Improvement (0-7) |
|----------|------------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Research | Comprehensive | Good research | Basic research | Limited research |
| Content | Thorough coverage | Good coverage | Adequate coverage | Incomplete |
| Analysis | Excellent synthesis | Good analysis | Basic analysis | Limited analysis |
| Presentation | Professional quality | Well organized | Adequately organized | Poor organization |
