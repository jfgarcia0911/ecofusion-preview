### 8.2 Planting Techniques by System

#### Raised Bed Planting

**From Seeds:**

```
DIRECT SEEDING IN RAISED BEDS

Step 1: Create planting rows or patterns
├─ Use string as guide for straight rows
├─ Or plant in square foot grid
└─ Or intensive planting (irregular spacing)

Step 2: Plant at correct depth
├─ Rule of thumb: 2-3× seed diameter
├─ Small seeds (lettuce): ¼" deep
├─ Medium seeds (beans): 1" deep
└─ Large seeds (squash): 2" deep

Step 3: Spacing
├─ Read seed packet
├─ Thin later if too crowded
└─ Mark rows with labels

Step 4: Cover and water
├─ Gently cover with soil
├─ Pat down lightly (soil contact)
├─ Water gently (mist/sprinkle)
└─ Keep moist until germination
```

**Spacing Guide:**

| Crop | Seeds Per Ft | Thin To | Between Rows |
|------|-------------|---------|--------------|
| **Lettuce** | 8-10 | Every 6" | 8" |
| **Radish** | 12-16 | Every 2" | 4" |
| **Carrots** | 16-20 | Every 3" | 4" |
| **Spinach** | 12-15 | Every 4" | 6" |
| **Beans** | 6-8 | Every 4" | 18" |

**From Transplants:**

```
TRANSPLANTING SEEDLINGS

1. Dig hole slightly larger than root ball
2. Remove transplant from container gently
3. Loosen root ball if pot-bound
4. Place in hole at same depth (or slightly deeper for tomatoes)
5. Fill in around roots with soil
6. Firm soil gently around base
7. Water immediately and thoroughly
8. Shade for first 2-3 days if sunny

Transplant Spacing:
├─ Lettuce: 6-8" apart
├─ Tomatoes: 24-36" apart
├─ Peppers: 18-24" apart
├─ Herbs: 12-18" apart
```

**Hardening Off** (If Starting Indoors):
- Day 1-2: Outside 1-2 hours, shade
- Day 3-4: Outside 3-4 hours, partial sun
- Day 5-6: Outside 6-8 hours, more sun
- Day 7+: Outside full-time, transplant when ready

#### Hydroponic Planting (Kratky)

**You've Already Planted!** (Module 6)
- Plants should be establishing now
- Monitor daily
- Watch for root growth

**Adding More Plants:**

```
TRANSPLANTING INTO KRATKY

Best Practice:
1. Start seeds in rockwool cubes separately
2. When 2-4 true leaves appear (10-14 days)
3. Place cube in net pot
4. Surround with clay pebbles
5. Insert into system

Initial Care:
├─ Top-water for first 2-3 days
├─ Until roots reach solution
├─ Then hands-off (beauty of Kratky!)
└─ Monitor pH and solution level
```

**Succession Planting in Hydroponics:**
- Start new seedlings every 2 weeks
- Harvest and replace mature plants
- Maintain continuous production
- Rotate crop types for variety

### 8.3 Daily and Weekly Care Routines

#### Daily Tasks (5-10 minutes)

```
MORNING ROUTINE

1. OBSERVE (2 min)
   ├─ General plant health check
   ├─ Any wilting, yellowing, or damage?
   ├─ Pest or disease signs?
   └─ Weather conditions

2. WATER CHECK (3 min)
   ├─ Soil beds: Finger test 2" deep
   ├─ Hydroponics: Solution level check
   └─ Greenhouse: Varies by weather

3. ENVIRONMENTAL (2 min)
   ├─ Temperature reading
   ├─ Adjust ventilation if needed
   └─ Note weather forecast

4. RECORD (2 min)
   ├─ Quick log entry
   ├─ Photo if significant change
   └─ Note any actions taken
```

**Watering Wisdom:**

| Indicator | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Soil dry 2" down** | Water thoroughly until drains |
| **Soil moist** | No water needed |
| **Wilting in morning** | Water immediately |
| **Wilting midday heat** | Normal, check evening |
| **Yellow leaves + wet soil** | Overwatering - let dry |

**Hydroponic Daily:**
- Check solution level (mark for reference)
- Observe plant color and growth
- Check pH first 2 weeks (daily), then weekly
- Look for root health in clear cup test plants

#### Weekly Tasks (30-60 minutes)

```
WEEKLY MAINTENANCE

WEEDING (if soil-based)
├─ Remove all weeds (compete for resources)
├─ Pull when small (easier, less root disturbance)
└─ Mulch to reduce future weeds

FEEDING
├─ Soil: Side-dress with compost if slow growth
├─ Hydro: Check EC/PPM, adjust if low
└─ Follow crop-specific needs

PRUNING/TRAINING
├─ Remove dead/damaged leaves
├─ Thin overcrowded plants
├─ Train vining plants up supports
└─ Pinch herbs to encourage bushiness

PEST/DISEASE CHECK
├─ Inspect undersides of leaves
├─ Look for chewing damage
├─ Check for powdery/fuzzy spots
└─ Catch problems early!

HARVESTING
├─ Pick ripe produce promptly
├─ Encourages more production
├─ Prevents overripening/bolting
└─ Enjoy your success!
```

### 8.4 Succession Planting for Continuous Harvest

#### The Succession Strategy

```
SUCCESSION PLANTING TIMELINE

Week 1: Plant Batch 1 (Lettuce)
        │
        ▼
Week 3: Plant Batch 2 (Lettuce)
        │
        ▼
Week 5: Harvest Batch 1 → Plant Batch 3
        │
        ▼
Week 7: Harvest Batch 2 → Plant Batch 4
        │
        ▼
Week 9: Harvest Batch 3 → Plant Batch 5

Result: Continuous harvest every 2 weeks!
```

**Fast Crops for Succession:**

| Crop | Days to Harvest | Succession Interval |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Lettuce** | 30-45 | Every 2-3 weeks |
| **Radish** | 25-30 | Every 2 weeks |
| **Spinach** | 40-50 | Every 3 weeks |
| **Arugula** | 30-40 | Every 2 weeks |
| **Cilantro** | 40-50 | Every 3 weeks |

**Planting Calendar Example (12-week season):**

| Week | Plant | Expected Harvest |
|------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Lettuce Batch 1, Radish Batch 1 | Week 5-6, Week 4 |
| 3 | Lettuce Batch 2, Spinach Batch 1 | Week 7-8, Week 9 |
| 5 | Lettuce Batch 3, Radish Batch 2 | Week 9-10, Week 8 |
| 7 | Lettuce Batch 4, Spinach Batch 2 | Week 11-12, Week 13 |

### 8.5 Common Early Challenges

#### Seedling Problems

```
TROUBLESHOOTING SEEDLINGS

Problem: Seeds don't germinate
Causes & Solutions:
├─ Too cold: Wait for soil >50°F, use row cover
├─ Too deep: Plant shallower next time
├─ Too dry: Keep moist until sprouted
├─ Old seeds: Check germination date, test batch
└─ Eaten by pests: Protect with row cover

Problem: Seedlings are leggy (tall and thin)
Causes & Solutions:
├─ Not enough light: Move to sunnier spot, add grow light
└─ Too warm: Normal for some, thin to strongest

Problem: Seedlings die after sprouting
Causes & Solutions:
├─ Damping off (fungus): Improve air circulation, don't overwater
├─ Too dry: Keep consistently moist
└─ Too cold: Protect from cold nights
```

#### Growing Problems

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Yellow leaves (lower first)** | Nitrogen deficiency or overwatering | Add compost or reduce water |
| **Curled leaves** | Pests, heat stress, or herbicide drift | Inspect for pests, provide shade, investigate chemicals |
| **Holes in leaves** | Caterpillars, slugs, or beetles | Hand-pick, use organic pest control |
| **Wilting despite water** | Root damage, disease, or extreme heat | Check roots, investigate disease, provide shade |
| **Slow growth** | Too cold, poor nutrition, or inadequate light | Wait for warmth, fertilize, improve light |
| **Purple leaves** | Phosphorus deficiency or cold | Add fertilizer, wait for warm weather |

#### Hydroponic-Specific

```
HYDROPONIC TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: pH won't stabilize
├─ New system: Normal for first week, adjust daily
├─ Poor water quality: Use filtered/RO water
└─ Algae growth: Ensure light-tight container

Problem: Nutrient solution is cloudy
├─ Algae: Light is entering, seal all gaps
├─ Bacterial bloom: Change solution, clean system
└─ Nutrient precipitation: Check pH, may be too high/low

Problem: Plants aren't growing
├─ pH wrong: Check and correct (5.5-6.5)
├─ EC too low: Add nutrients
├─ EC too high: Dilute with water
├─ Temperature wrong: Adjust location
└─ Light inadequate: Supplement or move
```

### 8.6 Seasonal Planning

#### Extending the Season

**Spring:**
- Start early with cold-hardy crops (lettuce, spinach, peas)
- Use row covers for frost protection
- Watch for late cold snaps

**Summer:**
- Provide shade in extreme heat
- Water more frequently
- Watch for heat stress and bolting
- Plant heat-lovers (tomatoes, peppers, squash)

**Fall:**
- Second spring! Plant cool-season crops again
- Extend with row covers and cold frames
- Greenhouse advantage: Keep growing

**Winter:**
- Indoor systems shine
- Greenhouse with heating can produce year-round
- Outdoor beds: Cover crops or rest period
- Plan and dream for spring

```
YEAR-ROUND GROWING POTENTIAL

Outdoor Raised Beds:
├─ Spring: 8-12 weeks
├─ Summer: 12-16 weeks
├─ Fall: 8-12 weeks
└─ Winter: Dormant (or cover crops)
    Total: 28-40 weeks

With Season Extension (row covers, cold frames):
├─ Add 4-8 weeks spring
└─ Add 4-8 weeks fall
    Total: 36-56 weeks

Indoor Hydroponics/Greenhouse:
└─ 52 weeks continuous!
```

### 8.7 Harvesting Your Success

#### When to Harvest

**Leafy Greens:**
- Lettuce: When leaves are 4-6" long, before bolting
- Spinach: Pick outer leaves, let center grow
- Herbs: Regularly trim, promotes bushiness

**Root Vegetables:**
- Radishes: When tops push up (3-4 weeks)
- Carrots: When shoulders show (pull one to check)

**Fruiting Vegetables:**
- Tomatoes: When fully colored and slightly soft
- Peppers: Green when immature, colored when ripe
- Beans: When pods are full but still tender

```
HARVEST TECHNIQUES

Cut-and-Come-Again (Lettuce, Spinach, Herbs):
├─ Cut outer leaves, leave center
├─ Plant continues producing
└─ Harvest regularly for tenderness

Pull Entire Plant (Radish, Carrots):
├─ Harvest when ready
├─ Replace with new planting
└─ Succession for continuous supply

Continuous Harvest (Tomatoes, Peppers, Beans):
├─ Pick ripe fruits regularly
├─ Encourages more production
└─ Don't let overripen on plant
```
