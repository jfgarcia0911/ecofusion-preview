### 6.1 Raised Bed Completion

#### Filling with Soil Mix

**Preparation:**
- Have soil delivered or bags ready
- Wheelbarrow and shovel on hand
- Rake for spreading
- Water source nearby

**Filling Process:**

```
LAYERED FILLING METHOD

Step 1: Base Layer (4-6")
├─ Coarse material (optional)
├─ Sticks, leaves, grass clippings
└─ Improves drainage, adds organic matter

Step 2: Main Fill (remaining depth)
├─ Your soil mix recipe
├─ Fill in layers, not all at once
└─ Water each layer to settle

Step 3: Top Layer (2-3")
├─ Finest, richest compost
├─ Where seeds will germinate
└─ Level and smooth

     ┌─────────────────────┐
     │  Rich compost      │ ← 2-3"
     ├─────────────────────┤
     │  Main soil mix     │ ← 8-10"
     ├─────────────────────┤
     │  Coarse organic    │ ← 4-6" (optional)
     └─────────────────────┘
```

**Mixing On-Site:**

If mixing your own blend:
1. **Spread ingredients in layers** on tarp or in bed
2. **Mix with shovel** - turn like making a salad
3. **Add water as you mix** - slightly moist throughout
4. **Fill bed** with mixed soil
5. **Settle by watering** - will sink 2-3 inches

**How Much Soil:**
- Calculate volume: Length × Width × Depth (in feet) = cubic feet
- Convert to cubic yards: cubic feet ÷ 27
- Or count bags: cubic feet ÷ 2 (typical bag size)

**Settling:**
- Soil will settle 10-20% after watering
- Top off after a few days
- Expect to add more over first season
- This is normal!

### 6.2 Hydroponic System Completion

#### Preparing Nutrient Solution

**Water Quality First:**
- **Use clean water** (tap is usually fine)
- **If chlorinated:** Let sit 24 hours or use dechlorinator
- **If hard water:** May need RO filter or adjustments
- **Temperature:** Room temperature (65-75°F ideal)

**Mixing Nutrients:**

```
NUTRIENT SOLUTION MIXING

For 5-gallon bucket (4.5 gallons water):

1. Fill container with clean water
   Leave 0.5 gallon air gap (critical!)

2. Add Part A nutrient
   └─ 9 ml (2 ml per gallon × 4.5 gallons)

3. Stir thoroughly

4. Add Part B nutrient
   └─ 9 ml (2 ml per gallon × 4.5 gallons)

5. Stir again

6. Check pH (should be 5.5-6.5)
   Adjust if needed with pH up/down

7. Check EC/PPM if you have meter
   Lettuce: 800-1200 PPM
```

**IMPORTANT:**
- Never mix Part A and Part B directly together (concentrated)
- Always add to water separately
- Different plants have different nutrient needs
- Start with manufacturer recommendations

**pH Adjustment:**

```
pH SCALE FOR HYDROPONICS

Too Acidic        Ideal         Too Alkaline
    │              │                 │
    ▼              ▼                 ▼
  5.0───────5.5──6.0──6.5───────7.0

For most vegetables: 5.5-6.5
For lettuce: 6.0-6.5

Adjust:
├─ Too low (acidic): Add pH Up
└─ Too high (alkaline): Add pH Down

Add in small amounts, wait, re-test
```

#### Planting in Kratky System

**Step 1: Prepare Plants**
- Seedlings should have 2-4 true leaves
- Gently remove from soil or rockwool cube
- Rinse roots if soiled
- Inspect for pests/disease

**Step 2: Place in Net Pots**

```
NET POT ASSEMBLY

      Net pot
        ┌┐
        ││  ← Seedling stem
    ┌───┘└───┐
    │ ◘ ◘ ◘ │  ← Clay pebbles
    │ ◘ ◘ ◘ │     filling gaps
    │◘◘◘◘◘◘◘│
    └───┬─┬──┘
        │ │
      Roots grow down

1. Place seedling in center of pot
2. Hold straight while adding clay pebbles
3. Fill around roots, supporting plant
4. Roots should reach through bottom
```

**Step 3: Install in Lid**
- Place net pots in pre-cut holes
- Should sit securely, not fall through
- Roots should be able to reach water
- Lid should fit snugly (limit light entry)

**Step 4: Monitor Air Gap**

```
KRATKY AIR GAP (Critical!)

    Lid with net pot
    ┌────┬─┐
    │    │🌱│
    └────┴─┤
           │
    AIR ─► │  ← 1-2" gap (grows as plant drinks)
           │
    ░░░░░░░│  ← Nutrient solution
    ░░░░░░░│
    └───────┘

As plants grow, roots grow longer
As plants drink, water level drops
Roots in water, roots in air = perfect
Never top off water (breaks the system)
```

### 6.3 Greenhouse Final Assembly

#### Frame Completion

**Joining Sections:**
- Follow manufacturer instructions exactly
- Check square at every connection
- Hand-tighten first, check alignment
- Final tighten only when square and level
- Use helper for large panels

**Anchoring:**

```
GREENHOUSE ANCHORING

Wind can lift/damage greenhouse
Must be secured to ground

Methods:
├─ Ground stakes (temporary)
├─ Auger anchors (semi-permanent)
├─ Concrete footers (permanent)
└─ Attached to deck/foundation

Minimum: 4 anchors (one per corner)
Better: 6-8 anchors
Best: Continuous foundation
```

#### Covering Installation

**Plastic Film:**
1. Choose warm, calm day (film is pliable)
2. Unroll along one side
3. Attach one end securely
4. Pull taught (not tight) over frame
5. Attach opposite end
6. Work around, eliminating wrinkles
7. Use wiggle wire, clips, or battens

**Polycarbonate Panels:**
1. Handle carefully (can crack)
2. Orient correctly (UV coating out)
3. Pre-drill all holes (prevent cracking)
4. Use gaskets/washers (allow expansion)
5. Don't over-tighten (allow movement)
6. Seal joints with appropriate caulk

#### Ventilation Setup

```
VENTILATION REQUIREMENTS

Minimum:
├─ Roof vent (heat escape)
└─ Door (air intake)

Better:
├─ Roof vent(s)
├─ Wall vents opposite side
└─ Door

Best:
├─ Automated roof vents (temperature-controlled)
├─ Circulation fans
├─ Shade cloth for summer
└─ Proper sizing (20-30% of floor area)
```

**Vent Installation:**
- Follow manufacturer specs
- Ensure weathertight seal
- Test operation before covering
- Consider automatic openers (temperature-activated)

### 6.4 Quality Assurance Checklist

#### Structural Integrity

```
QUALITY CHECK - STRUCTURE

Raised Beds:
□ All corners are square (90°)
□ Frame is level
□ No wobbling or rocking
□ Screws are secure and countersunk
□ No large gaps in joints
□ Corner stakes are driven deep
□ Beds are stable when pressed

Hydroponics:
□ Containers are light-tight (no leaks)
□ Lids fit securely
□ Net pots sit firmly in holes
□ No sharp edges (safe to handle)
□ System is on stable surface
□ Easy access for monitoring

Greenhouse:
□ Frame is square and plumb
□ All connections are tight
□ Structure is firmly anchored
□ Covering is secure and taut
□ Vents operate smoothly
□ Door opens/closes properly
□ No gaps in weather sealing
```

#### Functionality Check

**Raised Beds:**
- Walk around: Any shifting?
- Push corners: Firm and stable?
- Check drainage: Water doesn't pool
- Verify access: Easy to reach center

**Hydroponics:**
- pH reading: 5.5-6.5?
- Solution level: Adequate air gap?
- Plants stable: Upright and supported?
- Light-tight: No algae growth potential?

**Greenhouse:**
- Open/close vents: Smooth operation?
- Check seals: Weather-tight?
- Walk inside: Adequate headroom?
- Foundation: Stable and level?

### 6.5 Final Preparations for Planting

#### Pre-Planting Tasks

**Soil Systems (Raised Beds):**

```
PRE-PLANTING CHECKLIST

□ Soil has been watered and settled
□ Surface is level and smooth
□ Added compost/amendments fully mixed
□ Paths around beds are clear
□ Water source tested and working
□ Tools gathered (trowel, dibber, markers)
□ Seeds/transplants selected
□ Planting plan sketched
□ Plant markers/labels ready
□ Calendar marked for care schedule
```

**Hydroponic Systems:**

```
PRE-PLANTING CHECKLIST

□ Nutrient solution mixed and pH-adjusted
□ Plants are ready (2-4 true leaves)
□ Growing medium is clean
□ Backup nutrients on hand
□ pH testing routine established
□ Light source adequate (if indoors)
□ Temperature appropriate (65-75°F)
□ Record-keeping system set up
□ Reference charts printed/accessible
```

### 6.6 Troubleshooting Final Issues

| Issue | Diagnosis | Fix |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Soil too compact** | Hard to dig, poor drainage | Add perlite, remix, fluff up |
| **Soil too loose** | Won't hold water | Add compost, coconut coir |
| **Bed wobbly** | Stakes not deep enough | Drive stakes deeper, add bracing |
| **Hydro pH won't stabilize** | Water quality issue | Use filtered water, adjust in small increments |
| **Net pots falling through** | Holes too large | Use larger pots or add support ring |
| **Greenhouse gaps** | Poor installation | Add weather stripping, re-secure panels |
