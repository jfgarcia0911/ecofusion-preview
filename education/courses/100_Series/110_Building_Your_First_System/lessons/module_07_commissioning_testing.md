## Lesson Content

### 7.1 Pre-Commissioning Checklist

#### System-Specific Readiness

```
RAISED BED READINESS

□ Soil filled to within 2" of top
□ Soil has been watered and settled
□ No pooling water (good drainage)
□ Paths around bed are complete
□ Water access tested and working
□ Soil temperature appropriate (>50°F for most crops)
□ Any amendments mixed in
□ Surface is smooth and level
□ Markers/stakes ready for planting
```

```
HYDROPONIC READINESS

□ Nutrient solution mixed correctly
□ pH tested and adjusted (5.5-6.5)
□ EC/PPM measured (appropriate for crops)
□ Water level marked for monitoring
□ Air gap verified (0.5-1" minimum)
□ Containers are light-tight
□ Growing area has adequate light
□ Temperature appropriate (65-75°F)
□ Seedlings ready at correct stage
□ Backup nutrients available
```

```
GREENHOUSE READINESS

□ Structure is secure and weathertight
□ Vents operate smoothly
□ Door closes/latches properly
□ Interior is clean and organized
□ Thermometer installed
□ Watering system functional
□ Growing beds/benches ready
□ Adequate ventilation verified
□ Shade cloth ready (if needed)
□ Heating/cooling plan in place
```

### 7.2 Testing Protocols

#### Water Management Testing

**Raised Bed Drainage Test:**

```
DRAINAGE TEST PROCEDURE

1. Water bed thoroughly until saturated
2. Wait 30 minutes
3. Dig down 6" with trowel
4. Observe moisture:

Ideal: Moist but not soggy, crumbles in hand
Too wet: Water pools, soil is muddy
Too dry: Dust dry (need more water retention)

Solution if too wet:
├─ Add perlite or vermiculite
├─ Ensure drainage holes clear
└─ Check if bed is sitting in depression

Solution if too dry:
├─ Add compost or peat moss
├─ Add mulch layer on surface
└─ Check irrigation coverage
```

**Hydroponic System Checks:**

| Test | Procedure | Target | Action if Off |
|------|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **pH** | Test with kit/meter | 5.5-6.5 | Adjust with pH up/down |
| **EC/PPM** | Test with EC meter | 800-1400 PPM (lettuce) | Add water (dilute) or nutrients |
| **Solution temp** | Thermometer | 65-75°F | Move location or add cooling/heating |
| **Light-tight** | Dark room, look for leaks | No light visible | Tape gaps, paint white/black |
| **Air gap** | Measure with ruler | 0.5-2" | Adjust initial fill level |

**pH Stability Test:**
1. Mix solution to target pH
2. Test immediately
3. Test again after 24 hours
4. Test again after 48 hours

**Result:**
- Stable (< 0.3 pH change) = Good water quality
- Unstable (> 0.5 pH change) = May need better water or buffering

#### Environmental Monitoring

**Greenhouse Climate Test:**

```
TEMPERATURE MONITORING (24-hour test)

Install min/max thermometer
Record readings every 4 hours for 24 hours

Time    | Temp  | Notes
--------|-------|------------------
8 AM    | ___°F |
12 PM   | ___°F |
4 PM    | ___°F |
8 PM    | ___°F |
12 AM   | ___°F |
4 AM    | ___°F |
8 AM    | ___°F | (24 hr mark)

Ideal range for most crops: 60-80°F
Too hot (>85°F): Need more ventilation/shade
Too cold (<50°F): Need heating or wait for warmer weather
Large swings (>30°F): Need thermal mass or heating/cooling
```

**Ventilation Test:**
1. Close all vents
2. Note temperature
3. Open vents fully
4. Measure time to drop 10°F

**Result:**
- < 15 minutes = Excellent ventilation
- 15-30 minutes = Good ventilation
- > 30 minutes = Need more/larger vents

### 7.3 Establishing Monitoring Routines

#### Daily Checks

```
DAILY MONITORING ROUTINE (5-10 minutes)

Raised Beds:
□ Visual inspection (pests, disease, wilting)
□ Soil moisture check (finger test)
□ Weather observation
□ Note any changes

Hydroponics:
□ Water level check
□ pH test (first 2 weeks daily, then weekly)
□ Plant health observation
□ Root inspection (through clear cup method)
□ Note solution clarity

Greenhouse:
□ Temperature min/max review
□ Ventilation adjustment
□ Plant health observation
□ Watering needs check
```

#### Weekly Checks

| System | Weekly Tasks |
|--------|--------------|
| **Raised Beds** | Weed removal, deep watering check, pest inspection, plant support adjustment |
| **Hydroponics** | EC/PPM test, full pH test, clean any algae, inspect roots for health |
| **Greenhouse** | Clean glazing, check seals, lubricate vents, inspect for pests |

#### Monthly Checks

- **All Systems:** Review records, assess growth rate, plan succession plantings
- **Raised Beds:** Add compost/mulch as needed
- **Hydroponics:** Change solution completely (some systems)
- **Greenhouse:** Deep clean, check structure integrity

### 7.4 Record-Keeping Systems

#### Why Keep Records?

**Benefits:**
- Track what works (and what doesn't)
- Troubleshoot problems
- Plan future seasons
- Optimize yields
- Satisfy curiosity
- Document learning journey

#### What to Record

**Essential Data:**

```
BASIC GROWING LOG

Date | Observation | Action Taken | Results
-----|-------------|--------------|--------
5/1  | Planted seeds | Lettuce x12 | Germinated 5/7
5/3  | Dry soil | Watered 2 gal | Moist next day
5/8  | Aphids on tomato | Sprayed neem oil | Reduced by 5/10
5/15 | Lettuce ready | Harvested 6 heads | Delicious!
```

**Hydroponic Log:**

| Date | pH | EC/PPM | Water Level | Notes |
|------|----|----|-------------|-------|
| 5/1 | 6.2 | 1100 | Full | Initial fill |
| 5/2 | 6.4 | 1080 | -0.5" | Slight drift |
| 5/3 | 6.8 | 1050 | -1.0" | Adjusted pH down |
| 5/4 | 6.3 | 1040 | -1.5" | Stable |

**Greenhouse Climate Log:**

| Date | Min Temp | Max Temp | Vent Status | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 5/1 | 58°F | 82°F | Auto | Good range |
| 5/2 | 62°F | 91°F | Auto + manual | Too hot, added shade |

#### Record-Keeping Methods

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Paper notebook** | Tactile, always available | Can get lost, hard to search |
| **Spreadsheet** | Organized, searchable, graphs | Requires computer |
| **Garden app** | Convenient, reminders, photos | Dependent on phone/internet |
| **Photo diary** | Visual record, easy to capture | Large file sizes, hard to search |
| **Combination** | Best of all methods | More effort |

**Recommended:** Simple notebook + phone photos = Easy and effective

### 7.5 Troubleshooting Common Issues

#### Soil-Based Systems

```
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE - SOIL

Problem: Water pools on surface
Causes:
├─ Compacted soil
├─ High clay content
└─ Drainage blocked

Solutions:
├─ Aerate with fork
├─ Add perlite/vermiculite
└─ Check/clear drainage holes

Problem: Soil dries out too fast
Causes:
├─ Too sandy
├─ Not enough organic matter
└─ Excessive heat/wind

Solutions:
├─ Add compost
├─ Add mulch layer
└─ Water more frequently or install drip irrigation

Problem: Soil compacts/hardens
Causes:
├─ Low organic matter
├─ Overworking when wet
└─ Poor soil mix

Solutions:
├─ Add compost regularly
├─ Never work wet soil
└─ Top-dress with compost
```

#### Hydroponic Systems

```
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE - HYDROPONICS

Problem: pH constantly drifts up
Causes:
├─ Plants taking up nutrients
├─ Water quality (high alkalinity)
└─ Algae growth

Solutions:
├─ Normal - adjust daily at first
├─ Use filtered/RO water
└─ Ensure light-tight container

Problem: Roots are brown/slimy
Causes:
├─ Root rot (too warm)
├─ Low oxygen
└─ Disease

Solutions:
├─ Cool solution to 65-70°F
├─ Increase air gap
├─ Use beneficial bacteria (hydroguard)
└─ Change solution, clean system

Problem: Algae in solution
Causes:
└─ Light exposure

Solutions:
├─ Ensure containers are opaque
├─ Cover gaps with tape
└─ Paint containers white (outside) for reflection
```

#### Greenhouse Systems

```
TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE - GREENHOUSE

Problem: Too hot during day
Solutions:
├─ Increase ventilation
├─ Add shade cloth (30-50%)
├─ Paint with shade compound
├─ Install circulation fans
└─ Wet down floor for evaporative cooling

Problem: Too cold at night
Solutions:
├─ Add thermal mass (water barrels)
├─ Use row covers inside greenhouse
├─ Seal gaps and cracks
├─ Install heater (as last resort)
└─ Delay planting until warmer

Problem: Excessive humidity/condensation
Solutions:
├─ Increase ventilation
├─ Reduce watering frequency
├─ Water in morning (not evening)
└─ Install circulation fans
```

### 7.6 System Optimization

#### Making Improvements

**Quick Wins:**

| Improvement | Benefit | Cost | Time |
|-------------|---------|------|------|
| **Add mulch** | Water retention, weed control | $ | 1 hour |
| **Install drip irrigation** | Consistency, time savings | $$ | 2-3 hours |
| **Add labels** | Organization, tracking | $ | 30 min |
| **Setup rain gauge** | Monitor water inputs | $ | 15 min |
| **Add thermometer** | Monitor temperature | $ | 5 min |

**Longer-Term Enhancements:**
- Automatic watering system
- Companion planting design
- Succession planting calendar
- Integrated pest management
- Season extension tools (row covers, cold frames)

### 7.7 Preparing for First Planting

#### Crop Selection for Testing

**Best "First Crops" (Forgiving & Fast):**

| Crop | Why Good for First Time | Days to Harvest |
|------|------------------------|-----------------|
| **Lettuce** | Fast, easy, productive | 30-45 |
| **Radishes** | Fastest of all, clear success/failure | 25-30 |
| **Herbs (basil, cilantro)** | Forgiving, high value | 40-60 |
| **Spinach** | Cool season, easy | 40-50 |
| **Green beans** | Productive, visible progress | 50-60 |

**Start Small:**
- Plant 25-50% of your system first
- Observe and learn
- Expand as confidence grows
- Don't fear failure - it's learning!

#### Pre-Planting Preparation

```
FINAL PRE-PLANTING CHECKLIST

System Verification:
□ All tests passed or issues resolved
□ Monitoring routine established
□ Record-keeping system ready
□ Tools and supplies gathered

Plant Preparation:
□ Seeds or transplants acquired
□ Planting calendar created
□ Plant spacing researched
□ Growing requirements reviewed
□ Succession plan drafted

Support Systems:
□ Watering plan confirmed
□ Pest monitoring protocol ready
□ Fertilization schedule (if needed)
□ Harvesting plan considered
□ Time commitment realistic
```

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Commissioning** | Testing and starting up a system before full use |
| **pH** | Measure of acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale |
| **EC/PPM** | Electrical conductivity / Parts per million - nutrient concentration |
| **Air Gap** | Space between solution and lid in Kratky systems |
| **Thermal Mass** | Material that stores heat (water, concrete, stone) |
| **Succession Planting** | Planting in intervals for continuous harvest |
| **Baseline** | Starting measurements for comparison |
