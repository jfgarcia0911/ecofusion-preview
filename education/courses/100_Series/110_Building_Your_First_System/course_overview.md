## Course Description

This hands-on foundational course guides learners through the complete process of planning, building, and commissioning their first growing system. From system selection to first harvest, students will gain practical experience with beginner-appropriate systems including Deep Water Culture (DWC), Kratky Method, and simple ebb & flow designs.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Select** an appropriate beginner system based on space, budget, and goals
2. **Plan** a complete growing system with proper scope and specifications
3. **Procure** materials and components for system construction
4. **Assemble** a functional growing system following safety guidelines
5. **Commission** and test a new system before planting
6. **Manage** a first crop through to harvest
