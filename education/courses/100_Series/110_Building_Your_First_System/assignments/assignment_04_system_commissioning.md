# Assignment 4: System Commissioning and Startup

## Course 110: Building Your First System

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Commission system and document startup procedures.

## Commissioning Steps

### 1. Pre-Startup Checks (4 pts)
- Leak test (24-hour water hold)
- Electrical safety verification
- Flow rate measurements
- Equipment function tests

### 2. Water Quality Setup (4 pts)
- Fill with appropriate water
- Dechlorinate if needed
- Mix initial nutrients
- Adjust pH to target range
- Test and record all parameters

### 3. Plant Installation (3 pts)
- Transplant seedlings properly
- Ensure root contact with water/media
- Stabilize plants
- Document varieties and dates

### 4. Operating Procedures (4 pts)
Write startup documentation:
- Daily monitoring checklist
- Weekly maintenance tasks
- Troubleshooting guide
- Emergency procedures

## Deliverable
Commissioning report with test results, photos, and operating manual
