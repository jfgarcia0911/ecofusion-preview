# Quiz 04: Construction Basics

**Course:** 110 - Building Your First System
**Module:** 04 - Construction Basics
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important safety consideration during construction?

a) Speed of completion
b) Wearing appropriate safety gear and working carefully
c) Aesthetics
d) Safety doesn't matter for small projects

### Question 2
Why should you assemble components in a logical sequence?

a) Sequence doesn't matter
b) Prevents having to disassemble completed work to install other parts
c) Random assembly is more creative
d) Follow any order you want

### Question 3
What should you do before making holes in tanks or containers?

a) Drill immediately
b) Measure carefully, mark locations, and double-check placement
c) Guess and drill
d) Holes aren't needed

### Question 4
Why is it important to level your system components?

a) Leveling is purely aesthetic
b) Ensures proper water flow and prevents structural stress
c) Nothing needs to be level
d) Only commercial systems need leveling

### Question 5
What is the purpose of test-fitting before permanent assembly?

a) Test-fitting wastes time
b) Verifies components fit correctly before gluing or fastening permanently
c) Just glue everything immediately
d) Test-fitting is for professionals only

### Question 6
How should you prepare PVC pipe before gluing?

a) Glue immediately from the package
b) Clean, deburr, dry, apply primer, then glue
c) Just use tape
d) PVC doesn't need preparation

### Question 7
What should you do if you make a mistake during construction?

a) Hide it and hope it works
b) Stop, assess the issue, and fix it properly before continuing
c) Mistakes can't be fixed
d) Keep building and ignore problems

### Question 8
Why is it helpful to take photos during construction?

a) Photos serve no purpose
b) Documents process for troubleshooting and helps remember assembly
c) Only for social media
d) Photos slow down construction

### Question 9
What tools are essential for basic system construction?

a) Only specialized expensive tools
b) Drill, saw, measuring tape, level, wrenches, and safety equipment
c) No tools needed
d) Borrow everything; don't own tools

### Question 10
When should you ask for help during construction?

a) Never ask for help
b) When lifting heavy items, unfamiliar with a task, or need second opinion
c) Only professionals can help
d) Asking for help shows weakness
