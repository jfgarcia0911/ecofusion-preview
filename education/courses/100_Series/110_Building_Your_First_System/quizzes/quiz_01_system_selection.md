# Quiz 01: System Selection

**Course:** 110 - Building Your First System
**Module:** 01 - System Selection
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important factor when choosing your first growing system?

a) Selecting the most expensive option
b) Matching system type to your goals, space, budget, and skill level
c) Copying a friend's system exactly
d) Choosing the largest system possible

### Question 2
Which system type is generally recommended for absolute beginners?

a) Large commercial aquaponics
b) Small-scale Deep Water Culture (DWC) or media bed system
c) Complex vertical farming setup
d) Multi-acre greenhouse operation

### Question 3
What should you consider when evaluating your available space?

a) Only floor area
b) Floor space, height, access to water/power, ventilation, and light
c) Space doesn't matter
d) Just buy a system and figure it out later

### Question 4
Why is it recommended to start small with your first system?

a) Small systems are inferior
b) Minimizes investment risk and allows learning before scaling
c) Small systems produce nothing
d) There's no reason; go big immediately

### Question 5
What is the main advantage of Deep Water Culture (DWC) for beginners?

a) Most complicated system
b) Simple design with fewer components and easier monitoring
c) Requires extensive technical knowledge
d) DWC is only for experts

### Question 6
When should you choose aquaponics over hydroponics for your first system?

a) Always choose aquaponics regardless
b) When you're interested in fish keeping and integrated systems
c) Never start with aquaponics
d) The choice doesn't matter

### Question 7
What role does climate play in system selection?

a) Climate is irrelevant
b) Affects indoor/outdoor feasibility and heating/cooling requirements
c) Only matters for outdoor farming
d) All systems work in any climate

### Question 8
Why should beginners avoid overly complex automation initially?

a) Automation is always bad
b) Complexity can overwhelm beginners; master basics first
c) Automation is too expensive for everyone
d) Manual systems are always better

### Question 9
What is an appropriate first-system budget for a beginner?

a) $10,000 minimum
b) $200-$1,000 depending on size and type
c) $100,000 for anything worthwhile
d) Budget doesn't matter

### Question 10
How can you test if a system type is right for you before committing?

a) Just buy it and hope
b) Research, visit operating systems, talk to growers, start very small
c) Testing is unnecessary
d) Only experts can evaluate systems

4. **b** - Minimizes investment risk and allows learning before scaling
5. **b** - Simple design with fewer components and easier monitoring
