# Quiz 08: First Planting

**Course:** 110 - Building Your First System
**Module:** 08 - First Planting
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What types of plants are best for beginners?

a) Rare exotic species
b) Fast-growing, forgiving crops like lettuce, herbs, and leafy greens
c) Only difficult plants to challenge yourself
d) Trees and shrubs

### Question 2
How should you prepare seedlings before transplanting to your system?

a) Plant them immediately in any condition
b) Rinse soil from roots gently and acclimate to system conditions
c) Preparation is unnecessary
d) Keep all soil on roots

### Question 3
What is the recommended plant spacing for lettuce?

a) Plants touching each other
b) 6-8 inches apart
c) 3 feet apart
d) Spacing doesn't matter

### Question 4
When is the best time to add plants to a new system?

a) Before cycling begins
b) After system is cycled and stable, or during cycling with hardy plants
c) Plants can go in anytime
d) Never add plants

### Question 5
What should you monitor closely after planting?

a) Nothing specific
b) Plant health, water parameters, nutrient levels, and growth rates
c) Just growth height
d) Monitoring is unnecessary

### Question 6
Why might you choose to start plants from seed?

a) Seeds are always harder
b) Cost-effective, wider variety, control over entire growth process
c) Seedlings are always better
d) Starting from seed is impossible in hydroponics

### Question 7
What indicates plants are getting too much light?

a) Healthy green growth
b) Bleached or scorched leaves, wilting despite adequate water
c) Too much light is impossible
d) Rapid, vigorous growth

### Question 8
How can you support climbing or vining plants?

a) Let them sprawl on ground
b) Install trellises, strings, or stakes for vertical growth
c) Climbing plants can't be grown in hydroponics
d) Support is unnecessary

### Question 9
What is succession planting?

a) Planting everything at once
b) Staggering planting times for continuous harvest
c) Only planting once per year
d) Succession planting is complicated and unnecessary

### Question 10
What should you do if plants show signs of stress after transplanting?

a) Remove them immediately
b) Adjust lighting, check water parameters, ensure proper acclimation
c) Ignore the stress
d) Stressed plants can't recover

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Fast-growing, forgiving crops like lettuce, herbs, and leafy greens
2. **b** - Rinse soil from roots gently and acclimate to system conditions
3. **b** - 6-8 inches apart
4. **b** - After system is cycled and stable, or during cycling with hardy plants
5. **b** - Plant health, water parameters, nutrient levels, and growth rates
6. **b** - Cost-effective, wider variety, control over entire growth process
7. **b** - Bleached or scorched leaves, wilting despite adequate water
8. **b** - Install trellises, strings, or stakes for vertical growth
9. **b** - Staggering planting times for continuous harvest
10. **b** - Adjust lighting, check water parameters, ensure proper acclimation
