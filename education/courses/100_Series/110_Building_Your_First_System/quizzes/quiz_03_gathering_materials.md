**Course:** 110 - Building Your First System
**Module:** 03 - Gathering Materials

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important consideration when sourcing materials?

a) Brand names only
b) Food-safe rating, quality, and cost-effectiveness
c) Cheapest price regardless of quality
d) Most expensive is always best

### Question 2
Where can you find affordable food-grade containers?

a) Only specialty aquaponics stores
b) Restaurant supply stores, agricultural suppliers, or repurposed food-grade totes
c) Containers can't be found affordably
d) Use any container regardless of previous contents

### Question 3
What does "food-grade" certification mean?

a) It's just a marketing term
b) Material is safe for contact with food and won't leach harmful chemicals
c) Food-grade is unnecessary for growing
d) All plastics are food-grade

### Question 4
Why should you avoid using containers that held toxic materials?

a) It's fine; just rinse them out
b) Residues can contaminate your system and harm plants or fish
c) Toxic containers work better
d) All containers are safe after cleaning

### Question 5
What is the advantage of buying materials in person vs. online?

a) Online is always better
b) Can inspect quality, ask questions, and avoid shipping costs
c) In-person shopping is obsolete
d) No advantage exists

### Question 6
When is it appropriate to use reclaimed or recycled materials?

a) Never use recycled materials
b) When materials are food-safe and structurally sound
c) Always use recycled regardless of safety
d) Only for decorative elements

### Question 7
What should you verify before purchasing a water pump?

a) Just buy the cheapest
b) Flow rate, head height capacity, energy use, and reliability reviews
c) Brand name only
d) All pumps are identical

### Question 8
Why is it helpful to buy slightly more materials than calculated?

a) To waste money
b) Accounts for mistakes, damage, or design adjustments during building
c) Always buy exactly what's calculated
d) Buy half of what you need

### Question 9
What is the benefit of purchasing from local suppliers?

a) No benefits exist
b) Supports local economy, enables returns, and provides expert advice
c) Local suppliers are always more expensive
d) Only online shopping makes sense

### Question 10
How can you save money on materials without compromising quality?

a) Buy the cheapest everything
b) Shop sales, buy in bulk, use food-safe repurposed items, compare prices
c) Money-saving always compromises quality
d) Never try to save money
