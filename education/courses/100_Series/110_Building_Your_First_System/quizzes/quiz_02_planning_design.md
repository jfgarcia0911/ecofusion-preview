# Quiz 02: Planning & Design

**Course:** 110 - Building Your First System
**Module:** 02 - Planning & Design
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the first step in planning your system?

a) Buying all the materials
b) Defining clear goals for what you want to grow and achieve
c) Starting construction immediately
d) Asking friends what to do

### Question 2
Why should you create a detailed design before building?

a) Designs are just for professionals
b) Identifies potential problems and ensures you have all needed materials
c) Designs waste time
d) Improvisation is always better

### Question 3
What should a system diagram include?

a) Just a rough sketch
b) Component placement, plumbing routes, electrical, and dimensions
c) Diagrams aren't necessary
d) Only artistic elements

### Question 4
How do you determine appropriate system size?

a) As big as possible
b) Based on available space, budget, time commitment, and goals
c) Copy standard sizes only
d) Size doesn't matter

### Question 5
What is the purpose of creating a materials list?

a) To have something to write
b) Ensures you purchase everything needed and stay within budget
c) Materials lists are optional
d) Buy materials randomly as you go

### Question 6
Why should you plan for system access and maintenance?

a) Access isn't important
b) Proper access prevents future frustration and enables routine care
c) Squeeze everything into minimal space
d) Deal with access issues later

### Question 7
What should you consider when planning water source location?

a) Water source location doesn't matter
b) Proximity to system, water quality, and ease of filling
c) Place system as far from water as possible
d) Only aesthetics

### Question 8
How detailed should your first system plan be?

a) No plan needed
b) Detailed enough to guide construction but flexible for adjustments
c) Plans must be absolutely perfect and unchangeable
d) Just a vague idea is sufficient

### Question 9
What is the benefit of researching similar system builds?

a) To copy exactly without thinking
b) Learn from others' successes and mistakes to inform your design
c) Research wastes time
d) Your system should be completely unique

### Question 10
When should you finalize your design?

a) Never finalize; change constantly
b) After research, planning, and before purchasing materials
c) After you've built everything
d) Designs should be finalized immediately
