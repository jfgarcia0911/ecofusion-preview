**Module:** 03 - Financial Planning
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three primary financial statements every business should maintain?

a) Budget, wish list, and expenses
b) Income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
c) Sales report, inventory list, and tax forms
d) Marketing plan, operations plan, and growth plan

### Question 2
What is the purpose of a startup budget?

a) To guess randomly at costs
b) To estimate initial investment needed and plan resource allocation
c) To impress the bank
d) Budgets are unnecessary for startups

### Question 3
What does ROI stand for?

a) Rate of Income
b) Return on Investment
c) Record of Invoices
d) Rapid Operational Increase

### Question 4
How do you calculate gross profit margin?

a) Revenue minus all expenses
b) (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) / Revenue x 100
c) Total sales divided by 12
d) Net income plus taxes

### Question 5
What is working capital?

a) The capital city where you work
b) Current assets minus current liabilities
c) Total revenue
d) Employee salaries

### Question 6
Why is financial forecasting important for new businesses?

a) It's not important
b) To project future revenue, expenses, and cash needs for planning
c) Only banks require it
d) To create complicated spreadsheets

### Question 7
What is the difference between profit and cash flow?

a) They are exactly the same
b) Profit is revenue minus expenses; cash flow is actual cash in and out
c) Cash flow is always higher
d) There is no difference

### Question 8
What is a reasonable profit margin target for agricultural businesses?

a) 200-300%
b) 10-30% depending on production method and market
c) 1-2%
d) Profit margins don't matter

### Question 9
What should you do if your financial projections show the business won't be profitable?

a) Ignore the data and proceed anyway
b) Revise your business model, pricing, or costs before launching
c) Give up immediately
d) Blame the spreadsheet

### Question 10
What is the purpose of tracking your burn rate?

a) To monitor fire safety
b) To measure how quickly you're spending cash reserves
c) To track employee productivity
d) Burn rate is irrelevant

5. **b** - Current assets minus current liabilities

7. **b** - Profit is revenue minus expenses; cash flow is actual cash in and out
