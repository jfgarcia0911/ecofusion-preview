# Quiz 08: Scaling & Growth

**Course:** 108 - Business Operations
**Module:** 08 - Scaling & Growth
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
What does "scaling a business" mean?

a) Weighing products
b) Growing the business while maintaining or improving efficiency and profitability
c) Making everything bigger regardless of profit
d) Scaling is the same as starting over

### Question 2
When is the right time to consider scaling your operation?

a) Immediately on day one
b) When current capacity is consistently meeting demand and systems are proven
c) When you're losing money
d) Never scale; stay small forever

### Question 3
What is organic growth?

a) Only growing organic crops
b) Growth funded by reinvesting profits rather than external capital
c) Fast expansion using loans
d) Growth that happens accidentally

### Question 4
Why is it important to have systems in place before scaling?

a) Systems aren't important
b) To ensure quality and efficiency are maintained as you grow
c) To impress investors
d) Systems can be developed after scaling

### Question 5
What is a potential risk of scaling too quickly?

a) Making too much profit
b) Cash flow problems, quality issues, and operational overwhelm
c) No risks exist
d) Customers complaining about success

### Question 6
How can automation support business growth?

a) Automation is too expensive
b) Reduces labor needs, increases consistency, and allows focus on strategy
c) Automation eliminates all jobs
d) Automation is only for factories

### Question 7
What role does delegation play in scaling?

a) Delegation is avoiding work
b) Enables owner to focus on growth while others handle operations
c) You should do everything yourself
d) Delegation is only for lazy people

### Question 8
Why might you need additional funding when scaling?

a) Funding is never needed
b) To invest in equipment, inventory, facilities, and working capital
c) Only to pay salaries
d) Funding is only for startups

### Question 9
What is a growth strategy?

a) Hoping for the best
b) A planned approach to expanding market reach and revenue
c) Copying successful businesses
d) Random experimentation

### Question 10
How should you measure the success of scaling efforts?

a) Only by revenue increase
b) Revenue growth, profit margins, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency
c) Number of employees only
d) Social media followers

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Growing the business while maintaining or improving efficiency and profitability
2. **b** - When current capacity is consistently meeting demand and systems are proven
3. **b** - Growth funded by reinvesting profits rather than external capital
4. **b** - To ensure quality and efficiency are maintained as you grow
5. **b** - Cash flow problems, quality issues, and operational overwhelm
6. **b** - Reduces labor needs, increases consistency, and allows focus on strategy
7. **b** - Enables owner to focus on growth while others handle operations
8. **b** - To invest in equipment, inventory, facilities, and working capital
9. **b** - A planned approach to expanding market reach and revenue
10. **b** - Revenue growth, profit margins, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency
