## Questions

### Question 1
What are the 4 P's of marketing?

a) People, Places, Plans, Profits
b) Product, Price, Place, Promotion
c) Planning, Producing, Pricing, Presenting
d) Potential, Performance, Persistence, Profit

### Question 2
What is a Unique Selling Proposition (USP)?

a) A special discount code
b) What makes your product different and better than competitors
c) Your most expensive product
d) A legal requirement

### Question 3

a) National television advertising
b) Farmers markets, local partnerships, and social media
c) Billboard advertising only
d) Radio ads in other states

### Question 4

a) To fill up your website
b) To provide valuable information that attracts and engages customers
c) To copy competitor content
d) Content marketing doesn't work

### Question 5

a) Copy competitor prices exactly
b) Consider costs, market rates, perceived value, and profit margins
c) Charge the absolute highest price possible
d) Price doesn't matter

### Question 6

a) A physical tool for sales
b) The journey from awareness to purchase with decreasing prospects at each stage
c) A filtering system for bad customers
d) An outdated concept

### Question 7
Why is customer retention important?

a) New customers are better
b) Retaining customers costs less than acquiring new ones and builds loyalty
c) Retention doesn't matter
d) Only for subscription businesses

### Question 8

a) No role; farmers don't need social media
b) Builds community, showcases products, and connects with customers
c) Only for large corporations
d) Social media is a waste of time

### Question 9
What should be included in a marketing budget?

a) Random guesses
b) Advertising, promotions, website, content creation, and events
c) Only paid advertising
d) Marketing should be free

### Question 10
How do you measure marketing effectiveness?

a) Gut feeling only
b) Track metrics like customer acquisition cost, conversion rates, and ROI
c) Don't measure; just spend money
d) Count social media likes only
