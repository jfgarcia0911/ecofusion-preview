# Quiz 04: Operations Management

**Course:** 108 - Business Operations
**Module:** 04 - Operations Management
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary goal of operations management?

a) To make work complicated
b) To efficiently produce and deliver products while maximizing quality
c) To hire as many people as possible
d) To avoid planning

### Question 2
What is a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)?

a) A suggestion for how work might be done
b) A documented step-by-step process for completing specific tasks consistently
c) An optional guideline
d) Something only large companies need

### Question 3
Why is inventory management important?

a) It's not important
b) To balance having enough supply while minimizing waste and storage costs
c) To fill up warehouse space
d) Only for retail businesses

### Question 4
What is the purpose of a production schedule?

a) To confuse employees
b) To plan when and how much to produce based on demand and resources
c) To work randomly
d) Schedules are unnecessary

### Question 5
What does "just-in-time" inventory mean?

a) Arriving to work on time
b) Ordering supplies to arrive exactly when needed to minimize storage
c) Rushing all orders
d) Emergency purchasing only

### Question 6
Why should you establish quality control procedures?

a) To create extra work
b) To ensure consistent product quality and customer satisfaction
c) To reject all products
d) Quality control is optional

### Question 7
What is the benefit of documenting your processes?

a) Creates paperwork
b) Enables consistency, training, and continuous improvement
c) Wastes time
d) Only required for certifications

### Question 8
How can technology improve operations management?

a) Technology always makes things worse
b) Automates tasks, improves tracking, and provides data for decisions
c) Technology is too expensive to consider
d) Only for tech companies

### Question 9
What should you do when you identify bottlenecks in your operation?

a) Ignore them
b) Analyze causes and implement solutions to improve flow
c) Work around them forever
d) Blame employees

### Question 10
Why is supplier relationship management important?

a) It's not important
b) Reliable suppliers ensure consistent quality, pricing, and delivery
c) To have people to complain to
d) Only for large businesses

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To efficiently produce and deliver products while maximizing quality
2. **b** - A documented step-by-step process for completing specific tasks consistently
3. **b** - To balance having enough supply while minimizing waste and storage costs
4. **b** - To plan when and how much to produce based on demand and resources
5. **b** - Ordering supplies to arrive exactly when needed to minimize storage
6. **b** - To ensure consistent product quality and customer satisfaction
7. **b** - Enables consistency, training, and continuous improvement
8. **b** - Automates tasks, improves tracking, and provides data for decisions
9. **b** - Analyze causes and implement solutions to improve flow
