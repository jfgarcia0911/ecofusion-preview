## Questions

### Question 1
Why is it important to understand regulations before starting a food business?

a) Regulations don't apply to small businesses
b) To ensure compliance, avoid penalties, and operate legally

b) Business license, health permits, zoning approval, and water use permits

### Question 9

b) Consult with local authorities, extension offices, or legal professionals

6. **b** - Good Agricultural Practices certification for food safety
7. **b** - Business license, health permits, zoning approval, and water use permits
