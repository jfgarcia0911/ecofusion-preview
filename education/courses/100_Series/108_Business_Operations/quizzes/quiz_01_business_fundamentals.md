# Quiz 01: Business Fundamentals

**Course:** 108 - Business Operations
**Module:** 01 - Business Fundamentals
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of a business plan?

a) To impress investors only
b) To serve as a roadmap for starting and operating your business
c) To satisfy legal requirements
d) To make the business look legitimate

### Question 2
Which business structure provides personal liability protection while avoiding double taxation?

a) Sole Proprietorship
b) General Partnership
c) Limited Liability Company (LLC)
d) No structure provides this

### Question 3
What is a mission statement?

a) A list of products you sell
b) A clear declaration of your business's purpose and core values
c) Your marketing slogan
d) A financial projection

### Question 4
Why is it important to identify your target market?

a) It's not important; sell to everyone
b) To focus marketing efforts and tailor products to customer needs
c) To exclude certain customers
d) Only for large businesses

### Question 5
What is the difference between fixed and variable costs?

a) There is no difference
b) Fixed costs stay constant; variable costs change with production level
c) Variable costs never change
d) Fixed costs are always higher

### Question 6
What should a break-even analysis tell you?

a) When to give up
b) The point at which total revenue equals total costs
c) Maximum possible profit
d) Competitor pricing

### Question 7
Which is an example of a competitive advantage?

a) Being located anywhere
b) Unique technology, superior quality, or specialized expertise
c) Following all competitors exactly
d) Having the highest prices

### Question 8
What is the purpose of conducting a SWOT analysis?

a) To create busy work
b) To identify Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats
c) To copy competitors
d) To avoid planning

### Question 9
Why is cash flow management critical for new businesses?

a) It's not important
b) Businesses can fail even with profits if they run out of cash
c) Only for large corporations
d) Banks require it

### Question 10
What should be included in a value proposition?

a) Just your price
b) Clear explanation of how you solve customer problems better than alternatives
c) Your business address
d) Your personal biography

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To serve as a roadmap for starting and operating your business
2. **c** - Limited Liability Company (LLC)
3. **b** - A clear declaration of your business's purpose and core values
4. **b** - To focus marketing efforts and tailor products to customer needs
5. **b** - Fixed costs stay constant; variable costs change with production level
6. **b** - The point at which total revenue equals total costs
7. **b** - Unique technology, superior quality, or specialized expertise
8. **b** - To identify Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats
9. **b** - Businesses can fail even with profits if they run out of cash
10. **b** - Clear explanation of how you solve customer problems better than alternatives
