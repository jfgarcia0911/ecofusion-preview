# Quiz 02: Market Analysis

**Course:** 108 - Business Operations
**Module:** 02 - Market Analysis
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of market research?

a) To waste time before starting
b) To understand customer needs, preferences, and market conditions
c) To copy competitors
d) To delay business launch

### Question 2
Which is the most reliable source of primary market research data?

a) Guessing
b) Direct surveys and interviews with potential customers
c) Reading old newspapers
d) Assumptions based on personal preferences

### Question 3
What does market segmentation involve?

a) Dividing the market into distinct groups with similar characteristics
b) Selling to everyone equally
c) Ignoring customer differences
d) Focusing only on age demographics

### Question 4
Why is it important to analyze your competition?

a) To copy everything they do
b) To understand market dynamics and identify differentiation opportunities
c) To spread negative information
d) Competition analysis is unnecessary

### Question 5
What is a buyer persona?

a) A fictional character for entertainment
b) A detailed profile of your ideal customer based on research
c) Any random person
d) Your best friend

### Question 6
Which factor is most important when determining market size?

a) Your personal opinion
b) Number of potential customers and their purchasing power
c) Competitor count only
d) Geographic area alone

### Question 7
What does TAM stand for in market analysis?

a) Total Annual Marketing
b) Total Addressable Market
c) Target Audience Metrics
d) Typical Average Margin

### Question 8
How can you identify unmet customer needs?

a) Ignore customer feedback
b) Conduct surveys, interviews, and analyze complaints about existing solutions
c) Assume you know everything
d) Copy competitor products exactly

### Question 9
What is the purpose of analyzing market trends?

a) To predict the future perfectly
b) To anticipate changes and position your business for future opportunities
c) To follow fads blindly
d) Trends don't matter

### Question 10
Why should you understand your customers' buying behavior?

a) To manipulate them
b) To align your sales and marketing strategies with how they make decisions
c) It's not important
d) Only for retail businesses

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To understand customer needs, preferences, and market conditions
2. **b** - Direct surveys and interviews with potential customers
3. **a** - Dividing the market into distinct groups with similar characteristics
4. **b** - To understand market dynamics and identify differentiation opportunities
5. **b** - A detailed profile of your ideal customer based on research
6. **b** - Number of potential customers and their purchasing power
7. **b** - Total Addressable Market
8. **b** - Conduct surveys, interviews, and analyze complaints about existing solutions
9. **b** - To anticipate changes and position your business for future opportunities
10. **b** - To align your sales and marketing strategies with how they make decisions
