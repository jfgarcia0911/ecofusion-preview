# Assignment 4: Business Plan Development

## Course 108: Business & Operations

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Create outline for comprehensive business plan.

## Required Sections

### 1. Executive Summary (2 pts)
- Business concept
- Goals and objectives
- Financial highlights
- Funding needs

### 2. Market Analysis (3 pts)
- Target market
- Competition
- Market trends
- Pricing strategy

### 3. Operations Plan (4 pts)
- Production methods
- Facility requirements
- Equipment needs
- Staffing plan

### 4. Financial Projections (4 pts)
- Startup costs
- Monthly operating budget
- Revenue projections
- 3-year profit/loss forecast

### 5. Marketing Strategy (2 pts)
- Branding
- Sales channels
- Customer acquisition
- Retention tactics

## Submission
Detailed outline, 4-5 pages with preliminary numbers
