# Assignment 5: Operational Plan Project

## Course 108: Business & Operations

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Develop complete operational plan for CEA business.

## Business Parameters
Choose scale:
- Micro (hobby to side income)
- Small (part-time commercial)
- Medium (full-time operation)

## Project Components

### 1. Production Planning (4 pts)
- Crop selection and rotation
- Production schedule
- Yield projections
- Quality standards

### 2. Standard Operating Procedures (4 pts)
- Daily/weekly/monthly tasks
- Quality control protocols
- Inventory management
- Record keeping systems

### 3. Financial Management (4 pts)
- Detailed startup budget
- Operating expense breakdown
- Revenue model
- Cash flow projections (12 months)
- Break-even analysis

### 4. Marketing & Sales (3 pts)
- Brand identity
- Sales channels and pricing
- Customer acquisition plan
- Growth strategy

## Deliverables
- Complete operational manual (8-10 pages)
- Financial spreadsheets
- SOP templates
- Implementation timeline

## Grading
Comprehensiveness, financial accuracy, practical feasibility, and professional presentation
