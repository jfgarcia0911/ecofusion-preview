# Assignment 1: Market Analysis Exercise

## Course 108: Business & Operations

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 10 | **Time:** 15-20 min

## Objective
Analyze local market opportunities for fresh produce sales.

## Analysis Components

**1. Customer Segmentation (3 pts)**
Identify 3 target customer types:
- Demographics
- Buying preferences
- Price sensitivity

**2. Competitor Analysis (4 pts)**
Research 3 competitors:
- Products offered
- Pricing
- Distribution channels
- Strengths/weaknesses

**3. Market Opportunity (3 pts)**
- Estimated market size
- Your potential market share
- Differentiation strategy
