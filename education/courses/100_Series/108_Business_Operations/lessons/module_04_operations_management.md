### 4.1 Production Planning

#### Succession Planting Strategy
```
CONTINUOUS HARVEST MODEL

Week 1: Plant 100 lettuce
Week 2: Plant 100 lettuce
Week 3: Plant 100 lettuce
Week 4: Plant 100 lettuce
Week 5: Plant 100 lettuce + Harvest Week 1
Week 6: Plant 100 lettuce + Harvest Week 2
...continues...

Result: Weekly harvests after initial 5-week ramp-up
Total capacity: 500 plants at various stages
```

### 4.2 Workflow Optimization
```
DAILY WORKFLOW EXAMPLE

6:00 AM - System Check
    • Check pumps, temperature, pH
    • Feed fish (if applicable)
    • Visual inspection

7:00 AM - Seeding/Transplanting
    • Plant new seedlings
    • Transplant mature seedlings
    • Update planting log

9:00 AM - Harvesting
    • Harvest mature crops
    • Quality inspection
    • Weigh and record

11:00 AM - Processing/Packaging
    • Wash and trim
    • Package for sale
    • Label with dates

1:00 PM - Sales/Delivery
    • Farmers market OR
    • Restaurant deliveries OR
    • Customer pickups

3:00 PM - Maintenance
    • Clean tools and workspace
    • System maintenance
    • Plant care (pruning, etc.)

5:00 PM - Admin/Planning
    • Update records
    • Plan next day
    • Customer communications
```

### 4.3 Quality Control
```
QUALITY STANDARDS CHECKLIST

HARVEST CRITERIA
□ Proper size/maturity
□ No pest damage
□ Clean (no media/debris)
□ Fresh appearance
□ Within temperature spec

PACKAGING STANDARDS
□ Correct weight
□ Proper labeling
□ Clean packaging
□ Harvest date marked
□ Storage temp maintained

REJECT REASONS
✗ Undersized
✗ Pest damage
✗ Disease present
✗ Off-color
✗ Past prime
```

### 4.4 Inventory Management
```
INVENTORY TRACKING SYSTEM

GROWING INVENTORY
    Track by:
        • Planting date
        • Variety
        • Location (bed #)
        • Quantity
        • Expected harvest date

SUPPLIES INVENTORY
    Minimum Stock Levels:
        • Seeds: 2-month supply
        • Nutrients: 1-month supply
        • Packaging: 2-week supply
        • Growing media: 1-month supply

FINISHED GOODS
    • Harvest daily
    • First-in, first-out (FIFO)
    • Maximum 3-day inventory
        (sell fresh!)
```

### 4.6 Standard Operating Procedures
```
SOP TEMPLATE: LETTUCE HARVESTING

PURPOSE: Ensure consistent harvest quality

FREQUENCY: Daily, morning

MATERIALS NEEDED:
    • Harvest knife/scissors
    • Collection bins
    • Scale
    • Harvest log

PROCEDURE:
1. Identify ready-to-harvest plants
   (size, maturity, planting date)

2. Cut at base, leaving roots

3. Remove any damaged outer leaves

4. Place in clean collection bin

5. Weigh and record in log

6. Refrigerate immediately (34-38°F)

QUALITY CHECKS:
    • Size: 6-8" diameter minimum
    • Color: Deep green
    • Texture: Crisp leaves
    • No pest damage

SAFETY:
    • Sharp knife handling
    • Lift properly (bins)
    • Wash hands before/after
```
