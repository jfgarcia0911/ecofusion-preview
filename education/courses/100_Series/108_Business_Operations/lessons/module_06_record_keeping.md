### 6.3 Accounting Software
```
SOFTWARE OPTIONS

QUICKBOOKS ONLINE ($30-60/month)
    ✓ Industry standard
    ✓ Full features
    ✓ Bank integration
    ✓ Inventory tracking
    ✓ Tax reports
    Best for: Growing businesses

WAVE (FREE)
    ✓ Free accounting
    ✓ Invoicing
    ✓ Receipt scanning
    ⚠ Limited inventory features
    Best for: Startups, side businesses

FARMLOG/AGWORLD (Farm-specific)
    ✓ Production tracking
    ✓ Compliance records
    ⚠ Limited accounting
    Best for: Supplement to accounting software

SPREADSHEETS (FREE)
    ✓ Flexible
    ✓ Low learning curve
    ⚠ Manual entry
    ⚠ No automation
    Best for: Very small operations

RECOMMENDATION: QuickBooks or Wave + Farm-specific app
```

### 6.4 Inventory Management
```
INVENTORY TRACKING SYSTEM

GROWING INVENTORY (Work in Progress)
    Track value at cost:
        100 lettuce plants
        × $1.50 cost per head
        = $150 inventory value

    Update weekly as plants grow

FINISHED GOODS
    Harvested products ready for sale
        95 lettuce heads ready
        × $1.50 cost
        = $142.50 inventory value

    FIFO: First In, First Out
    (Sell oldest first)

SUPPLIES
    Track quantities:
        • Seeds
        • Nutrients
        • Packaging materials
        • Growing media

    Reorder when reaching minimum levels
```

### 6.6 Food Safety Records
```
GOOD AGRICULTURAL PRACTICES (GAP)

REQUIRED RECORDS
    □ Water testing results
    □ Input tracking (seeds, nutrients)
    □ Harvest dates and handlers
    □ Temperature logs
    □ Cleaning/sanitation schedules
    □ Employee training
    □ Corrective actions

TRACEABILITY
    Lot tracking: YYMMDD-Product-Bed
        Example: 250220-LET-B1
        (Feb 20, 2025, Lettuce, Bed 1)

    Track from:
        Seed → Planting → Harvest → Sale

    Customer recall ability:
        Invoice → Lot # → Exact plants

TEMPERATURE LOG

Date | Time | Cooler Temp | Action
-----|------|-------------|--------
2/20 | 8am | 36°F | ✓
2/20 | 4pm | 38°F | ✓
2/21 | 8am | 42°F | Adjusted thermostat

Target: 34-38°F
```

### 6.7 Tax Preparation
```
TAX RECORD KEEPING

INCOME TRACKING
    • All sales receipts
    • Invoices
    • 1099s received
    • Grant income

EXPENSE TRACKING
    • All receipts (keep originals/digital)
    • Mileage log
    • Credit card statements
    • Bank statements

QUARTERLY ESTIMATED TAXES
    If net profit > $1,000:
        Pay estimated taxes quarterly
        Form 1040-ES

    Calculate: Net profit × 15.3% (self-employment)
              + Income tax rate

YEAR-END
    • Profit & Loss Statement
    • Balance Sheet
    • Depreciation schedule
    • File Schedule F (Farm Income)
    • File Schedule SE (Self-employment tax)

WORK WITH ACCOUNTANT
    Find farm-familiar CPA
    Cost: $500-2,000/year
    Worth it for:
        • Tax strategy
        • Deduction optimization
        • Audit protection
```
