### 3.2 Revenue Projections

#### Production-Based Revenue Model

```
REVENUE CALCULATION: LETTUCE PRODUCTION

System Capacity:
    Growing area: 1,000 sq ft
    Plant density: 4 heads per sq ft
    Total capacity: 4,000 heads

Harvest Cycle:
    Crop time: 35 days
    Annual cycles: 365 ÷ 35 = 10.4 cycles

Annual Production:
    4,000 heads × 10.4 cycles = 41,600 heads/year
```

### 3.4 Profitability Analysis

#### Break-Even Analysis

```
BREAK-EVEN CALCULATION

Fixed Costs (annual): $85,200
Variable Costs per unit (lettuce): $0.80
Selling Price per unit: $4.00

Contribution Margin:
    $4.00 - $0.80 = $3.20 per head

Break-Even Volume:
    $85,200 ÷ $3.20 = 26,625 heads/year
    = 2,219 heads/month
    = 512 heads/week

With 37,440 heads/year capacity:
    You need to sell 71% of capacity to break even
    Margin of safety: 29%
```

### 3.5 Cash Flow Management

#### Monthly Cash Flow Projection

| Month | Revenue | Expenses | Net Cash | Cumulative |
|-------|---------|----------|----------|------------|
| **1** | $0 | $15,000 | -$15,000 | -$15,000 |
| **2** | $2,500 | $9,500 | -$7,000 | -$22,000 |
| **3** | $8,000 | $9,000 | -$1,000 | -$23,000 |
| **4** | $11,000 | $8,500 | $2,500 | -$20,500 |
| **5** | $12,500 | $8,300 | $4,200 | -$16,300 |
| **6** | $13,500 | $8,300 | $5,200 | -$11,100 |
| **7-12** | $14,000+ | $8,300 | $5,700+ | Positive by month 9 |

```
CASH FLOW MANAGEMENT TIPS

TIMING ISSUES
    ⚠ Revenue lags expenses (growing time)
    ⚠ Startup period: negative cash flow
    ⚠ Seasonal fluctuations
    ⚠ Payment terms (restaurants: 30 days)

SOLUTIONS
    ✓ Maintain cash reserve (3-6 months)
    ✓ CSA for upfront payment
    ✓ Farmers market for immediate cash
    ✓ Line of credit for smoothing
    ✓ Manage inventory (don't overproduce)
```

### 3.6 Key Financial Metrics

#### Performance Indicators

```
IMPORTANT METRICS TO TRACK

PROFITABILITY
    Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue
    Target: >60% for sustainable farms

    Net Margin = Net Profit ÷ Revenue
    Target: >20% for healthy operation

    ROI = Net Profit ÷ Total Investment
    Target: >20% annually

EFFICIENCY
    Revenue per Square Foot
    Target: $100-150/sq ft/year

    Labor Productivity
    Target: >$40/hour worked

SUSTAINABILITY
    Break-Even Percentage
    Target: <80% of capacity

    Cash Reserve
    Target: 3-6 months expenses
```

### 3.7 Financing Options

#### Sources of Capital

| Source | Amount | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|--------|--------|------|------|------|
| **Personal Savings** | Varies | 0% | No debt, full control | Risk personal funds |
| **Friends & Family** | $5k-50k | 0-5% | Flexible terms | Relationship risk |
| **Bank Loan** | $25k-500k+ | 6-10% | Larger amounts | Requires collateral, credit |
| **SBA Loan** | $50k-5M | 7-9% | Favorable terms | Application complexity |
| **Grants** | $5k-100k | 0% | Free money | Competitive, restrictions |
| **Crowdfunding** | $5k-100k | 5-10% | Marketing benefit | Significant effort |
| **Investors** | $50k-1M+ | 20-40% equity | Expertise, network | Give up ownership |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **COGS** | Cost of Goods Sold - direct costs of production |
| **EBITDA** | Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization |
| **Gross Margin** | Revenue minus COGS, divided by revenue |
| **Net Margin** | Net profit divided by revenue |
| **Break-Even** | Point where revenue equals total costs |
| **Cash Flow** | Movement of money in and out of business |
| **ROI** | Return on Investment - profitability measure |
| **Working Capital** | Funds needed for day-to-day operations |
