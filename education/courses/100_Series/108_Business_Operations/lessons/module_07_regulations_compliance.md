### 7.1 Business Licenses and Permits
```
ESSENTIAL REGISTRATIONS

FEDERAL
    □ EIN (Employer Identification Number)
        Apply: IRS.gov (free, immediate)

    □ USDA Registration (if selling across state lines)

STATE
    □ Business Entity Registration
        LLC, Corporation, etc.
        Cost: $100-500

    □ Sales Tax Permit
        Required for retail sales
        Apply: State revenue department

    □ Agricultural Registration
        Varies by state
        May include pesticide license (if applicable)

LOCAL
    □ Business License
        City/county requirement
        Cost: $50-500/year

    □ Zoning Approval
        Verify agricultural use allowed
        Get in writing!

    □ Health Department Permit
        For direct food sales
        May require inspection

    □ Cottage Food License
        If making value-added products
        Varies by state
```

### 7.2 Food Safety Regulations
```
FSMA (FOOD SAFETY MODERNIZATION ACT)

WHO IS COVERED?
    Farms with >$25,000 annual sales AND
    Selling through distributors/retail

EXEMPTIONS
    • Direct-to-consumer sales ONLY
    • Sales <$25,000/year
    • Organic certified (separate regs)

REQUIREMENTS IF COVERED
    □ Written food safety plan
    □ Hazard analysis
    □ Preventive controls
    □ Monitoring procedures
    □ Corrective actions
    □ Verification
    □ Record keeping

GOOD AGRICULTURAL PRACTICES (GAP)

Purpose: Minimize food safety risks

Core Elements:
    ✓ Water quality testing
    ✓ Soil amendments (if used)
    ✓ Worker hygiene
    ✓ Equipment sanitation
    ✓ Harvest procedures
    ✓ Post-harvest handling
    ✓ Traceability

Certification Process:
    1. Attend GAP training
    2. Implement practices
    3. Schedule USDA audit
    4. Pass inspection
    5. Annual renewal

Cost: $500-2,000 (audit + annual fee)
Benefit: Required by many wholesale buyers
```

### 7.3 Water Quality and Testing
```
WATER SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

TESTING FREQUENCY
    Production Water:
        • Test quarterly minimum
        • After flooding/contamination event
        • Before season start

    Irrigation Water:
        • Test annually minimum
        • More frequent if source questionable

WHAT TO TEST
    □ E. coli
    □ Total coliforms
    □ pH
    □ Heavy metals (if concern)

ACCEPTABLE LIMITS
    • E. coli: 0 CFU per 100ml (drinking water)
    • E. coli: <126 CFU per 100ml (irrigation)
    • Generic E. coli threshold varies by use

WATER TREATMENT
    If needed:
        • UV filtration
        • Chemical treatment
        • Chlorination
        • Filtration

DOCUMENTATION
    Keep all test results
    Record treatment activities
    Note corrective actions
```

### 7.4 Labeling Requirements
```
PRODUCT LABELING (Federal Requirements)

REQUIRED ELEMENTS
    1. Product name
        "Lettuce" or "Buttercrunch Lettuce"

    2. Net weight/count
        "1 head" or "8 oz"

    3. Business name and address
        "Green Roots Farm
         123 Farm Rd, City, State ZIP"

    4. Country of origin (if not USA)

OPTIONAL (But Recommended)
    • Lot/batch code (traceability)
    • Harvest date
    • Handling instructions
    • Contact information

PROHIBITED CLAIMS (Without Certification)
    ✗ "Organic" (requires USDA certification)
    ✗ "Non-GMO" (requires verification)
    ✗ "All Natural" (vague, avoid)

ALLOWED CLAIMS
    ✓ "Locally Grown"
    ✓ "Pesticide-Free" (if true)
    ✓ "Hydroponically Grown"
    ✓ "Farm Fresh"
    ✓ "Sustainably Grown"
```

### 7.5 Organic Certification
```
USDA ORGANIC CERTIFICATION

REQUIREMENTS
    ✓ 3-year transition period (no prohibited substances)
    ✓ Organic system plan
    ✓ Annual inspection
    ✓ Detailed record keeping
    ✓ Certification fee

PROHIBITED IN AQUAPONICS/HYDROPONICS
    ⚠ As of 2024, hydroponics CAN be organic
    ⚠ Aquaponics generally NOT organic (animal integration issues)
    ⚠ Rules evolving - check current USDA guidance

ALTERNATIVES
    • "Pesticide-Free"
    • "Chemical-Free"
    • "Sustainably Grown"
    • "Naturally Grown" (Certified Naturally Grown program)

COST
    Application: $200-2,000
    Annual: $750-2,500+
    Depends on sales volume

WORTH IT?
    Consider if:
        ✓ Wholesale/retail buyers require
        ✓ Price premium justifies cost
        ✓ Strong organic market

    Skip if:
        ✗ Direct-to-consumer only
        ✗ Can differentiate another way
        ✗ Cost too high for volume
```

### 7.6 Employment and Labor Laws
```
HIRING EMPLOYEES

FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS
    □ Form I-9 (verify work eligibility)
    □ Form W-4 (tax withholding)
    □ Payroll tax registration
    □ Workers compensation insurance
    □ OSHA safety compliance
    □ Minimum wage ($7.25 federal, varies by state)
    □ Overtime (>40 hrs/week)

STATE REQUIREMENTS
    □ State tax withholding
    □ Unemployment insurance
    □ State-specific labor laws
    □ New hire reporting

RECORD KEEPING
    • Hours worked
    • Wages paid
    • Tax withholdings
    • Keep 3-4 years

ALTERNATIVES TO EMPLOYEES
    • Independent contractors
        (Be careful - IRS scrutiny!)
    • Seasonal help
    • Interns (must follow DOL rules)
    • Family members

PAYROLL SOFTWARE
    • Gusto: $40/month + $6/employee
    • QuickBooks Payroll: $50/month
    • Square Payroll: $35/month

    Worth it to avoid compliance errors!
```

### 7.7 Zoning and Land Use
```
ZONING COMPLIANCE

CHECK BEFORE STARTING
    □ Is agricultural use allowed?
    □ Can you sell on-site?
    □ Are structures permitted?
    □ Setback requirements?
    □ Sign restrictions?
    □ Home occupation rules (if home-based)?

COMMON ZONING TYPES
    • Agricultural (A): ✓ Usually allowed
    • Residential (R): ⚠ May have restrictions
    • Commercial (C): ✓ Often allowed
    • Industrial (I): ⚠ Check local codes

GETTING APPROVAL
    1. Contact zoning department
    2. Explain operation
    3. Get variance if needed
    4. Obtain permits for structures
    5. Document everything

NEIGHBORS
    • Notify neighbors
    • Address concerns proactively
    • Good relationships prevent complaints
    • HOAs may have additional rules
```

### 7.8 Environmental Regulations
```
ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

WATER DISCHARGE
    • Most systems: No discharge (recirculating)
    • If discharging: May need permit
    • Storm water management
    • Prevent runoff contamination

WASTE MANAGEMENT
    • Plant waste: Compost on-site OK
    • Fish waste (aquaponics): Use in system OR compost
    • Nutrient solutions: Cannot discharge to water
    • Packaging: Recycle where possible

PESTICIDES (If Used)
    □ Applicator license required
    □ Record keeping mandatory
    □ Follow label exactly
    □ Restricted-use products need certification

    Best Practice: Avoid pesticides entirely!
        Use IPM, beneficial insects, barriers
```

### 7.9 Insurance Requirements
```
ESSENTIAL INSURANCE

GENERAL LIABILITY
    Protects: Bodily injury, property damage
    Coverage: $1-2 million
    Cost: $500-1,500/year
    Required: Most wholesale/retail contracts

PRODUCT LIABILITY
    Protects: Illness/injury from products
    Often: Included in general liability
    Critical: For food sales

PROPERTY INSURANCE
    Covers: Greenhouse, equipment
    Cost: Varies by value
    Consider: Replacement cost coverage

WORKERS COMPENSATION
    Required: If employees
    Covers: Work-related injuries
    Cost: % of payroll (varies by state)

CROP INSURANCE
    Available: Through USDA
    Covers: Loss from weather, disease
    Cost: Subsidized premiums
    Note: Limited for hydroponics/aquaponics

BUSINESS INTERRUPTION
    Covers: Lost income during disruption
    Optional: But valuable
    Example: Greenhouse damage, can't produce
```
