    Instagram: 4-7× per week

    Share customer photos (with permission)
    Use local hashtags #EatLocal #[YourCity]

### 5.4 Sales Strategies by Channel
```
FARMERS MARKET SALES

BOOTH SETUP
    ✓ Eye-catching display
    ✓ Clear pricing
    ✓ Samples (if allowed)
    ✓ Business cards
    ✓ Email signup sheet

SALES TECHNIQUES
    • Greet everyone
    • Tell your story (briefly)
    • Offer samples
    • Suggest uses
    • Ask for the sale
    • Collect emails

RESTAURANT SALES

APPROACH
    1. Research restaurants
    2. Call for chef's name
    3. Email sample request
    4. Follow up with visit
    5. Bring samples
    6. Discuss needs/pricing
    7. Trial order
    8. Regular relationship

VALUE PROPOSITION
    • Freshness (same-day delivery)
    • Consistency
    • Unique varieties
    • Reliable supply
    • Local story for menu
```

### 5.5 Customer Loyalty
```
RETENTION STRATEGIES

EMAIL LIST BUILDING
    Collect at every touchpoint:
    • Farmers market signup
    • Website purchases
    • Farm tours
    • Events

LOYALTY REWARDS
    • 10th purchase free
    • Referral discounts
    • Seasonal specials
    • VIP early access

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
    Weekly email:
        - What's available
        - Recipes
        - Farm updates
        - Special offers

RETENTION > ACQUISITION
    • 5× more expensive to acquire new customer
    • Repeat customers spend 67% more
    • Focus on retention!
```

### 5.6 Marketing Budget
```
ANNUAL MARKETING BUDGET

For $150k revenue operation:
    Marketing budget: $7,500 (5% of revenue)

ALLOCATION
    Website/hosting           $600
    Social media ads        $1,200
    Print materials          $400
    Farmers market fees    $1,500
    Photography/content      $800
    Email service            $300
    Events/sampling          $500
    Promotions/discounts   $1,200
    Miscellaneous         $1,000
    ────────────────────────────
    TOTAL                 $7,500

EARLY STAGE: Start with 10% of revenue
MATURE BUSINESS: 3-5% of revenue
```

### 5.7 Measuring Marketing ROI
```
TRACKING METRICS

AWARENESS
    • Social media followers
    • Website visitors
    • Email list size

ENGAGEMENT
    • Social media engagement rate
    • Email open rate (target: 20-30%)
    • Click-through rate (target: 2-5%)

CONVERSION
    • Farmers market sales/day
    • Website conversion rate
    • New customer acquisition cost

    Example:
        Spent $500 on ads
        Acquired 25 new customers
        Cost per acquisition: $20

        Average customer value: $200/year
        ROI: $200/$20 = 10:1 (excellent!)

RETENTION
    • Repeat purchase rate (target: >60%)
    • Customer lifetime value
    • Referral rate
```
