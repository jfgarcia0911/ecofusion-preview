## Course Description

Transform your growing skills into a successful business. This comprehensive course covers business planning, market analysis, financial management, regulatory compliance, sales and marketing, and operational excellence. Learn the business fundamentals needed to launch, operate, and scale a profitable controlled environment agriculture operation.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Develop** a comprehensive business plan for a CEA operation
2. **Analyze** market opportunities and competitive landscape
3. **Calculate** production costs and pricing strategies
4. **Navigate** regulatory requirements and food safety standards
5. **Create** sales and marketing strategies for multiple channels
6. **Implement** standard operating procedures (SOPs)
7. **Manage** inventory, labor, and operational workflows
8. **Track** key performance indicators (KPIs) for continuous improvement

## Key Topics

### Business Planning
- Vision, mission, and value proposition
- Business model canvas
- Startup capital requirements
- Funding options (loans, grants, investors)
- Legal structure selection
- Insurance requirements

### Market Analysis
- Target customer identification
- Competitor analysis
- Market sizing and share
- Pricing strategies
- Differentiation opportunities
- Local food system analysis

### Financial Management
- Cost of production calculations
- Break-even analysis
- Cash flow projections
- Profit margins by crop
- Capital expenditure planning
- Operating expense budgeting

### Regulatory & Food Safety
- FDA regulations
- State and local requirements
- GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)
- FSMA compliance
- Organic certification
- Traceability requirements

### Sales & Marketing
- Direct-to-consumer sales
- Farmer's markets and CSA
- Restaurant and chef sales
- Wholesale and distribution
- Retail partnerships
- Online sales and delivery

### Operations Management
- Production planning
- Quality control programs
- Inventory management
- Labor scheduling
- Equipment maintenance
- Record keeping systems

## Financial Reference Guide

### Startup Cost Ranges

| Operation Type | Space | Capital Cost |
|----------------|-------|--------------|
| Hobby/Micro | <200 sq ft | $1,000-5,000 |
| Small Commercial | 200-1,000 sq ft | $10,000-50,000 |
| Medium Commercial | 1,000-5,000 sq ft | $50,000-250,000 |
| Large Commercial | 5,000-20,000 sq ft | $250,000-1,000,000 |
| Industrial | >20,000 sq ft | $1,000,000+ |

### Operating Cost Breakdown (Typical)

| Category | % of Revenue |
|----------|--------------|
| Labor | 25-40% |
| Energy | 10-25% |
| Seeds/Inputs | 5-10% |
| Packaging | 3-8% |
| Marketing | 3-5% |
| Rent/Mortgage | 5-15% |
| Insurance | 2-4% |
| Other | 5-10% |

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Target Range |
|-----|--------------|
| Yield per sq ft (greens) | 0.2-0.5 lb/sqft/cycle |
| Cycles per year | 8-15 (greens) |
| Labor per lb produced | 0.1-0.3 hours |
| Energy cost per lb | $0.10-0.50 |
| Gross margin | 40-60% |
| Net profit margin | 10-25% |
