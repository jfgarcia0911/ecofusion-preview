## Sample Partial Project (Reference Only)

**Scenario:** Backyard Family System

**Executive Summary (excerpt):**
"This design presents a 300-gallon media bed aquaponic system for a family of four in Portland, Oregon. The system will produce approximately 25 pounds of tilapia and 200+ pounds of vegetables annually. Using a flood-and-drain media bed configuration, this beginner-friendly design incorporates a 150-gallon fish tank, two 75-gallon grow beds, and a simple bell siphon automation. Total investment is $2,450..."

**Fish Tank Calculation (excerpt):**
```
Target: 25 lbs fish/year
Species: Tilapia (Nile)
Harvest weight: 1.25 lbs
Fish needed: 25 ÷ 1.25 = 20 fish
Add 15% mortality: 20 × 1.15 = 23 fish to stock
Stocking density: 0.5 lb/gallon (conservative)
Tank size: 25 lbs ÷ 0.5 = 50 gallons minimum
Selected: 150 gallon stock tank (allows growth room)
```
