# Course 102: Aquaponics Fundamentals - Grading Rubric

## Course Assessment Overview

This rubric defines the evaluation criteria for all graded components in Course 102: Aquaponics Fundamentals. All assessments align with the course learning outcomes and are designed to measure both theoretical understanding and practical application skills.

---

## Assessment Components

### Total Points: 200

| Assessment Type | Quantity | Points Each | Total Points |
|----------------|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Module Quizzes | 11 | 5 | 55 |
| Lab Reports | 4 | 10 | 40 |
| Discussion Participation | 6 | 5 | 30 |
| In-Class Assignments | 3 | 10-15 | 40 |
| Take-Home Assignment | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| System Design Project | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| Final Exam | 1 | 25 | 25 |

---

## Grading Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Points | Description |
|-------|------------|--------|-------------|
| A | 90-100% | 180-200 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |

| C | 70-79% | 140-159 | Satisfactory - Meets most expectations |

## Module Quiz Rubric (5 points each)

Quizzes are auto-graded based on correctness. Each quiz contains 5 questions worth 1 point each.

- **5 points:** All questions correct
- **4 points:** 4 questions correct
- **3 points:** 3 questions correct
- **2 points:** 2 questions correct
- **1 point:** 1 question correct
- **0 points:** No questions correct

---

## Final Exam Rubric (25 points)

The final exam consists of:
- Multiple choice/True-False (10 points)
- Short answer questions (10 points)
- Applied problem-solving (5 points)

| Section | Criteria | Points |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **Multiple Choice** | Factual knowledge and concept understanding | 10 |
| **Short Answer** | Explanation and application of concepts | 10 |
| **Applied Problems** | System calculations and troubleshooting scenarios | 5 |

---

## Late Work Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% deduction
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% deduction
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: 50% deduction
- Assignments submitted more than 7 days late: Not accepted (0 points)

**Exceptions:** Extensions may be granted for documented emergencies or extenuating circumstances. Contact instructor before the deadline.

---

## Academic Integrity

All work must be original. Plagiarism or cheating will result in:
- First offense: Zero on the assignment
- Second offense: Failure of the course
- All offenses reported to academic administration

---

## Feedback Timeline

- Quizzes: Immediate automated feedback
- Assignments: Within 5 business days
- Projects: Within 7 business days
- Final Exam: Within 10 business days

---

## Revision Policy

Students may revise and resubmit one assignment during the course for re-grading (up to 80% of original points possible). Revision must be submitted within 7 days of receiving initial feedback.
