## Assessment Categories Explained

### 1. Module Quizzes (55 points total)

**Purpose:** Assess understanding of key concepts from each module

- **Quantity:** 11 quizzes
- **Points Each:** 5 points
- **Format:** Multiple choice, true/false, matching
- **Time Limit:** 15 minutes each
- **Attempts:** 2 attempts per quiz (highest score counts)
- **Open Resources:** No (closed-book)

**Topics Covered:**
1. What is Aquaponics?
2. The Nitrogen Cycle
3. System Components
4. Fish Selection
5. Plant Selection
6. System Design
7. Water Quality
8. System Startup
9. Daily Operations
10. Harvesting
11. Troubleshooting

---

### 2. Lab Reports (40 points total)

**Purpose:** Develop hands-on skills and scientific documentation abilities

- **Quantity:** 4 lab reports
- **Points Each:** 10 points
- **Format:** Written report with data, analysis, and conclusions
- **Length:** 2-3 pages each

**Lab Topics:**
1. **Water Quality Testing Lab** - Practice testing methods and interpretation
2. **System Diagram Lab** - Create detailed system schematics
3. **Cycling Simulation Lab** - Track nitrogen cycle establishment
4. **Plant Propagation Lab** - Document germination and growth

---

### 3. Discussion Participation (30 points total)

**Purpose:** Foster community learning and peer interaction

- **Quantity:** 6 discussions
- **Points Each:** 5 points
- **Requirements:**
  - Initial post: 150-200 words (3 points)
  - Response to 2 peers: 50+ words each (2 points)

**Discussion Topics:**
1. Personal aquaponics goals and interests
2. Nitrogen cycle challenges and solutions
3. Fish and plant species experiences
4. System design considerations
5. Water quality troubleshooting
6. Lessons learned and future applications

---

### 4. In-Class Assignments (40 points total)

**Purpose:** Apply concepts in guided, time-limited scenarios

#### Assignment 1: Nitrogen Cycle Diagram (10 points)
- **Time:** 15-20 minutes
- **Format:** In-class activity
- **Task:** Create accurate nitrogen cycle diagram with all key components

#### Assignment 2: Water Quality Testing (15 points)
- **Time:** 20-30 minutes
- **Format:** In-class lab
- **Task:** Perform water tests and interpret results

#### Assignment 3: System Component Selection (15 points)
- **Time:** 30-45 minutes
- **Format:** In-class worksheet
- **Task:** Select and size components for a given scenario

---

### 5. Take-Home Assignment (15 points)

**Purpose:** Develop comprehensive planning skills with adequate time for research

#### Assignment 4: Aquaponics System Proposal
- **Time:** 1-2 hours
- **Format:** Written proposal with diagrams
- **Length:** 3-4 pages plus appendices
- **Components:**
  - System type and configuration
  - Fish and plant species selection
  - Equipment list with specifications
  - Maintenance schedule
  - Budget estimate

---

### 6. Project Assignment (15 points)

**Purpose:** Demonstrate mastery through comprehensive system design

#### Assignment 5: Home Aquaponics System Design Project
- **Time:** 2-4 hours
- **Format:** Complete system design package
- **Deliverables:**
  - Detailed system drawings (plan and elevation views)
  - Complete bill of materials
  - Sizing calculations
  - Operation manual outline
  - Construction timeline

---

### 7. Final Exam (25 points)

**Purpose:** Comprehensive assessment of course knowledge and application

- **Format:** Mixed question types
- **Time Limit:** 90 minutes
- **Structure:**
  - Multiple Choice/True-False: 10 points (20 questions)
  - Short Answer: 10 points (5 questions)
  - Applied Problems: 5 points (2 scenarios)

**Content Coverage:**
- Nitrogen cycle and biological processes (20%)
- System components and functions (15%)
- Fish and plant selection (15%)
- Water quality management (20%)
- System design and sizing (15%)
- Operations and maintenance (10%)
- Troubleshooting (5%)

## Incomplete Grades

An Incomplete (I) grade may be assigned only when:
- Student has completed 75% or more of coursework
- Documented emergency prevents completion
- Student is currently passing (C or better)
- Written agreement with instructor on completion timeline

---

## Grade Appeal Process

If you believe a grade was assigned in error:

1. Review the grading rubric and your work
2. Contact instructor within 5 business days of grade posting
3. Provide specific rationale for appeal
4. Instructor will review and respond within 3 business days
5. If unresolved, may appeal to Academic Director

## Tips for Success

To achieve an A (90%+):
- Complete all quizzes on first attempt after thorough review
- Submit all assignments on time with careful attention to rubrics
- Engage meaningfully in discussions with insightful contributions
- Seek feedback and apply it to subsequent work

- Start projects early to allow time for refinement

To achieve a B (80%+):
- Stay current with all modules and quizzes
- Complete all assignments and labs
- Participate actively in discussions
- Review feedback and ask questions
- Allocate sufficient time for the final project
