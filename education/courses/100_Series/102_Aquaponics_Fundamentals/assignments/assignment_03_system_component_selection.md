**Question 2a:** Calculate required pump flow rate. (2 points)

### Step 6: Account for Head Height

Head height = vertical distance pump must push water

**Given:** Pump will be in fish tank, grow beds are 3 feet above water level.

**Question 2b:** Select an appropriate pump accounting for head loss. (2 points)

Most pumps lose about 10% flow rate per foot of head height.

**Calculation:**
```
Adjusted Flow = Rated Flow - (Rated Flow × 0.10 × Head Height)
```

If you need 300 GPH after 3 ft head loss, what rated GPH do you need?

## Tips for Success

### Calculation Tips:
1. **Show your work** - partial credit available for correct methodology
2. **Include units** - gallons, GPH, cubic feet, etc.
3. **Round sensibly** - to nearest whole number for most calculations
4. **Double-check math** - use calculator carefully

### Selection Tips:
1. **Think practically** - beginner-friendly, reliable, available
2. **Consider maintenance** - fewer complex parts = easier
3. **Balance cost vs. quality** - cheapest isn't always best
4. **Build in safety margin** - slightly oversize rather than undersize

### Budget Tips:
1. **Research real prices** - use online retailers for estimates
2. **Don't forget small items** - fittings, sealant, etc. add up
3. **Include contingency** - 10% buffer for unexpected costs
4. **Prioritize essentials** - can add upgrades later

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Forgetting to account for head loss on pump
- ❌ Undersizing grow bed volume (below 1:1 ratio)
- ❌ Oversizing to the point of exceeding budget
- ❌ Selecting components incompatible with each other
- ❌ Not including monitoring equipment in budget
- ❌ Forgetting plumbing and misc. items
- ❌ Not showing calculation steps
