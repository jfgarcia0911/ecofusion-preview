## Section 1: Executive Summary

### What to Include:

Write a compelling 1-2 paragraph summary that includes:
- **Purpose:** Why is this system being built?
- **Goals:** What do you hope to achieve? (education, food production, community engagement, etc.)
- **System Overview:** Brief description of type and size
- **Key Outcomes:** Expected production, learning objectives, or other benefits
- **Investment:** Total estimated startup cost

### Example Opening:
*"This proposal outlines a 300-gallon aquaponic system designed for Jefferson Elementary School's science education program. The system will enable 200 students annually to learn about sustainable food systems, the nitrogen cycle, and plant science through hands-on experience. The system will produce approximately 40 pounds of fresh lettuce and herbs monthly for the school cafeteria..."*

## Section 2: System Design

### Required Information:

#### System Type Selection
Choose and justify one of these configurations:
- **Media Bed System:** Best for beginners, grows variety of crops
- **Deep Water Culture (DWC):** Efficient for leafy greens
- **Nutrient Film Technique (NFT):** Commercial-style production
- **Hybrid System:** Combines multiple methods

**Your Justification:** Explain why this type is best for your chosen application (home, school, community, restaurant).

#### System Specifications

Provide detailed specifications:
- **Fish tank volume:** _____ gallons
- **Grow bed volume:** _____ gallons (and ratio to fish tank)
- **Total system water volume:** _____ gallons
- **Growing area:** _____ square feet
- **Footprint:** _____ ft × _____ ft
- **Location:** Indoor, greenhouse, outdoor (specify)

#### Component List

List all major components:
1. Fish tank (type, size, material)
2. Grow beds (quantity, dimensions, material)
3. Water pump (GPH rating)
4. Air pump (LPM rating)
5. Grow media (type, volume)
6. Plumbing (brief description)
7. Monitoring equipment
8. Optional: Heater, shade cloth, backup systems

#### System Diagram

Include a simple diagram showing:
- Fish tank location
- Grow bed layout
- Water flow direction (arrows)
- Major components labeled
- Scale or dimensions

**Note:** Hand-drawn diagrams are acceptable if neat and clear. Can also use software like PowerPoint, Google Drawings, or specialized tools.

## Section 3: Species Selection

### Fish Selection

Choose one fish species and provide:
- **Species name:** (Common and scientific)
- **Justification:** Why this species? Consider:
  - Temperature requirements and local climate
  - Legal status (some species restricted in certain areas)
  - Growth rate and harvest size
  - Feed availability and cost
  - Ease of care (especially for schools)
  - Edibility (if harvesting for food)

#### Recommended Fish Species by Application:

**Home/Commercial:** Tilapia (fast growing, good eating)
**Schools:** Goldfish or Koi (hardy, non-edible, long-lived)
**Community:** Catfish (hardy, local species)
**Warm Climate:** Tilapia, Jade Perch
**Cool Climate:** Trout, Perch

**Stocking Density:**
- Rule of thumb: 1 lb of fish per 5-10 gallons of fish tank
- Your stocking: _____ fish at _____ lb each = _____ total lbs
- Stocking density: _____ lbs per gallon

## Tips for Success

### Research:
- Review all course modules, especially Modules 4-6
- Browse online aquaponic suppliers for realistic prices
- Look at example systems similar to your chosen application
- Check local regulations for fish species restrictions

### Writing:
- Use clear, professional language
- Define technical terms on first use
- Use bullet points and tables for organization
- Proofread carefully before submission

### Design:
- Start with user needs (what does a school need vs. a home?)
- Be realistic about budget and maintenance time
- Choose simple, proven approaches over complex innovations
- Include safety considerations (electrical, access, etc.)

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Choosing incompatible fish and plants (temperature, pH)
- ❌ Unrealistic budget (too low or not researched)
- ❌ Overlooking maintenance requirements (especially for schools)
- ❌ Inadequate system diagram or missing diagram
- ❌ Vague species selection without justification
- ❌ No consideration of who will maintain system
- ❌ Missing one of the required sections
- ❌ Poor formatting or unprofessional presentation
