## Course Description

Dive into the fascinating world of aquaponics, where fish and plants form a symbiotic relationship to produce food sustainably. This comprehensive course covers the science behind aquaponics, system components, the nitrogen cycle, fish and plant selection, system design, water quality management, and hands-on operation skills. Graduates will have the knowledge to design, build, and maintain their own aquaponic systems.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Explain** the science behind aquaponic systems and the nitrogen cycle
2. **Identify** and describe all major system components and their functions
3. **Select** appropriate fish and plant combinations for different systems
4. **Design** a basic aquaponic system with proper sizing and ratios
5. **Perform** water quality testing and interpret results
6. **Execute** routine system maintenance and troubleshoot common problems
7. **Manage** system startup, cycling, and ongoing operations
