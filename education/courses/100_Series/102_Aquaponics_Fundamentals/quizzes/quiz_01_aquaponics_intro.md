## Questions

### Question 1
**What is aquaponics?**

A) Growing plants in soil with fish fertilizer

B) Raising fish in outdoor ponds

C) Integrated system combining fish farming and soilless plant growing ✓

D) Growing aquatic plants only

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Aquaponics is a bio-integrated system that combines aquaculture (raising fish) with hydroponics (growing plants without soil) in a symbiotic environment.*

---

### Question 2
**What are the three main components of an aquaponics system?**

A) Soil, water, and air

B) Fish, plants, and bacteria ✓

C) Pumps, filters, and lights

D) Seeds, feed, and fertilizer

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The three living components are fish (producing waste), bacteria (converting waste to nutrients), and plants (absorbing nutrients and filtering water).*

---

### Question 3
**How does aquaponics save water compared to traditional farming?**

A) It doesn't—it uses more water

B) Water is recirculated continuously, reducing consumption by 90% ✓

C) It only uses rainwater

D) Plants need no water in aquaponics

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics recirculates water between fish tanks and plant beds, using 90% less water than conventional soil agriculture.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the primary source of nutrients for plants in aquaponics?**

A) Chemical fertilizers

B) Compost tea

C) Fish waste converted by bacteria ✓

D) Soil minerals

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Fish produce ammonia-rich waste, which beneficial bacteria convert into nitrites and then nitrates—the primary nitrogen source for plants.*

---

### Question 5
**Which of the following is NOT a benefit of aquaponics?**

A) Produces both fish and vegetables

B) Requires no learning curve ✓

C) Eliminates soil-borne diseases

D) Uses minimal water

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics requires significant learning about fish health, water chemistry, plant nutrition, and system management. The other options are genuine benefits.*

---

### Question 6
**What does "symbiotic" mean in aquaponics?**

A) The system runs automatically

B) Fish and plants mutually benefit each other ✓

C) Only one species can survive

D) Fish eat the plants

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Symbiotic means mutually beneficial—fish waste feeds plants, and plants clean water for fish, creating a balanced ecosystem.*

---

### Question 7
**Which ancient civilization practiced early forms of aquaponics?**

A) Vikings

B) Aztecs (chinampas) ✓

C) Ancient Egyptians

D) Mongolians

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The Aztec chinampas system grew crops on floating islands in lake ecosystems, integrating fish and plant cultivation—an early form of aquaponics.*

---

### Question 8
**What is the main difference between aquaponics and hydroponics?**

A) Aquaponics uses fish to provide nutrients; hydroponics uses synthetic fertilizers ✓

B) Hydroponics uses soil; aquaponics doesn't

C) Aquaponics grows only vegetables

D) There is no difference

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: The key distinction is the nutrient source—aquaponics derives nutrients from fish waste, while hydroponics uses commercially formulated synthetic or organic nutrients.*

---

### Question 9
**What role do plants play in helping fish in an aquaponics system?**

A) They produce oxygen through roots

B) They filter and clean the water by removing nutrients ✓

C) They provide shade only

D) They have no role in helping fish

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Plants absorb nitrates and other dissolved compounds from the water, effectively filtering and cleaning it before it returns to the fish tank.*

---

### Question 10
**Why is aquaponics considered sustainable?**

A) It requires no inputs whatsoever

B) It mimics natural ecosystems, minimizes waste, and conserves resources ✓

C) It only grows organic certified products

D) It eliminates the need for fish food

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Aquaponics is sustainable because it creates a closed-loop ecosystem that minimizes water use, eliminates chemical fertilizers, and converts waste into resources.*
