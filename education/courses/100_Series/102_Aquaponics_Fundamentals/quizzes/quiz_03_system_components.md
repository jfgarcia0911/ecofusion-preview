## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the primary function of the fish tank in an aquaponics system?**

A) To filter the water

B) To house fish and allow waste production ✓

C) To grow plants

D) To store clean water only

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The fish tank houses fish, provides them with a living environment, and is where fish produce ammonia-rich waste that drives the system's nutrient cycle.*

---

### Question 2
**What is a biofilter?**

A) A mechanical screen to catch solid waste

B) Media or surface where beneficial bacteria colonize to convert ammonia ✓

C) A UV light for water sterilization

D) A pump that moves water

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The biofilter provides high surface area for beneficial bacteria (Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter) to colonize and perform nitrification, converting ammonia to nitrates.*

---

### Question 3
**In a media bed system, what does the grow media do?**

A) Only supports plant roots

B) Provides surface for bacteria AND supports plants AND filters solids ✓

C) Decorates the system

D) Stores fish food

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Grow media serves triple duty: supporting plant roots, providing surface area for bacterial colonization, and mechanically filtering solid waste from the water.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the purpose of a sump tank?**

A) To hold extra fish

B) To maintain consistent water level and house equipment ✓

C) To grow extra plants

D) To dispose of waste water

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A sump tank acts as a reservoir that maintains water level stability, houses pumps and heaters, and provides a buffer against system fluctuations.*

---

### Question 5
**Which component moves water through the aquaponics system?**

A) Gravity only

B) Water pump ✓

C) Fish swimming

D) Plant roots

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Water pumps circulate water from the fish tank through biofilters and grow beds and back, maintaining system flow and oxygenation.*

---

### Question 6
**What are the three main aquaponics system types?**

A) Small, medium, large

B) Media bed, NFT (Nutrient Film Technique), DWC (Deep Water Culture) ✓

C) Indoor, outdoor, greenhouse

D) Freshwater, saltwater, brackish

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The three primary configurations are media bed systems, NFT systems, and DWC (raft) systems, each with distinct growing methods and advantages.*

---

### Question 7
**What is a bell siphon used for?**

A) Calling fish to feed

B) Automatically draining and flooding media beds ✓

C) Aerating fish tanks

D) Filtering solid waste

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Bell siphons create flood-and-drain cycles in media beds, providing roots with alternating periods of water/nutrients and oxygen exposure.*

---

### Question 8
**Why is aeration important in aquaponics?**

A) It makes bubbles that look nice

B) Fish, plants, and bacteria all require dissolved oxygen ✓

C) It's not important

D) Only to keep water moving

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Dissolved oxygen is critical for fish respiration, healthy root function, and aerobic bacterial nitrification. Insufficient oxygen can crash the system.*

---

### Question 9
**What is a solids filter?**

A) Device that removes solid fish waste before water enters biofilter ✓

B) A type of grow media

C) A fish food container

D) A plant support structure

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Solids filters (swirl filters, radial filters, settling tanks) remove solid waste particles to prevent clogging biofilters and improve system efficiency.*

---

### Question 10
**Which system configuration is BEST for beginners?**

A) Complex recirculating system

B) Media bed system ✓

C) Large commercial NFT

D) Multi-tank industrial setup

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Media bed systems are most forgiving for beginners because the media provides biological, mechanical, and plant support functions in one simple component.*
