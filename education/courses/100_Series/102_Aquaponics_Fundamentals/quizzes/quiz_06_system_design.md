## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the recommended ratio of fish tank volume to grow bed volume?**

A) 1:1 (equal volumes) ✓

B) 10:1 (much more fish tank)

C) 1:10 (much more grow bed)

D) Ratio doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: A 1:1 ratio (equal fish tank and grow bed volumes) is a common starting point, providing adequate filtration for fish waste and sufficient nutrients for plants.*

---

### Question 2
**How deep should a media bed typically be?**

A) 2-4 inches

B) 12 inches ✓

C) 36 inches

D) 6 feet

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Media beds are typically 12 inches deep—providing enough depth for root growth and bacterial colonization while maintaining proper flood and drain cycles.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the minimum recommended system water volume for beginners?**

A) 5 gallons

B) 10 gallons

C) 50 gallons ✓

D) 500 gallons

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Larger systems (50+ gallons) are more stable and forgiving of mistakes. Small systems experience rapid parameter swings that are difficult for beginners to manage.*

---

### Question 4
**Where should you locate an indoor aquaponics system?**

A) Dark basement with no light

B) Near a window with supplemental grow lights, accessible to water and power ✓

C) Outdoor in freezing weather

D) Location doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Systems need access to natural or artificial light for plants, proximity to water source and drainage, electrical outlets, and moderate temperature stability.*

---

### Question 5
**What is the purpose of system redundancy?**

A) To make the system look complex

B) Backup pumps and aerators prevent total failure if equipment fails ✓

C) To waste money on extra equipment

D) Redundancy has no purpose

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Backup pumps, air stones, and heaters ensure the system continues functioning if primary equipment fails, preventing fish death and system crashes.*

---

### Question 6
**How should you calculate fish tank size?**

A) As small as possible to save space

B) Based on desired fish harvest weight and stocking density ✓

C) Always use the largest available

D) Fish tank size is irrelevant

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Calculate tank size by determining target harvest weight (e.g., 50 lbs of fish) and applying stocking density rules (e.g., 0.5 lbs/gallon = 100-gallon tank).*

---

### Question 7
**What is "system cycling time" in design?**

A) How fast water circulates

B) The time for water to complete one full circuit through system ✓

C) How often to feed fish

D) Plant growth rate

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Cycling time is how long it takes water to travel from fish tank through biofilter and grow beds and back. Aim for 1-2 cycles per hour for optimal filtration.*

---

### Question 8
**Why is slope important in NFT channel design?**

A) For aesthetics only

B) Proper slope (1-2%) ensures water flows continuously without pooling ✓

C) Slope should be as steep as possible

D) NFT channels should be completely flat

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: NFT channels require a 1-2% slope to maintain thin film of flowing water along channel bottom, preventing standing water and ensuring oxygen reaches roots.*

---

### Question 9
**What should you consider when choosing grow media?**

A) Color and appearance only

B) pH neutrality, surface area, durability, and cost ✓

C) Use any rocks from outside

D) Media choice doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Good grow media is pH neutral, provides high surface area for bacteria, is durable and reusable, allows good drainage, and fits your budget.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the ideal fish tank shape?**

A) Square for aesthetics

B) Circular or round-bottomed for optimal water flow and waste collection ✓

C) Rectangular is always best

D) Shape makes no difference

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Circular or cone-bottom tanks promote rotational water flow, concentrating solids in the center for easy removal and preventing dead zones.*
