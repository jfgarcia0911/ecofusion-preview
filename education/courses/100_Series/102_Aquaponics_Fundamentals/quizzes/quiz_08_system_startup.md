## Questions

### Question 1
**What is "cycling" an aquaponics system?**

a) Running the water pump

b) Establishing beneficial bacterial colonies to process waste ✓

c) Adding fish in rotation

d) Changing water regularly

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Cycling establishes populations of Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter bacteria that convert toxic ammonia to nitrite then nitrate, making the system safe for fish.*

---

### Question 2
**How long does fishless cycling typically take?**

a) 1-3 days

b) 1-2 weeks

c) 4-6 weeks ✓

d) 6 months

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Fishless cycling takes 4-6 weeks for bacterial colonies to establish sufficiently to process ammonia and nitrite to zero within 24 hours.*

---

### Question 3
**What ammonia source can be used for fishless cycling?**

a) Pure household ammonia (unscented) ✓

b) Bleach

c) Dish soap

d) Vinegar

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: Pure ammonia (without surfactants or scents) at 2-4 ppm provides food for bacteria to colonize. Fish food or urine can also be used.*

---

### Question 4
**When is a system fully cycled?**

a) As soon as water is added

b) When ammonia and nitrite drop to 0 within 24 hours of adding ammonia ✓

c) After exactly 30 days

d) When plants are growing

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: A cycled system can process 2-4 ppm ammonia to 0 ammonia and 0 nitrite within 24 hours, indicating sufficient bacterial populations.*

---

### Question 5
**Should you add fish immediately to a new system?**

a) Yes, add full stock immediately

b) No, cycle fishless first OR add very few fish gradually ✓

c) Only add goldfish

d) Fish aren't needed

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Either cycle fishless with ammonia, or do "fish-in" cycling with very few hardy fish, gradually increasing as bacteria establish. Never fully stock uncycled systems.*

---

### Question 6
**What accelerates the cycling process?**

a) Adding filter media from established system ✓

b) Using hot water

c) Adding chlorine

d) Keeping the system dark

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: "Seeding" with media, water, or filters from an established system introduces bacterial colonies, cutting cycling time significantly.*

---

### Question 7
**During cycling, you see ammonia dropping and nitrite rising. This means:**

a) System has failed

b) Nitrosomonas established, Nitrobacter still developing ✓

c) Add more ammonia immediately

d) System is fully cycled

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: This is mid-cycle: ammonia-oxidizing bacteria are working, but nitrite-oxidizing bacteria haven't caught up yet. Continue cycling.*

---

### Question 8
**When should you add plants during cycling?**

a) Never add plants

b) After system is fully cycled

c) Plants can be added early—they help consume ammonia/nitrite ✓

d) Only after harvesting fish

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Plants can be added during cycling as they help consume nutrients and won't be harmed by elevated ammonia/nitrite like fish would be.*

---

### Question 9
**What temperature is best for fast cycling?**

a) Near freezing (32°F / 0°C)

b) Room temperature (65-75°F / 18-24°C)

c) Warm (75-85°F / 24-29°C) ✓

d) Very hot (100°F+ / 38°C+)

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Nitrifying bacteria multiply fastest at 75-85°F. Cold temperatures dramatically slow cycling; extremely high temps can harm bacteria.*

---

### Question 10
**What should you do after the system is cycled?**

a) Empty it and start over

b) Gradually stock with fish, monitor water parameters ✓

c) Add all fish and plants immediately

d) Stop testing water

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: After cycling, gradually add fish (start with 25-50% of final stock), continue monitoring parameters, and adjust feeding as bacteria adapt to increasing bioload.*
