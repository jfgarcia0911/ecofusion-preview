## Questions

### Question 1
**Which fish species is most commonly used in aquaponics?**

A) Goldfish

B) Tilapia ✓

C) Sharks

D) Saltwater bass

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia are ideal for aquaponics due to their hardiness, tolerance of varying water conditions, fast growth rate, and good taste as a food fish.*

---

### Question 2
**What temperature range do tilapia prefer?**

A) 32-40°F (0-4°C)

B) 50-60°F (10-15°C)

C) 75-86°F (24-30°C) ✓

D) 95-105°F (35-40°C)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Tilapia are warm-water fish that thrive at 75-86°F. Below 50°F they become stressed and stop eating; below 50°F can be fatal.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the recommended stocking density for fish in aquaponics?**

A) 1 pound of fish per 100 gallons

B) 0.5 pounds of fish per gallon ✓

C) 10 pounds of fish per gallon

D) No limit—stock as many as possible

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A common guideline is 0.5 lbs of fish per gallon of water, though this varies by species, system filtration, and aeration capacity.*

---

### Question 4
**Which fish are best for cold-water aquaponics systems?**

A) Tilapia

B) Koi and trout ✓

C) Betta fish

D) Tropical angelfish

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Trout and koi thrive in cooler water (50-65°F), making them suitable for unheated systems in temperate climates.*

---

### Question 5
**What should you feed aquaponics fish?**

A) Only vegetables

B) High-quality commercial fish feed appropriate for species ✓

C) Table scraps only

D) They don't need feeding

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Fish need nutritionally balanced commercial feed formulated for their species (herbivore, omnivore, or carnivore) with appropriate protein levels.*

---

### Question 6
**How much should you feed fish daily?**

A) As much as they want at all times

B) 1-2% of their body weight ✓

C) 10% of their body weight

D) Only once per week

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The general rule is 1-2% of total fish body weight per day, adjusted based on temperature, growth stage, and water quality parameters.*

---

### Question 7
**What is a sign of stressed or unhealthy fish?**

A) Active swimming and bright colors

B) Gasping at surface, clamped fins, lethargy ✓

C) Eating enthusiastically

D) Schooling behavior

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Stressed fish show symptoms like surface gasping (low oxygen), clamped fins, erratic swimming, lethargy, loss of appetite, or color changes.*

---

### Question 8
**Why can't you use goldfish for commercial aquaponics?**

A) They produce too much waste

B) They're not food-grade fish in most markets ✓

C) They grow too quickly

D) They require saltwater

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: While goldfish work for hobby systems, they're not marketable as food fish and grow slowly, making them unsuitable for commercial production.*

---

### Question 9
**What is "cycling" fish into a new system?**

A) Teaching fish to swim in circles

B) Gradually introducing fish to allow bacteria to establish ✓

C) Removing old fish

D) Rotating different fish species

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Cycling with fish means starting with a small number and gradually adding more as beneficial bacteria populations grow to handle increasing waste levels.*

---

### Question 10
**Which fish species is illegal to own in some US states?**

A) Catfish

B) Bluegill

C) Tilapia ✓

D) Trout

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Tilapia are illegal in some states (like California and Florida) due to concerns about them becoming invasive if released into natural waterways.*
