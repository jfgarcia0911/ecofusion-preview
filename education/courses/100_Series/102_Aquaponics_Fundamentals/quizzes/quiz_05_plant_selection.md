## Questions

### Question 1
**Which plants grow BEST in aquaponics systems?**

A) Large root vegetables like carrots

B) Leafy greens and herbs ✓

C) Drought-tolerant cacti

D) Trees and large shrubs

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Leafy greens (lettuce, kale, chard) and herbs (basil, cilantro, mint) thrive in aquaponics with relatively low nutrient demands and fast growth cycles.*

---

### Question 2
**Why are fruiting plants (tomatoes, peppers) more challenging in aquaponics?**

A) They can't grow without soil

B) They require higher nutrient levels, especially potassium ✓

C) They need no light

D) Fish don't like them

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Fruiting plants have higher nutrient demands, particularly for potassium and calcium, which may require supplementation beyond what fish waste provides.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the benefit of growing multiple plant types together?**

A) They compete and grow slower

B) Different nutrient needs balance system consumption ✓

C) It's purely aesthetic

D) It has no benefit

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Growing plants with varying nutrient requirements (light feeders like lettuce and heavy feeders like tomatoes) helps balance nutrient levels in the system.*

---

### Question 4
**How should you start plants in an aquaponics system?**

A) Always plant seeds directly in grow beds

B) Start seeds in soil then transplant

C) Start in inert media or net pots, transplant when established ✓

D) Buy mature plants from any source

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Start seeds in inert media (rockwool, coco coir) or purchase aquaponics-ready seedlings. Avoid transplanting from soil to prevent introducing soil pathogens.*

---

### Question 5
**What is the main reason root vegetables are difficult in media bed aquaponics?**

A) They require too much water

B) Restricted root expansion and difficulty harvesting without disturbing media ✓

C) They grow too fast

D) Fish eat them

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Root vegetables like carrots and potatoes need deep, loose soil for proper root development and are difficult to harvest from media beds without system disruption.*

---

### Question 6
**Which herb is particularly well-suited for aquaponics?**

A) Rosemary (prefers dry conditions)

B) Basil (loves constant moisture and nutrients) ✓

C) Lavender (prefers dry, alkaline soil)

D) Sage (prefers dry conditions)

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Basil thrives in aquaponics with consistent moisture, warm temperatures, and abundant nutrients, making it a profitable and easy crop.*

---

### Question 7
**What does "nutrient dense" mean for plants?**

A) Plants that need few nutrients

B) Plants that require high levels of nutrients for fruiting and flowering ✓

C) Plants with dense leaves

D) Plants that grow slowly

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Nutrient-dense plants (tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers) require higher concentrations of nutrients, particularly during flowering and fruiting stages.*

---

### Question 8
**Why should you avoid planting everything at once in a new system?**

A) Plants will compete too much

B) Bacteria and fish aren't ready to support full plant load ✓

C) It's too much work

D) There's no reason to avoid it

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: In new systems, start with fewer plants as bacterial populations and fish waste production build up gradually to support increasing plant nutrient demands.*

---

### Question 9
**What spacing should you use for lettuce in aquaponics?**

A) 1 inch apart

B) 6-8 inches apart ✓

C) 24 inches apart

D) They can touch each other

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Lettuce typically needs 6-8 inches between plants to allow proper head development and air circulation, though spacing varies by variety.*

---

### Question 10
**Which plants can help supplement nutrients that fish waste lacks?**

A) Any plants will balance the system

B) Nitrogen-fixing plants like beans can add nitrogen ✓

C) No plants can supplement nutrients

D) Only ornamental plants

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Legumes (beans, peas) have nitrogen-fixing bacteria in root nodules that can add nitrogen, though this is a minor contribution in aquaponics.*
