#### Component Specifications

| Component | Function | Key Considerations |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Fish Tank** | House fish, primary system volume | Size determines system capacity |
| **Grow Beds** | Plant growing area, filtration | Ratio to fish tank critical |
| **Water Pump** | Circulate water through system | Must match system volume |
| **Biofilter** | House beneficial bacteria | Surface area essential |
| **Aeration** | Oxygenate water for fish/bacteria | Fish and bacteria need O₂ |
| **Plumbing** | Connect components, control flow | Proper sizing prevents clogs |

### 3.2 Fish Tanks

#### Tank Selection Criteria

**Material Options:**

| Material | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|----------|------|------|----------|
| **IBC Tote** | Cheap, 275 gal, readily available | Not UV-resistant, industrial look | DIY, temporary setups |
| **Polyethylene** | Food-safe, durable, UV-resistant | Can be expensive | Permanent installations |
| **Fiberglass** | Custom shapes, very durable | Expensive, heavy | Commercial operations |
| **Livestock Tanks** | Readily available, food-safe | Limited sizes | Small-medium systems |
| **Aquarium Glass** | See-through, educational | Fragile, limited sizes | Indoor, display systems |

#### Sizing Your Fish Tank

```
FISH TANK SIZING GUIDE

Starting Point: How much produce do you want?

10 heads lettuce/week
    ↓
Requires: ~10 sq ft growing space
    ↓
Media bed: 10 sq ft × 12" deep = ~75 gallons
    ↓
Fish tank: 1:1 ratio = 75 gallons
    ↓
Fish: 75 gal ÷ 5 gal/lb = 15 lbs of fish at maturity

OR

Commercial Production: 100 lbs lettuce/week
    ↓
Requires: ~200 sq ft growing space
    ↓
DWC rafts: 200 sq ft × 12" deep = ~1,500 gallons
    ↓
Fish tank: 1:2 ratio = 750 gallons
    ↓
Fish: 750 gal ÷ 5 gal/lb = 150 lbs of fish
```

**Essential Elements:**

```

    │  ░ 🐟 🐟 🐟 🐟 ░  │
    │  ░░ 🐟 🐟 🐟 ░░  │ ← Air stone
    │  ░░░░ 🐟 ░░░░░░  │   ○ ○ ○

    └───────┬─────────────┘
            │
            ▼
    Drain with standpipe
    (Solids settle here)
```

**Design Requirements:**

| Feature | Purpose | Specification |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **Depth** | Fish comfort, water volume | 18-24 inches minimum |
| **Shape** | Water circulation | Round or square with rounded corners |
| **Drain** | Remove water and solids | 1-2" bulkhead at lowest point |
| **Overflow** | Prevent flooding | Standpipe or overflow pipe |
| **Access** | Feeding, observation | Top opening or viewing window |
| **Cover** | Prevent jumping, debris | Screen or solid lid with access |

### 3.3 Grow Beds

#### Media Bed Systems

**Media Selection:**

| Media Type | Size | Pros | Cons | Cost |
|------------|------|------|------|------|
| **Expanded Clay (LECA)** | 8-16mm | Lightweight, pH neutral, reusable | Expensive | $$$ |
| **Lava Rock** | 1/2-3/4" | Cheap, locally available | Heavy, variable pH | $ |
| **Gravel** | 1/2-3/4" | Readily available, cheap | Very heavy, may affect pH | $ |
| **Growstones** | 10-14mm | Lightweight, good surface area | Can break down | $$ |

**Grow Bed Specifications:**

```

│         AIR ZONE         │  2" - Dry zone, prevents algae
├──────────────────────────┤
│                          │
│      ROOT ZONE           │  6-8" - Plants grow, bacteria colonize
│      ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒       │
│      ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒       │
├──────────────────────────┤
│   WATER ZONE             │  2-4" - Ensures moisture

         │

**Bell Siphon (Most Common):**

│ ≈≈≈≈ Rising ≈≈≈ │             │                 │
│ ≈≈≈≈ Water ≈≈≈≈ │             │   Draining ↓    │
│ ┌───┐  ≈≈≈≈≈≈≈ │             │ ┌───┐     ↓     │
│ │ B │   ≈≈≈≈≈≈ │             │ │ B │ Siphoning │
│ │ E │    ≈≈≈≈≈ │             │ │ E │     ↓     │
│ │ L │     ≈≈≈≈ │             │ │ L │     ↓     │
│ │ L │      ≈≈≈ │             │ │ L │     ↓     │
└─┴───┴───────────┘             └─┴───┴─────↓─────┘
  Standpipe                        ↓↓↓↓↓↓↓↓↓

Cycle: 15-minute flood, 45-minute drain (typical)
```

**Timer-Based Systems:**

| Method | Mechanism | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| **Bell Siphon** | Auto-siphon when full | No timer needed, reliable | Can be tricky to tune |
| **Timer + Pump** | Pump on/off cycles | Simple, predictable | Timer failure risk |
| **Float Switch** | Water level triggers pump | Automatic, responsive | More components |

### 3.4 Water Pumps

#### Pump Sizing Calculations

**Critical Factors:**

```
PUMP SIZING FORMULA

1. System Volume
   Example: 100 gal fish tank + 100 gal grow bed = 200 gallons

2. Turnover Rate
   Goal: Cycle entire volume 1-2 times per hour
   200 gallons × 1 = 200 GPH (minimum)
   200 gallons × 2 = 400 GPH (ideal)

3. Head Height
   Vertical distance from pump to highest point
   │
   │  6 ft head height
   │
   ▼
   Pump (add 30% capacity per foot of head)
   400 GPH × 1.3 = 520 GPH required

4. Final Selection
   Choose pump rated 520+ GPH at 6ft head
```

#### Pump Types

| Type | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Submersible** | Quiet, easy to hide, self-priming | Heat water, harder to service | Fish tanks, small systems |
| **External** | Easy service, efficient, cool | Noisy, must be primed | Large systems, commercial |
| **Air Lift** | No electricity in water, simple | Lower flow, needs air pump | Backup, small systems |

**Energy Efficiency Considerations:**

| Factor | Impact | Recommendation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Pump Size** | Larger = more power | Size appropriately, not oversized |
| **Head Height** | More height = more power | Minimize vertical lift |
| **Plumbing** | Restrictions = more power | Use larger diameter pipes |
| **Operating Hours** | 24/7 operation | Choose efficient models |

### 3.5 Biofilter Systems

#### Media Bed as Biofilter

```
MEDIA BED BIOFILTER

Surface Area Calculation:
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│  100 gallons grow bed          │
│  × 12 inches deep              │
│  × LECA media (200 sq ft/cu ft)│
│  = ~200 square feet surface    │
└────────────────────────────────┘

Stocking Ratio:
200 sq ft ÷ 100 sq ft per pound fish = 2 lbs fish capacity
(Conservative ratio for reliable performance)
```

#### Separate Biofilter Designs

**For NFT and DWC Systems:**

**1. Moving Bed Biofilter (MBBR):**

```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│  Water flows through    │
│  ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○   │ ← Plastic media moves
│  ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○   │   with water current
│  ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○   │
│  Air bubbles from below │
└─────────────────────────┘

Pros: High surface area, self-cleaning
Cons: Requires aeration, more complex
```

**2. Static Media Filter:**

```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│   Water inlet (top)     │
│         ↓               │
│    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓         │ ← Bio balls, lava rock,
│    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓         │   or netting
│    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓         │
│         ↓               │
│   Water outlet (bottom) │
└─────────────────────────┘

Pros: Simple, reliable, no moving parts
Cons: Can clog, requires periodic cleaning
```

#### Biofilter Sizing

| System Type | Biofilter Surface Area per lb Fish | Media Volume |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|--------------|
| **Media Bed** | 100+ sq ft | Integrated in grow bed |
| **NFT** | 50-100 sq ft | 1-2 cu ft per 10 lbs fish |
| **DWC** | 50-100 sq ft | 1-2 cu ft per 10 lbs fish |

### 3.6 Aeration Systems

#### Why Aeration Matters

**Oxygen Requirements:**

```
OXYGEN DEMAND

FISH TANK                        BIOFILTER
┌────────────────┐              ┌────────────────┐
│ Fish respire   │              │ Bacteria need  │
│ O₂ → CO₂       │              │ O₂ for         │
│                │              │ nitrification  │
│ Minimum: 5 ppm │              │ Minimum: 4 ppm │
│ Optimal: 6-8   │              │ Optimal: 5-7   │
└────────────────┘              └────────────────┘

Without sufficient O₂:
• Fish stress, gasping, death
• Bacteria stop working
• Ammonia accumulates
• System crashes
```

#### Aeration Components

| Component | Function | Sizing |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Air Pump** | Generate air pressure | 1 watt per 4 gallons (minimum) |
| **Air Stones** | Diffuse bubbles | 1 per 50 gallons |
| **Tubing** | Deliver air | 1/4" - 3/8" airline tubing |
| **Check Valves** | Prevent backflow | One per air line |
| **Manifold** | Split air to multiple outlets | Based on number of lines |

**Air Pump Sizing:**

```
CALCULATING AIR REQUIREMENTS

Example System:
• 100 gallon fish tank
• 100 gallon grow bed
• Total: 200 gallons

Minimum: 200 gal ÷ 4 = 50 watts
Recommended: 200 gal ÷ 3 = 67 watts

Select: 70+ watt air pump with multiple outlets
        OR two 40-watt pumps for redundancy
```

### 3.7 Plumbing and Flow Control

#### Pipe Sizing

**Flow Rate vs. Pipe Diameter:**

| Flow Rate (GPH) | Minimum Pipe Diameter | Recommended |
|-----------------|----------------------|-------------|
| 0-200 | 1/2" | 3/4" |
| 200-500 | 3/4" | 1" |
| 500-1000 | 1" | 1.5" |
| 1000-2000 | 1.5" | 2" |
| 2000+ | 2"+ | 3"+ |

#### Essential Plumbing Components

```
PLUMBING SYSTEM DIAGRAM

Fish Tank                        Grow Bed
┌────────┐                      ┌────────┐
│        │                      │        │
│   ○    │ ← Float/overflow    │   ▒▒   │
│   │    │                      │   ▒▒   │
│   🐟   │                      │   ▒▒   │
└───┬────┘                      └───┬────┘
    │                               │
    │ Bulkhead                      │ Bell siphon
    │ Fitting                       │ or drain
    ▼                               ▼
┌──────┐                        Return
│ Pump │ ─────────────────────► to fish
└──────┘                        tank

Key Components:
• Bulkhead fittings (watertight connections)
• Ball valves (flow control)
• Union fittings (easy disconnection)
• Check valves (prevent backflow)
• Flexible tubing (vibration dampening)
```

#### Plumbing Best Practices

| Practice | Reason | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Slope drains** | Gravity assists flow | 1/4" drop per foot of run |
| **Minimize elbows** | Reduce friction loss | Use swept elbows, not 90° |
| **Secure pipes** | Prevent leaks, vibration | PVC cement, pipe hangers |
| **Ball valves** | Flow control, maintenance | One on each major line |
| **Union fittings** | Easy disassembly | Before/after pump, filters |
| **Label everything** | Troubleshooting | Valve tags, pipe markers |

### 3.9 Component Quality and Selection

#### Quality Indicators

**Fish Tanks:**

| Factor | Poor Quality | Good Quality |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Material** | Thin, brittle plastic | Food-grade polyethylene, UV-resistant |
| **Thickness** | <1/8" walls | 1/4"+ walls |
| **Seams** | Glued, welded poorly | Rotomolded (seamless) |
| **Drain** | Pre-drilled low quality | Reinforced bulkhead area |

**Pumps:**

| Factor | Poor Quality | Good Quality |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Energy** | High wattage for GPH | Energy Star rated |
| **Warranty** | <1 year | 2-3 years |
| **Noise** | Loud vibration | Quiet operation |
| **Parts** | Proprietary | Standard, replaceable |

**Plumbing:**

| Component | Avoid | Choose |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Pipes** | Corrugated, thin-wall | Schedule 40 PVC or HDPE |
| **Fittings** | Compression only | Solvent-weld or threaded |
| **Valves** | Plastic ball valves | PVC or brass ball valves |
| **Sealant** | Plumber's putty | Silicone (aquarium-safe) |
