## The Golden Ratio: Fish to Plants

```
    ╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║                    FISH : PLANT RATIO GUIDE                          ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   RULE OF THUMB:                                                      ║
    ║   ═══════════════                                                     ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   For every 1 lb of fish → 1-2 sq ft of grow bed                     ║
    ║   OR                                                                  ║
    ║   For every 1 gallon of fish tank → 1 gallon of grow bed             ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   MEDIA BED SYSTEMS:                                                  ║
    ║   • 1:1 fish tank volume to grow bed volume                          ║
    ║   • 12" deep media bed (minimum)                                     ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   DWC/RAFT SYSTEMS:                                                   ║
    ║   • 1 lb fish per 5-7 gallons of total system water                 ║
    ║   • Need separate biofilter (sized for fish load)                    ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ║   FEEDING RATIO:                                                      ║
    ║   • 20-40g fish feed per square meter of plants per day             ║
    ║   • Adjust based on nitrate levels                                   ║
    ║                                                                       ║
    ╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Step 2: Size Your Grow Beds

```
    GROW BED DIMENSIONS

    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                                │
    │   STANDARD MEDIA BED:                                          │
    │   • Width: 2-4 feet (for arm reach)                           │
    │   • Length: Any (modular)                                      │
    │   • Depth: 12 inches (standard)                               │
    │                                                                │
    │   VOLUME FORMULA:                                              │
    │   L (ft) × W (ft) × 1 (ft depth) = cubic feet                 │
    │   × 7.48 = gallons                                             │
    │                                                                │
    │   EXAMPLE:                                                     │
    │   4ft × 4ft × 1ft = 16 cubic feet = 120 gallons               │
    │                                                                │
    └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

    ║   ═════                                                              ║
    ║   Temperature: 55-65°F (cold!)                                      ║

    ║   Growth rate: Moderate (12-18 months)                              ║

## Stocking Density Guide

```
    FISH STOCKING LEVELS

    LIGHT STOCKING (beginner recommended):
    ═══════════════════════════════════════
    1 lb fish per 10 gallons
    • Easier to maintain
    • More forgiving of mistakes
    • Lower risk of system crash

    MODERATE STOCKING:
    ═══════════════════
    1 lb fish per 5-7 gallons
    • Good balance
    • Standard recommendation
    • Requires consistent management

    HEAVY STOCKING (advanced):
    ═══════════════════════════
    1 lb fish per 3-4 gallons
    • Maximum production
    • Requires excellent filtration
    • Very little margin for error
    • Supplemental aeration critical
```

## Quick Reference: System Parameters

| Parameter | Ideal Range | Test Frequency |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| pH | 6.8-7.2 | Daily → Weekly |
| Ammonia | 0-0.25 ppm | Daily during cycling |
| Nitrite | 0 ppm | Daily during cycling |
| Nitrate | 20-80 ppm | Weekly |
| Temperature | Species-specific | Daily |
| Dissolved O₂ | >6 mg/L | Weekly |
