### Part C: Nitrate Test (NO3-)

**Time: ~10 minutes**

#### Procedure:

1. **Fill** test tube to 5ml line
2. **Add** 10 drops of Nitrate Test Solution #1
3. **Cap** and invert several times
4. **Shake** Nitrate Test Solution #2 vigorously for 30 seconds (IMPORTANT!)
5. **Add** 10 drops of Nitrate Test Solution #2
6. **Shake** tube vigorously for 1 minute
7. **Wait** 5 minutes
8. **Compare** to color card
9. **Record** result

⚠️ **Note:** Nitrate Bottle #2 must be shaken before each use!

## Interpretation Guide

### Reading Your Results

**Ammonia:**
| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| 0 ppm | Ideal - bacteria working well | None |
| 0.25 ppm | Slight elevation | Monitor, reduce feeding |
| 0.5+ ppm | Dangerous | Stop feeding, water change |

**Nitrite:**
| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| 0 ppm | Ideal - cycle complete | None |
| 0.25-0.5 ppm | Mid-cycle or stress | Monitor closely |
| 1+ ppm | Dangerous | Reduce feeding, add salt |

**Nitrate:**
| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| 0-5 ppm | Too low - plants hungry | Add more fish/feed |
| 20-60 ppm | Optimal | Maintain current balance |
| 80-100 ppm | Getting high | Add plants, reduce feeding |
| 150+ ppm | Too high | Water change, more plants |

**pH:**
| Reading | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Below 6.0 | Too acidic - bacteria stressed | Buffer with calcium carbonate |
| 6.0-6.7 | Acceptable, slightly acidic | Consider buffering |
| 6.8-7.2 | Optimal range | Maintain |
| 7.3-7.8 | Acceptable, slightly alkaline | Monitor |
| Above 8.0 | Too alkaline - ammonia more toxic | Identify source |

## Troubleshooting Common Test Issues

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| Colors don't match card | Old reagents, timing wrong | Use fresh kit, follow timing |
| Results seem wrong | Contaminated tube | Rinse thoroughly, retest |
| Inconsistent results | Technique variation | Standardize procedure |
| Nitrate always reads 0 | Forgot to shake Bottle #2 | Shake 30 sec before use |
| pH bounces around | Testing too soon after feeding | Wait 2+ hours after feeding |
