## Questions

### Question 1
What is the purpose of a bell siphon in aquaponics?

a) To make noise when water flows
b) To automatically drain grow beds in flood and drain cycles
c) To filter fish waste
d) To add oxygen to water

### Question 2

a) Coupling
b) T-fitting
c) Elbow
d) Union

### Question 3
What is the main advantage of using unions in plumbing?

a) They are decorative
b) They allow easy disconnection for maintenance
c) They are the cheapest option
d) They increase water pressure

### Question 4

a) To check water quality
b) To prevent backflow and water flowing in the wrong direction
c) To increase flow rate
d) To reduce noise

### Question 5

a) To play with
b) To control or shut off water flow completely
c) To filter debris
d) To heat water

### Question 6

a) It looks bad aesthetically
b) Air can become trapped creating airlocks
c) It uses too much piping
d) It's against building codes

### Question 7

a) Perfectly level
b) 1/4 inch drop per foot of run
c) 45-degree angle
d) Vertical only

### Question 8

a) Copper
b) Galvanized steel
c) PVC or HDPE
d) Lead pipes

### Question 9
What is the purpose of a bulkhead fitting?

a) To make the system look professional
b) To create a watertight connection through a tank wall
c) To filter water
d) To measure water flow

### Question 10
Why is it important to test your plumbing system before adding fish or plants?

a) It's not important
b) To identify and fix leaks before they cause system failure
c) To impress visitors
d) Building codes require it

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To automatically drain grow beds in flood and drain cycles
2. **c** - Elbow
3. **b** - They allow easy disconnection for maintenance
4. **b** - To prevent backflow and water flowing in the wrong direction
5. **b** - To control or shut off water flow completely
6. **b** - Air can become trapped creating airlocks
7. **b** - 1/4 inch drop per foot of run
8. **c** - PVC or HDPE
9. **b** - To create a watertight connection through a tank wall
10. **b** - To identify and fix leaks before they cause system failure
