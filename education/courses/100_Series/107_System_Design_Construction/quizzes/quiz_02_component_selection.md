# Quiz 02: Component Selection

**Course:** 107 - System Design & Construction
**Module:** 02 - Component Selection
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important factor when selecting a water pump for your system?

a) Brand popularity
b) Flow rate and head height requirements
c) Color and appearance
d) Price alone

### Question 2
Which material is generally NOT recommended for food-grade growing systems?

a) HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)
b) PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)
c) Treated lumber with toxic preservatives
d) Food-grade polypropylene

### Question 3
When selecting grow media, which characteristic is most important for root health?

a) Aesthetic appeal
b) Drainage and air porosity
c) Color
d) Weight

### Question 4
What is the primary consideration when choosing an air pump for aquaponics?

a) Noise level only
b) Adequate oxygen delivery for fish biomass
c) Energy consumption only
d) Brand reputation

### Question 5
Which type of grow light is most energy-efficient for indoor systems?

a) Incandescent bulbs
b) Fluorescent tubes
c) LED grow lights
d) Halogen lamps

### Question 6
When selecting piping for your system, what is the minimum consideration?

a) Matching your favorite color scheme
b) UV resistance and food-grade certification
c) Transparency for visibility
d) Maximum flexibility

### Question 7
What size of grow bed depth is typically recommended for media-based systems?

a) 2-4 inches
b) 6-12 inches
c) 18-24 inches
d) 36 inches

### Question 8
Which factor is most critical when selecting a fish tank for aquaponics?

a) Transparency for viewing
b) Volume capacity and food-grade material
c) Decorative appearance
d) Stackability

### Question 9
When choosing a water heater for aquaponics, what should guide your selection?

a) The cheapest option available
b) System water volume and desired temperature range
c) The most powerful heater you can afford
d) Aesthetic design

### Question 10
What is the advantage of selecting modular components over custom-built pieces?

a) They always cost more
b) They are harder to replace
c) They allow for easier system expansion and part replacement
d) They look more professional

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Flow rate and head height requirements
2. **c** - Treated lumber with toxic preservatives
3. **b** - Drainage and air porosity
4. **b** - Adequate oxygen delivery for fish biomass
5. **c** - LED grow lights
6. **b** - UV resistance and food-grade certification
7. **b** - 6-12 inches
8. **b** - Volume capacity and food-grade material
9. **b** - System water volume and desired temperature range
10. **c** - They allow for easier system expansion and part replacement
