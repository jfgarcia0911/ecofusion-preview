# Quiz 06: Construction Methods

**Course:** 107 - System Design & Construction
**Module:** 06 - Construction Methods
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the first step in any construction project?

a) Buying all materials
b) Careful planning and creating detailed diagrams
c) Starting to build immediately
d) Hiring contractors

### Question 2
Why is it important to level your system components?

a) For aesthetic appeal only
b) To ensure proper water flow and prevent stress on structures
c) It's not important
d) To meet building codes

### Question 3
What is the recommended approach when working with PVC pipe?

a) Force fittings together without preparation
b) Clean, deburr, prime, and glue properly for strong joints
c) Use tape instead of glue
d) Skip the primer to save money

### Question 4
When constructing a wooden frame for grow beds, what is most important?

a) Using the cheapest wood available
b) Selecting rot-resistant, untreated or food-safe treated lumber
c) Painting all wood bright colors
d) Using thin, weak boards to save money

### Question 5
What safety equipment should you wear during construction?

a) Nothing special
b) Safety glasses, gloves, and appropriate protective gear
c) Just fashionable clothes
d) Only expensive brand-name gear

### Question 6
Why should you test-fit components before permanently assembling them?

a) To waste time
b) To ensure everything fits correctly and identify issues early
c) It's not necessary
d) To show off your parts

### Question 7
What is the purpose of a sump tank in many system designs?

a) To make the system more complicated
b) To collect water and house pumps at the lowest point in the system
c) To store fish food
d) To hold decorations

### Question 8
When drilling holes through tanks or containers, what is essential?

a) Going as fast as possible
b) Using appropriate drill bits and creating clean, smooth holes
c) Making rough, jagged holes
d) Avoiding holes entirely

### Question 9
What is the advantage of building a stand or support structure for your system?

a) It looks professional
b) Elevates components for gravity flow and easier access
c) It wastes materials
d) It's required by law

### Question 10
Why is it important to follow a logical construction sequence?

a) It's not important; random order is fine
b) To avoid having to disassemble completed work to install other components
c) To impress others with your organization
d) Construction sequence doesn't matter

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Careful planning and creating detailed diagrams
2. **b** - To ensure proper water flow and prevent stress on structures
3. **b** - Clean, deburr, prime, and glue properly for strong joints
4. **b** - Selecting rot-resistant, untreated or food-safe treated lumber
5. **b** - Safety glasses, gloves, and appropriate protective gear
6. **b** - To ensure everything fits correctly and identify issues early
7. **b** - To collect water and house pumps at the lowest point in the system
8. **b** - Using appropriate drill bits and creating clean, smooth holes
9. **b** - Elevates components for gravity flow and easier access
10. **b** - To avoid having to disassemble completed work to install other components
