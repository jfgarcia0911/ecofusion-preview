# Quiz 01: Design Principles

**Course:** 107 - System Design & Construction
**Module:** 01 - Design Principles
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary goal when designing a sustainable aquaponics or hydroponics system?

a) Maximizing crop yield at any cost
b) Creating a balanced, efficient system that optimizes resource use
c) Using the most expensive equipment available
d) Designing the largest possible system

### Question 2
Which design principle is most important for long-term system sustainability?

a) Aesthetic appeal
b) Resource efficiency and waste minimization
c) Maximum automation
d) Complex component integration

### Question 3
What is the recommended approach when designing a system for a specific location?

a) Use a generic design that works anywhere
b) Copy another successful system exactly
c) Conduct site-specific analysis including climate, space, and resources
d) Design based solely on budget constraints

### Question 4
In aquaponics system design, what is the typical recommended ratio of fish tank volume to grow bed volume?

a) 1:1
b) 1:2
c) 2:1
d) 3:1

### Question 5
Which factor should be considered FIRST when designing a controlled environment agriculture system?

a) Equipment vendor selection
b) Crop selection and production goals
c) Aesthetic preferences
d) Marketing strategy

### Question 6
What does "modular design" mean in the context of growing systems?

a) Using only modern equipment
b) Designing systems that can be easily expanded or modified
c) Incorporating smart technology
d) Building multiple identical systems

### Question 7
Which design consideration is most critical for system accessibility and maintenance?

a) Positioning all components for easy reach and service
b) Hiding components for aesthetic appeal
c) Minimizing walkways to maximize growing space
d) Placing equipment in the cheapest locations

### Question 8
What is the purpose of incorporating redundancy in critical system components?

a) To increase initial costs
b) To ensure system continues functioning if one component fails
c) To comply with building codes
d) To impress potential investors

### Question 9
When designing for energy efficiency, which approach is most effective?

a) Using the largest pumps available
b) Running equipment 24/7 for consistency
c) Selecting appropriately sized equipment and optimizing run times
d) Prioritizing cheap equipment over efficiency ratings

### Question 10
What is the benefit of creating detailed system diagrams before construction?

a) They look professional for presentations
b) They help identify potential problems and optimize component placement
c) They are required by law
d) They eliminate the need for measurements

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Creating a balanced, efficient system that optimizes resource use
2. **b** - Resource efficiency and waste minimization
3. **c** - Conduct site-specific analysis including climate, space, and resources
4. **a** - 1:1
5. **b** - Crop selection and production goals
6. **b** - Designing systems that can be easily expanded or modified
7. **a** - Positioning all components for easy reach and service
8. **b** - To ensure system continues functioning if one component fails
9. **c** - Selecting appropriately sized equipment and optimizing run times
10. **b** - They help identify potential problems and optimize component placement
