## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of system commissioning?

a) To celebrate completion
b) To test all components and ensure the system functions properly before adding living organisms
c) To show the system to friends
d) It's an unnecessary step

### Question 2
What should you do first when commissioning a new system?

a) Add fish immediately
b) Fill system with water and check for leaks
c) Add plants
d) Turn on all equipment at once

### Question 3
How long should you run a new system with water before adding fish?

a) 5 minutes
b) At least 24-48 hours to verify stability and identify issues
c) 1 hour
d) You can add fish immediately

### Question 4
What is cycling in the context of aquaponics commissioning?

a) Riding a bicycle near the system
b) Establishing beneficial bacteria to process fish waste
c) Running pumps in cycles
d) Moving water in circles

### Question 5
Which method is commonly used for fishless cycling?

a) Adding pure ammonia to feed bacteria growth
b) Using hot water
c) Adding sugar
d) Using bleach

### Question 6
What water parameters should you test during commissioning?

a) Only temperature
b) pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature
c) Just color
d) Testing is unnecessary

### Question 7
When is it safe to add fish to a new aquaponics system?

a) Immediately after filling with water
b) After cycling is complete and ammonia/nitrite levels are at zero
c) After 1 hour
d) Whenever you feel like it

### Question 8
What should you do if you discover leaks during commissioning?

a) Ignore them
b) Drain the system, fix the leaks properly, and retest
c) Add more water to compensate
d) Cover them with tape temporarily

### Question 9
Why is it important to monitor system performance during the first few weeks?

a) It's not important
b) To catch and address issues early before they become serious problems
c) To have something to do
d) Only for commercial systems

### Question 10
What is the recommended approach when first stocking fish?

a) Add maximum capacity immediately
b) Start with a small population and gradually increase
c) Overstock to speed up cycling
d) Stock randomly without planning

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To test all components and ensure the system functions properly before adding living organisms
2. **b** - Fill system with water and check for leaks
3. **b** - At least 24-48 hours to verify stability and identify issues
4. **b** - Establishing beneficial bacteria to process fish waste
5. **a** - Adding pure ammonia to feed bacteria growth
6. **b** - pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature
7. **b** - After cycling is complete and ammonia/nitrite levels are at zero
8. **b** - Drain the system, fix the leaks properly, and retest
9. **b** - To catch and address issues early before they become serious problems
10. **b** - Start with a small population and gradually increase
