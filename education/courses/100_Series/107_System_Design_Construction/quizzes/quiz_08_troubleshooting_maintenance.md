# Quiz 08: Troubleshooting & Maintenance

**Course:** 107 - System Design & Construction
**Module:** 08 - Troubleshooting & Maintenance
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important aspect of system maintenance?

a) Waiting until something breaks
b) Regular preventive maintenance and monitoring
c) Ignoring small problems
d) Only fixing major failures

### Question 2
If your pump stops working, what should you check first?

a) Buy a new pump immediately
b) Power supply and check for clogs or blockages
c) Blame the manufacturer
d) Give up on the system

### Question 3
What does cloudy water in an aquaponics system typically indicate?

a) The system is perfect
b) Bacterial bloom, overfeeding, or inadequate filtration
c) It's normal and desired
d) The water is too clean

### Question 4
How often should you inspect your system for potential issues?

a) Once a year
b) Daily visual checks with weekly detailed inspections
c) Never
d) Only when problems occur

### Question 5
What should you do if you notice your pH is drifting out of range?

a) Ignore it
b) Test regularly and adjust gradually using appropriate buffers
c) Add random chemicals
d) Replace all the water immediately

### Question 6
If plants show yellowing leaves, what might be the cause?

a) They are supposed to be yellow
b) Nutrient deficiency, pH imbalance, or insufficient light
c) Too much health
d) It's purely aesthetic

### Question 7
What is the purpose of keeping a maintenance log?

a) To create paperwork
b) To track system performance and identify patterns or recurring issues
c) To impress inspectors
d) It serves no purpose

### Question 8
How should you clean biofilter media?

a) With soap and hot water
b) Gently rinse with system water to preserve beneficial bacteria
c) With bleach for deep cleaning
d) Replace it entirely each time

### Question 9
What should you have on hand for emergency repairs?

a) Nothing; order parts when needed
b) Spare parts like extra pumps, tubing, fittings, and repair materials
c) Just duct tape
d) Emergency repairs are never needed

### Question 10
If fish are gasping at the water surface, what is the likely problem?

a) They are exercising
b) Low dissolved oxygen levels
c) They are happy
d) Water temperature is perfect

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Regular preventive maintenance and monitoring
2. **b** - Power supply and check for clogs or blockages
3. **b** - Bacterial bloom, overfeeding, or inadequate filtration
4. **b** - Daily visual checks with weekly detailed inspections
5. **b** - Test regularly and adjust gradually using appropriate buffers
6. **b** - Nutrient deficiency, pH imbalance, or insufficient light
7. **b** - To track system performance and identify patterns or recurring issues
8. **b** - Gently rinse with system water to preserve beneficial bacteria
9. **b** - Spare parts like extra pumps, tubing, fittings, and repair materials
10. **b** - Low dissolved oxygen levels
