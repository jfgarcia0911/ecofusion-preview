## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended stocking density for tilapia in a home aquaponics system?

a) 10 pounds per gallon
b) 1 pound per gallon
c) 0.5 pounds per gallon
d) 5 pounds per gallon

### Question 2
If you have a 100-gallon fish tank, approximately how much grow bed volume should you plan for in a media-based aquaponics system?

a) 50 gallons
b) 100 gallons
c) 200 gallons
d) 25 gallons

### Question 3
How do you calculate the pump flow rate needed for a system?

a) Multiply tank volume by 10
b) System should cycle total volume 1-2 times per hour
c) Use the smallest pump available
d) Flow rate doesn't matter

### Question 4

a) 10% of fish body weight
b) 1-2% of total fish biomass
c) As much as they want
d) 0.1% of fish body weight

a) 1 CFM (cubic foot per minute) per 100 gallons

### Question 6

a) 1 square inch
b) 6-8 inches spacing
c) 24 inches spacing
d) 3 feet spacing

### Question 7

a) 1-2 inches
b) 6-10 inches
c) 24 inches
d) 36 inches

### Question 8
When calculating system water volume, which components should be included?

a) Only the fish tank
b) Only the grow beds
c) All tanks, grow beds, sump, and plumbing
d) Just the visible water

### Question 9
What is the typical plant density for commercial lettuce production in hydroponics?

a) 5 plants per square foot
b) 16-20 plants per square foot
c) 1 plant per square foot
d) 50 plants per square foot

### Question 10

a) Use 1 watt per gallon as a baseline
b) Use 5 watts per gallon
c) Heaters are not necessary
d) Use the largest heater available

## Answer Key

1. **c** - 0.5 pounds per gallon
2. **b** - 100 gallons
3. **b** - System should cycle total volume 1-2 times per hour
4. **b** - 1-2% of total fish biomass
5. **a** - 1 CFM (cubic foot per minute) per 100 gallons
6. **b** - 6-8 inches spacing
7. **b** - 6-10 inches
8. **c** - All tanks, grow beds, sump, and plumbing
9. **b** - 16-20 plants per square foot
