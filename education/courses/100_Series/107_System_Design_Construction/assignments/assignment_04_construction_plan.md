# Assignment 4: Construction Planning Document

## Course 107: System Design & Construction

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Create complete construction plan for system build.

## Requirements

### 1. Bill of Materials (5 pts)
Complete itemized list with:
- Part specifications
- Quantities
- Sources/suppliers
- Costs
- Organized by category

### 2. Build Sequence (5 pts)
Step-by-step construction timeline:
- Pre-build preparation
- Major assembly phases
- Critical path items
- Testing and commissioning
- Estimated time per phase

### 3. Tools & Equipment (2 pts)
- Tools required
- Safety equipment
- Specialty items
- Rental vs. purchase decisions

### 4. Contingency Planning (3 pts)
- Common build challenges
- Mitigation strategies
- Budget contingency (10%)
- Timeline buffer

## Submission
Professional construction document, 3-4 pages
