### 6.3 Lipids (Fats and Oils)

#### Functions of Dietary Lipids

**Roles in Fish Nutrition:**
1. **Energy source** - 9 calories/gram (vs. 4 for protein/carbs)
2. **Essential fatty acids** - Omega-3, Omega-6
3. **Vitamin carrier** - Fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K
4. **Cell membranes** - Phospholipids
5. **Hormone production** - Steroid hormones

#### Essential Fatty Acids

```
FATTY ACID FAMILIES

OMEGA-3 (n-3)              OMEGA-6 (n-6)
   ↓                           ↓
ALA (Alpha-linolenic)      LA (Linoleic)
   ↓                           ↓
EPA (Eicosapentaenoic)     ARA (Arachidonic)
   ↓
DHA (Docosahexaenoic)

FISH NEED BOTH FAMILIES
Freshwater: Can convert ALA→EPA→DHA
Marine: Often need preformed EPA/DHA
```

**Lipid Requirements:**

| Species | Lipid % | n-3 Need | n-6 Need | Best Sources |
|---------|---------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Trout** | 10-15% | High | Moderate | Fish oil, canola |
| **Salmon** | 15-20% | Very high | Moderate | Fish oil, krill |
| **Tilapia** | 5-8% | Moderate | Moderate | Soy oil, fish oil |
| **Catfish** | 5-8% | Moderate | Moderate | Soy oil, fish oil |
| **Carp** | 5-10% | Moderate | Moderate | Plant oils |

**Lipid Storage and Rancidity:**
- **Oxidation risk** - Fats can go rancid
- **Antioxidants needed** - Vitamin E, ethoxyquin
- **Cool, dry storage** - Extends feed shelf life
- **Use within 6 months** - Maximum freshness

#### Reading a Feed Label

**Sample Feed Label Analysis:**

```
TROUT GROWER FEED - 42% Protein

GUARANTEED ANALYSIS:
Crude Protein (min) ......... 42.0%
Crude Fat (min) ............. 12.0%
Crude Fiber (max) ............ 3.0%
Moisture (max) .............. 10.0%
Ash (max) .................... 8.0%

INGREDIENTS:
Fishmeal, soybean meal, wheat middlings,
fish oil, vitamin premix, mineral premix,
vitamin C, vitamin E, astaxanthin

FEEDING RATE:
Feed 1-3% body weight daily
Adjust based on temperature and response
```

**What to Look For:**
1. **Protein level** - Match to species/life stage
2. **Protein sources** - Quality ingredients first
3. **Fat content** - Appropriate level
4. **Vitamins supplemented** - Especially C and E
5. **Pellet size** - Match to fish size
6. **Freshness date** - Use within 6 months

### 6.7 Feeding Strategies

#### Calculating Feed Requirements

**Basic Feeding Rate Formula:**

```
DAILY FEED CALCULATION

Feed Amount = (Body Weight) × (Feeding Rate %)
              × (Number of Fish)

EXAMPLE:
1,000 tilapia @ 0.5 lb each
Feeding rate: 2% body weight

Daily feed = 500 lbs total × 2%
           = 10 lbs per day
```

**Feeding Rate Tables:**

**Tilapia (75-85°F):**

| Fish Size | % Body Weight/Day | Frequency |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| <1g | 10-15% | 6-8x |
| 1-10g | 5-10% | 4-6x |
| 10-50g | 3-5% | 3-4x |
| 50-200g | 2-3% | 2-3x |
| >200g | 1-2% | 1-2x |

**Temperature Adjustment:**

```
FEEDING RATE vs TEMPERATURE

100%│         ████████████
    │      ███
    │    ██
 50%│  ██
    │██
    │
  0%└─────────────────────
    50  60  70  80  90°F

Below optimal: Reduce feeding
Above optimal: Reduce/stop feeding
```

#### Feeding Methods

**Hand Feeding:**
- **Advantages** - Observe fish, adjust to appetite, bond with stock
- **Disadvantages** - Labor intensive, requires consistency
- **Best for** - Small operations, high-value fish

**Automatic Feeders:**
- **Advantages** - Consistent, reduces labor, multiple feedings
- **Disadvantages** - Initial cost, can malfunction, less observation
- **Best for** - Larger operations, set schedules

**Demand Feeders:**
- **Advantages** - Fish control intake, reduce waste
- **Disadvantages** - Only some species, can lead to overfeeding
- **Best for** - Trout, catfish in ponds

```
FEEDING SCHEDULE EXAMPLE

TIME      AMOUNT    METHOD
────────────────────────────
7:00 AM    30%      Hand
11:00 AM   20%      Automatic
3:00 PM    30%      Hand
7:00 PM    20%      Automatic
────────────────────────────
           100% daily ration
```

#### Monitoring Feed Efficiency

**Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR):**

```
FCR = Feed Given (lbs)
      ─────────────────
      Weight Gain (lbs)

EXAMPLE:
100 lbs feed → 65 lbs fish growth
FCR = 100 ÷ 65 = 1.54

Lower FCR = Better efficiency
```

**Factors Affecting FCR:**

| Factor | Effect on FCR | Management |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Water temperature** | Outside optimal = worse | Maintain optimal |
| **Feed quality** | Poor quality = worse | Use fresh, quality feed |
| **Fish health** | Disease = worse | Prevention, treatment |
| **Stocking density** | Too high = worse | Optimal density |
| **Feeding rate** | Over/underfeeding = worse | Adjust to appetite |
| **Genetics** | Selective breeding improves | Use quality stock |

### 6.8 Feed Economics

#### Feed as Production Cost

```
PRODUCTION COST BREAKDOWN (Typical)

Feed ████████████████ 50-65%
Labor ██████ 15-20%
Fingerlings ████ 10-15%
Utilities ███ 5-10%
Other ███ 5-10%
──────────────────────
      100%

FEED IS LARGEST SINGLE EXPENSE
```

**Cost Analysis Example:**

```
TILAPIA PRODUCTION ECONOMICS

Input:
• 1,000 fingerlings @ $0.30 = $300
• Feed (1,500 lbs @ $0.50/lb) = $750
• Labor, utilities, etc. = $250
TOTAL COST = $1,300

Output:
• 1,000 fish @ 1.5 lbs = 1,500 lbs
• Sold @ $2.00/lb = $3,000

GROSS PROFIT = $1,700
FEED % of cost = 58%
```

#### Strategies to Reduce Feed Costs

**1. Improve FCR:**
- Optimal feeding rate
- Quality feed
- Healthy fish
- Proper temperature

**2. Alternative Ingredients:**
- On-farm feed production
- Local ingredients
- Insect protein
- Single-cell proteins

**3. Supplemental Feeding:**
- Natural productivity (ponds)
- Fertilization for plankton
- Integrated systems

**4. Bulk Purchasing:**
- Buy in volume for discount
- Cooperative purchasing
- Seasonal pricing

```
FEED COST REDUCTION

Standard Feed          Optimized Approach
FCR: 2.0              FCR: 1.6
Cost: $0.50/lb        Cost: $0.45/lb
                           ↓
To grow 100 lbs fish:
• Standard: 200 lbs × $0.50 = $100
• Optimized: 160 lbs × $0.45 = $72
                           ↓
              SAVINGS: $28 per 100 lbs (28%)
```

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)** | Pounds of feed per pound of fish growth |
| **Essential Amino Acid** | Amino acid that must be provided in diet (cannot be synthesized) |
| **Essential Fatty Acid** | Fatty acid required in diet (omega-3, omega-6) |
| **Crude Protein** | Total nitrogen × 6.25 - measures all protein in feed |
| **Digestibility** | Percentage of nutrient absorbed by fish |
| **Extruded Feed** | Pellets made by heat/pressure, usually floating |
| **Pellet** | Compressed feed in cylindrical form |
| **Rancidity** | Fat oxidation making feed unpalatable/toxic |
