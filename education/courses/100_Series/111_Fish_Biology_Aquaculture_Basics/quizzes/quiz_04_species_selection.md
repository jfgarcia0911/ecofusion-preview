## Instructions

## Questions

### Question 1
Which factor is MOST important when selecting fish species for aquaculture?
- a) The color of the fish
- b) Market demand and system compatibility
- c) The speed at which fish swim
- d) The size of the fish's eyes

### Question 2
What temperature range do tilapia prefer?
- a) 45-55°F (7-13°C)
- b) 60-70°F (16-21°C)
- c) 75-86°F (24-30°C)
- d) 90-100°F (32-38°C)

### Question 3
Which fish species is known for being extremely hardy and tolerant of poor water quality?
- a) Rainbow trout
- b) Catfish
- c) Salmon
- d) Goldfish

### Question 4
What is a key advantage of raising tilapia in aquaculture?
- a) They require very cold water
- b) They are fast-growing and omnivorous
- c) They only eat live prey
- d) They require minimal oxygen

### Question 5
Which species is commonly used in aquaponics systems due to their hardiness?
- a) Pike
- b) Barramundi
- c) Tilapia and catfish
- d) Tuna

### Question 6
What water temperature do trout require?
- a) Warm water (75-85°F / 24-29°C)
- b) Cool water (50-65°F / 10-18°C)
- c) Hot water (85-95°F / 29-35°C)
- d) Freezing water (32-40°F / 0-4°C)

### Question 7
Which fish characteristic is important for high-density aquaculture systems?
- a) Aggressive territorial behavior
- b) Tolerance for crowding and social compatibility
- c) Requirement for large swimming areas
- d) Preference for solitude

### Question 8
What does "omnivorous" mean in terms of fish feeding habits?
- a) Eating only plants
- b) Eating only meat
- c) Eating both plant and animal matter
- d) Not eating at all

### Question 9
Which factor affects the legal aspects of species selection?
- a) The fish's color pattern
- b) Local and state regulations regarding invasive species
- c) The size of the fish's fins
- d) The fish's swimming speed

### Question 10
What is the growth rate consideration in species selection?
- a) Slower growth is always preferred
- b) Growth rate affects production timeline and profitability
- c) Growth rate is irrelevant in aquaculture
- d) All fish grow at the same rate

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Market demand and system compatibility
2. **c** - 75-86°F (24-30°C)
3. **b** - Catfish
4. **b** - They are fast-growing and omnivorous
5. **c** - Tilapia and catfish
6. **b** - Cool water (50-65°F / 10-18°C)
7. **b** - Tolerance for crowding and social compatibility
8. **c** - Eating both plant and animal matter
9. **b** - Local and state regulations regarding invasive species
10. **b** - Growth rate affects production timeline and profitability
