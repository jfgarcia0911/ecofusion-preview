# Quiz 07: Stocking and Management

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 07 - Stocking and Management
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is stocking density?
- a) The weight of the tank
- b) The number or biomass of fish per unit volume of water
- c) The depth of water in the system
- d) The number of plants in the system

### Question 2
What is the primary risk of overstocking a fish system?
- a) Fish grow too quickly
- b) Increased stress, poor water quality, and disease outbreaks
- c) Lower feed costs
- d) Improved fish health

### Question 3
Which factor should be considered when determining appropriate stocking density?
- a) The color of the tank
- b) Species tolerance, system capacity, and water quality management
- c) The day of the week
- d) The size of the building

### Question 4
What is "grading" in aquaculture management?
- a) Assigning letter grades to fish
- b) Sorting fish by size to ensure uniform growth
- c) Testing water pH only
- d) Painting identification marks on fish

### Question 5
Why is it important to acclimate new fish before adding them to a system?
- a) To teach them to swim
- b) To reduce temperature and water chemistry shock
- c) To make them hungry
- d) To change their color

### Question 6
What does "carrying capacity" refer to in aquaculture?
- a) The weight a tank can hold
- b) The maximum fish biomass a system can support sustainably
- c) The number of workers needed
- d) The amount of feed storage

### Question 7
Which management practice helps reduce aggression and competition among fish?
- a) Reducing feeding frequency
- b) Providing adequate space and grading by size
- c) Increasing water temperature significantly
- d) Mixing different species randomly

### Question 8
What is the purpose of regular fish sampling and weighing?
- a) To stress the fish
- b) To monitor growth rates and adjust feeding programs
- c) To reduce fish numbers
- d) To test equipment only

### Question 9
When should fish be harvested in a production system?
- a) As soon as they are stocked
- b) When they reach market size and weight
- c) Only during winter months
- d) Every day regardless of size

### Question 10
What is a key indicator that stocking density is too high?
- a) Fish are growing rapidly
- b) Water quality parameters deteriorate rapidly despite management efforts
- c) Feed costs decrease
- d) Fish become more colorful

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - The number or biomass of fish per unit volume of water
2. **b** - Increased stress, poor water quality, and disease outbreaks
3. **b** - Species tolerance, system capacity, and water quality management
4. **b** - Sorting fish by size to ensure uniform growth
5. **b** - To reduce temperature and water chemistry shock
6. **b** - The maximum fish biomass a system can support sustainably
7. **b** - Providing adequate space and grading by size
8. **b** - To monitor growth rates and adjust feeding programs
9. **b** - When they reach market size and weight
10. **b** - Water quality parameters deteriorate rapidly despite management efforts
