# Quiz 02: Fish External Anatomy

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 02 - Fish External Anatomy
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary function of fish scales?
- a) To help fish float
- b) To provide protection and reduce friction
- c) To attract mates
- d) To regulate temperature

### Question 2
Which fin is primarily responsible for forward propulsion in most fish?
- a) Dorsal fin
- b) Pectoral fins
- c) Caudal fin (tail fin)
- d) Anal fin

### Question 3
What is the lateral line system used for?
- a) Detecting light
- b) Detecting vibrations and water movement
- c) Producing sounds
- d) Regulating buoyancy

### Question 4
What are the operculum's primary functions?
- a) Covering and protecting the gills
- b) Helping with locomotion
- c) Storing energy
- d) Sensing temperature changes

### Question 5
Which type of scale is most common in modern bony fish?
- a) Placoid scales
- b) Ganoid scales
- c) Cycloid and ctenoid scales
- d) Cosmoid scales

### Question 6
What is the function of the pectoral fins?
- a) Only for stability
- b) Steering, braking, and maneuvering
- c) Reproduction
- d) Digestion

### Question 7
What does the term "fusiform" describe in fish anatomy?
- a) A flat, disc-like body shape
- b) A streamlined, torpedo-shaped body
- c) A snake-like elongated body
- d) A box-like compressed body

### Question 8
The mucus layer on fish skin serves what primary purpose?
- a) To make fish slippery for escape only
- b) To protect against pathogens and reduce friction
- c) To attract food
- d) To regulate salt balance exclusively

### Question 9
What is the barbel's function in catfish and other species?
- a) Defense mechanism
- b) Sensory organ for taste and touch
- c) Reproductive organ
- d) Swimming stabilizer

### Question 10
Which fin helps prevent the fish from rolling side to side?
- a) Caudal fin
- b) Pelvic fins
- c) Dorsal fin
- d) Adipose fin

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - To provide protection and reduce friction
2. **c** - Caudal fin (tail fin)
3. **b** - Detecting vibrations and water movement
4. **a** - Covering and protecting the gills
5. **c** - Cycloid and ctenoid scales
6. **b** - Steering, braking, and maneuvering
7. **b** - A streamlined, torpedo-shaped body
8. **b** - To protect against pathogens and reduce friction
9. **b** - Sensory organ for taste and touch
10. **c** - Dorsal fin
