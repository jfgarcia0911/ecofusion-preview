# Quiz 01: Introduction to Aquaculture

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 01 - Introduction to Aquaculture
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is aquaculture?
- a) The cultivation of aquatic organisms under controlled conditions
- b) The study of water quality in natural ecosystems
- c) The fishing of wild fish populations
- d) The conservation of marine habitats

### Question 2
Which of the following is NOT a major type of aquaculture system?
- a) Recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS)
- b) Pond-based systems
- c) Flow-through systems
- d) Atmospheric systems

### Question 3
What percentage of global seafood consumption comes from aquaculture?
- a) Approximately 25%
- b) Approximately 35%
- c) Approximately 50%
- d) Approximately 75%

### Question 4
Which ancient civilization is credited with early aquaculture practices dating back over 4,000 years?
- a) Romans
- b) Egyptians
- c) Chinese
- d) Greeks

### Question 5
What is the primary advantage of recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS)?
- a) Lower initial investment costs
- b) Water conservation and waste management
- c) Requires less technical knowledge
- d) Eliminates the need for water quality monitoring

### Question 6
In aquaponics, what is the relationship between fish and plants?
- a) Plants compete with fish for oxygen
- b) Fish waste provides nutrients for plants
- c) Plants produce food for the fish
- d) They have no beneficial relationship

### Question 7
What does the term "aquaculture sustainability" primarily refer to?
- a) Maximizing fish production at any cost
- b) Balancing economic viability, environmental health, and social responsibility
- c) Using only organic feed
- d) Raising only native species

### Question 8
Which of the following is a key challenge in modern aquaculture?
- a) Too much available land
- b) Disease management and biosecurity
- c) Excessive rainfall
- d) Overabundance of wild fish stocks

### Question 9
What is polyculture in aquaculture?
- a) Raising a single species in multiple tanks
- b) Raising multiple species together in the same system
- c) Using multiple types of feed
- d) Growing fish in different seasons

### Question 10
Which international organization provides guidelines and standards for responsible aquaculture?
- a) World Health Organization (WHO)
- b) Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
- c) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- d) World Trade Organization (WTO)

---

## Answer Key

1. **a** - The cultivation of aquatic organisms under controlled conditions
2. **d** - Atmospheric systems
3. **c** - Approximately 50%
4. **c** - Chinese
5. **b** - Water conservation and waste management
6. **b** - Fish waste provides nutrients for plants
7. **b** - Balancing economic viability, environmental health, and social responsibility
8. **b** - Disease management and biosecurity
9. **b** - Raising multiple species together in the same system
10. **b** - Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
