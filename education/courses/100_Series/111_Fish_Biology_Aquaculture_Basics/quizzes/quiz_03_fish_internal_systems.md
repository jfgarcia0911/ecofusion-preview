## Questions

### Question 1
How do fish extract oxygen from water?
- a) Through their skin only
- b) Through gills using countercurrent exchange
- c) Through their mouths directly
- d) Through specialized fins

### Question 2
What is the swim bladder's primary function?
- a) Storing food
- b) Filtering water
- c) Controlling buoyancy
- d) Producing hormones

### Question 3
How many chambers does a typical fish heart have?
- a) Two
- b) Three
- c) Four
- d) Five

### Question 4
What is unique about the fish digestive system compared to mammals?
- a) Fish have no stomach
- b) Fish lack intestines
- c) Many fish lack a stomach or have a simple digestive tract
- d) Fish digest food in their gills

### Question 5
Which organ is responsible for filtering waste from fish blood?
- a) Liver
- b) Kidneys
- c) Spleen
- d) Pancreas

### Question 6
What is the primary waste product excreted by most fish?
- a) Urea
- b) Uric acid
- c) Ammonia
- d) Carbon dioxide only

### Question 7
What role does the liver play in fish physiology?
- a) Pumping blood only
- b) Producing digestive enzymes, storing energy, and detoxification
- c) Extracting oxygen
- d) Controlling buoyancy

### Question 8
How do fish maintain osmotic balance in freshwater?
- a) They actively excrete excess water and retain salts
- b) They drink large amounts of water
- c) They stop urinating completely
- d) They store salt in their swim bladder

### Question 9
What is the countercurrent exchange mechanism in fish gills?
- a) Water and blood flow in opposite directions for efficient oxygen transfer
- b) Water flows in circles around the gills
- c) Blood reverses direction every few seconds
- d) Oxygen is stored and released periodically

### Question 10
Which system controls reproduction and growth in fish?
- a) Digestive system
- b) Circulatory system
- c) Endocrine system
- d) Respiratory system

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Through gills using countercurrent exchange
2. **c** - Controlling buoyancy
3. **a** - Two
4. **c** - Many fish lack a stomach or have a simple digestive tract
5. **b** - Kidneys
6. **c** - Ammonia
7. **b** - Producing digestive enzymes, storing energy, and detoxification
8. **a** - They actively excrete excess water and retain salts
9. **a** - Water and blood flow in opposite directions for efficient oxygen transfer
10. **c** - Endocrine system
