# Quiz 08: Aquaculture Best Practices

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 08 - Aquaculture Best Practices
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the foundation of sustainable aquaculture?
- a) Maximum production at minimum cost
- b) Balancing environmental stewardship, economic viability, and social responsibility
- c) Using only wild-caught fish
- d) Avoiding all technology

### Question 2
Which practice is essential for maintaining biosecurity in aquaculture facilities?
- a) Sharing equipment between facilities
- b) Controlled access, sanitation protocols, and quarantine procedures
- c) Allowing visitors unrestricted access
- d) Mixing fish from multiple sources

### Question 3
What is the purpose of maintaining detailed production records?
- a) To increase paperwork
- b) To track performance, identify issues, and improve decision-making
- c) To satisfy curiosity only
- d) Records are not necessary in aquaculture

### Question 4
Which water quality parameter should be monitored daily in most aquaculture systems?
- a) Water color only
- b) Temperature, dissolved oxygen, and pH
- c) Water age
- d) Container material

### Question 5
What does "integrated pest management" mean in aquaculture?
- a) Using only chemical treatments
- b) Combining biological, cultural, and minimal chemical methods for parasite control
- c) Ignoring all parasites
- d) Removing all fish when pests appear

### Question 6
Why is staff training important in aquaculture operations?
- a) It is not important
- b) It ensures proper system management, fish welfare, and safety
- c) To increase labor costs only
- d) To slow down production

### Question 7
What is the benefit of regular equipment maintenance?
- a) It wastes time
- b) It prevents failures, extends equipment life, and ensures consistent performance
- c) It increases equipment wear
- d) It is only cosmetic

### Question 8
Which factor is critical for ensuring fish welfare in aquaculture?
- a) Maximizing stocking density
- b) Providing appropriate environmental conditions and minimizing stress
- c) Feeding fish irregularly
- d) Frequent handling

### Question 9
What role does environmental monitoring play in sustainable aquaculture?
- a) It is unnecessary
- b) It helps minimize environmental impact and ensures regulatory compliance
- c) It only increases costs
- d) It replaces the need for water treatment

### Question 10
Which certification program promotes responsible aquaculture practices?
- a) International Space Station (ISS)
- b) Best Aquaculture Practices (BAP) or Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC)
- c) Fast Food Association (FFA)
- d) Pet Store Certification (PSC)

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Balancing environmental stewardship, economic viability, and social responsibility
2. **b** - Controlled access, sanitation protocols, and quarantine procedures
3. **b** - To track performance, identify issues, and improve decision-making
4. **b** - Temperature, dissolved oxygen, and pH
5. **b** - Combining biological, cultural, and minimal chemical methods for parasite control
6. **b** - It ensures proper system management, fish welfare, and safety
7. **b** - It prevents failures, extends equipment life, and ensures consistent performance
8. **b** - Providing appropriate environmental conditions and minimizing stress
9. **b** - It helps minimize environmental impact and ensures regulatory compliance
10. **b** - Best Aquaculture Practices (BAP) or Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC)
