# Quiz 06: Fish Nutrition

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 06 - Fish Nutrition
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three primary macronutrients required by fish?
- a) Water, air, and minerals
- b) Proteins, lipids (fats), and carbohydrates
- c) Vitamins, minerals, and fiber
- d) Sugars, salts, and acids

### Question 2
Why is protein particularly important in fish diets?
- a) It provides energy only
- b) It is essential for growth, tissue repair, and enzyme production
- c) It helps fish float
- d) It improves water quality

### Question 3
What is the typical protein requirement for carnivorous fish?
- a) 10-20% of diet
- b) 25-35% of diet
- c) 40-50% or higher
- d) 5-10% of diet

### Question 4
Which feeding strategy involves providing small amounts of food multiple times per day?
- a) Binge feeding
- b) Demand feeding
- c) Frequent feeding
- d) Seasonal feeding

### Question 5
What does FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) measure?
- a) The color of the feed
- b) The efficiency of converting feed into fish biomass
- c) The speed of feeding
- d) The temperature of the feed

### Question 6
Which vitamin deficiency can lead to poor growth and skeletal deformities in fish?
- a) Vitamin B
- b) Vitamin C
- c) Vitamin D
- d) Vitamin K

### Question 7
What is the primary risk of overfeeding fish?
- a) Fish become too healthy
- b) Water quality degradation from uneaten food and excess waste
- c) Fish grow too quickly
- d) Feed becomes more nutritious

### Question 8
What are lipids (fats) primarily used for in fish?
- a) Only for buoyancy
- b) Energy source and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins
- c) Scale production
- d) Gill function

### Question 9
Which type of feed is most commonly used in commercial aquaculture?
- a) Live insects only
- b) Formulated pellets
- c) Raw vegetables
- d) Untreated grains

### Question 10
How does water temperature affect fish feeding rates?
- a) Temperature has no effect
- b) Higher temperatures generally increase metabolic rate and feeding
- c) Fish eat the same amount regardless of temperature
- d) Colder temperatures always increase appetite

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Proteins, lipids (fats), and carbohydrates
2. **b** - It is essential for growth, tissue repair, and enzyme production
3. **c** - 40-50% or higher
4. **c** - Frequent feeding
5. **b** - The efficiency of converting feed into fish biomass
6. **c** - Vitamin D
7. **b** - Water quality degradation from uneaten food and excess waste
8. **b** - Energy source and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins
9. **b** - Formulated pellets
10. **b** - Higher temperatures generally increase metabolic rate and feeding
