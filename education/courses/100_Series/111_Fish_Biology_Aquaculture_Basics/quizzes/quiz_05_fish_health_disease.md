# Quiz 05: Fish Health and Disease

**Course:** 111 - Fish Biology and Aquaculture Basics
**Module:** 05 - Fish Health and Disease
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three main categories of fish diseases?
- a) Bacterial, viral, and fungal
- b) Infectious, nutritional, and environmental
- c) Parasitic, genetic, and chemical
- d) Respiratory, digestive, and circulatory

### Question 2
Which of the following is an early sign of stress or disease in fish?
- a) Increased appetite
- b) Loss of color and lethargy
- c) Faster swimming
- d) Increased aggression

### Question 3
What is the most important disease prevention strategy in aquaculture?
- a) Using antibiotics regularly
- b) Maintaining optimal water quality and biosecurity
- c) Feeding fish more frequently
- d) Reducing water temperature

### Question 4
What does "quarantine" mean in aquaculture?
- a) Treating all fish with medication
- b) Isolating new fish before introducing them to the main system
- c) Removing all fish from the system
- d) Changing the water completely

### Question 5
Which parasite is commonly seen as white spots on fish skin?
- a) Flukes
- b) Anchor worms
- c) Ich (Ichthyophthirius multifiliis)
- d) Tapeworms

### Question 6
What is the primary cause of most disease outbreaks in aquaculture?
- a) Genetic defects
- b) Environmental stress and poor water quality
- c) Overfeeding
- d) Too much oxygen

### Question 7
How can you prevent the spread of disease between aquaculture systems?
- a) Share equipment freely
- b) Implement strict biosecurity protocols
- c) Mix fish from different sources
- d) Avoid cleaning equipment

### Question 8
What should you do if you observe abnormal fish behavior or symptoms?
- a) Wait several weeks to see if it resolves
- b) Immediately isolate affected fish and assess water quality
- c) Add more fish to the system
- d) Decrease water flow

### Question 9
Which water quality parameter is most closely linked to fish stress and disease?
- a) Water color
- b) Ammonia levels
- c) Water taste
- d) Container shape

### Question 10
What is the benefit of maintaining proper fish stocking density?
- a) It increases disease transmission
- b) It reduces stress and disease susceptibility
- c) It has no effect on fish health
- d) It makes fish more aggressive

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Infectious, nutritional, and environmental
2. **b** - Loss of color and lethargy
3. **b** - Maintaining optimal water quality and biosecurity
4. **b** - Isolating new fish before introducing them to the main system
5. **c** - Ich (Ichthyophthirius multifiliis)
6. **b** - Environmental stress and poor water quality
7. **b** - Implement strict biosecurity protocols
8. **b** - Immediately isolate affected fish and assess water quality
9. **b** - Ammonia levels
10. **b** - It reduces stress and disease susceptibility
