# Assignment 3: Fish Health Assessment Lab

## Course 105: Fish Biology & Aquaculture Basics

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 30-45 min

## Objective
Assess fish health through observation and identify common diseases.

## Assessment Protocol
1. Visual inspection checklist
2. Behavior observation
3. Common disease identification from photos/samples
4. Treatment recommendations

## Common Diseases Covered
- Ich (white spot disease)
- Fin rot
- Fungal infections
- Parasites
- Stress indicators
