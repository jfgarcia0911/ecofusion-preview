# Assignment 5: Aquaculture System Design Project

## Course 105: Fish Biology & Aquaculture Basics

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Design complete aquaculture system with fish biology principles applied.

## Components

### 1. Species Selection & Justification (4 pts)
- Species choice based on biology
- Temperature and water quality needs
- Growth characteristics
- Stocking density calculations

### 2. System Design (5 pts)
- Tank sizing and configuration
- Filtration requirements
- Aeration and circulation
- Water quality management

### 3. Health Management Plan (3 pts)
- Disease prevention protocols
- Quarantine procedures
- Monitoring schedule
- Treatment options

### 4. Production Plan (3 pts)
- Stocking schedule
- Growth projections
- Feed budget
- Harvest timing
