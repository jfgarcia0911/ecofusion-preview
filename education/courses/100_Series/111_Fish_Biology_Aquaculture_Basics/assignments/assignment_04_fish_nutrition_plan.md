# Assignment 4: Fish Nutrition & Feeding Plan

## Course 105: Fish Biology & Aquaculture Basics

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Develop comprehensive feeding and nutrition plan for aquaculture operation.

## Requirements

### 1. Nutritional Needs Analysis (5 pts)
- Protein requirements by species
- Essential amino acids
- Lipids and carbohydrates
- Vitamins and minerals

### 2. Feed Selection (5 pts)
- Commercial feed options
- Feed size by life stage
- Quality indicators
- Cost comparison

### 3. Feeding Schedule (5 pts)
- Feeding rate calculations (% body weight)
- Frequency by age/size
- Seasonal adjustments
- Monitoring and adjustment protocols
