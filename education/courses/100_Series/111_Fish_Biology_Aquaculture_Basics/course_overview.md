## Course Description

This foundational course provides an introduction to fish biology and aquaculture principles essential for aquaponics practitioners. Learners will study fish anatomy, physiology, species selection, nutrition, and health management to build the knowledge foundation needed for successful fish husbandry in integrated growing systems.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Identify** major anatomical structures and their functions in fish
2. **Explain** fish physiology including respiration, digestion, and osmoregulation
3. **Select** appropriate fish species for different aquaculture and aquaponics systems
4. **Assess** fish health indicators and common disease symptoms
5. **Develop** basic fish nutrition and feeding plans
6. **Apply** fundamental aquaculture principles to system management
