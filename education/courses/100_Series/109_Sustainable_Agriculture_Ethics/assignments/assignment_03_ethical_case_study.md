# Assignment 3: Agricultural Ethics Case Study

## Course 109: Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 30-45 min

## Objective
Analyze ethical dilemmas in agriculture and develop reasoned positions.

## Case Study Topics (choose one)

**1. Food Waste vs. Food Security**
Should imperfect produce be sold at discount or composted?

**2. Technology Access**
Is high-tech CEA deepening inequality in food production?

**3. Local vs. Imported**
When is importing food more sustainable than local production?

**4. Labor and Automation**
Balancing automation efficiency with employment

## Analysis Framework
- Stakeholder identification
- Ethical considerations
- Alternative perspectives
- Reasoned conclusion
