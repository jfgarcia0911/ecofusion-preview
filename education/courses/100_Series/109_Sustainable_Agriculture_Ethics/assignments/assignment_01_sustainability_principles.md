# Assignment 1: Sustainability Principles Analysis

## Course 109: Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 10 | **Time:** 15-20 min

## Objective
Define and apply three pillars of sustainability to agriculture.

## Three Pillars Analysis

**1. Environmental Sustainability**
- Resource conservation
- Ecosystem protection
- Pollution prevention

**2. Economic Sustainability**
- Profitability
- Long-term viability
- Fair compensation

**3. Social Sustainability**
- Food security
- Community impact
- Labor practices
- Food justice

## Application
Evaluate a farming practice against all three pillars
