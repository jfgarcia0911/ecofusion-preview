# Assignment 5: Sustainable Food System Project

## Course 109: Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics

**Type:** Final Project | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 2-4 hours

## Project Overview
Design a sustainable food system intervention for your community.

## Project Components

### 1. Community Needs Assessment (4 pts)
- Food access gaps
- Community assets
- Stakeholder analysis
- Opportunities identified

### 2. Intervention Design (5 pts)
Choose one approach:
- Community garden program
- School food education
- Urban farm development
- Food recovery program
- CEA social enterprise

### 3. Sustainability Plan (3 pts)
Address all three pillars:
- Environmental practices
- Economic model
- Social impact

### 4. Implementation Strategy (3 pts)
- Timeline and phases
- Resource requirements
- Partnership development
- Metrics for success

## Deliverable
Proposal document (5-6 pages) with community focus and sustainability integration
