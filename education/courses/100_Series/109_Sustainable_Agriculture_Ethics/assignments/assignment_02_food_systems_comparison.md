# Assignment 2: Food Systems Comparison

## Course 109: Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics

**Type:** In-Class | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 20-30 min

## Objective
Compare conventional, organic, and CEA food production systems.

## Comparison Criteria

**Environmental Impact:**
- Water use
- Energy consumption
- Pesticide use
- Land requirements
- Carbon footprint

**Economic Factors:**
- Production costs
- Labor requirements
- Yield potential
- Market prices

**Social Considerations:**
- Food access
- Nutrition quality
- Community impact
- Worker welfare

## Analysis
Which system is "most sustainable"? Consider trade-offs and context.
