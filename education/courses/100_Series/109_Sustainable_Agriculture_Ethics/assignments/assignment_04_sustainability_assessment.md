# Assignment 4: Farm Sustainability Assessment

## Course 109: Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics

**Type:** Take-Home | **Points:** 15 | **Time:** 1-2 hours

## Objective
Conduct comprehensive sustainability assessment of an agricultural operation.

## Requirements

### 1. Operation Overview (3 pts)
- Type of farm/operation
- Scale and production methods
- Products and markets

### 2. Environmental Assessment (5 pts)
- Resource use (water, energy, land)
- Waste management
- Environmental impacts
- Sustainability metrics

### 3. Economic Assessment (4 pts)
- Viability and profitability
- Risk factors
- Economic resilience
- Community economic impact

### 4. Social Assessment (3 pts)
- Food access contribution
- Community engagement
- Labor practices
- Educational impact

## Deliverable
Sustainability report with scores and recommendations
