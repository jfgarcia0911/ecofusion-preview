## Course Description

This foundational course explores the ethical dimensions of sustainable agriculture, examining how food production choices impact communities, environments, and future generations. Learners will analyze the three pillars of sustainability, investigate food justice issues, and develop frameworks for making ethical decisions in agricultural contexts.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Define** the three pillars of sustainability and their application to agriculture
2. **Analyze** ethical considerations in food production and distribution
3. **Evaluate** farming practices against environmental, economic, and social criteria
4. **Compare** conventional and sustainable agriculture from ethical perspectives
5. **Develop** a personal framework for ethical food system decision-making
6. **Articulate** the principles of food justice and equitable food access
