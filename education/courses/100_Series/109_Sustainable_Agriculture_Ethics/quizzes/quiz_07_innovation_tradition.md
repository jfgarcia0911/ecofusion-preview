# Quiz 07: Innovation & Tradition

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 07 - Innovation & Tradition
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
How can modern technology and traditional farming wisdom coexist?

a) They are incompatible
b) Technology can enhance time-tested practices while respecting traditional knowledge
c) Only modern technology matters
d) Only traditional methods should be used

### Question 2
What is traditional ecological knowledge (TEK)?

a) Outdated farming methods
b) Indigenous and local knowledge about ecosystems developed over generations
c) TEK has no value today
d) Just superstitions

### Question 3
Why should innovation in agriculture be carefully evaluated?

a) All innovation is automatically good
b) New methods should be assessed for unintended consequences and sustainability
c) Innovation should be avoided entirely
d) Evaluation delays progress unnecessarily

### Question 4
How can farmers honor traditional practices while adopting new technology?

a) Abandon all traditions
b) Integrate beneficial innovations that align with core sustainable principles
c) Reject all technology
d) Traditions and technology cannot mix

### Question 5
What role does experimentation play in sustainable agriculture?

a) Experimentation is too risky
b) Controlled trials help identify improvements while managing risk
c) Never try new methods
d) Experiment without planning or monitoring

### Question 6
Why is it important to document both successes and failures in farming?

a) Only successes should be recorded
b) Both contribute to knowledge base and help others learn
c) Documentation wastes time
d) Failures should be hidden

### Question 7
How can indigenous farming methods inform modern sustainable practices?

a) Indigenous methods are irrelevant
b) Traditional practices often demonstrate sustainable resource management principles
c) Modern farming has nothing to learn from tradition
d) Indigenous and modern farming are completely unrelated

### Question 8
What is appropriate technology in agriculture?

a) The most expensive technology available
b) Technology that fits the scale, context, and values of the operation
c) Any technology from developed countries
d) Technology doesn't need to be appropriate

### Question 9
Why should farmers be cautious about adopting every new trend?

a) All trends should be adopted immediately
b) Not all innovations align with sustainability goals or specific farm contexts
c) Never adopt any trends
d) Trends are always based on solid research

### Question 10
How can farming communities share knowledge effectively?

a) Keep all knowledge secret for competitive advantage
b) Through farmer networks, mentorships, and open documentation
c) Only through expensive consultants
d) Knowledge sharing reduces individual success

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Technology can enhance time-tested practices while respecting traditional knowledge
2. **b** - Indigenous and local knowledge about ecosystems developed over generations
3. **b** - New methods should be assessed for unintended consequences and sustainability
4. **b** - Integrate beneficial innovations that align with core sustainable principles
5. **b** - Controlled trials help identify improvements while managing risk
6. **b** - Both contribute to knowledge base and help others learn
7. **b** - Traditional practices often demonstrate sustainable resource management principles
8. **b** - Technology that fits the scale, context, and values of the operation
9. **b** - Not all innovations align with sustainability goals or specific farm contexts
10. **b** - Through farmer networks, mentorships, and open documentation
