# Quiz 02: Environmental Responsibility

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 02 - Environmental Responsibility
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary environmental benefit of aquaponics and hydroponics?

a) They use more water than traditional farming
b) Significant water conservation through recirculation systems
c) They eliminate all environmental impact
d) No environmental benefits exist

### Question 2
How does controlled environment agriculture reduce pesticide use?

a) It doesn't reduce pesticide use
b) Enclosed systems limit pest access and enable biological controls
c) Pesticides are always required equally
d) CEA uses more pesticides than field farming

### Question 3
What is a carbon footprint?

a) Footprints left in coal
b) Total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an activity or organization
c) The size of your farm
d) Carbon footprints don't exist

### Question 4
Why is energy efficiency important in indoor growing systems?

a) Energy use doesn't matter
b) Reduces environmental impact and operational costs
c) Only for marketing purposes
d) Efficiency decreases productivity

### Question 5
What is the concept of circular economy in agriculture?

a) Growing plants in circles
b) Minimizing waste by reusing resources in closed-loop systems
c) Selling at farmer's markets
d) Rotating crops

### Question 6
How can renewable energy support sustainable farming?

a) Renewable energy is too expensive
b) Solar, wind, or hydro power reduce fossil fuel dependence
c) Renewable energy doesn't work for agriculture
d) Only conventional energy works

### Question 7
What is biodiversity and why does it matter?

a) Diversity doesn't matter
b) Variety of life that supports ecosystem resilience and stability
c) Just a scientific term with no practical importance
d) Biodiversity only matters in rainforests

### Question 8
How does local food production reduce environmental impact?

a) It doesn't reduce impact
b) Decreases transportation emissions and supports local ecosystems
c) Local food always has higher impact
d) Distance doesn't affect environmental impact

### Question 9
What is the purpose of lifecycle assessment in agriculture?

a) Measuring plant lifespan
b) Evaluating total environmental impact from production to disposal
c) Lifecycle assessment is irrelevant
d) Only for manufactured goods

### Question 10
Why should farmers consider soil health even in soilless systems?

a) Soil health is irrelevant to hydroponics
b) Understanding soil ecology informs sustainable practices and resource management
c) Soilless means no environmental responsibility
d) Soil and water systems are completely unrelated

1. **b** - Significant water conservation through recirculation systems
2. **b** - Enclosed systems limit pest access and enable biological controls
3. **b** - Total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an activity or organization
4. **b** - Reduces environmental impact and operational costs
5. **b** - Minimizing waste by reusing resources in closed-loop systems
6. **b** - Solar, wind, or hydro power reduce fossil fuel dependence
7. **b** - Variety of life that supports ecosystem resilience and stability
8. **b** - Decreases transportation emissions and supports local ecosystems
9. **b** - Evaluating total environmental impact from production to disposal
