## Questions

### Question 1
What are the Five Freedoms of animal welfare?

a) Random freedoms with no basis
b) Freedom from hunger, discomfort, pain, fear, and freedom to express natural behavior
c) Five legal requirements only
d) Freedoms that don't apply to fish

### Question 2

a) Density doesn't matter
b) Overcrowding causes stress, disease, and poor welfare outcomes
c) More fish is always better
d) Fish don't experience stress

### Question 3

a) Water quality is irrelevant
b) Poor water quality causes stress, illness, and suffering in fish
c) Fish adapt to any water conditions
d) Only temperature matters

### Question 4

c) Humane practices don't apply to fish

### Question 6

a) Fish never show stress signs
b) Erratic swimming, loss of appetite, color changes, gasping
c) All fish behavior is normal
d) Stress doesn't affect fish

### Question 7
How does nutrition affect animal welfare?

a) Nutrition is unrelated to welfare
b) Proper nutrition supports health, growth, and overall wellbeing
c) Fish can survive on any food
d) Cheap feed is always adequate

### Question 8

b) Prevents disease introduction that would cause suffering
c) Only for large commercial operations
d) Biosecurity reduces animal welfare

### Question 9
What role does observation play in ensuring animal welfare?

a) Observation wastes time
b) Regular monitoring detects health issues and stress early
c) Fish care for themselves
d) Observation is only for research

### Question 10
How can farmers balance production goals with animal welfare?

a) Welfare and production are incompatible
b) Healthy, low-stress animals actually perform better productively
c) Always prioritize production over welfare
d) Welfare concerns are just marketing
