# Quiz 01: Foundations of Ethics

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 01 - Foundations of Ethics
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary focus of agricultural ethics?

a) Maximizing profits at any cost
b) Examining moral principles guiding food production and farming practices
c) Following trends in agriculture
d) Ignoring environmental concerns

### Question 2
Which ethical framework emphasizes the greatest good for the greatest number?

a) Deontology
b) Virtue Ethics
c) Utilitarianism
d) Egoism

### Question 3
What does sustainability mean in agriculture?

a) Only focusing on current profits
b) Meeting present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs
c) Using the newest technology available
d) Producing as much as possible

### Question 4
Why is ethical consideration important in food production?

a) It's not important
b) Food systems impact environment, health, communities, and animal welfare
c) Only for marketing purposes
d) To comply with regulations only

### Question 5
What is the triple bottom line in sustainable business?

a) Three profit streams
b) People, Planet, Profit
c) Past, Present, Future
d) Production, Processing, Packaging

### Question 6
Which principle is central to environmental stewardship?

a) Exploiting resources before they run out
b) Responsible management and protection of natural resources
c) Prioritizing short-term gains
d) Avoiding any use of natural resources

### Question 7
What does food justice address?

a) Criminal justice in restaurants
b) Equitable access to healthy, affordable, culturally appropriate food
c) Pricing disputes
d) Restaurant regulations

### Question 8
Why might a farmer consider animal welfare in their practices?

a) Animals don't have moral consideration
b) Ethical treatment respects animal sentience and improves outcomes
c) Only because of regulations
d) Animal welfare doesn't affect production

### Question 9
What is the precautionary principle?

a) Being overly cautious about everything
b) Taking preventive action when activities may harm environment or health
c) Never trying new methods
d) Ignoring potential risks

### Question 10
How does transparency relate to ethical food production?

a) Transparency isn't important
b) Open communication builds trust and accountability in food systems
c) Farmers should keep all practices secret
d) Transparency only matters for large corporations

---

## Answer Key

1. **b** - Examining moral principles guiding food production and farming practices
2. **c** - Utilitarianism
3. **b** - Meeting present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs
4. **b** - Food systems impact environment, health, communities, and animal welfare
5. **b** - People, Planet, Profit
6. **b** - Responsible management and protection of natural resources
7. **b** - Equitable access to healthy, affordable, culturally appropriate food
8. **b** - Ethical treatment respects animal sentience and improves outcomes
9. **b** - Taking preventive action when activities may harm environment or health
10. **b** - Open communication builds trust and accountability in food systems
