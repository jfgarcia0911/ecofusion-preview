# Quiz 06: Transparency & Accountability

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 06 - Transparency & Accountability
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
Why is transparency important in food production?

a) Transparency reveals too much information
b) Builds consumer trust and allows informed purchasing decisions
c) Farmers should keep practices secret
d) Transparency is only for marketing

### Question 2
What does farm-to-table traceability mean?

a) Literally transporting food from farm to table
b) Tracking food products through entire supply chain from origin to consumer
c) Traceability is unnecessary
d) Only for restaurant food

### Question 3
How can farmers demonstrate their sustainable practices?

a) Just claim sustainability without proof
b) Through certifications, farm tours, documentation, and transparent communication
c) Demonstrations are unnecessary
d) Only through expensive advertising

### Question 4
What is the purpose of third-party certification?

a) To create unnecessary bureaucracy
b) Independent verification of claims builds credibility
c) Self-certification is always sufficient
d) Certifications are only for marketing

### Question 5
Why should farmers be honest about challenges and failures?

a) Never admit problems
b) Honesty builds trust and contributes to industry knowledge
c) Only share successes
d) Failures should be hidden

### Question 6
How does labeling support transparency?

a) Labels are just decorative
b) Provides consumers information about production methods and contents
c) Minimal labeling is always better
d) Labels confuse consumers

### Question 7
What role do farm visits play in transparency?

a) Visitors are a nuisance
b) Allow consumers to see practices firsthand and build connections
c) Farms should always be closed to public
d) Only for agritourism businesses

### Question 8
Why is it important to accurately represent your products?

a) Exaggeration is expected in marketing
b) Honesty maintains trust and avoids legal and ethical issues
c) Consumers don't check claims
d) Accuracy doesn't matter if sales increase

### Question 9
How can farmers be accountable for their environmental impact?

a) Ignore environmental impacts
b) Measure, report, and continuously improve environmental performance
c) Accountability is only for large corporations
d) Environmental impact is unmeasurable

### Question 10
What is greenwashing and why should it be avoided?

a) Washing vegetables with green soap
b) Making misleading claims about environmental benefits to deceive consumers
c) Greenwashing is an acceptable marketing tactic
d) The term doesn't relate to agriculture
