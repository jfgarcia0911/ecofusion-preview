# Quiz 08: Personal Ethical Framework

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 08 - Personal Ethical Framework
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
Why is it important to develop a personal ethical framework for farming?

a) Ethics are predetermined and universal
b) A clear framework guides consistent decision-making aligned with values
c) Personal ethics don't apply to business
d) Frameworks are unnecessary complexity

### Question 2
What should inform your ethical framework in agriculture?

a) Only profit considerations
b) Personal values, scientific evidence, community needs, and environmental impact
c) Following trends blindly
d) What competitors are doing

### Question 3
How should farmers handle ethical dilemmas?

a) Ignore conflicts and hope they resolve
b) Reflect on core values, seek input, and make principled decisions
c) Always choose the easiest path
d) Let others decide for you

### Question 4
What role does self-reflection play in ethical farming?

a) Self-reflection is navel-gazing
b) Regular reflection ensures actions align with stated values
c) Reflection wastes productive time
d) Once values are set, never reconsider them

### Question 5
Why might a farmer's ethics evolve over time?

a) Ethics should never change
b) New knowledge, experiences, and circumstances inform understanding
c) Changing ethics shows weakness
d) Only young farmers develop ethics

### Question 6
How can farmers address conflicts between economic and ethical goals?

a) Always prioritize economics over ethics
b) Seek creative solutions that honor both financial needs and values
c) Ethics and economics never conflict
d) Give up on either profit or principles

### Question 7
What is the value of articulating your farm's ethical principles?

a) Principles should remain unspoken
b) Clear principles guide decisions, communicate values, and build accountability
c) Written principles are just bureaucracy
d) Only large organizations need principles

### Question 8
How should farmers respond to ethical criticism?

a) Ignore all criticism
b) Listen thoughtfully, evaluate validity, and adjust practices when appropriate
c) Become defensive and dismissive
d) Change everything based on any criticism

### Question 9
Why is consistency between stated values and practices important?

a) Consistency doesn't matter
b) Alignment builds credibility and demonstrates genuine commitment
c) Everyone expects hypocrisy
d) Values are just marketing, not practice

### Question 10
What is ethical leadership in farming communities?

a) Telling others what to do
b) Modeling values-based practices and supporting others in ethical development
c) Leadership and ethics are unrelated
d) Only designated leaders have ethical responsibilities
