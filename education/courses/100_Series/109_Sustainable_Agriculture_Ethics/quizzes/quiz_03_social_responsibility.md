# Quiz 03: Social Responsibility

**Course:** 109 - Sustainable Agriculture & Ethics
**Module:** 03 - Social Responsibility
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each question. Each question is worth 10 points.

## Questions

### Question 1
What does social responsibility mean for farmers?

a) Avoiding all social interaction
b) Considering impacts on workers, communities, and consumers
c) Only focusing on profit
d) Social responsibility doesn't apply to agriculture

### Question 2
Why is fair labor treatment important in agriculture?

a) It's not important
b) Respects human dignity and creates sustainable livelihoods
c) Only required by law
d) Fair treatment reduces productivity

### Question 3
What is food security?

a) Locking up food supplies
b) When all people have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food
c) Security systems in grocery stores
d) Food security is not a real concern

### Question 4
How can small-scale farming contribute to community resilience?

a) Small farms can't contribute meaningfully
b) Provides local food sources, jobs, and strengthens local economies
c) Only large farms matter
d) Community resilience is unrelated to farming

### Question 5
What is the connection between agriculture and public health?

a) No connection exists
b) Food production practices directly affect nutrition, safety, and community wellbeing
c) Only doctors affect public health
d) Agriculture and health are separate topics

### Question 6
Why might a farmer choose to pay living wages?

a) It's legally required everywhere
b) Supports worker wellbeing, reduces turnover, and aligns with ethical values
c) To waste money
d) Living wages decrease quality

### Question 7
What role does education play in sustainable agriculture?

a) Education is unnecessary
b) Empowers farmers and consumers to make informed, sustainable choices
c) Only formal education matters
d) Traditional knowledge should be ignored

### Question 8
How can farming businesses support local communities?

a) Businesses should avoid community involvement
b) Through employment, local purchasing, education, and food access programs
c) Only through tax payments
d) Community support reduces profits

### Question 9
What is cultural appropriateness in food systems?

a) Food culture doesn't matter
b) Respecting and providing food that aligns with cultural traditions and preferences
c) Everyone should eat the same foods
d) Cultural concerns are outdated

### Question 10
Why is accessibility important in sustainable food production?

a) Accessibility only matters for buildings
b) Ensuring diverse people can participate in and benefit from food systems
c) Accessibility decreases efficiency
d) Only able-bodied people farm
