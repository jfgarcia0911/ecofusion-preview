## Project Overview

Research a specific plant species and analyze its biology, growing requirements, and optimal cultivation practices based on plant science principles learned in this course.

## Project Requirements

### Part 1: Species Selection (2 points)

**Choose ONE plant species** commonly grown in controlled environments:
- Tomato, Lettuce, Basil, Strawberry, Pepper, Cucumber, Kale, or instructor-approved alternative

**Include:**
- Common and scientific name
- Plant family
- Origin and natural habitat
- Why you selected this species

### Part 2: Biological Analysis (6 points)

**Photosynthetic Characteristics (2 points):**
- C3, C4, or CAM pathway?
- Typical photosynthetic rate
- Light saturation point
- Photoperiod requirements (long-day, short-day, day-neutral)

**Nutrient Requirements (2 points):**
- NPK ratio preferences
- Critical micronutrients
- pH preferences
- Common deficiencies and symptoms

**Environmental Responses (2 points):**
- Optimal temperature range (day/night)
- Humidity preferences
- Response to environmental stress
- Growth hormones and their effects

### Part 3: Optimal Growing Conditions (4 points)

Create a comprehensive growing guide including:

**Light Requirements:**
- PPFD range: _______ μmol/m²/s
- DLI requirement: _______ mol/m²/day
- Photoperiod: _______ hours
- Spectrum preferences

**Environmental Conditions:**
- Temperature: _______°F day / _______°F night
- Relative Humidity: _______%
- CO₂: _______ ppm (ambient or enriched?)
- Air circulation requirements

**Nutrition:**
- EC range: _______ mS/cm
- pH range: _______
- Specific nutrient needs
- Fertigation schedule

**Growth Stages:**
- Germination requirements
- Vegetative stage duration and needs
- Flowering/fruiting triggers
- Days to harvest

### Part 4: Common Problems & Solutions (3 points)

**Identify and explain:**
1. Three common nutrient deficiencies
   - Symptoms
   - Causes
   - Solutions

2. Two environmental stress responses
   - What causes the stress
   - How the plant responds physiologically
   - Prevention/mitigation

3. Optimal harvest timing
   - Indicators of maturity
   - How plant physiology changes at harvest readiness
