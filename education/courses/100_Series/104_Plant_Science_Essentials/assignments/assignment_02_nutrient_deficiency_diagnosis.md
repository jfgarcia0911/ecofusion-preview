# Assignment 2: Nutrient Deficiency Diagnosis

## Course 104: Plant Science Essentials

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Type** | In-Class Diagnostic Activity |
| **Points** | 15 points |
| **Time** | 20-30 minutes |
| **LO** | LO4: List essential nutrients and recognize deficiency symptoms |

## Objective

Diagnose nutrient deficiencies from plant symptoms and recommend appropriate treatments.

## Materials

- Photo cards showing 6 different nutrient deficiency symptoms
- Nutrient deficiency reference chart
- Worksheet for recording diagnoses

## Procedure

For each of the 6 plant photos provided, identify:

1. **Deficient Nutrient** (1 point each = 6 points)
2. **Key Symptoms Observed** (0.5 points each = 3 points)
3. **Mobile or Immobile Nutrient** (0.5 points each = 3 points)
4. **Treatment Recommendation** (0.5 points each = 3 points)

## Example Format

**Photo 1:**
- **Deficient Nutrient:** _____________
- **Key Symptoms:** (List 2-3 observable symptoms)
  1. _____________
  2. _____________
- **Mobility:** ☐ Mobile ☐ Immobile
- **Treatment:** _____________

## Common Deficiencies Covered

- Nitrogen (N) - yellowing older leaves
- Phosphorus (P) - purple/red discoloration
- Potassium (K) - brown leaf edges
- Iron (Fe) - interveinal chlorosis in new leaves
- Calcium (Ca) - tip burn, distorted new growth
- Magnesium (Mg) - interveinal yellowing older leaves

## Grading Rubric

| Component | Excellent (13-15) | Good (10-12) | Satisfactory (7-9) | Needs Improvement (0-6) |
|-----------|------------------|--------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Identification** | 5-6 correct | 4 correct | 2-3 correct | 0-1 correct |
| **Symptom Analysis** | Detailed, accurate | Mostly accurate | Partial accuracy | Inaccurate |
| **Treatment** | Specific, appropriate | Generally appropriate | Vague | Incorrect |

## Tips for Success

1. **Remember mobility:** Mobile nutrients show deficiency in OLD leaves first, immobile in NEW leaves
2. **Look for patterns:** Interveinal vs. overall, tips vs. edges
3. **Consider multiple symptoms:** Plants often show multiple signs
4. **Think correction:** How would you address each deficiency?
