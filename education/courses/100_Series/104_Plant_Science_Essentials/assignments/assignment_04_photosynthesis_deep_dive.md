## Assignment Purpose

Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of photosynthesis and apply this knowledge to optimize growing conditions.

## Requirements

### Part 1: Photosynthesis Explanation (7 points)

Write a detailed explanation (2-3 pages) covering:

**Section 1: Light Reactions (2 points)**
- Where they occur (thylakoid membranes)
- Inputs required (light, water)
- Outputs produced (ATP, NADPH, O₂)
- Role of chlorophyll and photosystems

**Section 2: Calvin Cycle (2 points)**
- Where it occurs (stroma)
- Inputs required (CO₂, ATP, NADPH)
- Outputs produced (glucose/G3P)
- Carbon fixation process

**Section 3: Factors Affecting Photosynthesis (3 points)**
Explain how each factor affects photosynthetic rate:
- Light intensity
- CO₂ concentration
- Temperature
- Water availability
- Nutrient availability (especially N, Mg, Fe)

### Part 2: Application to Growing (5 points)

**Question 1 (2 points):**
You're growing lettuce indoors. Based on your understanding of photosynthesis, explain:
- Why increasing light beyond a certain point doesn't increase growth
- What is the "light saturation point" and why does it occur?
- How can you determine if your plants are light-limited?

**Question 2 (2 points):**
Some commercial greenhouses enrich CO₂ to 800-1200 ppm (vs. 400 ppm ambient). Explain:
- Why higher CO₂ increases photosynthetic rate
- Under what conditions is CO₂ enrichment most beneficial?
- What other factors must be optimized for CO₂ enrichment to be effective?

**Question 3 (1 point):**
Explain why C4 plants (like corn) are more efficient than C3 plants (like lettuce) at high temperatures and why this matters for crop selection.

### Part 3: Professional Quality (3 points)

- Clear, organized writing
- Proper scientific terminology
- Diagrams or figures (if helpful)
- Citations for any references used
- Correct grammar and spelling
