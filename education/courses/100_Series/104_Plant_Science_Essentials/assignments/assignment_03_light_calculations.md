## Objective

Calculate Daily Light Integral (DLI) requirements and evaluate lighting systems for different crops.

## Part 1: DLI Calculations (6 points)

### Scenario 1:
You have an LED fixture providing 400 μmol/m²/s PPFD.
- Photoperiod: 16 hours/day
- Calculate DLI: ____________ mol/m²/day

**Formula:** DLI = (PPFD × photoperiod in hours × 3600) / 1,000,000

### Scenario 2:
Tomatoes require 25-35 mol/m²/day DLI.
- Your fixture provides 500 μmol/m²/s PPFD
- How many hours per day should lights run? ____________ hours

### Scenario 3:
Lettuce needs minimum 12 mol/m²/day DLI.
- Lights run 14 hours/day
- What minimum PPFD is required? ____________ μmol/m²/s

## Part 2: PPFD Measurements (5 points)

Using a PAR meter, measure PPFD at different distances from a grow light:

| Distance | PPFD Reading | Suitable For |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| 6 inches | ______ μmol/m²/s | ____________ |
| 12 inches | ______ μmol/m²/s | ____________ |
| 18 inches | ______ μmol/m²/s | ____________ |
| 24 inches | ______ μmol/m²/s | ____________ |

**Analysis:** Describe the relationship between distance and light intensity (1-2 sentences)

## Part 3: Application (4 points)

### Question 1 (2 points):
You're growing basil (requires 15-20 mol/m²/day) under LED lights providing 350 μmol/m²/s. Your electricity costs $0.12/kWh and the fixture uses 100W.

Calculate:
- Required photoperiod: _______ hours/day
- Daily electricity cost: $_______ /day
- Monthly electricity cost: $_______ /month

### Question 2 (2 points):
Compare two lighting options for lettuce production (200 sq ft area):

**Option A:** Natural sunlight + supplemental LED
- Free sunlight provides 8 mol/m²/day average
- Need to supplement to 14 mol/m²/day total
- LED cost: $500, uses 200W

**Option B:** Full LED (sole-source)
- Provides all 14 mol/m²/day
- LED cost: $1,200, uses 600W

Calculate annual electricity costs for each option and recommend the best choice. Justify your answer.
