## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
- Apply systematic diagnosis techniques to identify nutrient deficiencies
- Practice distinguishing between similar symptoms
- Understand the importance of symptom location in diagnosis
- Develop problem-solving skills for real growing scenarios

---

## The Diagnostic Framework

Before starting, review the key diagnostic steps:

```
    DIAGNOSIS PROCESS

    1. WHERE are symptoms?
       → Old leaves = Mobile nutrient (N, P, K, Mg)
       → New leaves = Immobile nutrient (Ca, S, Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, B)

    2. WHAT does it look like?
       → Uniform yellowing
       → Interveinal chlorosis (yellow between green veins)
       → Necrosis (dead tissue)
       → Color changes (purple, red)
       → Distortion

    3. WHAT ELSE is happening?
       → Environmental factors
       → pH status
       → Recent changes

    4. CONFIRM and CORRECT
```

## Answer Key

**Case 1:** Nitrogen deficiency
- Q1: Old growth | Q2: Mobile | Q3: Uniform | Q4: Nitrogen
- Q5: Low EC (0.8) suggests insufficient nutrients overall
- Q6: Increase EC/nitrogen in solution

**Case 2:** Blossom End Rot (Calcium)
- Q7: Blossom end rot | Q8: Calcium | Q9: Transport
- Q10: High EC, reduced watering during hot weather
- Q11: Ca goes to transpiring tissues; fruit transpires less
- Q12: Consistent watering, reduce EC, improve airflow

**Case 3:** Iron deficiency due to high pH
- Q13: Iron | Q14: pH of 7.4 is too high
- Q15: Iron is locked out at high pH
- Q16: Lower pH to 5.8-6.2

**Case 4:** Potassium deficiency
- Q17: Potassium | Q18: Mobile
- Q19: K is water-soluble and leaches easily
- Q20: High N can antagonize K uptake
- Q21: Potassium sulfate or balanced fertilizer

**Case 5:** Phosphorus deficiency due to cold
- Q22: Phosphorus | Q23: Mobile (but cold affects whole plant)
- Q24: Cold root zone (12°C) severely limits P uptake
- Q25: Warm up the root zone/greenhouse at night

**Reference Table:**
| Symptom | Location | Deficiency |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Uniform yellowing | Old | Nitrogen |
| Interveinal chlorosis | Old | Magnesium |
| Interveinal chlorosis | New | Iron |
| Brown leaf edges | Old | Potassium |
| Tip burn, BER | New/fruit | Calcium |
| Purple coloration | Throughout | Phosphorus |
