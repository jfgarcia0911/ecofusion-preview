## Part 5: Stomatal Print (Optional Advanced)

### Create a Stomatal Cast

This technique allows you to see stomata more clearly.

**Method 1: Tape Method**
1. Press clear tape firmly onto leaf underside
2. Peel off carefully
3. Stick tape to white paper
4. Observe under magnification

**Method 2: Nail Polish Method**
1. Paint thin layer of clear nail polish on leaf underside
2. Let dry completely (5-10 minutes)
3. Peel off dried polish carefully
4. Place on glass slide or clear plastic
5. Observe under magnification

**Your Results:**

Could you see stomata using this method? Yes / No
