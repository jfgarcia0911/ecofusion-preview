## Course Description

Develop a deep understanding of plant biology to become a more effective grower. This course covers plant anatomy, physiology, nutrition, and environmental responses, providing the scientific foundation for all cultivation methods. Whether you're growing in soil, hydroponics, or aquaponics, understanding how plants work will transform your results.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Identify** plant structures and explain their functions
2. **Describe** the process of photosynthesis and plant energy metabolism
3. **Explain** how plants absorb and transport water and nutrients
4. **List** essential plant nutrients and recognize deficiency symptoms
5. **Analyze** how environmental factors affect plant growth
6. **Apply** plant science principles to optimize cultivation practices

## Key Topics

### Plant Anatomy
- Root structure: root tip, root hairs, vascular tissue
- Stem structure: xylem, phloem, cambium
- Leaf structure: epidermis, stomata, mesophyll, chloroplasts
- Flower and fruit development

### Photosynthesis
- Light reactions (thylakoid)
- Calvin cycle (carbon fixation)
- C3, C4, and CAM pathways
- Photosynthetic efficiency
- Factors affecting photosynthesis

### Plant Nutrition
- 17 essential elements
- Mobile vs. immobile nutrients
- Deficiency and toxicity symptoms
- Nutrient uptake mechanisms

### Environmental Responses
- Phototropism (light)
- Gravitropism (gravity)
- Thigmotropism (touch)
- Photoperiodism (day length)
- Vernalization (cold)

## Nutrient Reference Guide

### Essential Elements

| Element | Symbol | Role | Deficiency Symptoms |
|---------|--------|------|---------------------|
| **Nitrogen** | N | Leaf growth, chlorophyll | Yellow leaves (older first) |
| **Phosphorus** | P | Energy, roots, flowers | Purple leaves, stunted |
| **Potassium** | K | Overall health, disease resistance | Brown leaf edges |
| **Calcium** | Ca | Cell walls, root tips | Blossom end rot, tip burn |
| **Magnesium** | Mg | Chlorophyll center | Interveinal yellowing |
| **Sulfur** | S | Amino acids, proteins | Light green/yellow new leaves |
| **Iron** | Fe | Chlorophyll synthesis | Interveinal chlorosis (new) |
| **Manganese** | Mn | Photosynthesis | Interveinal chlorosis |
| **Zinc** | Zn | Enzyme activation | Small leaves, short internodes |
| **Copper** | Cu | Photosynthesis, lignin | Wilting, light green |
| **Boron** | B | Cell wall, flowers | Hollow stems, distorted growth |
| **Molybdenum** | Mo | Nitrogen fixation | Marginal scorch |
| **Chlorine** | Cl | Photosynthesis | Wilting, chlorosis |

### Mobile vs. Immobile Nutrients

```
MOBILE NUTRIENTS                    IMMOBILE NUTRIENTS
(Deficiency shows in OLD leaves)    (Deficiency shows in NEW leaves)

• Nitrogen (N)                      • Calcium (Ca)
• Phosphorus (P)                    • Sulfur (S)
• Potassium (K)                     • Iron (Fe)
• Magnesium (Mg)                    • Manganese (Mn)
• Chlorine (Cl)                     • Zinc (Zn)
• Molybdenum (Mo)                   • Copper (Cu)
                                    • Boron (B)
```

## Light Science Overview

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Target Range |
|--------|------------|------|--------------|
| **PAR** | Photosynthetically Active Radiation (400-700nm) | N/A | All visible light |
| **PPF** | Photosynthetic Photon Flux (total light output) | μmol/s | Fixture specification |
| **PPFD** | Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density (light intensity) | μmol/m²/s | 200-1000+ |
| **DLI** | Daily Light Integral (total daily light) | mol/m²/day | 12-40+ |

### DLI Requirements by Crop

| Crop | DLI (mol/m²/day) |
|------|------------------|
| Lettuce, leafy greens | 12-17 |
| Herbs | 15-20 |
| Peppers | 20-30 |
| Tomatoes | 25-35 |
| Cannabis | 35-45+ |
