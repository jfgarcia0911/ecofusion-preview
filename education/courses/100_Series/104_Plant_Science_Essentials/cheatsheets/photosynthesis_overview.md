## Course 104: Plant Science Essentials - Cheatsheet

## The Big Picture

```
    PHOTOSYNTHESIS EQUATION

    6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + LIGHT ENERGY → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂

    Carbon    Water                    Glucose   Oxygen
    Dioxide                            (Sugar)
```

**In Simple Terms:** Plants use light to combine carbon dioxide and water into sugar, releasing oxygen.

## Light Reactions Summary

```
    THYLAKOID MEMBRANE

         Light          Light
           │              │
           ▼              ▼
      [Photosystem II] → [Photosystem I] → NADPH
           │
           ▼
        H₂O → O₂ + H⁺

           │
           └──────► ATP (via ATP Synthase)
```

**Key Points:**
- Chlorophyll absorbs light (mostly blue & red)
- Water is split → releases O₂ (this is where our oxygen comes from!)
- Energy stored in ATP and NADPH

## Calvin Cycle Summary

```
    THE CALVIN CYCLE (3 turns = 1 G3P exit)

         CO₂
          │
          ▼
    ┌─────────────────────┐
    │   CARBON FIXATION   │
    │   RuBisCO enzyme    │
    │   CO₂ + RuBP → 2×3-PGA
    └─────────┬───────────┘
              │
              ▼
    ┌─────────────────────┐
    │     REDUCTION       │
    │   ATP + NADPH used  │
    │   3-PGA → G3P       │
    └─────────┬───────────┘
              │
      ┌───────┴───────┐
      │               │
      ▼               ▼
    [Exit]         [Recycle]
    → Glucose      → RuBP (restart)
```

**Key Points:**
- RuBisCO is the key enzyme (most abundant protein on Earth)
- Requires 6 turns to make 1 glucose
- Uses ATP and NADPH from light reactions

## C3, C4, and CAM Compared

| Feature | C3 Plants | C4 Plants | CAM Plants |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **First product** | 3-carbon (3-PGA) | 4-carbon (OAA) | 4-carbon (malate) |
| **CO₂ fixation** | Direct by RuBisCO | Pre-fixed, then Calvin | Night fixation |
| **Photorespiration** | High | Low | Low |
| **Best climate** | Cool, moist | Hot, sunny | Arid |
| **Examples** | Lettuce, wheat, rice | Corn, sugarcane | Cacti, pineapple |
| **Water efficiency** | Low | Medium | High |

```
    C4: SPATIAL SEPARATION          CAM: TEMPORAL SEPARATION
    (Different cells)               (Different times)

    Mesophyll → CO₂ fixed           NIGHT: Stomata open
         ↓                                  CO₂ → Malate
    Bundle sheath → Calvin cycle            (stored)

                                    DAY:   Stomata closed
                                           Malate → CO₂
                                           Calvin cycle runs
```

## Factors Affecting Photosynthesis

### Light Intensity

```
    Photosynthesis
    Rate
        │                    ┌──────────────
        │                   /  Light saturated
        │                  /   (CO₂ or temp limited)
        │                 /
        │                /
        │               /
        │              /  Light limited
        │_____________/
        └─────────────────────────────────
                    Light Intensity (PPFD)
```

### CO₂ Concentration

| Level | Typical |
|-------|---------|
| Ambient | ~420 ppm |
| Optimal C3 | 800-1200 ppm |
| Diminishing returns | >1500 ppm |

### Temperature

- Too cold: Enzymes slow
- Optimal: Species-specific (20-30°C typical)
- Too hot: Enzymes denature, stomata close

## Key Light Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Unit |
|--------|------------------|------|
| **PAR** | Usable light range | 400-700 nm |
| **PPF** | Total light output (fixture) | μmol/s |
| **PPFD** | Light intensity at a point | μmol/m²/s |
| **DLI** | Total daily light | mol/m²/day |

### DLI Quick Calculator

```
    DLI = PPFD × Hours × 0.0036

    Example: 400 PPFD × 16 hours × 0.0036 = 23 DLI
```

### DLI Targets

| Crop | DLI (mol/m²/day) |
|------|------------------|
| Lettuce | 12-17 |
| Herbs | 15-20 |
| Tomatoes | 25-35 |
| Peppers | 20-30 |

## Quick Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Slow growth | Insufficient light | Increase PPFD or photoperiod |
| Stretched plants | Low light intensity | Raise PPFD, add blue light |
| Light bleaching | Too much light | Raise fixture, reduce intensity |
| Yellow leaves | Multiple factors | Check: light, N, pH |

**Golden Rule:** Photosynthesis is limited by whichever factor is in shortest supply. Optimize the limiting factor first!
