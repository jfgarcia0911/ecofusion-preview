## DLI Quick Calculator

```
    FORMULA:
    DLI = PPFD × Hours × 0.0036

    EXAMPLES:
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  PPFD     Hours     ×0.0036    =    DLI                │
    ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │  200   ×   16    ×  0.0036    =    11.5 mol/m²/day    │
    │  300   ×   16    ×  0.0036    =    17.3 mol/m²/day    │
    │  400   ×   16    ×  0.0036    =    23.0 mol/m²/day    │
    │  500   ×   14    ×  0.0036    =    25.2 mol/m²/day    │
    │  600   ×   12    ×  0.0036    =    25.9 mol/m²/day    │
```

## Spectrum Effects

```
    WAVELENGTH EFFECTS ON PLANTS

    UV (280-400nm)
    ══════════════
    • Secondary metabolites ↑
    • Compact growth
    • Stress response
    • Use sparingly!

    BLUE (400-500nm)
    ════════════════
    • Compact growth ✓
    • Chlorophyll synthesis ✓
    • Stomatal opening
    • Vegetative development

    GREEN (500-600nm)
    ═════════════════
    • Canopy penetration ✓
    • Lower leaf photosynthesis
    • Working visibility

    RED (600-700nm)
    ═══════════════
    • Photosynthesis efficiency ✓
    • Flowering promotion
    • Stem elongation

    FAR-RED (700-800nm)
    ═══════════════════
    • Stem elongation
    • Shade avoidance
    • Flowering signals
    • R:FR ratio important
```

## Photoperiod Quick Reference

```
    PHOTOPERIOD RESPONSES

    SHORT-DAY PLANTS (flower when nights are LONG)
    ═════════════════════════════════════════════
    • Cannabis, chrysanthemums, poinsettias
    • Need 12+ hours uninterrupted darkness
    • Night interruption prevents flowering

    LONG-DAY PLANTS (flower when nights are SHORT)
    ══════════════════════════════════════════════
    • Lettuce (bolting), spinach, wheat
    • Long photoperiods trigger flowering
    • Short days keep vegetative

    DAY-NEUTRAL PLANTS (flower by age/size)
    ═══════════════════════════════════════
    • Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers
    • Flower based on maturity, not photoperiod
    • Can use any reasonable photoperiod
```

## Common Light Problems

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Stretching | Too little light | Increase PPFD, lower fixture |
| Pale color | Insufficient DLI | Increase PPFD or photoperiod |
| Bleaching | Too much light | Raise fixture, dim if possible |
| Leaf curl (up) | Light/heat stress | Raise fixture, improve cooling |
| Slow flowering | Wrong photoperiod | Adjust for crop type |
| Bolting | Long days (leafy greens) | Reduce photoperiod |

## Quick Light Audit

```
    LIGHT ASSESSMENT CHECKLIST

    □ What is PPFD at canopy level?
    □ How many hours of light per day?
    □ Calculate DLI: PPFD × hours × 0.0036
    □ Compare to crop requirements
    □ Check uniformity across grow area
    □ Is spectrum appropriate for stage?
    □ Any signs of light stress?
```
