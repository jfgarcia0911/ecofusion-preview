# Course 104: Plant Science Essentials - Grading Rubric

## Course Assessment Overview

This rubric defines the evaluation criteria for all graded components in Course 104: Plant Science Essentials.

---

## Assessment Components

### Total Points: 150

| Assessment Type | Quantity | Points Each | Total Points |
|----------------|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Module Quizzes | 10 | 5 | 50 |
| Lab Activities | 3 | 10 | 30 |
| Leaf Analysis Project | 1 | 20 | 20 |
| In-Class Assignments | 3 | 10-15 | 35 |
| Take-Home Assignment | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| Project | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| Final Exam | 1 | 50 | 50 |

## Grading Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Points |
|-------|------------|--------|
| A | 90-100% | 135-150 |
| B (Passing) | 80-89% | 120-134 |

**Passing Threshold:** 80% (120 points)

---

## Assignment Rubrics

### In-Class Assignments (35 points total)

#### Assignment 1: Plant Anatomy Diagram (10 points)
- Accuracy of structures: 5 points
- Labeling and detail: 3 points
- Function annotations: 2 points

#### Assignment 2: Nutrient Deficiency Diagnosis (15 points)
- Correct identification: 7 points
- Symptom analysis: 4 points
- Treatment recommendations: 4 points

#### Assignment 3: Light Calculations (15 points)
- DLI calculations: 6 points
- PPFD measurements: 5 points
- Application recommendations: 4 points

### Take-Home Assignment (15 points)
- Photosynthesis explanation: 7 points
- Application to growing: 5 points
- Professional quality: 3 points

### Project (15 points)
- Research quality: 6 points
- Analysis depth: 5 points
- Documentation: 4 points
