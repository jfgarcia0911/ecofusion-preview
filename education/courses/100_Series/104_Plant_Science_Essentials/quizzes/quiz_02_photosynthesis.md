## Fill in the Blank

**9. The two products of the light reactions that are used in the Calvin cycle are ATP and _____________.**

---

**10. Photorespiration is wasteful because RuBisCO grabs _____________ instead of CO₂, which doesn't produce useful products.**

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) Thylakoid membranes** - Light reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes where chlorophyll is located.

2. **C) Water** - Water is split during the light reactions (photolysis), releasing O₂.

3. **C) Fixing CO₂ into organic compounds** - RuBisCO catalyzes the first step of the Calvin cycle, attaching CO₂ to RuBP.

4. **C) CAM plants** - CAM plants open stomata at night, store CO₂ as malate, and use it during the day.

5. **B) It levels off and stays constant** - At saturation, other factors (CO₂, temperature) become limiting.

6. **B) False** - Plants appear green because chlorophyll REFLECTS green light; it absorbs red and blue.

7. **A) True** - C4 plants reduce photorespiration by concentrating CO₂ in bundle sheath cells.

8. **B) False** - The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH from light reactions but doesn't directly require light.

9. **NADPH** - The light reactions produce ATP and NADPH, which power the Calvin cycle.

10. **oxygen (O₂)** - When RuBisCO binds O₂ instead of CO₂, it creates compounds that must be recycled without producing sugar.
