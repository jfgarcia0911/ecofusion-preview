## Questions

### Question 1
What are root hairs?

a) Decorative structures
b) Tiny extensions that increase surface area for absorption ✓
c) Roots of hair-like plants
d) Fungal infections

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Root hairs are single-cell extensions that dramatically increase root surface area (up to 10x), maximizing water and nutrient absorption.*

---

### Question 2
What is the root cap?

a) A protective covering over the root tip ✓
b) The top part of the root
c) A disease
d) Root hair cluster

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: The root cap protects the delicate growing tip as roots push through soil or media. It secretes mucilage to aid penetration.*

---

### Question 3
Where is the zone of cell division in roots?

a) At the root tip, behind the cap ✓
b) At the base of the stem
c) In older root sections
d) Only in leaves

**Correct Answer: a**

*Explanation: The apical meristem (zone of cell division) is just behind the root cap where new cells are produced for root growth.*

---

### Question 4
What is the purpose of the Casparian strip?

a) Decorative pattern on roots
b) Controls what enters the vascular system by forcing selective uptake ✓
c) Provides root color
d) Stores food

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: The Casparian strip is a waxy barrier in root endodermis that forces water and nutrients through cell membranes, enabling selective filtering.*

---

### Question 5
What is the difference between xylem and phloem?

a) They're the same thing
b) Xylem transports water up; phloem transports sugars throughout plant ✓
c) Xylem is only in leaves
d) Phloem transports water only

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Xylem vessels transport water and minerals upward from roots. Phloem sieve tubes transport sugars and nutrients bidirectionally throughout the plant.*

---

### Question 6
Why do roots need oxygen?

a) For photosynthesis
b) For aerobic respiration to power nutrient uptake and growth ✓
c) They don't need oxygen
d) To produce flowers

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Roots respire aerobically, requiring oxygen to produce ATP energy for active nutrient transport, growth, and cellular functions.*

---

### Question 7
What happens if roots lack oxygen?

a) They grow faster
b) Roots suffocate, turn brown, and die (root rot) ✓
c) Nothing changes
d) They photosynthesize instead

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Without oxygen, roots can't respire efficiently. Anaerobic conditions lead to root death, brown/mushy roots, and opportunistic pathogen attacks.*

---

### Question 8
What is gravitropism?

a) Fear of gravity
b) Root growth response to gravity (downward growth) ✓
c) Plant floating ability
d) Leaf orientation

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Gravitropism causes roots to grow downward (positive gravitropism) in response to gravity, while shoots grow upward (negative gravitropism).*

---

### Question 9
How do roots absorb nutrients?

a) By passive diffusion only
b) Through active transport using energy and selective uptake ✓
c) They don't absorb nutrients
d) Only through leaf surfaces

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Roots use active transport (requiring ATP energy) to selectively absorb nutrients against concentration gradients, plus some passive diffusion.*

---

### Question 10
What is root zone temperature's effect on nutrient uptake?

a) No effect
b) Temperature affects membrane function and uptake rates ✓
c) Hotter is always better
d) Colder is always better

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Root zone temperature affects membrane fluidity, enzyme activity, and metabolic rate. Too cold slows uptake; too hot damages membranes.*
