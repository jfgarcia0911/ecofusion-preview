## Questions

### Question 1
What is plant respiration?

a) Process of making oxygen
b) Process of breaking down sugars to release energy ✓
c) Absorbing water through roots
d) Producing flowers

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Respiration is the cellular process where plants break down glucose (from photosynthesis) to produce ATP energy, releasing CO₂ and water.*

---

### Question 2
When does plant respiration occur?

a) Only during the day
b) Only at night
c) 24 hours a day, continuously ✓
d) Only when photosynthesis stops

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Plants respire continuously, day and night. During the day, photosynthesis produces more oxygen than respiration consumes.*

---

### Question 3
What is the simplified equation for respiration?

a) CO₂ + H₂O → Sugar + O₂
b) Sugar + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy ✓
c) N₂ → Protein
d) Light + Water → Food

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Respiration is essentially the reverse of photosynthesis: C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + ATP (energy).*

---

### Question 4
What is ATP?

a) A type of fertilizer
b) The energy currency molecule in cells ✓
c) A plant hormone
d) A disease

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: ATP (adenosine triphosphate) is the universal energy molecule. Respiration produces ATP, which powers all cellular activities.*

---

### Question 5
Why do plants need a dark period (night)?

a) They don't—24-hour light is always better
b) For repair, growth processes, and to convert photosynthesis products ✓
c) To sleep like animals
d) Darkness has no purpose

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Dark periods allow plants to convert photosynthesis products into usable compounds, perform growth and repair, and maintain healthy metabolism.*

---

### Question 6
What happens to respiration rate at higher temperatures?

a) It decreases significantly
b) It stays exactly the same
c) It increases up to a point, then declines ✓
d) It stops completely

**Correct Answer: c**

*Explanation: Respiration rate roughly doubles for every 10°C increase, until temperatures become damaging (>95°F) and enzymes denature.*

---

### Question 7
Where in plant cells does respiration occur?

a) Only in leaves
b) In mitochondria of all living cells ✓
c) In chloroplasts only
d) In the nucleus

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Mitochondria are the "powerhouses" where respiration occurs. Every living plant cell has mitochondria and respires.*

---

### Question 8
What is the relationship between photosynthesis and respiration?

a) They're the same process
b) They're opposite processes that balance each other ✓
c) They never occur in the same plant
d) Respiration destroys what photosynthesis makes

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Photosynthesis builds sugars using light energy; respiration breaks sugars down to release that energy. Both are essential and complementary.*

---

### Question 9
Why do roots respire even though they're underground?

a) They don't respire
b) All living cells need energy, including root cells ✓
c) Only to annoy growers
d) Roots photosynthesize underground

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Roots need energy for nutrient uptake, growth, and maintenance. They respire aerobically, requiring oxygen in the root zone.*

---

### Question 10
What is the carbon balance in plants?

a) Always positive (make more than use)
b) Net difference between photosynthesis (C gain) and respiration (C loss) ✓
c) Always negative
d) Has nothing to do with carbon

**Correct Answer: b**

*Explanation: Carbon balance = photosynthesis minus respiration. Positive balance = plant growth; negative = plant uses stored reserves and may decline.*
