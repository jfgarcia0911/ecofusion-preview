## Fill in the Blank

**9. The _____________ is the plant tissue responsible for active cell division and growth, found at root tips and shoot tips.**

---

**10. When you remove the apical meristem (topping a plant), the lateral buds are released from _____________ dominance and begin to grow.**

---

## Answer Key

1. **C) Vacuole** - The central vacuole fills with water, creating turgor pressure that keeps cells firm.

2. **C) Root hair zone (maturation zone)** - Root hairs dramatically increase surface area for absorption.

3. **B) To force water and solutes through selective membranes** - The waxy Casparian strip blocks the apoplastic pathway, ensuring the plant controls what enters the vascular system.

4. **B) Xylem** - Xylem transports water and minerals upward; phloem transports sugars.

5. **C) Lower epidermis** - Most stomata are on the lower leaf surface to reduce water loss.

6. **B) False** - Root hairs are temporary, living only days to weeks before being replaced.

7. **A) True** - Palisade mesophyll is optimized for photosynthesis with densely packed chloroplasts.

8. **B) False** - Dicots have vascular bundles arranged in a ring; monocots have scattered bundles.

9. **meristem** (or meristematic tissue)

10. **apical** - Apical dominance is maintained by auxin from the shoot tip.
