### Questions

**1. Which renewable energy source is most commonly integrated with CEA facilities?**

A) Geothermal
B) Solar photovoltaic (PV)
C) Tidal power
D) Hydroelectric dams

**Answer: B) Solar photovoltaic (PV)**

---

**2. What is "net metering" for solar installations?**

A) Measuring fishing nets
B) Credit system where excess solar generation is fed to grid and credited against future consumption
C) Internet speed measurement
D) Equipment weighing

**Answer: B) Credit system for excess solar generation fed to grid**

---

**3. What is the primary challenge of solar energy for CEA facilities?**

A) Too much energy generated
B) Intermittency (sun doesn't shine at night or consistently) requiring storage or grid connection
C) Solar panels are too small
D) No challenges exist

**Answer: B) Intermittency requiring storage or grid connection**

---

**4. What is "energy storage" and why is it important for renewable integration?**

A) Storing food energy
B) Capturing excess renewable energy (e.g., in batteries) for use when generation is low
C) Warehouse space
D) Employee energy levels

**Answer: B) Capturing excess renewable energy for use when generation is low**

---

**5. What is a "microgrid"?**

A) Small electrical grid
B) Localized energy system that can operate independently or connected to main grid
C) Microscopic grid patterns
D) Social media platform

**Answer: B) Localized energy system that can operate independently or connected to main grid**

---

**6. What is the typical payback period for commercial solar installations?**

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-5 years
C) 7-12 years
D) 25-30 years

**Answer: C) 7-12 years**

---

**7. What is "Power Purchase Agreement" (PPA) in renewable energy?**

A) Agreement to purchase equipment
B) Contract to purchase electricity from a renewable energy provider at a fixed rate
C) Employee purchase program
D) Product purchasing agreement

**Answer: B) Contract to purchase electricity from renewable energy provider**

---

**8. What percentage of CEA operational costs is typically energy?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-20%
C) 30-40%
D) 60-70%

**Answer: C) 30-40%**

---

**9. What is "renewable energy credit" (REC)?**

A) Credit card for renewable purchases
B) Tradable certificate representing the environmental attributes of renewable energy generation
C) Employee recognition
D) Financial loan for renewables

**Answer: B) Tradable certificate representing environmental attributes of renewable generation**

---

**10. What is a co-benefit of integrating renewable energy in CEA?**

A) No benefits exist
B) Reduced operating costs over time, energy independence, and reduced carbon footprint
C) Increased complexity only
D) Higher costs with no benefits

**Answer: B) Reduced operating costs, energy independence, and reduced carbon footprint**
