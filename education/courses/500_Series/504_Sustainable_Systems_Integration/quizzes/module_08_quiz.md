### Questions

**1. What is the "Zero Waste Hierarchy"?**

A) Employee organizational chart
B) Prioritized approach: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Rot (compost), with disposal as last resort
C) Building elevation levels
D) Random waste categories

**Answer: B) Prioritized approach: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Rot, with disposal as last resort**

---

**2. What percentage waste diversion rate is typically required for "Zero Waste" certification?**

A) 50%
B) 70%
C) 90%+
D) 100% (perfect)

**Answer: C) 90%+**

---

**3. What is "waste stream analysis"?**

A) River pollution studies
B) Systematic assessment of waste types, quantities, and sources to identify reduction opportunities
C) Streaming waste data online
D) Water flow analysis

**Answer: B) Systematic assessment of waste types, quantities, and sources**

---

**4. In CEA, what is the largest waste stream by volume?**

A) Office paper
B) Plant biomass (stems, leaves, roots)
C) Plastic packaging
D) Equipment

**Answer: B) Plant biomass (stems, leaves, roots)**

---

**5. What is "composting" and why is it valuable for CEA?**

A) Musical composition
B) Aerobic decomposition of organic matter creating nutrient-rich soil amendment, closing nutrient loops
C) Computer processing
D) Employee composition

**Answer: B) Aerobic decomposition of organic matter creating nutrient-rich amendment**

---

**6. What is "source reduction" in waste management?**

A) Reducing water sources
B) Preventing waste generation at the source through design, purchasing, and operational choices
C) Reducing employee headcount
D) Shrinking facilities

**Answer: B) Preventing waste generation at the source**

---

**7. What is "vermicomposting"?**

A) Computer verification
B) Using worms to decompose organic waste into valuable compost
C) Vermiculite production
D) Pest control method

**Answer: B) Using worms to decompose organic waste into compost**

---

**8. What is the environmental benefit of diverting organic waste from landfills?**

A) No benefit
B) Reduces methane emissions (potent greenhouse gas) and recovers nutrients
C) Increases landfill space
D) Creates more waste

**Answer: B) Reduces methane emissions and recovers nutrients**

---

**9. What is "industrial ecology" in waste management?**

A) Factory landscaping
B) Systems approach where waste from one process becomes input for another, mimicking natural ecosystems
C) Employee ecology training
D) Equipment ecology

**Answer: B) Systems approach where waste from one process becomes input for another**

---

**10. What is a "waste audit"?**

A) Financial audit of waste costs
B) Systematic measurement and analysis of waste generation to establish baseline and identify opportunities
C) Inspecting garbage bins
D) Employee waste training

**Answer: B) Systematic measurement and analysis of waste generation**
