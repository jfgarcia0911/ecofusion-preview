### Questions

**1. What is the "Energy-Water-Food Nexus"?**

A) Three separate systems
B) The interconnected relationship between energy, water, and food systems where actions in one affect the others
C) A conference name
D) Three unrelated industries

**Answer: B) The interconnected relationship between energy, water, and food systems**

---

**2. Approximately what percentage of global freshwater withdrawals are used for agriculture?**

A) 10%
B) 30%
C) 50%
D) 70%

**Answer: D) 70%**

---

**3. What percentage of global energy consumption is used in the food system?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-20%
C) 30-35%
D) 50-60%

**Answer: C) 30-35%**

---

**4. In CEA, what is a major energy consumer?**

A) Employee computers only
B) HVAC and lighting systems
C) Office furniture
D) Parking lots

**Answer: B) HVAC and lighting systems**

---

**5. What is "nexus thinking" in sustainable systems integration?**

A) Connecting wires
B) Considering interdependencies and optimizing across energy, water, and food systems holistically
C) Networking events
D) Sequential planning

**Answer: B) Considering interdependencies and optimizing across systems holistically**

---

**6. What is a co-benefit of integrating water recirculation in CEA?**

A) No benefits exist
B) Reduced water use AND reduced energy for water pumping and treatment
C) Increased costs only
D) More complexity only

**Answer: B) Reduced water use AND reduced energy for pumping and treatment**

---

**7. What is "trade-off analysis" in nexus optimization?**

A) International trade agreements
B) Evaluating how improving one nexus element might negatively impact another to find optimal balance
C) Employee swap programs
D) Product exchanges

**Answer: B) Evaluating how improving one element might impact another to find balance**

---

**8. How can CEA reduce the water-energy nexus pressure compared to field agriculture?**

A) It cannot
B) By using recirculating systems that dramatically reduce water needs and locating near consumption to reduce transport energy
C) By using more resources
D) By avoiding the nexus entirely

**Answer: B) By using recirculating systems and locating near consumption**

---

**9. What is "virtual water" in agricultural products?**

A) Computer simulations
B) The total volume of water used to produce a product, including embedded water in production
C) Digital water meters
D) Online water sales

**Answer: B) The total volume of water used to produce a product**

---

**10. What strategy addresses all three nexus elements in CEA?**

A) Ignoring sustainability
B) Renewable energy-powered facilities with water recirculation and optimized climate control for food production
C) Outsourcing everything
D) Traditional agriculture only

**Answer: B) Renewable energy-powered facilities with water recirculation and climate control**
