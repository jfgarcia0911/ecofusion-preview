### Questions

**1. What does "carbon neutrality" mean?**

A) Ignoring carbon emissions
B) Achieving net zero carbon emissions by balancing emissions with removal or offsetting
C) Using only carbon-based fuels
D) Eliminating all carbon from products

**Answer: B) Achieving net zero carbon emissions by balancing emissions with removal or offsetting**

---

**2. What are "Scope 1 emissions"?**

A) First-year emissions
B) Direct emissions from owned or controlled sources (e.g., on-site combustion, company vehicles)
C) Employee commuting
D) Supply chain emissions

**Answer: B) Direct emissions from owned or controlled sources**

---

**3. What are "Scope 2 emissions"?**

A) Secondary importance emissions
B) Indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling
C) Customer use of products
D) Employee travel

**Answer: B) Indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling**

---

**4. What are "Scope 3 emissions"?**

A) Third-party audits
B) All other indirect emissions in the value chain (supply chain, transportation, product use, waste)
C) Third-year emissions
D) Emissions from three facilities

**Answer: B) All other indirect emissions in the value chain**

---

**5. What is "carbon footprinting"?**

A) Measuring shoe sizes
B) Calculating total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an organization, product, or activity
C) Tracking foot traffic
D) Carbon dating

**Answer: B) Calculating total greenhouse gas emissions**

---

**6. What is the hierarchy of carbon reduction strategies (most to least preferred)?**

A) Offset, Reduce, Avoid
B) Avoid, Reduce, Offset/Remove
C) Remove, Offset, Reduce
D) All strategies are equal

**Answer: B) Avoid, Reduce, Offset/Remove**

---

**7. What is a "carbon offset"?**

A) Accounting error
B) Reduction in emissions in one place to compensate for emissions elsewhere (e.g., funding renewable energy or reforestation)
C) Time zone adjustment
D) Price discount

**Answer: B) Reduction in emissions in one place to compensate for emissions elsewhere**

---

**8. What is "carbon sequestration"?**

A) Organizing carbon data
B) Long-term storage of carbon dioxide removed from the atmosphere
C) Carbon trading
D) Carbon taxation

**Answer: B) Long-term storage of carbon dioxide removed from the atmosphere**

---

**9. What GHG Protocol is the international standard for carbon accounting?**

A) ISO 9001
B) Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHG Protocol)
C) HACCP
D) OSHA

**Answer: B) Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHG Protocol)**

---

**10. How can CEA facilities reduce Scope 2 emissions?**

A) By increasing energy use
B) By sourcing renewable energy (solar, wind, renewable energy credits) for electricity
C) By ignoring electricity sources
D) By using only fossil fuels

**Answer: B) By sourcing renewable energy for electricity**
