### Questions

**1. What is "water stewardship"?**

A) Managing employee beverages
B) Responsible use and management of water resources to benefit people and nature
C) Water sales management
D) Irrigation scheduling only

**Answer: B) Responsible use and management of water resources to benefit people and nature**

---

**2. What is a "water footprint"?**

A) Wet footprints
B) Total volume of freshwater used to produce goods and services consumed by an individual or organization
C) Puddle sizes
D) Water meter readings

**Answer: B) Total volume of freshwater used to produce goods and services**

---

**3. Compared to field agriculture, CEA can reduce water use by approximately:**

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50-70%
D) 90-95%

**Answer: D) 90-95%**

---

**4. What is "source water protection"?**

A) Protecting water bottles
B) Protecting the quality and quantity of water sources (aquifers, rivers, lakes) from contamination and depletion
C) Employee water access
D) Water heater protection

**Answer: B) Protecting water sources from contamination and depletion**

---

**5. What is the "Alliance for Water Stewardship" (AWS) Standard?**

A) Water sports organization
B) International framework for responsible water use with certification
C) Employee alliance
D) Water equipment brand

**Answer: B) International framework for responsible water use with certification**

---

**6. What are the three components of water footprint?**

A) Blue, Green, Grey
B) Red, Yellow, Blue
C) Fresh, Salt, Brackish
D) Clean, Dirty, Recycled

**Answer: A) Blue, Green, Grey**

---

**7. What does "blue water footprint" represent?**

A) Ocean water
B) Consumption of surface and groundwater resources
C) Blue-colored water
D) Swimming pools

**Answer: B) Consumption of surface and groundwater resources**

---

**8. What is "rainwater harvesting" in CEA context?**

A) Collecting employee ideas
B) Collecting and storing rainwater from roofs and surfaces for use in operations
C) Weather prediction
D) Crop harvesting in rain

**Answer: B) Collecting and storing rainwater from roofs for use in operations**

---

**9. What is "water quality management" in CEA?**

A) Taste testing
B) Monitoring and controlling water parameters (pH, EC, pathogens) to ensure optimal growing conditions and safety
C) Employee drinking water only
D) Aesthetic water features

**Answer: B) Monitoring and controlling water parameters for optimal growing and safety**

---

**10. What is "community water stewardship"?**

A) Community swimming pools
B) Engaging with local stakeholders on shared water challenges and collaborative solutions
C) Municipal water billing
D) Neighborhood watch

**Answer: B) Engaging with local stakeholders on shared water challenges**
