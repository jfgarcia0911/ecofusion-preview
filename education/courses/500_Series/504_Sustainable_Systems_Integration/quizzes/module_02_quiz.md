### Questions

**1. What is "biomimicry" in sustainable design?**

A) Copying competitor designs
B) Learning from and mimicking nature's strategies to solve human design challenges
C) Biological research only
D) Animal behavior studies

**Answer: B) Learning from and mimicking nature's strategies to solve human design challenges**

---

**2. What does "Life Cycle Assessment" (LCA) evaluate?**

A) Employee career paths
B) Environmental impacts of a product or system throughout its entire life from raw materials to disposal
C) Plant growth cycles only
D) Building lifespan

**Answer: B) Environmental impacts of a product or system throughout its entire life from raw materials to disposal**

---

**3. What are the four phases of a Life Cycle Assessment?**

A) Design, Build, Operate, Demolish
B) Goal and Scope Definition, Inventory Analysis, Impact Assessment, Interpretation
C) Plan, Do, Check, Act
D) Research, Develop, Test, Deploy

**Answer: B) Goal and Scope Definition, Inventory Analysis, Impact Assessment, Interpretation**

---

**4. What is "cradle-to-cradle" design?**

A) Designing children's furniture
B) Creating products where all materials can be continuously cycled as nutrients or technical materials
C) Product lifecycle from birth to death
D) Recycling programs only

**Answer: B) Creating products where all materials can be continuously cycled as nutrients or technical materials**

---

**5. What is "regenerative design"?**

A) Designing generators
B) Systems that restore, renew, and revitalize their own sources of energy and materials
C) Copying old designs
D) Equipment repair procedures

**Answer: B) Systems that restore, renew, and revitalize their own sources of energy and materials**

---

**6. What is "performance-based design"?**

A) Design focused on aesthetics only
B) Design approach focused on achieving specific performance outcomes rather than prescriptive solutions
C) Employee performance reviews
D) Theater stage design

**Answer: B) Design approach focused on achieving specific performance outcomes**

---

**7. What is the primary principle of "integrated design"?**

A) Combining different colors
B) Coordinating all building systems and components to work together synergistically for optimal performance
C) Software integration
D) Team building activities

**Answer: B) Coordinating all building systems and components to work together synergistically**

---

**8. In biomimicry, what does nature's 3.8 billion years of R&D teach us?**

A) Nothing useful
B) Strategies for efficiency, resilience, and sustainability that have been tested and refined through evolution
C) How to be slower
D) Only biological processes

**Answer: B) Strategies for efficiency, resilience, and sustainability tested through evolution**

---

**9. What is "embodied energy" in building materials?**

A) Energy stored in batteries
B) Total energy consumed to extract, manufacture, transport, and install materials
C) Employee energy levels
D) Solar energy collection

**Answer: B) Total energy consumed to extract, manufacture, transport, and install materials**

---

**10. What is the goal of "closed-loop" systems in integrated design?**

A) Creating circular pathways
B) Eliminating waste by cycling all outputs back as inputs in continuous loops
C) Security systems
D) Closed facilities

**Answer: B) Eliminating waste by cycling all outputs back as inputs in continuous loops**
