### Questions

**1. What is the fundamental principle of a "circular economy"?**

A) Circular facilities
B) Designing out waste and pollution, keeping products and materials in use, and regenerating natural systems
C) Round-trip shipping
D) Circular reasoning

**Answer: B) Designing out waste, keeping materials in use, and regenerating natural systems**

---

**2. What are the "9 R's" of circular economy? (Select the category they fall into)**

A) Reduce, Reuse, Recycle only
B) Refuse, Rethink, Reduce, Reuse, Repair, Refurbish, Remanufacture, Repurpose, Recycle
C) Three R's repeated
D) Random sustainability terms

**Answer: B) Refuse, Rethink, Reduce, Reuse, Repair, Refurbish, Remanufacture, Repurpose, Recycle**

---

**3. What is "industrial symbiosis"?**

A) Corporate mergers
B) Collaboration where waste or by-products from one industry become inputs for another
C) Employee cooperation
D) Biological partnerships

**Answer: B) Collaboration where waste from one industry becomes inputs for another**

---

**4. What is "waste valorization"?**

A) Calculating waste disposal costs
B) Converting waste materials into valuable products, energy, or resources
C) Waste incineration only
D) Landfill management

**Answer: B) Converting waste materials into valuable products, energy, or resources**

---

**5. In CEA, what is an example of circular economy practice?**

A) Sending all waste to landfills
B) Composting plant waste and using it to grow inputs or generate energy
C) Buying new equipment yearly
D) Single-use packaging only

**Answer: B) Composting plant waste and using it to grow inputs or generate energy**

---

**6. What is "material flow analysis" (MFA)?**

A) Fluid dynamics
B) Systematic assessment of flows and stocks of materials within a defined system
C) Employee movement tracking
D) Traffic analysis

**Answer: B) Systematic assessment of flows and stocks of materials within a system**

---

**7. What is the "Ellen MacArthur Foundation" known for?**

A) Art galleries
B) Leading global organization promoting the circular economy transition
C) Traditional economics
D) Linear economy advocacy

**Answer: B) Leading global organization promoting the circular economy transition**

---

**8. What circular economy business model involves retaining product ownership and selling services?**

A) Traditional sales
B) Product-as-a-Service (PaaS) or performance-based models
C) Wholesale distribution
D) Retail only

**Answer: B) Product-as-a-Service (PaaS) or performance-based models**

---

**9. What is "design for disassembly"?**

A) Designing weak products
B) Designing products to be easily taken apart for repair, refurbishment, or material recovery
C) Demolition planning
D) Avoiding assembly

**Answer: B) Designing products to be easily taken apart for repair or material recovery**

---

**10. How does CEA contribute to circular economy compared to traditional agriculture?**

A) It doesn't
B) Closed-loop water and nutrient systems, reduced food waste through local production, and resource efficiency
C) By using more resources
D) By increasing waste

**Answer: B) Closed-loop systems, reduced food waste, and resource efficiency**
