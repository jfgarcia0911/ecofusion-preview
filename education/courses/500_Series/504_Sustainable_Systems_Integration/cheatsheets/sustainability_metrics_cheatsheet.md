# Sustainability Metrics Quick Reference

## Environmental Metrics

### Energy
```
Energy Use Intensity (EUI)
Formula: kWh / kg produced OR kWh / m² / year
Target: <8 kWh/kg (excellent: <5 kWh/kg)

Renewable Energy Percentage
Formula: (Renewable kWh / Total kWh) × 100%
Target: >60% (excellent: 100%)

Energy Productivity
Formula: $ Revenue / kWh consumed
Target: Increasing year-over-year
```

### Water
```
Water Use Efficiency
Formula: L water / kg produced
Target: <10 L/kg (excellent: <5 L/kg)

Water Recycling Rate
Formula: (Recycled water / Total water) × 100%
Target: >90% (excellent: >95%)

Water Productivity
Formula: $ Revenue / m³ water
Target: >$500/m³ (excellent: >$1,000/m³)
```

### Carbon
```
Carbon Intensity
Formula: kg CO2e / kg produced
Target: <2.0 (excellent: <1.0, best: negative)

Scope Calculation:
Scope 1: Direct emissions (gas, vehicles)
Scope 2: Purchased electricity emissions
Scope 3: Value chain emissions

Total Footprint = Scope 1 + Scope 2 + Scope 3
```

### Waste
```
Diversion Rate
Formula: (Recycled + Composted + Reused) / Total Waste × 100%
Target: >90% (excellent: >95%)

Waste Intensity
Formula: kg waste / kg produced
Target: <0.15 (excellent: <0.10)

Circularity Rate
Formula: (Recycled + Reused) / Total Material Input × 100%
Target: >60% (excellent: >80%)
```

## Social Metrics

### Employment
```
Living Wage Ratio
Formula: Average wage / Living wage
Target: >1.10 (excellent: >1.20)

Turnover Rate
Formula: (Departures / Average employees) × 100%
Target: <15% (excellent: <10%)

Employee Engagement
Survey-based score
Target: >75% (excellent: >85%)
```

### Safety
```
TRIR (Total Recordable Incident Rate)
Formula: (Incidents × 200,000) / Total hours worked
Target: <2.0 (excellent: <1.0)

LTIFR (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate)
Formula: (Lost time injuries × 1,000,000) / Total hours
Target: <0.5 (excellent: 0)
```

### Diversity
```
Diversity Index
Formula: 1 - Σ(proportion of group i)²
Target: >0.60 (higher = more diverse)

Pay Equity Ratio
Formula: Underrepresented pay / Majority pay (same role)
Target: 0.95-1.05 (within 5%)
```

## Economic Metrics

### Sustainability ROI
```
Sustainability Investment ROI
Formula: (Annual benefits - Annual costs) / Investment
Target: >15% (excellent: >25%)

Payback Period
Formula: Investment / Annual net benefit
Target: <5 years (excellent: <3 years)

NPV (Net Present Value)
Formula: Σ(Benefit_year / (1+discount rate)^year) - Investment
Target: >$0 (positive)
```

## Reporting Metrics

### B Impact Assessment
```
Minimum Score: 80/200 points
Categories:
- Governance: 18 points possible
- Workers: 35 points
- Community: 40 points
- Environment: 35 points
- Customers: 7 points (if applicable)

Median Score: 50.9
Good Score: 80-100
Excellent Score: 100-120+
```

### GRI Disclosures
```
Essential Disclosures:
- GRI 302: Energy
- GRI 303: Water
- GRI 305: Emissions
- GRI 306: Waste
- GRI 401: Employment
- GRI 403: Occupational Health & Safety

Material Topics: 8-12 topics typical
Reporting Frequency: Annual
Assurance: Recommended for key metrics
```

## Quick Conversion Factors

### Energy
```
1 therm natural gas = 29.3 kWh
1 gallon gasoline = 33.7 kWh
1 gallon diesel = 38.6 kWh
1 ton coal = 6,150 kWh
```

### Carbon
```
Grid electricity (US average): 0.45 kg CO2e/kWh
Natural gas: 0.18 kg CO2e/kWh or 5.3 kg CO2e/therm
Gasoline: 2.31 kg CO2e/L or 8.78 kg CO2e/gallon
Diesel: 2.68 kg CO2e/L or 10.15 kg CO2e/gallon
```

### Water
```
1 cubic meter (m³) = 1,000 liters = 264 gallons
1 gallon = 3.785 liters
1 acre-foot = 1,233 m³
```

## Benchmarking Targets

### By 2030 (Science-Based)
- GHG Emissions: -50% (Scope 1+2), -30% (Scope 3) vs. baseline
- Renewable Energy: 100%
- Water Recycling: >95%
- Waste Diversion: >95%
- Living Wage: +20% above threshold
- Carbon: Net-zero or negative

### Industry-Leading CEA
- Energy: <5 kWh/kg, 100% renewable
- Water: <5 L/kg, >95% recycled
- Carbon: <1.0 kg CO2e/kg or negative
- Waste: >95% diverted
- Social: B Corp certified, living wage +15%+
- Reporting: GRI + SASB + third-party assured
