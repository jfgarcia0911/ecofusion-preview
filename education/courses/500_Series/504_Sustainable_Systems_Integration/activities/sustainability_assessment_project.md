# Sustainability Assessment & Roadmap Project

## Activity Overview
**Type:** Comprehensive Capstone Project
**Duration:** 20-30 hours
**Format:** Individual or team (2-4 people)
**Deliverables:** Written report (30-50 pages) + Presentation (20 minutes)

## Purpose
Apply course concepts to conduct a comprehensive sustainability assessment of a real or hypothetical CEA facility and develop a strategic implementation roadmap.

---

## Part 1: Facility Profile (5% of grade)

### Task
Create a detailed profile of your CEA facility (real or hypothetical).

### Required Information
- **Location:** City, state, climate zone
- **Size:** Production area (m²), total facility area
- **Crop Type(s):** Primary crops and annual production volume
- **Technology:** Growing system (hydroponic/aquaponic/aeroponic), lighting type
- **Energy Source:** Current energy mix and annual consumption
- **Water Source:** Municipal, well, rainwater, and annual usage
- **Workforce:** Number of employees, wage structure
- **Annual Revenue:** Approximate annual sales
- **Ownership Structure:** Private, cooperative, public company
- **Years in Operation:** Establish baseline year

### Deliverable
2-3 page facility description with key performance data

---

## Part 2: Baseline Sustainability Assessment (20% of grade)

### Task
Conduct comprehensive baseline assessment across all sustainability dimensions.

### 2.1 Environmental Baseline

**Energy:**
- Total annual consumption (kWh)
- Energy sources and percentages
- Calculate energy intensity (kWh/kg)
- Identify energy end-uses (lighting, HVAC, etc.)

**Water:**
- Total annual consumption (m³)
- Water sources
- Calculate water use efficiency (L/kg)
- Current recycling rate (%)

**Carbon:**
- Calculate Scope 1 emissions
- Calculate Scope 2 emissions
- Estimate major Scope 3 emissions
- Total carbon footprint (tons CO2e)
- Carbon intensity (kg CO2e/kg product)

**Waste:**
- Annual waste generation by category
- Current diversion rate (%)
- Major waste streams identified

### 2.2 Social Baseline

**Labor Practices:**
- Current wage vs. living wage (ratio)
- Benefits package description
- Employee turnover rate
- Safety performance (TRIR, incidents)

**Diversity:**
- Workforce demographics
- Leadership demographics
- Pay equity analysis

**Community:**
- Local employment percentage
- Community engagement programs
- Local procurement percentage

### 2.3 Economic Baseline

**Performance:**
- Annual revenue
- Operating margin
- Sustainability-related costs
- Investment in sustainability to date

### Deliverable
8-12 page baseline assessment with tables, charts, and calculations

---

## Part 3: Materiality Assessment (15% of grade)

### Task
Determine which sustainability topics are most material to your facility.

### Process

**Step 1: Stakeholder Identification**
- List all stakeholder groups (minimum 8)
- Categorize by influence and interest
- Create stakeholder map

**Step 2: Impact Identification**
- Identify 20-30 potential sustainability topics
- For each topic, describe:
  * Positive/negative impacts
  * Actual/potential impacts
  * Scale and scope

**Step 3: Significance Assessment**
- Rate each topic on scale of 1-5:
  * Significance to stakeholders
  * Significance of environmental/social impacts
- Plot on materiality matrix

**Step 4: Material Topics Selection**
- Select 8-12 most material topics
- Justify selections
- Validate with stakeholder input (if possible)

### Deliverable
4-6 page materiality assessment with stakeholder map and matrix

---

## Part 4: Gap Analysis & Target Setting (15% of grade)

### Task
Compare current performance to best practices and set science-based targets.

### 4.1 Benchmarking

Compare your facility to:
- Industry averages
- Leading CEA facilities (from case studies)
- Science-based targets (Paris Agreement)
- Certification requirements (B Corp, LEED, etc.)

Create comparison table:
```
Metric              Current    Industry Avg    Leading    Target 2030
Energy (kWh/kg)     7.5        6.0             4.5        <5.0
Water (L/kg)        12.0       8.0             4.0        <6.0
Carbon (kg CO2e/kg) 3.5        2.5             0.8        <1.0
Waste diversion     75%        80%             95%        >90%
[etc.]
```

### 4.2 Target Setting

For each material topic, set:
- **1-year targets** (quick wins)
- **3-year targets** (substantial improvement)
- **5-year targets** (transformational)
- **2030 targets** (science-based, if applicable)

Ensure targets are:
- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable (but ambitious)
- Relevant
- Time-bound

### Deliverable
5-8 page gap analysis with benchmarking table and target framework

---

## Part 5: Strategy Development (25% of grade)

### Task
Develop comprehensive strategies for each material topic.

### Required Components

For EACH material topic (8-12 topics total):

**Current State:**
- Baseline performance
- Current practices
- Gaps identified

**Strategy:**
- Overall approach
- Key initiatives (3-5 per topic)
- Technology/systems needed
- Organizational changes required

**Implementation:**
- Phasing (Year 1, 2, 3, etc.)
- Resource requirements
- Responsibilities
- Success metrics

**Economics:**
- Capital investment required
- Operating cost changes
- Revenue/savings benefits
- Payback period / ROI

### Example Strategy Format:

**Material Topic: Energy Management**

*Current State:*
- 3,500 MWh annual consumption
- 85% grid electricity (0.45 kg CO2e/kWh)
- 15% solar PV
- EUI: 7.5 kWh/kg

*Strategy: Transition to 100% Renewable + Efficiency*

*Key Initiatives:*
1. LED retrofit (500 MWh/year savings)
2. Solar PV expansion to 1.5 MW (1,800 MWh/year)
3. Battery storage 2 MWh (peak shifting)
4. HVAC optimization (300 MWh/year savings)
5. Demand response participation

*Implementation Timeline:*
- Year 1: LED retrofit, HVAC optimization
- Year 2: Solar PV Phase 1 (750 kW)
- Year 3: Battery + Solar Phase 2 (750 kW)
- Year 4: Demand response enrollment
- Year 5: 100% renewable achieved

*Economics:*
- CAPEX: $1.8M ($200K LED, $1.4M solar, $200K battery)
- Annual savings: $250K (energy) + $80K (demand charges)
- ROI: 18% annual return
- Payback: 5.5 years

*Success Metrics:*
- MWh consumption: 2,800 by Year 5
- Renewable %: 100% by Year 5
- EUI: <5.0 kWh/kg by Year 5
- Grid independence: 85% by Year 5

### Deliverable
15-20 pages of detailed strategies (approximately 1.5-2 pages per material topic)

## Part 6: Integration & Systems Analysis (10% of grade)

### Task
Demonstrate systems thinking by identifying synergies and integration opportunities.

### Required Analysis

**6.1 Nexus Analysis:**
- Map energy-water-food interdependencies
- Identify optimization opportunities
- Quantify synergies

Example:
- LED waste heat recovery reduces heating fuel (energy-energy synergy)
- Solar PV powers water treatment (energy-water synergy)
- Composting waste creates growing media (food-food circular synergy)

**6.2 Integration Opportunities:**
Identify at least 5 integration opportunities across strategies, such as:
- Combining renewable energy with water recycling systems
- Linking waste valorization with nutrient management
- Connecting employee training with safety improvements
- Integrating biodiversity programs with community engagement

**6.3 Unintended Consequences:**
For each major strategy, identify potential:
- Negative side effects
- Mitigation approaches

**6.4 Resilience Analysis:**
How do integrated strategies enhance:
- Climate resilience
- Water security
- Energy security
- Supply chain resilience
- Economic resilience

### Deliverable
4-6 pages of integration analysis with systems diagrams

---

## Part 7: Implementation Roadmap (10% of grade)

### Task
Create 5-year implementation roadmap with clear milestones and accountability.

### Roadmap Components

**7.1 Timeline:**
Create Gantt chart showing:
- All major initiatives
- Milestones
- Dependencies
- Critical path

**7.2 Resource Plan:**
- Capital budget by year
- Operating budget changes
- Staffing requirements
- External support needed (consultants, contractors)

**7.3 Governance:**
- Sustainability leadership structure
- Board/executive oversight
- Stakeholder engagement plan
- Reporting cadence

**7.4 Risk Management:**
- Key risks identified
- Mitigation strategies
- Contingency plans

**7.5 Monitoring & Reporting:**
- KPIs to track (monthly, quarterly, annually)
- Reporting framework (GRI, SASB, or other)
- Third-party verification plan
- Communication strategy

### Deliverable
6-8 pages with visual timeline, budget tables, and governance structure

---

## Part 8: Business Case & Financing (Bonus 10%)

### Task (Optional for Extra Credit)
Develop comprehensive business case and financing strategy.

### Components

**Financial Analysis:**
- Total investment required (5 years)
- Year-by-year cash flows
- Cumulative cash flow
- NPV at appropriate discount rate
- IRR
- Payback period

**Non-Financial Benefits:**
- Risk reduction (quantify if possible)
- Brand value enhancement
- Employee retention
- Customer loyalty
- Regulatory compliance
- Market positioning

**Financing Options:**
- Internal funding capacity
- Green bonds
- Sustainability-linked loans
- Impact investors
- Grants and incentives
- Crowdfunding or community investment

**Recommendation:**
- Preferred financing approach
- Justification
- Implementation strategy

### Deliverable
5-8 pages financial analysis and financing plan

2. Baseline Assessment
3. Materiality Assessment
4. Gap Analysis & Targets
5. Strategies (by topic)
6. Integration Analysis
7. Implementation Roadmap

## Presentation Requirements (20 minutes)

### Format
- PowerPoint, Google Slides, or similar
- 15-20 slides maximum
- 15-minute presentation + 5-minute Q&A

### Content
1. Facility introduction (2 min)
2. Current state & materiality (3 min)
3. Vision & targets (2 min)
4. Top 3-5 strategic priorities (5 min)
5. Implementation plan & timeline (2 min)
6. Business case summary (1 min)
7. Call to action / Next steps (1 min)

### Presentation Tips
- Use visuals (charts, diagrams, photos)
- Tell a compelling story
- Focus on key insights, not all details
- Practice timing
- Prepare for questions

## Tips for Success

1. **Start Early:** This is a substantial project requiring 20-30 hours
2. **Use Real Data:** If analyzing a real facility, gather actual data; if hypothetical, research realistic values
3. **Show Your Work:** Include calculations and assumptions
4. **Think Systems:** Demonstrate integration and synergies
5. **Be Realistic:** Ambitious but achievable strategies
6. **Quantify:** Use numbers and metrics throughout
7. **Visual Communication:** Use charts, diagrams, and tables
8. **Cite Sources:** Reference course materials, case studies, and external research
9. **Professional Quality:** This should be portfolio-worthy work
10. **Seek Feedback:** Consult with instructor during development
