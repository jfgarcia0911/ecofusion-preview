# Lesson 8: Waste Elimination Strategies

## Learning Objectives
- Apply zero waste hierarchy to CEA operations
- Conduct comprehensive waste stream analysis
- Design process optimizations for waste reduction
- Implement composting and organic waste management systems

## Introduction

Zero waste represents a systems-based approach to redesigning resource flows so that all materials are reused, with nothing sent to landfill, incineration, or the environment. For CEA facilities, achieving zero waste creates economic value, reduces environmental impact, and demonstrates circular economy leadership.

## 1. Zero Waste Principles

### 1.1 Zero Waste Hierarchy

```

===================

- Extend product life
- Repurpose materials

    |

    |

    |

- Landfill

- Environmental release
    |
LOWEST PRIORITY

```

## 3. Packaging and Plastic Waste

### 3.1 Sustainable Packaging Design

```
PACKAGING OPTIMIZATION
======================

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:

1. ELIMINATE
   - Remove unnecessary packaging
   - Combine functions
   - Minimize protective layers

2. REUSABLE SYSTEMS
   [Producer] --> [Reusable container] --> [Customer]
        ^                                        |
        |                                        |
        +------------- [Return] <----------------+

   Examples:
   - Reusable harvest crates
   - Returnable product containers
   - Refillable supply packaging

   Economics:
   - Reusable crate: $15 (lasts 50+ cycles)
   - Cardboard box: $2 (single use)
   - Break-even: 8 cycles
   - Savings: $1.70/cycle after break-even

3. COMPOSTABLE MATERIALS
   Materials:
   - PLA (polylactic acid) - industrial compost
   - PHA (polyhydroxyalkanoate) - home compost
   - Cellulose films
   - Mushroom packaging
   - Seaweed-based

   Requirements:
   - Certified compostable (BPI, TÜV Austria)
   - Complete breakdown <180 days
   - No toxic residues

4. RECYCLABLE DESIGN
   - Single material (no mixed plastics)
   - Clear labeling
   - Easy separation
   - Market for recycling exists

   Best materials for CEA:
   - #1 PET: Widely recycled
   - #2 HDPE: Widely recycled
   - #5 PP: Growing infrastructure
   - Cardboard: Universal recycling
```

RECYCLING OPTIONS:

1. MECHANICAL RECYCLING
   Process: Shred --> Melt --> Pelletize --> New products
   Quality: Downcycling (lower grade)
   Cost: $0/ton (revenue from recycler)
   Best for: Clean, sorted plastics

2. CHEMICAL RECYCLING
   Process: Depolymerization --> Monomers --> Virgin-quality plastic
   Quality: Upcycling (same or better)
   Cost: Emerging (not widely available)
   Future: 2025-2030 commercial scale

3. TERRACYCLE PROGRAMS
   Hard-to-recycle plastics
   Specialized processing
   Cost: $100-400/ton
   Use: Last resort for mixed plastics

4. ENERGY RECOVERY (AVOID)
   Waste-to-energy incineration
   Not considered recycling
   Use only if no other option

IRRIGATION LINE RECYCLING:

Annual waste: 500-2,000 m of drip line
Material: PE or PVC
Solution:
  - Partner with manufacturer take-back
  - Clean and return for recycling
  - Closed-loop: 85% recycled content available
  - Cost neutral (free pickup >500 kg)
```

Final disposal:
- Mix into compost
- Soil amendment
- Construction aggregate
- Zero landfill

### 5.2 Tracking and Metrics

```
ZERO WASTE KPIs
===============

1. DIVERSION RATE
   Formula: (Recycled + Composted + Reused) / (Total Waste Generated)
   Target progression:
   - Year 1: >75%
   - Year 2: >85%
   - Year 3: >90%
   - Year 5: >95%

2. WASTE INTENSITY
   Formula: kg waste / kg product
   Baseline: 0.3-0.5 kg/kg
   Target: <0.1 kg/kg

3. LANDFILL ELIMINATION
   Absolute measure: kg to landfill
   Target: <5% by Year 3, 0% by Year 5

4. CIRCULAR REVENUE
   Revenue from waste streams
   Target: $10,000-100,000/year

5. CONTAMINATION RATE

   Target: <5%

## Practical Exercise

**Exercise: Zero Waste Action Plan**

1. **Waste Audit:**
   - Conduct 2-week waste audit

   - Identify top 5 waste sources

     * Prevention potential
     * Reuse options
     * Recycling/composting pathway
     * Valorization possibilities

   - Set 1-year, 3-year, 5-year targets
   - Prioritize interventions
   - Design systems and infrastructure
