## Learning Objectives
- Anticipate emerging sustainability regulations and standards

## Introduction

Sustainability practice is rapidly evolving, driven by climate urgency, technological innovation, regulatory development, and shifting stakeholder expectations. This final lesson explores emerging trends that will shape sustainable CEA operations over the next 5-10 years, enabling proactive strategic planning.

## 1. Regulatory Trends

### 1.1 Climate Disclosure Mandates

```
EMERGING CLIMATE REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
========================================

UNITED STATES:
SEC Climate Disclosure Rule (Proposed 2022, Expected 2024-2025)
Requirements (public companies):
- Scope 1 & 2 GHG emissions (mandatory)
- Scope 3 emissions (if material or have targets)
- Climate-related risks and impacts
- Governance and risk management
- Targets and transition plans
- Assurance (phased in)

Timeline:
- Large accelerated filers: 2024-2025
- Accelerated filers: 2025-2026
- All others: 2026-2027

Impact on CEA:
- Most are private (not directly applicable)
- BUT: Customer requirements increasing
- Supply chain disclosure pressure
- Prepare as if will be required

EUROPEAN UNION:
Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
Effective: 2024-2028 (phased)
Requirements:
- Double materiality assessment
- EU Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)
- Third-party assurance
- Digital tagging (XBRL)

Applicability:
- EU companies (Large: >250 employees)
- EU-listed companies (All)
- Non-EU with significant EU operations

Timeline:
- 2024: Large public companies
- 2025: Large companies (>250 employees)
- 2026: Listed SMEs
- 2028: Non-EU companies (>€150M EU revenue)

Impact on CEA:
- Affects EU operations directly
- Affects US companies exporting to EU
- De facto global standard emerging

CALIFORNIA:
Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (SB 253)
Effective: 2026
Requirements:
- Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions disclosure
- All companies >$1B revenue doing business in CA

Climate-Related Financial Risk Act (SB 261)
Effective: 2026
Requirements:
- TCFD-aligned disclosure
- Companies >$500M revenue

Impact on CEA:
- Many operations in California
- Revenue threshold significant
- State-level trend

GLOBAL CONVERGENCE:
International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB)
IFRS S1: General Requirements for Disclosure
IFRS S2: Climate-related Disclosures

Status: Effective 2024
Adoption: Expected globally 2024-2027
Incorporates: TCFD, SASB, CDSB

Forecast: Mandatory climate disclosure becomes global norm by 2027-2030
```
