## Section 1: EIA Frameworks and LCA Fundamentals (Questions 1-10)

**1. The primary purpose of screening in the EIA process is to:**
A) Eliminate all project alternatives except the cheapest
B) Determine whether formal EIA is required and at what level
C) Complete the environmental baseline survey
D) Calculate the project's carbon footprint

**Answer: B**

**2. According to ISO 14044, which LCA phases are mandatory?**
A) Goal & Scope, Inventory, Impact Assessment, Interpretation
B) Only Goal & Scope and Inventory
C) All phases including normalization and weighting
D) Only Impact Assessment and Interpretation

**Answer: A**

**3. For an LCA comparing CEA lettuce to field lettuce, the most appropriate functional unit is:**
A) 1 hectare of production area
B) 1 kg of lettuce (fresh weight)
C) 1 day of farming operations
D) 1 kWh of energy input

**Answer: B**

**4. In a multi-product system (e.g., aquaponics producing fish and vegetables), ISO 14044's preferred allocation approach is:**
A) Economic value allocation
B) Mass-based allocation
C) Avoid allocation through system expansion or subdivision
D) Equal allocation (50-50 split)

**Answer: C**

**5. The IFC Performance Standard 6 specifically addresses:**
A) Labor practices and working conditions
B) Biodiversity conservation and natural resources
C) Land acquisition and resettlement
D) Climate change mitigation

**Answer: B**

**6. Which characterization factor is used in ReCiPe 2016 for climate change?**
A) Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP)
B) Global Warming Potential (GWP)
C) Acidification Potential (AP)
D) Eutrophication Potential (EP)

**Answer: B**

**7. TRACI 2.1 LCIA method is most appropriate for:**
A) European Union projects
B) Global comparative studies
C) North American (U.S.) projects
D) Developing countries only

**Answer: C**

**8. Normalization in LCIA allows:**
A) Converting all impacts to monetary values
B) Comparing impacts across different categories by referencing to a common scale
C) Eliminating uncertainty in results
D) Calculating product-specific emission factors

**Answer: B**

**9. According to ISO 14044, weighting is:**
A) Mandatory for all LCA studies
B) Prohibited for comparative assertions disclosed to the public
C) Required only for corporate LCA
D) The first step in impact assessment

**Answer: B**

**10. The Pedigree Matrix in LCA data quality assessment evaluates:**
A) Only the cost of data collection
B) Reliability, completeness, temporal, geographical, and technological correlation
C) Only the statistical confidence intervals
D) The experience level of the LCA practitioner

**Answer: B**

## Section 2: Carbon and Water Footprints (Questions 11-20)

**11. Calculate Scope 2 emissions for a facility using 15,000 MWh/year with a grid factor of 180 kg CO₂/MWh:**
A) 270 tonnes CO₂
B) 2,700 tonnes CO₂
C) 27,000 tonnes CO₂
D) 270,000 tonnes CO₂

**Answer: B (15,000 × 180 = 2,700,000 kg = 2,700 tonnes)**

**12. Which GHG scope includes emissions from purchased nutrients and equipment?**
A) Scope 1
B) Scope 2 location-based
C) Scope 2 market-based
D) Scope 3 Category 1

**Answer: D**

**13. A 20 kg leak of R-410A refrigerant (GWP 2,256) equals:**
A) 20 kg CO₂eq
B) 2,256 kg CO₂eq
C) 45,120 kg CO₂eq
D) 452 kg CO₂eq

**Answer: C (20 × 2,256 = 45,120)**

**14. Blue water footprint refers to:**
A) Water from blue-colored pipes
B) Oceanic water consumption
C) Surface and groundwater consumption
D) Recycled wastewater

**Answer: C**

**15. Grey water footprint is calculated as:**
A) Total water withdrawn
B) Water incorporated into products
C) Volume needed to dilute pollutants to acceptable concentrations
D) Rainwater harvested

**Answer: C**

**16. For a CEA facility, indirect water footprint from electricity is typically:**
A) Negligible (<1% of total)
B) 10-20% of total
C) 50-80% of total
D) Equal to direct water use

**Answer: C**

**17. Water stress index (WSI) values above 0.4 indicate:**
A) Water abundance
B) Low stress
C) Medium stress
D) High stress

**Answer: D**

**18. A facility consuming 5,000 m³ water producing 1,000,000 kg lettuce has a blue water footprint of:**
A) 0.005 L/kg
B) 0.5 L/kg
C) 5 L/kg
D) 50 L/kg

**Answer: C (5,000,000 L / 1,000,000 kg = 5 L/kg)**

**19. ISO 14046 requires water footprint assessment to consider:**
A) Only volumetric water consumption
B) Water scarcity and quality degradation impacts
C) Only blue water
D) Economic water costs only

**Answer: B**

**20. The Alliance for Water Stewardship standard emphasizes:**
A) Minimizing water costs
B) Catchment-level water governance and sustainability
C) Only facility-level water efficiency
D) Eliminating all water use

**Answer: B**

## Section 3: Energy, Waste, and Biodiversity (Questions 21-30)

**21. Energy Use Intensity (EUI) for buildings is expressed as:**
A) kWh/kg product
B) kWh/m²/year
C) kWh/employee
D) kWh/dollar revenue

**Answer: B**

**22. In energy balance calculations for CEA, lighting typically contributes what percentage of total energy?**
A) 5-10%
B) 20-30%
C) 40-60%
D) 80-90%

**Answer: C**

**23. Exergy analysis differs from energy analysis because exergy:**
A) Includes renewable energy only
B) Accounts for energy quality and useful work potential
C) Is always equal to energy
D) Ignores thermodynamic losses

**Answer: B**

**24. Fan power consumption changes with speed according to:**
A) Linear relationship (power ∝ speed)
B) Square relationship (power ∝ speed²)
C) Cubic relationship (power ∝ speed³)
D) Exponential relationship

**Answer: C**

**25. For a CEA facility, the waste stream typically largest by mass is:**
A) Plastic packaging
B) Electronic equipment
C) Organic plant waste
D) Chemical waste

**Answer: C**

**26. Grey water footprint from nutrient discharge can be eliminated by:**
A) Increasing discharge volume
B) Diluting nutrients with more water
C) Implementing zero-discharge recirculating systems
D) Discharging to ocean instead of freshwater

**Answer: C**

**27. Under IFC PS6, critical habitat is defined by the presence of:**
A) Any native species
B) Critically Endangered or Endangered species, or unique ecosystems
C) Agricultural land
D) Previously developed areas

**Answer: B**

**28. The biodiversity mitigation hierarchy prioritizes:**
A) Offset, Minimize, Avoid, Restore
B) Minimize, Restore, Offset, Avoid
C) Avoid, Minimize, Restore, Offset
D) Restore, Avoid, Offset, Minimize

**Answer: C**

**29. Light pollution impacts on wildlife can be minimized by:**
A) Using bright white lights
B) Maximizing upward-directed lighting
C) Using warm-spectrum LEDs (<3000K) with full cutoff fixtures
D) Operating lights 24/7 for consistency

**Answer: C**

**30. A biodiversity offset ratio of 3:1 means:**
A) 3 hectares impacted requires 1 hectare offset
B) 1 hectare impacted requires 3 hectares offset
C) Offset costs 3× the project cost
D) Monitoring for 3 years per 1 year of impact

**Answer: B**

## Section 4: Social Impacts and Cumulative Effects (Questions 31-40)

**31. Social Impact Assessment should evaluate:**
A) Only employment numbers
B) Employment, community impacts, human rights, equity, and cultural heritage
C) Only economic contributions
D) Only compliance with labor laws

**Answer: B**

**32. Economic multiplier effects in CEA capture:**
A) Only direct jobs at the facility
B) Direct, indirect, and induced economic impacts
C) Only tax revenues
D) Electricity consumption multiplied by carbon intensity

**Answer: B**

**33. A grievance mechanism for communities should be:**
A) Complex and technical to filter complaints
B) Available only during business hours
C) Accessible, transparent, and responsive
D) Managed exclusively by company lawyers

**Answer: C**

**34. Cumulative environmental effects result from:**
A) Single project impacts only
B) The combination of multiple projects and activities over time and space
C) Only past impacts
D) Only the largest project in a region

**Answer: B**

**35. Valued Ecosystem Components (VECs) are selected based on:**
A) Random selection
B) Lowest cost to monitor
C) Ecological, social, or economic importance and vulnerability
D) Government mandate only

**Answer: C**

**36. In cumulative effects assessment, temporal bounding typically includes:**
A) Only the present
B) Only the future
C) Past, present, and reasonably foreseeable future
D) Only the project construction phase

**Answer: C**

**37. System dynamics modeling for cumulative effects uses:**
A) Only linear regression
B) Stock-and-flow diagrams with feedback loops
C) Simple addition of impacts
D) Qualitative descriptions only

**Answer: B**

**38. A threshold in cumulative impact analysis represents:**
A) The minimum detectable change
B) The point beyond which non-linear or irreversible changes occur
C) The cost limit for mitigation
D) The regulatory compliance limit

**Answer: B**

**39. Spatial analysis tools for cumulative effects include:**
A) Financial accounting software
B) Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
C) Text editors
D) Calendar applications

**Answer: B**

**40. Synergistic cumulative effects occur when:**
A) Impact A + Impact B = A + B
B) Impact A + Impact B < A + B
C) Impact A + Impact B > A + B (combined effect greater than sum)
D) Impacts don't interact

**Answer: C**

## Section 5: Management, Reporting, and Compliance (Questions 41-50)

**41. ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management System requires:**
A) Certification within 6 months of implementation
B) Understanding organizational context, leadership, planning, and continual improvement
C) Only large corporations to comply
D) Annual public sustainability reports

**Answer: B**

**42. In ISO 14001, significant environmental aspects are determined by:**
A) Random selection
B) Evaluating severity, likelihood, and detectability
C) Choosing only aspects with regulations
D) Stakeholder voting

**Answer: B**

**43. The PDCA cycle (Plan-Do-Check-Act) is used for:**
A) Financial reporting
B) Continuous improvement
C) One-time projects only
D) Hiring decisions

**Answer: B**

**44. GRI Standards provide guidance for:**
A) Life cycle assessment methodology
B) Sustainability reporting on economic, environmental, and social topics
C) Carbon footprint calculation only
D) Engineering design specifications

**Answer: B**

**45. TCFD framework focuses on disclosure of:**
A) All sustainability topics equally
B) Climate-related risks, opportunities, and financial implications
C) Only Scope 1 emissions
D) Employee satisfaction surveys

**Answer: B**

**46. A GRI materiality assessment identifies:**
A) What topics are most important to the organization and stakeholders
B) Only financial materiality
C) What reduces costs most
D) What competitors are reporting

**Answer: A**

**47. Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) are based on:**
A) Marketing claims
B) Life cycle assessment per ISO 14040/14044
C) Facility tours
D) Customer opinions

**Answer: B**

**48. CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project) scoring ranges from:**
A) 0-100
B) A to D-
C) 1 to 5 stars
D) Pass/Fail

**Answer: B**

**49. Under RCRA, a facility generating 100-1,000 kg/month of hazardous waste is classified as:**
A) Very Small Quantity Generator
B) Small Quantity Generator
C) Large Quantity Generator
D) Exempt

**Answer: B**

**50. Best Available Techniques (BAT) in pollution control refers to:**
A) The cheapest available technology
