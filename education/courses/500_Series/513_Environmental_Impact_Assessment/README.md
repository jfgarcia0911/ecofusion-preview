# Course 513: Environmental Impact Assessment

**Level:** Master/Executive (500-Series)
**Duration:** 14 weeks
**Credits:** 4.0
**Institution:** EcoFusion Academy

---

## Course Overview

This comprehensive master-level course provides rigorous training in environmental impact assessment methodologies specifically designed for Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) facilities. Students master systematic approaches to evaluating environmental performance, including life cycle assessment, carbon and water footprint analysis, energy modeling, and social impact evaluation.

---

## Course Structure

### Lessons (14 modules)
Comprehensive content covering all aspects of environmental impact assessment:

1. **Environmental Impact Assessment Frameworks** - EIA principles, international standards, regulatory requirements
2. **Life Cycle Assessment Fundamentals** - ISO 14040/14044, goal & scope, inventory analysis
3. **Life Cycle Impact Assessment** - LCIA methodologies (ReCiPe, TRACI, CML), characterization
4. **Carbon Footprint Analysis** - GHG Protocol, Scope 1/2/3 emissions, mitigation strategies
5. **Water Footprint Assessment** - Blue/green/grey water, scarcity indicators, efficiency
6. **Energy Analysis and Modeling** - Energy balance, exergy, EnergyPlus, renewable integration
7. **Waste and Emissions Assessment** - Waste characterization, air/water emissions, pollution prevention
8. **Biodiversity Impact Assessment** - Habitat assessment, species impacts, mitigation hierarchy
9. **Social Impact Assessment** - Stakeholder analysis, economic impacts, community engagement
10. **Cumulative Impact Analysis** - VECs, spatial/temporal analysis, system dynamics
11. **Mitigation and Management Strategies** - Best available techniques, adaptive management
12. **Environmental Management Systems** - ISO 14001, EMS design, internal auditing
13. **Environmental Reporting and Disclosure** - GRI, TCFD, CDP, EPDs
14. **Regulatory Compliance and Continuous Improvement** - Permits, compliance, PDCA cycles

### Assessments

**Weekly Quizzes** (14 quizzes)
- 10 questions each
- Multiple choice format
- Answer keys provided
- 20% of final grade

**Final Examination**
- 50 comprehensive questions
- Covers all 14 modules
- 3-hour time limit
- 30% of final grade

### Practical Activities

**Activity 1: Life Cycle Assessment Case Study** (30% of grade)
- Complete LCA for vertical farm lettuce production
- Apply ISO 14040/14044 methodology
- Compare to conventional agriculture
- 10-15 page professional report

**Activity 2: Carbon Footprint Calculation** (included in activities grade)
- Calculate Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions
- Apply GHG Protocol standards
- Design reduction strategies
- 8-10 page carbon footprint report

**Activity 3: Environmental Impact Assessment** (optional extension)
- Comprehensive EIA for proposed CEA facility
- All impact categories
- Mitigation planning
- Full EIA report

### Support Materials

**Cheatsheets** (3 quick reference guides)
1. LCA Quick Reference - Methods, formulas, software guidance
2. Carbon & Water Footprint - Emission factors, calculations, benchmarks
3. EIA Process & Regulatory Compliance - Frameworks, permits, standards

**Handouts**
1. Emission Factors Reference Guide - Comprehensive factor tables for calculations
2. Additional handouts available for specific topics

**Resources**
- Comprehensive Glossary - 150+ terms, acronyms, units, and conversions
- Software tutorials (openLCA, EnergyPlus)
- Standards references (ISO, GHG Protocol, GRI)

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

1. Design and execute comprehensive environmental impact assessments for CEA facilities
2. Apply LCA methodology using ISO 14040/14044 standards
3. Calculate and analyze carbon footprints using GHG Protocol
4. Conduct water footprint assessments with scarcity weighting
5. Perform energy analysis and modeling for optimization
6. Assess waste, emissions, and biodiversity impacts
7. Conduct social and cumulative impact analyses
8. Design mitigation strategies using established hierarchies
9. Implement ISO 14001 environmental management systems
10. Prepare professional environmental reports meeting international standards
11. Navigate environmental regulatory frameworks
12. Develop continuous improvement programs

## Student Success Tips

1. **Start early** on activities - LCA and carbon footprinting are time-intensive
2. **Use the cheatsheets** - keep them handy during calculations
3. **Practice with software** - openLCA and EnergyPlus have learning curves
4. **Form study groups** - peer discussion deepens understanding
5. **Ask questions** - instructor and TAs are here to help
6. **Apply to your work** - if you're employed, try using course methods on real projects (with supervisor approval)
7. **Build a portfolio** - your LCA and carbon footprint reports are professional work samples
8. **Network** - connect with classmates for future collaborations
9. **Stay current** - follow sustainability news and EIA developments
10. **Think holistically** - remember that environmental, social, and economic dimensions are interconnected
