## Questions

**1. Which of the following best defines Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)?**

A) A financial analysis tool for evaluating project costs
B) A systematic process to identify, predict, evaluate, and mitigate environmental effects before decisions are made
C) A post-construction monitoring program for compliance verification
D) A voluntary sustainability reporting framework

**Answer: B**

---

**2. The precautionary principle in EIA states that:**

A) All projects must be approved regardless of potential impacts
B) Scientific certainty must be achieved before taking action
C) Lack of full scientific certainty should not prevent cost-effective measures to avoid environmental degradation
D) Economic benefits always outweigh environmental concerns

**Answer: C**

---

**3. Which is NOT a typical stage in the EIA process?**

A) Screening
B) Scoping
C) Financial auditing
D) Impact assessment

**Answer: C**

---

**4. Screening in the EIA process serves to:**

A) Determine whether an EIA is required and at what level
B) Identify all possible impacts for detailed assessment
C) Select the project alternative with lowest cost
D) Complete the final Environmental Impact Statement

**Answer: A**

---

**5. The International Finance Corporation (IFC) Performance Standard 3 addresses:**

A) Labor and working conditions
B) Community health and safety
C) Resource efficiency and pollution prevention
D) Land acquisition and resettlement

**Answer: C**

---

**6. Under NEPA (U.S. framework), an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) is required for:**

A) All construction projects over $1 million
B) Major federal actions significantly affecting the environment
C) Any project that uses fossil fuels
D) All agricultural facilities regardless of size

**Answer: B**

---

**7. In the Leopold Matrix method for scoping, what do the two numbers in each cell represent?**

A) Cost and benefit
B) Magnitude and importance
C) Probability and consequence
D) Duration and reversibility

**Answer: B**

---

**8. Stakeholder mapping based on influence and interest helps to:**

A) Eliminate stakeholder concerns
B) Determine which stakeholders should be ignored
C) Design appropriate engagement strategies for different groups
D) Reduce the cost of EIA preparation

**Answer: C**

---

**9. For a 75,000 sq ft CEA facility with 800 kW peak demand, high water use, and location near residential areas, the appropriate assessment level would likely be:**

A) No assessment required
B) Environmental checklist only
C) Environmental assessment or full EIA
D) Strategic environmental assessment

**Answer: C**

---

**10. The key difference between consultation and participation in stakeholder engagement is:**

A) Consultation is more expensive than participation
B) Consultation seeks input while participation involves stakeholders in decision-making
C) Participation is only required for projects over 100,000 sq ft
D) Consultation requires written responses while participation does not

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Systematic process definition
2. C - Precautionary principle definition
3. C - Financial auditing not part of EIA
4. A - Screening determines if EIA required
5. C - IFC PS3 is resource efficiency
6. B - NEPA EIS for major federal actions
7. B - Leopold Matrix uses magnitude and importance
8. C - Stakeholder mapping informs engagement strategies
9. C - Multiple high-impact factors trigger EA/EIA
10. B - Participation involves decision-making role
