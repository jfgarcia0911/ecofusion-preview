## Questions

**1. What are the three components of water footprint according to the Water Footprint Network?**

A) Fresh, salt, and brackish water
B) Blue, green, and grey water
C) Surface, ground, and rain water
D) Potable, process, and waste water

**Answer: B**

---

**2. What does "blue water footprint" represent?**

A) Ocean water usage
B) Consumption of surface and groundwater resources
C) Rainwater consumption
D) Polluted water volume

**Answer: B**

---

**3. What does "green water footprint" specifically measure?**

A) Recycled water usage
B) Consumption of rainwater stored in soil (relevant for traditional agriculture)
C) Treated wastewater
D) Irrigation system color

**Answer: B**

---

**4. How is "grey water footprint" defined?**

A) Water from grey-colored pipes
B) Volume of freshwater needed to assimilate pollutants to meet water quality standards
C) Secondary treated wastewater
D) Cloudy drinking water

**Answer: B**

---

**5. For CEA hydroponic systems with recirculation, which water footprint component is most significant?**

A) Green water (minimal, as rain isn't primary source)
B) Blue water (for initial fill and evapotranspiration losses)
C) Grey water only
D) None - hydroponics use no water

**Answer: B**

---

**6. What is "virtual water" content of a product?**

A) Computer simulations of water
B) Total water used throughout production chain to create the product
C) Imaginary water calculations
D) Water visible in the final product

**Answer: B**

---

**7. Which water scarcity indicator combines quantity and temporal variability?**

A) Water Stress Index (WSI)
B) Annual rainfall only
C) Population density
D) River length

**Answer: A**

---

**8. How can CEA facilities reduce their blue water footprint?**

A) Using more water
B) Implementing recirculation systems, condensate capture, and efficient irrigation
C) Eliminating all water use
D) Converting to rainfed agriculture

**Answer: B**

---

**9. What does "water footprint sustainability assessment" evaluate?**

A) Financial sustainability only
B) Whether water footprint exceeds environmental limits and social equity considerations
C) Employee water consumption
D) Building plumbing quality

**Answer: B**

---

**10. For a CEA facility producing lettuce, how would you calculate water footprint per kg of product?**

A) Total facility water bill divided by revenue
B) Total blue, green, grey water consumed divided by kg of lettuce produced
C) Number of employees times average consumption
D) Facility square footage times rainfall

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Blue, green, and grey water
2. B - Surface and groundwater consumption
3. B - Rainwater stored in soil
4. B - Freshwater to assimilate pollutants to standards
5. B - Blue water for fill and evapotranspiration
6. B - Total water throughout production chain
7. A - Water Stress Index
8. B - Recirculation, condensate capture, efficient irrigation
9. B - Environmental limits and social equity
10. B - Total water divided by kg produced
