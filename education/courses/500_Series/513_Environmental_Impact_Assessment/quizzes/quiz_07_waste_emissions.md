## Instructions
Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

**1. What is the waste hierarchy order from most to least preferred?**

A) Disposal, recycling, reduction, reuse
B) Prevention, minimization, reuse, recycling, energy recovery, disposal
C) Recycling only
D) Disposal first, then consider other options

**Answer: B**

---

**2. In waste characterization, what does "composition analysis" determine?**

A) Musical composition of waste
B) Types and percentages of materials in waste stream (organics, plastics, metals, etc.)
C) Employee composition
D) Financial composition

**Answer: B**

---

**3. Which air emission is a primary concern from CEA facility combustion equipment?**

A) Oxygen (O₂)
B) Nitrogen oxides (NOx) and particulate matter
C) Nitrogen (N₂)
D) Pure water vapor only

**Answer: B**

---

**4. What does the Gaussian plume model estimate?**

A) Product pricing
B) Dispersion and concentration of air pollutants downwind from emission source
C) Employee performance
D) Water flow rates

**Answer: B**

---

**5. For CEA facilities using nutrient solutions, what is the primary water contamination concern?**

A) Excess sugar
B) High nitrate and phosphate concentrations if discharged untreated
C) Pure distilled water
D) Oxygen enrichment

**Answer: B**

---

**6. What is "source reduction" in pollution prevention?**

A) Reducing the size of pollution reports
B) Eliminating or minimizing waste generation at the source through process changes
C) Moving pollution sources
D) Ignoring pollution sources

**Answer: B**

---

**7. In the context of CEA, what is "nutrient runoff" and why is it concerning?**

A) Employees running with food
B) Excess nutrients entering water bodies causing eutrophication and algal blooms
C) Nutritious meals for staff
D) Product delivery schedules

**Answer: B**

---

**8. Which waste management approach converts organic waste to valuable compost?**

A) Incineration only
B) Aerobic or anaerobic composting/digestion
C) Landfilling
D) Ocean dumping

**Answer: B**

---

**9. What is "fate and transport modeling" for contaminants?**

A) Shipping route planning
B) Predicting how contaminants move through and change in environmental media
C) Employee career paths
D) Product distribution

**Answer: B**

---

**10. For CEA plant waste, which circular economy approach creates value?**

A) Immediate landfilling
B) Composting for soil amendment or anaerobic digestion for biogas
C) Burning without energy recovery
D) Exporting to other countries

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Prevention, minimization, reuse, recycling, recovery, disposal
2. B - Types and percentages of materials in waste
3. B - Nitrogen oxides and particulate matter
4. B - Dispersion of air pollutants downwind
5. B - High nitrate and phosphate concentrations
6. B - Eliminate waste generation at source
7. B - Excess nutrients causing eutrophication
8. B - Aerobic/anaerobic composting/digestion
9. B - Predicting contaminant movement and transformation
10. B - Composting or anaerobic digestion
