## Questions

**1. What is the primary purpose of Life Cycle Impact Assessment (LCIA)?**

A) To reduce project costs
B) To translate inventory data into potential environmental impacts
C) To calculate financial returns
D) To schedule project activities

**Answer: B**

---

**2. Which LCIA method is developed by the EPA for North American context?**

A) ReCiPe
B) CML
C) TRACI (Tool for Reduction and Assessment of Chemicals and Environmental Impacts)
D) Eco-indicator 99

**Answer: C**

---

**3. What are the mandatory elements of LCIA according to ISO 14044?**

A) Selection, Classification, and Characterization only
B) Classification and Characterization only
C) All elements including normalization and weighting
D) Financial analysis

**Answer: A**

---

**4. In LCIA, what does "characterization" do?**

A) Describes product features
B) Converts inventory data to common units for each impact category using factors
C) Creates marketing personas
D) Defines employee roles

**Answer: B**

---

**5. Which impact category addresses the depletion of stratospheric ozone?**

A) Global warming potential
B) Acidification potential
C) Ozone depletion potential
D) Eutrophication potential

**Answer: C**

---

**6. What does "normalization" in LCIA accomplish?**

A) Standardizing data collection procedures
B) Comparing impact category results to reference values (e.g., regional/global impacts)
C) Ensuring normal distribution of data
D) Averaging all results

**Answer: B**

---

**7. For a CEA facility, which impact category would capture greenhouse gas emissions?**

A) Acidification
B) Climate change / Global warming potential
C) Ecotoxicity
D) Land use

**Answer: B**

---

**8. What is "weighting" in LCIA and why is it optional?**

A) Measuring product mass; it's not optional
B) Assigning relative importance to impact categories; it's value-based and subjective
C) Statistical analysis; it's always required
D) Cost allocation; it's mandatory

**Answer: B**

---

**9. Which midpoint impact category relates to nutrient enrichment of water bodies?**

A) Photochemical ozone formation
B) Eutrophication potential
C) Water depletion
D) Ionizing radiation

**Answer: B**

---

**10. What is a key challenge in LCIA for emerging technologies like CEA?**

A) Too much available data
B) Lack of characterization factors for novel substances or processes
C) LCIA is not applicable to CEA
D) No software tools exist

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Translate inventory to environmental impacts
2. C - TRACI (EPA North American method)
3. A - Selection, Classification, Characterization (mandatory)
4. B - Convert to common units using factors
5. C - Ozone depletion potential
6. B - Compare to reference values
7. B - Climate change / Global warming potential
8. B - Relative importance; value-based and subjective
9. B - Eutrophication potential
10. B - Lack of factors for novel substances/processes
