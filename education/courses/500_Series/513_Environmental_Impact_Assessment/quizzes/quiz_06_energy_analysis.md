## Questions

**1. What is the difference between energy and exergy analysis?**

A) There is no difference
B) Energy tracks quantity; exergy tracks quality and useful work potential
C) Exergy is only for electrical systems
D) Energy is more advanced than exergy

**Answer: B**

---

**2. In energy balance calculations for a CEA facility, which equation must hold true?**

A) Energy in = Energy out (including losses)
B) Energy in > Energy out always
C) Energy out > Energy in always
D) No relationship required

**Answer: A**

---

**3. What does "Energy Use Intensity" (EUI) measure for buildings?**

A) Color intensity of lighting
B) Energy consumption per unit area per year (e.g., kWh/m²/year)
C) Worker productivity
D) Equipment purchase cost

**Answer: B**

---

**4. For CEA facilities, which typically represents the largest energy end-use?**

A) Office computers
B) Lighting and HVAC systems
C) Security cameras
D) Employee vehicles

**Answer: B**

---

**5. What is the primary advantage of using EnergyPlus for CEA energy modeling?**

A) It's a spreadsheet tool
B) Detailed building physics simulation accounting for thermal dynamics, HVAC, and lighting
C) It only models solar panels
D) No advantages exist

**Answer: B**

---

**6. Which renewable energy source is most commonly integrated with CEA facilities?**

A) Tidal power
B) Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems
C) Geothermal in all locations
D) Wind turbines exclusively

**Answer: B**

---

**7. What does "Power Usage Effectiveness" (PUE) measure?**

A) Employee efficiency
B) Ratio of total facility energy to energy used by growing operations
C) Product quality
D) Marketing effectiveness

**Answer: B**

---

**8. In energy optimization for CEA, what is "demand response"?**

A) Customer service quality
B) Adjusting energy consumption based on grid signals or pricing to reduce costs/load
C) Immediate energy generation
D) Employee response time

**Answer: B**

---

**9. Which strategy can reduce CEA lighting energy consumption while maintaining productivity?**

A) Using incandescent bulbs
B) Optimizing LED spectrum and intensity for specific crop needs and growth stages
C) Eliminating all lighting
D) Constant maximum lighting 24/7

**Answer: B**

---

**10. What is "embodied energy" in building materials?**

A) Energy stored in batteries
B) Total energy consumed in extraction, manufacturing, and transportation of materials
C) Emotional energy of workers
D) Potential energy from height

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Energy = quantity; exergy = quality/useful work
2. A - Energy in = Energy out (with losses)
3. B - Energy per unit area per year
4. B - Lighting and HVAC systems
5. B - Detailed building physics simulation
6. B - Solar photovoltaic systems
7. B - Total energy to growing operations ratio
8. B - Adjusting consumption based on grid signals
9. B - Optimize LED spectrum/intensity for crops
10. B - Energy in extraction, manufacturing, transport
