# Quiz 8: Biodiversity and Social Impact Assessment

**Course:** 513 - Environmental Impact Assessment
**Module:** 8-9 of 14
**Total Points:** 10 questions × 1 point = 10 points
**Passing Score:** 7/10 (70%)
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes

---

## Instructions
Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Only one answer is correct per question.

---

## Questions

**1. Which biodiversity metric measures the number of different species in an area?**

A) Biomass
B) Species richness
C) Population density
D) Genetic uniformity

**Answer: B**

---

**2. What does "ecosystem services" refer to?**

A) Service industries located in ecosystems
B) Benefits people obtain from ecosystems (pollination, water filtration, climate regulation)
C) Landscaping services
D) Wildlife veterinary care

**Answer: B**

---

**3. In habitat assessment, what is "habitat fragmentation"?**

A) Breaking glass habitats
B) Division of continuous habitat into smaller, isolated patches reducing species viability
C) Soil particle sizes
D) Budget division

**Answer: B**

---

**4. For a CEA facility built on previously disturbed industrial land, what is the biodiversity impact compared to greenfield development?**

A) Identical impact
B) Lower impact as no natural habitat is destroyed
C) Higher impact always
D) No way to compare

**Answer: B**

---

**5. What is the mitigation hierarchy for biodiversity impacts?**

A) Compensate, restore, minimize, avoid
B) Avoid, minimize, restore, offset/compensate
C) Destroy, then mitigate
D) Ignore all impacts

**Answer: B**

---

**6. In social impact assessment, what are "affected communities"?**

A) Online social media groups
B) People/groups whose social, economic, or cultural conditions may be affected by project
C) Project investors only
D) Government officials exclusively

**Answer: B**

---

**7. Which is a key principle of effective community engagement?**

A) Minimal communication
B) Early, continuous, transparent, and meaningful participation in decision-making
C) Engagement only after project completion
D) Ignoring community concerns

**Answer: B**

---

**8. What does "free, prior, and informed consent" (FPIC) require?**

A) Free product samples
B) Indigenous peoples' right to give or withhold consent to projects affecting their lands/resources
C) Fast project approvals
D) No community consultation needed

**Answer: B**

---

**9. In assessing CEA facility social impacts, which factor should be evaluated?**

A) Product flavors only
B) Job creation, working conditions, community health, traffic, noise, and local economy
C) Executive compensation only
D) Social media followers

**Answer: B**

---

**10. What is "ecosystem services valuation" used for?**

A) Stock market trading
B) Quantifying economic value of benefits from ecosystems to inform decisions
C) Real estate pricing only
D) Employee performance reviews

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Species richness
2. B - Benefits from ecosystems (pollination, filtration, etc.)
3. B - Division into smaller isolated patches
4. B - Lower impact, no natural habitat destroyed
5. B - Avoid, minimize, restore, offset/compensate
6. B - People/groups affected by project conditions
7. B - Early, continuous, transparent, meaningful participation
8. B - Indigenous peoples' consent for land/resource projects
9. B - Jobs, conditions, health, traffic, noise, economy
10. B - Quantifying economic value of ecosystem benefits
