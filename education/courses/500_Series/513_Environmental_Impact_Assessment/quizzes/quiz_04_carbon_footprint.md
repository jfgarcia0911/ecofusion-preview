# Quiz 4: Carbon Footprint Analysis

**Course:** 513 - Environmental Impact Assessment
**Module:** 4 of 14
**Total Points:** 10 points
**Passing Score:** 7/10 (70%)

## Questions

**1. Scope 2 emissions refer to:**
A) Direct emissions from owned sources
B) Indirect emissions from purchased electricity
C) Value chain emissions
D) Employee commuting

**Answer: B**

**2. Calculate the CO₂eq emissions from combusting 100,000 m³ of natural gas using emission factor of 1.91 kg CO₂/m³:**
A) 19,100 kg
B) 191,000 kg
C) 1,910,000 kg
D) 191 kg

**Answer: B (100,000 × 1.91 = 191,000 kg)**

**3. The Global Warming Potential (GWP₁₀₀) of methane is approximately:**
A) 1
B) 10
C) 30
D) 300

**Answer: C (29.8, approximately 30)**

**4. Which Scope 3 category typically represents the largest emissions for CEA facilities?**
A) Employee commuting
B) Purchased goods and services (nutrients, equipment)
C) Waste disposal
D) Business travel

**Answer: B**

**5. Under GHG Protocol, both location-based and market-based Scope 2 must be reported when:**
A) Facilities are over 50,000 sq ft
B) Companies claim renewable energy purchases
C) Always - dual reporting is required
D) Only for public companies

**Answer: C**

**6. Embodied carbon in infrastructure should be:**
A) Excluded from carbon footprint
B) Amortized over the asset lifespan
C) Counted entirely in first year
D) Only counted if over 1,000 kg CO₂eq

**Answer: B**

**7. High-quality carbon offsets must be:**
A) As cheap as possible
B) Real, additional, permanent, and verified
C) Only from renewable energy projects
D) Located in the same country as emissions

**Answer: B**

**8. A refrigerant leak of 10 kg R-404A (GWP 3,922) results in:**
A) 10 kg CO₂eq
B) 392 kg CO₂eq
C) 3,922 kg CO₂eq
D) 39,220 kg CO₂eq

**Answer: D (10 × 3,922 = 39,220 kg CO₂eq)**

**9. The carbon mitigation hierarchy prioritizes:**
A) Offset, Reduce, Avoid, Substitute
B) Avoid, Reduce, Substitute, Offset
C) Reduce, Avoid, Offset, Substitute
D) Substitute, Avoid, Reduce, Offset

**Answer: B**

**10. For a credible carbon neutrality claim, an organization must:**
A) Purchase offsets for all emissions without reduction efforts
B) Reduce emissions AND offset remaining emissions with high-quality credits
C) Only report Scope 1 and 2 emissions
D) Achieve zero emissions without offsets

**Answer: B**
