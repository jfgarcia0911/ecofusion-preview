## Questions

**1. Which ISO standards govern Life Cycle Assessment methodology?**

A) ISO 9001 and ISO 9002
B) ISO 14040 and ISO 14044
C) ISO 27001 and ISO 27002
D) ISO 45001 and ISO 45002

**Answer: B**

---

**2. What are the four phases of LCA according to ISO 14040?**

A) Plan, Do, Check, Act
B) Goal and Scope, Inventory Analysis, Impact Assessment, Interpretation
C) Research, Design, Build, Test
D) Screening, Scoping, Assessment, Mitigation

**Answer: B**

---

**3. What is a "functional unit" in LCA?**

A) The physical size of a product
B) Quantified performance of a product system for use as reference unit
C) The cost per unit produced
D) The manufacturing facility location

**Answer: B**

---

**4. In defining system boundaries for a CEA facility LCA, which approach includes extraction of raw materials through end-of-life?**

A) Gate-to-gate
B) Cradle-to-gate
C) Cradle-to-grave
D) Gate-to-grave

**Answer: C**

---

**5. What does "allocation" address in LCA?**

A) Budget distribution
B) Partitioning environmental impacts among multiple products from the same process
C) Employee assignments
D) Geographic location selection

**Answer: B**

---

**6. Which LCA database is widely used for agricultural and food systems?**

A) ecoinvent
B) Microsoft Excel
C) Google Sheets
D) Social media platforms

**Answer: A**

---

**7. What is "cut-off criteria" in LCA inventory analysis?**

A) Deadline for data collection
B) Threshold for excluding minor flows based on mass, energy, or environmental relevance
C) Maximum project cost
D) Employee termination policies

**Answer: B**

---

**8. For a CEA tomato production LCA, which functional unit would be most appropriate?**

A) Per square foot of facility
B) Per kilogram of marketable tomatoes produced
C) Per employee hour
D) Per dollar of revenue

**Answer: B**

---

**9. What is "foreground data" in LCA?**

A) Data collected in the morning
B) Primary data specific to the system being studied (e.g., actual energy use of the CEA facility)
C) Generic industry averages only
D) Historical data from 10+ years ago

**Answer: B**

---

**10. Which is a limitation of LCA that practitioners must acknowledge?**

A) LCA is too simple to be useful
B) Uncertainty from data quality, allocation choices, and methodological assumptions
C) LCA never requires data collection
D) LCA eliminates all environmental impacts

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - ISO 14040 and ISO 14044
2. B - Goal/Scope, Inventory, Impact Assessment, Interpretation
3. B - Quantified performance as reference unit
4. C - Cradle-to-grave (full life cycle)
5. B - Partitioning impacts among multiple products
6. A - ecoinvent
7. B - Threshold for excluding minor flows
8. B - Per kilogram of marketable tomatoes
9. B - Primary data specific to studied system
10. B - Uncertainty from data and methodological choices
