## Screening Criteria for CEA

| Criterion | Low Impact | Medium | High Impact → EIA Likely |
|-----------|------------|---------|-------------------------|
| **Size** | <10,000 ft² | 10-100k ft² | >100,000 ft² |
| **Energy** | <100 kW | 100-1,000 kW | >1 MW |
| **Water** | <10k gal/day | 10-100k gal/day | >100k gal/day |
| **Location** | Industrial | Mixed-use | Near sensitive receptors |
| **Technology** | Established | Proven | Novel/experimental |

**Decision Rule:**
- All LOW → Checklist only
- Any MEDIUM → Environmental Assessment
- Any HIGH → Full EIA/EIS

## Mitigation Hierarchy

```
Priority 1: AVOID
├─ Site selection (brownfield vs. greenfield)
├─ Project redesign
└─ Alternative technology

Priority 2: MINIMIZE
├─ Reduce footprint
├─ Limit hours of operation
└─ Seasonal timing

Priority 3: RESTORE
├─ Revegetate disturbed areas
├─ Habitat rehabilitation
└─ Water quality improvement

Priority 4: OFFSET (last resort)
├─ Biodiversity offsets
├─ Carbon credits
└─ Community compensation
```

## Key Regulations (U.S.)

### Federal Level

**NEPA (National Environmental Policy Act)**
- Trigger: Major federal action
- Tiers: EA (Environmental Assessment) or EIS (Environmental Impact Statement)
- Public comment: 30-45 days

**Clean Air Act**
- Permits for air emissions
- Title V: Major sources (>100 tonnes/yr criteria pollutants)
- Most CEA: Minor source or exempt

**Clean Water Act**
- NPDES: Discharge to surface water
- Pretreatment: Discharge to sewer
- Zero-discharge facilities exempt

**RCRA (Resource Conservation & Recovery Act)**
- Hazardous waste management
- Generator classification:
  - VSQG: <100 kg/month
  - SQG: 100-1,000 kg/month
  - LQG: >1,000 kg/month

**Endangered Species Act**
- Consultation if threatened/endangered species present
- Critical habitat protection

### State/Local
- Often more stringent than federal
- Zoning and land use permits
- Building codes
- Water rights and allocation
- Odor and noise ordinances

## EIS Contents (NEPA)

1. **Cover Sheet** - Title, location, contacts
2. **Executive Summary**
3. **Purpose and Need**
4. **Alternatives** (including No Action)
5. **Affected Environment** (baseline)
6. **Environmental Consequences**
   - Direct, indirect, cumulative
   - By alternative
7. **Mitigation Measures**
8. **Consultation and Coordination**
9. **Appendices** (technical studies)

Typical length: 150-500 pages

## Impact Significance Criteria

**Magnitude:**
- Negligible, Low, Medium, High

**Duration:**
- Temporary (<1 year)
- Short-term (1-5 years)
- Long-term (>5 years)
- Permanent

**Extent:**
- Localized (site only)
- Local (within 1 km)
- Regional (5-50 km)
- Global (e.g., GHG)

**Reversibility:**
- Reversible (can be restored)
- Irreversible (permanent change)

**Significance Matrix:**
```
Significance = Magnitude × Duration × Extent × Reversibility

High magnitude + Long-term + Regional + Irreversible
  = SIGNIFICANT (detailed assessment and mitigation required)
```

**9. Performance Evaluation**

- Continual improvement

### Aspects & Impacts Example
```
Activity: LED lighting operation
Aspect: Electricity consumption
Impact: GHG emissions, resource depletion

Significance Score = Severity (4) × Likelihood (5) × Detectability (3)
                   = 60 (High - needs objective/target)
```

## Compliance Monitoring

### Hierarchy of Controls
```
1. PROACTIVE (Prevent non-compliance)
   - Real-time sensors and alarms
   - Preventive maintenance
   - Management systems

2. COMPLIANCE (Verify meeting requirements)
   - Permit monitoring
   - Inspections and audits
   - Records management

3. REACTIVE (Respond to violations)
   - Corrective actions
   - Root cause analysis
   - Regulatory reporting
```

### Key Performance Indicators
- Days without permit exceedances
- Audit findings closure rate
- Training completion %
- Incident frequency
- Cost of compliance vs. budget

           └──────┬──────┘

           │  ADMINISTRATIVE   │ (Violations)
           │  PENALTIES/FINES  │
           └──────┬────────────┘
           ┌──────┴──────────────┐
           │  WARNING LETTER     │ (Minor issues)

- Proactive management systems
- Self-audits and disclosure
- Rapid correction of issues
- Regulator communication

## Decision Trees

### Do I Need an EIA?
```
Is project subject to regulation requiring EIA?
├─ YES → Full EIA required
└─ NO → ↓

Does project exceed screening thresholds?
├─ YES → Full EIA likely required
└─ NO → ↓

Is location environmentally sensitive?
├─ YES → EIA or EA required
└─ NO → Environmental checklist may suffice
```

### Which LCIA Method?
```
Location: U.S.?
├─ YES → TRACI 2.1
└─ NO → ↓

Location: Europe?
├─ YES → ReCiPe 2016 or EF method
└─ NO → ReCiPe 2016 (Global)

Need endpoint results?
├─ YES → ReCiPe 2016 (Hierarchist)
└─ NO → Any midpoint method (TRACI, CML, ReCiPe)
```

- EPA National Response Center: 1-800-424-8802
- OSHA: 1-800-321-6742

**Spill Response:**
1. Ensure safety
2. Contain spill
3. Notify authorities (if reportable)
4. Document and investigate
