## Course 501: CEA Executive Leadership
**Assignment Type:** In-Class Activity

## Assignment Overview

Effective CEA executives must work skillfully with boards of directors, managing governance relationships, presenting strategic recommendations, and navigating challenging situations. This simulation exercise provides hands-on practice in board dynamics, decision-making, and executive-board communication.

---

## Simulation Scenario

**Company:** AquaGrow Systems
- Commercial aquaponics operation with 2 facilities
- $12M annual revenue, profitable
- Board composition: 5 directors (2 founders, 2 investors, 1 independent)
- Recent disagreement over growth strategy

**Your Role:** Chief Executive Officer

**The Situation:**
You have identified a significant strategic opportunity that requires urgent board action:

A competitor with complementary technology and 3 facilities in different markets is available for acquisition at $25M (2x revenue multiple). The deal requires a decision within 72 hours due to competing bidders.

**Board Dynamics:**
- **Founder Directors (2):** Risk-averse, protective of culture, concerned about debt
- **Investor Directors (2):** Growth-focused, want ROI, open to M&A
- **Independent Director (1):** Focused on governance, fiduciary duties, process

---

## Assignment Tasks

### Part 1: Role Assignment (2 minutes)

Students will be assigned to groups of 6:
- 1 CEO (you)
- 5 Board Members (roles assigned randomly)

Each group simulates a 15-minute emergency board meeting.

### Part 2: CEO Preparation (5 minutes)

As CEO, prepare your presentation covering:

1. **Opportunity Summary**
   - What is being proposed?
   - Why is it strategic?
   - What is the timeline?

2. **Strategic Rationale**
   - How does this align with company vision?
   - What are the strategic benefits?
   - What are the risks?

3. **Financial Implications**
   - Purchase price and structure
   - Financing approach (equity, debt, hybrid)
   - Expected ROI and payback period
   - Impact on existing shareholders

4. **Implementation Approach**
   - Integration plan overview
   - Key risks and mitigation
   - Timeline and milestones

5. **Recommendation**
   - What action are you asking the board to approve?
   - What governance process is appropriate?
   - What are alternatives if board declines?

**Deliverable:** Brief presentation outline (bullet points acceptable)

### Part 3: Board Meeting Simulation (15 minutes)

**Meeting Flow:**
1. **CEO Presentation (5 minutes):** Present opportunity and recommendation
2. **Board Q&A (5 minutes):** Board members ask questions based on their roles
3. **Board Deliberation (3 minutes):** Board discusses among themselves (CEO listens)
4. **Decision (2 minutes):** Board votes and provides direction to CEO

**Board Members:** Each should:
- Ask questions aligned with your director perspective
- Challenge assumptions respectfully
- Consider fiduciary duties
- Participate in decision-making
- Provide clear direction to CEO

### Part 4: Individual Reflection (5 minutes)

After the simulation, each participant completes a brief reflection:

**For CEOs:**
- What went well in your board presentation?
- What was challenging about managing board dynamics?
- How would you improve your approach?
- What surprised you about board member concerns?

**For Board Members:**
- Did the CEO provide sufficient information for decision-making?
- Were governance processes appropriate for the situation?
- How well did you fulfill your fiduciary duties?
- What could the CEO have done differently?

## Director Role Profiles

### Founder Director #1
**Background:** Co-founder, former CEO, still emotionally invested
**Priorities:** Company culture, employee welfare, sustainable growth, avoiding excessive risk
**Concerns:** Integration challenges, cultural fit, preserving company values, debt burden
**Style:** Protective, thoughtful, asks about people and culture implications

### Founder Director #2
**Background:** Co-founder, current COO, operations-focused
**Priorities:** Operational integration, production continuity, technology compatibility
**Concerns:** Implementation complexity, distraction from current operations, integration costs
**Style:** Detail-oriented, practical, focuses on execution feasibility

### Investor Director #1
**Background:** Venture capital representative, Series A lead investor
**Priorities:** Growth, market share, financial returns, competitive positioning
**Concerns:** Valuation, dilution, ROI timeline, exit strategy implications
**Style:** Financial, strategic, focused on value creation and returns

### Investor Director #2
**Background:** Growth equity investor, Series B lead
**Priorities:** Scaling efficiently, market expansion, profitability improvement
**Concerns:** Financing structure, leverage ratios, impact on next funding round
**Style:** Strategic but pragmatic, asks about financial assumptions and risks

### Independent Director
**Background:** Former public company CFO, governance expert
**Priorities:** Process integrity, fiduciary duties, shareholder protection, transparency
**Concerns:** Decision timeline, due diligence adequacy, governance process, conflicts of interest
**Style:** Process-focused, asks about governance and decision-making framework

## Facilitation Tips

### For CEOs:
- **Prepare thoroughly** even with limited time
- **Anticipate questions** from each director perspective
- **Be transparent** about risks and unknowns
- **Listen actively** to board concerns
- **Facilitate** don't dominate
- **Respect** governance process and timeline concerns
- **Be decisive** while being open to input

### For Board Members:
- **Stay in character** based on your director role
- **Ask substantive questions** relevant to your perspective
- **Balance challenge with support** - board's job is both
- **Consider fiduciary duties** in all questions and decisions
- **Be constructive** not combative
- **Respect time** limits for discussion
- **Make decision** based on information provided

---

## Common Challenges and How to Handle Them

### Challenge: CEO Gets Defensive
**Response:** Board members should remain professional and clarify they're fulfilling fiduciary duties, not attacking personally.

### Challenge: Board Can't Reach Consensus
**Response:** Independent director can facilitate discussion of decision-making process needed.

### Challenge: Insufficient Information
**Response:** Board should clearly articulate what additional information is needed and what timeline is feasible.

### Challenge: Unrealistic Timeline Pressure
**Response:** Discuss appropriate governance process for major decisions vs. emergency situations.
