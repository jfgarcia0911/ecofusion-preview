# Assignment 01: Strategic Vision Workshop

## Course 501: CEA Executive Leadership
**Assignment Type:** In-Class Activity
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Points:** 25 points
**Module:** Module 2 - Strategic Vision and Planning
**Learning Objectives:** Develop and articulate compelling strategic visions for CEA organizations

---

## Assignment Overview

In this in-class activity, you will practice developing and articulating a compelling strategic vision statement for a CEA organization. This executive skill is fundamental to leadership and requires the ability to synthesize market insights, organizational capabilities, and stakeholder expectations into a clear, inspiring direction.

---

## Scenario

You have just been appointed as the new CEO of **VerdantTech Farms**, a mid-sized vertical farming company with:
- 3 operational facilities producing leafy greens and herbs
- 120 employees
- $18M annual revenue
- Break-even profitability
- Aging technology (5-7 years old)
- Strong local brand in the Northeast US
- Interested investor backing for expansion

The board has asked you to articulate a bold but achievable 5-year strategic vision to guide the company's next phase of growth.

---

## Assignment Tasks

### Part 1: Individual Vision Development (10 minutes)

Develop a strategic vision that addresses:

1. **Aspirational Future State**
   - Where will the company be in 5 years?
   - What will differentiate VerdantTech from competitors?
   - What impact will the company have on stakeholders?

2. **Core Strategic Themes** (Select 2-3):
   - Geographic expansion
   - Technology modernization and innovation
   - Product diversification
   - Sustainability leadership
   - Operational excellence
   - Strategic partnerships or M&A
   - Market segment focus

3. **Vision Statement**
   - Craft a 2-3 sentence vision statement that is:
     - Inspiring and aspirational
     - Specific and actionable
     - Achievable within 5 years
     - Aligned with market opportunities

**Deliverable Format:**
- Handwritten or typed brief document
- Vision statement (2-3 sentences)
- 3-5 supporting bullet points explaining strategic themes
- Key success metrics (2-3 measurable outcomes)

### Part 2: Peer Sharing and Feedback (5-10 minutes)

**Small Group Discussion (3-4 people):**
- Each person shares their vision statement (1 minute each)
- Group provides constructive feedback:
  - Is the vision inspiring and clear?
  - Is it achievable yet ambitious?
  - Does it differentiate the company?
  - What strategic risks might it face?

**Rotate roles:** Each person gets feedback from peers

|----------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------|

## Example Vision Statement (For Reference)

**Example for a Different Company:**

*"Within five years, GreenHarvest Innovations will become North America's leading sustainable protein producer, operating 15 carbon-neutral facilities producing 50,000 tons annually of premium plant-based proteins while setting the industry standard for environmental stewardship and community impact."*

**Supporting Themes:**
- Aggressive but strategic geographic expansion (15 facilities)
- Product diversification into high-value proteins
- Sustainability leadership (carbon-neutral operations)
- Premium market positioning
- Community and stakeholder value creation

**Success Metrics:**
- 15 operational facilities by Year 5
- 50,000 tons annual production
- Carbon-neutral certification for all facilities
- Premium price positioning (top quartile)
- Industry awards for sustainability leadership

## Tips for Success

### Do:
- Be specific and concrete about the future state
- Use aspirational but achievable language
- Consider market trends and opportunities
- Think about competitive differentiation
- Include measurable outcomes
- Make it memorable and inspiring
- Consider multiple stakeholder perspectives (investors, employees, customers)

### Don't:
- Be vague or generic ("We will be the best")
- Set unrealistic or impossible goals
- Ignore market realities or competitive dynamics
- Focus only on financial outcomes
- Use jargon or buzzwords without substance
- Make it too long or complicated
- Copy existing company vision statements

### Strategic Thinking Framework:

**VMOST Analysis:**
- **Vision:** Where are we going?
- **Mission:** Why do we exist?
- **Objectives:** What must we achieve?
- **Strategy:** How will we compete?
- **Tactics:** What actions will we take?

**This assignment focuses primarily on Vision and supporting Strategic themes.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I make assumptions about the scenario?**
A: Yes, reasonable assumptions are acceptable and expected. State any major assumptions clearly.

**Q: How ambitious should the vision be?**
A: Strike a balance between "stretch goals" that inspire and realistic goals that are achievable with strong execution and some favorable conditions.

**Q: Should I focus on financial or non-financial outcomes?**
A: Both. Effective visions include business outcomes (revenue, profitability, scale) and mission outcomes (sustainability, impact, innovation).

**Q: What if I don't finish in 10 minutes?**
A: This is a timed exercise to simulate real executive pressure. Do your best work in the time available - perfection is not expected.

**Q: Can I change my vision after peer feedback?**
A: For this exercise, submit your original vision. Learning comes from both creating and receiving feedback.
