## Assignment Overview

As a CEA executive, you will frequently lead organizational transformations - from technology adoptions and operational restructuring to cultural shifts and strategic pivots. This assignment challenges you to develop a comprehensive change management plan for a significant organizational transformation, demonstrating your ability to lead change while minimizing resistance and maintaining organizational performance.

---

## Assignment Scenario

Select ONE of the following transformation scenarios (or propose your own with instructor approval):

### Scenario A: Digital Transformation
**Company:** MidSized CEA operation with legacy manual systems
**Transformation:** Implementation of fully integrated ERP system connecting production, inventory, sales, finance, and quality management
**Challenge:** Long-tenured employees comfortable with current processes; skepticism about technology; fear of job displacement

### Scenario B: Sustainability Transformation
**Company:** Profitable CEA operation focused primarily on financial performance
**Transformation:** Becoming carbon-neutral and achieving B-Corp certification within 18 months
**Challenge:** Will require operational changes, capital investment, and cultural shift; some team members don't see value in sustainability beyond compliance

### Scenario C: Geographic Expansion with Centralization
**Company:** CEA company with 3 autonomous regional facilities
**Transformation:** Expanding to 8 facilities while centralizing production, procurement, and quality functions
**Challenge:** Regional teams fear loss of autonomy; concerns about standardization limiting innovation; cultural resistance to centralization

### Scenario D: Product Portfolio Pivot
**Company:** Leafy greens specialist with established market position
**Transformation:** Diversifying into high-value specialty crops (herbs, microgreens, strawberries)
**Challenge:** Requires new expertise, equipment, and markets; concern about losing focus on core business; unknown market dynamics

## Assignment Requirements

Develop a comprehensive change management plan (8-12 pages) addressing all elements below:

### 1. Executive Summary (1 page)
- Brief overview of the transformation
- Change management approach and philosophy
- Key success factors
- Timeline overview

### 2. Transformation Vision and Rationale (1-2 pages)

**Address:**
- What is the transformation and why is it necessary?
- What is the compelling vision for the future state?
- What are the business drivers and strategic imperatives?
- What happens if we don't change?
- How does this align with company mission and values?

**Include:**
- Clear vision statement for post-transformation state
- Business case summary
- Strategic alignment explanation

### 3. Stakeholder Analysis and Engagement Strategy (2-3 pages)

**Identify and analyze key stakeholder groups:**
- Who will be affected by this change?
- What is their current level of support (champion, supporter, neutral, skeptic, resistor)?
- What are their interests, concerns, and fears?
- What do they need to support the change?
- How will you engage each group?

**Develop engagement strategies:**
- Communication approach for each stakeholder group
- Involvement and participation opportunities
- Addressing resistance and concerns
- Building champions and advocates

**Create a stakeholder map** showing:
- Stakeholder influence vs. support level
- Engagement strategy for each quadrant

### 4. Change Management Strategy and Approach (2-3 pages)

**Using a recognized change management framework (select one):**
- Kotter's 8-Step Process
- ADKAR Model
- Prosci Methodology
- McKinsey 7-S Framework
- Or another established framework (cite source)

**Explain:**
- Why you selected this framework
- How you will apply each element of the framework
- Timeline and sequencing of change activities
- Integration with project management approach
- How you'll maintain business continuity during change

**Address:**
- Communication strategy and cadence
- Training and capability building approach
- Leadership alignment and role modeling
- Measurement and feedback mechanisms
- Sustainability and reinforcement plans

### 5. Resistance Management Plan (1-2 pages)

**Anticipate sources of resistance:**
- What resistance do you expect? From whom?
- What are the root causes (fear, misunderstanding, cultural, rational concerns)?
- How severe is the resistance likely to be?

**Develop mitigation strategies:**
- Proactive measures to prevent resistance
- Reactive approaches when resistance emerges
- Converting resistors to supporters
- Managing persistent resistance

**Include:**
- Early warning indicators of resistance
- Escalation procedures for significant resistance
- Coaching and support for managers dealing with resistance

### 6. Communication Plan (1-2 pages)

Develop a comprehensive communication strategy including:

**Communication Principles:**
- Transparency and honesty guidelines
- Two-way communication mechanisms
- Frequency and channels

**Communication Timeline:**
- Pre-launch communications
- Launch communications
- During transformation communications
- Post-transformation communications

**Messages for Different Audiences:**
- What message for executives/leadership?
- What message for middle managers?
- What message for frontline employees?
- What message for external stakeholders (customers, investors)?

**Channels and Tools:**
- Town halls and all-hands meetings
- Department meetings
- Email updates
- Intranet/collaboration tools
- One-on-one conversations
- Visual dashboards and progress tracking

### 7. Success Metrics and Monitoring (1 page)

**Define success metrics:**
- How will you measure change adoption?
- What KPIs indicate successful transformation?
- What are leading vs. lagging indicators?

**Examples might include:**
- Employee engagement scores
- System/process adoption rates
- Performance metrics improvement
- Resistance incidents
- Training completion rates
- Time to competency

**Monitoring approach:**
- How frequently will you measure?
- Who is responsible for tracking?
- How will you report progress?
- What triggers corrective action?

### 8. Risk Assessment and Mitigation (1 page)

**Identify transformation risks:**
- What could derail the transformation?
- What are the consequences of failure?
- What are early warning signs?

**Develop mitigation plans:**
- Risk prevention strategies
- Contingency plans for major risks
- Decision points and go/no-go criteria

### 9. Leadership and Governance (1 page)

**Change leadership structure:**
- Who is the executive sponsor?
- Who leads the change management effort?
- What is the governance structure (steering committee, working groups)?
- What are roles and responsibilities?
- How are decisions made?

**Leadership requirements:**
- What must leaders do to support this change?
- How will you ensure leadership alignment?
- What leadership development is needed?

## Tips for Success

**DO:**
- Select a scenario you can relate to or that interests you
- Use a proven change management framework as foundation
- Be specific and actionable in your recommendations
- Consider the human element of change (emotions, fears, culture)
- Provide realistic timelines and resource requirements
- Use real CEA industry examples where relevant
- Support recommendations with change management research
- Think from multiple stakeholder perspectives

**DON'T:**
- Underestimate resistance or assume change will be easy
- Ignore the emotional and cultural dimensions of change
- Create generic plans that could apply to any company
- Forget about business continuity during transformation
- Neglect the role of leadership and sponsorship
- Make the plan overly complex or bureaucratic
- Skip proper citation of frameworks and sources

## Resources

### Recommended Change Management Frameworks:
- **Kotter's 8 Steps:** Create urgency, build coalition, form vision, communicate, empower action, create wins, build on change, anchor in culture
- **ADKAR:** Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement
- **Prosci 3-Phase Process:** Prepare, Manage, Reinforce
- **McKinsey 7-S:** Strategy, Structure, Systems, Skills, Style, Staff, Shared Values

### Recommended Reading:
- "Leading Change" by John Kotter
- "Switch: How to Change Things When Change Is Hard" by Chip and Dan Heath
- "The Heart of Change" by John Kotter and Dan Cohen
- Prosci Change Management Resources

### CEA-Specific Considerations:
- Operational continuity is critical (can't stop production)
- Workforce may have technical skills but limited change experience
- Seasonal/production cycles affect implementation timing
- Food safety and quality cannot be compromised during change
- Customer supply commitments must be maintained
