## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
Which organizational structure is most common in early-stage CEA startups?

A) Matrix organization
B) Functional organization
C) Flat/entrepreneurial structure
D) Divisional organization

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Early-stage CEA startups typically adopt flat, entrepreneurial structures with minimal hierarchy, enabling rapid decision-making and direct communication as the team is small and roles are flexible.

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
What are the three core elements of organizational culture according to Edgar Schein's model?

A) Mission, vision, values
B) Artifacts, espoused values, basic assumptions
C) Structure, systems, strategy
D) People, process, technology

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Schein's organizational culture model identifies three levels: (1) Artifacts (visible structures and processes), (2) Espoused values (stated beliefs and strategies), and (3) Basic underlying assumptions (unconscious beliefs and perceptions).

---

### Question 3 (10 points)
In a matrix organization structure, employees typically report to:

A) One manager only
B) Two or more managers (functional and project/product)
C) The CEO directly
D) No one specifically

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** In a matrix structure, employees have dual reporting relationships, typically to both a functional manager (e.g., head of engineering) and a project or product manager, enabling both specialization and cross-functional collaboration.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
What is "span of control" in organizational design?

A) The geographic area a manager oversees
B) The number of direct reports a manager supervises
C) The budget authority a manager has
D) The decision-making power of executives

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Span of control refers to the number of employees who directly report to a manager. Optimal spans vary by role complexity, but typically range from 5-10 for knowledge work and up to 15-20 for operational roles.

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
Which type of organizational culture emphasizes innovation, risk-taking, and entrepreneurship?

A) Clan culture
B) Adhocracy culture
C) Market culture
D) Hierarchy culture

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** In the Competing Values Framework, adhocracy culture emphasizes innovation, agility, risk-taking, and entrepreneurship, making it well-suited for dynamic industries like CEA where innovation is critical.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
What is the primary advantage of a divisional organizational structure?

A) Maximum efficiency through specialization
B) Clear accountability for business unit performance
C) Lowest overhead costs
D) Easiest to implement

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Divisional structures organize by product, market, or geography, giving each division clear accountability for its own performance, enabling focused management and entrepreneurial ownership of results.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
In building organizational culture, which approach is most effective for leaders?

A) Publishing values statements only
B) Modeling desired behaviors and reinforcing through systems and recognition
C) Hiring consultants to define culture
D) Copying successful companies' cultures

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Culture is built through leader behavior modeling, reinforcement through hiring, performance management, recognition systems, and storytelling. Leaders must "walk the talk" as actions speak louder than written values.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
What is "organizational alignment"?

A) Physical arrangement of office space
B) Ensuring structure, systems, people, and culture support strategic objectives
C) Aligning work schedules across teams
D) Matching employee skills to job descriptions

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Organizational alignment ensures that all elements—structure, systems, processes, people capabilities, and culture—work together coherently to support strategic objectives and drive performance.

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
Which organizational design principle suggests that "form follows function"?

A) Organizations should be designed based on aesthetic preferences
B) Organizational structure should be determined by strategy and work requirements
C) All organizations should use the same structure
D) Structure is more important than strategy

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The principle that "form follows function" means organizational structure should be designed based on the organization's strategy, work requirements, and operational needs rather than imposed templates.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
What is a key indicator of strong organizational health?

A) Zero employee turnover
B) No conflicts among team members
C) High employee engagement and alignment with mission
D) Maximum number of policies and procedures

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Strong organizational health is characterized by high employee engagement, alignment with mission and values, productive collaboration, effective communication, and the ability to execute strategy—not by avoiding all conflict or turnover.
