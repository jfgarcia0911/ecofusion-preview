## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
What is the primary challenge in CEA talent acquisition?

A) Too many qualified candidates
B) Shortage of candidates with cross-functional expertise in agriculture, engineering, and technology
C) Excessive salary expectations
D) Geographic limitations only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA's primary talent challenge is the scarcity of professionals with cross-functional expertise spanning agriculture, engineering, technology, and business—requiring companies to hire for potential and invest in development.

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
What is a "talent acquisition strategy" versus "recruitment"?

A) They are the same thing
B) Strategy is long-term workforce planning; recruitment is filling specific open positions
C) Strategy is only for executives
D) Recruitment is more important than strategy

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Talent acquisition strategy is the long-term, strategic approach to workforce planning, employer branding, and talent pipeline development, while recruitment is the tactical process of filling specific open positions.

---

### Question 3 (10 points)
Which compensation philosophy element is most important for attracting talent in early-stage CEA companies?

A) High base salaries
B) Equity/ownership opportunities
C) Extensive benefits packages
D) Signing bonuses

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Early-stage CEA companies often can't compete on base salary but can offer significant equity/ownership opportunities that align employee interests with company success and provide substantial upside potential.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
What is the "rule of thumb" for time-to-fill for executive-level positions?

A) 2-4 weeks
B) 1-2 months
C) 3-6 months
D) 6-12 months

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Executive-level positions typically require 3-6 months to fill due to the need for extensive search, multiple interviews, thorough vetting, and negotiation processes. Rushing executive hires often leads to poor matches.

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
What is the "70-20-10" model for leadership development?

A) 70% classroom training, 20% reading, 10% mentoring
B) 70% on-the-job experience, 20% coaching/mentoring, 10% formal training
C) 70% salary, 20% bonus, 10% equity
D) 70% technical skills, 20% soft skills, 10% strategic thinking

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The 70-20-10 model states that development comes from 70% challenging on-the-job experiences, 20% coaching and mentoring relationships, and 10% formal training programs—emphasizing learning through doing.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
What is "succession planning"?

A) Planning the order of production cycles
B) Identifying and developing future leaders to fill key positions
C) Creating organization charts
D) Employee termination processes

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Succession planning is the systematic process of identifying and developing employees with the potential to fill key leadership positions, ensuring organizational continuity and reduced risk of leadership gaps.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
Which interview technique is most effective for assessing past performance and predicting future success?

A) Hypothetical situational questions
B) Behavioral interview questions (e.g., "Tell me about a time when...")
C) Brain teasers and puzzles
D) Discussing hobbies and interests only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Behavioral interview questions that explore past experiences and actions are the best predictor of future performance, as they reveal how candidates actually handled real situations rather than how they think they would respond.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
What is a "retention strategy" and why is it critical in CEA?

A) Keeping produce fresh longer
B) Strategies to retain employees, critical in CEA due to talent scarcity and high replacement costs
C) Customer loyalty programs
D) Water retention in growing media

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Retention strategy encompasses all efforts to keep valuable employees, including competitive compensation, career development, culture, and engagement. In CEA, where talent is scarce, retention is more cost-effective than replacement.

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
What is the recommended approach for building a diverse and inclusive workforce?

A) Quotas for hiring
B) Intentional strategies including diverse candidate sourcing, unbiased processes, and inclusive culture
C) Avoiding the topic entirely
D) Focusing only on technical qualifications

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Building diversity requires intentional strategies including diverse candidate sourcing, structured interviews to reduce bias, diverse hiring panels, inclusive culture development, and accountability for diversity goals.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
What is the purpose of a "performance improvement plan" (PIP)?

A) To document reasons for termination
B) To provide clear expectations, support, and timeline for underperforming employees to improve
C) To reduce employee compensation
D) To promote high performers

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A Performance Improvement Plan is a structured approach to address performance gaps by clearly defining expectations, providing support and resources, establishing timelines, and giving employees a fair opportunity to improve before considering termination.
