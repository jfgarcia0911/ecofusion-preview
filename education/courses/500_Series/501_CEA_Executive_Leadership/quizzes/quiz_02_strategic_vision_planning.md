## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
Which strategic thinking framework emphasizes analyzing Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats?

A) Porter's Five Forces
B) SWOT Analysis
C) Blue Ocean Strategy
D) Balanced Scorecard

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** SWOT Analysis is a strategic planning framework that evaluates internal Strengths and Weaknesses alongside external Opportunities and Threats to inform strategic decision-making.

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
In Porter's Five Forces framework, which force is NOT included?

A) Threat of new entrants
B) Bargaining power of suppliers
C) Government regulations
D) Threat of substitute products

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Porter's Five Forces include: (1) Threat of new entrants, (2) Bargaining power of suppliers, (3) Bargaining power of buyers, (4) Threat of substitute products, and (5) Competitive rivalry. Government regulations are not one of the five forces.

---

### Question 3 (10 points)
What time horizon typically defines a strategic vision for CEA organizations?

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-5 years
C) 5-10 years
D) 15-20 years

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Strategic visions for CEA organizations typically span 5-10 years, providing a long-term direction while remaining adaptable to industry changes and technological innovations.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
Which scenario planning approach involves developing multiple plausible future scenarios?

A) Single-point forecasting
B) Linear projection
C) Multiple futures/contingency planning
D) Wishful thinking

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Scenario planning develops multiple plausible future scenarios (typically 3-4) to prepare organizations for different potential outcomes and identify robust strategies that work across multiple futures.

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
What is the primary purpose of a strategic roadmap?

A) To create detailed operational procedures
B) To visualize the path from current state to strategic vision with key milestones
C) To replace the need for annual planning
D) To eliminate uncertainty in business planning

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A strategic roadmap visualizes the journey from the current state to the desired future state, outlining key milestones, initiatives, and decision points along the way.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
In competitive positioning, what does a "differentiation strategy" focus on?

A) Being the lowest-cost provider
B) Creating unique value that customers are willing to pay premium prices for
C) Serving a narrow market segment
D) Copying competitor offerings

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A differentiation strategy focuses on creating unique value through superior quality, innovation, service, or brand that distinguishes the organization from competitors and justifies premium pricing.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
What is a "strategic inflection point"?

A) A minor adjustment to tactics
B) A time when fundamental business dynamics change, requiring strategic redirection
C) The end of a fiscal quarter
D) A moment when all competitors make the same move

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A strategic inflection point is a time in the life of a business when its fundamentals are about to change, requiring leaders to recognize the shift and adjust strategy accordingly.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
Which stakeholder communication approach is most effective for articulating strategic vision?

A) Detailed financial projections only
B) Technical specifications and metrics
C) Compelling narrative with clear purpose, vision, and call to action
D) Avoiding specific details to maintain flexibility

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Effective strategic vision communication uses compelling narratives that articulate purpose, paint a clear picture of the future, and inspire action while remaining memorable and authentic.

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
What percentage of strategies fail due to poor execution rather than poor strategy?

A) 30-40%
B) 50-60%
C) 70-80%
D) 90-95%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Research shows that approximately 70-80% of strategies fail due to poor execution rather than poor strategy formulation, highlighting the critical importance of implementation excellence.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
In the context of CEA strategic planning, what does "strategic agility" refer to?

A) The ability to change strategy every month
B) Avoiding long-term commitments
C) The capacity to quickly sense and respond to market changes while maintaining strategic direction
D) Operating without a formal strategy

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Strategic agility is the ability to rapidly sense and respond to market changes, technological shifts, and emerging opportunities while maintaining overall strategic direction and core values.
