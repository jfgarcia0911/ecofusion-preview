## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
What are the three primary fiduciary duties of board members?

A) Loyalty, Care, Obedience
B) Strategy, Finance, Compliance
C) Hiring, Firing, Compensation
D) Vision, Mission, Values

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Board members have three primary fiduciary duties: (1) Duty of Loyalty (act in organization's best interests), (2) Duty of Care (exercise reasonable judgment), and (3) Duty of Obedience (ensure compliance with laws and mission).

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
How often should boards of high-growth CEA companies typically meet?

A) Annually
B) Semi-annually
C) Quarterly
D) Monthly

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** High-growth CEA companies typically benefit from monthly board meetings to provide adequate oversight, strategic guidance, and support during rapid scaling phases. More mature companies may transition to quarterly meetings.

---

### Question 3 (10 points)
Which board committee is responsible for CEO performance evaluation and compensation?

A) Audit Committee
B) Compensation Committee
C) Governance Committee
D) Strategy Committee

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Compensation Committee (sometimes called Compensation and Human Capital Committee) is responsible for CEO and executive compensation, performance evaluation, and ensuring alignment of incentives with company performance.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
What is the recommended composition for an effective board of directors?

A) All inside directors (company executives)
B) All outside directors with no company connection
C) Majority independent directors with diverse expertise
D) Equal number of investors and founders

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Effective boards typically have a majority of independent directors with diverse expertise, industry knowledge, and functional backgrounds, while including key executives and major investors as appropriate.

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
What information should be included in board materials sent in advance of meetings?

A) Meeting agenda only
B) Agenda, financial statements, and KPI dashboard
C) Comprehensive materials including agenda, financial reports, KPIs, strategic updates, and discussion materials
D) Only items requiring board votes

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Board materials should be comprehensive and sent 5-7 days in advance, including agenda, financial statements, KPI dashboard, strategic updates, discussion materials, and decision items to enable informed preparation.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
What is "board governance" versus "board management"?

A) They are the same thing
B) Governance focuses on oversight and strategic direction; management focuses on day-to-day operations
C) Governance is for public companies only
D) Management is more important than governance

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Board governance focuses on strategic oversight, policy setting, CEO accountability, and fiduciary responsibilities, while management (which boards should avoid) involves day-to-day operational decisions that are the CEO's domain.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
What is the appropriate role of board members between meetings?

A) Direct oversight of employees
B) Making operational decisions
C) Available for CEO counsel, industry connections, and specific expertise as needed
D) No involvement until the next meeting

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Between meetings, effective board members serve as resources for the CEO, providing counsel, industry connections, and specific expertise while respecting the boundary between governance and management.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
Which of the following is a red flag in board relations?

A) Constructive debate on strategic issues
B) Board members contacting employees directly without CEO knowledge
C) Requesting additional information on key decisions
D) Annual board self-evaluation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Board members circumventing the CEO to contact employees directly undermines CEO authority and blurs governance-management boundaries. All board communication should flow through the CEO except in specific circumstances (like investigation of CEO).

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
What should be included in effective CEO reporting to the board?

A) Only positive accomplishments
B) Financial results only
C) Balanced view including progress, challenges, risks, and decisions needed
D) Detailed operational metrics only

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Effective CEO reporting provides a balanced view including strategic progress, financial performance, key accomplishments, challenges and setbacks, risk factors, and decisions where board input is needed.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
How should boards approach CEO succession planning?

A) Only address it when the CEO announces departure
B) Ongoing process with emergency and planned succession scenarios
C) Delegate entirely to the CEO
D) Wait until the company is profitable

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Effective boards engage in ongoing CEO succession planning, developing both emergency succession plans (unexpected departure) and planned succession strategies, ensuring business continuity and leadership pipeline development.
