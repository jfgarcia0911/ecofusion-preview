## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
What are the three phases of crisis management?

A) Planning, Reaction, Recovery
B) Prevention, Response, Recovery
C) Preparation, Mitigation, Response
D) Detection, Containment, Resolution

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The three phases of crisis management are: (1) Prevention/Preparedness (planning and risk reduction), (2) Response (immediate action during crisis), and (3) Recovery (returning to normal and learning).

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
What is the most critical action for executives in the first hours of a crisis?

A) Calling a press conference
B) Assessing the situation, ensuring safety, and activating crisis response team
C) Assigning blame
D) Consulting lawyers only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** In crisis first response, priorities are: (1) Ensure safety of people, (2) Assess situation and gather facts, (3) Activate crisis response team, (4) Begin stakeholder communication, (5) Contain the immediate threat.

---

### Question 3 (10 points)
What types of crises are most common in CEA operations?

A) Product recalls only
B) Operational crises (crop failures, contamination, equipment failures, power outages)
C) Natural disasters only
D) Cybersecurity breaches only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA facilities face primarily operational crises including crop failures, contamination events, critical equipment failures, climate control malfunctions, and power outages, though other crisis types can also occur.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
What is a "crisis communication plan"?

A) Marketing strategy during downturns
B) Pre-developed protocols, message templates, and stakeholder contact lists for crisis communication
C) Social media strategy
D) Customer service scripts

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A crisis communication plan includes pre-developed protocols for who communicates what to whom, message templates, stakeholder contact lists, designated spokespersons, and communication channels for various crisis scenarios.

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
What principle should guide executive communication during a crisis?

A) Say nothing until all facts are known
B) Shift blame to others
C) Be transparent, acknowledge the situation, communicate what's known and what's being done
D) Minimize the situation's severity

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Crisis communication should be transparent, acknowledge the situation honestly, communicate what's known (and what's not yet known), outline actions being taken, and provide regular updates. Transparency builds trust even in difficult situations.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
What is "business continuity planning" (BCP)?

A) Sales forecasting
B) Strategies and procedures to ensure critical operations continue during and after disruptions
C) Succession planning
D) Marketing during recessions

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Business Continuity Planning identifies critical business functions, potential disruptions, and strategies/procedures to ensure operations can continue or quickly resume during and after crises or disasters.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
What is the recommended frequency for testing crisis response plans?

A) Only after a crisis occurs
B) Every 5 years
C) Annually or bi-annually through tabletop exercises and drills
D) Never, plans should just exist on paper

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Crisis response plans should be tested annually or bi-annually through tabletop exercises, simulations, and drills to identify gaps, train team members, update procedures, and ensure readiness when real crises occur.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
What is a "post-crisis review" and why is it important?

A) Legal proceeding to assign fault
B) Structured analysis of crisis response to identify lessons learned and improve future preparedness
C) Press conference
D) Employee performance review

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Post-crisis review (also called After-Action Review) is a structured process to analyze what happened, how response performed, what worked well, what didn't, and what can be improved—turning crises into learning opportunities.

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
Which stakeholder group should typically be informed first during a food safety crisis?

A) Media
B) Investors
C) Regulatory authorities (FDA, health departments)
D) Social media followers

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** In food safety crises, regulatory authorities (FDA, state health departments) must be notified immediately as required by law, often within 24 hours. This ensures proper oversight and coordinated public health response.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
What leadership qualities are most critical during crisis situations?

A) Optimism and denial
B) Calm decisiveness, clear communication, empathy, and taking responsibility
C) Blame assignment and defensiveness
D) Delegation and absence

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Effective crisis leadership requires calm decisiveness under pressure, clear and honest communication, empathy for those affected, taking responsibility, visible presence, and inspiring confidence while managing the situation.
