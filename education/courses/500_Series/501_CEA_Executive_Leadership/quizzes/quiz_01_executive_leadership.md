## Questions

### Question 1 (10 points)
Which of the following is NOT one of the five core competency domains in the CEA Leadership Pentagon?

A) Strategic Thinking
B) Technical Credibility
C) Marketing Excellence
D) Financial Acumen
E) Stakeholder Management

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The CEA Leadership Pentagon consists of: (1) Strategic Thinking, (2) Technical Credibility, (3) Financial Acumen, (4) Stakeholder Management, and (5) Organizational Leadership. While marketing is important, it's not one of the five core competency domains in this framework.

---

### Question 2 (10 points)
According to the industry evolution timeline, which phase is the CEA industry currently in (as of 2025)?

A) Pioneers (1990-2010)
B) Venture-Backed Growth (2010-2018)
C) Market Correction (2018-2022)
D) Strategic Maturation (2022-Present)

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** The course identifies Strategic Maturation (2022-Present) as the current phase, characterized by sustainable business models emerging, hybrid approaches, and climate resilience as the primary value proposition.

### Question 3 (10 points)
What is the recommended target for employee engagement scores on a 5-point scale?

A) 3.0 or higher
B) 3.5 or higher
C) 4.0 or higher
D) 4.5 or higher

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The lesson recommends an overall engagement score of 4.0 or higher on a 5-point scale as a target metric for organizational health.

---

### Question 4 (10 points)
In the CEA organizational culture challenge, which three cultures must CEA organizations integrate?

A) Agriculture, Manufacturing, Retail
B) Agriculture, Technology, Business
C) Technology, Retail, Logistics
D) Engineering, Sales, Operations

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA organizations must bridge and integrate Agriculture Culture (patient, seasonal, craft-based), Technology Culture (fast iteration, data-driven), and Business Culture (quarterly results, metrics-focused).

---

### Question 5 (10 points)
According to the typical CEO time distribution, what percentage of time should be allocated to strategic planning and decision-making?

A) 15%
B) 20%
C) 25%
D) 30%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The lesson indicates that CEOs typically allocate approximately 25% of their time to strategic planning and decision-making, making it one of the top priorities alongside stakeholder management and organizational issues.

---

### Question 6 (10 points)
What was the primary lesson from the AppHarvest case study regarding scaling?

A) Always pursue rapid growth to achieve market leadership
B) Match expansion pace to operational capability
C) Focus exclusively on technology before expanding
D) Prioritize multiple facilities over single-site profitability

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A key lesson from AppHarvest's bankruptcy was the importance of capital discipline and matching expansion pace to operational capability—proving unit economics at one facility before replicating.

---

### Question 7 (10 points)
Which leadership level represents the highest degree of board engagement?

A) Level 1: Fiduciary
B) Level 2: Strategic Partner
C) Level 3: Operational Involvement
D) All levels are equally appropriate

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Level 2 (Strategic Partner) is the target level where boards provide strategic guidance, leverage networks, and coach executives. Level 3 (Operational Involvement) is actually a red flag indicating inappropriate micromanagement.

---

### Question 8 (10 points)
In building an executive network, which category is NOT identified as a strategic relationship category?

A) Industry Peers
B) Capital Partners
C) Competitive Intelligence
D) Strategic Partners

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The four strategic relationship categories are: Industry Peers, Capital Partners, Strategic Partners, and Advisory Resources. While competitive intelligence is important, it's not listed as a formal relationship category.

---

### Question 9 (10 points)
What distinguishes CEA executive leadership from traditional agriculture or pure technology sectors?

A) Lower capital requirements
B) Shorter product development cycles
C) Must bridge multiple domains (ag, engineering, biotech, logistics, retail)
D) Less regulatory complexity

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A defining characteristic of CEA executive leadership is the need to bridge multiple domains including agriculture, engineering, biotechnology, logistics, and retail—unlike traditional agriculture or pure technology sectors.

---

### Question 10 (10 points)
According to the lesson, which statement best describes the role of a personal leadership philosophy?

A) It should be kept private and not shared with employees
B) It guides decisions, defines success, and articulates values and purpose
C) It's only necessary for CEOs, not other executives
D) It should focus exclusively on financial performance

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A personal leadership philosophy should guide decisions, define what success looks like, and articulate core values, leadership purpose, approach, and legacy intent. It should be authentic and can be shared to inspire and align teams.
