# Glossary: CEA Executive Leadership Terminology

**Course 501: CEA Executive Leadership**

---

## A

**Adjacent Innovation**
Innovation that extends into nearby markets, products, or capabilities. Lower risk than breakthrough but higher than incremental. Example: Adding strawberries when you grow leafy greens.

**Adhocracy Culture**
Organizational culture emphasizing innovation, entrepreneurship, and risk-taking. One of four types in the Competing Values Framework.

**AgTech**
Agricultural technology; technologies applied to agriculture to improve efficiency, yields, and sustainability.

**Ambidextrous Organization**
An organization that can simultaneously operate efficiently (exploit current business) and innovate (explore new opportunities).

**ASP (Average Selling Price)**
The average price received per unit sold. Critical metric in CEA unit economics.

---

## B

**Blue Ocean Strategy**
Strategy focused on creating uncontested market space rather than competing in existing markets. Uses ERRC framework (Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create).

**Board of Directors**
Governing body elected by shareholders to oversee management and protect shareholder interests. Responsibilities include strategy oversight, CEO selection, and fiduciary duty.

**Breakeven Analysis**
Calculation of the sales volume at which total revenue equals total costs, resulting in zero profit or loss.

**Breakthrough Innovation**
Fundamental innovation creating new products, technologies, or business models. High risk, high return, 3-10 year timeframe.

**Burn Rate**
The rate at which a company spends its cash reserves, typically measured monthly. Critical metric for cash runway.

---

## C

**Cap Table (Capitalization Table)**
A spreadsheet showing the equity ownership of a company, including shares, options, and convertible securities.

**CapEx (Capital Expenditures)**
Funds used to acquire, upgrade, or maintain physical assets like facilities and equipment.

**Cash Conversion Cycle**
The time (in days) it takes to convert resource inputs into cash. Calculated as DSO + DIO - DPO.

**CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture)**
Agriculture practiced in controlled environments such as greenhouses, vertical farms, or indoor facilities to optimize growing conditions.

**C-Suite**
The group of top executives in an organization, typically with titles starting with "Chief" (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, etc.).

**Change Management**
The systematic approach to transitioning individuals, teams, and organizations from current state to desired future state.

**Circular Economy**
Economic model focused on eliminating waste through reduce, reuse, recycle, and regenerate principles.

**COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**
Direct costs attributable to producing goods sold, including materials and direct labor.

**Contribution Margin**
Revenue minus variable costs. Indicates how much each unit contributes to covering fixed costs and profit.

---

## D

**DCF (Discounted Cash Flow)**
Valuation method calculating present value of future cash flows using a discount rate.

**DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion)**
Organizational commitment to diverse representation, fair opportunity, and sense of belonging for all.

**DIO (Days Inventory Outstanding)**
Average number of days inventory is held before being sold. Lower is better for fresh produce.

**DPO (Days Payable Outstanding)**
Average number of days a company takes to pay suppliers. Balances cash management with supplier relationships.

**DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)**
Average number of days to collect payment after a sale. Target <40 days for CEA.

**Due Diligence**
Comprehensive investigation and analysis conducted before business transaction (M&A, investment, partnership).

**Duty of Care**
Fiduciary duty requiring directors to be informed, attentive, and exercise reasonable judgment.

**Duty of Loyalty**
Fiduciary duty requiring directors to act in the company's best interests, not personal interests.

**Duty of Obedience**
Fiduciary duty requiring directors to ensure company complies with laws and regulations.

---

## E

**EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization)**
Measure of operating profitability. Key metric for CEA companies. Target 15-25% margin at scale.

**Earnout**
Performance-based payment in M&A transaction. Seller receives additional compensation if targets are met.

**Ecosystem**
Network of interconnected partners creating and delivering value. More collaborative than linear supply chain.

**Enterprise Value (EV)**
Total value of a company, calculated as market capitalization plus debt minus cash.

**ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**
Integrated software system managing core business processes (finance, HR, operations, supply chain).

**ERRC Framework**
Blue Ocean Strategy tool: Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create. Identifies differentiation opportunities.

**ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance)**
Framework for measuring organizational sustainability and ethical impact beyond financial performance.

---

## F

**Fiduciary Duty**
Legal obligation to act in another party's best interests. Board members owe fiduciary duty to shareholders.

**Free Cash Flow**
Cash generated after capital expenditures. Indicator of financial health and available cash for growth or returns.

**FSMA (Food Safety Modernization Act)**
FDA regulation focusing on preventing food safety problems. Includes Produce Safety Rule applicable to CEA.

**Founder Transition**
Process of founder moving from CEO role to other role (executive chair, board, exit) as company matures.

---

## G

**GFSI (Global Food Safety Initiative)**
Industry initiative providing framework for food safety certification schemes (SQF, BRCGS, etc.).

**Greenwashing**
Making misleading or exaggerated environmental claims to appear more sustainable than reality.

**Gross Margin**
Revenue minus cost of goods sold, expressed as percentage of revenue. Target 40-60% for CEA.

**Growth Mindset**
Belief that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work. Contrasts with fixed mindset.

---

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)**
Systematic preventive approach to food safety identifying physical, chemical, and biological hazards.

**High-Potential Employee**
Employee identified as having capacity for advancement to senior leadership roles. Focus of succession planning.

**Horizon Scanning**
Systematic examination of potential threats, opportunities, and likely future developments. Used in strategic foresight.

**HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning)**
Climate control systems. Critical and energy-intensive in CEA facilities.

---

## I

**IDP (Individual Development Plan)**
Personalized plan outlining employee's development goals, actions, and timeline.

**Incremental Innovation**
Continuous improvement of existing products or processes. Low risk, moderate return, short timeframe.

**IP (Intellectual Property)**
Creations of the mind with commercial value, protected by patents, trademarks, copyrights, or trade secrets.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**
Metric used to estimate profitability of investments. Discount rate that makes NPV equal zero.

---

## J

**Jobs-to-be-Done Framework**
Innovation framework focused on understanding customer needs (functional, emotional, social jobs) rather than product features.

**Joint Venture (JV)**
Business arrangement where two or more parties create new entity, sharing ownership, returns, and governance.

---

## K

**Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**
Measurable value demonstrating how effectively a company achieves key business objectives.

**Kotter's 8-Step Change Model**
Framework for leading organizational change: Create Urgency → Build Coalition → Form Vision → Enlist Army → Enable Action → Generate Wins → Sustain Acceleration → Institute Change.

---

## L

**LCA (Life Cycle Assessment)**
Comprehensive analysis of environmental impacts throughout product lifecycle, from raw materials to disposal.

**LED (Light Emitting Diode)**
Energy-efficient lighting technology. Largest energy consumer in many vertical farms (40-60% of total).

**Legacy**
Lasting impact a leader has on organization, people, industry, and world. Key consideration in final career phase.

**Letter of Intent (LOI)**
Document outlining preliminary agreement between parties in business transaction. Mostly non-binding except exclusivity and confidentiality.

---

## M

**M&A (Mergers and Acquisitions)**
Consolidation of companies through mergers (combination) or acquisitions (purchase).

**Market Capitalization**
Total market value of company's outstanding shares. Stock price × shares outstanding.

**Materiality Assessment**
Process of identifying ESG topics most important to business and stakeholders. Focuses reporting on material topics.

**MVP (Minimum Viable Product)**
Product with minimum features to satisfy early customers and provide feedback for development.

---

## N

**Net Debt**
Total debt minus cash and cash equivalents. Used in calculating equity value from enterprise value.

**Net Zero**
Achieving balance between greenhouse gas emissions produced and removed from atmosphere. Typically 2040-2050 target.

**NPV (Net Present Value)**
Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment. If positive, investment creates value.

**NPS (Net Promoter Score)**
Customer loyalty metric based on likelihood to recommend. Scale -100 to +100; target 70+ for excellence.

---

## O

**OKR (Objectives and Key Results)**
Goal-setting framework. Objective = qualitative goal; Key Results = quantitative measures (2-4 per objective).

**OODA Loop**
Decision-making framework: Observe-Orient-Decide-Act. Useful in crisis and fast-moving situations.

**Open Innovation**
Leveraging external ideas, technologies, and partnerships alongside internal R&D. Contrasts with closed innovation.

**Organic Growth**
Growth from company's existing business through increased output, customer base, or new products. Contrasts with M&A growth.

---

## P

**P&L (Profit and Loss Statement)**
Financial statement showing revenues, costs, and expenses over period. Also called income statement.

**Patent**
Exclusive right granted for invention. Protects novel, non-obvious, and useful inventions for 20 years.

**PIP (Performance Improvement Plan)**
Formal document outlining performance issues, expectations, timeline, and consequences. Used to address underperformance.

**PMI (Post-Merger Integration)**
Process of combining companies after M&A transaction. Critical for value realization.

**Porter's Five Forces**
Framework analyzing competitive intensity: Threat of New Entrants, Supplier Power, Buyer Power, Threat of Substitutes, Industry Rivalry.

**PPA (Power Purchase Agreement)**
Long-term contract to purchase renewable energy at fixed price. Reduces energy cost risk and enables renewable energy claims.

---

## R

**R&D (Research and Development)**
Activities companies undertake to innovate and introduce new products or services.

**ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)**
Measure of how well company generates returns from invested capital. Formula: NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital.

**ROI (Return on Investment)**
Measure of gain or loss relative to investment. Formula: (Gain - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100.

**RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**
Target time to restore function after disruption. Used in business continuity planning.

---

## S

**SaaS (Software as a Service)**
Software delivery model where applications hosted by provider and accessed via internet. Common in CEA technology platforms.

**SASB (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)**
Industry-specific ESG disclosure standards. Investor-focused, material topics by industry.

**SBT (Science-Based Target)**
Emissions reduction target aligned with climate science (1.5°C or 2°C pathway). Validated by Science Based Targets initiative.

**Scenario Planning**
Strategic planning method using multiple plausible future scenarios to prepare for uncertainty. Typically 2×2 matrix = 4 scenarios.

**Scope 1, 2, 3 Emissions**
GHG accounting categories: Scope 1 = direct emissions; Scope 2 = purchased energy; Scope 3 = value chain.

**Stage-Gate Process**
Innovation process with stages (ideation, feasibility, development, launch) and gates (go/no-go decision points).

**Stakeholder**
Individual or group with interest in company: shareholders, employees, customers, suppliers, community, regulators.

**Strategic Alliance**
Cooperative agreement between companies to achieve strategic objectives while remaining independent.

**SWOT Analysis**
Framework assessing Strengths, Weaknesses (internal), Opportunities, Threats (external).

---

## T

**TAM (Total Addressable Market)**
Total revenue opportunity available if company achieved 100% market share. Used in market sizing.

**TCFD (Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures)**
Framework for climate risk and opportunity disclosure. Covers governance, strategy, risk management, metrics/targets.

**Term Sheet**
Document outlining key terms and conditions of investment or business agreement. Typically non-binding except specific provisions.

**360-Degree Feedback**
Performance feedback gathered from multiple sources: supervisor, peers, direct reports, self. Used for development.

**Trade Secret**
Confidential business information providing competitive advantage. Protected indefinitely if kept secret (e.g., Coca-Cola formula).

---

## U

**Unit Economics**
Revenues and costs associated with single unit of product. Foundation of CEA financial modeling.

**Utilization Rate**
Actual production as percentage of nameplate capacity. Target 85-90% for mature CEA facilities.

---

## V

**Valuation**
Process of determining current worth of company or asset. Methods include multiples, DCF, replacement cost.

**VC (Venture Capital)**
Financing provided to startups and growth companies with high growth potential. Equity investment in exchange for ownership.

**Vertical Integration**
Strategy where company controls multiple stages of value chain (backward = suppliers; forward = distribution/retail).

**Vision Statement**
Aspirational description of what organization wants to achieve in future. Guides strategic direction.

---

## W

**Working Capital**
Current assets minus current liabilities. Funds available for day-to-day operations. Formula: (Cash + AR + Inventory) - (AP + Accrued Exp).

## Industry-Specific Terms

**Brix**
Measure of sugar content in produce, indicating sweetness and flavor quality. Higher brix often correlates with better taste.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)**
Total amount of photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) received per day. Critical growing parameter in CEA.

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)**
Hydroponic method where plant roots suspended in nutrient-rich oxygenated water.

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)**
Measure of nutrient concentration in hydroponic solution. Monitored to ensure optimal nutrition.

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)**
Hydroponic method with thin film of nutrient solution flowing over roots. Not related to crypto NFTs.

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)**
Light spectrum (400-700nm) plants use for photosynthesis. Measured in µmol/m²/s.

**pH**
Measure of acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale. Optimal range for most crops: 5.5-6.5 in hydroponics.

**PPM (Parts Per Million)**
Concentration measure for nutrients or contaminants in solution.

**Rockwool**
Growing medium made from spun volcanic rock. Common in CEA but environmental concerns around disposal.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)**
Difference between water vapor pressure in air and at saturation. Optimized for plant transpiration and growth.
