# Grading Rubric - Course 501: CEA Executive Leadership

## Course Overview
**Level:** Master/Executive (500-Series)
**Total Points:** 1000 points
**Passing Grade:** 800 points (80%)

## Assessment Categories

### 1. Module Quizzes (300 points total - 30%)
**14 Module Quizzes × 21.43 points each**

Each quiz assesses comprehension of executive leadership concepts, frameworks, and strategic thinking.

| Criteria | Exceptional (19-21 pts) | Proficient (16-18 pts) | Developing (13-15 pts) | Unsatisfactory (0-12 pts) |
|----------|------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| Knowledge Mastery | Demonstrates exceptional understanding of strategic leadership concepts | Shows solid grasp of key concepts | Demonstrates basic understanding with gaps | Significant gaps in knowledge |
| Application | Applies concepts to complex scenarios with nuance | Applies concepts appropriately | Limited application ability | Cannot apply concepts effectively |
| Critical Thinking | Shows sophisticated strategic analysis | Demonstrates good analytical skills | Shows some analytical thinking | Lacks critical analysis |

**Total Quiz Points:** 300 points (30%)

### 2. Strategic Plan Project (250 points - 25%)

Comprehensive strategic plan for a CEA organization demonstrating executive-level planning capabilities.

#### Section Breakdown:

**A. Executive Summary (30 points)**
- Clarity and conciseness
- Strategic vision articulation
- Key priorities and rationale
- Stakeholder appeal

**B. Situational Analysis (50 points)**
- Industry landscape assessment
- Competitive positioning analysis
- SWOT analysis depth
- Market opportunities identification
- Data-driven insights

**C. Strategic Vision & Goals (40 points)**
- Compelling vision statement
- Clear strategic objectives
- Alignment with market realities
- Measurability and time-bound goals
- Stakeholder value proposition

**D. Strategic Initiatives (60 points)**
- Initiative comprehensiveness
- Resource allocation logic
- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Innovation and differentiation
- Operational feasibility

**E. Implementation Roadmap (40 points)**
- Phasing and prioritization
- Timeline realism
- Resource requirements
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Governance structure

**F. Financial Projections (30 points)**
- Revenue and cost forecasting
- Capital requirements
- ROI analysis
- Financial sustainability
- Assumption transparency

| Criteria | Exceptional (A) | Proficient (B) | Developing (C) | Unsatisfactory (D-F) |
|----------|----------------|----------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Strategic Thinking | Demonstrates sophisticated, innovative strategic framework | Shows solid strategic planning skills | Basic strategic planning evident | Lacks strategic depth |
| Executive Communication | Professional, compelling, board-ready quality | Clear and well-organized | Adequate but needs refinement | Poor communication quality |
| Data & Analysis | Rigorous, data-driven with multiple sources | Good use of data and research | Limited data utilization | Insufficient analysis |
| Feasibility | Highly realistic and actionable | Generally feasible | Questionable feasibility | Unrealistic or vague |

**Total Strategic Plan Points:** 250 points (25%)

### 3. Board Presentation (200 points - 20%)

Executive presentation to simulated board of directors (30 minutes + 15 minutes Q&A).

#### Evaluation Criteria:

**A. Content Quality (80 points)**
- Strategic clarity and coherence (20 pts)
- Data and evidence quality (20 pts)
- Recommendation strength (20 pts)
- Risk awareness and mitigation (20 pts)

**B. Presentation Skills (60 points)**
- Executive presence and confidence (20 pts)
- Communication clarity and impact (20 pts)
- Time management (20 pts)

**C. Visual Communication (30 points)**
- Slide design and professionalism (15 pts)
- Data visualization effectiveness (15 pts)

**D. Q&A Performance (30 points)**
- Response quality and depth (15 pts)
- Composure under pressure (15 pts)

| Criteria | Exceptional (90-100%) | Proficient (80-89%) | Developing (70-79%) | Unsatisfactory (<70%) |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Board-Level Quality | C-suite presentation quality, board-ready | Strong professional presentation | Competent but needs polish | Below professional standard |
| Strategic Acumen | Demonstrates executive-level thinking | Shows good strategic judgment | Basic strategic understanding | Lacks strategic perspective |
| Stakeholder Management | Expertly addresses board concerns | Handles questions well | Adequate responses | Poor stakeholder engagement |

**Total Board Presentation Points:** 200 points (20%)

### 4. Final Examination (250 points - 25%)

Comprehensive assessment of executive leadership competencies across all modules.

**Format:** 50 questions covering 14 modules
- 30 Multiple choice/Multiple select (3 points each = 90 points)
- 15 Scenario-based questions (8 points each = 120 points)
- 5 Short essay questions (8 points each = 40 points)

#### Content Distribution:

| Module Area | Points | Focus |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Strategic Leadership & Vision | 40 pts | Vision development, strategic thinking |
| Organizational Excellence | 35 pts | Structure, culture, governance |
| Financial Leadership | 35 pts | Capital strategy, financial decision-making |
| Talent & Change Management | 30 pts | Team building, transformation leadership |
| Crisis & Growth Leadership | 30 pts | Crisis response, M&A, partnerships |
| Industry Leadership & Influence | 30 pts | Policy, sustainability, innovation |
| Board Relations & Legacy | 25 pts | Governance, succession planning |
| Integration & Application | 25 pts | Cross-module synthesis |

**Question Type Rubrics:**

**Scenario-Based Questions (8 points each):**
- 8 points: Exceptional strategic analysis with nuanced recommendations
- 6-7 points: Strong analysis with appropriate recommendations
- 4-5 points: Adequate analysis with basic recommendations
- 0-3 points: Weak analysis or inappropriate recommendations

**Short Essay Questions (8 points each):**
- 8 points: Comprehensive, insightful response with executive perspective
- 6-7 points: Solid response demonstrating good understanding
- 4-5 points: Adequate response with basic understanding
- 0-3 points: Incomplete or incorrect response

**Total Final Examination Points:** 250 points (25%)

## Assignment Submission Requirements

### Professional Standards
All submissions must meet executive-level professional standards:
- **Format:** Professional business document formatting
- **Citations:** Proper citation of sources (APA or Harvard style)
- **Length:** Adherence to specified page/time limits
- **Quality:** Free of grammatical errors, typos, professional tone
- **Originality:** All work must be original; plagiarism = automatic failure

### Late Submission Policy
- **On-time:** Full points available
- **1-24 hours late:** 10% penalty
- **24-48 hours late:** 25% penalty
- **>48 hours late:** Not accepted without prior arrangement
- **Extensions:** Request at least 48 hours in advance with valid reason

### Resubmission Policy
- Strategic Plan: One revision opportunity with 10% penalty
- Board Presentation: No resubmission; live delivery only
- Final Exam: No retakes except in documented emergency situations

## Academic Integrity

This executive program maintains the highest standards of academic integrity:

**Zero Tolerance for:**
- Plagiarism (automatic course failure)
- Unauthorized collaboration
- Use of AI for original written work without disclosure
- Fabrication of data or sources
- Examination misconduct

**Permitted:**
- Collaboration on case study discussions (when specified)
- Peer review and feedback (when structured)
- Use of executive coaches and advisors for guidance
- AI tools for editing and formatting (with disclosure)

**Violations:** Result in immediate course failure and potential dismissal from program
