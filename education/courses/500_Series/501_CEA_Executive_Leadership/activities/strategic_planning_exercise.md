# Activity: Strategic Planning Exercise

**Course 501: CEA Executive Leadership**
**Activity Type:** Strategic Planning Simulation
**Time Required:** 4-6 hours (can be done over multiple sessions)
**Format:** Individual or small team (3-5 people)

---

## Overview

This exercise guides you through developing a comprehensive 3-year strategic plan for a CEA company. You'll apply frameworks and tools from the course to create a realistic, actionable strategy.

---

## Company Scenario

**GreenLeaf Farms** is a mid-stage CEA company with the following profile:

**Current State (Year 0):**
- **Facilities:** 2 operating vertical farms (one 3 years old, one 1 year old)
- **Location:** East Coast metro areas (Philadelphia, Baltimore)
- **Products:** Leafy greens (lettuce, kale, arugula) and herbs (basil, cilantro)
- **Revenue:** $8M annually
- **EBITDA:** $400K (5% margin)
- **Employees:** 65 full-time
- **Customers:** Regional grocery chains (3 major accounts), some foodservice
- **Funding:** $35M raised to date (Series A $8M, Series B $27M)
- **Cash:** $6M (approx. 18 months runway at current burn)

**Challenges:**
- Profitability marginal, investors want path to stronger EBITDA
- Customer concentration (top 3 = 75% of revenue)
- Facility 1 underperforming (75% utilization vs. 88% at Facility 2)
- Competition increasing in markets
- Energy costs rising (20% increase in past year)

**Opportunities:**
- Strong brand recognition and customer satisfaction
- Proprietary growing system with better yields
- Potential new markets (Southeast, Midwest)
- Interest from strategic investors and M&A
- New crop development potential (strawberries, tomatoes)

**Leadership:**
- **You are the CEO** (joined 18 months ago from CPG background)
- **COO:** Strong operations leader, with company 4 years
- **CFO:** Experienced finance executive, joined 6 months ago
- **VP Sales:** Building out commercial team
- **Board:** 5 members (CEO, 2 VC reps, 2 independents with ag/food experience)

## Evaluation Criteria

Your strategic plan will be evaluated on:

**Strategic Thinking (30%):**
- Quality of analysis (SWOT, Five Forces, scenarios)
- Soundness of strategic logic and choices
- Creativity and insight

**Financial Rigor (25%):**
- Realistic and detailed financial projections
- Sound assumptions and sensitivity analysis
- Capital plan and funding strategy

**Execution Clarity (25%):**
- Clear initiatives and milestones
- Realistic timelines and ownership
- Organization and talent plan
- Risk awareness and mitigation

**Communication (20%):**
- Clarity and conciseness
- Compelling narrative and vision
- Professional presentation
- Executive-level polish

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Be realistic:** Better to have conservative, achievable goals than overly optimistic
2. **Show your work:** Document assumptions and analysis
3. **Think strategically:** Don't just extrapolate; consider what should change
4. **Focus on critical few:** 3-5 priorities, not 20
5. **Balance short and long:** Quick wins AND long-term value
6. **Consider stakeholders:** What will board, investors, employees, customers think?
7. **Test rigorously:** Stress test your assumptions and financials
8. **Communicate clearly:** Executives don't have time for fluff; be concise
