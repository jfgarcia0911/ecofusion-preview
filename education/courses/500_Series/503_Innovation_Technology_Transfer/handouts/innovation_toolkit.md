## 5. IP Decision Framework

### Patent vs. Trade Secret Decision

**Innovation:** _____________________

**Answer these questions:**

1. Can competitors easily reverse engineer it?
   - □ Yes → Consider Patent
   - □ No → Consider Trade Secret

2. How long do you need protection?
   - □ 20 years sufficient → Patent
   - □ Indefinite preferred → Trade Secret

3. Can you maintain confidentiality?
   - □ Yes → Trade Secret viable
   - □ No → Must patent

4. Is public disclosure a problem?
   - □ Yes → Trade Secret
   - □ No → Patent acceptable

5. Do you have budget for patenting?
   - □ Yes ($10K-50K+ per patent) → Patent feasible
   - □ No → Trade Secret

**Recommendation:** □ Patent  □ Trade Secret  □ Both

**Additional IP:**
- □ Trademark(s) for brand/product names
- □ Copyright for software/content
- □ Industrial design protection
