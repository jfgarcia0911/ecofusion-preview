## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary benefit of university-industry partnerships for companies?

A) Free labor
B) Access to cutting-edge research, expertise, facilities, and talent pipeline
C) Tax deductions only
D) Marketing opportunities

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is a "Cooperative Research and Development Agreement" (CRADA)?

A) Agricultural cooperative membership
B) Agreement between government lab, university, and industry for collaborative research
C) Employee development program
D) International trade agreement

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Who typically owns IP developed during sponsored university research?

A) Always the company funding the research
B) Always the university
C) Determined by the agreement; often university with company licensing rights
D) Public domain automatically

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is a "Master Research Agreement" (MRA)?

A) Agreement for graduate student research only
B) Framework agreement establishing terms for multiple research projects over time
C) Agreement for senior researchers
D) Annual research summary

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What challenge is common in university-industry partnerships?

A) Too much funding available
B) Misaligned timelines and expectations (academic vs commercial priorities)
C) Excessive communication
D) Too many successful outcomes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is "technology transfer office" (TTO) in universities?

A) IT support department
B) Office managing commercialization of university research through licensing and partnerships
C) Office supplies department
D) Technology training center

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is "sponsored research"?

A) Celebrity endorsements
B) Research funded by external organization (company, foundation, government) with defined objectives
C) Free research
D) Student scholarship program

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is a common royalty structure for university IP licenses?

A) No payment required
B) 2-5% of net sales plus upfront and milestone payments
C) 50% of all revenue
D) One-time payment of $100

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is the benefit of universities in industry partnerships?

A) No benefits exist
B) Funding for research, real-world applications, industry connections, and student opportunities
C) Avoiding teaching responsibilities
D) Reducing faculty

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is an "industry advisory board" for university programs?

A) Corporate board of directors
B) Group of industry professionals providing guidance on curriculum, research priorities, and industry needs
C) Student organization
D) Alumni association

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Access to cutting-edge research, expertise, facilities, and talent pipeline
2. B - Agreement between government lab, university, and industry for collaborative research
3. C - Determined by the agreement; often university with company licensing rights
4. B - Framework agreement establishing terms for multiple research projects
5. B - Misaligned timelines and expectations
6. B - Office managing commercialization of university research
7. B - Research funded by external organization with defined objectives
8. B - 2-5% of net sales plus upfront and milestone payments
9. B - Funding for research, real-world applications, industry connections, and student opportunities
10. B - Group of industry professionals providing guidance on curriculum and research priorities
