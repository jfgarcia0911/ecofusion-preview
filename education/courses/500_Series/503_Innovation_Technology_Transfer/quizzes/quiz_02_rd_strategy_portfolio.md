## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of an R&D strategy?

A) To spend research budget as quickly as possible
B) To align research activities with business objectives and create competitive advantage
C) To publish as many papers as possible
D) To hire more scientists

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
In portfolio management, what does "balancing the portfolio" refer to?

A) Equal budget to all projects
B) Distributing resources across short-term, medium-term, and long-term initiatives with varying risk levels
C) Keeping financial records accurate
D) Hiring equal numbers of different types of scientists

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is a "stage-gate process" in R&D management?

A) Physical gates at research facilities
B) Structured approach with decision points (gates) between project phases where continuation is evaluated
C) Employee security clearance levels
D) Construction project management

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What percentage of R&D projects typically fail to reach commercialization?

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50-70%
D) 80-90%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
What is "technology readiness level" (TRL) used for?

A) Employee skill assessment
B) Systematic measurement of technology maturity from concept to operational deployment
C) Equipment age tracking
D) Software version control

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
In R&D portfolio management, what is a "strategic bucket"?

A) Container for storing samples
B) Pre-allocated resources for specific strategic categories (e.g., product improvement, new platforms, breakthrough innovation)
C) Budget planning tool
D) Water management system

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the recommended R&D investment level for innovation-driven CEA companies?

A) Less than 1% of revenue
B) 1-3% of revenue
C) 5-10% of revenue
D) 25-30% of revenue

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is "project portfolio optimization"?

A) Organizing files alphabetically
B) Selecting and prioritizing projects to maximize strategic value within resource constraints
C) Making projects look attractive
D) Eliminating all high-risk projects

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What criteria should be used for R&D project prioritization?

A) Which project the CEO likes best
B) Strategic fit, market potential, technical feasibility, resource requirements, and risk-return profile
C) Alphabetical order
D) Random selection

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a "technology roadmap" in R&D strategy?

A) GPS for laboratory equipment
B) Visual strategy showing technology evolution, development timelines, and alignment with business goals
C) Physical map of research facilities
D) Employee career paths

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - To align research activities with business objectives and create competitive advantage
2. B - Distributing resources across short-term, medium-term, and long-term initiatives with varying risk levels
3. B - Structured approach with decision points between project phases
4. C - 50-70%
5. B - Systematic measurement of technology maturity
6. B - Pre-allocated resources for specific strategic categories
7. C - 5-10% of revenue
8. B - Selecting and prioritizing projects to maximize strategic value
9. B - Strategic fit, market potential, technical feasibility, resource requirements, and risk-return profile
10. B - Visual strategy showing technology evolution and alignment with business goals
