## Questions

### Question 1
What is "corporate venture capital" (CVC)?

A) Venture capital for large corporations only
B) Investment arm of a corporation making equity investments in startups for strategic and financial returns
C) Corporate training programs
D) Employee stock purchase plans

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What distinguishes CVC from traditional venture capital?

A) CVC seeks only financial returns
B) CVC seeks both strategic value (technology access, market insights) and financial returns
C) CVC is always larger
D) No difference exists

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is a "corporate accelerator"?

A) Equipment for faster production
B) Fixed-term program providing startups with mentorship, resources, and investment to accelerate growth
C) Employee training program
D) Fast-track promotion program

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is a "corporate incubator"?

A) Equipment for hatching eggs
B) Program providing longer-term support, space, and resources for early-stage ventures
C) Employee onboarding program
D) Customer service center

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the primary difference between accelerators and incubators?

A) No difference
B) Accelerators are time-limited (typically 3-6 months) and cohort-based; incubators are longer-term and flexible
C) Accelerators are for agriculture only
D) Incubators are obsolete

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a "strategic investment" in corporate venturing?

A) Any investment in stocks
B) Investment made primarily for strategic benefits (technology access, market position) rather than just financial returns
C) Long-term bonds
D) Real estate investment

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is "acqui-hiring"?

A) Acquiring equipment and hiring staff
B) Acquiring a company primarily to gain its talent and team
C) Hiring acquisition specialists
D) Agricultural hiring practices

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What percentage equity stake do CVCs typically take in startup investments?

A) 1-3%
B) 5-20%
C) 51% (controlling stake)
D) 100% (full acquisition)

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is a "strategic partnership" with a startup?

A) Any business relationship
B) Collaborative relationship providing mutual benefits (e.g., technology access, market channels, validation)
C) Competitor relationship
D) Customer relationship only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a common challenge in corporate venturing?

A) Too many successful startups
B) Cultural mismatch between corporate bureaucracy and startup agility
C) Excessive funding available
D) Too much innovation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Investment arm of a corporation making equity investments in startups
2. B - CVC seeks both strategic value and financial returns
3. B - Fixed-term program providing startups with mentorship and resources
4. B - Program providing longer-term support and resources for early-stage ventures
5. B - Accelerators are time-limited and cohort-based; incubators are longer-term
6. B - Investment made primarily for strategic benefits
7. B - Acquiring a company primarily to gain its talent
8. B - 5-20%
9. B - Collaborative relationship providing mutual benefits
10. B - Cultural mismatch between corporate bureaucracy and startup agility
