## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Each question is worth 1 point.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is "technology scouting"?

A) Camping for scientists
B) Systematic process of identifying, evaluating, and tracking emerging technologies relevant to organizational strategy
C) Equipment shopping
D) Employee recruitment

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which of the following is a key source for technology scouting?

A) Social media influencers only
B) Academic research, startups, competitors, conferences, and patent databases
C) Random internet searches
D) Personal opinions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is "technology intelligence"?

A) IQ tests for equipment
B) Systematic gathering, analysis, and dissemination of information about technological developments
C) Artificial intelligence systems
D) Computer processing power

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
In technology assessment, what does "fit" refer to?

A) Physical dimensions
B) How well the technology aligns with organization's strategy, capabilities, and resources
C) Employee fitness levels
D) Fashion compatibility

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "technology due diligence"?

A) Being polite to technology vendors
B) Comprehensive evaluation of technology's technical merit, IP status, market potential, and risks
C) Paying technology bills on time
D) Equipment maintenance

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a "technology assessment framework"?

A) Physical structure for testing equipment
B) Structured methodology for evaluating technologies against defined criteria
C) Software coding structure
D) Building design specifications

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which factor is NOT typically part of technology assessment?

A) Technical feasibility
B) Market potential
C) Employee favorite color preferences
D) Intellectual property landscape

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is "competitive technology intelligence"?

A) Spying on competitors illegally
B) Ethical monitoring and analysis of competitors' technology strategies and capabilities
C) Competing in technology competitions
D) Artificial intelligence competitions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is a "technology radar"?

A) Equipment for detecting radio waves
B) Visual tool categorizing technologies by maturity and strategic importance (e.g., adopt, trial, assess, hold)
C) Weather monitoring system
D) Security system

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is the primary goal of technology scouting in CEA?

A) To adopt every new technology available
B) To identify technologies that can provide competitive advantage and solve operational challenges
C) To spend technology budget
D) To impress investors with technology jargon

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Systematic process of identifying, evaluating, and tracking emerging technologies
2. B - Academic research, startups, competitors, conferences, and patent databases
3. B - Systematic gathering, analysis, and dissemination of information about technological developments
4. B - How well the technology aligns with organization's strategy, capabilities, and resources
5. B - Comprehensive evaluation of technology's technical merit, IP status, market potential, and risks
6. B - Structured methodology for evaluating technologies against defined criteria
7. C - Employee favorite color preferences
8. B - Ethical monitoring and analysis of competitors' technology strategies and capabilities
9. B - Visual tool categorizing technologies by maturity and strategic importance
10. B - To identify technologies that can provide competitive advantage and solve operational challenges
