## Questions

### Question 1
What is "technology licensing"?

A) Government permits for technology use
B) Granting rights to use, develop, or commercialize intellectual property in exchange for compensation
C) Employee technology training
D) Software installation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the difference between "exclusive" and "non-exclusive" licenses?

A) No difference
B) Exclusive grants rights to one licensee; non-exclusive can be granted to multiple licensees
C) Exclusive is free; non-exclusive costs money
D) Exclusive is permanent; non-exclusive is temporary

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What are the typical components of licensing fees?

A) One flat rate only
B) Upfront fee, milestone payments, and ongoing royalties
C) No fees required
D) Equity only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is "technology valuation"?

A) Determining the financial worth of technology for licensing or acquisition
B) Rating technology on a scale of 1-10
C) Counting patents
D) Measuring equipment age

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
What is a "sublicense"?

A) Temporary license
B) Permission from licensee to grant rights to a third party (requires licensor approval)
C) Discount license
D) Student license

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What does "field of use" restriction mean in licensing?

A) Agricultural applications only
B) Limiting the license to specific applications, markets, or industries
C) Geographic boundaries
D) Outdoor use only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a typical royalty rate for agricultural technology licenses?

A) 0.5-1%
B) 2-8%
C) 25-50%
D) 75-90%

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is "out-licensing"?

A) Licensing your technology to others
B) Licensing technology from others
C) Canceling a license
D) Outdoor licensing

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 9
What is "in-licensing"?

A) Licensing your technology to others
B) Bringing external technology into your organization through licensing
C) Indoor use only
D) Internal employee licensing

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a "licensing negotiation"?

A) Mandatory arbitration
B) Process of discussing and agreeing on licensing terms, fees, rights, and obligations
C) Technology demonstration
D) Patent filing

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Granting rights to use, develop, or commercialize intellectual property
2. B - Exclusive grants rights to one licensee; non-exclusive can be granted to multiple
3. B - Upfront fee, milestone payments, and ongoing royalties
4. A - Determining the financial worth of technology
5. B - Permission from licensee to grant rights to a third party
6. B - Limiting the license to specific applications, markets, or industries
7. B - 2-8%
8. A - Licensing your technology to others
9. B - Bringing external technology into your organization through licensing
10. B - Process of discussing and agreeing on licensing terms
