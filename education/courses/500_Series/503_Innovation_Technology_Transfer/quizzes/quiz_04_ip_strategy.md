## Questions

### Question 1
What are the four main types of intellectual property protection?

A) Patents, Trademarks, Copyrights, Trade Secrets
B) Licenses, Contracts, Agreements, NDAs
C) Products, Services, Brands, Ideas
D) Hardware, Software, Processes, Materials

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
How long does utility patent protection typically last in the United States?

A) 10 years from filing
B) 14 years from grant
C) 20 years from filing date
D) Indefinitely

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What is a "trade secret"?

A) Illegal business practice
B) Confidential information providing competitive advantage, protected by keeping it secret
C) International trade agreement
D) Secret handshake

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What does "freedom-to-operate" (FTO) analysis assess?

A) Employee work flexibility
B) Whether commercializing a technology would infringe on existing patents or IP rights
C) Export restrictions
D) Operational hours

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "prior art" in patent law?

A) Ancient artwork
B) Previously existing knowledge or publications that may prevent a patent from being granted
C) Preliminary designs
D) Historical architecture

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a "patent portfolio"?

A) A folder for storing patent documents
B) Collection of patents owned by an organization, managed strategically for competitive advantage
C) Investment portfolio for patent attorneys
D) Patent office filing system

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is "defensive patenting"?

A) Patents for security systems
B) Creating patents primarily to prevent competitors from patenting similar innovations
C) Patents for military technology
D) Protecting existing products only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What does "IP infringement" mean?

A) Respecting intellectual property
B) Unauthorized use, sale, or manufacture of protected intellectual property
C) Sharing open-source software
D) Licensing agreements

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is the typical cost range for filing and prosecuting a utility patent in the US?

A) $500 - $1,000
B) $2,000 - $5,000
C) $10,000 - $25,000+
D) $100,000 - $500,000

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
In CEA, which type of innovation is often best protected as a trade secret rather than a patent?

A) Novel hardware designs that are visible
B) Proprietary growing recipes, environmental control algorithms, or operational know-how
C) Product names and logos
D) Published research findings

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Patents, Trademarks, Copyrights, Trade Secrets
2. C - 20 years from filing date
3. B - Confidential information providing competitive advantage, protected by keeping it secret
4. B - Whether commercializing a technology would infringe on existing patents or IP rights
5. B - Previously existing knowledge or publications that may prevent a patent from being granted
6. B - Collection of patents owned by an organization, managed strategically
7. B - Creating patents primarily to prevent competitors from patenting similar innovations
8. B - Unauthorized use, sale, or manufacture of protected intellectual property
9. C - $10,000 - $25,000+
10. B - Proprietary growing recipes, environmental control algorithms, or operational know-how
