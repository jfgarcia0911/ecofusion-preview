## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Each question is worth 1 point.

## Questions

### Question 1
What is "open innovation" according to Henry Chesbrough?

A) Public disclosure of all company information
B) Using both internal and external ideas and paths to market to advance innovation
C) Free access to all patents
D) Eliminating R&D departments

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is "crowdsourcing" in innovation?

A) Gathering crowds for events
B) Obtaining ideas, solutions, or contributions from a large group, often via online platforms
C) Crowdfunding only
D) Public protests

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is an "innovation challenge" or "prize competition"?

A) Challenging employees to work harder
B) Offering rewards for solving specific problems or achieving defined innovation goals
C) Athletic competitions
D) Competitive bidding

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is an "innovation platform"?

A) Physical stage for presentations
B) Digital or organizational infrastructure enabling collaboration and co-creation with external partners
C) Computer operating system
D) Equipment mounting surface

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "co-creation" in open innovation?

A) Two companies forming
B) Collaborative development where companies and partners jointly create value and innovations
C) Employee partnerships
D) Customer service

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is the primary risk of open innovation?

A) Too much innovation
B) Loss of control over IP, knowledge leakage, and coordination complexity
C) Excessive cost savings
D) Too many successful products

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is an "innovation ecosystem"?

A) Environmental conservation program
B) Network of interconnected organizations, individuals, and resources that collaborate to drive innovation
C) Indoor growing system
D) Software ecosystem

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is "outside-in" open innovation?

A) Outdoor research facilities
B) Bringing external ideas, technologies, and knowledge into the organization
C) Export activities
D) Window replacement

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is "inside-out" open innovation?

A) Inverting organizational structure
B) Taking internal technologies to market through external channels (licensing, spin-offs, partnerships)
C) Outdoor activities
D) Exit strategies only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a successful example of open innovation in agriculture?

A) Completely closed proprietary systems
B) Industry consortia for pre-competitive research or open-source platforms for data sharing
C) Eliminating all partnerships
D) Avoiding collaboration

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Using both internal and external ideas and paths to market
2. B - Obtaining ideas, solutions, or contributions from a large group
3. B - Offering rewards for solving specific problems
4. B - Digital or organizational infrastructure enabling collaboration with external partners
5. B - Collaborative development where companies and partners jointly create value
6. B - Loss of control over IP, knowledge leakage, and coordination complexity
7. B - Network of interconnected organizations that collaborate to drive innovation
8. B - Bringing external ideas, technologies, and knowledge into the organization
9. B - Taking internal technologies to market through external channels
10. B - Industry consortia for pre-competitive research or open-source platforms
