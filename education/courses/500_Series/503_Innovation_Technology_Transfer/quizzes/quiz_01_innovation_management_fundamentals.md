# Quiz 1: Innovation Management Fundamentals

**Course:** 503 - Innovation & Technology Transfer
**Module:** 1 - Innovation Management Fundamentals in CEA
**Total Points:** 10 questions × 1 point each = 10 points
**Passing Score:** 7/10 (70%)

---

## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Each question is worth 1 point.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What distinguishes innovation from invention?

A) Innovation is more creative than invention
B) Innovation requires successful implementation and value creation
C) Innovation is always patentable while invention is not
D) Innovation only applies to products, not processes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which type of innovation creates new markets by serving overlooked segments and eventually displaces incumbents?

A) Incremental innovation
B) Architectural innovation
C) Radical innovation
D) Disruptive innovation

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 3
According to the Three Horizons Framework, what percentage of innovation resources should typically be allocated to Horizon 1 (core business)?

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50-60%
D) 70-80%

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 4
In Chesbrough's Open Innovation model, what is "inbound open innovation"?

A) Licensing out internal technologies to external parties
B) Bringing external ideas and technologies into the organization
C) Creating spin-off ventures from internal innovations
D) Publishing research findings openly

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is a Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 7-9 activity?

A) Basic principles observed and reported
B) Experimental proof of concept
C) Technology validated in relevant environment
D) System prototype demonstration in operational environment

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 6
What is the primary purpose of an Innovation Value Chain?

A) To calculate the financial value of innovations
B) To map the three phases: idea generation, conversion, and diffusion
C) To chain together suppliers in the innovation process
D) To determine patent value

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
In the Ambidextrous Organization model, what does "exploitation" refer to?

A) Taking advantage of employees' innovative ideas
B) Refining existing capabilities and incremental innovation
C) Discovering new opportunities through radical innovation
D) Extracting maximum profit from customers

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which innovation metric is an "output" metric rather than an "outcome" metric?

A) Revenue from new products
B) Market share gains
C) Patents filed
D) Return on innovation investment

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is the main barrier to innovation characterized by "Not Invented Here" syndrome?

A) Lack of funding
B) Organizational resistance to external ideas
C) Poor leadership
D) Insufficient technical capability

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In the AeroFarms case study, what was a key lesson learned about their innovation approach?

A) Innovation should only focus on long-term breakthrough technologies
B) A balanced portfolio approach with different time horizons is effective
C) All innovation should be kept as trade secrets
D) External partnerships are unnecessary for CEA operators

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Innovation requires successful implementation and value creation
2. D - Disruptive innovation
3. D - 70-80%
4. B - Bringing external ideas and technologies into the organization
5. D - System prototype demonstration in operational environment
6. B - To map the three phases: idea generation, conversion, and diffusion
7. B - Refining existing capabilities and incremental innovation
8. C - Patents filed
9. B - Organizational resistance to external ideas
10. B - A balanced portfolio approach with different time horizons is effective
