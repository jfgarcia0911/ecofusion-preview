# Activity: Technology Assessment Exercise

**Course 503: Innovation & Technology Transfer**
**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Format:** Individual or small group (2-4 people)
**Level:** Master

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Apply systematic technology assessment frameworks
2. Evaluate technologies across multiple dimensions
3. Make evidence-based technology sourcing decisions
4. Present recommendations with supporting rationale

---

## Background

You are the Innovation Manager for **GreenTech Farms**, a vertical farm operator with three facilities producing leafy greens and herbs. Your company is evaluating new technologies to improve competitiveness. You've been asked to assess a specific technology and recommend whether to pursue it.

**Company Context:**
- Annual revenue: $15M
- R&D budget: $1.5M/year (10% of revenue)
- Current focus: Cost reduction and quality improvement
- Strategic goal: Expand to strawberries within 2 years

---

## Part 1: Technology Selection

**Choose ONE technology to assess:**

### Option A: AI-Powered Climate Control System
- **Description:** Machine learning system that optimizes temperature, humidity, CO2, and airflow in real-time based on plant response
- **Provider:** TechGrow AI (startup, 2 years old, 5 employees)
- **Stage:** TRL 6 (demonstrated in research environment)
- **Claimed benefits:** 15% yield improvement, 10% energy savings
- **Investment:** $150K setup + $2K/month SaaS fee per facility

### Option B: Robotic Seeding and Transplanting System
- **Description:** Automated system for seeding and transplanting seedlings to growing towers
- **Provider:** AutoFarm Systems (established company, 10 years, 50 employees)
- **Stage:** TRL 8 (commercially proven in greenhouse, new to vertical farms)
- **Claimed benefits:** 60% labor reduction in seeding/transplanting, improved consistency
- **Investment:** $500K per facility (equipment + integration)

### Option C: UV-C LED Pathogen Control System
- **Description:** UV-C LED modules integrated into growing environment for continuous pathogen suppression
- **Provider:** University technology (licensed through AgriTech Innovations)
- **Stage:** TRL 5 (validated in controlled environment)
- **Claimed benefits:** 80% reduction in fungal/bacterial issues, reduced chemical use
- **Investment:** $200K per facility + $2K/month maintenance

## Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (9-10) | Good (7-8) | Satisfactory (5-6) | Needs Improvement (<5) |
|-----------|------------------|------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Analysis Depth** | Comprehensive, rigorous analysis across all dimensions | Solid analysis with minor gaps | Basic analysis completed | Superficial or incomplete |
| **Framework Application** | Frameworks applied correctly and insightfully | Frameworks applied correctly | Frameworks used with some errors | Poor framework application |
| **Economic Analysis** | Detailed, accurate financial modeling with sensitivity | Accurate financial analysis | Basic financial analysis | Weak or incorrect financials |
| **Risk Assessment** | Thorough risk identification and mitigation | Good risk assessment | Basic risk identification | Inadequate risk analysis |
| **Recommendation Quality** | Clear, well-justified, actionable recommendation | Good recommendation with support | Adequate recommendation | Weak or unsupported recommendation |
| **Presentation** | Professional, clear, well-organized | Good presentation | Acceptable presentation | Poor presentation |

**Total: _____ / 60 points**

## Learning Extensions

After completing the exercise:

1. **Compare assessments:** If working in groups, compare how different groups assessed the same technology. What accounted for differences?

2. **Alternative technologies:** Conduct abbreviated assessment of the other technology options for comparison.

3. **Real-world application:** Identify a technology relevant to your organization and conduct an actual assessment.

4. **Presentation:** Present your recommendation to the class/team for feedback.
