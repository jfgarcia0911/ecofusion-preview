# Course 503: Innovation & Technology Transfer

## Master-Level Course | EcoFusion Academy

---

## Course Description

This master-level course provides comprehensive training in innovation management and technology commercialization specifically tailored for Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) professionals. Students will develop strategic capabilities to identify, evaluate, protect, and commercialize agricultural technologies while building innovation ecosystems that drive competitive advantage.

The course integrates innovation theory with practical frameworks for managing R&D portfolios, navigating intellectual property landscapes, establishing university-industry partnerships, and executing technology transfer strategies. Through real-world case studies from the CEA industry, participants will learn to bridge the gap between research and market-ready solutions.

**Course Level:** Master/Executive (500-level)
**Duration:** 14 weeks
**Estimated Time:** 8-10 hours per week
**Credits:** 3 graduate credits
**Prerequisites:** Course 401 CEA Facility Design & Architecture OR equivalent industry experience; Course 406 Bioprocess Engineering recommended

---

## Course Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Design and implement** comprehensive innovation management systems for CEA organizations
2. **Develop and execute** R&D strategies aligned with business objectives and market opportunities
3. **Assess and evaluate** emerging technologies using systematic frameworks and criteria
4. **Formulate and protect** intellectual property strategies for agricultural innovations
5. **Establish and manage** productive university-industry research partnerships
6. **Negotiate and execute** technology licensing agreements and commercialization deals
7. **Implement** open innovation models and corporate venturing programs
8. **Build and leverage** innovation ecosystems for competitive advantage
9. **Create** technology roadmaps that align research, development, and commercialization timelines
10. **Design and execute** proof-of-concept studies and pilot programs
11. **Plan and manage** scale-up processes from pilot to commercial production
12. **Measure and optimize** innovation performance using appropriate metrics and ROI frameworks
13. **Cultivate** organizational cultures that support continuous innovation
14. **Navigate** regulatory, legal, and ethical considerations in agricultural technology transfer
