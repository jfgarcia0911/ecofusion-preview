# Grading Rubric
## Course 503: Innovation Technology Transfer

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 503. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

## Grading Scale

| Percentage | Letter Grade | Point Range | Performance Level |
|------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 90-100% | A | 99-110 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |
| 80-89% | B | 88-98 | Good - Meets all requirements |
| 70-79% | C | 77-87 | Fair - Meets most requirements |
| 60-69% | D | 66-76 | Poor - Below expectations |
| Below 60% | F | 0-65 | Failing - Does not meet requirements |

**Note:** A minimum of 80% (88 points) is required to pass this course and receive a Certificate of Completion.
