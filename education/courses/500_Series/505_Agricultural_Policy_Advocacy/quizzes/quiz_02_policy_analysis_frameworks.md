# Quiz 2: Policy Analysis Frameworks

**Module**: 2 - Policy Analysis Frameworks | **Points**: 10

## Questions

**1. What does NPV stand for in cost-benefit analysis?**
- A) National Policy Value
- B) Net Present Value
- C) New Program Validation
- D) Nominal Price Variable

**Answer**: B

**2. In stakeholder analysis, which quadrant represents "Key Players"?**
- A) High Power / Low Interest
- B) Low Power / High Interest
- C) High Power / High Interest
- D) Low Power / Low Interest

**Answer**: C

**3. What discount rate range does the federal government typically use for regulatory analysis?**
- A) 1% and 5%
- B) 3% and 7%
- C) 5% and 10%
- D) 7% and 12%

**Answer**: B

**4. Which type of equity treats similar situations similarly?**
- A) Vertical equity
- B) Horizontal equity
- C) Intergenerational equity
- D) Geographic equity

**Answer**: B

**5. What is the formula for the benefit-cost ratio?**
- A) Total Costs / Total Benefits
- B) Total Benefits - Total Costs
- C) Total Benefits / Total Costs
- D) (Benefits - Costs) / Costs

**Answer**: C

**6. In the policy analysis process, what comes after "Identify Alternative Policies"?**
- A) Define the Problem
- B) Evaluate Alternatives
- C) Make Recommendations
- D) Establish Evaluation Criteria

**Answer**: B

**7. What does MCDA stand for?**
- A) Multiple Criteria Decision Analysis
- B) Mandatory Cost Distribution Assessment
- C) Market Comparison Data Aggregation
- D) Multi-Coalition Development Approach

**Answer**: A

**8. Which is NOT one of the "Five E's" of policy evaluation?**
- A) Effectiveness
- B) Efficiency
- C) Equity
- D) Excellence

**Answer**: D

**9. Elasticity measures:**
- A) Responsiveness of quantity to price changes
- B) Flexibility of policy implementation
- C) Durability of legislative language
- D) Strength of stakeholder support

**Answer**: A

**10. What is the primary limitation of cost-benefit analysis?**
- A) Requires too much data
- B) Takes too long to complete
- C) Difficulty monetizing all impacts
- D) Only works for agriculture

**Answer**: C
