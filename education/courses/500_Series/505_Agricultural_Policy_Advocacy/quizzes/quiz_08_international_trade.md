## Questions

### Question 1
What does WTO stand for?

A) World Trade Organization
B) Worldwide Trading Office
C) Western Trade Operations
D) World Transportation Organization

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The World Trade Organization (WTO) is the international organization that establishes rules for global trade and resolves trade disputes between member nations.

---

### Question 2
What trade agreement replaced NAFTA in 2020?

A) TPP
B) USMCA
C) CAFTA
D) FTAA

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) replaced the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) in 2020, modernizing trade relations among the three countries.

---

### Question 3
What does MFN status mean in international trade?

A) Most Favorable Nation
B) Mutual Finance Network
C) Most Favored Nation
D) Maximum Foreign Negotiation

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Most Favored Nation (MFN) status means that a country grants the same trade advantages (like low tariffs) to all WTO members as it grants to its "most favored" trading partner.

---

### Question 4
What is a TRQ (Tariff Rate Quota)?

A) Trade Request Qualification
B) Total Revenue Quotient
C) Transportation Rate Query
D) Tariff Rate Quota - allows a certain quantity at a lower tariff, then higher tariff applies

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** A Tariff Rate Quota (TRQ) allows imports of a specified quantity at a lower tariff rate; once that quota is filled, a higher tariff rate applies to additional imports.

---

### Question 5
What is the USTR?

A) United States Trade Representative
B) Universal Standards for Trade Regulation
C) United States Transport Registry
D) Union of State Trade Representatives

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The United States Trade Representative (USTR) is the cabinet-level official responsible for developing and coordinating U.S. international trade, commodity, and direct investment policy.

---

### Question 6
What does the WTO's SPS Agreement cover?

A) Special Payment Systems
B) Sanitary and Phytosanitary measures (food safety and animal/plant health)
C) Sustainable Production Standards
D) Strategic Partnership Services

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The SPS (Sanitary and Phytosanitary) Agreement sets rules for food safety and animal and plant health standards to ensure they're based on science and not used as disguised trade barriers.

---

### Question 7
Approximately what value of agricultural products does the U.S. export annually?

A) $50 billion
B) $100 billion
C) $175+ billion
D) $300 billion

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The United States exports approximately $175+ billion in agricultural products annually, making it one of the world's largest agricultural exporters.

---

### Question 8
The "Phase One" trade deal signed in 2020 was with which country?

A) Japan
B) European Union
C) Mexico
D) China

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** The Phase One trade deal was signed with China in January 2020, addressing intellectual property, technology transfer, agriculture, financial services, and currency issues.

---

### Question 9
What is the APAC in agricultural trade?

A) Asia-Pacific Agricultural Council
B) Agricultural Policy Advisory Committee
C) American Produce Allocation Center
D) Agricultural Product Approval Code

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Agricultural Policy Advisory Committee (APAC) advises the USTR on agricultural trade policy matters and trade agreement negotiations.

---

### Question 10
What is Section 301 used to address?

A) Building codes for agricultural facilities
B) Unfair trade practices and intellectual property violations by foreign countries
C) Domestic farm subsidies
D) Environmental regulations

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 allows the U.S. to investigate and retaliate against unfair trade practices and intellectual property violations by foreign countries.
