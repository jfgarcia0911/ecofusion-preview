# Quiz 1: Agricultural Policy Landscape

**Course**: AG-505 Agricultural Policy & Advocacy
**Module**: 1 - Agricultural Policy Landscape
**Total Points**: 10 questions × 1 point each = 10 points

---

## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Each question is worth 1 point.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the approximate frequency of Farm Bill reauthorization?

A) Every 2 years
B) Every 5 years
C) Every 10 years
D) Annually

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which USDA agency is responsible for administering crop insurance programs?

A) Farm Service Agency (FSA)
B) Risk Management Agency (RMA)
C) Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)
D) Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS)

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What percentage of the Farm Bill spending goes to nutrition assistance programs (SNAP)?

A) Approximately 25%
B) Approximately 50%
C) Approximately 80%
D) Approximately 10%

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 4
Which agency regulates most food safety (except meat, poultry, and processed eggs)?

A) USDA FSIS
B) EPA
C) FDA
D) USDA AMS

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 5
The "three streams model" of policy windows includes all of the following EXCEPT:

A) Problem stream
B) Policy stream
C) Political stream
D) Procedural stream

**Correct Answer**: D

---

### Question 6
Which was a major innovation of the New Deal era agricultural policy?

A) Crop insurance
B) Farm Bill consolidation
C) Organic standards
D) Trade agreements

**Correct Answer**: A

---

### Question 7
What does SNAP stand for?

A) Sustainable Nutrition Assistance Program
B) Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program
C) Special Needs Agricultural Program
D) State Nutrition and Agriculture Plan

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
Which of the following is NOT a House Agriculture Committee subcommittee jurisdiction area?

A) Commodity Markets
B) Conservation and Research
C) Foreign Affairs
D) Livestock, Dairy, and Poultry

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 9
The 2018 Farm Bill included provisions for which of the following?

A) Hemp legalization
B) Urban agriculture programs
C) Both A and B
D) Neither A nor B

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 10
Which farm organization is generally considered the most conservative and largest?

A) National Farmers Union
B) American Farm Bureau Federation
C) National Grange
D) National Sustainable Agriculture Coalition

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Every 5 years
2. B - Risk Management Agency (RMA)
3. C - Approximately 80%
4. C - FDA
5. D - Procedural stream (not part of the model)
6. A - Crop insurance
7. B - Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program
8. C - Foreign Affairs
9. C - Both A and B
10. B - American Farm Bureau Federation
