# Handout 1: Farm Bill Structure & Key Programs

## The 12 Titles of the Farm Bill

### Title 1: Commodities
**Purpose**: Price and income support for program crops
**Major Programs**:
- Price Loss Coverage (PLC)
- Agriculture Risk Coverage (ARC)
- Marketing Assistance Loans
- Dairy Margin Protection Program
**Crops Covered**: Corn, soybeans, wheat, cotton, rice, peanuts, others
**2018 Spending**: ~$60 billion over 10 years

### Title 2: Conservation
**Purpose**: Encourage environmental stewardship
**Major Programs**:
- Conservation Reserve Program (CRP): Land retirement
- Environmental Quality Incentives Program (EQIP): Working lands cost-share
- Conservation Stewardship Program (CSP): Rewards for conservation
- Agricultural Conservation Easement Program (ACEP): Permanent easements
**2018 Spending**: ~$60 billion over 10 years

### Title 3: Trade
**Purpose**: Export promotion and food aid
**Major Programs**:
- Market Access Program (MAP): Export marketing cost-share
- Foreign Market Development: Long-term market building
- Export Credit Guarantee Program
- Food for Peace: International food aid
**2018 Spending**: ~$3 billion over 10 years

### Title 4: Nutrition
**Purpose**: Food assistance programs
**Major Programs**:
- Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP): Food assistance for low-income
- The Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP): Food banks
- Commodity Supplemental Food Program: Seniors and children
**2018 Spending**: ~$664 billion over 10 years (80% of Farm Bill)

### Title 5: Credit
**Purpose**: Farm loans and credit access
**Major Programs**:
- Farm Service Agency Direct and Guaranteed Loans
- Beginning Farmer and Rancher Development Program
- Emergency loans
**2018 Spending**: ~$5 billion over 10 years

### Title 6: Rural Development
**Purpose**: Infrastructure and economic development in rural areas
**Major Programs**:
- Rural Business Development Grants
- Water and Waste Disposal programs
- Rural Energy for America Program (REAP)
- Broadband programs
**2018 Spending**: ~$1 billion over 10 years

### Title 7: Research & Extension
**Purpose**: Agricultural research and education
**Major Programs**:
- Agriculture and Food Research Initiative (AFRI): Competitive grants
- Specialty Crop Research Initiative (SCRI)
- Organic Agriculture Research and Extension Initiative (OREI)
- Sustainable Agriculture Research and Education (SARE)
- Extension and integrated programs
**2018 Spending**: ~$2 billion over 10 years

### Title 8: Forestry
**Purpose**: Forest management and programs
**Coverage**: National forests, private forests, wildfire, biomass
**2018 Spending**: Minimal

### Title 9: Energy
**Purpose**: Renewable energy and biofuels
**Major Programs**:
- Biorefinery Assistance
- Biomass Crop Assistance Program
- Rural Energy for America Program (REAP)
**2018 Spending**: ~$1 billion over 10 years

### Title 10: Horticulture & Specialty Crops
**Purpose**: Fruits, vegetables, nursery crops
**Major Programs**:
- Specialty Crop Block Grant Program: State grants for specialty crops
- Organic Certification Cost-Share
- Farmers Market and Local Food Promotion
- Urban Agriculture and Innovative Production (NEW in 2018)
**2018 Spending**: ~$3 billion over 10 years

### Title 11: Crop Insurance
**Purpose**: Risk management through insurance
**Major Programs**:
- Federal Crop Insurance: USDA-subsidized insurance
- Noninsured Crop Disaster Assistance Program (NAP)
**Structure**: Public-private partnership
**2018 Spending**: ~$88 billion over 10 years

### Title 12: Miscellaneous
**Various Provisions**:
- Hemp legalization (2018 addition)
- Socially Disadvantaged and Beginning Farmers
- Animal disease programs
- Agricultural security
- Other provisions not fitting other titles

## Farm Bill Timeline

**Typical Cycle** (5 years):
1. **Year 0-1**: Previous Farm Bill implementation, early stakeholder engagement
2. **Year 1-2**: Comprehensive hearings across the country
3. **Year 2-3**: Committee development, markup, floor passage
4. **Year 3**: Conference committee, final passage, presidential signature
5. **Year 3-5**: Implementation, regulations, program delivery
6. **Year 5+**: Expiration, extension, or reauthorization

**Recent Farm Bills**:
- 2002: Farm Security and Rural Investment Act
- 2008: Food, Conservation, and Energy Act
- 2014: Agricultural Act of 2014
- 2018: Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018
- 2023: Expired September 30, 2023; extended

## Spending Breakdown

**2018 Farm Bill - 10 Year Spending (~$867 billion)**:
- Nutrition (SNAP): ~77%
- Crop Insurance: ~10%
- Commodities: ~7%
- Conservation: ~7%
- All Other Titles: <1% each

**Mandatory vs. Discretionary**:
- **Mandatory**: Authorized in Farm Bill, doesn't require annual appropriations (most of above)
- **Discretionary**: Requires annual appropriations (some research, rural development)

## Key Stakeholders in Farm Bill

**Commodity Groups**: National Corn Growers, Soybeans, Wheat, Cotton, Rice, etc.
**General Farm**: American Farm Bureau, National Farmers Union, National Grange
**Specialty Crops**: United Fresh, specialty crop organizations
**Conservation**: National Wildlife Federation, Environmental Defense Fund
**Nutrition**: Feeding America, anti-hunger organizations
**Research**: Land-grant universities, APLU
**Rural Development**: National Association of Counties, rural groups

## CEA and the Farm Bill

**Current Inclusion** (limited):
- Title 7: Research (AFRI, SCRI - eligible but competitive)
- Title 10: Urban Agriculture and Innovative Production office
- Title 10: Specialty Crop Block Grants (state discretion)
- Title 6: Rural Energy for America Program (renewable energy)

**Opportunities for Expansion**:
- Dedicated CEA research initiative
- Crop insurance pathways
- Conservation programs (water, energy efficiency)
- Beginning farmer programs
- Local food procurement
