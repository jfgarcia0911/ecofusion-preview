# Handout 2: Key Federal Agencies & Contacts

## U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA)

**Main Website**: usda.gov
**Headquarters**: 1400 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20250

### Farm Production and Conservation Mission Area

**Farm Service Agency (FSA)**
- Programs: Commodity programs, farm loans, disaster assistance, conservation
- Website: fsa.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-3467

**Risk Management Agency (RMA)**
- Programs: Federal crop insurance
- Website: rma.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 690-2803

**Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)**
- Programs: Conservation technical assistance, EQIP, CSP, CRP
- Website: nrcs.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-3210

### Food, Nutrition, and Consumer Services

**Food and Nutrition Service (FNS)**
- Programs: SNAP, WIC, school meals
- Website: fns.usda.gov
- Contact: (703) 305-2062

### Marketing and Regulatory Programs

**Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS)**
- Programs: Marketing orders, organic certification, grading, country of origin labeling
- Website: ams.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-8998
- **National Organic Program**: (202) 720-3252

**Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS)**
- Programs: Plant and animal health, quarantines, biotechnology regulation
- Website: aphis.usda.gov
- Contact: (301) 851-2908

**Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS)**
- Programs: Meat, poultry, egg product inspection and food safety
- Website: fsis.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-7943

### Research, Education, and Economics

**Agricultural Research Service (ARS)**
- Programs: Intramural research
- Website: ars.usda.gov
- Contact: (301) 504-1656

**National Institute of Food and Agriculture (NIFA)**
- Programs: Competitive grants (AFRI, SCRI, OREI), Extension, Land-grant support
- Website: nifa.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-4423

**Economic Research Service (ERS)**
- Programs: Economic research and analysis
- Website: ers.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 694-5000

**National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS)**
- Programs: Agricultural data, Census of Agriculture
- Website: nass.usda.gov
- Contact: (202) 720-2707

### Other USDA Offices

**Office of Urban Agriculture and Innovative Production**
- Programs: Urban agriculture, CEA, innovative production
- Website: usda.gov/urban
- Contact: (202) 720-6350

**Office of Partnerships and Public Engagement**
- Programs: Socially disadvantaged farmers, beginning farmers
- Website: usda.gov/partnerships
- Contact: (202) 720-6350

## Tips for Effective Communication

**With Agencies**:
- Be specific about your issue
- Provide relevant details (location, program, regulation)
- Be patient (government bureaucracy is slow)
- Follow up in writing

**With Congress**:
- Be a constituent (district/state connection)
- Be brief and specific
- Provide local examples
- Make clear ask
- Build long-term relationships
