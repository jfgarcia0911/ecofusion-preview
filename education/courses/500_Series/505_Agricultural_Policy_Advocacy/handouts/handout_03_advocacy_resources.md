### Subscription Services
- **Bloomberg Government (BGOV)**: Comprehensive tracking and analysis

## Data and Statistics

### Government Data
- **USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS)**: nass.usda.gov
  - Census of Agriculture (every 5 years)
  - Quick Stats database
  - Crop production reports

- **USDA Economic Research Service**: ers.usda.gov

  - Food price outlook
  - Agricultural trade data

- **Bureau of Labor Statistics**: bls.gov
  - Agricultural employment

- **U.S. Census Bureau**: census.gov

## Tips for Building Your Resource Library

1. **Bookmark Key Websites**: Create organized folders
2. **Set Up Alerts**: Google Alerts, Congressional alerts, Federal Register
3. **Subscribe to Newsletters**: 3-5 high-quality sources
4. **Join Professional Associations**: Networking and resources
5. **Build Personal Network**: Contacts in agencies, Congress, organizations
6. **Maintain Contact Database**: CRM or spreadsheet with key contacts
7. **Archive Important Documents**: Policy briefs, testimony, reports
8. **Social Media Lists**: Organized Twitter lists, LinkedIn groups

---

**Remember**: This is a starting point. Build your own customized resource library based on your specific policy interests and geographic focus.
