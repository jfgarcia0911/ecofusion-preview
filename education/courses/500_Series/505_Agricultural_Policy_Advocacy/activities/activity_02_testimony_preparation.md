# Activity 2: Congressional Testimony Preparation & Delivery

**Course**: AG-505 | **Time**: 4-5 hours | **Points**: 100

## Objective
Prepare and deliver testimony before a mock congressional hearing on an agricultural policy issue, building skills in expert communication, Q&A response, and professional presentation.

## Assignment
1. Prepare written testimony (5-7 pages)
2. Develop oral summary (5 minutes)
3. Deliver testimony to mock committee
4. Respond to questions from "members"

## Hearing Scenario

**Committee**: House Committee on Agriculture or Senate Agriculture, Nutrition & Forestry Committee
**Topic**: [Instructor assigns or students choose from approved list]
**Role**: Expert witness representing organization, university, or industry sector

## Example Hearing Topics
- Farm Bill reauthorization priorities
- CEA research funding
- Organic standards for hydroponics
- Climate-smart agriculture programs
- H-2A program reform
- FSMA produce safety regulations
- Agricultural trade policy

## Written Testimony Format

**Letterhead**: Organization name, contact information

**Salutation**:
"Chairman [Name], Ranking Member [Name], and Members of the Committee:"

**Introduction** (1 paragraph):
- Thank committee
- Introduce yourself and credentials
- State organization you represent
- Briefly state your position

**Executive Summary** (1/2 page):
- 3-5 key points you'll make
- Bottom-line recommendation

**Body** (3-4 pages):
- Background and context
- Problem statement with evidence
- Analysis and recommendations
- Support with data, examples, stories
- Address counterarguments

**Conclusion** (1/2 page):
- Summarize key points
- Restate recommendation
- Thank committee
- Offer to be resource

**Appendices** (optional):
- Supporting data tables
- Charts and graphs
- Research citations
- Letters of support

## Oral Testimony Preparation

**5-Minute Summary**:
- Summarize written testimony
- Hit 3-4 key points
- Use stories and examples
- Make eye contact
- Stay within time limit (practice!)

**Tips**:
- Start strong (hook attention)
- Avoid reading; speak conversationally from notes
- Use visual aids if helpful (charts, photos)
- End with clear call to action
- Thank committee again

## Q&A Preparation

**Anticipate Questions**:
- Research committee members' interests
- Prepare for friendly and hostile questions
- Have data and examples ready
- Practice concise answers (1-2 minutes)

**Q&A Techniques**:
- Listen to full question
- Answer the question asked
- Bridge to your messages
- If you don't know: "I'll follow up with that information"
- Stay calm and professional, even if attacked
- Use opportunities to restate key points

**Common Question Types**:
- Clarification on your position
- Cost and economic impact
- Comparison to alternatives
- How it affects specific districts/states
- Opposition arguments

## Mock Hearing Format

**Participants**:
- 3-4 students: Committee members (majority and minority)
- 1 student: Witness (testifier)
- Others: Observers (provide feedback)

**Procedure**:
1. Chairman opens hearing, introduces witness (1 min)
2. Witness sworn in (optional, for realism)
3. Witness delivers 5-minute oral testimony
4. Q&A round: Each "member" asks 2-3 questions (15-20 min)
5. Chairman closes hearing
6. Debrief and feedback (10 min)
