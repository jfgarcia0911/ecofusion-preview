# Activity 1: Policy Brief Writing Exercise

**Course**: AG-505 Agricultural Policy & Advocacy
**Time Required**: 3-4 hours (plus research time)
**Format**: Individual assignment
**Points**: 100

---

## Objective

Develop a professional policy brief analyzing an agricultural policy issue and presenting evidence-based recommendations. This exercise builds skills in policy analysis, research synthesis, and professional communication.

---

## Assignment

Write a 4-6 page policy brief on an agricultural policy issue relevant to controlled environment agriculture, sustainable agriculture, or agricultural innovation.

---

## Policy Issue Options

Choose ONE of the following or propose your own (with instructor approval):

1. **CEA Research Funding**: Advocating for dedicated USDA research funding for vertical farming and CEA technologies

2. **Organic Hydroponics**: Resolving the debate over hydroponic eligibility for organic certification

3. **Crop Insurance for CEA**: Extending federal crop insurance programs to indoor agriculture operations

4. **Urban Agriculture Zoning**: Model zoning ordinance to support urban agriculture and CEA

5. **Energy Policy for CEA**: Tax incentives and programs to support renewable energy adoption in energy-intensive indoor agriculture

6. **H-2A Year-Round Use**: Allowing H-2A temporary agricultural workers for year-round CEA operations

7. **Food Safety Standards**: Science-based FSMA standards tailored to controlled environment production

8. **Water Policy**: Recognition of CEA water efficiency in water-scarce regions and policy incentives

9. **Climate-Smart Agriculture**: Including CEA in USDA climate-smart agriculture programs and carbon markets

10. **Local Food Procurement**: Policies supporting CEA products in federal nutrition programs (schools, SNAP)

## Policy Brief Format

### Page 1: Executive Summary and Overview

**Executive Summary** (1/2 page):
- 2-3 sentence issue summary
- Key findings (3-5 bullet points)
- Clear recommendation

**Problem Statement** (1/2 page):
- What is the policy problem or opportunity?
- Who is affected and how?
- Why does it require policy action?
- Evidence of the problem (data, examples)

### Pages 2-3: Analysis

**Background and Context** (3/4 page):
- Historical context
- Current policy status
- Key stakeholders
- Recent developments

**Policy Options** (1 page):
- Option 1: Status quo
- Option 2: Your recommendation
- Option 3: Alternative approach(s)
- Brief description of each

**Evaluation** (3/4 page):
- Criteria for evaluation (effectiveness, feasibility, cost, etc.)
- Analysis of each option against criteria
- Evidence and data supporting analysis

### Pages 4-5: Recommendation and Implementation

**Recommendation** (1 page):
- Clearly state preferred policy option
- Justification based on analysis
- Expected outcomes and benefits
- Address potential objections

**Implementation** (1/2 page):
- Legislative or regulatory pathway
- Key decision-makers
- Timeline
- Resource requirements
- Potential challenges

**Stakeholder Impacts** (1/2 page):
- Who benefits and how
- Who bears costs
- Equity considerations
- Coalition building opportunities

### Page 6: Conclusion and References

**Conclusion** (1/4 page):
- Summarize key points
- Call to action
- Next steps

**References**:
- Minimum 8-10 credible sources
- Government documents, academic research, reputable organizations
- Properly cited (APA, MLA, or Chicago style)

## Example Policy Brief Outline

**Title**: "Extending Federal Crop Insurance to Vertical Farming Operations"

**Executive Summary**:
- Vertical farming excluded from crop insurance
- Limits investment and expansion
- Recommend pilot program
- Expected benefits: Risk mitigation, sector growth

**Problem**:
- CEA lacks insurance available to field agriculture
- Weather-independent but faces other risks (equipment, disease, market)
- Industry data on crop losses
- Stakeholder interviews

**Options**:
1. Status quo (no crop insurance)
2. Pilot program for CEA crop insurance
3. Full inclusion in existing programs

**Analysis**:
- Effectiveness, feasibility, cost, equity
- Data on CEA risks and losses
- Precedent from greenhouse industry

**Recommendation**: Pilot program
- Justification based on analysis
- Phased approach reduces risk
- Build data for full program

**Implementation**:
- Farm Bill language or RMA pilot authority
- Target: Senate and House Ag Committees
- Timeline: 2023 Farm Bill
- Stakeholder coalition

**Conclusion**: Clear benefits, manageable approach, advance sector

## Tips for Success

**Start Early**: Quality policy briefs require research time

**Focus**: Don't try to solve everything; narrow your scope

**Audience**: Write for busy policymaker who needs quick brief

**Evidence**: Use data and examples, not just opinions

**Clarity**: Clear, direct language; avoid academic jargon

**Professional**: Appearance matters; proofread carefully

**Specific**: Vague recommendations don't help; be specific and actionable

**Balance**: Acknowledge trade-offs and limitations of your recommendation
