# Course 505: Agricultural Policy & Advocacy

## Course Level
**Master/Executive Level (500-Series)**

## Course Duration
14 weeks | 42 contact hours | 8-10 hours per week self-study

---

## Course Description

Agricultural Policy & Advocacy is an advanced master-level course designed for professionals seeking to influence agricultural policy, engage in legislative processes, and advocate for controlled environment agriculture (CEA) and sustainable food systems. This course provides comprehensive training in policy analysis, legislative strategy, regulatory affairs, coalition building, and advocacy communications.

Students will master frameworks for analyzing agricultural policies, learn to navigate federal and state legislative processes, develop strategies for effective lobbying and advocacy, and understand the intersection of agriculture with environmental, trade, labor, and food safety regulations. The course emphasizes practical skills including policy brief writing, testimony preparation, stakeholder engagement, and building coalitions for policy change.

Through case studies of successful agricultural policy initiatives, regulatory battles, and advocacy campaigns, students will develop the expertise needed to serve as policy advisors, government relations professionals, agricultural lobbyists, trade association executives, or advocacy organization leaders.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

### Policy Analysis & Development
- Analyze agricultural policies using established frameworks and evaluation criteria
- Assess policy impacts on different stakeholder groups and agricultural sectors
- Develop evidence-based policy recommendations supported by research and data
- Understand policy cycles, windows of opportunity, and implementation challenges

### Legislative Engagement
- Navigate federal and state legislative processes effectively
- Develop legislative strategy and identify key decision-makers
- Draft legislative language, amendments, and position statements
- Build relationships with legislators, staff, and committee personnel

### Regulatory Affairs
- Understand administrative rulemaking and regulatory development processes
- Engage in public comment periods and regulatory hearings
- Monitor regulatory changes affecting agriculture and food systems
- Navigate compliance requirements and enforcement mechanisms

### Advocacy & Communications
- Develop compelling advocacy messages for diverse audiences
- Prepare written and oral testimony for legislative hearings
- Create policy briefs, fact sheets, and advocacy materials
- Utilize media relations and digital communications for advocacy

### Coalition Building & Stakeholder Engagement
- Identify and engage diverse stakeholders in policy coalitions
- Navigate agricultural politics and competing interest groups
- Build consensus among diverse stakeholder perspectives
- Manage coalition dynamics and maintain organizational partnerships

### Strategic Leadership
- Develop comprehensive advocacy campaigns and strategies
- Assess political feasibility and identify policy opportunities
- Integrate grassroots and grasstops advocacy approaches
- Lead policy initiatives from conception through implementation

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Agricultural Policy Landscape
- History of agricultural policy in the United States
- Current policy framework and major legislation
- Key agencies, committees, and decision-makers
- Agricultural policy trends and emerging issues
- CEA and urban agriculture in policy context

### Module 2: Policy Analysis Frameworks
- Policy analysis methodologies and tools
- Cost-benefit analysis and economic impact assessment
- Stakeholder analysis and power mapping
- Evidence-based policymaking and data utilization
- Policy evaluation criteria and metrics

### Module 3: Legislative Process & Strategy
- How a bill becomes law: federal and state processes
- Committee systems and legislative calendars
- Legislative strategy development and timing
- Building legislative champions and sponsorship
- Legislative tracking and intelligence gathering

### Module 4: Regulatory Affairs Management
- Administrative Procedure Act and rulemaking
- Federal Register and public comment processes
- Regulatory impact analysis and economic studies
- USDA, FDA, EPA regulations affecting agriculture
- Compliance, enforcement, and legal challenges

### Module 5: Coalition Building & Stakeholder Engagement
- Stakeholder identification and power analysis
- Coalition formation and governance structures
- Managing diverse interests and finding common ground
- Agricultural politics and interest group dynamics
- Building and maintaining strategic partnerships

### Module 6: Public Affairs & Communications
- Strategic communications planning for advocacy
- Message development and framing strategies
- Media relations and press engagement
- Digital advocacy and social media campaigns
- Crisis communications and issue management

### Module 7: Lobbying Strategy & Ethics
- Fundamentals of lobbying and government relations
- Federal and state lobbying laws and regulations
- Ethics rules, disclosure requirements, and compliance
- Direct lobbying versus grassroots advocacy
- Building relationships with policymakers

### Module 8: International Trade Policy
- International trade agreements and agriculture
- Tariffs, quotas, and non-tariff trade barriers
- WTO, USMCA, and bilateral trade agreements
- Export promotion and market access issues
- Trade policy advocacy and negotiation processes

### Module 9: Food Safety & Labeling Regulation
- FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)
- Food safety standards for produce and processing
- Labeling requirements and claims regulation
- Organic, non-GMO, and specialty certifications
- State versus federal food safety jurisdiction

### Module 10: Environmental Policy & Agriculture
- Clean Water Act and agricultural runoff
- Clean Air Act and agricultural emissions
- Endangered Species Act and farming operations
- Climate policy and agriculture
- Environmental advocacy and agricultural exemptions

### Module 11: Labor & Immigration Policy
- Agricultural labor force and workforce challenges
- H-2A visa program and immigration reform
- Labor standards, wages, and working conditions
- Farmworker protections and safety regulations
- Immigration policy advocacy

### Module 12: Research Funding & Innovation Policy
- USDA research programs and funding mechanisms
- Land-grant universities and agricultural research
- SBIR/STTR programs for agricultural innovation
- Farm Bill research provisions
- Advocacy for research funding and priorities

### Module 13: Local & State Policy Engagement
- State agricultural policies and programs
- Local zoning, land use, and right-to-farm laws
- State incentive programs for agriculture
- Regional policy variations and opportunities
- Building state and local advocacy capacity

### Module 14: Future Trends & Emerging Policy Issues
- Technology policy and precision agriculture
- Vertical farming and CEA policy needs
- Alternative proteins and cultivated agriculture
- Carbon markets and climate-smart agriculture
- Food security, nutrition, and agricultural policy
- Policy entrepreneurship and innovation

## Course Policies

### Professional Conduct
Students are expected to maintain the highest standards of ethical conduct in all policy and advocacy work. Understanding and complying with lobbying laws, disclosure requirements, and professional ethics is paramount.

### Confidentiality
Policy work often involves confidential strategies, coalition negotiations, and sensitive political information. Students must respect confidentiality and exercise appropriate discretion.

### Political Neutrality
This course teaches advocacy skills applicable across the political spectrum. Students should focus on policy analysis and advocacy techniques rather than partisan political positions.
