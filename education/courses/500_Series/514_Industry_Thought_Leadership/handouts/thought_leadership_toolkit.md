## 1. Personal Brand Package Templates

### A. Positioning Statement Formula

```
I am the [unique descriptor]
helping [specific audience]
[achieve specific outcome]
through [unique approach/differentiator]
```

**Example:**
"I am the engineer-turned-grower helping vertical farm operators achieve profitability through data-driven operational optimization."

### B. Bio Templates

**Micro Bio (25 words)**
```
[Name] is a [role/expertise] specializing in [focus area].
[One key credential/achievement]. [Current position/affiliation].
```

**Short Bio (50-75 words)**
```
[Name] is a [role] with [X years] experience in [domain].
[Key expertise areas]. [Notable achievement or differentiation].
[Current role]. [Education or credential]. [One personal element if appropriate].
```

**Medium Bio (150-200 words)**
Include: Background journey, expertise areas, specific achievements, current work, credentials, what makes you unique, personal connection to work.

**Long Bio (300-400 words)**
Comprehensive narrative including: Origin story, career progression, major accomplishments, philosophy/approach, current focus, credentials, affiliations, personal elements.

### C. Elevator Pitch Framework

**30-Second Version:**
```
"I [what you do] for [who you serve].
What makes me different is [unique approach].
For example, [brief specific result or example].
I'm currently focused on [current priority/offering]."
```

**60-Second Version:**
Add: More context on problem you solve, additional example or proof point, clearer call to action or conversation invitation.

### D. Value Proposition Statement

```
For [target audience]
who [have specific need/problem],
[your name/offering] provides [solution/benefit]
through [unique approach/methodology].
Unlike [alternative approaches/competitors],
[unique differentiator or proof].
```

### B. Speaker One-Sheet Structure

**Page Layout:**
- Professional headshot (top)
- Name and title
- One-sentence positioning
- Speaking topics (3-5 with brief descriptions)
- Notable speaking experience highlights
- Audience testimonials (2-3)
- Contact information
- Website/social media links

### C. Presentation Design Checklist

Before Every Presentation:
☐ One main idea per slide
☐ Minimal text (headlines + key points only)
☐ High-quality, relevant images
☐ Consistent visual design
☐ Readable fonts (24pt minimum)
☐ High contrast for readability
☐ No walls of text
☐ Clear section transitions
☐ Strong opening and closing slides
☐ Contact info on final slide


### B. Quarterly Review Questions

- What content performed best?
- What topics resonated most?
- What platforms drove most engagement?
- What should I do more/less of?

**Network and Relationships:**
- What new relationships developed?
- What connections deepened?
- How effectively am I nurturing my network?
- What networking approaches worked best?

**Visibility and Opportunities:**
- What opportunities emerged?

- What doors opened?
- What surprised me?

**Goals and Strategy:**
- Progress toward annual goals?
- What's working in my strategy?
- What needs adjustment?
- What should I start/stop/continue?

## Final Tips for Using This Toolkit

1. **Customize Templates**: Adapt all templates to your authentic voice and style
2. **Start Simple**: Don't try to implement everything at once
3. **Test and Iterate**: Find what works for you through experimentation
4. **Stay Organized**: Keep templates accessible and updated
5. **Share Generously**: When appropriate, share these resources with others
6. **Update Regularly**: Refine templates based on what works
