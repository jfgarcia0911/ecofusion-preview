## Section 1: Foundations and Brand Development (Questions 1-10)

**1. The primary purpose of thought leadership is:**
A) Building personal wealth
B) Becoming famous in your industry
C) Serving the community by advancing knowledge and practices
D) Getting speaking engagements

**Answer: C**

**2. Which best describes authentic personal branding?**
A) Creating a persona different from your true self
B) Genuine alignment between who you are, what you believe, and how you communicate
C) Copying successful thought leaders
D) Focus solely on visual identity

**Answer: B**

**3. The Thought Leadership Triangle requires:**
A) Money, time, and effort
B) Credentials, connections, and charisma
C) Expertise, communication, and contribution
D) Platform, content, and audience

**Answer: C**

**4. What is "white space" in personal brand positioning?**
A) Gaps in your resume
B) Areas where you have expertise but competition is limited
C) Time between posts
D) Unused website space

**Answer: B**

**5. How long does it typically take to build authentic thought leadership?**
A) 3-6 months
B) 1 year
C) Multiple years of consistent effort
D) It happens overnight with the right strategy

**Answer: C**

## Section 2: Communication and Content (Questions 11-20)

**6. The 70-20-10 content rule recommends:**
A) 70% promotional content
B) 70% core expertise/value, 20% curated, 10% personal/promotional
C) 70% written, 20% video, 10% images
D) 70% LinkedIn, 20% Twitter, 10% other platforms

**Answer: B**

**7. The BLUF principle stands for:**
A) Be Logical, Useful, and Factual
B) Bottom Line Up Front
C) Build, Launch, Update, Follow
D) Brief, Limited, Understandable, Focused

**Answer: B**

**8. What makes content "scannable"?**
A) Short paragraphs, headers, bullets, whitespace
B) Using as many words as possible
C) Avoiding all formatting
D) Wall-of-text presentation

**Answer: A**

**9. The inverted pyramid structure places:**
A) Most important information first
B) Most important information last
C) Equal weight throughout
D) Introduction before everything

**Answer: A**

**10. For LinkedIn, the optimal post length to avoid "see more" is:**
A) 500 characters
B) 1300 characters
C) 3000 characters
D) Unlimited

**Answer: B**
