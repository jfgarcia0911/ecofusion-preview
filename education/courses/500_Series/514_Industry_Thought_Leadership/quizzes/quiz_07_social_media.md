# Quiz 7: Social Media Strategy

## Course 514: Industry Thought Leadership

**Module**: 8 - Social Media Strategy
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
Which social media platform is most important for B2B thought leadership in CEA?

A) TikTok exclusively
B) LinkedIn for professional networking and industry content
C) Snapchat only
D) Dating apps

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: LinkedIn is the primary platform for B2B thought leadership, enabling professional networking, long-form content, and industry engagement.

---

### Question 2
What is "engagement rate" and why does it matter more than follower count?

A) Number of followers only
B) Ratio of interactions to reach, indicating content resonance and audience connection
C) Post frequency
D) Time spent on platform

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Engagement (likes, comments, shares) relative to reach shows content is resonating. 1000 engaged followers outweigh 10,000 passive ones.

---

### Question 3
How should thought leaders balance personal and professional content on social media?

A) Only personal content
B) Primarily professional with selective personal elements that humanize and build connection
C) Only professional with zero personality
D) Equal parts unrelated personal content

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Effective professional accounts focus on expertise while showing personality strategically to build authentic connections.

---

### Question 4
What is the optimal frequency for LinkedIn posts to maintain visibility without overwhelming?

A) Every 5 minutes
B) 2-5 times per week with consistent quality
C) Once per year
D) Random with no pattern

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Consistent posting (2-5x weekly) maintains visibility in feeds while allowing time for quality content creation.

---

### Question 5
How should thought leaders respond to comments and engagement?

A) Ignore all comments
B) Respond thoughtfully to build community and deepen relationships
C) Only like comments without responding
D) Argue with anyone who disagrees

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Engaging with comments builds community, shows you value input, and deepens relationships—essential for thought leadership.

---

### Question 6
What is "social listening" and how does it benefit thought leaders?

A) Eavesdropping on private conversations
B) Monitoring industry conversations to identify trends, questions, and engagement opportunities
C) Only listening to your own content
D) Avoiding all social media

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Social listening tracks industry discussions, revealing emerging topics, audience questions, and opportunities to contribute value.

---

### Question 7
How should thought leaders approach influencer collaborations?

A) Copy everything influencers do
B) Partner with aligned influencers for authentic mutual value creation
C) Avoid all collaborations
D) Pay for fake endorsements

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Authentic collaborations with aligned influencers extend reach and credibility when based on genuine shared values and mutual benefit.

---

### Question 8
What analytics are most important for measuring social media thought leadership success?

A) Vanity metrics only (followers, likes)
B) Engagement, reach, conversions (speaking, partnerships), and share of voice
C) Platform usage time only
D) Number of posts created

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Meaningful metrics include engagement quality, reach expansion, conversions to opportunities, and influence in industry conversations.

---

### Question 9
How should thought leaders handle negative comments or criticism on social media?

A) Delete all negative feedback
B) Respond professionally, address valid concerns, and take heated discussions offline
C) Engage in public arguments
D) Ignore completely

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Professional responses to valid criticism build credibility. Address concerns transparently and move detailed discussions to private channels.

---

### Question 10
What is the best approach to content variety on social media?

A) Only post one content type
B) Mix formats (articles, videos, polls, images) testing what resonates with audience
C) Never try new formats
D) Copy exactly what competitors post

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Varied content formats appeal to different preferences, maintain interest, and help identify what resonates most with your audience.

---

## Answer Key

1. B - LinkedIn for professional B2B engagement
2. B - Interaction ratio showing content resonance
3. B - Professional focus with humanizing personal elements
4. B - 2-5 times weekly with quality
5. B - Respond thoughtfully to build community
6. B - Monitor conversations for trends and opportunities
7. B - Authentic partnerships for mutual value
8. B - Engagement, reach, conversions, share of voice
9. B - Professional response, address concerns
10. B - Mix formats to test audience preferences
