# Quiz 6: Media Relations and Interviews

## Course 514: Industry Thought Leadership

**Module**: 7 - Media Relations and Interviews
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is a "media pitch" and what makes it effective?

A) Sales pitch for products
B) Concise story idea tailored to journalist's audience with clear news value
C) Generic mass email to all media
D) Demanding coverage

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Effective pitches offer specific, timely story ideas relevant to the journalist's beat and audience, demonstrating clear news value.

---

### Question 2
What is the "bridging technique" in media interviews?

A) Discussing bridge construction
B) Transitioning from reporter's question to your key message
C) Avoiding all questions
D) Only discussing bridges

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Bridging allows you to acknowledge questions while steering conversation to your key messages (e.g., "That's interesting, and what's important to understand is...").

---

### Question 3
How should thought leaders prepare for media interviews?

A) No preparation needed
B) Identify key messages, prepare examples, anticipate questions, and practice delivery
C) Memorize everything word-for-word
D) Prepare completely different topics

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Preparation includes clarifying 2-3 key messages, supporting examples, anticipated questions, and practice to ensure confident, on-message delivery.

---

### Question 4
What is "off the record" and how should it be handled?

A) Anything can be said freely
B) Statements not for publication, but risky and should be avoided or used very carefully
C) Preferred way to communicate everything
D) No different from on-record

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: "Off the record" means not for publication, but is risky as understandings can differ. Best practice is to assume everything is on-record.

---

### Question 5
How should spokespeople respond to "I don't know" situations in interviews?

A) Make up an answer
B) Acknowledge the question, commit to finding information, and provide what you do know
C) Ignore the question
D) End the interview immediately

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Honesty builds credibility. Acknowledge gaps, commit to follow-up, and bridge to related information you can provide.

---

### Question 6
What is the purpose of a "press release" in media relations?

A) Advertising disguised as news
B) Formal announcement of newsworthy information in standardized format
C) Personal opinion pieces
D) Internal company communication only

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Press releases announce genuine news (product launches, research, partnerships) in journalistic format, making it easy for media to cover.

---

### Question 7
In broadcast interviews, what is most important about body language?

A) Sitting completely still
B) Confident posture, engaged expression, and natural gestures supporting messages
C) Constant movement and distraction
D) Looking away from camera/interviewer

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Nonverbal communication conveys confidence and engagement. Natural posture, eye contact, and purposeful gestures reinforce verbal messages.

---

### Question 8
How should thought leaders handle hostile or "gotcha" questions?

A) Become defensive or aggressive
B) Stay calm, reframe the question, and bridge to key messages
C) Refuse to continue interview
D) Attack the interviewer

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Remaining composed and professional while reframing negative questions demonstrates expertise and media savvy.

---

### Question 9
What is a "media kit" and what should it include?

A) Physical toolkit for journalists
B) Package of materials including bio, headshot, backgrounders, and contact information
C) Advertising brochures only
D) Product samples

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Media kits provide journalists with ready-to-use materials: professional bio, high-res photo, company background, and relevant fact sheets.

---

### Question 10
How frequently should thought leaders proactively engage with media?

A) Only when facing crisis
B) Regularly with valuable insights, responding to trends and offering expert perspective
C) Daily regardless of news value
D) Never proactively

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Consistent proactive engagement around relevant industry topics builds relationships and positions you as a go-to expert for journalists.

---

## Answer Key

2. B - Transitioning to your key messages
3. B - Identify messages, prepare examples, practice
4. B - Not for publication but risky, use carefully
5. B - Acknowledge, commit to follow-up, provide related info
6. B - Formal newsworthy announcement
7. B - Confident posture, engaged expression, natural gestures
8. B - Stay calm, reframe, bridge to messages
9. B - Bio, headshot, backgrounders, contact info
