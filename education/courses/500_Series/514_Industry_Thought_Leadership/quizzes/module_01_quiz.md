# Module 1 Quiz: Foundations of Thought Leadership

## Instructions
- 10 multiple choice questions
- Select the best answer for each question
- Passing score: 80% (8/10 correct)
- Time limit: 15 minutes

---

**1. Which of the following BEST defines thought leadership in the CEA industry?**

A) Having the most social media followers
B) Being the loudest voice at conferences
C) Expressing informed ideas that demonstrate expertise and move the industry forward
D) Publishing the most articles in trade magazines

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**2. What is the primary difference between a subject matter expert and a thought leader?**

A) Thought leaders have more credentials
B) Thought leaders proactively share insights and shape conversations beyond answering questions
C) Subject matter experts are more knowledgeable
D) There is no significant difference

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. According to the Thought Leadership Triangle, which three elements must be present?**

A) Expertise, Money, Fame
B) Expertise, Communication, Contribution
C) Knowledge, Connections, Credentials
D) Experience, Visibility, Charisma

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**4. Which of the following is a WARNING SIGN that you may NOT be ready for thought leadership?**

A) Having 5+ years of CEA experience
B) Feeling motivated to share knowledge beyond your organization
C) Being primarily motivated by ego or self-promotion
D) Having clear professional goals

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**5. What is the typical time investment required to build authentic thought leadership?**

A) 3-6 months of intensive effort
B) 1-2 years total commitment
C) Multiple years requiring consistent, ongoing effort
D) Can be achieved in a few weeks with the right strategy

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**6. Which ethical obligation is MOST important for thought leaders?**

A) Promoting their employer's products
B) Accuracy and truth in all claims and statements
C) Always agreeing with industry consensus
D) Maximizing social media engagement

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. According to the lesson, which statement about thought leadership is a MYTH?**

A) Thought leadership takes years to develop
B) Quality of engagement matters more than follower count
C) Thought leaders are born, not made
D) Thought leadership requires consistent practice

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**8. What is the purpose of creating a "Why Statement" for thought leadership?**

A) To impress potential employers
B) To articulate your motivation, unique expertise, and commitment
C) To create marketing copy
D) To meet course requirements

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**9. Which is an example of Level 5 on the Thought Leadership Spectrum?**

A) Competent execution of work within your organization
B) Recognized expertise in a specific area
C) Defining industry direction and creating new paradigms
D) Active sharing through content and speaking

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**10. What is the Paradox of Thought Leadership regarding confidence and humility?**

A) You should always be humble and never confident
B) You must be extremely confident with no humility
C) You must assert perspectives while acknowledging limitations
D) Confidence and humility are unrelated to thought leadership

**Correct Answer: C**
