# Course 509: Teaching & Curriculum Design for CEA

## Master Level - Educational Program Development

### Course Description

This advanced master-level course prepares professionals to design, develop, and deliver high-quality educational programs in Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA). Students will master instructional design principles, curriculum development frameworks, and assessment strategies specifically tailored for CEA education. The course covers adult learning theory, hands-on instructional methods, online and hybrid delivery models, and the development of industry-aligned certification programs. Participants will learn to create comprehensive educational experiences that bridge theoretical knowledge with practical skills required in modern CEA operations.

**Course Code:** CEA-509
**Level:** 500 (Master/Executive)
**Credits:** 3 Graduate Credits
**Duration:** 14 Weeks
**Contact Hours:** 42 hours instruction + 84 hours independent work
**Delivery Format:** Hybrid (Online lectures, in-person workshops, practicum)

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Apply adult learning theories and principles to CEA educational program design
2. Develop comprehensive curriculum frameworks aligned with industry competencies
3. Create measurable learning objectives and outcomes using established taxonomies
4. Design effective instructional materials for technical CEA content
5. Implement hands-on learning strategies for laboratory and field-based instruction
6. Develop valid and reliable assessment instruments for CEA competencies
7. Create engaging online and hybrid learning experiences for diverse audiences
8. Design safe and effective laboratory and practical training sessions
9. Build strategic partnerships between educational institutions and industry
10. Develop professional certification programs aligned with industry standards
11. Conduct comprehensive training needs assessments for CEA organizations
12. Evaluate educational program effectiveness using multiple metrics
13. Implement continuous improvement processes for educational programs
14. Build sustainable educational programs from concept to implementation

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Foundations of Adult Learning in CEA
**Duration:** Week 1
Introduction to andragogy and adult learning principles specific to technical agriculture education. Understanding learner characteristics, motivation, and prior experience in CEA contexts.

### Module 2: Curriculum Design Frameworks and Models
**Duration:** Week 1
Comprehensive exploration of curriculum design frameworks including backward design, competency-based education, and industry-aligned curriculum development for CEA programs.

### Module 3: Learning Objectives and Outcomes Development
**Duration:** Week 1
Mastery of learning objective writing using Bloom's Taxonomy, SMART criteria, and competency-based frameworks. Aligning objectives with industry standards and certification requirements.

### Module 4: Instructional Design for Technical CEA Content
**Duration:** Week 1
Application of ADDIE, SAM, and other instructional design models to create effective CEA learning experiences. Developing instructional strategies for complex technical content.

### Module 5: Hands-On Learning Strategies
**Duration:** Week 2
Design and implementation of experiential learning activities, problem-based learning, project-based learning, and hands-on training methods for CEA operations.

### Module 6: Assessment Design and Evaluation Methods
**Duration:** Week 2
Creating valid and reliable assessments including performance-based assessments, skills demonstrations, written exams, and authentic assessments for CEA competencies.

### Module 7: Online and Hybrid Course Delivery
**Duration:** Week 2
Designing effective online and hybrid learning experiences using learning management systems, video instruction, virtual labs, and synchronous/asynchronous methods.

### Module 8: Laboratory and Practical Training Sessions
**Duration:** Week 2
Planning and conducting safe, effective laboratory and field-based training sessions. Managing equipment, materials, safety protocols, and skill development.

### Module 9: Industry Partnerships in Education
**Duration:** Week 2
Building and maintaining strategic partnerships with CEA companies, developing apprenticeships, internships, advisory committees, and industry-sponsored projects.

### Module 10: Professional Certification Program Development
**Duration:** Week 2
Creating industry-recognized certification programs including competency mapping, credential development, examination design, and maintenance of certification.

### Module 11: Training Needs Assessment
**Duration:** Week 2
Conducting comprehensive needs assessments for organizations and learners. Gap analysis, stakeholder consultation, and data-driven program planning.

### Module 12: Evaluating Educational Effectiveness
**Duration:** Week 2
Implementing Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Evaluation, measuring learning outcomes, assessing program impact, and collecting meaningful evaluation data.

### Module 13: Continuous Improvement in Education
**Duration:** Week 2
Quality assurance processes, systematic program review, data-driven decision making, and iterative improvement of educational programs.

### Module 14: Building Comprehensive Educational Programs
**Duration:** Week 2
Integrating all course concepts to design complete educational programs from concept through implementation, sustainability, and scaling.

## Major Projects

### 1. Curriculum Development Project (25%)

Design a complete curriculum for a CEA educational program (course, certificate, or degree). Include:
- Needs assessment summary
- Program goals and learning outcomes
- Course sequence and descriptions
- Competency framework
- Assessment strategy
- Implementation plan

**Deliverable:** 20-25 page curriculum document
**Due:** Week 12

### 2. Lesson Plan Portfolio (20%)

Develop five comprehensive lesson plans covering different CEA topics and instructional methods:
- One lecture-based lesson
- One hands-on laboratory lesson
- One online asynchronous lesson
- One problem-based learning lesson
- One assessment-focused lesson

Each lesson plan must include objectives, materials, procedures, assessments, and supporting materials.

**Deliverable:** Portfolio with five complete lesson plans
**Due:** Weeks 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 (one per submission)

### 3. Assessment Design Package (15%)

Create a comprehensive assessment suite for a CEA topic including:
- Formative assessments (3)
- Summative assessment (1)
- Performance-based assessment (1)
- Rubrics and scoring guides
- Answer keys and sample responses
- Validity and reliability documentation

**Deliverable:** Complete assessment package
**Due:** Week 10
