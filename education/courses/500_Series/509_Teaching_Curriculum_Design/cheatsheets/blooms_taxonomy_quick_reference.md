# Bloom's Taxonomy Quick Reference Guide

## Teaching & Curriculum Design for CEA - Cheatsheet #1

---

## Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (2001)

### Hierarchy: Lower to Higher Order Thinking

```
        CREATE (Highest)
              ↑
          EVALUATE
              ↑
          ANALYZE
              ↑
           APPLY
              ↑
        UNDERSTAND
              ↑
         REMEMBER (Lowest)
```

---

## Level 1: REMEMBER
**Definition:** Recall facts and basic concepts

**Action Verbs:**
- Define, List, Identify, Name, Label, Describe, Match, State, Recognize, Recall, Select

**CEA Examples:**
- List the 17 essential plant nutrients
- Define photosynthetically active radiation (PAR)
- Identify components of an NFT system from a diagram
- State the optimal pH range for hydroponic lettuce

**Assessment:**
- Multiple choice, True/False, Matching, Fill-in-the-blank, Labeling diagrams

---

## Level 2: UNDERSTAND
**Definition:** Explain ideas or concepts

**Action Verbs:**
- Explain, Summarize, Classify, Compare, Contrast, Interpret, Paraphrase, Discuss, Describe, Illustrate

**CEA Examples:**
- Explain the relationship between temperature and humidity in VPD
- Compare DWC and NFT hydroponic systems
- Summarize the nitrogen cycle in aquaponic systems
- Classify nutrient deficiencies as mobile or immobile

**Assessment:**
- Short answer, Concept maps, Summaries, Compare/contrast essays

---

## Level 3: APPLY
**Definition:** Use information in new situations

**Action Verbs:**
- Apply, Calculate, Solve, Demonstrate, Use, Execute, Implement, Operate, Show, Construct

**CEA Examples:**
- Calculate daily light integral (DLI) from PPFD and photoperiod
- Demonstrate proper transplanting technique
- Apply nutrient mixing formulas to create stock solutions
- Operate climate control systems to maintain targets

**Assessment:**
- Problem sets, Demonstrations, Simulations, Practical exams

---

## Level 4: ANALYZE
**Definition:** Draw connections among ideas; break into parts

**Action Verbs:**
- Analyze, Compare, Contrast, Differentiate, Examine, Investigate, Categorize, Organize, Deconstruct

**CEA Examples:**
- Analyze crop failure data to identify environmental causes
- Examine energy usage patterns to find inefficiencies
- Differentiate between pest damage and nutrient deficiency symptoms
- Investigate relationships between variables in production data

**Assessment:**
- Case studies, Data analysis projects, Troubleshooting scenarios, Research reports

---

## Level 5: EVALUATE
**Definition:** Justify decisions or courses of action

**Action Verbs:**
- Evaluate, Judge, Critique, Assess, Justify, Defend, Recommend, Prioritize, Rate, Argue

**CEA Examples:**
- Evaluate three lighting systems and recommend the best for energy efficiency
- Critique a facility design for workflow and safety
- Assess crop varieties for market suitability
- Justify selection of specific growing system for business model

**Assessment:**
- Recommendation reports, Critiques, Position papers, Design evaluations

---

## Level 6: CREATE
**Definition:** Produce new or original work

**Action Verbs:**
- Create, Design, Develop, Formulate, Construct, Plan, Produce, Invent, Generate, Compose

**CEA Examples:**
- Design a complete vertical farm for urban location
- Develop an integrated pest management protocol
- Create a year-round production schedule
- Formulate a business plan for CEA startup

**Assessment:**
- Design projects, Business plans, Research proposals, Original protocols, Comprehensive plans

## Tips for Using Bloom's Taxonomy

### When Writing Learning Objectives:
1. **Start with the level:** Choose appropriate cognitive level for your students
2. **Use specific action verb:** Pick clear, measurable verb from that level
3. **Add content:** What specifically will they do?
4. **Include criteria:** How well must they do it?

**Example:**
- **Level:** Apply
- **Verb:** Calculate
- **Content:** nutrient requirements for lettuce production
- **Criteria:** with 95% accuracy
- **Full Objective:** "Calculate nutrient requirements for lettuce production with 95% accuracy"

### Progression Across a Course:
- **Week 1-2:** Remember, Understand (Foundation)
- **Week 3-5:** Apply (Practice)
- **Week 6-8:** Analyze, Evaluate (Integration)
- **Week 9-10:** Create (Synthesis)

### Balance is Key:
- Don't stay only at lower levels (boring, no depth)
- Don't jump to highest levels without foundation (frustrating, impossible)
- Build progressively from lower to higher

## Psychomotor Domain (Skills)

**Dave's Taxonomy:**

1. **Imitation:** Copy observed action
   - *Example:* Follow demonstration to transplant seedlings

2. **Manipulation:** Perform from instruction
   - *Example:* Transplant following written procedure

3. **Precision:** Perform accurately without help
   - *Example:* Transplant independently with 95% survival

4. **Articulation:** Coordinate multiple skills
   - *Example:* Adapt transplanting for different crops efficiently

5. **Naturalization:** Automatic expert performance
   - *Example:* Master transplanting; 50+ plants/hour consistently

---

## Affective Domain (Attitudes/Values)

**Krathwohl's Taxonomy:**

1. **Receiving:** Awareness and willingness to hear
   - *Example:* Listen to perspectives on sustainability

2. **Responding:** Active participation
   - *Example:* Participate in food safety discussions

3. **Valuing:** Attaching worth
   - *Example:* Demonstrate commitment to safety protocols

4. **Organizing:** Prioritizing values
   - *Example:* Integrate sustainability into decision-making

5. **Internalizing:** Values guide behavior
   - *Example:* Exemplify ethical CEA leadership

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**DON'T use non-measurable verbs:**
- ~~Know, Understand (as sole verb), Appreciate, Believe, Learn~~

**DO use observable action verbs:**
- Explain (shows understanding), Calculate (shows application), Design (shows creation)

**DON'T combine multiple levels:**
- ~~"Students will understand, apply, and evaluate systems"~~ (Pick one)

**DO focus each objective:**
- "Students will evaluate system designs using cost-benefit analysis"

---

## Quick Decision Tree

**Need foundational knowledge?** → Remember, Understand

**Need application of skills?** → Apply

**Need critical thinking?** → Analyze, Evaluate

**Need innovation/synthesis?** → Create

**Need hands-on skills?** → Use Psychomotor Domain

**Need professional attitudes?** → Use Affective Domain

---

## CEA-Specific Applications

**Introductory Courses (100-200 level):**
Focus: Remember, Understand, Apply (60-70% of objectives)

**Intermediate Courses (300-400 level):**
Focus: Apply, Analyze, Evaluate (60-70% of objectives)

**Advanced Courses (500+ level):**
Focus: Analyze, Evaluate, Create (70-80% of objectives)
