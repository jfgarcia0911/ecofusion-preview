# Instructional Design Models Quick Reference

## CEA-509 Cheatsheet #2

---

## ADDIE Model

**Most widely used systematic approach**

### A - ANALYSIS
**Purpose:** Understand the problem and context

**Key Questions:**
- Who are the learners?
- What do they need to learn?
- Why do they need it?
- What constraints exist?
- What's the environment?

**Outputs:** Needs assessment, learner analysis, context analysis

---

### D - DESIGN
**Purpose:** Create blueprint for instruction

**Key Activities:**
- Write learning objectives
- Plan assessment strategy
- Select instructional methods
- Outline content
- Sequence topics

**Outputs:** Course outline, objectives, assessment plan, instructional strategy

---

### D - DEVELOPMENT
**Purpose:** Create materials

**Key Activities:**
- Write lesson plans
- Create presentations
- Develop activities
- Build assessments
- Produce media

**Outputs:** All instructional materials ready to use

---

### I - IMPLEMENTATION
**Purpose:** Deliver instruction

**Key Activities:**
- Train instructors
- Prepare facilities
- Enroll students
- Deliver training
- Support learners

**Outputs:** Training delivered, students engaged

---

### E - EVALUATION
**Purpose:** Assess effectiveness

**Key Activities:**
- Measure learning (Kirkpatrick Levels)
- Gather feedback
- Analyze data
- Identify improvements

**Outputs:** Evaluation report, improvement plan

---

## Backward Design (UbD)

**Start with the end in mind**

### Stage 1: Identify Desired Results
- **Enduring Understandings:** Big ideas to retain
- **Essential Questions:** Frame inquiry
- **Learning Objectives:** Specific outcomes

### Stage 2: Determine Evidence
- **Performance Tasks:** Authentic demonstrations
- **Other Evidence:** Tests, quizzes, observations
- Design assessment BEFORE instruction

### Stage 3: Plan Learning Experiences
- **WHERETO Framework:**
  - W: Where & Why
  - H: Hook & Hold
  - E: Explore & Equip
  - R: Reflect & Rethink
  - E: Evaluate
  - T: Tailored
  - O: Organized

---

## SAM (Successive Approximation Model)

**Agile, iterative approach**

### Phase 1: Savvy Start
- Brainstorm
- Gather information
- Quick prototype

### Phase 2: Iterative Design
- Design prototype
- Review with stakeholders
- Refine based on feedback
- Repeat

### Phase 3: Iterative Development
- Develop
- Test with learners
- Refine
- Repeat until ready

**When to Use:** Tight timelines, e-learning, unclear requirements

---

## Kemp Design Model

**Circular, non-linear approach**

**9 Components (can start anywhere):**
1. Identify problems/goals
2. Analyze learners
3. Identify content
4. Specify objectives
5. Sequence content
6. Design strategies
7. Plan instruction
8. Develop materials
9. Evaluate/revise

**When to Use:** Revising existing courses, flexible development

## Comparison Table

| Model | Linear? | Best For | Time | Flexibility |
|-------|---------|----------|------|-------------|
| ADDIE | Yes | Comprehensive projects | Long | Structured |
| Backward Design | Yes | Academic courses | Medium | Structured |
| SAM | No (iterative) | E-learning, rapid development | Short | Very flexible |
| Kemp | No (circular) | Revisions, ongoing | Medium | Very flexible |

## Choosing the Right Model

**Use ADDIE when:**
- Starting from scratch
- Need systematic approach
- Have adequate time
- Want thorough analysis

**Use Backward Design when:**
- Designing academic courses
- Want strong alignment
- Assessment-focused
- K-12 or higher ed

**Use SAM when:**
- Rapid development needed
- E-learning project
- Want stakeholder involvement
- Agile approach preferred

**Use Kemp when:**
- Revising existing course
- Flexible entry point needed
- Cyclical development
- Continuous improvement focus

---

## Universal Principles (All Models)

1. **Start with learners:** Understand your audience
2. **Define outcomes:** Clear, measurable objectives
3. **Align everything:** Objectives ↔ Assessment ↔ Instruction
4. **Iterate:** Test, gather feedback, improve
5. **Evaluate:** Measure effectiveness, make data-driven changes

---

## CEA Application Tips

**For Workshop (1-3 days):**
- Use simplified ADDIE or SAM
- Focus on quick needs analysis
- Rapid prototyping

**For Certificate/Degree:**
- Full ADDIE or Backward Design
- Comprehensive analysis
- Systematic development

**For Online Modules:**
- SAM for speed
- Rapid iterations
- Frequent testing

**For Hands-On Labs:**
- Backward Design for alignment
- Start with performance task
- Design assessment early

---

**Remember:** Models are guides, not rigid rules. Adapt to your context!
