## Questions

**1. What does ADDIE stand for in instructional design?**

A) Assess, Design, Develop, Implement, Execute
B) Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation
C) Apply, Deliver, Document, Implement, Evaluate
D) Analyze, Decide, Develop, Install, Execute

**2. In the ADDIE model, what is the primary focus of the Analysis phase?**

A) Creating learning materials
B) Identifying learner needs, gaps, and context
C) Delivering instruction
D) Grading assessments

**3. Which instructional design model emphasizes rapid prototyping and iterative development?**

A) ADDIE
B) SAM (Successive Approximation Model)
C) Dick and Carey Model
D) Kemp Design Model

**4. When designing instruction for complex CEA technical skills, which approach is most effective?**

A) Lecture only
B) Chunking information with guided practice and scaffolding
C) Providing all information at once
D) Self-study with no support

**5. What is "cognitive load" in instructional design?**

A) Weight of textbooks
B) Mental effort required to process information
C) Number of students in class
D) Instructor's workload

**6. Which strategy helps manage cognitive load when teaching complex CEA systems?**

A) Present all information simultaneously
B) Use only text-based materials
C) Segment content, use worked examples, and eliminate extraneous information
D) Increase speed of instruction

**7. What is the purpose of a "worked example" in technical instruction?**

A) To show students how much work is required
B) To demonstrate step-by-step problem-solving process
C) To test prior knowledge
D) To replace all practice

**8. In Gagne's Nine Events of Instruction, what should come FIRST?**

A) Present content
B) Provide feedback
C) Gain attention and inform learners of objectives
D) Assess performance

**9. Which media type is most effective for demonstrating hands-on CEA procedures?**

A) Audio podcast only
B) Text description only
C) Video demonstration combined with hands-on practice
D) Written exam

**10. When designing online learning for CEA topics, what is the recommended length for video segments?**

A) 2 hours continuous
B) 6-10 minutes per segment
C) 30 seconds only
D) Video should never be used

---

## Answer Key

**1. B** - Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation

*Explanation:* ADDIE is the classic instructional design framework consisting of five phases: Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation.

**2. B** - Identifying learner needs, gaps, and context

*Explanation:* The Analysis phase focuses on understanding learners, identifying knowledge/skill gaps, and analyzing the learning context before designing instruction.

**3. B** - SAM (Successive Approximation Model)

*Explanation:* SAM emphasizes rapid iterative design with quick prototyping and revision cycles, contrasting with ADDIE's more linear approach.

**4. B** - Chunking information with guided practice and scaffolding

*Explanation:* Complex skills are best taught by breaking content into manageable chunks, providing structured practice, and gradually reducing support.

**5. B** - Mental effort required to process information

*Explanation:* Cognitive load refers to the amount of mental effort being used in working memory, which has limited capacity.

**6. C** - Segment content, use worked examples, and eliminate extraneous information

*Explanation:* Effective cognitive load management involves chunking content, using worked examples to reduce problem-solving load, and removing unnecessary information.

**7. B** - To demonstrate step-by-step problem-solving process

*Explanation:* Worked examples show the complete process of solving problems, reducing cognitive load and providing models for learners to follow.

**8. C** - Gain attention and inform learners of objectives

*Explanation:* Gagne's first two events are gaining attention and informing learners of objectives, establishing purpose and motivation before content delivery.

**9. C** - Video demonstration combined with hands-on practice

*Explanation:* Psychomotor skills are best learned through demonstration followed by guided hands-on practice, leveraging multiple learning modalities.

**10. B** - 6-10 minutes per segment

*Explanation:* Research shows 6-10 minute video segments optimize engagement and learning, preventing cognitive overload and maintaining attention.
