# Module 2 Quiz: Curriculum Design Frameworks and Models

**Time Limit:** 20 minutes | **Points:** 10 points

---

## Questions

**1. In backward design (Understanding by Design), what is the correct sequence?**
A) Instruction → Assessment → Learning Outcomes
B) Learning Outcomes → Instruction → Assessment
C) Learning Outcomes → Assessment → Instruction
D) Assessment → Learning Outcomes → Instruction

**2. Which curriculum design model is MOST appropriate for developing a CEA certification program focused on demonstrable skills?**
A) Subject-based design
B) Competency-based design
C) Thematic design
D) Problem-based design

**3. In the WHERETO framework (stage 3 of backward design), the "H" stands for:**
A) Help and support for struggling learners
B) Hook and hold students' attention
C) Homework assignments and practice
D) Higher-order thinking skills

**4. An "enduring understanding" in backward design refers to:**
A) Skills practiced repeatedly
B) Core concepts students should retain years after the course
C) Facts memorized for exams
D) Prerequisites for the course

**5. When conducting a job/task analysis for curriculum development, you should:**
A) Only interview recent graduates
B) Focus solely on academic theory
C) Observe workers, interview employers, and analyze job descriptions
D) Copy curriculum from similar programs

**6. Scope and sequence documents show:**
A) Only the topics covered in a course
B) Only the order of content delivery
C) What content is covered (scope) and in what order (sequence)
D) Student learning outcomes exclusively

**7. Which is an example of an "essential question" for a CEA curriculum?**
A) What is the pH range for hydroponic lettuce?
B) How do we balance productivity with sustainability in CEA?
C) When were hydroponic systems invented?
D) Who are the major CEA equipment suppliers?

**8. Spiral curriculum refers to:**
A) Learning content in circular classrooms
B) Returning to concepts multiple times with increasing sophistication
C) Teaching all concepts simultaneously
D) A curriculum that never changes

**9. An industry advisory committee should:**
A) Meet once and never return
B) Be composed entirely of faculty members
C) Include diverse industry representation and meet 2-3 times annually
D) Make all curriculum decisions without faculty input

**10. Which sequencing principle suggests teaching "How to transplant seedlings" before "How to optimize transplanting for maximum efficiency"?**
A) Abstract to concrete
B) Complex to simple
C) Simple to complex
D) Random sequencing

---

## Answer Key

1. **C** - Learning Outcomes → Assessment → Instruction (Backward design starts with desired results, determines evidence, then plans instruction)

2. **B** - Competency-based design (Best for demonstrable skills with flexible pathways and clear performance standards)

3. **B** - Hook and hold students' attention (Engaging students from start and maintaining engagement throughout)

4. **B** - Core concepts students should retain years after the course (Big ideas that have lasting value beyond specific facts)

5. **C** - Observe workers, interview employers, and analyze job descriptions (Multiple sources provide comprehensive understanding of actual job requirements)

6. **C** - What content is covered (scope) and in what order (sequence) (Both breadth/depth and ordering are shown)

7. **B** - How do we balance productivity with sustainability in CEA? (Open-ended question that frames ongoing inquiry, not a simple factual answer)

8. **B** - Returning to concepts multiple times with increasing sophistication (Learners revisit topics, building deeper understanding each time)

9. **C** - Include diverse industry representation and meet 2-3 times annually (Ensures relevance through regular diverse input)

10. **C** - Simple to complex (Basic skills before advanced optimization)
