# Quiz 7: Online and Hybrid Course Delivery

**Course:** CEA-509 Teaching & Curriculum Design for CEA
**Module:** 7 - Online and Hybrid Learning
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes
**Points:** 10 points (1 point each)

---

## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. This quiz assesses your understanding of online and hybrid learning design for CEA education.

---

## Questions

**1. What is the defining characteristic of "hybrid" or "blended" learning?**

A) Using only online delivery
B) Combining face-to-face and online learning activities
C) Traditional classroom only
D) No technology use

**2. Which online learning activity type is MOST effective for building community and discussion?**

A) Pre-recorded lectures only
B) Asynchronous discussion forums with structured prompts
C) Individual reading assignments
D) Avoiding all interaction

**3. What is the recommended structure for online CEA modules?**

A) Random organization
B) Clear structure with objectives, content, activities, and assessments
C) Only text-based content
D) No structure needed

**4. In online CEA courses, what is the maximum recommended length for instructional videos?**

A) 2-3 hours continuous
B) 6-12 minutes per segment
C) 30 seconds only
D) Video should never be used

**5. What is "Universal Design for Learning" (UDL) in online education?**

A) Design only for average students
B) Designing courses to be accessible and effective for diverse learners
C) Universal student uniforms
D) Standardized testing only

**6. Which technology is most effective for demonstrating hands-on CEA procedures in online courses?**

A) Text description only
B) High-quality video demonstrations from multiple angles
C) Audio podcast
D) Written exams

**7. What is the recommended instructor response time for student questions in online courses?**

A) Within 1 month
B) Within 24-48 hours on business days
C) Never respond
D) Immediately within minutes 24/7

**8. How should hands-on laboratory skills be addressed in hybrid CEA courses?**

A) Eliminate all hands-on learning
B) Online theory preparation with scheduled in-person laboratory sessions
C) Only online simulations
D) Hands-on learning is impossible in hybrid format

**9. What is "cognitive presence" in online learning communities?**

A) Physical attendance
B) Extent to which learners can construct and confirm meaning through reflection and discourse
C) Taking attendance online
D) Video camera requirements

**10. Which assessment approach is most appropriate for online CEA courses?**

A) Only proctored closed-book exams
B) Varied assessments including discussions, projects, and authentic tasks
C) No assessment possible online
D) Self-grading only

---

## Answer Key

**1. B** - Combining face-to-face and online learning activities

*Explanation:* Hybrid/blended learning intentionally integrates online and face-to-face instruction to optimize learning.

**2. B** - Asynchronous discussion forums with structured prompts

*Explanation:* Well-designed discussion forums with clear prompts, expectations, and facilitation build learning communities effectively.

**3. B** - Clear structure with objectives, content, activities, and assessments

*Explanation:* Effective online modules have transparent structure helping learners navigate and understand expectations.

**4. B** - 6-12 minutes per segment

*Explanation:* Research shows 6-12 minute videos optimize engagement and learning in online environments.

**5. B** - Designing courses to be accessible and effective for diverse learners

*Explanation:* UDL provides multiple means of representation, action/expression, and engagement to reach all learners.

**6. B** - High-quality video demonstrations from multiple angles

*Explanation:* Multi-angle video demonstrations allow students to observe procedures clearly and review as needed.

**7. B** - Within 24-48 hours on business days

*Explanation:* Timely responses (24-48 hours) maintain engagement and support learning without requiring unrealistic 24/7 availability.

**8. B** - Online theory preparation with scheduled in-person laboratory sessions

*Explanation:* Hybrid approaches use online delivery for theory and asynchronous content, reserving face-to-face time for hands-on skill development.

**9. B** - Extent to which learners can construct and confirm meaning through reflection and discourse

*Explanation:* Cognitive presence (from Community of Inquiry framework) involves critical thinking and knowledge construction through collaborative inquiry.

**10. B** - Varied assessments including discussions, projects, and authentic tasks

*Explanation:* Online courses should use diverse assessment methods appropriate to learning objectives and online environment.
