## Questions

**1. What is the difference between formative and summative assessment?**

A) Formative is formal; summative is informal
B) Formative provides feedback during learning; summative evaluates achievement at the end
C) Formative is for students; summative is for instructors
D) There is no difference

**2. Which assessment type is most appropriate for evaluating a student's ability to calibrate pH meters?**

A) Multiple choice exam
B) Essay
C) Performance-based assessment with rubric
D) True/false quiz

**3. What is "assessment validity"?**

A) Whether the assessment is legal
B) Whether the assessment measures what it's intended to measure
C) How difficult the assessment is
D) How long the assessment takes

**4. In a well-designed rubric for CEA skills, criteria should be:**

A) Vague and open to interpretation
B) Clear, specific, and aligned with learning objectives
C) Based only on effort
D) Rubrics are not useful

**5. What is the primary purpose of formative assessment?**

A) To assign final grades
B) To provide feedback and guide ongoing learning
C) To compare students
D) To satisfy accreditation requirements

**6. Which question stem is MOST effective for assessing higher-order thinking?**

A) "List the..."
B) "Define..."
C) "Analyze why... and predict the consequences if..."
D) "Recall when..."

**7. What is "inter-rater reliability" in assessment?**

A) Internet connection speed
B) Consistency of scoring between different evaluators
C) Student satisfaction ratings
D) Assessment difficulty level

**8. When designing a performance checklist for CEA procedures, what should be included?**

A) Only final outcomes
B) Critical steps, safety procedures, and quality standards
C) Student names only
D) Checklists are not appropriate

**9. What is the recommended frequency of formative assessment in a CEA course?**

A) Once at the end of semester
B) Never conduct formative assessment
C) Regularly throughout the course to monitor and support learning
D) Only during the first week

**10. Which assessment approach provides the MOST comprehensive picture of student competency?**

A) Single multiple-choice exam
B) Multiple assessment methods including knowledge tests, skills demonstrations, and projects
C) Attendance points only
D) Self-assessment only

---

## Answer Key

**1. B** - Formative provides feedback during learning; summative evaluates achievement at the end

*Explanation:* Formative assessment supports ongoing learning through feedback; summative assessment measures final achievement of objectives.

**2. C** - Performance-based assessment with rubric

*Explanation:* Psychomotor skills like equipment calibration require direct observation and evaluation of performance, best assessed with performance rubrics.

**3. B** - Whether the assessment measures what it's intended to measure

*Explanation:* Validity refers to the extent an assessment accurately measures the intended learning outcomes.

**4. B** - Clear, specific, and aligned with learning objectives

*Explanation:* Effective rubrics have clear criteria and performance levels directly tied to what students are expected to learn.

**5. B** - To provide feedback and guide ongoing learning

*Explanation:* Formative assessment's primary purpose is improving learning through timely feedback, not grading.

**6. C** - "Analyze why... and predict the consequences if..."

*Explanation:* Higher-order questions require analysis, synthesis, or evaluation rather than simple recall or recognition.

**7. B** - Consistency of scoring between different evaluators

*Explanation:* Inter-rater reliability measures whether different evaluators would score the same work similarly, indicating assessment quality.

**8. B** - Critical steps, safety procedures, and quality standards

*Explanation:* Effective procedural checklists include all essential elements: key steps, safety requirements, and quality criteria.

**9. C** - Regularly throughout the course to monitor and support learning

*Explanation:* Frequent formative assessment throughout the course enables timely intervention and learning support.

**10. B** - Multiple assessment methods including knowledge tests, skills demonstrations, and projects

*Explanation:* Comprehensive competency assessment requires multiple methods to evaluate different types of learning outcomes.
