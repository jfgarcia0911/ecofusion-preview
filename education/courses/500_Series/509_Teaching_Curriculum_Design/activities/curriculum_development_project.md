# Activity 1: Curriculum Development Project

## CEA-509 Major Assignment

**Type:** Comprehensive Design Project
**Duration:** 8-10 weeks (completed in stages)
**Points:** 25% of course grade
**Format:** Individual work with peer review

---

## Project Overview

Design a complete curriculum for a CEA educational program. This capstone project integrates concepts from all course modules and demonstrates your ability to create industry-aligned, learner-centered educational programs.

---

## Project Options

**Choose ONE:**

**Option A: Certificate Program (12-18 credits)**
- Non-credit or credit-bearing
- 3-6 month duration
- Professional development focus

**Option B: Workshop Series (15-30 hours)**
- 3-5 connected workshops
- Specific skill development
- For working professionals

**Option C: Academic Course (3-4 credits)**
- University or community college level
- One semester
- Part of larger program

**Option D: Certification Preparation Program**
- Prepares for industry certification
- Competency-based
- Flexible pacing

---

## Required Components

### Part 1: Needs Assessment & Justification (Due Week 4)
**Length:** 3-4 pages

**Include:**

**1. Target Audience Analysis**
- Demographics (age, education, experience)
- Current knowledge/skills
- Learning preferences
- Motivations and goals
- Barriers to learning

**2. Industry Needs Evidence**
- Labor market data
- Employer surveys/interviews
- Job posting analysis
- Skills gap identification
- Industry trends

**3. Competitive Analysis**
- Existing similar programs
- Geographic service area
- Unique value proposition
- Market positioning

**4. Justification**
- Why is this program needed?
- Who will it serve?
- What gap does it fill?
- Expected demand/enrollment

**Deliverable:** Written report with data/evidence

**Grading (25 points):**
- Thoroughness of analysis (10 pts)
- Quality of evidence (8 pts)
- Clear justification (5 pts)
- Professional presentation (2 pts)

---

### Part 2: Program Design (Due Week 8)
**Length:** 8-10 pages

**Include:**

**1. Program Overview**
- Title and description
- Target audience
- Duration and format
- Delivery modality
- Prerequisites

**2. Program-Level Learning Outcomes (5-8)**
- What graduates will be able to do
- Aligned with industry needs
- Measurable and achievable
- Use Bloom's Taxonomy

**Example:**
"Design and implement commercial-scale hydroponic production systems that achieve target yields while optimizing resource efficiency"

**3. Competency Framework**
- Domains/categories
- Specific competencies within each
- Performance standards
- Progression from novice to proficient

**4. Curriculum Map**
- Course/module sequence
- Topics covered in each
- How competencies are addressed
- Prerequisites and dependencies
- Credit hours/contact time

**Example Format:**

| Course | Credits | Topics | Competencies Addressed | Prerequisites |
|--------|---------|--------|----------------------|---------------|
| CEA 101 | 3 | Intro to CEA | 1.1, 1.2, 2.1 | None |
| CEA 102 | 4 | Hydroponic Systems | 2.2, 2.3, 3.1 | CEA 101 |

**5. Course Descriptions**
- Brief description (2-3 sentences) for each course/module
- Key topics
- Learning emphasis

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive program design document

**Grading (30 points):**
- Clear, measurable outcomes (8 pts)
- Comprehensive competency framework (8 pts)
- Logical curriculum structure (8 pts)
- Professional course descriptions (4 pts)
- Presentation quality (2 pts)

---

### Part 3: Detailed Course Plan (Due Week 10)
**Length:** 10-12 pages

**Select ONE course from your curriculum and develop it fully**

**Include:**

**1. Course Syllabus**
- Course description and objectives
- Weekly schedule/topics
- Assessment plan and grading
- Required materials
- Policies (attendance, late work, etc.)

**2. Backward Design Application**

**Stage 1: Desired Results**
- Course-level outcomes (5-8)
- Enduring understandings
- Essential questions
- Key knowledge and skills

**Stage 2: Evidence of Learning**
- Formative assessments (list and describe)
- Summative assessments (detailed)
- Rubrics for major assignments
- Alignment matrix (outcomes × assessments)

**Stage 3: Learning Plan**
- Weekly topics and activities
- Instructional methods used
- Hands-on components
- Online/in-person balance (if hybrid)
- Resources needed

**3. Sample Lesson Plan**
- Choose one important topic
- Develop complete lesson plan (see template)
- Include all materials

**4. Assessment Package**
- Sample quiz (10 questions with key)
- Sample exam (25 questions with key)
- Major assignment with rubric
- Performance assessment with criteria

**Deliverable:** Complete course package

**Grading (30 points):**
- Comprehensive syllabus (5 pts)
- Backward design quality (8 pts)
- Assessment alignment (7 pts)
- Sample lesson completeness (6 pts)
- Professional quality (4 pts)

---

### Part 4: Implementation & Evaluation Plan (Due Week 12)
**Length:** 5-7 pages

**Include:**

**1. Resource Requirements**
- Faculty/instructor qualifications
- Facilities needed (classrooms, labs, greenhouse)
- Equipment list and costs
- Technology requirements
- Materials and supplies
- Library/learning resources

**2. Industry Partnerships**
- Advisory committee plan
- Internship/practicum opportunities
- Guest speaker possibilities
- Equipment donations potential
- Job placement connections

**3. Marketing and Recruitment**
- Target audience recruitment strategies
- Marketing messages and channels
- Value proposition
- Competitive positioning
- Enrollment projections

**4. Financial Sustainability**
- Revenue sources and estimates
- Cost projections (fixed and variable)
- Break-even analysis
- 3-year pro forma budget

**5. Evaluation Plan**
- Kirkpatrick's 4 Levels applied
- Specific metrics for each level
- Data collection methods
- Timeline for evaluation
- How data will drive improvement

**6. Implementation Timeline**
- Development phases
- Approval processes
- Launch preparation
- Rollout schedule

**Deliverable:** Implementation and evaluation plan

**Grading (15 points):**
- Realistic resource planning (4 pts)
- Partnership strategy (3 pts)
- Marketing approach (2 pts)
- Financial projections (3 pts)
- Comprehensive evaluation plan (3 pts)

---

## Final Submission Requirements

### Complete Package (Due Week 12)
Submit all four parts together as one comprehensive document (25-35 pages total)

**Include:**
- Title page
- Table of contents
- Executive summary (1 page)
- All four parts (may be revised based on feedback)
- References/bibliography
- Appendices (optional: surveys, additional materials)

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Start early** - This is a major project requiring substantial time
2. **Choose wisely** - Select program type you're passionate about
3. **Be realistic** - Design what could actually be implemented
4. **Seek feedback** - Use peer review and instructor office hours
5. **Iterate** - Revise based on feedback
6. **Think industry** - Always consider employer/industry needs
7. **Document sources** - Keep track of data and references
8. **Professional quality** - This could be part of your portfolio
