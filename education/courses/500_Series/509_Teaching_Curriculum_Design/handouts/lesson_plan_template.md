## Example: Climate Control Lesson (Abbreviated)

**Title:** Introduction to Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD)
**Duration:** 75 minutes
**Audience:** Intro to CEA

**Objectives:**
1. Explain relationship between temp, humidity, and VPD (Understand)
2. Interpret VPD charts (Apply)
3. Adjust controls to achieve target VPD (Apply)

**Materials:** PPT, VPD charts, practice scenarios, quiz

**Sequence:**
- Intro (10 min): Sweating analogy, why VPD matters
- Activity 1 (15 min): Lecture on VPD concept with visuals
- Activity 2 (10 min): Practice reading charts in pairs
- Activity 3 (15 min): Optimal ranges for growth stages
- Practice (15 min): Adjustment scenarios
- Assessment (10 min): Quiz and exit ticket
- Closure (5 min): Summary and connection to next lesson
