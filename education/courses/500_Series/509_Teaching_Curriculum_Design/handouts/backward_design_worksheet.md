## Quick Tips for Using This Worksheet

1. **Don't skip Stage 1** - Clarity here drives everything else
2. **Design assessment (Stage 2) BEFORE instruction (Stage 3)** - This is the "backward" part!
3. **Make it messy first** - Brainstorm, then refine
4. **Get feedback** - Share with colleague or advisor
5. **Iterate** - Improve based on pilot testing
6. **Keep student-focused** - Always ask "How does this serve learning?"

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Starting with activities instead of outcomes
- Vague or unmeasurable enduring understandings
- Assessments that don't match objectives
- Jumping to instruction without clear assessment plan
- Forgetting about differentiation
- No reflection/revision built in
