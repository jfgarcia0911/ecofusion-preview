## 3. Communication Templates

### 3.1 Initial Outreach Email

**Subject:** [Personalized reference to their company/priority]

Hi [First Name],

I came across [recent news/achievement about their company] and was impressed by [specific detail].

At [Your Company], we've been working with [similar companies/industry] to [achieve specific outcome]. For example, we recently helped [Company X] [achieve Y result].

I'm reaching out because I see potential for [Your Company] and [Their Company] to [specific opportunity or value proposition].

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute call to explore whether there's a fit? I'm happy to work around your schedule.

[If there's a mutual connection: "Our mutual connection [Name] suggested I reach out."]

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Contact Information]

---

### 3.2 Follow-Up Email (After No Response)

**Subject:** RE: [Previous Subject] - Quick Question

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to follow up on my email from [date]. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief.

I'm curious if [specific challenge or opportunity relevant to them] is a priority for [Their Company]? We've developed an approach that might be relevant.

If timing isn't right now, no problem - I'd be happy to reconnect in [timeframe].

Best,
[Your Name]

P.S. [Add value: link to relevant article, insight, or resource]

## 4. Analysis Frameworks

### 4.1 SWOT Analysis Template

**Internal Factors**

**Strengths:**
- What do we do well?
- What unique capabilities?
- What advantages do we have?

**Weaknesses:**
- What can we improve?
- What resources do we lack?
- What do competitors do better?

**External Factors**

**Opportunities:**
- What market trends favor us?
- What needs are underserved?
- What partnerships are possible?

**Threats:**
- What competitive threats?
- What market changes concern us?
- What regulatory risks?

**Strategic Implications:**
- SO: Use strengths to pursue opportunities
- WO: Overcome weaknesses to pursue opportunities
- ST: Use strengths to mitigate threats
- WT: Minimize weaknesses and avoid threats

### 5.3 Meeting Preparation Checklist

**Before the Meeting:**

**Research:**
- [ ] Company background reviewed
- [ ] Recent news and announcements checked
- [ ] LinkedIn profiles reviewed
- [ ] Financial information gathered (if public)
- [ ] Competitive positioning understood

**Preparation:**
- [ ] Meeting objectives defined
- [ ] Agenda prepared and shared
- [ ] Materials prepared
- [ ] Questions prepared
- [ ] Anticipated objections/questions identified
- [ ] Team roles assigned (if multiple attendees)

**Logistics:**
- [ ] Meeting time confirmed
- [ ] Video link tested (if virtual)
- [ ] Location confirmed (if in-person)
- [ ] Materials uploaded/printed
- [ ] Calendar reminder set

**During the Meeting:**

- [ ] Take notes
- [ ] Listen actively
- [ ] Ask open-ended questions
- [ ] Confirm understanding
- [ ] Clarify next steps
- [ ] Get specific commitments

**After the Meeting:**

- [ ] Send thank-you and summary email (within 24 hours)
- [ ] Update CRM
- [ ] Complete action items
- [ ] Schedule follow-up
- [ ] Debrief with team
