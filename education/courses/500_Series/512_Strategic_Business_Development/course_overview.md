# Course 512: Strategic Business Development

## Master Level - EcoFusion Academy

### Course Description

Strategic Business Development is an advanced master-level course designed for executives and senior leaders responsible for driving growth and expansion in controlled environment agriculture (CEA) organizations. This comprehensive program explores sophisticated business development strategies, market intelligence techniques, partnership structuring, and organizational scaling. Students will master the frameworks, methodologies, and tactical approaches required to identify opportunities, develop strategic relationships, negotiate complex agreements, and build high-performing business development organizations in the competitive CEA landscape.

The course integrates real-world case studies from successful CEA companies, technology providers, and agricultural enterprises, providing actionable insights into market entry strategies, channel development, key account management, and strategic alliances. Participants will gain expertise in international business development, government sales, M&A strategy, and metrics-driven performance management essential for executive-level business development leadership.
