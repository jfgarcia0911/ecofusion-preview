# Quiz 7: Strategic Selling Methodologies

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 7 - Strategic Selling Methodologies
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
In MEDDPICC methodology, what does "Economic Buyer" identify?

A) The cheapest purchasing option
B) The person with authority and budget to make the final purchasing decision
C) Any employee at the customer company
D) Accounting department staff

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
What is "solution selling" focused on?

A) Selling products based on features only
B) Understanding customer problems and positioning offerings as solutions to specific business challenges
C) Lowest price competition
D) Product demonstrations without context

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
In consultative selling, what should sales professionals do first?

A) Present product features immediately
B) Ask questions to deeply understand customer needs, challenges, and goals
C) Discuss pricing
D) Show competitor weaknesses

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
What does "Champion" refer to in strategic selling frameworks?

A) Competition winners
B) Internal advocate at customer organization who supports your solution
C) Company executives
D) Marketing campaigns

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
In value-based selling, how should ROI be presented?

A) Generic industry averages only
B) Quantified specific to customer's situation with their metrics and timeframes
C) No ROI needed
D) Competitor ROI only

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
What is the purpose of "discovery calls" in complex B2B sales?

A) Finding customer office locations
B) Uncovering customer business challenges, decision criteria, and buying process
C) Presenting product features only
D) Negotiating final pricing

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
In MEDDPICC, what does "Decision Criteria" identify?

A) Arbitrary selection factors
B) The formal and informal criteria the customer will use to evaluate and select vendors
C) Internal company policies only
D) Random preferences

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
What characterizes a "multi-threaded" approach in enterprise sales?

A) Selling multiple products
B) Building relationships with multiple stakeholders at different levels in customer organization
C) Using multiple sales reps inefficiently
D) Contacting only one person

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is "value proposition alignment" in strategic selling?

A) Creating generic sales pitches
B) Tailoring messaging to specific stakeholder priorities and what they value most
C) Using the same pitch for everyone
D) Focusing only on product features

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
In complex CEA sales, what is most important for winning deals?

A) Lowest price always wins
B) Understanding customer business outcomes and demonstrating measurable value
C) Longest sales presentation
D) Most product features listed

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Person with authority and budget for decision
2. B - Positioning offerings as solutions to challenges
3. B - Ask questions to understand needs deeply
4. B - Internal advocate supporting your solution
5. B - Quantified specific to customer situation
6. B - Uncovering challenges, criteria, buying process
7. B - Criteria customer uses to evaluate vendors
8. B - Relationships with multiple stakeholders
9. B - Tailoring to specific stakeholder priorities
10. B - Understanding outcomes and demonstrating value
