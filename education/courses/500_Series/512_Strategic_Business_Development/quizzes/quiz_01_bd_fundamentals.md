# Quiz 1: Strategic Business Development Fundamentals

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: Lesson 1
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between business development and sales?

A) Business development focuses on short-term revenue while sales focuses on long-term relationships
B) Business development creates long-term strategic value while sales focuses on quota achievement
C) Business development is B2C while sales is B2B
D) There is no significant difference between them

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which of the following best describes the "Three Horizons of Growth" framework?

A) Past, present, and future analysis
B) Defend/extend core, build emerging, create transformational opportunities
C) Product development, market development, and diversification
D) Research, development, and commercialization

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
In the CEA context, which represents a Horizon 3 (transformational) opportunity?

A) Expanding leafy greens production to meet existing customer demand
B) Entering an adjacent geographic market with proven products
C) Developing pharmaceutical-grade medicinal compounds in controlled environments
D) Adding new retail customers in current markets

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 4
According to the Strategic BD Canvas, which of the following is NOT one of the nine building blocks?

A) Key Partnerships
B) Competitive Advantages
C) Value Propositions
D) Revenue Streams

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is a key characteristic of strategic business development?

A) Focus on quarterly revenue targets
B) Transaction-based customer interactions
C) Long-term value creation through partnerships and markets
D) Individual contributor responsibilities

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 6
In the CEA industry, which trend is driving the evolution toward "Ecosystem Orchestration"?

A) Decreasing technology costs
B) Multi-stakeholder value creation and platform strategies
C) Reduction in retail partnerships
D) Shift to commodity pricing

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
Which component is NOT part of the Strategic BD Value Chain?

A) Market Intelligence
B) Relationship Building
C) Product Manufacturing
D) Value Capture

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 8
What percentage of time horizon typically characterizes strategic business development activities?

A) 0-6 months
B) 6-12 months
C) 1-5 years
D) 5-10 years

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 9
In the Ansoff Growth Matrix, what strategy involves new products in existing markets?

A) Market Penetration
B) Market Development
C) Product Development
D) Diversification

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 10
Which is a key BD opportunity in CEA for creating long-term strategic value?

A) Reducing production costs
B) Technology licensing and strategic partnerships
C) Increasing marketing spend
D) Hiring more salespeople

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Business development creates long-term strategic value while sales focuses on quota achievement
2. B - Defend/extend core, build emerging, create transformational opportunities
3. C - Developing pharmaceutical-grade medicinal compounds in controlled environments
4. B - Competitive Advantages
5. C - Long-term value creation through partnerships and markets
6. B - Multi-stakeholder value creation and platform strategies
7. C - Product Manufacturing
8. C - 1-5 years
9. C - Product Development
10. B - Technology licensing and strategic partnerships
