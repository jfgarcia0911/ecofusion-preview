# Quiz 3: Competitive Strategy and Positioning

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 3 - Competitive Strategy and Positioning
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
In Porter's Five Forces framework, which force examines the power of suppliers in an industry?

A) Threat of new entrants
B) Bargaining power of suppliers
C) Threat of substitute products
D) Rivalry among existing competitors

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
What characterizes a "Blue Ocean" strategy?

A) Competing in existing markets with lower prices
B) Creating uncontested market space making competition irrelevant
C) Only selling ocean-based products
D) Reducing product quality to cut costs

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
For a CEA company, which would be an example of Blue Ocean strategy?

A) Growing the same leafy greens as competitors at lower cost
B) Developing pharmaceutical-grade plant compounds unavailable from traditional agriculture
C) Matching competitor pricing in existing markets
D) Opening facilities in locations competitors already serve

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
In value proposition design, what does "jobs to be done" refer to?

A) Employment opportunities at the company
B) The functional, emotional, and social tasks customers are trying to accomplish
C) Internal employee responsibilities
D) Manufacturing processes

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
Which competitive positioning strategy focuses on serving a narrow market segment exceptionally well?

A) Cost leadership
B) Broad differentiation
C) Focus/niche strategy
D) Stuck in the middle

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 6
What is "strategic group analysis" used for?

A) Team building exercises
B) Identifying clusters of competitors with similar strategies to understand competitive dynamics
C) Organizing office groups
D) Supply chain management

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
In the Value Proposition Canvas, what are the three components of "customer gains"?

A) Revenue, profit, and market share
B) Required, expected, desired, and unexpected positive outcomes
C) Product features only
D) Price reductions

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
What risk does a "stuck in the middle" competitive position create?

A) Guaranteed market leadership
B) Failing to achieve cost leadership OR differentiation, resulting in poor performance
C) Too much profitability
D) No competition

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
For CEA businesses, which factor typically enables sustainable differentiation?

A) Location alone
B) Proprietary technology, unique growing methods, or superior genetics
C) Lower employee wages
D) Reducing product quality

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
What is the purpose of a competitive positioning map?

A) Physical maps of competitor locations
B) Visualizing where companies compete on key dimensions to identify market gaps
C) Employee organizational charts
D) Supply chain routing

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Bargaining power of suppliers
2. B - Creating uncontested market space
3. B - Pharmaceutical-grade plant compounds
4. B - Tasks customers are trying to accomplish
5. C - Focus/niche strategy
6. B - Identifying competitor clusters and strategies
7. B - Required, expected, desired, unexpected outcomes
8. B - Failing to achieve clear competitive advantage
9. B - Proprietary technology, methods, or genetics
10. B - Visualizing competitive positions and market gaps
