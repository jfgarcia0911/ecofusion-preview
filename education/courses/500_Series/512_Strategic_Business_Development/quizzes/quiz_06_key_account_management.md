# Quiz 6: Key Account Management

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 6 - Key Account Management
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
What distinguishes a "key account" from a regular customer?

A) Any customer who makes a purchase
B) Strategic importance, revenue potential, and requiring dedicated relationship management
C) Customers who complain the most
D) Alphabetically first customers

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
In strategic account planning, what does "white space analysis" identify?

A) Blank pages in contracts
B) Unmet customer needs and expansion opportunities within existing accounts
C) Empty office space
D) Unused marketing materials

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is the purpose of an "account team" structure in key account management?

A) To increase overhead costs
B) To coordinate cross-functional resources delivering comprehensive solutions to major accounts
C) To replace individual salespeople entirely
D) To eliminate customer contact

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
Which relationship level represents the strongest strategic partnership in key account management?

A) Transactional buyer-seller
B) Preferred supplier
C) Strategic partner with mutual value creation and integration
D) Occasional purchaser

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 5
What does "executive sponsorship" provide in key account management?

A) Financial donations
B) Senior-level relationships, strategic alignment, and escalation path for major issues
C) Catering for meetings
D) Social media followers

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
In account planning, what is a "relationship map"?

A) Geographic maps of customer locations
B) Visual representation of stakeholders, relationships, and influence within customer organization
C) Floor plans of customer offices
D) Marketing campaign timelines

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What metric best measures account health and retention risk?

A) Number of emails sent
B) Net Promoter Score (NPS), usage patterns, engagement levels, and renewal likelihood
C) Distance to customer office
D) Number of contracts signed historically

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
For CEA companies, what is a key element of customer success programs?

A) Ignoring customers after sale
B) Proactive support ensuring customers achieve desired outcomes and ROI from products
C) Selling only, never supporting
D) Limiting communication with customers

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is "share of wallet" in key account management?

A) Percentage of customer's budget you own
B) Percentage of customer's total spending in your category that goes to your company
C) Physical wallet inventory
D) Stock price performance

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
Which strategy best prevents key account churn?

A) Reactive problem solving only
B) Proactive value delivery, regular business reviews, and strategic planning together
C) Reducing product quality
D) Eliminating customer support

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Strategic importance, revenue potential, dedicated management
2. B - Unmet needs and expansion opportunities
3. B - Coordinate cross-functional resources for solutions
4. C - Strategic partner with mutual value creation
5. B - Senior relationships, alignment, escalation path
6. B - Visual of stakeholders and influence
7. B - NPS, usage, engagement, renewal likelihood
8. B - Proactive support ensuring customer outcomes/ROI
9. B - Percentage of category spending with your company
10. B - Proactive value, business reviews, joint planning
