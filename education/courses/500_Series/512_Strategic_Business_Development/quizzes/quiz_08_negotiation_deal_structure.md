# Quiz 8: Contract Negotiation and Deal Structuring

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 8 - Contract Negotiation and Deal Structuring
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
What is the fundamental principle of "win-win" negotiation?

A) One party wins, one loses
B) Creating mutual value where both parties achieve important objectives
C) Avoiding negotiation entirely
D) Compromising on everything

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
What is BATNA in negotiation theory?

A) A negotiation certification
B) Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement - your fallback option
C) A contract template
D) Buyer's annual terms and agreements

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
In deal structuring, what does "risk allocation" address?

A) Insurance policies only
B) How risks are distributed between parties through contract terms and responsibilities
C) Stock market investments
D) Employee assignments

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
Which pricing model aligns vendor success with customer outcomes?

A) Fixed price regardless of results
B) Performance-based or success-fee pricing tied to customer metrics
C) Unlimited free services
D) Random pricing

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is the purpose of "payment terms" in contract negotiation?

A) Avoiding payment entirely
B) Defining when and how payments occur, balancing cash flow needs of both parties
C) Maximizing delays
D) Creating confusion

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
In CEA technology licensing deals, what does "exclusivity" grant?

A) Nothing of value
B) Sole rights to use technology in defined territory, market, or application
C) Unlimited free usage
D) Automatic ownership transfer

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What is a "term sheet" in deal negotiations?

A) Final binding contract
B) Non-binding outline of key deal terms before formal contract drafting
C) Employee termination document
D) Marketing materials

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
Which negotiation tactic involves making multiple issues negotiable to create trade-offs?

A) Single-issue positional bargaining
B) Integrative or multi-issue negotiation enabling creative value creation
C) Avoiding all discussions
D) Ultimatums only

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What does "earn-out" structure accomplish in M&A or partnership deals?

A) Guaranteed immediate full payment
B) Deferred payments contingent on future performance, bridging valuation gaps and aligning incentives
C) Eliminating all future payments
D) Fixed pricing only

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
What is the most important factor in successful deal closing?

A) Aggressive pressure tactics
B) Clear mutual understanding of value, terms, and addressing all stakeholder concerns
C) Rushing to signature
D) Ignoring customer questions

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Creating mutual value for both parties
2. B - Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement
3. B - Distribution of risks through contract terms
4. B - Performance-based pricing tied to metrics
5. B - Defining payment timing and methods
6. B - Sole rights in defined territory/market/application
7. B - Non-binding outline of key terms
8. B - Integrative multi-issue negotiation
9. B - Deferred contingent payments bridging gaps
10. B - Clear mutual understanding and concern resolution
