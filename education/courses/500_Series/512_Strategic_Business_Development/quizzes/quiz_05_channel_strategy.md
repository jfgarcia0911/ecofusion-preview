# Quiz 5: Channel Strategy and Development

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 5 - Channel Strategy and Development
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
What is the primary advantage of direct sales channels for CEA products?

A) Lower cost per sale in all cases
B) Greater control over customer experience, margins, and data
C) No need for sales team
D) Unlimited scalability without investment

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which indirect channel strategy involves partners reselling products under their own brand?

A) Referral partnership
B) White-label or private label partnership
C) Direct sales
D) Digital advertising

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is "channel conflict" and when does it occur?

A) Physical fights between partners
B) Competition or overlap between different channel partners causing friction
C) Product quality issues
D) Pricing agreements

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
In channel economics, what does "channel margin" represent?

A) Physical space between products
B) The profit percentage or absolute profit retained by channel partners
C) Shipping costs
D) Marketing expenses

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is a key component of an effective channel partner enablement program?

A) Ignoring partner needs
B) Training, marketing materials, technical support, and sales tools
C) Reducing partner commissions
D) Limiting partner communication

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
For a CEA company selling to grocery retailers, which channel strategy is most appropriate?

A) E-commerce only
B) Direct to consumer home delivery exclusively
C) Wholesale/distribution channel through food distributors
D) No channel strategy needed

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 7
What does "channel coverage" analysis help determine?

A) Geographic roof coverage of buildings
B) Whether current channels adequately reach target customer segments and markets
C) Insurance policies needed
D) Employee coverage for shifts

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
In multi-channel strategies, what is "channel orchestration"?

A) Musical performances in stores
B) Coordinating multiple channels to provide seamless customer experience
C) Using only one channel exclusively
D) Eliminating all channels

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What incentive structure is most effective for motivating channel partners?

A) No incentives needed
B) Performance-based incentives aligned with both parties' objectives (volume, new customers, etc.)
C) One-time payment only
D) Delayed payments without clear terms

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
Which metric is most important for evaluating channel partner performance?

A) Number of lunch meetings
B) Revenue generated, customer acquisition, and margin contribution relative to investment
C) Office location aesthetics
D) Social media followers

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Control over experience, margins, and data
2. B - White-label or private label partnership
3. B - Competition/overlap between channels causing friction
4. B - Profit percentage retained by partners
5. B - Training, materials, support, and tools
6. C - Wholesale/distribution through food distributors
7. B - Whether channels reach target segments adequately
8. B - Coordinating channels for seamless experience
9. B - Performance-based aligned incentives
10. B - Revenue, acquisition, margin vs. investment
