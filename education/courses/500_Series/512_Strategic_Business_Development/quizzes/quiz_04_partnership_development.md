# Quiz 4: Strategic Partnership Development

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

**Module**: 4 - Strategic Partnership Development
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between a strategic partnership and a transactional vendor relationship?

A) Strategic partnerships focus on short-term cost savings only
B) Strategic partnerships create mutual long-term value beyond simple transactions
C) There is no difference
D) Strategic partnerships always cost more

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
In partnership qualification, what does "strategic fit" assess?

A) Physical compatibility of equipment
B) Alignment of goals, values, capabilities, and long-term vision
C) Office location proximity
D) Employee headcount

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
Which partnership structure involves two or more parties creating a new legal entity together?

A) Reseller agreement
B) Joint venture
C) Licensing agreement
D) Customer relationship

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
What is "value chain mapping" used for in partnership development?

A) Calculating product costs
B) Identifying where partners can contribute complementary capabilities across the value chain
C) Measuring employee performance
D) Tracking inventory

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
In partnership governance, what is the role of a "steering committee"?

A) Driving delivery trucks
B) Providing strategic oversight, resolving conflicts, and ensuring partnership objectives are met
C) Managing daily operations exclusively
D) Handling customer service calls

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
For a CEA company, which type of partnership would provide access to retail distribution channels?

A) Technology vendor partnership
B) Channel/distribution partnership with grocery retailers
C) Research institution collaboration
D) Financial services provider

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What is "co-opetition"?

A) Refusing to work with anyone
B) Cooperating with competitors in areas of mutual benefit while competing in others
C) Complete merger with competitors
D) Price-fixing arrangements

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
Which metric is most important for measuring partnership success?

A) Number of meetings held
B) Value created for both parties relative to partnership objectives and KPIs
C) Emails exchanged
D) Partnership agreement page count

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of a partnership "MOU" (Memorandum of Understanding)?

A) Final binding legal contract
B) Non-binding agreement outlining key terms and intentions before formal contract
C) Marketing brochure
D) Employee handbook

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
In ecosystem partnerships, what does "orchestration" mean?

A) Musical performance planning
B) Coordinating multiple partners to create integrated value propositions and customer solutions
C) Managing a single vendor relationship
D) Organizing company events

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Create mutual long-term value beyond transactions
2. B - Alignment of goals, values, capabilities, and vision
3. B - Joint venture
4. B - Identifying complementary capabilities across value chain
5. B - Strategic oversight and conflict resolution
6. B - Channel/distribution partnership with retailers
7. B - Cooperating with competitors in some areas
8. B - Value created relative to objectives and KPIs
9. B - Non-binding agreement outlining intentions
10. B - Coordinating multiple partners for integrated solutions
