# Activity: Market Entry Strategy Development

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

---

## Project Overview

**Objective**: Develop a comprehensive market entry strategy for a CEA company expanding to a new market

**Duration**: 3 weeks

**Deliverable**: 20-25 page strategic plan with financial model and executive presentation

**Weight**: 20% of final grade

---

## Assignment Overview

You will develop a detailed market entry strategy for GreenTech Farms (see scenario below) entering either:
1. A new geographic market (domestic or international)
2. A new customer segment (e.g., food service, food processing, direct-to-consumer)
3. A new product category (e.g., functional foods, pharmaceutical crops)

Your plan should demonstrate mastery of market analysis, competitive strategy, partnership development, financial planning, and execution planning.

---

## Scenario: GreenTech Farms

**Company Profile:**
- Founded: 2018
- Current operations: 2 vertical farms in Northeast US (Pennsylvania)
- Total growing space: 200,000 sq ft
- Current production: 75,000 lbs/week
- Products: Leafy greens (8 varieties), herbs (4 varieties), microgreens
- Revenue: $15M annually (FY 2024)
- EBITDA: $2M (13% margin)
- Employees: 60
- Funding: Series B complete ($30M raised), $10M available for growth
- Technology: Proprietary growing system with moderate automation

**Current Market Position:**
- Supply to 25 grocery stores (premium and mainstream)
- 15 restaurant/food service accounts
- Strong quality reputation
- Limited brand recognition beyond region
- Technology competitive but not leading-edge

**Strategic Imperative:**
Board has approved expansion initiative with goal of reaching $50M revenue by 2027. You have been hired as VP of Business Development to lead market entry strategy and execution.

## Tips for Success

### Research
- Use real market data and sources
- Conduct primary research if possible (interviews)
- Cite sources appropriately
- Go beyond readily available information
- Validate assumptions with multiple sources

### Analysis
- Use frameworks from the course
- Show your analytical work
- Consider multiple perspectives
- Challenge your own assumptions
- Quantify wherever possible

### Strategy
- Be specific and actionable
- Make clear choices and trade-offs
- Justify recommendations with data
- Address obvious counter-arguments
- Think long-term, plan short-term

### Financial Modeling
- Clearly document assumptions
- Make assumptions realistic and defensible
- Show your work (don't hide in formulas)
- Include scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Present key insights, not just numbers

### Professional Quality
- Executive-ready presentation quality
- Clear, concise writing
- Compelling visual design
- Proofread thoroughly
- Tell a coherent story
