# Activity: Strategic Partnership Proposal Project

## Course 512: Strategic Business Development

---

## Project Overview

**Objective**: Develop a comprehensive strategic partnership proposal for a CEA organization

**Duration**: 3 weeks

**Deliverable**: 15-20 page partnership proposal with executive presentation

**Weight**: 15% of final grade

---

## Scenario

You are the VP of Business Development for **GreenTech Farms**, a mid-size vertical farming company with:
- 3 facilities in the Northeast US
- $12M annual revenue
- Strong leafy greens production (lettuce, kale, arugula)
- Growing herbs and microgreens capabilities
- Proprietary growing technology with some automation
- 45 employees
- Funded through Series B ($25M raised)

**Strategic Challenge:**
GreenTech needs to accelerate growth to reach profitability and position for Series C funding. The board has directed you to develop a strategic partnership that could:
- Accelerate market access
- Provide additional resources or capabilities
- Create competitive differentiation
- Support path to $50M revenue in 3 years

---

## Assignment Parts

### Part 1: Partner Identification and Selection (Week 1)

**Deliverable**: 3-5 page analysis

#### 1.1 Partnership Strategy
- Define strategic objectives for partnership
- Identify types of partnerships to consider
- Establish evaluation criteria

#### 1.2 Potential Partner Landscape
Research and identify 5 potential partners across categories:
- Retail/distribution
- Technology
- Financial/strategic investor
- Real estate/facilities
- Research/innovation

For each potential partner, provide:
- Company overview
- Strategic rationale (why them?)
- Preliminary assessment of fit

#### 1.3 Partner Selection
Complete detailed qualification for each:

| Partner | Strategic Value | Capability Match | Cultural Fit | Risk Level | Total Score |
|---------|----------------|------------------|--------------|------------|-------------|
| Partner A | (1-10) | (1-10) | (1-10) | (1-10) | |

Select top partner and justify selection.

---

### Part 2: Partnership Value Proposition (Week 2)

**Deliverable**: 5-7 page value proposition and structure

#### 2.1 Value Proposition for Partner
**Value Proposition Canvas:**
- Partner's jobs-to-be-done
- Partner's pains (problems, obstacles, risks)
- Partner's gains (required, expected, desired outcomes)
- Our products/services
- How we relieve pains
- How we create gains

**Value Proposition Statement:**
"For [Partner] who [need], our partnership offers [benefit] that [point of difference]."

#### 2.2 Mutual Value Creation
**For Partner:**
- Strategic value
- Financial benefits
- Operational benefits
- Competitive advantages

**For GreenTech:**
- Market access
- Capabilities gained
- Resources accessed
- Strategic positioning

**Synergies:**
- 1+1 = 3 opportunities
- Unique combined capabilities
- Competitive moats created

#### 2.3 Partnership Structure
**Recommend structure** (select and justify):
- Simple supply agreement
- Strategic alliance
- Joint venture
- Licensing agreement
- Equity investment

**Key terms to include:**
- Scope of partnership
- Economic model (revenue sharing, pricing, investment)
- Governance structure
- Exclusivity provisions
- Term and renewal

#### 3.1 Implementation Roadmap

**Phase 2: Launch (Months 4-9)**
- Key activities and milestones

## Tips for Success

### Research
- Use real companies and real data where possible
- Leverage course materials and frameworks
- Conduct primary research if possible (interviews)
- Cite sources appropriately

### Analysis
- Go beyond surface-level analysis
- Use frameworks from the course
- Show your work and reasoning
- Consider multiple perspectives

### Recommendations
- Be definitive in your recommendations
- Support with data and logic
- Address counterarguments
- Show confidence

### Professional Quality
- Proofread carefully
- Use professional formatting
- Create compelling visuals
- Executive-ready quality

### Creative Thinking
- Don't just follow templates
- Bring original insights
- Consider non-obvious partners
- Think strategically
