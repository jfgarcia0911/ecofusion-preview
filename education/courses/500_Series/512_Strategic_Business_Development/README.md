# Course 512: Strategic Business Development

## Master Level - EcoFusion Academy

### Course Overview

Strategic Business Development is an advanced master-level course designed for executives and senior leaders responsible for driving growth and expansion in controlled environment agriculture (CEA) organizations. This comprehensive 14-week program covers sophisticated business development strategies, market intelligence, partnership development, and organizational scaling essential for executive-level BD leadership.

---

## Course Structure

### Duration
- **14 weeks** total
- **8-12 hours/week** commitment
- **14 interactive workshops**
- **70-90 hours** self-paced learning
- **30-40 hours** capstone project

### Prerequisites
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent
- Minimum 5 years BD/sales/strategic planning experience
- Understanding of CEA industry fundamentals
- Completion of Course 508 or equivalent
- CRM system experience

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need CEA industry experience to take this course?**
A: While helpful, specific CEA experience is not required. The course teaches business development in a CEA context, and frameworks apply across industries.

**Q: Can I complete this course while working full-time?**
A: Yes, the course is designed for working professionals with flexible self-paced components and evening/weekend live sessions.

**Q: What if I miss a live workshop?**
A: All sessions are recorded and available within 24 hours. However, active participation is strongly encouraged.

**Q: Are there team projects?**
A: While major projects are individual, optional peer collaboration and study groups are encouraged.

**Q: How quickly can I complete the course?**
A: The standard pace is 14 weeks. Accelerated options may be available for experienced professionals.

**Q: What's the time commitment?**
A: Plan for 8-12 hours per week including live sessions, self-paced learning, and project work.

**Q: Is financial aid available?**
A: Merit-based scholarships and corporate sponsorship programs are available. Contact admissions for details.
