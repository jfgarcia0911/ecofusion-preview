## Questions

### Question 1
In Integrated Pest Management (IPM), what is the recommended action hierarchy?

A) Chemical control first, then prevention
B) Prevention → Monitoring → Cultural/Physical → Biological → Chemical (as last resort)
C) Only use biological controls
D) No pest management needed in CEA

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the Economic Injury Level (EIL) in IPM?

A) Cost of pest control products
B) Pest population density at which control costs equal crop damage prevention value
C) Insurance coverage limits
D) Worker injury rates

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
Which biological control strategy is most effective for two-spotted spider mites in CEA?

A) Ladybugs only
B) Phytoseiulus persimilis (predatory mites)
C) Bacillus thuringiensis
D) Nematodes

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is the primary challenge of using chemical pesticides in CEA leafy green production?

A) Cost only
B) Short Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) and risk to beneficial organisms
C) They are always completely safe
D) No challenges exist

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
How often should Master Growers conduct pest scouting in active production areas?

A) Once per year
B) Monthly
C) 2-3 times per week minimum, daily in high-risk periods
D) Scouting is unnecessary

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
What is the "banker plant" system in biological control?

A) Plants used for financial transactions
B) Non-crop plants that maintain beneficial insect populations
C) Most expensive plants
D) Plants in bank vaults

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
Which environmental condition MOST promotes powdery mildew development?

A) Low humidity (< 40% RH)
B) High humidity with poor air circulation and moderate temperatures
C) Very high temperatures (> 35°C)
D) Freezing temperatures

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What is resistance management in pest control?

A) Building physical barriers only
B) Rotating pesticide modes of action to prevent pest adaptation
C) Training pests to resist disease
D) Resistance management is not important

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
In root zone disease management for hydroponics, what is the most effective preventive strategy?

A) Ignore sanitation
B) Maintain optimal dissolved oxygen, temperature, and system sanitation
C) Use maximum fertilizer concentration
D) Add soil to the system

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What is the primary indicator that a pest management strategy needs adjustment?

A) Pest populations are decreasing
B) Increasing pest pressure despite interventions; damage approaching economic threshold
C) No pests ever observed
D) Beneficial insects are establishing

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Prevention → Monitoring → Cultural/Physical → Biological → Chemical (as last resort)
2. B - Pest population density at which control costs equal crop damage prevention value
3. B - Phytoseiulus persimilis (predatory mites)
4. B - Short Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) and risk to beneficial organisms
5. C - 2-3 times per week minimum, daily in high-risk periods
6. B - Non-crop plants that maintain beneficial insect populations
7. B - High humidity with poor air circulation and moderate temperatures
8. B - Rotating pesticide modes of action to prevent pest adaptation
9. B - Maintain optimal dissolved oxygen, temperature, and system sanitation
10. B - Increasing pest pressure despite interventions; damage approaching economic threshold
