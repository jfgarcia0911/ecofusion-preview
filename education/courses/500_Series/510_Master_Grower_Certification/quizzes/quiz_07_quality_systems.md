# Quiz 7: Documentation, Compliance & Quality Systems

## Instructions
- 10 multiple-choice questions
- Minimum passing score: 85% (9/10 correct)
- Time limit: 20 minutes
- Select the BEST answer for each question

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) certification?

A) Marketing only
B) Food safety assurance and regulatory compliance
C) Increasing yields
D) GAP certification is optional and provides no value

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which record-keeping system is most critical for food safety compliance?

A) Employee time cards only
B) Traceability system linking inputs to harvested products
C) Accounting records only
D) No record-keeping needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and why is it important?

A) Unnecessary paperwork
B) Documented step-by-step instructions ensuring consistency and quality
C) Government regulation only
D) Optional guideline

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
How long should production records typically be retained for food safety compliance?

A) 1 week
B) 1 month
C) 2-3 years minimum (or as required by regulations/certifications)
D) Records can be discarded immediately

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What is the "lot number" system and why does it matter?

A) Parking lot assignments
B) Unique identifier for production batches enabling traceability and recall capability
C) Lottery system
D) Lot numbers serve no purpose

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
In food safety protocols, what is the primary purpose of environmental monitoring?

A) Weather forecasting
B) Detecting potential contamination sources before they affect products
C) Monitoring is unnecessary
D) Only for outdoor production

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What should Master Growers do in preparation for third-party food safety audits?

A) Hide all documentation
B) Conduct internal audits, verify compliance, train team, organize records
C) Cancel production during audits
D) Audits cannot be prepared for

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
Which water quality parameter is MOST critical to monitor for food safety?

A) Water color
B) Microbial contamination (E. coli, coliforms)
C) Water temperature only
D) Water quality monitoring is unnecessary

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of Corrective Action/Preventive Action (CAPA) systems?

A) Punishment system for employees
B) Systematic process to address root causes and prevent recurrence of issues
C) Documentation for appearance only
D) CAPA is not relevant to CEA

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
How should Master Growers ensure sanitation protocols are followed consistently?

A) Assume compliance without verification
B) Written protocols, training, monitoring, and regular verification
C) Sanitation is not important
D) Only sanitize during audits

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Food safety assurance and regulatory compliance
2. B - Traceability system linking inputs to harvested products
3. B - Documented step-by-step instructions ensuring consistency and quality
4. C - 2-3 years minimum (or as required by regulations/certifications)
5. B - Unique identifier for production batches enabling traceability and recall capability
6. B - Detecting potential contamination sources before they affect products
7. B - Conduct internal audits, verify compliance, train team, organize records
8. B - Microbial contamination (E. coli, coliforms)
9. B - Systematic process to address root causes and prevent recurrence of issues
10. B - Written protocols, training, monitoring, and regular verification
