## Questions

### Question 1
What is Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and why is it critical for Master Growers?

A) Air pressure measurement
B) Difference between actual and saturated vapor pressure; drives transpiration and nutrient uptake
C) Water pressure in irrigation lines
D) Deficit in water supply

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the optimal VPD range for most leafy greens in vegetative growth?

A) 0.1-0.3 kPa
B) 0.8-1.2 kPa
C) 2.5-3.0 kPa
D) 4.0+ kPa

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
How does the Daily Light Integral (DLI) affect crop production decisions?

A) DLI is not relevant to CEA
B) DLI (mol/m²/day) determines total light energy available; affects growth rate, quality, and scheduling
C) Only affects ornamental plants
D) Only matters for outdoor production

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
In multi-zone climate control, what is the primary advantage of zoning by growth stage?

A) Easier cleaning
B) Allows optimization of environmental parameters specific to each developmental stage
C) Reduces costs with no benefit
D) Zoning provides no advantages

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What is the relationship between temperature and relative humidity in controlling VPD?

A) They are unrelated
B) Both affect VPD; increasing temperature decreases RH at constant moisture, increasing VPD
C) Only temperature matters for VPD
D) Only humidity matters for VPD

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
For leafy green production, what is the typical optimal nighttime temperature offset?

A) Same as daytime (no DIF)
B) 2-4°C (4-7°F) cooler than daytime
C) 10°C warmer than daytime
D) Temperature doesn't matter at night

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of CO2 enrichment in CEA, and when is it most effective?

A) CO2 has no benefit
B) Enhances photosynthesis; most effective during high-light periods when stomata are open
C) Only needed at night
D) Decreases plant growth

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
How should air circulation be managed in CEA facilities?

A) Maximum airspeed at all times
B) No air movement needed
C) Gentle, uniform air movement (0.5-1.5 m/s) for transpiration, CO2 distribution, and disease prevention
D) Only circulate air once per day

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
What is the primary risk of high VPD (> 1.5 kPa) in leafy green production?

A) Increased disease pressure
B) Excessive transpiration leading to water stress, stomatal closure, and tipburn
C) Too much growth
D) No risks associated with high VPD

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
In precision environmental control, what is a PID controller?

A) Plant identification device
B) Proportional-Integral-Derivative controller that precisely maintains setpoints
C) Pest identification database
D) Personnel identity document

**Correct Answer: B**

---
