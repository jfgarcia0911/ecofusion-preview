## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of succession planting in CEA?

A) To grow different species together
B) To ensure continuous harvest and consistent supply by staggering planting dates
C) To reduce costs
D) Succession planting is not necessary

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
In production planning, what is "turns per year"?

A) Employee turnover rate
B) Number of complete crop cycles possible in a year
C) Equipment rotation schedule
D) Crop rotation patterns

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is the critical path in production scheduling?

A) Most expensive activities
B) Sequence of activities determining minimum project duration
C) Physical pathways in the facility
D) Emergency exit routes

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
How should Master Growers account for crop timing variability in production schedules?

A) Ignore variability
B) Build in buffer time (5-10%) and monitor actual vs. planned duration
C) Only use exact estimates
D) Cancel production when timing varies

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What is capacity utilization and why does it matter?

A) Employee utilization only
B) Percentage of production area actively growing crops; affects revenue and efficiency
C) Equipment usage only
D) Capacity utilization is irrelevant

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
In multi-crop production facilities, how should crop scheduling be optimized?

A) Plant all crops on the same day
B) Coordinate based on growth duration, market demand, and resource availability
C) Random scheduling
D) Only grow one crop type ever

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What is the typical target for production area utilization in mature CEA operations?

A) 30-40%
B) 50-60%
C) 85-95%
D) Over 100%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 8
How should seasonal market demand fluctuations be addressed in CEA production planning?

A) Ignore market demand
B) Adjust production volume, species mix, and inventory to match demand patterns
C) Produce same amount year-round regardless of demand
D) Close during low-demand periods

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of a "planting calendar" in CEA operations?

A) Decoration
B) Visual tool showing scheduled transplants, expected harvests, and resource needs over time
C) Employee vacation schedules
D) Calendars are not useful

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
When should production schedules be reviewed and adjusted?

A) Never adjust schedules
B) Weekly review with adjustments based on actual crop progression and market changes
C) Once per decade
D) Only during facility emergencies

**Correct Answer: B**

---
