# Quiz 6: Team Leadership for Production Excellence

## Instructions
- 10 multiple-choice questions
- Minimum passing score: 85% (9/10 correct)
- Time limit: 20 minutes
- Select the BEST answer for each question

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most important quality in a Master Grower's leadership style?

A) Autocratic control of all decisions
B) Ability to develop team capabilities while maintaining accountability
C) Avoiding difficult conversations
D) Delegating all responsibilities

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
How should Master Growers handle knowledge transfer to junior growers?

A) Never share knowledge to protect job security
B) Structured mentoring, documentation, and hands-on training
C) Expect junior growers to figure everything out alone
D) Only verbal instructions with no documentation

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is the recommended approach for providing corrective feedback to team members?

A) Public criticism in front of entire team
B) Ignore problems hoping they resolve themselves
C) Private, specific, timely feedback focused on improvement
D) Only provide negative feedback, never positive

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
In high-performing production teams, what is the typical ratio of positive to corrective feedback?

A) All positive, no corrective feedback
B) 3-5 positive interactions for every corrective one
C) All corrective, no positive feedback
D) Feedback ratios don't matter

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
How should Master Growers build accountability in production teams?

A) Blame team members for all problems
B) Clear expectations, regular monitoring, and consistent consequences
C) No expectations or accountability needed
D) Micromanage every action

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What is the most effective way to handle a team member consistently missing quality standards?

A) Immediate termination without discussion
B) Ignore the issues
C) Document issues, provide training/support, set improvement timeline, follow through
D) Reassign all quality control to others

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
In team communication, what is the "morning huddle" and why is it valuable?

A) Exercise routine
B) Brief daily meeting to align priorities, address issues, and maintain team connection
C) Time-wasting activity
D) Huddles serve no purpose

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
How should Master Growers delegate responsibilities to assistant growers?

A) Never delegate anything
B) Delegate tasks matching capabilities with clear authority, support, and accountability
C) Delegate everything immediately
D) Only delegate unimportant tasks

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
What is the primary cause of team turnover in CEA operations?

A) Competitive salaries from other industries
B) Poor leadership, lack of growth opportunities, and inadequate training
C) Teams never leave good operations
D) Weather conditions

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
How should Master Growers recognize and reward exceptional team performance?

A) Never recognize performance
B) Public recognition, specific praise, and advancement opportunities
C) Recognition undermines performance
D) Only focus on problems

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Ability to develop team capabilities while maintaining accountability
2. B - Structured mentoring, documentation, and hands-on training
3. C - Private, specific, timely feedback focused on improvement
4. B - 3-5 positive interactions for every corrective one
5. B - Clear expectations, regular monitoring, and consistent consequences
6. C - Document issues, provide training/support, set improvement timeline, follow through
7. B - Brief daily meeting to align priorities, address issues, and maintain team connection
8. B - Delegate tasks matching capabilities with clear authority, support, and accountability
9. B - Poor leadership, lack of growth opportunities, and inadequate training
10. B - Public recognition, specific praise, and advancement opportunities
