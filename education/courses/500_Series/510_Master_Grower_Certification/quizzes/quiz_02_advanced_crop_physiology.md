## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary function of Rubisco in photosynthesis?

A) Water uptake
B) Catalyzing CO2 fixation in the Calvin cycle
C) Producing chlorophyll
D) Root development

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which hormone is primarily responsible for apical dominance in plants?

A) Ethylene
B) Gibberellin
C) Auxin
D) Cytokinin

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What is the Compensation Point in relation to photosynthesis?

A) Maximum photosynthetic rate
B) Light intensity where photosynthesis equals respiration
C) Optimal temperature for growth
D) CO2 concentration in ambient air

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
In CEA production, manipulating the Red:Far-Red light ratio primarily affects:

A) Root growth only
B) Plant morphology and shade avoidance responses
C) Water uptake
D) Nutrient absorption

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What is photorespiration and why is it problematic in CEA?

A) Beneficial process that increases yields
B) Wasteful process where Rubisco fixes O2 instead of CO2, reducing efficiency
C) Nighttime respiration only
D) Enhanced photosynthesis

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Which environmental factor has the MOST direct impact on stomatal conductance?

A) Soil color
B) Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and light intensity
C) Container size
D) Music frequency

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What is the Dark Reaction (Calvin Cycle) and when does it occur?

A) Only occurs at night
B) CO2 fixation that can occur in light or dark, requiring ATP and NADPH from light reactions
C) Occurs only in roots
D) Process of water uptake

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
How does elevated CO2 (above ambient 400ppm) typically affect C3 plants in CEA?

A) No effect on growth
B) Decreases photosynthesis
C) Increases photosynthetic rate and water use efficiency
D) Causes immediate plant death

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
What is the primary cause of tipburn in leafy greens?

A) Excessive watering
B) Calcium deficiency in young tissue due to rapid growth and low transpiration
C) High nitrogen levels
D) Cold temperatures

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Which statement about C3 vs C4 plants is MOST accurate for CEA production?

A) C4 plants are always superior in CEA
B) C3 plants (most leafy greens) benefit more from elevated CO2 than C4 plants
C) There is no difference between C3 and C4 in CEA
D) C4 plants require no CO2 supplementation

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Catalyzing CO2 fixation in the Calvin cycle
2. C - Auxin
3. B - Light intensity where photosynthesis equals respiration
4. B - Plant morphology and shade avoidance responses
5. B - Wasteful process where Rubisco fixes O2 instead of CO2, reducing efficiency
6. B - Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and light intensity
7. B - CO2 fixation that can occur in light or dark, requiring ATP and NADPH from light reactions
8. C - Increases photosynthetic rate and water use efficiency
9. B - Calcium deficiency in young tissue due to rapid growth and low transpiration
10. B - C3 plants (most leafy greens) benefit more from elevated CO2 than C4 plants
