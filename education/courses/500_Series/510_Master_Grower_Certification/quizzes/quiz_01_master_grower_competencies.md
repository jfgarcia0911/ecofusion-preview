# Quiz 1: Master Grower Competencies & Professional Standards

## Instructions
- 10 multiple-choice questions
- Minimum passing score: 85% (9/10 correct)
- Time limit: 20 minutes
- Select the BEST answer for each question

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary role of a Master Grower in a commercial CEA facility?

A) Maximize short-term profits regardless of sustainability
B) Serve as the senior technical authority responsible for all aspects of crop production
C) Focus exclusively on pest management and crop protection
D) Handle all facility maintenance and equipment repairs

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
According to the Master Grower competency framework, which THREE categories of competencies are essential for success?

A) Technical, operational, and leadership competencies
B) Financial, marketing, and sales competencies
C) Engineering, construction, and design competencies
D) Legal, regulatory, and compliance competencies

**Correct Answer: A**

---

### Question 3
In the Master Grower career pathway, what is the typical experience requirement before reaching Master Grower level?

A) 1-2 years as a crop technician
B) 3-4 years in any agricultural role
C) 5-10 years of progressively responsible CEA production experience
D) Advanced degree with no practical experience required

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
Which of the following is NOT one of the key ethical principles that should guide Master Grower decisions?

A) Food safety and public health commitment
B) Environmental stewardship and sustainability
C) Maximizing production at any cost
D) Professional integrity and honesty

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended span of control (supervisor to workers ratio) for production management in CEA?

A) 1 supervisor : 2-3 workers
B) 1 supervisor : 5-10 workers
C) 1 supervisor : 20-30 workers
D) 1 supervisor : 50+ workers

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Which certification is specifically focused on food safety in agricultural production and commonly required by retailers?

A) Certified Organic
B) LEED Certification
C) GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)
D) B Corporation Certification

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
According to the systems thinking principle, what should Master Growers prioritize for maximum impact?

A) Low-leverage points requiring minor adjustments
B) High-leverage points where small changes create large impacts
C) Only focus on the most expensive equipment
D) Implement all changes simultaneously without prioritization

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What percentage of a Master Grower's effort should be focused on PREVENTION according to the lesson?

A) 20% prevention, 80% intervention
B) 50% prevention, 50% intervention
C) 80% prevention, 15% monitoring, 5% intervention
D) 100% intervention, 0% prevention

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
In the case study example, what was the PRIMARY factor that led to improved team retention and productivity?

A) Higher salaries for all employees
B) Systematic approach including training, communication, and culture building
C) Replacing entire workforce with experienced growers
D) Reducing work hours and expectations

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Which of the following daily practices is MOST important for Master Growers?

A) Conduct morning walk-through of all production areas
B) Spend entire day in office reviewing reports
C) Focus only on problem areas, ignore functioning zones
D) Delegate all observation tasks to junior staff

**Correct Answer: A**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Primary role is senior technical authority for crop production
2. A - Technical, operational, and leadership competencies
3. C - 5-10 years of progressively responsible experience typical
4. C - Maximizing at any cost contradicts ethical principles
5. B - 1:5-10 ratio is typical and manageable
6. C - GAP (Good Agricultural Practices) is food safety focused
7. B - High-leverage points deliver maximum ROI on effort
8. C - 80% prevention is foundation of effective management
9. B - Systematic, integrated approach was key to success
10. A - Daily walk-throughs essential for awareness and leadership presence
