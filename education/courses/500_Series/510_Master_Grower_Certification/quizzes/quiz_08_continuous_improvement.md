# Quiz 8: Continuous Improvement Systems

## Instructions

- Minimum passing score: 85% (9/10 correct)

## Questions

### Question 1
What does the Kaizen philosophy emphasize in production operations?

A) Large, infrequent changes only
B) Continuous small improvements involving all team members
C) Avoiding all changes
D) Only management-driven changes

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle used for?

A) Employee scheduling
B) Systematic approach to testing improvements and implementing changes
C) Production planning only
D) PDCA is not relevant to CEA

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
In data-driven decision making, what is the minimum dataset needed to identify trends?

A) Single data point
B) Multiple observations over time under controlled conditions
C) No data needed
D) Assumptions are better than data

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is "root cause analysis" and when should it be used?

A) Analysis of plant roots only
B) Systematic investigation to identify underlying causes of problems
C) Basic symptom treatment
D) Root cause analysis is unnecessary

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
Which continuous improvement tool helps prioritize problems based on frequency?

A) Fishbone diagram
B) Pareto chart (80/20 rule)
C) Control chart
D) Scatter plot

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What is the purpose of conducting controlled experiments in CEA production?

A) Entertainment
B) Systematically test changes to validate improvements before full implementation
C) Experiments waste time
D) Always implement changes without testing

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
How should Master Growers approach technology adoption for operations improvement?

A) Adopt every new technology immediately
B) Evaluate based on ROI, fit with operations, and proven results before implementation
C) Never adopt any new technology
D) Random technology selection

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What is "benchmarking" in continuous improvement?

A) Physical bench measurements
B) Comparing performance against industry standards or best practices
C) Sitting on benches
D) Benchmarking provides no value

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
How often should Master Growers review and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs)?

A) Once per decade
B) Weekly for operational KPIs; monthly for strategic metrics
C) Never review KPIs
D) Only when problems are obvious

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What is the "5 Whys" technique in problem-solving?

A) Asking random questions
B) Asking "why" repeatedly to drill down to root causes
C) Only asking questions five times total
D) Problem-solving technique is unnecessary

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Continuous small improvements involving all team members
2. B - Systematic approach to testing improvements and implementing changes
3. B - Multiple observations over time under controlled conditions
4. B - Systematic investigation to identify underlying causes of problems
5. B - Pareto chart (80/20 rule)
6. B - Systematically test changes to validate improvements before full implementation
7. B - Evaluate based on ROI, fit with operations, and proven results before implementation
8. B - Comparing performance against industry standards or best practices
9. B - Weekly for operational KPIs; monthly for strategic metrics
10. B - Asking "why" repeatedly to drill down to root causes
