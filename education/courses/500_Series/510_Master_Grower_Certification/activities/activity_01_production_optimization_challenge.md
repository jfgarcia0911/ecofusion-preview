# Activity 1: Production Optimization Challenge

## Overview

**Activity Type**: Hands-on practical exercise / Case study analysis
**Duration**: 3-4 hours
**Format**: Individual or small group (2-3 people)
**Difficulty**: Advanced
**Learning Objectives**:
- Apply integrated systems thinking to complex production challenges
- Analyze data to identify root causes and optimization opportunities
- Develop comprehensive improvement plans with ROI analysis
- Present findings and recommendations professionally

---

## Scenario: GreenLeaf Vertical Farm

### Facility Information

**Operation**:
- 10,000 sq ft vertical farm (4 tiers)
- Production: Butterhead lettuce (primary), basil (secondary)
- Market: Local restaurants and retailers
- Age: 3 years in operation
- Team: 1 Master Grower (you!), 2 lead growers, 8 production technicians

**Current Performance** (Last Quarter Average):

```
Lettuce Production:
- Yield: 4,200 heads/week
- Average head weight: 265g (target: 300g)
- Crop cycle: 42 days (target: 35 days)
- Quality defect rate: 18% (tip burn, undersized, pest damage)
- Space utilization: 68% (32% of production area idle or in transition)

Basil Production:
- Yield: 850 units/week (4 oz clamshells)
- Harvest cycle: 28 days first harvest, 21 days successive harvests
- Quality issues: Flowering (12% of plants), small leaves

Resource Metrics:
- Water use: 22 L/kg produce
- Energy use: 32 kWh/kg produce
- Labor: 0.45 hours/kg produce

Financial Performance:
- Revenue: $15,000/week ($780K annually)
- COGS: $9,500/week ($494K annually)
- Gross Margin: $5,500/week (37%)
- Target Gross Margin: 50%+
```

### Environmental Data (Last 30 Days)

```
Zone 1 (Lettuce Tier 1-2):
- Temperature: 21.5°C avg (range: 18-26°C, high variability)
- Humidity: 75% avg (range: 60-85%)
- VPD: 0.65 kPa avg (target: 0.8-1.2 kPa)
- Light (DLI): 13.8 mol/m²/day (target: 16-17 mol/m²/day)
- CO₂: 650 ppm avg during photoperiod (target: 1000 ppm)

Zone 2 (Basil Tier 3-4):
- Temperature: 24°C avg (range: 22-27°C)
- Humidity: 68% avg (range: 55-78%)
- VPD: 0.95 kPa (better than lettuce)
- Light (DLI): 16.5 mol/m²/day (adequate)
- CO₂: 720 ppm avg (suboptimal)

Issues Noted:
- Temperature spikes during hot afternoons (inadequate cooling capacity)
- Humidity control inconsistent (dehumidification undersized)
- Lighting fixtures showing 15% degradation (not replaced in 3 years)
- CO₂ injection system set to 800 ppm but achieving only 650-720 ppm (leak suspected)
```

### Team Observations

**Lead Grower #1** (Lettuce):
- "We're always rushing at harvest - never seem to catch up"
- "Lots of tip burn, especially in summer months"
- "Hard to maintain consistent quality"
- "Crew turnover is high - just trained someone, they quit"

**Lead Grower #2** (Basil):
- "Basil keeps flowering even though we're on 16-hour photoperiod"
- "Customers want larger leaves like they see from greenhouse growers"
- "We're spending a lot of time removing flowers"

**Production Technician Feedback**:
- "Sometimes we don't know what to do - procedures aren't clear"
- "Quality standards seem to change day to day"
- "It's hard work but I want to do a good job - just need better direction"

## Evaluation Criteria

**Analysis Quality** (30 points):
- Correct identification of root causes
- Data-driven reasoning
- Comprehensive systems thinking

**Plan Effectiveness** (30 points):
- Realistic and achievable goals
- Proper prioritization
- Integration of multiple improvement strategies
- Addresses root causes, not just symptoms

**Financial Justification** (20 points):
- Accurate ROI calculations
- Reasonable cost and benefit estimates
- Clear investment prioritization

**Implementation Strategy** (20 points):
- Practical and detailed action steps
- Clear responsibilities and timelines
- Risk mitigation
- Team development consideration

**Total**: 100 points

## Instructor Notes

**This activity integrates**:
- Environmental control optimization (Lesson 3)
- Systems thinking and resource efficiency (Lessons 4, 8)
- Problem-solving and root cause analysis (Lesson 10)
- Continuous improvement (Lesson 11)
- Team leadership (Lesson 9)
- Financial acumen (Lesson 14)

**Facilitation tips**:
- Encourage data-driven analysis over assumptions
- Challenge students to think systemically (not just individual issues)
- Emphasize practical implementation over theoretical perfection
- Use this as basis for group discussion and peer learning

**Time management**:
- This activity can be compressed to 2 hours (skip some detail) or expanded to full day workshop
- Consider group work for efficiency and peer learning
- Presentations can be full group (all groups present) or rotating (groups present to each other)

---

*This activity simulates real-world Master Grower challenges. There is no single "right" answer - multiple approaches can be valid if well-reasoned and data-supported.*
