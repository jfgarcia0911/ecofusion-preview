# Course 510: Master Grower Certification

## Course Level
**Master/Executive (500-Level)**

## Course Duration
**16 weeks** (8-12 hours per week)

## Course Description

The Master Grower Certification represents the pinnacle of professional achievement in controlled environment agriculture. This capstone course integrates advanced technical knowledge, operational excellence, leadership capabilities, and strategic thinking to prepare candidates for the highest level of responsibility in CEA production facilities.

Master Growers serve as the technical and operational leaders of production facilities, responsible for crop quality, yield optimization, team development, and continuous improvement. This certification validates comprehensive expertise in all aspects of commercial CEA production, from cellular-level crop physiology to facility-wide strategic planning.

Through advanced theory, real-world case studies, complex problem-solving scenarios, and practical assessments, candidates will demonstrate mastery of the competencies required to lead successful CEA operations at any scale.

## Target Audience

- Senior growers with 5+ years of CEA experience
- Head growers seeking certification
- Production managers transitioning to technical leadership
- Agricultural consultants specializing in CEA
- Facility operators preparing for expansion
- Experienced growers seeking professional recognition

## Assessment Methods

### Continuous Assessment (40%)
- Weekly quizzes (14 quizzes × 10 questions each)
- Case study analyses
- Problem-solving exercises
- Production optimization projects

### Practical Assessments (30%)
- Diagnostic and troubleshooting scenarios
- Production system design project
- Quality optimization plan
- Team training demonstration

### Final Certification Exam (30%)
- Comprehensive 50-question examination
- Covers all modules and competencies
- Scenario-based questions requiring applied knowledge
- Minimum 85% required for certification

### Certification Requirements
- Complete all 14 modules
- Achieve minimum 85% on all quizzes
- Successfully complete all practical assessments
- Score minimum 85% on final certification exam
- Submit professional portfolio documenting experience
- Complete peer review and case study presentation
