# Course 508: CEA Venture Development

**Level:** Master/Executive (500-Series)
**Course Code:** CEA-508
**Prerequisites:** CEA-401 or equivalent industry experience, basic financial literacy
**Duration:** 14 weeks
**Credits:** 4.0

---

## Course Description

CEA Venture Development is an advanced master-level course designed for entrepreneurs, investors, and executives seeking to launch or scale controlled environment agriculture ventures. This comprehensive program covers the complete entrepreneurial journey from opportunity identification through exit strategies, with specific focus on the unique challenges and opportunities within the CEA sector.

Students will develop practical skills in business model design, fundraising, financial modeling, market entry, and scaling operations while learning from real-world CEA startup case studies. The course combines theoretical frameworks with hands-on exercises including pitch deck development, financial modeling, and go-to-market strategy creation.

This program is ideal for aspiring CEA entrepreneurs, investors evaluating CEA opportunities, corporate innovation leaders, and experienced operators planning venture launches or expansions.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Identify and validate** CEA venture opportunities using systematic evaluation frameworks
2. **Design innovative business models** tailored to CEA market dynamics and value chains
3. **Develop comprehensive financial models** for CEA startups including capital requirements, unit economics, and growth projections
4. **Create compelling investment materials** including pitch decks, executive summaries, and financial presentations
5. **Navigate fundraising processes** across angels, VCs, private equity, and strategic investors
6. **Structure legal entities** and cap tables optimized for CEA venture requirements
7. **Build and manage founding teams** with complementary skills for CEA ventures
8. **Design and execute go-to-market strategies** for CEA products and services
9. **Plan and implement scaling strategies** addressing operational, financial, and market expansion
10. **Evaluate exit options** and prepare ventures for acquisition, merger, or public offerings
11. **Apply lessons learned** from successful and failed CEA ventures
12. **Pitch effectively** to investors, partners, and customers in the CEA ecosystem

## Course Structure

### Weekly Format
- **Lectures:** 3 hours of instruction covering theoretical frameworks and practical applications
- **Case Studies:** 1-2 hours analyzing real CEA ventures and business decisions
- **Practical Exercises:** Hands-on work developing venture components
- **Guest Speakers:** Industry entrepreneurs, investors, and executives (select sessions)

### Major Deliverables
1. **Opportunity Assessment:** Market analysis and validation study
2. **Business Plan:** Comprehensive venture plan with financial model
3. **Pitch Deck:** Investment-ready presentation
4. **Go-to-Market Plan:** Detailed market entry strategy
5. **Final Presentation:** Investor pitch to panel

---

## Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Module Quizzes | 20% | 14 quizzes covering key concepts |
| Opportunity Assessment | 15% | Market validation exercise |
| Business Plan | 25% | Complete venture plan with financials |
| Pitch Deck | 15% | Investment presentation |

## Course Policies

### Attendance
Regular attendance is expected. Case study discussions and guest speaker sessions are particularly valuable and difficult to replicate.

### Collaboration
Group work is encouraged for learning, but individual deliverables must represent original work. Business plans and pitch decks may be developed in teams of 2-3.

### Confidentiality
Students may be exposed to confidential business information through case studies and guest speakers. Professional discretion is required.

### Professional Conduct
This course simulates real-world venture development. Professional behavior, punctuality, and preparedness are expected at all times.
