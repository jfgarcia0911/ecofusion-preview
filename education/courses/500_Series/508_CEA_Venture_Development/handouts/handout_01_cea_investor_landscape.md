## Alternative Funding Sources

### Revenue-Based Financing
- **Providers:** Lighter Capital, Clearco (formerly Clearbanc)
- **Structure:** Repay based on revenue % until cap reached
- **Typical Terms:** Repay 1.5-2x invested amount
- **Benefit:** No equity dilution
- **Suitable For:** Revenue-generating companies

### Equipment Financing
- **Providers:** Traditional lenders, specialized ag equipment lenders
- **Structure:** Loan secured by equipment
- **Typical Terms:** 5-10 years, 5-9% interest
- **Suitable For:** Production operations with equipment needs

### Project Finance
- **Providers:** Investment banks, private debt funds
- **Structure:** Non-recourse or limited recourse loans
- **Based On:** Cash flows from specific project/facility
- **Typical Use:** Large facility construction
- **Amount:** $5M-$50M+

### Crowdfunding
- **Equity Platforms:** WeFunder, Republic, StartEngine
- **Regulation:** Reg CF (up to $5M), Reg A+ (up to $75M)
- **Benefit:** Marketing + capital, community building
- **Challenge:** Time-intensive, requires strong story

### Strategic Partnerships
- **Structure:** Development agreements, joint ventures
- **Partners:** Retailers, food service, real estate developers
- **Benefit:** Capital, offtake agreements, market access
- **Example:** Grocery chain financing facility in exchange for exclusive supply

## Investor Expectations by Stage

### Seed Stage
- **Traction:** Early customer validation, pilot programs
- **Team:** Strong founders with relevant expertise
- **Market:** Large opportunity, clear problem
- **Product:** MVP or prototype demonstrating feasibility
- **Financials:** Unit economics model, path to profitability
- **Return Target:** 10-30x

### Series A
- **Traction:** Proven product-market fit, $1M-$3M revenue
- **Team:** Key hires made, experienced leadership
- **Market:** TAM >$1B, clear beachhead strategy
- **Product:** Scalable, repeatable offering
- **Financials:** Positive unit economics, clear scaling path
- **Return Target:** 5-10x

### Series B+
- **Traction:** Strong growth, $10M+ revenue run rate
- **Team:** Complete management team
- **Market:** Market leader or clear path to leadership
- **Product:** Proven at scale, competitive advantages
- **Financials:** Path to profitability, efficient growth
- **Return Target:** 3-5x

## Tips for Approaching Investors

### Research and Preparation
1. **Target appropriate investors** - Match stage, sector, check size
2. **Understand investment thesis** - What are they looking for?
3. **Review portfolio** - Do they have relevant experience?
4. **Check references** - Talk to portfolio company founders
5. **Prepare materials** - Pitch deck, financial model, data room

### Getting Introductions
1. **Warm introductions preferred** - From portfolio founders, advisors
2. **Leverage networks** - Industry events, accelerators, advisors
3. **Cold outreach** - Targeted, personalized emails if no warm intro
4. **Multiple paths** - Approach different partners at same firm
5. **Timing matters** - Know their fundraising cycles

### Pitching Best Practices
1. **Tell a story** - Problem, solution, opportunity, team, traction
2. **Know your numbers** - Revenue, costs, unit economics, projections
3. **Show traction** - Customers, revenue, partnerships, milestones
4. **Address risks** - Be honest about challenges and mitigation
5. **Ask for specific amount** - Clear funding ask and use of funds

### Due Diligence Process
1. **Be prepared** - Have documents organized (financials, contracts, IP)
2. **Be responsive** - Answer questions quickly and thoroughly
3. **Be honest** - Disclose issues upfront (they'll find them anyway)
4. **Facilitate calls** - Connect investors with customers, partners
5. **Manage timeline** - Keep process moving, multiple investors in parallel

### Negotiating Terms
1. **Understand terms** - Not just valuation; all terms matter
2. **Get legal counsel** - Experienced startup attorney essential
3. **Consider strategic value** - What does investor bring beyond capital?
4. **Don't over-optimize** - Balance current and future rounds
5. **Build relationships** - You're choosing a partner, not just money

## Red Flags in Investors

Watch out for:
- **Lack of relevant experience** - No ag-tech or relevant investments
- **Slow decision-making** - Months of "we're interested but..."
- **Unclear terms** - Vague about investment structure or amount
- **Bad references** - Portfolio founders report negative experiences
- **Overpromising** - Guarantee outcomes or connections
- **Excessive control** - Want too much board control or protective provisions
- **Unfavorable terms** - Participating preferred, harsh anti-dilution, high liquidation preference
- **Pressure tactics** - Rushed decisions, ultimatums
