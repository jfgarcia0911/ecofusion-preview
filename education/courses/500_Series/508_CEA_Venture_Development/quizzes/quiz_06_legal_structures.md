## Questions

### Question 1
Which legal entity structure is preferred by most venture capital investors for startups?

A) Sole proprietorship
B) Partnership
C) C-Corporation
D) Limited Liability Company (LLC)

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
What is a "cap table" (capitalization table)?

A) Table of office furniture
B) Spreadsheet showing company ownership structure and equity distribution
C) Product pricing table
D) Marketing budget

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the typical vesting schedule for founder equity?

A) Immediate 100% vesting
B) 4 years with 1-year cliff
C) 10 years linear vesting
D) No vesting for founders

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What does a "1-year cliff" mean in a vesting schedule?

A) Company closes after one year
B) No equity vests until the first anniversary, then a year's worth vests at once
C) Stock price drops after one year
D) Founders must work from a cliff location

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which intellectual property issue is MOST important to address at company formation?

A) Trademark for product names
B) Assignment of IP from founders to the company
C) Patent portfolio size
D) Copyright on marketing materials

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a "stock option pool" and why is it important?

A) Swimming pool at company facility
B) Reserved shares for employee compensation to attract and retain talent
C) Public stock trading mechanism
D) Investment fund

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which state is most commonly chosen for incorporation by venture-backed startups?

A) Wyoming
B) California
C) Delaware
D) Texas

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is the purpose of a founders' agreement?

A) To document founder compensation only
B) To outline equity splits, roles, vesting, IP assignment, and dispute resolution
C) To satisfy legal filing requirements only
D) Founders' agreements are not necessary

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is "fully diluted" share count?

A) Total shares including all options, warrants, and convertible securities
B) Only common shares issued to founders
C) Shares held by the CEO only
D) Shares available for sale to public

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 10
For a CEA venture with environmental mission, which entity type provides legal recognition of social purpose?

A) S-Corporation
B) Benefit Corporation (B-Corp)
C) General Partnership
D) Sole Proprietorship

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. C - C-Corporation
2. B - Spreadsheet showing company ownership structure and equity distribution
3. B - 4 years with 1-year cliff
4. B - No equity vests until the first anniversary, then a year's worth vests at once
5. B - Assignment of IP from founders to the company
6. B - Reserved shares for employee compensation to attract and retain talent
7. C - Delaware
8. B - To outline equity splits, roles, vesting, IP assignment, and dispute resolution
9. A - Total shares including all options, warrants, and convertible securities
10. B - Benefit Corporation (B-Corp)
