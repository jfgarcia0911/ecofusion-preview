# Quiz 2: Opportunity Identification and Validation

**Course:** CEA-508 CEA Venture Development
**Module:** 2 of 14

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three primary sources of entrepreneurial opportunities identified in the lesson?

A) Technology, market, and financial
B) Problem-driven, technology-driven, and market-driven
C) Customer, competitor, and supplier-driven
D) Internal, external, and strategic

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
In the TAM/SAM/SOM framework, what does SAM stand for?

A) Strategic Addressable Market
B) Serviceable Addressable Market
C) Standard Available Market
D) Scaled Accessible Market

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
According to the customer discovery methodology, what is the recommended number of customer interviews per segment?

A) 5-10 interviews
B) 10-15 interviews
C) 20-30 interviews
D) 50-100 interviews

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is the "80/20 rule" in customer interviews?

A) 80% of revenue comes from 20% of customers
B) Listen 80% of the time, talk 20% of the time
C) 80% of features are used by 20% of users
D) Spend 80% on product, 20% on marketing

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is a "Concierge MVP" approach?

A) Creating a landing page to measure interest
B) Delivering service manually before building automated product
C) Building a prototype with all planned features
D) Launching to entire market simultaneously

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What does the Value Proposition Canvas consist of on the customer side?

A) Products, services, and features
B) Revenue streams, costs, and profits
C) Customer jobs, pains, and gains
D) Marketing, sales, and distribution

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
In the Lean Startup methodology, what is the sequence of the core loop?

A) Learn - Build - Measure
B) Build - Measure - Learn
C) Measure - Learn - Build
D) Build - Learn - Measure

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What indicates a need for a "pivot" rather than "persevere"?

A) Strong customer interest and improving metrics
B) Single failure in one experiment
C) Repeated invalidation of core assumptions
D) Positive unit economics validation

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
Which top-down market sizing method uses industry analyst reports and research?

A) Value theory approach
B) Bottom-up calculation
C) Customer counting method
D) Market research approach

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 10
What was a key success factor in the Vertical Harvest case study?

A) Building immediately at massive scale
B) Extensive validation before major capital commitment
C) No customer pre-sales or validation
D) Focusing only on technology without market research

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Problem-driven, technology-driven, and market-driven
2. B - Serviceable Addressable Market
3. C - 20-30 interviews
4. B - Listen 80% of the time, talk 20% of the time
5. B - Delivering service manually before building automated product
6. C - Customer jobs, pains, and gains
7. B - Build - Measure - Learn
8. C - Repeated invalidation of core assumptions
9. D - Market research approach
10. B - Extensive validation before major capital commitment
