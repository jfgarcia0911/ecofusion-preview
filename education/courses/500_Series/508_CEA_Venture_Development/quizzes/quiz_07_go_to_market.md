# Quiz 7: Go-to-Market Strategy and Execution

**Module:** 11 of 14

## Instructions

Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question. Each question is worth 10 points.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is a "beachhead market" strategy?

A) Selling products at the beach
B) Focusing initial efforts on a specific, winnable market segment before expanding
C) Marketing to coastal regions only
D) Avoiding market entry

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
For a new CEA produce startup, which initial customer segment is typically MOST attractive?

A) Large national grocery chains requiring massive volume
B) Local restaurants and specialty retailers valuing quality and story
C) International export markets
D) Institutional food service with lowest price requirements

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Which pricing strategy involves setting initial prices low to gain market share quickly?

A) Premium pricing
B) Cost-plus pricing
C) Penetration pricing
D) Value-based pricing

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is customer acquisition cost (CAC)?

A) Cost to produce the product
B) Total cost to acquire one new customer (marketing + sales expenses)
C) Customer's purchase amount
D) Shipping costs

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is a healthy ratio of Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) to Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)?

A) LTV should equal CAC (1:1)
B) LTV should be less than CAC
C) LTV should be 3x or more than CAC (3:1 or higher)
D) The ratio doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
Which sales channel typically has the HIGHEST customer acquisition cost?

A) Word of mouth and referrals
B) Direct sales force
C) Online self-service
D) Partnerships

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the primary benefit of a direct-to-consumer (DTC) model for CEA startups?

A) Lowest operational complexity
B) Higher margins and direct customer relationships/data
C) Easiest to scale quickly
D) Requires no marketing investment

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which marketing approach is most effective for building brand awareness for premium CEA products?

A) Mass advertising only
B) Content marketing, farm tours, chef partnerships, and storytelling
C) Price discounting
D) No marketing needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is "product-market fit"?

A) Physical fit of product in packaging
B) When product satisfies strong market demand evidenced by organic growth
C) Product dimensions
D) Market size calculation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
For a CEA technology startup, which go-to-market motion is typically most effective?

A) Consumer mass marketing
B) Enterprise B2B sales with pilot projects and case studies
C) Door-to-door sales
D) Trade shows only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Focusing initial efforts on a specific, winnable market segment before expanding
2. B - Local restaurants and specialty retailers valuing quality and story
3. C - Penetration pricing
4. B - Total cost to acquire one new customer (marketing + sales expenses)
5. C - LTV should be 3x or more than CAC (3:1 or higher)
6. B - Direct sales force
7. B - Higher margins and direct customer relationships/data
8. B - Content marketing, farm tours, chef partnerships, and storytelling
9. B - When product satisfies strong market demand evidenced by organic growth
10. B - Enterprise B2B sales with pilot projects and case studies
