## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical seed funding round size for early-stage CEA ventures?

A) $10,000-50,000
B) $500,000-2M
C) $10M-20M
D) $100M+

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which investor type is typically most appropriate for very early-stage CEA ventures?

A) Public market investors
B) Angel investors and friends & family
C) Private equity firms
D) Sovereign wealth funds

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is "pre-money valuation"?

A) Company valuation before receiving investment
B) Company valuation after receiving investment
C) Total amount being raised
D) Investor's profit projection

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 4
Which term sheet provision gives investors the right to maintain their ownership percentage in future rounds?

A) Liquidation preference
B) Anti-dilution protection
C) Pro-rata rights
D) Board seat

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
What is the typical equity stake investors expect for a Series A investment?

A) 1-5%
B) 10-25%
C) 51-75%
D) 90-100%

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Which of the following is a RED FLAG for venture investors evaluating CEA startups?

A) Experienced management team
B) Founders who have never worked together and lack CEA experience
C) Clear go-to-market strategy
D) Realistic financial projections

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the primary purpose of a pitch deck?

A) To provide complete business details
B) To generate investor interest and secure meeting for deeper discussion
C) To replace the business plan entirely
D) To list all possible risks

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
How long should a pitch deck be for initial investor outreach?

A) 2-3 slides
B) 10-15 slides
C) 50-100 slides
D) 200+ slides

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is "due diligence" in the fundraising context?

A) Legal requirement to pay taxes
B) Investor's investigation and verification of company claims before investing
C) Company's obligation to pay employees
D) Marketing research

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Which metric do investors focus on most when evaluating CEA venture scalability?

A) Number of employees
B) Founder's age
C) Unit economics and path to profitability at scale
D) Office location

**Correct Answer:** C

---
