## Questions

### Question 1
Which of the following represents the current "wave" of CEA entrepreneurship as of 2025?

A) First wave: Traditional greenhouse operations
B) Second wave: High-tech greenhouses and early vertical farms
C) Third wave: Venture-backed vertical farming and CEA technology
D) Fourth wave: Consolidation, technology maturation, and new business models

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 2
What is typically the most capital-intensive CEA venture type?

A) Software and data companies
B) Consulting and service providers
C) Vertical farming production operations
D) Franchise and licensing models

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
Which business model generates revenue primarily through technology licensing rather than direct operations?

A) Production-focused vertical farms
B) Platform and network models
C) Direct-to-consumer operations
D) Fully integrated production and distribution

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the projected compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for the global CEA market through 2030?

A) 3-5%
B) 6-8%
C) 10-12%
D) 15-20%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is NOT a common failure mode for CEA ventures?

A) Over-engineering and excessive complexity
B) Under-estimation of biological complexity
C) Premature scaling before proving unit economics
D) Excessive focus on customer validation

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 6
According to the lesson, which CEA company example represents a "high-tech/premium" positioning on the competitive matrix?

A) AppHarvest
B) AeroFarms
C) Regional greenhouse operators
D) Traditional field agriculture

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a critical success factor that distinguishes successful CEA ventures?

A) Rapid expansion to multiple facilities in first year
B) Technology-only focus without agricultural expertise
C) Exceptional team with complementary skills
D) Minimizing investment in R&D and innovation

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
Which emerging opportunity area involves growing crops for pharmaceutical or nutraceutical applications?

A) Commodity leafy greens production
B) Specialized medicinal plants
C) Traditional vegetable farming
D) Ornamental flowers only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What gross margin percentage is typically considered healthy for CEA production operations?

A) 10-20%
B) 25-35%
C) 40%+
D) 60-70%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
Which attribute is MOST important for long-term CEA entrepreneurship success?

A) Quick profits within first 6 months
B) Long-term orientation and patient capital approach
C) Avoiding any technology and focusing only on traditional methods
D) Operating in isolation without industry connections

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. D - Fourth wave: Consolidation, technology maturation, and new business models
2. C - Vertical farming production operations
3. B - Platform and network models
4. C - 10-12%
5. D - Excessive focus on customer validation
6. B - AeroFarms
7. C - Exceptional team with complementary skills
8. B - Specialized medicinal plants
9. C - 40%+
10. B - Long-term orientation and patient capital approach
