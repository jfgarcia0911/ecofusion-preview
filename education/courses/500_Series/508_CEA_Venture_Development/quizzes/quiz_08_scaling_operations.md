## Questions

### Question 1
What is the MOST common mistake CEA ventures make when scaling?

A) Growing too slowly
B) Premature scaling before proving unit economics and operational consistency
C) Hiring too few people
D) Over-communication with customers

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which operational metric is most critical to stabilize before geographic expansion?

A) Office aesthetics
B) Consistent yields, quality, and positive unit economics
C) Number of social media followers
D) Employee ping pong tournament results

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the recommended approach for multi-site expansion in CEA?

A) Open all new facilities simultaneously
B) Sequential rollout with learning captured from each site
C) Random geographic selection
D) Never expand beyond one location

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Which organizational challenge typically emerges as CEA ventures scale from 10 to 50+ employees?

A) Too few customers
B) Need for formal processes, systems, and middle management layer
C) Excessive profitability
D) Too much capital

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "operational leverage" in the context of scaling?

A) Using debt to finance operations
B) Ability to increase output/revenue without proportional increase in costs
C) Physical levers in equipment
D) Employee benefits package

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Which function should be standardized and documented FIRST when scaling CEA operations?

A) Corporate events planning
B) Core production processes and quality control procedures
C) Executive compensation
D) Office decoration guidelines

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a "runbook" or standard operating procedure (SOP)?

A) Exercise routine for employees
B) Documented step-by-step instructions for operational processes
C) Financial audit report
D) Marketing campaign plan

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
When should a CEO consider hiring a COO (Chief Operating Officer)?

A) On day one of company formation
B) When operational complexity exceeds CEO's capacity and expertise
C) Never hire a COO
D) Only after IPO

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Which technology investment typically provides the highest ROI when scaling CEA operations?

A) Fancy office furniture
B) Data systems for production tracking, inventory, and quality management
C) Executive company cars
D) Expensive art for offices

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is the primary risk of scaling too quickly?

A) Becoming too profitable
B) Burning cash faster than generating revenue, quality degradation, operational chaos
C) Too much media attention
D) Excessive customer satisfaction

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Premature scaling before proving unit economics and operational consistency
2. B - Consistent yields, quality, and positive unit economics
3. B - Sequential rollout with learning captured from each site
4. B - Need for formal processes, systems, and middle management layer
5. B - Ability to increase output/revenue without proportional increase in costs
6. B - Core production processes and quality control procedures
7. B - Documented step-by-step instructions for operational processes
8. B - When operational complexity exceeds CEO's capacity and expertise
9. B - Data systems for production tracking, inventory, and quality management
10. B - Burning cash faster than generating revenue, quality degradation, operational chaos
