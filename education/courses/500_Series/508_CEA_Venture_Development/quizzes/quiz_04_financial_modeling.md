## Questions

### Question 1
What are the "three statements" in a comprehensive financial model?

A) Mission, vision, and values
B) Income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
C) Sales forecast, expense budget, and headcount plan
D) Product roadmap, marketing plan, and sales strategy

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
For a vertical farming startup, which is typically the LARGEST capital expenditure?

A) Office furniture
B) Facility buildout, growing systems, and environmental controls
C) Marketing budget
D) Legal fees

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is "unit economics" and why is it critical for CEA ventures?

A) The cost of purchasing one share of stock
B) Economics of producing one unit of product (e.g., per kg or pound)
C) University economics courses
D) Standardized pricing regulations

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is a reasonable assumption for capacity utilization in Year 1 of a new CEA facility?

A) 100% from day one
B) 50-70% as operations ramp up
C) 10-20% maximum
D) Capacity utilization doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
In sensitivity analysis, which assumption typically has the HIGHEST impact on CEA venture returns?

A) Office supply costs
B) Yield per square foot and product pricing
C) Travel expenses
D) Depreciation method

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is the typical payback period investors expect for commercial CEA ventures?

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-5 years
C) 5-7 years
D) 15-20 years

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
Which metric is most important for assessing ongoing financial health of a CEA operation?

A) Accumulated deficit
B) EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization) margin
C) Number of employees
D) Square footage

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is working capital and why does it matter for CEA startups?

A) Capital invested in office buildings
B) Cash needed to fund day-to-day operations (inventory, receivables, payables)
C) Employee retirement accounts
D) Depreciation reserves

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
In financial modeling, what is the purpose of creating base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios?

A) To confuse investors
B) To understand range of potential outcomes and key risk drivers
C) To make the model more complex
D) Scenarios are not useful

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
For a CEA startup seeking venture capital, what IRR (Internal Rate of Return) do investors typically require?

A) 5-10%
B) 12-15%
C) 25-40%+
D) 100%+

**Correct Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
2. B - Facility buildout, growing systems, and environmental controls
3. B - Economics of producing one unit of product (e.g., per kg or pound)
4. B - 50-70% as operations ramp up
5. B - Yield per square foot and product pricing
6. C - 5-7 years
7. B - EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization) margin
8. B - Cash needed to fund day-to-day operations (inventory, receivables, payables)
9. B - To understand range of potential outcomes and key risk drivers
10. C - 25-40%+
