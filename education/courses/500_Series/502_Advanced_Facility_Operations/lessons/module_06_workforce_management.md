# Module 6: Workforce Management at Scale

## Master Level - Course 502: Advanced Facility Operations

### Module Overview

This module addresses the complexities of managing workforce operations in scaled CEA environments, including organizational design, talent acquisition and retention, training systems, performance management, and culture development.

**Duration:** 4 hours
**Difficulty:** Master

### Learning Objectives

1. Design organizational structures for multi-facility operations
2. Develop talent acquisition and retention strategies
3. Implement comprehensive training and development programs
4. Create performance management systems
5. Build high-performance operational cultures
6. Manage labor costs and productivity
7. Navigate labor relations and compliance

**Span of Control Principles:**

```
MANAGEMENT RATIOS
=================

Executive Level:
├── CEO to direct reports: 5-8
├── Focus: Strategy, leadership
└── Typical: Monthly 1-on-1s

Senior Management:
├── Directors to managers: 6-10
├── Focus: Functional excellence
└── Typical: Weekly check-ins

Middle Management:
├── Managers to supervisors: 8-12
├── Focus: Coordination, development
└── Typical: Daily huddles

Frontline Supervision:
├── Supervisors to workers: 12-20
├── Focus: Execution, coaching
├── Varies by: Task complexity, shift coverage
└── Typical: Constant presence

Optimal Ratios for CEA:
├── Production supervisor: 15:1
├── Quality supervisor: 20:1
├── Maintenance manager: 8:1
└── General manager (facilities): 4-5 sites

```

```
=================

Goal Setting (Quarterly)
├── SMART objectives aligned to company goals

├── Metrics and key results defined
├── Manager and employee agreement
└── Documentation in system

Continuous Feedback (Ongoing)
├── Weekly 1-on-1 meetings

├── Coaching conversations
├── Course correction as needed
└── Documentation of key discussions

Quarterly Reviews
├── Goal progress review

Annual Performance Review
├── Year-in-review summary

├── Career development planning
├── Compensation review
├── Performance rating

Performance Rating Scale:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 5 - Exceptional (5%)                │

│ Significant impact on business      │
│ Role model for others               │
│                                     │
│ 4 - Exceeds Expectations (15%)      │
│ Regularly exceeds goals             │
│ High quality work                   │
│ Positive influence                  │
│                                     │
│ 3 - Meets Expectations (70%)        │
│ Achieves goals                      │

│                                     │

│ Inconsistent performance            │

Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):
├── Triggered by rating of 2 or pattern of issues

├── Success: Return to good standing
├── Failure: Potential termination
└── Documentation critical
```

```
Scheduling Optimization:
├── Demand-based scheduling
├── Cross-training for flexibility
├── Part-time/seasonal workers for peaks
├── Minimize overtime
└── Balanced workload distribution
```

### Key Takeaways

1. **Design for Scale:** Organizational structure must support growth and efficiency

2. **Invest in Talent:** Competitive compensation and development opportunities attract and retain the best

3. **Train Continuously:** Comprehensive training improves performance, quality, and safety

4. **Manage Performance:** Clear expectations, regular feedback, and accountability drive results

5. **Build Culture:** Strong culture differentiates great companies from good ones

6. **Measure and Optimize:** Track metrics and continuously improve workforce effectiveness
