# Module 11: Environmental Management Systems

## Master Level - Course 502: Advanced Facility Operations

### Module Overview

Environmental management systems, ISO 14001 certification, resource efficiency, waste reduction, carbon footprint management, and circular economy principles for CEA operations.

**Duration:** 4 hours

### Learning Objectives

1. Implement ISO 14001 environmental management systems
2. Measure and reduce environmental footprint
3. Design resource efficiency programs
4. Implement zero-waste and circular economy strategies
5. Achieve sustainability certifications
6. Manage carbon emissions and climate impact
7. Communicate environmental performance to stakeholders

### Resource Efficiency

```
ENERGY MANAGEMENT
=================

Energy Consumption Breakdown (Typical CEA):
├── Lighting: 30-40%
├── HVAC: 35-45%
├── Dehumidification: 10-15%
├── Water systems: 5-10%
├── Other: 5-10%
└── Total: 15-25 kWh per sq ft per year

Energy Efficiency Strategies:

Lighting Optimization:
├── LED technology (vs HPS: 40% savings)
├── Spectrum tuning for efficiency
├── Dimming and zoning controls
├── Light leak prevention
├── Reflective surfaces
└── Maintenance (clean fixtures)

HVAC Efficiency:
├── High-efficiency equipment (SEER >16)
├── Variable frequency drives (VFDs)
├── Heat recovery systems
├── Economizer cycles (outside air)
├── Demand-controlled ventilation
├── Insulation optimization
└── Regular maintenance

Dehumidification:
├── Dedicated dehumidification units
├── Heat recovery from condensate
├── Vapor barriers
├── Staged operation
└── Integration with HVAC

Renewable Energy:
├── On-site solar (rooftop, carport)
├── Off-site power purchase agreements (PPAs)
├── Renewable energy credits (RECs)
├── Battery storage (demand shifting)
└── Combined heat and power (CHP)

Energy Management System:
├── Real-time monitoring
├── Sub-metering by zone/system
├── Benchmarking and targets
├── Energy audits (annual)
├── ISO 50001 certification
└── Continuous improvement

Target: Reduce energy intensity 25% in 3 years
```

```
WATER MANAGEMENT
================

Water Use in CEA:
├── Plant irrigation: 70-80%
├── Cooling/HVAC: 10-15%
├── Cleaning/sanitation: 5-10%
├── Domestic use: 5%
└── Typical: 2-5 gal per lb produce (vs 25-50 gal field)

Water Efficiency Strategies:

Irrigation Optimization:
├── Recirculating systems (95%+ recovery)
├── Precision fertigation
├── Leak detection and repair
├── Water quality monitoring
├── Treatment and reuse
└── Crop selection (water use)

Water Recovery:
├── Condensate capture (dehumidifiers)
├── HVAC condensate (50-200 gal/day potential)
├── Reverse osmosis reject water (reuse)
├── Rainwater harvesting
└── Greywater systems (wash water)

Conservation Measures:
├── Low-flow fixtures
├── Waterless urinals
├── Automatic shutoffs
├── Equipment optimization
└── Employee awareness

Water Treatment:
├── Filtration (sediment, carbon)
├── UV sterilization
├── Reverse osmosis (if needed)
├── Ozonation
└── pH adjustment

Wastewater Management:
├── Pre-treatment before discharge
├── Nutrient recovery
├── Beneficial reuse (approved)
├── Monitoring and reporting
└── Compliance with permits

Target: <2 gallons per pound of produce
```

### Waste Reduction

```
ZERO WASTE HIERARCHY
====================

1. Refuse (Prevent)
├── Eliminate unnecessary materials
├── Vendor packaging programs (returnables)
├── Digital documentation (paperless)
└── Bulk purchasing (reduce packaging)

2. Reduce (Minimize)
├── Optimize production (reduce waste)
├── Right-size packaging
├── Just-in-time inventory
└── Efficient processes

3. Reuse (Extend Life)
├── Reusable containers and pallets
├── Equipment refurbishment
├── Modular systems
└── Creative repurposing

4. Recycle (Process)
├── Organic waste composting
├── Cardboard and paper recycling
├── Plastic recycling (film, containers)
├── Metal recycling
└── E-waste programs

5. Recover (Energy)
├── Anaerobic digestion
├── Waste-to-energy (if available)
└── Heat recovery

6. Dispose (Last Resort)
├── Minimize landfill
├── Proper hazardous waste disposal
└── Tracking and reporting

Waste Stream Analysis:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Waste Type      │ Volume │ Diversion│
│ ────────────────┼────────┼─────────│
│ Organic (plant) │  60%   │  100%   │
│ Cardboard       │  20%   │  100%   │
│ Plastic film    │  10%   │   75%   │
│ Mixed recycling │   5%   │   90%   │
│ Trash (landfill)│   5%   │    0%   │
│ ────────────────┴────────┴─────────│
│ Total Diversion Rate:  93%          │
│ Target:  95%+                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

Organic Waste Management:
├── Composting (on-site or partner)
├── Anaerobic digestion
├── Animal feed (if approved)
├── Donation (food banks)
└── Nutrient recovery

Target: 95%+ waste diversion rate
```

### Carbon Management

```
CARBON FOOTPRINT ASSESSMENT
============================

Scope 1 (Direct Emissions):
├── Natural gas (heating)
├── Propane (CO2 generation)
├── Diesel (generators, vehicles)
├── Refrigerants (HVAC leaks)
└── Calculation: Volume × Emission factor

Scope 2 (Indirect - Purchased Energy):
├── Electricity (grid)
├── Steam/hot water (if purchased)
└── Calculation: kWh × Grid emission factor

Scope 3 (Value Chain):
├── Upstream
│   ├── Purchased goods (seeds, materials)
│   ├── Transportation (inbound)
│   ├── Business travel
│   └── Employee commuting
│
└── Downstream
    ├── Transportation (outbound)
    ├── Product use (minimal for fresh produce)
    └── End-of-life (packaging)

Carbon Footprint Example:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 50,000 sq ft vertical farm          │
│ 1,000,000 lbs/year production       │
│                                     │
│ Scope 1: 250 MT CO2e                │
│ ├── Natural gas: 200 MT             │
│ ├── Propane (CO2): 40 MT            │
│ └── Vehicles: 10 MT                 │
│                                     │
│ Scope 2: 1,200 MT CO2e              │
│ └── Electricity: 2,500,000 kWh      │
│                                     │
│ Scope 3: 350 MT CO2e                │
│ ├── Materials: 200 MT               │
│ ├── Transport: 100 MT               │
│ └── Other: 50 MT                    │
│                                     │
│ Total: 1,800 MT CO2e                │
│ Per pound: 1.8 kg CO2e/lb           │
│                                     │
│ vs. Field agriculture: 2.5-4 kg/lb  │
│ Advantage: 30-55% lower             │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

Reduction Strategies:
├── Energy efficiency (biggest impact)
├── Renewable energy procurement
├── Refrigerant management
├── Sustainable transportation
├── Supplier engagement
└── Circular economy practices

Carbon Neutrality Pathway:
Year 1-2: Measure and reduce (20-30%)
Year 3-4: Renewable energy (50-70% reduction)
Year 5: Carbon offsets (remaining emissions)
Certification: Carbon Neutral, Climate Neutral

Target: Carbon neutral by 2030
```

### Circular Economy

```
CIRCULAR ECONOMY MODEL
======================

Linear Economy (Traditional):
Take → Make → Use → Dispose

Circular Economy (Sustainable):
Design → Use → Return → Recover → Regenerate
            ↑_____________↓

CEA Circular Strategies:

Design for Circularity:
├── Modular equipment (upgrade vs. replace)
├── Durable materials
├── Repairable systems
├── Recyclable packaging
└── Product-as-a-service models

Resource Recovery:
├── Organic waste → Compost → Soil amendment
├── Water → Recirculate → Reuse 95%+
├── CO2 from compost → Plant growth
├── Heat from lights → Space heating
└── Nutrients from waste → Fertilizer

Material Loops:
├── Packaging: Reusable containers
├── Growing media: Renewable, compostable
├── Inputs: Recycled/bio-based where possible
├── Equipment: Refurbish and resell
└── Buildings: Adaptive reuse

Symbiotic Relationships:
├── Waste heat to neighbor facility
├── CO2 from brewery/ethanol plant
├── Organic waste to anaerobic digester
├── Digestate back as fertilizer
└── Industrial ecology park

Economic Benefits:
├── Resource cost savings: 20-40%
├── New revenue streams (waste → product)
├── Resilience (less input dependency)
├── Regulatory compliance
└── Brand differentiation

Example: Closed-Loop Nutrient System
─────────────────────────────────────
Plant waste → Anaerobic digester →
  Biogas (energy) + Digestate (nutrients) →
    Nutrient solution → Plants →
      [Loop repeats]

Results:
├── 80% reduction in purchased nutrients
├── 100% diversion of organic waste
├── On-site energy generation
└── Carbon sequestration in compost
```

### Assessment Questions

1. What are the key elements of ISO 14001 environmental management system?
2. How do you calculate carbon footprint across Scopes 1, 2, and 3?
3. Describe the zero-waste hierarchy and provide examples for each level
4. What is circular economy, and how can it be applied to CEA?
5. What are the major sustainability reporting frameworks?

### Key Takeaways

1. **Measure First:** Baseline data is essential for improvement
2. **Energy is Key:** Largest environmental impact and cost in CEA
3. **Water Efficiency:** CEA advantage, but continuous improvement needed
4. **Circular Thinking:** Design systems that eliminate waste
5. **Certify and Report:** Third-party verification builds credibility
6. **Engage Employees:** Culture change drives lasting results
7. **Business Case is Strong:** Environmental performance = financial performance
