## HACCP Seven Principles

```
1. CONDUCT HAZARD ANALYSIS
   ├─ Identify biological, chemical, physical hazards
   ├─ Assess risk (likelihood × severity)
   └─ Determine significance

2. DETERMINE CRITICAL CONTROL POINTS (CCPs)
   ├─ Use decision tree
   ├─ Point where control can eliminate/prevent hazard
   └─ Typical CEA CCPs:
       ├─ Water quality (pathogen control)
       ├─ Nutrient concentration
       ├─ Wash water sanitization
       ├─ Cold storage temperature
       └─ Package integrity

3. ESTABLISH CRITICAL LIMITS
   ├─ Maximum/minimum values for CCPs
   ├─ Based on science/regulations
   └─ Example: Wash water chlorine 50-200 ppm

4. ESTABLISH MONITORING PROCEDURES
   ├─ What, how, when, who
   ├─ Continuous or periodic
   └─ Example: Test chlorine every 2 hours

5. ESTABLISH CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
   ├─ When critical limit deviation occurs
   ├─ Immediate action
   ├─ Root cause investigation
   ├─ Product disposition
   └─ Document

6. ESTABLISH VERIFICATION PROCEDURES
   ├─ Confirm HACCP plan is working
   ├─ Calibration, testing, audits
   ├─ Review records
   └─ Validate critical limits

7. ESTABLISH RECORD KEEPING
   ├─ HACCP plan
   ├─ Hazard analysis
   ├─ CCP monitoring records
   ├─ Corrective actions
   ├─ Verification activities
   └─ Retention: 2 years minimum
```

## FSMA (Food Safety Modernization Act)

### Preventive Controls for Human Food

```
WRITTEN FOOD SAFETY PLAN REQUIRED:

1. Hazard Analysis
   ├─ Identify known or reasonably foreseeable hazards
   ├─ Evaluate severity and likelihood
   └─ Determine preventive controls needed

2. Preventive Controls
   ├─ Process Controls
   │   └─ Parameters critical to food safety
   ├─ Food Allergen Controls (if applicable)
   │   └─ Prevent cross-contact
   ├─ Sanitation Controls
   │   └─ Cleanliness of surfaces, prevention of contamination
   ├─ Supply Chain Controls
   │   └─ Verification of suppliers
   └─ Recall Plan
       └─ Strategy for rapid product recovery

3. Monitoring
   └─ Ensure controls are effective

4. Corrective Actions
   └─ When preventive control is not met

5. Verification
   ├─ Validation (controls are adequate)
   ├─ Verification activities
   └─ Reanalysis (every 3 years minimum)

6. Record Keeping
   └─ Documentation of all above
```

## Good Manufacturing Practices (GMPs)

### Personnel (21 CFR 117 Subpart B)

```
Health & Hygiene:
├─ Health screening and illness reporting
├─ No working while ill (communicable disease)
├─ Hand washing (before work, after breaks, after handling contamination)
├─ Gloves (clean, intact, changed frequently)
├─ Hair restraints (hats, nets, beard covers)
├─ Jewelry (remove or cover)
├─ Clean uniforms or outer garments
├─ No eating, drinking, smoking in production areas
└─ Visitor controls

Training:
├─ GMP training for all employees
├─ Job-specific food safety training
├─ Refresher training (annual minimum)
└─ Documentation of training
```

### Facilities & Equipment

```
Design & Construction:
├─ Adequate space for operations
├─ Separate areas (raw, processing, finished goods)
├─ Proper ventilation and lighting
├─ Walls, floors, ceilings (cleanable, non-porous)
├─ Hand washing stations (accessible, stocked)
├─ Toilet facilities (adequate number, maintained)
└─ Water supply (adequate, potable)

Equipment:
├─ Food-grade materials (non-toxic, cleanable)
├─ Designed for sanitation (no dead spots)
├─ Proper installation (accessible for cleaning)
├─ Calibration and maintenance
└─ Cleaning and sanitizing schedules

Pest Control:
├─ Exclusion (seal entry points, screens, doors)
├─ Monitoring (traps, inspections)
├─ Control (IPM approach, licensed PCO)
├─ Documentation (sightings, treatments)
└─ No evidence of pests in production areas
```

## Sanitation

### Master Sanitation Schedule

```
FREQUENCY    ITEM                    METHOD
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Daily        Food contact surfaces   Clean & sanitize
Daily        Floors (production)     Sweep & mop
Daily        Hand wash stations      Clean & restock
Daily        Trash removal           Empty & clean cans

Weekly       Drains                  Clean & sanitize
Weekly       Walls (splash zones)    Wash & sanitize
Weekly       Equipment (non-daily)   Clean & sanitize
Weekly       Coolers/cold storage    Clean & organize

Monthly      Light fixtures          Clean & inspect
Monthly      HVAC filters            Replace/clean
Monthly      Deep equipment clean    Disassemble & sanitize
Monthly      Exterior areas          Clean & inspect

Quarterly    High surfaces           Clean & inspect
Quarterly    Ventilation systems     Professional cleaning
Quarterly    Pest control treatment  Licensed applicator

Annual       Full facility deep clean  Professional service
Annual       Equipment certification   Calibration & validation
```

### Sanitation Chemicals

```
Cleaning Agents:
├─ Detergents (remove soil)
├─ Alkaline cleaners (grease, protein)
├─ Acid cleaners (mineral deposits)
└─ Enzymatic cleaners (organic matter)

Sanitizers (Approved for Food Contact):
├─ Chlorine (50-200 ppm, pH 6-7.5)
├─ Quaternary ammonium (quat, 200-400 ppm)
├─ Iodine (12.5-25 ppm)
├─ Peroxyacetic acid (PAA, 80 ppm)
└─ Hot water (171°F for 30 seconds)

Sanitation Procedure (Food Contact Surfaces):
1. Remove gross soil
2. Wash with detergent
3. Rinse with water
4. Apply sanitizer (correct concentration, contact time)
5. Air dry (no towel drying)
6. Verify (ATP test, visual inspection)
```

### Sanitation Verification

```
ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) Testing:
├─ Measures organic matter on surfaces
├─ Rapid results (<30 seconds)
├─ Pass/Fail based on RLU (Relative Light Units)
│   ├─ <10 RLU: Excellent
│   ├─ 10-30 RLU: Pass
│   ├─ 30-100 RLU: Caution (re-clean)
│   └─ >100 RLU: Fail (re-clean and re-test)
└─ Frequency: Daily (random swabs), weekly (comprehensive)

Environmental Monitoring:
├─ Swab non-food contact surfaces
├─ Test for indicator organisms
│   ├─ Aerobic plate count (APC)
│   ├─ Coliforms
│   ├─ Enterobacteriaceae
│   └─ Pathogens (Listeria, Salmonella) in Zone 1
├─ Zones:
│   ├─ Zone 1: Food contact surfaces
│   ├─ Zone 2: Near food contact (splash zones)
│   ├─ Zone 3: More remote (floors, drains)
│   └─ Zone 4: Outside production (offices, hallways)
└─ Frequency: Weekly to monthly (risk-based)
```

## Recall Procedure

```
1. NOTIFICATION (< 2 hours)
   ├─ Identify issue (internal or customer report)
   ├─ Notify recall team
   ├─ Begin investigation
   └─ Preserve evidence

2. ASSESSMENT (< 4 hours)
   ├─ Determine scope (products, lots, dates)
   ├─ Health hazard evaluation
   ├─ Recall classification (Class I, II, or III)
   └─ Trace affected products

3. NOTIFICATION (< 24 hours)
   ├─ Notify customers (direct contact)
   ├─ Notify distributors
   ├─ Notify regulatory agencies (FDA, state)
   ├─ Media notification (if required)
   └─ Consumer notification (if retail)

4. EXECUTION (< 72 hours to initiate)
   ├─ Issue recall notice
   ├─ Retrieve product from market
   ├─ Account for product (reconciliation)
   ├─ Quarantine recalled product
   └─ Document all actions

5. EFFECTIVENESS CHECK (Ongoing)
   ├─ Verify customers received notice
   ├─ Track product returns
   ├─ Reconcile quantities
   └─ Report to regulators

6. DISPOSITION & CLOSURE
   ├─ Destroy or rework product
   ├─ Root cause analysis
   ├─ Corrective actions
   ├─ Regulatory approval to close
   └─ After-action review

Recall Effectiveness Test: Conduct annually
Target: 100% of product located within 24 hours
```

## OSHA Recordkeeping

```
OSHA 300 Log (Injury & Illness Log):
├─ Record all work-related injuries and illnesses
├─ EXCEPT: First aid only
├─ Update within 7 days of knowledge
├─ Retain for 5 years
└─ Annual summary (300A) posted Feb 1 - Apr 30

Recordable Criteria:
├─ Death
├─ Days away from work
├─ Restricted work or job transfer
├─ Medical treatment beyond first aid
├─ Loss of consciousness
└─ Significant injury/illness diagnosed by HCP

First Aid (NOT recordable):
├─ Band-aids, wound cleaning
├─ Hot/cold therapy
├─ Non-prescription medications (aspirin, ibuprofen)
├─ Finger guards
├─ Massage
└─ Drinking fluids for heat stress
```

## Quick Reference Numbers

```
Water Quality:
├─ E. coli: 0 CFU/100mL (drinking water)
├─ Total coliforms: <1 CFU/100mL
├─ pH: 5.5-7.5 (most crops)
└─ Chlorine residual: 0.5-2 ppm (disinfection)

Product Testing:
├─ E. coli: <10 CFU/g
├─ Salmonella: Negative in 25g
├─ Listeria: Negative in 25g (ready-to-eat)
└─ APC (Aerobic Plate Count): <10,000 CFU/g

Sanitation:
├─ Chlorine sanitizer: 50-200 ppm
├─ Quat sanitizer: 200-400 ppm
├─ ATP: <30 RLU (pass)
└─ Contact time: 30 seconds minimum

Temperature:
├─ Cold storage: 34-38°F
├─ Freezer: 0°F or below
├─ Hot holding: >135°F
└─ Danger zone: 41-135°F (minimize time)
```
