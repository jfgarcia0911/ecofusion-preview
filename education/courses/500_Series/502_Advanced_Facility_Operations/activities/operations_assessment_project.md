# Operations Assessment and Improvement Project

**Course 502: Advanced Facility Operations**
**Activity Type:** Capstone Project
**Duration:** 4-6 weeks (ongoing throughout course)
**Points:** 30% of course grade

---

## Project Overview

This capstone project requires you to conduct a comprehensive operational assessment of a CEA facility (your own or a partner facility) and develop a strategic improvement plan. You will apply the concepts, frameworks, and tools learned throughout the course to analyze current performance, identify opportunities, and create an actionable roadmap for operational excellence.

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this project, you will:
1. Apply master-level operations management concepts to a real facility
2. Conduct systematic operational assessments using industry frameworks
3. Identify and prioritize improvement opportunities
4. Develop strategic and tactical improvement plans
5. Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders
6. Demonstrate readiness for senior operations leadership roles

---

## Project Components

### Part 1: Facility Assessment (Weeks 1-2)

**A. Facility Overview**

Document the following:
- Facility size, location, and layout
- Production systems and technology
- Product portfolio and customers
- Organizational structure and headcount
- Annual production volume and revenue
- Current operational challenges

**B. Baseline Performance Metrics**

Collect 12 months of data (if available) for:

Financial:
- Revenue per square foot
- Gross margin %
- EBITDA %
- Labor cost % of revenue
- Cost per pound produced

Production:
- Yield per square foot per cycle
- Cycles per year
- Capacity utilization %
- First-pass yield (quality) %
- OEE (if applicable)

Supply Chain:
- Inventory turnover
- On-time delivery %
- Fill rate %
- Supplier performance

People:
- Employee turnover rate
- Training hours per employee
- Safety incident rate (TRIR)
- Employee engagement score

**C. Process Assessment**

Evaluate the maturity and effectiveness of:
- Production planning and scheduling
- Quality management systems
- Maintenance programs
- Supply chain management
- Technology integration
- Continuous improvement culture
- Environmental sustainability

Use a 1-5 scale:
1 = Ad hoc/non-existent
2 = Repeatable but informal
3 = Defined and documented
4 = Managed and measured
5 = Optimized and continuously improving

**Deliverable:** 5-10 page assessment report with data, charts, and analysis

---

### Part 2: Benchmarking Analysis (Week 3)

**A. Internal Benchmarking**

If multi-facility operation:
- Compare performance across facilities
- Identify best performers
- Document best practices

**B. External Benchmarking**

Compare your facility to:
- Industry benchmarks (from course materials, industry reports)
- Best-in-class performance (top quartile)
- Calculate performance gaps

Create benchmarking table:
```
Metric                 | Your Facility | Industry Avg | Top 25% | Gap
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yield (lbs/sq ft/yr)   |               |              |         |
Gross margin %         |               |              |         |
Labor % of revenue     |               |              |         |
[etc...]
```

**C. Competitive Positioning**

- Identify 2-3 direct or comparable competitors
- Assess strengths and weaknesses
- Determine competitive advantages and disadvantages

**Deliverable:** 3-5 page benchmarking report with gaps identified and prioritized

---

### Part 3: Improvement Opportunity Identification (Week 4)

**A. Apply Course Frameworks**

Use tools from the course to identify opportunities:

Lean Analysis:
- Waste assessment (8 wastes / DOWNTIME)
- Value stream mapping (select 1 product)
- 5S opportunities

Six Sigma Analysis:
- High-variation processes (candidates for DMAIC)
- Quality defects and root causes
- Process capability gaps

Operations Analysis:
- Theory of Constraints (identify bottlenecks)
- Capacity utilization opportunities
- Production optimization

Strategic Analysis:
- Multi-facility operations (if applicable)
- Technology integration gaps
- Sustainability improvements

**B. Prioritization Matrix**

Create an impact vs. effort matrix for all opportunities:

```
                High Impact
                    ↑
    Quick Wins  │  Major Projects
    (Do First)  │  (Plan & Execute)
    ────────────┼────────────
    Low Value   │  Fill-Ins
    (Avoid)     │  (Do if time/resources)
                ↓
                Low Impact
        ←Low Effort    High Effort→
```

**C. Opportunity Quantification**

For top 10 opportunities, estimate:
- Annual financial impact ($)
- Implementation cost ($)
- Timeline (weeks/months)
- ROI or payback period
- Risk level (low/medium/high)

**Deliverable:** Opportunity analysis with prioritization and business case summary

---

### Part 4: Strategic Improvement Plan (Week 5)

**A. 3-Year Operational Excellence Roadmap**

Develop a phased improvement plan:

**Year 1: Foundation**
- Priority initiatives (list 5-8 projects)
- Resource requirements
- Budget ($)
- Expected outcomes

**Year 2: Build Capability**
- Priority initiatives
- Resource requirements
- Budget
- Expected outcomes

**Year 3: Optimize and Excel**
- Priority initiatives
- Resource requirements
- Budget
- Expected outcomes

**B. Detailed Plan for Top 3 Initiatives**

For each of your top 3 improvement opportunities, develop:

1. **Project Charter**
   - Problem statement
   - Goal statement (SMART)
   - Scope (in/out of scope)
   - Business case (financial impact)
   - Team members and roles
   - Timeline and milestones

2. **Implementation Plan**
   - Phases and tasks
   - Responsibilities
   - Dependencies
   - Risks and mitigation
   - Resource requirements
   - Budget

3. **Measurement Plan**
   - Baseline metrics
   - Target metrics
   - Monitoring frequency
   - Success criteria

**C. Organizational Change Management**

Address how you will:
- Secure leadership support and resources
- Engage and communicate with employees
- Provide necessary training
- Overcome resistance
- Celebrate successes

**Deliverable:** 10-15 page strategic improvement plan with detailed project plans

---

### Part 5: Executive Presentation (Week 6)

**A. Presentation Content**

Prepare a 20-minute executive presentation covering:

1. **Current State** (3-4 minutes)
   - Facility overview
   - Baseline performance
   - Key strengths and challenges

2. **Benchmarking Insights** (2-3 minutes)
   - Performance vs. industry
   - Competitive positioning
   - Largest gaps

3. **Improvement Opportunities** (4-5 minutes)
   - Prioritized opportunities
   - Quantified business case
   - Top 3 initiatives detailed

4. **Strategic Roadmap** (4-5 minutes)
   - 3-year phased plan
   - Resource requirements
   - Expected outcomes

5. **Implementation Approach** (3-4 minutes)
   - Change management strategy
   - Quick wins (first 90 days)
   - Risks and mitigation

6. **Recommendation and Call to Action** (2-3 minutes)
   - Executive summary
   - Decision sought
   - Next steps

**B. Presentation Format**

- PowerPoint or similar (12-20 slides)
- Professional design and formatting
- Data visualizations (charts, graphs)
- Appendix with supporting details

**C. Q&A Preparation**

Anticipate and prepare for:
- Challenging questions about assumptions
- Alternative approaches
- Resource constraints
- Implementation risks
- ROI validation

**Deliverable:** Presentation slides and 5-minute video recording (or live presentation)

## Tips for Success

1. **Start Early:** Begin data collection immediately; don't wait
2. **Be Realistic:** Use actual data; estimates are okay but note assumptions
3. **Think Like an Executive:** Focus on business impact, ROI, feasibility
4. **Show Your Work:** Document sources, calculations, logic
5. **Be Specific:** Vague recommendations are weak; be concrete and actionable
6. **Tell a Story:** Connect the dots from current state to future vision
7. **Practice Presentation:** Rehearse to stay within time and flow smoothly
8. **Seek Feedback:** Share drafts with peers or mentors for input

## Example Project Outline

**Facility:** GreenLeaf Farms, 30,000 sq ft vertical farm, lettuce and herbs

**Part 1 Findings:**
- Yield: 22 lbs/sq ft/yr (below average)
- Gross margin: 38% (good but could improve)
- Turnover: 32% (high)
- Quality: 92% (below target 95%)

**Part 2 Insights:**
- Yield gap: 30% below top quartile
- Labor efficiency: 20% below average
- Quality systems: Mature but not certified

**Part 3 Opportunities (Top 5):**
1. LED upgrade → +20% yield, ROI 2 years, $120K
2. Succession planting → +15% capacity, ROI 6 months, $10K
3. SQF certification → Access to retail, ROI 18 months, $75K
4. Preventive maintenance → -30% downtime, ROI 1 year, $40K
5. Employee training program → -40% turnover, ROI 1 year, $25K

**Part 4 Plan:**
- Year 1: LED upgrade, succession planting, PM program ($170K)
- Year 2: SQF certification, training program ($100K)
- Year 3: Automation, expansion ($300K)
- 3-Year Impact: +$1.2M revenue, +8% EBITDA

**Part 5 Ask:**
- Approve Year 1 initiatives ($170K investment)
- Commit to 3-year operational excellence journey
- Assign implementation resources (project manager, team)
