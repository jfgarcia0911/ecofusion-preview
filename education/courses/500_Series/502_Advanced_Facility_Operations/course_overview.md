# Course 502: Advanced Facility Operations

## Master Level - EcoFusion Academy

### Course Description

Advanced Facility Operations is a comprehensive master-level course designed for executives, operations directors, and facility managers responsible for large-scale Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) operations. This course provides advanced strategies, frameworks, and best practices for managing multi-facility operations, optimizing enterprise resources, and achieving operational excellence in commercial CEA environments.

Students will master enterprise-level operations management, including ERP systems, supply chain optimization, quality management, workforce development, and continuous improvement methodologies. The course emphasizes data-driven decision making, risk management, and technology integration strategies essential for scaling CEA operations to enterprise levels.

### Target Audience

- Operations Directors and VPs of Operations
- Multi-facility Managers
- General Managers of large-scale CEA facilities
- Business Owners scaling operations
- Operations Consultants
- Supply Chain Directors
- Quality Assurance Managers

### Course Duration

- **Total Hours:** 60-65 contact hours
- **Format:** 14 comprehensive modules
- **Delivery:** Blended (online, in-person workshops, facility visits)
- **Assessment:** Module quizzes, practical exercises, final comprehensive exam

### Prerequisites

**Required:**
- Course 201: Commercial Aquaponics Operations OR equivalent commercial CEA experience
- Course 203: System Automation & Monitoring OR equivalent technical background
- Minimum 2 years managing commercial CEA operations
- Understanding of business operations and financial management

**Recommended:**
- Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture
- Course 411: Vertical Farm Engineering
- MBA or business management background
- Six Sigma or Lean certification

5. **Quality Systems**

6. **Workforce Excellence**

   - Implement training and development programs

7. **Continuous Improvement**
