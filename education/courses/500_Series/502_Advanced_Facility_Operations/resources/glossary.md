# Operations Management Glossary

**Course 502: Advanced Facility Operations**

---

## A

**ABC Analysis** - Inventory classification method where items are categorized by value (A=high value, B=medium, C=low), typically following the Pareto principle (80/20 rule).

**Andon** - Visual signal (light, alarm) used in Lean manufacturing to indicate status or call for help when problems occur.

**ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)** - Statistical method to compare means of three or more groups to determine if differences are significant.

**ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) Testing** - Rapid sanitation verification method that measures organic matter on surfaces.

**Autonomation (Jidoka)** - Automation with human intelligence; equipment stops automatically when problems are detected.

**Availability** - Percentage of time equipment is operational and ready to produce (Uptime / Total Time).

---

## B

**Balanced Scorecard** - Performance measurement framework using multiple perspectives (financial, customer, internal process, learning/growth).

**Batch** - Specific quantity of product produced under uniform conditions, identified by unique lot code.

**Benchmark** - Standard or point of reference against which performance can be measured and compared.

**Best Practice** - Method or technique that has proven to reliably lead to superior results; industry-recognized approach.

**BIA (Business Impact Analysis)** - Process to determine potential effects of disruption to critical business operations.

**Bottleneck** - Process step with the least capacity, constraining overall system throughput.

**BRCGS (British Retail Consortium Global Standards)** - Global food safety certification scheme recognized by GFSI.

---

## C

**Calibration** - Process of configuring measurement equipment to provide accurate results by comparison to known standards.

**Capacity Utilization** - Percentage of potential output actually achieved (Actual Output / Design Capacity × 100%).

**CapEx (Capital Expenditure)** - Funds used to acquire or upgrade physical assets (buildings, equipment).

**CCP (Critical Control Point)** - Step in food production where control can be applied to prevent/eliminate food safety hazard.

**CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture)** - Method of growing crops in controlled indoor environments (vertical farms, greenhouses).

**CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System)** - Software to manage maintenance activities, assets, and work orders.

**COA (Certificate of Analysis)** - Document certifying product meets quality specifications, typically includes test results.

**Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)** - Ongoing effort to improve products, services, or processes through incremental changes.

**Cp (Process Capability Index)** - Measure of process capability relative to specification limits (Cp = (USL-LSL) / 6σ).

**Cpk (Process Capability Index - Centered)** - Measure accounting for process centering between specification limits.

**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** - System for managing customer interactions, sales, and service.

**CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)** - Metric measuring customer satisfaction, typically on scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).

**CTQ (Critical to Quality)** - Key measurable characteristic essential to customer satisfaction.

---

## D

**Dashboard** - Visual display of key performance indicators and metrics, often real-time or near real-time.

**DMAIC** - Six Sigma problem-solving methodology: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.

**DOE (Design of Experiments)** - Systematic method to determine relationship between factors and output, optimize processes.

**DOWNTIME** - Acronym for eight wastes in Lean: Defects, Overproduction, Waiting, Non-utilized talent, Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Extra processing.

**Drum-Buffer-Rope** - Theory of Constraints scheduling method using constraint as drum (pace), buffer (protection), rope (release).

---

## E

**EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization)** - Measure of operating profitability.

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measure of nutrient solution concentration, indicates total dissolved salts.

**Efficiency** - Ratio of actual output to standard or expected output, expressed as percentage.

**EOQ (Economic Order Quantity)** - Optimal order quantity minimizing total inventory costs (ordering + holding).

**EPLI (Employment Practices Liability Insurance)** - Insurance covering employment-related claims (discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination).

**ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)** - Integrated software platform managing business processes (finance, operations, HR, supply chain).

**ERM (Enterprise Risk Management)** - Comprehensive approach to identifying and managing organizational risks.

**ETL (Extract, Transform, Load)** - Data integration process: extract from sources, transform to standard format, load to destination.

---

## F

**First Pass Yield** - Percentage of units produced correctly without rework or scrap on first attempt.

**Fishbone Diagram (Ishikawa)** - Visual tool for root cause analysis, organizing potential causes into categories.

**5S** - Workplace organization methodology: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain.

**5 Whys** - Root cause analysis technique asking "why" repeatedly (typically five times) to find underlying cause.

**FSMA (Food Safety Modernization Act)** - US food safety law shifting focus from response to prevention.

**FSSC 22000** - Food safety certification scheme based on ISO 22000, recognized by GFSI.

---

## G

**GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)** - Voluntary food safety certification for agricultural operations.

**Gemba** - Japanese term meaning "the real place"; where value is created (production floor).

**GFSI (Global Food Safety Initiative)** - Organization benchmarking food safety certification schemes.

**GHS (Globally Harmonized System)** - International standard for chemical classification and labeling.

**GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices)** - Regulations and practices ensuring products are consistently produced and controlled according to quality standards.

**Gross Margin** - Revenue minus cost of goods sold, expressed as percentage of revenue.

---

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)** - Systematic preventive approach to food safety addressing biological, chemical, and physical hazards.

**HazCom (Hazard Communication)** - OSHA standard requiring information about chemical hazards in the workplace.

**HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning)** - System controlling environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, air quality).

---

## I

**IoT (Internet of Things)** - Network of physical devices embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity for data exchange.

**IPM (Integrated Pest Management)** - Ecosystem-based strategy for pest control using combination of techniques.

**ISO (International Organization for Standardization)** - Independent organization developing international standards.
  - **ISO 9001:** Quality management systems
  - **ISO 14001:** Environmental management systems
  - **ISO 22000:** Food safety management systems
  - **ISO 45001:** Occupational health and safety
  - **ISO 50001:** Energy management systems

---

## J

**Jidoka** - See Autonomation; building in quality at the source.

**JIT (Just-In-Time)** - Inventory strategy where materials arrive exactly when needed, minimizing inventory.

---

## K

**Kaizen** - Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement involving everyone from executives to workers.

**Kaizen Event (Blitz)** - Focused, short-term (3-5 days) project to improve specific process or area.

**Kanban** - Visual signaling system to trigger movement, production, or supply of materials in pull system.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)** - Measurable value demonstrating how effectively objectives are being achieved.

**KRI (Key Risk Indicator)** - Metric indicating increasing risk exposure in specific area.

---

## L

**Lead Time** - Total time from start to completion of process; time between order and delivery.

**Lean** - Management philosophy focused on maximizing customer value while minimizing waste.

**LOTO (Lockout/Tagout)** - Safety procedure ensuring dangerous equipment is properly shut off and cannot start during maintenance.

---

## M

**Malcolm Baldrige Award** - US national quality award recognizing performance excellence.

**MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)** - Measure of forecast accuracy.

**MES (Manufacturing Execution System)** - Software system tracking and documenting transformation of raw materials to finished goods.

**MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)** - Average time between equipment failures; reliability metric.

**MTTR (Mean Time To Repair)** - Average time required to repair failed equipment and return to operation.

**Muda** - Japanese term for waste; any activity not adding value.

---

## N

**Net Promoter Score (NPS)** - Customer loyalty metric based on likelihood to recommend (% Promoters - % Detractors).

**Nonconformance** - Failure to meet specified requirement or standard.

---

## O

**OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)** - Metric combining Availability × Performance × Quality; world-class = 85%+.

**OpEx (Operational Expenditure)** - Ongoing costs for running business (salaries, utilities, supplies).

**OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration)** - US agency setting and enforcing workplace safety standards.

**OT (Operational Technology)** - Hardware and software monitoring/controlling physical devices and processes.

---

## P

**Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)** - Principle that 80% of effects come from 20% of causes.

**PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)** - Iterative four-step management method for continuous improvement.

**PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)** - Industrial computer controlling manufacturing processes.

**Poka-Yoke** - Error-proofing; designing processes to prevent mistakes.

**PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)** - Equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards (gloves, glasses, etc.).

**PPM (Parts Per Million)** - Measure of defect rate; 1 PPM = 0.0001%.

**Predictive Maintenance** - Maintenance performed based on condition monitoring data predicting when failure will occur.

**Preventive Maintenance** - Scheduled maintenance performed to prevent equipment failure.

**Process Capability** - Statistical measure of process's ability to produce output within specification limits.

---

## Q

**QMS (Quality Management System)** - Formalized system documenting processes, procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies and objectives.

**Quat (Quaternary Ammonium)** - Type of sanitizer used in food production facilities.

---

## R

**RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)** - Method of restricting system access based on user roles.

**RCA (Root Cause Analysis)** - Method of problem solving identifying underlying causes rather than symptoms.

**Recall** - Removal or correction of marketed product violating laws or presenting health hazard.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - Performance measure evaluating efficiency of investment (Gain / Cost × 100%).

**ROP (Reorder Point)** - Inventory level triggering new order to prevent stockout.

**RPO (Recovery Point Objective)** - Maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time.

**RTO (Recovery Time Objective)** - Maximum acceptable time to restore function after disruption.

---

## S

**Safety Stock** - Extra inventory held to mitigate risk of stockout due to demand or supply variability.

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)** - System for remote monitoring and control of industrial processes.

**SDS (Safety Data Sheet)** - Document providing information about chemical substances (hazards, handling, emergency measures).

**Sigma Level** - Measure of process capability; Six Sigma = 3.4 defects per million opportunities.

**Six Sigma** - Data-driven methodology for eliminating defects (3.4 defects per million).

**SMART Goals** - Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound objectives.

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)** - Detailed written instructions for routine operations.

**SPC (Statistical Process Control)** - Using statistical methods to monitor and control process.

**SQF (Safe Quality Food)** - Food safety and quality certification recognized by GFSI.

**Stakeholder** - Person, group, or organization with interest or concern in organization.

**Standard Work** - Documented, best-known method for performing task safely and effectively.

---

## T

**Takt Time** - Available production time / Customer demand; pace of production to meet demand.

**TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)** - Comprehensive assessment of all costs over entire lifecycle.

**Theory of Constraints (TOC)** - Management philosophy identifying and systematically improving system constraint.

**Throughput** - Rate at which system generates money through sales; in TOC: sales revenue - truly variable costs.

**TPM (Total Productive Maintenance)** - Approach to equipment maintenance maximizing OEE with participation of all employees.

**Traceability** - Ability to track product movement through production, processing, and distribution.

**TRIR (Total Recordable Incident Rate)** - OSHA metric: Number of recordable incidents × 200,000 / Total hours worked.

---

## U

**UAT (User Acceptance Testing)** - Testing by end users to verify system meets requirements before deployment.

**Uptime** - Time equipment is operational and available for production.

---

## V

**Value-Add** - Activity transforming product in way customer is willing to pay for.

**Value Stream** - All actions (value-add and non-value-add) required to bring product from concept to customer.

**Value Stream Mapping (VSM)** - Lean tool for visualizing material and information flow, identifying waste.

**Variance** - Statistical measure of variability; square of standard deviation.

**VOC (Voice of Customer)** - Process for capturing customer requirements and expectations.

---

## W

**Waste** - Any activity consuming resources but not creating value for customer.

**Work-In-Process (WIP)** - Inventory between raw materials and finished goods; partially completed products.

**World-Class** - Performance level equal to or exceeding best companies in world for specific metric or practice.

**Wrench Time** - Percentage of maintenance time actually spent working (vs. traveling, waiting, planning).

---

## Y

**Yield** - Amount of usable product; percentage of product meeting quality standards.

---

## Z

**Zero Defects** - Quality management concept emphasizing doing work right first time to eliminate waste.

**Zone** - Defined area in facility, often for environmental monitoring or security purposes.
