### Questions

**1. What is the primary purpose of an ERP system in CEA operations?**

A) Replace all employees with automation
B) Integrate business processes across operations, finance, supply chain, and HR into a single system
C) Provide entertainment for staff
D) Only track financial transactions

**Answer: B) Integrate business processes across operations, finance, supply chain, and HR into a single system**

---

**2. Which of the following is typically a core module in a CEA-focused ERP system?**

A) Social media management
B) Production planning and tracking
C) Gaming applications
D) Personal calendar management

**Answer: B) Production planning and tracking**

---

**3. What is the typical implementation timeline for a comprehensive ERP system?**

A) 1-2 weeks
B) 1-2 months
C) 6-18 months
D) 5-10 years

**Answer: C) 6-18 months**

---

**4. Which implementation approach involves deploying all ERP modules simultaneously?**

A) Phased approach
B) Big Bang approach
C) Modular approach
D) Gradual approach

**Answer: B) Big Bang approach**

---

**5. What is "data migration" in the context of ERP implementation?**

A) Birds flying south for winter
B) Transferring data from legacy systems to the new ERP system
C) Moving facilities to new locations
D) Employee relocation programs

**Answer: B) Transferring data from legacy systems to the new ERP system**

---

**6. Which of the following is a leading ERP vendor that offers agriculture/food production modules?**

A) Instagram
B) SAP or Microsoft Dynamics
C) Snapchat
D) TikTok

**Answer: B) SAP or Microsoft Dynamics**

---

**7. What is "change management" in ERP implementation?**

A) Counting cash register change
B) Managing the people side of transition, including training, communication, and adoption
C) Changing passwords regularly
D) Rearranging office furniture

**Answer: B) Managing the people side of transition, including training, communication, and adoption**

---

**8. What percentage of ERP implementations fail to meet their objectives on time and budget?**

A) Less than 10%
B) 25-30%
C) 50-60%
D) 90-95%

**Answer: C) 50-60%**

---

**9. Which stakeholder group should be heavily involved in ERP system selection?**

A) Only IT department
B) Only executives
C) End users from all departments who will use the system
D) External consultants only

**Answer: C) End users from all departments who will use the system**

---

**10. What is a "dashboard" in ERP systems?**

A) The front panel of a vehicle
B) A visual display of key performance indicators and metrics in real-time
C) A skateboarding surface
D) Employee name tags

**Answer: B) A visual display of key performance indicators and metrics in real-time**
