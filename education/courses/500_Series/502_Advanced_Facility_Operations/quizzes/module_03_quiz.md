### Questions

**1. What is "capacity utilization" in CEA operations?**

A) The amount of water plants use
B) The percentage of available growing space/capacity that is actively in production
C) Employee headcount
D) Building square footage

**Answer: B) The percentage of available growing space/capacity that is actively in production**

---

**2. What is the primary goal of production scheduling in CEA?**

A) Creating employee work schedules
B) Maximizing output while meeting customer demand, optimizing resources, and maintaining quality
C) Planning company parties
D) Scheduling maintenance only

**Answer: B) Maximizing output while meeting customer demand, optimizing resources, and maintaining quality**

---

**3. What does "succession planting" refer to in CEA production?**

A) Corporate succession planning
B) Staggered planting schedules to ensure continuous harvest and consistent supply
C) Replacing failed crops
D) Generational farming transitions

**Answer: B) Staggered planting schedules to ensure continuous harvest and consistent supply**

---

**4. Which production strategy involves producing only what is needed when it's needed?**

A) Just-in-Time (JIT) production
B) Mass production
C) Batch production
D) Overproduction

**Answer: A) Just-in-Time (JIT) production**

---

**5. What is "yield optimization" in CEA?**

A) Giving up when things get difficult
B) Strategies to maximize crop production per square foot or per growing cycle
C) Traffic sign compliance
D) Employee productivity only

**Answer: B) Strategies to maximize crop production per square foot or per growing cycle**

---

**6. What is a typical target for overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) in well-managed operations?**

A) 50%
B) 65%
C) 85% or higher
D) 100% (perfect)

**Answer: C) 85% or higher**

---

**7. Which factor is typically the largest variable cost in CEA production?**

A) Marketing
B) Labor or energy
C) Office supplies
D) Travel expenses

**Answer: B) Labor or energy**

---

**8. What is "crop rotation" in the context of CEA?**

A) Physically rotating plants
B) Varying which crops are grown in specific zones over time to optimize conditions and manage resources
C) Employee job rotation
D) Spinning growing towers

**Answer: B) Varying which crops are grown in specific zones over time to optimize conditions and manage resources**

---

**9. What is the "Theory of Constraints" focused on?**

A) Legal restrictions on operations
B) Identifying and addressing the bottleneck that most limits system performance
C) Employee contract terms
D) Building code limitations

**Answer: B) Identifying and addressing the bottleneck that most limits system performance**

---

**10. What is a "production KPI"?**

A) A type of growing media
B) Key Performance Indicator - a metric used to track production performance
C) A pest control method
D) An equipment brand

**Answer: B) Key Performance Indicator - a metric used to track production performance**
