### Questions

**1. What does KPI stand for?**

A) Key Performance Indicator
B) Knowledge Processing Information
C) Kinetic Production Index
D) Kitchen Production Inventory

**Answer: A) Key Performance Indicator**

---

**2. What is the difference between "leading" and "lagging" indicators?**

A) Leading indicators are first in alphabetical order
B) Leading indicators predict future performance; lagging indicators measure past results
C) Leading indicators are more important
D) There is no difference

**Answer: B) Leading indicators predict future performance; lagging indicators measure past results**

---

**3. What is "Overall Equipment Effectiveness" (OEE)?**

A) Employee efficiency rating
B) Metric combining Availability, Performance, and Quality to measure equipment productivity
C) Energy consumption measurement
D) Customer satisfaction score

**Answer: B) Metric combining Availability, Performance, and Quality to measure equipment productivity**

---

**4. What is a "dashboard" in operational analytics?**

A) Vehicle instrument panel
B) Visual display of key metrics and KPIs for real-time monitoring and decision-making
C) Skateboard surface
D) Door panel

**Answer: B) Visual display of key metrics and KPIs for real-time monitoring and decision-making**

---

**5. What is "yield per square foot" measuring?**

A) Building structural integrity
B) Production output (weight or units) per square foot of growing area
C) Employee density
D) Light intensity

**Answer: B) Production output (weight or units) per square foot of growing area**

---

**6. What does "First Pass Yield" (FPY) measure?**

A) First harvest of the season
B) Percentage of products that meet quality standards without rework or correction
C) New employee productivity
D) Initial investment returns

**Answer: B) Percentage of products that meet quality standards without rework or correction**

---

**7. What is "predictive analytics" in operations?**

A) Fortune telling
B) Using data, algorithms, and machine learning to forecast future outcomes and trends
C) Weather forecasting only
D) Employee scheduling

**Answer: B) Using data, algorithms, and machine learning to forecast future outcomes and trends**

---

**8. What is the recommended frequency for reviewing operational KPIs?**

A) Annually only
B) Daily for operational metrics, weekly/monthly for strategic metrics
C) Only when problems occur
D) Every 5 years

**Answer: B) Daily for operational metrics, weekly/monthly for strategic metrics**

---

**9. What is "benchmarking" in performance measurement?**

A) Furniture testing
B) Comparing performance metrics to industry standards, competitors, or best practices
C) Weight measurement
D) Employee seating arrangements

**Answer: B) Comparing performance metrics to industry standards, competitors, or best practices**

---

**10. What is "data-driven decision making"?**

A) Transportation route planning
B) Making decisions based on data analysis and interpretation rather than intuition alone
C) Computer programming
D) Autonomous vehicle technology

**Answer: B) Making decisions based on data analysis and interpretation rather than intuition alone**
