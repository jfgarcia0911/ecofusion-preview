### Questions

**1. What are the five major components of supply chain management?**

A) Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return
B) Buy, Sell, Ship, Store, Market
C) Grow, Harvest, Package, Ship, Sell
D) Order, Receive, Process, Ship, Invoice

**Answer: A) Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return**

---

**2. What is "procurement" in supply chain management?**

A) Criminal proceedings
B) The process of sourcing and purchasing goods and services needed for operations
C) Production planning
D) Marketing activities

**Answer: B) The process of sourcing and purchasing goods and services needed for operations**

---

**3. What inventory management approach aims to minimize inventory holding costs while ensuring adequate stock?**

A) Overstocking everything
B) Just-in-Time (JIT) inventory
C) Random ordering
D) Never reordering

**Answer: B) Just-in-Time (JIT) inventory**

---

**4. What is "lead time" in supply chain management?**

A) The time it takes for leadership to make decisions
B) The time between placing an order and receiving the goods
C) Employee training time
D) The heaviest metal used in construction

**Answer: B) The time between placing an order and receiving the goods**

---

**5. What does EOQ stand for in inventory management?**

A) End of Quarter
B) Economic Order Quantity
C) Equipment Quality
D) Emergency Operations Queue

**Answer: B) Economic Order Quantity**

---

**6. Which distribution strategy delivers products directly from production facility to end customers?**

A) Multi-tier distribution
B) Retail distribution
C) Direct-to-consumer (DTC)
D) Wholesale distribution

**Answer: C) Direct-to-consumer (DTC)**

---

**7. What is "vendor management" focused on?**

A) Managing vending machines
B) Developing and maintaining effective relationships with suppliers to ensure quality, reliability, and value
C) Employee management
D) Customer relationship management

**Answer: B) Developing and maintaining effective relationships with suppliers to ensure quality, reliability, and value**

---

**8. What is "cold chain logistics"?**

A) Unfriendly customer service
B) Temperature-controlled supply chain for perishable products
C) Winter delivery routes
D) Refrigerated office spaces

**Answer: B) Temperature-controlled supply chain for perishable products**

---

**9. What is the primary benefit of "supply chain visibility"?**

A) Glass-walled warehouses
B) Real-time tracking and transparency of inventory, orders, and shipments throughout the supply chain
C) Security camera systems
D) Employee monitoring

**Answer: B) Real-time tracking and transparency of inventory, orders, and shipments throughout the supply chain**

---

**10. What is a "3PL" provider?**

A) Third Party Logistics provider offering outsourced logistics services
B) Three Phase Lighting
C) Third Production Line
D) Three Person Limit

**Answer: A) Third Party Logistics provider offering outsourced logistics services**
