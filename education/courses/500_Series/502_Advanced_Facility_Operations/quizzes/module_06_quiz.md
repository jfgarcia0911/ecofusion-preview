### Questions

**1. What is the typical organizational structure for a commercial-scale CEA facility?**

A) Flat with no hierarchy
B) Hierarchical with General Manager, Department Heads, Supervisors, and Production Staff
C) Matrix with dual reporting only
D) No formal structure

**Answer: B) Hierarchical with General Manager, Department Heads, Supervisors, and Production Staff**

---

**2. What is "span of control" and what is a typical ratio for production supervisors?**

A) Equipment operation; 1:5 ratio
B) Number of direct reports; typically 1:10 to 1:15 for production supervisors
C) Budget authority; unlimited
D) Facility size; 10,000 sq ft

**Answer: B) Number of direct reports; typically 1:10 to 1:15 for production supervisors**

---

**3. What is the purpose of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)?**

A) To create unnecessary paperwork
B) To document consistent, step-by-step instructions for routine operations ensuring quality and safety
C) To replace training programs
D) To punish employees

**Answer: B) To document consistent, step-by-step instructions for routine operations ensuring quality and safety**

---

**4. What is "cross-training" and why is it valuable?**

A) Exercise programs for employees
B) Training employees to perform multiple roles, providing flexibility and reducing dependency on single individuals
C) International training programs
D) Competitive sports training

**Answer: B) Training employees to perform multiple roles, providing flexibility and reducing dependency on single individuals**

---

**5. What is typical annual employee turnover in agriculture/CEA operations?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-25%
C) 30-50% or higher
D) 75-90%

**Answer: C) 30-50% or higher**

---

**6. What is "onboarding" in workforce management?**

A) Getting on boats
B) The process of integrating new employees, including orientation, training, and cultural integration
C) Boarding aircraft
D) Storage shelving

**Answer: B) The process of integrating new employees, including orientation, training, and cultural integration**

---

**7. What is a "performance management system"?**

A) Computer performance monitoring
B) Structured approach to setting expectations, providing feedback, evaluating performance, and supporting development
C) Equipment efficiency tracking
D) Production output measurement only

**Answer: B) Structured approach to setting expectations, providing feedback, evaluating performance, and supporting development**

---

**8. What is the primary purpose of safety training in CEA facilities?**

A) Meeting insurance requirements only
B) Preventing workplace injuries, ensuring compliance, and creating a safety culture
C) Avoiding lawsuits only
D) Making employees scared

**Answer: B) Preventing workplace injuries, ensuring compliance, and creating a safety culture**

---

**9. What is "workforce planning"?**

A) Daily work schedules only
B) Strategic process of forecasting future workforce needs and developing strategies to meet those needs
C) Office floor plans
D) Parking lot assignments

**Answer: B) Strategic process of forecasting future workforce needs and developing strategies to meet those needs**

---

**10. What is the recommended ratio of training hours for new production employees in CEA?**

A) 2-4 hours total
B) 8-16 hours (1-2 days)
C) 40-80 hours (1-2 weeks)
D) 6-12 months

**Answer: C) 40-80 hours (1-2 weeks)**
