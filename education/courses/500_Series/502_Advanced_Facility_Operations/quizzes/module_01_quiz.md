### Questions

**1. What are the three primary multi-site operational models?**

A) Centralized, Decentralized, and Regional
B) Centralized, Decentralized, and Hybrid
C) Corporate, Regional, and Facility
D) Strategic, Tactical, and Operational

**Answer: B) Centralized, Decentralized, and Hybrid**

---

**2. According to the 80/20 standardization principle, approximately what percentage of processes should be standardized across facilities?**

A) 50%
B) 60%
C) 80%
D) 100%

**Answer: C) 80%**

---

**3. Which operational model is generally recommended for most CEA operations?**

A) Fully centralized (all decisions at corporate)
B) Fully decentralized (each facility autonomous)
C) Hybrid (mix of centralized and decentralized)
D) Matrix (dual reporting lines)

**Answer: C) Hybrid (mix of centralized and decentralized)**

---

**4. At what stage of the scalability model would a company typically have 4-10 facilities organized into regional clusters?**

A) Stage 1: Single Facility
B) Stage 2: Initial Expansion
C) Stage 3: Regional Network
D) Stage 4: National/Multi-Regional

**Answer: C) Stage 3: Regional Network**

---

**5. Which function is typically best handled at the CENTRALIZED level in a hybrid model?**

A) Daily production scheduling
B) Local hiring decisions
C) Technology platforms
D) Tactical problem-solving

**Answer: C) Technology platforms**

---

**6. What is the primary advantage of a centralized operations model?**

A) Local decision-making authority
B) Market responsiveness
C) Strong standardization and economies of scale
D) Entrepreneurial culture

**Answer: C) Strong standardization and economies of scale**

---

**7. In the enterprise operations scorecard, which of the following is NOT one of the five major performance categories?**

A) Financial Performance
B) Operational Excellence
C) Marketing Effectiveness
D) Employee Engagement

**Answer: C) Marketing Effectiveness**

---

**8. What is a key risk of expanding too quickly before operations are stable?**

A) Over-capitalization
B) Too much standardization
C) Premature expansion leading to quality issues and management gaps
D) Excessive local autonomy

**Answer: C) Premature expansion leading to quality issues and management gaps**

---

**9. Which level of decision-making authority should be responsible for capital investments over a certain threshold (e.g., $X)?**

A) Facility level
B) Regional level
C) Corporate level
D) Individual department managers

**Answer: C) Corporate level**

---

**10. What is the recommended communication frequency for tactical-level multi-site operations coordination?**

A) Daily
B) Weekly
C) Monthly
D) Quarterly

**Answer: B) Weekly**
