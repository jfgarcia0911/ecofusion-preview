### Questions

**1. What does ISO 9001 certification focus on?**

A) Environmental management
B) Quality management systems
C) Food safety only
D) Occupational health and safety

**Answer: B) Quality management systems**

---

**2. What does HACCP stand for?**

A) Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points
B) Health and Care Compliance Program
C) Harvest Analysis and Crop Control Process
D) High Accuracy Climate Control Protocol

**Answer: A) Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points**

---

**3. What is GAP certification in agriculture?**

A) Gap Year Program
B) Good Agricultural Practices
C) General Accounting Principles
D) Growing Area Protection

**Answer: B) Good Agricultural Practices**

---

**4. How many principles are in the HACCP system?**

A) 5
B) 7
C) 10
D) 12

**Answer: B) 7**

---

**5. What is an "internal audit" in quality management?**

A) Financial audit by company accountants
B) Systematic examination of quality system processes to ensure compliance and identify improvements
C) Employee background checks
D) Building inspection

**Answer: B) Systematic examination of quality system processes to ensure compliance and identify improvements**

---

**6. What does SQF certification stand for?**

A) Superior Quality Foods
B) Safe Quality Food
C) Standardized Quantified Farming
D) Special Quick Freezing

**Answer: B) Safe Quality Food**

---

**7. What is "corrective action" in quality management?**

A) Punishment for mistakes
B) Action taken to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity or problem
C) Positive reinforcement
D) Employee training only

**Answer: B) Action taken to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity or problem**

---

**8. What is the purpose of "document control" in quality systems?**

A) Organizing filing cabinets
B) Ensuring documents are current, approved, accessible, and properly managed throughout their lifecycle
C) Preventing employees from reading documents
D) Creating more paperwork

**Answer: B) Ensuring documents are current, approved, accessible, and properly managed throughout their lifecycle**

---

**9. What is a "non-conformance" in quality management?**

A) Employee dress code violation
B) Failure to meet specified requirements or standards
C) Equipment that doesn't fit
D) Unusual plant shapes

**Answer: B) Failure to meet specified requirements or standards**

---

**10. Which certification is most focused on food safety for fresh produce?**

A) ISO 14001
B) OHSAS 18001
C) FSMA Produce Safety Rule compliance
D) ISO 27001

**Answer: C) FSMA Produce Safety Rule compliance**
