### Questions

**1. What does "Lean" manufacturing focus on eliminating?**

A) Employees
B) Waste (Muda) in all forms while maximizing value
C) Profits
D) Equipment

**Answer: B) Waste (Muda) in all forms while maximizing value**

---

**2. How many types of waste are identified in traditional Lean methodology?**

A) 3
B) 5
C) 7
D) 10

**Answer: C) 7**

---

**3. What does DMAIC stand for in Six Sigma?**

A) Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control
B) Design, Make, Assemble, Inspect, Complete
C) Develop, Manage, Approve, Implement, Close
D) Direct, Monitor, Assess, Innovate, Communicate

**Answer: A) Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control**

---

**4. What is a "Kaizen event"?**

A) A Japanese celebration
B) A focused, short-term project to improve a specific process or area
C) Annual company party
D) Long-term strategic planning

**Answer: B) A focused, short-term project to improve a specific process or area**

---

**5. What is "Value Stream Mapping" used for?**

A) Creating treasure maps
B) Visualizing and analyzing the flow of materials and information required to deliver products to customers
C) Employee value assessment
D) Financial valuation

**Answer: B) Visualizing and analyzing the flow of materials and information required to deliver products to customers**

---

**6. What is the goal of "5S" methodology?**

A) Five-star ratings
B) Workplace organization and standardization: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain
C) Five sigma quality
D) Five-year planning

**Answer: B) Workplace organization and standardization: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain**

---

**7. What does "Poka-Yoke" mean?**

A) A type of plant
B) Error-proofing or mistake-proofing systems and processes
C) Japanese management style
D) A harvesting technique

**Answer: B) Error-proofing or mistake-proofing systems and processes**

---

**8. What is the "Plan-Do-Check-Act" (PDCA) cycle used for?**

A) Daily scheduling
B) Continuous improvement through iterative problem-solving and learning
C) Employee hiring
D) Financial planning only

**Answer: B) Continuous improvement through iterative problem-solving and learning**

---

**9. What level of quality does "Six Sigma" target?**

A) 3.4 defects per thousand opportunities
B) 3.4 defects per million opportunities
C) Zero defects (100% perfect)
D) 50% defect reduction

**Answer: B) 3.4 defects per million opportunities**

---

**10. What is "Gemba" in Lean terminology?**

A) Corporate headquarters
B) The actual place where work is done (e.g., production floor)
C) Break room
D) Parking lot

**Answer: B) The actual place where work is done (e.g., production floor)**
