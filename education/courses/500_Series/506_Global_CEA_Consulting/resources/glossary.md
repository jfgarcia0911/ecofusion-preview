# Global CEA Consulting Glossary

## A

**Addressable Market**
The portion of the total market that a company can realistically serve, considering factors like geography, technology capabilities, and target customer segments.

**Agronomic Performance**
The effectiveness of crop production measured by yield, quality, consistency, and resource efficiency.

**As-Built Documentation**
Final construction drawings and documentation showing the facility as actually built, including any changes from original design.

**Assumptions Log**
Documentation of all key assumptions underlying analysis, projections, or recommendations in consulting engagements.

**Autonomation**
Automation with human intelligence; systems that can detect problems and alert operators while running automatically under normal conditions.

---

## B

**Bankable Feasibility Study**
Enhanced feasibility study with additional rigor and independent verification meeting lender requirements for project financing.

**Benchmarking**
Comparing performance metrics against industry standards, best practices, or peer organizations to identify improvement opportunities.

**Billable Hours**
Hours worked that can be charged to clients, typically 50-70% of total work hours for consultants (remainder is business development, admin, etc.).

**Blended Rate**
Average hourly rate calculated across a consulting team with different individual rates (senior, mid-level, junior consultants).

**Bottom-Up Market Sizing**
Approach to estimating market size by aggregating specific customer demand to calculate total market opportunity.

**Business Development**
Activities to identify and acquire new clients including marketing, networking, proposals, and relationship building.

---

## C

**CapEx (Capital Expenditure)**
One-time investment in assets like facility, equipment, and technology required to establish operations.

**Capacity Utilization**
Percentage of designed production capacity actually utilized; e.g., producing 85,000 kg/year in facility designed for 100,000 kg represents 85% utilization.

**Change Order**
Formal modification to project scope, schedule, or budget requiring documentation and approval.

**Client Acquisition**
Process of converting prospects into engaged clients through discovery, proposals, negotiation, and contracting.

**Commissioning**
Systematic process of verifying that all facility systems and assemblies are designed, installed, tested, operated, and maintained according to design intent.

**Competitive Positioning**
How a company or offering is perceived relative to competitors on key dimensions like price, quality, technology, or service.

**Confidentiality**
Ethical and legal obligation to protect client proprietary information and not disclose without permission.

**Conflict of Interest**
Situation where consultant's judgment or objectivity could be compromised by competing interests, relationships, or incentives.

**Contingency**
Budget reserve (typically 10-15% of capital or project cost) for unforeseen costs or risks.

**Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA)**
Agricultural production in structures with environmental control (temperature, humidity, light, CO2) including greenhouses, vertical farms, and plant factories.

**CQ (Cultural Quotient)**
Measure of cultural intelligence - ability to function effectively across national, ethnic, and organizational cultures.

**Cultural Due Diligence**
Research and assessment of cultural factors that might impact business relationships, operations, or success in international contexts.

---

## D

**Daily Light Integral (DLI)**
Total amount of photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) received per day, measured in mol/m²/day; key factor in plant growth.

**Deliverable**
Tangible work product provided to client as part of consulting engagement (report, presentation, model, recommendation, etc.).

**Design-Build**
Project delivery method where single entity (design-builder) contracts for both design and construction, offering single-point responsibility.

**DIF (Temperature Difference)**
Difference between day and night temperature used to control plant stem elongation and morphology.

**Discount Rate**
Interest rate used to calculate present value of future cash flows in NPV analysis, reflecting time value of money and risk.

**Due Diligence**
Comprehensive assessment and investigation conducted to evaluate an investment, acquisition, or major decision; in CEA typically technical, financial, and commercial analysis.

**Duty of Care**
Legal obligation of professionals to adhere to reasonable standard of care, competence, and diligence when providing services.

---

## E

**Earned Value Management (EVM)**
Project management technique comparing planned progress and cost to actual performance to forecast completion.

**EBITDA**
Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization; measure of operating profitability.

**Economies of Scale**
Cost advantages gained by increasing production volume, as fixed costs are spread over more units.

**Engagement**
Specific consulting project or relationship with defined scope, timeline, deliverables, and fees.

**EPCM (Engineering, Procurement, Construction Management)**
Delivery model where firm provides engineering, manages procurement, and oversees construction on behalf of owner.

**Executive Summary**
Concise overview (typically 5-10% of full document length) presenting key findings, conclusions, and recommendations for senior decision-makers.

---

## F

**Feasibility Study**
Comprehensive analysis evaluating technical, market, financial, and organizational viability of a proposed project to inform go/no-go decision.

**Fixed Fee**
Consulting pricing model where total project fee is agreed upfront regardless of actual hours spent (vs. hourly or daily rates).

**Force Majeure**
Unforeseeable circumstances preventing contract fulfillment (natural disasters, war, pandemics) typically releasing parties from obligations.

**Functional Testing**
Verification that equipment and systems perform according to specifications under design operating conditions.

---

## G

**Gantt Chart**
Visual project timeline showing tasks, durations, dependencies, and milestones as horizontal bars along a calendar.

**GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)**
Food safety certification program with standards for produce production, handling, and traceability.

**Go-to-Market Strategy**
Plan for how products/services will be marketed, sold, and distributed to reach target customers.

**Greenfield Investment**
Building new facility from ground up rather than acquiring or retrofitting existing operation.

**Gross Margin**
Revenue minus cost of goods sold, expressed as percentage: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100%.

---

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points)**
Systematic preventive approach to food safety identifying physical, chemical, and biological hazards in production.

**High-Context Culture**
Cultural communication style relying heavily on implicit messages, nonverbal cues, and shared understanding (Asian, Middle Eastern cultures).

**Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions**
Framework for understanding cultural differences across dimensions like power distance, individualism vs. collectivism, and uncertainty avoidance.

**Hurdle Rate**
Minimum acceptable rate of return on investment used to evaluate project viability; below hurdle rate, project rejected.

---

## I

**Intellectual Property (IP)**
Intangible assets including patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and proprietary methodologies.

**Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
Discount rate that makes net present value of all cash flows equal to zero; represents annualized return on investment.

**IPM (Integrated Pest Management)**
Ecosystem-based pest control strategy combining biological, cultural, physical, and chemical tools to minimize risks.

---

## J

**Joint Venture (JV)**
Business arrangement where two or more parties agree to pool resources for specific project or business activity, sharing profits, losses, and control.

**Job-to-be-Done**
Framework focusing on customer needs - what "job" customers are "hiring" product or service to accomplish.

---

## K

**Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**
Measurable value demonstrating how effectively organization achieves key business objectives.

**Kickoff Meeting**
Initial project meeting bringing together consultant and client teams to align on objectives, scope, approach, roles, and working relationships.

**Knowledge Management**
Systematic process of capturing, organizing, and sharing institutional knowledge, methodologies, and insights.

---

## L

**Letter of Intent (LOI)**
Non-binding document outlining preliminary agreement and willingness to enter into future contract; shows serious interest.

**Lessons Learned**
Documented insights, successes, and failures from completed project used to improve future performance.

**Liability Insurance**
Professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance protecting consultants against claims of inadequate work or negligent advice.

**Limitation of Liability**
Contract clause capping consultant's financial exposure for claims, typically to fees paid or insurance coverage.

**Low-Context Culture**
Cultural communication style relying on explicit verbal messages with minimal implicit meaning (North American, Northern European cultures).

---

## M

**Market Entry Strategy**
Plan for entering new geographic or customer markets including mode of entry, positioning, partnerships, and implementation.

**Market Sizing**
Process of estimating total available market opportunity using top-down, bottom-up, or value-theory approaches.

**Methodology**
Systematic, structured approach to consulting work including frameworks, processes, tools, and techniques.

**Milestone**
Significant point or event in project timeline, often tied to deliverables or payments.

**Mitigation Strategy**
Actions to reduce likelihood or impact of identified risks.

---

## N

**NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)**
Legal contract establishing confidentiality obligations when sharing sensitive information.

**Net Present Value (NPV)**
Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment, discounted at specific rate; positive NPV indicates profitable project.

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)**
Hydroponic method where thin film of nutrient solution flows through channels, roots partially submerged.

**Non-Billable Hours**
Time spent on activities that can't be charged to clients (business development, admin, training); typically 30-40% of consultant time.

---

## O

**OpEx (Operating Expenditure)**
Ongoing costs to run operations including labor, utilities, supplies, maintenance, and overhead.

**Opportunity Cost**
Value of next best alternative foregone when making a decision; implicit cost of chosen option.

**Organizational Feasibility**
Assessment of whether organization has or can develop capabilities, resources, and capacity needed to execute project successfully.

---

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)**
Light wavelengths (400-700nm) usable by plants for photosynthesis; measured in μmol/m²/s.

**Payback Period**
Time required to recover initial investment through project cash flows; shorter payback indicates faster return.

**Porter's Five Forces**
Framework analyzing competitive forces: threat of new entrants, bargaining power of suppliers and buyers, threat of substitutes, competitive rivalry.

**Power Distance**
Cultural dimension measuring extent to which less powerful members accept and expect unequal power distribution.

**Pre-Feasibility Study**
Preliminary, high-level feasibility analysis to screen opportunities before investing in comprehensive study.

**Proposal**
Formal document presenting consultant's understanding, approach, qualifications, and pricing for potential engagement.

**Proprietary Information**
Confidential business information providing competitive advantage that owner has right to protect from competitors.

**Punch List**
List of deficiencies or incomplete work items identified during final project inspection requiring correction before acceptance.

---

## Q

**Qualification**
Process of assessing whether potential client engagement is good fit based on needs, budget, timeline, expertise required, and likelihood of success.

**Quality Assurance (QA)**
Planned and systematic activities ensuring deliverables and processes meet defined quality standards.

**Quality Control (QC)**
Operational techniques and activities used to fulfill quality requirements and identify defects.

---

## R

**Request for Proposal (RFP)**
Formal solicitation document inviting consultants to submit proposals for specified work.

**Retainer**
Ongoing consulting arrangement where client pays regular fee (monthly/quarterly) for defined availability and support.

**Return on Investment (ROI)**
Measure of profitability calculated as: (Gain from Investment - Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment × 100%.

**Risk Assessment**
Systematic process of identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and prioritizing risks to inform mitigation strategies.

**Risk Matrix**
Visual tool plotting risks by likelihood and impact to prioritize attention and response.

**Root Cause Analysis**
Problem-solving methodology to identify fundamental cause of issues rather than addressing symptoms (uses tools like 5 Whys, fishbone diagrams).

---

## S

**Scope Creep**
Uncontrolled expansion of project scope without adjustments to time, cost, and resources; major cause of project problems.

**Scope of Work (SOW)**
Detailed description of work to be performed, deliverables, timelines, and acceptance criteria.

**Sensitivity Analysis**
Examination of how changes in key variables (price, costs, yields) impact project outcomes and returns.

**Service Level Agreement (SLA)**
Contract defining expected level of service including response times, deliverables, and performance metrics.

**Stakeholder**
Individual or group with interest or concern in project, able to affect or be affected by project outcomes.

**Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)**
Documented step-by-step instructions for routine operations ensuring consistency and quality.

**SWOT Analysis**
Framework assessing Strengths, Weaknesses (internal), Opportunities, and Threats (external).

---

## T

**Technical Feasibility**
Assessment of whether project can be built and operated successfully with available technology, resources, and knowledge.

**Thought Leadership**
Positioning as recognized expert through speaking, writing, research, and sharing innovative ideas and insights.

**Top-Down Market Sizing**
Approach starting with total addressable market and narrowing to specific opportunity through filters and assumptions.

**Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)**
Comprehensive assessment of all costs associated with acquiring, operating, and maintaining asset over lifetime.

**Traceability**
Ability to track product through all stages of production, processing, and distribution from origin to consumer.

**Trust Equation**
Framework for building trust: Trust = (Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy) / Self-Orientation.

**Turnkey Solution**
Complete, ready-to-operate system or facility provided by single vendor, typically with performance guarantees.

---

## U

**Uncertainty Avoidance**
Cultural dimension measuring degree to which members feel uncomfortable with uncertainty and ambiguity.

**Utilization Rate**
Percentage of available time spent on billable work; for consultants, typically 50-70% (remainder is business development, admin, etc.).

---

## V

**Value-Based Pricing**
Pricing methodology based on perceived value to client rather than costs or hours required.

**Value Engineering (VE)**
Systematic approach to improving project value by examining function, alternatives, and cost-benefit trade-offs.

**Value Proposition**
Clear statement of tangible results client gets from engaging consultant; why client should choose you.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)**
Difference between amount of moisture air can hold and actual moisture content; key environmental parameter for plant growth.

**Vertical Farming**
Practice of growing crops in vertically stacked layers, typically in controlled indoor environments using artificial lighting.

---

## W

**Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**
Hierarchical decomposition of project into smaller, manageable components organized by deliverables and work packages.

**Working Capital**
Funds needed to cover day-to-day operations; typically 3-6 months of operating expenses for startup capital budgets.

**Work Product**
Any tangible or intangible deliverable resulting from consulting work (reports, models, presentations, recommendations, etc.).

---

## Y

**Yield**
Amount of product harvested per unit area per unit time; for CEA typically expressed as kg/m²/year.

---

## Z

**Zero-Based Budgeting**
Budgeting approach starting from zero each period, requiring justification for all expenses rather than incrementing previous budget.

## UNITS OF MEASURE

**Production:**
- kg/m²/year - kilograms per square meter per year (yield)
- kg/FTE/year - kilograms per full-time equivalent per year (labor productivity)
- kg/week - kilograms per week (production rate)

**Energy:**
- kWh - kilowatt-hour (energy consumption)
- kWh/kg - kilowatt-hours per kilogram (energy intensity)
- W/m² - watts per square meter (power density)

**Light:**
- μmol/m²/s - micromoles per square meter per second (light intensity/PPFD)
- mol/m²/day - moles per square meter per day (DLI)

**Water:**
- L/kg - liters per kilogram (water use efficiency)
- L/day - liters per day (water consumption)

**Area:**
- m² - square meters
- sq ft - square feet (1 m² = 10.764 sq ft)
- acre - 4,047 m² or 43,560 sq ft

**Financial:**
- $ or USD - United States Dollars
- $/sq ft - dollars per square foot (CapEx intensity)
- $/kg - dollars per kilogram (price or cost)
- $/kWh - dollars per kilowatt-hour (energy price)
