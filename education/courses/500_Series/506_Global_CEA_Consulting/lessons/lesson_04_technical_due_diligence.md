# Lesson 4: Technical Due Diligence

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Design and execute technical due diligence engagements
- Conduct comprehensive facility and operations assessments
- Evaluate technology systems and equipment condition
- Assess agronomic performance and crop management
- Identify technical risks and operational issues
- Develop actionable recommendations and risk mitigation strategies
- Prepare professional due diligence reports for investors and lenders

## Introduction

Technical due diligence is a critical service for investors, lenders, and acquirers evaluating CEA opportunities. Whether assessing a startup seeking investment, an operating facility for acquisition, or a project under development, rigorous technical assessment identifies risks, validates performance claims, and informs transaction decisions. This lesson covers proven methodologies for conducting thorough technical due diligence on CEA operations and projects.

## 1. Due Diligence Framework

### Purpose and Scope

**Primary Objectives:**
- Validate technical claims and performance representations
- Identify technical risks and operational challenges
- Assess technology condition and maintenance needs
- Evaluate operational capabilities and management
- Determine capital expenditure requirements
- Support valuation and transaction structuring

**Typical Engagement Triggers:**
- Investment due diligence (equity or debt)
- Merger and acquisition assessment
- Partnership or joint venture evaluation
- Technology licensing or transfer
- Lender technical review (bankable feasibility)
- Insurance or risk assessment

### Due Diligence Levels

**Level 1: Desktop Review (1-2 weeks, $15K-30K)**
- Document review and analysis
- Management interviews
- High-level risk identification
- Red flag screening
- Go/no-go recommendation

**Level 2: Standard Due Diligence (3-4 weeks, $40K-75K)**
- Comprehensive document review
- Site visit and physical inspection
- Stakeholder interviews
- Performance data analysis
- Risk assessment and quantification
- Detailed findings report

**Level 3: Deep Due Diligence (6-8 weeks, $75K-150K)**
- Exhaustive document review
- Multiple site visits
- Independent testing and verification
- Third-party expert engagement
- Detailed technical audit
- Comprehensive report with warranties

### Information Request List

**Corporate and Legal:**
- Articles of incorporation and bylaws
- Shareholder agreements
- Material contracts and agreements
- Permits and licenses
- Compliance documentation
- Insurance policies and claims history
- Litigation and disputes

**Technical Documentation:**
- Facility design drawings and specifications
- Equipment lists and specifications
- Operation and maintenance manuals
- Commissioning and performance test results
- Technology supplier contracts and warranties
- Intellectual property documentation

**Operational Data:**
- Production data (yields, quality, waste)
- Crop records and protocols
- Maintenance records and schedules
- Quality control data
- Food safety records
- Incident and downtime logs
- Energy and resource consumption

**Financial Information:**
- Capital expenditure records
- Operating cost data by category
- Revenue and sales data
- Customer contracts and pricing
- Budget vs. actual analysis
- Future CapEx plans and budgets

**Human Resources:**
- Organizational charts
- Key personnel resumes
- Compensation and benefits
- Training programs and records
- Employee turnover data
- Labor relations and agreements

## 2. Facility and Infrastructure Assessment

### Site Evaluation

**Location and Access:**
- Proximity to markets and customers
- Transportation access and logistics
- Labor availability and housing
- Utility infrastructure availability
- Future expansion potential
- Zoning and land use compliance

**Physical Site:**
- Land ownership or lease terms
- Site area and configuration
- Topography and drainage
- Soil conditions (if relevant)
- Environmental conditions
- Neighboring uses and impacts

**Building and Structures:**
- Age and construction type
- Structural condition
- Foundation and floor systems
- Roof condition and waterproofing
- Fire protection systems
- Life safety and egress
- ADA compliance

### Utility Systems Assessment

**Electrical Systems:**

**Review:**
- Service capacity and utilization
- Load calculations and demand
- Distribution system condition
- Backup power systems
- Power quality and reliability
- Utility rate structures

**Inspection:**
- Main service and transformers
- Switchgear and distribution panels
- Branch circuit distribution
- Grounding and bonding
- Emergency systems
- Lighting (non-growing)

**Testing (if needed):**
- Infrared thermography
- Power quality monitoring
- Ground resistance testing

**Key Findings to Report:**
- Current capacity vs. demand
- Upgrade or expansion needs
- Safety issues or code violations
- Energy efficiency opportunities
- Maintenance requirements

**Water Systems:**

**Review:**
- Water source and capacity
- Water quality testing results
- Treatment systems
- Storage and distribution
- Wastewater handling
- Regulatory compliance

**Inspection:**
- Source wells or municipal connections
- Treatment equipment condition
- Storage tanks and systems
- Distribution piping and valves
- Fertigation systems
- Wastewater systems

**Issues to Identify:**
- Capacity constraints
- Water quality problems
- Treatment system performance
- Contamination risks
- Regulatory compliance gaps
- Maintenance needs

**HVAC and Climate Control:**

**Review:**
- Design conditions and loads
- System capacity and sizing
- Control sequences and setpoints
- Performance data and efficiency
- Maintenance records

**Inspection:**
- Mechanical equipment condition
- Ductwork and distribution
- Controls and sensors
- Filtration systems
- Dehumidification equipment
- Heat recovery systems

**Testing:**
- Temperature and humidity mapping
- Airflow measurements
- Control system verification
- Energy consumption analysis

**Assessment:**
- Ability to maintain environmental setpoints
- Energy efficiency and optimization opportunities
- Equipment condition and remaining life
- Control system functionality
- Maintenance adequacy

### Growing Systems Evaluation

**Structures and Racking:**
- Design and structural adequacy
- Material condition and corrosion
- Stability and safety
- Accessibility for maintenance
- Modularity and expansion capability

**Irrigation/Fertigation Systems:**
- System design and capacity
- Pump and valve condition
- Distribution uniformity
- Mixing and injection systems
- Filtration and treatment
- Monitoring and controls

**Lighting Systems:**
- Fixture type and specifications
- Light output and spectrum
- Distribution and uniformity
- Electrical condition and safety
- Control systems
- Expected remaining life

**Environmental Controls:**
- Sensor coverage and accuracy
- Control system functionality
- Integration and automation
- Data logging and analysis
- User interface and accessibility

## 3. Technology Assessment

### Technology Stack Evaluation

**Hardware Assessment:**

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Technology maturity and proven application
- Performance vs. specifications
- Reliability and uptime
- Maintenance requirements
- Parts availability and support
- Expected remaining useful life
- Obsolescence risk

**Key Systems to Assess:**
- Growing structures and systems
- Lighting systems and fixtures
- Climate control equipment
- Irrigation and fertigation
- Material handling equipment
- Post-harvest processing
- Monitoring and control systems

**Software and Controls:**

**Assessment Areas:**
- System architecture and design
- Functionality and capabilities
- Integration and interoperability
- Data management and security
- User interface and ease of use
- Vendor support and updates
- Customization and flexibility

**Red Flags:**
- Custom software with no vendor support
- Proprietary systems with single source dependency
- Outdated or unsupported software versions
- Poor integration between systems
- Inadequate cybersecurity
- Insufficient backup and disaster recovery

### Vendor and Supply Chain Assessment

**Technology Vendors:**

**Evaluation Factors:**
- Vendor financial stability
- Market position and reputation
- Quality of support and service
- Parts availability and lead times
- Warranty coverage and responsiveness
- Relationship quality
- Alternative sources availability

**Risk Assessment:**
- Vendor financial distress or failure
- Single-source dependency
- Proprietary technology lock-in
- Long lead times for critical parts
- Warranty disputes or non-performance
- Technology obsolescence

**Input Suppliers:**

**Assessment:**
- Supplier diversity and alternatives
- Pricing and contract terms
- Quality and reliability
- Delivery performance
- Relationship strength
- Future availability and risk

## 4. Operational Assessment

### Production Performance Analysis

**Yield Analysis:**

**Data to Review:**
- Yield by crop and variety
- Yield trends over time
- Yield vs. targets or benchmarks
- Yield variability and consistency
- Factors impacting yield

**Analysis Questions:**
- Are yields meeting design specifications?
- How do yields compare to industry benchmarks?
- What explains yield variations?
- Are yields improving or declining over time?
- What is realistic sustainable yield expectation?

**Example Yield Assessment:**

```
Facility Claims: 300 kg/m²/year leafy greens

Data Analysis:
- Actual average: 245 kg/m²/year (82% of claim)
- Range: 200-280 kg/m²/year
- Recent trend: Improving (was 220, now 260)
- Best crops: Baby lettuce (280), Arugula (270)
- Worst crops: Kale (200), Spinach (210)

Assessment:
- Current yields 18% below claims (significant gap)
- Performance improving with experience
- Variation by crop type (management or environmental)
- Realistic Year 3+ expectation: 270 kg/m²/year
- Risk: May not achieve claimed performance
```

**Quality Assessment:**

**Evaluation:**
- Product quality standards and metrics
- Quality control processes and data
- Customer complaints and returns
- Quality trends over time
- Food safety records and incidents
- Quality-related costs

**Key Concerns:**
- Consistent quality issues
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Food safety incidents or near-misses
- High waste or rejection rates
- Quality control gaps

**Capacity Utilization:**

**Analysis:**
- Design capacity vs. actual production
- Capacity utilization trends
- Bottlenecks and constraints
- Downtime causes and frequency
- Expansion potential

**Example:**

```
Design Capacity: 100,000 kg/year
Actual Production:
- Year 1: 45,000 kg (45%)
- Year 2: 68,000 kg (68%)
- Current: 75,000 kg (75%)

Analysis:
- Still ramping up (reasonable for Year 3)
- Primary constraint: Growing area utilization
- Secondary constraint: Labor during harvest peaks
- Improvement opportunities: Process optimization
- Realistic steady-state: 85-90% (industry standard)
```

### Agronomic Assessment

**Growing Protocols:**

**Review:**
- Crop selection and varieties
- Growing recipes and parameters
- Nutrient formulations and management
- IPM and crop protection
- Harvest timing and protocols
- Post-harvest handling

**Assessment:**
- Based on best practices and science?
- Optimized for facility and market?
- Consistently followed?
- Documented and controlled?
- Continuous improvement process?

**Crop Management:**

**Evaluation:**
- Grower expertise and experience
- Decision-making processes
- Response to issues and problems
- Use of data and analytics
- Innovation and optimization

**Red Flags:**
- Frequent crop failures or losses
- Inconsistent practices
- Poor problem-solving
- Lack of documentation
- No performance improvement

**Pest and Disease Management:**

**Assessment:**
- Pest and disease pressure and history
- Prevention and monitoring systems
- Treatment approaches and effectiveness
- Biosecurity protocols
- Compliance with regulations

**Concerns:**
- Recurring pest or disease problems
- Inadequate prevention or monitoring
- Excessive or inappropriate pesticide use
- Biosecurity weaknesses
- Regulatory compliance issues

### Operations Management Assessment

**Standard Operating Procedures:**

**Review:**
- Comprehensiveness and completeness
- Quality and clarity
- Training and implementation
- Compliance and adherence
- Update and revision processes

**Maturity Assessment:**
- **Level 1 (Ad Hoc)**: No written SOPs, tribal knowledge
- **Level 2 (Documented)**: Basic SOPs exist but inconsistently followed
- **Level 3 (Standardized)**: Comprehensive SOPs, generally followed
- **Level 4 (Managed)**: SOPs integrated with training, auditing, improvement
- **Level 5 (Optimized)**: Continuous SOP optimization based on data

**Maintenance Programs:**

**Assessment:**
- Preventive maintenance schedules
- Maintenance execution and tracking
- Spare parts inventory management
- Equipment condition tracking
- Maintenance team capabilities

**Effectiveness Indicators:**
- Equipment uptime and reliability
- Mean time between failures (MTBF)
- Maintenance cost trends
- Emergency vs. planned maintenance ratio
- Backlog of maintenance needs

**Quality and Food Safety:**

**Evaluation:**
- Quality management system maturity
- Food safety plan and HACCP
- Testing and monitoring programs
- Certifications (GAP, organic, etc.)
- Audit results and corrective actions
- Traceability systems

**Compliance:**
- Regulatory requirements
- Certification standards
- Customer requirements
- Insurance requirements
- Best practices

## 6. Risk Identification and Assessment

### Technical Risk Categories

**Design and Engineering Risks:**
- Facility design inadequacies
- System sizing or capacity issues
- Integration and compatibility problems
- Code compliance gaps
- Expansion constraints

**Equipment and Technology Risks:**
- Equipment condition and reliability
- Technology obsolescence
- Vendor support concerns
- Single-source dependencies
- Inadequate redundancy

**Operational Risks:**
- Production performance below expectations
- Quality or food safety issues
- Maintenance inadequacy
- Operational inefficiencies
- Resource constraints (water, energy, labor)

**Management and Organizational Risks:**
- Management team gaps or weaknesses
- Organizational capacity constraints
- Key person dependencies
- High staff turnover
- Inadequate systems and processes

### Risk Quantification

**Impact Assessment:**

**Financial Impact:**
- Cost to remediate or mitigate
- Ongoing operational cost impact
- Revenue impact from performance issues
- Capital requirements for improvements
- Value impact on transaction

**Example:**

```
Risk: HVAC system undersized, cannot maintain setpoints in summer

Technical Impact:
- Temperature excursions 5-8°C above optimal
- Affects 40% of growing area
- Reduces yield by estimated 15-20%
- Limits crop selection (heat-sensitive crops)

Financial Impact:
- Revenue loss: $180K/year (15% yield reduction)
- Remediation cost: $450K (system upgrade)
- Timeline: 6 months design/installation
- Disruption during installation

Recommendation: Critical risk, address before transaction close or escrow funds for remediation
```

**Probability Assessment:**

- **Certain**: Will definitely occur (90-100%)
- **Highly Likely**: Very likely to occur (70-90%)
- **Likely**: More likely than not (50-70%)
- **Possible**: Could occur (25-50%)
- **Unlikely**: Probably won't occur (10-25%)
- **Rare**: Very unlikely (0-10%)

**Risk Matrix:**

```
         Critical  Major  Moderate  Minor
Certain     RED     RED     YELLOW   YELLOW
Likely      RED     YELLOW  YELLOW   GREEN
Possible    YELLOW  YELLOW  GREEN    GREEN
Unlikely    YELLOW  GREEN   GREEN    GREEN

RED = Deal breaker or major value impact
YELLOW = Address or price into transaction
GREEN = Monitor, document, but acceptable
```

## 7. Due Diligence Reporting

### Findings Documentation

**Issue Tracking:**

**Standard Format:**
- Issue Number and Category
- Description and Details
- Impact Assessment (technical and financial)
- Risk Rating
- Recommendation and Remediation
- Cost Estimate and Timeline
- Responsible Party

**Example:**

```
ISSUE #12: Growing System Corrosion
Category: Equipment Condition
Severity: MODERATE

Description:
Significant corrosion observed on steel racking in growing zones 2 and 3, affecting approximately 30% of growing area. Corrosion is surface rust progressing to material loss in some areas. Attributed to high humidity and inadequate protective coating.

Technical Impact:
- Structural integrity concerns if progression continues
- Contamination risk to crops
- Safety hazard from weakened structures
- Equipment life reduction (15+ years expected, may be 8-10 years)

Financial Impact:
- Replacement cost: $180,000 (30% of racking)
- Accelerated depreciation impact on valuation
- Potential crop losses if failure occurs
- Safety liability exposure

Recommendation:
1. Immediate: Engineer structural assessment of affected areas
2. Short-term: Implement corrosion treatment program
3. Medium-term: Budget for racking replacement (Years 4-6)
4. Long-term: Improve humidity control to prevent recurrence

Cost Estimate:
- Engineering assessment: $8,000
- Corrosion treatment: $15,000
- Future replacement reserve: $180,000
- Humidity improvements: $45,000

Transaction Impact: Adjust valuation or require seller remediation/escrow
```

### Due Diligence Report Structure

**1. Executive Summary (3-5 pages)**
- Overall assessment and conclusion
- Key findings and risks
- Critical issues requiring attention
- Impact on transaction value
- Recommendations

**2. Introduction and Methodology**
- Scope and objectives
- Information reviewed
- Site visits and interviews
- Analysis approach
- Limitations and assumptions

**3. Facility Overview**
- Project description
- Technology and systems
- Production capabilities
- Current operations and performance

**4. Technical Assessment**
- Site and infrastructure
- Building and utilities
- Growing systems and equipment
- Technology and controls
- Findings and issues

**5. Operational Assessment**
- Production performance
- Agronomics and crop management
- Maintenance and reliability
- Quality and food safety
- Findings and issues

**6. Management and Organization**
- Management team
- Organizational structure
- Staffing and capabilities
- Systems and processes
- Findings and issues

**7. Risk Assessment**
- Risk identification and categorization
- Risk prioritization and quantification
- Impact on value and returns
- Mitigation recommendations

**8. Capital Requirements**
- Immediate repairs and remediation
- Near-term capital needs (1-2 years)
- Longer-term capital requirements
- Cost estimates and priorities

**9. Conclusions and Recommendations**
- Overall technical assessment
- Transaction recommendations
- Value impacts
- Contingencies and conditions
- Post-closing actions

**10. Appendices**
- Detailed findings register
- Site visit photos
- Document review checklist
- Test results and data
- Expert reports
- Management responses

### Report Quality Standards

**Technical Rigor:**
- Thorough and systematic assessment
- Data-driven analysis
- Expert judgment applied appropriately
- Assumptions clearly stated
- Limitations acknowledged

**Objectivity:**
- Independent and unbiased
- Fact-based findings
- Balanced perspective
- Risks and strengths both reported
- Transparent about uncertainties

**Clarity:**
- Clear, concise writing
- Logical organization
- Effective use of visuals
- Technical terms explained
- Executive-level accessibility

**Actionability:**
- Specific, actionable recommendations
- Prioritized by importance and urgency
- Cost estimates provided
- Implementation guidance
- Impact on transaction clearly stated

## 8. Case Study: Vertical Farm Acquisition Due Diligence

### Situation

**Client**: Private equity firm
**Target**: Vertical farming company seeking $15M Series B
**Claims**:
- 200,000 kg/year capacity
- $3.2M annual revenue
- 40% gross margins
- Industry-leading technology

**DD Scope**: 4-week standard due diligence, $60K fee

### Due Diligence Process

**Week 1: Document Review**
- Reviewed facility design and engineering
- Analyzed 18 months of production data
- Reviewed financial statements and operating data
- Assessed equipment and technology documentation

**Week 2: Site Visits and Interviews**
- Two-day comprehensive site inspection
- Interviews with CEO, COO, Head Grower, Operations Manager
- Customer interviews (3 major accounts)
- Technology vendor discussions

**Week 3: Analysis**
- Production performance analysis
- Equipment condition assessment
- Financial analysis and validation
- Risk identification and assessment
- Benchmarking against industry data

**Week 4: Reporting**
- Findings documentation
- Financial impact quantification
- Report preparation and client presentation

### Key Findings

**Critical Issues (RED):**

**1. Production Capacity Overstated**
- **Claim**: 200,000 kg/year capacity
- **Finding**: Actual capacity ~140,000 kg/year
- **Issue**: Calculation error in capacity model (24/7 vs. actual growing cycles)
- **Impact**: 30% revenue overstatement, significant valuation impact
- **Recommendation**: Adjust projections and valuation

**2. Equipment Nearing End of Life**
- **Claim**: "State-of-art equipment, long useful life"
- **Finding**: First-generation LED fixtures (7 years old), efficiency degraded 25%, failures increasing
- **Impact**: $850K replacement required within 18 months, not in capital budget
- **Recommendation**: Require seller to address or escrow replacement cost

**Major Issues (YELLOW):**

**3. Margin Claims Not Validated**
- **Claim**: 40% gross margins
- **Finding**: Actual margins 28-32%, but accounting differences
- **Issue**: Some costs misclassified, energy costs understated
- **Impact**: Profitability overstated, operating model less attractive
- **Recommendation**: Restate financials with standardized accounting

**4. Key Person Risk**
- **Finding**: Head Grower is critical to performance, no succession plan, not under long-term contract
- **Risk**: Performance degradation if departures
- **Recommendation**: Secure key person retention agreements as condition of closing

**5. Customer Concentration**
- **Finding**: 65% of revenue from 2 customers, contracts expire within 6 months
- **Risk**: Significant revenue loss if contracts not renewed
- **Recommendation**: Make contract renewal condition of closing or value adjustment

**Moderate Issues (GREEN but Document):**

**6. Maintenance Backlog**
- Finding: $85K of deferred maintenance
- Recommendation: Complete before closing or adjust value

**7. Quality Control Systems**
- Finding: Adequate but not formalized
- Recommendation: Implement post-closing improvement plan

### Financial Impact Summary

```
Initial Valuation: $35M (based on company claims)

Adjustments:
1. Revenue restatement (capacity): -$8M
2. LED replacement requirement: -$2M
3. Margin restatement: -$3M
4. Customer concentration risk: -$2M
5. Maintenance backlog: -$0.5M

Adjusted Valuation: $19.5M (44% reduction)

Transaction Recommendation:
- Proceed with investment BUT at adjusted valuation
- Make lighting replacement seller responsibility
- Require key customer contract renewals before closing
- Secure head grower retention agreement
- Escrow $500K for maintenance backlog
- Post-closing operational improvement plan
```

### Client Outcome

Client used DD findings to:
- Renegotiate valuation from $35M to $22M
- Require seller to replace LED fixtures ($850K)
- Make largest customer contract renewal condition precedent
- Secure 3-year head grower retention agreement with incentives
- Develop 100-day operational improvement plan
- Close transaction with confidence and realistic expectations

DD firm:
- Saved client $13M+ in valuation adjustment
- Identified critical risk factors
- Built strong client relationship
- Engaged for post-close operational advisory ($25K/month, 12 months)
- Referrals to other PE firms in agriculture tech

## Key Takeaways

1. **Technical due diligence** provides independent assessment of CEA facility and operations to inform investment and transaction decisions

2. **Comprehensive assessment** covers facility/infrastructure, technology systems, production performance, operations, and management

3. **Facility evaluation** assesses site, building, utilities, and growing systems for condition, capacity, compliance, and future requirements

4. **Technology assessment** evaluates hardware and software for performance, reliability, vendor support, and obsolescence risk

5. **Operational analysis** validates production performance claims, assesses agronomic practices, and evaluates management systems

6. **Risk identification** systematically identifies technical, operational, and organizational risks with quantified impacts

7. **Professional reporting** documents findings, quantifies financial impacts, and provides actionable recommendations that inform transaction decisions

8. **Value protection** through rigorous technical DD protects client investment by validating claims and identifying hidden risks and costs
