### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of technical due diligence in CEA consulting?

A) To design the facility
B) To validate technical claims and identify risks for investment or acquisition decisions
C) To operate the facility
D) To market the products

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Technical due diligence involves independent verification of technical capabilities, claims, and risks to inform investment, acquisition, or partnership decisions.

---

### Question 2
Which area typically receives insufficient attention during CEA due diligence, leading to post-acquisition surprises?

A) Building structure
B) Operational complexity, staffing requirements, and process documentation
C) Product pricing
D) Marketing materials

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Buyers often focus on technology and infrastructure while underestimating operational complexity, expertise requirements, and the importance of documented processes.

---

### Question 3
When evaluating a CEA facility's performance claims, what is the most reliable verification method?

A) Review marketing materials
B) Accept management representations at face value
C) Independent analysis of production data, yield records, and quality metrics over time
D) Tour the facility once

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Independent verification of actual performance data over sufficient time periods provides the most reliable assessment of capabilities and consistency.

---

### Question 4
In technology due diligence, which question is most critical for assessing proprietary technology?

A) How much did the technology cost to develop?
B) Is the technology truly differentiated, protectable, and does it deliver measurable value?
C) What color is the equipment?
D) Where was it manufactured?

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Technology value depends on genuine differentiation, intellectual property protection, and demonstrated performance advantages, not just development costs or aesthetics.

---

### Question 5
What is the typical scope of a vendor due diligence engagement for CEA equipment suppliers?

A) 1-2 day site visit only
B) Review of sales brochures
C) Comprehensive assessment of technology, manufacturing capability, financial stability, and reference checking
D) Phone interview with CEO

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Vendor due diligence must evaluate technical capabilities, manufacturing quality, financial viability, delivery track record, and customer satisfaction to assess reliability.

---

### Question 6
When conducting operational audits of existing CEA facilities, which finding typically indicates the HIGHEST risk?

A) Minor equipment maintenance issues
B) Lack of documented standard operating procedures and quality control systems
C) Slightly lower yields than industry benchmarks
D) Older computer systems

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Absence of documented processes and quality systems indicates reliance on individual knowledge rather than scalable, consistent systems, creating major operational and quality risks.

---

### Question 7
In financial due diligence for CEA operations, which area most commonly reveals discrepancies?

A) Revenue recognition and true unit economics
B) Office supply expenses
C) Insurance costs
D) Legal fees

**Correct Answer: A**
**Explanation:** Revenue timing, customer concentration, and accurate unit economics (including all direct and indirect costs) are frequently misrepresented or misunderstood.

---

### Question 8
What is the appropriate way to address identified risks in a due diligence report?

A) Ignore minor risks to keep the report positive
B) Only mention risks without quantification
C) Categorize by severity, quantify potential impact, and recommend mitigation strategies
D) List everything as high risk to protect yourself

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Professional due diligence includes risk prioritization, impact quantification, and practical mitigation recommendations to help clients make informed decisions.

---

### Question 9
When should intellectual property (IP) due diligence be conducted?

A) After the acquisition closes
B) Only if litigation is pending
C) Early in the process, as IP issues can be deal-breakers
D) Never necessary for agricultural operations

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** IP issues (ownership, validity, freedom-to-operate, infringement risks) can fundamentally affect value and should be assessed early to avoid late-stage deal complications.

---

### Question 10
What is the typical timeline for comprehensive technical due diligence on a CEA facility or company?

A) 1-2 days
B) 1-2 weeks
C) 4-8 weeks
D) 6-12 months

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Thorough due diligence typically requires 4-8 weeks for document review, site visits, data analysis, expert consultations, reference checking, and report preparation.
