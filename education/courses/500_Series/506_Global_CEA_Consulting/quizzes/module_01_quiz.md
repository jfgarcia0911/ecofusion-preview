### Question 1
What is the PRIMARY characteristic that distinguishes professional consulting from other professional services?

A) Long-term employment relationship
B) Advisory role providing expert recommendations
C) Direct management of client operations
D) Guaranteed outcomes and results

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Professional consulting is characterized by an advisory role where consultants provide expert recommendations and influence through expertise, not direct authority or employment.

---

### Question 2
Which CEA consulting service type focuses on helping investors evaluate whether to proceed with projects?

A) Operational consulting
B) Strategic consulting
C) Feasibility studies and due diligence
D) Technical advisory

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Feasibility studies and due diligence services help investors assess project viability, risks, and returns before making investment decisions.

---

### Question 3
In the consulting lifecycle, which phase involves developing proposals and negotiating contracts?

A) Business Development
B) Client Acquisition
C) Engagement Delivery
D) Client Retention and Growth

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Client Acquisition includes proposal development, presentation, negotiation, and contract execution.

---

### Question 4
Which business model provides the HIGHEST level of control and potential returns but also the highest risk for the consultant?

A) Solo practice
B) In-house consulting (corporate)
C) Boutique firm partnership
D) Virtual/network model

**Correct Answer: A (for individual) or C (for firm building)**
**Explanation:** Solo practice gives maximum control over all decisions with highest personal returns, while also carrying all the risk. Boutique firm provides high control and returns for building institutional value.

---

### Question 5
According to the Institute of Management Consultants, which of these is a VIOLATION of professional ethics?

A) Providing services to two non-competing clients in the same industry
B) Serving two competing clients without their consent
C) Declining an engagement outside your area of expertise
D) Recommending that a client not proceed with a project

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Serving competing clients without their explicit consent creates a conflict of interest and violates ethical standards.

---

### Question 6
What is the PRIMARY benefit of developing a specialized consulting focus (e.g., vertical farming automation)?

A) Ability to charge premium rates for specialized expertise
B) Avoiding competition with generalist consultants
C) Reducing the need for continuous learning
D) Simplifying marketing and business development

**Correct Answer: A**
**Explanation:** Specialized expertise commands premium pricing due to unique value, though all other factors can be secondary benefits.

---

### Question 7
Which service packaging model provides ongoing advisory access for a fixed monthly or quarterly fee?

A) Project-based engagement
B) Day-rate consulting
C) Retainer arrangement
D) Success-based compensation

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Retainer arrangements provide clients ongoing access to advisory services for a regular fee.

---

### Question 8
In professional consulting, what should you do if a potential client requests services outside your area of expertise?

A) Accept the work and learn as you go
B) Decline and refer to an appropriate specialist
C) Accept but outsource the work to an expert
D) Provide general advice based on research

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Professional ethics require working within your competence. The appropriate response is to decline and refer to qualified specialists.

---

### Question 9
Which of the following is the MOST important factor in building trust with consulting clients?

A) Having impressive credentials and degrees
B) Offering the lowest fees in the market
C) Consistently delivering on commitments
D) Having a large firm with many consultants

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Trust is built primarily through reliability—consistently doing what you say you'll do—though credentials, value, and firm strength also contribute.

---

### Question 10
What is the typical revenue potential for CEA consulting engagements?

A) $5,000-15,000 per engagement
B) $25,000-500,000 per engagement
C) $1,000-10,000 per engagement
D) Over $1,000,000 per engagement

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** The course indicates typical CEA consulting engagements range from $25,000-500,000 depending on type and scope, with most in the $50,000-150,000 range.
