### Question 1
A comprehensive CEA feasibility study should include which core components?

A) Technical feasibility only
B) Market analysis only
C) Technical, market, financial, and risk assessment
D) Financial projections only

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Complete feasibility studies must address technical viability, market opportunity, financial returns, and risks to provide clients with a comprehensive go/no-go decision framework.

---

### Question 2
What is the typical timeline for developing a comprehensive CEA feasibility study?

A) 1-2 weeks
B) 4-8 weeks
C) 6-12 weeks
D) 6-12 months

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Thorough feasibility studies typically require 6-12 weeks for data collection, analysis, modeling, stakeholder consultation, and report development.

---

### Question 3
In financial feasibility analysis, which metric is most critical for investor decision-making?

A) Total revenue
B) Internal Rate of Return (IRR) and Net Present Value (NPV)
C) Gross margin percentage
D) Operating expenses

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** IRR and NPV are the primary metrics investors use to evaluate whether projected returns justify the investment and exceed their required hurdle rates.

---

### Question 4
What is the recommended approach for handling uncertainty in feasibility study financial projections?

A) Present only the most optimistic scenario
B) Use single-point estimates for all assumptions
C) Develop base, conservative, and optimistic scenarios with sensitivity analysis
D) Avoid mentioning uncertainties to maintain client confidence

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Multiple scenarios and sensitivity analysis help clients understand the range of potential outcomes and key drivers of success or failure.

---

### Question 5
In technical feasibility assessment for CEA projects, what is the most common oversight that leads to project failure?

A) Over-specification of environmental controls
B) Underestimation of biological complexity and operational requirements
C) Too much focus on crop selection
D) Excessive attention to water quality

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Many projects fail because developers underestimate the complexity of growing systems, crop management requirements, and the expertise needed for consistent production.

---

### Question 6
Which stakeholder group typically has the MOST influence on feasibility study recommendations?

A) The consultant's personal preferences
B) The project sponsor's objectives and decision criteria
C) Industry best practices alone
D) Technology vendors

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Feasibility studies must be tailored to the sponsor's specific objectives, risk tolerance, and decision criteria while incorporating objective analysis and industry best practices.

---

### Question 7
What is the primary purpose of the "go/no-go" recommendation in a feasibility study?

A) To guarantee project success
B) To provide an objective, evidence-based recommendation on whether to proceed
C) To always recommend proceeding to secure future work
D) To minimize client decision-making responsibility

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** The go/no-go recommendation synthesizes all analysis into a clear, objective recommendation that helps clients make informed investment decisions.

---

### Question 8
In market feasibility analysis, which question is most critical to answer?

A) What is the total population of the region?
B) Who will buy our product, why, and at what price and volume?
C) How many competitors exist?
D) What is the average income level?

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Understanding specific customer segments, their motivations, willingness to pay, and purchase volumes is essential for validating market viability.

---

### Question 9
Which risk assessment approach is most appropriate for complex CEA projects?

A) Qualitative risk identification only
B) Ignore risks to maintain positive outlook
C) Quantitative and qualitative assessment with mitigation strategies
D) Focus only on financial risks

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Comprehensive risk assessment combines qualitative identification and prioritization with quantitative impact analysis and specific mitigation strategies for critical risks.

---

### Question 10
What is the appropriate consultant response when a feasibility study indicates a project should NOT proceed?

A) Adjust assumptions to make the project appear viable
B) Recommend not proceeding and clearly explain the reasoning
C) Avoid giving a recommendation and let the client decide without guidance
D) Recommend proceeding anyway to maintain the relationship

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Professional ethics require honest, objective recommendations even when the conclusion is unfavorable. Clear explanation of the reasoning maintains credibility and client trust.
