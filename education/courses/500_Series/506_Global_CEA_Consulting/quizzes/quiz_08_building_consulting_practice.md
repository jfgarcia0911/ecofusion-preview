### Question 1
What is the recommended business development time allocation for establishing a new consulting practice?

A) 10-20% of time
B) 30-40% of time
C) 50-60% of time initially, decreasing as practice matures
D) 5% of time

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** New practices require significant business development investment (50-60% of time) to build pipeline and client base, decreasing as the practice matures and generates referrals.

---

### Question 2
Which marketing approach typically delivers the highest ROI for CEA consulting practices?

A) Traditional advertising
B) Thought leadership through speaking, writing, and industry engagement
C) Cold calling
D) Direct mail campaigns

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Thought leadership establishes expertise and credibility, attracting qualified inbound leads more cost-effectively than traditional outbound marketing.

---

### Question 3
What is the typical utilization rate target for sustainable consulting practices?

A) 95-100% billable time
B) 75-85% billable time
C) 50-60% billable time
D) 30-40% billable time

**Correct Answer: C**
**Explanation:** Sustainable practices target 50-60% billable utilization, allowing time for business development, administration, professional development, and proposal development.

---

### Question 4
When building a consulting practice, which financial metric is most critical to monitor?

A) Total revenue only
B) Cash flow and accounts receivable aging
C) Office lease costs
D) Number of business cards printed

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Cash flow is critical for consulting business survival. Long payment cycles and poor collections can sink otherwise successful practices.

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended approach for expanding service offerings?

A) Add all possible services immediately
B) Start with core expertise, expand adjacently based on client demand and capability development
C) Offer services you don't yet have capability in
D) Never expand beyond initial offerings

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Sustainable expansion builds on core expertise, adds adjacent capabilities based on market demand, and ensures quality by developing capability before marketing new services.

---

### Question 6
Which client acquisition channel typically has the LOWEST cost per acquisition for established consultants?

A) Paid advertising
B) Referrals from satisfied clients
C) Cold outreach
D) Trade show booths

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Client referrals have the lowest acquisition cost and highest conversion rates because they come with built-in trust and validation from the referring client.

---

### Question 7
What is the appropriate response to a request for free consulting advice from a potential client?

A) Provide detailed free advice to demonstrate expertise
B) Politely decline or offer limited initial consultation with clear boundaries
C) Ignore the request
D) Demand immediate payment

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Professional consultants may offer brief initial consultations but must establish clear boundaries between free discussions and paid advisory to maintain business viability.

---

### Question 8
When should a consulting practice consider hiring additional consultants?

A) Immediately upon launching
B) When consistently operating at capacity with qualified pipeline exceeding capacity
C) Never hire employees
D) When office feels empty

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Hiring should occur when sustained demand exceeds capacity and pipeline supports additional resources, ensuring new hires can be kept productively utilized.

---

### Question 9
What is the typical client concentration risk threshold that practices should avoid exceeding?

A) 10% of revenue from single client
B) 30% of revenue from single client
C) 50% of revenue from single client
D) 100% from single client is ideal

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Exceeding 30% revenue concentration with a single client creates significant business risk if that relationship ends. Diversification protects practice stability.

---

### Question 10
Which professional insurance is most critical for consulting practices?

A) Property insurance only
B) Professional liability (errors & omissions) insurance
C) Life insurance
D) Auto insurance only

**Correct Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Professional liability (E&O) insurance protects consultants against claims of negligent advice or work product errors, which can result in significant financial damages.
