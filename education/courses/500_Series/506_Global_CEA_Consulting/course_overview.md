# Course 506: Global CEA Consulting

## Course Level
Master/Executive Level (500-Series)

## Course Duration
14 Weeks (140-160 hours total commitment)

## Course Description

This master-level course prepares experienced CEA professionals to establish and operate successful international consulting practices in controlled environment agriculture. Students will develop advanced consulting skills, learn proven methodologies for client engagement, and master the strategic, technical, and business aspects of providing high-value advisory services to CEA organizations worldwide.

The curriculum covers the complete consulting lifecycle from business development and client acquisition through engagement delivery and knowledge management. Through case studies, real-world scenarios, and practical exercises, participants will learn to conduct market assessments, feasibility studies, technical audits, and strategic planning engagements across diverse international markets.

This course emphasizes the unique challenges and opportunities of CEA consulting including technology evaluation, project development, operational optimization, and international market entry. Students will develop expertise in cross-cultural business practices, proposal development, pricing strategies, and building sustainable consulting practices.

## Assessment Methods

### Graded Components

1. **Module Quizzes (14)** - 20%
   - 10 questions per module
   - Multiple choice and scenario-based
   - Assess understanding of key concepts

2. **Case Study Analyses (6)** - 25%
   - Written analysis of complex scenarios
   - Application of frameworks and methodologies
   - Recommendations and justifications

3. **Practical Assignments (5)** - 30%
   - Market assessment report
   - Feasibility study (major project)
   - Client proposal and presentation
   - Technical audit and recommendations
   - Consulting business plan

4. **Final Examination** - 15%
   - Comprehensive assessment
   - 50 questions covering all modules
   - Scenario-based problem solving

5. **Capstone Project** - 10%
   - Consulting engagement simulation
   - Complete consulting deliverable
   - Client presentation
   - Peer and instructor evaluation

### Grading Scale
- A: 93-100% (Exceptional mastery)
- A-: 90-92% (Excellent performance)
- B+: 87-89% (Very good performance)
- B: 83-86% (Good performance)
- B-: 80-82% (Satisfactory performance)
- C+: 77-79% (Acceptable performance)
- C: 70-76% (Minimum passing)
- F: Below 70% (Not passing)
