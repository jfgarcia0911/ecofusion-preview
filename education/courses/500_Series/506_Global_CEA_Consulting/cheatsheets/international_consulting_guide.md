# Cheatsheet: International Consulting Quick Guide

## CULTURAL DIMENSIONS SUMMARY

### High-Context vs. Low-Context Communication

**HIGH-CONTEXT** (Asia, Middle East, Mediterranean)
- Implicit, indirect communication
- Read between the lines
- Relationships provide context
- Non-verbal cues critical
- Silence has meaning

**LOW-CONTEXT** (USA, Germany, Scandinavia)
- Explicit, direct communication
- Say what you mean
- Message stands alone
- Verbal content primary
- Silence means nothing more

### Power Distance

**HIGH** (Asia, Middle East, Latin America)
- Hierarchical relationships
- Respect for authority
- Formal communication
- Use titles and surnames
- Don't skip levels

**LOW** (Scandinavia, Netherlands)
- Egalitarian relationships
- Question authority acceptable
- Informal communication
- First names common
- Flat organizational structure

### Individualism vs. Collectivism

**INDIVIDUALIST** (USA, UK, Australia)
- Personal achievement
- Individual decision-making
- Direct feedback acceptable
- "I" focus

**COLLECTIVIST** (Asia, Latin America, Middle East)
- Group harmony
- Consensus decision-making
- Indirect feedback
- "We" focus

```
Subject: Request for Your Valuable Input

Dear Tanaka-san,

I hope this finds you well.

I am writing to share the design documents
from our last meeting. I have attached
them for your review at your convenience.

If possible, we would appreciate your
valuable insights on sections 3 and 5
before our Thursday meeting.

Please let me know if you need clarification.

Thank you for your continued guidance.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

## COUNTRY-SPECIFIC TIPS

### UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

**Business Culture:**
- Relationship-focused, patient
- Hierarchy important, respect for seniority
- Hospitality central (accept tea/coffee)
- Right hand for greetings and documents
- Friday-Saturday weekend (Sun-Thu work week)

**Do:**
- Dress conservatively and professionally
- Use titles (Sheikh, Dr., Engineer)
- Build relationships before business
- Be patient with pace
- Show respect for Islam and culture

**Don't:**
- Rush to business before relationship
- Show soles of feet (sitting)
- Eat/drink in public during Ramadan
- Public displays of affection
- Criticize or cause loss of face

**Business Tips:**
- Decisions at senior level
- Government relationships critical
- Food security drives CEA interest
- Strong government incentives available
- Partnership with local essential

### JAPAN

**Business Culture:**
- High-context, indirect communication
- Harmony and consensus critical
- Respect for hierarchy and age
- Formality and protocol important
- Long-term relationship focus

**Do:**
- Exchange business cards with both hands, bow slightly
- Use family name + "-san" (Tanaka-san)
- Patient with decision process
- Dress formally (dark suits)
- Respect hierarchy in meetings

**Don't:**
- Write on business cards
- Say direct "no" (implies failure)
- Rush decisions or show impatience
- Speak loudly or emotionally
- Interrupt or contradict seniors

**Business Tips:**
- Nemawashi (consensus building) takes time
- Group decision-making
- Trust builds very slowly
- Quality and perfection valued
- Technology leadership important

### NETHERLANDS

**Business Culture:**
- Direct communication, low-context
- Egalitarian, flat organizations
- Punctuality essential
- Work-life balance valued
- Consensus-oriented but efficient

**Do:**
- Be direct and honest
- Arrive exactly on time
- Stick to agenda and schedule
- Be prepared with facts
- Respect work-life boundaries

**Don't:**
- Be late (very offensive)
- Small talk excessively
- Be overly formal or hierarchical
- Waste time in meetings
- Interrupt personal time

**Business Tips:**
- Global CEA technology leader
- Innovation and sustainability focus
- Direct negotiation style
- Decisions relatively quick
- Quality and efficiency valued

### SINGAPORE

**Business Culture:**
- Multicultural blend (Chinese, Malay, Indian, Western)
- English business language
- Efficiency and meritocracy valued
- Moderate hierarchy
- Fast-paced, results-oriented

**Do:**
- Be punctual and efficient
- Dress formally (tropical climate considered)
- Respect ethnic diversity
- Use English in business
- Focus on results and ROI

**Don't:**
- Waste time
- Be overly casual initially
- Criticize government or policies
- Litter or chew gum
- Assume one cultural approach

**Business Tips:**
- Urban farming innovation hub
- Limited land drives vertical farming
- Government support strong
- Technology adoption rapid
- Quality and efficiency paramount

## CONTRACT CONSIDERATIONS

### International Consulting Agreements

**Essential Clauses:**
- Governing law and jurisdiction
- Dispute resolution (international arbitration)
- Language (English + local if required)
- Currency and exchange rate
- Payment method and timing
- Withholding taxes
- Force majeure
- Confidentiality and IP
- Liability and indemnification
- Insurance requirements

**Payment Protection:**
- 30-50% deposit common internationally
- Letters of credit for large engagements
- Escrow for milestone payments
- Currency hedging if significant exposure
- Clear FX responsibility

**Example Payment Terms:**
```
Fee: $100,000 USD
Currency: USD (client assumes FX risk)

Schedule:
- 40% ($40,000) upon contract execution
- 30% ($30,000) upon interim deliverable
- 30% ($30,000) upon final deliverable

Method: Wire transfer to [bank details]
Terms: Within 15 days of invoice
Withholding Tax: Client pays additional
to gross up for any withholding

Expenses: Pre-approved, reimbursed at cost + 10%
```

## CROSS-CULTURAL NEGOTIATION

### Negotiation Styles

**COMPETITIVE** (USA, Russia)
- Win-lose orientation
- Direct and aggressive
- Quick decision pressure
- Individual achievement

**COLLABORATIVE** (Japan, Scandinavia)
- Win-win orientation
- Indirect and patient
- Consensus building
- Group harmony

**HIERARCHICAL** (Middle East, Latin America)
- Senior decision authority
- Relationship-based
- Patient process
- Respect and face-saving

### Negotiation Tips by Culture

**HIGH-CONTEXT CULTURES:**
- Build relationships first (weeks/months)
- Indirect communication about concerns
- Patience with process
- Face-saving critical
- Multiple meetings expected
- Senior relationships key

**LOW-CONTEXT CULTURES:**
- Get to business quickly
- Direct about issues and concerns
- Efficiency valued
- Logic and facts primary
- Fewer meetings needed
- Professional level OK

## COMMON PITFALLS & RECOVERY

### Common Mistakes

❌ **Assuming Similarity**
- "They're just like us"
- Projecting your norms
- Missing important differences

❌ **Stereotyping**
- Applying generalizations rigidly
- Not seeing individuals
- Causing offense

❌ **Impatience**
- Frustration with different pace
- Rushing relationships/decisions
- Showing irritation

❌ **Inappropriate Humor**
- Jokes that don't translate
- Humor at others' expense
- Sensitive topics

❌ **Disrespecting Customs**
- Violating social norms
- Inappropriate dress/behavior
- Ignoring protocols

### Recovery Strategy

**When Mistakes Happen:**

1. **Recognize** - Be aware of reactions
2. **Apologize** - Sincere acknowledgment
3. **Learn** - Understand what went wrong
4. **Adapt** - Change behavior going forward
5. **Rebuild** - Demonstrate learning consistently

**Example Recovery:**
```
"I apologize for interrupting earlier.
I realize I didn't follow proper protocol.
I'm still learning your business customs.
Could you help me understand the
appropriate way to handle that situation?
I want to be respectful going forward."
```

## TRAVEL & LOGISTICS TIPS

**Pre-Travel:**
- Research visa requirements early
- Book business-class for long flights (productivity + client perception)
- Notify credit card companies
- Local SIM or international phone plan
- Cultural orientation review

**In-Country:**
- Professional driver/car service (safer, productive)
- Quality hotel in business district
- Flexible schedule for delays/changes
- Local business cards if appropriate
- Small gifts for hosts (research appropriateness)

**Health & Safety:**
- Travel insurance always
- Vaccinations if required
- Register with embassy if extended stay
- Know emergency contacts
- Be aware of local safety considerations
