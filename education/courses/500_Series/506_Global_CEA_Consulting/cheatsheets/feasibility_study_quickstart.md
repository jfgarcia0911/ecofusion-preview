# Cheatsheet: CEA Feasibility Study Quick Reference

## Purpose
Quick reference guide for conducting comprehensive CEA feasibility studies

---

## FEASIBILITY STUDY FRAMEWORK

### Five Pillars
1. **Market Feasibility** - Is there demand?
2. **Technical Feasibility** - Can we build and operate it?
3. **Financial Feasibility** - Does it make economic sense?
4. **Organizational Feasibility** - Do we have capability?
5. **Risk Assessment** - What could go wrong?

---

## TYPICAL TIMELINE & BUDGET

**Pre-Feasibility Study:**
- Duration: 2-4 weeks
- Budget: $15,000-40,000
- Purpose: Initial screening

**Full Feasibility Study:**
- Duration: 6-10 weeks
- Budget: $50,000-150,000
- Purpose: Investment decision

**Bankable Feasibility:**
- Duration: 8-12 weeks
- Budget: $100,000-250,000
- Purpose: Secure financing

## FINANCIAL MODELING ESSENTIALS

### Capital Requirements
```
Site and Building: 30-45% of total
Equipment and Systems: 40-55% of total
Working Capital: 5-10% of total
Contingency: 10-15% of total
```

### Operating Costs (per kg)
```
Variable Costs: $3-5/kg
- Seeds, media, nutrients, packaging, energy
Fixed Costs: Divide annual by production volume
- Labor, facility, maintenance, overhead
```

### Revenue Projections
```
Year 1: 40-60% of capacity (ramp-up)
Year 2: 70-80% of capacity
Year 3+: 85-95% steady state

Price: Market analysis + premium justification
```

### Key Metrics
```
Gross Margin Target: 35-50%
EBITDA Margin Target: 15-25%
Payback Period: 5-8 years
IRR Target: 15-25%
NPV: Positive at 10-12% discount rate
```

---

## FINANCIAL MODEL STRUCTURE

**Essential Tabs:**
1. Assumptions (all inputs)
2. Capital Requirements
3. Operating Costs
4. Revenue Projections
5. Cash Flow (10 years)
6. Returns Analysis (NPV, IRR, Payback)
7. Sensitivity Analysis
8. Scenarios
9. Dashboard (executive summary)

**Key Sensitivities to Model:**
- Price: ±10%, ±20%
- Volume/Yield: ±10%, ±20%
- Energy costs: ±20%, ±30%
- Capital costs: ±15%
- Operating costs: ±10%

## RISK ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Risk Categories
- **Market**: Demand, pricing, competition
- **Technical**: Technology, performance, operational
- **Financial**: Costs, financing, cash flow
- **External**: Regulatory, economic, climate

### Risk Prioritization Matrix
```
           Critical  Major  Moderate  Minor
Certain       RED    RED    YELLOW   YELLOW
Likely        RED    YELLOW YELLOW   GREEN
Possible     YELLOW YELLOW  GREEN    GREEN
Unlikely     YELLOW  GREEN  GREEN    GREEN

RED = Address before proceeding
YELLOW = Mitigate and monitor
GREEN = Accept and document
```

### Risk Response Strategies
- **Avoid**: Eliminate the risk
- **Mitigate**: Reduce probability or impact
- **Transfer**: Shift risk (insurance, contracts)
- **Accept**: Acknowledge and plan contingency

## QUICK CALCULATIONS

### Space Requirements
```
Target Production (kg/year) ÷ Yield (kg/m²/year) = Growing Area (m²)
Growing Area × 2.5 = Total Facility Area (rule of thumb)
```

### Energy Consumption
```
Lighting: 30-40 kWh/kg (vertical farming)
HVAC: 8-15 kWh/kg
Total: 40-60 kWh/kg for vertical farms
15-30 kWh/kg for greenhouses
```

### Labor Requirements
```
Cultivation: 20,000-30,000 kg/FTE/year
Harvest/Pack: 15,000-25,000 kg/FTE/year
Overall: 10,000-15,000 kg/FTE/year including all roles
```

### Water Usage
```
Leafy greens: 8-15 L/kg
Fruiting crops: 15-30 L/kg
(95% less than field agriculture)
```

## COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

❌ **Overly optimistic yield assumptions**
✓ Use conservative, validated benchmarks

❌ **Underestimating energy costs**
✓ Model actual kWh usage and local rates

❌ **Ignoring ramp-up period**
✓ Model 3-4 year ramp to full capacity

❌ **Insufficient market validation**
✓ Conduct minimum 12-15 customer interviews

❌ **Single-point estimates**
✓ Always provide sensitivity and scenarios

❌ **Technology selection before market validation**
✓ Market requirements drive technology choice

❌ **Inadequate contingency**
✓ 10-15% minimum for capital budget

❌ **Forgetting working capital**
✓ 3-6 months operating expenses in startup capital

## SUCCESS FACTORS

1. **Rigorous market validation** through primary research
2. **Conservative assumptions** grounded in data
3. **Comprehensive risk assessment** with mitigation strategies
4. **Independent, objective analysis** without bias
5. **Transparent methodology** with documented assumptions
6. **Actionable recommendations** with clear next steps
7. **Professional deliverables** that inspire confidence
8. **Sensitivity analysis** showing range of outcomes

## QUICK REFERENCE: GO/NO-GO CRITERIA

### GREEN LIGHT (Proceed)
✓ IRR > 15%, NPV positive
✓ Strong market demand validated
✓ Proven technology and team
✓ Manageable, mitigable risks
✓ Clear competitive advantage
✓ Sufficient capital available

### YELLOW LIGHT (Proceed with Caution)
⚠ IRR 10-15%, marginal NPV
⚠ Market demand reasonable but not validated
⚠ Technology proven but implementation complex
⚠ Some significant risks require mitigation
⚠ Competitive but not clearly differentiated
⚠ Financing contingent or challenging

### RED LIGHT (Do Not Proceed)
❌ IRR < 10%, negative NPV
❌ Insufficient market demand
❌ Unproven technology or team
❌ Critical risks without mitigation
❌ Uncompetitive positioning
❌ Inadequate capital or financing

## NEXT STEPS AFTER FEASIBILITY

If **GO Decision:**
1. Design development (detailed)
2. Vendor selection and procurement
3. Financing finalization
4. Permitting and approvals
5. Construction and installation
6. Commissioning and startup

If **NO-GO Decision:**
1. Document reasons and learnings
2. Consider alternative approaches
3. Reassess timing or scale
4. Evaluate different markets
5. Archive for future reference

---

**Remember:** Feasibility studies serve clients best through rigorous, objective analysis—not by telling clients what they want to hear. Your credibility depends on honest, data-driven assessments.
