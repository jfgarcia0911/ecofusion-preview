## Section 1: Research Program Strategy (Questions 1-8)

### Question 1

B) Mapping research capabilities to organizational strategic priorities

B) 20%

## Section 10: Integrated Scenarios (Questions 45-50)

### Question 45
**Scenario**: You receive an invitation to review a manuscript that describes work very similar to your ongoing unpublished research. The appropriate action is:

A) Accept the review to see what competitors are doing
B) Accept the review and delay the competing paper as long as possible
C) Decline the review due to conflict of interest
D) Accept but have your graduate student review it instead

**Correct Answer**: C

### Question 46
**Scenario**: An industry partner in a sponsored research agreement requests that you delay publication of negative results that show their product performs poorly. You should:

A) Comply immediately to maintain the relationship
B) Refer to the agreement terms regarding publication rights and assert your right to publish
C) Publish immediately without notifying them
D) Delete the data and pretend the experiment never happened

**Correct Answer**: B

### Question 47
**Scenario**: Your research program has $5M annual budget and 18 active projects. Publications have declined and team members seem spread thin. The best strategy is:

A) Add more projects to increase productivity
B) Hire more people to maintain all projects
C) Consolidate the portfolio by terminating or combining projects to focus resources
D) Continue as-is; the problem will resolve itself

**Correct Answer**: C

### Question 48
**Scenario**: You're writing a grant proposal and have preliminary data supporting your hypothesis, but also one dataset that contradicts it. You should:

A) Only include the supporting data
B) Include all data and acknowledge the contradictory finding with explanation
C) Fabricate data to make it consistent
D) Abandon the proposal

**Correct Answer**: B

### Question 49

A) File provisional patent application immediately, then allow publication
B) Force them to delay graduation indefinitely until patent is issued
C) Publish without patent protection
D) Remove them from the project and patent it yourself
