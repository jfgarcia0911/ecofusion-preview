# Course 507: Research Program Development

## Course Level
**Master/Executive Level (500-Series)**

## Course Description

Research Program Development is an advanced master-level course designed for professionals aspiring to lead agricultural research programs in controlled environment agriculture (CEA), aquaponics, hydroponics, and sustainable food systems. This course provides comprehensive training in strategic research planning, portfolio management, funding acquisition, team leadership, and research impact maximization.

Students will learn to design and manage world-class research programs that advance scientific knowledge while delivering practical value to industry and society. The curriculum covers the complete research lifecycle from concept development and funding acquisition through execution, publication, and technology transfer. Emphasis is placed on building sustainable research programs that balance scientific excellence with commercial relevance and social impact.

This course is essential for:
- Research directors and program managers in agricultural technology companies
- University faculty developing research programs
- Government agency research administrators
- Agricultural innovation leaders
- Consultants advising on research strategy
- Entrepreneurs building R&D-driven ventures

## Duration
- **Total Course Time**: 45-50 hours
- **Format**: Self-paced online learning
- **Modules**: 14 comprehensive modules
- **Practical Components**: Grant proposal development, research plan creation, portfolio analysis

## Prerequisites

### Required:
- Completion of 400-level EcoFusion course or equivalent professional experience
- Advanced degree (Master's or PhD) in agriculture, horticulture, aquaculture, engineering, or related field (or 7+ years relevant experience)
- Experience conducting or managing research projects
- Understanding of scientific methodology and experimental design
- Familiarity with academic or industry research environments

### Recommended:
- Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture
- Course 406: Bioprocess Engineering
- Course 511: CEA Systems Architecture
- Publication experience in peer-reviewed journals
- Experience with grant applications or research funding
- Team leadership or management experience

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

### Strategic Planning
1. Develop comprehensive research program strategies aligned with organizational goals
2. Design balanced research portfolios spanning basic to applied research
3. Create long-term research roadmaps with measurable milestones
4. Assess and prioritize research opportunities using strategic frameworks

### Funding and Resources
5. Navigate the complex landscape of agricultural research funding
6. Write competitive grant proposals for federal, foundation, and industry sponsors
7. Build and manage research budgets exceeding $1M annually
8. Develop sustainable funding models combining multiple revenue streams

### Team Leadership
9. Recruit, develop, and retain top research talent
10. Build collaborative research cultures that drive innovation
11. Manage diverse teams including PhDs, technicians, and students
12. Foster productive partnerships with industry and academic collaborators

### Operations Management
13. Design and equip modern research laboratories and facilities
14. Implement quality systems and research compliance frameworks
15. Manage intellectual property strategy and technology transfer
16. Ensure ethical conduct and regulatory compliance in all research

### Impact and Communication
17. Develop publication strategies that maximize research visibility
18. Measure and communicate research impact to stakeholders
19. Build research reputation and thought leadership
20. Translate research findings into commercial and societal value

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Research Program Strategy and Vision
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Defining research program mission and vision
- Strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Research portfolio theory and design
- Competitive analysis and positioning
- Building the business case for research investment

### Module 2: Research Portfolio Management
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Portfolio balance: basic vs. applied research
- Risk management in research portfolios
- Resource allocation frameworks
- Pipeline management and stage-gate processes
- Portfolio optimization techniques

### Module 3: Agricultural Research Funding Landscape
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Federal funding agencies (USDA, NSF, DOE, etc.)
- Private foundations and non-profit funders
- Industry-sponsored research opportunities
- International funding sources
- Emerging funding mechanisms (crowdfunding, impact investing)

### Module 4: Grant Writing Excellence
**Duration**: 4 hours
- Grant proposal architecture and components
- Writing compelling narratives
- Budget development and justification
- Review criteria and scoring systems
- Common pitfalls and success strategies

### Module 5: Building and Leading Research Teams
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Team composition and role design
- Recruiting top talent in competitive markets
- Mentoring early-career researchers
- Performance management for research staff
- Building inclusive and innovative team cultures

### Module 6: Laboratory and Facility Management
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Research facility design and layout
- Equipment selection and procurement
- Inventory and supply chain management
- Safety and compliance systems
- Maintenance and calibration programs

### Module 7: Research Partnerships and Collaborations
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Academic-industry collaboration models
- Multi-institutional research consortia
- International research partnerships
- Collaboration agreements and contracts
- Managing collaborative relationships

### Module 8: Intellectual Property Strategy
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- IP landscape in agricultural research
- Patent strategy and portfolio management
- Licensing and commercialization pathways
- Trade secrets and know-how protection
- Freedom-to-operate analysis

### Module 9: Publication and Dissemination Strategy
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Strategic publication planning
- Selecting target journals and conferences
- Open access vs. traditional publishing
- Research communication to non-scientific audiences
- Social media and digital presence for researchers

### Module 10: Research Impact Assessment
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Defining and measuring research impact
- Bibliometric analysis and citation metrics
- Economic impact assessment
- Social and environmental impact measurement
- Communicating impact to funders and stakeholders

### Module 11: Research Compliance and Ethics
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Research ethics frameworks
- Institutional review boards and approval processes
- Data management and FAIR principles
- Conflict of interest management
- Responsible conduct of research

### Module 12: Industry-Sponsored Research Programs
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Structuring industry partnerships
- Contract negotiation for sponsored research
- Managing IP and publication rights
- Balancing confidentiality with academic freedom
- Building long-term industry relationships

### Module 13: Technology Transfer and Commercialization
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Technology transfer office relationships
- Startup creation from research
- Licensing strategies and deal structures
- Proof-of-concept and de-risking technologies
- Engaging with investors and entrepreneurs

### Module 14: Building Research Reputation and Leadership
**Duration**: 3.5 hours
- Personal branding for research leaders
- Thought leadership development
- Speaking engagements and invited presentations
- Advisory boards and professional service
- Building influence in your field

## Assessment Structure

### Quizzes (30%)
- 14 module quizzes (10 questions each)
- Multiple choice and scenario-based questions
- Minimum 80% required to pass each quiz
- Unlimited attempts allowed

### Practical Assignments (40%)
- **Assignment 1**: Research Program Strategy Document (15%)
  - 5-year strategic plan for a research program
  - Portfolio design and resource allocation

- **Assignment 2**: Grant Proposal Development (15%)
  - Full grant proposal for a federal or foundation program
  - Budget, timeline, and evaluation plan

- **Assignment 3**: Research Impact Report (10%)
  - Assessment of research program impact
  - Metrics, stakeholder communication, future directions

### Final Examination (30%)
- 50 comprehensive questions
- Covers all course modules
- Scenario-based problem solving
- Minimum 80% required to pass
- 2 attempts allowed

### Overall Passing Requirements
- Minimum 80% overall course grade
- All three practical assignments completed
- Final exam passed (≥80%)
