# Quiz 2: Research Portfolio Management

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 2 - Research Portfolio Management
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
In research portfolio management, what is the primary risk of having ALL projects in Horizon 1 (short-term applied research)?

A) Projects will be too expensive
B) Portfolio lacks long-term innovation pipeline and becomes obsolete
C) Research will be too theoretical
D) Projects will take too long to complete

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which framework is most effective for balancing risk across a research portfolio?

A) Putting all resources into the safest projects
B) Three Horizons model distributing resources across short, medium, and long-term projects
C) Only funding high-risk projects
D) Avoiding any portfolio planning

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is a "stage-gate" process in research portfolio management?

A) Physical gates at the laboratory entrance
B) Decision points where projects are evaluated before receiving continued funding
C) Scheduling gates for equipment access
D) Security clearance levels

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
When should you consider terminating a research project that is underperforming?

A) Never terminate any project once started
B) After systematic evaluation shows continued investment unlikely to achieve objectives or better opportunities exist
C) Immediately at first sign of difficulty
D) Only when funding runs out

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended frequency for formal portfolio review in active research programs?

A) Once per year only
B) Every 5 years
C) Quarterly or semi-annually with monthly monitoring
D) Daily review of all projects

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 6
In portfolio resource allocation, which approach typically delivers the best results?

A) Allocate resources equally across all projects
B) Concentrate all resources on a single project
C) Prioritize based on strategic alignment, probability of success, and potential impact
D) Allocate randomly

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 7
What is "opportunity cost" in research portfolio management?

A) The cost of research equipment
B) The value of the next best alternative use of resources
C) The cost of failed projects
D) Operating expense budgets

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
Which metric is most useful for comparing projects with different timelines and investment levels?

A) Total budget size
B) Number of researchers
C) Net Present Value (NPV) or Return on Investment (ROI)
D) Project duration

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 9
What is the primary benefit of maintaining a "pipeline" of projects at different stages?

A) Appears busy to administrators
B) Ensures continuous flow of results and reduces program volatility
C) Maximizes total number of projects
D) Complicates management

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
How should research programs handle breakthrough opportunities that arise unexpectedly?

A) Always reject them to maintain plan
B) Always accept them regardless of fit
C) Evaluate against portfolio strategy and reserve capacity or rebalance portfolio if warranted
D) Create entirely new programs for each opportunity

**Correct Answer**: C

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Portfolio lacks long-term innovation pipeline and becomes obsolete
2. B - Three Horizons model distributing resources across short, medium, and long-term projects
3. B - Decision points where projects are evaluated before receiving continued funding
4. B - After systematic evaluation shows continued investment unlikely to achieve objectives or better opportunities exist
5. C - Quarterly or semi-annually with monthly monitoring
6. C - Prioritize based on strategic alignment, probability of success, and potential impact
7. B - The value of the next best alternative use of resources
8. C - Net Present Value (NPV) or Return on Investment (ROI)
9. B - Ensures continuous flow of results and reduces program volatility
10. C - Evaluate against portfolio strategy and reserve capacity or rebalance portfolio if warranted
