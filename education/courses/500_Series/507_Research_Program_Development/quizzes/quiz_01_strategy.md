# Quiz 1: Research Program Strategy and Vision

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 1 - Research Program Strategy and Vision
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between a vision statement and a mission statement for a research program?

A) Vision statements are shorter than mission statements
B) Vision statements describe aspirational long-term goals while mission statements define current purpose and activities
C) Mission statements are only for universities while vision statements are for companies
D) There is no significant difference; the terms are interchangeable

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
According to the Three Horizons Framework for research portfolios, what characterizes Horizon 2 research?

A) Short-term applied research with 0-2 year timeline and low risk
B) Long-term basic research with 5-10+ year timeline and high uncertainty
C) Medium-term translational research with 2-5 year timeline and moderate risk
D) Immediate problem-solving research completed in under 6 months

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 3
What is the recommended portfolio allocation for a balanced research program according to the lesson?

A) 80% Horizon 1, 15% Horizon 2, 5% Horizon 3
B) 33% Horizon 1, 33% Horizon 2, 33% Horizon 3
C) 40% Horizon 1, 40% Horizon 2, 20% Horizon 3
D) 20% Horizon 1, 30% Horizon 2, 50% Horizon 3

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 4
Which of the following is NOT one of Porter's Five Forces as adapted for research programs?

A) Competitive rivalry among research programs
B) Bargaining power of funders
C) Quality of research publications
D) Threat of new entrants

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 5
In Stokes' Quadrant Model, "Pasteur's Quadrant" refers to research that is:

A) Pure basic research with high fundamental understanding and low practical use
B) Pure applied research with low fundamental understanding and high practical use
C) Use-inspired basic research with both high fundamental understanding and high practical relevance
D) Curiosity-driven research with neither fundamental understanding nor practical use

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 6
When building a business case for research investment, which component is MOST essential for securing funding from industry partners?

A) List of publications you plan to produce
B) Demonstration of economic ROI and value creation for the company
C) Description of your laboratory equipment
D) Your personal career goals and aspirations

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What is a "white space opportunity" in competitive research analysis?

A) Empty laboratory space available for expansion
B) Research questions or areas not adequately addressed by existing programs
C) Time in your schedule for additional projects
D) Blank sections in your strategic plan that need to be filled

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
For a university research center, which stakeholder value proposition would be MOST compelling to administration?

A) Research will lead to breakthrough scientific discoveries
B) Research will generate indirect cost recovery, enhance reputation, and attract top students
C) Research will be personally fulfilling for faculty
D) Research will result in numerous conference presentations

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is the primary purpose of defining core values for a research program?

A) To satisfy institutional paperwork requirements
B) To guide research decisions and create a shared culture
C) To compete for awards and recognition
D) To increase publication output

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
According to the lesson, what is a reasonable ROI expectation for a research program business case?

A) 10-25% ROI
B) 100-200% ROI
C) 400-1000% ROI
D) ROI cannot be calculated for research programs

**Correct Answer**: C

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Vision statements describe aspirational long-term goals while mission statements define current purpose and activities
2. C - Medium-term translational research with 2-5 year timeline and moderate risk
3. C - 40% Horizon 1, 40% Horizon 2, 20% Horizon 3
4. C - Quality of research publications
5. C - Use-inspired basic research with both high fundamental understanding and high practical relevance
6. B - Demonstration of economic ROI and value creation for the company
7. B - Research questions or areas not adequately addressed by existing programs
8. B - Research will generate indirect cost recovery, enhance reputation, and attract top students
9. B - To guide research decisions and create a shared culture
10. C - 400-1000% ROI
