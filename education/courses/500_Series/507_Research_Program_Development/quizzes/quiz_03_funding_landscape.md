# Quiz 3: Agricultural Research Funding Landscape

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 3 - Agricultural Research Funding Landscape
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

## Questions

### Question 1
Which federal agency is the PRIMARY funder of agricultural research in the United States?

A) National Science Foundation (NSF)
B) Department of Energy (DOE)
C) United States Department of Agriculture (USDA)
D) Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 2
What is the primary difference between USDA NIFA competitive grants and formula funds?

A) Competitive grants are awarded through peer review; formula funds are allocated to land-grant universities by formula
B) Formula funds are larger
C) Competitive grants are only for students
D) There is no difference

**Correct Answer**: A

---

### Question 3
Which type of funder typically has the MOST flexibility in funding timelines and research topics?

A) Federal agencies with strict appropriations
B) Private foundations and family offices
C) State agricultural departments
D) International development banks

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
What is a "Request for Applications" (RFA) in federal research funding?

A) A student application for admission
B) A specific funding opportunity announcement with defined scope, budget, and timeline
C) A request for laboratory equipment
D) An employment application

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
Which funding mechanism typically provides the LONGEST project duration and largest budgets?

A) Seed grants ($10-50K, 1 year)
B) Standard research grants ($250-500K, 2-3 years)
C) Center grants ($2-10M+, 5+ years)
D) Equipment grants

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 6
What is "indirect cost recovery" (IDC) in research grants?

A) Direct research expenses like supplies
B) Overhead costs (facilities, administration) charged as percentage of direct costs
C) Costs that cannot be recovered
D) Equipment depreciation

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
Which international funding source focuses specifically on agricultural development in lower-income countries?

A) Gates Foundation
B) CGIAR (Consultative Group on International Agricultural Research)
C) European Research Council
D) Japan Society for Promotion of Science

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
What is the typical success rate (funding rate) for competitive federal agricultural research grants?

A) 80-90%
B) 50-60%
C) 10-20%
D) Less than 5%

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 9
Which funding model involves companies paying universities to conduct specific research?

A) Federal grants
B) Industry-sponsored research contracts
C) Foundation grants
D) Student tuition

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
What emerging funding mechanism involves individual donors supporting research through online platforms?

A) Venture capital
B) Research crowdfunding
C) Bank loans
D) Government bonds

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. C - United States Department of Agriculture (USDA)
2. A - Competitive grants are awarded through peer review; formula funds are allocated to land-grant universities by formula
3. B - Private foundations and family offices
4. B - A specific funding opportunity announcement with defined scope, budget, and timeline
5. C - Center grants ($2-10M+, 5+ years)
6. B - Overhead costs (facilities, administration) charged as percentage of direct costs
7. B - CGIAR (Consultative Group on International Agricultural Research)
8. C - 10-20%
9. B - Industry-sponsored research contracts
10. B - Research crowdfunding
