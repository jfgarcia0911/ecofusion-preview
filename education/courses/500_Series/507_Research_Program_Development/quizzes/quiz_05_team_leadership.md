# Quiz 5: Building and Leading Research Teams

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 5 - Building and Leading Research Teams
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended span of control (direct reports) for research program directors?

A) 2-3 direct reports
B) 5-8 direct reports
C) 15-20 direct reports
D) Unlimited direct reports

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which factor is MOST important when recruiting postdoctoral researchers?

A) Willingness to work for lowest salary
B) Alignment of research interests with program goals and career development potential
C) Coming from prestigious institutions only
D) Immediate local availability

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is the primary purpose of individual development plans (IDPs) for research team members?

A) To document performance problems
B) To align personal career goals with research program needs and plan skill development
C) To satisfy HR requirements only
D) To determine salary increases

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
How frequently should research program leaders conduct one-on-one meetings with team members?

A) Once per year
B) Monthly or bi-weekly for direct reports
C) Only when problems arise
D) Daily for all team members

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
Which leadership approach is most effective for managing creative knowledge workers?

A) Micromanagement of all activities
B) Providing autonomy with clear goals, support, and accountability
C) Hands-off approach with no guidance
D) Authoritarian command and control

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
What is the most challenging aspect of team diversity in research programs?

A) Diversity provides no benefits
B) Managing different perspectives and communication styles while leveraging complementary strengths
C) Technical skill differences
D) Scheduling meetings

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
When should performance feedback be provided to research team members?

A) Only during annual reviews
B) Continuously through regular coaching with formal reviews periodically
C) Never provide feedback
D) Only when terminating employment

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
What is the recommended approach for handling underperforming team members?

A) Immediate termination
B) Ignore the problem hoping it resolves
C) Clear communication of expectations, support for improvement, and accountability for results
D) Reassign all their work to other team members

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 9
Which team structure is most effective for large research programs?

A) Flat structure with no organization
B) Hierarchical structure with research groups led by senior scientists
C) One person doing all the work
D) Completely autonomous individuals with no coordination

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
What is the primary factor in retaining top research talent?

A) Highest possible salary
B) Combination of meaningful work, career growth, supportive culture, and competitive compensation
C) Free coffee in the break room
D) Fancy job titles

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - 5-8 direct reports
2. B - Alignment of research interests with program goals and career development potential
3. B - To align personal career goals with research program needs and plan skill development
4. B - Monthly or bi-weekly for direct reports
5. B - Providing autonomy with clear goals, support, and accountability
6. B - Managing different perspectives and communication styles while leveraging complementary strengths
7. B - Continuously through regular coaching with formal reviews periodically
8. C - Clear communication of expectations, support for improvement, and accountability for results
9. B - Hierarchical structure with research groups led by senior scientists
10. B - Combination of meaningful work, career growth, supportive culture, and competitive compensation
