# Quiz 6: Intellectual Property Strategy

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 8 - Intellectual Property Strategy
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
Under the Bayh-Dole Act, who typically owns intellectual property developed with federal research funding?

A) The federal government owns all IP
B) The performing institution owns IP with certain federal rights
C) Individual researchers own all IP
D) IP cannot be owned when federal funds are used

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
What is the typical timeline for filing a provisional patent application after invention disclosure?

A) Within 1 week
B) Within 1-3 months to preserve rights before publications or public disclosure
C) Within 5 years
D) No timeline requirements exist

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
Which form of IP protection is most appropriate for plant varieties?

A) Only patents
B) Plant Variety Protection (PVP) or utility patents
C) Trademarks only
D) No protection available for plants

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
What is "freedom to operate" (FTO) analysis?

A) Analysis of laboratory safety procedures
B) Assessment of whether your technology infringes on others' IP rights
C) Employee scheduling analysis
D) Budget planning process

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
In university research programs, who typically makes final decisions about patent filings?

A) Individual researchers
B) Technology transfer office in consultation with inventors
C) Department chairs
D) Students

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
What is the primary advantage of trade secret protection versus patents?

A) Trade secrets never expire if maintained confidential
B) Trade secrets are easier to enforce
C) Trade secrets require government approval
D) Trade secrets are automatically protected without any effort

**Correct Answer**: A

---

### Question 7
Which invention characteristic is MOST important for patent viability?

A) Complexity
B) Novelty, non-obviousness, and utility
C) Cost to develop
D) Number of inventors

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 8
What is a typical royalty rate range for agricultural technology licenses?

A) 50-75% of gross sales
B) 2-8% of net sales
C) 100% of revenue
D) No royalties ever charged

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
When should researchers disclose inventions to their technology transfer office?

A) After patents are granted
B) Immediately upon conception, before any public disclosure
C) Never disclose inventions
D) Only if commercialization is guaranteed

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
What happens to patent rights if research results are published before filing a patent application?

A) Nothing changes
B) Publication may destroy patentability in most countries (US has 1-year grace period)
C) Publication strengthens patent rights
D) Publication is required before filing

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - The performing institution owns IP with certain federal rights
2. B - Within 1-3 months to preserve rights before publications or public disclosure
3. B - Plant Variety Protection (PVP) or utility patents
4. B - Assessment of whether your technology infringes on others' IP rights
5. B - Technology transfer office in consultation with inventors
6. A - Trade secrets never expire if maintained confidential
7. B - Novelty, non-obviousness, and utility
8. B - 2-8% of net sales
9. B - Immediately upon conception, before any public disclosure
10. B - Publication may destroy patentability in most countries (US has 1-year grace period)
