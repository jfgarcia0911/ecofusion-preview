# Quiz 8: Research Impact Assessment

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 10 - Research Impact Assessment
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three main categories of research impact?

A) Fast, medium, and slow impact
B) Academic, economic, and societal impact
C) Local, regional, and global impact
D) High, medium, and low impact

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which approach provides the most comprehensive assessment of research impact?

A) Citation counts only
B) Mixed-methods combining quantitative metrics, qualitative evidence, and stakeholder testimonials
C) Survey of researchers only
D) Budget size as proxy for impact

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is the typical time lag between research investment and measurable economic impact in agriculture?

A) 1-6 months
B) 1-2 years
C) 5-15 years or more
D) 50-100 years

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 4
Which stakeholder typically cares MOST about economic return on research investment?

A) Academic peers
B) Funding agencies and industry partners
C) Students
D) Competitors

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is a "logic model" in research impact assessment?

A) A statistical analysis method
B) A framework showing connections between inputs, activities, outputs, and outcomes
C) A type of computer program
D) A laboratory protocol

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
Which metric would best demonstrate the impact of research on agricultural sustainability?

A) Number of publications only
B) Documented adoption of practices leading to measurable environmental outcomes
C) Grant funding received
D) Number of conference presentations

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What is "technology readiness level" (TRL) and why is it important for impact assessment?

A) A scale (1-9) indicating how close technology is to commercialization
B) A measure of researcher training
C) A type of patent classification
D) A laboratory safety rating

**Correct Answer**: A

---

### Question 8
How should research programs measure societal impact?

A) Only through citations
B) By documenting improvements in food security, nutrition, livelihoods, or other social outcomes
C) Societal impact cannot be measured
D) By counting social media followers

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of an impact case study in research assessment?

A) To document legal cases
B) To provide detailed narrative evidence of how research led to specific impacts
C) To study disease impacts on crops
D) To analyze failed projects only

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
Which research communication strategy is most effective for maximizing impact beyond academia?

A) Only publish in academic journals
B) Multi-channel dissemination including extension, industry partnerships, and policy engagement
C) Avoid all communication with non-academics
D) Communicate only through university press releases

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Academic, economic, and societal impact
2. B - Mixed-methods combining quantitative metrics, qualitative evidence, and stakeholder testimonials
3. C - 5-15 years or more
4. B - Funding agencies and industry partners
5. B - A framework showing connections between inputs, activities, outputs, and outcomes
6. B - Documented adoption of practices leading to measurable environmental outcomes
7. A - A scale (1-9) indicating how close technology is to commercialization
8. B - By documenting improvements in food security, nutrition, livelihoods, or other social outcomes
9. B - To provide detailed narrative evidence of how research led to specific impacts
10. B - Multi-channel dissemination including extension, industry partnerships, and policy engagement
