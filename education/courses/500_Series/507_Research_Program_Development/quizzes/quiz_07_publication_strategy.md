# Quiz 7: Publication and Dissemination Strategy

**Course**: 507 Research Program Development
**Module**: 9 - Publication and Dissemination Strategy
**Total Questions**: 10
**Passing Score**: 80%

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the "h-index" and why is it important for researchers?

A) A measure of laboratory size
B) A metric combining publication quantity and citation impact
C) A funding amount
D) A teaching evaluation score

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 2
Which publication strategy is most effective for maximizing research impact?

A) Publish only in the highest impact factor journals
B) Strategic mix of high-impact journals, specialty journals, and rapid dissemination channels
C) Never publish to maintain trade secrets
D) Only publish in predatory journals

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 3
What is the primary advantage of open access publication?

A) Open access is always free to publish
B) Broader readership and citation potential due to unrestricted access
C) Lower quality review process
D) Faster peer review

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 4
When should authorship and author order be determined for research publications?

A) After the paper is published
B) Early in the project based on expected contributions
C) By journal editors
D) Randomly assigned

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 5
What is a preprint server and why are they increasingly important?

A) Old printing equipment
B) Platforms for sharing research before peer review to accelerate dissemination
C) Commercial publishers
D) Physical archives

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 6
Which metric is becoming more important than journal impact factor for assessing research quality?

A) Number of pages
B) Article-level metrics and alternative metrics (altmetrics)
C) Journal subscription cost
D) Author's age

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 7
What is the typical timeline from submission to publication in peer-reviewed agricultural journals?

A) 1-2 weeks
B) 1-2 months
C) 6-12 months
D) 5-10 years

**Correct Answer**: C

---

### Question 8
Which communication channel is most effective for reaching industry practitioners versus academics?

A) High-impact academic journals for both
B) Industry publications and conferences for practitioners; peer-reviewed journals for academics
C) Social media only for both
D) No difference in channels needed

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 9
What is the primary purpose of a press release for research findings?

A) To replace peer-reviewed publication
B) To communicate significant findings to media and general public
C) To advertise the research institution
D) Press releases are never appropriate for research

**Correct Answer**: B

---

### Question 10
How should research programs handle potentially sensitive or controversial findings?

A) Never publish controversial results
B) Publish results responsibly with appropriate context and stakeholder communication
C) Exaggerate findings for attention
D) Hide results that might be controversial

**Correct Answer**: B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - A metric combining publication quantity and citation impact
2. B - Strategic mix of high-impact journals, specialty journals, and rapid dissemination channels
3. B - Broader readership and citation potential due to unrestricted access
4. B - Early in the project based on expected contributions
5. B - Platforms for sharing research before peer review to accelerate dissemination
6. B - Article-level metrics and alternative metrics (altmetrics)
7. C - 6-12 months
8. B - Industry publications and conferences for practitioners; peer-reviewed journals for academics
9. B - To communicate significant findings to media and general public
10. B - Publish results responsibly with appropriate context and stakeholder communication
