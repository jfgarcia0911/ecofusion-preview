# Quiz 4: Stakeholder Engagement and Partnership Development

## Course 515: Capstone Practicum

**Module**: 4 - Stakeholder Engagement and Partnership Development
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
Why is early stakeholder engagement critical for capstone project success?

A) Not important at all
B) Ensures alignment, builds support, and provides access to resources and expertise
C) Delays project start
D) Creates unnecessary complications

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Early engagement builds buy-in, clarifies expectations, provides guidance, and secures access to data, expertise, and other resources.

---

### Question 2
What is a "stakeholder map" used for?

A) Geographic locations only
B) Identifying all relevant stakeholders and their relationships, interests, and influence
C) Road navigation
D) Office seating charts

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Stakeholder mapping identifies who should be engaged, their interests, power dynamics, and relationships to inform engagement strategies.

---

### Question 3
How should capstone students approach requesting industry expert input?

A) Demanding their time without explanation
B) Clear value proposition, respect for time, specific requests, and gratitude
C) Avoiding experts entirely
D) Assuming they have unlimited availability

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Professional requests articulate project value, show respect for busy schedules, make specific asks, and express appreciation.

---

### Question 4
What is "active listening" in stakeholder interviews?

A) Hearing background noise
B) Fully focusing, understanding, responding, and remembering what's communicated
C) Waiting to talk
D) Multitasking during conversations

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Active listening involves full attention, comprehension, appropriate responses, and retention—essential for gathering quality insights.

---

### Question 5
How should conflicting stakeholder interests be managed?

A) Ignoring all conflicts
B) Transparent acknowledgment, seeking common ground, and principled decision-making
C) Choosing sides arbitrarily
D) Avoiding all stakeholder input

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Address conflicts openly, seek areas of alignment, and make decisions based on project objectives and evidence.

---

### Question 6
What makes a "partnership agreement" effective for capstone projects?

A) Verbal agreements only
B) Clear expectations, roles, deliverables, timelines, and confidentiality terms
C) Vague understandings
D) One-sided arrangements

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Written agreements clarify mutual expectations, define roles, specify deliverables, and protect both parties through clear terms.

---

### Question 7
How should capstone students maintain stakeholder relationships throughout projects?

A) Contact only at beginning and end
B) Regular updates, progress sharing, seeking input, and demonstrating value
C) Avoiding all communication
D) Only reaching out when problems arise

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Consistent communication maintains engagement, demonstrates progress, leverages expertise, and builds lasting professional relationships.

---

### Question 8
What is "expectation management" with stakeholders?

A) Telling stakeholders what they want to hear
B) Clear communication about what can/cannot be delivered within constraints
C) Making unrealistic promises
D) Ignoring stakeholder expectations

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Managing expectations through honest communication about scope, timeline, and deliverables prevents disappointment and maintains trust.

---

### Question 9
How should capstone students acknowledge stakeholder contributions?

A) No acknowledgment needed
B) Appropriate recognition in documentation, presentations, and final deliverables
C) Taking sole credit for all work
D) Exaggerating contributions

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Proper acknowledgment demonstrates professionalism, gratitude, and academic integrity while maintaining relationships.

---

### Question 10
What is the value of diverse stakeholder perspectives in capstone work?

A) Creates confusion only
B) Provides multifaceted insights, identifies blind spots, and strengthens analysis
C) Should be avoided
D) Slows down all progress

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Diverse perspectives enrich understanding, reveal assumptions, challenge thinking, and produce more robust solutions.

---
