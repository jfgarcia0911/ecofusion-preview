# Quiz 5: Project Management and Execution Planning

## Course 515: Capstone Practicum

**Module**: 5 - Project Management and Execution Planning

**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the "critical path" in project management?

A) Most important walking route
B) Longest sequence of dependent tasks determining minimum project duration
C) Shortcut to completion
D) Most expensive activities

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: The critical path identifies task dependencies and determines the minimum time needed to complete the project—delays on this path delay the entire project.

---

### Question 2
What is the purpose of "work breakdown structure" (WBS)?

A) Documenting employee performance issues
B) Decomposing project into hierarchical, manageable tasks and work packages
C) Building demolition plans
D) Financial accounting only

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: WBS breaks complex projects into smaller, manageable components, facilitating planning, resource allocation, and progress tracking.

---

### Question 3
What does "resource leveling" address in project management?

A) Flattening ground for construction
B) Balancing resource allocation to avoid over-allocation and optimize utilization
C) Making all resources identical
D) Reducing all budgets equally

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Resource leveling adjusts task timing to prevent resource conflicts and over-allocation while respecting dependencies.

---

### Question 4
What is a project "milestone" and how does it differ from a task?

A) Physical markers on roads
B) Significant checkpoint or achievement with no duration; tasks have duration and create deliverables
C) Identical to tasks
D) Unimportant dates

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Milestones mark key achievements or decision points (e.g., "Proposal Approved") while tasks are work activities with duration.

---

### Question 5
What is "earned value management" (EVM)?

A) Employee bonus calculation
B) Technique comparing planned vs. actual progress and costs to forecast project performance
C) Stock market analysis
D) Customer loyalty programs

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: EVM integrates scope, schedule, and cost to assess project health by comparing planned value, earned value, and actual costs.

---

### Question 6
How should project managers handle scope changes during capstone execution?

A) Accept all changes immediately
B) Evaluate through change control process assessing impact on timeline, resources, and objectives
C) Reject all changes automatically
D) Ignore change requests

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Formal change control evaluates proposed changes against constraints, ensuring only beneficial, feasible changes are approved.

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of regular project status reporting?

A) Bureaucratic requirement only
B) Communicate progress, identify issues, maintain stakeholder engagement, and enable course correction
C) Creating busy work
D) Hiding problems

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Status reports keep stakeholders informed, surface issues early, document progress, and enable timely interventions.

---

### Question 8
What does "agile project management" emphasize?

A) Physical flexibility exercises
B) Iterative development, adaptability, and continuous stakeholder feedback
C) Rigid unchangeable plans
D) Avoiding all planning

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Agile approaches use short iterations, frequent feedback, and adaptive planning rather than rigid upfront plans.

---

### Question 9
How should project risks be monitored and controlled?

A) Identify once at start then ignore
B) Continuous monitoring, updating risk register, and activating mitigation plans as needed
C) Only worry about risks after they occur
D) Risks don't need management

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Active risk management includes ongoing monitoring, updating assessments, and implementing mitigation strategies proactively.

---

### Question 10
What is a "lessons learned" session and when should it occur?

A) Classroom lectures only
B) Structured reflection on successes and challenges to improve future performance; held throughout and at project end
C) Blame assignment meeting
D) Unnecessary activity

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Lessons learned capture what worked and what didn't, enabling continuous improvement throughout the project and benefiting future work.

---

6. B - Change control process assessing impacts
7. B - Progress communication, issue identification, engagement
8. B - Iterative development, adaptability, feedback
9. B - Continuous monitoring and mitigation activation
