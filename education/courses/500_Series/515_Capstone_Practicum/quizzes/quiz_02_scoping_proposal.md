# Quiz 2: Project Scoping and Proposal Development

## Course 515: Capstone Practicum

**Module**: 2 - Project Scoping and Proposal Development
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the purpose of defining clear project scope in capstone work?

A) Limiting creativity
B) Establishing boundaries, deliverables, and success criteria to ensure feasibility
C) Avoiding all planning
D) Making project as large as possible

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Clear scope defines what will and won't be accomplished, ensuring the project is achievable within time/resource constraints while meeting objectives.

---

### Question 2
What is a SMART objective in project planning?

A) Intelligence-based goals
B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound objectives
C) Simple goals only
D) Strategic management assessment review targets

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: SMART criteria ensure objectives are well-defined, trackable, realistic, aligned with goals, and have clear deadlines.

---

### Question 3
In stakeholder analysis for capstone projects, what does "power-interest grid" help determine?

A) Electrical requirements
B) How to prioritize and engage different stakeholders based on influence and interest
C) Employee salaries
D) Project budgets

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: The power-interest grid maps stakeholders by their influence and interest, guiding engagement strategies for each group.

---

### Question 4
What should a capstone project proposal clearly articulate?

A) Only the project title
B) Problem, objectives, methodology, deliverables, timeline, and expected outcomes
C) Personal preferences only
D) Unrelated topics

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Comprehensive proposals demonstrate project viability by articulating the challenge, approach, deliverables, and expected value.

---

### Question 5
Why is it important to identify project constraints early?

A) To give up immediately
B) To plan realistically around limitations in time, resources, access, or expertise
C) Constraints don't matter
D) To ignore all limitations

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Understanding constraints (budget, time, data access) enables realistic planning and prevents scope creep or project failure.

---

### Question 6
What is "scope creep" and how should it be managed?

A) Beneficial expansion of all projects
B) Uncontrolled expansion beyond original scope; managed through change control processes
C) Normal and should be encouraged always
D) Moving slowly through projects

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Scope creep threatens project completion. Formal change control evaluates proposed additions against time/resource impacts.

---

### Question 7
In capstone proposals, what makes success criteria effective?

A) Vague aspirational statements
B) Specific, measurable indicators directly tied to project objectives
C) Impossible targets
D) No criteria needed

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Effective success criteria are specific and measurable, enabling clear evaluation of whether objectives were achieved.

---

### Question 8
What is the purpose of a project timeline/Gantt chart?

A) Decorative element only
B) Visual representation of tasks, dependencies, and milestones over time
C) Financial accounting tool
D) Employee scheduling only

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Timelines show task sequences, dependencies, and deadlines, helping manage project execution and identify critical paths.

---

### Question 9
How should capstone students approach selecting project topics?

A) Choose easiest possible topic
B) Balance personal interest, industry relevance, and feasibility within constraints
C) Select topics they know nothing about
D) Avoid all challenges

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Best topics combine genuine interest (motivation), industry value (impact), and realistic feasibility (completion likelihood).

---

### Question 10
What should be included in a project risk assessment?

A) Only positive outcomes
B) Potential risks, likelihood, impact, and mitigation strategies
C) Ignoring all risks
D) Fears without analysis

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Risk assessment identifies potential issues, evaluates probability and impact, and develops mitigation strategies to address them proactively.

---
