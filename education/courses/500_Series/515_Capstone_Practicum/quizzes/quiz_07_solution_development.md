# Quiz 7: Solution Development and Innovation

## Course 515: Capstone Practicum

**Module**: 8 - Solution Development and Innovation
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What makes a capstone solution "innovative"?

A) Using newest technology regardless of fit
B) Novel approach or application addressing problem in new, valuable way
C) Being expensive
D) Complexity for its own sake

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Innovation applies new thinking or approaches to create meaningful value, not technology or complexity for its own sake.

---

### Question 2
What is "design thinking" and how does it support solution development?

A) Graphic design only
B) Human-centered, iterative approach emphasizing empathy, ideation, and prototyping
C) Architectural planning
D) Fashion design

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Design thinking focuses on understanding user needs, generating creative solutions, rapid prototyping, and iterative refinement.

---

### Question 3
What is a "minimum viable product" (MVP) concept?

A) Lowest quality acceptable
B) Simplest version delivering core value to test assumptions and gather feedback
C) Maximum features possible
D) Avoiding all testing

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: MVP includes only essential features needed to test core hypotheses and learn from real users, enabling efficient iteration.

---

### Question 4
How should feasibility be assessed for proposed solutions?

A) Gut feeling only
B) Evaluate technical, economic, operational, and scheduling feasibility systematically
C) Ignore feasibility concerns
D) Assume everything is feasible

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Comprehensive feasibility assessment examines technical capabilities, economic viability, operational requirements, and realistic timelines.

---

### Question 5
What is "benchmarking" in solution development?

A) Furniture selection
B) Comparing proposed solution against industry standards, best practices, or competitors
C) Weightlifting exercises
D) Avoiding comparisons

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Benchmarking identifies performance gaps, validates competitiveness, and informs design by comparing to established standards or leaders.

---

### Question 6
What role does "iteration" play in solution development?

A) Slowing down progress
B) Refining solutions through repeated cycles of design, testing, and improvement
C) Getting it perfect on first try
D) Avoiding all changes

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Iterative development improves solutions through testing, feedback, and refinement cycles, leading to better final outcomes.

---

### Question 7
How should user/stakeholder feedback be incorporated into solutions?

A) Ignore all feedback
B) Systematically collect, analyze, prioritize, and integrate relevant feedback
C) Accept every suggestion without evaluation
D) Only collect feedback, never use it

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Effective feedback integration involves systematic collection, analysis against objectives, prioritization, and thoughtful incorporation.

---

### Question 8
What is "value engineering" in solution development?

A) Adding maximum features
B) Optimizing design to maximize value while minimizing cost and complexity
C) Cheapest possible approach
D) Engineering degrees only

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Value engineering analyzes functions to deliver required performance and quality at optimal cost through efficient design.

---

### Question 9
How should competing solution alternatives be evaluated?

A) Random selection
B) Systematic evaluation against weighted criteria aligned with objectives
C) Choosing most expensive option
D) First idea is always best

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Decision matrices with weighted criteria enable objective comparison of alternatives against project objectives and constraints.

---

### Question 10
What makes a solution "sustainable" in the CEA context?

A) Short-term profit only
B) Environmental responsibility, economic viability, and social benefit over long term
C) Ignoring environmental impacts
D) Maximum resource consumption

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Sustainable solutions balance environmental stewardship, economic viability, and social value across the triple bottom line.

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Novel approach creating meaningful value
2. B - Human-centered iterative approach with prototyping
3. B - Simplest version for testing and learning
4. B - Systematic technical, economic, operational, schedule evaluation
5. B - Comparing to standards and best practices
6. B - Refining through design-test-improve cycles
7. B - Systematically collect, analyze, prioritize, integrate
8. B - Maximize value while minimizing cost and complexity
9. B - Systematic evaluation with weighted criteria
10. B - Environmental, economic, social balance long-term
