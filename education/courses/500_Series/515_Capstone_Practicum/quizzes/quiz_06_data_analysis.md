# Quiz 6: Advanced Analysis and Synthesis

## Course 515: Capstone Practicum

**Module**: 7 - Advanced Analysis and Synthesis
**Time Limit**: 15 minutes
**Passing Score**: 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the difference between analysis and synthesis in research?

A) No difference exists
B) Analysis breaks down data into components; synthesis integrates findings into coherent whole
C) Synthesis is always easier
D) Analysis is not important

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Analysis examines data components and patterns. Synthesis combines insights from multiple sources into integrated understanding and conclusions.

---

### Question 2
What is "thematic analysis" in qualitative research?

A) Analyzing themes in literature
B) Identifying, analyzing, and reporting patterns/themes within qualitative data
C) Random categorization
D) Quantitative statistical testing

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Thematic analysis systematically identifies recurring themes, patterns, and meanings in qualitative data like interviews or observations.

---

### Question 3
What does "statistical significance" indicate?

A) Data importance to stakeholders
B) Low probability that observed results occurred by chance alone
C) Large sample size only
D) Practical importance

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Statistical significance (e.g., p < 0.05) indicates results are unlikely due to random chance, though it doesn't guarantee practical importance.

---

### Question 4
What is "data triangulation" and why is it valuable?

A) Creating triangle charts
B) Comparing multiple data sources to validate findings and increase credibility
C) Using three researchers only
D) Geometric analysis

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Data triangulation uses multiple sources (interviews, documents, observations) to corroborate findings, increasing validity and confidence.

---

### Question 5
How should outliers in quantitative data be handled?

A) Always delete them
B) Investigate cause, assess impact, and make informed decision about inclusion with documentation
C) Ignore completely
D) Change values to match expectations

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Outliers may represent errors or important phenomena. Investigate causes, assess impact on results, and document decisions about treatment.

---

### Question 6
What is "pattern recognition" in data analysis?

A) Fabric pattern design
B) Identifying recurring trends, relationships, or structures in data
C) Random observations
D) Visual art creation

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Pattern recognition identifies consistent trends, correlations, or structures in data that reveal insights and support conclusions.

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of "cross-case analysis" in multi-site studies?

A) Legal case reviews
B) Comparing findings across different cases/sites to identify common patterns and unique variations
C) Packaging design
D) Criminal investigations

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Cross-case analysis reveals which findings are generalizable across contexts and which are site-specific, enriching understanding.

---

### Question 8
How should contradictory findings be addressed in analysis?

A) Hide contradictions
B) Acknowledge openly, investigate causes, and incorporate into nuanced interpretation
C) Select only findings that agree
D) Ignore conflicting data

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Contradictions may reveal important nuances. Acknowledge them, explore explanations, and develop more sophisticated interpretations.

---

### Question 9
What is "data saturation" in qualitative research?

A) Too much water in samples
B) Point where collecting additional data yields no new themes or insights
C) Computer storage limits
D) Maximum sample size

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Saturation occurs when new data no longer generates new themes, indicating sufficient data collection for the research question.

---

### Question 10
What makes data visualization effective for presenting findings?

A) Maximum colors and effects
B) Clear, accurate representation aligned with data characteristics and audience needs
C) Deceptive scaling
D) Complexity for its own sake

**Correct Answer**: B

**Explanation**: Effective visualizations accurately represent data, choose appropriate chart types, and communicate insights clearly to target audiences.
