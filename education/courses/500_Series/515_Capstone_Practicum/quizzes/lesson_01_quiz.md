### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of the capstone practicum?

A) To complete a traditional academic thesis for publication
B) To synthesize and apply knowledge through a significant professional project
C) To fulfill a graduation requirement with minimal effort
D) To conduct basic research supervised closely by faculty

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The capstone practicum is designed as a professional applied experience that synthesizes knowledge from across the 500-series curriculum to address real-world challenges, demonstrating professional competence and creating industry impact.

---

### Question 2
Which of the following is NOT one of the six main capstone project categories?

A) Strategic Business Case Development
B) Industry Innovation Project
C) Traditional Laboratory Research
D) Consulting Engagement

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** The six capstone categories are: Strategic Business Case Development, Industry Innovation Project, Consulting Engagement, Research and Analysis Study, Implementation Plan Development, and Policy/Regulatory Analysis. Traditional laboratory research is not a designated category.

---

### Question 3
What does the SMART framework help ensure when defining project objectives?

A) That objectives sound impressive and ambitious
B) That objectives are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-Bound
C) That objectives require maximum resources
D) That objectives are simple and require minimal effort

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** SMART objectives must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-Bound to ensure they are well-defined, feasible, and can be evaluated for success.

---

### Question 4
When selecting a capstone project, which consideration is MOST important for long-term success?

A) Choosing the easiest possible topic
B) Selecting whatever your advisor recommends without input
C) Aligning the project with your career goals and genuine interests
D) Picking the most complex technical challenge available

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Project success depends heavily on sustained motivation and strategic career positioning. Selecting a project aligned with genuine interests and career goals ensures engagement through challenges and builds credibility in your target career path.

---

### Question 5
What is the main difference between a capstone practicum and a traditional master's thesis?

A) Capstone requires less work than thesis
B) Capstone focuses on professional application while thesis emphasizes academic research
C) Capstone doesn't require advisor approval
D) Capstone has no written deliverables

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Capstone practicums emphasize solving real-world problems and demonstrating professional competence with industry-ready deliverables, while traditional theses focus on original academic research contributing to scholarly knowledge with publication goals.

---

### Question 6
Which of the following is a common pitfall to avoid when scoping a capstone project?

A) Defining clear boundaries and constraints
B) Attempting to solve massive, complex problems comprehensively
C) Validating feasibility with advisors
D) Focusing on specific, well-defined aspects of larger problems

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Overly ambitious scope is a major pitfall. It's better to thoroughly address a focused challenge (depth) than superficially touch on many issues (breadth). Clear scoping ensures feasibility within timeline and resource constraints.

---

### Question 7
In self-assessment for project selection, which question is MOST important to consider?

A) What project will require the least amount of work?
B) What topic did my advisor publish on most recently?
C) What aspects of CEA genuinely excite me and align with my career goals?
D) What project will impress people the most?

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Successful projects are driven by genuine interest and strategic career alignment. Personal passion sustains you through challenges, and career alignment ensures the work builds credibility for your professional future.

---

### Question 8
What is the recommended approach when you identify multiple strong project concepts?

A) Choose randomly to avoid overthinking
B) Pursue all concepts simultaneously
C) Use systematic evaluation criteria to assess and compare concepts
D) Always choose the first idea you thought of

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Systematic evaluation using criteria like strategic importance, feasibility, resource access, and impact potential enables informed decision-making. Evaluation matrices help objectively compare concepts and identify the strongest option.

---

### Question 9
Why is stakeholder engagement important from the project conceptualization phase?

A) It's required by university policy
B) Stakeholders provide access to data, validation of ideas, and potential career opportunities
C) It makes the project easier to complete
D) Advisors require at least 10 stakeholder signatures

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Early stakeholder engagement provides access to necessary resources (data, expertise, facilities), validates project relevance and feasibility, and builds relationships that can support both project success and future career opportunities.

---

### Question 10
Which statement BEST describes the relationship between capstone project selection and career planning?

A) They are completely unrelated; capstone is just an academic requirement
B) Your capstone should directly contradict your career interests to show versatility
C) Your capstone serves as a portfolio piece demonstrating expertise for your target career path
D) Career planning should only begin after completing your capstone

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Strategic capstone selection builds credibility and visibility in your target career area. The project serves as a professional portfolio piece demonstrating competencies to potential employers, making it a key career development tool, not just an academic requirement.
