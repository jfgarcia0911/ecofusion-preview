# Course 515 Final Exam: Capstone Evaluation Rubric

## Comprehensive Capstone Assessment

This document outlines the evaluation criteria for the complete capstone practicum, including all deliverables and the final defense.

---

## Assessment Components

| Component | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Project Proposal | 10% | Quality, feasibility, and strategic alignment of initial proposal |
| Project Management and Execution | 15% | Timeline adherence, stakeholder engagement, adaptation |
| Written Deliverables | 30% | Comprehensive report, executive summary, documentation quality |
| Final Presentation | 25% | Presentation quality, delivery, visual communication |
| Defense Performance | 10% | Q&A handling, depth of knowledge, professional presence |
| Professional Portfolio | 5% | Portfolio quality, career readiness documentation |
| Reflection and Growth | 5% | Critical reflection, learning articulation, professional development |

**Total: 100%**

---

## Detailed Evaluation Rubrics

### 1. Project Proposal (10%)

- Innovative approach to meaningful challenge

#### Excellent (8-8.9%)
- Clear problem statement and strong rationale
- Solid literature review with good coverage

- Realistic timeline and resource plan
- Good stakeholder identification
- Professional writing quality
- Addresses significant challenge

#### Proficient (7-7.9%)
- Adequate problem statement
- Sufficient literature review
- Appropriate methodology
- Reasonable timeline
- Basic stakeholder plan
- Generally professional writing
- Meets baseline requirements

#### Developing (6-6.9%)
- Unclear problem statement or weak rationale
- Limited literature review
- Methodology gaps or concerns
- Timeline questionable
- Weak stakeholder planning
- Writing quality issues
- Concerns about feasibility

#### Insufficient (<6%)

## Overall Performance Standards

### Exceptional (93-100% = A)
Demonstrates exceptional mastery of capstone competencies. Work exceeds all requirements with innovation, rigor, and professional excellence. Deliverables are publication or professional-use quality. Student shows readiness for senior leadership roles. Makes significant contribution to CEA field.

### Excellent (90-92% = A-)
Demonstrates strong mastery of competencies. Work meets all requirements at high quality level. Professional-grade deliverables suitable for industry use. Student shows readiness for professional practice. Makes solid contribution.

### Proficient (87-89% = B+)
Demonstrates solid proficiency. Work meets all requirements with good quality. Deliverables are professional and suitable for portfolio. Student shows competence for entry to mid-level professional roles. Adequate contribution.

### Competent (83-86% = B)
Demonstrates adequate competence. Work meets baseline requirements. Deliverables are acceptable quality. Student shows readiness for entry-level professional roles with continued development needed. Meets standards.

### Developing (80-82% = B-)
Demonstrates developing competence. Work meets most requirements but with quality concerns in some areas. Additional development needed for professional practice. Revisions may be required.

### Insufficient (<80% = No Pass)
Does not meet minimum requirements for capstone completion. Significant gaps in competencies or quality. Major revisions or re-work required before degree conferral.

---

## Pass/Fail Determination

**Pass (80% or above)**: Student has demonstrated master-level competence and may receive degree upon completion of any required minor revisions.

**Conditional Pass (80-85% with required revisions)**: Pass contingent upon completing specified revisions within defined timeline.

**Fail (<80%)**: Does not meet standards for capstone completion. Must complete major revisions and potentially re-defend, or select alternative capstone path in consultation with advisor.

---

## Panel Feedback Components

The defense panel will provide written feedback addressing:

1. **Strengths**: What the student did exceptionally well
2. **Areas for Improvement**: Constructive suggestions for development
3. **Required Revisions**: Any mandatory changes before final acceptance
4. **Professional Readiness**: Assessment of readiness for industry practice
5. **Future Directions**: Suggestions for continued growth and development

---

## Post-Defense Requirements

### For All Passing Students:
- Submit final revised capstone report within 2 weeks
- Address any panel-required revisions
- Submit one copy to university archive
- Complete exit survey and evaluation

### For Conditional Pass:
- Complete specified revisions within 4 weeks
- Submit for advisor re-review
- May require panel re-approval depending on extent of revisions
