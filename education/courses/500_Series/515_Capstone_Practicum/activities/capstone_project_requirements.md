# Capstone Practicum Project Requirements

## Master-Level Culminating Experience

### Course 515: Capstone Practicum
**EcoFusion Academy Master's Program**
**Credit Hours**: 6 credits
**Duration**: 16 weeks

---

## Project Overview

The capstone practicum is the culminating experience of your master's education in Controlled Environment Agriculture, requiring independent development and execution of a significant professional project that demonstrates mastery-level competence in research, analysis, problem-solving, and professional communication.

---

## Project Objectives

Through completion of the capstone practicum, you will:

1. Synthesize and apply knowledge from across the 500-series curriculum
2. Address a significant real-world challenge or opportunity in the CEA industry
3. Conduct rigorous research and analysis using appropriate methodologies
4. Develop innovative, feasible, and impactful solutions or recommendations
5. Produce professional-quality written and visual deliverables
6. Present and defend your work before faculty and industry experts
7. Demonstrate readiness for leadership roles in the CEA sector

---

## Project Selection

### Approved Categories

Select ONE project category aligned with your interests and career goals:

#### 1. Strategic Business Case Development
Develop comprehensive business case for significant CEA initiative including market analysis, financial modeling, operational planning, and implementation strategy.

**Example Projects:**
- Business case for vertical farm in specific market
- Market entry strategy for new geography
- Technology investment decision analysis
- Acquisition or partnership evaluation
- Business model transformation plan

#### 2. Industry Innovation Project
Develop, test, and validate innovative technologies, processes, or business models advancing CEA capabilities.

**Example Projects:**
- Novel growing system development
- Process optimization innovation
- CEA-as-a-Service business model
- Software or digital platform development
- Circular economy or sustainability innovation

#### 3. Consulting Engagement
Professional consulting project addressing specific client challenge with strategic recommendations and implementation support.

**Example Projects:**
- Operational efficiency improvement
- Strategic planning engagement
- Market entry or expansion consulting
- Technology selection and implementation
- Organizational development project

#### 4. Research and Analysis Study
Original research investigating significant industry questions through systematic inquiry and rigorous analysis.

**Example Projects:**
- Market trends and future scenarios
- Technology assessment and comparison
- Best practices research and benchmarking
- Consumer preference or behavior study
- Supply chain or logistics analysis

#### 5. Implementation Plan Development
Detailed planning for major initiatives including facility development, technology deployment, or organizational change.

**Example Projects:**
- New facility design and development plan
- Technology deployment and integration plan
- Organizational transformation plan
- Sustainability program implementation
- Quality management system implementation

#### 6. Policy or Regulatory Analysis
Comprehensive analysis of policy or regulatory issues with recommendations for compliance, advocacy, or strategic response.

**Example Projects:**
- Regulatory compliance framework
- Policy impact analysis and recommendations
- Advocacy strategy for industry priorities
- International regulatory comparison
- Food safety or labeling policy analysis

- Resource requirements
- Stakeholder engagement plan

**Deliverable Format:**
- Professional document formatting
- APA citation style
- Table of contents
- Page numbers
- Figures and tables as needed

**Due:** Week 2
**Presentation:** 20-minute proposal presentation to advisor

---

### 2. Progress Reports (Ongoing)

**Requirements:**
- Weekly status reports to advisor
- Bi-weekly detailed progress updates

- Issue and risk logs

**Format:**
- Standard template (provided in handouts)
- 2-3 pages per bi-weekly report
- Metrics dashboard
- Evidence of progress

**Due:** Weekly (brief), Bi-weekly (detailed)

---

### 3. Comprehensive Capstone Report (Week 14)

**Requirements:**
- 75-100 pages comprehensive report
- Executive summary (3-5 pages standalone)
- Full documentation of project
- Professional publication quality
- Portfolio-ready presentation

**Structure:**
1. **Front Matter**
   - Title page
   - Abstract (250-300 words)
   - Table of contents
   - List of figures and tables
   - Acknowledgments (optional)

2. **Executive Summary** (3-5 pages)
   - Problem and significance
   - Approach and methodology
   - Key findings
   - Recommendations
   - Expected impact

3. **Introduction** (8-12 pages)
   - Problem statement and context
   - Research questions/objectives
   - Significance and contribution
   - Scope and boundaries
   - Document roadmap

4. **Literature Review and Background** (10-15 pages)
   - Theoretical foundations
   - Industry context
   - Prior research and practice
   - Gaps and opportunities
   - Conceptual framework

5. **Methodology** (8-12 pages)
   - Research design
   - Data collection methods
   - Sampling and participants
   - Analysis procedures
   - Quality assurance
   - Limitations

6. **Findings and Analysis** (15-20 pages)
   - Organized by research questions or themes
   - Evidence and examples
   - Tables, charts, and quotes
   - Objective presentation

7. **Discussion and Interpretation** (10-15 pages)
   - Interpretation of findings
   - Connection to literature
   - Implications (theoretical and practical)
   - Synthesis across findings
   - Alternative explanations

8. **Recommendations and Implementation** (12-18 pages)
   - Strategic recommendations
   - Solution design and specifications
   - Implementation roadmap
   - Resource requirements
   - Risk management
   - Success metrics

9. **Conclusion** (5-8 pages)
   - Summary of work
   - Key contributions
   - Limitations and future research
   - Reflection on learning

10. **References** (5-10 pages)
    - Complete bibliography in APA format
    - Minimum 40-50 quality sources

11. **Appendices** (as needed)
    - Survey instruments
    - Interview protocols
    - Detailed data tables
    - Technical specifications
    - Financial models
    - Supporting analysis

**Quality Standards:**
- Professional writing quality (clear, concise, error-free)
- Rigorous methodology and analysis
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Actionable recommendations
- Professional formatting and design
- Suitable for publication or professional use

**Due:** Week 14 (draft for review), Week 15 (final submission)

---

### 4. Final Presentation (Week 15)

**Requirements:**
- 30-40 minute formal presentation
- Professional slide deck (20-30 slides)
- Compelling narrative and delivery
- Clear communication of complex concepts
- Engaging visual design
- Q&A preparedness

**Presentation Structure:**
1. Opening and context (3-5 min)
2. Problem statement and significance (3-5 min)
3. Approach and methodology (3-5 min)
4. Key findings (10-12 min)
5. Recommendations and implementation (8-10 min)
6. Conclusion (2-3 min)
7. Q&A (15-20 min)

**Deliverable Format:**
- PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides
- Professional design and branding
- Readable fonts (minimum 24pt body, 36pt headings)
- High-quality visuals and charts
- Consistent formatting
- Presenter notes included

**Due:** Week 15
**Presentation:** Formal defense before faculty panel and invited guests

---

### 5. Capstone Defense (Week 15)

**Requirements:**
- Formal defense of work before faculty panel
- 90-120 minute session including presentation and Q&A
- Demonstrate mastery of subject matter
- Defend methodology, findings, and recommendations
- Handle challenging questions professionally
- Show depth of knowledge and critical thinking

**Panel Composition:**
- Faculty advisor (chair)
- 2-3 additional faculty members
- Industry practitioner (optional)

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Presentation quality and delivery
- Depth of knowledge and understanding
- Rigor of methodology and analysis
- Quality of findings and recommendations
- Critical thinking and defense capability
- Professional presence and communication

**Outcome:**
- Pass (with or without minor revisions)
- Pass with major revisions required
- Fail (revision and re-defense required)

## Evaluation and Grading

### Overall Grade Components

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Project Proposal | 10% |
| Project Management | 15% |
| Written Deliverables | 30% |
| Final Presentation | 25% |
| Defense Performance | 10% |

### Performance Standards

- **Exceptional (93-100% = A)**: Exceeds all requirements, publication-quality work, exceptional mastery
- **Excellent (90-92% = A-)**: Meets all requirements at high quality, strong professional competence
- **Proficient (87-89% = B+)**: Solid work meeting standards, good professional quality
- **Competent (83-86% = B)**: Adequate work meeting baseline requirements
- **Developing (80-82% = B-)**: Meets most requirements, some quality concerns
- **Insufficient (<80% = No Pass)**: Does not meet minimum standards

**Minimum passing grade: 80% (B-)**

Students receiving below 80% will need to complete major revisions and potentially re-defend.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change my project topic after proposal approval?**
A: Minor refinements are acceptable; major changes require new proposal and advisor approval.

**Q: What if I can't get enough survey responses or interview participants?**
A: Discuss with advisor immediately. May need to adjust methodology, extend timeline, or modify scope.

**Q: How often should I meet with my advisor?**
A: Weekly meetings strongly recommended; minimum bi-weekly required.

**Q: Can I work with a team?**
A: No, capstone must be individual work. You can collaborate for peer review but project must be yours.

**Q: What if I fail the defense?**
A: Rare. Panel will specify required revisions and timeline for re-defense (typically 4-8 weeks).

**Q: Can I use my capstone for publication or business venture?**
A: Yes! Many students publish findings or launch businesses. Discuss IP considerations with advisor.

**Q: What if my industry partner withdraws support?**
A: Notify advisor immediately. May need to adjust scope or find alternative sources.
