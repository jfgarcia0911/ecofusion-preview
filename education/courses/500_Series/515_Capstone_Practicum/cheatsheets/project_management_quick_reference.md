# Capstone Project Management Quick Reference

## Essential Project Management Tools and Frameworks

---

## 1. Project Planning Essentials

### SMART Objectives
- **S**pecific: Clearly defined, no ambiguity
- **M**easurable: Quantifiable success criteria
- **A**chievable: Realistic given constraints
- **R**elevant: Aligned with goals
- **T**ime-bound: Clear deadline

**Example:**
❌ "Improve vertical farm efficiency"
✅ "Reduce energy cost per kg lettuce by 15% through lighting optimization by Week 10"

---

### Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
```
Project
├── Phase 1: Initiation
│   ├── Proposal Development
│   └── Stakeholder Identification
├── Phase 2: Research
│   ├── Literature Review
│   ├── Data Collection
│   └── Interviews
├── Phase 3: Analysis
│   ├── Quantitative Analysis
│   └── Qualitative Analysis
├── Phase 4: Solution Development
└── Phase 5: Documentation
```

**Tips:**
- Break down to manageable 4-8 hour tasks
- Each task should have clear completion criteria
- Assign responsibility for each task

---

## 2. Timeline Management

### Critical Path Method
**Critical Path** = Longest sequence of dependent tasks

**Identify:**
1. List all tasks
2. Identify dependencies
3. Estimate duration for each
4. Map dependencies
5. Calculate longest path
6. Monitor critical path tasks closely

**Float/Slack** = How much task can be delayed without impacting end date
- Zero float = On critical path
- Positive float = Has flexibility

---

### Gantt Chart Essentials
```
Task                Week 1  Week 2  Week 3  Week 4
Proposal            [====]
Literature Review       [========]
Survey Design              [====]
Survey Deployment                [=========]
Interviews                   [==============]
Analysis                                  [======]
Milestone: ✓                 ✓            ✓
```

**Key:**
- [====] = Task duration
- ✓ = Milestone
- Dependencies shown by sequence

## 3. Stakeholder Management

### Power-Interest Matrix
```
         High Power
             |
    Keep     |  Manage
  Satisfied  |  Closely
_____________|____________
             |
   Monitor   |   Keep
             |  Informed
             |
         Low Interest → High Interest
```

**Actions:**
- **Manage Closely**: Advisor, key partners (weekly engagement)
- **Keep Informed**: Experts, peers (regular updates)
- **Keep Satisfied**: Executives, sponsors (periodic briefings)
- **Monitor**: General audience (minimal engagement)

---

### Communication Plan Template

|-------------|------|------|-----|-------|
| Advisor | Progress, issues | Weekly | Meeting | You |
| Partner | Updates, findings | Bi-weekly | Email + call | You |
| SME | Specific questions | As needed | Phone | You |

## 4. Risk Management

### Risk Assessment Matrix
```
        High Impact
            |
  Medium  |  HIGH
   RISK   |  RISK
__________|__________
            |
   LOW     | Medium
   RISK    |  RISK
            |
    Low Prob → High Prob
```

**Risk Register:**
| Risk | Prob | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|------|--------|------------|-------|
| Low survey response | H | M | Over-recruit, incentives | You |
| SME unavailable | M | M | Backup experts identified | You |

**Probability:** H = >50%, M = 20-50%, L = <20%
**Impact:** H = Jeopardizes success, M = Significant, L = Minor

## 5. Scope Management

### Scope Control Questions
Before adding anything new, ask:
1. Is this in my approved proposal? (If no → stop)
2. Does this advance my objectives? (If no → stop)
3. Can I do this in available time? (If no → stop)
4. Will this improve quality significantly? (If no → stop)

### Scope Parking Lot
**Keep list of "good ideas for later"**
- Interesting tangents
- "Nice to have" additions
- Future research opportunities

Don't add to current scope, but document for portfolio or future work.

## 7. Time Management

### Weekly Planning Ritual
**Sunday/Monday:**
1. Review overall project status
2. Identify week's top 3 priorities (MITs)
3. Schedule MITs in calendar (time blocking)
4. Prep materials needed
5. Set weekly success criteria

**Daily:**
- Start with MIT #1
- Time block deep work
- Minimize distractions
- End with tomorrow prep

### Pomodoro Technique
```
[25 min work] → [5 min break] → [25 min work] → [5 min break]
→ [25 min work] → [5 min break] → [25 min work] → [15 min break]
```

**Use for:** Focused analytical work, writing, analysis

- 🟢 **Green**: On track, no issues
- 🟡 **Yellow**: Minor issues, manageable
- 🔴 **Red**: Significant problems, need help

**Apply to:**
- Scope (delivering what planned?)
- Schedule (on timeline?)
- Quality (meeting standards?)
- Stakeholder (relationships healthy?)

## 9. Decision-Making Framework

### RAPID Model
- **R**ecommend: Who proposes?
- **A**pprove: Who decides? (Usually advisor)
- **P**erform: Who executes? (Usually you)
- **I**nput: Who provides input?
- **D**ecide: Final decision maker

### When Stuck on Decision:
1. What's the decision?
2. What are the options?
3. What are pros/cons of each?
4. What do data/analysis suggest?
5. What does advisor recommend?
6. Make decision, document rationale
7. Execute and monitor

## 10. Meeting Management

### Advisor Meeting Template (30 min)

**Prep (before meeting):**
- Send agenda 24 hours ahead
- Prepare materials
- Identify decisions needed

**Structure:**
1. **Check-in** (2 min): Overall status
2. **Progress review** (8 min): What's done
3. **Issues/decisions** (12 min): Where need help
4. **Next steps** (5 min): Plan forward
5. **Wrap-up** (3 min): Action items

**Follow-up (after meeting):**
- Send summary with action items
- Complete agreed tasks
- Update project plan

## 11. Emergency Protocols

### When Falling Behind Schedule
1. **Assess:** How far behind? Why?
2. **Triage:** Must-have vs. nice-to-have
3. **Compress:** Can tasks overlap?
4. **Simplify:** Reduce scope appropriately
5. **Communicate:** Alert advisor immediately
6. **Adjust:** Update plan realistically
7. **Execute:** Focus on critical path

### When Data Quality Issues
1. **Diagnose:** What's the problem?
2. **Impact:** How does this affect conclusions?
3. **Options:** Can you fix? Work around? Collect more?
4. **Decide:** Choose path forward
5. **Document:** Note limitations
6. **Consult:** Get advisor input

## 12. Productivity Hacks

### Beat Procrastination
- **5-Minute Rule**: Just start for 5 minutes
- **Tiny Tasks**: Break overwhelming into tiny
- **Accountability**: Report to peer or advisor
- **Rewards**: Celebrate small wins
- **Environment**: Change location if stuck

### Maintain Momentum
- **Quick Wins**: Start week with easy task
- **Visual Progress**: Use progress bars/charts
- **Peer Support**: Regular check-ins with cohort
- **Self-Care**: Sleep, exercise, breaks
- **Perspective**: Remember why this matters
