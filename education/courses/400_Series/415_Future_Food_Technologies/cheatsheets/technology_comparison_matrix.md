## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS BY TECHNOLOGY

### Precision Fermentation
✓ **Growth factor cost reduction** (90% of COGS)
✓ Scale-up to 100,000L+ bioreactors
✓ Downstream processing efficiency
✓ GRAS approval pathway

### Cultured Meat
✓ **Serum-free media** (<$10/L target)
✓ Cell line immortalization or high passage stability
✓ Scaffold/texture engineering
✓ Regulatory approval (FDA/USDA)
✓ Consumer acceptance

### Insect Protein
✓ **Automation** (labor <5% of costs)
✓ Scale (10,000+ tons/year facilities)
✓ Feed sourcing (organic waste ideal)
✓ Consumer acceptance for human food

### 3D Food Printing
✓ **Speed** (<2 min/serving for viability)
✓ Material diversity (beyond pastes)
✓ Cost reduction (equipment + materials)
✓ Killer app identification (personalized nutrition)

### Molecular Farming
✓ **Containment** (prevent gene flow)
✓ Non-food crop platforms (tobacco, safflower)
✓ Glycoengineering for complex proteins
✓ Regulatory dual oversight (FDA + USDA)

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR EACH TECHNOLOGY

**PRECISION FERMENTATION:**
- Identical molecules to animal products, no cells in final product
- Cost approaching parity (2027-2030)
- Established regulatory path (GRAS)
- Best near-term commercial potential

**CULTURED MEAT:**
- Real meat from cells, not plants
- Still 5-20x more expensive than conventional
- Regulatory approvals emerging (Singapore, USA, Israel)
- 2030+ for price parity

**INSECT PROTEIN:**
- Best resource efficiency (FCR 1.5-2.0)
- Animal feed market larger than human food
- Consumer acceptance main barrier (Western markets)
- Already profitable at scale

**3D FOOD PRINTING:**
- Enables personalization and customization
- Slow and expensive currently
- Clinical nutrition is killer app
- 2030+ for broader adoption

**MOLECULAR FARMING:**
- Plants as bioreactors for proteins/pharma
- Extremely low cost potential
- Containment challenges critical
- Pandemic response applications

**GENE-EDITED CROPS:**
- Precision improvements without foreign DNA
- Regulatory advantage in USA vs. EU
- Faster than traditional breeding
- Climate adaptation primary use case
