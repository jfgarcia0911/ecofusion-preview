## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of Technology Readiness Levels (TRL) in food innovation?

A) To rank technologies by market share
B) To assess the maturity and commercialization status of technologies
C) To determine the nutritional value of food products
D) To measure consumer acceptance levels

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** TRL is a systematic framework (1-9 scale) used to assess how mature a technology is from basic research through commercial deployment.

---

### Question 2
According to the Gartner Hype Cycle framework presented in the module, where would precision fermentation most likely be positioned in 2025?

A) Innovation Trigger
B) Peak of Inflated Expectations
C) Trough of Disillusionment
D) Slope of Enlightenment

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Precision fermentation has moved past early hype, overcome initial challenges, and is now scaling rapidly with multiple commercial products (Perfect Day, Impossible Foods, etc.), placing it on the Slope of Enlightenment.

---

### Question 3
Which of the following is NOT typically a characteristic of technologies in the "Trough of Disillusionment" phase?

A) Slower than expected adoption
B) Some companies failing or exiting
C) Massive media attention and enthusiasm
D) Reality setting in about limitations

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Massive media attention characterizes the "Peak of Inflated Expectations." The Trough is marked by reduced interest as reality doesn't match initial hype.

---

### Question 4
What was the approximate timeline for precision fermentation to go from innovation trigger to commercial products?

A) 2-3 years
B) 5-7 years
C) 10-12 years
D) 20+ years

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Modern precision fermentation for food began ~2011-2014 (Perfect Day, Impossible Foods founded) with first commercial products 2020-2023, approximately 10 years.

---

### Question 5
Technology convergence in food systems refers to:

A) All companies merging into larger corporations
B) Integration of multiple technologies (biotech, AI, materials science) to create innovations
C) Countries adopting the same regulations
D) Standardization of food products globally

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Technology convergence is when multiple distinct technologies combine synergistically (e.g., AI + biotech + robotics in vertical farms).

---

### Question 6
Which investment trend characterizes the food technology sector from 2020-2025?

A) Declining overall investment
B) Shift from plant-based to cellular agriculture focus
C) Consolidation and focus on profitability after peak valuations
D) Government funding replacing private capital

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** After peak investment and valuations in 2021-2022, the sector experienced consolidation with increased focus on unit economics and profitability.

---

### Question 7
What is a "weak signal" in the context of technology foresight?

A) A technology with low market potential
B) A small indicator today that could become a major trend
C) A failed product launch
D) Consumer disinterest in innovation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Weak signals are early, subtle indicators of potential future changes that might seem minor now but could become significant trends.

---

### Question 8
According to the technology S-curve model, which phase is characterized by rapid growth and scaling?

A) Emergence Phase (TRL 1-3)
B) Growth Phase (TRL 4-7)
C) Maturity Phase (TRL 8-9)
D) Decline Phase

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Growth Phase features rapid adoption, scaling up, and major capital investment as technology proves viable and scales.

---

### Question 9
Which of the following best describes the current status (2025) of cultured meat technology according to the module?

A) Widely available in supermarkets globally
B) First regulatory approvals with limited commercial availability
C) Still in basic research phase
D) Fully cost-competitive with conventional meat

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Cultured meat has received approvals in Singapore, USA, and Israel but is not yet widely available or cost-competitive, with only limited restaurant/pilot sales.

---

### Question 10
PESTEL analysis for evaluating food technologies includes which of the following dimensions? (SELECT ALL THAT APPLY)

A) Political
B) Economic
C) Scientific
D) Technological
E) Environmental
F) Legal

**Correct Answers:** A, B, D, E, F

**Explanation:** PESTEL = Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal. "Scientific" is not one of the six dimensions (though it's related to Technological).
