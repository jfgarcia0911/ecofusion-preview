### Question 1
Mathematical optimization in agriculture aims to:

A) Make problems more complex
B) Find best solution subject to constraints (e.g., minimize water use while meeting yield target)
C) Ignore all constraints
D) Use maximum resources

**Correct Answer: B**

### Question 2

### Question 10

## Answer Key

1. B - Find best solution subject to constraints
2. B - Optimizing linear objective with linear constraints
3. B - Optimal irrigation timing and amounts
4. B - Growth stage, uptake rates, system capacity
5. B - Lighting, HVAC, crop type, weather
6. B - Trade-offs between water, nutrients, energy, labor
7. B - Solutions meet physical and operational limits
8. B - Recommends specific actions to optimize outcomes
9. B - Trade-off solutions (Pareto frontier)
10. B - Fast algorithms and current data for dynamic adjustments
