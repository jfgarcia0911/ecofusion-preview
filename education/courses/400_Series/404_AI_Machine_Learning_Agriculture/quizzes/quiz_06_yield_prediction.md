### Question 1
Feature engineering for yield prediction includes creating variables like:

A) Random noise
B) Cumulative growing degree days, cumulative light integral, nutrient uptake rates
C) Unrelated market data only
D) No features needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which ensemble method combines multiple weak learners into a strong predictor?

A) Single decision tree
B) Random Forest or Gradient Boosting
C) Linear regression only
D) No combination

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
XGBoost is:

A) A video game
B) Extreme Gradient Boosting - powerful ensemble algorithm
C) Simple linear model
D) Database system

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
For yield prediction, historical data should include:

A) Only current season
B) Multiple seasons, environmental data, and management practices
C) Random information
D) No data needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What does MAE (Mean Absolute Error) measure?

A) Maximum error only
B) Average magnitude of prediction errors
C) No errors
D) Model training time

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Confidence intervals in yield predictions provide:

A) Exact future values
B) Range of likely outcomes with uncertainty estimate
C) Random guesses
D) Historical data only

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
Deep learning for yield prediction might use inputs including:

A) Only soil type
B) Time-series sensor data, images, and environmental variables
C) Random values
D) No inputs

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
For multi-crop yield modeling, what approach handles different crop types?

A) Ignore crop differences
B) Crop-specific models or multi-task learning
C) Single universal constant
D) No modeling possible

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
Uncertainty quantification helps by:

A) Hiding all errors
B) Providing prediction confidence and risk assessment
C) Making predictions worse
D) Eliminating variability

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
A yield model with R² = 0.85 means:

A) 15% of variance explained
B) 85% of variance in yield explained by model
C) Model is 85 years old
D) 85 data points used

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Cumulative GDD, light integral, nutrient uptake
2. B - Random Forest or Gradient Boosting
3. B - Extreme Gradient Boosting algorithm
4. B - Multiple seasons, environmental data, management
5. B - Average magnitude of prediction errors
6. B - Range of likely outcomes with uncertainty
7. B - Time-series data, images, environmental variables
8. B - Crop-specific models or multi-task learning
9. B - Prediction confidence and risk assessment
10. B - 85% of variance explained by model
