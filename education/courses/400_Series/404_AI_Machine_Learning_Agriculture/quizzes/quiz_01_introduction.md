## Instructions
- Total Questions: 10
- Passing Score: 70%

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between traditional automation and AI-powered systems in agriculture?

A) AI systems are more expensive
B) AI systems learn from data and adapt without explicit programming
C) Traditional automation is faster
D) AI systems require less maintenance

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which type of machine learning is best suited for climate control optimization in greenhouses?

A) Supervised Learning
B) Unsupervised Learning
C) Reinforcement Learning
D) Semi-supervised Learning

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
In the ML development lifecycle, what comes immediately after Data Collection?

A) Model Deployment
B) Data Preparation
C) Model Development
D) Problem Definition

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is the typical CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) for the agricultural AI market from 2020-2030?

A) 10-15%
B) 18-22%
C) 25-30%
D) 35-40%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is NOT a direct benefit of AI/ML implementation in CEA?

A) Labor reduction
B) Resource efficiency improvement
C) Elimination of all crop diseases
D) Yield improvement

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
What metric measures the proportion of variance explained by a regression model?

A) MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
B) RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)
C) R² Score
D) F1-Score

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
In a typical ROI calculation for agricultural AI implementation, what is the average payback period for a well-planned project?

A) 1-3 months
B) 3-6 months
C) 1-2 years
D) 3-5 years

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
Which Python library is specifically designed for time-series forecasting with built-in seasonality detection?

A) TensorFlow
B) scikit-learn
C) Prophet
D) NumPy

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
What does overfitting mean in machine learning?

A) Model trains too quickly
B) Model performs well on training data but poorly on new data
C) Model uses too few features
D) Model requires too much computational power

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Which of the following is the recommended first step when starting an ML project?

A) Choose the most complex algorithm
B) Start with simple models and increase complexity
C) Immediately deploy to production
D) Use only deep learning methods

**Correct Answer: B**
