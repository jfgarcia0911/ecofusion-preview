### Question 1
Reinforcement Learning (RL) is suitable for climate control because:

A) It requires no data
B) Agent learns optimal actions through trial-and-error interaction with environment
C) It's the simplest approach
D) It eliminates all sensors

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
In RL, what is the "reward function"?

A) Monetary payment to programmer
B) Signal indicating how good an action was (e.g., energy saved, optimal growth)
C) Random number
D) Temperature sensor reading

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
Q-Learning is an example of:

A) Supervised learning
B) Model-free reinforcement learning
C) Unsupervised clustering
D) Linear regression

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
Deep Q-Networks (DQN) combine:

A) Nothing useful
B) Deep neural networks with Q-learning
C) Only shallow models
D) Random algorithms

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
Multi-objective optimization for HVAC systems balances:

A) Single objective only
B) Conflicting goals like energy efficiency, crop quality, and comfort
C) No objectives
D) Random targets

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Model-based RL differs from model-free because it:

A) Uses no models
B) Learns a model of environment dynamics to improve sample efficiency
C) Is always slower
D) Requires more data

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
An RL agent for greenhouse climate control might optimize:

A) Only aesthetics
B) Temperature, humidity, CO2, and light while minimizing energy
C) Random settings
D) Maximum energy consumption

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
"Exploration vs. exploitation" dilemma means:

A) Mining for resources
B) Balancing trying new actions vs. using known good actions
C) Always exploiting
D) Never exploring

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
Transfer learning in RL for agriculture allows:

A) Deleting all previous knowledge
B) Applying knowledge from one greenhouse to another similar system
C) Starting from scratch every time
D) No knowledge sharing

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Safety constraints in RL climate control ensure:

A) No constraints needed
B) Actions don't harm crops (e.g., extreme temperatures avoided)
C) Maximum risk
D) Random operations

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Agent learns optimal actions through trial-and-error
2. B - Signal indicating how good an action was
3. B - Model-free reinforcement learning
4. B - Deep neural networks with Q-learning
5. B - Conflicting goals like energy, quality, and comfort
6. B - Learns environment model to improve efficiency
7. B - Temperature, humidity, CO2, light while minimizing energy
8. B - Balancing new actions vs. known good actions
9. B - Applying knowledge from one system to another
10. B - Actions don't harm crops
