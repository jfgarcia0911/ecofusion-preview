# Quiz 7: Disease & Pest Detection Systems

## Instructions
- Total Questions: 10
- Passing Score: 70%
- Time Limit: 15 minutes

---

### Question 1
Image classification for disease detection assigns:

A) Random labels
B) A disease category label to entire image or plant
C) No information
D) Only numerical values

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Transfer learning for disease detection uses:

A) Training from scratch only
B) Pre-trained models (e.g., ImageNet) fine-tuned on agricultural data
C) No training
D) Deleting all knowledge

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is the PRIMARY benefit of early disease detection systems?

A) No benefit
B) Intervention before widespread outbreak, reducing crop loss
C) Delaying all actions
D) Increasing disease spread

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
Data augmentation for training disease detectors includes:

A) Deleting images
B) Rotation, flipping, brightness changes to increase dataset diversity
C) Using only one image
D) No data needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
Anomaly detection algorithms identify:

A) Normal patterns only
B) Unusual patterns that may indicate emerging problems
C) Nothing
D) Random noise

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
For real-time disease monitoring, models should be deployed:

A) On supercomputers only
B) On edge devices (cameras, mobile) or cloud with fast inference
C) Never deployed
D) Only on paper

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
Integration with IPM (Integrated Pest Management) means:

A) Ignoring all pest data
B) Using AI detection to inform targeted interventions
C) Maximum pesticide use
D) No management

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
MobileNet and EfficientNet are designed for:

A) Desktop supercomputers only
B) Efficient deployment on mobile and edge devices
C) Slow processing
D) Maximum energy consumption

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
Class imbalance (e.g., few diseased images vs. many healthy) is addressed by:

A) Ignoring minority class
B) Oversampling, undersampling, or weighted loss functions
C) Deleting all data
D) No solution exists

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Explainable AI for disease detection helps by:

A) Hiding all reasoning
B) Showing which image regions influenced the diagnosis
C) Making models more complex
D) Reducing accuracy

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - A disease category label to image/plant
2. B - Pre-trained models fine-tuned on agricultural data
3. B - Intervention before outbreak, reducing loss
4. B - Rotation, flipping, brightness changes
5. B - Unusual patterns indicating problems
6. B - Edge devices or cloud with fast inference
7. B - Using AI to inform targeted interventions
8. B - Efficient deployment on mobile/edge devices
9. B - Oversampling, undersampling, or weighted loss
10. B - Shows which regions influenced diagnosis
