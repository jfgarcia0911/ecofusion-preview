### Question 1
Which time-series model is best for data with strong seasonal patterns?

A) Linear Regression
B) ARIMA
C) Prophet or Seasonal ARIMA (SARIMA)
D) Logistic Regression

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) networks are a type of:

A) Decision Tree
B) Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)
C) Linear model
D) K-means clustering

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
For harvest timing prediction, which approach is MOST appropriate?

A) Random guessing
B) Time-series forecasting of growth stages
C) Image classification only
D) Ignoring all data

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is the purpose of train-test split in predictive modeling?

A) To increase dataset size
B) To evaluate model performance on unseen data
C) To delete data
D) To slow down training

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
Cross-validation helps with:

A) Deleting outliers
B) Assessing model generalization and reducing overfitting risk
C) Increasing model complexity
D) Eliminating all errors

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
For nutrient demand forecasting, which features are MOST relevant?

A) Stock market prices
B) Plant growth stage, biomass, temperature, and growth rate
C) Random numbers
D) Historical weather from 100 years ago

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
Hyperparameter tuning uses methods including:

A) Random trial and error only
B) Grid search, random search, or Bayesian optimization
C) No tuning needed
D) Guessing

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
A model has high training accuracy but low test accuracy. This indicates:

A) Perfect model
B) Overfitting to training data
C) Underfitting
D) Optimal performance

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
Feature importance analysis reveals:

A) Which variables have no impact
B) Which input variables most influence predictions
C) Random rankings
D) Only aesthetic preferences

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
For continuous target variables (e.g., yield in kg), use:

A) Classification algorithms
B) Regression algorithms
C) Clustering only
D) No algorithm needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Prophet or Seasonal ARIMA
2. B - Recurrent Neural Network
3. B - Time-series forecasting of growth stages
4. B - Evaluate performance on unseen data
5. B - Assess generalization and reduce overfitting risk
6. B - Plant growth stage, biomass, temperature, growth rate
7. B - Grid search, random search, or Bayesian optimization
8. B - Overfitting to training data
9. B - Which variables most influence predictions
10. B - Regression algorithms
