# Module 11: Human-Robot Collaboration

## Overview

Collaborative robots (cobots) work alongside humans, combining human adaptability with robotic consistency. This module covers cobot technologies, safety systems, workspace design, and best practices for human-robot teams in agricultural settings.

**Duration:** 1 week
**Level:** Expert

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand collaborative robot technologies and capabilities
2. Conduct risk assessments for collaborative workspaces (ISO/TS 15066)
3. Design safe and efficient human-robot work cells
4. Implement safety-rated monitoring systems
5. Develop training programs for human operators
6. Optimize task allocation between humans and robots
7. Ensure regulatory compliance for collaborative applications

---

## Key Topics

### 1. Collaborative Robot Technologies
- Power and force limiting (inherently safe)
- Safety-rated monitored stop
- Hand guiding mode
- Speed and separation monitoring
- Key manufacturers (Universal Robots, ABB YuMi, FANUC CR series)

### 2. Safety Standards and Risk Assessment
- ISO/TS 15066: Collaborative robots
- ISO 10218: Industrial robot safety
- Risk assessment methodology
- Safety validation and testing
- Documentation requirements

### 3. Workspace Design
- Layout principles for collaboration
- Physical barriers vs. virtual safety zones
- Ergonomic considerations for human workers
- Tool and material accessibility
- Visual indicators (lights, signals)

### 4. Applications in Agriculture
- Co-packing (human quality check + robot box filling)
- Assisted harvesting (human picks, robot transports)
- Inspection support (robot holds/positions plants)
- Training applications (robot demonstrates, human learns)

### 5. Training and Change Management
- Operator training curricula
- Addressing worker concerns and skepticism
- Continuous improvement and feedback loops
- Union and regulatory engagement

---

## Key Takeaways

- Cobots enable flexible automation without full human displacement
- Safety is paramount: must comply with ISO/TS 15066
- Workspace design critical for efficiency and acceptance
- Proper training reduces resistance and improves outcomes
- ROI includes both productivity and worker satisfaction improvements
