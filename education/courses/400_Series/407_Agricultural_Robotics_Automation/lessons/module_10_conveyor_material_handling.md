# Module 10: Conveyor and Material Handling Systems

## Overview

Efficient material flow is critical in CEA facilities. This module covers conveyor systems, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), robotic palletizing, and integration strategies for seamless logistics from propagation to packaging.

**Duration:** 1 week
**Level:** Expert

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Select appropriate conveyor types for different materials and layouts
2. Design material flow systems for optimized facility throughput
3. Implement automated sorting and grading systems
4. Integrate robotic palletizing and depalletizing
5. Optimize warehouse automation for harvest storage
6. Calculate system capacity and identify bottlenecks
7. Implement traceability and tracking systems

---

## Key Topics

### 1. Conveyor Systems
- Belt conveyors (flat, troughed, cleated)
- Roller conveyors (powered, gravity)
- Chain conveyors (overhead, floor-mounted)
- Sorting systems (diverters, pop-up transfers)
- Selection criteria (capacity, speed, elevation changes)

### 2. Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs)
- vs. AMRs (fixed path vs. flexible navigation)
- Guidance systems (magnetic tape, laser, vision)
- Fleet management and traffic control
- Payload capacity and speed optimization

### 3. Robotic Palletizing
- Delta robots for high-speed picking
- Articulated arms for mixed pallets
- Gripper types (vacuum, mechanical)
- Pallet patterns and stability
- Throughput optimization

### 4. Sorting and Grading
- Weight-based sorting (inline scales)
- Vision-based grading (size, color, defects)
- Multi-tier sorting (primary, secondary grading)
- Reject handling and analytics

### 5. Integration Strategies
- Facility layout optimization
- Synchronization with production rhythms
- Buffer zones and surge capacity
- Emergency stop and safety systems

---

## Key Takeaways

- Material handling typically 15-25% of operational labor
- Conveyor automation provides immediate productivity gains
- Robotic palletizing achieves consistent patterns and speeds
- Integration complexity increases with automation level
- ROI typically 1-3 years for high-throughput facilities
