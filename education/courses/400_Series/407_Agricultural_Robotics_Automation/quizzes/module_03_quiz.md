### Question 1
What is the primary challenge in designing grippers for delicate plant materials?

A) Achieving sufficient grip force without causing tissue damage
B) Minimizing gripper weight
C) Maximizing opening width
D) Reducing manufacturing cost

**Correct Answer: A**

---

### Question 2
What type of gripper is most suitable for harvesting ripe tomatoes?

A) Parallel jaw gripper with metal fingers
B) Soft pneumatic gripper with compliant materials
C) Magnetic gripper
D) Adhesive gripper

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical gripping force range for handling leafy greens?

A) 0.1-0.5 N
B) 1-3 N
C) 5-10 N
D) 15-25 N

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
Which sensor type is most commonly integrated into agricultural grippers for force feedback?

A) Capacitive sensors
B) Strain gauges or force/torque sensors
C) Ultrasonic sensors
D) Temperature sensors

**Correct Answer: B**

### Question 5
What is the advantage of vacuum grippers for leafy crops?

A) Higher precision than mechanical grippers
B) Distributed contact force reducing damage
C) Lower energy consumption
D) Simpler mechanical design

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Multi-fingered grippers with 3+ fingers provide what key advantage?

A) Faster operation
B) Better force distribution and object stability
C) Lower cost
D) Easier control algorithms

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What material property is most important for soft robotic grippers?

A) High stiffness
B) Electrical conductivity
C) Compliance and elasticity
D) Thermal resistance

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 8

A) Operate faster
B) Switch between different end effectors for various tasks
C) Reduce power consumption

**Correct Answer: B**

### Question 9
What is the typical actuation time for pneumatic soft grippers?

A) <50 milliseconds
B) 100-500 milliseconds
C) 1-2 seconds
D) 5-10 seconds

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
Which design principle helps prevent bruising when gripping fruit?

A) Maximum contact area with minimum pressure
B) Minimum contact area with maximum pressure
C) Rigid metal fingers
D) High-speed gripping action

**Correct Answer: A**

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Balancing grip force vs. tissue damage is primary challenge
2. B - Soft pneumatic grippers prevent bruising
3. B - 1-3 N typical for leafy greens
4. B - Strain gauges/F/T sensors provide force feedback
5. B - Vacuum distributes force, reduces damage
6. B - Multiple fingers improve stability and force distribution
7. C - Compliance/elasticity essential for soft grippers
8. B - Tool changers enable multi-task capability
9. B - Pneumatic actuation typically 100-500 ms
10. A - Large contact area with low pressure prevents damage
