### Question 1
What is the key difference between AMRs and AGVs?

A) AMRs can navigate dynamically without fixed infrastructure; AGVs follow fixed paths
B) AMRs are slower than AGVs
C) AMRs cannot carry payloads
D) AGVs are more expensive

**Correct Answer: A**

---

### Question 2
Which wheel configuration provides holonomic motion (movement in any direction)?

A) Differential drive
B) Ackermann steering
C) Mecanum wheels or omnidirectional wheels
D) Tank treads

**Correct Answer: C**
