### Question 1
What is the typical accuracy for automated precision seeding systems?

A) 70-80%
B) 85-90%
C) 95-99%

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 2

B) Vacuum-based singulation
C) Belt seeder
D) Broadcast spreader

**Correct Answer: B**
