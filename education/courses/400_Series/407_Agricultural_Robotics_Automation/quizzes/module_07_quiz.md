### Question 1
What is the primary bottleneck limiting harvesting robot adoption?

A) Power consumption
B) Detection accuracy and picking speed compared to human labor
C) Robot weight
D) Programming complexity

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which crop type is currently EASIEST for robots to harvest successfully?

A) Strawberries
B) Tomatoes on the vine
C) Leafy greens (lettuce)
D) Apples

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical harvesting rate for advanced robotic lettuce harvesters?

A) 10-20 heads per hour
B) 50-100 heads per hour
C) 500-1000 heads per hour
D) 5000+ heads per hour

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
How do harvesting robots typically assess fruit ripeness?

A) Touching and feeling firmness only
B) Computer vision analyzing color, size, and sometimes spectral data
C) Random selection
D) Harvesting all fruit regardless of ripeness

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What cutting mechanism is most common for harvesting leafy crops?

A) Laser cutting
B) Water jet
C) Rotary blade or reciprocating knife
D) Manual scissors

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
What is a major challenge specific to strawberry harvesting robots?

A) Locating the fruit
B) Navigating between rows
C) Gentle handling without bruising and peduncle cutting accuracy
D) Power supply

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What percentage of commercial tomato harvesting is currently automated by robots?

A) <1% (very limited)
B) 10-20%
C) 40-50%
D) >70%

**Correct Answer: A**

---

### Question 8
Which approach helps improve harvesting rate in multi-arm systems?

A) Coordinated parallel operation with task allocation
B) Operating arms sequentially one at a time
C) Using only one arm at a time
D) Random arm selection

**Correct Answer: A**

---

### Question 9
What bin management strategy maximizes robot efficiency?

A) Manual bin replacement by workers
B) Autonomous bin switching or AMR-based bin delivery
C) Fixed bins that never move
D) Single large bin for entire shift

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What is the typical success rate for current strawberry harvesting robots?

A) 20-40%
B) 50-70%
C) 75-85%
D) 95-100%

**Correct Answer: C**
