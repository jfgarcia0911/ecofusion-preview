# Course 406: Bioprocess Engineering for CEA
## Expert Level Course

### Course Description

This advanced course explores the principles and applications of bioprocess engineering in controlled environment agriculture (CEA) systems. Students will gain expertise in designing, optimizing, and scaling biological processes that enhance nutrient cycling, waste valorization, and resource efficiency in CEA facilities. The course integrates microbiology, biochemical engineering, process control, and economic analysis to provide a comprehensive understanding of bioprocess applications in modern agriculture.

Through rigorous theoretical foundations and practical applications, students will learn to engineer biological systems including nitrification reactors, anaerobic digesters, composting operations, algae cultivation systems, and biocontrol production facilities. The course emphasizes quantitative analysis, kinetic modeling, process optimization, and regulatory compliance.

**Course Duration:** 14 weeks (140 hours total)
- Lecture/Theory: 56 hours
- Laboratory/Practical: 42 hours
- Project Work: 28 hours
- Assessment: 14 hours

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Apply Bioprocess Fundamentals**
   - Analyze microbial growth kinetics and metabolic pathways
   - Design and optimize bioreactor systems for CEA applications
   - Calculate mass and energy balances for biological processes

2. **Engineer Nutrient Cycling Systems**
   - Design nitrification and denitrification reactors
   - Optimize biofilter performance for aquaponic systems
   - Implement biological nutrient recovery technologies

3. **Develop Waste Valorization Processes**
   - Engineer anaerobic digestion systems for organic waste
   - Design aerobic composting operations with process control
   - Optimize resource recovery from agricultural waste streams

4. **Design Cultivation Systems**
   - Engineer photobioreactors for algae production
   - Design fermentation systems for beneficial organisms
   - Scale biocontrol agent production facilities

5. **Implement Process Control**
   - Develop monitoring strategies for biological processes
   - Design automated control systems for bioprocesses
   - Troubleshoot process deviations and optimize performance

6. **Conduct Engineering Analysis**
   - Perform techno-economic analysis of bioprocess systems
   - Model process scale-up from laboratory to commercial scale
   - Evaluate environmental impacts and sustainability metrics

7. **Navigate Regulatory Framework**
   - Ensure compliance with biosafety regulations
   - Design processes meeting environmental discharge standards
   - Implement quality control for biological products

### Course Modules

#### Module 1: Introduction to Bioprocess Engineering for CEA
- Bioprocess engineering principles and applications
- Overview of biological processes in CEA systems
- Integration of bioprocesses in facility design
- Economic drivers for bioprocess adoption

#### Module 2: Microbial Kinetics and Growth Modeling
- Microbial growth phases and kinetics
- Monod and non-Monod growth models
- Substrate utilization and product formation
- Mathematical modeling of microbial systems

#### Module 3: Bioreactor Design Principles
- Bioreactor configurations and selection criteria
- Mass transfer and oxygen delivery
- Mixing and fluid dynamics in bioreactors
- Scale-up and scale-down considerations

#### Module 4: Nitrification Process Engineering
- Nitrifying bacteria physiology and kinetics
- Moving bed bioreactor (MBBR) design
- Trickling filter and RAS optimization
- Process control for nitrification stability

#### Module 5: Denitrification and Nitrogen Management
- Heterotrophic denitrification processes
- Carbon source optimization
- Anammox process engineering
- Integrated nitrogen removal systems

#### Module 6: Anaerobic Digestion Systems
- Anaerobic digestion fundamentals and microbiology
- Digester design and configuration
- Biogas production and utilization
- Digestate processing and nutrient recovery

#### Module 7: Aerobic Composting Process Engineering
- Composting microbiology and thermodynamics
- Aerated static pile and in-vessel systems
- Process monitoring and control parameters
- Compost quality and maturity assessment

#### Module 8: Algae Cultivation and Harvesting
- Microalgae and macroalgae for CEA applications
- Photobioreactor and open pond design
- Light delivery and CO2 mass transfer
- Harvesting technologies and downstream processing

#### Module 9: Fermentation for Agricultural Applications
- Solid-state and submerged fermentation
- Production of biostimulants and enzymes
- Fermenter design and operation
- Product recovery and formulation

#### Module 10: Biocontrol Agent Production
- Commercial production of beneficial microorganisms
- Bacterial and fungal biocontrol agents
- Quality control and formulation stability
- Regulatory requirements for biological products

#### Module 11: Waste Stream Valorization
- Characterization of CEA waste streams
- Integrated biorefinery concepts
- Resource recovery technologies
- Circular economy implementation

#### Module 12: Process Monitoring and Control
- Critical process parameters for bioprocesses
- Online and offline monitoring technologies
- Process analytical technology (PAT)
- Automated control strategies and optimization

#### Module 13: Scale-up and Economic Analysis
- Scale-up methodologies and challenges
- Techno-economic modeling frameworks
- Capital and operating cost estimation
- Sensitivity and risk analysis

#### Module 14: Regulatory Compliance and Sustainability
- Biosafety and environmental regulations
- Discharge standards and permitting
- Life cycle assessment of bioprocesses
- Sustainability metrics and certification
