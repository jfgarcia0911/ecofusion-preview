### Question 1
The specific growth rate (μ) is defined as:

A) The total increase in biomass per unit time
B) The change in biomass per unit volume
C) (1/X) × (dX/dt)
D) The doubling time of the culture

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
If a bacterial culture has a specific growth rate of 0.693 h⁻¹, what is its doubling time?

A) 0.5 hours
B) 1.0 hours
C) 1.5 hours
D) 2.0 hours

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
In the Monod equation, the half-saturation constant (Ks) is the substrate concentration at which:

A) μ = 0
B) μ = μmax
C) μ = μmax/2
D) μ = 2 × μmax

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
Which phase of batch microbial growth is characterized by the maximum specific growth rate (μmax)?

A) Lag phase
B) Exponential phase
C) Stationary phase
D) Death phase

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
The yield coefficient (YX/S) represents:

A) Grams of substrate consumed per gram of cells produced
B) Grams of cells produced per gram of substrate consumed
C) The growth rate at substrate saturation
D) The maintenance energy requirement

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What happens in a CSTR if the dilution rate (D) exceeds μmax?

A) The process reaches steady state faster
B) Biomass concentration increases
C) Washout occurs (X → 0)
D) Product formation increases

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
Temperature affects microbial growth rate according to which relationship?

A) Linear increase with temperature
B) Arrhenius relationship (exponential)
C) Inverse relationship (higher T, lower μ)
D) No relationship exists

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
For nitrifying bacteria at 25°C, typical μmax values are:

A) 0.001-0.005 h⁻¹
B) 0.02-0.05 h⁻¹
C) 0.2-0.5 h⁻¹
D) 1.0-2.0 h⁻¹

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
The Haldane model extends the Monod equation to account for:

A) Multiple substrate limitation
B) Product inhibition
C) Substrate inhibition at high concentrations
D) Temperature effects

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 10
In a continuous fermentation at steady state, which relationship is true?

A) μ > D
B) μ < D
C) μ = D
D) μ = D²

**Correct Answer: C**
