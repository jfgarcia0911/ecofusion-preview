### Question 1
What are the four main stages of anaerobic digestion in order?

A) Acidogenesis, Hydrolysis, Acetogenesis, Methanogenesis
B) Hydrolysis, Acidogenesis, Acetogenesis, Methanogenesis
C) Methanogenesis, Acetogenesis, Acidogenesis, Hydrolysis
