### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of bioprocess engineering in CEA systems?

A) To increase crop yields through genetic modification
B) To apply engineering principles to biological processes for nutrient cycling and waste valorization
C) To replace all synthetic fertilizers with organic alternatives
D) To eliminate the need for environmental control systems

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which of the following is NOT one of the four stages of anaerobic digestion?

A) Hydrolysis
B) Nitrification
C) Acetogenesis
D) Methanogenesis

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
In a nitrification process, ammonia (NH₃) is converted to nitrate (NO₃⁻). How much oxygen is theoretically required per gram of NH₄⁺-N oxidized?

A) 2.86 g O₂
B) 4.57 g O₂
C) 7.14 g O₂
D) 10.0 g O₂

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What percentage of water savings can be achieved with aquaponic systems using biofilters compared to traditional agriculture?

A) 50-60%
B) 70-80%
C) 85-90%
D) 95-98%

**Correct Answer: D**

---

### Question 5
Which of the following bioprocesses produces methane (CH₄) as a primary product?

A) Nitrification
B) Aerobic composting
C) Anaerobic digestion
D) Denitrification

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
Typical space allocation for biofilters in aquaponic facilities is:

A) 1-2% of total facility
B) 3-8% of total facility
C) 15-20% of total facility
D) 25-30% of total facility

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
Which equation represents the overall nitrification process?

A) NH₃ + O₂ → NO₂⁻ + H₂O
B) NO₂⁻ + O₂ → NO₃⁻
C) NH₃ + 2 O₂ → NO₃⁻ + H⁺ + H₂O
D) CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 8
What is a primary challenge in scaling up bioprocesses from laboratory to commercial scale?

A) All microorganisms grow faster at large scale
B) Process stability is easier to maintain at large scale
C) Mass transfer limitations become more significant
D) Temperature control becomes simpler

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
In the context of CEA, what does "circular economy" primarily refer to?

A) Using circular growing beds for plants
B) Recycling nutrients and energy within the facility to minimize waste
C) Operating the facility 24/7 in continuous cycles
D) Rotating crops in a circular pattern

**Correct Answer: B**

### Question 10

A) USDA (United States Department of Agriculture)
B) FDA (Food and Drug Administration)
