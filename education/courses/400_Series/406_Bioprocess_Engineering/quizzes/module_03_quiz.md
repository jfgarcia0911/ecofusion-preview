### Question 1
Which type of bioreactor is most commonly used in aquaponic systems for nitrification?

A) Continuously Stirred Tank Reactor (CSTR)
B) Plug Flow Reactor (PFR)
C) Moving Bed Bioreactor (MBBR)
D) Membrane Bioreactor (MBR)

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
What is the primary factor limiting oxygen transfer in bioreactors?

A) Temperature
B) The gas-liquid interface area
C) Nutrient concentration
D) pH level

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
The volumetric oxygen transfer coefficient (kLa) is measured in which units?

A) mg/L
B) L/min
C) h⁻¹ (per hour)
D) g/m²

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
In scaling up from a 10L laboratory bioreactor to a 1000L production bioreactor, which parameter typically becomes MORE challenging to maintain?

A) Temperature control
B) pH monitoring
C) Oxygen transfer and mixing uniformity
D) Sterility

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What is the typical power input for mixing in aerobic bioreactors?

A) 0.1-0.5 W/L
B) 1-5 W/L
C) 10-20 W/L
D) 50-100 W/L

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
Which dimensionless number is used to characterize mixing in bioreactors?

A) Reynolds number only
B) Sherwood number
C) Weber number
D) Reynolds and Power numbers

**Correct Answer: D**

### Question 7
What is the recommended hydraulic retention time (HRT) for a typical nitrification bioreactor in aquaponics?

A) 5-15 minutes
B) 30-60 minutes
C) 2-6 hours
D) 24-48 hours

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 8
In airlift bioreactors, what drives the circulation of liquid?
