# Course 406: Bioprocess Engineering for CEA

## Expert Level Course - EcoFusion Academy

### Course Overview

This comprehensive expert-level course provides advanced training in bioprocess engineering principles and applications for Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) systems. Students will gain expertise in designing, optimizing, and scaling biological processes that enhance nutrient cycling, waste valorization, and resource efficiency in modern agricultural facilities.

**Duration:** 14 weeks (140 hours total)
**Level:** Expert/Professional (400-level)
**Prerequisites:** Course 401 (CEA Facility Design), understanding of microbiology and biochemistry

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

### Technical Skills
- Design and size nitrification, denitrification, and anaerobic digestion systems
- Calculate microbial growth kinetics and process rates
- Perform mass and energy balances for bioprocess systems
- Select and specify bioreactor configurations and equipment
- Design process monitoring and control strategies
- Conduct techno-economic analysis and scale-up calculations

### Applied Knowledge
- Integrate bioprocesses into CEA facility design
- Optimize process performance through parameter control
- Troubleshoot common bioprocess problems
- Ensure regulatory compliance for bioprocess operations
- Implement circular economy principles through waste valorization
- Evaluate sustainability metrics and environmental impacts

### Professional Practice
- Prepare professional engineering documentation
- Conduct feasibility studies for bioprocess investments
- Communicate technical concepts to diverse stakeholders
- Apply biosafety and worker protection protocols

## How to Use These Materials

### For Instructors

1. **Course Setup:**
   - Review all lesson modules
   - Customize examples for your local context
   - Set up laboratory access for hands-on exercises
   - Establish grading rubrics

2. **Weekly Schedule:**
   - Week 1: Module 1 (Introduction)
   - Weeks 2-13: Modules 2-13 (one per week)
   - Week 14: Module 14 + Review
   - Week 15: Final Exam + Capstone Presentations

3. **Assessment:**
   - Assign quizzes after each module
   - Schedule mid-term design project (Module 7-8)
   - Final exam in week 15
   - Capstone project due week 15

### For Students

1. **Study Approach:**
   - Read lesson module thoroughly
   - Work through example calculations
   - Complete review questions
   - Take module quiz to assess understanding
   - Refer to cheatsheets for quick reference

2. **Exam Preparation:**
   - Review all modules
   - Focus on problem-solving and calculations
   - Use glossary for terminology review
   - Practice with quizzes and review questions
   - Understand conceptual relationships between topics

3. **Project Work:**
   - Start projects early
   - Use cheatsheets and lessons as reference
   - Collaborate with peers (if permitted)
   - Consult instructor during office hours
   - Document all calculations and assumptions
