# Cheatsheet 3: Business Models, Metrics & Implementation

## Circular Business Model Archetypes

### 1. Circular Supplies
Replace virgin materials with renewable/recycled inputs
**Example**: Using recovered nutrients instead of synthetic fertilizers

### 2. Resource Recovery
Turn waste into value through recovery and recycling
**Example**: Biogas from organic waste, nutrient extraction

### 3. Product Life Extension
Extend product lifespan through maintenance, upgrade, remanufacturing
**Example**: Refurbished equipment, modular systems

### 4. Sharing Platforms
Enable shared use/access to assets
**Example**: Shared equipment cooperatives, land sharing

### 5. Product-as-Service
Customers pay for function, not ownership
**Example**: Lighting-as-service, irrigation-as-service

## Financial Metrics Formulas

### Net Present Value (NPV)
```
NPV = Σ [Cash Flow_t / (1 + r)^t] - Initial Investment

Where:
t = time period
r = discount rate (typically 6-10%)
```

**Decision**: If NPV > 0, project is viable

---

### Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
```
IRR is the discount rate where NPV = 0

Use financial calculator or software (Excel: =IRR function)
```

**Decision**: If IRR > required return, project is attractive

---

### Simple Payback Period
```
Payback (years) = Initial Investment / Annual Cash Flow
```

**Target**: <5 years for most agricultural projects

---

### Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)
```
LCOE = (CAPEX × CRF + Annual OPEX) / Annual Energy Production

CRF = r(1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]
Where: n = project life, r = discount rate
```

## Circularity Metrics

### Material Circularity Indicator (MCI)
```
MCI = 0 to 1 scale

0 = Completely linear
1 = Perfectly circular

Considers:
- Virgin material input
- Recycled content
- Product lifespan
- End-of-life recovery
```

**Target**: MCI > 0.6 for good performance

---

### Resource Efficiency Ratios

**Nutrient Use Efficiency (NUE)**:
```
NUE (%) = (Nutrient in harvest / Nutrient input) × 100

Target: >70%
```

**Water Productivity**:
```
WP = Output value ($) / Water consumed (m³)

OR

WP = Yield (kg) / Water consumed (m³)
```

**Energy Ratio**:
```
ER = Energy output / Energy input

Target: ER > 1.0 (net positive)
```

---

### Waste Metrics

**Waste Diversion Rate**:
```
WDR (%) = (Waste recovered / Total waste generated) × 100

Target: >95% (zero waste goal)
```

**Circularity Rate**:
```
CR (%) = (Recycled materials / Total materials used) × 100

EU 2030 target: 65%
```

## Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) Basics

### Four Phases
1. **Goal & Scope Definition**: Define functional unit, boundaries
2. **Inventory Analysis**: Quantify inputs/outputs
3. **Impact Assessment**: Calculate environmental impacts
4. **Interpretation**: Draw conclusions and recommendations

### Common Impact Categories
- **Climate Change**: kg CO₂-eq
- **Eutrophication**: kg PO₄-eq (phosphate equivalents)
- **Water Depletion**: m³ water consumed
- **Energy Demand**: MJ primary energy
- **Land Use**: m²·year

### Functional Unit Examples
- "1,000 kg tomatoes delivered to market"
- "1 MWh of electricity generated"
- "Treatment of 1 m³ wastewater to irrigation standard"

## Policy & Incentive Navigator

### Financial Incentives (Examples - varies by region)

**Grants**:
- USDA REAP (Renewable Energy): Up to 50% cost share
- EU LIFE Programme: Environmental innovation
- State/provincial ag innovation grants

**Tax Credits**:
- Investment Tax Credit (ITC): Solar, biogas
- Production Tax Credit (PTC): Renewable energy
- Accelerated depreciation

**Renewable Credits**:
- Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)
- Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS) credits
- Carbon credits/offsets

**Feed-in Tariffs**: Guaranteed price for renewable energy (where available)

## Quick Decision Tools

### Circular Opportunity Scoring Matrix

Score each opportunity (1-5) on:
- **Impact**: Environmental/resource benefit
- **Feasibility**: Technical readiness
- **Economics**: ROI potential
- **Strategic Fit**: Alignment with goals

**Total Score**: 20 max. Prioritize >15.

---

### Technology Readiness Level (TRL)

1-3: **Research** - Proof of concept
4-6: **Development** - Prototype/pilot
7-9: **Deployment** - Commercial scale

**Recommendation**: Choose TRL 7+ for core operations, TRL 5-6 for pilots

## Red Flags & Risk Factors

🚩 **Economic Red Flags**:
- Payback >10 years without incentives
- Volatile commodity markets for recovered products
- High operational complexity

🚩 **Technical Red Flags**:
- Unproven technology (TRL <5) at scale
- Poor data quality for design
- Inadequate infrastructure

🚩 **Organizational Red Flags**:
- Lack of leadership commitment
- Insufficient staff capacity
- Resistance to change
