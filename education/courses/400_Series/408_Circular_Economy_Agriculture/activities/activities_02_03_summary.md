# Activities 2 & 3: Practical Exercises

## Activity 2: Circular Business Model Design

### Objective
Design a circular business model for an agricultural operation, identifying multiple revenue streams and value propositions.

**Duration**: 3 hours
**Format**: Individual or small group project

---

### Scenario
You manage a 20-hectare diversified farm producing:
- Vegetables (10 ha): Various crops, 200 tonnes/year
- Orchard (5 ha): Apples, 100 tonnes/year
- Livestock (5 ha): 50 dairy cows, 300,000 L milk/year

**Current Waste Streams**:
- Manure: 600 tonnes/year
- Crop residues: 150 tonnes/year
- Fruit culls/processing waste: 25 tonnes/year
- Wastewater: 15,000 m³/year

**Current Revenue**: $800,000/year
**Current Waste Disposal Cost**: $25,000/year

---

### Part 1: Value Proposition Canvas

Complete for three customer segments:
1. Direct consumers (farmers market, CSA)
2. Wholesale buyers (grocery stores)
3. Institutions (schools, hospitals)

For each, identify:
- Customer jobs (what they're trying to accomplish)
- Pains (challenges, risks, obstacles)
- Gains (desired outcomes, benefits)

Then design:
- Products & services (your offerings)
- Pain relievers (how you address pains)
- Gain creators (how you create gains)

---

### Part 2: Circular Business Model Canvas

Complete all nine building blocks:

**1. Customer Segments**
- Who are you creating value for?
- Include new segments enabled by circular strategies

**2. Value Propositions**
- What value do you deliver?
- Which problems do you solve?
- How does circularity enhance value?

**3. Channels**
- How do you reach customers?
- How do you deliver value?

**4. Customer Relationships**
- What relationships do you establish?
- How are they integrated with business model?

**5. Revenue Streams**
- What are customers willing to pay for?
- Current: Product sales
- New circular revenues:
  - Waste-derived products (compost, biogas, nutrients)
  - Service offerings
  - Energy sales
  - Carbon credits
  - Consultancy/education

**6. Key Resources**
- What assets are required?
- Physical: Land, equipment, waste processing facilities
- Intellectual: Knowledge, certifications
- Human: Skills, expertise

**7. Key Activities**
- What do you need to do?
- Production + circular activities (recovery, processing)

**8. Key Partnerships**
- Who are your partners?
- Suppliers, processors, symbiosis partners, certifiers

**9. Cost Structure**
- What are the costs?
- Fixed, variable, economies of scale
- Circular investments vs. savings

---

### Part 3: Financial Projections

**Circular Interventions Proposed**:

1. **Anaerobic Digestion + CHP**
   - Inputs: All manure + crop residues + food waste
   - Biogas: 85,000 m³/year
   - CHP output: 150 MWh elec, 200 MWh heat
   - Digestate: 550 tonnes/year (fertilizer)
   - Investment: $350,000
   - Annual benefit: $45,000 (energy) + $15,000 (fertilizer)

2. **Compost Premium Product**
   - High-quality certified organic compost
   - Production: 100 tonnes/year
   - Market price: $80/tonne
   - Investment: $25,000
   - Annual revenue: $8,000

3. **Water Treatment & Reuse**
   - Treat and recycle wastewater
   - Water savings: 12,000 m³/year
   - Investment: $60,000
   - Annual savings: $9,000

4. **Carbon Credit Program**
   - Soil carbon sequestration
   - Biogas emission reduction
   - Credits: 400 tonnes CO₂e/year
   - Revenue: $10,000/year (@$25/tonne)

5. **Agri-Tourism & Education**
   - Farm tours, workshops
   - "Circular Farm" differentiation
   - Revenue: $15,000/year
   - Investment: $10,000

**Calculate**:
- Total investment required: $___________
- Total new annual revenue: $___________
- Total annual cost savings: $___________
- Simple payback period: ___________ years
- 10-year NPV (6% discount rate): $___________
- IRR: ___________%

---

### Part 4: Implementation Roadmap

Create a 3-year roadmap:

**Year 1**: Quick wins
- Compost operation (low investment, immediate revenue)
- Begin planning for AD system
- Water audit and treatment design
- Carbon credit application

**Year 2**: Major infrastructure
- AD + CHP installation
- Water treatment system
- Training and capacity building
- Begin agri-tourism program

**Year 3**: Optimization
- Full operations
- Performance monitoring
- Continuous improvement
- Scale successful elements

---

### Deliverable
- Completed Business Model Canvas (poster format)
- Financial analysis spreadsheet
- 3-year implementation timeline
- 5-minute pitch presentation

## Activity 3: Circular Economy System Design Challenge

### Objective
Design a comprehensive circular economy system for a specific agricultural context, integrating lessons from entire course.

**Duration**: 6-8 hours (capstone project)
**Format**: Team project (3-5 members)

---

### Challenge

Design a circular economy system for:

**Green Future Farms**
- **Type**: Large-scale greenhouse complex
- **Size**: 50,000 m² growing area (5 hectares under glass)
- **Production**:
  - Tomatoes: 2,500 tonnes/year
  - Cucumbers: 1,500 tonnes/year
  - Lettuce: 800 tonnes/year
- **Technology**: Recirculating hydroponic + aquaponics pilot
- **Energy Use**: 6,000 MWh/year (heating, lighting, climate control)
- **Water Use**: 400,000 m³/year
- **Labor**: 60 employees

**Waste Streams**:
- Plant residues: 500 tonnes/year fresh weight
- Drainage water: 200,000 m³/year (nutrients, salts)
- Fish waste (aquaponics): 20 tonnes/year
- Packaging waste: 80 tonnes/year
- CO₂ demand: 600 tonnes/year (currently purchased)

**Goals**:
- Achieve 80% water circularity
- Achieve 70% energy self-sufficiency
- Zero waste to landfill
- 50% reduction in external fertilizer inputs
- Carbon neutral operations
- 20% cost reduction over 5 years

---

### Part 1: System Analysis (2 hours)

**1.1 Material Flow Analysis**
- Conduct MFA for water, nutrients (N, P), and energy
- Identify major losses and inefficiencies
- Quantify recovery potential

**1.2 Stakeholder Analysis**
- Map all stakeholders (internal and external)
- Assess interests and influence
- Identify collaboration opportunities

**1.3 Regulatory Review**
- Identify applicable regulations (water reuse, waste, energy)
- Assess compliance requirements
- Identify incentive programs

---

### Part 2: Technology Selection (2 hours)

Design integrated system including:

**2.1 Water Management**
- Multi-barrier treatment train for drainage water
- Technology specifications
- Water balance showing 80% recovery

**2.2 Nutrient Recovery**
- Phosphorus recovery system
- Nitrogen recovery system
- Integration with existing fertigation

**2.3 Energy Systems**
- Anaerobic digestion of plant and fish waste
- CHP system sizing
- Solar PV (if applicable)
- Heat recovery and storage
- Energy balance achieving 70% self-sufficiency

**2.4 Waste Valorization**
- Packaging circularity (reusable system design)
- Byproduct development (e.g., extracting value compounds)
- Industrial symbiosis opportunities

**2.5 CO₂ Management**
- Biogas cleaning and CO₂ extraction
- Integration with greenhouse CO₂ enrichment
- Achieve 60% of CO₂ needs from internal sources

---

### Part 3: Business Case (2 hours)

**3.1 Financial Analysis**
- Itemized capital costs (all systems)
- Annual operating costs
- Revenue streams (savings + new revenues)
- NPV, IRR, payback calculations
- Sensitivity analysis

**3.2 Risk Assessment**
- Technical risks
- Market risks
- Financial risks
- Mitigation strategies

**3.3 Funding Strategy**
- Equity vs. debt
- Grants and incentives
- Phasing to manage cash flow

---

### Part 4: Implementation Plan (1.5 hours)

**4.1 Phased Implementation**
- Phase 1 (Year 1): Quick wins, planning
- Phase 2 (Years 2-3): Major infrastructure
- Phase 3 (Years 4-5): Optimization, scaling

**4.2 Change Management**
- Communication plan
- Training program
- Performance monitoring
- Continuous improvement processes

**4.3 Metrics & Reporting**
- KPIs for each goal
- Monitoring frequency
- Reporting framework (GRI/SASB)

---

### Part 5: Presentation (0.5 hour preparation)

**Deliverables**:
1. System diagram showing all circular flows
2. Technology specification sheets
3. Financial analysis spreadsheet
4. Implementation timeline (Gantt chart)
5. 15-minute presentation with Q&A

**Presentation Structure**:
- Problem statement (current linear system)
- Circular vision and goals
- Integrated system design
- Business case
- Implementation roadmap
- Expected impact (environmental, economic, social)
