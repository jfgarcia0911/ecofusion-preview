## Phase 2: System Design

### 2.1 Design Principles Checklist

Apply these principles to every design decision:

- [ ] **Design out waste** - Can waste be eliminated at source?
- [ ] **Cascade materials** - Are materials used for highest value first?
- [ ] **Close loops** - Can materials circle back to production?
- [ ] **Regenerate nature** - Does system enhance ecosystem health?
- [ ] **Optimize systems** - Are synergies between processes leveraged?
- [ ] **Durability** - Are systems designed for longevity?
- [ ] **Flexibility** - Can system adapt to changing conditions?
- [ ] **Modularity** - Can components be upgraded independently?

## Phase 3: Economic Analysis

### 3.1 Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

**Formula:**
```
TCO = CAPEX + Σ(OPEX_annual / (1 + r)^t) - Σ(Revenue_annual / (1 + r)^t)

Where:
CAPEX = Initial capital expenditure
OPEX = Annual operating costs
Revenue = Annual revenues/savings from circular strategies
r = Discount rate (typically 6-10%)
t = Year (1 to project lifetime)
```

## Appendix: Useful Formulas & Conversions

### Energy Conversions
- 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
- 1 m³ methane = 35.8 MJ = 9.94 kWh
- 1 kg diesel = 45.6 MJ
- 1 therm natural gas = 105.5 MJ

### Nutrient Conversions
- N to protein: Multiply by 6.25
- P₂O₅ to P: Multiply by 0.436
- K₂O to K: Multiply by 0.830

### Water Quality
- 1 ppm = 1 mg/L (for dilute solutions)
- EC (dS/m) ≈ TDS (mg/L) / 640 (rough estimate)

### Financial
- Capital Recovery Factor (CRF) = r(1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]
- Present Value = Future Value / (1 + r)^t
