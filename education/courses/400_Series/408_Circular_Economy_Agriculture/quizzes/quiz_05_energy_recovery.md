## Questions

### Question 1
What are the four sequential stages of anaerobic digestion?

A) Aerobic digestion, acidification, gas production, stabilization
B) Hydrolysis, acidogenesis, acetogenesis, methanogenesis
C) Fermentation, oxidation, reduction, gasification
D) Breakdown, synthesis, conversion, emission

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the optimal carbon-to-nitrogen (C:N) ratio for anaerobic digestion?

A) 5-10
B) 20-30
C) 50-60
D) 80-100

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical methane content in biogas produced from anaerobic digestion?

A) 25-35%
B) 40-50%
C) 55-65%
D) 75-85%

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
What temperature range defines mesophilic anaerobic digestion?

A) 10-20°C
B) 20-30°C
C) 30-40°C
D) 40-50°C

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What is the typical hydraulic retention time (HRT) for a mesophilic CSTR digester?

A) 3-7 days
B) 15-30 days
C) 45-60 days
D) 90-120 days

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What is the main contaminant requiring biogas cleaning before energy use?

A) Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
B) Nitrogen (N₂)
C) Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S)
D) Water vapor (H₂O)

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What is the typical electrical efficiency of combined heat and power (CHP) systems?

A) 15-25%
B) 35-45%
C) 55-65%
D) 75-85%

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What methane content is required for biomethane grid injection?

A) >60%
B) >75%
C) >85%
D) >95%

**Answer: D**

---

### Question 9
What is the energy content of methane?

A) 10.2 MJ/m³
B) 23.5 MJ/m³
C) 35.8 MJ/m³
D) 50.1 MJ/m³

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 10
At what minimum scale does biogas CHP become economically viable without subsidies?

A) >10 kW
B) >50 kW
C) >100 kW
D) >500 kW

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Hydrolysis, acidogenesis, acetogenesis, methanogenesis
2. B - C:N ratio of 20-30 optimal
3. C - Biogas typically contains 55-65% methane
4. C - Mesophilic range 30-40°C
5. B - 15-30 days typical HRT
6. C - H₂S is main contaminant requiring removal
7. B - CHP electrical efficiency 35-45%
8. D - >95% methane required for grid injection
9. C - Methane energy content 35.8 MJ/m³
10. B - >50 kW minimum for unsubsidized viability
