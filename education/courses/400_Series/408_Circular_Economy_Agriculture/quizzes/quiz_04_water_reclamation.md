## Questions

### Question 1
What turbidity level is required for effective UV disinfection?

A) <1 NTU
B) <5 NTU
C) <10 NTU
D) <20 NTU

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which membrane filtration technology has the smallest pore size?

A) Microfiltration (MF)
B) Ultrafiltration (UF)
C) Nanofiltration (NF)
D) Reverse osmosis (RO)

**Answer: D**

---

### Question 3
What UV dose is typically required to achieve 4-log pathogen inactivation?

A) 10 mJ/cm²
B) 40 mJ/cm²
C) 100 mJ/cm²
D) 200 mJ/cm²

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is the multi-barrier treatment approach in water reclamation?

A) Using a single highly effective treatment
B) Multiple redundant treatment steps to ensure safety
C) Treating water only once before reuse
D) Relying solely on chemical disinfection

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What percentage water recovery is typical for greenhouse drainage reclamation systems?

A) 50-70%
B) 70-85%
C) 90-95%
D) >98%

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
Which pathogen type is most resistant to UV disinfection?

A) Viruses
B) Bacteria
C) Bacterial spores
D) Protozoa

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What is a Critical Control Point (CCP) in water reuse systems?

A) Any monitoring location
B) A point where hazards must be controlled to ensure safety
C) The final discharge point
D) The water intake location

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What electrical conductivity (EC) level is acceptable for sensitive crops?

A) <1.0 dS/m
B) <2.0 dS/m
C) <5.0 dS/m
D) <10.0 dS/m

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
Which technology most effectively removes dissolved salts from water?

A) Sand filtration
B) Activated carbon
C) Reverse osmosis
D) UV treatment

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 10
What is the recommended frequency for E. coli testing in reclaimed water systems?

A) Monthly
B) Weekly
C) Daily
D) After every batch

**Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Turbidity <5 NTU required for UV effectiveness
2. D - RO has smallest pore size (~0.0001 micron)
3. B - 40 mJ/cm² achieves 4-log reduction
4. B - Multiple barriers provide redundancy
5. C - 90-95% recovery typical for greenhouse systems
6. C - Bacterial spores most UV-resistant
7. B - CCP is critical control point for safety
8. B - EC <2.0 dS/m suitable for sensitive crops
9. C - RO removes dissolved salts effectively
10. C - Daily E. coli testing recommended
