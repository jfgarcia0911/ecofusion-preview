## Questions

### Question 1
What is the highest tier in the valorization hierarchy for agricultural byproducts?

A) Animal feed
B) Compost
C) Pharmaceuticals and nutraceuticals
D) Biofuel production

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
What is the cascading use principle in byproduct valorization?

A) Discarding waste after single use
B) Extracting maximum value through sequential processing
C) Using all waste for energy recovery
D) Selling byproducts without processing

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
Which extraction method is considered the "greenest" for high-value compounds?

A) Hexane extraction
B) Acid hydrolysis
C) Supercritical CO₂ extraction
D) Methanol extraction

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
What is the typical protein content in plant waste on a dry basis?

A) 2-5%
B) 10-15%
C) 20-30%
D) 40-50%

**Answer: A**

---

### Question 5
What market value threshold typically defines "high-value" byproducts?

A) >$100/tonne
B) >$500/tonne
C) >$1,000/tonne
D) >$5,000/tonne

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
What is the main barrier to byproduct valorization at small scale?

A) Lack of technology
B) Environmental regulations
C) Economics and market access
D) Transportation challenges

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What quality standard applies to food-grade byproducts?

A) ISO 9001
B) FDA/EFSA food safety regulations
C) ASTM standards only
D) No specific standards required

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What determines the optimal valorization pathway for a byproduct?

A) Volume of waste produced
B) Color and appearance
C) Market value versus processing cost
D) Source location only

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
What is the biorefinery concept?

A) A facility that only produces biofuels
B) Integrated facility converting biomass to multiple products
C) A traditional oil refinery
D) A composting operation

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What documentation ensures product traceability in valorization chains?

A) Sales receipts only
B) Chain of custody records
C) Marketing materials
D) Employee schedules

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Pharmaceuticals/nutraceuticals highest value tier
2. B - Cascading use maximizes value through sequential steps
3. C - Supercritical CO₂ is greenest extraction method
4. A - Plant waste typically 2-5% protein (dry basis)
5. C - >$1,000/tonne defines high-value byproducts
6. C - Economics and market access main small-scale barrier
7. B - FDA/EFSA regulations for food-grade products
8. C - Value vs. cost determines optimal pathway
9. B - Biorefinery produces multiple products from biomass
10. B - Chain of custody ensures traceability
