## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three Rs of sustainable packaging?

A) Recycle, Renew, Restore
B) Reduce, Reuse, Recycle
C) Repair, Remanufacture, Recover
D) Redistribute, Refurbish, Remake

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the most circular packaging option?

A) Single-use recyclable packaging
B) Biodegradable packaging
C) Reusable containers with return system
D) Paper-based packaging

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What percentage of ocean plastic pollution comes from packaging?

A) ~10%
B) ~25%
C) ~50%
D) ~75%

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
What makes packaging certified as "home compostable"?

A) It dissolves in water
B) Breaks down in home compost conditions meeting EN 13432 standard
C) Made from recycled materials
D) Contains no plastic

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What is reverse logistics?

A) Shipping products faster
B) Return flow of packaging from consumer to producer
C) International shipping
D) Warehouse storage systems

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) metric compares packaging options?

A) Color and appearance
B) Manufacturing speed
C) Environmental footprint per functional unit
D) Shelf space requirements

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What primarily drives consumer acceptance of reusable packaging?

A) Environmental messaging only
B) Convenience and cost savings
C) Government mandates
D) Packaging color

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What is the optimal deposit amount for returnable packaging?

A) As low as possible
B) As high as possible
C) Sufficient to motivate return without excessive barrier
D) No deposit needed

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
Which packaging material generally has the lowest carbon footprint?

A) Single-use plastic
B) Single-use paper
C) Recyclable aluminum
D) Depends on lifecycle - often reusable > recyclable > compostable > single-use

**Answer: D**

---

### Question 10
What regulation drives packaging circularity in the European Union?

A) Clean Water Act
B) Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive
C) Paris Agreement
D) ISO 14001

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Reduce, Reuse, Recycle are the three Rs
2. C - Reusable containers with returns most circular
3. C - ~50% of ocean plastic from packaging
4. B - EN 13432 defines home compostable standards
5. B - Reverse logistics returns packaging to producer
6. C - LCA measures environmental footprint per function
7. B - Convenience and savings drive acceptance
8. C - Optimal deposit balances motivation and accessibility
9. D - Lifecycle analysis shows reusable often lowest impact
10. B - EU Packaging Directive drives circularity
