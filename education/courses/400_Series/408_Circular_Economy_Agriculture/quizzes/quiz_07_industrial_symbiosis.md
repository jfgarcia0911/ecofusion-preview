## Questions

### Question 1
What is industrial symbiosis?

A) Competition between industrial facilities
B) Collaboration where one facility's waste becomes another's input
C) Government regulation of industries
D) Merger of two companies

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
What is the most famous example of an eco-industrial park?

A) Silicon Valley, USA
B) Kalundborg, Denmark
C) Ruhr Valley, Germany
D) Pearl River Delta, China

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What tool is used to map geographic resource exchange opportunities?

A) Spreadsheet software
B) GIS (Geographic Information Systems)
C) Word processors
D) Email systems

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
What is the key success factor for industrial symbiosis networks?

A) Large company size
B) Government ownership
C) Geographic proximity and compatible flows
D) International shipping capabilities

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What governance structure best supports symbiosis networks?

A) No formal agreements needed
B) Formal agreements and coordinating body
C) Verbal handshake agreements
D) Government mandate only

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What is the typical geographic radius for effective symbiosis networks?

A) <1 km
B) 10-50 km
C) 100-200 km
D) >500 km

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What is the primary risk that symbiosis partners must manage?

A) Weather variations
B) Supply reliability and quality consistency
C) Employee turnover
D) Marketing challenges

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 8
What typically initiates most industrial symbiosis networks?

A) Random chance
B) Economic opportunity or regulatory pressure
C) Social media campaigns
D) Academic research projects

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
What role do facilitators play in symbiosis networks?

A) Provide funding only
B) Match potential partners and broker agreements
C) Enforce regulations
D) Conduct scientific research

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What metrics measure network performance in industrial symbiosis?

A) Number of meetings held
B) Waste diverted and economic value created
C) Social media followers
D) Number of employees

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Industrial symbiosis is collaborative waste-to-input exchange
2. B - Kalundborg, Denmark is most famous eco-industrial park
3. B - GIS maps geographic exchange opportunities
4. C - Proximity and compatible flows are key success factors
5. B - Formal agreements and coordination support networks
6. B - 10-50 km typical effective radius
7. B - Supply reliability and quality are primary risks
8. B - Economic opportunity or regulation initiates networks
9. B - Facilitators match partners and broker deals
10. B - Waste diverted and value created measure performance
