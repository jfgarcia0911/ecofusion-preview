## Questions

### Question 1
What is the fundamental principle underlying all material flow analysis?

A) Energy conservation
B) Conservation of mass
C) Economic efficiency
D) Thermodynamic equilibrium

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
In a mass balance equation, if inputs = 1,000 kg and outputs = 900 kg with no transformation, what is the change in stock (ΔS)?

A) -100 kg
B) 0 kg
C) +100 kg
D) +1,900 kg

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What is the primary difference between Material Flow Analysis (MFA) and Substance Flow Analysis (SFA)?

A) MFA tracks total mass; SFA tracks specific substances
B) SFA is more expensive to conduct
C) MFA is only used in agriculture
D) There is no difference

**Answer: A**
