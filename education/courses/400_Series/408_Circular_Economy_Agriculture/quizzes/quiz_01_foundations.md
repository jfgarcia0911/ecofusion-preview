## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three core principles of circular economy according to the Ellen MacArthur Foundation?

A) Reduce, reuse, recycle
B) Design out waste and pollution; keep products and materials in use; regenerate natural systems
C) Sustainability, profitability, scalability
D) Conservation, efficiency, innovation

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
In a circular economy, materials are categorized into two main cycles. What are they?

A) Input and output cycles
B) Natural and synthetic cycles
C) Biological and technical cycles
D) Renewable and non-renewable cycles

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What is the primary difference between a linear and circular agricultural model?

A) Linear models use more technology
B) Linear models extract resources, use them once, and dispose of waste; circular models regenerate and cycle resources
C) Circular models are always more expensive
D) Linear models are more environmentally friendly

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
According to the waste hierarchy, which is the MOST preferred option?

A) Recycling
B) Energy recovery
C) Prevention
D) Disposal

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
In the cascading use principle, what should be the first priority for biomass utilization?

A) Energy recovery
B) High-value applications (human food/feed)
C) Composting
D) Animal feed

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
What percentage of mined phosphorus actually reaches human food in traditional linear systems?

A) 20%
B) 50%
C) 75%
D) 90%

**Answer: A**

---

### Question 7
Which of the following is NOT a key driver for circular agriculture adoption?

A) Resource scarcity and price volatility
B) Environmental regulations
C) Decreased consumer demand for sustainability
D) Climate change mitigation requirements

**Answer: C**

---

### Question 8
What is the typical nitrogen use efficiency (NUE) range for conventional field crops?

A) 10-20%
B) 40-60%
C) 70-90%
D) 90-100%

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
In systems thinking applied to agriculture, what is a "feedback loop"?

A) Customer satisfaction surveys
B) A mechanism where outputs influence inputs, creating self-regulating behavior
C) Annual financial reports
D) Irrigation system controls

**Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
According to the case study in the lesson, what was the approximate percentage reduction in operating costs achieved by transitioning from linear to circular lettuce production?

A) 15%
B) 30%
C) 53%
D) 85%

**Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Core principles from Ellen MacArthur Foundation
2. C - Biological and technical cycles
3. B - Resource extraction vs. regeneration
4. C - Prevention is most preferred in waste hierarchy
5. B - High-value applications prioritized in cascading use
6. A - Only 20% of mined P reaches human food
7. C - Consumer demand for sustainability is INCREASING, not decreasing
8. B - 40-60% NUE for conventional field crops
9. B - Feedback loops are self-regulating mechanisms
10. C - 53% operating cost reduction in case study
