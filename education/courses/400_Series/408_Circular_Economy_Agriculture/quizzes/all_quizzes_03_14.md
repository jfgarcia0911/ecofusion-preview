# Quizzes 3-14: Complete Assessment Package

## Quiz 3: Nutrient Cycling & Recovery Technologies

1. What is struvite? **Answer: Magnesium ammonium phosphate hexahydrate (MgNH₄PO₄·6H₂O)**
2. At what pH range is struvite precipitation optimal? **Answer: 8.5-9.5**
3. What is typical phosphorus recovery efficiency from struvite precipitation? **Answer: 70-90%**
4. Which method is most effective for nitrogen removal from digestate liquid? **Answer: Ammonia stripping**
5. What is biochar's primary benefit for nutrient management? **Answer: Retention and slow release**
6. What percentage of nitrogen in digestate is typically in NH₄-N form? **Answer: 60-80%**
7. What technology achieves highest purity for recovered nutrients? **Answer: Ion exchange**
8. What are maximum cadmium limits in organic fertilizers (EU)? **Answer: 1.5 mg/kg**
9. What determines nutrient partition between solid and liquid digestate fractions? **Answer: Chemical properties and separation technology**
10. What certification is required for commercial fertilizer products? **Answer: Guaranteed analysis and contaminant testing**

## Quiz 4: Advanced Water Reclamation & Reuse

1. What turbidity level is required for UV disinfection effectiveness? **Answer: <5 NTU**
2. Which membrane has smallest pore size? **Answer: Reverse osmosis (RO)**
3. What UV dose achieves 4-log pathogen inactivation? **Answer: 40 mJ/cm²**
4. What is the multi-barrier treatment approach? **Answer: Multiple redundant treatment steps**
5. What percentage water recovery is typical for greenhouse drainage treatment? **Answer: 90-95%**
6. Which pathogen is most UV-resistant? **Answer: Bacterial spores**
7. What is critical control point (CCP) in water reuse? **Answer: Point where hazards must be controlled**
8. What EC level is acceptable for sensitive crops? **Answer: <2.0 dS/m**
9. What removes dissolved salts most effectively? **Answer: Reverse osmosis**
10. What frequency should E. coli testing occur? **Answer: Daily**

2. What is optimal C/N ratio for AD? **Answer: 20-30**

4. What temperature range is mesophilic AD? **Answer: 30-40°C**

7. What is typical CHP electrical efficiency? **Answer: 35-45%**
8. What methane content is required for biomethane grid injection? **Answer: >95%**
9. What is energy content of methane? **Answer: 35.8 MJ/m³**
10. At what scale does biogas CHP become economically viable without subsidies? **Answer: >50 kW**

3. What extraction method is greenest? **Answer: Supercritical CO₂**

5. What market value defines "high-value" byproducts? **Answer: >$1,000/tonne**

8. What determines optimal valorization pathway? **Answer: Market value vs. processing cost**

## Quiz 7: Industrial Symbiosis & Network Design

1. What is industrial symbiosis? **Answer: Collaboration where one facility's waste becomes another's input**
2. What is most famous example of eco-industrial park? **Answer: Kalundborg, Denmark**
3. What tool maps geographic resource exchange opportunities? **Answer: GIS (Geographic Information Systems)**
4. What is key success factor for industrial symbiosis? **Answer: Geographic proximity and compatible flows**
5. What governance structure supports symbiosis networks? **Answer: Formal agreements and coordinating body**
6. What is typical symbiosis network radius? **Answer: 10-50 km**
7. What risk must partners manage? **Answer: Supply reliability and quality consistency**
8. What initiates most symbiosis networks? **Answer: Economic opportunity or regulatory pressure**
9. What role do facilitators play? **Answer: Match potential partners and broker agreements**
10. What metric measures network performance? **Answer: Waste diverted and economic value created**

## Quiz 8: Circular Packaging & Distribution

1. What are three Rs of packaging? **Answer: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle**
2. What is most circular packaging option? **Answer: Reusable containers with return system**
3. What percentage of ocean plastic comes from packaging? **Answer: ~50%**
4. What makes packaging "home compostable"? **Answer: Breaks down in home compost conditions (EN 13432)**
5. What is reverse logistics? **Answer: Return flow of packaging from consumer to producer**
6. What LCA metric compares packaging options? **Answer: Environmental footprint per functional unit**
7. What drives consumer acceptance of reusable packaging? **Answer: Convenience and cost savings**
8. What is optimal deposit amount for returns? **Answer: Sufficient to motivate return without excessive barrier**
9. What packaging material has lowest carbon footprint? **Answer: Depends on lifecycle—often reusable > recyclable > compostable > single-use**
10. What regulation drives packaging circularity in EU? **Answer: Packaging and Packaging Waste Directive**

## Quiz 10: Business Models for Circular Economy

1. What are main circular business model archetypes? **Answer: Product-as-service, sharing, circular supplies, resource recovery, product life extension**
2. What is product-as-service model? **Answer: Customer pays for function, not ownership**
3. What financial metric evaluates circular ventures? **Answer: NPV, IRR, payback period with full cost accounting**
4. What is biggest risk in circular business models? **Answer: Market acceptance and scale-up challenges**
5. What revenue stream do many circular businesses overlook? **Answer: Waste disposal savings**
6. How do circular models change customer relationships? **Answer: From transactional to ongoing service relationships**
7. What enables scaling of circular businesses? **Answer: Standardized processes, partnerships, technology**
8. What pricing strategy suits recovered materials? **Answer: Cost-plus or market-based depending on quality**
9. What differentiates circular value proposition? **Answer: Environmental benefit plus economic value**
10. What is critical success factor for circular businesses? **Answer: Closing material loops economically**

## Quiz 12: Measuring & Reporting Circularity

1. What is Material Circularity Indicator (MCI)? **Answer: Metric measuring how restorative material flows are (0-1 scale)**
2. What does LCA assess? **Answer: Environmental impacts across product lifecycle**
3. What are LCA stages? **Answer: Goal & scope, inventory, impact assessment, interpretation**
4. What is functional unit in LCA? **Answer: Reference measure for comparing alternatives**
5. What greenhouse gases are included in carbon footprint? **Answer: All GHGs converted to CO₂ equivalents**
6. What is water footprint? **Answer: Total freshwater consumption and pollution**
7. What framework guides sustainability reporting? **Answer: GRI (Global Reporting Initiative)**
8. What is EPD? **Answer: Environmental Product Declaration—standardized environmental data**
9. How often should circularity metrics be reported? **Answer: Annually at minimum, quarterly for management**
10. What validates circularity claims? **Answer: Third-party verification and audit**

## Quiz 14: Future Trends & Innovation Frontiers

1. What is precision fermentation? **Answer: Using microbes to produce specific compounds (proteins, fats, etc.)**
2. What is cellular agriculture? **Answer: Growing agricultural products from cell cultures**
3. How does AI optimize circular systems? **Answer: Predictive analytics, resource allocation, demand forecasting**
4. What is digital twin in agriculture? **Answer: Virtual replica of physical system for simulation and optimization**
5. What is role of synthetic biology? **Answer: Engineering organisms for novel functions (e.g., nutrient production)**
6. How does circular economy support climate goals? **Answer: Reduces emissions through efficiency, substitution, and carbon storage**
7. What is regenerative agriculture? **Answer: Farming that restores ecosystem health and builds soil carbon**
8. What market trend favors circular products? **Answer: Growing consumer demand for sustainability and transparency**
9. What policy trend accelerates circularity? **Answer: Mandatory targets, EPR expansion, green procurement**
10. What is biggest research gap? **Answer: Scaling technologies and understanding long-term system impacts**
