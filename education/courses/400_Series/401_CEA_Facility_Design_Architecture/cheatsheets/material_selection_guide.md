# Material Selection Guide for CEA Facilities

## Glazing Materials Comparison

| Material | Light Trans. | U-Value | Lifespan | Cost | Best Use |
|----------|-------------|---------|----------|------|----------|
| **Single Glass** | 90-92% | 1.13 | 30+ yr | $$$ | High-light crops, mild climates |
| **Double Glass** | 78-82% | 0.50 | 30+ yr | $$$$ | Energy efficiency, year-round |
| **16mm Polycarb** | 75-78% | 0.44 | 15-20 yr | $$$ | Balance of performance/cost |
| **Double Poly** | 78-84% | 0.70 | 4-5 yr | $ | Budget greenhouses, replaceable |

## Structural Materials

### Steel
- **Use**: Primary structure, vertical farm racks
- **Finish**: Hot-dip galvanized (high humidity areas)
- **Advantages**: High strength, cost-effective, readily available
- **Considerations**: Corrosion protection required, thermal bridging

### Aluminum
- **Use**: Greenhouse framing, gutters, glazing bars
- **Finish**: Mill finish or powder coat
- **Advantages**: Corrosion-resistant, lightweight
- **Considerations**: Higher cost, lower strength than steel

### Stainless Steel
- **Use**: Post-harvest areas, food contact surfaces
- **Grade**: 304 (standard) or 316 (corrosive environments)
- **Advantages**: Excellent corrosion resistance, food-safe
- **Considerations**: Expensive, requires specialized fabrication

## Insulation Materials

### Rigid Foam Board
- **Types**: XPS, polyiso, EPS
- **R-value**: 4-6.5 per inch
- **Use**: Walls, roofs, perimeter
- **Moisture**: Excellent resistance

### Spray Foam
- **Types**: Closed-cell (preferred), open-cell
- **R-value**: 6-7 per inch (closed-cell)
- **Use**: Irregular spaces, air sealing
- **Moisture**: Vapor barrier (closed-cell)

### Batt Insulation
- **Types**: Fiberglass, mineral wool
- **R-value**: 3-4 per inch
- **Use**: Stud cavities (if moisture-controlled)
- **Moisture**: Requires vapor retarder in high-humidity areas

## Floor Finishes

### Growing Areas
- **Recommended**: Epoxy-coated concrete
- **Thickness**: 1/8" - 1/4" coating
- **Properties**: Seamless, chemical-resistant, easy to clean
- **Cost**: $3-8/sq ft installed

### Post-Harvest/Pack Areas
- **Recommended**: Food-grade epoxy or tile
- **Requirements**: NSF/USDA approved, slip-resistant
- **Cost**: $4-12/sq ft

## Wall Finishes

### High-Moisture Areas
1. **FRP Panels** (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic)
   - 4×8 or 4×10 sheets
   - White color for light reflection
   - $1.50-3.00/sq ft

2. **Epoxy Paint on CMU**
   - Two-part epoxy system
   - Washable, durable
   - $1.00-2.00/sq ft

### Office/Administrative
- **Painted Gypsum Board** (moisture-resistant)
- **Vinyl Wall Covering** (Type II commercial grade)

## Piping Materials

| System | Material | Size Range | Advantages |
|--------|----------|------------|------------|
| **Potable Water** | PEX or CPVC | 1/2" - 2" | Corrosion-resistant, flexible |
| **Irrigation Main** | PVC Schedule 40 | 1" - 6" | Low cost, durable, easy install |
| **Nutrient Solution** | PVC or HDPE | 3/4" - 4" | Chemical-resistant |
| **Drainage** | PVC DWV | 2" - 6" | Standard, code-approved |
| **Underground** | HDPE | 1" - 8" | Freeze-resistant, flexible |

## Corrosion Protection

### High-Humidity Environment Finishes
- **Hot-Dip Galvanizing**: Best for steel structures (3-5 oz/sq ft)
- **Powder Coating**: Excellent for equipment, decorative
- **Stainless Steel**: Ultimate protection, food areas
- **Marine-Grade Hardware**: All fasteners in greenhouses

### Avoid in CEA Environments
- ❌ Uncoated mild steel
- ❌ Aluminum in direct contact with fertilizers
- ❌ Standard paint without primer (will fail)
- ❌ Wood (unless pressure-treated, limited use)

## Material Selection Decision Matrix

### Priority Factors
1. **Moisture Resistance** (Critical in CEA)
2. **Chemical Compatibility** (Fertilizers, cleaners)
3. **Life Cycle Cost** (Not just initial cost)
4. **Maintenance Requirements**
5. **Food Safety** (If applicable)
6. **Aesthetics** (Customer-facing areas)

### Budget vs. Performance
- **Budget Approach**: Double poly greenhouse, basic finishes
- **Mid-Range**: Polycarbonate or single glass, epoxy floors
- **Premium**: Double glass, stainless steel, automated systems

---

**Quick Selection Rules:**
1. When in doubt in high-humidity areas → Choose corrosion-resistant
2. Food contact surfaces → Stainless steel or food-grade materials
3. Long-term value → Calculate life cycle cost, not just first cost
4. Critical systems → Redundancy and premium materials justified
