# Technical Glossary: CEA Facility Design & Architecture

## A

**ACH (Air Changes per Hour)** - Number of times the complete air volume in a space is replaced in one hour; common ventilation metric.

**ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)** - Federal law requiring accessible design for people with disabilities.

**AFUE (Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency)** - Measure of furnace/boiler efficiency expressed as a percentage.

**ASCE (American Society of Civil Engineers)** - Professional organization that publishes structural design standards including ASCE 7 (loads).

**ASHRAE** - American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers; publishes HVAC design standards.

**Ampacity** - Current-carrying capacity of an electrical conductor in amperes.

## B

**Backflow Preventer** - Device preventing reverse flow of water in plumbing systems; critical for fertigation systems.

**BAS (Building Automation System)** - Computer-based control system for building mechanical and electrical equipment.

**Bearing Capacity** - Maximum pressure soil can support without shear failure; measured in psf or kPa.

**BTU (British Thermal Unit)** - Amount of heat required to raise one pound of water by 1°F; 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/hr.

**Busway** - Prefabricated electrical distribution system using enclosed bus bars instead of cable.

## C

**CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture)** - Indoor/protected crop production with environmental control.

**CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute)** - Volumetric air flow rate; standard unit for HVAC calculations.

**CHP (Combined Heat and Power)** - System generating both electricity and useful thermal energy.

**COP (Coefficient of Performance)** - Ratio of useful heating or cooling output to energy input; higher is better.

**CSI MasterFormat** - Standard for organizing construction specifications by division (e.g., Division 23 = HVAC).

## D

**Demand Factor** - Ratio of actual maximum demand to total connected load; accounts for non-simultaneous use.

**Dew Point** - Temperature at which air becomes saturated and condensation begins.

**DIF (Difference)** - Temperature difference between day and night; used to control plant height.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)** - Total photosynthetically active light received per day; measured in mol/m²/day.

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)** - Hydroponic system with plant roots submerged in oxygenated nutrient solution.

## E

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measure of dissolved salts in water/nutrient solution; measured in μS/cm or mS/cm.

**EER (Energy Efficiency Ratio)** - Cooling capacity (BTU/hr) divided by power input (watts); steady-state metric.

**Enthalpy** - Total heat content of air (sensible + latent); measured in BTU/lb or kJ/kg.

**ERV (Energy Recovery Ventilator)** - Device transferring heat and moisture between exhaust and supply air streams.

**EUI (Energy Use Intensity)** - Annual energy consumption divided by floor area; benchmark metric (kBTU/sf/yr).

## F

**Fertigation** - Application of fertilizers through irrigation system.

**FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic)** - Durable wall panels suitable for high-moisture environments.

**Frost Depth** - Maximum depth of seasonal frost penetration; foundations must extend below this depth.

## G

**GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter)** - Safety device detecting current leakage and interrupting circuit; required in wet locations.

**GPM (Gallons Per Minute)** - Volumetric water flow rate.

**Gutter** - Horizontal structural member collecting water and supporting glazing in gutter-connected greenhouses.

## H

**HAF (Horizontal Air Flow)** - Circulation fan system moving air horizontally to prevent stratification.

**HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)** - Durable plastic pipe material; common for irrigation and underground applications.

**HSPF (Heating Seasonal Performance Factor)** - Seasonal heating efficiency of heat pumps; higher is better.

**HVAC** - Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems.

**Humidity Ratio** - Mass of water vapor per mass of dry air; measured in grains/lb or kg/kg.

## I

**IBC (International Building Code)** - Model building code published by ICC; widely adopted in US.

**IMC (International Mechanical Code)** - Model code for HVAC and mechanical systems.

**Infiltration** - Uncontrolled air leakage into building through cracks and openings.

**IPC (International Plumbing Code)** - Model code for plumbing systems.

**IPLV (Integrated Part Load Value)** - Weighted efficiency metric accounting for part-load operation.

## K

**K-factor** - Thermal conductivity of materials; lower K = better insulation.

**kPa (Kilopascal)** - Metric pressure unit; 1 kPa = 0.145 psi.

**kWh (Kilowatt-hour)** - Energy unit; 1 kWh = 3,412 BTU.

## L

**Latent Heat** - Energy associated with moisture/phase change; no temperature change.

**LCC (Life Cycle Cost)** - Total cost of owning and operating equipment over its useful life.

**LED (Light Emitting Diode)** - Solid-state lighting technology dominant in CEA applications.

**LEED** - Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design; green building certification system.

**Live Load** - Temporary, movable loads (people, equipment); as opposed to permanent dead loads.

**LRFD (Load and Resistance Factor Design)** - Structural design method using factored loads and resistances.

## M

**MCC (Motor Control Center)** - Centralized assembly of motor starters and controls.

**MEP** - Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing systems.

**Modulus of Elasticity** - Material stiffness; resistance to deformation under load.

## N

**NEC (National Electrical Code)** - Standard for electrical installation; NFPA 70.

**NEMA** - National Electrical Manufacturers Association; publishes enclosure ratings (e.g., NEMA 4X).

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)** - Hydroponic system with thin film of nutrient solution flowing over roots.

**NGMA** - National Greenhouse Manufacturers Association; publishes greenhouse structural standards.

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)** - Light spectrum (400-700 nm) used by plants for photosynthesis.

**PF (Power Factor)** - Ratio of real power to apparent power; 1.0 is ideal, <0.90 problematic.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)** - Light intensity; measured in μmol/m²/s.

**Psychrometrics** - Study of air-moisture mixtures and their thermodynamic properties.

**PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)** - Common plastic pipe material for plumbing and irrigation.

## R

**R-value** - Thermal resistance; higher R = better insulation (units: ft²·°F·hr/BTU).

**RFI (Request for Information)** - Formal request for clarification during construction.

**RH (Relative Humidity)** - Percentage of water vapor in air relative to saturation at that temperature.

**RP (Reduced Pressure)** - High-level backflow preventer required for hazardous applications like fertigation.

## S

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)** - Industrial control system for monitoring and controlling processes.

**SEER (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio)** - Seasonal cooling efficiency; higher is better.

**Sensible Heat** - Heat causing temperature change (vs. latent heat).

**SHGC (Solar Heat Gain Coefficient)** - Fraction of solar radiation admitted through glazing; 0 to 1.

**SHR (Sensible Heat Ratio)** - Ratio of sensible to total cooling capacity.

## T

**TAB (Testing, Adjusting, and Balancing)** - Process of testing and adjusting HVAC systems to meet design specifications.

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Concentration of dissolved substances in water; measured in ppm or mg/L.

**Thermal Bridging** - Heat flow path through less-insulating material (e.g., metal stud through insulation).

**THD (Total Harmonic Distortion)** - Distortion of electrical waveform; should be <5% for power quality.

**Transpiration** - Water loss from plant leaves; creates cooling load and humidity.

**Tributary Area** - Area of structure supported by a specific structural member.

## U

**U-value** - Overall heat transfer coefficient; lower U = better insulation (BTU/hr·ft²·°F).

**UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)** - Battery backup system providing emergency power.

**UV (Ultraviolet)** - Light below 400 nm wavelength; used for water disinfection in CEA.

## V

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)** - Device controlling motor speed by varying electrical frequency; major energy saver.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)** - Difference between saturation and actual vapor pressure; drives transpiration (kPa).

## W

**Wet Bulb Temperature** - Temperature of air cooled to saturation by evaporation; related to humidity.

**W/sq ft** - Power density; watts per square foot of floor/growing area.
