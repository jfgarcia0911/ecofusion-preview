# Module 13: Renovation and Retrofit Projects

## Learning Objectives

- Assess existing facilities for CEA conversion potential
- Design retrofit strategies for various building types
- Plan phased construction maintaining operations
- Evaluate structural modifications and upgrades
- Conduct ROI analysis for retrofit projects

## 13.1 Existing Facility Assessment (building evaluation, structural capacity, systems analysis, code compliance, feasibility studies)

## 13.2 Retrofit Design Strategies (warehouse conversions, greenhouse upgrades, lighting retrofits, HVAC improvements, technology integration)

## 13.3 Phased Construction Planning (operational continuity, sequencing, temporary systems, production scheduling, risk management)

## 13.4 Structural Modifications (adding floor loads, roof reinforcement, foundation upgrades, seismic retrofits, expansion planning)

## 13.5 Economic Analysis for Retrofits (cost estimating, ROI calculations, financing options, incentive programs, risk assessment)

## Summary
Retrofit projects offer opportunities to enter CEA with lower capital investment but require careful evaluation and planning.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Thorough existing conditions assessment is critical**
2. **Code compliance for existing buildings may differ**
3. **Phasing strategy protects revenue during construction**
4. **Structural capacity often limits retrofit options**
5. **Economic analysis must include all hidden costs**
