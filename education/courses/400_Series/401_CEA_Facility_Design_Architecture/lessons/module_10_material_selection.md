# Module 10: Material Selection and Specification

## Learning Objectives

- Compare glazing materials and select based on performance criteria
- Specify insulation materials for various applications
- Select structural materials for longevity and performance
- Specify finishes suitable for controlled environments
- Write technical specifications and procurement documents

## 10.1 Glazing Materials and Properties (content summary - key comparisons, specifications, selection criteria for glass, polycarbonate, acrylic, polyethylene films)

## 10.2 Insulation Materials and Thermal Performance (rigid foam, spray foam, batt insulation, thermal breaks, performance calculations)

## 10.3 Structural Materials for CEA (steel, aluminum, composite materials, corrosion resistance, durability analysis)

## 10.4 Finishes and Coatings (floor coatings, wall finishes, moisture-resistant materials, antimicrobial options)

## 10.5 Specification Writing (CSI MasterFormat organization, technical specifications, performance vs. prescriptive specs, procurement strategies)

## Summary
Material selection impacts facility longevity, energy performance, and maintenance costs over decades of operation.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Glazing selection balances light transmission and insulation**
2. **Corrosion resistance is critical in high-humidity environments**
3. **Specifications must address CEA-specific conditions**
4. **Life cycle costs often favor premium materials**
5. **Procurement strategy affects project budget and timeline**
