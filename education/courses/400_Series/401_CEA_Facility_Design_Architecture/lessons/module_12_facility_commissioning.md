# Module 12: Facility Commissioning and Startup

## Learning Objectives

- Develop commissioning processes and protocols
- Conduct system testing and performance verification
- Perform functional performance testing
- Deliver training and documentation packages
- Manage warranty periods and ongoing support

## 12.2 System Testing and Verification (HVAC TAB, electrical testing, controls verification, water system testing, integrated systems)

## 12.3 Functional Performance Testing (sequences of operation, setpoint verification, alarm testing, emergency systems, automation)

## 12.4 Training and Documentation (operator training, O&M manuals, as-built drawings, warranty information, maintenance schedules)

## 12.5 Warranty Management (warranty periods, deficiency resolution, performance guarantees, ongoing support)

## Summary
Comprehensive commissioning ensures facility systems operate as designed and staff are prepared for operations.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Commissioning begins in design, not construction**
2. **Functional testing verifies real-world performance**
3. **Training investment prevents operational problems**
4. **Complete documentation enables effective maintenance**
5. **Warranty tracking protects owner investment**
