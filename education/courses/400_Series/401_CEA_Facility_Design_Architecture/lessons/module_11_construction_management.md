## Learning Objectives

- Compare project delivery methods and select appropriately
- Manage bidding and contractor selection processes
- Develop construction documentation packages
- Conduct construction administration and site supervision
- Implement quality control and safety management protocols

## Summary
Effective construction management ensures projects are completed on time, within budget, and to specified quality standards.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Delivery method selection impacts project risk and control**
2. **Thorough documentation prevents costly changes**
3. **Contractor prequalification reduces problems**
4. **Proactive RFI management maintains schedule**
5. **Quality control integrated throughout construction**
