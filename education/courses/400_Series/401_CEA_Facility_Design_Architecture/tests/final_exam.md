## Section 1: Design Fundamentals & Architecture (Questions 1-8)

### Question 1
A client requests a facility capable of producing 10,000 lbs/week of leafy greens year-round. Using vertical farming with 6 levels and assuming 4 lbs/sq ft/week productivity, what minimum footprint is required?

A) 400 sq ft
B) 2,500 sq ft
C) 15,000 sq ft
D) 60,000 sq ft

**Correct Answer: A**
*Calculation: 10,000 lbs/week ÷ 4 lbs/sq ft/week ÷ 6 levels = 417 sq ft (round to 400)*

### Question 2
Which building envelope material provides the BEST combination of light transmission (>80%) and insulation (U<0.50) for greenhouses?

A) Single glass
B) Double glass
C) 8mm polycarbonate
D) 16mm polycarbonate

**Correct Answer: D**

### Question 3
For a greenhouse in Boston, MA with design temperature differential of 75°F and 6,800 sq ft glazing area (U=0.50), what is the approximate heating load?

A) 100,000 BTU/hr
B) 175,000 BTU/hr
C) 255,000 BTU/hr
D) 400,000 BTU/hr

**Correct Answer: C**
*Calculation: Q = U × A × ΔT = 0.50 × 6,800 × 75 = 255,000 BTU/hr*

## Section 2: Structural Engineering (Questions 9-14)

### Question 9
Per ASCE 7 load combinations, which combination includes both wind and earthquake loads?

A) 1.2D + 1.6L + 0.5S
B) 1.2D + 1.0W + L + 0.5S
C) 1.2D + 1.0E + L + 0.2S
D) Both B and C must be checked

**Correct Answer: D**

### Question 10
A greenhouse column supports 288 sq ft tributary area with dead load of 8 psf and snow load of 30 psf. Using load combination 1.2D + 1.6S, what is the design load?

A) 10,944 lbs
B) 13,824 lbs
C) 16,589 lbs
D) 19,200 lbs

**Correct Answer: C**
*Calculation: (1.2×8 + 1.6×30) × 288 = 57.6 psf × 288 = 16,589 lbs*

### Question 16
A vertical farm requires removal of 750 lbs/hr of moisture. If the dehumidifier reduces humidity ratio by 50 grains/lb, what CFM must pass through the unit?

A) 750 CFM
B) 1,250 CFM
C) 1,750 CFM
D) 2,500 CFM

**Correct Answer: C**
*Calculation: CFM = (750 lbs/hr × 7,000 grains/lb) / (50 grains/lb × 60 min/hr) = 1,750 CFM*

## Section 4: Electrical Systems (Questions 25-32)

### Question 18
A vertical farm has 8,400 kW of connected LED lighting load. Applying a demand factor of 1.0 and adding 25% for the largest motor (818 kW chiller), what is the total lighting and motor demand?

A) 8,400 kW
B) 8,605 kW
C) 9,218 kW
D) 9,604 kW

**Correct Answer: C**
*Calculation: 8,400 + (818 × 0.25) = 8,400 + 205 = 8,605 kW*

### Question 19
For a 200 kW LED lighting panel at 480V, 3-phase with 0.95 power factor, what is the current (before applying 125% continuous factor)?

A) 178 A
B) 253 A
C) 316 A
D) 417 A

**Correct Answer: B**
*Calculation: I = 200,000 / (480 × 1.732 × 0.95) = 253 A*

## Section 7: Energy & Efficiency (Questions 45-50)

### Question 25
A vertical farm uses 25,000,000 kWh/year over 40,000 sq ft. What is the Energy Use Intensity (EUI)?

A) 625 kBTU/sf/yr
B) 1,277 kBTU/sf/yr
C) 2,132 kBTU/sf/yr
D) 3,205 kBTU/sf/yr

**Correct Answer: C**
*Calculation: (25,000,000 kWh × 3,412 BTU/kWh) / 40,000 sf = 2,132,500 BTU/sf/yr = 2,132 kBTU/sf/yr*

### Question 26
If LED lighting represents 68% of total energy use (25,000,000 kWh/yr) and improving efficacy by 20% is possible, what are the annual savings?

A) 1,700,000 kWh
B) 3,400,000 kWh
C) 5,000,000 kWh
D) 6,800,000 kWh

**Correct Answer: B**
*Calculation: 25,000,000 × 0.68 × 0.20 = 3,400,000 kWh*

### Question 27
A 700-ton chiller upgrade from 11 EER to 14 EER requires $150,000 additional investment and saves $98,230/year. What is the simple payback period?

A) 0.7 years
B) 1.5 years
C) 2.3 years
D) 3.8 years

**Correct Answer: B**
*Calculation: $150,000 / $98,230 = 1.53 years*
