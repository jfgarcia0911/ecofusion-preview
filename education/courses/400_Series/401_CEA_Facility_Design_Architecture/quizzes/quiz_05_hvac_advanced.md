### Question 4
A heat recovery system captures 70% of exhaust heat. If exhaust contains 100,000 BTU/hr, how much is recovered?

A) 30,000 BTU/hr
B) 50,000 BTU/hr
C) 70,000 BTU/hr
D) 100,000 BTU/hr

**Correct Answer: C**
*Explanation: 100,000 BTU/hr × 0.70 = 70,000 BTU/hr recovered*
