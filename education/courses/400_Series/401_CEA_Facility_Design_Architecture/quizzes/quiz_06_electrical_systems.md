### Question 1
What voltage is typically used for high-power LED lighting systems in large CEA facilities?

A) 120V single-phase
B) 208V three-phase
C) 277V or 480V three-phase
D) 12V DC

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
A vertical farm has 500 kW of LED lighting. If the utility rate is $0.12/kWh and lights run 18 hours/day, what is the daily electricity cost?

A) $600
B) $1,080
C) $1,440
D) $2,160

**Correct Answer: B**
*Explanation: 500 kW × 18 hrs × $0.12/kWh = $1,080/day*

### Question 6
A facility requires 2,000 kW total electrical load. What minimum service capacity should be installed with 20% safety margin?

A) 2,000 kW
B) 2,200 kW
C) 2,400 kW
D) 3,000 kW

**Correct Answer: C**
*Explanation: 2,000 kW × 1.20 = 2,400 kW*
