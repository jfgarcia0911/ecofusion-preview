### Question 1

### Question 4
Which site selection factor typically carries the HIGHEST weight in evaluation?

A) Location factors (market proximity, labor)
B) Physical factors (topography, soil)
C) Utility factors (electric, water)

**Correct Answer: A**

### Question 5

A) 10-15 feet

C) 20-30 feet or to bedrock
D) 30-40 feet

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 6
For a vertical farm with 50,000 sq ft footprint and 6 growing levels, what is the total growing surface area?

A) 50,000 sq ft
B) 150,000 sq ft
C) 250,000 sq ft
D) 300,000 sq ft

**Correct Answer: D**
*Explanation: 50,000 × 6 = 300,000 sq ft*
