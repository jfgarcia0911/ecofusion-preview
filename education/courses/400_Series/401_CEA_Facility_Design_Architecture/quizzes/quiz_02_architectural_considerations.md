### Question 1
What is the primary function of the building envelope in CEA facilities?

A) Aesthetic appeal for visitors
B) Control heat transfer and maintain optimal growing conditions
C) Provide structural support only
D) Maximize natural light penetration

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which glazing material offers the best insulation properties for greenhouse applications?

A) Single-pane glass
B) Double-pane glass
C) Triple-wall polycarbonate
D) Polyethylene film

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
In functional space planning, what is the recommended ratio of production area to post-harvest processing area?

A) 2:1
B) 5:1
C) 10:1
D) 20:1

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
What is the optimal Daily Light Integral (DLI) range for most leafy greens in CEA?

A) 8-12 mol/m²/day
B) 12-17 mol/m²/day
C) 20-25 mol/m²/day
D) 30-40 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer: B**

### Question 5
Which material is MOST suitable for interior surfaces in high-humidity growing areas?

A) Untreated wood
B) Standard drywall
C) FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Panels)
D) Painted concrete blocks

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 6
What is the primary design consideration for integrating natural and artificial lighting?

A) Minimizing all natural light to maintain control
B) Maximizing natural light to reduce operating costs
C) Balancing natural light contribution with supplemental lighting control
D) Using only natural light during daytime

**Correct Answer: C**

### Question 7

A) Centralized processing area
B) Unidirectional flow from propagation to packaging

### Question 10

A) 8-10 feet
B) 12-14 feet
