### Question 1
Which load type varies most significantly by geographic location?

A) Dead load
B) Live load
C) Snow load
D) Crop load

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
What is the typical snow load capacity for greenhouse structures in northern climates?

A) 10-15 psf
B) 20-30 psf
C) 30-50 psf or higher
D) Snow load is not a design consideration

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
A greenhouse measures 100 ft x 200 ft. If the dead load is 5 psf and live load is 20 psf, what is the total design load?

A) 25 psf
B) 100 psf
C) 500,000 lbs
D) 25 psf (must also add snow and wind loads)

**Correct Answer: D**
*Explanation: Total is 25 psf from dead + live, but snow and wind loads must also be included in final design*

### Question 10
A vertical farm growing system weighs 50 lbs/sq ft including plants, water, and media. For a 1,000 sq ft level, what is the total load?

B) 25,000 lbs
