### Question 3
A greenhouse has 10,000 sq ft growing area. Peak solar gain is 200 BTU/hr/sq ft. What is the total cooling load from solar radiation alone?

A) 200,000 BTU/hr
B) 500,000 BTU/hr
C) 1,000,000 BTU/hr
D) 2,000,000 BTU/hr

**Correct Answer: D**
*Explanation: 10,000 sq ft × 200 BTU/hr/sq ft = 2,000,000 BTU/hr*

### Question 10
A facility requires 500,000 BTU/hr of cooling. If using a chiller with COP of 3.5, what is the electrical power requirement?

A) 41 kW
B) 61 kW
C) 142 kW
D) 175 kW

**Correct Answer: A**
*Explanation: 500,000 BTU/hr ÷ 3.412 BTU/W = 146,500 W = 146.5 kW thermal; 146.5 kW ÷ 3.5 COP = 41.9 kW electrical*
