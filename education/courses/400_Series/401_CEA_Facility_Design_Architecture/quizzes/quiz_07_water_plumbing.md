### Question 1
What is the typical water quality requirement (EC) for hydroponic lettuce production?

A) 0.1-0.5 mS/cm
B) 1.0-2.0 mS/cm
C) 3.0-4.0 mS/cm
D) 5.0-10.0 mS/cm

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 2
Which water treatment method is MOST effective for removing bacteria and pathogens?

A) Sediment filtration
B) Carbon filtration
C) UV sterilization or ozone
D) Water softening

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
A 50,000 sq ft greenhouse uses 0.5 gallons per sq ft per day. What is the daily water requirement?

A) 10,000 gallons
B) 25,000 gallons
C) 50,000 gallons
D) 100,000 gallons

**Correct Answer: B**
*Explanation: 50,000 sq ft × 0.5 gal/sq ft/day = 25,000 gallons/day*
