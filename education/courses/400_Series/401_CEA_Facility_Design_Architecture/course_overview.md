# Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture

## Course Level
Expert/Professional (400-Level)

## Course Description

This expert-level course provides comprehensive training in the design and architecture of Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) facilities. Students will master the technical, engineering, and regulatory aspects of designing greenhouses, vertical farms, and hybrid CEA facilities. The curriculum covers structural engineering, mechanical systems integration, electrical design, regulatory compliance, and project management for large-scale CEA construction projects.

This course prepares professionals to lead facility design projects, collaborate with engineering teams, and deliver efficient, compliant, and economically viable CEA infrastructure.

## Prerequisites

Required:
- Course 301: Advanced Greenhouse Management Systems
- Course 302: Vertical Farming Technologies
- Course 303: Climate Control Engineering

Recommended:
- Professional experience in agriculture, engineering, or construction
- Basic understanding of building codes and construction practices
- Familiarity with CAD or building design software

## Target Audience

- Agricultural engineers
- Facility designers and architects
- Construction project managers
- CEA business owners planning facility development
- Consultants specializing in controlled environment agriculture
- Engineering firms entering the CEA market

## Course Duration

- **Total Hours**: 80-100 hours
- **Lecture/Reading**: 40-50 hours
- **Hands-on Activities**: 20-25 hours
- **Assessments**: 10-15 hours
- **Final Project**: 10 hours

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Design complete CEA facilities** from concept through construction documentation
2. **Integrate mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems** for optimal facility performance
3. **Perform structural calculations** for greenhouse and vertical farm structures
4. **Develop HVAC systems** tailored to specific crop requirements and climate zones
5. **Design electrical distribution systems** meeting code requirements and operational needs
6. **Navigate building codes and permitting processes** for CEA facilities
7. **Create energy models** to optimize facility efficiency and reduce operating costs
8. **Select appropriate materials and technologies** for various CEA applications
9. **Manage construction projects** from bid to commissioning
10. **Implement commissioning protocols** to ensure system performance
11. **Design retrofit and renovation projects** for existing facilities
12. **Future-proof designs** for technological advancement and operational flexibility

## Course Modules

### Module 1: CEA Facility Design Fundamentals
- Overview of CEA facility types and configurations
- Design process and methodology
- Stakeholder requirements and needs assessment
- Site selection and analysis
- Conceptual design development

### Module 2: Architectural Considerations for CEA
- Functional space planning and workflow design
- Building envelope design for climate control
- Natural vs. artificial lighting integration
- Material selection for controlled environments
- Aesthetic considerations and community integration

### Module 3: Structural Engineering for CEA Facilities
- Load calculations (dead, live, snow, wind, seismic)
- Greenhouse structural systems and materials
- Vertical farm structural design
- Foundation design and soil mechanics
- Structural analysis and safety factors

### Module 4: HVAC System Design - Part 1: Fundamentals
- Psychrometrics and air properties
- Heating and cooling load calculations
- Ventilation requirements and strategies
- Dehumidification and humidity control
- Air distribution system design

### Module 5: HVAC System Design - Part 2: Advanced Integration
- Multi-zone climate control systems
- Heat recovery and energy efficiency
- Integration with growing systems
- Control strategies and automation
- System sizing and equipment selection

### Module 6: Electrical Systems and Power Distribution
- Electrical load calculations
- Power distribution system design
- Lighting system electrical design
- Emergency and backup power systems
- Electrical code compliance (NEC)

### Module 7: Water and Plumbing Systems Design
- Water supply and treatment system design
- Irrigation system integration
- Drainage and waste management
- Plumbing code compliance
- Water conservation and recycling systems

### Module 8: Building Codes and Regulatory Compliance
- International Building Code (IBC) requirements
- Agricultural vs. industrial building classifications
- Fire protection and life safety systems
- Accessibility requirements (ADA)
- Local zoning and land use regulations

### Module 9: Energy Modeling and Efficiency Optimization
- Energy modeling methodologies and software
- Baseline energy consumption analysis
- Efficiency improvement strategies
- Renewable energy integration
- Economic analysis and payback calculations

### Module 10: Material Selection and Specification
- Glazing materials and properties
- Insulation materials and thermal performance
- Structural materials (steel, aluminum, wood, composite)
- Finishes and coatings for controlled environments
- Specification writing and procurement

### Module 11: Construction Management for CEA Projects
- Project delivery methods (design-bid-build, design-build, CM)
- Bidding and contractor selection
- Construction documentation and drawings
- Construction administration and site supervision
- Quality control and safety management

### Module 12: Facility Commissioning and Startup
- Commissioning process and protocols
- System testing and performance verification
- Functional performance testing
- Training and documentation
- Warranty and ongoing support

### Module 13: Renovation and Retrofit Projects
- Existing facility assessment and analysis
- Retrofit design strategies
- Phased construction and operational continuity
- Structural modifications and upgrades
- ROI analysis for retrofit projects

### Module 14: Future-Proofing and Emerging Technologies
- Modular and flexible design strategies
- Technology integration pathways
- Adaptive reuse considerations
- Sustainability and resilience design
- Industry trends and future developments

- Passing score: 80% or higher
