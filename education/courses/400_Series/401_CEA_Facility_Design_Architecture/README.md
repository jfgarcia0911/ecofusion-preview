# Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture

## Expert/Professional Level (400-Level)

Welcome to the comprehensive course on Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) facility design and architecture. This expert-level course provides the technical knowledge and practical skills needed to design, engineer, and deliver world-class CEA facilities from concept through commissioning.

---

## Course Overview

This course prepares professionals to lead CEA facility design projects, collaborate with engineering teams, and deliver efficient, compliant, and economically viable infrastructure for greenhouses, vertical farms, and hybrid controlled environment agriculture operations.

**Duration**: 80-100 hours
**Prerequisites**: Courses 301, 302, 303 (or equivalent professional experience)
**Certification**: Certificate of Completion upon achieving 80% on all assessments

---

## Course Structure

### Lesson Modules (14 Modules)

#### Fundamentals & Planning (Modules 1-2)
1. **CEA Facility Design Fundamentals** - Design process, facility types, site selection, stakeholder requirements
2. **Architectural Considerations for CEA** - Space planning, envelope design, lighting integration, materials

#### Structural & Mechanical Systems (Modules 3-5)
3. **Structural Engineering for CEA Facilities** - Load calculations, greenhouse structures, vertical farm racks, foundations
4. **HVAC System Design - Part 1: Fundamentals** - Psychrometrics, load calculations, ventilation, dehumidification
5. **HVAC System Design - Part 2: Advanced Integration** - Multi-zone control, heat recovery, controls, equipment selection

#### Building Systems (Modules 6-7)
6. **Electrical Systems and Power Distribution** - Load calculations, distribution design, lighting systems, emergency power, NEC compliance
7. **Water and Plumbing Systems Design** - Water treatment, irrigation integration, drainage, plumbing codes, conservation

#### Compliance & Optimization (Modules 8-9)
8. **Building Codes and Regulatory Compliance** - IBC requirements, agricultural vs. industrial classification, fire protection, ADA, zoning
9. **Energy Modeling and Efficiency Optimization** - Energy modeling, baseline analysis, efficiency strategies, renewable energy, economics

#### Implementation (Modules 10-12)
10. **Material Selection and Specification** - Glazing, insulation, structural materials, finishes, specification writing
11. **Construction Management for CEA Projects** - Project delivery, bidding, documentation, administration, quality control
12. **Facility Commissioning and Startup** - Commissioning process, system testing, training, warranty management

#### Advanced Topics (Modules 13-14)
13. **Renovation and Retrofit Projects** - Facility assessment, retrofit strategies, phased construction, ROI analysis
14. **Future-Proofing and Emerging Technologies** - Modular design, technology integration, sustainability, industry trends
