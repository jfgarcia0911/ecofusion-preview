## Assessment Component Breakdown

### 1. Module Quizzes (40 points)
**14 quizzes × 2.86 points each**

#### Grading Criteria per Quiz:
- **10/10 correct (100%):** 2.86 points
- **9/10 correct (90%):** 2.57 points
- **8/10 correct (80%):** 2.29 points

- Technical calculations and engineering problems
- Code compliance and regulatory knowledge
- System integration concepts
- Material selection and specification
- Safety and commissioning protocols

## Detailed Scoring Rubrics

### Technical Calculation Problems

#### Structural Load Calculations:
- **Full Points:** Correct methodology, accurate calculations, proper units, safety factors applied
- **Partial Credit (75%):** Minor calculation errors but correct approach
- **Partial Credit (50%):** Correct methodology but significant errors
- **No Credit:** Incorrect approach or methodology

#### HVAC Load Calculations:
- **Full Points:** Accurate psychrometric analysis, proper equipment selection
- **Partial Credit (75%):** Minor errors in calculations
- **Partial Credit (50%):** Correct process but equipment undersized/oversized
- **No Credit:** Fundamental errors in methodology

#### Electrical Load Calculations:
- **Full Points:** Accurate load calculations, NEC compliant
- **Partial Credit (75%):** Minor code compliance issues
- **Partial Credit (50%):** Calculation errors but safe design
- **No Credit:** Non-compliant or unsafe design

## Late Submission Policy

- **Hands-On Activities:**
  - Within 24 hours: 10% deduction
  - 24-48 hours: 25% deduction
  - 48-72 hours: 50% deduction

## Reattempt Policy

- **Quizzes:** Unlimited attempts until passing score achieved
- **Hands-On Activities:** One revision allowed if score below 80%
- **Final Examination:** One retake allowed if initial score below 80%

## Academic Integrity

All work must be original. Violations include:
- Copying calculations or designs from others
- Using unauthorized resources during examinations
- Submitting work completed by others
- Plagiarizing industry designs without attribution

**Penalty:** Zero points on assignment, possible course failure
