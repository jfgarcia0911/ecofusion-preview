## Project Brief

As lead facility designer for AgriTech Solutions, you have been contracted to design a new controlled environment agriculture facility for Fresh Greens LLC. The client requires a complete preliminary design package including architectural layouts, MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) system designs, code compliance analysis, energy modeling, and construction budget.

This comprehensive project integrates all 14 modules of Course 401 into a single cohesive design deliverable that demonstrates professional-level facility design competency.

---

## Client Requirements

**Fresh Greens LLC - Urban Vertical Farm**

**Production Goals:**
- Crop: Baby leafy greens (lettuce, arugula, kale, chard) and culinary herbs
- Annual production target: 1,000,000 lbs/year
- Production method: Vertical NFT systems with LED lighting
- Market: Direct-to-consumer and restaurant trade within 50-mile radius
- Quality standards: Organic practices, food safety certification required

**Site Information:**
- Location: Portland, Oregon (Climate Zone 4C)
- Available building: Former warehouse, 50,000 sq ft footprint, 24-ft clear height
- Utilities: 3-phase 480V electrical (1,600A available), municipal water/sewer
- Zoning: Light industrial, agricultural use permitted
- Budget: $8M total project (construction + equipment)

**Operational Requirements:**
- Production area: 40,000 sq ft minimum
- Support spaces: Processing/pack house, cold storage, offices, labs
- Staffing: 25 employees (15 production, 10 support)
- Operations: 24/7 production with day/evening shifts
- Sustainability: LEED certification desired, energy efficiency priority

## Required Deliverables

### Part 1: Facility Layout and Space Planning (8 points)

**1.1 Floor Plan Layout**

Create a scaled floor plan (can be sketch or CAD) showing:
- Production zones with vertical rack configuration
- Processing and packaging area
- Cold storage (walk-in cooler/freezer)
- Seed starting/propagation area
- Offices and employee facilities
- Mechanical/electrical rooms
- Loading dock and material flow

**Requirements:**
- Show dimensions and square footages
- Indicate workflow patterns (material flow, personnel circulation)
- Identify critical adjacencies
- Show emergency egress paths
- Include equipment placement (major items)

**1.2 Production Capacity Calculations**

Calculate:
- Number of growing levels (vertical tiers)
- Total growing area (sq ft accounting for racks)
- Production cycles per year (based on crop rotation)
- Annual yield and verification against 1M lbs target
- Growing density and space utilization efficiency

---

### Part 2: Structural and Architectural Systems (6 points)

**2.1 Vertical Rack System Design**

Specify:
- Rack configuration (tiers, spacing, dimensions)
- Structural material (steel, aluminum)
- Loading capacity per tier (lbs/sq ft)
- Seismic considerations and bracing requirements
- Foundation requirements for concentrated loads

**2.2 Building Envelope Modifications**

Describe necessary modifications to existing warehouse:
- Insulation upgrades (walls, roof) with R-values
- Vapor barrier and moisture control
- Air sealing strategy for climate control
- Door and access point specifications
- Cleanroom features for food safety zones

---

### Part 3: Mechanical (HVAC) System Design (8 points)

**3.1 Load Calculations**

Provide summary calculations for:
- Peak cooling load (tons) including LED heat gains
- Peak heating load (MBH)
- Dehumidification capacity (lbs water/hr)
- Ventilation requirements (CFM fresh air)
- Zone-by-zone breakdown (production vs. support)

**3.2 HVAC System Configuration**

Design and specify:
- Primary HVAC system type (DOAS, VAV, etc.) with justification
- Major equipment schedule (chillers, boilers, AHUs, dehumidifiers)
- Air distribution strategy (ductwork layout concept)
- Temperature and humidity control zones
- Heat recovery and energy efficiency features
- Control system architecture

**3.3 Energy Model Summary**

Estimate annual energy consumption:
- HVAC: _____ kWh/year
- Lighting: _____ kWh/year
- Other: _____ kWh/year
- Total: _____ kWh/year
- Energy cost @ $0.10/kWh: $_____/year
- Energy per lb produced: _____ kWh/lb

---

### Part 4: Electrical System Design (6 points)

**4.1 Load Summary and Service Sizing**

Calculate:
- Total connected load (kW)
- Demand load with NEC demand factors (kW)
- Service entrance size (Amps)
- Emergency backup power requirements (kW)

**4.2 Electrical Distribution**

Provide:
- Single-line diagram showing service entrance to main distribution panels
- Panel schedule for major electrical panels (lighting, HVAC, production)
- Emergency power distribution (generator backed systems)
- Lighting control system architecture
- Power quality considerations (harmonics, power factor)

---

### Part 5: Water and Plumbing Systems (4 points)

**5.1 Water Requirements**

Calculate:
- Peak irrigation water demand (GPM)
- Domestic water needs (restrooms, break room) (GPM)
- Water treatment system capacity (RO or filtration)
- Storage tank sizing
- Recirculation and water recovery strategy

**5.2 Plumbing System Design**

Specify:
- Water distribution system (pipe sizing, materials)
- Drainage system for production areas
- Backflow prevention
- Hot water system for sanitation
- Plumbing code compliance (IPC)

---

### Part 6: Code Compliance and Permitting (4 points)

**6.1 Building Code Analysis**

Address compliance with International Building Code (IBC):
- Occupancy classification (Group F? Special agricultural?)
- Fire protection requirements (sprinklers, fire alarm)
- Egress requirements (exit quantities, travel distance)
- Accessibility (ADA compliance for employee areas)
- Structural loading (live loads, concentrated loads from racks)

**6.2 Food Safety Requirements**

Design features for FSMA compliance:
- Separation of processing from growing areas
- Cleanroom / positive pressure zones
- Sanitation stations and handwashing
- Material flow (prevent cross-contamination)
- Pest exclusion and air filtration

---
