## Scenario

You are a consultant hired by GreenTech Ventures to evaluate three potential sites for a new 50,000 sq ft vertical farm facility. The facility will produce leafy greens and herbs for urban markets, requiring significant electrical power, water access, and proximity to distribution channels.

### Site Options:

**Site A: Former Manufacturing Building**
- Location: Urban industrial district, 2 miles from city center
- Building: 60,000 sq ft warehouse, 28-foot clear height
- Utilities: 3-phase 480V electrical service (800A), municipal water, sewer connection
- Zoning: Light industrial, agricultural use permitted with special permit
- Access: Loading docks, 0.5 miles from major highway
- Cost: $2.8M purchase or $18/sq ft annual lease

**Site B: Suburban Greenfield**
- Location: Suburban area, 15 miles from city center
- Land: 5 acres, vacant lot
- Utilities: Single-phase 240V (200A) - upgrade required, well water available, septic needed
- Zoning: Agricultural, CEA permitted by right
- Access: Rural road, 2 miles from highway
- Cost: $750K land purchase, new construction required

**Site C: Urban Redevelopment Zone**
- Location: Downtown redevelopment area
- Building: New construction on city-owned land
- Utilities: Full utility infrastructure planned, city incentives available
- Zoning: Mixed-use, agricultural encouraged
- Access: Public transit adjacent, 1 mile from highway
- Cost: $1.2M land lease (99 years), construction incentives available

## Grading Rubric

### Part 1: Site Evaluation Matrix (8 points)
- **8 points:** All sites scored appropriately with accurate weighted calculations, scores justified by site characteristics
- **6 points:** Scoring mostly appropriate, minor calculation errors
- **4 points:** Scoring lacks clear justification, multiple errors
- **0-2 points:** Incomplete or inaccurate scoring

### Part 2: Critical Issue Identification (6 points)
- **6 points:** All six issues identified are critical, specific, and actionable
- **4-5 points:** Most issues identified are relevant, some lack specificity
- **2-3 points:** Issues identified are generic or not most critical
- **0-1 points:** Issues missing or not relevant

### Part 3: Recommendation (6 points)
- **6 points:** Clear recommendation with strong justification tied to analysis, specific actionable items
- **4-5 points:** Reasonable recommendation with adequate justification
- **2-3 points:** Weak justification or generic action items
- **0-1 points:** Recommendation not supported by analysis
