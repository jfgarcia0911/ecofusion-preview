**Question 1:** What is the primary goal of sustainable supply chain management in food enterprises?**

A) Minimizing costs only
B) Integrating environmental, social, and economic performance across the value chain
C) Maximizing speed regardless of impact
D) Eliminating all suppliers

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Sustainable supply chains balance efficiency with environmental stewardship, social equity, and economic viability across all tiers of suppliers and partners.*

---

**Question 2:** What does supply chain traceability enable?**

A) Lower costs
B) Tracking product origin, movement, and custody from source to consumer
C) Faster delivery only
D) Reduced quality

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Traceability systems track products through the supply chain, enabling food safety response, authenticity verification, and sustainability claims validation.*

---

**Question 3:** What circular economy principle is most applicable to food supply chains?**

A) Linear take-make-dispose model
B) Designing waste out of the system through composting, anaerobic digestion, and byproduct valorization
C) Increasing single-use packaging
D) Maximizing waste generation

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Circular food systems eliminate waste by returning organic matter to soil, capturing biogas, and converting byproducts to animal feed or other valuable outputs.*

---

**Question 4:** What is Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 supplier mapping?**

A) Ranking suppliers by importance
B) Identifying direct suppliers (Tier 1), their suppliers (Tier 2), and upstream (Tier 3+) to understand full supply chain
C) Pricing tiers
D) Delivery speed categories

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Supply chain mapping beyond Tier 1 reveals hidden risks (e.g., labor practices at farms, environmental impacts of input production) and improvement opportunities.*

---

**Question 5:** What is the purpose of supplier codes of conduct?**

A) Legal compliance only
B) Establishing minimum standards for labor, environmental, and ethical practices that suppliers must meet
C) Reducing supplier costs
D) Eliminating competition

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Codes of conduct define non-negotiable standards (e.g., no child labor, environmental compliance, fair wages) that suppliers must uphold as condition of partnership.*

---

**Question 6:** For a vertically integrated aquaponics enterprise, what is a key supply chain resilience strategy?**

A) Single-source all inputs from one supplier
B) Diversifying critical input suppliers (feed, seedlings, packaging) and maintaining safety stock
C) Just-in-time only with no inventory
D) Ignoring risks

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Resilience requires supplier diversification for critical inputs, strategic inventory of long-lead items, and relationships with backup suppliers.*

---

**Question 7:** What blockchain technology application is most promising for food supply chains?**

A) Cryptocurrency only
B) Immutable record-keeping for traceability, authenticity verification, and smart contracts
C) Social media
D) Gaming

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Blockchain creates tamper-proof records of product movement, certifications, and transactions, enhancing trust in sustainability and food safety claims.*

---

**Question 8:** What is "collaborative planning, forecasting, and replenishment" (CPFR) in supply chains?**

A) Independent planning without communication
B) Buyers and suppliers sharing forecasts and coordinating plans to optimize inventory and reduce waste
C) Competitive bidding only
D) Eliminating forecasts

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: CPFR builds supplier partnerships through shared data and synchronized planning, reducing stockouts, excess inventory, and waste.*

---

**Question 9:** For perishable food products, what supply chain metric is most critical?**

A) Lowest cost per unit only
B) Cold chain integrity and days of inventory/freshness upon delivery
C) Longest possible transit time
D) Maximum handling steps

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Perishable product quality depends on unbroken cold chain and minimal time from harvest to consumption—requiring fast, temperature-controlled logistics.*

---

**Question 10:** What is supplier relationship management (SRM) best practice?**

A) Adversarial negotiations focused only on price
B) Strategic partnerships with key suppliers including development support, fair pricing, and long-term commitments
C) Changing suppliers constantly
D) Minimal communication

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: Strategic SRM builds mutually beneficial partnerships through transparent communication, fair terms, capability development, and shared risk/reward.*
