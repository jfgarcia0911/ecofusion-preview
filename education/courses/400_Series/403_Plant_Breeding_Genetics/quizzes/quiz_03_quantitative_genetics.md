## Questions

### Question 1
Quantitative traits are characterized by:

A) Single gene control
B) Discrete phenotypic classes
C) Continuous variation controlled by multiple genes
D) No environmental influence

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
What does broad-sense heritability (H²) represent?

A) Only additive genetic variance
B) Total genetic variance / Total phenotypic variance
C) Environmental variance only
D) Dominance effects only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
If narrow-sense heritability (h²) = 0.6 and selection differential = 10, what is the expected response to selection?

A) 3
B) 6
C) 10
D) 16.7

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Response = h² × Selection differential = 0.6 × 10 = 6

---

### Question 4
Which heritability estimate is MORE useful for predicting breeding progress?

A) Broad-sense heritability (H²)
B) Narrow-sense heritability (h²)
C) Both equally useful
D) Neither is useful

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** h² includes only additive effects passed to offspring

---

### Question 5
Genotype × Environment (G×E) interaction means:

A) All genotypes perform identically in all environments
B) Genotype rankings change across different environments
C) Environment has no effect on phenotype
D) Only genetics matters

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
A trait has H² = 0.8. This indicates:

A) 80% of variation is environmental
B) 80% of variation is due to total genetic effects
C) 80% will respond to selection
D) The trait cannot be improved

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Polygenic inheritance results in:

A) Distinct phenotypic classes
B) Bell-shaped (normal) distribution of phenotypes
C) Only two phenotypes
D) No phenotypic variation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
If VP (phenotypic variance) = 100 and VG (genetic variance) = 40, what is H²?

A) 0.2
B) 0.4
C) 0.6
D) 2.5

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** H² = VG/VP = 40/100 = 0.4

---

### Question 9
In CEA breeding, why is G×E interaction particularly important?

A) It's not important
B) Different LED spectra and systems can cause variety performance changes
C) All varieties perform identically in CEA
D) Genetics don't matter in controlled environments

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Which variance component is NOT transmitted to offspring?

A) Additive genetic variance (VA)
B) Dominance variance (VD)
C) Environmental variance (VE)
D) Both B and C

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Only additive effects are reliably transmitted; dominance and environmental effects are not inherited

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Continuous variation controlled by multiple genes
2. B - Total genetic variance / Total phenotypic variance
3. B - 6
4. B - Narrow-sense heritability (h²)
5. B - Genotype rankings change across different environments
6. B - 80% of variation is due to total genetic effects
7. B - Bell-shaped (normal) distribution of phenotypes
8. B - 0.4
9. B - Different LED spectra and systems can cause variety performance changes
10. D - Both dominance and environmental variance
