## Questions

### Question 1
Micropropagation is advantageous because it:

A) Produces genetically diverse offspring
B) Rapidly produces many genetically identical plants
C) Increases mutation rates
D) Eliminates need for parent plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which plant growth regulator class promotes shoot formation?

A) Auxins
B) Cytokinins
C) Abscisic acid
D) Ethylene

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the typical auxin:cytokinin ratio for root induction?

A) High auxin : Low cytokinin
B) Low auxin : High cytokinin
C) Equal ratios
D) Neither hormone needed

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 4
Somatic embryogenesis produces:

A) Seeds from sexual reproduction
B) Embryo-like structures from somatic cells
C) Only roots
D) Flowers only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Meristem culture is used for:

A) Increasing plant height
B) Virus elimination and clean stock production
C) Seed production
D) Increasing genetic diversity

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Protoplast fusion (somatic hybridization) combines:

A) Two egg cells
B) Cells from different species/varieties after wall removal
C) Seeds
D) Only compatible species

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Doubled haploid (DH) technology creates:

A) Triploid plants
B) Completely homozygous lines in one generation
C) Only heterozygous plants
D) Hexaploid plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is the PRIMARY advantage of DH production for breeding?

A) Increases genetic diversity
B) Reduces time to achieve homozygosity from 6-8 to 1-2 generations
C) Makes plants larger
D) Eliminates all recessive alleles

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Anther or microspore culture produces:

A) Diploid plants
B) Haploid plants (n) that can be chromosome-doubled
C) Polyploid plants only
D) No viable plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In vitro selection can be used to screen for:

A) Plant height only
B) Tolerance to stress, disease, or herbicides at cellular level
C) Flower color only
D) Nothing useful

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Rapidly produces many genetically identical plants
2. B - Cytokinins
3. A - High auxin : Low cytokinin
4. B - Embryo-like structures from somatic cells
5. B - Virus elimination and clean stock production
6. B - Cells from different species after wall removal
7. B - Completely homozygous lines in one generation
8. B - Reduces time to homozygosity from 6-8 to 1-2 generations
9. B - Haploid plants that can be chromosome-doubled
10. B - Tolerance to stress, disease, or herbicides at cellular level
