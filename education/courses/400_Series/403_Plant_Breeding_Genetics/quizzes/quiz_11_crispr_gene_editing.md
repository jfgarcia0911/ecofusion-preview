## Questions

### Question 1
CRISPR stands for:

A) Controlled Replication In Specific Plant Regions
B) Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats
C) Chemical Reaction Involving Systematic Plant Responses
D) Cellular RNA Interference System for Plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Cas9 is:

A) A type of plant
B) An endonuclease enzyme that cuts DNA
C) A chromosome
D) A growth regulator

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
CRISPR gene knockout creates:

A) Overexpression of target gene
B) Loss-of-function mutations in target gene
C) New genes from nothing
D) Plant hybrids

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Gene editing differs from traditional GMO transgenics because:

A) It inserts foreign DNA permanently
B) It can make precise changes without inserting foreign DNA
C) It's less precise
D) It only works in animals

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Multiplexed CRISPR editing allows:

A) Editing only one gene
B) Simultaneous editing of multiple genes with multiple guide RNAs
C) No editing capability
D) Editing only non-coding regions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
In many jurisdictions, CRISPR-edited plants without foreign DNA are regulated as:

A) Traditional GMOs requiring extensive approval
B) Potentially similar to conventional breeding (less stringent)
C) Completely banned
D) Unregulated drugs

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
CRISPR could be used in CEA breeding to:

A) Change species identity
B) Knock out genes for bolting, improve shelf-life, or enhance nutrition
C) Eliminate photosynthesis
D) Create animals from plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is "base editing" in CRISPR technology?

A) Random mutations
B) Precise single nucleotide changes without double-strand breaks
C) Deleting entire chromosomes
D) Inserting large genes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
An ethical consideration for gene editing in crops is:

A) Plants have rights
B) Consumer acceptance and transparency in labeling
C) All editing is inherently unethical
D) No ethical considerations exist

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
CRISPR delivery to plant cells commonly uses:

A) Oral administration
B) Agrobacterium-mediated transformation or particle bombardment
C) Watering plants
D) Sunlight

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats
2. B - An endonuclease enzyme that cuts DNA
3. B - Loss-of-function mutations in target gene
4. B - Can make precise changes without inserting foreign DNA
5. B - Simultaneous editing of multiple genes
6. B - Potentially similar to conventional breeding (jurisdiction-dependent)
7. B - Knock out bolting genes, improve shelf-life, enhance nutrition
8. B - Precise single nucleotide changes without DSBs
9. B - Consumer acceptance and transparency
10. B - Agrobacterium or particle bombardment
