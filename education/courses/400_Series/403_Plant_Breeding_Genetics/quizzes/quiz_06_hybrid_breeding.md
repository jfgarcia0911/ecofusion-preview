## Questions

### Question 1
Heterosis (hybrid vigor) refers to:

A) Inbred depression
B) F1 hybrid superior performance compared to parents
C) Reduced yield in hybrids
D) Genetic uniformity

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the PRIMARY disadvantage of hybrid varieties for growers?

A) Lower yields
B) Poor uniformity
C) Must purchase new seed each season (F2 segregates)
D) Hybrids are always inferior

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
Cytoplasmic Male Sterility (CMS) is used in hybrid seed production to:

A) Increase female fertility
B) Prevent self-pollination and eliminate need for manual emasculation
C) Reduce hybrid vigor
D) Create tetraploid plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
In a successful F1 hybrid, the two inbred parents should be:

A) Genetically identical
B) From different heterotic groups (complementary)
C) Both male sterile
D) Unrelated to the crop species

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Mid-parent heterosis is calculated as:

A) F1 - P1
B) (F1 - midpoint of parents) / midpoint of parents × 100
C) F1 ÷ P1
D) P1 + P2

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
If parent A yields 100 and parent B yields 80, and F1 hybrid yields 110, what is better-parent heterosis?

A) 5%
B) 10%
C) 22%
D) 50%

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** (110 - 100) / 100 × 100 = 10%

---

### Question 7
Inbred line development for hybrid production typically requires:

A) 1-2 generations
B) 3-4 generations
C) 6-8 generations of selfing
D) No inbreeding needed

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is a "three-way hybrid"?

A) Cross of three species
B) (A × B) × C, where A×B is F1 hybrid crossed to inbred C
C) Three generations crossed
D) Not a real breeding term

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
For CEA leafy greens, hybrids offer advantages including:

A) Uniformity, vigor, and specific trait combinations
B) Lower cost
C) Ability to save seed
D) Slower growth

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 10
Genic Male Sterility (GMS) differs from CMS because:

A) It's nuclear gene-controlled, not cytoplasmic
B) It produces fertile pollen
C) It's not useful for hybrids
D) It only works in animals

**Correct Answer:** A

---

## Answer Key

1. B - F1 hybrid superior performance compared to parents
2. C - Must purchase new seed each season
3. B - Prevent self-pollination and eliminate manual emasculation
4. B - From different heterotic groups (complementary)
5. B - (F1 - midpoint of parents) / midpoint × 100
6. B - 10%
7. C - 6-8 generations of selfing
8. B - (A × B) × C
9. A - Uniformity, vigor, and specific trait combinations
10. A - Nuclear gene-controlled, not cytoplasmic
