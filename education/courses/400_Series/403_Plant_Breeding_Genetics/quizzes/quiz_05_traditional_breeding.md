## Questions

### Question 1
Mass selection is MOST effective for traits with:

A) Low heritability
B) High heritability
C) No genetic variation
D) Complex epistatic interactions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
In the pedigree breeding method, selection begins in which generation?

A) F1
B) F2
C) F5 or later when lines are near homozygous
D) P1 (parents)

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Individual plant selection typically starts in F2, with line selection in later generations

---

### Question 3
What is the PRIMARY advantage of Single Seed Descent (SSD)?

A) Allows thorough field evaluation at each generation
B) Rapidly advances generations to achieve homozygosity
C) Maximizes selection intensity
D) Eliminates all undesirable traits

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Backcross breeding is used to:

A) Create entirely new varieties
B) Transfer one or few genes to an elite variety
C) Increase genetic diversity
D) Eliminate all dominant alleles

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
How many backcross generations are typically needed to recover >99% of the recurrent parent genome?

A) BC1
B) BC3
C) BC6
D) BC10

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
Bulk population method differs from pedigree method because:

A) No crosses are made
B) Plants are grown in bulk without individual plant selection until later generations
C) Only F1 generation is used
D) Selection occurs in every generation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Recurrent selection is primarily used to:

A) Purify inbred lines
B) Gradually improve population mean for quantitative traits
C) Create F1 hybrids
D) Eliminate genetic variation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
In a pure line breeding program, when is a line considered "fixed"?

A) F1 generation
B) F2 generation
C) F5-F8 when >98% homozygous
D) Never achieves fixation

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
Which breeding method allows the MOST genetic recombination events?

A) Pedigree method with early selection
B) Single Seed Descent (SSD)
C) Recurrent selection
D) All methods equal

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
For CEA breeding, which method is BEST for rapidly developing homozygous lines?

A) Mass selection in field
B) Single Seed Descent (SSD) with rapid generation cycling
C) Open pollination
D) No breeding method needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** SSD allows multiple generations per year in controlled environments

---

## Answer Key

1. B - High heritability
2. B - F2
3. B - Rapidly advances generations to achieve homozygosity
4. B - Transfer one or few genes to an elite variety
5. C - BC6
6. B - Plants grown in bulk without individual selection until later
7. B - Gradually improve population mean for quantitative traits
8. C - F5-F8 when >98% homozygous
9. C - Recurrent selection
10. B - Single Seed Descent with rapid generation cycling
