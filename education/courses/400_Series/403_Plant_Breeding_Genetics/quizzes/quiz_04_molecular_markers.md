## Questions

### Question 1
What does SNP stand for in molecular genetics?

A) Simple Nucleotide Pattern
B) Single Nucleotide Polymorphism
C) Standard Nuclear Primer
D) Sequence Number Protocol

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which molecular marker type is MOST abundant in plant genomes?

A) RFLP
B) RAPD
C) SNP (Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms)
D) Isozymes

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What is a QTL (Quantitative Trait Locus)?

A) A single gene with major effect
B) A chromosomal region associated with a quantitative trait
C) A type of molecular marker
D) A disease in plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS) are used to:

A) Identify marker-trait associations across entire genome
B) Count chromosomes
C) Produce hybrids
D) Eliminate genetic variation

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
What is the PRIMARY advantage of molecular markers over phenotypic selection?

A) Lower cost
B) Selection can occur at seedling stage independent of environment
C) No equipment needed
D) Eliminates need for field trials

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
SSR (Simple Sequence Repeat) markers are also known as:

A) SNPs
B) Microsatellites
C) RFLPs
D) Chromosomes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Genomic selection uses:

A) Single marker
B) Genome-wide markers to predict breeding value
C) No markers
D) Only phenotypic data

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
A marker is 2 cM (centiMorgans) from a target gene. What is the recombination frequency?

A) 1%
B) 2%
C) 4%
D) 20%

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 1 cM = 1% recombination frequency

---

### Question 9
Which bioinformatics tool is commonly used for sequence alignment?

A) Microsoft Word
B) BLAST (Basic Local Alignment Search Tool)
C) Excel
D) PowerPoint

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In marker-assisted selection, "foreground selection" refers to:

A) Selecting for the target gene/QTL
B) Recovering the recurrent parent genome
C) Random selection
D) Environmental selection

**Correct Answer:** A

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Single Nucleotide Polymorphism
2. C - SNP
3. B - A chromosomal region associated with a quantitative trait
4. A - Identify marker-trait associations across entire genome
5. B - Selection can occur at seedling stage independent of environment
6. B - Microsatellites
7. B - Genome-wide markers to predict breeding value
8. B - 2%
9. B - BLAST
10. A - Selecting for the target gene/QTL
