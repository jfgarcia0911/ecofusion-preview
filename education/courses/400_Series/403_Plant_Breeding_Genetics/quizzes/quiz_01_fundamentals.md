## Questions

### Question 1
What are the three main components of a nucleotide?

A) Sugar, phosphate, amino acid
B) Deoxyribose sugar, phosphate group, nitrogenous base
C) Ribose sugar, protein, nitrogen
D) Glucose, phosphate, base pair

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
During which phase of meiosis does crossing over occur?

A) Prophase I
B) Metaphase I
C) Prophase II
D) Metaphase II

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 3
If a plant has 2n = 24 chromosomes, how many chromosomes will be in its gametes?

A) 6
B) 12
C) 24
D) 48

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the relationship between genotype and phenotype?

A) Genotype = Phenotype
B) Phenotype = Genotype + Environment
C) Genotype = Phenotype - Environment
D) They are unrelated

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which type of cell division produces genetically identical daughter cells?

A) Meiosis
B) Mitosis
C) Both equally
D) Neither

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a polyploid plant?

A) A plant with one set of chromosomes
B) A plant with two sets of chromosomes
C) A plant with more than two complete sets of chromosomes
D) A plant with incomplete chromosome sets

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
Blue LED light primarily affects which plant processes? (Select the BEST answer)

A) Root development only
B) Photomorphogenesis, stomatal opening, and anthocyanin synthesis
C) Fruit ripening exclusively
D) Seed germination only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is the main advantage of the chloroplast genome for CEA breeding?

A) It controls all plant traits
B) It affects photosynthetic efficiency which is critical under LED lighting
C) It is larger than the nuclear genome
D) It mutates more frequently

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
A chromosomal inversion involves:

A) Loss of a chromosome segment
B) Duplication of a chromosome segment
C) Reversal of a chromosome segment
D) Movement of a segment to a different chromosome

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
Why is understanding G×E (genotype × environment) interactions particularly important in CEA breeding?

A) CEA environments are identical to field conditions
B) Different LED spectra, temperatures, and systems can cause differential variety performance
C) G×E interactions don't occur in controlled environments
D) All varieties perform the same in CEA

**Correct Answer:** B

---
