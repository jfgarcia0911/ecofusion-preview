## Questions

### Question 1
LED spectrum optimization through breeding focuses on:

A) Making plants glow
B) Selecting varieties with maximum photosynthetic efficiency under specific LED wavelengths
C) Eliminating chlorophyll
D) Only red light response

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
For vertical farm shelving with 30 cm spacing, ideal lettuce height is:

A) >50 cm
B) <20-25 cm
C) 1 meter
D) Height doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Aquaponic system compatibility requires breeding for:

A) High nutrient requirements
B) Tolerance to organic nutrient forms and variable nutrient levels
C) Intolerance to fish waste
D) Only synthetic fertilizers

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
High-density production traits include:

A) Large sprawling growth
B) Compact architecture, uniform maturity, disease resistance
C) Slow growth
D) Random plant size

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Shelf-life breeding for CEA crops prioritizes:

A) Rapid deterioration
B) Slow senescence, firm texture, minimal water loss post-harvest
C) Wilting immediately
D) Short shelf-life

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
For baby leaf production, ideal traits are:

A) Slow growth, thick stems
B) Rapid leaf development, tender texture, delayed stem elongation
C) Flowering at germination
D) Large mature leaves only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Breeding strawberries for vertical farms should emphasize:

A) Long runners and sprawling growth
B) Compact habit, day-neutrality, continuous production
C) Single annual harvest
D) Very tall plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Why is disease resistance MORE critical in CEA than field production?

A) CEA has no diseases
B) High humidity and plant density increase disease pressure
C) Diseases don't spread in CEA
D) Pesticides are unlimited in CEA

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Climate change adaptation breeding for CEA focuses on:

A) Varieties perform only in current climates
B) Heat tolerance and efficiency to reduce cooling costs
C) Cold-only tolerance
D) No adaptation needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Future CEA breeding directions include:

A) Abandoning all technology
B) Integration of AI phenotyping, genomic selection, and gene editing
C) Reverting to 1900s methods only
