## Questions

### Question 1
In a monohybrid cross (Aa × Aa), what is the expected phenotypic ratio if A is completely dominant?

A) 1:1
B) 1:2:1
C) 3:1
D) 9:3:3:1

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
What is the genotypic ratio of a monohybrid cross (Aa × Aa)?

A) 3:1
B) 1:2:1
C) 1:1:1:1
D) 9:3:3:1

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** AA : Aa : aa = 1:2:1

---

### Question 3
In a dihybrid cross (AaBb × AaBb) with independent assortment, how many phenotypic classes result?

A) 2
B) 4
C) 9
D) 16

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Four phenotypes in 9:3:3:1 ratio

---

### Question 4
Epistasis occurs when:

A) Two genes are on the same chromosome
B) One gene masks the expression of another gene
C) Genes mutate frequently
D) Alleles are codominant

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Two genes are 20 map units apart. What is the recombination frequency?

A) 10%
B) 20%
C) 40%
D) 80%

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 1 map unit = 1% recombination frequency

---

### Question 6
In complementary gene action (9:7 ratio), what genotype produces the recessive phenotype?

A) A_B_ only
B) A_bb, aaB_, and aabb
C) aabb only
D) All genotypes equally

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Sex-linked traits in plants are typically associated with:

A) Nuclear genes only
B) Dioecious species with sex chromosomes
C) All plant species equally
D) Mitochondrial DNA

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
A test cross is performed by crossing an individual with unknown genotype to:

A) Another unknown individual
B) A homozygous dominant individual
C) A homozygous recessive individual
D) A heterozygous individual

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
If two genes show 50% recombination frequency, they are:

A) Tightly linked
B) On different chromosomes or far apart on same chromosome
C) Always on the same chromosome
D) Lethal when combined

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In a cross AaBbCc × AaBbCc with independent assortment, how many different gamete types does each parent produce?

A) 3
B) 4
C) 6
D) 8

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** 2³ = 8 different gamete combinations

---

## Answer Key

1. C - 3:1
2. B - 1:2:1
3. B - 4 phenotypes
4. B - One gene masks the expression of another gene
5. B - 20%
6. B - A_bb, aaB_, and aabb
7. B - Dioecious species with sex chromosomes
8. C - A homozygous recessive individual
9. B - On different chromosomes or far apart on same chromosome
10. D - 8
