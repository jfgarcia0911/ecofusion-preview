## Questions

### Question 1
High-throughput phenotyping platforms use which technologies?

A) Manual measurements only
B) Computer vision, sensors, and automated imaging
C) Traditional rulers and scales only
D) No technology needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) measures:

A) Plant temperature
B) Plant water content and photosynthetic activity
C) Root depth
D) Seed size

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Chlorophyll fluorescence (Fv/Fm) is an indicator of:

A) Plant height
B) Photosystem II efficiency and stress
C) Flower color
D) Seed germination rate

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the PRIMARY advantage of image-based phenotyping?

A) Lower accuracy
B) Non-destructive, high-throughput data collection
C) Requires no equipment
D) Only works outdoors

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
3D imaging can measure plant traits including:

A) DNA sequence
B) Canopy architecture, leaf area, and growth rates
C) Soil nutrients
D) Light spectrum

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Gas exchange measurements using a LI-COR device can quantify:

A) Root biomass
B) Photosynthetic rate, stomatal conductance, and transpiration
C) Flower number
D) Seed protein content

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Root phenotyping in CEA is challenging because:

A) Roots don't exist in CEA
B) Roots are hidden in media; specialized imaging systems needed
C) Roots are identical in all varieties
D) Root traits don't matter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
RGB imaging can be used to estimate:

A) Genetic sequence
B) Plant area, color, and growth rate over time
C) Chromosome number
D) Invisible traits only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Environmental control in phenotyping is critical to:

A) Make plants grow slower
B) Minimize environmental variation and reveal genetic differences
C) Increase costs
D) Confuse the data

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Data management for phenotyping requires:

A) Paper records only
B) Databases linking phenotype, genotype, and environment data
C) No record keeping
D) Memory only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Computer vision, sensors, and automated imaging
2. B - Plant water content and photosynthetic activity
3. B - Photosystem II efficiency and stress
4. B - Non-destructive, high-throughput data collection
5. B - Canopy architecture, leaf area, and growth rates
6. B - Photosynthetic rate, stomatal conductance, and transpiration
7. B - Roots hidden in media; specialized systems needed
8. B - Plant area, color, and growth rate over time
9. B - Minimize environmental variation and reveal genetic differences
10. B - Databases linking phenotype, genotype, and environment
