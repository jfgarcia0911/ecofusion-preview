## Questions

### Question 1
An ideotype is:

A) A perfect plant model describing desired trait combinations
B) A disease
C) A type of seed
D) A regulatory document

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
Germplasm collections provide:

A) No value to breeding
B) Genetic diversity and sources of valuable traits
C) Only ornamental plants
D) Marketing materials

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
In variety testing, how many locations and years are typically recommended?

A) 1 location, 1 year
B) 2-3 locations, 1 year
C) Multiple locations and 2-3 years minimum
D) Testing not necessary

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is the typical timeline from initial cross to commercial variety release?

A) 6 months
B) 1-2 years
C) 5-10 years or more
D) 50 years

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
Statistical analysis of trial data should include:

A) No analysis needed
B) ANOVA, heritability estimates, and G×E analysis
C) Simple averages only
D) Random guessing

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Seed multiplication maintains:

A) Genetic purity and produces commercial quantities
B) Only small samples
C) Unrelated varieties
D) Weed seeds

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 7
For CEA-specific variety trials, environments should include:

A) Only outdoor fields
B) Different LED spectra, systems (NFT, DWC), and climate regimes
C) Only one identical chamber
D) No environmental variation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Breeder seed is:

A) Any commercial seed
B) Foundation seed maintained by breeder with highest genetic purity
C) Weed seeds
D) Expired seed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Multi-location testing reveals:

A) Nothing useful
B) Genotype × Environment interactions and variety stability
C) Only soil types
D) Market prices

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Variety registration and naming require:

A) No documentation
B) Distinctness, Uniformity, and Stability (DUS) testing
C) Random name selection
D) Marketing approval only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. A - A perfect plant model describing desired traits
2. B - Genetic diversity and sources of valuable traits
3. C - Multiple locations and 2-3 years minimum
4. C - 5-10 years or more
5. B - ANOVA, heritability estimates, and G×E analysis
6. A - Genetic purity and commercial quantities
7. B - Different LED spectra, systems, and climates
8. B - Foundation seed with highest genetic purity
9. B - G×E interactions and variety stability
10. B - Distinctness, Uniformity, and Stability testing
