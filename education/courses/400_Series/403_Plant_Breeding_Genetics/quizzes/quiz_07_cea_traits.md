## Questions

### Question 1
What is the ideal plant height for lettuce in a typical vertical farm with 30-40 cm shelf spacing?

A) <15 cm
B) <25 cm
C) <40 cm
D) Height doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
The sp (self-pruning) gene in tomato causes:

A) Indeterminate growth
B) Determinate, compact growth
C) Increased plant height
D) Reduced fruit number

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
If lettuce variety A matures in 35 days and variety B in 28 days, how many more crop cycles per year can be achieved with variety B?

A) 0.5 more
B) 1.0 more
C) 2.0 more
D) 2.6 more

**Correct Answer:** D
**Calculation:** Variety A: 365/35 = 10.4 cycles; Variety B: 365/28 = 13.0 cycles; Difference = 2.6

---

### Question 4
Why is photoperiod insensitivity important for CEA crops?

A) It allows year-round production regardless of photoperiod
B) It increases yield
C) It reduces disease
D) It improves flavor

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
Disease resistance is MORE critical in CEA than field production because:

A) CEA has fewer diseases
B) High humidity, dense planting, and recirculating systems increase disease pressure
C) Pesticides cannot be used in CEA
D) CEA plants are naturally more susceptible

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is gene pyramiding in the context of disease resistance?

A) Growing plants in pyramid structures
B) Combining multiple resistance genes in one variety
C) Selecting the tallest plants
D) Organizing breeding programs hierarchically

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which trait combination is MOST important for lettuce in vertical farms? (Select BEST answer)

A) Tall, slow-growing, heat tolerant
B) Compact, rapid growth, late bolting, disease resistant
C) Large, bitter, early bolting
D) Sprawling, deep roots, drought tolerant

**Correct Answer:** B

### Question 8
LED-optimized photosynthesis breeding focuses on:

A) Maximizing chlorophyll content regardless of efficiency
B) High quantum yield and efficient light use under target LED spectrum
C) Reducing photosynthesis to save energy
D) Only blue light response

**Correct Answer:** B

### Question 9
What is a potential trade-off when selecting for rapid growth?

A) Increased disease resistance
B) Better root systems
C) Compromised flavor, texture, or shelf-life
D) Improved nutritional content

**Correct Answer:** C
