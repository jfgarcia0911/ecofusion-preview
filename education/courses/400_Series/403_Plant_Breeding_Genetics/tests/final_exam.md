## Section 2: Quantitative Genetics (Questions 6-15)

### Question 6
If h² = 0.6 and selection differential (S) = 20 g, what is the expected response to selection (R)?

A) 6 g
B) 12 g
C) 20 g
D) 33.3 g

**Answer:** B (R = h² × S = 0.6 × 20 = 12 g)

---

### Question 7
Narrow-sense heritability (h²) is defined as:

A) VG / VP
B) VA / VP
C) VE / VP
D) (VA + VD) / VP

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
A trait with h² = 0.2 is considered:

A) Highly heritable
B) Moderately heritable
C) Lowly heritable
D) Not heritable at all

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What does a crossover G×E interaction indicate?

A) Variety rankings change across environments

C) No environmental effect

---

### Question 10
To find 5 individuals with three independent recessive traits (each at 25% frequency in F2), approximately how many F2 plants should you grow?

A) 64
B) 100
C) 300
D) 1000

**Answer:** C (frequency = 0.25³ = 1/64, need ~300 to find ~5)
