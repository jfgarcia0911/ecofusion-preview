# Activity 1: Breeding Program Design Exercise

## Design a Complete Breeding Program for CEA Lettuce

**Objective:** Apply course concepts to design a comprehensive breeding program for developing a new lettuce variety optimized for vertical farm production.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Format:** Individual or group (2-3 people)
**Deliverable:** Written breeding program proposal

---

## Background Scenario

**Your Role:** Head Breeder at VertiFresh Seeds Company

**Company Goal:** Develop a new red butterhead lettuce variety specifically for vertical farm production to compete in the premium CEA market.

**Current Market Leader:** 'RedLux' variety
- Fresh weight: 200 g
- Days to harvest: 32
- Plant height: 22 cm
- Color: Medium red
- Shelf-life: 9 days
- Downy mildew: Susceptible
- Retail price: $3.50/head

**Your Target:** Beat 'RedLux' on all or most parameters

---

## Part 1: Ideotype Definition (20 points)

Define your target variety ideotype for vertical farm production.

**Instructions:** Create a detailed specification sheet including:

1. **Plant Architecture:**
   - Maximum height
   - Rosette diameter
   - Leaf number target
   - Compactness index
   - Root system requirements

2. **Growth Parameters:**
   - Days to harvest target
   - Growth rate (g/day)
   - Germination requirements
   - Transplant specifications

3. **Quality Traits:**
   - Fresh weight target
   - Color specifications (under red/blue LED)
   - Texture attributes
   - Shelf-life target
   - Acceptable defects

4. **Resistance/Tolerance:**
   - Priority diseases
   - Environmental stress tolerance
   - Production system compatibility

5. **Production Requirements:**
   - LED spectrum optimization
   - Nutrient efficiency
   - Uniformity standards (CV%)
   - Mechanization compatibility

**Deliverable:** Complete ideotype specification table with justification for each target value.

---

## Part 2: Germplasm Selection (15 points)

Identify parent materials for your breeding program.

**Available Germplasm:**

| Accession | Type | Height | Days | Weight | Color | DM Resistance | Notes |
|-----------|------|--------|------|--------|-------|---------------|-------|
| A-101 | Butterhead | 25 cm | 35 | 220 g | Green | S | Commercial variety |
| A-102 | Butterhead | 20 cm | 30 | 180 g | Deep Red | R (Dm-1) | Compact |
| A-103 | Crisphead | 28 cm | 38 | 280 g | Light Red | S | High yield |
| A-104 | Oakleaf | 18 cm | 28 | 150 g | Medium Red | MR | Very compact, fast |
| A-105 | Wild relative | 15 cm | 45 | 80 g | Green | R (Dm-1, Dm-3) | Multiple resistance |
| A-106 | Butterhead | 24 cm | 32 | 210 g | Deep Red | S | Excellent color |
| A-107 | Breeding line | 22 cm | 29 | 190 g | Red | R (Dm-2) | LED-optimized |
| A-108 | Butterhead | 26 cm | 31 | 200 g | Medium Red | S | Long shelf-life (14 days) |

**Your Task:**
1. Select 3-5 accessions as parents
2. Justify each selection based on traits they contribute
3. Design crossing scheme (which × which)
4. Explain expected trait combinations

**Deliverable:** Parent selection table with rationale and crossing diagram.

---

## Part 3: Breeding Method Selection (20 points)

Choose appropriate breeding method(s) and justify.

**Consider:**
- Pedigree method
- Bulk method
- Single seed descent (SSD)
- Backcross breeding
- Hybrid approach (combining methods)

**Your Task:**
1. Select primary breeding method
2. Justify choice based on:
   - Number of traits to improve
   - Trait heritabilities
   - Time constraints
   - Resources available
   - Need for pedigree information

3. Describe modifications specific to CEA environment

4. Timeline from cross to F6 advanced lines

**Deliverable:** Breeding method description with year-by-year plan.

---

## Part 4: Selection Strategy (25 points)

Design selection criteria and procedures for each generation.

**Template to Complete:**

**F2 Generation:**
- Population size: ________
- Growing conditions: ________
- Traits to select: ________
- Selection intensity: ________%
- Number advanced: ________
- Selection method: (mass, individual, family)

**F3 Generation:**
- Design: (plant-to-row, bulk, etc.)
- Reps/plots: ________
- Traits measured: ________
- Selection criteria: ________
- Number advanced: ________

**Continue for F4, F5, F6...**

**Also Include:**
1. Selection index (if using)
   - Formula with weights
   - Justification for weights

2. Independent culling levels (if using)
   - Minimum standards for each trait
   - Rationale

3. CEA-specific testing protocols
   - Light spectrum for selection
   - Growing system
   - Plant density
   - Environmental parameters

**Deliverable:** Complete generation-by-generation selection plan.

---

## Part 5: Molecular Tools Integration (15 points)

Incorporate molecular markers or genomic tools.

**Available Tools:**
- Dm-1, Dm-2, Dm-3 resistance gene markers
- QTL markers for shelf-life (2 QTL identified)
- SNP panel (200 markers) for background selection
- Budget: $10,000 for molecular work

**Your Task:**
1. Decide which molecular tools to use and when
2. Specify:
   - Marker-assisted selection strategy
   - Generations to implement MAS
   - Expected benefits vs. cost
   - How to integrate with phenotypic selection

3. If doing backcross: Design marker-assisted backcrossing strategy

**Deliverable:** Molecular breeding strategy with cost-benefit analysis.

---

## Part 6: Testing and Evaluation (15 points)

Design multi-environment trial plan for advanced lines.

**Specify:**

**Preliminary Trials (Year X):**
- Number of lines: ________
- Locations: ________
- Environments/seasons: ________
- Reps: ________
- Design: (RCBD, augmented, etc.)
- Traits measured: ________

**Advanced Trials (Year X+1):**
- Lines: ________
- Expanded testing locations
- Additional environments
- On-farm validation

**Pre-Commercial (Year X+2):**
- Final candidates: ________
- Commercial-scale trials
- Grower cooperators
- Market testing

**Statistical Analysis:**
- ANOVA model
- Stability analysis approach
- Selection criteria

**Deliverable:** Complete trial design with analysis plan.

---

## Part 7: IP Protection and Release (10 points)

Plan variety protection and commercialization.

**Address:**
1. **Intellectual Property:**
   - Apply for PVP? Utility patent? Both?
   - Rationale for choice
   - Timeline for application

2. **Variety Naming:**
   - Propose 3 variety names
   - Check against guidelines

3. **Seed Production:**
   - Breeder seed maintenance plan
   - Multiplication classes
   - Quality standards
   - Projected volumes

4. **Market Strategy:**
   - Target customers
   - Pricing strategy
   - Differentiation from 'RedLux'
   - Marketing message

**Deliverable:** Commercialization plan.

---

## Submission Format

**Written Report Structure:**

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Program overview
   - Timeline
   - Expected outcome

2. **Detailed Breeding Plan** (8-12 pages)
   - All 7 parts addressed
   - Tables, diagrams, flowcharts
   - Justifications and rationale

3. **Timeline Gantt Chart** (1 page)
   - Year-by-year activities
   - Milestones
   - Decision points

4. **Budget Estimate** (1 page)
   - Personnel
   - Facilities/land
   - Molecular work
   - Trials
   - Total cost

5. **Risk Assessment** (1 page)
   - Potential challenges
   - Contingency plans
   - Alternative approaches

*(Continue for all parameters...)*

**Example Cross:**
```
Cross 1: A-102 (Compact, Dm-1) × A-107 (Fast, Dm-2, LED-opt)
Objective: Combine compactness, speed, LED optimization, multiple DM resistance

Cross 2: A-106 (Deep red) × A-108 (Shelf-life)

Subsequent: Best from Cross 1 × Best from Cross 2
Final pyramid: All desired traits
```
