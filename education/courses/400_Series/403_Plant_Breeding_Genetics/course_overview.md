# Course 403: Plant Breeding & Genetics for CEA

## Course Level
**Expert / Professional (400-Level)**

## Course Description

This advanced course provides comprehensive training in plant genetics and breeding specifically optimized for controlled environment agriculture (CEA). Students will master the principles of classical and molecular genetics, quantitative trait analysis, and modern breeding techniques including marker-assisted selection and genomic approaches. The course emphasizes practical application of breeding strategies to develop crop varieties with superior performance under LED lighting, vertical farm conditions, and other CEA environments.

Participants will gain expertise in selection strategies for CEA-specific traits such as compact architecture, rapid growth, enhanced photosynthetic efficiency under artificial lighting, and nutritional quality optimization. The curriculum integrates traditional breeding methods with cutting-edge technologies including tissue culture, molecular markers, and an introduction to CRISPR-based gene editing.

## Target Audience

- Plant breeders transitioning to CEA environments
- CEA facility managers seeking to optimize variety selection
- Agricultural biotechnology professionals
- Research scientists in controlled environment agriculture
- Advanced horticulture specialists
- Seed company professionals developing CEA varieties

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Explain** the fundamental principles of Mendelian and quantitative genetics as applied to crop improvement
2. **Analyze** genetic inheritance patterns and calculate heritability of key agricultural traits
3. **Design** breeding programs specifically optimized for CEA environments
4. **Evaluate** germplasm and select parent lines for hybrid and variety development
5. **Apply** molecular markers and genomic tools for marker-assisted selection
6. **Implement** tissue culture and micropropagation techniques for rapid variety multiplication
7. **Develop** phenotyping protocols for assessing plant performance in controlled environments
8. **Create** selection strategies for CEA-specific traits including light use efficiency, compact growth, and rapid cycling
9. **Understand** intellectual property considerations including plant patents and PVP certificates
10. **Assess** emerging technologies including CRISPR and gene editing for crop improvement
11. **Integrate** breeding objectives with facility design parameters and production economics
12. **Execute** complete variety development pipelines from concept to commercialization

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Fundamentals of Plant Genetics for CEA
- DNA structure and gene expression
- Cell division: mitosis and meiosis
- Chromosome structure and behavior
- Genetic variation and mutation
- CEA-relevant genetic concepts
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 2: Mendelian Inheritance and Gene Interactions
- Mendel's laws and monohybrid crosses
- Dihybrid and polyhybrid crosses
- Gene interactions and epistasis
- Linkage and recombination
- Sex-linked traits
- Pedigree analysis
- **Duration**: 3.5 hours

### Module 3: Quantitative Genetics and Trait Analysis
- Continuous vs. discontinuous variation
- Polygenic inheritance
- Heritability concepts (broad-sense and narrow-sense)
- Genotype × environment interactions in CEA
- Variance components
- Response to selection
- **Duration**: 4 hours

### Module 4: Molecular Markers and Genomics
- Types of molecular markers (RFLP, RAPD, SSR, SNP)
- Marker development and validation
- Genetic mapping and QTL analysis
- Genome-wide association studies (GWAS)
- Genomic selection
- Bioinformatics tools for breeding
- **Duration**: 4 hours

### Module 5: Traditional Breeding Methods
- Mass selection and pure line selection
- Pedigree breeding method
- Bulk population method
- Single seed descent (SSD)
- Backcross breeding
- Recurrent selection
- **Duration**: 3.5 hours

### Module 6: Hybrid Breeding and Heterosis
- Genetic basis of heterosis
- Hybrid vigor in CEA crops
- Inbred line development
- Male sterility systems
- Hybrid seed production
- Economic considerations
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 7: Selection Strategies for CEA Traits
- Compact plant architecture (dwarfing genes)
- Photoperiod insensitivity
- Light use efficiency under LEDs
- Rapid growth and short cycle time
- Enhanced nutritional quality
- Disease resistance in high-density systems
- Root system optimization
- **Duration**: 4 hours

### Module 8: Phenotyping in Controlled Environments
- High-throughput phenotyping platforms
- Image analysis and computer vision
- Chlorophyll fluorescence and gas exchange
- Spectral reflectance indices
- Root phenotyping techniques
- Data management and analysis
- Environmental control for phenotyping
- **Duration**: 3.5 hours

### Module 9: Tissue Culture and Micropropagation
- Micropropagation techniques
- Somatic embryogenesis
- Meristem culture and virus elimination
- Protoplast culture and somatic hybridization
- Doubled haploid production
- Genetic transformation basics
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 10: Marker-Assisted Selection (MAS)
- MAS principles and applications
- Foreground and background selection
- Gene pyramiding
- Marker-assisted backcrossing (MABC)
- Cost-benefit analysis of MAS
- Integration into breeding programs
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 11: CRISPR and Gene Editing Technologies
- CRISPR/Cas9 mechanism and applications
- Gene knockout vs. gene editing
- Multiplexed editing for trait stacking
- Regulatory landscape for gene-edited crops
- Ethical considerations
- Applications in CEA crop improvement
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 12: Variety Development Pipeline
- Ideotype development for CEA environments
- Germplasm collection and evaluation
- Crossing and population development
- Multi-location and multi-season trials in CEA
- Data analysis and variety selection
- Seed multiplication and purification
- **Duration**: 3 hours

### Module 13: Intellectual Property and Plant Patents
- Plant Variety Protection (PVP) certificates
- Utility patents for plants
- Essentially derived varieties (EDV)
- Licensing and royalty structures
- Open-source breeding initiatives
- International treaties (UPOV)
- **Duration**: 2.5 hours

### Module 14: Breeding for Specific CEA Environments
- LED spectrum optimization through breeding
- Vertical farm architecture considerations
- Aquaponic and hydroponic system compatibility
- High-density production traits
- Shelf-life and post-harvest quality
- Case studies: leafy greens, herbs, tomatoes, strawberries
- Future directions in CEA breeding
- **Duration**: 3 hours
