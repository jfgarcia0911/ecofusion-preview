# Technical Glossary

## Course 402: Advanced Aquaculture Systems

**Comprehensive Reference of Aquaculture and RAS Terminology**

---

## A

**Acclimation** - Gradual adjustment of fish to new water conditions (temperature, salinity, pH) to minimize stress.

**Aerobic** - Requiring oxygen; aerobic bacteria in biofilters need dissolved oxygen to function.

**Aeration** - Process of adding air to water to increase dissolved oxygen and remove carbon dioxide.

**Alkalinity** - Water's capacity to neutralize acid; measured as mg/L CaCO₃; critical for pH buffering in RAS.

**Ammonia (NH₃/NH₄⁺)** - Toxic nitrogenous waste excreted by fish; exists as toxic unionized (NH₃) or less toxic ionized (NH₄⁺) forms.

**Anaerobic** - Absence of oxygen; anaerobic conditions occur in denitrification reactors.

**AOB (Ammonia Oxidizing Bacteria)** - Bacteria that convert ammonia to nitrite (Nitrosomonas, Nitrosospira).

**Aquaponics** - Integration of aquaculture with hydroponic plant production, utilizing fish waste as plant nutrients.

---

## B

**Backup System** - Redundant equipment to prevent catastrophic failure; essential for RAS (generators, pumps, blowers).

**Bead Filter** - Floating plastic bead media filter providing both solids removal and biofiltration; requires periodic backwashing.

**Biofilm** - Layer of bacteria growing on surfaces in biofilters; performs nitrification.

**Biofilter** - System component housing beneficial bacteria that convert toxic ammonia to less toxic compounds.

**Biofloc** - Suspended aggregates of bacteria, algae, and organic matter in water; used in some culture systems.

**Biomass** - Total weight of fish in system; typically expressed as kg.

**Biosecurity** - Practices to prevent disease introduction and spread; includes quarantine, disinfection, and access control.

**BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)** - Amount of oxygen required to decompose organic matter; indicator of organic loading.

**Broodstock** - Adult fish maintained for reproduction; genetically superior individuals selected for breeding.

---

## C

**CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)** - Initial investment costs for facility construction and equipment.

**Carrying Capacity** - Maximum fish biomass a system can support based on water treatment capacity.

**Cohort** - Group of fish of similar age/size stocked together.

**COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)** - Total oxygen required to oxidize organic and inorganic matter in water.

**Conductivity** - Measure of water's ability to conduct electricity; indicates dissolved ion concentration.

**Culture Density** - Fish biomass per unit volume; expressed as kg/m³.

---

## D

**Degassing** - Removal of dissolved gases (primarily CO₂) from water; achieved via aeration, cascades, or packed columns.

**Denitrification** - Anaerobic biological process converting nitrate (NO₃⁻) to nitrogen gas (N₂).

**DHA (Docosahexaenoic Acid)** - Essential omega-3 fatty acid (22:6 n-3); critical for marine fish nutrition.

**Digestibility** - Percentage of feed absorbed by fish; affects FCR and waste production.

**Disinfection** - Killing or inactivating pathogens; methods include UV, ozone, chlorine, heat.

**DO (Dissolved Oxygen)** - Amount of oxygen dissolved in water; measured in mg/L or % saturation; most critical water quality parameter.

**Drum Filter** - Rotary microscreen filter continuously removing suspended solids; mesh sizes 40-200 microns.

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)** - EBITDA divided by annual debt payment; lenders typically require >1.25.

---

## E

**EBITDA** - Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization; measure of operating profitability.

**Effluent** - Water discharged from system.

**EPA (Eicosapentaenoic Acid)** - Essential omega-3 fatty acid (20:5 n-3); important for fish health.

**Exophthalmia** - "Pop-eye"; protrusion of eyes due to disease, gas supersaturation, or poor water quality.

---

## F

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** - Feed required to produce unit weight gain; FCR = Feed Given / Weight Gain; lower is better.

**Fingerling** - Young fish, typically 1-10g; between fry and juvenile stages.

**Fluidized Bed** - Biofilter where media is suspended by upward water flow; high loading capacity.

**Foam Fractionation** - Process using fine bubbles to remove dissolved organics; protein skimmer.

**Formalin** - Formaldehyde solution used to treat external parasites; 25-50 ppm typical dose.

**Fry** - Newly hatched fish after yolk-sac absorption; very small (0.01-1g).

---

## G

**Gas Bubble Disease** - Condition caused by gas supersaturation (>110%); bubbles form in tissues and blood.

**Genetic Gain** - Improvement in trait value per generation through selective breeding.

**GnRHa (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone analog)** - Hormone used to induce spawning in fish.

**Grading** - Sorting fish by size to reduce variation; improves feeding efficiency and reduces cannibalism.

**Gynogenesis** - Production of all-female offspring through genetic manipulation.

---

## H

**Hatchery** - Facility for breeding fish, incubating eggs, and rearing larvae to fingerling stage.

**Head Loss** - Pressure drop as water flows through pipes and equipment; measured in meters or feet.

**Heritability (h²)** - Proportion of phenotypic variation due to genetics; ranges 0-1; affects breeding response.

**HMI (Human-Machine Interface)** - Touchscreen or computer interface for controlling and monitoring RAS systems.

**Hydraulic Loading** - Water flow rate per unit filter area; m³/m²/hr.

**Hybrid** - Offspring from crosses between different species or strains.

---

## I

**IMTA (Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture)** - Combining species from different trophic levels to recycle nutrients.

**Inbreeding** - Mating of related individuals; causes inbreeding depression (reduced performance).

**Influent** - Water entering a treatment component.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)** - Discount rate making NPV = 0; measures project profitability.

---

## K

**KLa (Mass Transfer Coefficient)** - Measure of oxygen transfer rate in aeration systems; higher is better.

---

## L

**Larva (plural: Larvae)** - Early life stage between hatching and juvenile; often requires live feed.

**LHO (Low Head Oxygenator)** - Packed column aerator/oxygenator with low pressure drop; efficient for O₂ addition and CO₂ removal.

**Loading Rate** - Amount of waste (TAN, feed, solids) applied to treatment component per unit time; critical for sizing.

---

## M

**Make-Up Water** - Fresh water added to replace losses from evaporation, backwashing, and system cleaning.

**MAS (Motile Aeromonas Septicemia)** - Common bacterial disease caused by Aeromonas spp.

**MBBR (Moving Bed Bioreactor)** - Biofilter using free-floating plastic media kept in suspension by aeration.

**Methemoglobin** - Oxidized hemoglobin that cannot carry oxygen; caused by nitrite toxicity ("brown blood disease").

**Micron (μm)** - Unit of length = 1/1000 millimeter; used for filter mesh size and particle size.

**Monosex** - Population of single sex (all male or all female); achieved through hormones or genetics.

---

## N

**Ne (Effective Population Size)** - Number of breeding individuals contributing to next generation; affects genetic diversity.

**Nitrification** - Two-step process converting ammonia → nitrite → nitrate via bacteria.

**Nitrite (NO₂⁻)** - Intermediate product of nitrification; toxic to fish; causes methemoglobinemia.

**Nitrate (NO₃⁻)** - End product of nitrification; relatively non-toxic; accumulates in RAS.

**NOB (Nitrite Oxidizing Bacteria)** - Bacteria converting nitrite to nitrate (Nitrobacter, Nitrospira).

**NPV (Net Present Value)** - Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment; positive NPV = good investment.

---

## O

**OPEX (Operating Expenditure)** - Ongoing costs to operate facility; feed, labor, energy, etc.

**ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential)** - Measure of water's oxidizing/reducing capacity; measured in mV; indicator of disinfection.

**OTE (Oxygen Transfer Efficiency)** - Percentage of supplied oxygen that dissolves; LHO: 60-90%, diffusers: 2-8%.

**Ozone (O₃)** - Powerful oxidizer used for disinfection and organic removal; toxic to fish and biofilter; must be removed.

**Oxygenation** - Addition of pure oxygen to water; distinct from aeration (air addition).

---

## P

**Parallel Flow** - RAS configuration where all tanks receive equally treated water.

**Pathogen** - Disease-causing organism (bacteria, virus, parasite, fungus).

**Pellet** - Compressed feed particle; characterized by size, density (floating/sinking), stability.

**pH** - Measure of acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale; 7 = neutral; <7 = acidic; >7 = basic.

**Photoperiod** - Day length (hours of light); manipulated to control reproduction and growth.

**PIT Tag** - Passive Integrated Transponder; injectable microchip for individual fish identification.

**PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)** - Computer-based controller for automation; backbone of RAS control systems.

**Polyculture** - Culturing multiple species together.

**PSA (Pressure Swing Adsorption)** - Technology for on-site oxygen generation from air.

---

## Q

**Quarantine** - Isolation of new fish for 30-60 days to prevent disease introduction.

**QTL (Quantitative Trait Locus)** - Genomic region associated with quantitative trait; used in marker-assisted selection.

---

## R

**RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System)** - Intensive aquaculture system reusing water through continuous treatment.

**Redox** - See ORP.

**Retention Time** - Average time water spends in system component; calculated as Volume/Flow Rate.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - (Profit / Investment) × 100; annual percentage return.

---

## S

**Salinity** - Concentration of dissolved salts in water; measured in ppt (parts per thousand) or g/L.

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)** - Computer system for monitoring and controlling industrial processes.

**Serial Flow** - RAS configuration where water flows sequentially through tanks.

**SGR (Specific Growth Rate)** - Daily growth rate as percentage; SGR = [(ln W₂ - ln W₁) / Days] × 100.

**SOTR (Standard Oxygen Transfer Rate)** - Oxygen transfer under standard conditions (20°C, 0 mg/L DO, 1 atm).

**Spawning** - Reproductive process; release of eggs and sperm.

**Stocking Density** - See Culture Density.

**Supersaturation** - Dissolved gas concentration >100% saturation; can cause gas bubble disease.

**Survival Rate** - Percentage of stocked fish surviving to harvest.

**Swirl Separator** - Cone-shaped tank using centrifugal force to settle solids.

---

## T

**TAN (Total Ammonia Nitrogen)** - Sum of NH₃ and NH₄⁺; total ammonia concentration.

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Total dissolved substances in water; measured via conductivity or evaporation.

**Theront** - Free-swimming infective stage of Ichthyophthirius (Ich); vulnerable to treatment.

**Triploid** - Fish with three sets of chromosomes instead of two; typically sterile; produced via pressure or heat shock.

**Trophic Level** - Position in food chain; used in IMTA planning.

**TSS (Total Suspended Solids)** - Solid particles suspended in water; measured by filtering and weighing; target <10 mg/L in RAS.

**Turnover Rate** - Number of times system volume passes through treatment per unit time; typical: 0.5-2 per hour.

---

## U

**Unionized Ammonia (NH₃)** - Toxic form of ammonia; percentage increases with pH and temperature.

**UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)** - Battery backup for critical equipment during power outages.

**UV (Ultraviolet) Sterilization** - Use of UV-C light (254 nm) to inactivate pathogens; dose: 30-100 mJ/cm².

**UVT (UV Transmittance)** - Percentage of UV light passing through water; >75% needed for effective disinfection.

---

## V

**Venturi** - Device using water velocity to create vacuum; draws air or oxygen into water stream.

**Vitellogenesis** - Yolk formation in eggs; requires high-quality broodstock nutrition.

---

## W

**Water Exchange Rate** - Percentage of system volume replaced daily; RAS: 3-7%, flow-through: 100%+.

**Withdrawal Period** - Time between last treatment and safe harvest for human consumption; drug-specific.

---

## X

**XY Chromosomes** - Sex determination system in many fish; XX = female, XY = male (varies by species).

---

## Y

**YY Males** - "Super males" with YY chromosomes; produce all-male offspring when bred with XX females.

**Yolk-Sac** - Nutrient reserve in newly hatched larvae; absorbed over 3-14 days depending on species.

---

## Z

**Zooplankton** - Microscopic animals in water; includes rotifers, copepods, Artemia (brine shrimp); used as live feed for larvae.

## Units of Measurement

### Volume
- 1 m³ = 1,000 liters = 264.2 gallons
- 1 gallon = 3.785 liters

### Mass
- 1 kg = 2.205 pounds
- 1 tonne (metric ton) = 1,000 kg = 2,205 pounds

### Concentration
- 1 mg/L = 1 ppm (parts per million)
- 1 g/L = 1,000 mg/L = 1 kg/m³
- 1 ppt (parts per thousand) = 1 g/L = 1,000 mg/L

### Flow
- 1 m³/hr = 16.67 L/min = 4.4 gpm
- 1 gpm (gallon per minute) = 3.785 L/min
- 1 CFM (cubic feet per minute) = 28.3 L/min

### Power
- 1 kW = 1.34 horsepower
- 1 hp = 0.746 kW

### Temperature
- °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32
- °C = (°F - 32) / 1.8

## Key Equations Reference

### Biofilter Sizing
```
Surface Area (m²) = TAN production (g/day) / Loading rate (g/m²/day)
```

### Oxygen Demand
```
O₂ (kg/day) = Feed (kg/day) × 0.5
```

### FCR
```
FCR = Feed given (kg) / Weight gain (kg)
```

### SGR
```
SGR (%/day) = [(ln W₂ - ln W₁) / Days] × 100
```

### Flow Rate
```
Q (m³/hr) = Volume (m³) / Retention Time (hr)
```

### Break-Even
```
BE volume = Fixed Costs / (Price/kg - Variable Cost/kg)
```
