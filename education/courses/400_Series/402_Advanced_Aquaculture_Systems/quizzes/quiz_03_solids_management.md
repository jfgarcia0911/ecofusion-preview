### Questions

**1. What particle size range is considered "settleable solids"?**

A) <10 microns
B) 10-50 microns
C) >50-100 microns
D) >100 microns

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**2. What is the PRIMARY purpose of a swirl separator (radial flow settler)?**

A) Aeration
B) Remove settleable solids through centrifugal force
C) Biological filtration
D) pH adjustment

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. A system produces 100 kg feed/day with 30% solid waste production. What is daily solid waste generation?**

A) 10 kg
B) 20 kg
C) 30 kg
D) 50 kg

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: 100 kg × 0.30 = 30 kg solid waste/day

---

**4. Drum filters (microscreen filters) typically capture particles down to what size?**

A) 10-20 microns
B) 40-100 microns
C) 200-500 microns
D) >1,000 microns

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**5. What is the recommended Total Suspended Solids (TSS) level in well-managed RAS?**

A) <5-10 mg/L
B) 20-40 mg/L
C) 50-100 mg/L
D) >200 mg/L

**Correct Answer: A**

---

**6. Foam fractionation (protein skimming) is MOST effective for removing:**

A) Settleable solids only
B) Dissolved and colloidal organic matter
C) Ammonia
D) Heavy metals

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. What is the typical solids capture efficiency of a well-designed swirl separator?**

A) 20-30%
B) 40-60%
C) 70-90%
D) 99-100%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**8. Sludge dewatering using belt presses or centrifuges achieves what typical dry matter content?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-25%
C) 30-40%
D) 80-90%

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**9. What beneficial reuse application is MOST common for RAS sludge?**

A) Direct discharge to waterways
B) Composting or land application as fertilizer
C) Incineration only
D) No reuse possible

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. In a multi-stage solids removal system, components should be arranged in what order?**

A) Fine filter → Coarse filter → Biofilter
B) Swirl separator → Drum filter → Foam fractionator
C) Random order is acceptable
D) Only one stage needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - >50-100 microns
2. B - Remove settleable solids through centrifugal force
3. C - 30 kg
4. B - 40-100 microns
5. A - <5-10 mg/L
6. B - Dissolved and colloidal organic matter
7. C - 70-90%
8. B - 15-25%
9. B - Composting or land application as fertilizer
10. B - Swirl separator → Drum filter → Foam fractionator
