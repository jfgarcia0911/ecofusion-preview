### Questions

**1. What is the PRIMARY purpose of broodstock conditioning?**

A) Prepare fish for market
B) Synchronize spawning and optimize gamete quality
C) Reduce feeding costs
D) Eliminate all males

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**2. Photoperiod manipulation typically involves:**

A) Keeping constant light 24/7
B) Simulating seasonal light cycles to trigger reproduction
C) Eliminating all light
D) Random lighting schedules

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. Which hormone is commonly used for induced spawning in finfish?**

A) Insulin
B) HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) or GnRH analogs
C) Adrenaline
D) Cortisol

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**4. What is the typical fertilization rate goal in a well-managed hatchery?**

A) 10-20%
B) 40-60%
C) >80-90%
D) 100% always achievable

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**5. Egg disinfection is performed using:**

A) Soap and water
B) Iodine solution or similar approved disinfectants
C) Ammonia
D) No disinfection needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**6. What water quality parameter is MOST critical during egg incubation?**

A) Salinity
B) Dissolved oxygen (near saturation) and temperature stability
C) High ammonia levels
D) Turbidity

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. Larval fish require what type of initial feed?**

A) Adult fish pellets
B) Live foods (rotifers, artemia) or specialized larval diets
C) Vegetables
D) No food for first 30 days

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. A female produces 100,000 eggs with 85% fertilization and 70% hatch rate. How many larvae result?**

A) 49,500
B) 59,500
C) 70,000
D) 85,000

**Correct Answer: B**

Explanation: 100,000 × 0.85 × 0.70 = 59,500 larvae

---

**9. What is the purpose of "green water" technique in larval rearing?**

A) Aesthetic appeal
B) Algae provide nutrition and water quality stabilization
C) pH adjustment only
D) Waste removal

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. Optimal spawning sex ratio for most finfish is typically:**

A) 10 males : 1 female
B) 1 male : 1 female
C) 1 male : 2-3 females (or species-specific)
D) 1 male : 100 females

**Correct Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Synchronize spawning and optimize gamete quality
2. B - Simulating seasonal light cycles to trigger reproduction
3. B - HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) or GnRH analogs
4. C - >80-90%
5. B - Iodine solution or similar approved disinfectants
6. B - Dissolved oxygen (near saturation) and temperature stability
7. B - Live foods (rotifers, artemia) or specialized larval diets
8. B - 59,500
9. B - Algae provide nutrition and water quality stabilization
10. C - 1 male : 2-3 females (or species-specific)
