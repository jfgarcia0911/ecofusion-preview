### Questions

**1. What is the minimum dissolved oxygen (DO) level for most finfish species?**

A) 1-2 mg/L
B) 3-4 mg/L
C) 5-6 mg/L
D) 10-12 mg/L

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**2. A system has 1,000 kg of fish consuming 2% body weight in feed daily. Oxygen demand is approximately 0.25 kg O₂/kg feed. What is daily O₂ requirement?**

A) 2.5 kg O₂
B) 5 kg O₂
C) 10 kg O₂
D) 20 kg O₂

**Correct Answer: B**

Explanation: Feed = 1,000 kg × 0.02 = 20 kg/day; O₂ = 20 kg × 0.25 = 5 kg O₂/day

---

**3. What does OTE stand for in oxygenation systems?**

A) Optimal Temperature Efficiency
B) Oxygen Transfer Efficiency
C) Operational Time Estimate
D) Overall Tank Environment

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**4. Low Head Oxygenators (LHO) typically achieve what OTE?**

A) 20-40%
B) 50-70%
C) 80-95%
D) 99-100%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**5. What is the PRIMARY advantage of pure oxygen injection systems?**

A) Lower cost than air
B) Higher oxygen transfer rates and efficiency
C) Eliminates need for monitoring
D) Reduces fish growth

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**6. Excessive dissolved CO₂ levels (>20-30 mg/L) can cause:**

A) Increased growth rates
B) Respiratory stress and acidosis in fish
C) Improved feed conversion
D) No negative effects

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. Which degassing method is MOST effective for CO₂ removal?**

A) Submerged diffusers
B) Cascade aeration or packed column degasser
C) Oxygen cones
D) Heat exchange

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. A system requires 100 kg O₂/day. Using LOX (liquid oxygen) with 90% transfer efficiency, how much LOX is needed?**

A) 90 kg
B) 100 kg
C) 111 kg
D) 150 kg

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: 100 kg ÷ 0.90 = 111 kg LOX required

---

**9. What is the typical ratio of CO₂ produced to O₂ consumed in fish respiration?**

A) 0.5:1
B) 0.8-1.0:1
C) 2:1
D) 5:1

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. Emergency oxygen backup systems should provide a minimum of how many hours of oxygen supply?**

A) 30 minutes
B) 2-4 hours
C) 12-24 hours
D) 7 days

**Correct Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - 5-6 mg/L
2. B - 5 kg O₂
3. B - Oxygen Transfer Efficiency
4. C - 80-95%
5. B - Higher oxygen transfer rates and efficiency
6. B - Respiratory stress and acidosis in fish
7. B - Cascade aeration or packed column degasser
8. C - 111 kg
9. B - 0.8-1.0:1
10. C - 12-24 hours
