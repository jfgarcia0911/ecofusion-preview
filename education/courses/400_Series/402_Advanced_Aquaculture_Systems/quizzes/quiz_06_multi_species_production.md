### Questions

**1. What is Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA)?**

A) Growing multiple species that don't interact
B) Combining species from different trophic levels to utilize waste products
C) Rotating species seasonally
D) Growing only herbivorous species

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**2. Which species combination is an example of compatible polyculture?**

A) Multiple aggressive carnivorous species
B) Tilapia (herbivore) + Catfish (bottom feeder) + Shrimp (detritivore)
C) Only same species at different sizes
D) Species with identical environmental requirements

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. What is the PRIMARY benefit of multi-species production?**

A) Increased system complexity only
B) Risk diversification and improved resource utilization
C) Higher labor requirements
D) Reduced total production

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**4. In a tiered trophic system, effluent from carnivorous fish tanks can be used to grow:**

A) More carnivorous fish
B) Filter-feeding organisms or plants
C) Nothing - must be discarded
D) Only bacteria

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**5. What factor is MOST important when selecting species for polyculture?**

A) All species must be same color
B) Compatible environmental requirements (temp, pH, DO)
C) All species must grow at same rate
D) Species origin location

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**6. Shrimp are beneficial in fish polyculture systems because they:**

A) Increase ammonia production
B) Consume uneaten feed and detritus
C) Require no oxygen
D) Eliminate all diseases

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. A system produces 100 kg fish and 20 kg shrimp. What is the total market diversification benefit?**

A) Single product dependency
B) Multiple market channels and price risk reduction
C) No benefit
D) Increased risk only

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. What challenge is MOST common in multi-species systems?**

A) Too simple to manage
B) Balancing different feeding strategies and environmental needs
C) Excessive profits
D) No challenges exist

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**9. Stocking density in polyculture should consider:**

A) Only the primary species
B) Combined biomass of all species and system carrying capacity
C) Aesthetic appearance only
D) Random stocking acceptable

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. Which aquatic plant is commonly integrated with fish production for nutrient uptake?**

A) Oak trees
B) Seaweed, duckweed, or water spinach
C) Terrestrial grasses
D) No plants compatible

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Combining species from different trophic levels to utilize waste products
2. B - Tilapia (herbivore) + Catfish (bottom feeder) + Shrimp (detritivore)
3. B - Risk diversification and improved resource utilization
4. B - Filter-feeding organisms or plants
5. B - Compatible environmental requirements (temp, pH, DO)
6. B - Consume uneaten feed and detritus
7. B - Multiple market channels and price risk reduction
8. B - Balancing different feeding strategies and environmental needs
9. B - Combined biomass of all species and system carrying capacity
10. B - Seaweed, duckweed, or water spinach
