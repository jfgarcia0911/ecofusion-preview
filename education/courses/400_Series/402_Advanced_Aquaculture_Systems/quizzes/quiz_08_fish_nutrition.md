### Questions

**1. What is the typical protein requirement for carnivorous finfish feeds?**

A) 10-20%
B) 25-32%
C) 38-50%
D) 60-80%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**2. What does FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) measure?**

A) Feed quality only
B) kg of feed required to produce 1 kg of fish weight gain
C) Fish color
D) Water temperature

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. A system uses 1,000 kg feed and produces 800 kg fish growth. What is the FCR?**

A) 0.8
B) 1.0
C) 1.25
D) 2.0

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: FCR = 1,000 kg feed ÷ 800 kg growth = 1.25

---

**4. Which essential fatty acids are critical for fish health?**

A) Saturated fats only
B) EPA (Eicosapentaenoic acid) and DHA (Docosahexaenoic acid)
C) Trans fats
D) No fatty acids needed

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**5. What is the PRIMARY challenge with high-protein diets in RAS?**

A) Improved growth rates
B) Increased nitrogen waste production
C) Lower costs
D) Better feed stability

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**6. Vitamin C deficiency in fish causes:**

A) Increased growth
B) Skeletal deformities and impaired immune function
C) No symptoms
D) Excess energy

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. What feeding rate (% body weight/day) is typical for growout finfish?**

A) 0.1-0.5%
B) 1-3%
C) 10-15%
D) 25-30%

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. Feed pellet water stability is important because:**

A) Aesthetic reasons only
B) Prevents nutrient leaching and maintains water quality
C) Makes feed more expensive
D) Not important in RAS

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**9. Which alternative protein source is increasingly used to replace fishmeal?**

A) Sand
B) Insect meal, algae meal, or plant proteins
C) Plastic
D) Mineral supplements only

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. Proper feed storage requires:**

A) Direct sunlight exposure
B) Cool, dry conditions with low humidity to prevent oxidation
C) Outdoor uncovered storage
D) High heat and moisture

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - 38-50%
2. B - kg of feed required to produce 1 kg of fish weight gain
3. C - 1.25
4. B - EPA and DHA
5. B - Increased nitrogen waste production
6. B - Skeletal deformities and impaired immune function
7. B - 1-3%
8. B - Prevents nutrient leaching and maintains water quality
9. B - Insect meal, algae meal, or plant proteins
10. B - Cool, dry conditions with low humidity to prevent oxidation
