### Questions

**1. What is the primary function of nitrifying bacteria in a biofilter?**

A) Convert ammonia to oxygen
B) Convert ammonia to nitrite, then nitrite to nitrate
C) Remove solid waste
D) Produce beneficial algae

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**2. What is the optimal pH range for nitrification in RAS biofilters?**

A) 5.0-6.0
B) 6.5-8.5
C) 9.0-10.0
D) pH doesn't affect nitrification

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. A system produces 5 kg TAN/day. With a biofilter nitrification rate of 0.5 kg TAN/m³/day, what biofilter volume is required?**

A) 2.5 m³
B) 5 m³
C) 10 m³
D) 15 m³

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: 5 kg TAN/day ÷ 0.5 kg/m³/day = 10 m³

---

**4. Which biofilter type has the HIGHEST surface area per unit volume?**

A) Trickling filter with 2-inch media
B) Moving Bed Bioreactor (MBBR)
C) Fluidized sand bed
D) Simple gravel bed

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**5. What is the typical surface area requirement for nitrification media?**

A) 10-50 m²/m³
B) 100-300 m²/m³
C) 500-1,200 m²/m³
D) 2,000-5,000 m²/m³

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**6. During biofilter start-up, how long does it typically take to establish full nitrification capacity?**

A) 1-3 days
B) 1-2 weeks
C) 3-6 weeks
D) 6-12 months

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**7. What causes a "biofilter crash" (sudden loss of nitrification)?**

A) pH drop below 6.0, antibiotic exposure, or oxygen depletion
B) Too much oxygen
C) Excessive feeding only
D) Temperature increase

**Correct Answer: A**

---

**8. MBBR systems typically fill what percentage of reactor volume with biofilm carriers?**

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50-70%
D) 90-100%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**9. What is the PRIMARY purpose of denitrification in advanced RAS?**

A) Convert nitrate (NO3-) to nitrogen gas
B) Increase nitrate levels
C) Remove phosphorus
D) Kill bacteria

**Correct Answer: A**

---

**10. Optimal temperature range for nitrifying bacteria is:**

A) 10-15°C
B) 20-30°C
C) 35-40°C
D) Temperature has no effect

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Convert ammonia to nitrite, then nitrite to nitrate
2. B - 6.5-8.5
3. C - 10 m³
4. C - Fluidized sand bed
5. C - 500-1,200 m²/m³
6. C - 3-6 weeks
7. A - pH drop below 6.0, antibiotic exposure, or oxygen depletion
8. C - 50-70%
9. A - Convert nitrate (NO3-) to nitrogen gas
10. B - 20-30°C
