### Questions

**1. What is the toxic form of nitrogen in aquaculture?**

A) Nitrate (NO₃⁻)
B) Nitrite (NO₂⁻) and Un-ionized Ammonia (NH₃)
C) Nitrogen gas (N₂)
D) Protein

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**2. At pH 8.0 and 25°C, what approximate percentage of Total Ammonia Nitrogen is toxic un-ionized ammonia (NH₃)?**

A) 2%
B) 5%
C) 10%
D) 50%

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. What is the recommended alkalinity level for stable pH in RAS?**

A) 10-20 mg/L as CaCO₃
B) 50-100 mg/L as CaCO₃
C) 150-200 mg/L as CaCO₃
D) 500-1,000 mg/L as CaCO₃

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**4. Ozone treatment in RAS provides benefits including:**

A) Nutrient addition
B) Disinfection, organic oxidation, and water clarification
C) pH reduction only
D) Increased ammonia levels

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**5. What is the safe maximum nitrite level (NO₂⁻-N) for most finfish?**

A) <0.5 mg/L
B) <1.0 mg/L
C) <5.0 mg/L
D) <20 mg/L

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**6. UV disinfection effectiveness is measured in:**

A) Watts
B) mJ/cm² (millijoules per square centimeter)
C) Degrees Celsius
D) PSI

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**7. A system's pH is dropping. What is the MOST likely cause in a functioning RAS?**

A) Excessive feeding
B) Alkalinity depletion from nitrification
C) Too much oxygen
D) High nitrate levels

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. What is the typical safe maximum nitrate level for most fish species?**

A) <10 mg/L
B) <50 mg/L
C) <100-200 mg/L
D) Nitrate is never toxic

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**9. Real-time monitoring systems should measure which parameters at minimum? (Select BEST answer)**

A) Temperature only
B) DO, pH, temperature, and ORP (oxidation-reduction potential)
C) Water level only
D) Color of water

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**10. In a water quality emergency (low DO), what is the FIRST action?**

A) Call for help and wait
B) Stop feeding and increase aeration/oxygenation immediately
C) Drain the system
D) Add chemicals

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Nitrite (NO₂⁻) and Un-ionized Ammonia (NH₃)
2. B - 5%
3. C - 150-200 mg/L as CaCO₃
4. B - Disinfection, organic oxidation, and water clarification
5. B - <1.0 mg/L
6. B - mJ/cm² (millijoules per square centimeter)
7. B - Alkalinity depletion from nitrification
8. C - <100-200 mg/L
9. B - DO, pH, temperature, and ORP
10. B - Stop feeding and increase aeration/oxygenation immediately
