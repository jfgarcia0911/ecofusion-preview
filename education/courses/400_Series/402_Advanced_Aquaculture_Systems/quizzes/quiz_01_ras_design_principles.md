### Questions

**1. What is the typical water replacement rate in a well-functioning RAS?**

A) 20-30% daily
B) 10-15% daily
C) 3-7% daily
D) <1% daily

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**2. In a parallel flow RAS configuration, what is a PRIMARY advantage?**

A) Lower pumping costs due to sequential flow
B) All tanks receive equally treated water
C) Progressive water quality degradation improves efficiency
D) Requires fewer treatment components

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**3. For a 100 m³ system with a target turnover rate of 1.0 per hour, what is the required flow rate?**

A) 50 m³/hr
B) 75 m³/hr
C) 100 m³/hr
D) 200 m³/hr

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: Q = V / RT = 100 m³ / 1 hour = 100 m³/hr

---

**4. What is the optimal depth-to-diameter ratio for circular culture tanks?**

A) 1:1
B) 1:2
C) 1:4
D) 1:8

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**5. A RAS is feeding 100 kg/day with an estimated TAN production coefficient of 0.03 (3%). What is the daily TAN production?**

A) 1 kg TAN/day
B) 2 kg TAN/day
C) 3 kg TAN/day
D) 5 kg TAN/day

**Correct Answer: C**

Explanation: 100 kg feed × 0.03 = 3 kg TAN/day

---

**6. Which tank drain configuration is MOST effective for solids removal?**

A) Single bottom drain
B) Single surface drain
C) Dual drain (bottom + surface)
D) Side-wall overflow only

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**7. What is the typical make-up water requirement for a RAS as a percentage of total system volume?**

A) 1-2% daily
B) 3-7% daily
C) 10-15% daily
D) 20-30% daily

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**8. When scaling up RAS facilities, which ratio should be maintained?**

A) Tank volume to building size
B) Biofilter:Tank volume
C) Number of tanks to operators
D) Pump size to electrical capacity

**Correct Answer: B**

---

**9. What is the PRIMARY purpose of the sump tank in a RAS?**

A) Solids settling
B) Biofilter support
C) Flow buffering and equalization
D) Oxygen injection

**Correct Answer: C**

---

**10. For optimal water circulation in circular tanks, inlet water should be positioned:**

A) At the tank center, flowing downward
B) Tangentially, 10-20 cm below surface
C) At the bottom, flowing upward
D) Multiple locations around perimeter

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - 3-7% daily
2. B - All tanks receive equally treated water
3. C - 100 m³/hr
4. C - 1:4
5. C - 3 kg TAN/day
6. C - Dual drain (bottom + surface)
7. B - 3-7% daily
8. B - Biofilter:Tank volume
9. C - Flow buffering and equalization
10. B - Tangentially, 10-20 cm below surface
