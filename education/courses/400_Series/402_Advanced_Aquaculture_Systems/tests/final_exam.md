## Section 1: RAS Design & Engineering (Questions 1-10)

**1. A 500 m³ RAS system requires a turnover rate of 0.8 per hour. What total flow rate is needed?**

A) 400 m³/hr
B) 500 m³/hr
C) 625 m³/hr
D) 800 m³/hr

**Answer: A** (500 × 0.8 = 400 m³/hr)

---

**2. Which RAS configuration provides the BEST biosecurity between cohorts?**

A) Serial flow with progressive degradation
B) Parallel flow with independent treatment
C) Hybrid with shared biofilter
D) Single-tank recirculation

**Answer: B**
