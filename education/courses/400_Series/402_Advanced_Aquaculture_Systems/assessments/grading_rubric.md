# Course 402: Advanced Aquaculture Systems
## Grading Rubric

---

## Assessment Component Breakdown

### 1. Module Quizzes (28 points - 28%)
**14 quizzes × 2 points each**

#### Grading per Quiz:
- **10/10 correct:** 2.0 points
- **9/10 correct:** 1.8 points
- **8/10 correct:** 1.6 points
- **7/10 or below:** Below passing, retake required

**Quiz Content:**
- RAS design calculations and system sizing
- Biofilter kinetics and microbial management
- Water quality parameters and treatment

- Production planning and economics
- Technical problem-solving scenarios

### 6. Capstone Design Project (20 points - 20%)

**Complete RAS facility design and feasibility study**

#### Project Requirements:

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive RAS facility design proposal including:
- System specifications and engineering design
- Production planning and crop/species selection
- Economic feasibility analysis
- Operational protocols
- Risk assessment

#### Detailed Rubric:

| Component | Points | Excellent (100%) | Proficient (85%) | Developing (70%) | Inadequate (<70%) |
|-----------|--------|------------------|------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **System Design** | 6 | Complete engineering specifications, optimal design choices, detailed diagrams | Good design with minor gaps | Basic design, some errors | Incomplete or flawed design |
| **Technical Calculations** | 4 | Accurate hydraulics, biofilter sizing, water treatment capacity | Minor calculation errors | Significant errors but reasonable approach | Fundamentally incorrect |
| **Production Plan** | 3 | Detailed stocking, growth, harvest schedule with realistic yields | Adequate plan with minor issues | Basic plan lacking detail | Unrealistic or incomplete |
| **Economic Analysis** | 4 | Comprehensive CAPEX/OPEX, cash flow, ROI with sensitivity analysis | Good analysis with some gaps | Basic financial model | Inadequate financial analysis |
| **Risk Assessment** | 2 | Thorough risk identification and mitigation strategies | Adequate risk analysis | Limited risk discussion | Missing or superficial |
| **Professional Presentation** | 1 | Exceptional documentation, clear communication | Professional quality | Acceptable but rough | Poor quality or organization |

**Submission Requirements:**
- Technical report: 4,000-6,000 words
- Engineering drawings and schematics
- Financial pro forma (5-year projection)
- Executive summary (1-2 pages)
- Appendices with calculations and supporting data

**Presentation:** 15-minute virtual presentation to class and instructor panel

## Academic Policies

### Late Submission Policy:
- **Module Quizzes:** No late submissions (must complete during module period)
- **Activities:** 10% deduction per day, maximum 3 days late
- **Case Study:** 15% deduction per day, not accepted after 5 days
- **Capstone Project:** 20% deduction per day, maximum 2 days late
- **Final Exam:** No late submissions

### Retake Policy:
- **Quizzes:** Unlimited attempts during module availability
- **Activities:** One revision opportunity if score below 70%
- **Case Study:** One revision within 7 days if score below 75%
- **Final Exam:** One retake allowed if score below 70%
- **Capstone Project:** Revisions allowed before final deadline based on instructor feedback

### Academic Integrity:
- All work must be original
- Proper citation required for all sources
- Collaboration allowed on discussions, not on individual assignments
- **Violations:** Zero on assignment; repeated violations result in course failure
