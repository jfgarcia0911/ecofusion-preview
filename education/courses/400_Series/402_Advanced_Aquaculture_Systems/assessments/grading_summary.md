## Module Quizzes (28 points)

- **Quantity:** 14 quizzes
- **Points Each:** 2 points
- **Format:** 10 questions per quiz
- **Time Limit:** 30 minutes
- **Attempts:** Unlimited
- **Minimum Score:** 70% (7/10 correct)

### Quiz Schedule:
Week 1-14: One quiz per module at module completion

### Content Focus:
- System design calculations
- Water quality management
- Fish nutrition and health
- Production economics
- Technical problem-solving

## Hands-On Activities (15 points)

### Activity 1: RAS System Design Exercise (5 points)
**Week 4 - After Module 4**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Design recirculating system components
- Calculate hydraulics and flow rates
- Size biofilter and water treatment systems
- Create system schematic diagram

### Activity 2: Water Quality Management Case Study (5 points)
**Week 9 - After Module 9**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Diagnose water quality issues
- Develop treatment protocol
- Create monitoring and response plan
- Document preventive measures

### Activity 3: Production Economics Analysis (5 points)
**Week 13 - After Module 13**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Build financial model for RAS operation
- Calculate production costs and revenues
- Perform sensitivity analysis
- Recommend optimization strategies

**Due: Week 7 (Mid-Term)**

- Improvement recommendations (2 points)

### Requirements:
- Professional report format
- Minimum 5 peer-reviewed citations
- Include calculations and diagrams
- Executive summary

## Final Comprehensive Exam (20 points)

**Week 14**

### Exam Details:
- **Questions:** 50 total
  - 25 multiple choice (5 points)
  - 10 technical calculations (8 points)
  - 10 short answer (5 points)
  - 1 system design problem (2 points)
- **Time:** 3 hours
- **Format:** Open notes (course materials)
- **Minimum Score:** 70% (14 points)
- **Attempts:** Maximum 2

### Coverage:
- All 14 modules proportionally represented
- Emphasis on integration and application
- Real-world problem scenarios

## Capstone Design Project (20 points)

**Due: Week 14**

### Project Scope:
Complete RAS facility design and feasibility study

### Deliverables:
1. Technical Report (4,000-6,000 words)
2. Engineering Drawings/Schematics
3. Financial Pro Forma (5-year projection)
4. Executive Summary (1-2 pages)
5. Presentation (15 minutes)

### Grading Breakdown:
- System Design & Engineering: 6 points
- Technical Calculations: 4 points
- Production Planning: 3 points
- Economic Analysis: 4 points
- Risk Assessment: 2 points
- Professional Presentation: 1 point

### Success Criteria:
- Technically sound and buildable design
- Economically viable with clear ROI
- Comprehensive risk mitigation
- Professional-quality documentation
