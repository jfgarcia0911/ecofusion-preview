# Course 402: Advanced Aquaculture Systems

## Expert Level | 400-Series Professional Course

---

## Course Description

This expert-level course provides comprehensive training in advanced recirculating aquaculture system (RAS) design, engineering, and management. Students will master the principles of high-performance aquaculture systems, including biofilter optimization, multi-species production, broodstock management, and hatchery operations. The course integrates engineering principles, fish physiology, microbiology, and economic analysis to prepare professionals for leadership roles in commercial aquaculture operations.

Through rigorous technical instruction and practical applications, students will develop the expertise required to design, optimize, and manage state-of-the-art RAS facilities producing finfish, crustaceans, and other aquatic organisms at commercial scale.

**Course Duration:** 14 weeks
**Level:** Expert (400-level)
**Estimated Time Commitment:** 15-20 hours per week
**Certification:** EcoFusion Advanced Aquaculture Systems Professional Certificate

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Advanced RAS Design Principles (Week 1)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand advanced RAS configurations and design philosophies
- Calculate system hydraulics, retention times, and flow rates
- Design optimal tank geometry and water distribution systems
- Integrate multiple treatment technologies

**Key Topics:**
- RAS system architecture and configurations
- Hydraulic design and flow management
- Tank design and culture density optimization
- System sizing and scale-up principles
- Make-up water requirements and discharge management

---

### Module 2: Biofilter Engineering & Optimization (Week 2)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Design and optimize nitrification biofilter systems
- Manage nitrifying bacterial communities
- Calculate biofilter loading rates and capacity
- Troubleshoot biofilter performance issues

**Key Topics:**
- Nitrification kinetics and bacterial ecology
- Moving bed bioreactors (MBBR) design
- Trickling filters and fluidized bed systems
- Biofilter media selection and surface area calculations
- Start-up protocols and biofilm development
- Denitrification systems for nitrate management

---

### Module 3: Solids Management Systems (Week 3)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Design comprehensive solids removal systems
- Optimize particle capture efficiency
- Manage sludge production and disposal
- Integrate mechanical and biological solids treatment

**Key Topics:**
- Particle size distribution and settling characteristics
- Swirl separators and radial flow settlers
- Drum filters and microscreens
- Foam fractionation and protein skimming
- Sludge dewatering and beneficial reuse
- Total suspended solids (TSS) management

---

### Module 4: Gas Transfer & Oxygenation Systems (Week 4)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Calculate oxygen requirements and transfer rates
- Design efficient oxygenation systems
- Manage carbon dioxide stripping
- Optimize gas exchange economics

**Key Topics:**
- Dissolved oxygen dynamics and fish respiration
- Oxygen transfer efficiency (OTE) and standard oxygen transfer rate (SOTR)
- Low head oxygenators (LHO) and oxygen cones
- Pure oxygen injection systems
- CO2 degassing towers and cascade aerators
- Emergency backup systems

---

### Module 5: Advanced Water Quality Management (Week 5)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Monitor and control critical water quality parameters
- Implement advanced treatment technologies
- Manage alkalinity and pH stability
- Prevent and mitigate water quality emergencies

**Key Topics:**
- Nitrogen cycle management (TAN, NO2, NO3)
- pH, alkalinity, and buffering capacity
- Ozone and UV disinfection systems
- Heavy metal and micronutrient management
- Real-time monitoring and automated control
- Water quality emergency response protocols

---

### Module 6: Multi-Species Production Systems (Week 6)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Design integrated multi-trophic aquaculture (IMTA) systems
- Manage polyculture production strategies
- Optimize species compatibility and stocking
- Maximize system productivity through species integration

**Key Topics:**
- Finfish polyculture systems
- Integrated fish and crustacean production
- Tiered trophic level production
- Species selection criteria and compatibility
- Feeding strategies for mixed species
- Market diversification through multi-species culture

---

### Module 7: Broodstock Management & Hatchery Operations (Week 7)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Manage broodstock conditioning and selection
- Implement spawning protocols and hormone therapy
- Design commercial hatchery systems
- Optimize egg incubation and larval rearing

**Key Topics:**
- Broodstock nutrition and photoperiod manipulation
- Reproductive physiology and spawning triggers
- Hormone-induced spawning protocols
- Egg collection, disinfection, and incubation
- Hatchery water quality requirements
- Larval tank design and management

---

### Module 8: Fish Nutrition & Feed Formulation (Week 8)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand advanced fish nutritional requirements
- Formulate species-specific feeds
- Optimize feeding protocols and FCR
- Manage feed quality and storage

**Key Topics:**
- Protein, lipid, and energy requirements by species
- Essential amino acids and fatty acids (EPA/DHA)
- Vitamin and mineral supplementation
- Feed formulation and ingredient selection
- Feeding rate optimization and growth models
- Feed manufacturing and quality control
- Alternative protein sources and sustainability

---

### Module 9: Disease Prevention & Biosecurity (Week 9)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Implement comprehensive biosecurity protocols
- Prevent disease introduction and transmission
- Diagnose and treat common fish diseases
- Design quarantine and health management systems

**Key Topics:**
- Biosecurity risk assessment and protocols
- Pathogen transmission routes and prevention
- Bacterial, viral, and parasitic diseases
- Health monitoring and diagnostic techniques
- Therapeutic treatments and withdrawal periods
- Vaccination programs and prophylaxis
- Quarantine facility design and protocols

---

### Module 10: Genetics & Selective Breeding (Week 10)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand quantitative genetics in aquaculture
- Design selective breeding programs
- Manage genetic diversity and inbreeding
- Implement family-based selection protocols

**Key Topics:**
- Heritability and genetic gain
- Selection methods: mass, family, and genomic selection
- Breeding program design and generation intervals
- Mating strategies and inbreeding management
- Traits of interest: growth, disease resistance, quality
- Triploid and monosex production techniques
- Genetic traceability and record keeping

---

### Module 11: Production Planning & Optimization (Week 11)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Develop comprehensive production plans
- Optimize stocking density and cohort management
- Calculate carrying capacity and production cycles
- Implement grading and size management strategies

**Key Topics:**
- Production planning and scheduling
- Stocking density optimization
- Growth modeling and harvest projections
- Cohort management and split stocking
- Grading protocols and equipment
- Harvest planning and logistics
- Inventory management and tracking

---

### Module 12: System Automation & Monitoring (Week 12)
**Learning Outcomes:**

**Key Topics:**

### Module 13: Economic Analysis & Feasibility (Week 13)

- Financing options and investment structures

### Module 14: Case Studies & Industry Applications (Week 14)
**Learning Outcomes:**

- Apply learned principles to real-world scenarios
- Evaluate emerging technologies and innovations
- Develop comprehensive RAS design proposals

**Key Topics:**
- Case studies: Atlantic salmon, tilapia, barramundi, shrimp
- RAS facility tours and industry analysis
- Emerging technologies: aquaponics integration, waste valorization
- Regulatory compliance and permitting
- Environmental impact and sustainability metrics
- Future trends in RAS technology
- Capstone project: Comprehensive RAS design

- **Module Quizzes** (28%): 14 quizzes × 2% each

### Final Assessment (40%):
- **Comprehensive Final Exam** (20%): 50-question examination covering all modules
- **Capstone Design Project** (20%): Complete RAS facility design and feasibility study

**Passing Grade:** 75% overall
**Certificate of Excellence:** 90% or higher
