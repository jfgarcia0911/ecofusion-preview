# Quiz 5: Work Breakdown Structures & Project Organization

**Course:** 413 - CEA Project Management
**Module:** 5 - Work Breakdown Structures & Project Organization
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

**1. What is a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)?**

A) A salary structure for workers
B) A hierarchical decomposition of project scope into manageable work packages
C) An organizational chart
D) A breakdown of equipment failures

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** WBS hierarchically divides project scope into progressively smaller components, creating manageable work packages that can be estimated, scheduled, assigned, and tracked.

---

**2. What is the "100% rule" for WBS development?**

A) All workers must be 100% utilized
B) The WBS must include 100% of the project scope, with each level summing to the level above
C) Projects must be 100% complete
D) Budget must equal $100,000

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The 100% rule ensures the WBS captures all project work—nothing more, nothing less—with child elements completely representing parent elements.

---

**3. What is an appropriate level of detail for WBS work packages in CEA projects?**

A) Individual tasks taking minutes
B) Work packages representing 40-80 hours of effort that can be assigned and tracked
C) Only major milestones
D) No detail needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Work packages should be detailed enough for assignment, estimation, and tracking (typically 40-80 hours or 1-2 weeks effort) without excessive granularity.

---

**4. What is a RACI matrix?**

A) A racial diversity plan
B) A responsibility assignment matrix defining who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed
C) A risk assessment tool
D) A financial calculation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** RACI matrices clarify roles: Responsible (does the work), Accountable (approves), Consulted (provides input), Informed (kept updated)—reducing confusion and gaps.

---

**5. In a WBS for a vertical farm, which represents the highest level breakdown?**

A) Install pipe support bracket #427
B) HVAC System Installation
C) Major phases: Design, Procurement, Construction, Commissioning
D) Individual weld inspections

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Top WBS levels represent major phases or systems; middle levels are subsystems; lowest levels are specific work packages—from general to specific hierarchy.

---

**6. What is the primary benefit of using a WBS dictionary?**

A) Translation to other languages
B) Detailed descriptions of work packages including scope, deliverables, resources, and acceptance criteria
C) List of technical terms
D) Budget reduction

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** WBS dictionaries document each work package's scope, deliverables, responsible parties, resources, duration estimates, and acceptance criteria—essential for clarity and execution.

---

**7. For a CEA project, which WBS organization approach is most common?**

A) Random grouping
B) Hybrid approach combining project phases and major systems/disciplines
C) Alphabetical listing only
D) No organization

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA projects typically use hybrid WBS combining phases (design, construction) with systems (HVAC, electrical, automation) and disciplines (mechanical, civil, agronomic).

---

**8. What is a deliverable-oriented WBS?**

A) WBS organized by departments
B) WBS focused on tangible outputs (completed systems, approved documents) rather than activities
C) Delivery schedule only
D) Vendor list

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Deliverable-oriented WBS focuses on outcomes (completed HVAC system, commissioned control system) rather than activities, clearly defining what must be produced.

---

**9. At what point in the project should the WBS be developed?**

A) After construction is complete
B) During planning phase, after requirements are defined but before detailed scheduling
C) Never
D) During operations

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** WBS development follows requirements definition and precedes detailed scheduling—it provides the scope framework for time and cost estimates.

---

**10. What is the relationship between WBS and project schedule?**

A) No relationship
B) WBS work packages become the activities in the project schedule
C) They are identical
D) Schedule comes first, then WBS

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** WBS work packages form the foundation for schedule activities—each work package is broken into schedulable tasks with durations, dependencies, and resources.
