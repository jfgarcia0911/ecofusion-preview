# Quiz 3: Stakeholder Identification & Management

**Course:** 413 - CEA Project Management
**Module:** 3 - Stakeholder Identification & Management
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

**1. In stakeholder mapping using the power/interest grid, which stakeholders require the most management attention?**

A) Low power, low interest
B) High power, low interest
C) Low power, high interest
D) High power, high interest

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** High power, high interest stakeholders (e.g., major investors, key regulatory agencies) require close engagement and active management as they significantly influence project success.

---

**2. What is the primary objective of stakeholder engagement in CEA projects?**

A) To eliminate all opposition
B) To build relationships, manage expectations, and maintain support throughout the project
C) To reduce project costs
D) To avoid communication

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Effective stakeholder engagement builds trust, aligns expectations, addresses concerns proactively, and maintains support critical for project success.

---

**3. Which stakeholder group typically has regulatory authority over CEA facility development?**

A) Investors only
B) Local planning/zoning boards, building departments, and health authorities
C) Equipment vendors
D) Employees

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Multiple regulatory agencies govern CEA projects: zoning approvals, building permits, electrical/plumbing inspections, food safety compliance, and environmental permits.

---

**4. How should project managers handle stakeholders with high power but low interest?**

A) Ignore them completely
B) Keep them satisfied with periodic updates; monitor for changes in interest level
C) Engage them daily
D) Remove them from the project

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** High power, low interest stakeholders (e.g., certain board members, utilities) should receive regular updates to keep them informed and satisfied without excessive engagement.

---

**5. What is the purpose of a stakeholder engagement matrix?**

A) To rank stakeholders by importance only
B) To document stakeholder needs, communication preferences, and engagement strategies
C) To eliminate stakeholders
D) To assign tasks randomly

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Engagement matrices document each stakeholder's interests, influence, communication preferences, and tailored engagement strategies, ensuring systematic relationship management.

---

**6. For investor relations in CEA projects, what communication frequency is typically appropriate during construction?**

A) Annual updates only
B) Monthly progress reports with quarterly detailed reviews
C) Daily emails
D) No communication until completion

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Investors typically expect monthly progress updates on schedule, budget, and risks, with quarterly detailed financial reviews and board presentations.

---

**7. When engaging with local community stakeholders about a new vertical farm, what concerns should be proactively addressed?**

A) Traffic, employment opportunities, environmental impacts, and facility appearance
B) Stock prices only
C) Technical engineering details
D) Ignore community concerns

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Communities care about traffic impacts, local jobs, environmental benefits (water use, no pesticides), aesthetics, and whether the facility enhances the neighborhood.

---

**8. What is the best approach when stakeholder requirements conflict?**

A) Ignore all stakeholders
B) Choose one stakeholder and satisfy only them
C) Facilitate discussion, identify trade-offs, and negotiate solutions that balance competing needs
D) Cancel the project

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Conflicting requirements require facilitated negotiation to understand underlying needs, explore trade-offs, and develop solutions balancing stakeholder priorities.

---

**9. Which internal stakeholder group is often underestimated in CEA project planning?**

A) Investors
B) Future operations team members who will run the facility
C) Board of directors
D) Attorneys

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Operations teams possess critical practical knowledge about workflow, maintenance, and daily operations that should inform design decisions, yet they're often engaged too late.

---

**10. What metric can be used to track stakeholder engagement effectiveness?**

A) Number of stakeholders identified
B) Stakeholder satisfaction surveys and issue resolution time
C) Total budget spent on engagement
D) Number of meetings held

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Effectiveness is measured by stakeholder satisfaction, timely issue resolution, approval acquisition, and whether relationships support (rather than hinder) project progress.
