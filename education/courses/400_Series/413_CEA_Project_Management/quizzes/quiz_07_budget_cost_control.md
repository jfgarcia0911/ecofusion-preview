## Questions

**1. What is bottom-up cost estimating?**

A) Starting with total budget and dividing
B) Estimating costs for individual work packages and aggregating to total project cost
C) Using only historical data
D) Guessing the lowest possible cost

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Bottom-up estimating starts with detailed work packages, estimates each, then sums to create total—most accurate but time-intensive approach.

---

**2. For a Class 3 (±20%) cost estimate, what level of design completion is typically required?**

A) No design needed
B) 30-50% design completion
C) 100% complete construction documents
D) Concept only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Class 3 estimates at 30-50% design provide ±20% accuracy for budget authorization, balancing detail with design investment before full engineering.

---

**3. What is Earned Value Management (EVM)?**

A) Payroll system
B) Integrated approach measuring project performance by comparing planned value, earned value, and actual cost
C) Bonus structure
D) Equipment depreciation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** EVM integrates scope, schedule, and cost to measure performance: EV (work completed), PV (work planned), AC (actual cost)—providing objective performance metrics.

---

**4. Calculate Cost Performance Index (CPI) given: Earned Value = $400,000, Actual Cost = $450,000**

A) 0.89
B) 1.00
C) 1.13
D) 0.75

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** CPI = EV / AC = $400,000 / $450,000 = 0.89. CPI < 1.0 indicates over budget (getting $0.89 of value per dollar spent).

---

**5. What is management reserve in project budgeting?**

A) Money for the project manager's salary
B) Budget held by management for unforeseen scope changes outside the baseline
C) Same as contingency reserve
D) Equipment storage costs

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Management reserve covers unknown unknowns (out-of-scope risks), controlled by senior management, separate from contingency (for known risks within scope).

---

**6. For a $15 million CEA project with CPI = 0.90, what is the Estimate at Completion (EAC)?**

A) $13.5 million
B) $15.0 million
C) $16.7 million
D) $18.0 million

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** EAC = BAC / CPI = $15,000,000 / 0.90 = $16,667,000 ≈ $16.7 million (forecasting 10% overrun based on current performance).

---

**7. What contingency percentage is appropriate for a well-defined CEA project at 60% design completion?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-20%
C) 30-40%
D) 50%+

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** At 60% design, remaining unknowns warrant 15-20% contingency. Early conceptual (30-40%), final design (5-10%), complete certainty (0%).

---

**8. What is the purpose of cash flow forecasting in CEA projects?**

A) To predict rainfall
B) To project when funds are needed and ensure financing availability
C) To eliminate budgets
D) To increase costs

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Cash flow forecasts predict monthly/quarterly fund requirements, ensuring financing/draws are available when needed for payments, avoiding delays or penalties.

---

**9. What cost control measure is most effective for managing change orders?**

A) Approving all changes without review
B) Formal change control process with impact analysis and authorization
C) Preventing all changes
D) Ignoring cost impacts

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Formal change control evaluates technical need, cost/schedule impacts, and alternatives before authorization, preventing uncontrolled cost growth.

---

**10. For a project with BAC = $10M, EV = $6M, AC = $7M, PV = $6.5M, what is the Cost Variance (CV)?**

A) -$1M (over budget)
B) +$500K (under budget)
C) $0 (on budget)
D) -$500K (over budget)

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** CV = EV - AC = $6M - $7M = -$1M. Negative CV indicates over budget (spent more than value earned).
