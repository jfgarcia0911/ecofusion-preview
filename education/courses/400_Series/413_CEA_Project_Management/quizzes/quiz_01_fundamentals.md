# Quiz 1: CEA Project Management Fundamentals

**Course:** 413 - CEA Project Management
**Module:** 1 - Fundamentals
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

## Questions

**1. What is the primary characteristic that distinguishes CEA projects from traditional construction projects?**

A) Higher capital costs
B) Integration of biological systems with engineered environments
C) Longer construction timelines
D) More complex permitting requirements

**2. In the PMI/PMBOK framework, which process group involves tracking project performance and making corrective adjustments?**

A) Initiating
B) Planning
C) Executing
D) Monitoring & Controlling

**3. What is the typical range for capital costs per square foot for vertical farm facilities?**

A) $25-$75/sf
B) $100-$300/sf
C) $400-$600/sf
D) $700-$1000/sf

**4. In a matrix organization structure, project managers typically face which primary challenge?**

A) Unlimited budget authority
B) Dual reporting relationships creating potential conflicts
C) Direct control over all resources
D) Minimal stakeholder communication requirements

**5. According to the "triple constraint" model, the three primary project constraints are:**

A) Scope, schedule, and cost
B) Quality, risk, and resources
C) Stakeholders, budget, and timeline
D) Design, construction, and commissioning

**6. What percentage of CEA projects typically experience cost overruns of 20-40%?**

A) Less than 10%
B) 25-35%
C) Common occurrence (many projects)
D) Over 75%

**7. In the phase-gate model for CEA projects, what is the primary decision made at Gate 1?**

A) Construction approval
B) Feasibility approval (go/no-go)
C) Commissioning authorization
D) Operational handover

**8. Which knowledge area focuses on coordinating all project elements and managing change?**

A) Scope Management
B) Integration Management
C) Communications Management
D) Stakeholder Management

**9. What is a typical project duration from concept to operational acceptance for a 50,000 sf vertical farm?**

A) 6-12 months
B) 12-18 months
C) 24-30 months
D) 36-48 months

**10. The Cost Performance Index (CPI) is calculated as:**

A) Actual Cost / Planned Value
B) Earned Value / Actual Cost
C) Planned Value / Earned Value
D) Budget at Completion / Estimate at Completion

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Integration of biological systems with engineered environments
   - *Explanation:* CEA projects uniquely combine living plant systems with precisely controlled mechanical/electrical systems, requiring integration of agricultural science with engineering.

2. **D** - Monitoring & Controlling
   - *Explanation:* The Monitoring & Controlling process group tracks performance, compares to baseline, and implements corrective actions.

3. **B** - $100-$300/sf
   - *Explanation:* Vertical farms typically range from $100-$300/sf all-in (turnkey), while high-tech greenhouses are $25-$75/sf.

4. **B** - Dual reporting relationships creating potential conflicts
   - *Explanation:* In matrix organizations, team members report to both functional managers and project managers, creating complexity in authority and priorities.

5. **A** - Scope, schedule, and cost
   - *Explanation:* The traditional "triple constraint" (also called "iron triangle") consists of scope, schedule (time), and cost (budget).

6. **C** - Common occurrence (many projects)
   - *Explanation:* Cost overruns of 20-40% are unfortunately common in CEA projects due to inadequate feasibility analysis, optimistic assumptions, and technology complexity.

7. **B** - Feasibility approval (go/no-go)
   - *Explanation:* Gate 1 reviews the feasibility study and business case to make the fundamental decision whether to proceed with the project.

8. **B** - Integration Management
   - *Explanation:* Integration Management coordinates all project elements, manages change control, and ensures the project works as a cohesive whole.

9. **C** - 24-30 months
   - *Explanation:* A typical 50,000 sf vertical farm takes approximately 24-30 months from concept development through commissioning and operational acceptance.

10. **B** - Earned Value / Actual Cost
    - *Explanation:* CPI = EV / AC. A CPI > 1.0 indicates under budget (good), while CPI < 1.0 indicates over budget.
