## Questions

**1. What is the difference between functional and technical requirements in CEA projects?**

A) There is no difference
B) Functional requirements describe WHAT the system must do; technical requirements describe HOW it will be achieved
C) Technical requirements are always more important
D) Functional requirements only apply to software

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Functional requirements define capabilities (e.g., "maintain 22°C ±1°C"), while technical requirements specify implementation (e.g., "using variable-speed HVAC with PID control").

---

**2. What is scope creep and why is it problematic in CEA projects?**

A) The gradual expansion of project scope without corresponding budget/schedule adjustments, leading to cost overruns
B) A beneficial addition of features
C) Normal project evolution
D) Improved project management

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Scope creep occurs when requirements expand without formal change control, consuming contingency, delaying completion, and causing budget overruns—particularly problematic in capital-intensive CEA projects.

---

**3. For a vertical farm project, which requirement would be classified as "functional"?**

A) Use of Siemens PLCs for automation
B) Stainless steel piping specifications
C) "System shall produce 500 kg/day of leafy greens"
D) Building constructed per IBC 2021

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Functional requirements define performance objectives (production capacity), while technical requirements specify implementation details (equipment brands, materials, codes).

---

**4. What is a requirements traceability matrix?**

A) A GPS tracking system
B) A document linking requirements to design, testing, and deliverables to ensure all are addressed
C) A shipping document
D) An organizational chart

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Traceability matrices track each requirement through design, implementation, and validation, ensuring nothing is missed and changes are managed systematically.

---

**5. Who should be involved in requirements gathering for a CEA facility?**

A) Project manager only
B) Engineers only
C) Cross-functional team including operations, engineering, finance, food safety, and stakeholders
D) Contractors only

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Requirements gathering requires diverse perspectives: operations (workflow), engineering (feasibility), finance (cost), food safety (compliance), and stakeholders (strategic objectives).

---

**6. What is the purpose of a formal change control process?**

A) To prevent all changes
B) To evaluate, approve/reject, and document scope changes with budget/schedule impacts
C) To allow unlimited changes
D) To eliminate stakeholder input

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Change control provides governance for evaluating proposed changes, assessing impacts on cost/schedule/quality, obtaining approvals, and maintaining baseline integrity.

---

**7. At what project phase should requirements be most rigorously defined?**

A) During construction
B) At project closeout
C) During planning/design phase before major commitments
D) Requirements don't need definition

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Requirements must be well-defined during planning/design before procurement and construction commitments, as changes become exponentially more expensive later.

---

**8. What is a user story in agile requirements management?**

A) A fictional narrative
B) A statement from the user's perspective describing a need: "As a [user], I want [feature] so that [benefit]"
C) A biography of the project manager
D) Historical project data

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** User stories capture requirements from the end-user perspective, focusing on needs and benefits rather than technical specifications, common in iterative/agile approaches.

---

**9. What should be done when stakeholders have conflicting requirements?**

A) Choose randomly
B) Ignore conflicting requirements
C) Facilitate prioritization, explore trade-offs, and document decisions with rationale
D) Cancel the project

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Conflicts require facilitated discussion to prioritize must-haves, explore alternatives, make trade-off decisions, and document rationale for future reference.

---

**10. How detailed should requirements be for equipment procurement?**

A) Vague descriptions only
B) Specific enough to ensure vendor proposals are comparable and meet project needs
C) Brand names only, no specifications
D) No requirements needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Procurement requirements must be specific enough for competitive bidding and comparison while allowing vendor flexibility for innovative solutions meeting performance criteria.
