# Quiz 8: Risk Identification & Quantitative Analysis

**Course:** 413 - CEA Project Management
**Module:** 8 - Risk Identification & Quantitative Analysis
**Time Limit:** 20 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Questions

**1. What is a project risk?**

A) Guaranteed problems
B) An uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has positive or negative effect on objectives
C) Only negative outcomes
D) Equipment failures only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Risks are uncertain future events (threats or opportunities) that could affect scope, cost, schedule, or quality—requiring proactive management.

---

**2. In a probability/impact matrix, which risks receive highest priority?**

A) Low probability, low impact
B) High probability, low impact
C) Low probability, high impact
D) High probability, high impact

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** High probability, high impact risks pose the greatest threat to project success and require immediate attention and mitigation strategies.

---

**3. What risk response strategy involves transferring risk to a third party (e.g., through insurance or contracts)?**

A) Avoid
B) Mitigate
C) Transfer
D) Accept

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Transfer shifts risk consequences to a third party through insurance, warranties, performance bonds, or contract terms (e.g., fixed-price contracts transfer cost risk).

---

**4. What is Monte Carlo simulation used for in project risk analysis?**

A) Casino gambling
B) Running thousands of scenarios with varying inputs to produce probabilistic cost/schedule outcomes
C) Weather forecasting
D) Equipment selection

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Monte Carlo simulation models uncertainty by running thousands of iterations with randomized inputs (within defined ranges) to produce probability distributions of outcomes.

---

**5. For a CEA project, which represents a technical risk?**

A) Permitting delays
B) Unproven automation technology failing to meet performance specifications
C) Market price fluctuations
D) Investor withdrawal

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Technical risks involve technology performance, integration challenges, or unproven systems—common in CEA due to emerging technologies and custom integration.

---

**6. What is the risk register used for?**

A) Recording visitor signatures
B) Documenting identified risks, probability, impact, ownership, and response strategies
C) Equipment inventory
D) Employee attendance

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Risk registers catalog all identified risks with analysis (probability, impact, priority), assigned owners, planned responses, and status—the central risk management tool.

---

**7. Which risk response is appropriate for low-probability, low-impact risks?**

A) Extensive mitigation planning
B) Active acceptance (contingency reserve) or passive acceptance (monitoring only)
C) Immediate escalation to executives
D) Project cancellation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Low priority risks don't warrant extensive response planning—acceptance with monitoring is appropriate, using contingency if they occur.

---

**8. What supply chain risk is particularly critical for CEA projects?**

A) Office supply shortages
B) Long lead times and limited suppliers for specialized equipment (LED fixtures, HVAC, automation)
C) Excess inventory
D) Too many vendors

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA equipment often has 3-6 month lead times with limited qualified suppliers—delays cascade through critical path, requiring early procurement and supplier management.

---

**9. What is a risk mitigation strategy?**

A) Ignoring risks
B) Taking action to reduce probability or impact of a threat
C) Accepting all consequences
D) Eliminating the project

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Mitigation proactively reduces risk through actions like: prototyping unproven technology, vendor qualification, redundant systems, or phased implementation.

---

**10. For regulatory and permitting risks in CEA projects, what is the most effective response?**

A) Ignore regulations
B) Early engagement with agencies, pre-application meetings, and experienced permitting consultants
C) Start construction without permits
D) Hope for the best

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Permitting risks are mitigated through early agency engagement, understanding requirements, hiring experienced consultants, and building schedule buffers for approvals.
