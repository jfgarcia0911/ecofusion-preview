# Activity: Project Charter Development Exercise

## Learning Objectives

By completing this exercise, you will:
- Apply project charter development principles to a realistic CEA scenario
- Practice stakeholder identification and analysis
- Define clear project objectives using SMART criteria
- Establish project governance and decision-making authority
- Develop high-level scope, schedule, and budget estimates

---

## Scenario: MidWest Fresh Vertical Farm

### Background

**Organization:** MidWest Fresh Foods, Inc.
- Established produce distributor serving grocery chains in 5-state region
- Annual revenue: $120M
- Looking to vertically integrate with CEA production
- Board approved concept study; now seeking detailed feasibility and charter

**Strategic Drivers:**
- Customers demanding year-round local leafy greens supply
- Current suppliers unreliable (weather-dependent, quality inconsistent)
- Opportunity to differentiate with "ultra-fresh" same-day delivery
- CEO vision: "From seed to shelf in 48 hours"

**Initial Concept:**
- 35,000 sf vertical farm in Indianapolis, IN
- Target production: 1.8M lbs/year leafy greens (lettuce, herbs, microgreens)
- Advanced automation and data analytics
- USDA GAP certified operations
- Estimated capital requirement: $22-28M
- Target ROI: 18%+ IRR, <6 year payback
