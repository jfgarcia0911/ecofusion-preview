# Course 413: CEA Project Management
## Expert Level - Professional Certification Track

### Course Description

This expert-level course provides comprehensive project management training specifically designed for controlled environment agriculture (CEA) facility development and operations. Students will master the complete project lifecycle from initial concept through post-commissioning evaluation, applying industry-standard PM methodologies adapted for the unique challenges of CEA projects.

The course integrates traditional project management frameworks (PMI/PMBOK) with agile methodologies, addressing the technical complexity, regulatory requirements, capital intensity, and stakeholder diversity inherent in CEA facility development. Students will develop practical skills in planning, executing, and controlling multi-million dollar CEA projects while managing cross-functional teams of engineers, agronomists, contractors, and investors.

**Course Level:** 400 (Expert/Professional)
**Prerequisites:**
- Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture (Required)
- Course 411: Vertical Farm Engineering (Required)
- Course 203: System Automation & Monitoring (Recommended)
- 3+ years experience in CEA operations, construction, or related field (Recommended)
- Basic understanding of business finance and accounting

**Course Duration:** 14 weeks
**Estimated Time Commitment:** 12-15 hours per week
**Credits:** 4.0 CEA Professional Credits

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Strategic Planning & Initiation**
   - Develop comprehensive business cases and feasibility studies for CEA projects
   - Conduct stakeholder analysis and develop engagement strategies
   - Create project charters with clear success criteria and governance structures
   - Perform site selection analysis with technical and regulatory considerations

2. **Scope & Requirements Management**
   - Define and document technical requirements for CEA facilities
   - Create detailed work breakdown structures (WBS) for complex CEA projects
   - Manage scope changes through formal change control processes
   - Balance competing requirements from multiple stakeholder groups

3. **Planning & Scheduling**
   - Develop integrated project schedules using critical path methodology
   - Create resource-loaded schedules with labor, equipment, and material planning
   - Identify and manage project dependencies and constraints
   - Apply schedule compression techniques when required

4. **Budget & Cost Management**
   - Prepare detailed cost estimates for CEA facility construction and equipment
   - Develop project budgets with appropriate contingency allocations
   - Implement earned value management (EVM) for cost/schedule integration
   - Manage cash flow and funding drawdowns for capital projects

5. **Risk Management**
   - Identify and assess technical, financial, and operational risks
   - Develop quantitative risk models using Monte Carlo simulation
   - Create risk response strategies and mitigation plans
   - Manage supply chain and vendor-related risks

6. **Quality & Technical Management**
   - Develop quality management plans specific to CEA systems
   - Implement inspection and testing protocols for mechanical/electrical systems
   - Manage technical specifications and performance requirements
   - Ensure compliance with food safety and building code requirements

7. **Procurement & Contracts**
   - Develop procurement strategies for specialized CEA equipment
   - Manage vendor selection and contract negotiation processes
   - Administer construction contracts and change orders
   - Navigate international procurement and import regulations

8. **Construction & Commissioning**
   - Coordinate multi-trade construction activities in CEA facilities
   - Implement safety management programs for construction sites
   - Develop and execute comprehensive commissioning plans
   - Manage punch list completion and warranty documentation

9. **Team Leadership & Communication**
   - Lead cross-functional project teams in matrix organizations
   - Develop stakeholder communication and reporting frameworks
   - Manage conflict resolution and team performance
   - Facilitate decision-making processes with diverse stakeholder groups

10. **Project Closeout & Continuous Improvement**
    - Execute formal project handover and closeout procedures
    - Conduct post-project evaluations and lessons learned sessions
    - Document best practices and knowledge transfer
    - Establish operational transition and training programs

### Course Modules

#### Module 1: CEA Project Management Fundamentals
- Project management in the CEA industry context
- PMI/PMBOK framework adapted for agriculture technology
- CEA project lifecycle phases and gate reviews
- Organizational structures and project governance
- Role of the CEA project manager

#### Module 2: Project Initiation & Feasibility Analysis
- Strategic alignment and business case development
- Market analysis and competitive positioning
- Financial feasibility and pro forma modeling
- Site selection criteria and evaluation methods
- Preliminary engineering and conceptual design
- Go/no-go decision frameworks

#### Module 3: Stakeholder Identification & Management
- Stakeholder mapping and power/interest analysis
- Engagement strategy development
- Managing investor relations and board communications
- Community engagement and public relations
- Regulatory agency coordination
- Internal stakeholder alignment

#### Module 4: Scope Definition & Requirements Management
- Requirements gathering methodologies
- Functional vs. technical requirements
- Production capacity and crop planning requirements
- Environmental control system specifications
- Automation and data system requirements
- Creating traceability matrices

#### Module 5: Work Breakdown Structures & Project Organization
- WBS development for CEA facilities
- Organizing work packages by system and discipline
- Defining deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Creating responsibility assignment matrices (RACI)
- Integration with organizational breakdown structure
- Dictionary and baseline documentation

#### Module 6: Schedule Development & Management
- Activity definition and sequencing
- Dependency mapping (FS, SS, FF, SF relationships)
- Duration estimating (analogous, parametric, three-point)
- Critical path method (CPM) and float analysis
- Resource leveling and optimization
- Schedule baseline and performance measurement

#### Module 7: Budget Development & Cost Control
- Cost estimation techniques (bottom-up, parametric, vendor quotes)
- Equipment procurement budgeting
- Construction cost estimation
- Contingency and escalation allowances
- Cost baseline and S-curve development
- Earned value management implementation

#### Module 8: Risk Identification & Quantitative Analysis
- Risk identification techniques (brainstorming, Delphi, SWOT)
- Qualitative risk assessment (probability/impact matrix)
- Quantitative methods (Monte Carlo simulation, decision trees)
- Technology and performance risks in CEA
- Supply chain and vendor risks
- Regulatory and permitting risks

#### Module 9: Quality Management & Technical Assurance
- Quality planning and standards definition
- Inspection and test plans (ITP)
- Factory acceptance testing (FAT)
- Site acceptance testing (SAT)
- Commissioning quality requirements
- Non-conformance management and corrective action

#### Module 10: Procurement Strategy & Vendor Management
- Make-or-buy analysis
- Procurement packaging strategies
- RFP/RFQ development and evaluation
- Contract types (lump sum, unit price, cost-plus, GMP)
- Vendor qualification and performance management
- International procurement considerations

#### Module 11: Construction Management & Site Coordination
- Construction execution planning
- Trade coordination and logistics
- Safety management and site security
- Quality control and inspection programs
- Change order management
- Progress monitoring and reporting

#### Module 12: Commissioning, Startup & Handover
- Commissioning planning and sequencing
- Systems integration testing
- Performance validation and optimization
- Operational readiness reviews
- Training and knowledge transfer
- Warranty and maintenance documentation
- Substantial completion and final acceptance

#### Module 13: Post-Project Evaluation & Lessons Learned
- Project performance assessment
- Financial performance vs. baseline
- Technical performance vs. requirements
- Stakeholder satisfaction surveys
- Root cause analysis of variances
- Knowledge management and documentation
- Continuous improvement recommendations

#### Module 14: Agile & Hybrid Approaches for CEA Projects
- Agile principles in capital project context
- Scrum for R&D and technology development
- Hybrid waterfall-agile methodologies
- Iterative design and prototyping
- Managing changing requirements
- Integration with traditional construction management
