# Glossary: CEA Project Management Technical Terms

## A

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Specific, measurable standards that deliverables must meet to be accepted by the customer or stakeholder.

**Actual Cost (AC)**
- Total costs incurred for work performed on an activity or project during a specific time period. Also called Actual Cost of Work Performed (ACWP).

**Agile**
- Project management approach that uses iterative development, frequent delivery, and continuous stakeholder feedback.

**Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ)**
- Organization, office, or individual responsible for enforcing codes and approving equipment, materials, installation, or procedures (e.g., building inspector, fire marshal).

---

## B

**Backward Pass**
- Critical path method technique that calculates late finish and late start dates by working backwards through the schedule from the completion date.

**Baseline**
- Approved version of work product that can only be changed through formal change control procedures. Used as comparison basis for performance measurement.

**Budget at Completion (BAC)**
- Sum of all budgets allocated to the project. The total planned cost.

**Building Management System (BMS)**
- Computer-based control system that monitors and controls building mechanical and electrical equipment (HVAC, lighting, power, etc.). Also called SCADA.

---

## C

**Capital Expenditure (CapEx)**
- Funds used to acquire or upgrade physical assets such as property, buildings, or equipment.

**Change Order**
- Written order directing the contractor to make changes in the work within the general scope of the contract.

**Commissioning**
- Systematic process of verifying and documenting that all facility systems perform interactively according to design intent and owner's operational needs.

**Contingency Reserve**
- Budget allocated for identified risks (known-unknowns). Managed by project manager.

**Cost Performance Index (CPI)**
- Measure of cost efficiency calculated as Earned Value divided by Actual Cost (CPI = EV/AC).

**Cost Variance (CV)**
- Difference between earned value and actual cost (CV = EV - AC).

**Crashing**
- Schedule compression technique where additional resources are added to critical path activities to reduce duration, typically increasing cost.

**Critical Path**
- Longest sequence of activities in a project schedule network, determining the shortest possible project duration. Activities on critical path have zero float.

**Critical Path Method (CPM)**
- Schedule network analysis technique used to determine minimum project duration and amount of schedule flexibility.

---

## D

**Daily Stand-up**
- Brief daily team meeting (typically 15 minutes) where members share progress, plans for the day, and obstacles.

**Deliverable**
- Unique and verifiable product, result, or capability produced to complete a process, phase, or project.

**Dependency**
- Relationship in which a schedule activity relies on the completion of another activity.

**Design-Build**
- Project delivery method where design and construction services are contracted with a single entity.

---

## E

**Earned Value (EV)**
- Value of work actually completed, expressed in terms of the approved budget. Also called Budgeted Cost of Work Performed (BCWP).

**Earned Value Management (EVM)**
- Methodology that integrates scope, schedule, and cost to measure project performance against baseline.

**Estimate at Completion (EAC)**
- Expected total cost of completing all project work, calculated based on project performance to date.

**Estimate to Complete (ETC)**
- Expected cost to finish all remaining project work.

---

## F

**Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)**
- Testing conducted at vendor's facility to verify equipment meets specifications before shipment.

**Fast-Tracking**
- Schedule compression technique where activities normally done in sequence are performed in parallel.

**Feasibility Study**
- Assessment of the practicality and viability of a proposed project from technical, operational, and economic perspectives.

**Finish-to-Start (FS)**
- Dependency where successor activity cannot start until predecessor activity has finished. Most common dependency type.

**Float**
- Amount of time an activity can be delayed without delaying the project finish date (total float) or subsequent activity (free float).

**Forward Pass**
- Critical path method technique that calculates early start and early finish dates by working forward through the schedule.

**Functional Requirement**
- Description of what a product or system must be capable of doing.

---

## G

**Gantt Chart**
- Bar chart showing project schedule with activities listed on vertical axis and time on horizontal axis.

**Gate Review**
- Formal approval point in phase-gate process where continuation is decided based on established criteria.

**General Contractor (GC)**
- Prime contractor responsible for overall construction project coordination and completion.

**Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP)**
- Contract type where contractor is reimbursed for actual costs plus a fee, but total cost cannot exceed agreed maximum.

---

## H

**Hold Point**
- Mandatory inspection or verification point where work cannot proceed until formal approval/release is received.

**HVAC**
- Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems.

**Hybrid Project Management**
- Approach combining elements of both traditional (waterfall) and agile methodologies.

---

## I

**Inspection and Test Plan (ITP)**
- Document defining all inspections and tests required, including methods, criteria, and hold points.

**Integrated Project Delivery (IPD)**
- Collaborative project delivery approach that integrates people, systems, and practices into a process that harnesses talents of all participants.

**Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
- Discount rate at which Net Present Value of project equals zero. Higher IRR indicates more profitable investment.

---

## K

**Kanban**
- Visual system for managing work as it moves through a process, highlighting potential bottlenecks.

**Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**
- Measurable value demonstrating how effectively objectives are being achieved.

---

## L

**Lessons Learned**
- Knowledge gained during project that shows how events were addressed or should be addressed in the future.

**Life Safety Systems**
- Building systems critical to occupant safety (fire alarm, sprinklers, emergency egress, etc.).

**Liquidated Damages**
- Contractually agreed amount paid by contractor for each day of delay beyond specified completion date.

**Long-Lead Item**
- Material or equipment with extended procurement or fabrication time requiring early ordering.

---

## M

**Management Reserve**
- Budget allocated for unforeseeable work within project scope (unknown-unknowns). Managed by executive management.

**Matrix Organization**
- Organizational structure where project managers share resources and authority with functional managers.

**Milestone**
- Significant point or event in the project schedule, typically with zero duration.

**Monte Carlo Simulation**
- Technique that performs project simulation many times to calculate distribution of possible outcomes.

---

## N

**Net Present Value (NPV)**
- Value of expected future cash flows in today's dollars, calculated by discounting at appropriate rate.

**Non-Conformance**
- Deficiency in characteristic, documentation, or procedure that renders quality of item unacceptable or indeterminate.

---

## O

**Operating Expenditure (OpEx)**
- Ongoing costs for running a business, product, or system (as opposed to capital expenditure).

**Operations & Maintenance (O&M) Manual**
- Documentation providing information needed to properly operate and maintain facility systems and equipment.

---

## P

**Percent Plan Complete (PPC)**
- Lean construction metric measuring reliability of weekly work planning.

**Phase-Gate Process**
- Project management approach dividing project into distinct phases separated by decision gates.

**Planned Value (PV)**
- Authorized budget assigned to scheduled work. Also called Budgeted Cost of Work Scheduled (BCWS).

**PMBOK (Project Management Body of Knowledge)**
- PMI's collection of processes and knowledge areas generally accepted as best practice.

**PMI (Project Management Institute)**
- Professional organization for project management profession.

**PMP (Project Management Professional)**
- Professional certification offered by PMI.

**Procurement**
- Process of obtaining products, services, or results from outside the project team.

**Project Charter**
- Document issued by project sponsor that formally authorizes project and gives project manager authority.

**Punch List**
- List of incomplete or deficient items to be completed before final project acceptance.

---

## Q

**Quality Assurance (QA)**
- Process-oriented approach to prevent defects through planned and systematic activities.

**Quality Control (QC)**
- Product-oriented approach to identify defects through inspections and testing.

---

## R

**RACI Matrix**
- Responsibility assignment matrix defining roles (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) for each task.

**Request for Information (RFI)**
- Formal request from contractor to designer to clarify or obtain information about contract documents.

**Request for Proposal (RFP)**
- Document used to solicit proposals from prospective vendors or contractors.

**Resource Leveling**
- Schedule network analysis technique to address resource over-allocations, typically by delaying activities within their float.

**Retainage**
- Portion of contract payment withheld until project completion to ensure contractor completes all work (typically 5-10%).

**Risk**
- Uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has positive or negative effect on project objectives.

**Risk Register**
- Document listing identified risks, their characteristics, and planned responses.

---

## S

**Schedule Performance Index (SPI)**
- Measure of schedule efficiency calculated as Earned Value divided by Planned Value (SPI = EV/PV).

**Schedule Variance (SV)**
- Difference between earned value and planned value (SV = EV - PV).

**Scope**
- Sum of products, services, and results to be provided as a project.

**Scope Creep**
- Uncontrolled expansion of project scope without adjustments to time, cost, and resources.

**Scrum**
- Agile framework using iterative cycles (sprints) to deliver working product increments.

**Site Acceptance Test (SAT)**
- Testing conducted at project site to verify installed systems meet specifications under actual operating conditions.

**SMART Objectives**
- Goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

**Stakeholder**
- Individual, group, or organization that may affect, be affected by, or perceive itself to be affected by project decision or outcome.

**Steering Committee**
- Group of senior stakeholders responsible for providing guidance and making key project decisions.

**Substantial Completion**
- Stage of project when work is sufficiently complete for owner to occupy and use for intended purpose.

---

## T

**Technical Requirement**
- Specification describing how a product or system will be built or how it will perform.

**Three-Point Estimate**
- Estimating technique using three values: optimistic, most likely, and pessimistic.

**To-Complete Performance Index (TCPI)**
- Calculated cost performance index needed on remaining work to achieve BAC or EAC.

**Total Float**
- Amount of time activity can be delayed without delaying project completion date.

**Turnkey Project**
- Project delivered complete and ready for immediate use by owner.

---

## V

**Variance**
- Quantifiable deviation from known baseline or expected value.

**Variance Analysis**
- Technique for determining cause and degree of difference between baseline and actual performance.

**Variance at Completion (VAC)**
- Projection of budget surplus or deficit at project completion (VAC = BAC - EAC).

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)**
- Electronic device that controls motor speed by varying frequency and voltage of power supplied to motor.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)**
- Difference between amount of moisture in air and how much it can hold when saturated. Critical for plant transpiration.

---

## W

**Warranty Period**
- Time period after substantial completion during which contractor must repair or replace defective work.

**WBS (Work Breakdown Structure)**
- Hierarchical decomposition of total scope of work into manageable components.

**WBS Dictionary**
- Document providing detailed information about each WBS element (description, deliverables, resources, etc.).

**Work Package**
- Lowest level of WBS at which cost and schedule can be estimated and managed.

## CEA-Specific Terms

**Crop Cycle**
- Time from seeding/transplanting to harvest for a specific crop.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)**
- Total amount of photosynthetically active radiation received per day (mol/m²/day).

**Fertigation**
- Application of fertilizers through irrigation system.

**Growing Media**
- Substrate used to support plant roots (rockwool, coconut coir, perlite, etc.).

**Microgreens**
- Young vegetable greens harvested 7-21 days after germination.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)**
- Measure of light intensity at plant canopy (μmol/m²/s).

**Vertical Farm**
- Indoor agriculture facility using vertically stacked growing layers under artificial lighting.
