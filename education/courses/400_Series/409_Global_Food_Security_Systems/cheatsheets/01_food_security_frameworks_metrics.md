## Key Metrics Quick Reference

### Prevalence of Undernourishment (PoU)
- % of population with dietary energy below minimum requirement
- Global (2023): ~9.2% (735 million people)

### Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES)
- **Food Secure:** None of 8 indicators present
- **Mild:** Worried about food, limited variety
- **Moderate:** Skipped meals, reduced intake
- **Severe:** Went hungry, didn't eat for whole days
- Global severe food insecurity: ~11.3% (900 million)

### Global Hunger Index (GHI)
- Composite: Undernourishment + Child stunting + Child wasting + Child mortality
- **Scale:** 0-9.9 (low), 10-19.9 (moderate), 20-34.9 (serious), 35-49.9 (alarming), ≥50 (extremely alarming)

### Malnutrition Indicators
- **Stunting (children <5):** 148 million (22.3%)
- **Wasting (children <5):** 45 million (6.8%)
- **Anemia (women reproductive age):** 571 million

## CEA Contribution to Food Security Pillars

### AVAILABILITY ✓✓✓
- **10-20x yields** per area vs. field agriculture
- **Year-round production** (12+ cycles/year vs. 1-2)
- **Climate-independent** (no crop failures from weather)
- **Limitations:** Currently focused on vegetables/herbs, not staples

### ACCESS ✓✓
- **Urban production** reduces transport costs and distances
- **Price stability** from consistent supply
- **Local jobs** created (20-40 per hectare equivalent)
- **Challenges:** Higher costs than field agriculture (currently)

### UTILIZATION ✓✓✓
- **High nutritional quality** (vitamins, minerals)
- **No pesticides** required (controlled environment)
- **Food safety** easier to control
- **Freshness** (harvest to consumer in hours, not days)

### STABILITY ✓✓✓
- **Climate resilience** (protected from droughts, floods, extreme weather)
- **Consistent supply** (no seasonal gaps)
- **Disaster resilient** (rapid deployment possible)
- **Predictable economics** (less volatility)

**Overall:** CEA excels at nutritious fresh produce but cannot replace staple calories

## Climate Change Impacts on Agriculture

### Temperature
- **Current:** +1.1-1.2°C above pre-industrial
- **Projections 2050:** +1.6-2.4°C (scenario dependent)
- **Yield impacts:** -6% per °C above optimum (wheat), -3.2% (rice), -7.4% (maize)

### Extreme Events
- **Heat waves:** +50% frequency since 1980s
- **Droughts:** +29% frequency since 2000
- **Floods:** +134% since 1980s
- **Agricultural disaster losses:** $200B+ annually

### Regional Vulnerabilities (Highest Risk)
1. **Sub-Saharan Africa:** -20 to -50% maize yields by 2050
2. **South Asia:** -10 to -30% rice yields by 2050
3. **Small Island Developing States:** 10-50% arable land loss
4. **Mediterranean:** -20 to -40% precipitation decrease

## Key Terminology

- **Food Security:** Physical, economic, social access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food
- **Malnutrition:** Undernutrition, micronutrient deficiencies, or overweight/obesity
- **Hidden Hunger:** Micronutrient deficiencies affecting 2+ billion globally
- **Food Desert:** Area with limited access to affordable, nutritious food
- **Food Sovereignty:** Right to healthy, culturally appropriate, sustainably produced food
- **Nutrition Transition:** Shift from traditional to Western diets (more processed, animal products)
