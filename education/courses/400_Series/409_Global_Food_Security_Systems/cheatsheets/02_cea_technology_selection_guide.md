## Decision Tree for Technology Selection

```
START: What is your primary objective?
│
├─ Commercial production, maximum profit
│  └─ Urban location? YES → Vertical farm or rooftop greenhouse
│     NO → High-tech greenhouse (peri-urban)
│
├─ Community food security, affordable
│  └─ High capital available? YES → Community greenhouse
│     NO → Simple greenhouse or open-field hydro
│
├─ Disaster/emergency response
│  └─ Rapid deployment needed? YES → Container farms
│     NO → Modular greenhouses
│
├─ Extreme climate (desert, Arctic)
│  └─ Energy available? YES → Vertical farm (climate control)
│     NO → Greenhouse with passive design
│
├─ Protein + vegetables needed
│  └─ Technical capacity? YES → Aquaponics
│     NO → Greenhouse + separate protein source
│
└─ Demonstration/education
   └─ Multiple technologies for learning
```

## Climate Suitability Guide

### Desert/Arid (High heat, low humidity, minimal rainfall)
**Recommended:** High-tech greenhouse OR Vertical farm
**Key Features:**
- Evaporative cooling (greenhouses)
- AC climate control (vertical farms)
- Closed-loop water recycling
- Solar PV energy
- Shading and reflective materials

### Arctic/Cold (Extreme cold, limited sunlight, permafrost)
**Recommended:** Insulated greenhouse OR Container farm
**Key Features:**
- R-30 to R-50 insulation
- Triple/quadruple glazing
- LED lighting (winter darkness)
- Waste heat utilization
- Permafrost-appropriate foundations

### Tropical (High heat, high humidity, heavy rainfall)
**Recommended:** Greenhouse with excellent ventilation
**Key Features:**
- Maximum natural ventilation
- Humidity control (disease prevention)
- Shade cloth (reduce solar load)
- Elevated structures (flood risk)
- Disease-resistant varieties

### Temperate (Moderate, seasonal variation)
**Recommended:** Any greenhouse type (most versatile climate)
**Key Features:**
- Seasonal climate control
- Heating (winter), cooling (summer)
- Natural light maximization
- Thermal curtains

### Urban Core (Limited space, high land costs)
**Recommended:** Vertical farm OR Rooftop greenhouse
**Key Features:**
- Minimal land footprint
- Vertical stacking
- Building integration
- Proximity to consumers

## Scale Recommendations

### Home/Community Scale (<100 m²)
- **Technology:** Simple greenhouse, small container
- **Production:** 500-2,000 kg/year
- **Serves:** 5-20 families (supplemental)
- **Investment:** $5,000-$25,000

### Small Commercial (100-1,000 m²)
- **Technology:** Mid-tech greenhouse, container cluster
- **Production:** 5-50 tonnes/year
- **Serves:** 200-2,000 people OR niche market
- **Investment:** $50,000-$500,000

### Medium Commercial (1,000-10,000 m²)
- **Technology:** High-tech greenhouse, small vertical farm
- **Production:** 50-500 tonnes/year
- **Serves:** 2,000-20,000 people OR wholesale market
- **Investment:** $0.5M-$10M

### Large Commercial (>10,000 m²)
- **Technology:** Greenhouse cluster, large vertical farm
- **Production:** >500 tonnes/year
- **Serves:** City-scale, export markets
- **Investment:** $10M-$100M+

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Choosing highest-tech when context doesn't support it**
- High-tech requires ongoing technical support, spare parts, energy
- If these aren't available reliably, system will fail

❌ **Underestimating operating costs**
- Capital cost is one-time; operating costs are ongoing
- Energy, labor, inputs add up quickly

❌ **Ignoring climate specifics**
- One-size-fits-all designs often fail
- Adapt technology to local climate conditions

❌ **Overlooking training and capacity**
- Technology without knowledge = failure
- Invest in operator training from day one

❌ **Mismatching scale to market**
- Building production capacity without market leads to waste
- Secure off-take before building large facilities

❌ **Neglecting renewable energy integration**
- High energy costs kill profitability
- Solar PV increasingly cost-effective for CEA

## Deployment Timeline Expectations

### Basic Greenhouse
- **Planning:** 1-2 months
- **Construction:** 2-4 weeks
- **Startup:** 1-2 weeks
- **First harvest:** 4-6 weeks after planting
- **Full production:** 2-3 months

### High-Tech Greenhouse
- **Planning:** 3-6 months
- **Construction:** 3-6 months
- **Startup:** 1-2 months (system tuning)
- **First harvest:** 4-6 weeks after planting
- **Full production:** 6-9 months

### Vertical Farm
- **Planning:** 6-12 months
- **Construction:** 6-12 months
- **Startup:** 2-3 months (technology integration)
- **First harvest:** 3-5 weeks (fast-growing crops)
- **Full production:** 9-18 months

### Container Farm (Emergency)
- **Mobilization:** 24-48 hours
- **Transport:** 1-7 days
- **Installation:** 1-2 days
- **First harvest:** 21-35 days
- **Full production:** 2-3 months

**Remember: The best technology is the one that is:**
1. **Appropriate for context** (climate, resources, capacity)
2. **Economically sustainable** (revenue > operating costs)
3. **Technically manageable** (can be operated and maintained)
4. **Aligned with objectives** (food security, commercial, education, etc.)

**Not necessarily the most advanced or expensive technology!**
