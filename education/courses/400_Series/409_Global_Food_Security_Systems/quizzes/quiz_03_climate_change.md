## Questions

### Question 1
What is the projected global temperature increase by 2100 under current emission trajectories?

A) 0.5-1.0°C
B) 1.5-2.0°C
C) 2.5-4.0°C
D) 5.0-7.0°C

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Under current emission trajectories, global temperatures are projected to increase 2.5-4.0°C by 2100, with significant impacts on agriculture.

---

### Question 2
Which agricultural region is most vulnerable to climate change impacts?

A) Temperate zones with abundant water
B) Tropical and sub-tropical regions, small island states
C) Arctic regions
D) Mediterranean climates

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Tropical and subtropical regions, along with small island developing states, are most vulnerable due to limited adaptive capacity and exposure to extreme weather.

---

### Question 3
How does climate change affect crop yields in many regions?

A) Always increases yields due to CO₂ fertilization
B) Decreases yields through heat stress, water scarcity, and extreme weather
C) No significant effect on yields
D) Only affects livestock, not crops

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Climate change generally decreases yields in vulnerable regions through heat stress, water scarcity, changing precipitation patterns, and extreme weather events.

---

### Question 4
What percentage of global freshwater withdrawals does agriculture account for?

A) 15-25%
B) 40-50%
C) 70-80%
D) 90-95%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Agriculture accounts for approximately 70-80% of global freshwater withdrawals, making it highly vulnerable to water scarcity from climate change.

---

### Question 5
Which crop category is most sensitive to heat stress during critical growth stages?

A) Root vegetables
B) Leafy greens
C) Cereals during flowering and grain fill
D) Perennial fruits

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Cereals (wheat, rice, maize) are particularly sensitive to heat stress during flowering and grain filling, which can drastically reduce yields.

---

### Question 6
What is the primary advantage of CEA in addressing climate change impacts?

A) CEA eliminates all climate risks
B) CEA provides controlled environments independent of external weather
C) CEA requires no energy inputs
D) CEA only works in cold climates

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA creates controlled environments that are largely independent of external weather conditions, providing climate resilience.

---

### Question 7
How do extreme weather events affect food security?

A) They have minimal impact
B) They disrupt production, supply chains, and increase price volatility
C) They only affect luxury foods
D) They improve food security through increased awareness

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Extreme weather events disrupt agricultural production, damage infrastructure, disrupt supply chains, and create price volatility, undermining food security.

---

### Question 8
What is the relationship between climate change and pest/disease pressure?

A) Climate change reduces pest problems
B) Climate change expands pest ranges and increases disease pressure
C) No relationship exists
D) Affects only tropical pests

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Climate change expands the geographic range of many pests and diseases and can increase their reproduction rates, intensifying agricultural challenges.

---

### Question 9
Which adaptation strategy is most effective for smallholder farmers?

A) Relocating to different regions
B) Climate-smart agriculture practices and crop diversification
C) Abandoning farming
D) Growing only luxury crops

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Climate-smart agriculture practices, including crop diversification, improved water management, and resilient varieties, are most effective and accessible for smallholders.

---

### Question 10
What is the estimated reduction in global agricultural productivity by 2050 due to climate change without adaptation?

A) 1-3%
B) 5-10%
C) 10-25%
D) 40-50%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Without significant adaptation measures, climate change could reduce global agricultural productivity by 10-25% by 2050, varying by region and crop.

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - 2.5-4.0°C projected increase by 2100
2. B - Tropical/subtropical regions most vulnerable
3. B - Climate change decreases yields through multiple stressors
4. C - Agriculture uses 70-80% of freshwater
5. C - Cereals most sensitive during flowering/grain fill
6. B - CEA provides climate-independent controlled environment
7. B - Extreme weather disrupts production and supply chains
8. B - Climate expands pest ranges and disease pressure
9. B - Climate-smart practices most effective for smallholders
10. C - 10-25% productivity reduction possible without adaptation
