## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary food security challenge during humanitarian crises?

A) Food preferences
B) Rapid disruption of production and supply chains
C) Over-production
D) Export limitations

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Humanitarian crises rapidly disrupt both local food production and supply chain infrastructure, creating immediate food access challenges.

---

### Question 2
What is the typical deployment timeframe for container-based emergency CEA systems?

A) 6-12 months
B) 2-4 months
C) Days to weeks
D) Years

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Containerized CEA systems are designed for rapid deployment within days to weeks, providing quick emergency food production.

---

### Question 3
Which crop type is prioritized in emergency CEA deployments?

A) Grains and rice
B) Fast-growing, nutrient-dense greens and microgreens
C) Tree fruits
D) Root vegetables only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Fast-growing crops like microgreens, sprouts, and leafy greens provide rapid nutrition and can be harvested within days to weeks.

---

### Question 4
What is a key advantage of CEA over emergency food aid in prolonged crises?

A) Lower initial cost
B) Local production reducing logistics and providing sustainable nutrition
C) No setup time required
D) Works without any infrastructure

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA enables local production, reducing complex logistics, providing fresh nutrition, and creating sustainable food sources for prolonged crises.

---

### Question 5
What percentage of global refugees remain in displacement for more than 5 years?

A) <10%
B) 25%
C) 50%
D) >75%

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** More than 75% of refugees remain displaced for more than 5 years, making sustainable food production systems essential rather than temporary aid.

---

### Question 6
Which energy source is most practical for emergency CEA systems in remote locations?

A) Grid electricity only
B) Solar with battery backup
C) Nuclear generators
D) No power needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Solar energy with battery backup provides reliable, deployable power for CEA systems in locations with damaged or absent grid infrastructure.

---

### Question 7
What is the primary nutritional benefit of fresh greens in emergency contexts?

A) High calories
B) Vitamins, minerals, and dietary diversity in ration-dependent diets
C) Protein content
D) Long shelf life

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Fresh greens provide essential vitamins, minerals, and dietary diversity that are often lacking in emergency rations and preserved foods.

---

### Question 8
What role does CEA play in disaster preparedness planning?

A) Complete food security solution
B) Complementary resilience infrastructure for critical facilities
C) Decoration only
D) No role in preparedness

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA serves as complementary resilience infrastructure for hospitals, shelters, and critical facilities to maintain fresh food access during disruptions.

---

### Question 9
Which international organization has piloted hydroponic systems in refugee camps?

A) NATO
B) World Bank only
C) UNHCR and WFP
D) IMF

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** UNHCR and the World Food Programme have piloted hydroponic and aquaponic systems in refugee camps to provide sustainable nutrition.

---

### Question 10
What is the estimated fresh vegetable production capacity of a 40-foot container CEA system?

A) 10-20 kg per week
B) 50-100 kg per week
C) 200-400 kg per week
D) 1000+ kg per week

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A well-designed 40-foot container CEA system can produce approximately 200-400 kg of fresh vegetables per week, depending on crop selection.

7. B - Vitamins, minerals, and dietary diversity primary benefit
