## Questions

### Question 1
According to the FAO definition, food security exists when:

A) There is sufficient food production globally
B) All people, at all times, have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe food
C) Food prices are stable and affordable
D) Agricultural productivity is increasing

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The FAO definition emphasizes universal access (all people), temporal stability (at all times), multiple dimensions (physical, social, economic access), and quality (sufficient, safe, nutritious food that meets dietary needs).

---

### Question 2
Which of the four pillars of food security focuses on proper biological use of food, requiring clean water and sanitation?

A) Availability
B) Access
C) Utilization
D) Stability

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Food Utilization concerns the proper biological use of food, which requires adequate diet, clean water, sanitation, healthcare, and food preparation knowledge.

---

### Question 3
Approximately how many people globally were experiencing hunger in 2022-2023?

A) 250 million
B) 485 million
C) 735 million
D) 1.2 billion

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** According to recent FAO data, approximately 735 million people (9.2% of global population) were experiencing hunger in 2022-2023, representing an increase since 2015.

---

### Question 4
The Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES) categorizes food insecurity into how many severity levels?

A) Two (food secure vs. insecure)
B) Three (mild, moderate, severe)
C) Four (food secure, mild, moderate, severe)
D) Five (very low to very high food security)

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** FIES categorizes into four levels: Food secure (none of the 8 indicators), Mild (worried about food, limited variety), Moderate (skipping meals, reducing intake), and Severe (going hungry, not eating for days).

---

### Question 5
Which region currently has the highest prevalence of undernourishment?

A) Asia
B) Africa
C) Latin America and Caribbean
D) Oceania

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Africa has the highest prevalence of undernourishment at approximately 19.7%, significantly higher than other regions.

---

### Question 6
Which of the following is a PRIMARY driver of food insecurity at the basic cause level?

A) Inadequate dietary intake
B) Disease
C) Poverty
D) Limited agricultural production

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** In the causality framework, poverty is a basic/root cause of food insecurity. Inadequate intake and disease are immediate causes, while limited production is an underlying cause.

---

### Question 7
CEA addresses which pillar of food security LEAST effectively in its current state?

A) Availability of fresh vegetables
B) Availability of staple calories (wheat, rice, maize)
C) Stability of fresh vegetable supply
D) Utilization through improved nutritional quality

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA currently excels at producing fresh vegetables and herbs but cannot economically produce staple calories like grains. It contributes excellently to availability, stability, and utilization of vegetables but not staple crops.

---

### Question 8
What percentage of global population is moderately or severely food insecure?

A) 9.2%
B) 18.5%
C) 29.6%
D) 41.3%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Approximately 2.4 billion people out of 8.1 billion globally (29.6%) are moderately or severely food insecure, meaning they lack regular access to sufficient, nutritious food.

---

### Question 9
The "stability" pillar of food security primarily concerns:

A) Political stability in food-producing regions
B) Ability to obtain food over time without risk from shocks
C) Stable food prices
D) Consistent dietary preferences

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Stability pillar focuses on maintaining food security over time, not losing access due to sudden shocks (disasters, conflicts, price spikes) or cyclical events (seasons, economic cycles).

---

### Question 10
Which statement about CEA's contribution to food security is MOST accurate?

A) CEA can completely replace traditional agriculture for food security
B) CEA addresses nutrition (micronutrients) better than calories (staples)
C) CEA is only viable in wealthy countries with high technology capacity
D) CEA's high water use makes it unsuitable for water-scarce regions

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA excels at producing nutrient-dense vegetables and herbs (micronutrients) but cannot currently economically replace staple crops for calories. Statement A is false (CEA complements, not replaces), C is false (viable in many contexts including developing countries), and D is false (CEA uses 90-95% less water than traditional agriculture).
