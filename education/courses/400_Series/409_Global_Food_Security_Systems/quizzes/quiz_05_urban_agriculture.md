## Questions

### Question 1
What percentage of global food production currently comes from urban agriculture?

A) 1-3%
B) 5-10%
C) 15-20%
D) 30-40%

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Urban agriculture currently contributes approximately 5-10% of global food production, though this varies significantly by region and city.

---

### Question 2
Which urban agriculture model provides the highest production per square meter?

A) Rooftop gardens
B) Community gardens
C) Vertical farms with CEA technology
D) Urban orchards

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Vertical farms using CEA technology achieve the highest production density through multi-layer stacking and year-round production.

---

### Question 3
What is the primary barrier to urban agriculture expansion?

A) Lack of sunlight
B) Land costs and zoning restrictions
C) Water availability
D) Consumer demand

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** High land costs in urban areas and restrictive zoning regulations are the primary barriers to urban agriculture expansion.

---

### Question 4
Which food category is most suitable for urban production?

A) Wheat and rice
B) Beef cattle
C) Leafy greens, herbs, and microgreens
D) Tree fruits

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Fast-growing, high-value crops like leafy greens, herbs, and microgreens are most suitable for urban production due to space and economic constraints.

---

### Question 5
What is a key social benefit of community gardens in urban areas?

A) Maximum production efficiency
B) Community building, education, and food access for low-income residents
C) Industrial-scale employment
D) Export production

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Community gardens provide important social benefits including community cohesion, education, therapeutic activities, and food access, beyond just production.

---

### Question 6
What building type is most commonly converted for urban CEA facilities?

A) Residential homes
B) Warehouses and industrial buildings
C) Office towers
D) Shopping malls

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Warehouses and industrial buildings are most commonly converted to CEA facilities due to suitable structure, height, and lower costs than other urban real estate.

---

### Question 7
What is the typical transportation distance reduction for urban-grown produce?

A) 10-20%
B) 30-50%
C) 60-80%
D) 90-99%

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Urban-grown produce can reduce "food miles" by 90-99% compared to rural-to-urban supply chains, significantly reducing emissions and improving freshness.

---

### Question 8
Which urban agriculture policy support is most effective?

A) Complete deregulation
B) Tax incentives, zoning allowances, and technical assistance
C) Mandatory quotas
D) No government involvement

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A combination of tax incentives, flexible zoning, and technical assistance programs most effectively supports urban agriculture development.

---

### Question 9
What is aquaponics' primary advantage in urban settings?

A) Lowest capital cost
B) Integrated fish and vegetable production maximizing space efficiency
C) Simplest operation
D) No electricity requirement

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Aquaponics integrates fish and vegetable production in a symbiotic system, maximizing productivity from limited urban space.

---

### Question 10
What percentage of a city's fresh vegetable needs could realistically be met by urban CEA?

A) 100% of all vegetables
B) 50-80% of leafy greens and herbs
C) <5% of vegetables
D) All food needs including grains

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Urban CEA could realistically meet 50-80% of a city's leafy greens and herbs demand, though not all vegetables or staple crops.

---
