## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary barrier to CEA technology adoption in developing countries?

A) Lack of interest
B) High capital costs and limited access to financing
C) Too much rainfall
D) Cultural food preferences only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** High upfront capital costs and limited access to appropriate financing are the primary barriers to CEA adoption in developing economies.

---

### Question 2
What is "appropriate technology" in the context of international development?

A) The most advanced technology available
B) Technology suited to local context, skills, and resources
C) The cheapest option only
D) Technology from developed countries only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Appropriate technology is suited to the local economic, social, cultural, and environmental context, ensuring sustainability and adoption.

---

### Question 3
What is the most effective model for technology transfer?

A) Donating equipment without training
B) Integrated approach with training, ongoing support, and local adaptation
C) One-time demonstration
D) Written manuals only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Effective technology transfer requires integrated training, ongoing technical support, local adaptation, and capacity building for sustainability.

---

### Question 4
What role do local champions play in technology adoption?

A) No significant role
B) Early adopters who demonstrate success and mentor others
C) Government officials only
D) Foreign consultants

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Local champions who successfully adopt and demonstrate technology play critical roles in peer-to-peer learning and broader adoption.

---

### Question 5
What percentage of international development projects fail within 2 years without ongoing support?

A) <10%
B) 20-30%
C) 50-70%
D) >90%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Studies show that 50-70% of development projects fail within 2 years without sustained technical support and capacity building.

---

### Question 6
Which training approach is most effective for agricultural technology transfer?

A) Classroom lectures only
B) Hands-on practical training with demonstration sites
C) Online videos only
D) Written instructions only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Hands-on practical training with demonstration sites allows learning by doing and provides visible proof of concept for adoption.

---

### Question 7
What is the value of incorporating local knowledge in technology adaptation?

A) No value; use standard designs only
B) Ensures cultural relevance and increases adoption success
C) Slows down implementation
D) Reduces technology effectiveness

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Incorporating local knowledge ensures cultural relevance, addresses context-specific challenges, and significantly increases adoption rates.

---

### Question 8
What financing model has proven most effective for smallholder CEA adoption?

A) Large bank loans only
B) Microfinance with technical assistance
C) No financing needed
D) Foreign direct grants only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Microfinance combined with technical assistance provides accessible capital with support for successful implementation at small scale.

---

### Question 9
What is a key component of sustainable capacity building?

A) One-time training event
B) Train-the-trainer programs creating local expertise
C) Foreign experts permanently operating systems
D) No follow-up needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Train-the-trainer programs create local expertise and knowledge networks that sustain technology adoption beyond initial implementation.

---

### Question 10
Which partnership model is most sustainable for long-term technology transfer?

A) Donor-dependent charity model
B) Public-private partnerships with local ownership
C) Complete foreign control
D) No partnerships needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Public-private partnerships that emphasize local ownership and capacity create sustainable, self-sufficient technology adoption.

---

7. B - Local knowledge ensures relevance and adoption

10. B - Public-private partnerships with local ownership
