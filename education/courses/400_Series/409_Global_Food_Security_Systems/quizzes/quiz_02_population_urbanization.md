## Questions

### Question 1
What is the projected global population by 2050 according to UN estimates?

A) 8.5 billion
B) 9.7 billion
C) 11.2 billion
D) 12.5 billion

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** UN projections estimate the global population will reach approximately 9.7 billion by 2050, requiring significant increases in food production capacity.

---

### Question 2
What percentage of the global population is expected to live in urban areas by 2050?

A) 50%
B) 60%
C) 68%
D) 75%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Approximately 68% of the global population is projected to live in urban areas by 2050, up from about 55% in 2020.

---

### Question 3
Which region is experiencing the most rapid urbanization?

A) North America
B) Europe
C) Africa and Asia
D) South America

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Africa and Asia are experiencing the most rapid urbanization, with major implications for food security and agricultural systems.

---

### Question 4
What is a "food desert"?

A) Agricultural land affected by drought
B) Urban area with limited access to affordable, nutritious food
C) Desert region unsuitable for farming
D) Area with no restaurants

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Food deserts are urban or rural areas where residents have limited access to affordable and nutritious food, often due to lack of grocery stores or transportation.

---

### Question 5
What percentage of food needs to be transported into typical urban areas?

A) 20-30%
B) 40-50%
C) 60-70%
D) 80-95%

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Most urban areas must import 80-95% of their food from rural and peri-urban agricultural regions, creating vulnerability in supply chains.

---

### Question 6
What is the primary food security challenge posed by urbanization?

A) Increased food waste
B) Loss of agricultural land and extended supply chains
C) Higher food prices only
D) Reduced dietary diversity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Urbanization converts agricultural land to urban use and extends food supply chains, increasing vulnerability and environmental impact.

---

### Question 7
Which demographic factor most impacts future food demand?

A) Average height
B) Life expectancy
C) Population growth and dietary shifts
D) Education levels only

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Population growth combined with dietary shifts toward more resource-intensive foods (especially meat and dairy) drives future food demand.

---

### Question 8
What is the "youth bulge" phenomenon in food security?

A) Young people eating more food
B) Large young populations in developing countries requiring employment and food
C) Children preferring processed foods
D) Teenagers skipping meals

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The youth bulge refers to large populations of young people in many developing countries who will require employment opportunities and access to food.

---

### Question 9
How does CEA address urban food security challenges?

A) By replacing all traditional agriculture
B) By producing fresh vegetables close to consumption points
C) By eliminating food imports
D) By reducing population density

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA provides locally-produced fresh vegetables in urban areas, reducing transportation needs and increasing access to nutritious food.

---

### Question 10
What is the estimated additional food production needed by 2050 to meet demand?

A) 10-20% increase
B) 25-35% increase
C) 50-70% increase
D) 100% increase (doubling)

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Most estimates suggest 50-70% increase in food production will be needed by 2050 to meet population growth and dietary shifts, though estimates vary.

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - 9.7 billion projected by 2050
2. C - 68% urban by 2050
3. C - Africa and Asia fastest urbanization
4. B - Food desert = limited access to nutritious food
5. D - 80-95% of urban food is imported
6. B - Loss of ag land and extended supply chains
7. C - Population growth and dietary shifts
8. B - Youth bulge = large young populations
9. B - CEA produces fresh food close to consumers
10. C - 50-70% increase in production needed
