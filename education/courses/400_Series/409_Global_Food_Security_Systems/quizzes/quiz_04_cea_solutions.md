## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary water use advantage of CEA over traditional field agriculture?

A) 20-30% water reduction
B) 50-60% water reduction
C) 90-95% water reduction
D) No water savings

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** CEA systems typically use 90-95% less water than traditional field agriculture through recirculation and precision delivery.

---

### Question 2
Which crop category is CEA currently MOST economically viable for?

A) Staple grains (wheat, rice, maize)
B) High-value leafy greens and herbs
C) Root vegetables (potatoes, carrots)
D) Tree nuts

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA is currently most economically viable for high-value, fast-growing crops like leafy greens and herbs with short production cycles.

---

### Question 3
What is the typical yield increase for lettuce in CEA versus field production per unit area per year?

A) 2-3x higher
B) 5-7x higher
C) 10-20x higher
D) 50-100x higher

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** CEA lettuce production can achieve 10-20x higher yields per unit area per year through multiple harvests and optimized conditions.

---

### Question 4
What is the main limitation of CEA for addressing global food security?

A) Water requirements
B) Unable to produce staple calories economically at current technology levels
C) Lack of technology
D) Consumer rejection

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA cannot currently produce staple calorie crops (grains) economically, limiting its role to complementary nutrition rather than primary calorie source.

---

### Question 5
Which food security pillar does CEA address MOST effectively?

A) Availability and stability of fresh vegetables year-round
B) Availability of staple grains
C) Cultural food preferences
D) Affordability of all food types

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** CEA excels at providing stable, year-round availability of fresh vegetables, regardless of external weather conditions.

---

### Question 6
What is a key advantage of CEA in urban food security?

A) Lower cost than all other production methods
B) Proximity to consumers reducing transportation and increasing freshness
C) Requires no skilled labor
D) Works without electricity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA located near urban consumers reduces transportation costs, emissions, and time from harvest to consumption, increasing freshness and nutritional value.

---

### Question 7
What percentage of nutrients (vitamins, minerals) does CEA contribute to compared to calories?

A) CEA provides primarily calories, limited nutrients
B) Equal contribution of calories and nutrients
C) CEA provides high nutrients but limited calories
D) CEA provides neither calories nor nutrients

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** CEA excels at producing nutrient-dense vegetables (vitamins, minerals, antioxidants) but cannot economically produce staple calorie crops.

---

### Question 8
Which environmental condition does CEA control MOST effectively?

A) External weather patterns
B) Internal temperature, humidity, light, and CO₂
C) Soil composition only
D) Rainfall patterns

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA provides precise control over internal environmental conditions including temperature, humidity, light, and CO₂, optimizing plant growth.

---

### Question 9
What is the role of CEA in a comprehensive food security strategy?

A) Complete replacement of all traditional agriculture
B) Complementary system providing fresh produce while traditional agriculture provides staples
C) Only for wealthy countries
D) Temporary solution until climate change is reversed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CEA serves as a complementary system providing fresh, nutritious vegetables while traditional agriculture continues to provide staple calories.

---

### Question 10
What is the estimated job creation potential of CEA per hectare compared to traditional field agriculture?

A) 50% fewer jobs
B) Equal employment
C) 3-5x more jobs per hectare
D) 20x more jobs per hectare

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** CEA typically creates 3-5x more jobs per hectare than traditional field agriculture due to intensive production and technical requirements.

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. C - 90-95% water reduction in CEA
2. B - High-value leafy greens most viable
3. C - 10-20x yield increase per area per year
4. B - Cannot produce staple calories economically
5. A - Best for fresh vegetable availability and stability
6. B - Proximity to consumers reduces transportation
7. C - High nutrients, limited calories
8. B - Controls internal environment precisely
9. B - Complementary to traditional agriculture
10. C - 3-5x more jobs per hectare
