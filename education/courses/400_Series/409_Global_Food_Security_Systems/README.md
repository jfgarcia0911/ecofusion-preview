# Course 409: Global Food Security Systems

## Expert/Professional Level (400-Level)

### Course Overview

This comprehensive expert-level course examines the critical intersection of global food security challenges and Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) solutions. Students analyze complex systems affecting food access, availability, and sustainability at regional and global scales, integrating agricultural technology, policy frameworks, economic development, and social equity considerations.

---

## Course Structure

### Duration
- **Total Hours:** 45-50 hours
- **Self-Paced:** 12-16 weeks recommended
- **Instructor-Led:** 10 weeks intensive

### Prerequisites
- Course 312: Climate Change Adaptation for Agriculture (or equivalent)
- Course 313: International CEA Markets (or equivalent)
- Minimum 3 years professional experience in agriculture, food systems, or related field

### Passing Score
75% or higher

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, students will be able to:

1. **Analyze global food security dynamics** using FAO frameworks and metrics
2. **Evaluate CEA's role** in addressing food security across different contexts
3. **Design strategic implementation plans** for CEA deployment
4. **Navigate policy and governance frameworks** for food security
5. **Assess climate change impacts** and design resilient food systems
6. **Conduct economic analysis** including CBA, SROI, and investment proposals
7. **Promote social equity** through inclusive design and implementation
8. **Lead multi-stakeholder initiatives** for food system transformation
