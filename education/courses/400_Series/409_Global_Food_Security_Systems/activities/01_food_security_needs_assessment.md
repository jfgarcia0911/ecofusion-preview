# Activity 1: Food Security Needs Assessment and CEA Feasibility Study

**Course 409: Global Food Security Systems**
**Type:** Strategic Planning Exercise
**Duration:** 3-4 hours
**Format:** Individual or small group (2-4 people)
**Level:** Expert/Professional

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Conduct a comprehensive food security needs assessment
2. Analyze context-specific drivers of food insecurity
3. Evaluate CEA feasibility for a specific location
4. Develop evidence-based recommendations
5. Practice professional assessment and reporting skills

---

## Activity Overview

You will select a real location (city, region, or country) and conduct a complete food security needs assessment to determine whether and how CEA could contribute to food security. Your final deliverable will be a professional feasibility report.

---

## Part 1: Location Selection and Background Research (30-45 minutes)

### Step 1: Choose Your Location

Select ONE of the following (or instructor-approved alternative):

**Option A: Urban Food Desert** - Specific neighborhood in a city
- Example: South Side Chicago, East London, Jakarta informal settlements

**Option B: Climate-Vulnerable Region** - Area affected by climate change
- Example: Sub-Saharan African region, Small Island Developing State, drought-prone area

**Option C: Refugee or Displacement Setting** - Camp or displaced population
- Example: Rohingya camps (Bangladesh), Syrian refugees (Jordan/Lebanon), Venezuelan displacement

**Option D: Extreme Environment** - Harsh climate location
- Example: Arctic community, desert city (UAE, Saudi Arabia), high-altitude region (Tibet, Andes)

**Option E: Rapidly Urbanizing Area** - Fast-growing city
- Example: Nairobi, Dhaka, Lagos, Delhi outskirts

## Evaluation Criteria

Your work will be assessed on:

**Analysis Quality (40%)**
- Comprehensive four-pillar food security assessment
- Thorough CEA feasibility analysis
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Critical thinking and nuance

**Recommendations (30%)**
- Appropriate technology selection
- Realistic phasing and scale
- Viable business model
- Risk awareness and mitigation

**Professionalism (20%)**
- Clear and organized presentation
- Proper use of data and citations
- Executive summary quality
- Writing clarity

**Feasibility and Realism (10%)**
- Context-appropriate solutions
- Realistic assumptions
- Acknowledgment of limitations
- Practical implementation path
