## Critical Soil Health Indicators

### Physical
- **Aggregate Stability:** Slake test score 4-5
- **Infiltration:** >2 inches/hour (good), >4 in/hr (excellent)
- **Bulk Density:** <1.4 g/cm³ (loam), <1.2 g/cm³ (clay)
- **Structure:** Crumbly, friable, earthworm channels visible

### Chemical
- **Organic Matter:** >4% (good), >6% (excellent)
- **pH:** 6.0-7.0 (most crops)
- **CEC:** >15 meq/100g
- **Active Carbon (POXC):** >500 ppm

### Biological
- **Respiration:** >50 ppm CO₂ burst
- **Microbial Biomass:** >500 μg C/g soil
- **Earthworms:** >10 per shovel (excellent)
- **Fungal:Bacterial Ratio:** 1-5 (regenerative target)

## Composting Quick Guide

### Ideal Parameters
- **C:N Ratio:** 25-30:1 (start)
- **Moisture:** 50-60% (squeeze test: few drops)
- **Oxygen:** >5% throughout (turn or aerate)
- **Temperature:** 55-70°C (thermophilic phase)
- **pH:** 6-9 (self-regulating)

### Compost Materials C:N Ratios

```
HIGH CARBON (Browns)        HIGH NITROGEN (Greens)
====================        ======================
Sawdust: 200-500:1         Food waste: 15-20:1
Straw: 80-100:1            Grass clippings: 15-25:1
Dry leaves: 40-80:1        Coffee grounds: 20:1
                           Manure: 10-30:1
```

### Temperature Management
- **<40°C:** Inactive (check C:N, moisture, size)
- **40-55°C:** Active mesophilic (maintain)
- **55-70°C:** Thermophilic (ideal for pathogen kill)
- **>70°C:** Too hot (turn, add dry material)

### Maturity Tests
- **Color:** Dark brown to black
- **Odor:** Earthy, pleasant
- **Germination:** >80% vs control
- **Temperature:** <20°C rise in 24 hrs (Dewar test)

## Soil Building Action Plan

### Year 1-2: Foundation
- [ ] Baseline soil tests (physical, chemical, biological)
- [ ] Reduce tillage (transition to no-till or minimal)
- [ ] Plant cover crops (legume-grass mix)
- [ ] Apply compost (5-10 tons/acre)
- [ ] Establish monitoring protocol

### Year 3-5: Building
- [ ] Diverse cover crop cocktails (6-10 species)
- [ ] Maintain year-round living roots
- [ ] Integrate animals (if applicable)
- [ ] Continue compost (3-5 tons/acre)
- [ ] Add perennial strips or alleys

### Year 6-10: Optimization
- [ ] Fine-tune based on monitoring data
- [ ] Maximize diversity and complexity
- [ ] Reduce amendments (maintenance only)
- [ ] Document successes and share knowledge

**Expected SOM Increase:** 2-5% over 10 years

## Microbial Inoculation

### Compost Tea (Aerated)

**Recipe (5 gallons):**
- Water (chlorine-free): 5 gallons
- Compost: 2-3 cups
- Molasses: 1-2 Tbsp
- Kelp meal: 1-2 Tbsp
- Aerate: 24-48 hours

**Target Counts:**
- Bacteria: >1000 μg/ml
- Fungi: >10 μg/ml
- Protozoa: >10,000/ml

**Application:**
- Foliar: 5-20 gal/acre
- Soil drench: 10-50 gal/acre
- Frequency: Monthly

### Mycorrhizal Inoculation
- **Rate:** 10-20 spores per seed/transplant
- **Method:** Seed coating or transplant root dip
- **Avoid:** High P soils (>50 ppm)
- **Maintain:** No-till, reduce fungicides

## Common Problems & Solutions

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Compaction | Tillage, traffic, low OM | Subsoil, cover crops, OM |
| Poor drainage | Compaction, low aggregation | Improve structure, subsoil |
| Low yields | Nutrient deficiency, biology | Test soil, add compost, reduce till |
| Erosion | Bare soil, poor structure | Cover crops, perennials, reduce till |
| Weed pressure | Soil disturbance, bare soil | Living mulch, sheet mulching, cover crops |
