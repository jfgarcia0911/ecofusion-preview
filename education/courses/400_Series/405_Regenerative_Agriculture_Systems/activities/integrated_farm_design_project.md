# Activity 1: Integrated Regenerative Farm Design Project

**Course 405: Regenerative Agriculture Systems**
**Estimated Time:** 8-12 hours
**Type:** Comprehensive Design Exercise
**Deliverables:** Written report + Visual diagram

---

## Project Overview

Design a complete integrated regenerative agriculture system combining CEA with outdoor regenerative production. This capstone project synthesizes knowledge from all course modules into a viable, site-specific farm plan.

---

## Project Parameters

### Site Specifications

**Choose ONE scenario or use your own site:**

**Option A: Temperate Suburban (Northeast US)**
- Location: Pennsylvania
- Size: 5 acres available
- Climate: USDA Zone 6b (cold winters, warm summers)
- Rainfall: 42 inches annually
- Soil: Clay loam, 3-8% slopes
- Water: Municipal available, stream on property
- Budget: $250,000 initial investment

**Option B: Arid Southwest (Arizona)**
- Location: Arizona
- Size: 10 acres available
- Climate: USDA Zone 9b (hot, arid)
- Rainfall: 8 inches annually
- Soil: Sandy loam, flat
- Water: Well (limited), no surface water
- Budget: $300,000 initial investment

**Option C: Humid Southeast (Georgia)**
- Location: Georgia
- Size: 8 acres available
- Climate: USDA Zone 8a (mild winters, hot humid summers)
- Rainfall: 50 inches annually
- Soil: Sandy clay, 1-3% slopes
- Water: Abundant surface water, high rainfall
- Budget: $275,000 initial investment

**Option D: Your Own Site**
- Document actual site conditions
- Must include minimum 2 acres

## Project Requirements

### 1. Site Analysis and Assessment (15%)

**Required Elements:**
- Climate data (temperatures, rainfall, growing season)
- Topography and drainage patterns
- Soil characteristics (texture, drainage, existing health)
- Water resources (availability, quality, rights)
- Solar exposure analysis
- Wind patterns and protection needs
- Existing vegetation and habitats
- Market analysis (local food demand, competition, pricing)

**Deliverable:** 2-3 page site analysis with maps/diagrams

---

### 2. System Design (30%)

**Required Components:**

**A. Production Areas**
Design and justify allocation of land to:
- CEA facilities (greenhouses, indoor, high tunnels)
- Outdoor annual vegetable production
- Perennial crops (orchard, berries, asparagus, etc.)
- Pasture/silvopasture (if including animals)
- Agroforestry zones
- Habitat and biodiversity areas (10-20% of site)

**B. Infrastructure**
- Water systems (capture, storage, distribution, treatment)
- Energy systems (solar, wind, biomass, grid)
- Waste processing (compost, vermicompost, BSFL, digester)
- Processing/packing facilities
- Storage (cold, dry, equipment)
- Access roads and pathways

**C. Integration Strategies**
- Material flows between subsystems
- Nutrient cycling pathways
- Water reuse and treatment
- Energy sharing and efficiency
- Seasonal production complementarity

**Deliverable:** Site plan diagram (to scale) + 3-5 page design description

---

### 3. Regenerative Practices Plan (20%)

**Document how you will implement:**

**Soil Health:**
- Tillage approach (no-till, minimal, strategic)
- Cover cropping strategy (species, timing, management)
- Compost production and application
- Microbial inoculation programs
- Organic matter building timeline (5-10 year targets)

**Biodiversity:**
- Habitat areas and corridor design
- Pollinator support (plants, nesting, water)
- Beneficial insect production/attraction
- Native species integration
- Wildlife management

**Water Management:**
- Rainwater harvesting (catchment area, storage sizing)
- Greywater systems (if applicable)
- Constructed wetlands (for treatment and/or production)
- Conservation strategies
- Drought resilience planning

**Carbon Sequestration:**
- Estimated annual sequestration (tons CO₂eq)
- Key practices contributing to sequestration
- Monitoring and verification approach

**Deliverable:** 3-4 page practices implementation plan

---

### 4. Production and Financial Plan (25%)

**Production Plan:**
- Crop/livestock selection with rationale
- Year-round production calendar
- Yield estimates (by product, by season)
- Labor requirements (hours/week by season)
- Equipment and supply needs

**Financial Projections (5 years):**
- **Revenue:** By product line, by season
- **Costs:** Fixed and variable, categorized
- **Investment:** Phased over Years 0-2
- **Cash flow:** Monthly for Year 1, annual thereafter
- **Profitability:** Break-even analysis, ROI calculation
- **Sensitivity analysis:** ±20% revenue, ±15% costs

**Revenue Diversification:**
Must include 5-7 revenue streams:
- Primary production (vegetables, fruits, etc.)
- Animal products (if applicable)
- Value-added products
- Ecosystem services (carbon credits, etc.)
- Agritourism/education
- Consulting/knowledge sharing
- Other

**Deliverable:** 4-5 page financial plan with spreadsheets

---

### 5. Monitoring and Impact Assessment (10%)

**Establish baseline and monitoring plan for:**
- Soil health indicators (physical, chemical, biological)
- Biodiversity metrics (species counts, diversity indices)
- Water quality and quantity
- Carbon sequestration (estimation method)
- Economic performance (key KPIs)
- Social impact (jobs, education, food access)

**Include:**
- Specific metrics to track
- Measurement methods and frequency
- Target values at 1, 3, 5, and 10 years
- How data will inform adaptive management

**Deliverable:** 1-2 page monitoring plan

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with site analysis** - Let site conditions drive design decisions
2. **Think in systems** - Every output should be an input somewhere
3. **Phase implementation** - Don't try to build everything in Year 1
4. **Be realistic** - Conservative estimates are better than optimistic
5. **Show your work** - Document assumptions and calculations
6. **Seek feedback** - Share drafts with peers or mentors
7. **Iterate and refine** - First draft is rarely final design
