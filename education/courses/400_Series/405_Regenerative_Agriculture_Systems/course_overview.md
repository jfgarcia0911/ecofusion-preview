# Course 405: Regenerative Agriculture Systems

## Expert Level | 400-Series Professional Course

### Course Description

This expert-level course explores the integration of regenerative agriculture principles with controlled environment agriculture (CEA) to create holistic, sustainable food production systems. Students will master the science and practice of soil health restoration, carbon sequestration, closed-loop nutrient cycling, and biodiversity enhancement while learning to design and implement integrated indoor-outdoor agricultural systems that regenerate rather than deplete natural resources.

The course examines cutting-edge approaches to combining pasture-raised aquaponics, agroforestry, beneficial insect production, and composting systems with advanced CEA technologies. Students will develop expertise in life cycle assessment, regenerative certification standards, and economic modeling for regenerative enterprises. This course is designed for agricultural professionals, system designers, consultants, and entrepreneurs seeking to lead the transition to regenerative food production systems.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Design Integrated Systems**: Develop comprehensive regenerative agriculture systems that integrate CEA with outdoor production, livestock, and ecosystem restoration
2. **Assess Soil Health**: Evaluate and implement soil health restoration strategies including carbon sequestration and biological enhancement
3. **Engineer Closed-Loop Systems**: Design and optimize closed-loop nutrient cycling systems that eliminate waste and maximize resource efficiency
4. **Implement Water Systems**: Create advanced water harvesting, conservation, and treatment systems for integrated operations
5. **Enhance Biodiversity**: Develop strategies for beneficial insect production, habitat creation, and ecosystem service enhancement
6. **Integrate Agroforestry**: Design and implement agroforestry systems that complement CEA operations
7. **Conduct LCA**: Perform comprehensive life cycle assessments of agricultural systems and identify improvement opportunities
8. **Navigate Certification**: Understand and implement regenerative agriculture certification standards and verification protocols
9. **Model Economics**: Develop sophisticated economic models for regenerative agriculture enterprises including ecosystem services valuation
10. **Scale Operations**: Design scalable regenerative systems and develop implementation roadmaps for commercial operations
11. **Optimize Resource Flows**: Analyze and optimize material, energy, and nutrient flows across integrated systems
12. **Lead Transformation**: Develop strategies for transitioning conventional operations to regenerative systems

### Course Modules

#### Module 1: Foundations of Regenerative Agriculture
- History and philosophy of regenerative agriculture
- Principles of ecosystem restoration and soil health
- Regenerative vs. sustainable vs. conventional agriculture
- Systems thinking and holistic management
- Integration opportunities with CEA

#### Module 2: Soil Health and Carbon Sequestration
- Soil biology and the soil food web
- Carbon sequestration mechanisms and measurement
- Soil organic matter building strategies
- Microbial inoculation and enhancement
- Soil health indicators and assessment tools

#### Module 3: Integrated Indoor-Outdoor System Design
- Complementary system architecture
- Material and nutrient flow optimization
- Seasonal production planning
- Climate adaptation strategies
- Risk management in integrated systems

#### Module 4: Composting and Organic Waste Cycling
- Advanced composting science and engineering
- Vermicomposting systems design
- Black soldier fly larvae production
- Anaerobic digestion integration
- Compost extract and tea production

#### Module 5: Beneficial Insect Production and Integration
- Beneficial insect biology and ecology
- Production systems for predatory and pollinator insects
- Habitat design and management
- Integration with IPM strategies
- Commercial insectary operations

#### Module 6: Closed-Loop Nutrient Systems
- Nutrient cycling principles and design
- Waste stream valorization
- Nutrient recovery technologies
- System mass balance analysis
- Zero-waste system architecture

#### Module 7: Water Harvesting and Conservation
- Rainwater harvesting system design
- Greywater treatment and reuse
- Constructed wetlands for water treatment
- Aquifer recharge strategies
- Water footprint reduction

#### Module 8: Biodiversity Enhancement
- Ecosystem services and agricultural productivity
- Habitat corridors and edge effects
- Native species integration
- Pollinator conservation strategies
- Biodiversity monitoring and assessment

#### Module 9: Agroforestry Integration
- Silvopasture and alley cropping systems
- Forest gardens and food forests
- Timber and non-timber forest products
- Shade integration with CEA facilities
- Multistrata system design

#### Module 10: Pasture-Raised Aquaponics
- Outdoor aquaponics system design
- Polyculture pond management
- Pasture-based fish production
- Seasonal operation strategies
- Predator management and protection

#### Module 11: Life Cycle Assessment and Impact Analysis
- LCA methodology and standards (ISO 14040/14044)
- System boundary definition
- Data collection and quality assessment
- Impact category analysis
- Comparative assessment and optimization

#### Module 12: Certification and Regenerative Standards
- Regenerative Organic Certification (ROC)
- Land to Market verification
- Carbon credit protocols
- Ecosystem services markets
- Audit preparation and compliance

#### Module 13: Economic Models for Regenerative Systems
- Regenerative business model canvas
- Ecosystem services valuation
- Multiple revenue stream development
- Risk-adjusted financial modeling
- Investment structures and funding sources

#### Module 14: Scaling Regenerative Practices
- Transition planning and implementation
- Stakeholder engagement and education
- Technology transfer and adoption
- Regional network development
- Policy advocacy and systems change
