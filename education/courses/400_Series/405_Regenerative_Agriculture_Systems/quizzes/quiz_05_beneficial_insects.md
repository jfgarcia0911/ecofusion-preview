## Questions

### Question 1
Beneficial insects in agriculture include:

A) Only pests
B) Predators, parasitoids, and pollinators
C) No useful insects exist
D) Only mosquitoes

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Beneficial insects include predators (ladybugs), parasitoids (parasitic wasps), and pollinators (bees) that support crop production.

---

### Question 2
Predatory insects control pests by:

A) Avoiding all contact
B) Directly consuming pest species
C) Producing pesticides
D) Damaging crops

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Parasitoid wasps are beneficial because they:

A) Sting humans
B) Lay eggs in/on pest insects, killing them during development
C) Destroy all insects
D) Have no agricultural value

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Habitat design for beneficial insects should include:

A) Monocultures with no flowers
B) Diverse flowering plants, shelter, and minimal pesticides
C) Maximum pesticide use
D) Bare soil only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Banker plant systems involve:

A) Financial transactions
B) Non-crop plants supporting beneficial insect populations
C) Removing all plants
D) Only money plants

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Banker plants host non-pest prey/hosts that sustain beneficial insects even when crop pests are absent.

---

### Question 6
Commercial insectary operations produce:

A) Only pest species
B) Beneficial insects for sale to growers
C) No insects
D) Only ornamental insects

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Flower strips in and around production areas provide:

A) Aesthetic value only
B) Nectar, pollen, and habitat for pollinators and beneficials
C) Pest harborage only
D) No ecological function

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Integration with IPM (Integrated Pest Management) means:

A) Using maximum pesticides
B) Combining biological control with other pest management strategies
C) Eliminating all pest management
D) Using only one control method

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Native bee species conservation is important because:

A) They provide no pollination
B) They offer diverse, often more effective pollination than honeybees alone
C) Honeybees do everything
D) Bees are harmful

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Pesticide timing to protect beneficials involves:

A) Applying pesticides constantly
B) Selecting selective products and applying when beneficials least active
C) Using most toxic options
D) No consideration needed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Protecting beneficial insects requires selective pesticides (if used), proper timing, and preference for biological controls.
