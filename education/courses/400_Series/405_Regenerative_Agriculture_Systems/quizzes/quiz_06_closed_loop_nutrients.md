## Questions

### Question 1
A closed-loop nutrient system aims to:

A) Import maximum external inputs
B) Recycle and reuse all nutrients with minimal external inputs or waste
C) Export all nutrients
D) Ignore nutrient management

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Closed-loop systems capture, process, and recycle nutrients, minimizing waste discharge and external fertilizer needs.

---

### Question 2
Waste stream valorization means:

A) Disposing of all waste
B) Converting waste into valuable products (e.g., compost, biogas, animal feed)
C) Increasing waste production
D) Ignoring waste streams

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Nutrient recovery technologies include:

A) Dumping nutrients in waterways
B) Phosphorus precipitation, nitrogen stripping, or mineralization
C) No recovery possible
D) Burning all materials

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
System mass balance analysis tracks:

A) Employee attendance
B) Nutrient inputs, outputs, and accumulation to identify losses and opportunities
C) Only financial flows
D) Building weight

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Mass balance quantifies where nutrients enter, where they go, and where they accumulate or are lost, informing system optimization.

---

### Question 5
Zero-waste system architecture requires:

A) No waste management
B) Designing processes where all "waste" becomes input to another process
C) Maximum waste generation
D) External disposal only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Aquaponics integrates:

A) Unrelated systems
B) Fish production with hydroponic plant growth using fish waste as nutrients
C) Only ornamental fish
D) No nutrient cycling

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Phosphorus recovery is particularly important because:

A) It's unlimited and cheap
B) Rock phosphate is finite and phosphorus pollution causes eutrophication
C) Phosphorus is not needed by plants
D) No phosphorus exists

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Mineralization of organic matter releases:

A) Only carbon
B) Plant-available nutrients like nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium
C) No nutrients
D) Only toxins

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Nitrogen fixation by legumes provides:

A) No nitrogen
B) Biological conversion of atmospheric N2 to plant-available forms
C) Only nitrogen export
D) Nitrogen destruction

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Legumes host rhizobia bacteria that fix atmospheric nitrogen, reducing fertilizer needs in integrated systems.

C) Maximum resource extraction
