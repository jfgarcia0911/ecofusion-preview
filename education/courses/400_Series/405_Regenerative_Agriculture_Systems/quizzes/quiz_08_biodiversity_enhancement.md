## Questions

### Question 1
Ecosystem services provided by biodiversity include:

A) No benefits
B) Pollination, pest control, soil health, and nutrient cycling
C) Only aesthetics
D) Decreased productivity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Diverse ecosystems provide critical services that support agricultural productivity and resilience.

---

### Question 2
Habitat corridors facilitate:

A) Blocking all wildlife movement
B) Movement of wildlife and genetic exchange between habitat patches
C) Maximum fragmentation
D) No ecological function

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Edge effects in agriculture refer to:

A) Sharp field boundaries only
B) Different conditions and species interactions at habitat boundaries
C) Center-of-field phenomena only
D) No ecological significance

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Edges between different habitats create unique microclimates and support diverse species assemblages.

---

### Question 4
Native species integration benefits include:

A) No advantages
B) Adapted to local conditions, support local food webs, require fewer inputs
C) Only invasive species are useful
D) Native species reduce yields

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Pollinator conservation strategies include:

A) Eliminating all flowers
B) Diverse flowering plants, nesting habitat, and reduced pesticides
C) Maximum pesticide use
D) Monoculture landscapes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Biodiversity monitoring methods include:

A) Ignoring all species
B) Species surveys, eDNA analysis, camera traps, and indicator species
C) No monitoring needed
D) Counting only one species

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Polyculture cropping systems support biodiversity by:

A) Growing single crops
B) Providing diverse habitats and resources for multiple species
C) Eliminating all non-crop species
D) Reducing all diversity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Diverse crop plantings create varied microhabitats supporting beneficial insects, soil organisms, and wildlife.

---

### Question 8
Hedgerows and windbreaks provide:

A) No function
B) Wildlife habitat, wind protection, and beneficial insect refuges
C) Only visual barriers
D) Crop damage

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Functional diversity refers to:

A) Number of species only
B) Variety of ecological roles and traits in a community
C) Genetic uniformity
D) Single species dominance

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Functional diversity measures the range of ecological functions performed, often more important than species counts alone.

---

### Question 10
Integrating biodiversity into production systems:

A) Always reduces yields
B) Can enhance resilience, pest control, and long-term productivity
C) Has no effect
D) Only increases costs

**Correct Answer:** B
