## Questions

### Question 1
The optimal C:N (Carbon to Nitrogen) ratio for composting is approximately:

A) 5:1
B) 25-30:1
C) 100:1
D) 500:1

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** C:N of 25-30:1 provides balanced nutrition for decomposer microorganisms, resulting in efficient composting.

---

### Question 2
Thermophilic composting temperatures (130-160°F) are important because they:

A) Slow decomposition
B) Kill pathogens, weed seeds, and accelerate breakdown
C) Preserve all seeds
D) Stop all microbial activity

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Vermicomposting uses which organisms?

A) Bacteria only
B) Earthworms (often Eisenia fetida - red wigglers)
C) Large mammals
D) Only fungi

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL) production provides:

A) No benefits
B) Rapid waste processing and high-protein animal feed
C) Only aesthetic value
D) Soil destruction

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** BSFL efficiently convert organic waste to protein-rich larvae (animal feed) and nutrient-rich frass (fertilizer).

---

### Question 5
Anaerobic digestion differs from composting by:

A) Using identical processes
B) Occurring without oxygen and producing biogas (methane)
C) Being slower in all cases
D) Requiring aerobic conditions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Compost extract (tea) is used to:

A) Drink as beverage
B) Apply beneficial microorganisms to plants and soil
C) Clean equipment
D) No agricultural use

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Finished compost should have:

A) Foul odor and recognizable materials
B) Earthy smell, dark color, and uniform texture
C) High heat and steam
D) White appearance

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Aeration in composting is achieved by:

A) Sealing compost in airtight container
B) Turning piles or forced air systems
C) Adding water only
D) Compaction

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Regular turning or forced aeration provides oxygen for aerobic decomposition and prevents anaerobic odors.

---

### Question 9
Compost maturity testing includes:

A) Visual inspection only
B) Phytotoxicity bioassays and respiration tests
C) Weighing only
D) No testing needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In closed-loop systems, composting CEA plant waste returns:

A) Nothing of value
B) Nutrients and organic matter to production systems
C) Only diseases
D) Toxic materials

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Composted plant waste recycles nutrients and builds soil health, closing nutrient loops in integrated systems.
