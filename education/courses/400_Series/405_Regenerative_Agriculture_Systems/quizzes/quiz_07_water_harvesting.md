## Questions

### Question 1
Rainwater harvesting systems capture:

A) Only groundwater
B) Precipitation from roofs and surfaces for storage and use
C) Only municipal water
D) No water sources

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the typical first-flush diverter used for?

A) Maximum water collection
B) Diverting initial dirty runoff before cleaner water enters storage
C) Decorative purposes
D) No specific function

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** First-flush diverters remove contaminated initial runoff (bird droppings, dust, debris) before capturing cleaner water.

---

### Question 3
Greywater includes:

A) Toilet wastewater
B) Wastewater from sinks, showers, and laundry (not toilets)
C) Only drinking water
D) Industrial chemicals

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Constructed wetlands for water treatment use:

A) Concrete channels only
B) Plants, microbes, and soil to filter and treat wastewater
C) Chemical treatments only
D) No biological processes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Aquifer recharge strategies involve:

A) Pumping water out only
B) Infiltrating water to replenish groundwater supplies
C) Sealing all soil
D) Maximum extraction

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Managed aquifer recharge infiltrates treated water or stormwater to restore groundwater levels.

---

### Question 6
Water footprint reduction in agriculture includes:

A) Maximum water waste
B) Efficient irrigation, recycling, and drought-tolerant crops
C) No water management
D) Only surface water use

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
A cistern stores:

A) Only chemicals
B) Collected rainwater for later use
C) No liquids
D) Only wastewater

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Drip irrigation reduces water use by:

A) Flooding fields
B) Delivering water directly to root zones with minimal evaporation
C) Spraying water in air
D) Random water application

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Bioswales are designed to:

A) Only aesthetic landscaping
B) Slow, filter, and infiltrate stormwater runoff
C) Increase runoff velocity
D) Prevent all water movement

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Bioswales are vegetated channels that manage stormwater through infiltration, filtration, and biological uptake.

Water reuse in integrated systems might include:
