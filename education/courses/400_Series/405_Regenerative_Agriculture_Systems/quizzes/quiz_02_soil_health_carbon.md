## Questions

### Question 1
The soil food web includes all of the following EXCEPT:

A) Bacteria and fungi
B) Protozoa and nematodes
C) Earthworms and arthropods
D) Concrete and steel

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** The soil food web consists of living organisms; concrete and steel are not biological components.

---

### Question 2
Carbon sequestration in soil primarily occurs through:

A) Atmospheric deposition only
B) Photosynthesis capturing CO2, converting to plant biomass, then to stable soil organic matter
C) Industrial processes
D) Volcanic activity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Plants capture atmospheric CO2, which becomes biomass and eventually stable soil organic carbon through decomposition and microbial processing.

---

### Question 3
What is the typical carbon sequestration rate for well-managed regenerative systems?

A) 0.1-0.5 tons CO2e/acre/year
B) 1-3 tons CO2e/acre/year
C) 10-15 tons CO2e/acre/year
D) Carbon cannot be sequestered in soil

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Mycorrhizal fungi benefit plants by:

A) Competing for nutrients
B) Extending root reach and improving nutrient/water uptake
C) Destroying root systems
D) Producing toxins

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Mycorrhizae form symbiotic relationships, extending the effective root zone and improving access to phosphorus, water, and other nutrients.

---

### Question 5
Which practice MOST damages soil biology?

A) Cover cropping
B) Compost application
C) Intensive tillage
D) Mulching

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Tillage disrupts fungal networks, exposes organic matter to oxidation, and destroys soil structure and biology.

---

### Question 6
Soil Organic Matter (SOM) content of 5% is considered:

A) Very poor
B) Adequate
C) Good to excellent
D) Impossible to achieve

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Most agricultural soils have 1-3% SOM; 5% indicates healthy, well-managed soil with good carbon storage.

---

### Question 7
Aggregate stability in soil indicates:

A) Soil degradation
B) Strong soil structure and resistance to erosion
C) Compaction problems
D) Nutrient deficiency

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Stable aggregates indicate healthy soil structure, good water infiltration, and resistance to erosion and compaction.

---

### Question 8
Microbial inoculation involves:

A) Sterilizing all soil microbes
B) Introducing beneficial bacteria, fungi, or other microorganisms
C) Applying pesticides
D) Removing all organic matter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
The Haney Soil Health Test measures:

A) Only NPK levels
B) Biological activity and nutrient availability
C) Soil color
D) Rock content

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Haney test assesses soil respiration, water-extractable nutrients, and microbial biomass carbon - indicators of biological health.

---

### Question 10
Permanence of soil carbon sequestration is enhanced by:

A) Annual tillage
B) Formation of stable humus and aggregate-protected carbon
C) Removing all crop residues
D) Bare soil exposure

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Stable humus and physical protection within aggregates increases carbon residence time in soil, enhancing long-term sequestration.
