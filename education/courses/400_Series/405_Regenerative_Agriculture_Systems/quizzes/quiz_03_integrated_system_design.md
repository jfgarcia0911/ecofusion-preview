## Questions

### Question 1
Integrated indoor-outdoor systems provide advantages including:

A) Single production mode only
B) Year-round production, risk diversification, and complementary resource use
C) Increased complexity with no benefits
D) Higher costs with lower yields

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Complementary system architecture means:

A) Systems that compete for resources
B) Designing CEA and outdoor systems to support each other
C) Isolated operations with no interaction
D) Identical systems duplicated

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Complementary design leverages strengths of each system - CEA for consistent quality and outdoor for volume and ecological benefits.

---

### Question 3
Material flow optimization in integrated systems involves:

A) Wasting all byproducts
B) Using outputs from one system as inputs to another (e.g., compost, water)
C) Purchasing all inputs externally
D) Disposing of all waste

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Seasonal production planning in integrated systems should:

A) Ignore seasonal variations
B) Use CEA for year-round supply and outdoor for peak season volume
C) Only produce in summer
D) Shut down half the year

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Climate adaptation in integrated systems addresses:

A) Operating in single climate only
B) Using CEA as climate buffer and outdoor for site-appropriate crops
C) Ignoring climate change
D) No adaptation needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Risk management benefits of integration include:

A) Putting all resources in one system
B) Diversified production reduces impact of failure in any single system
C) Increased vulnerability
D) No risk management value

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Water sharing between systems might involve:

A) Using potable water for everything
B) CEA effluent irrigating outdoor crops or reed beds
C) Separate water sources with no connection
D) Dumping all CEA water

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Energy synergies in integrated systems include:

A) Maximum energy waste
B) Waste heat from CEA warming greenhouses or animal shelters
C) No energy sharing
D) Separate isolated energy systems

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Nutrient cycling between indoor and outdoor involves:

A) Importing all nutrients
B) Plant waste composted and returned to production systems
C) Disposing of all organic matter
D) No nutrient management

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Space utilization in integrated designs optimizes:

A) Leaving most land unused
B) Vertical CEA structures over outdoor production areas or complementary zoning
C) Random placement
D) Maximum building footprint

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Efficient designs might place CEA structures over parking, pathways, or even some outdoor production, or zone areas by function.
