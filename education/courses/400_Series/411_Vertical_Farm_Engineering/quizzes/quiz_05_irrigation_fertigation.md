### Question 1
What is the recommended flow velocity range for irrigation distribution pipes to prevent sediment settling while minimizing erosion?

A) 0.5-1.0 ft/s
B) 2-5 ft/s
C) 8-12 ft/s
D) 15-20 ft/s

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Flow velocities of 2-5 ft/s prevent sediment deposition (which occurs below 2 ft/s) while avoiding excessive pressure losses and erosion (which increase above 5 ft/s).

### Question 2
Calculate the pump Total Dynamic Head (TDH) for a system with 15 ft elevation change, 8 psi friction loss, and 5 psi operating pressure requirement:

A) 28 ft
B) 45 ft
C) 56 ft
D) 65 ft

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** TDH = Elevation head + Friction loss + Operating pressure. Convert PSI to feet: (8 + 5 psi) × 2.31 ft/psi = 30 ft. TDH = 15 + 30 + (operating pressure already included) = 15 ft + 13 psi × 2.31 = 15 + 41 = 56 ft.

### Question 3
What is the typical EC (electrical conductivity) range for hydroponic nutrient solutions for leafy greens?

A) 0.5-1.0 mS/cm
B) 1.5-2.5 mS/cm
C) 3.5-4.5 mS/cm
D) 5.0-6.0 mS/cm

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Leafy greens typically perform best at EC 1.5-2.5 mS/cm. Lower values provide insufficient nutrients, while higher values can cause osmotic stress.

### Question 4
For a fertigation system delivering 1,000 L/hour of irrigation with a target EC of 2.0 mS/cm from concentrate at 20 mS/cm, what is the injection rate?

A) 50 L/hour
B) 100 L/hour
C) 150 L/hour
D) 200 L/hour

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Injection rate = (Target EC / Concentrate EC) × Total flow = (2.0/20) × 1,000 = 100 L/hour

### Question 5
What is the primary purpose of using multiple fertilizer stock tanks (A, B, and acid) in commercial systems?

A) Reduce equipment costs
B) Prevent precipitation of incompatible nutrients (e.g., calcium and sulfates)
C) Simplify mixing procedures
D) Eliminate the need for monitoring

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Separate tanks prevent chemical reactions between incompatible nutrients like calcium nitrate and sulfates/phosphates, which would form insoluble precipitates.

### Question 6
What filtration level (microns) is typically recommended before UV disinfection systems?

A) 1-5 microns
B) 10-20 microns
C) 50-100 microns
D) 200+ microns

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 10-20 micron filtration removes particles that could shadow microorganisms from UV light, ensuring effective disinfection while not being excessively restrictive.

### Question 7
Calculate the pipe diameter needed for 500 GPM flow rate at 4 ft/s velocity:

A) 3 inches
B) 4 inches
C) 6 inches
D) 8 inches

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Using formula: Diameter (in) = √[(GPM × 0.408) / velocity(ft/s)] = √[(500 × 0.408)/4] = √51 = 7.14 inches. Nearest standard size: 6-inch pipe (8-inch would be over-sized and more expensive).

### Question 8
What is the recommended recirculation rate (system turnovers per hour) for DWC (Deep Water Culture) systems?

A) 0.5-1 turnover/hour
B) 2-4 turnovers/hour
C) 6-8 turnovers/hour
D) 12-15 turnovers/hour

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** DWC systems typically maintain 2-4 complete turnovers per hour to ensure uniform nutrient distribution and dissolved oxygen levels without excessive turbulence.

---

### Question 9
What is the typical target pH range for hydroponic nutrient solutions?

A) 4.0-4.5
B) 5.5-6.5
C) 7.0-7.5
D) 8.0-8.5

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** pH 5.5-6.5 optimizes nutrient availability for most hydroponic crops. Iron and micronutrients become less available above 6.5, while below 5.5 can cause toxicity issues.

### Question 10
What water use efficiency metric is most appropriate for evaluating vertical farm irrigation performance?

A) Liters per plant per day
B) Liters per kilogram of harvested produce
C) Total daily water consumption
D) Percentage runoff

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Liters per kilogram harvested normalizes water use against productivity, allowing meaningful comparisons across different crops, systems, and facilities.
