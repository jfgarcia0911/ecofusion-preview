### Question 1
What is the typical live load rating required for vertical farm racking systems (including plants, water, and equipment)?

A) 25-50 psf
B) 75-125 psf
C) 150-250 psf
D) 300-400 psf

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Vertical farm racking typically requires 75-125 psf (pounds per square foot) live load capacity, accounting for growing channels, water, plants, and operational loads.

### Question 3
Which growing system typically provides the highest plant density per square meter of floor space?

A) NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) horizontal channels
B) DWC (Deep Water Culture) rafts
C) Vertical towers with 360° planting
D) Dutch bucket systems

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Vertical towers can achieve 500-1,000+ plants per square meter of floor space through vertical stacking, significantly exceeding horizontal systems like NFT (30-50 plants/m²) or DWC (20-40 plants/m²).

---

### Question 4
What material is most commonly used for growing channels in commercial vertical farms due to corrosion resistance and food safety?

A) Galvanized steel
B) PVC (polyvinyl chloride)
C) Food-grade stainless steel or ABS plastic
D) Untreated aluminum

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Food-grade stainless steel (304/316) or ABS plastic are preferred due to corrosion resistance, food safety compliance, and durability in humid, nutrient-rich environments.

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended aisle width for manual harvesting operations in vertical farms?

A) 18-24 inches
B) 36-48 inches
C) 60-72 inches
D) 96+ inches

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 36-48 inch aisles provide adequate space for workers with harvest bins while maximizing growing area. Narrower aisles impede workflow; wider aisles waste space.

### Question 6
For modular racking systems, what is a key advantage over custom-built systems?

A) Higher initial cost
B) Scalability and ability to reconfigure as needs change
C) Requires specialized engineering for each installation
D) Limited to specific crops

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Modular systems offer flexibility to expand, reconfigure, or relocate equipment as production needs evolve, with standardized components reducing costs and lead times.

### Question 7
What is the typical channel slope for NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) systems?

A) 0.1-0.5% (1:1000 to 1:200)
B) 1-2% (1:100 to 1:50)
C) 5-8% (1:20 to 1:12)
D) 10-15% (1:10 to 1:7)

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** NFT channels require 1-2% slope to maintain proper nutrient film flow without stagnation (too flat) or excessive velocity causing root damage (too steep).

---

### Question 8
Calculate the plant capacity for a 1,000 m² facility with 6 growing levels, 70% usable space, and 40 plants/m²:

A) 84,000 plants
B) 126,000 plants
C) 168,000 plants
D) 240,000 plants

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Plant capacity = 1,000 m² × 6 levels × 0.70 × 40 plants/m² = 168,000 plants

---

### Question 9
What safety factor is typically applied to racking structural design for vertical farms?

A) 1.2:1
B) 1.5:1
C) 2.0:1
D) 3.0:1

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A 2.0:1 safety factor is standard for racking design, meaning the structure can support twice the expected maximum load to account for dynamic loads, impact, and safety margins.

### Question 10
For mobile racking systems, what is the primary benefit compared to stationary systems?

A) Lower equipment costs
B) Increased growing area by eliminating permanent aisles (higher space utilization)
C) Easier installation
D) Reduced structural requirements

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Mobile racking systems eliminate multiple permanent aisles by creating access where needed, increasing growing area by 30-50% compared to stationary systems with fixed aisles.
