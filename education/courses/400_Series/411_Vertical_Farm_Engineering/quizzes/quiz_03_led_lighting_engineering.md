## Questions

### Question 1
What is the photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD) range generally recommended for leafy greens in vertical farms?

A) 50-100 μmol/m²/s
B) 150-250 μmol/m²/s
C) 400-600 μmol/m²/s
D) 800-1000 μmol/m²/s

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Leafy greens typically require 150-250 μmol/m²/s PPFD for optimal growth. Lower values (50-100) are insufficient, while higher values provide diminishing returns and waste energy.

---

### Question 2
Calculate the Daily Light Integral (DLI) for a system operating at 200 μmol/m²/s for 18 hours per day:

A) 10.8 mol/m²/day
B) 12.96 mol/m²/day
C) 15.84 mol/m²/day
D) 18.72 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** DLI = PPFD × photoperiod × 0.0036. DLI = 200 × 18 × 0.0036 = 12.96 mol/m²/day

### Question 3
What is the typical photosynthetic photon efficacy (PPE) range for modern horticultural LED fixtures?

A) 1.0-1.5 μmol/J
B) 2.0-2.5 μmol/J
C) 2.8-3.2 μmol/J
D) 4.0-5.0 μmol/J

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Modern horticultural LEDs achieve 2.8-3.2 μmol/J PPE, with top-tier fixtures reaching 3.0+ μmol/J. Earlier generation fixtures were in the 2.0-2.5 range.

### Question 4
If an LED fixture consumes 600W with 40% conversion to light and 60% to heat, what is the heat load per fixture?

A) 240 W
B) 360 W
C) 480 W
D) 600 W

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Heat load = Total power × Heat percentage = 600W × 0.60 = 360W. All electrical energy eventually becomes heat in the controlled environment.

### Question 5
For uniform light distribution, what is the recommended maximum spacing-to-mounting-height (S/MH) ratio for linear LED fixtures?

A) 0.5:1
B) 1.0:1 to 1.5:1
C) 2.0:1 to 2.5:1
D) 3.0:1 or higher

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Linear LED fixtures should be spaced at 1.0-1.5 times their mounting height to achieve uniformity ratios of 0.8 or better.

### Question 6
What percentage of total facility electrical load typically comes from LED lighting in vertical farms?

A) 20-30%
B) 40-50%
C) 60-75%
D) 80-90%

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** LED lighting typically represents 60-75% of total electrical load in vertical farms, with HVAC and other systems comprising the remainder.

### Question 7
Which light spectrum combination is most commonly used for vegetative growth of leafy greens?

A) 100% blue light (450nm)
B) 80% red (660nm) + 20% blue (450nm)
C) 50% red + 50% blue
D) Full spectrum white light only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A ratio of approximately 80% red to 20% blue provides optimal growth for most leafy greens, balancing photosynthetic efficiency with morphological development.

### Question 8
What is the primary advantage of using dimmable LED drivers in vertical farm lighting systems?

A) Lower initial equipment cost
B) Ability to adjust light intensity for different growth stages and crop types
C) Reduced electrical code requirements
D) Elimination of heat dissipation needs

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Dimmable drivers allow precise control of light intensity to optimize DLI for different crops and growth stages, improving both crop quality and energy efficiency.

### Question 9
Calculate the electrical circuit requirement for 20 LED fixtures rated at 600W each operating on 277V (assuming 80% continuous load factor per NEC):

A) 30 Amps
B) 43 Amps
C) 54 Amps
D) 65 Amps

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Total load = 20 × 600W = 12,000W. Current = 12,000W ÷ 277V = 43.3A. With 80% continuous load factor: 43.3A ÷ 0.8 = 54.1A (requires 60A circuit).

### Question 10
What is the typical lifespan (L90) of commercial horticultural LED fixtures?

A) 15,000-20,000 hours
B) 30,000-40,000 hours
C) 50,000-60,000 hours
D) 100,000+ hours

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Quality horticultural LEDs typically achieve 50,000-60,000 hours L90 lifespan (90% of initial output), approximately 5-7 years of continuous operation.
