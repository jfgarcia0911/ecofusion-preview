# Module 2 Quiz: Structural Engineering for Multi-Level Systems

**Questions:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80% | **Time Limit:** 20 minutes

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical design load (including safety factors) for an NFT channel system per level?
A) 150 kg/m²
B) 250 kg/m²
C) 300 kg/m²
D) 450 kg/m²

**Answer:** C - With dead load ~35 kg/m², live load ~150 kg/m², and safety factors applied: (35×1.2) + (150×1.6) = 282 kg/m², design to 300 kg/m².

### Question 2
For a post supporting 11,520 kg in an 8-level system, which structural member is adequate?
A) 75mm × 75mm × 3mm SHS
B) 100mm × 100mm × 3mm SHS
C) 100mm × 100mm × 4mm SHS
D) 150mm × 150mm × 5mm SHS

**Answer:** C - 100mm × 100mm × 4mm SHS with capacity of 15 metric tons provides adequate margin.

### Question 3
What minimum concrete slab thickness is recommended for vertical farm racking supporting 2,000 kg/m²?
A) 100-125mm
B) 150-175mm
C) 200-250mm
D) 300-350mm

**Answer:** C - Heavy loads require 200-250mm thickness with #5 rebar @ 200mm o.c.

### Question 4
In moderate seismic zones, lateral bracing should be installed in which bays?
A) End bays only
B) End bays and every other bay
C) End bays and every 3-4 bays
D) Every bay

**Answer:** C - End bays (mandatory) and every 3-4 bays along length provides adequate lateral support.

### Question 5
What is the recommended coating for structural steel in high-humidity vertical farms?
A) Powder coating only
B) Hot-dip galvanizing
C) Paint with primer
D) No coating needed if stainless steel

**Answer:** B - Hot-dip galvanizing (minimum 85 microns) provides 20-30+ year protection.

### Question 6
Calculate the floor point load pressure from an 11,520 kg post with a 400mm × 400mm base plate:
A) 288,000 kg/m² (2.88 MPa)
B) 72,000 kg/m² (0.72 MPa)
C) 32,000 kg/m² (0.32 MPa)
D) 11,520 kg/m² (0.12 MPa)

**Answer:** B - Load / Area = 11,520 kg / 0.16 m² = 72,000 kg/m² = 0.72 MPa

### Question 7
What bolt grade is minimum recommended for structural connections in vertical farms?
A) Grade 5.8
B) Grade 8.8
C) Grade 10.9
D) Grade 12.9

**Answer:** B - Grade 8.8 or higher provides adequate strength for structural applications.

### Question 8
Recommended deflection limit for a 12m beam under growing system load:
A) L/120 (100mm)
B) L/180 (67mm)
C) L/240 (50mm)
D) L/360 (33mm)

**Answer:** C - L/240 is conservative and prevents drainage and alignment issues.

### Question 9
Which material combination should be avoided due to galvanic corrosion?
A) Galvanized steel with galvanized bolts
B) Galvanized steel with stainless steel bolts
C) Aluminum with stainless steel bolts
D) Stainless steel with stainless steel bolts

**Answer:** B - Galvanized steel and stainless steel create galvanic cell causing accelerated corrosion.

### Question 10
What is the primary structural concern unique to vertical farms compared to warehouse racking?
A) Seismic loading
B) Continuous live loads (always fully loaded)
C) Corrosion from humidity
D) Floor loading

**Answer:** B - Unlike warehouses with intermittent loading, vertical farms maintain continuous full loads 24/7.
