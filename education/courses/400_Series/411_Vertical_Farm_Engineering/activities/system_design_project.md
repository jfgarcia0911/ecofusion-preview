# Activity 1: Comprehensive Vertical Farm System Design Project

## Overview
Design a complete vertical farm facility from concept through detailed engineering specifications. This capstone activity integrates all course modules into a realistic design project.

## Project Scope
**Design a 1,500 m² commercial vertical farm for leafy green production**

## Requirements

### Business Requirements
- Location: Choose a city (specify climate zone)
- Target market: Local restaurants and retailers
- Production goal: 120 metric tons/year
- Budget: $3.5 million total capital
- Timeline: 12 months design + construction

### Technical Requirements
- Growing system: Student's choice (justify selection)
- Crop mix: 60% lettuce, 30% basil, 10% specialty greens
- Quality: Premium grade (>95% Grade A)
- Food safety: FSMA compliant, organic certification ready
- Sustainability: Target <14 kWh/kg energy use

## Deliverables

### Part 1: Facility Configuration (Week 1)
**Tasks:**
1. Calculate required growing area
   - Determine yield per m²
   - Calculate turns per year
   - Size total growing area

2. Select growing system
   - NFT, DWC, towers, or hybrid
   - Justify selection based on crops and economics

3. Develop facility layout
   - Number of levels
   - Aisle configuration
   - Support space allocation
   - Workflow design

**Deliverable:** Layout drawing (can be hand-sketch or CAD) with calculations

---

### Part 2: Structural System Design (Week 2)
**Tasks:**
1. Calculate design loads
   - Dead load estimate
   - Live load estimate
   - Apply safety factors

2. Size primary structural members
   - Vertical posts
   - Horizontal beams
   - Decking/support

3. Design foundation interface
   - Point load calculations
   - Base plate sizing or concrete pads

**Deliverable:** Structural calculations and member specifications

---

### Part 3: Lighting System Engineering (Week 3)
**Tasks:**
1. Determine lighting requirements
   - Target PPFD by crop type
   - DLI goals
   - Photoperiod schedule

2. Design LED system
   - Calculate total power required
   - Select fixture type and specifications
   - Determine fixture spacing and layout

3. Electrical design
   - Circuit calculations
   - Panel sizing
   - Power distribution

**Deliverable:** Lighting plan with power calculations

---

### Part 4: HVAC System Design (Week 4)
**Tasks:**
1. Perform load calculations
   - Sensible loads (lighting, equipment, envelope)
   - Latent loads (transpiration)
   - Total cooling requirement

2. Select HVAC equipment
   - Chiller/AC unit sizing
   - Dehumidification system
   - Air distribution design

3. Consider energy efficiency
   - Heat recovery opportunities
   - VFD applications
   - Control strategies

**Deliverable:** HVAC design with equipment specifications and load calculations

---

### Part 5: Irrigation and Water Systems (Week 5)
**Tasks:**
1. Design irrigation system
   - Calculate water requirements
   - Size pumps and pipes
   - Filtration and treatment

2. Fertigation system
   - Dosing system selection
   - Stock solution calculations
   - EC/pH control strategy

3. Water management
   - Recirculation percentage
   - Drainage design
   - Water conservation measures

**Deliverable:** Irrigation system design with hydraulic calculations

---

### Part 6: Control and Automation (Week 6)
**Tasks:**
1. Sensor network design
   - Sensor types and quantities
   - Placement strategy
   - Communication architecture

2. Control system specification
   - BMS platform selection
   - Zone control strategy
   - Alarm configuration

3. Automation opportunities
   - Identify tasks to automate
   - Calculate ROI for automation
   - Phased implementation plan

**Deliverable:** Control system architecture and automation plan

---

### Part 7: Food Safety and Operations (Week 7)
**Tasks:**
1. Food safety system design
   - Facility zoning
   - Air filtration and pressure
   - Sanitation infrastructure

2. Operational planning
   - Staffing requirements
   - Daily workflows
   - Maintenance schedules

3. Commissioning plan
   - Testing procedures
   - Performance verification
   - Training requirements

**Deliverable:** Food safety plan and operations manual outline

---

### Part 8: Financial Analysis (Week 8)
**Tasks:**
1. Capital cost estimate
   - Itemize all major systems
   - Include installation and soft costs
   - Compare to $3.5M budget

2. Operating cost projection
   - Annual energy costs
   - Labor costs
   - Consumables (seeds, nutrients, packaging)
   - Maintenance

3. Financial model
   - Revenue projection ($/kg by crop type)
   - 5-year pro forma
   - Payback period
   - IRR calculation

**Deliverable:** Complete financial model (Excel or similar)

---

## Final Presentation

### Format
- 30-minute presentation
- Professional slides (PowerPoint, Google Slides, etc.)
- Q&A session (10 minutes)

### Content Requirements
1. **Executive Summary** (3 minutes)
   - Project overview
   - Key design decisions
   - Financial highlights

2. **Technical Design** (15 minutes)
   - Facility layout
   - Major systems overview
   - Engineering calculations highlights
   - Integration approach

3. **Operations and Food Safety** (5 minutes)
   - Operational workflows
   - Food safety measures
   - Staffing and training

4. **Financial Analysis** (5 minutes)
   - Capital and operating costs
   - Revenue projections
   - ROI and payback

5. **Conclusions** (2 minutes)
   - Key success factors
   - Risks and mitigation
   - Recommendations

### Evaluation Criteria
- **Technical accuracy** (40%): Calculations correct, systems properly sized
- **Integration** (20%): Systems work together effectively
- **Practicality** (15%): Design is buildable and operable
- **Financial viability** (15%): Economics are realistic and attractive
- **Presentation quality** (10%): Clear communication, professional delivery

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with requirements** - Understand what you're trying to achieve before selecting solutions

2. **Show your work** - Include all calculations and assumptions

3. **Be realistic** - Use actual equipment specifications and costs from manufacturers

4. **Consider operations** - Design for the people who will operate the facility daily

5. **Iterate** - Your first design won't be optimal; refine based on calculations

6. **Use resources** - Reference course materials, manufacturer data, case studies

7. **Justify decisions** - Explain why you selected specific technologies or approaches

8. **Budget wisely** - Allocate capital where it provides most value
