# Course 411: Vertical Farm Engineering - Expert Level

## Course Overview

This expert-level course provides comprehensive training in the specialized engineering disciplines required for designing, constructing, and operating commercial vertical farming facilities. Students master advanced engineering principles including structural systems, precision lighting engineering, advanced HVAC design, automated material handling, and integrated control systems.

**Level:** 400-Level Expert/Professional
**Duration:** 90-110 hours
**Prerequisites:** Courses 302, 303, and 401

---

## Course Structure

### Module Overview (14 Modules)

1. **Vertical Farm System Architectures** - Layout configurations, production capacity, growing systems, workflow design
2. **Structural Engineering for Multi-Level Systems** - Load calculations, racking design, foundation requirements, seismic considerations
3. **LED Lighting System Engineering** - Photobiological engineering, electrical design, thermal management, uniformity optimization
4. **Advanced HVAC Design** - Multi-zone control, dehumidification, air distribution, heat recovery
5. **Precision Irrigation and Fertigation Engineering** - Hydraulic design, fertigation systems, water treatment, recirculation
6. **Racking and Growing System Design** - Modular systems, materials selection, accessibility, safety
7. **Material Handling and Logistics Engineering** - Vertical transport, AGVs, workflow optimization, waste management
8. **Environmental Control System Integration** - Sensor networks, control hierarchies, PID control, alarms
9. **Energy Systems and Efficiency Optimization** - Energy analysis, renewable integration, demand management
10. **Automation and Robotics Integration** - Automated seeding/transplanting, robotic harvesting, machine vision
11. **Food Safety Engineering and Sanitation** - FSMA compliance, cleanroom design, water safety, pest exclusion
12. **System Commissioning and Validation** - Functional testing, performance verification, training, documentation
13. **Performance Monitoring and Optimization** - KPIs, data analytics, continuous improvement, predictive maintenance
14. **Case Studies of Successful Facilities** - Real-world implementations, lessons learned, best practices

## Course Philosophy

This course emphasizes:

1. **Engineering Fundamentals** - Solid understanding of physics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and structural principles
2. **Systems Thinking** - Integration across disciplines, not isolated component design
3. **Practical Application** - Real-world problems, actual specifications, buildable designs
4. **Economic Viability** - Technical performance balanced with cost-effectiveness
5. **Safety and Compliance** - Food safety, building codes, worker safety as non-negotiable
6. **Continuous Improvement** - Data-driven optimization, learning from operations
7. **Sustainability** - Resource efficiency, energy optimization, environmental stewardship
