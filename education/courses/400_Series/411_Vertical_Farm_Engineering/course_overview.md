# Course 411: Vertical Farm Engineering

## Course Level
Expert/Professional (400-Level)

## Course Description

This expert-level course provides comprehensive training in the specialized engineering disciplines required for designing, constructing, and operating commercial vertical farming facilities. Students will master advanced engineering principles including structural systems for multi-level growing environments, precision lighting engineering, advanced HVAC design, automated material handling, and integrated control systems. The curriculum emphasizes engineering calculations, system integration, energy optimization, and the unique challenges of creating controlled environments for multi-story crop production.

This course prepares engineering professionals to lead vertical farm development projects, optimize facility performance, and implement cutting-edge automation and robotics systems for maximum productivity and efficiency.

## Prerequisites

Required:
- Course 302: Vertical Farming Technologies
- Course 303: Climate Control Engineering
- Course 401: CEA Facility Design & Architecture

Recommended:
- Professional engineering experience in mechanical, electrical, or structural disciplines
- Understanding of automation and control systems
- Familiarity with agricultural production systems
- Experience with engineering design software and calculations

## Target Audience

- Mechanical and electrical engineers specializing in CEA
- Structural engineers working on vertical farm projects
- Automation and controls engineers
- Vertical farm facility managers and operators
- Engineering consultants in the vertical farming industry
- Technology providers and equipment manufacturers
- Research and development engineers in agriculture technology

- **Total Hours**: 90-110 hours

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Design complete vertical farm systems** integrating all engineering disciplines
2. **Perform structural calculations** for multi-level growing systems and racking
3. **Engineer LED lighting systems** optimized for energy efficiency and crop production
4. **Design advanced HVAC systems** for vertical farm environmental control
5. **Develop irrigation and fertigation systems** with precision control
6. **Integrate automation and robotics** for material handling and operations
7. **Optimize energy systems** including renewable energy integration
8. **Design food safety systems** meeting regulatory requirements
9. **Create commissioning protocols** for system validation and optimization
10. **Analyze system performance** and implement continuous improvement strategies
11. **Calculate return on investment** for engineering improvements
12. **Apply engineering principles** learned from successful facility case studies

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Vertical Farm System Architectures
- Overview of vertical farm configurations and layouts
- Production capacity and space utilization calculations
- Growing system selection and engineering requirements
- Workflow design and operational efficiency
- Modular vs. custom system architectures

### Module 2: Structural Engineering for Multi-Level Systems
- Load calculations for vertical growing systems
- Racking and support structure design
- Floor loading and reinforcement requirements
- Seismic considerations for tall structures
- Material selection and structural analysis

### Module 3: LED Lighting System Engineering
- Photobiological engineering fundamentals
- LED technology selection and specifications
- Lighting system electrical design and power distribution
- Thermal management for LED systems
- Light uniformity calculations and optimization

### Module 4: Advanced HVAC Design for Vertical Farms
- Multi-zone environmental control strategies
- Dehumidification system design for high-density growing
- Air distribution in vertical configurations
- Heat recovery and energy efficiency
- Integration with lighting thermal loads

### Module 5: Precision Irrigation and Fertigation Engineering
- Irrigation system hydraulics and design
- Fertigation injection and mixing systems
- Water treatment and filtration engineering
- Drainage and recirculation system design
- Flow monitoring and control systems

### Module 6: Racking and Growing System Design
- Modular racking system engineering
- Growing channel and tray design
- Mobile vs. stationary systems
- Load distribution and safety factors
- Accessibility and maintenance considerations

### Module 7: Material Handling and Logistics Engineering
- Vertical transport systems (elevators, conveyors)
- Automated guided vehicles (AGV) integration
- Harvesting and packaging logistics
- Waste management systems
- Ergonomic design for manual operations

### Module 8: Environmental Control System Integration
- Sensor networks and data acquisition
- Control system architecture and programming
- Multi-zone control strategies
- Feedback loops and PID control
- Alarm systems and fault detection

### Module 9: Energy Systems and Efficiency Optimization
- Energy consumption analysis and modeling
- Power factor correction and electrical efficiency
- Renewable energy integration (solar, wind)
- Battery storage systems
- Peak demand management and utility optimization

### Module 10: Automation and Robotics Integration
- Automated seeding and transplanting systems
- Robotic harvesting technology
- Automated crop monitoring and data collection
- Machine vision and AI integration
- Human-robot collaboration systems

### Module 11: Food Safety Engineering and Sanitation
- Food safety system design (HACCP, FSMA)
- Sanitation system engineering
- Cleanroom design principles for vertical farms
- Air filtration and positive pressure systems
- Water safety and treatment validation

### Module 12: System Commissioning and Validation
- Commissioning process and documentation
- Performance testing protocols
- System integration testing
- Validation of critical parameters
- Startup troubleshooting and optimization

### Module 13: Performance Monitoring and Optimization
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) for vertical farms
- Data analytics and performance modeling
- Continuous improvement methodologies
- Predictive maintenance systems
- Retrofit and upgrade engineering

### Module 14: Case Studies: Successful Vertical Farm Facilities
- Large-scale commercial facility analysis
- Technology integration case studies
- Performance benchmarking and best practices
- Lessons learned from operational facilities
- Future trends and emerging technologies
