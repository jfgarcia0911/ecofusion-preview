# Course 411: Vertical Farm Engineering - Technical Glossary

## A

**ACH (Air Changes per Hour)** - Number of times the entire volume of air in a space is replaced per hour. Typical vertical farms: 15-25 ACH.

**Aeroponics** - Growing method where plant roots are suspended in air and misted with nutrient solution. Requires 80-100 PSI pressure, 50-80 micron droplets.

**AGV (Automated Guided Vehicle)** - Mobile robot used for material transport. Navigation methods: magnetic tape, laser, or vision-based.

**ASHRAE** - American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers. Publishes standards and handbooks for HVAC design.

**As-Built Drawings** - Final construction documents showing actual installed conditions, including any changes from original design.

## B

**BACnet** - Building Automation and Control Network. Communication protocol for building management systems.

**BMS (Building Management System)** - Computerized control system managing HVAC, lighting, and other facility systems.

**BTU (British Thermal Unit)** - Unit of energy. 1 BTU = heat required to raise 1 pound of water by 1°F. 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/hr.

## C

**CAD (Computer-Aided Design)** - Software for creating technical drawings and 3D models.

**CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture)** - Agricultural production in enclosed structures with environmental control.

**CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute)** - Volumetric flow rate of air. 1 CFM = 28.3 L/min.

**CIP (Clean-in-Place)** - Automated cleaning system for pipes and tanks without disassembly.

**Cobot (Collaborative Robot)** - Robot designed to work safely alongside humans without safety caging.

**Commissioning** - Systematic process of verifying that all building systems perform according to design intent.

**CV (Coefficient of Variation)** - Statistical measure of dispersion. CV = (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100%. Lower CV indicates better uniformity.

## D

**DALI (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface)** - Digital protocol for lighting control with bidirectional communication.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)** - Total photosynthetically active photons delivered per day. Units: mol/m²/day. Calculated: DLI = PPFD × photoperiod × 0.0036.

**DMAIC** - Six Sigma methodology: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.

**DO (Dissolved Oxygen)** - Concentration of oxygen dissolved in water. Critical for root health. Target: >6 mg/L.

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)** - Hydroponic method where plant roots are submerged in aerated nutrient solution.

## E

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measure of nutrient concentration in solution. Units: mS/cm or μS/cm. Typical range: 1.5-2.5 mS/cm for leafy greens.

**EER (Energy Efficiency Ratio)** - Cooling capacity (BTU/hr) divided by power input (watts). Higher is better. Target for VF: >12.

**EPDM** - Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer. Synthetic rubber used for pond liners and roofing.

**Ergonomics** - Science of designing for human use. Critical for operator safety and productivity.

## F

**Fertigation** - Application of fertilizer through irrigation system.

**FRP (Fiber-Reinforced Plastic)** - Composite material with excellent corrosion resistance. Used in harsh environments.

**FSMA (Food Safety Modernization Act)** - FDA regulation requiring preventive controls in food production.

**FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)** - Measure of staffing. 1 FTE = 2,080 hours/year (40 hours/week × 52 weeks).

## G

**GAC (Granular Activated Carbon)** - Filter media for removing chlorine, organics, and odors from water.

**Germination** - Process of seed sprouting and early growth. Typically occurs in dedicated climate room at 75-85°F, 95-98% RH.

**GPM (Gallons Per Minute)** - Volumetric flow rate. 1 GPM = 3.785 L/min.

**GPP (Good Production Practices)** - Standard operating procedures ensuring food safety and quality.

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)** - Systematic food safety management system.

**HARPC (Hazard Analysis and Risk-Based Preventive Controls)** - Food safety approach required by FSMA.

**HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)** - Durable plastic for tanks and containers. Food-safe, chemical-resistant.

**HEPA (High-Efficiency Particulate Air)** - Filter removing 99.97% of particles ≥0.3 μm. Used in cleanrooms.

**HMI (Human-Machine Interface)** - Touchscreen or control panel for system operation.

**HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning)** - Climate control systems.

## I

**IBC (International Building Code)** - Model building code adopted by most U.S. jurisdictions.

**IPM (Integrated Pest Management)** - Holistic approach to pest control using multiple methods, minimizing pesticides.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)** - Discount rate where NPV = 0. Higher IRR indicates better investment.

**ISO Classification** - Cleanroom air quality standards. ISO 8 typical for vertical farms (3.52M particles/m³ ≥0.5μm).

## K

**kPa (Kilopascal)** - Unit of pressure. 1 kPa = 0.145 PSI. Used for VPD: typical target 0.8-1.2 kPa.

**kWh (Kilowatt-hour)** - Unit of energy. 1 kWh = 3,412 BTU. Used for electricity billing.

## L

**Latent Load** - Cooling load from moisture removal. In vertical farms, primarily from plant transpiration.

**LED (Light-Emitting Diode)** - Solid-state lighting technology. Horticultural LEDs: 2.5-3.2 μmol/J efficacy.

**LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)** - Laser-based navigation system for AGVs and robotics.

**L70** - LED lifespan definition: hours until 70% of initial light output remains. Typical: 50,000+ hours.

## M

**Machine Vision** - Use of cameras and AI to automate inspection, identification, and guidance.

**MERV (Minimum Efficiency Reporting Value)** - Air filter rating. Higher MERV = finer filtration. Typical VF: MERV 8-13.

**Modbus** - Communication protocol for industrial devices. Variants: RTU (serial) and TCP/IP (Ethernet).

**MPa (Megapascal)** - Unit of pressure. 1 MPa = 145 PSI = 10 bar.

## N

**NEC (National Electrical Code)** - U.S. standard for safe electrical installation.

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)** - Hydroponic method using shallow flowing film of nutrient solution. Flow: 1-2 L/min, slope: 1-2%.

**NDIR (Non-Dispersive Infrared)** - CO₂ sensor technology. Accuracy: ±40 ppm typical.

**NPV (Net Present Value)** - Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment. Positive NPV indicates profitable investment.

## O

**ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential)** - Measure of water's disinfection capacity. Used to monitor sanitizer levels.

**O&M Manual (Operations and Maintenance Manual)** - Documentation of system operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting procedures.

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)** - Light wavelengths plants use: 400-700 nm.

**PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)** - Continuous improvement methodology.

**PE (Professional Engineer)** - Licensed engineer authorized to stamp designs and certify compliance with codes.

**PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative)** - Control algorithm for maintaining setpoints. Requires tuning of Kp, Ki, Kd parameters.

**PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)** - Industrial computer for automation control.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)** - Light intensity for plants. Units: μmol/m²/s. Typical leafy greens: 200-300 μmol/m²/s.

**PPM (Parts Per Million)** - Concentration unit. 1 ppm = 1 mg/L in water.

**PSI (Pounds per Square Inch)** - Pressure unit. 1 PSI = 6.89 kPa = 0.0689 bar.

**PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness)** - Data center efficiency metric, adapted for vertical farms. PUE-VF = Total Power / Growing System Power. Target: <1.4.

**PWM (Pulse Width Modulation)** - Dimming method using rapid on/off cycling.

## Q

**Quantum Sensor** - Instrument measuring PPFD. Calibrated for 400-700 nm spectral range.

## R

**RH (Relative Humidity)** - Percentage of moisture in air relative to saturation at that temperature. Typical VF target: 60-75%.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - Financial metric. ROI% = (Gain - Cost) / Cost × 100%.

**RO (Reverse Osmosis)** - Water purification removing dissolved solids. Recovery: 75-85% typical. Output: <50 ppm TDS.

**RTD (Resistance Temperature Detector)** - Precision temperature sensor. Accuracy: ±0.1-0.3°C.

**RTU (Roof Top Unit)** - Packaged HVAC system mounted on building roof.

## S

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)** - Industrial control system for facility-wide monitoring and control.

**Sensible Load** - Cooling load from temperature change (no moisture change). Includes lights, equipment, envelope.

**SHR (Sensible Heat Ratio)** - Sensible load / Total load. Low SHR (0.3-0.4) typical in vertical farms due to high humidity loads.

**Six Sigma** - Quality improvement methodology targeting <3.4 defects per million. Uses DMAIC approach.

**SHS (Square Hollow Section)** - Structural steel tube with square cross-section. Common sizes: 100×100mm, 150×150mm.

**SQF (Safe Quality Food)** - Food safety certification program recognized by GFSI.

## T

**TDH (Total Dynamic Head)** - Total pressure pump must overcome. TDH = Static + Friction + Pressure. Units: meters or feet of water column.

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Total concentration of dissolved substances in water. Units: ppm or mg/L. Target source water: <300 ppm.

**THD (Total Harmonic Distortion)** - Measure of power quality. Target: <20% for electrical systems.

**Transpiration** - Water evaporation from plant leaves. Primary source of humidity in vertical farms. Rate: 3-6 L/m²/day during photoperiod.

## U

**U-value** - Thermal transmittance of building envelope. Lower is better insulation. Units: W/m²·K or BTU/hr·ft²·°F.

**UV (Ultraviolet)** - Light below 400 nm wavelength. UV-C (254 nm) used for water disinfection.

## V

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)** - Motor controller varying speed by changing frequency. Energy savings: Fan power ∝ speed³.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)** - Driving force for transpiration. VPD = Saturation VP - Actual VP. Units: kPa. Optimal: 0.8-1.2 kPa for most crops.

## W

**WWF (Welded Wire Fabric)** - Reinforcement for concrete slabs. Example: 6×6-W2.9×W2.9 = 6-inch grid, W2.9 wire.

## Z

**Zoning** - Dividing facility into independently controlled areas. Typical: 4-12 zones per facility.

## Units and Conversions Quick Reference

### Temperature
- °C = (°F - 32) / 1.8
- °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32
- K = °C + 273.15

### Pressure
- 1 bar = 14.5 PSI = 100 kPa = 10 m H₂O
- 1 PSI = 6.89 kPa = 0.0689 bar
- 1 atm = 101.3 kPa = 14.7 PSI = 1.013 bar

### Power/Energy
- 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/hr = 1.34 HP
- 1 HP = 746 W = 2,545 BTU/hr
- 1 kWh = 3,412 BTU = 3.6 MJ

### Flow
- 1 GPM = 3.785 L/min = 0.227 m³/hr
- 1 CFM = 28.3 L/min = 1.70 m³/hr
- 1 L/s = 15.85 GPM = 2.12 CFM

### Area/Volume
- 1 m² = 10.76 ft²
- 1 m³ = 35.3 ft³ = 264 gallons
- 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.47 acres

### Mass
- 1 kg = 2.2 lb
- 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg = 2,205 lb
