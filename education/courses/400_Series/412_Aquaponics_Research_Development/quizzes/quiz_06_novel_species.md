## Questions

### Question 1
What is the most critical water quality parameter to match when evaluating fish-plant compatibility?

A) Color
B) pH and temperature range overlap
C) Dissolved oxygen levels above 5 mg/L only
D) Turbidity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** pH and temperature must be compatible for both fish and plants. Mismatched ranges compromise either fish health or plant nutrient uptake, making this the primary compatibility criterion.

---

### Question 2
When researching novel fish species for aquaponics, what feed conversion ratio (FCR) threshold indicates good commercial potential?

A) FCR > 3.0
B) FCR 2.0-2.5
C) FCR < 2.0
D) FCR doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** FCR < 2.0 indicates efficient feed conversion, reducing operating costs and waste production. Species with FCR > 2.5 are generally less economically viable for commercial aquaponics.

---

### Question 3
What is a key consideration when introducing ornamental fish species to aquaponics research?

A) Only growth rate matters
B) Market value, disease resistance, and tolerance to system fluctuations
C) They cannot be used in research
D) Color preference only

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Ornamental species must balance aesthetic value (market demand) with practical considerations: disease resistance, environmental tolerance, and ease of management in recirculating systems.

---

### Question 4
For evaluating plant species compatibility, what nutrient uptake characteristic is most important?

A) Ability to thrive in nutrient concentrations provided by fish waste
B) Preference for soil only
C) Requirement for fertilizer supplements
D) Color of flowers

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants must efficiently utilize the nutrient profile from fish waste (lower phosphorus, variable micronutrients) without requiring extensive supplementation for economic viability.

---

### Question 5
What behavioral trait makes a fish species unsuitable for aquaponics despite good growth performance?

A) Slow swimming
B) Extreme aggression or cannibalism at commercial densities
C) Diurnal activity patterns
D) Bottom-dwelling behavior

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Extreme aggression or cannibalism increases mortality, stress, and management complexity, making otherwise productive species impractical for commercial-density culture.

---

### Question 6
When conducting growth performance trials for novel species, what is the typical study duration?

A) 1-2 weeks
B) 4-6 weeks
C) One complete production cycle from juvenile to market size
D) One day

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Growth trials should span a complete production cycle to assess cumulative growth, FCR, survival, and economic viability under commercial-relevant conditions.

---

### Question 7
What fish stocking density parameter is most appropriate for comparing species compatibility?

A) Number of fish per tank
B) Biomass density (kg fish/m³ or kg fish/L)
C) Tank surface area
D) Total facility size

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Biomass density (kg/m³) normalizes for fish size and species, allowing meaningful comparisons of carrying capacity and stress tolerance across different species.

---

### Question 8
For novel leafy green species in aquaponics, what is a critical selection criterion?

A) Growth must be identical to lettuce
B) Rapid growth, market demand, and tolerance to hydroponic nutrient profiles
C) Must only grow in soil
D) Flower color

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Commercial viability requires fast crop cycles, market acceptance, and ability to thrive in fish-derived nutrients without deficiencies or toxicities.

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of conducting tolerance range studies for novel species?

A) To determine acceptable ranges for temperature, pH, salinity, and oxygen
B) To measure pain sensitivity
C) To test pesticide resistance
D) To evaluate color preferences

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Tolerance range studies identify the upper and lower limits for critical water quality parameters, defining safe operating ranges and stress thresholds for the species.

---

### Question 10
When evaluating native vs. non-native species for aquaponics research, what is a key biosecurity consideration?

A) Native species always perform better
B) Risk of escape and establishment in local waterways (invasive potential)
C) Non-native species cannot be researched
D) Temperature doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Non-native species pose invasion risks if they escape. Research must include biosecurity protocols (containment, sterilization of effluent) and consider regulatory restrictions.
