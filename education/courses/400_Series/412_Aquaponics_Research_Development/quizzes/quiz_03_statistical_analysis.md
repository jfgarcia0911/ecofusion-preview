## Questions

### Question 1
What is the appropriate statistical test for comparing mean fish weights across three different feed types?

A) Student's t-test
B) One-way ANOVA
C) Chi-square test
D) Linear regression

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** One-way ANOVA compares means across three or more independent groups. T-tests are limited to two groups, while chi-square tests categorical data.

---

### Question 2
If your data violate the normality assumption for ANOVA, which non-parametric alternative should you use?

A) Mann-Whitney U test
B) Kruskal-Wallis test
C) Fisher's exact test
D) Friedman test

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Kruskal-Wallis test is the non-parametric alternative to one-way ANOVA for comparing three or more independent groups with non-normal data.

---

### Question 3
In regression analysis, what does an R² value of 0.85 indicate?

A) 85% of the data points fall on the regression line
B) 85% of the variation in the dependent variable is explained by the independent variable(s)
C) There is an 85% probability the relationship is real
D) The regression slope equals 0.85

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** R² (coefficient of determination) represents the proportion of variance in the dependent variable explained by the independent variable(s). R² = 0.85 means 85% of variation is explained.

---

### Question 4
What is the purpose of a post-hoc test (e.g., Tukey's HSD) following ANOVA?

A) To verify ANOVA assumptions
B) To determine which specific group means differ from each other
C) To calculate effect sizes
D) To transform non-normal data

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Post-hoc tests compare all pairwise combinations of groups to identify which specific means differ significantly, following a significant ANOVA result.

---

### Question 5
Which correlation coefficient is appropriate for measuring the relationship between two continuous, normally-distributed variables?

A) Spearman's rho (ρ)
B) Kendall's tau
C) Pearson's r
D) Phi coefficient

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Pearson's r measures linear correlation between two continuous, normally-distributed variables. Spearman's rho and Kendall's tau are non-parametric alternatives for ordinal or non-normal data.

---

### Question 6
What does a p-value of 0.03 indicate?

A) There is a 3% chance the null hypothesis is true
B) There is a 3% chance of observing results this extreme if the null hypothesis is true
C) The effect size is 0.03
D) 97% of the data support the alternative hypothesis

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** P-value represents the probability of obtaining results as extreme as observed, assuming the null hypothesis is true. P = 0.03 means 3% probability of these results under H₀.

---

### Question 7
For a repeated measures ANOVA analyzing fish growth over 8 weeks, what is the within-subjects factor?

A) Fish species
B) Feed type
C) Time (week)
D) Tank number

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The within-subjects factor is the variable measured repeatedly on the same subjects. Time is measured on the same fish multiple times, making it the within-subjects factor.

---

### Question 8
What measure of central tendency is most appropriate for highly skewed data (e.g., fish mortality rates with most values at zero)?

A) Mean
B) Median
C) Mode
D) Standard deviation

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The median is robust to outliers and skewness, providing a better representation of central tendency for skewed distributions than the mean, which is pulled toward extreme values.

---

### Question 9
In a 2 × 3 factorial ANOVA, an interaction effect means:

A) One factor has no effect on the response
B) The effect of one factor depends on the level of the other factor
C) Both factors have additive effects
D) The experiment failed

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** An interaction occurs when the effect of one factor varies depending on the level of another factor (e.g., optimal fish density varies by plant species).

---

### Question 10
What is the appropriate method for handling missing data points in a longitudinal aquaponics study?

A) Delete all subjects with any missing data
B) Replace missing values with zeros
C) Use appropriate imputation methods or mixed-effects models
D) Ignore missing data in analysis

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Modern approaches use multiple imputation, last observation carried forward (LOCF), or mixed-effects models that can handle missing data appropriately without biasing results or losing power.
