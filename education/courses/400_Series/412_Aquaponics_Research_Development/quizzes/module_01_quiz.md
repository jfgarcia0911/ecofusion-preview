## Questions

### Question 1
Which component of the research cycle comes immediately after "Question Formulation"?

A) Data Collection & Analysis
B) Literature Review
C) Hypothesis Development
D) Experimental Design

**Correct Answer:** B) Literature Review
**Explanation:** The research cycle progresses from observation to question formulation, then to literature review to understand existing knowledge before developing hypotheses.

---

### Question 2
When conducting a literature search for aquaponics research, which database would be MOST appropriate for finding peer-reviewed articles on agricultural engineering aspects?

A) PubMed
B) ASABE Digital Library
C) Google Scholar
D) AGRICOLA

**Correct Answer:** B) ASABE Digital Library
**Explanation:** ASABE (American Society of Agricultural and Biological Engineers) Digital Library specializes in agricultural engineering, making it the most targeted resource for this specific focus.

---

### Question 3
According to the FINER criteria for research questions, what does the "N" stand for?

A) Necessary
B) Novel
C) Numerical
D) Normalized

**Correct Answer:** B) Novel
**Explanation:** FINER stands for Feasible, Interesting, Novel, Ethical, and Relevant. Novel means the research provides new insights or extends previous findings.

---

### Question 4
In hypothesis testing, if you fail to reject the null hypothesis when it is actually false, what type of error have you committed?

A) Type I error (α)
B) Type II error (β)
C) Sampling error
D) Measurement error

**Correct Answer:** B) Type II error (β)
**Explanation:** A Type II error (false negative) occurs when you fail to detect an effect that actually exists. Type I error is rejecting a true null hypothesis.

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is the MOST appropriate null hypothesis for testing whether water temperature affects tilapia growth?

A) Water temperature significantly affects tilapia specific growth rate
B) Water temperature has no significant effect on tilapia specific growth rate
C) Higher water temperature increases tilapia growth
D) The optimal temperature for tilapia is 28-30°C

**Correct Answer:** B) Water temperature has no significant effect on tilapia specific growth rate
**Explanation:** The null hypothesis always states there is no effect or no difference. It is the default position that the research seeks to test.

---

### Question 6
According to the "Three R's" framework for ethical animal research, which principle involves minimizing the number of animals used?

A) Replace
B) Reduce
C) Refine
D) Review

**Correct Answer:** B) Reduce
**Explanation:** The 3Rs are Replace (use alternatives when possible), Reduce (minimize animal numbers), and Refine (minimize pain and suffering).

---

### Question 7
What is the recommended method for euthanizing research fish according to best practices?

A) Rapid freezing
B) Cervical dislocation only
C) Two-step process: deep anesthesia followed by physical method
D) Asphyxiation in air

**Correct Answer:** C) Two-step process: deep anesthesia followed by physical method
**Explanation:** Best practice involves first anesthetizing the fish until opercular movement ceases, then following with a physical method to ensure death.

---

### Question 8
Which of the following is considered research misconduct?

A) Making an honest mistake in data entry
B) Selective reporting of only favorable results (cherry-picking)
C) Having your statistical analysis reviewed by a colleague
D) Revising your hypothesis after preliminary data collection

**Correct Answer:** B) Selective reporting of only favorable results (cherry-picking)
**Explanation:** Cherry-picking data is a form of falsification. Honest mistakes are not misconduct if corrected, peer review is encouraged, and hypothesis revision can be legitimate if properly documented.

---

### Question 9
When writing a research plan, how long should raw data typically be retained after publication?

A) 1 year
B) 2 years
C) At least 5 years
D) Forever

**Correct Answer:** C) At least 5 years
**Explanation:** Best practices and most institutional policies require retaining research data for a minimum of 5 years post-publication to allow for verification and reanalysis.

---

### Question 10
Which of the following best describes a "knowledge gap" in aquaponics research?

A) A disagreement between two research findings
B) Fundamental mechanisms that are not yet understood
C) A commonly known fact in the field
D) A research method that needs improvement

**Correct Answer:** B) Fundamental mechanisms that are not yet understood
**Explanation:** Knowledge gaps are areas where information is lacking or unknown. While conflicts and methodological gaps exist, knowledge gaps specifically refer to what is unknown about fundamental aspects.
