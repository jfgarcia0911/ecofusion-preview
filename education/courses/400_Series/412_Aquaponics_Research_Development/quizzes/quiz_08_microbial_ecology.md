## Questions

### Question 1
What molecular technique is currently the gold standard for characterizing bacterial communities in aquaponics systems?

A) Traditional plate culturing only
B) Microscopy
C) 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing
D) Visual observation

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** 16S rRNA gene sequencing identifies bacterial species in complex communities without cultivation, revealing diversity far beyond what plate culturing can detect (most bacteria are unculturable).

---

### Question 2
What are the two key functional groups of nitrifying bacteria in aquaponics biofilters?

A) Photosynthetic and chemosynthetic bacteria
B) Ammonia-oxidizing bacteria (AOB) and nitrite-oxidizing bacteria (NOB)
C) Aerobic and anaerobic bacteria
D) Gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Nitrification requires two groups: AOB (e.g., Nitrosomonas) convert ammonia to nitrite, and NOB (e.g., Nitrobacter, Nitrospira) convert nitrite to nitrate.

---

### Question 3
For DNA extraction from aquaponics biofilm samples, what is a critical initial step?

A) Heating to 100°C
B) Mechanical or enzymatic disruption of biofilm matrix to release cells
C) Dilution with distilled water
D) Adding soap

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Biofilms have protective extracellular matrices requiring mechanical disruption (bead beating) or enzymatic treatment to release bacterial cells for effective DNA extraction.

---

### Question 4
What does "operational taxonomic unit" (OTU) represent in microbiome analysis?

A) A functional group of similar sequences representing a species or genus-level taxon
B) The optimal temperature for bacteria
C) A unit of measurement for water flow
D) The number of bacteria per milliliter

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** OTUs cluster DNA sequences with high similarity (typically 97% for 16S rRNA), serving as proxies for bacterial species or genera in microbiome diversity analysis.

---

### Question 5
What functional gene is targeted to study ammonia-oxidizing bacteria specifically?

A) 16S rRNA gene only
B) amoA gene (ammonia monooxygenase)
C) nifH gene (nitrogen fixation)
D) rbcL gene (photosynthesis)

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The amoA gene encodes ammonia monooxygenase, the enzyme catalyzing ammonia oxidation. Targeting this gene specifically identifies and quantifies ammonia-oxidizing bacteria and archaea.

---

### Question 6
What is the primary advantage of culture-independent molecular methods over traditional plating?

A) Lower cost
B) Faster results
C) Detection of unculturable bacteria comprising >99% of environmental microbes
D) Easier to perform

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Most environmental bacteria cannot be cultured on standard media. Molecular methods reveal the vast unculturable majority, providing complete community characterization.

---

### Question 7
In qPCR (quantitative PCR) studies of nitrifying bacteria, what does Ct (cycle threshold) indicate?

A) The number of bacterial cells
B) The PCR cycle at which fluorescence crosses a threshold, inversely related to starting template amount
C) Temperature of the reaction
D) pH of the sample

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Lower Ct values indicate more starting template (DNA/bacteria). Ct values enable quantification of specific bacterial groups in samples.

---

### Question 8
What sampling strategy provides the most representative characterization of biofilter microbial communities?

A) Single sample from one location
B) Composite sampling from multiple locations and depths throughout the biofilter
C) Only sampling the water, not the biofilm
D) Random single grab sample

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Biofilter microbial communities vary spatially (depth, flow patterns, oxygen gradients). Composite sampling from multiple locations provides representative community characterization.

---

### Question 9
What preservation method is recommended for aquaponics samples intended for DNA extraction?

A) Freezing at -80°C or storage in DNA preservation buffer
B) Room temperature storage
C) Boiling
D) Exposure to sunlight

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Immediate freezing at -80°C or preservation in specialized buffers (e.g., RNAlater) prevents DNA degradation and maintains sample integrity for molecular analysis.

---

### Question 10
What does high bacterial diversity (Shannon or Simpson index) in biofilters generally indicate?

A) System failure
B) Healthy, resilient microbial community with functional redundancy
C) Contamination
D) Low nitrification rates

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** High diversity indicates complex, stable communities with functional redundancy—multiple species performing similar functions—contributing to system resilience against disturbances.
