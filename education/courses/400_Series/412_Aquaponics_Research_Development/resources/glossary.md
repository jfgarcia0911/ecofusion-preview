# Technical Glossary: Aquaponics Research & Development

**Course 412 - EcoFusion Academy**

---

## A

**Accuracy** - Closeness of a measurement to the true value; differs from precision.

**Alpha Diversity** - Species diversity within a single sample or ecosystem; measured by richness and evenness.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)** - The research hypothesis proposing that an effect or difference exists; opposite of null hypothesis.

**Ammonia Oxidizing Bacteria (AOB)** - Microorganisms (e.g., *Nitrosomonas*) that convert ammonia to nitrite in the first step of nitrification.

**ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)** - Statistical method comparing means of three or more groups.

**Aquaponics** - Integration of recirculating aquaculture and hydroponics in a symbiotic system.

**Atom% Excess** - Enrichment of stable isotope above natural background levels, used in tracer studies.

---

## B

**Beta Diversity** - Differences in species composition between samples or ecosystems.

**Biofilter** - System component containing nitrifying bacteria that convert toxic ammonia to nitrate.

**Biofortification** - Process of increasing nutrient content in crops through agricultural practices or breeding.

**BLAST (Basic Local Alignment Search Tool)** - Algorithm for comparing genetic sequences to databases.

**Blocking** - Experimental design strategy grouping similar units to reduce variability.

**Bonferroni Correction** - Conservative adjustment for multiple statistical comparisons.

---

## C

**Calibration** - Process of adjusting instrument to match known standards.

**CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)** - One-time costs for equipment and infrastructure.

**Chain of Custody** - Documentation tracking sample handling from collection to analysis.

**Chelate** - Organic molecule that binds metal ions, improving nutrient bioavailability.

**Coefficient of Variation (CV)** - Standard deviation expressed as percentage of mean; measure of relative variability.

**Cohen's d** - Measure of effect size for difference between two means.

**Completely Randomized Design (CRD)** - Basic experimental design with random assignment of treatments to units.

**Confounding Variable** - Factor that influences both independent and dependent variables, causing spurious association.

**Coupled System** - Aquaponics configuration where fish and plants share the same water loop.

**CRM (Certified Reference Material)** - Sample with known composition for quality control.

---

## D

**Decoupled System** - Aquaponics with separate loops for fish and plants, allowing independent optimization.

**Denitrification** - Microbial conversion of nitrate to nitrogen gas under anaerobic conditions.

**Dependent Variable** - Outcome measured in experiment; what you measure.

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** - Concentration of oxygen dissolved in water, critical for aquatic life (mg/L).

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)** - Hydroponic method where plant roots are suspended in oxygenated nutrient solution.

---

## E

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measure of water's ability to conduct electricity; indicates total dissolved ions (mS/cm).

**Effect Size** - Quantitative measure of the magnitude of a phenomenon; complements p-value.

**ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)** - Biochemical assay detecting specific compounds using antibodies.

**Experimental Unit** - Smallest unit receiving a treatment independently; true replicate.

---

## F

**Factorial Design** - Experimental design testing multiple factors and their interactions simultaneously.

**False Discovery Rate (FDR)** - Expected proportion of false positives among rejected hypotheses.

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** - Feed given divided by weight gained; efficiency metric (target <1.5 for fish).

**FINER Criteria** - Framework for evaluating research questions: Feasible, Interesting, Novel, Ethical, Relevant.

**Foreground IP** - Intellectual property generated during a collaborative project.

**F-ratio** - Test statistic in ANOVA; ratio of between-group to within-group variance.

---

## G

**Gap Analysis** - Systematic identification of missing knowledge in a research area.

**GC-MS (Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry)** - Analytical technique for identifying and quantifying compounds.

**Gold Open Access** - Immediate free public access to research article upon publication (author pays fee).

---

## H

**HARKing** - "Hypothesizing After Results are Known"; research misconduct where post-hoc hypotheses presented as a priori.

**Heterotrophic Bacteria** - Organisms obtaining carbon from organic compounds; decomposers in aquaponics.

**Homogeneity of Variance** - ANOVA assumption that groups have equal variances.

**HRT (Hydraulic Retention Time)** - Average time water remains in system component (hours or days).

**Hypothesis** - Testable prediction about relationship between variables.

---

## I

**IACUC (Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee)** - Committee reviewing animal research protocols for ethical compliance.

**ICP-MS (Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry)** - Analytical method for detecting metals at trace levels.

**IMRAD** - Standard scientific paper structure: Introduction, Methods, Results, And Discussion.

**Independent Variable** - Factor manipulated by researcher; what you change.

**Interaction Effect** - When effect of one factor depends on level of another factor.

**Ion Selective Electrode (ISE)** - Sensor measuring specific ion concentration in solution.

**Isotope Tracer** - Stable or radioactive isotope used to track compounds through systems.

---

## K

**Kjeldahl Method** - Laboratory procedure determining total nitrogen content through digestion.

**Kruskal-Wallis Test** - Non-parametric alternative to one-way ANOVA.

---

## L

**LC₅₀ (Lethal Concentration 50%)** - Concentration of substance lethal to 50% of test organisms.

**Levene's Test** - Statistical test for homogeneity of variance assumption.

**Liebig's Law of the Minimum** - Growth limited by scarcest resource.

**LOD (Limit of Detection)** - Lowest concentration reliably distinguished from zero.

**LOQ (Limit of Quantification)** - Lowest concentration measured with acceptable accuracy (typically 3×LOD).

---

## M

**Main Effect** - Effect of one factor averaged across levels of other factors.

**Mann-Whitney U Test** - Non-parametric test comparing two independent groups.

**Mass Balance** - Accounting for all inputs, outputs, and transformations of a substance.

**MBBR (Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor)** - Biofilter using suspended plastic media for bacterial growth.

**Metagenomics** - Study of genetic material from environmental samples (not pure cultures).

**Metatranscriptomics** - Analysis of RNA from environmental samples; shows active genes.

**Michaelis-Menten Kinetics** - Model describing enzymatic reaction rates and nutrient uptake.

**Mineralization** - Conversion of organic matter to inorganic forms by microbial decomposition.

**Monod Kinetics** - Mathematical model of microbial growth rate as function of substrate concentration.

**MPN (Most Probable Number)** - Statistical method estimating microbial concentration.

---

## N

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)** - Hydroponic method using thin film of flowing nutrient solution.

**Nitrification** - Two-step microbial conversion of ammonia to nitrate via nitrite.

**Nitrite Oxidizing Bacteria (NOB)** - Microorganisms (e.g., *Nitrobacter*, *Nitrospira*) converting nitrite to nitrate.

**NOB (Nitrite Oxidizing Bacteria)** - See above.

**Null Hypothesis (H₀)** - Default assumption of no effect or no difference; tested statistically.

---

## O

**Observational Study** - Research without manipulation; describes associations, not causation.

**OCR (Oxygen Consumption Rate)** - Rate fish consume oxygen; varies with size, activity, temperature.

**One-tailed Test** - Statistical test with directional hypothesis (effect goes one way).

**OPEX (Operational Expenditure)** - Ongoing costs for supplies, labor, utilities.

**Ordination** - Statistical technique projecting multidimensional data to 2-3 dimensions for visualization.

**ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential)** - Measure of water's oxidizing/reducing capacity (mV).

**OTU (Operational Taxonomic Unit)** - Cluster of similar sequences; proxy for species in microbial ecology.

---

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)** - Light wavelengths (400-700 nm) used for photosynthesis.

**PCoA (Principal Coordinates Analysis)** - Ordination method based on distance matrix.

**PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)** - Molecular technique amplifying specific DNA sequences.

**p-Hacking** - Manipulating data or analysis to achieve p<0.05; research misconduct.

**Phylum** - High-level taxonomic rank; major division of kingdom.

**Post-hoc Test** - Statistical comparison performed after significant ANOVA to identify specific differences.

**Power (Statistical)** - Probability of detecting real effect; 1 - β; target ≥0.80.

**Precision** - Reproducibility of measurements; differs from accuracy.

**Proximate Analysis** - Standard food composition tests (moisture, protein, fat, ash, carbohydrate).

**Pseudoreplication** - Treating non-independent samples as independent; inflates sample size incorrectly.

**p-value** - Probability of obtaining results as extreme as observed if null hypothesis is true.

---

## Q

**QA/QC (Quality Assurance/Quality Control)** - Procedures ensuring data quality and reliability.

**qPCR (Quantitative PCR)** - Real-time PCR quantifying initial DNA/RNA amount.

**Q-Q Plot (Quantile-Quantile Plot)** - Graphical test of data normality.

**QuEChERS** - Sample preparation method for pesticide residue analysis ("Quick, Easy, Cheap, Effective, Rugged, Safe").

---

## R

**R² (Coefficient of Determination)** - Proportion of variance in dependent variable explained by model.

**Randomization** - Random assignment of treatments to minimize bias.

**Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD)** - Experimental design using blocks to control known variation.

**RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System)** - Aquaculture system reusing water through filtration.

**Relative Percent Difference (RPD)** - Measure of agreement between duplicates: |A-B|/[(A+B)/2] × 100%.

**Repeated Measures Design** - Experimental design with multiple measurements on same subject over time.

**Replication** - Independent repetition of treatments; provides estimate of experimental error.

**Reynolds Number (Re)** - Dimensionless number predicting flow regime (laminar vs. turbulent).

**RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)** - Measure of model fit; average prediction error.

---

## S

**Sample Size** - Number of experimental units per treatment; determines statistical power.

**Sequencing (DNA/RNA)** - Determining order of nucleotides in genetic material.

**SGR (Specific Growth Rate)** - Exponential growth rate: [(ln W₂ - ln W₁)/days] × 100.

**Shapiro-Wilk Test** - Statistical test for normality of data distribution.

**16S rRNA Gene** - Conserved genetic marker used for bacterial identification and phylogeny.

**Sphericity** - Repeated measures ANOVA assumption of equal variance of differences between time points.

**Split-Plot Design** - Experimental design with factors applied at different scales.

**Standard Deviation (SD)** - Measure of variability around mean.

**Standard Error (SE)** - Standard deviation of sampling distribution; SE = SD/√n.

**Statistical Significance** - Result unlikely due to chance alone (typically p<0.05); doesn't imply practical importance.

---

## T

**TAN (Total Ammonia Nitrogen)** - Sum of NH₃ (un-ionized) and NH₄⁺ (ionized) forms.

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Total concentration of dissolved ions (mg/L); estimated from EC.

**TGC (Thermal Growth Coefficient)** - Growth metric adjusted for temperature; allows comparison across temperatures.

**Three R's (Research Design)** - Replication, Randomization, Control.

**Three R's (Animal Ethics)** - Replace, Reduce, Refine.

**TKN (Total Kjeldahl Nitrogen)** - Organic nitrogen plus ammonia nitrogen.

**Tukey's HSD (Honestly Significant Difference)** - Conservative post-hoc test for pairwise comparisons.

**t-test** - Statistical test comparing means of two groups.

**Two-tailed Test** - Statistical test for non-directional hypothesis (effect either direction).

**Type I Error (α)** - False positive; rejecting true null hypothesis; typically set at 0.05.

**Type II Error (β)** - False negative; failing to reject false null hypothesis; related to power (1-β).

---

## U

**Un-ionized Ammonia (NH₃)** - Toxic form of ammonia; fraction increases with pH and temperature.

---

## V

**Valorization** - Process of converting waste into valuable products.

**von Bertalanffy Growth Model** - Mathematical model of fish growth: Lt = L∞(1 - e^(-K(t-t₀))).

---

## W

**Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test** - Non-parametric alternative to paired t-test.

---

## Z

**Zero-Order Kinetics** - Reaction rate independent of substrate concentration (saturated).
