# Activity: Developing a Research Proposal

**Course 412: Aquaponics Research & Development**
**Estimated Time:** 8-10 hours
**Format:** Individual or group (2-3 people)

---

## Objective

Develop a complete research proposal for an aquaponics study, applying concepts from Modules 1-6. This activity simulates the pre-grant writing process and prepares you for actual research planning.

---

## Scenario

You are a researcher at a university with access to:
- Greenhouse space (50 m²)
- 6 identical aquaponics systems (1000 L each)
- Standard laboratory equipment
- Budget: $15,000 for 12-month project
- 1 graduate student (your time equivalent)

**Industry partner** has expressed interest in improving basil production in their aquaponics facility and is willing to collaborate.

### Part 2: Hypothesis and Objectives (1 hour)

**Task 2.1: Hypotheses**
Write:
- Null hypothesis (H₀)
- Alternative hypothesis (H₁)
- Mechanistic rationale (why you expect this outcome)

**Task 2.2: Specific Objectives**
List 3-4 specific objectives that address your research question.

**Format:**
```
Objective 1: [Action verb] + [what] + [how/where]
Example: "Determine the optimal light intensity for basil
         essential oil production in aquaponics systems"
```

## Submission Format

**Compile into single document:**

1. Cover Page
   - Title
   - Your name(s)
   - Date

2. Research Question & Hypotheses (1 page)

3. Literature Review & Gap Analysis (2-3 pages)

4. Experimental Design (2-3 pages)
   - Include layout diagram

5. Methods (3-4 pages)

6. Statistical Analysis Plan (1 page)

7. Budget & Justification (1 page)

8. Expected Outcomes (1 page)

9. References (1-2 pages)

**Total length:** 12-15 pages (single-spaced, 12-pt font)

---

## Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Research Question** | 15 | Clear, specific, feasible, novel |
| **Experimental Design** | 25 | Appropriate design, adequate power, proper controls |
| **Methods** | 20 | Detailed, reproducible, scientifically sound |
| **Statistical Plan** | 15 | Correct tests, assumptions addressed |
| **Budget** | 10 | Realistic, itemized, justified |
| **Clarity & Organization** | 10 | Well-written, logical flow |
| **Innovation** | 5 | Creative approach, novel methods |
| **Total** | **100** | |

**Passing score:** 80/100

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with literature review** - Understand what's been done
2. **Be specific** - Vague plans are poor plans
3. **Design before methods** - Good design can't be fixed by analysis
4. **Calculate sample sizes** - Don't guess!
5. **Budget realistically** - Research always costs more than expected
6. **Think feasibility** - Can this actually be done in 12 months?
