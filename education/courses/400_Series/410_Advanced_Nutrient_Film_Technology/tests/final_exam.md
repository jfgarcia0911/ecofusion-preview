C) 0.015-0.020

**Answer: B**

### Question 2

A) 1.5 m
B) 2.5 m
C) 3.5 m
D) 4.5 m

**Answer: B** (2m static + friction + channel drop + safety margin)

## Section 7: Economics and Operations (Questions 48-50)

### Question 48
For a 130,000 plants/year operation with $322,000 operating costs, the break-even wholesale price per plant is approximately:
A) $1.85
B) $2.48
C) $3.12
D) $3.75

**Answer: B** ($322,000 / 130,000 = $2.48)

### Question 49

A) Energy efficiency improvements (typical payback: 1-3 years)
B) Labor automation (typical payback: 6-18 months)
C) Bulk purchasing discounts (immediate return)
D) In-house seedling production (payback: 12-18 months)

**Answer: B**

### Question 50

**Answer: C** (Fixed costs of ~$171k require higher pricing to break even)
