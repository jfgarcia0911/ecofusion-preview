## Profitability Analysis

### Break-Even Analysis

```
BREAK-EVEN CALCULATION:

Fixed costs: $171,776/year
Variable cost per unit: $1.16/plant
Annual production capacity: 130,000 plants

Break-even units:
BEP_units = Fixed Costs / (Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

At different price points:

Price $2.00:  BEP = $171,776 / ($2.00 - $1.16) = 204,495 plants
              Exceeds capacity - NOT VIABLE

Price $2.50:  BEP = $171,776 / ($2.50 - $1.16) = 128,190 plants
              Just below capacity - MARGINAL

Price $3.00:  BEP = $171,776 / ($3.00 - $1.16) = 93,357 plants
              71.8% of capacity - VIABLE

Price $3.50:  BEP = $171,776 / ($3.50 - $1.16) = 73,413 plants
              56.5% of capacity - COMFORTABLE

Price $4.00:  BEP = $171,776 / ($4.00 - $1.16) = 60,485 plants
              46.5% of capacity - EXCELLENT


Break-even revenue:
At $3.00 average: 93,357 × $3.00 = $280,071

Current revenue (optimized case): $412,220
Safety margin: ($412,220 - $280,071) / $412,220 = 32.1%

Interpretation: Can withstand 32% revenue decline and still break even
```

## Conclusion

Economic viability of NFT operations depends on premium product positioning, operational efficiency, cost control, and diversified markets. Commodity wholesale pricing rarely supports profitability. Success requires systematic analysis of capital investments, operating costs, revenue optimization, and continuous financial improvement.

## Key Takeaways

1. Initial capital investment typically $300-$500 per m² growing area
2. Operating costs range $2.00-$3.00 per plant depending on scale and efficiency
3. Break-even pricing typically $2.50-$3.00 wholesale for leafy greens
4. Premium positioning essential: target $3.50-$5.00+ average price
5. Energy represents 7-10% of operating costs, high ROI on efficiency improvements
6. Labor is largest operating expense (40-50%), automation provides good returns
7. Diversified product mix and markets reduce risk and increase profitability
8. In-house seedling production can reduce costs 60% (payback ~12 months)
9. Brand development and local positioning justify 15-30% price premiums
10. Well-managed operations achieve 20-40% operating margins and 2-3 year payback
