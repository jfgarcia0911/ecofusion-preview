## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended channel slope for NFT systems growing leafy greens?

A) 0.1-0.5%
B) 1-2%
C) 3-5%
D) 5-10%

**Correct Answer:** B) 1-2%

**Explanation:** A slope of 1-2% (1-2 cm drop per meter) provides adequate flow velocity without excessive turbulence or film disruption. Steeper slopes can create too much velocity and erosion.

---

### Question 2
How does flow rate affect film uniformity in NFT channels?

A) Higher flow always improves uniformity
B) Optimal flow maintains consistent film depth without turbulence
C) Flow rate has no effect on film uniformity
D) Lower flow always better

**Correct Answer:** B) Optimal flow maintains consistent film depth without turbulence

**Explanation:** There is an optimal flow rate range that maintains uniform film depth throughout the channel without creating excessive turbulence or inadequate coverage.

---

### Question 3
What is the typical flow rate per NFT channel for lettuce production?

A) 0.1-0.5 L/min
B) 1-2 L/min
C) 5-10 L/min
D) 15-20 L/min

**Correct Answer:** B) 1-2 L/min

**Explanation:** Flow rates of 1-2 liters per minute per channel are typical for lettuce NFT systems, balancing nutrient delivery with oxygen availability.

---

### Question 4
What causes flow non-uniformity in long NFT channels?

A) Gravity only
B) Nutrient depletion, water uptake, and friction losses
C) Temperature changes only
D) Light intensity

**Correct Answer:** B) Nutrient depletion, water uptake, and friction losses

**Explanation:** Multiple factors cause flow changes along the channel including plant water uptake, evapotranspiration, nutrient depletion, and friction.

---

### Question 5
What is the purpose of a catchment/collection channel at the end of NFT rows?

A) Store nutrients
B) Collect solution for recirculation and maintain system pressure
C) Grow additional plants
D) Provide backup water

**Correct Answer:** B) Collect solution for recirculation and maintain system pressure

**Explanation:** Catchment channels collect solution from multiple NFT channels for return to the reservoir and help maintain consistent system hydraulics.

---

### Question 6
How is hydraulic radius (R) calculated for a rectangular channel?

A) R = Width × Depth
B) R = Cross-sectional Area / Wetted Perimeter
C) R = Length / Width
D) R = Flow Rate / Slope

**Correct Answer:** B) R = Cross-sectional Area / Wetted Perimeter

**Explanation:** Hydraulic radius is defined as the cross-sectional area of flow divided by the wetted perimeter, a key parameter in hydraulic calculations.

---

### Question 7
What channel material property is most important for NFT performance?

A) Color
B) Smooth interior surface to minimize friction
C) Flexibility
D) Transparency

**Correct Answer:** B) Smooth interior surface to minimize friction

**Explanation:** Smooth interior surfaces minimize friction losses and prevent turbulence, maintaining consistent film flow characteristics.

---

### Question 8
What is the maximum recommended length-to-width ratio for NFT channels?

A) 10:1
B) 50:1
C) 100:1
D) No limit

**Correct Answer:** C) 100:1

**Explanation:** Length-to-width ratios should generally not exceed 100:1 to maintain adequate film characteristics and prevent excessive nutrient gradients.

---

### Question 9
How does temperature affect solution viscosity and flow characteristics?

A) No effect
B) Higher temperature decreases viscosity and increases flow rate
C) Higher temperature increases viscosity
D) Temperature only affects plants, not flow

**Correct Answer:** B) Higher temperature decreases viscosity and increases flow rate

**Explanation:** As temperature increases, solution viscosity decreases, allowing faster flow. This relationship must be considered in system design.

---

### Question 10
What is the primary purpose of baffles or flow distributors at channel inlets?

A) Filter debris
B) Ensure uniform flow distribution across channel width
C) Increase flow velocity
D) Aerate the solution

**Correct Answer:** B) Ensure uniform flow distribution across channel width

**Explanation:** Baffles or distributors at inlets ensure uniform flow distribution across the channel width, preventing preferential flow paths.
