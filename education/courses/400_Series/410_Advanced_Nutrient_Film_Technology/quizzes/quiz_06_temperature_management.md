## Questions

### Question 1
What is the optimal root zone temperature for most leafy greens in NFT?

A) 10-14°C
B) 18-22°C
C) 26-30°C
D) 32-36°C

**Correct Answer:** B) 18-22°C

**Explanation:** Root zone temperatures of 18-22°C optimize nutrient uptake, enzymatic activity, and plant metabolism while maintaining adequate oxygen solubility.

---

### Question 2
What problem occurs when root zone temperature exceeds 28°C?

A) Improved growth
B) Reduced oxygen availability and increased disease susceptibility
C) Faster production
D) No problems

**Correct Answer:** B) Reduced oxygen availability and increased disease susceptibility

**Explanation:** Above 28°C, oxygen solubility decreases significantly and pathogen growth (especially Pythium) accelerates while root function declines.

---

### Question 3
Which cooling method is most energy-efficient for NFT systems?

A) Refrigeration chillers only
B) Heat exchangers utilizing cool groundwater or night air
C) Ice addition
D) No cooling needed

**Correct Answer:** B) Heat exchangers utilizing cool groundwater or night air

**Explanation:** Passive or semi-passive heat exchangers using environmental cool sources are most energy-efficient compared to active refrigeration.

---

### Question 4
How does solution temperature affect nutrient uptake rates?

A) No effect
B) Temperature affects enzymatic activity and membrane permeability
C) Only light affects uptake
D) Colder always better

**Correct Answer:** B) Temperature affects enzymatic activity and membrane permeability

**Explanation:** Temperature directly influences enzyme function and cell membrane permeability, with uptake rates typically peaking around 20-25°C for most crops.

---

### Question 5
What is the typical heat load from a 1000W HPS grow light in a closed system?

A) 100 W of heat
B) 500 W of heat
C) ~950 W of heat (95% converted to heat)
D) No heat generated

**Correct Answer:** C) ~950 W of heat (95% converted to heat)

**Explanation:** Approximately 95% of HPS light energy becomes heat in the growing environment, requiring management. LEDs are more efficient but still generate significant heat.

---

### Question 6
What insulation strategy is most effective for NFT channels?

A) No insulation needed
B) Reflective or foam insulation on channel exteriors
C) Transparent insulation
D) Metal cladding

**Correct Answer:** B) Reflective or foam insulation on channel exteriors

**Explanation:** Reflective or closed-cell foam insulation on NFT channels prevents heat gain from ambient air and radiant sources, stabilizing root zone temperature.

---

### Question 7
How does temperature stratification occur in NFT systems?

A) It doesn't occur
B) Warmer solution rises, creating temperature gradients in reservoirs
C) All solution stays same temperature
D) Only pH stratifies

**Correct Answer:** B) Warmer solution rises, creating temperature gradients in reservoirs

**Explanation:** Thermal stratification in reservoirs creates layers of different temperatures, requiring circulation to maintain uniformity.

---

### Question 8
What is the relationship between daytime air temperature and optimal root zone temperature?

A) Must be identical
B) Root zone should be cooler (5-10°C) than air temperature for best results
C) Root zone should be warmer than air
D) No relationship

**Correct Answer:** B) Root zone should be cooler (5-10°C) than air temperature for best results

**Explanation:** Optimal shoot:root temperature differential typically places roots 5-10°C cooler than shoots, optimizing both photosynthesis and nutrient uptake.

---

### Question 9
What monitoring frequency is recommended for solution temperature in commercial NFT?

A) Annual
B) Monthly
C) Continuous monitoring with data logging
D) Not necessary

**Correct Answer:** C) Continuous monitoring with data logging

**Explanation:** Continuous temperature monitoring enables real-time management, trend analysis, and automated cooling/heating system control.

---

### Question 10
Which thermal management approach is most sustainable long-term?

A) Continuous mechanical cooling
B) Integrated design minimizing heat gain through insulation, LED lights, and passive cooling
C) Constant ice addition
D) No management

**Correct Answer:** B) Integrated design minimizing heat gain through insulation, LED lights, and passive cooling

**Explanation:** Sustainable thermal management combines multiple strategies to minimize cooling loads: insulation, efficient lighting, passive cooling, and strategic facility design.
