## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical production cycle for butterhead lettuce in optimized NFT?

A) 2-3 weeks
B) 4-5 weeks
C) 6-8 weeks
D) 10-12 weeks

**Correct Answer:** B) 4-5 weeks

**Explanation:** Optimized NFT systems can produce market-ready butterhead lettuce in 4-5 weeks from transplant, faster than field production (8-12 weeks).

---

### Question 2
Which NFT parameter most affects basil aroma and essential oil content?

A) Channel length only
B) Light intensity and spectrum, plus nutrient stress strategies
C) Flow rate only
D) Channel color

**Correct Answer:** B) Light intensity and spectrum, plus nutrient stress strategies

**Explanation:** Basil essential oil production responds to light quality (blue/UV) and intensity, plus mild nutrient/water stress can enhance aromatics.

---

### Question 3
What challenge is unique to strawberry production in NFT?

A) Excessive growth
B) Supporting fruit weight and managing runners/daughter plants
C) Too much water uptake
D) No challenges

**Correct Answer:** B) Supporting fruit weight and managing runners/daughter plants

**Explanation:** Strawberries in NFT require fruit support to prevent contact with wet surfaces and careful runner management to prevent channel clogging.

---

### Question 4
What EC strategy optimizes lettuce flavor and texture?

A) Constant low EC
B) Constant high EC
C) Gradual EC increase in final week before harvest
D) Random EC changes

**Correct Answer:** C) Gradual EC increase in final week before harvest

**Explanation:** Gradually increasing EC to 2.0-2.5 dS/m in the final week concentrates sugars and improves texture without causing tip burn.

---

### Question 5
Which herb crop has the highest nutrient demand in NFT?

A) Basil
B) Cilantro
C) Parsley
D) Chives

**Correct Answer:** A) Basil

**Explanation:** Basil has relatively high nutrient demands, especially for nitrogen and potassium, requiring EC of 1.6-2.2 dS/m compared to 1.2-1.8 for lettuce.

---

### Question 6
What is the optimal DLI (Daily Light Integral) for NFT lettuce production?

A) 5-8 mol/m²/day
B) 12-17 mol/m²/day
C) 20-30 mol/m²/day
D) 40-50 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer:** B) 12-17 mol/m²/day

**Explanation:** Lettuce performs optimally with DLI of 12-17 mol/m²/day. Below this, growth slows; above this, marginal returns diminish and tip burn risk increases.

---

### Question 7
How does cultivar selection affect NFT performance?

A) All cultivars perform equally
B) Compact, bolt-resistant cultivars with strong root systems perform best
C) Field cultivars always best
D) Cultivar doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** B) Compact, bolt-resistant cultivars with strong root systems perform best

**Explanation:** NFT-specific cultivars with compact growth, bolting resistance, and vigorous root systems optimize space use and yield in controlled environments.

---

### Question 8
What water quality parameter is most critical for leafy greens?

A) Temperature only
B) Low sodium and chloride to prevent tip burn and edge necrosis
C) pH only
D) Color

**Correct Answer:** B) Low sodium and chloride to prevent tip burn and edge necrosis

**Explanation:** Sodium and chloride accumulate in leaf margins causing tip burn and edge necrosis in sensitive crops like lettuce, requiring low-Na/Cl source water.

---

### Question 9
How does photoperiod affect lettuce bolting in NFT?

A) No effect
B) Long days (>14 hours) can trigger bolting in sensitive cultivars
C) Short days trigger bolting
D) Light duration irrelevant

**Correct Answer:** B) Long days (>14 hours) can trigger bolting in sensitive cultivars

**Explanation:** While temperature is primary, photoperiod >14-16 hours can induce bolting in some cultivars, requiring cultivar selection or photoperiod management.

---

### Question 10
What is the primary difference in NFT management for microgreens versus mature lettuce?

A) No differences
B) Much shorter cycle (7-14 days), higher seeding density, different harvest technique
C) Longer cycle
D) Same cycle length

**Correct Answer:** B) Much shorter cycle (7-14 days), higher seeding density, different harvest technique

**Explanation:** Microgreens are harvested at cotyledon or first true leaf stage (7-14 days), grown at very high density, and require different NFT configurations.
