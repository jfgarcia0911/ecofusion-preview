## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary advantage of multi-tier NFT systems?

A) Simpler construction
B) Increased production per square meter of floor space
C) Lower cost
D) Easier maintenance

**Correct Answer:** B) Increased production per square meter of floor space

**Explanation:** Multi-tier systems can increase production 2-5x per square meter of floor space by stacking growing layers, critical for high-cost urban real estate.

---

### Question 2
What is the typical vertical spacing between tiers in lettuce production?

A) 10-20 cm
B) 30-45 cm
C) 60-80 cm
D) 100+ cm

**Correct Answer:** B) 30-45 cm

**Explanation:** Spacing of 30-45 cm accommodates mature lettuce head size while allowing adequate light penetration and air circulation between tiers.

---

### Question 3
What lighting challenge is unique to multi-tier systems?

A) No challenges
B) Ensuring uniform light distribution to all tiers, especially lower levels
C) Too much light
D) Light color only

**Correct Answer:** B) Ensuring uniform light distribution to all tiers, especially lower levels

**Explanation:** Each tier requires dedicated lighting as upper tiers block overhead light, with lower tiers often needing higher intensity to compensate for ambient light loss.

---

### Question 4
How does structural loading change in multi-tier systems?

A) No change
B) Cumulative weight of multiple tiers requires stronger support structures
C) Lighter structures needed
D) Only floor matters

**Correct Answer:** B) Cumulative weight of multiple tiers requires stronger support structures

**Explanation:** Multi-tier structures must support cumulative loads of channels, solution, plants, lights, and maintenance access, requiring engineered support systems.

---

### Question 5
What is the hydraulic challenge in multi-tier NFT?

A) No hydraulic issues
B) Managing multiple independent circuits or cascading flow between tiers
C) Single pump works for all tiers
D) Gravity eliminates all pumping

**Correct Answer:** B) Managing multiple independent circuits or cascading flow between tiers

**Explanation:** Multi-tier systems require either independent hydraulic circuits per tier or carefully engineered cascading systems to maintain proper film characteristics.

---

### Question 6
What air circulation requirement increases in multi-tier systems?

A) Air circulation not needed
B) Enhanced airflow to prevent CO₂ depletion and humidity buildup between tiers
C) Less airflow needed
D) Natural convection sufficient

**Correct Answer:** B) Enhanced airflow to prevent CO₂ depletion and humidity buildup between tiers

**Explanation:** Dense multi-tier systems can create stagnant air pockets with depleted CO₂ and excess humidity, requiring active circulation management.

---

### Question 7
What is the typical maximum number of tiers for leafy green production?

A) 2-3 tiers
B) 4-6 tiers
C) 8-12 tiers
D) Unlimited

**Correct Answer:** B) 4-6 tiers

**Explanation:** Most commercial systems use 4-6 tiers, balancing production density with practical constraints of lighting efficiency, access, and structural costs.

---

### Question 8
How does maintenance access change in multi-tier systems?

A) Easier than single tier
B) Requires walkways, ladders, or mobile platforms for upper tiers
C) No access needed
D) Identical to single tier

**Correct Answer:** B) Requires walkways, ladders, or mobile platforms for upper tiers

**Explanation:** Multi-tier systems require careful planning for safe worker access to all levels for planting, maintenance, and harvesting operations.

---

### Question 9
What is the energy trade-off in multi-tier systems?

A) Lower total energy use
B) Higher lighting energy per unit production but lower per square meter
C) No energy difference
D) Eliminate all energy use

**Correct Answer:** B) Higher lighting energy per unit production but lower per square meter

**Explanation:** While total lighting energy increases with tiers, energy cost per kilogram produced can be competitive due to increased production density and facility efficiency.

---

### Question 10
What fire safety consideration is critical in multi-tier systems?

A) No fire concerns
B) Adequate fire suppression and egress pathways between tall structures
C) Fire impossible
D) Safety not needed

**Correct Answer:** B) Adequate fire suppression and egress pathways between tall structures

**Explanation:** Multi-tier systems create tall, dense structures requiring proper fire suppression systems, clear egress paths, and compliance with building codes.
