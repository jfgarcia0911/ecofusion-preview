## Questions

### Question 1
What is the minimum dissolved oxygen (DO) concentration recommended for NFT systems?

A) 2 mg/L
B) 4 mg/L
C) 6 mg/L
D) 10 mg/L

**Correct Answer:** C) 6 mg/L

**Explanation:** DO should be maintained above 6 mg/L for optimal root function. Below this, root respiration and nutrient uptake become compromised.

---

### Question 2
What is the most energy-efficient method for oxygenating NFT nutrient solutions?

A) Air stones in reservoir
B) Venturi injectors in recirculation loop
C) Pure oxygen injection
D) Manual stirring

**Correct Answer:** B) Venturi injectors in recirculation loop

**Explanation:** Venturi injectors utilize existing pump energy to entrain air, providing efficient oxygenation without additional energy input for air pumps.

---

### Question 3
How does film turbulence affect oxygen transfer?

A) No effect
B) Increased turbulence at waterfalls and drops enhances oxygen uptake
C) Turbulence reduces oxygen
D) Only temperature matters

**Correct Answer:** B) Increased turbulence at waterfalls and drops enhances oxygen uptake

**Explanation:** Turbulent flow and waterfall effects increase air-water interface area, significantly enhancing oxygen transfer into solution.

---

### Question 4
What is the dissolved oxygen saturation at 20°C and sea level?

A) 5.1 mg/L
B) 7.5 mg/L
C) 9.1 mg/L
D) 12.5 mg/L

**Correct Answer:** C) 9.1 mg/L

**Explanation:** At 20°C and sea level atmospheric pressure, oxygen saturation is approximately 9.1 mg/L. This decreases with temperature and altitude.

---

### Question 5
What happens to plants when DO drops below 3 mg/L?

A) Growth increases
B) Root function impaired, anaerobic conditions favor pathogens
C) No effect
D) Flowering improves

**Correct Answer:** B) Root function impaired, anaerobic conditions favor pathogens

**Explanation:** Below 3 mg/L, roots shift to less efficient anaerobic respiration and become susceptible to pathogens like Pythium that thrive in low-oxygen conditions.

---

### Question 6
Which oxygenation method provides the highest oxygen transfer efficiency?

A) Surface aeration
B) Coarse bubble diffusion
C) Fine bubble diffusion or pure oxygen injection
D) Splashing only

**Correct Answer:** C) Fine bubble diffusion or pure oxygen injection

**Explanation:** Fine bubbles and pure oxygen provide maximum gas-liquid interface area and concentration gradient, achieving highest transfer efficiency.

---

### Question 7
How does root respiration rate relate to temperature?

A) No relationship
B) Respiration increases with temperature, consuming more oxygen
C) Respiration decreases with temperature
D) Only light affects respiration

**Correct Answer:** B) Respiration increases with temperature, consuming more oxygen

**Explanation:** Root respiration approximately doubles for every 10°C increase (Q10 ≈ 2), increasing oxygen demand while oxygen solubility decreases.

---

### Question 8
What is the purpose of a dissolved oxygen probe in commercial NFT?

A) Measure pH
B) Continuously monitor DO for automated control and alarm systems
C) Measure EC
D) Not needed

**Correct Answer:** B) Continuously monitor DO for automated control and alarm systems

**Explanation:** Continuous DO monitoring enables real-time management, automated aeration control, and early warning of system problems before plant damage.

---

### Question 9
How does organic matter accumulation affect DO?

A) Increases DO
B) Organic decomposition consumes oxygen, reducing DO levels
C) No effect
D) Improves oxygenation

**Correct Answer:** B) Organic decomposition consumes oxygen, reducing DO levels

**Explanation:** Microbial decomposition of organic matter (root exudates, dead roots) consumes oxygen, competing with plant roots and reducing available DO.

---

### Question 10
What is supersaturation of dissolved oxygen?

A) Impossible to achieve
B) DO concentration above normal atmospheric saturation
C) Below saturation
D) Same as saturation

**Correct Answer:** B) DO concentration above normal atmospheric saturation

**Explanation:** Supersaturation (>100% saturation, >9.1 mg/L at 20°C) can be achieved with pure oxygen or high-pressure injection, potentially improving plant performance.
