# Glossary of Technical Terms
## Course 410: Advanced Nutrient Film Technology

---

## A

**Aeration** - Process of introducing air or oxygen into nutrient solution to maintain adequate dissolved oxygen levels for root respiration.

**Aeroponic-NFT Hybrid** - System combining misting (aeroponics) in upper root zone with thin film collection (NFT) below, optimizing both oxygenation and nutrient delivery.

**A-Frame Configuration** - Triangular support structure for NFT channels arranged in two angled tiers, providing good light access and structural stability.

**Ammonia (NH₃)** - Toxic form of nitrogen; should be converted to nitrate (NO₃⁻) before use in NFT systems. Avoid ammonium at >10% of total nitrogen.

**Anthocyanin** - Purple/red pigments in plants, often increased by light stress or specific wavelengths (UV-A); can indicate stress or be desired trait.

---

## B

**Biofilm** - Layer of microorganisms adhering to surfaces in NFT system; can be beneficial (biofilter) or problematic (pathogen harbor, flow restriction).

**Boundary Layer** - Thin layer of relatively still fluid at root-solution interface where diffusion limits mass transfer; thinner layers improve nutrient uptake.

**Break-Even Point (BEP)** - Production level or price point where total revenue equals total costs; critical for financial viability assessment.

**Buffer Capacity** - Ability of solution to resist pH changes; affected by water hardness, carbonates, and solution volume.

---

## C

**Capillary Action** - Upward movement of solution through small spaces due to adhesive and cohesive forces; helps maintain film continuity in NFT.

**Cascade System** - Multi-tier NFT configuration where solution flows from upper tier to lower tiers by gravity, reducing pump requirements.

**Chelate** - Organic molecule that binds metal ions (especially iron) to keep them soluble and available in solution; examples: EDTA, DTPA, EDDHA.

**Chlorosis** - Yellowing of plant tissue due to insufficient chlorophyll production; often indicates nutrient deficiency (especially iron or nitrogen).

**Coefficient of Performance (COP)** - Ratio of cooling/heating provided to energy consumed; higher COP indicates better efficiency (typical chillers: 3.0-5.0).

**Cohesion-Tension Theory** - Explanation of water movement in plants driven by transpiration creating negative pressure in xylem, pulling solution from roots.

**CRISPR** - Gene editing technology enabling precise modifications to plant DNA; future applications include disease resistance and NFT-optimized traits.

---

## D

**Deep Water Culture (DWC)** - Hydroponic method with roots fully submerged in aerated solution; contrasts with NFT's thin film approach.

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** - Concentration of O₂ dissolved in nutrient solution, typically measured in mg/L or ppm; critical parameter for root health in NFT.

**Digital Twin** - Virtual replica of physical NFT system used for simulation, optimization, and predictive analysis before implementing changes.

**Duty Cycle** - Percentage of time equipment operates; for example, aeroponic misting may use 1-2% duty cycle (few seconds on, minutes off).

---

## E

**Electrical Conductivity (EC)** - Measure of dissolved salts in solution; indicates total nutrient concentration. Units: mS/cm or dS/m. Typical NFT range: 1.4-2.4 mS/cm.

**Etiolation** - Excessive stem elongation and pale color due to insufficient light; results in weak, spindly plants.

**Evapotranspiration (ET)** - Combined water loss from plant transpiration and evaporation from surfaces; affects solution volume and EC concentration.

---

## F

**Film Depth** - Thickness of nutrient solution layer flowing over roots in NFT channel; optimal 1-3mm for leafy greens, balancing hydration and oxygenation.

**Flow Rate** - Volume of solution moving through NFT channel per unit time; typically 1-2 L/min per channel for leafy greens.

**Foliar Spray** - Application of nutrients to leaves rather than roots; emergency measure for rapid nutrient correction, not primary feeding method.

**Fusarium** - Fungal pathogen causing vascular wilt disease; difficult to treat in hydroponics, prevention through sanitation essential.

---

## G

**Galvanic Sensor** - Type of dissolved oxygen sensor using electrochemical reaction that consumes oxygen; requires membrane replacement every 3-6 months.

**Gully** - Pre-formed NFT channel, typically U-shaped profile; commercial standard offering consistent dimensions and smooth flow characteristics.

---

## H

**Hazen-Williams Equation** - Formula for calculating friction losses in pipes; essential for hydraulic design and pump sizing in NFT systems.

**Header Pipe** - Large-diameter pipe distributing solution from pump to multiple NFT channels; must be oversized to maintain uniform pressure.

**Henry's Law** - Gas solubility is proportional to partial pressure; explains dissolved oxygen concentration relationships with temperature and altitude.

**Hydraulic Radius (R)** - Ratio of flow cross-sectional area to wetted perimeter (R = A/P); indicates flow efficiency, important in Manning's equation.

**Hyperspectral Imaging** - Advanced camera technology capturing 100+ spectral bands; enables non-contact detection of nutrient deficiencies and diseases.

---

## I

**Ion-Selective Electrode (ISE)** - Sensor measuring concentration of specific ions (nitrate, potassium, calcium) in real-time; emerging technology for precision management.

**Interveinal Chlorosis** - Yellowing between leaf veins while veins remain green; classic symptom of iron or manganese deficiency.

---

## K

**K_La** - Overall mass transfer coefficient for oxygen; indicates rate of O₂ transfer from gas to liquid phase. Higher values (10-20 hr⁻¹) indicate better aeration.

**K_m (Michaelis Constant)** - Substrate concentration at which enzyme reaction rate equals half maximum; used in nutrient uptake modeling.

---

## L

**Laminar Flow** - Smooth, predictable fluid flow with Reynolds number <2000; characteristic of NFT systems, contrasts with turbulent flow.

**Low-Head Oxygenation (LHO)** - Aeration method using waterfalls or cascades to entrain oxygen; effective and energy-efficient for NFT applications.

**Lux** - Measure of light intensity weighted for human vision; NOT appropriate for plant growth measurement (use PPFD instead).

---

## M

**Manning's Equation** - Open-channel flow formula relating flow rate to channel geometry, slope, and roughness; fundamental for NFT hydraulic design.

**Mass Transfer** - Movement of molecules (nutrients, oxygen) from one location to another; governs nutrient uptake and oxygen delivery in NFT.

**Microbiome** - Community of microorganisms inhabiting root zone; engineered beneficial microbiomes can enhance plant health and nutrient uptake.

**Microgreens** - Young vegetable greens harvested 7-21 days after germination; can be transitioned to NFT for extended baby-leaf production.

---

## N

**Necrosis** - Death of plant tissue appearing as brown/black areas; severe symptom indicating toxicity, disease, or extreme deficiency.

**Net Pot** - Perforated plastic container holding plant in NFT system; allows roots to grow through while supporting plant crown above solution.

**NDIR Sensor** - Non-Dispersive Infrared CO₂ sensor; standard technology for measuring carbon dioxide concentration in growing environment.

---

## O

**Optical DO Sensor** - Dissolved oxygen sensor using fluorescence quenching; more reliable than galvanic sensors, minimal maintenance, longer lifespan.

**Orifice** - Precisely-sized opening controlling flow rate; used in NFT distribution to ensure uniform flow across channels despite pressure variations.

**Osmotic Potential** - Component of water potential related to dissolved substances; high EC reduces water availability to plants through osmotic effect.

---

## P

**Peristaltic Pump** - Positive displacement pump using rotating rollers on flexible tubing; commonly used for precise nutrient and pH dosing.

**pH** - Measure of solution acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale; NFT optimal range 5.5-6.5 for most crops, affects nutrient availability.

**Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density (PPFD)** - Light measurement for plant growth in μmol/m²/s; represents photosynthetically active radiation (400-700nm).

**PID Control** - Proportional-Integral-Derivative control algorithm; provides smooth, responsive temperature and pH control avoiding oscillation.

**PPFD** - See Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density.

**Pythium** - Oomycete pathogen causing root rot; major threat in hydroponics, thrives in warm (>24°C), low-oxygen conditions.

---

## Q

**Q₁₀** - Temperature coefficient describing reaction rate doubling with 10°C increase; typically 2.0 for biological processes including root respiration.

**Quantum Sensor** - Instrument measuring PPFD for plant growth; provides μmol/m²/s readings essential for lighting design and monitoring.

---

## R

**Recirculating System** - Hydroponic configuration where solution returns to reservoir for reuse; characteristic of NFT, contrasts with run-to-waste.

**Reynolds Number (Re)** - Dimensionless parameter indicating flow regime; <2000 = laminar, >4000 = turbulent. NFT typically 500-1500.

**Root Hair** - Microscopic extensions of root epidermis providing 10-50× increase in absorption surface area; primary site of nutrient uptake.

**Root Mat** - Dense interwoven mass of roots in NFT channel; excessive mat (>80% coverage) can restrict flow and create anaerobic zones.

---

## S

**Slope** - Inclination of NFT channel; standard 1:100 (1%) provides reliable gravity flow. Too shallow risks stagnation, too steep causes film breakage.

**Specific Heat Capacity (c_p)** - Amount of energy to raise temperature of substance; water = 4,186 J/(kg·°C), determines thermal buffering capacity.

**Stock Solution** - Highly concentrated nutrient solution (typically 100×) diluted to working strength; separate tanks (A and B) prevent nutrient precipitation.

**Substrate-NFT Hybrid** - System using small amount of growing media (rockwool cube) for plant support while roots grow in NFT film below.

---

## T

**Thermal Mass** - Heat storage capacity of system; larger solution volumes provide greater temperature stability, reducing fluctuations.

**Tip Burn** - Brown, dead tissue at leaf margins; classic calcium deficiency symptom in lettuce, often caused by high EC or low humidity rather than insufficient Ca.

**Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)** - Total concentration of dissolved materials in solution; approximately 0.5-0.7 × EC in μS/cm. Less precise than EC.

**Total Dynamic Head (TDH)** - Total resistance pump must overcome including vertical lift, friction, velocity head, and safety margin; determines pump power requirements.

**Transition Zone** - Region of root mat where thin film contacts abundant root hairs; primary site of nutrient and water uptake in mature NFT systems.

---

## U

**Uptake Rate** - Speed at which plants absorb nutrients; varies by element, growth stage, and environmental conditions. Modeled by Michaelis-Menten kinetics.

**UV Sterilization** - Pathogen control using ultraviolet light (UV-C, 254nm) to destroy microorganisms in recirculating solution; 40-100 mJ/cm² typical dose.

---

## V

**Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD)** - Difference between saturation and actual vapor pressure; indicates atmospheric water demand. Optimal 0.8-1.2 kPa for leafy greens.

**Venturi Injector** - Device using high-velocity flow to create vacuum for air/gas entrainment; efficient oxygenation method for NFT (3-6% oxygen transfer efficiency).

**Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs)** - Gases emitted by plants, especially under stress or disease; emerging sensor technology for early problem detection.

---

## W

**Wetted Perimeter (P)** - Length of channel surface in contact with flowing solution; component of hydraulic radius calculation (R = A/P).

**Wetting Angle** - Contact angle between solution and surface; lower angles (<90°) indicate better wetting, essential for film adhesion to roots.

---

## X

**Xylem** - Vascular tissue conducting water and dissolved nutrients from roots to shoots; transpiration creates negative pressure pulling solution upward.

---

## Z

**Zero-Waste System** - NFT configuration with complete nutrient recycling, no solution discharge; requires advanced monitoring and targeted supplementation.

**Zoospore** - Motile spore of water molds (Pythium, Phytophthora); can swim through nutrient solution infecting roots, emphasizing importance of sanitation.

---

## Mathematical Symbols and Units

**Symbols:**
- α (alpha) - Absorption coefficient, thermal expansion coefficient
- δ (delta) - Boundary layer thickness, change/difference
- η (eta) - Efficiency (decimal or percentage)
- μ (mu) - Dynamic viscosity, micro- prefix (10⁻⁶)
- ρ (rho) - Density (typically 1000 kg/m³ for water)

**Common Units:**
- °C - Degrees Celsius (temperature)
- cm - Centimeter (length)
- EC: mS/cm (milliSiemens per centimeter)
- g - Gram (mass)
- J - Joule (energy)
- kg - Kilogram (mass)
- kW - Kilowatt (power), 1000 Watts
- L - Liter (volume)
- m - Meter (length)
- mg/L - Milligrams per liter (concentration), equivalent to ppm for water
- mS/cm - MilliSiemens per centimeter (EC)
- Pa - Pascal (pressure)
- ppm - Parts per million (concentration)
- PPFD: μmol/m²/s (light intensity for plants)
- W - Watt (power)

**Conversions:**
- 1 mS/cm = 1000 μS/cm
- 1 bar ≈ 10 m water column
- 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
- 1 gallon ≈ 3.785 L
- 1 inch ≈ 2.54 cm
- 1 foot ≈ 0.305 m
- TDS (ppm) ≈ EC (μS/cm) × 0.5-0.7
