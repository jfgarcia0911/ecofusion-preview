## Course Description

Master the art and science of greenhouse environmental control. This course covers heating, cooling, ventilation, humidity management, and climate optimization for year-round production. Learn to create optimal growing conditions while managing energy costs effectively.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Analyze greenhouse climate dynamics and heat transfer
2. Select and size climate control equipment
3. Implement energy-efficient heating and cooling strategies
4. Manage seasonal climate challenges
5. Optimize environments for specific crops
6. Design integrated climate control systems

## Key Climate Parameters

### Target Ranges by Growth Stage

| Stage | Day Temp | Night Temp | RH | CO2 |
|-------|----------|------------|-----|-----|
| Germination | 70-75°F | 65-70°F | 80-90% | Ambient |
| Seedling | 70-75°F | 60-65°F | 70-80% | Ambient |
| Vegetative | 70-80°F | 60-70°F | 60-70% | 800-1200 ppm |
| Flowering | 70-75°F | 60-65°F | 50-60% | 800-1200 ppm |
| Fruiting | 72-82°F | 65-70°F | 60-70% | 600-1000 ppm |

### Equipment Sizing Reference

| Heating | Formula |
|---------|---------|
| Heat loss | Q = U × A × ΔT |
| U-value double poly | 0.7 BTU/hr/ft²/°F |
| U-value polycarbonate | 0.5-0.6 BTU/hr/ft²/°F |
| Safety factor | 1.25-1.50× calculated |

| Ventilation | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| Winter minimum | 0.5-1 ACH |
| Normal | 1-2 ACH |
| Summer cooling | 40-60 ACH |
