## Critical Notes

**Temperature:**
- Day/night differential (DIF): +5 to +15°F promotes normal growth
- Negative DIF can be used for height control
- Avoid temperatures below 50°F or above 95°F for most crops

**Humidity/VPD:**
- Night humidity >80% increases disease risk
- VPD <0.4 kPa = disease risk, poor transpiration
- VPD >1.6 kPa = stress, stunted growth
- Target 0.8-1.2 kPa for most production

**Root Zone:**
- Keep within 5°F of air temperature when possible
- Cold roots (<60°F) reduce nutrient uptake
- Hot roots (>85°F) stress plants and risk disease

**CO₂:**
- Enrich to 800-1200 ppm when sealed (winter, early morning)
- Not cost-effective when ventilating heavily
- Requires adequate light to be beneficial
