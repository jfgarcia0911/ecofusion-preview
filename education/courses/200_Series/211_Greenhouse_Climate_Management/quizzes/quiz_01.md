## Questions

**1. Which of the following best describes VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)?**
- A) The percentage of moisture in the air
- B) The difference between actual vapor pressure and saturation vapor pressure
- C) The temperature at which condensation forms
- D) The amount of water plants transpire

**2. At 75°F with 65% RH, what is the approximate VPD? (Use SVP ≈ 2.5 kPa at 75°F)**
- A) 0.55 kPa
- B) 0.88 kPa
- C) 1.25 kPa
- D) 1.63 kPa

**3. What is the optimal VPD range for most vegetative growth?**
- A) 0.2-0.4 kPa
- B) 0.4-0.8 kPa
- C) 0.8-1.2 kPa
- D) 1.4-1.8 kPa

**4. Which climate parameter is considered the "master controller" of plant metabolism?**
- A) Humidity
- B) Light intensity
- C) Temperature
- D) CO₂ concentration

**5. TRUE or FALSE: Relative humidity stays constant when temperature changes, even if absolute moisture content doesn't change.**
- A) True
- B) False

**6. What happens when VPD is too low (<0.4 kPa)?**
- A) Plants wilt due to excessive transpiration
- B) Disease risk increases and transpiration weakens
- C) Photosynthesis increases dramatically
- D) Root growth accelerates

**7. The four pillars of greenhouse climate control are:**
- A) Temperature, humidity, light, and nutrients
- B) Temperature, humidity, airflow, and integration
- C) Heating, cooling, ventilation, and dehumidification
- D) Day temp, night temp, VPD, and CO₂

**8. Where should temperature sensors ideally be placed in a greenhouse?**
- A) At the ridge vent where it's hottest
- B) Near the heater outlet
- C) At canopy height, shielded from direct sun
- D) In the coldest corner to ensure adequate heating

**9. Why is transpiration important for plants?**
- A) Only for cooling the leaves
- B) Drives nutrient uptake and cooling
- C) Only for water transport
- D) Prevents wilting

**10. Positive DIF (day temp > night temp) results in:**
- A) Compact, shorter plants
- B) More stem elongation and taller plants
- C) No effect on plant morphology
- D) Increased root growth only

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - VPD is the difference between actual and saturation vapor pressure
2. **B** - VPD = 2.5 × (1 - 0.65) = 2.5 × 0.35 = 0.88 kPa
3. **C** - 0.8-1.2 kPa is optimal for vegetative growth
4. **C** - Temperature controls metabolic rate
5. **B** - False. RH changes with temperature even if absolute moisture stays the same
6. **B** - Low VPD means high humidity, disease risk, and weak transpiration
7. **B** - Temperature, humidity/VPD, airflow, and integration
8. **C** - Canopy height, representative location, shielded
9. **B** - Transpiration drives both nutrient transport and cooling
10. **B** - Positive DIF causes stretching/elongation
