## Questions

**1. Plants show leaf tip burn and marginal necrosis. Temperature is normal, but VPD is very high (>1.5 kPa). What is the likely problem?**
- A) Nutrient toxicity
- B) Excessive transpiration and water stress from low humidity
- C) Cold damage
- D) Disease infection

**2. What is the systematic approach to troubleshooting climate control problems?**
- A) Replace all sensors first
- B) Observe symptoms → Check data/sensors → Test components → Identify root cause → Fix and verify
- C) Call the manufacturer immediately
- D) Turn everything off and restart

**3. The greenhouse is too cold despite the heater running. What should you check first?**
- A) Replace the thermostat
- B) Verify the heater is producing heat (flame/hot air)
- C) Order a new heater
- D) Increase the temperature setpoint

**4. Condensation drips heavily on plants every morning. What is the most likely cause?**
- A) Overwatering
- B) High nighttime humidity and/or insufficient air circulation
- C) Excess fertilizer
- D) Too much light

**5. A HAF (Horizontal Air Flow) fan has stopped working. What is the first troubleshooting step?**
- A) Order a replacement motor
- B) Check if it's receiving power (breaker, plug, wiring)
- C) Disassemble the motor
- D) Replace the fan blade

**6. Temperature sensor reads 85°F, but you measure 72°F with a calibrated thermometer. What is the problem?**
- A) The thermometer is broken
- B) The sensor is out of calibration or in direct sun
- C) This difference is normal
- D) The greenhouse is too large

**7. TRUE or FALSE: If one component fails, it's best to replace all related components at the same time.**
- A) True
- B) False

**8. What plant symptoms indicate prolonged low VPD (high humidity)?**
- A) Wilting and leaf curl
- B) Edema, disease, weak stems, soft growth
- C) Sunburn and bleaching
- D) Rapid, tall growth

**9. An evaporative cooling pad is only wetting on one side. What is the likely cause?**
- A) Fans are too powerful
- B) Water distribution system is clogged or uneven
- C) Pad is too thick
- D) Outside humidity is too high

**10. What is the most important preventive maintenance task for climate control systems?**
- A) Replacing all components annually
- B) Regular cleaning, calibration, and inspection per manufacturer schedules
- C) Running systems continuously without breaks
- D) Upgrading to the newest technology every year

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - High VPD means low humidity; plants transpire excessively, can't keep up with water loss, leading to tip burn and stress
2. **B** - Systematic troubleshooting: observe, check data, test, diagnose root cause, fix, verify (not random replacement)
3. **B** - Verify the heater is actually operating; if burner isn't firing or airflow is blocked, the heater may run but not heat
4. **B** - Condensation forms when warm, humid air contacts cool glazing; improve ventilation, dehumidify, or use curtains to reduce surface cooling
5. **B** - Start simple: check power supply, breakers, connections before assuming motor failure
6. **B** - Sensor error; likely sensor is in direct sun, out of calibration, or damaged; recalibrate or relocate
7. **B** - False. Replace only failed components unless there's a systematic issue; batch replacement is wasteful
8. **B** - Low VPD (high RH) causes weak transpiration, edema (blisters), fungal diseases, and soft, lush growth
9. **B** - Uneven wetting indicates clogged nozzles, damaged distribution pipes, or improper water flow
10. **B** - Preventive maintenance (cleaning, calibration, inspection) prevents most failures and extends equipment life; cheaper than emergency repairs
