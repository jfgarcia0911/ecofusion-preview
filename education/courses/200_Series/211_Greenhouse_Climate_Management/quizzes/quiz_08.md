## Questions

**1. What does PAR stand for?**
- A) Plant Active Radiation
- B) Photosynthetically Active Radiation
- C) Passive Absorption Range
- D) Photon Amplitude Rating

**2. What wavelength range defines PAR?**
- A) 280-400 nm (UV)
- B) 400-700 nm (visible light)
- C) 700-1000 nm (infrared)
- D) 100-400 nm (UV and violet)

**3. What does DLI (Daily Light Integral) measure?**
- A) Total photons received per day
- B) Peak light intensity at noon
- C) Hours of daylight
- D) Light color spectrum

**4. What are typical DLI units?**
- A) Watts per square meter
- B) Lumens per square foot
- C) mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹ (moles per square meter per day)
- D) Footcandles per hour

**5. If a greenhouse receives an average of 400 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ PAR for 10 hours, what is the DLI?**
- A) 4 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹
- B) 8.6 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹
- C) 14.4 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹
- D) 24 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹

**6. Which crops are considered high-light requiring (DLI > 20)?**
- A) Lettuce and herbs
- B) Microgreens
- C) Tomatoes and peppers
- D) Ferns and orchids

**7. What is the primary benefit of using LED grow lights over HPS (High Pressure Sodium)?**
- A) LEDs are always cheaper to purchase
- B) LEDs are more energy efficient and produce less heat
- C) LEDs last fewer hours, reducing maintenance
- D) LEDs provide only red light

**8. For photoperiod control, what day length is considered "long day" for flowering plants?**
- A) 8-10 hours
- B) 10-12 hours
- C) 12-14 hours
- D) 14-16+ hours

**9. TRUE or FALSE: Adding supplemental lighting always increases profits if it increases plant growth.**
- A) True
- B) False

**10. What happens when supplemental lighting increases temperature too much?**
- A) Photosynthesis increases proportionally
- B) Plants grow faster without limits
- C) Heat stress can negate the benefits of added light
- D) Light intensity automatically decreases

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - PAR stands for Photosynthetically Active Radiation
2. **B** - PAR is defined as 400-700 nm, the visible light spectrum that plants use for photosynthesis
3. **A** - DLI measures the total amount of photosynthetically active photons received over a 24-hour period
4. **C** - DLI is measured in mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹ (moles of photons per square meter per day)
5. **C** - DLI = 400 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ × 10 hours × 3600 s/hr ÷ 1,000,000 = 14.4 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹
6. **C** - Fruiting crops like tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers require DLI > 20; lettuce needs only 12-16
7. **B** - LEDs are more electrically efficient (convert more energy to light vs. heat) and produce less waste heat
8. **D** - Long day is typically 14-16+ hours; short day is less than 12 hours; day-neutral is unaffected
9. **B** - False. Lighting costs (electricity) must be less than the increased revenue from faster growth or higher yields
10. **C** - Excessive heat from lights can cause heat stress, stomatal closure, and reduced photosynthesis, negating the benefits
