## Questions

**1. What is the primary principle behind evaporative cooling (pad-and-fan systems)?**
- A) Water absorbs heat when it evaporates
- B) Fans circulate cold air from outside
- C) Cooling pads filter hot air molecules
- D) Refrigeration units chill the water

**2. In an evaporative cooling system, if outdoor conditions are 95°F at 30% RH, approximately how much temperature reduction can be expected?**
- A) 5-10°F
- B) 15-20°F
- C) 25-30°F
- D) 35-40°F

**3. What is a major limitation of evaporative cooling systems?**
- A) They are too expensive to install
- B) They are ineffective in high humidity climates
- C) They consume too much electricity
- D) They cannot be automated

**4. For a 30' x 96' greenhouse requiring 60 air changes per hour, what CFM (cubic feet per minute) is needed from exhaust fans? (Assume 12' ridge height, 10' sidewall)**
- A) 10,560 CFM
- B) 17,280 CFM
- C) 31,680 CFM
- D) 63,360 CFM

**5. Which shade system provides the most energy savings?**
- A) Interior shade curtains
- B) Exterior shade structures
- C) Shade paint/whitewash
- D) All provide equal savings

**6. What is the recommended maximum distance from pad to fan in a pad-and-fan system?**
- A) 50 feet
- B) 100 feet
- C) 150 feet
- D) 200 feet

**7. High-pressure fogging systems work best when:**
- A) Outdoor humidity is already very high
- B) Combined with low ventilation rates
- C) Air is moving and humidity is moderate to low
- D) Temperature is below 70°F

**8. Which cooling method is most effective but also most expensive to operate?**
- A) Natural ventilation
- B) Evaporative cooling
- C) Shade systems
- D) Mechanical refrigeration (chilled water)

**9. What shade percentage is typically recommended for summer cooling of most crops?**
- A) 20-30%
- B) 40-60%
- C) 70-80%
- D) 90-100%

**10. TRUE or FALSE: Running exhaust fans without inlet vents or pads will create proper airflow for cooling.**
- A) True
- B) False

---

## Answer Key

1. **A** - Evaporative cooling works through the latent heat of vaporization - water absorbs heat from the air when it evaporates
2. **C** - In dry conditions (30% RH), evaporative cooling can achieve 25-30°F reduction (about 80-85% of the wet bulb depression)
3. **B** - Evaporative cooling is ineffective when outdoor humidity is high because the air is already saturated
4. **C** - Volume = 30 × 96 × 11 (avg height) = 31,680 ft³; 31,680 × 60 ACH / 60 min = 31,680 CFM
5. **B** - Exterior shade prevents heat from entering the greenhouse; interior shade absorbs heat that's already inside
6. **C** - Maximum recommended pad-to-fan distance is 150 feet to maintain adequate air velocity and cooling
7. **C** - Fogging requires air movement to evaporate droplets and works best when air can absorb more moisture (lower RH)
8. **D** - Mechanical refrigeration provides precise cooling but has very high operating costs compared to evaporative methods
9. **B** - 40-60% shade is typical for summer; too much reduces photosynthesis, too little allows overheating
10. **B** - False. Without proper inlets, fans create negative pressure without effective air exchange, causing uneven cooling and structural stress
