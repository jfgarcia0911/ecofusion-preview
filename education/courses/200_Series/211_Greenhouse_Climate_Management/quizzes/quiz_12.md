## Questions

**1. When designing a climate control system, what should be determined first?**
- A) Equipment brands to purchase
- B) Crop requirements and facility specifications
- C) Budget limitations
- D) Controller type

**2. A 5,000 sq ft greenhouse in Vermont will grow tomatoes year-round. What is the most critical climate system to size properly?**
- A) Cooling system
- B) Supplemental lighting
- C) Heating system
- D) Shade curtains

**3. What heating capacity (BTU/hr) is needed for a 30' × 100' × 12' greenhouse with a design temperature difference of 60°F? (Use heat loss factor of 1.2 BTU/hr/ft²/°F for double poly)**
- A) 144,000 BTU/hr
- B) 216,000 BTU/hr
- C) 360,000 BTU/hr
- D) 500,000 BTU/hr

**4. When selecting a climate controller, which feature is most important for integration?**
- A) Touchscreen display
- B) Ability to control multiple systems (heat, vent, humidity, CO₂) with interlocks
- C) Wireless capability
- D) Brand name recognition

**5. What is the recommended approach for implementing a new climate control system?**
- A) Install everything at once during peak season
- B) Phase installation, test components, commission properly before crop production
- C) Install without testing to save time
- D) Copy another greenhouse's system exactly

**6. TRUE or FALSE: The cheapest equipment always provides the best return on investment.**
- A) True
- B) False

**7. For a lettuce operation requiring DLI of 14 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹, winter provides only 6 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹. Supplemental lights will run 12 hours. What PPFD is needed from the lights?**
- A) 150 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- B) 185 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- C) 265 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- D) 380 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹

**8. What is a critical consideration when planning CO₂ enrichment?**
- A) CO₂ only works in summer
- B) System must integrate with ventilation to avoid waste
- C) All crops benefit equally from CO₂
- D) CO₂ eliminates the need for fertilizer

**9. When creating a climate implementation budget, what percentage should be reserved for contingencies?**
- A) 0% - budget must be exact
- B) 2-5%
- C) 10-20%
- D) 50%

**10. What is the purpose of a commissioning protocol for climate systems?**
- A) To obtain building permits
- B) To systematically test and verify all systems operate correctly before production
- C) To train sales staff
- D) To determine crop prices

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Crop needs (temperature, humidity, light, CO₂) and facility specs (size, location, climate) drive all equipment selection
2. **C** - Vermont has cold winters; heating is critical for year-round production (could be 40-60% of operating costs)
3. **B** - Area = 30 × 100 = 3,000 ft²; Heat loss = 3,000 × 1.2 × 60 = 216,000 BTU/hr
4. **B** - Integration and interlocks are essential; controller must manage all systems and prevent fighting (e.g., stop CO₂ when vents open)
5. **B** - Phased installation allows testing; commissioning ensures systems work before risking crops
6. **B** - False. Cheap equipment may have higher operating costs, shorter life, and poor performance; lifecycle cost matters more than purchase price
7. **B** - Need 8 mol·m⁻²·day⁻¹ from lights; 8 mol / 12 hr / 3600 s = 185 μmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
8. **B** - CO₂ enrichment must interlock with ventilation; enriching while venting wastes expensive CO₂
9. **C** - Reserve 10-20% for unexpected costs, modifications, and problems during installation
10. **B** - Commissioning is systematic testing of all equipment and control sequences to verify proper operation before crop production begins
