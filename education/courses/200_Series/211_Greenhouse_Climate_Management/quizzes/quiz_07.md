## Questions

**1. What is the ambient atmospheric CO₂ concentration?**
- A) 280 ppm
- B) 400 ppm
- C) 800 ppm
- D) 1200 ppm

**2. For optimal photosynthesis in most greenhouse crops, what CO₂ level is recommended during daylight hours?**
- A) 400-600 ppm
- B) 800-1200 ppm
- C) 1500-2000 ppm
- D) 2500-3000 ppm

**3. When is CO₂ enrichment most beneficial?**
- A) At night when respiration occurs
- B) During high light conditions with adequate temperature
- C) When vents are fully open
- D) In low light winter conditions

**4. What is a major safety concern with CO₂ enrichment?**
- A) Plants can be damaged by excess CO₂
- B) CO₂ is flammable
- C) High CO₂ levels are dangerous to humans (>5000 ppm)
- D) CO₂ causes greenhouse corrosion

**5. Which CO₂ generation method is most common for greenhouse use?**
- A) Compressed CO₂ tanks
- B) Propane or natural gas burners
- C) Dry ice sublimation
- D) Composting organic matter

**6. Why should CO₂ enrichment be suspended during ventilation?**
- A) CO₂ damages vent motors
- B) It's wasteful - enriched air is exhausted outside
- C) Plants cannot use CO₂ when air is moving
- D) It creates dangerous pressure imbalances

**7. A 30' × 96' × 12' greenhouse needs to maintain 1000 ppm CO₂. Starting from 400 ppm, how many cubic feet of pure CO₂ are needed to reach target? (Volume = 34,560 ft³)**
- A) 20.7 ft³
- B) 34.6 ft³
- C) 138.2 ft³
- D) 207.4 ft³

**8. What is a key indicator that CO₂ enrichment is economically justified?**
- A) Greenhouse is in a cold climate
- B) High-value crops with good light, temperature, and closed vents
- C) Crops are grown in soil
- D) Greenhouse is small (under 1000 sq ft)

**9. TRUE or FALSE: CO₂ enrichment is beneficial 24 hours per day.**
- A) True
- B) False

**10. What maintenance is critical for CO₂ burners?**
- A) Daily water changes
- B) Proper combustion to avoid ethylene and carbon monoxide
- C) Monthly CO₂ purity testing
- D) Pressure regulation every week

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Current atmospheric CO₂ is approximately 400 ppm (420 ppm as of 2024)
2. **B** - 800-1200 ppm is the optimal range for most crops; higher levels show diminishing returns
3. **B** - CO₂ enrichment is most beneficial when photosynthesis is limited by CO₂, not light or temperature (high light, adequate warmth, closed greenhouse)
4. **C** - CO₂ above 5000 ppm poses health risks to workers; OSHA limit is 5000 ppm for 8-hour exposure
5. **B** - Propane or natural gas burners are most common; they produce CO₂ and heat simultaneously
6. **B** - Enriching during ventilation wastes expensive CO₂ by venting it outside; controllers should interlock CO₂ with vent position
7. **A** - (1000 - 400) ppm = 600 ppm = 0.0006; 34,560 ft³ × 0.0006 = 20.7 ft³ of pure CO₂
8. **B** - Economics work best with high-value crops, good growing conditions, and ability to keep greenhouse closed to retain CO₂
9. **B** - False. CO₂ enrichment is only beneficial during photosynthesis (daylight hours); plants respire and release CO₂ at night
10. **B** - Incomplete combustion can produce toxic ethylene and CO, which damage plants and endanger workers; regular burner maintenance is essential
