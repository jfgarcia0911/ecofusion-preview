## Questions

**1. What is the relationship between temperature and VPD?**
- A) They are independent and don't affect each other
- B) Higher temperature increases VPD if RH stays constant
- C) Lower temperature always increases VPD
- D) Temperature has no effect on vapor pressure

**2. In an integrated climate strategy, when should CO₂ enrichment be activated?**
- A) Whenever the greenhouse is occupied
- B) Only at night during respiration
- C) During daylight when vents are closed or minimally open
- D) 24 hours per day year-round

**3. What is a "heating stage" in climate controller programming?**
- A) A physical area of the greenhouse
- B) A sequential step that activates when temperature drops to a setpoint
- C) The morning warm-up period
- D) A type of heater fuel

**4. If cooling and dehumidification are both needed, which strategy is most efficient?**
- A) Run cooling and dehumidification simultaneously
- B) Ventilate with outside air (if conditions allow)
- C) Turn off cooling and dehumidify only
- D) Increase heating to dry the air

**5. What is a common setpoint schedule strategy for energy savings?**
- A) Use constant temperature 24/7
- B) Lower nighttime temperature, increase during active growth hours
- C) Maximize temperature fluctuations randomly
- D) Set temperature based on outside conditions only

**6. How do thermal curtains interact with other climate systems?**
- A) They only affect light, not temperature
- B) They reduce heating load but may increase humidity
- C) They eliminate the need for ventilation
- D) They prevent CO₂ enrichment from working

**7. TRUE or FALSE: Running heating and cooling simultaneously (fighting systems) is sometimes necessary for precise control.**
- A) True
- B) False

**8. What is the purpose of a "dead band" in climate control?**
- A) Backup system when controllers fail
- B) Temperature range where no heating or cooling occurs
- C) Physical barrier between zones
- D) Time delay for equipment protection

**9. When integrating misting/fogging with cooling, what is the primary concern?**
- A) Misting interferes with fan motors
- B) Adding humidity while trying to cool can create uncomfortable conditions
- C) Fog blocks light completely
- D) Misting systems cannot run with fans

**10. In a multi-zone greenhouse, how should climate integration be approached?**
- A) Use one central controller for the entire facility
- B) Each zone may need independent control based on crop needs
- C) Zones should always have identical setpoints
- D) Climate integration is impossible with multiple zones

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - As temperature increases, saturation vapor pressure increases; if RH stays constant, VPD increases
2. **C** - CO₂ enrichment is most effective when the greenhouse is closed (vents shut/minimal), during daylight for photosynthesis
3. **B** - Heating stages are sequential control steps that progressively activate heating equipment as temperature drops below setpoints
4. **B** - Ventilating with drier outside air (when available) cools and dehumidifies simultaneously, most efficiently
5. **B** - Lowering nighttime temperature (when photosynthesis isn't occurring) saves energy while maintaining growth
6. **B** - Curtains save heating energy but trap moisture, potentially increasing humidity and condensation
7. **B** - False. Fighting systems waste energy; proper integration should use control logic and dead bands to prevent this
8. **B** - Dead band is a temperature range between heating and cooling setpoints where neither system runs
9. **B** - Evaporative cooling (misting/fogging) adds humidity, which can make conditions muggy if overdone; balance is needed
10. **B** - Different crops or growth stages may require different temperatures, humidity, or light; zones allow customization
