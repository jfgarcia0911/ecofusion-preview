# Climate Audit Exercise
## Course 211: Greenhouse Climate Management

---

## Objective

Conduct a comprehensive climate audit of a greenhouse facility to identify issues, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities. This hands-on exercise builds diagnostic and analytical skills essential for greenhouse operators.

---

## Materials Needed

- Thermometer (multiple)
- Hygrometer or RH meter
- Anemometer (air velocity)
- PAR meter (if available)
- Infrared thermometer (optional)
- Notebook and clipboard
- Camera or smartphone
- Measurement tape

---
