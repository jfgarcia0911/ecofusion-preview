### Section C: Short Answer

**26.** Temperature, Humidity/VPD, Air movement/circulation, Integration/control

**27.** VPD combines temperature and humidity into what plants actually experience. RH alone is temperature-dependent—the same RH at different temperatures creates different drying forces. VPD directly indicates the driving force for transpiration and plant water relations.

**28.** (Any three) Heat to raise temperature (lowers RH), ventilate to exchange humid air, use active dehumidifier, increase air circulation, reduce irrigation frequency, seal air leaks

**29.** (Any three) Thermal curtains, night setback temperature, seal air leaks, insulate north wall, HAF fans to eliminate stratification, maintain equipment for efficiency

**30.** (Any three) Wilting, stomatal closure, tip burn, stunted growth, leaf curling/cupping, reduced transpiration, stress symptoms

### Section D: Calculations

**31.**
VPD = SVP × (1 - RH/100)
VPD = 2.64 × (1 - 75/100)
VPD = 2.64 × 0.25
VPD = 0.66 kPa

This is below the optimal range (0.8-1.2 kPa)—conditions are too humid for ideal vegetative growth.

**32.**
Surface area (simplified):
Walls: (40×12×2) + (100×12×2) = 960 + 2,400 = 3,360 ft²
Roof: 40×100×1.15 = 4,600 ft²
Total ≈ 7,960 ft²

Q = U × A × ΔT
Q = 0.70 × 7,960 × (65-10)
Q = 0.70 × 7,960 × 55
Q = 306,460 BTU/hr (approximately 300,000 BTU/hr)

**33.**
Required CFM = 3,200 × 10 = 32,000 CFM
Number of fans = 32,000 ÷ 8,000 = 4 fans needed

### Section E: Case Analysis

**34a.**
VPD = 3.1 × (1 - 35/100) = 3.1 × 0.65 = 2.02 kPa

**34b.**
VPD is too high (>1.6 kPa), creating excessive transpirational demand. The air is too dry for the temperature, causing plant stress, stomatal closure, and tip burn. Plants cannot transpire adequately, leading to calcium deficiency symptoms (tip burn) and reduced growth.

**34c.**
(Any three appropriate solutions)
1. Increase humidity through fog/mist system to raise RH to 55-65%
2. Reduce temperature to 70-72°F to lower VPD
3. Improve air circulation to ensure even humidity distribution
4. Add shade to reduce heat load and temperature
5. Verify irrigation is adequate and timely
6. Monitor VPD continuously and maintain 0.8-1.2 kPa range
