# Module 7: CO₂ Enrichment
## Course 211: Greenhouse Climate Management

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
1. Understand the role of CO₂ in photosynthesis
2. Determine when CO₂ enrichment is economically justified
3. Select and install CO₂ generation or distribution systems
4. Monitor and control CO₂ levels safely
5. Calculate CO₂ requirements and costs
6. Integrate CO₂ enrichment with ventilation strategies
