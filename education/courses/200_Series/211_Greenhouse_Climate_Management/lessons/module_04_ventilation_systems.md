## Introduction

Ventilation serves multiple critical functions in greenhouses: temperature control, humidity management, CO₂ replenishment, and ethylene removal. Proper ventilation design is fundamental to successful climate control. Understanding both natural and mechanical systems allows you to select the right approach for your operation and climate.

---

## Ventilation Rate Calculations

### Summer Cooling Capacity

```
VENTILATION RATE FORMULA:

CFM = (Floor Area × 8) to (Floor Area × 12)

Example: 30' × 96' greenhouse = 2,880 ft²
CFM needed: 2,880 × 10 = 28,800 CFM

AIR CHANGES PER HOUR TARGETS:
Winter (minimum): 15-30 ACH
Spring/Fall: 30-60 ACH
Summer (maximum): 60-120 ACH
```

### Fan Selection and Sizing

Multiple smaller fans provide better control than one large fan through staging, redundancy, and improved distribution.

---

## Module Summary

Effective ventilation is essential for temperature control, humidity management, and maintaining healthy growing conditions. Choose natural ventilation for energy savings and gentle air exchange, or mechanical ventilation for precise control and reliable performance regardless of weather.
