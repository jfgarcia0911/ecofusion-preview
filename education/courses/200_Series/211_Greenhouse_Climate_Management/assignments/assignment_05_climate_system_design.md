# Assignment 5: Climate System Design
## Course 211: Greenhouse Climate Management

| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 14 |

## Required Components

### 1. Facility Specifications (4 pts)
- Greenhouse dimensions and volume
- Covering type
- Location and climate zone
- Crop requirements

### 2. Heating System (8 pts)
- Heat load calculations
- Heater type and capacity
- Distribution method
- Fuel selection and costs

### 3. Cooling/Ventilation System (8 pts)
- CFM requirements
- Fan selection and placement
- Intake design
- Evaporative cooling (if applicable)

### 4. Humidity Control (5 pts)
- Target VPD ranges
- Dehumidification strategy
- Condensation prevention

### 5. Control Integration (5 pts)
- Environmental controller selection
- Sensor placement
- Control algorithms
- Backup systems

**Deliverable:** 10-15 page climate control plan with equipment specifications, layout drawings, and cost analysis
