## Pricing Quick Reference

### Price Points by Channel

| Channel | % of Direct Price | Volume | Example (Lettuce) |
|---------|------------------|--------|-------------------|
| Farmers Market | 100% | Low | $4.50/head |
| CSA/Subscription | 90% | Medium | $4.00/head |
| Restaurant | 80% | Medium | $3.60/head |
| Grocery/Retail | 70% | High | $3.15/head |
| Wholesale | 60% | Very High | $2.70/head |

### Cost-Plus Formula

```
Price = Total Cost ÷ (1 - Target Margin)

Example:
Cost = $2.00/unit
Target Margin = 40%
Price = $2.00 ÷ 0.60 = $3.33/unit
```

## Daily Operations Checklist

### Morning (Start of Day)

```
□ System checks
  □ Water levels in all tanks/sumps
  □ Pump operation verification
  □ Aeration functioning
  □ No leaks or unusual sounds

□ Water testing
  □ Temperature
  □ pH
  □ Ammonia
  □ Dissolved oxygen (if applicable)

□ Fish check
  □ Behavior normal
  □ No visible disease/distress
  □ Mortality check
  □ Feed appropriate amount

□ Plant inspection
  □ Pest scouting (10% of plants)
  □ Disease check
  □ Nutrient deficiency signs
  □ Growth progress normal
```

### Production Tasks

```
□ Harvesting (as scheduled)
  □ Follow harvest list
  □ Quality check each unit
  □ Record quantities

□ Post-harvest
  □ Wash and sanitize
  □ Proper cooling
  □ Package and label
  □ Store at correct temperature

□ Transplanting (as scheduled)
  □ Transplant seedlings
  □ Record locations and quantities
  □ Clean and refill propagation trays

□ Seeding (as scheduled)
  □ Seed for future production
  □ Label with date and variety
  □ Proper moisture and coverage
```

### End of Day

```
□ Final system check
  □ All equipment operating
  □ Water levels stable
  □ No alarms or issues

□ Cleanup
  □ Work areas clean
  □ Tools sanitized
  □ Waste removed

□ Documentation
  □ Production records complete
  □ Any issues logged
  □ Next day prep identified

□ Security
  □ Facility secured
  □ Alarms set (if applicable)
  □ Climate controls verified
```

## Water Quality Quick Reference

### Target Parameters (Warm-water Systems)

| Parameter | Target Range | Action Level |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Temperature | 75-80°F | <70°F or >85°F |
| pH | 6.8-7.2 | <6.5 or >7.5 |
| Ammonia (NH3) | <0.5 ppm | >1.0 ppm |
| Nitrite (NO2) | <0.5 ppm | >1.0 ppm |
| Nitrate (NO3) | 20-100 ppm | >150 ppm |
| Dissolved Oxygen | >5 ppm | <4 ppm |

### Troubleshooting Quick Guide

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Immediate Action |
|---------|--------------|------------------|
| High ammonia | Overfeeding, dead fish, biofilter issue | Reduce feeding, check fish, test biofilter |
| Low pH | Normal system function | Add potassium hydroxide or calcium carbonate |
| High pH | New system, high carbonate water | Reduce base additions, check source water |
| Low oxygen | High temp, pump failure, overstocking | Increase aeration, check equipment |

## Quick Calculations

### Production Capacity

```
Plants per sq ft = 144 ÷ (spacing in inches)²
Example: 8"×8" spacing = 144 ÷ 64 = 2.25 plants/sq ft

Annual turns = 52 weeks ÷ (crop cycle in weeks)
Example: 5-week cycle = 52 ÷ 5 = 10.4 turns/year

Annual capacity = Plants/sq ft × Area × Turns
Example: 2.25 × 5,000 sq ft × 10 = 112,500 plants/year
```

### Financial Calculations

```
Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin
(Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

Labor Cost per Pound = Total Labor Cost ÷ Total Pounds Produced
```
