# Commercial Aquaponics Troubleshooting Guide
**Course 201 Cheatsheet**

---

## Water Quality Issues

### High Ammonia (>0.5 ppm)

**Symptoms:**
- Ammonia test shows elevated levels
- Fish gasping at surface
- Red or inflamed gills
- Lethargic fish behavior

**Common Causes:**
- Overfeeding
- Insufficient biofiltration
- Dead fish decomposing
- New system not fully cycled
- Biofilter disruption (cleaning, medication)

**Immediate Actions:**
1. STOP feeding (fast fish 24-48 hours)
2. Increase aeration (maximize dissolved oxygen)
3. Partial water exchange (25-50% with dechlorinated water)
4. Add ammonia binder/detoxifier (commercial product)
5. Test every 2-4 hours until dropping

**Long-Term Solutions:**
- Reduce feeding rate (calculate proper amount based on biomass)
- Expand biofilter capacity
- Improve water flow through biofilter
- Add beneficial bacteria supplement
- Never overclean biofilter (preserve bacteria colonies)

---

### High Nitrite (>0.5 ppm)

**Symptoms:**
- Brown or gray gills
- Fish gasping despite adequate aeration
- Lethargic behavior
- Nitrite test elevated

**Common Causes:**
- Incomplete nitrogen cycle
- Biofilter not established
- System overloaded

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Stop feeding
2. Add salt (1-3 ppt) to reduce nitrite toxicity
3. Increase aeration
4. Partial water exchange (25%)
5. Test daily

**Long-Term Solutions:**
- Allow biofilter to mature (patience)
- Add beneficial bacteria
- Reduce fish biomass if chronically high
- Ensure adequate biofilter surface area

---

### pH Crash (<6.0)

**Symptoms:**
- pH test shows <6.0
- Fish stress (gasping, erratic swimming)
- Slowed plant growth
- Potential ammonia spike (less nitrification)

**Common Causes:**
- Acid buildup from nitrification
- Insufficient buffering capacity
- Heavy fish load

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Stop feeding temporarily
2. Add pH buffer slowly (calcium carbonate, potassium carbonate)
3. Raise pH no faster than 0.2 units per hour
4. Increase aeration
5. Test every 30-60 minutes during correction

**Long-Term Solutions:**
- Regular buffer addition (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Add calcium or potassium carbonate proactively
- Monitor and maintain alkalinity (KH)
- Partial water exchanges with buffered water

---

### Low Dissolved Oxygen (<5 mg/L)

**Symptoms:**
- Fish gasping at surface
- Congregating near water inlet or aeration
- Reduced feeding activity
- DO meter shows <5 mg/L

**Common Causes:**
- Insufficient aeration
- Power failure to air pumps
- High water temperature (reduces DO capacity)
- Excessive algae bloom (nighttime respiration)
- Air stone clogging

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Increase aeration immediately
2. Add additional air pumps or stones
3. Reduce water temperature if possible
4. Reduce feeding (reduces oxygen demand)
5. Check all air pumps functioning

**Long-Term Solutions:**
- Size aeration for 1 CFM per 50-75 sq ft of grow space minimum
- Install backup air pumps
- Monitor DO regularly, especially in summer
- Keep spare air stones and pumps on hand
- Consider oxygen injection for intensive systems

## Plant Health Issues

### Nutrient Deficiencies

**Iron Deficiency**
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing between leaf veins (interveinal chlorosis), new growth affected first
- **Solution:** Add chelated iron (DTPA or EDDHA form), 2-3 ppm

**Potassium Deficiency**
- **Symptoms:** Brown edges on older leaves, weak stems
- **Solution:** Add potassium carbonate (also buffers pH), aim for 200-400 ppm

**Calcium Deficiency**
- **Symptoms:** Tip burn on lettuce, blossom end rot on tomatoes
- **Solution:** Add calcium carbonate or calcium chloride, improve water circulation

**Magnesium Deficiency**
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing between veins on older leaves
- **Solution:** Add magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt), 50 ppm

---

### Pests

**Aphids**
- **Identification:** Small green, black, or white insects clustered on new growth
- **Treatment:** Neem oil spray, insecticidal soap, introduce ladybugs
- **Prevention:** Monitor daily, maintain good airflow, quarantine new plants

**Fungus Gnats**
- **Identification:** Small flying insects around wet media or roots
- **Treatment:** Yellow sticky traps, beneficial nematodes, reduce moisture
- **Prevention:** Avoid standing water, good drainage

**Spider Mites**
- **Identification:** Fine webbing, stippled leaves, tiny mites visible with magnification
- **Treatment:** Neem oil, predatory mites, increase humidity
- **Prevention:** Regular monitoring, avoid overly dry conditions

**Thrips**
- **Identification:** Silvery streaks on leaves, tiny elongated insects
- **Treatment:** Spinosad spray, blue sticky traps, predatory mites
- **Prevention:** Screen greenhouse openings, monitor with sticky traps

---

### Diseases

**Pythium (Root Rot)**
- **Symptoms:** Brown, mushy roots, wilting despite adequate water
- **Treatment:** Remove affected plants, improve water circulation, reduce water temperature, hydrogen peroxide treatment
- **Prevention:** Maintain DO >5 mg/L, avoid stagnant water, don't overfeed fish (excess nutrients)

**Powdery Mildew**
- **Symptoms:** White powdery coating on leaves
- **Treatment:** Increase airflow, reduce humidity, potassium bicarbonate spray, neem oil
- **Prevention:** Good air circulation, avoid overhead watering, space plants properly

**Damping Off (Seedlings)**
- **Symptoms:** Seedling stems pinch off at soil line, collapse
- **Treatment:** Reduce moisture, increase air circulation, hydrogen peroxide drench
- **Prevention:** Don't oversaturate seed starts, good airflow in nursery

## Fish Health Issues

### Fish Not Feeding

**Possible Causes:**
- Water quality issues (test ammonia, nitrite, DO, pH)
- Temperature out of range
- Recent stress (handling, new fish)
- Disease onset
- Normal pre-spawning behavior

**Diagnosis Steps:**
1. Test all water quality parameters
2. Check temperature
3. Observe for other symptoms (spots, lesions, abnormal behavior)
4. Review recent changes (new fish, system work, etc.)
5. Fast for 24 hours and re-offer food

---

### Ich (White Spot Disease)

**Symptoms:**
- Small white spots on body and fins (look like salt grains)
- Fish flashing (rubbing against surfaces)
- Clamped fins

**Treatment:**
- Raise temperature to 80-82°F (speeds parasite life cycle)
- Add salt (1-3 ppt, safe for plants)
- Maintain for 10-14 days
- Improve water quality

**Prevention:**
- Quarantine new fish 2-4 weeks
- Reduce stress (good water quality, proper density)
- Stable temperature

---

### Columnaris (Bacterial Infection)

**Symptoms:**
- White or gray patches on body
- Frayed fins
- Gills affected (breathing difficulty)

**Treatment:**
- Improve water quality immediately
- Salt bath treatment (higher concentration)
- Antibiotic treatment (if permitted and necessary)
- Isolate affected fish

**Prevention:**
- Excellent water quality
- Reduce stress and overcrowding
- Quarantine new fish

## Production Issues

### Low Germination Rates

**Common Causes:**
- Old or improperly stored seeds
- Temperature too high or too low during germination
- Improper moisture (too wet or too dry)
- Seed planted too deep

**Solutions:**
- Use fresh seed from reputable suppliers
- Maintain 70-80°F for most crops
- Keep growing medium moist but not waterlogged
- Follow seed packet depth recommendations
- Test germination rate of seed lot before mass seeding

---

### Slow Plant Growth

**Possible Causes:**
- Inadequate light (intensity or duration)
- Suboptimal temperature
- Nutrient deficiency (test and supplement)
- Poor water circulation (roots not getting nutrients)
- pH out of range
- Root disease

**Diagnosis:**
- Check light levels with PAR meter (200-400 μmol/m²/s minimum for leafy greens)
- Verify temperature in optimal range (65-75°F for most)
- Test water quality and supplement as needed
- Ensure adequate water flow through grow beds/rafts
- Check pH (target 6.8-7.2)
- Inspect roots for health

---

### Inconsistent Sizing at Harvest

**Common Causes:**
- Variable planting dates within batch
- Uneven light distribution
- Inconsistent water flow/nutrient delivery
- Pest or disease pressure on some plants
- Genetic variability in seeds

**Solutions:**
- Standardize transplant timing (all same age)
- Ensure even light coverage (measure and adjust)
- Verify flow rates equal across all channels/rafts
- Remove diseased plants early
- Use quality, uniform seed varieties
- Cull undersized plants earlier to allocate resources

## Equipment Failures

### Pump Failure

**Symptoms:**
- No water flow
- Low flow rate
- Unusual noise or vibration

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Switch to backup pump if available
2. If no backup, increase aeration to maximum
3. Hand-circulate water if necessary (short-term)

**Troubleshooting:**
- Check power supply and breaker
- Verify impeller not clogged
- Check intake screen not blocked
- Verify plumbing not restricted
- Test capacitor (for single-phase pumps)

**Prevention:**
- Clean intake screens weekly
- Annual impeller inspection and cleaning
- Keep spare pump on hand
- Maintain pre-filter before pump

---

### Heater Failure

**Symptoms:**
- Water temperature dropping below target
- Heater not warm to touch when should be on

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Activate backup heater if available
2. Reduce water circulation (conserve heat)
3. Cover tanks or greenhouse to insulate
4. Emergency space heaters (carefully, watch for fire hazard)

**Prevention:**
- Insulate tanks and pipes
- Install backup heater
- Monitor temperature remotely with alerts
- Annual inspection of heating elements

---

### Power Outage

**Response Protocol:**
1. Start backup generator (manual or automatic)
2. Verify all critical systems running:
   - Fish tank aeration (highest priority)
   - Water circulation
   - Temperature control
3. Monitor fish behavior (gasping indicates insufficient O2)
4. If prolonged outage, reduce feeding (lower O2 demand)
5. Check generator fuel levels hourly during extended outages

**Preparedness:**
- Size generator for all critical loads + 20% buffer
- Test generator monthly
- Maintain 5-7 day fuel supply
- Have manual transfer switch or pre-wired connection
- Battery backup for monitoring systems
