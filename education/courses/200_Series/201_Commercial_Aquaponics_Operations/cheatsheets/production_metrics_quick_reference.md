## Production Capacity Calculations

### Formula
```
Annual Production (lbs) = Growing Area (sq ft) × Plants/sq ft ×
                          Harvests/year × Avg weight (lbs) × Success rate
```

## Fish Production Metrics

### Stocking Density
- **Conservative:** 0.5 lb fish per gallon at harvest
- **Moderate:** 0.75 lb/gallon
- **Intensive:** 1.0 lb/gallon (requires excellent management)

### Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

| Species | Target FCR | Excellent FCR |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Tilapia | <1.5 | 1.2-1.3 |
| Trout | <1.3 | 1.1-1.2 |
| Catfish | <1.8 | 1.5-1.6 |
| Bass | <1.7 | 1.5-1.6 |

**FCR Formula:** Total Feed Fed (lbs) ÷ Weight Gain (lbs)

### Feeding Rates (% of Body Weight per Day)
- Fingerlings: 3-5%
- Growing fish: 2-3%
- Finishing fish: 1-2%

## Water Quality Targets

| Parameter | Ideal Range | Action Required If |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Temperature | 72-78°F (tilapia) | <70°F or >82°F |
| pH | 6.8-7.2 | <6.5 or >7.5 |
| Ammonia (NH3) | 0-0.25 ppm | >0.5 ppm |
| Nitrite (NO2) | 0-0.25 ppm | >0.5 ppm |
| Nitrate (NO3) | 10-150 ppm | <5 or >200 ppm |
| Dissolved Oxygen | >6 mg/L | <5 mg/L |

## Break-Even Analysis

**Formula:**
```
Break-Even Revenue = Fixed Costs ÷ (1 - Variable Cost %)

Example:
Fixed Costs: $100,000/year
Variable Costs: 25% of revenue
Break-Even = $100,000 ÷ (1 - 0.25) = $133,333 annual revenue
```

## System Size Quick Calculator

### Fish to Plant Ratio (Feed-Based)
**40-60g feed per day per sq meter of plants**

**Example:**
- 500 sq m plants
- 50g feed per sq m per day
- = 25,000g = 55 lbs feed/day
- At 2% body weight feeding: 55 ÷ 0.02 = 2,750 lbs fish biomass needed

## Emergency Response Quick Checks

### Power Outage
1. Start generator (manual or auto)
2. Check fish behavior (gasping = O2 problem)
3. Verify aeration running
4. Monitor water temperature

### Ammonia Spike (>1.0 ppm)
1. STOP feeding immediately
2. Increase aeration (maximize DO)
3. Partial water exchange (25-50%)
4. Add ammonia binder
5. Test every 2 hours

### pH Crash (<6.0)
1. Stop feeding
2. Add buffer slowly (0.2 units/hour max)
3. Increase aeration
4. Test every 30-60 minutes

## Succession Planting Calculator

**Formula:**
```
Weekly Planting Needed = (Weekly Harvest Target ÷ Success Rate)

Example:
Target: 500 heads lettuce/week
Success rate: 90%
Weekly planting: 500 ÷ 0.90 = 556 plants

Production space needed:
556 plants ÷ 4 plants/sq ft = 139 sq ft planted weekly
139 sq ft × 5 weeks in cycle = 695 sq ft total
Add 20% buffer = 834 sq ft minimum
```
