# Commercial Aquaponics Startup Checklist
**Course 201 Cheatsheet**

---

## Phase 1: Planning & Validation (3-6 months before construction)

### Market Validation
- [ ] Identify target customer segments (restaurants, retail, D2C)
- [ ] Interview 20+ potential customers about needs and pricing
- [ ] Secure letters of intent or pre-sales from 5-10 customers
- [ ] Research competition and positioning
- [ ] Validate pricing assumptions with real market feedback

### Business Planning
- [ ] Complete commercial readiness self-assessment (Module 1)
- [ ] Develop comprehensive business plan
- [ ] Create detailed financial model (startup costs, 3-year projections)
- [ ] Build cash flow projections (monthly for Year 1)
- [ ] Calculate break-even point and timeline

### Location & Facility
- [ ] Research available properties/locations
- [ ] Verify zoning compliance (agricultural use permitted)
- [ ] Assess utility availability (3-phase power, water, sewer)
- [ ] Calculate distance to target markets (prefer <50 miles)
- [ ] Negotiate lease or purchase agreement

### Financing
- [ ] Calculate total capital needed (startup + 12-18 month reserve)
- [ ] Secure owner equity portion
- [ ] Apply for SBA loan or other financing (if needed)
- [ ] Explore grants (USDA, state agriculture programs)
- [ ] Ensure financing in place before construction

### Regulatory Research
- [ ] Identify all required permits and licenses
- [ ] Research state aquaculture regulations
- [ ] Understand FSMA requirements
- [ ] Contact local health department about food safety requirements
- [ ] Check insurance requirements

### Education & Experience
- [ ] Complete relevant training (PSA Grower Training, aquaponics courses)
- [ ] Visit 3-5 operating commercial aquaponics farms
- [ ] Work or apprentice at commercial operation (if limited experience)
- [ ] Join aquaponics associations and networks
- [ ] Identify mentors or advisors

---

## Phase 2: Design & Permitting (2-4 months)

### System Design
- [ ] Select production system type (DWC, NFT, media beds, hybrid)
- [ ] Design facility layout for optimal workflow
- [ ] Calculate production capacity needed
- [ ] Size fish tanks and biofiltration
- [ ] Plan for scalability (modular expansion)

### Detailed Planning
- [ ] Create CAD or detailed hand-drawn facility plans
- [ ] Develop equipment list with specifications
- [ ] Get quotes from suppliers (greenhouse, tanks, pumps, etc.)
- [ ] Plan electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems
- [ ] Design backup power and redundancy

### Permits & Approvals
- [ ] Submit building permit application (if constructing)
- [ ] Obtain business license
- [ ] Register with state department of agriculture
- [ ] Apply for aquaculture permits (if required in state)
- [ ] Secure water use permits (if applicable)
- [ ] Get health department approval (if processing on-site)

### Suppliers & Vendors
- [ ] Identify seed suppliers (primary and backup)
- [ ] Source fish fingerling supplier
- [ ] Establish fish feed supplier account
- [ ] Find packaging suppliers
- [ ] Identify equipment vendors
- [ ] Arrange test kit and supplies sources

---

## Phase 3: Construction & Setup (2-4 months)

### Facility Construction
- [ ] Site preparation and foundation
- [ ] Erect greenhouse or retrofit building
- [ ] Install electrical service (3-phase recommended)
- [ ] Plumbing installation (water supply, drains)
- [ ] Heating and cooling systems
- [ ] Lighting and ventilation

### Aquaponics System Installation
- [ ] Install fish tanks
- [ ] Set up biofilter system
- [ ] Install grow beds/rafts/channels
- [ ] Plumb all system connections
- [ ] Install aeration systems
- [ ] Set up sump tank and return systems

### Equipment & Infrastructure
- [ ] Install water pumps (primary and backup)
- [ ] Set up monitoring and control systems
- [ ] Install automated fish feeders
- [ ] Set up remote monitoring and alerts
- [ ] Install backup generator
- [ ] Set up workstations (harvest, transplant, packing)

### Food Safety Infrastructure
- [ ] Install handwashing stations (harvest and work areas)
- [ ] Set up bathroom facilities (compliant with code)
- [ ] Create cleaning and sanitation area
- [ ] Install refrigeration for post-harvest cooling
- [ ] Set up pest monitoring stations

---

## Phase 4: System Cycling (1-2 months)

### System Commissioning
- [ ] Fill system with water
- [ ] Test for leaks and fix any issues
- [ ] Run pumps and verify flow rates
- [ ] Test aeration and verify adequate DO
- [ ] Check all electrical and automation

### Cycling Process
- [ ] Add ammonia source (fish food or pure ammonia)
- [ ] Test water daily (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH)
- [ ] Monitor temperature
- [ ] Adjust pH as needed for bacterial growth
- [ ] Patience—typically 4-6 weeks until cycled

### Fish Stocking (Post-Cycle)
- [ ] Stock first cohort of fingerlings (start conservatively)
- [ ] Monitor fish health daily
- [ ] Begin feeding regimen (light at first)
- [ ] Track water quality closely for 2 weeks
- [ ] Gradually increase feeding as fish adapt

### First Plantings
- [ ] Set up nursery area
- [ ] Start seeds for first crops
- [ ] Transplant seedlings to system
- [ ] Monitor plant growth and nutrient uptake
- [ ] Adjust system as needed for plant health

---

## Phase 5: Operations Launch (Month 1-3)

### Team & Training
- [ ] Hire first employee(s) if planned
- [ ] Complete onboarding and training
- [ ] Review all SOPs with team
- [ ] Assign roles and responsibilities
- [ ] Establish communication systems

### Production Ramp-Up
- [ ] Implement succession planting schedule
- [ ] Track all plantings (dates, quantities, locations)
- [ ] Monitor growth rates and cycles
- [ ] Establish quality control checkpoints
- [ ] Begin regular data collection

### Customer Onboarding
- [ ] Contact pre-sold customers with launch timeline
- [ ] Schedule farm tours for key accounts
- [ ] Begin small deliveries (build confidence)
- [ ] Solicit feedback and adjust as needed
- [ ] Gradually increase volume

### Marketing Launch
- [ ] Launch website and social media
- [ ] Announce opening to local media
- [ ] Host farm opening event
- [ ] Begin farmers market presence (if planned)
- [ ] Start email newsletter

### Operations Systems
- [ ] Implement farm management software
- [ ] Set up bookkeeping and financial tracking
- [ ] Establish inventory management
- [ ] Create harvest and delivery schedules
- [ ] Begin customer invoicing

---

## Phase 6: Optimization (Month 4-12)

### Production Optimization
- [ ] Analyze yield data and adjust as needed
- [ ] Refine succession planting based on actual sales
- [ ] Optimize crop mix based on profitability
- [ ] Improve harvest efficiency
- [ ] Reduce waste through quality improvements

### Customer Development
- [ ] Expand customer base beyond pre-sales
- [ ] Diversify across channels (don't over-rely on one)
- [ ] Implement customer feedback loops
- [ ] Build loyalty through excellent service
- [ ] Track retention and acquisition costs

### Financial Management
- [ ] Review financial performance monthly
- [ ] Track against budget and projections
- [ ] Adjust pricing if needed
- [ ] Control costs vigilantly
- [ ] Build cash reserves

### Team Development
- [ ] Provide ongoing training
- [ ] Cross-train for redundancy
- [ ] Refine roles and workflows
- [ ] Address performance issues early
- [ ] Recognize and reward good performance

### Continuous Improvement
- [ ] Review and update SOPs based on learning
- [ ] Identify automation opportunities
- [ ] Implement efficiency improvements
- [ ] Expand product offerings carefully
- [ ] Plan for Year 2 growth

---

## Ongoing Monthly Checklist

### Operations
- [ ] Review production data vs. targets
- [ ] Update succession planting schedule
- [ ] Order seeds and supplies
- [ ] Preventive equipment maintenance
- [ ] Review and update SOPs as needed

### Financial
- [ ] Close monthly books
- [ ] Review P&L statement
- [ ] Analyze cash flow
- [ ] Invoice all customers
- [ ] Pay bills and manage payables

### Customer Management
- [ ] Review customer satisfaction
- [ ] Follow up on any quality issues
- [ ] Pipeline development for new customers
- [ ] Plan next month's deliveries
- [ ] Update website/social media

### Team
- [ ] Hold team meeting
- [ ] Provide feedback to employees
- [ ] Address any HR issues
- [ ] Plan training for next month
- [ ] Celebrate wins

---

## Quarterly Checklist

- [ ] Comprehensive financial review (compare to budget)
- [ ] Employee performance reviews
- [ ] Update business plan based on actuals
- [ ] Strategic planning for next quarter
- [ ] Equipment and facility deep maintenance
- [ ] Insurance and permit renewals (as needed)
- [ ] Market research and competitive analysis
- [ ] Technology and automation assessment

---

## Critical Success Reminders

**Financial:**
- Maintain 12-18 month cash reserves
- Track every dollar (revenue and expenses)
- Conservative revenue projections, comprehensive cost accounting
- No customer should be >20% of revenue

**Operations:**
- Quality and consistency above all else
- Document processes (SOPs) as you develop them
- Invest in team training and development
- Preventive maintenance beats emergency repairs

**Market:**
- Customer satisfaction drives retention
- Sell before you grow (don't overproduce)
- Continuous pipeline development
- Under-promise, over-deliver

**Personal:**
- Sustainable work pace (marathon, not sprint)
- Take time off (train team to cover you)
- Connect with peer farmers (you're not alone)
- Celebrate small wins along the way
