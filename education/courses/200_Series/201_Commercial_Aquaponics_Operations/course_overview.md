## Course Description

Transform your aquaponics knowledge into commercial-scale expertise. This comprehensive course covers facility design, production planning, economics, operations management, and the real-world challenges of running a profitable aquaponics business. Learn from industry case studies and develop the skills needed to plan, launch, and operate a successful commercial aquaponics venture.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:

1. **Design** commercial-scale aquaponic facilities with appropriate systems
2. **Develop** production plans for consistent, year-round output
3. **Calculate** and optimize operational economics for profitability
4. **Implement** standard operating procedures (SOPs) for quality and consistency
5. **Manage** staff, production schedules, and daily operations
6. **Navigate** regulatory requirements for commercial food production
7. **Analyze** real-world case studies to identify success factors

## Module Summaries

### Module 1: From Hobby to Business
Assess your readiness for commercial aquaponics. Understand the industry landscape, success factors, and common failure patterns. Complete a self-assessment for commercial viability.

**Key Topics:**
- Commercial readiness assessment
- Industry landscape and economics
- Success factors from industry leaders
- Common failure patterns and lessons learned

### Module 2: Facility Design & Layout
Apply commercial design principles to optimize workflow, efficiency, and scalability. Learn zone planning, space allocation, and facility layout best practices.

**Key Topics:**
- Facility zones and workflow optimization
- Space allocation guidelines
- Design principles for commercial success
- Scalability considerations

### Module 3: Production System Selection
Compare commercial system configurations and select appropriate systems for your production goals. Learn system integration approaches for optimal performance.

**Key Topics:**
- DWC, NFT, Media Bed, and Hybrid systems
- System selection decision matrices
- Commercial-scale considerations
- Integration approaches

### Module 4: Fish Production at Scale
Plan commercial fish production with multiple cohorts, optimize feed conversion, and manage continuous harvest for consistent revenue and nutrient production.

**Key Topics:**
- Species selection for commercial operations
- Cohort management for continuous production
- Feed economics and FCR optimization
- Harvest planning and fish sales

### Module 5: Plant Production Planning
Calculate production capacity, schedule plantings for continuous harvest, and manage crop rotation for year-round production and consistent revenue.

**Key Topics:**
- Production capacity calculations
- Succession planting schedules
- Crop mix strategy
- Seasonal production adjustments

### Module 6: Operations Management
Develop standard operating procedures and quality control protocols. Design daily operations schedules for consistent, efficient production.

**Key Topics:**
- Daily operations scheduling
- SOP development
- Quality control systems
- Efficiency optimization

### Module 7: Financial Management
Understand aquaponics financial structures, track key performance indicators, and develop pricing strategies for profitability.

**Key Topics:**
- Cost structure analysis
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Pricing strategy frameworks
- Financial reporting and analysis

### Module 8: Labor Management
Plan staffing for commercial operations, develop training programs, and manage performance for team effectiveness.

**Key Topics:**
- Staffing guidelines by scale
- Training program development
- Labor productivity targets
- Team management best practices

### Module 9: Sales & Customer Management
Identify and develop appropriate sales channels. Build customer relationships and manage customer acquisition and retention.

**Key Topics:**
- Sales channel comparison and selection
- Customer acquisition strategies
- Relationship management
- Order fulfillment and logistics

### Module 10: Regulatory Compliance
Navigate food safety regulations, permitting requirements, and compliance systems for commercial operations.

**Key Topics:**
- FDA FSMA requirements
- State and local regulations
- GAP certification
- Documentation requirements

### Module 11: Risk Management
Identify, assess, and mitigate risks in commercial aquaponics. Develop contingency plans and crisis response protocols.

**Key Topics:**
- Risk identification and assessment
- Insurance considerations
- Backup systems and redundancy
- Crisis management planning

### Module 12: Technology Integration
Leverage software and automation for improved operations. Implement data management systems for decision-making.

**Key Topics:**
- Farm management software
- Automation ROI analysis
- Data collection and analysis
- Technology implementation roadmap

### Module 13: Scaling Strategies
Plan for growth and facility expansion. Evaluate scaling approaches and timing for sustainable growth.

**Key Topics:**
- Growth planning frameworks
- Expansion decision criteria
- Financing growth
- Operational scaling challenges

### Module 14: Case Study Deep Dives
Analyze four real commercial aquaponics operations—successes and failures—to extract lessons and best practices.

**Key Topics:**
- Commercial farm case studies
- Success factor analysis
- Failure pattern recognition
- Lessons for your operation

### Module 15: Business Plan Development
Work through a guided business plan development process specifically for aquaponics ventures.

**Key Topics:**
- Business plan structure
- Financial projections
- Operations planning
- Executive summary writing

### Module 16: Final Project & Assessment
Present your commercial operations plan and demonstrate mastery of course concepts through comprehensive assessment.
