## KPI Definitions & Benchmarks

### Water Quality KPIs

| Parameter | Target | Alert Level | Critical |
|-----------|--------|-------------|----------|
| pH | 6.8-7.2 | <6.5 or >7.5 | <6.0 or >8.0 |
| Ammonia (TAN) | <1.0 mg/L | >1.5 mg/L | >3.0 mg/L |
| Nitrite | <1.0 mg/L | >1.5 mg/L | >3.0 mg/L |
| Nitrate | 40-150 mg/L | >200 mg/L | >300 mg/L |
| DO | >5 mg/L | <4 mg/L | <3 mg/L |
| Temperature | 75-80°F | <70°F or >85°F | <65°F or >90°F |

### Production KPIs

| Metric | Target | Calculation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| FCR | 1.5-1.8 | Feed consumed ÷ Weight gained |
| Fish Mortality | <5%/month | Deaths ÷ Starting population |
| Plant Loss | <10% | Culled ÷ Transplanted |
| Yield/Position | 6-10 oz | Harvest weight ÷ Positions |

### Financial KPIs

| Metric | Target | Calculation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Gross Margin | 45-55% | (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue |
| Net Margin | 15-25% | Net Profit ÷ Revenue |
| Revenue/sq ft | $50-100/yr | Annual Revenue ÷ Growing sq ft |
| Labor Cost/lb | $0.50-1.50 | Labor Cost ÷ lbs Produced |
