## System Component Details

### Fish Production
- 8 × 1,000-gallon tanks in cohort system
- Fingerling → Growout → Harvest progression
- 4-6 month production cycle
- Target harvest weight: 1.5-2.0 lbs

### Filtration
- Drum filter or radial flow settler for solids
- MBBR biofilters with K1 media
- Designed for 50+ lbs feed/day capacity

### Plant Production
- Dual system: NFT + DWC
- NFT for fast-growing leafy greens
- DWC for larger head lettuces
