## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Commercial Readiness Self-Assessment (15 points)

**Purpose:** Help you honestly evaluate whether you're ready to launch a commercial aquaponics operation.

**What You'll Do:**
- Complete comprehensive self-assessment questionnaire
- Analyze your target market and competitive landscape
- Identify skill and resource gaps
- Develop action plan to address gaps

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the gap between hobby and commercial operations
- Recognize critical success factors
- Identify areas needing further development

**Deliverable:** Completed self-assessment form with market analysis and gap analysis

---

### Assignment 2: Facility Layout Design Exercise (20 points)

**Purpose:** Apply commercial design principles to create an efficient facility layout.

**What You'll Do:**
- Plan functional zones (production, processing, storage, office, etc.)
- Optimize workflow to minimize movement and prevent cross-contamination
- Calculate space requirements for each zone
- Design for future scalability

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Master zone planning for commercial operations
- Understand workflow optimization principles
- Design scalable facilities

**Deliverable:** Facility layout drawing with zone labels and dimensions, workflow diagram, and justification narrative

---

### Assignment 3: Production Schedule Development (25 points)

**Purpose:** Create a detailed production schedule that ensures consistent weekly harvests.

**What You'll Do:**
- Select crop mix based on market demand and profitability
- Calculate planting frequencies for each crop
- Develop weekly seeding schedule
- Plan for seasonal adjustments
- Project weekly harvest volumes

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Master succession planting calculations
- Balance crop diversity with market demand
- Ensure consistent revenue through continuous harvests

**Deliverable:** 12-month production calendar with weekly planting and harvest schedules, including crop mix rationale

---

### Assignment 4: Financial Model Development (20 points)

**Purpose:** Build a comprehensive financial model demonstrating profitability potential.

**What You'll Do:**
- Project monthly revenues by product category
- Estimate all expense categories (labor, energy, supplies, etc.)
- Create monthly cash flow forecast
- Calculate break-even point
- Perform sensitivity analysis on key assumptions

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand aquaponics financial structures
- Project realistic revenues and expenses
- Manage cash flow and working capital
- Calculate profitability metrics

**Deliverable:** Excel/Google Sheets financial model with 3-year projections including P&L, cash flow, and break-even analysis

---

### Assignment 5: Commercial Business Plan (30 points)

**Purpose:** Create a complete, investor-ready business plan for a commercial aquaponics operation.

**What You'll Do:**
- Write compelling executive summary
- Describe company structure and management
- Analyze market opportunity and competition
- Define products, pricing, and sales strategy
- Detail operations plan with SOPs
- Present complete financial projections
- Address risks and mitigation strategies
- Request funding with clear use of proceeds

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Integrate all course concepts into cohesive plan
- Communicate business opportunity effectively
- Demonstrate operational and financial viability
- Prepare for funding conversations

**Deliverable:** Complete business plan (15-25 pages) following provided template, formatted professionally

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I submit assignments early?**
A: Yes! Early submissions are welcome and will be graded promptly.

**Q: What if I don't pass an assignment?**
A: Major projects (Assignments 4 and 5) may be revised and resubmitted once if your score is below 80%. In-class activities cannot be redone but are weighted less heavily.

**Q: Can I work with other students?**
A: In-class activities encourage discussion and collaboration. Take-home assignments and the final project must be your own individual work.

**Q: What resources can I use for assignments?**
A: You may use course materials, public research, industry reports, and any other ethical sources. Always cite your sources for data and research.

**Q: How detailed should the business plan be?**
A: Aim for 15-25 pages of substantive content. Focus on quality analysis over length. Use appendices for supporting documents.

**Q: Can I use my own business idea for assignments?**
A: Absolutely! Applying assignments to your real business plans makes the work more valuable and relevant.

**Q: What if I need an extension?**
A: Contact the instructor as soon as you know you need more time. Extensions are granted for valid reasons when requested in advance.

**Q: How is the final grade calculated?**
A: Your total points from all five assignments are summed (max 110 points). You need 88+ points (80%) to pass and receive your certificate.

## Academic Integrity Reminder

All submitted work must be your own original work. While you may discuss concepts and approaches with classmates, your written submissions must be independently created. Using someone else's work, copying from other business plans, or presenting AI-generated content as your own constitutes plagiarism and will result in serious consequences including possible dismissal from the course.

When using external sources (market research, industry data, etc.), always provide proper citations.
