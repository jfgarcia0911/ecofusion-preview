# Grading Rubric
## Course 201: Commercial Aquaponics Operations

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 201. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Commercial Readiness Self-Assessment | 15 | 13.6% | In-class activity evaluating business readiness |
| Assignment 2: Facility Layout Design Exercise | 20 | 18.2% | In-class facility design planning |
| Assignment 3: Production Schedule Development | 25 | 22.7% | In-class succession planting calendar |
| Assignment 4: Financial Model Development | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home P&L and cash flow modeling |
| Assignment 5: Commercial Business Plan | 30 | 27.3% | Comprehensive commercial operations plan |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

---

## Assignment 1: Commercial Readiness Self-Assessment (15 points)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Self-Assessment Completeness** (5 pts) | All sections thoroughly completed with honest reflection | Most sections completed adequately | Some sections missing or superficial | Minimal effort or incomplete |
| **Market Analysis** (5 pts) | Clear identification of target markets with supporting research | Basic market identification with some detail | Vague market understanding | No market analysis |
| **Gap Analysis** (5 pts) | Honest assessment of skills/resource gaps with specific action items | Some gaps identified with general plans | Limited gap identification | No gap analysis |

**Total:** 15 points

---

## Assignment 2: Facility Layout Design Exercise (20 points)
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Zone Planning** (5 pts) | All functional zones properly identified and sized | Most zones identified appropriately | Some zones missing or poorly sized | Inadequate zone planning |
| **Workflow Efficiency** (5 pts) | Excellent flow minimizing movement and cross-contamination | Good flow with minor inefficiencies | Poor flow with significant issues | No consideration of workflow |
| **Scalability** (5 pts) | Design clearly accommodates future expansion | Some scalability considered | Limited expansion capability | No scalability planning |
| **Space Utilization** (5 pts) | Optimal use of space with supporting calculations | Good space efficiency | Inefficient space use | Poor space planning |

**Total:** 20 points

---

## Assignment 3: Production Schedule Development (25 points)
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (6-7 pts) | Good (4-5 pts) | Fair (2-3 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Succession Planning** (7 pts) | Detailed weekly planting schedule for continuous harvest | Good schedule with minor gaps | Schedule has significant gaps | No succession planning |
| **Crop Mix Strategy** (6 pts) | Well-balanced mix optimized for revenue and risk | Reasonable crop diversity | Limited crop variety | Poor crop selection |
| **Harvest Scheduling** (6 pts) | Consistent weekly harvest volumes calculated | Mostly consistent harvest | Irregular harvest volumes | No harvest planning |
| **Seasonal Adjustments** (6 pts) | Clear adaptations for seasonal variations | Some seasonal consideration | Minimal seasonal planning | No seasonal adjustments |

**Total:** 25 points

---

## Assignment 4: Financial Model Development (20 points)
**Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Type:** Take-home assignment

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Revenue Projections** (5 pts) | Detailed, realistic projections with clear assumptions | Good projections with some support | Vague or unrealistic projections | Incomplete revenue model |
| **Expense Accuracy** (5 pts) | Comprehensive expense categories with accurate estimates | Most expenses identified reasonably | Significant expense gaps | Poor expense modeling |
| **Cash Flow Analysis** (5 pts) | Monthly cash flow with attention to timing and working capital | Basic cash flow projection | Limited cash flow consideration | No cash flow analysis |
| **Break-Even Analysis** (5 pts) | Clear break-even calculation with sensitivity analysis | Basic break-even identified | Unclear break-even | No break-even analysis |

**Total:** 20 points

---

## Assignment 5: Commercial Business Plan (30 points)
**Duration:** 2-4 hours | **Type:** Comprehensive project

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (7-8 pts) | Good (5-6 pts) | Fair (3-4 pts) | Poor (0-2 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Executive Summary** (7 pts) | Compelling, concise summary highlighting key points | Good summary covering main elements | Basic summary lacking clarity | Weak or missing summary |
| **Operations Plan** (8 pts) | Comprehensive SOPs, schedules, and quality systems | Good operations outline with detail | Limited operational planning | Inadequate operations plan |
| **Financial Projections** (8 pts) | Complete 3-year model with realistic assumptions | Good financials with minor gaps | Basic financials lacking detail | Poor or missing financials |
| **Market Strategy** (7 pts) | Clear target market, competitive advantage, and sales strategy | Good market understanding | Vague market approach | No market strategy |

**Total:** 30 points

## Grading Scale

| Percentage | Letter Grade | Point Range | Performance Level |
|------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 90-100% | A | 99-110 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |
| 80-89% | B | 88-98 | Good - Meets all requirements |
| 70-79% | C | 77-87 | Fair - Meets most requirements |
| 60-69% | D | 66-76 | Poor - Below expectations |
| Below 60% | F | 0-65 | Failing - Does not meet requirements |

**Note:** A minimum of 80% (88 points) is required to pass this course and receive a Certificate of Completion.

---

## Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% penalty
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% penalty
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval
- Extensions may be granted for extenuating circumstances with advance notice

---

## Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. While collaboration and discussion are encouraged during in-class activities, individual assignments must reflect your own analysis and work. Plagiarism or copying will result in a zero for the assignment and may result in dismissal from the course.

---

## Feedback and Revision

- In-class activities receive immediate feedback
- Take-home assignments graded within 5 business days
- Final projects graded within 7 business days
- Students may request clarification on grading decisions
- Major projects may be revised and resubmitted once if score is below 80%
