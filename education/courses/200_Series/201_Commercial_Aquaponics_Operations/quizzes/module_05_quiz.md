## Questions

**1. What is the typical growth cycle for butterhead lettuce from transplant to harvest?**
   - a) 14-21 days
   - b) 28-35 days
   - c) 35-42 days
   - d) 45-55 days

**2. For a 5,000 sq ft DWC facility with 35-day lettuce cycles, approximately how many harvests per year?**
   - a) 6-7 harvests
   - b) 8-9 harvests
   - c) 10-11 harvests
   - d) 13-15 harvests

**3. What is the recommended crop mix for first-year commercial operations?**
   - a) 100% lettuce for simplicity
   - b) 60% lettuce, 25% basil, 10% bok choy, 5% experimental
   - c) 50% fruiting crops, 50% leafy greens
   - d) Equal distribution across all crop types

**4. How much nursery space should be allocated as a percentage of total production area?**
   - a) 5-10%
   - b) 15-25%
   - c) 30-40%
   - d) 50-60%

**5. What is the primary indicator that seedlings are ready for transplanting?**
   - a) Height of 6-8 inches
   - b) 2-4 true leaves developed
   - c) 30 days after seeding
   - d) Roots visible on all sides of plug

**6. What is the target success rate for plants reaching market quality?**
   - a) 70-80%
   - b) 80-85%
   - c) 90-95%
   - d) 95-100%

**7. How often should succession plantings occur for continuous harvest?**
   - a) Daily
   - b) Every week or at minimum every 2 weeks
   - c) Monthly
   - d) Quarterly

**8. What is the recommended harvest efficiency target for experienced workers harvesting lettuce?**
   - a) 50-75 heads per hour
   - b) 100-150 heads per hour
   - c) 200-250 heads per hour
   - d) 300+ heads per hour

**9. Which crop typically generates the highest revenue per square foot annually?**
   - a) Butterhead lettuce
   - b) Basil
   - c) Bok choy
   - d) Kale

**10. What temperature range should seedlings be grown at in the nursery?**
   - a) 50-60°F
   - b) 65-75°F
   - c) 75-85°F
   - d) 85-95°F

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) 35-42 days** - Butterhead lettuce typically requires 35-42 days from transplant to harvest, though some varieties can be faster.

2. **c) 10-11 harvests** - With 35-day cycles, you can achieve 365 ÷ 35 = 10.4 harvest cycles per year.

3. **b) 60% lettuce, 25% basil, 10% bok choy, 5% experimental** - This de-risk strategy focuses on proven crops with established markets while allowing small-scale testing.

4. **b) 15-25%** - Nursery should be 15-25% of total production area to maintain proper succession planting pipeline.

5. **b) 2-4 true leaves developed** - The key indicator is 2-4 true leaves (beyond cotyledons), with strong roots and sturdy stem.

6. **c) 90-95%** - Target success rate of 90-95% allows for some loss while maintaining high productivity and quality.

7. **b) Every week or at minimum every 2 weeks** - Weekly or bi-weekly succession planting ensures continuous harvest and consistent customer supply.

8. **b) 100-150 heads per hour** - Experienced harvesters should achieve 100-150 lettuce heads per hour (120 is a good target).

9. **b) Basil** - Basil typically generates highest revenue per sq ft at $6-10/lb with 13 cycles annually, producing $7,000+ per 100 sq ft.

10. **b) 65-75°F** - Seedling growth area should maintain 65-75°F with proper lighting and airflow for strong stem development.
