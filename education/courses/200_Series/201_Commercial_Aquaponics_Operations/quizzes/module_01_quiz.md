**Course 201: Commercial Aquaponics Operations**

## Questions

**1. What percentage of commercial aquaponics ventures achieve profitability within 3 years?**
   - a) 10-20%
   - b) 30-40%
   - c) 50-60%
   - d) 70-80%

**2. What is the typical timeline to break-even for commercial aquaponics operations?**
   - a) 6-12 months
   - b) 12-24 months
   - c) 3-5 years
   - d) 7-10 years

**3. Which is the #1 reason commercial aquaponics ventures fail?**
   - a) Technical system problems
   - b) Running out of capital before profitability
   - c) Competition
   - d) Regulatory issues

**4. What is the recommended minimum operating reserve for commercial aquaponics startup?**
   - a) 3-6 months of expenses
   - b) 6-9 months of expenses
   - c) 12-18 months of expenses
   - d) 24 months of expenses

**5. Which business model typically offers the highest margins for commercial aquaponics?**
   - a) Wholesale distribution
   - b) Restaurant & specialty retail
   - c) Direct-to-consumer (farmers markets, CSA)
   - d) Food service institutional

**6. What is a critical success factor from industry leaders?**
   - a) Growing exotic crops
   - b) Market-first mindset (sell before you build)
   - c) Building largest facility possible
   - d) Focusing only on fish sales

**7. Which common failure pattern describes building full capacity before securing customers?**
   - a) Undercapitalization
   - b) Overbuilding
   - c) Technical overwhelm
   - d) Solo operator burnout

**8. In the commercial readiness self-assessment, what score indicates "strong candidate"?**
   - a) 50-64 points
   - b) 65-79 points
   - c) 80-89 points
   - d) 90-100 points

**9. What is a realistic expectation for startup capital for a small commercial operation (<5,000 sq ft)?**
   - a) $25,000-$50,000
   - b) $50,000-$100,000
   - c) $100,000-$250,000
   - d) $250,000-$500,000

**10. Which of these is NOT a key difference between hobby and commercial aquaponics?**
   - a) Focus shifts from learning to profit
   - b) Regulatory burden increases significantly
   - c) Fish species must be different
   - d) Quality standards must be consistent

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 30-40%** - Only 30-40% of commercial ventures achieve profitability within 3 years
2. **c) 3-5 years** - Realistic timeline to break-even is 3-5 years for most operations
3. **b) Running out of capital before profitability** - Financial issues are #1 cause of failure
4. **c) 12-18 months of expenses** - Recommended minimum operating reserve
5. **c) Direct-to-consumer** - D2C channels offer highest margins (50-80% gross margin)
6. **b) Market-first mindset** - Secure customers before building is critical success factor
7. **b) Overbuilding** - Building full capacity before proving market demand
8. **d) 90-100 points** - Indicates excellent readiness to proceed
9. **c) $100,000-$250,000** - Realistic startup capital for small commercial operation
10. **c) Fish species must be different** - Same species (tilapia, etc.) work at both scales
