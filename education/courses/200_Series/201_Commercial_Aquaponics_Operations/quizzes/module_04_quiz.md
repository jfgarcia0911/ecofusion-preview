## Questions

**1. What is the target Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) for tilapia in commercial aquaponics?**
   - a) 0.8-1.0
   - b) 1.3-1.5
   - c) 1.8-2.2
   - d) 2.5-3.0

**2. What percentage of fish production costs does feed typically represent?**
   - a) 10-20%
   - b) 20-30%
   - c) 40-60%
   - d) 70-80%

**3. What is the recommended stocking density for tilapia at harvest weight?**
   - a) 0.1-0.3 lb/gallon
   - b) 0.5-1.0 lb/gallon
   - c) 1.5-2.0 lb/gallon
   - d) 2.5-3.0 lb/gallon

**4. Why are multiple fish cohorts essential for commercial operations?**
   - a) To meet regulatory requirements
   - b) Continuous nutrient production and steady revenue
   - c) To reduce initial investment
   - d) To simplify management

**5. What is the typical time to market size for tilapia (1.0-1.5 lbs)?**
   - a) 3-4 months
   - b) 6-9 months
   - c) 12-15 months
   - d) 18-24 months

**6. Which species is best suited for cold climate operations (minimal heating)?**
   - a) Tilapia
   - b) Barramundi
   - c) Rainbow trout
   - d) Channel catfish

**7. What is the recommended feed-based calculation for plant capacity?**
   - a) 10-20g feed per day per sq meter
   - b) 20-30g feed per day per sq meter
   - c) 40-60g feed per day per sq meter
   - d) 80-100g feed per day per sq meter

**8. What percentage of fish revenue do most commercial aquaponics operations derive?**
   - a) 5-10%
   - b) 10-30%
   - c) 40-60%
   - d) 70-90%

**9. What is the minimum dissolved oxygen level for optimal fish health?**
   - a) >2 mg/L
   - b) >3 mg/L
   - c) >5 mg/L
   - d) >8 mg/L

**10. What is the recommended practice for fish before harvest?**
   - a) Increase feeding for maximum weight
   - b) Withhold feed for 24-48 hours
   - c) Add antibiotics for food safety
   - d) Lower water temperature

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 1.3-1.5** - Target FCR for tilapia is 1.3-1.5, meaning 1.3-1.5 lbs of feed per pound of fish weight gain.

2. **c) 40-60%** - Feed represents 40-60% of fish production costs in aquaponics, making FCR optimization critical.

3. **b) 0.5-1.0 lb/gallon** - Recommended stocking density is 0.5-1.0 lb/gallon at harvest, with 0.5 being safer for system stability.

4. **b) Continuous nutrient production and steady revenue** - Multiple cohorts ensure continuous nutrient production, steady fish sales, risk distribution, and consistent customer supply.

5. **b) 6-9 months** - Tilapia typically reach market size (1.0-1.5 lbs) in 6-9 months under optimal conditions.

6. **c) Rainbow trout** - Rainbow trout thrive in cold water (55-65°F) and are ideal for northern climates with minimal heating costs.

7. **c) 40-60g feed per day per sq meter** - The recommended calculation is 40-60g feed per day per square meter of plant growing area.

8. **b) 10-30%** - Most commercial aquaponics operations derive 10-30% of revenue from fish sales, with plants being primary revenue.

9. **c) >5 mg/L** - Minimum dissolved oxygen should be above 5 mg/L, with 6+ mg/L being optimal for fish health and feed conversion.

10. **b) Withhold feed for 24-48 hours** - Fish should be fasted for 24-48 hours before harvest to clean the gut and improve processing quality.
