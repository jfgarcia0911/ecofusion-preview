## Questions

**1. What is the typical operating cost for labor as a percentage of revenue in commercial aquaponics?**
   - a) 15-25%
   - b) 25-35%
   - c) 35-45%
   - d) 55-65%

**2. What is the target net profit margin for mature commercial aquaponics operations?**
   - a) 5-10%
   - b) 10-20%
   - c) 25-35%
   - d) 40-50%

**3. Which sales channel typically offers the highest profit margins?**
   - a) Wholesale/distribution
   - b) Restaurants
   - c) Grocery retail
   - d) Farmers market/direct-to-consumer

**4. What is the formula for calculating break-even point in units?**
   - a) Fixed Costs ÷ Selling Price
   - b) Fixed Costs ÷ Variable Costs
   - c) Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin
   - d) Total Costs ÷ Selling Price

**5. If your full cost per head of lettuce is $1.68 and you want a 40% margin, what should your minimum price be?**
   - a) $2.35
   - b) $2.80
   - c) $3.15
   - d) $3.50

**6. What percentage does energy (electric, HVAC, heating) typically represent of operating costs?**
   - a) 5-10%
   - b) 15-25%
   - c) 30-40%
   - d) 45-55%

**7. Which KPI measures the efficiency of space utilization?**
   - a) FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)
   - b) Revenue per square foot
   - c) Customer retention rate
   - d) Labor cost per pound

**8. What is the typical wholesale/distribution price discount compared to retail pricing?**
   - a) 10-20% lower
   - b) 25-40% lower
   - c) 50-60% lower
   - d) 70-80% lower

**9. What does a gross margin of 50% indicate?**
   - a) Net profit is 50% of revenue
   - b) Revenue minus COGS equals 50% of revenue
   - c) Operating expenses are 50% of revenue
   - d) Break-even has been achieved

**10. For a restaurant sales channel, what is the typical gross margin range?**
   - a) 20-40%
   - b) 50-70%
   - c) 80-100%
   - d) 100-120%

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) 35-45%** - Labor typically represents 35-45% of operating costs in commercial aquaponics, making it the largest expense category.

2. **b) 10-20%** - Target net margin for mature operations is 10-20%, reflecting the tight margins in agricultural production.

3. **d) Farmers market/direct-to-consumer** - Direct-to-consumer channels offer the highest margins (80-100%) but require more marketing effort.

4. **c) Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin** - Break-even units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit), which equals Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin.

5. **b) $2.80** - Price = Cost ÷ (1 - Margin%) = $1.68 ÷ 0.60 = $2.80 per head minimum for 40% margin.

6. **b) 15-25%** - Energy costs typically represent 15-25% of revenue, varying by climate and heating/cooling needs.

7. **b) Revenue per square foot** - Revenue per sq ft measures how effectively growing space is being utilized economically (target: $30-60/yr).

8. **b) 25-40% lower** - Wholesale pricing is typically 25-40% lower than retail to account for distributor margins and volume sales.

9. **b) Revenue minus COGS equals 50% of revenue** - Gross margin = (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) ÷ Revenue, so 50% means half of revenue remains after direct costs.

10. **b) 50-70%** - Restaurant sales channel typically offers 50-70% margins, balancing volume and pricing between wholesale and direct retail.
