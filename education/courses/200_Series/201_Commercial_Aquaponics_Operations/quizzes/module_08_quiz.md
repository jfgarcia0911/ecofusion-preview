## Questions

**1. For a small commercial operation (5,000-10,000 sq ft), what is the typical total staffing in FTE?**
   - a) 1.5-2 FTE
   - b) 3.5-4 FTE
   - c) 6-8 FTE
   - d) 12-15 FTE

**2. What is the typical compensation range for a Lead Grower/Production Manager?**
   - a) $25,000-$35,000
   - b) $35,000-$50,000
   - c) $55,000-$70,000
   - d) $75,000-$90,000

**3. During which week of onboarding should new employees begin independent work with oversight?**
   - a) Week 1
   - b) Week 2
   - c) Week 3-4
   - d) Month 2-3

**4. What is the target productivity rate for lettuce transplanting?**
   - a) 50 plants/hour
   - b) 100 plants/hour
   - c) 200 plants/hour
   - d) 300 plants/hour

**5. How much do payroll taxes and benefits typically add to base compensation costs?**
   - a) 10-15%
   - b) 20-30%
   - c) 40-50%
   - d) 60-70%

**6. What is the recommended frequency for formal quarterly performance reviews?**
   - a) Monthly
   - b) Every 3 months
   - c) Every 6 months
   - d) Annually only

**7. Which is NOT a common motivator for farm employees?**
   - a) Fair compensation and benefits
   - b) Connection to sustainability mission
   - c) Unlimited overtime opportunities
   - d) Learning and skill development

**8. What is the recommended target for harvest productivity (lettuce)?**
   - a) 60-80 heads/hour
   - b) 80-100 heads/hour
   - c) 120 heads/hour
   - d) 200+ heads/hour

**9. How long should a structured onboarding program last before employees achieve proficiency?**
   - a) 1 week
   - b) 2-3 weeks
   - c) 2-3 months
   - d) 6 months

**10. For sustainable scheduling, what is the maximum number of consecutive days recommended without a day off?**
   - a) 5 days
   - b) 6 days
   - c) 10 days
   - d) 14 days

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 3.5-4 FTE** - Small commercial operations (5,000-10,000 sq ft) typically require 3.5-4 FTE including owner/manager, lead grower, production assistant, and part-time harvest help.

2. **b) $35,000-$50,000** - Lead Grower/Production Manager compensation typically ranges from $35,000-$50,000 plus benefits, varying by region and experience.

3. **c) Week 3-4** - Weeks 3-4 of onboarding focus on independent work with oversight, allowing employees to execute tasks independently while receiving guidance.

4. **b) 100 plants/hour** - Target productivity for lettuce transplanting is 100 plants per hour, including preparation time.

5. **b) 20-30%** - Payroll taxes and benefits typically add 20-30% (often calculated at 25%) to base compensation costs.

6. **b) Every 3 months** - Formal quarterly reviews should occur every 3 months, with informal monthly check-ins and comprehensive annual reviews.

7. **c) Unlimited overtime opportunities** - While competitive pay matters, excessive overtime leads to burnout. Sustainable schedules are more motivating than unlimited overtime.

8. **c) 120 heads/hour** - Target harvest productivity for experienced workers is 100-150 heads per hour, with 120 being a good benchmark.

9. **c) 2-3 months** - Structured onboarding lasts 2-3 months (Months 2-3: Proficiency Development phase) before employees achieve full proficiency.

10. **b) 6 days** - While 7-day weeks may be necessary occasionally, sustainable scheduling avoids extended periods without days off. Staggering schedules and split weekend coverage help prevent burnout.
