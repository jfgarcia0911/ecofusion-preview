## Questions

**1. What is the typical production capacity range for DWC raft systems (lettuce)?**
   - a) 5-10 lbs/sq ft/year
   - b) 10-15 lbs/sq ft/year
   - c) 15-25 lbs/sq ft/year
   - d) 30-40 lbs/sq ft/year

**2. Which system type is most vulnerable to pump failure due to roots drying quickly?**
   - a) DWC raft systems
   - b) NFT systems
   - c) Media bed systems
   - d) Hybrid systems

**3. What is the recommended water depth for DWC raft channels?**
   - a) 4-6 inches
   - b) 6-8 inches
   - c) 8-10 inches
   - d) 10-12 inches

**4. Which crop category is BEST suited for media bed systems?**
   - a) Leafy greens only
   - b) Herbs requiring minimal support
   - c) Fruiting crops and root vegetables
   - d) Microgreens

**5. What is the typical capital cost range for NFT systems per square foot?**
   - a) $5-15/sq ft
   - b) $10-25/sq ft
   - c) $20-40/sq ft
   - d) $30-50/sq ft

**6. Why do most successful commercial operations use hybrid systems?**
   - a) Lower initial investment
   - b) Market diversity and risk mitigation
   - c) Simpler management
   - d) Regulatory requirements

**7. What is the recommended slope for NFT channels?**
   - a) 1:10 to 1:20
   - b) 1:30 to 1:100
   - c) 1:100 to 1:150
   - d) Completely level

**8. For a facility smaller than 2,000 sq ft, which system types are most appropriate?**
   - a) DWC or hybrid only
   - b) NFT or media beds
   - c) DWC only
   - d) Media beds only

**9. In a multi-loop system, what is the primary advantage over single-loop?**
   - a) Lower construction cost
   - b) Simpler operation and maintenance
   - c) Optimize water quality per crop type and isolate problems
   - d) Faster installation time

**10. What plant spacing is recommended for lettuce in DWC systems?**
   - a) 3 inches on center
   - b) 6 inches on center
   - c) 9 inches on center
   - d) 12 inches on center

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) 15-25 lbs/sq ft/year** - DWC raft systems typically produce 15-25 lbs/sq ft/year for lettuce with 10+ cycles annually.

2. **b) NFT systems** - NFT is most vulnerable to pump failure because roots are exposed to air and dry quickly without constant water flow.

3. **d) 10-12 inches** - DWC channels should maintain 10-12 inches minimum water depth, with 12-16 inch channel depth.

4. **c) Fruiting crops and root vegetables** - Media beds excel at growing tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, and root vegetables that need support.

5. **b) $10-25/sq ft** - NFT systems typically cost $10-25/sq ft, making them lower investment than DWC or media beds.

6. **b) Market diversity and risk mitigation** - Hybrid systems allow different crops, multiple revenue streams, risk distribution, and seasonal adaptation.

7. **b) 1:30 to 1:100** - NFT channels require 1:100 to 1:30 slope (1 inch drop per 50-100 inches length is optimal).

8. **b) NFT or media beds** - For facilities under 2,000 sq ft, NFT or media beds are more appropriate than large DWC systems.

9. **c) Optimize water quality per crop type and isolate problems** - Multi-loop systems allow different nutrient levels per crop and contain problems to single loops.

10. **b) 6 inches on center** - Standard lettuce spacing in DWC is 6 inches on center (8 inches for larger greens).
