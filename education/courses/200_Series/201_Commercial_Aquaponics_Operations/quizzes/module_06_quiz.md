## Questions

**1. What is the recommended time of day for harvest operations?**
   - a) Late afternoon for maximum sweetness
   - b) Midday for best logistics
   - c) Early morning for cool temperatures
   - d) Evening to allow overnight cooling

**2. Which section of an SOP defines "what to do if parameters are out of range"?**
   - a) Purpose
   - b) Procedure
   - c) Acceptable ranges
   - d) Corrective actions

**3. What percentage of leaves with damage/defects is acceptable for Grade A butterhead lettuce?**
   - a) 0% (zero tolerance)
   - b) <2%
   - c) <5%
   - d) <10%

**4. How often should formal performance reviews occur for employees?**
   - a) Monthly
   - b) Quarterly
   - c) Bi-annually
   - d) Annually

**5. What is the primary purpose of daily team huddles?**
   - a) Detailed training sessions
   - b) Review priorities, assign tasks, address immediate issues
   - c) Performance evaluations
   - d) Customer service calls

**6. Which maintenance frequency is recommended for calibrating pH and DO meters?**
   - a) Daily
   - b) Weekly
   - c) Monthly
   - d) Quarterly

**7. What is the recommended target for labor cost as a percentage of revenue?**
   - a) <15%
   - b) <25%
   - c) <35%
   - d) <50%

**8. At what inventory level should automatic reordering be triggered?**
   - a) When stock reaches zero
   - b) When stock reaches maximum level
   - c) When stock hits minimum level
   - d) On a fixed monthly schedule

**9. What is the recommended duration for comprehensive water quality testing?**
   - a) 5-10 minutes
   - b) 20 minutes
   - c) 45-60 minutes
   - d) 2 hours

**10. Which quality control checkpoint occurs 1-2 days before harvest?**
   - a) Seedling inspection
   - b) Mid-growth check
   - c) Pre-harvest assessment
   - d) Post-harvest quality check

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) Early morning for cool temperatures** - Harvest should occur in early morning when temperatures are cool to maintain product quality and shelf life.

2. **d) Corrective actions** - The corrective actions section defines specific steps to take when parameters fall outside acceptable ranges.

3. **b) <2%** - Grade A butterhead lettuce should have less than 2% damaged or discolored leaves for premium quality.

4. **b) Quarterly** - Formal performance reviews should occur quarterly, with informal monthly check-ins and comprehensive annual reviews.

5. **b) Review priorities, assign tasks, address immediate issues** - Daily huddles (10-15 minutes) focus on the day's priorities, task assignments, and immediate issues.

6. **b) Weekly** - pH and DO meters should be calibrated weekly to ensure accurate readings for water quality management.

7. **c) <35%** - Target labor cost should be less than 35% of revenue, though 35-45% is typical for aquaponics operations.

8. **c) When stock hits minimum level** - Reorder triggers should activate when inventory hits minimum level, factoring in lead time to avoid stockouts.

9. **b) 20 minutes** - Comprehensive water quality testing at all test points should take approximately 20 minutes when conducted efficiently.

10. **c) Pre-harvest assessment** - Pre-harvest assessment occurs 1-2 days before harvest to estimate size/weight, project quality grading, and plan customer allocation.
