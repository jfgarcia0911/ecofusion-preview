# Glossary: Commercial Aquaponics Operations
**Course 201 Resource**

---

## A

**Aeration** - Process of adding air/oxygen to water, critical for fish respiration and biofilter function.

**Alkalinity** - Water's buffering capacity, resistance to pH changes. Measured as carbonate hardness (KH).

**Ammonia (NH3)** - Toxic nitrogenous waste excreted by fish. First step in nitrogen cycle. Target: <0.25 ppm.

**Aquaculture** - Farming of aquatic organisms (fish, shellfish, aquatic plants).

**Aquaponics** - Integrated system combining aquaculture (fish farming) with hydroponics (soilless plant growing) in symbiotic environment.

---

## B

**Biofilter** - Component housing beneficial bacteria that convert ammonia to nitrite to nitrate (nitrification).

**Biofiltration** - Biological process of converting fish waste into plant-available nutrients.

**Break-Even Point** - Revenue level at which total costs equal total revenue (no profit, no loss).

**Buffer** - Substance that stabilizes pH by neutralizing acids. Common buffers: calcium carbonate, potassium carbonate.

---

## C

**Capex (Capital Expenditure)** - Upfront costs for long-term assets (greenhouse, tanks, equipment).

**CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute)** - Measurement of air flow rate for aeration systems.

**Chelated Iron** - Iron bonded to organic molecule for improved plant uptake in aquaponics systems.

**Cohort** - Group of fish of similar age/size, managed together.

**COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)** - Direct costs to produce goods (seeds, feed, packaging). Excludes operating expenses.

**Contribution Margin** - Revenue minus variable costs. Amount each sale contributes to covering fixed costs and profit.

**CSA (Community Supported Agriculture)** - Business model where customers buy shares upfront, receive regular product throughout season.

---

## D

**D2C (Direct-to-Consumer)** - Sales directly to end consumers (farmers markets, CSA, farm stand) vs. through intermediaries.

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** - Amount of oxygen dissolved in water. Critical for fish and plant roots. Target: >5 mg/L, ideally >6 mg/L.

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)** - Aquaponics system where plants float on rafts in channels of water. Also called raft system.

---

## E

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measurement of dissolved salts/minerals in water. Indicates nutrient levels.

**Effluent** - Water flowing out of a system component (e.g., biofilter effluent).

---

## F

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** - Pounds of feed required per pound of fish weight gain. Lower is better. Formula: Feed Fed ÷ Weight Gain.

**Fingerling** - Young fish, typically 1-3 inches long, ready for grow-out.

**FSMA (Food Safety Modernization Act)** - FDA law establishing food safety standards for farms, including Produce Safety Rule.

**FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)** - Measure of labor (1 FTE = 40 hours/week, 2,080 hours/year).

---

## G

**GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)** - USDA verification program for food safety practices on farms.

**Germination** - Process of seed sprouting and beginning growth.

**GPM (Gallons Per Minute)** - Flow rate measurement for water pumps.

**Gross Margin** - (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue. Percentage of revenue remaining after direct costs.

**Grow-out** - Process of raising fish from fingerlings to market size.

---

## H

**Hydroponic** - Method of growing plants without soil, using nutrient solution.

---

## I

**Influent** - Water flowing into a system component.

**Interveinal Chlorosis** - Yellowing between leaf veins, often indicates nutrient deficiency (iron, magnesium).

---

## K

**KH (Carbonate Hardness)** - Measure of alkalinity/buffering capacity. Prevents pH swings.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)** - Metric used to measure business performance (e.g., revenue per sq ft, customer retention rate).

---

## L

**LECA (Lightweight Expanded Clay Aggregate)** - Common growing media for media bed systems. Porous clay balls.

---

## M

**Media Bed** - Aquaponics system using solid media (clay pellets, gravel) as plant growing substrate and biofilter.

**Mineralization** - Breakdown of solid waste into dissolved nutrients available to plants.

---

## N

**Net Margin** - Net Profit ÷ Revenue. Percentage of revenue remaining after all costs (COGS + operating expenses).

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)** - System where thin film of water flows through channels with plant roots suspended in it.

**Nitrification** - Two-step bacterial process converting ammonia → nitrite → nitrate.

**Nitrite (NO2)** - Intermediate product in nitrogen cycle. Toxic to fish. Target: <0.25 ppm.

**Nitrate (NO3)** - End product of nitrification. Primary nitrogen source for plants. Target: 10-150 ppm.

**Nitrogen Cycle** - Process by which ammonia from fish waste is converted to nitrite then nitrate by beneficial bacteria.

---

## O

**Opex (Operating Expenditure)** - Ongoing operational costs (labor, utilities, supplies).

---

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)** - Light spectrum and intensity usable by plants for photosynthesis. Measured in μmol/m²/s.

**pH** - Measure of acidity/alkalinity on 0-14 scale. Target for aquaponics: 6.8-7.2.

**P&L (Profit & Loss Statement)** - Financial report showing revenue, costs, and profit over period.

**ppm (Parts Per Million)** - Concentration measurement for dissolved substances in water.

**Produce Safety Rule** - FDA regulation under FSMA establishing standards for growing, harvesting, packing produce.

**PSA (Produce Safety Alliance)** - Organization providing FSMA-compliant grower training.

---

## R

**Raft System** - See DWC (Deep Water Culture).

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - (Gain - Cost) ÷ Cost. Measures profitability of investment.

---

## S

**SBA (Small Business Administration)** - U.S. agency providing loans and support to small businesses.

**Solids Filtration** - Removal of solid fish waste before biofiltration. Protects biofilter from clogging.

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)** - Documented step-by-step process for performing tasks consistently.

**Stocking Density** - Amount of fish per unit of water. Measured in lbs/gallon or kg/liter.

**Succession Planting** - Staggered planting schedule to ensure continuous harvest rather than single large harvest.

**Sump** - Tank collecting water from system before pumping back to fish tanks or grow areas.

---

## T

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Measurement of all dissolved substances in water.

**Transplant** - Moving seedling from nursery to main production system.

**Turnover** - Number of growing cycles per year. Higher turnover = more harvests annually.

---

## V

**VAPG (Value-Added Producer Grant)** - USDA grant program for agricultural producers adding value to products.

**Variable Costs** - Costs that change with production volume (seeds, feed, packaging). Opposite of fixed costs.

---

## W

**Water Exchange** - Partial replacement of system water with fresh water. Used to dilute accumulated salts or correct water quality.

## Units of Measurement

**Volume:**
- Gallon (gal) = 3.785 liters
- Liter (L)
- Cubic feet = 7.48 gallons

**Weight:**
- Pound (lb) = 0.45 kilograms
- Ounce (oz) = 28.35 grams

**Temperature:**
- Fahrenheit (°F)
- Celsius (°C): °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9

**Concentration:**
- ppm (parts per million) = mg/L (milligrams per liter)

**Area:**
- Square feet (sq ft)
- Square meters (sq m): 1 sq m = 10.76 sq ft

**Light:**
- μmol/m²/s (micromoles per square meter per second)
- PPFD: Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density
