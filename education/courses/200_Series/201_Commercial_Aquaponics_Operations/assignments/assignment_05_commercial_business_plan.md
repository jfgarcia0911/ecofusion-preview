## Executive Summary (7 points)

**Length:** 1-2 pages | **Write this LAST** (even though it goes first)

This is the most important section—many readers only read the executive summary. Make it compelling.

### Required Elements:

**Business Concept (1 paragraph)**
- What is the business? What do you produce and sell?
- What problem does it solve or need does it meet?

**Mission & Vision (2-3 sentences each)**
- Mission: Why you exist, what you do for whom
- Vision: Where you're headed in 3-5 years

**Products & Target Market (1 paragraph)**
- Specific products and services offered
- Who buys them and why

**Competitive Advantage (1 paragraph)**
- What makes you different and better than alternatives
- Why customers will choose you

**Management Team (1 paragraph)**
- Key people and their relevant experience
- Advisory board or mentors

**Financial Highlights (bullet points)**
- Startup capital required
- Year 1 revenue projection
- Year 3 revenue and net income projections
- Break-even timeline
- Return on investment projection

**Funding Request (if applicable)**
- Amount requested
- Use of funds (high level)
- Proposed terms (equity, debt, etc.)

### Grading Criteria:
- Compelling and concise (7 pts)
- Covers all required elements clearly
- Could stand alone as pitch document
- Professional tone and error-free writing

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation (3 points)

**Length:** 1-2 pages

### Required Elements:

**Risk Identification**
Identify and analyze at least 8-10 risks across categories:
- **Production risks** (crop failure, fish mortality, system failure)
- **Market risks** (competition, price fluctuations, customer loss)
- **Financial risks** (cash flow gaps, cost overruns)
- **Operational risks** (key person dependency, supply chain)
- **External risks** (regulatory changes, climate events)

**Risk Assessment**
For each major risk:
- Likelihood (High/Medium/Low)
- Impact (High/Medium/Low)
- Priority (based on likelihood × impact)

**Mitigation Strategies**
For each high-priority risk:
- Specific mitigation strategy
- Implementation timeline
- Contingency plans

### Grading Criteria:
- Thorough risk identification (3 pts)
- Realistic assessments
- Practical mitigation strategies

## Tips for Success

### Before You Start
- Review all previous assignments—you'll build on them
- Study the sample business plans in course resources
- Outline your entire plan before writing
- Gather all research and data you'll need

### While Writing
- Write sections in order they're easiest, not document order
- Use data and facts, not just opinions
- Be specific—avoid vague generalities
- Show, don't just tell (use examples, data, specifics)
- Write clearly and concisely—avoid jargon

### Quality Control
- Read it out loud to catch awkward phrasing
- Use spell-check AND manual proofreading
- Have someone else review it for clarity
- Check all numbers twice
- Ensure consistent formatting throughout

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Too optimistic:** Be realistic, even conservative
- **Lacking specifics:** "Good marketing" isn't a strategy
- **Ignoring competition:** They exist—acknowledge and address
- **Weak financials:** Numbers must make sense and tie together
- **No risk discussion:** All businesses have risks
- **Poor presentation:** Typos and formatting matter
