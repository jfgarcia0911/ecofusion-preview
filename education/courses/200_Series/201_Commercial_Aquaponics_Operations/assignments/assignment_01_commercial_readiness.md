## Purpose

Transitioning from hobby aquaponics to commercial operations requires much more than scaling up your system. This self-assessment helps you honestly evaluate your readiness across multiple dimensions: skills, resources, market understanding, and personal commitment. The goal is not to discourage you, but to identify gaps early so you can address them before investing significant time and money.

## Tips for Success

- **Be honest:** This is for your benefit. Overestimating your readiness could lead to costly mistakes.
- **Be specific:** "Learn marketing" is too vague. "Complete online marketing course and attend 3 farmers markets" is actionable.
- **Do research:** Don't guess at market prices—actually visit markets and stores to gather real data.
- **Think critically:** Really imagine yourself running this business daily. Is this the life you want?
- **Ask for help:** If you're unsure how to answer a question, ask the instructor.
