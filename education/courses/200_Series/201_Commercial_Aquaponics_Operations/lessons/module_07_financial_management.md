## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Production KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Range | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Yield per sq ft** | Total lbs ÷ Grow Area | 15-40 lbs/yr | Space efficiency |
| **Revenue per sq ft** | Total Revenue ÷ Grow Area | $30-60/yr | Economic efficiency |
| **Turns per year** | Crop cycles completed | 6-12 (leafy) | Capacity utilization |
| **Crop loss rate** | Lost plants ÷ Planted | <10% | Production quality |
| **FCR (fish)** | Feed lbs ÷ Fish weight gain | <1.6 | Fish efficiency |

### Financial KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Range | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Gross margin** | (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue | 40-60% | Pricing effectiveness |
| **Net margin** | Net Profit ÷ Revenue | 10-20% | Overall profitability |
| **Labor cost/lb** | Labor $ ÷ Total lbs | <$1.50 | Labor efficiency |
| **Energy cost/lb** | Energy $ ÷ Total lbs | <$0.50 | Energy efficiency |
| **Break-even point** | Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin | Know yours | Survival threshold |

### Sales KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target Range | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Customer retention** | Repeat customers ÷ Total | >70% | Relationship strength |
| **Order fill rate** | Orders filled complete ÷ Total | >95% | Reliability |
| **Average order value** | Total Revenue ÷ Orders | Track trend | Sales effectiveness |
| **Customer acquisition cost** | Marketing $ ÷ New customers | Know yours | Marketing efficiency |

## Pricing Strategies

### Strategy 1: Cost-Plus Pricing

```
COST-PLUS PRICING CALCULATION

Step 1: Calculate Direct Costs
═══════════════════════════════
Seeds:           $0.05/head
Media:           $0.10/head
Labor (growing): $0.60/head
Nutrients:       $0.08/head
────────────────────────────
Direct Cost:     $0.83/head

Step 2: Add Overhead Allocation
═══════════════════════════════
Overhead per head: $0.85/head
(Facility, energy, admin, etc.)

Step 3: Calculate Full Cost
═══════════════════════════════
Direct + Overhead: $1.68/head

Step 4: Add Target Margin
═══════════════════════════════
Target Margin: 40%
Price = Cost ÷ (1 - Margin%)
Price = $1.68 ÷ 0.60 = $2.80/head

MINIMUM PRICE FOR 40% MARGIN: $2.80/head
```

### Strategy 2: Market-Based Pricing

```
MARKET-BASED PRICING ANALYSIS

Research Competitor Prices:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Conventional lettuce (grocery):     $1.50-2.00/head
Organic lettuce (grocery):          $3.00-4.00/head
Hydroponic lettuce (grocery):       $3.50-4.50/head
Local farm lettuce (farmers mkt):   $3.50-5.00/head

Position Your Product:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Local + sustainable aquaponics = premium positioning
• Premium over organic: 10-25%
• Premium over hydroponic: 5-15%

Price Range Analysis:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Bottom of range (meet competition): $3.50/head
Mid-range (slight premium):         $4.00/head
Top of range (full premium):        $4.50/head

MARKET-BASED TARGET PRICE: $3.75-4.25/head
```

### Strategy 3: Channel-Based Pricing

Different channels require different pricing:

| Channel | Margin | Volume | Price Point | Notes |
|---------|--------|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Wholesale/Distribution** | 20-40% | High | $2.50-3.00 | Volume, reliability |
| **Restaurants** | 50-70% | Medium | $3.25-3.75 | Relationship, consistency |
| **Farmers Market** | 80-100% | Low | $4.00-5.00 | Direct, premium story |
| **CSA/Subscription** | 70-90% | Medium | $3.75-4.25 | Commitment, regularity |
| **Grocery Retail** | 30-50% | High | $2.75-3.25 | Volume, shelf presence |

## Break-Even Analysis

### Calculating Break-Even Point

```
BREAK-EVEN CALCULATION

Step 1: Identify Fixed Costs (Monthly)
══════════════════════════════════════════
Rent:                    $2,000
Insurance:               $400
Base utilities:          $500
Loan payments:           $1,000
Manager salary:          $4,000
────────────────────────────────
Total Fixed Costs:       $7,900/month

Step 2: Calculate Contribution Margin
══════════════════════════════════════════
Average selling price:   $4.00/unit
Variable cost per unit:  $1.50/unit
────────────────────────────────
Contribution margin:     $2.50/unit

Step 3: Calculate Break-Even Units
══════════════════════════════════════════
Break-Even = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin
Break-Even = $7,900 ÷ $2.50 = 3,160 units/month

Step 4: Convert to Revenue
══════════════════════════════════════════
Break-Even Revenue = 3,160 × $4.00 = $12,640/month

YOUR MINIMUM TO SURVIVE: $12,640/month in sales
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Know your true costs** - Track everything; surprises kill businesses
2. **Labor is your biggest cost** - Efficiency and automation matter
3. **Price strategically** - Cost-plus sets the floor; market sets the ceiling
4. **Different channels, different pricing** - Adjust for volume and margin expectations
5. **Track KPIs religiously** - What gets measured gets managed
6. **Cash flow matters more than profit** - You can be profitable and still run out of cash
