## 6.1 Daily Operations Framework

### The Commercial Aquaponics Daily Cycle

**Morning Operations (6:00 AM - 10:00 AM):**
- System checks and monitoring
- Fish feeding (first feeding)
- Harvest execution
- Quality inspections
- Immediate post-harvest processing

**Midday Operations (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM):**
- Planting and transplanting
- Customer deliveries/pickups
- System maintenance
- Fish feeding (second feeding)
- Record keeping and data entry

**Afternoon Operations (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM):**
- Plant care (pruning, spacing, pest management)
- Cleaning and sanitation
- Equipment maintenance
- Supply management and ordering
- Planning for next day

**Evening Check (6:00 PM):**
- Final system check
- Fish feeding (third feeding if applicable)
- Temperature and automation verification
- Security check

## 6.2 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### Why SOPs Matter

**Benefits:**
- Consistency in product quality
- Easier training of new employees
- Reduced errors and waste
- Faster problem identification
- Compliance documentation for food safety
- Operational continuity when key staff absent

### Essential SOPs for Aquaponics Operations

**Core System SOPs:**
1. Daily system monitoring and checks
2. Water quality testing procedures
3. Fish feeding protocols
4. Planting and transplanting procedures
5. Harvest procedures by crop type
6. Post-harvest handling and storage
7. Cleaning and sanitation protocols
8. Equipment maintenance schedules
9. Emergency response procedures

### SOP Template Structure

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                        STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Number: AP-001
Title: Daily Water Quality Testing
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Department: Production
Approved by: Operations Manager

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. PURPOSE
   To ensure consistent and accurate water quality monitoring for optimal
   system health and food safety compliance.

2. SCOPE
   This procedure applies to all water quality testing performed daily in
   the commercial aquaponics facility.

3. FREQUENCY
   Daily at 3:00 PM (minimum)
   Additional testing as needed based on observations or system changes

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
   - Lead Grower: Conduct tests and record data
   - Manager: Review data weekly and identify trends

5. MATERIALS NEEDED
   □ API Freshwater Master Test Kit
   □ Digital pH meter (calibrated weekly)
   □ Dissolved oxygen meter
   □ Thermometer
   □ Water quality log sheets
   □ Clean sample containers
   □ Nitrile gloves

6. PROCEDURE

   6.1 PREPARATION
       a. Gather all testing materials
       b. Put on clean nitrile gloves
       c. Verify test kits are not expired
       d. Calibrate digital meters if needed

   6.2 SAMPLE COLLECTION
       a. Collect samples from designated test points:
          - Fish tank #1 (main production tank)
          - Biofilter effluent
          - DWC raft area (mid-system)
          - Sump tank
       b. Use clean containers for each sample
       c. Label samples with location and time

   6.3 TESTING SEQUENCE
       a. Temperature (all locations)
       b. Dissolved Oxygen (fish tank and DWC)
       c. pH (all locations)
       d. Ammonia (fish tank and biofilter effluent)
       e. Nitrite (fish tank and biofilter effluent)
       f. Nitrate (fish tank and DWC area)

   6.4 RECORDING DATA
       a. Record all values in daily log
       b. Note any unusual observations
       c. Compare to target ranges
       d. Flag any out-of-range values

   6.5 CORRECTIVE ACTIONS (if needed)
       a. pH out of range (6.0-7.5): Add buffer per pH Adjustment SOP
       b. Ammonia >0.5 ppm: Reduce feeding, check biofilter
       c. Nitrite >0.5 ppm: Reduce feeding, add aeration
       d. DO <5 mg/L: Check aerators, increase air supply
       e. Document all corrections made

7. ACCEPTABLE RANGES

   Parameter          Target Range       Action Required If
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Temperature        72-78°F            <70 or >82°F
   pH                 6.8-7.2            <6.5 or >7.5
   Dissolved Oxygen   >6 mg/L            <5 mg/L
   Ammonia            0-0.25 ppm         >0.5 ppm
   Nitrite            0-0.25 ppm         >0.5 ppm
   Nitrate            10-150 ppm         <5 or >200 ppm

8. DOCUMENTATION
   - Daily Water Quality Log (Form WQ-01)
   - Monthly Water Quality Summary (Form WQ-02)
   - Corrective Action Log (if actions taken)

9. REFERENCES
   - Fish Health Monitoring SOP (AP-003)
   - pH Adjustment SOP (AP-012)
   - Emergency Response Plan

10. REVISION HISTORY
    Version 1.0 - June 2024 - Initial procedure
    Version 2.0 - January 2025 - Added DO testing, updated ranges

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Quality Control Checkpoints

**Production Quality Checkpoints:**

1. **Seedling Inspection (at transplant)**
   - Minimum 2 true leaves
   - Healthy root development
   - No disease or pest signs
   - Reject <10% expected

2. **Mid-Growth Check (week 2-3)**
   - Growth rate on track
   - No nutrient deficiencies
   - Early pest detection
   - Spacing appropriate

3. **Pre-Harvest Assessment (1-2 days before)**
   - Size and weight estimation
   - Quality grading projection
   - Harvest timing confirmation
   - Customer allocation planning

4. **Harvest Quality Control (at harvest)**
   - Grade assignment (A, B, Cull)
   - Size and weight verification
   - Damage or defect identification
   - Sort and separate by grade

5. **Post-Harvest Quality Check (before delivery)**
   - Final inspection after washing/cooling
   - Packaging quality verification
   - Labeling accuracy
   - Temperature check

## 6.4 Workflow Optimization

### Facility Layout for Efficiency

**Workflow Principles:**
1. **Minimize unnecessary movement** - arrange areas logically
2. **Group related tasks** - planting, harvest, packing in zones
3. **Ergonomic work heights** - reduce bending and reaching
4. **Clear pathways** - adequate space for equipment and people
5. **Proximity of frequent tasks** - daily tasks near each other

### Labor Efficiency Strategies

**Task Batching:**
- Group similar tasks together
- Example: Harvest all lettuce at once vs. scattered throughout day
- Reduces setup time and context switching

**Standard Work Times:**
- Establish time standards for common tasks
- Track actual vs. standard
- Identify inefficiencies

**Example Standard Times:**
```
Task                          Standard Time       Notes
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Transplant lettuce (100)      45 min             Including prep
Harvest lettuce (100 heads)   40 min             Experienced worker
Wash & package lettuce (50)   60 min             Including cooling
Water quality testing         20 min             All test points
Feed fish (all tanks)         15 min             3 tanks
```

**Efficiency Metrics to Track:**
- Labor hours per pound produced
- Harvest rate (units per hour)
- Transplant rate (units per hour)
- Revenue per labor hour
- Labor cost as % of revenue (target: <35%)

## 6.5 Inventory and Supply Management

### Supply Categories

**Critical Supplies (zero tolerance for stockout):**
- Fish feed (1-month minimum on hand)
- Backup aeration equipment
- Water test kits and reagents
- pH buffers

**Regular Supplies (weekly/monthly replenishment):**
- Seeds and seedlings
- Growing media/plugs
- Cleaning and sanitation chemicals
- Packaging materials

**Periodic Supplies (quarterly/annual):**
- Replacement parts (pumps, plumbing)
- Growing equipment (rafts, net pots)
- Tools and safety equipment

### Inventory Management System

**Min/Max Inventory Approach:**

```
SUPPLY INVENTORY TRACKER

Item              Min Stock   Max Stock   Current   Reorder?   Lead Time
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Fish Feed (lbs)      500        1000        650        No         1 week
Lettuce Seeds       5,000      15,000      3,200       YES        2 weeks
Net Pots (2")       1,000       3,000      1,500       No         3 weeks
pH Down (gal)          2           5          3         No         1 week
Seed Plugs (tray)     50         150         40        YES        1 week
```

**Reorder Triggers:**
- Automatic reorder when inventory hits minimum
- Factor in lead time to avoid stockouts
- Bulk purchasing for cost savings where appropriate

## 6.6 Maintenance Programs

### Preventive Maintenance Schedule

**Daily Maintenance:**
- Visual equipment inspection
- Check for leaks or unusual sounds
- Clear any debris from pumps/filters
- Verify automation functioning

**Weekly Maintenance:**
- Clean pre-filters and screens
- Check and clean aeration diffusers
- Inspect plumbing connections
- Calibrate pH and DO meters

**Monthly Maintenance:**
- Deep clean biofilter media
- Inspect and service pumps
- Check electrical connections
- Inventory spare parts

**Quarterly Maintenance:**
- Replace worn pump parts
- Deep clean entire system
- Test backup power systems
- Service heating/cooling equipment

**Annual Maintenance:**
- Major equipment overhaul
- Replace aging components proactively
- Update automation software/firmware
- Professional inspections (electrical, structural)
