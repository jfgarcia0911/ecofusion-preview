# Activity: Commercial Facility Assessment
## Course 201: Commercial Aquaponics Operations

---

## Overview

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Activity Type** | Site Assessment Exercise |
| **Duration** | 2-3 hours |
| **Materials** | Assessment checklist, measuring tools |
| **Deliverable** | Complete facility assessment report |

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Evaluate potential sites for commercial aquaponics
2. Assess infrastructure requirements
3. Identify opportunities and constraints
4. Develop cost estimates for site preparation
