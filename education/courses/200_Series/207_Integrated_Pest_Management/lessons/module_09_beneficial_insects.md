# Module 9: Beneficial Insects Deep Dive
## Course 207: Integrated Pest Management

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Identify common beneficial insects in greenhouse environments
- Distinguish beneficial insects from pests
- Create habitat to support beneficial populations
- Implement conservation biological control strategies
- Understand beneficial insect life cycles
- Troubleshoot biological control programs
