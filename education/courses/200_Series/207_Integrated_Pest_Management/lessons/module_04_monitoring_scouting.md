║   ENVIRONMENTAL                                                           ║

║                                                                           ║
║   DOCUMENTATION                                                           ║

║   Comprehensive reports             Monthly              1-2 hours       ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ADJUST FREQUENCY BASED ON:                                              ║
║   • Pest pressure (increase if problems detected)                        ║

```

### Sample Weekly Schedule

```
    MONDAY          • Quick walk-through (15 min)
                    • Check sticky traps (15 min)
                    • Review environmental logs (5 min)

    TUESDAY         • Day off from scouting

    WEDNESDAY       • Detailed scouting - Section A (30 min)
                    • Record findings (10 min)

    THURSDAY        • Quick walk-through (15 min)

    FRIDAY          • Detailed scouting - Section B (30 min)
                    • Record findings (10 min)
                    • Weekly data review (15 min)

    WEEKEND         • Monitor only if critical issues present
```

║                                                                           ║
║                    STICKY TRAP SETUP & USE                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   YELLOW TRAPS                      BLUE TRAPS                           ║
║   ════════════                      ══════════                           ║
║   Target pests:                     Target pests:                        ║
║   • Whiteflies                      • Thrips (primary)                   ║
║   • Winged aphids                   • Leafminers                         ║
║   • Fungus gnats                                                         ║
║   • Leafminers                                                           ║
║   • Shore flies                                                          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TRAP PLACEMENT:                                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   VERTICAL ORIENTATION:                                                   ║
║   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐                   ║
║   │                                                  │                   ║
║   │   [TRAP]  ←→  [TRAP]  ←→  [TRAP]  ←→  [TRAP]   │                   ║
║   │     │           │           │           │        │                   ║
║   │   Plant       Plant       Plant       Plant     │                   ║
║   │                                                  │                   ║
║   │   Hang slightly above canopy level              │                   ║
║   │   Space evenly throughout facility              │                   ║
║   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DENSITY:                                                                ║
║   • General monitoring: 1 trap per 500-1000 sq ft                       ║
║   • Intensive monitoring: 1 trap per 100-200 sq ft                      ║
║   • At entry points and near vents                                       ║
║                                                                           ║
║   HORIZONTAL PLACEMENT (for fungus gnats):                               ║
║   Place on growing media surface                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   COUNTING & RECORDING:                                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   1. Number each trap location on facility map                           ║
║   2. Count pests weekly (same day/time)                                  ║
║   3. Record count for each trap                                          ║
║   4. Note trends (increasing/decreasing)                                 ║
║   5. Replace when full or every 2-4 weeks                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   COUNTING METHOD:                                                        ║
║   • Low catch: Count all                                                 ║
║   • High catch: Divide trap into quadrants, count 1, multiply by 4      ║
║   • Very high: Estimate or record as ">100"                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ACTION THRESHOLDS (examples - adjust for your operation):              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Whiteflies:     0-2 per trap = Monitor                                 ║
║                   3-10 per trap = Increased scouting, consider action    ║
║                   >10 per trap = Take action                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Fungus Gnats:   0-5 per trap = Acceptable                              ║
║                   6-20 per trap = Investigate moisture levels            ║
║                   >20 per trap = Action needed                           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Thrips:         0-1 per trap = Monitor                                 ║
║                   2-5 per trap = Increase inspection frequency           ║
║                   >5 per trap = Take action (virus risk!)                ║
║                                                                           ║

## Plant Inspection Techniques

### The "Top to Bottom" Method

```
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                             │
    │   SYSTEMATIC PLANT INSPECTION                               │
    │   ═══════════════════════════                               │
    │                                                             │
    │   START AT TOP, WORK DOWN:                                  │
    │                                                             │
    │   [1] GROWING POINT / NEWEST LEAVES                         │
    │       ╭─────╮                                               │
    │       │ ╭─╮ │  ← Check for:                                │
    │       │ │ │ │    • Aphids (love new growth)                │
    │       ╰─╯ ╰─╯    • Distortion (virus, mites)               │
    │                  • Thrips (hide in folds)                   │
    │                                                             │
    │   [2] UPPER CANOPY LEAVES                                   │
    │       ╭─────╮                                               │
    │       │     │  ← Check for:                                │
    │       ╰─────╯    • Spider mite webbing                      │
    │                  • Whiteflies on underside                  │
    │                  • Powdery mildew                           │
    │                                                             │
    │   [3] MID-LEVEL LEAVES                                      │
    │       ╭─────╮                                               │
    │       │     │  ← Check for:                                │
    │       ╰─────╯    • General health                           │
    │                  • Leaf spots                               │
    │                  • Chewing damage                           │
    │                                                             │
    │   [4] LOWER/OLDER LEAVES                                    │
    │       ╭─────╮                                               │
    │       │ ░░░ │  ← Check for:                                │
    │       ╰─────╯    • Early disease signs                      │
    │                  • Senescence (normal aging)                │
    │                  • Spider mites (often start here)          │
    │                                                             │
    │   [5] STEMS                                                 │
    │        │││   ← Check for:                                  │
    │        │││     • Lesions, cankers                           │
    │        │││     • Aphids, whitefly scales                    │
    │        │││     • Mechanical damage                          │
    │                                                             │
    │   [6] MEDIA SURFACE                                         │
    │      ═══════  ← Check for:                                  │
    │                • Fungus gnats                               │
    │                • Algae growth                               │
    │                • Shore flies                                │
    │                                                             │
    │   [7] ROOTS (periodically)                                  │
    │       │││││  ← Pull a plant to check:                      │
    │       │││││    • Root health (white/cream = good)           │
    │                • Root rot signs                             │
    │                • Root aphids                                │
    │                                                             │
    │   TIME PER PLANT: 15-30 seconds for quick check            │
    │                   1-2 minutes for detailed inspection       │
    │                                                             │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Inspection Checklist

```
    For each plant or area inspected:

    FOLIAGE:
    ☐ Color (yellowing, bronzing, dark green)
    ☐ Texture (curling, cupping, wilting)
    ☐ Spots or lesions
    ☐ Webbing or fuzzy growth
    ☐ Chewing damage
    ☐ Stunting or distortion

    PESTS OBSERVED:
    ☐ Type of pest
    ☐ Life stages present (eggs, nymphs, adults)
    ☐ Location on plant
    ☐ Approximate population (low/medium/high)

    OVERALL PLANT HEALTH:
    ☐ Vigor (1-5 scale)
    ☐ Growth rate (normal/slow/fast)
    ☐ Flowering/fruiting status
    ☐ Any unusual observations
```

### Sample Layout & Route

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║            GREENHOUSE SCOUTING PATTERN                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  ║
║   │ ENTRY →                                                           │  ║
║   │   ↓                                                               │  ║
║   │ ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════   │  ║
║   │                                                                   │  ║
║   │  [X]────→[X]────→[X]                                             │  ║
║   │   ↓       ↓       ↓                                               │  ║
║   │  [X]     [X]     [X]      ← Sticky traps (numbered)              │  ║
║   │   ↓       ↓       ↓                                               │  ║
║   │  [X]────→[X]────→[X]                                             │  ║
║   │   ↓                                                               │  ║
║   │ ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════   │  ║
║   │                                                                   │  ║
║   │  SCOUTING ROUTE:                                                  │  ║
║   │  ═══════════════                                                  │  ║
║   │  1. Start at entry point (avoid bringing pests in)               │  ║
║   │  2. Check sticky traps first (count, record)                     │  ║
║   │  3. Walk aisles in zigzag or "W" pattern                         │  ║
║   │  4. Inspect plants at regular intervals                          │  ║
║   │  5. Focus on high-risk areas:                                    │  ║
║   │     • Near doors and vents                                        │  ║
║   │     • Previously infested areas                                   │  ║
║   │     • New transplants                                             │  ║
║   │     • Stressed plants                                             │  ║
║   │  6. End at exit (avoid backtracking)                             │  ║
║   │                                                                   │  ║
║   │  SAMPLING DENSITY:                                                │  ║
║   │  • Uniform crop: 1 plant per 100-200 plants                      │  ║
║   │  • High-value: Every 3rd-5th plant                               │  ║
║   │  • Known problem areas: Every plant                              │  ║
║   │                                                                   │  ║
║   └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Regular monitoring is the foundation** of effective IPM programs
2. **Sticky traps provide early warning** before pest populations explode
3. **Systematic inspection methods** ensure thorough coverage and consistency
4. **Action thresholds guide decisions** about when to intervene
5. **Good record keeping** enables trend analysis and continuous improvement
6. **Consistency matters** - scout on a schedule, not when convenient

### Monitoring Best Practices Checklist

- ☐ Scout at least 2-3 times per week
- ☐ Use sticky traps throughout facility
- ☐ Follow consistent scouting route
- ☐ Inspect plants top to bottom
- ☐ Check undersides of leaves
- ☐ Record all findings immediately
- ☐ Track trends over time
- ☐ Establish and use action thresholds
- ☐ Increase monitoring after treatments
- ☐ Review and adjust program regularly
