### Sanitation Solutions

```
    COMMON SANITIZERS FOR GREENHOUSE USE:

    HYDROGEN PEROXIDE (H2O2)
    • Concentration: 3-10% solutions
    • Use: Surfaces, tools, irrigation systems
    • Benefits: No residue, breaks down to water/oxygen
    • Contact time: 10 minutes

    QUATERNARY AMMONIUM ("Quats")
    • Brands: Physan 20, KleenGrow
    • Use: Surfaces, pots, tools
    • Benefits: Long-lasting, broad spectrum
    • Note: Can be phytotoxic, rinse well

    BLEACH (Sodium Hypochlorite)
    • Concentration: 10% solution (1:9 bleach:water)
    • Use: Tools, surfaces, containers
    • Benefits: Cheap, effective
    • Limitations: Corrosive, degrades quickly

    PEROXYACETIC ACID (PAA)
    • Products: ZeroTol, OxiDate
    • Use: Irrigation water, surfaces
    • Benefits: No residue, works in organic matter
    • Contact time: 1-5 minutes

    APPLICATION TIPS:
    1. Clean debris first (sanitizers don't work in dirt)
    2. Follow label contact times
    3. Rinse if required by label
    4. Rotate sanitizers to prevent resistance
    5. Protect yourself (gloves, eye protection)
```

## Environmental Management

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║              OPTIMIZING ENVIRONMENT FOR PLANT HEALTH                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Principle: Healthy plants resist pests; stressed plants attract them   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TEMPERATURE MANAGEMENT:                                                 ║
║   ═══════════════════════                                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   For Leafy Greens:                                                       ║
║   • Optimal: 60-70°F (day), 55-65°F (night)                             ║
║   • Above 75°F: Aphids, spider mites thrive                             ║
║   • Below 50°F: Botrytis risk increases                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   For Fruiting Crops:                                                     ║
║   • Optimal: 65-75°F (day), 60-68°F (night)                             ║
║   • Above 85°F: Spider mites, blossom drop                              ║
║   • Poor air circulation: Powdery mildew, Botrytis                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   HUMIDITY MANAGEMENT:                                                    ║
║   ════════════════════                                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Target Range: 50-70% RH (general guideline)                            ║
║                                                                           ║
║   High Humidity (>80%) Problems:                                          ║
║   • Botrytis (gray mold)                                                 ║
║   • Bacterial diseases (leaf spot, soft rot)                             ║
║   • Powdery mildew (counterintuitively)                                  ║
║   • Slow drying after irrigation                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Low Humidity (<40%) Problems:                                           ║
║   • Spider mite population explosions                                    ║
║   • Plant stress, reduced growth                                         ║
║   • Tip burn in lettuce                                                  ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Solutions:                                                              ║
║   • Dehumidifiers (reduce humidity)                                      ║
║   • Humidifiers or foggers (increase humidity)                           ║
║   • Proper ventilation                                                   ║
║   • Avoid watering late in day                                           ║
║   • HAF fans for air circulation                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   AIR CIRCULATION:                                                        ║
║   ═══════════════                                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Goals:                                                                  ║
║   • Prevent stagnant air pockets                                         ║
║   • Dry foliage faster                                                   ║
║   • Uniform temperature/humidity                                         ║
║   • Discourage fungal spore settlement                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Methods:                                                                ║
║   • Horizontal Air Flow (HAF) fans                                       ║
║   • Circulation fans (not aimed directly at plants)                      ║
║   • Proper vent/fan placement                                            ║
║   • Avoid over-crowding plants                                           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Target: Gentle leaf movement throughout canopy                         ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Disease-Suppressive Conditions

```
    DISEASE          FAVORED BY                  SUPPRESS BY
    ═══════          ══════════                  ═══════════

    Powdery Mildew   • 60-80°F                   • Stable temps
                     • Moderate humidity          • <60% or >80% RH
                     • Poor air flow              • Good circulation
                     • Temp fluctuations          • Avoid stress

    Botrytis         • High humidity (>85%)      • <75% RH
                     • Stagnant air               • Air movement
                     • Free moisture              • Morning watering
                     • Cool temps                 • Remove debris

    Downy Mildew     • Leaf wetness 6+ hrs       • No overhead water
                     • Cool nights (50-60°F)      • Dry before night
                     • Warm days (70-80°F)        • Low night humidity
                     • High humidity              • Airflow

    Pythium          • Waterlogged media         • Proper drainage
                     • Warm temps (>75°F)         • Don't overwater
                     • Low oxygen                 • Aerated media
                     • Poor drainage              • Clean water

    Bacterial Spot   • Leaf wetness              • Drip irrigation
                     • Water splash               • No overhead water
                     • Warm + humid               • Reduce humidity
                     • Wounds                     • Gentle handling
```

## Exclusion Practices

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║              KEEPING PESTS OUT IS EASIER THAN REMOVAL                     ║
║                                                                           ║
║   PHYSICAL BARRIERS:                                                      ║
║   ══════════════════                                                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SCREENING:                                                              ║
║   ┌────────────────────┐                                                 ║
║   │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓   │  ← Insect screening on all vents                ║
║   │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓   │                                                 ║
║   │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓   │    Mesh sizes:                                  ║
║   └────────────────────┘    • 50 mesh (0.3mm): Most insects             ║
║                             • 80 mesh (0.2mm): Thrips                    ║
║                             • Note: Finer = less airflow                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ENTRY VESTIBULES:                                                       ║
║   • Double-door entry systems                                            ║
║   • Positive pressure in main growing area                               ║
║   • Air curtains at doorways                                             ║
║   • Keeps flying insects out                                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SEALING:                                                                ║
║   • Caulk cracks and gaps                                                ║
║   • Weather stripping on doors                                           ║
║   • Seal pipe/conduit penetrations                                       ║
║   • Repair damaged screening immediately                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   QUARANTINE PROCEDURES:                                                  ║
║   ═══════════════════════                                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   For ALL incoming plants:                                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   1. Inspect thoroughly before acceptance                                ║
║   2. Place in separate quarantine area (isolated from production)        ║
║   3. Monitor closely for 7-14 days                                       ║
║   4. Treat if pests/diseases found                                       ║
║   5. Only move to production if clean                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Quarantine Area Requirements:                                           ║
║   • Physically separated from main growing                               ║
║   • Separate air flow (don't share fans)                                 ║
║   • Dedicated tools and equipment                                        ║
║   • Work in quarantine LAST (after main production)                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   PEOPLE & EQUIPMENT SANITATION:                                          ║
║   ════════════════════════════════                                        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Foot Baths:                                                             ║
║   • At all entries to growing areas                                      ║
║   • Change solution weekly minimum                                       ║
║   • Use approved disinfectant                                            ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Hand Washing Stations:                                                  ║
║   • Before entering growing areas                                        ║
║   • After handling diseased plants                                       ║
║   • Between crop sections                                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Tool Sanitation:                                                        ║
║   • Sanitize pruners/knives between plants                               ║
║   • Keep sanitizer bucket handy (alcohol, bleach, or H2O2)              ║
║   • Replace frequently during use                                        ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Sanitation is non-negotiable** - daily removal of debris prevents pest establishment
2. **Environmental management** is powerful pest control - temperature, humidity, airflow matter
3. **Exclusion is easier than eradication** - screens, quarantine, sealing prevent entry
4. **Plant health is pest resistance** - proper spacing, nutrition, watering build resilience
5. **Resistant varieties are free insurance** - genetic resistance prevents problems before they start
6. **Cultural controls work 24/7** - unlike treatments, prevention is always active

### Cultural Controls Implementation Checklist

- ☐ Daily removal of plant debris
- ☐ Weekly deep sanitation
- ☐ Screens on all air intakes
- ☐ Quarantine protocol for incoming plants
- ☐ Drip irrigation (not overhead)
- ☐ Proper plant spacing for airflow
- ☐ Maintain optimal temperature/humidity
- ☐ Use disease-resistant varieties
- ☐ Balanced nutrition program
- ☐ Tool and surface sanitation protocols
