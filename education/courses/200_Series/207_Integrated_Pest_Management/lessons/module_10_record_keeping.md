# Module 10: IPM Record Keeping & Documentation
## Course 207: Integrated Pest Management

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Design comprehensive IPM record-keeping systems
- Create effective scouting logs and pest tracking forms
- Use data to make informed pest management decisions
- Meet regulatory documentation requirements
- Analyze trends to improve IPM programs
- Implement digital and paper-based tracking systems
