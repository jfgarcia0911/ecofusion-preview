# Module 12: IPM Capstone Project
## Course 207: Integrated Pest Management

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Apply all IPM concepts learned in a comprehensive project
- Develop a complete IPM plan for a specific scenario
- Troubleshoot complex pest management situations
- Present IPM recommendations professionally
- Integrate multiple control tactics effectively

## Project Requirements

### Submission Guidelines

- **Length:** 8-12 pages (excluding appendices)
- **Format:** Professional business document
- **Supporting Materials:** Maps, forms, photos, diagrams
- **Due Date:** End of course week 12

### Evaluation Criteria

- Comprehensiveness of approach
- Integration of multiple IPM tactics
- Feasibility and practicality
- Scientific accuracy
- Professional presentation
- Attention to detail
