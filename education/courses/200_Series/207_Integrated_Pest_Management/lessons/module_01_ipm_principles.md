## What is Integrated Pest Management?

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    IPM DEFINITION                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is a comprehensive approach to       ║
║   managing pests that:                                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   • Uses multiple strategies in combination                             ║
║   • Prioritizes prevention over reaction                                ║
║   • Considers economic and environmental impacts                        ║
║   • Minimizes risks to human health and environment                     ║
║   • Emphasizes monitoring and decision-making thresholds                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   IPM is NOT:                                                            ║
║   ✗ Organic farming (though compatible)                                 ║
║   ✗ Complete elimination of all pests                                   ║
║   ✗ Never using chemicals                                               ║
║   ✗ A one-size-fits-all approach                                        ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Core IPM Principles

### 1. Prevention is Primary

```
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                             │
    │   PREVENTIVE STRATEGIES                                     │
    │   ══════════════════════                                    │
    │                                                             │
    │   BEFORE pests arrive:                                      │
    │   • Screen all air intakes                                  │
    │   • Quarantine incoming plants                              │
    │   • Use pest-free growing media                             │
    │   • Maintain strict sanitation protocols                    │
    │   • Select resistant varieties                              │
    │                                                             │
    │   ENVIRONMENTAL PREVENTION:                                 │
    │   • Optimize temperature and humidity                       │
    │   • Ensure proper air circulation                           │
    │   • Avoid plant stress                                      │
    │   • Manage irrigation carefully                             │
    │                                                             │
    │   RESULT: Problems that never occur cost nothing to fix!    │
    │                                                             │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2. Accurate Identification

```
    Pest Observed
```

### 3. Monitoring & Thresholds

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    THRESHOLD CONCEPT                                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Pest Population Level                                                  ║
║        ▲                                                                 ║
║        │                                                                 ║
║   HIGH │           ╱╲     ← ECONOMIC DAMAGE LEVEL                       ║
║        │          ╱  ╲       (Unacceptable loss)                        ║
║        │    ────────────── ← ACTION THRESHOLD                           ║
║        │      ╱        ╲     (Time to intervene)                        ║
║        │     ╱          ╲                                               ║
║    LOW │────────────────────────────────────────────                    ║
║        └──────────────────────────────────────────── Time               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ACTION THRESHOLD = Population level that triggers treatment            ║
║   Set BELOW economic damage level to prevent losses                     ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

**Economic Threshold Examples:**
- Aphids on lettuce: 1-2 per plant (zero tolerance - aesthetic)
- Spider mites on tomatoes: 5-10 per leaflet (some damage acceptable)
- Fungus gnats: 5+ per yellow sticky card (nuisance but rarely damaging)

## IPM Benefits

### Economic Benefits
- **Reduced input costs:** Less pesticide use over time
- **Prevented crop losses:** Catching problems early
- **Higher yields:** Healthier plants from better management
- **Premium pricing:** Clean, chemical-free marketing
- **Lower labor:** Less emergency firefighting

### Environmental Benefits
- **Reduced chemical exposure:** Safer for workers and consumers
- **Preserved beneficial organisms:** Natural pest control
- **Prevented resistance:** Sustainable long-term control
- **Improved ecosystem health:** Better balance

### Operational Benefits
- **Better quality:** Consistent crop quality
- **Risk management:** Multiple backup strategies
- **Knowledge building:** Understanding your system
- **Regulatory compliance:** Meets food safety standards
- **Marketing advantage:** Story to tell customers

## Common IPM Misconceptions

```
    MYTH                              REALITY
    ════                              ═══════

    "IPM means no pesticides"    →    IPM includes pesticides as one tool,
                                      used judiciously when needed

    "IPM is only for organic"    →    IPM works in any system; organic
                                      must use IPM by definition

    "IPM is too expensive"       →    IPM reduces costs long-term by
                                      preventing problems and resistance

    "IPM takes too much time"    →    Proactive IPM saves time vs.
                                      constant crisis management

    "Zero pests is the goal"     →    Goal is keeping pests below
                                      economic damage thresholds

    "One strategy is enough"     →    Multiple tactics prevent resistance
                                      and provide backup options
```

## Starting Your IPM Program

```
    WEEK 1-2: ASSESSMENT
    ═══════════════════
    □ Identify current pest issues
    □ Establish baseline monitoring
    □ Review facility for entry points

    ══════════════════
    □ Improve sanitation protocols
    □ Install/repair screens
    □ Set up quarantine procedures
    □ Implement environmental controls

    WEEK 5-6: MONITORING
    ══════════════════
    □ Install sticky traps
    □ Create scouting schedule
    □ Train staff on identification
    □ Set up record-keeping system

    WEEK 7-8: ACTION PLANS
    ═══════════════════
    □ Define action thresholds
    □ Create pest-specific protocols

    ═══════════════════

    □ Adjust thresholds as needed
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **IPM is a philosophy** of comprehensive, sustainable pest management using multiple strategies
2. **Prevention is the foundation** - stop pests before they become problems
3. **Monitoring drives decisions** - scout regularly and use thresholds to guide action
4. **Multiple tactics work better** than relying on single approaches
5. **Record keeping is essential** for learning and improvement
6. **IPM saves money** long-term through prevention and reduced inputs

### IPM Principles Checklist
- ☐ Prevent pest problems through cultural and environmental management
- ☐ Monitor regularly to detect pests early
- ☐ Identify pests accurately before taking action
- ☐ Establish economic thresholds for decision-making
- ☐ Use multiple control tactics in combination
- ☐ Choose least-toxic effective options first
- ☐ Keep detailed records of all activities
- ☐ Evaluate results and adjust strategies
