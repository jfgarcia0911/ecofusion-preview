## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Distinguish between fungal, bacterial, and viral plant diseases
- Identify common greenhouse diseases by symptoms
- Understand disease life cycles and spread mechanisms
- Differentiate disease symptoms from nutrient deficiencies and environmental stress
- Use diagnostic tools for disease identification

## Fungal Diseases

### Powdery Mildew

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    POWDERY MILDEW                                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   APPEARANCE:                                                             ║
║   ══════════                                                              ║
║        Leaf surface:                                                      ║
║   ┌────────────────────┐                                                 ║
║   │ ░░░░░  ░░░░        │  ← White/gray powdery coating                  ║
║   │  ░░░░░░ ░░         │    Looks like flour dusted on                  ║
║   │   ░░░  ░░░░░       │    Usually on upper leaf surface               ║
║   │    ░░░░            │                                                 ║
║   └────────────────────┘                                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   IDENTIFICATION:                                                         ║
║   • Circular white/gray powdery patches                                  ║
║   • Starts as small spots, spreads to cover entire leaf                 ║
║   • Can affect leaves, stems, flowers, fruit                            ║
║   • Powder rubs off on fingers                                          ║
║   • Leaves may yellow and drop                                          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Moderate temperatures (60-80°F)                                      ║
║   • Moderate to high humidity (NOT wet leaves!)                         ║
║   • Poor air circulation                                                 ║
║   • Dense plant canopies                                                 ║
║   • Temperature fluctuations                                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SPREAD:                                                                 ║
║   • Airborne spores (millions produced)                                  ║
║   • Contact between plants                                               ║
║   • Workers and tools                                                    ║
║   • Fans and air movement                                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CROPS AFFECTED:                                                         ║
║   Cucurbits, peppers, tomatoes, beans, herbs (especially basil)         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   UNIQUE TRAIT:                                                           ║
║   Does NOT need free moisture on leaves (unlike most fungi!)             ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Botrytis (Gray Mold)

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    BOTRYTIS CINEREA (Gray Mold)                          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   APPEARANCE:                                                             ║
║   ══════════                                                              ║
║        Infected tissue:                                                   ║
║   ┌────────────────────┐                                                 ║
║   │    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓        │  ← Gray fuzzy mold                            ║
║   │   ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓       │    Brown/tan dying tissue                     ║
║   │  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓      │    Water-soaked appearance                    ║
║   │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓     │                                                ║
║   └────────────────────┘                                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   IDENTIFICATION:                                                         ║
║   • Gray fuzzy mold on flowers, fruit, leaves                           ║
║   • Brown, water-soaked lesions                                          ║
║   • Affects dying or wounded tissue first                                ║
║   • Dead leaves become covered in gray spores                            ║
║   • Stem cankers (brown, girdling lesions)                              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:                                                   ║
║   • High humidity (>85%)                                                 ║
║   • Poor air circulation                                                 ║
║   • Free moisture on plant surfaces                                      ║
║   • Cool to moderate temperatures (60-75°F)                              ║
║   • Wounded or senescing tissue                                          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ENTRY POINTS:                                                           ║
║   • Wounds (pruning, harvest, handling)                                  ║
║   • Flower petals (most common)                                          ║
║   • Senescent leaves                                                     ║
║   • Fruit touching wet soil/media                                        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SPREAD:                                                                 ║
║   • Millions of airborne spores                                          ║
║   • Water splash                                                         ║
║   • Contact/handling                                                     ║
║   • Spores survive for months in facility                                ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CROPS MOST AFFECTED:                                                    ║
║   Tomatoes, peppers, strawberries, lettuce, ornamentals                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   COMMON NAME: "Gray mold" or "Bud rot"                                  ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    PYTHIUM ROOT ROT                                       ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ABOVE-GROUND SYMPTOMS:                                                  ║
║   ══════════════════════                                                  ║
║        Wilted plant:                                                      ║
║           ╭─╮ ╭─╮                                                        ║
║          ╱   ╲│ ╲                                                        ║
║         │     │  │  ← Wilting despite adequate water                    ║
║         │     │  │    Yellowing lower leaves                            ║
║         │     │  │    Stunted growth                                     ║
║          ╲   ╱│ ╱     No recovery after watering                        ║
║           ╰─╯ ╰─╯                                                        ║
║            │││                                                            ║
║   ═════════════════════════════════════════════                          ║
║   Media surface                                                           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   BELOW-GROUND SYMPTOMS:                                                  ║

║   ─────────────         ───────────────                                  ║
║   │││││││││││           │ │  │   │  ← Brown, slimy                      ║
║   │││││││││││           │  │    │    Outer layer sloughs off            ║
║   White/cream color      │   │        No feeder roots                    ║
║   Firm texture           Brown/black  "Bare root" appearance            ║
║   Many laterals          Mushy core                                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Waterlogged media/roots                                              ║
║   • Poor drainage                                                        ║
║   • Low oxygen in root zone                                              ║
║   • Warm temperatures (>75°F)                                            ║
║   • Contaminated water/media                                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   SPREAD:                                                                 ║
║   • Swimming spores (zoospores) in water                                 ║
║   • Contaminated tools and hands                                         ║
║   • Recycled irrigation water                                            ║
║   • Infected transplants                                                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DIAGNOSIS:                                                              ║
║   Pull plant - if roots are brown, slimy, and outer layer                ║
║   slides off easily, suspect Pythium or other root rot                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CROPS AFFECTED:                                                         ║
║   All crops, especially seedlings and hydroponic crops                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ALSO CALLED: "Damping off" (in seedlings)                              ║
║                                                                           ║
```

```
║   ENTRY:                                                                  ║
║   Bacteria enter through natural openings (stomata) and wounds           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CROPS AFFECTED:                                                         ║

║                                                                           ║
║   DIFFICULT TO CONTROL once established - prevention key!                ║
║                                                                           ║
```

## Disease vs. Abiotic Disorders

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║           DON'T CONFUSE DISEASES WITH NUTRIENT/ENVIRONMENTAL ISSUES!      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DISEASE                         ABIOTIC DISORDER                        ║
║   ════════                        ══════════════════                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Progresses over time       │    Appears suddenly across crop           ║
║   Spreads to other plants    │    Affects all plants similarly           ║
║   Irregular pattern          │    Uniform pattern                        ║
║   Starts on few plants       │    Widespread from start                  ║
║   May have spores/lesions    │    No pathogen signs                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   LOOK-ALIKE CONDITIONS:                                                  ║
║                                                                           ║
║   YELLOWING LEAVES:                                                       ║
║   • Disease: Downy mildew, root rot                                      ║
║   • Nutrient: Nitrogen deficiency                                        ║
║   • Environmental: Overwatering, poor light                              ║
║                                                                           ║
║   WILTING:                                                                ║
║   • Disease: Fusarium/Verticillium wilt, Pythium                         ║
║   • Environmental: Underwatering, heat stress, root damage               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   LEAF SPOTS:                                                             ║
║   • Disease: Fungal/bacterial leaf spot                                  ║
║   • Nutrient: Calcium, potassium deficiency                              ║
║   • Environmental: Sunburn, cold damage, spray burn                      ║
║                                                                           ║
║   DISTORTED GROWTH:                                                       ║
║   • Disease: Virus, broad mites                                          ║
║   • Environmental: Herbicide drift, growth regulator                     ║
║   • Nutrient: Boron deficiency                                           ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Diagnostic Checklist

```
    When investigating plant health problems:

    ☐ Check environmental conditions (temp, humidity, light)
    ☐ Review recent cultural practices (watering, feeding)
    ☐ Look for pattern - affecting all plants or spreading?
    ☐ Examine roots (often overlooked!)
    ☐ Use hand lens to look for pathogens/pests
    ☐ Consider what changed recently
    ☐ Review records for similar past issues
    ☐ Take photos for documentation
    ☐ Send to diagnostic lab if uncertain
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Three main pathogen types**: Fungi (most common), bacteria (water-spread), viruses (no cure)
2. **Fungi need moisture**: Most require wet conditions, except powdery mildew
3. **Bacteria enter through wounds**: Sanitation and gentle handling critical
4. **Viruses have NO CURE**: Prevention through vector control and resistant varieties
5. **Environment matters**: Many diseases only occur under specific conditions
6. **Accurate ID is critical**: Disease vs. nutrient deficiency vs. environmental stress

### Common Disease Quick Reference

| Disease | Type | Key Symptom | Spread Method |
|---------|------|-------------|---------------|
| Powdery Mildew | Fungal | White powder on leaves | Airborne spores |
| Downy Mildew | Fungal | Yellow patches, purple fuzz underneath | Spores + moisture |
| Botrytis | Fungal | Gray fuzzy mold | Airborne spores |
| Pythium | Fungal | Brown slimy roots | Water, contaminated media |
| Bacterial Spot | Bacterial | Dark spots, yellow halos | Water splash |
| Soft Rot | Bacterial | Mushy tissue, foul odor | Wounds, contamination |
| TMV/ToMV | Viral | Mosaic pattern, distortion | Mechanical contact |
| TSWV | Viral | Bronze spots, rings | Thrips |
