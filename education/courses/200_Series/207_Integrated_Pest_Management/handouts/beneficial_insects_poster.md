## HABITAT FOR BENEFICIAL INSECTS

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║            CREATE A BENEFICIAL-FRIENDLY GREENHOUSE                        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   1. FLOWERING PLANTS (nectar and pollen sources):                       ║
║      ────────────────────────────────────────────                        ║
║      • Alyssum (sweet alyssum) - continuous blooms                       ║
║      • Dill, fennel, cilantro - umbrella-shaped flowers                  ║
║      • Yarrow - clusters of tiny flowers                                 ║
║      • Marigold - bright, attractive to many species                     ║
║      • Buckwheat - excellent nectar source                               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   2. BANKER PLANTS (pest hosts that don't affect crops):                 ║
║      ──────────────────────────────────────────────────                  ║
║      • Wheat or barley with grain aphids                                 ║
║        (grain aphids don't attack vegetables!)                           ║
║      • Keeps parasitic wasps reproducing between pest outbreaks          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   3. SHELTER AND OVERWINTERING SITES:                                    ║
║      ────────────────────────────────────────                            ║
║      • Undisturbed areas with leaf litter                                ║
║      • Native grasses along edges                                        ║
║      • Perennial herbs                                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   4. WATER SOURCES:                                                       ║
║      ──────────────                                                       ║
║      • Shallow dishes with pebbles                                       ║
║      • Moist sand/soil areas                                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   5. REDUCE PESTICIDE USE:                                                ║
║      ──────────────────────                                               ║
║      • Spot-treat only                                                   ║
║      • Use selective products when necessary                             ║
║      • Time applications to minimize beneficial impact                   ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
