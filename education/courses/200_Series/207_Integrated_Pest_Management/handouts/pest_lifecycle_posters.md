## APHID LIFECYCLE

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                      APHID LIFE CYCLE                                     ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Spring/Summer: ASEXUAL REPRODUCTION (Parthenogenesis)                  ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Female (no mating) →→ Lives birth to female nymphs →→ Mature in 7 days║
║                ↑_____________________________________________↓            ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Each female produces 5-10 nymphs per day for 20-30 days                ║
║   = 100+ offspring per adult!                                            ║
║                                                                           ║
║   NYMPH STAGES (4 instars):                                               ║
║   Day 0-2: 1st instar (tiny, pale)                                       ║
║   Day 2-4: 2nd instar                                                    ║
║   Day 4-6: 3rd instar                                                    ║
║   Day 6-7: 4th instar → ADULT                                            ║
║                                                                           ║
║   Winged forms develop when:                                              ║
║   • Colony overcrowded                                                   ║
║   • Plant quality declines                                               ║
║   • Environmental stress                                                 ║
║   → Fly to new plants!                                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   GENERATION TIME: 7-10 days (temperature dependent)                     ║
║   POPULATION GROWTH: Exponential! Can double every week                  ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CONTROL IMPLICATIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Catch early (populations explode fast)                               ║
║   • Target nymphs (adults fly away)                                      ║
║   • Multiple applications needed (overlapping generations)               ║
║   • Beneficials must reproduce faster than aphids                        ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## WHITEFLY LIFECYCLE

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    WHITEFLY LIFE CYCLE                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   EGG (7-10 days)                                                         ║
║   • Laid on leaf underside                                               ║
║   • Cone-shaped, about 0.2mm                                             ║
║   • Attached with short stalk                                            ║
║   • Yellow when laid, darken before hatch                                ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   CRAWLER (mobile for ~6 hours)                                           ║
║   • First instar briefly mobile                                          ║
║   • Finds feeding site                                                   ║
║   • Inserts mouthparts, becomes sessile                                  ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   SCALE STAGES (14-21 days total)                                         ║
║   • 2nd instar: Flat, oval, feeding                                      ║
║   • 3rd instar: Slightly larger                                          ║
║   • 4th instar (pupa): Thicker, raised, eye spots visible               ║
║   • All stages stuck to leaf (can't move!)                               ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   ADULT (10-14 days)                                                      ║
║   • Emerges from pupal case                                              ║
║   • White, winged, mobile                                                ║
║   • Feeds and mates within hours                                         ║
║   • Begins egg laying within 2-3 days                                    ║
║   • Lays 100-300 eggs in lifetime                                        ║
║          ↑_____________________________________________↓                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TOTAL CYCLE: 21-35 days (25°C/77°F)                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CONTROL IMPLICATIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Multiple life stages present simultaneously                          ║
║   • Scales resistant to many insecticides                                ║
║   • Adults fly, spread rapidly                                           ║
║   • Parasitoids attack scale stages (look for black scales)              ║
║   • Soaps/oils work on crawlers and adults only                         ║
║   • Need repeat applications to catch each generation                    ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## SPIDER MITE LIFECYCLE

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                  SPIDER MITE LIFE CYCLE                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   EGG (3 days @ 70°F)                                                     ║
║   • Tiny spheres on leaf underside                                       ║
║   • Clear to amber colored                                               ║
║   • Attached with silk                                                   ║
║   • 100-200 per female                                                   ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   LARVA (1-2 days)                                                        ║
║   • Six legs (only stage with 6!)                                        ║
║   • Very small, doesn't feed much                                        ║
║   • Molts to protonymph                                                  ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   PROTONYMPH (1-2 days)                                                   ║
║   • Eight legs                                                            ║
║   • Actively feeding                                                     ║
║   • Growing rapidly                                                      ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   DEUTONYMPH (1-2 days)                                                   ║
║   • Eight legs                                                            ║
║   • Nearly adult size                                                    ║
║   • Heavy feeding                                                        ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   ADULT (7-14 days lifespan)                                              ║
║   • Eight legs, two dark spots                                           ║
║   • Mates within hours                                                   ║
║   • Females lay eggs continuously                                        ║
║   • Males smaller than females                                           ║
║          ↑_____________________________________________↓                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TOTAL CYCLE:                                                            ║
║   • 7-10 days at 75-80°F                                                 ║
║   • 20+ days at 60°F                                                     ║
║   • 5 days at 90°F                                                       ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TEMPERATURE EFFECT:                                                     ║
║   Hot weather = FASTER reproduction = MORE mites                         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   POPULATION GROWTH:                                                      ║
║   Can double every 3 days in optimal conditions!                         ║
║   1 mite → 1,000,000 in 1 month (if unchecked)                          ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CONTROL IMPLICATIONS:                                                   ║
║   • All stages on plant simultaneously                                   ║
║   • Eggs resistant to most treatments                                    ║
║   • Need 3-4 applications, 3-5 days apart                               ║
║   • Predatory mites eat all stages                                       ║
║   • Hot spots spread rapidly                                             ║
║   • Start on lower leaves, move up                                       ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## FUNGUS GNAT LIFECYCLE

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                  FUNGUS GNAT LIFE CYCLE                                   ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ADULT (7-10 days)                                                       ║
║   • Dark gray/black fly, 3mm long                                        ║
║   • Delicate, mosquito-like                                              ║
║   • Poor fliers, run on media surface                                    ║
║   • Each female lays 100-300 eggs                                        ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   EGG (3-6 days)                                                          ║
║   • Laid in moist growing media                                          ║
║   • Tiny, oval, white                                                    ║
║   • In groups near media surface                                         ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   LARVA (10-14 days) ← THE DAMAGING STAGE!                               ║
║   • White/clear with black head capsule                                  ║
║   • Feeds on:                                                            ║
║     - Organic matter in media                                            ║
║     - Fungi and algae                                                    ║
║     - ROOT HAIRS (the problem!)                                          ║
║   • Most damaging to seedlings                                           ║
║   • Found in top 2 inches of media                                       ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   PUPA (3-6 days)                                                         ║
║   • In cocoon near media surface                                         ║
║   • Non-feeding stage                                                    ║
║   • Transforms to adult                                                  ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   → ADULT (completes cycle)                                               ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TOTAL CYCLE: 17-30 days (temperature dependent)                        ║
║                                                                           ║
║   FAVORED CONDITIONS:                                                     ║
║   • Wet/waterlogged media                                                ║
║   • Organic matter present                                               ║
║   • Algae growth on media surface                                        ║
║   • Poor drainage                                                        ║
║   • Warm temperatures                                                    ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CONTROL IMPLICATIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Adults are nuisance, larvae cause damage                             ║
║   • Target larvae in media (beneficials, Bti)                            ║
║   • Yellow sticky traps catch adults (monitoring/mass trapping)          ║
║   • Cultural control most important:                                     ║
║     → Don't overwater!                                                   ║
║     → Improve drainage                                                   ║
║     → Remove algae                                                       ║
║     → Use less organic media                                             ║
║   • Beneficial nematodes very effective against larvae                   ║
║   • Sand/perlite top dressing reduces egg laying                         ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## THRIPS LIFECYCLE

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                           ║
║                    THRIPS LIFE CYCLE                                      ║
║            (Western Flower Thrips - most common greenhouse pest)         ║
║                                                                           ║
║   EGG (2-4 days)                                                          ║
║   • Inserted into plant tissue                                           ║
║   • Kidney-shaped, microscopic                                           ║
║   • Protected inside plant                                               ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   LARVA - Stage 1 (1-2 days)                                              ║
║   • Yellow/white, no wings                                               ║
║   • Feeds on flowers, leaves                                             ║
║   • Very small, active                                                   ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   LARVA - Stage 2 (2-3 days)                                              ║
║   • Larger, feeding heavily                                              ║
║   • Causing visible damage                                               ║
║   • Last feeding stage                                                   ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   PREPUPA (1-2 days)                                                      ║
║   • Drops to media/soil                                                  ║
║   • Non-feeding                                                          ║
║   • Developing wing buds                                                 ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   PUPA (1-3 days)                                                         ║
║   • In media or leaf litter                                              ║
║   • Non-feeding                                                          ║
║   • Transforming to adult                                                ║
║          ↓                                                                ║
║   ADULT (20-40 days)                                                      ║
║   • Slender, brown/yellow, 1-2mm                                         ║
║   • Fringed wings                                                        ║
║   • Feeds and lays eggs                                                  ║
║   • Can fly between plants                                               ║
║   • 40-100 eggs per female                                               ║
║          ↑_____________________________________________↓                 ║
║                                                                           ║
║   TOTAL CYCLE: 12-20 days at 70-80°F                                     ║
║                                                                           ║
║   VIRUS TRANSMISSION: ★ CRITICAL CONCERN ★                               ║
║   • Transmit Tomato Spotted Wilt Virus (TSWV)                           ║
║   • Acquired as larvae, transmitted as adults                            ║
║   • NO CURE for plant viruses!                                           ║
║   • Even low thrips numbers can spread disease                           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   CONTROL IMPLICATIONS:                                                   ║
║   • Very low tolerance due to virus risk                                 ║
║   • Eggs inside plant tissue (protected from sprays)                     ║
║   • Pupae in media (not on plant)                                        ║
║   • Need to target larvae on plants + pupae in media                     ║
║   • Blue sticky traps for monitoring                                     ║
║   • Predatory mites (Amblyseius) eat larvae                             ║
║   • Multiple applications needed (3-5 days apart)                        ║
║   • Remove flowers (harboring sites)                                     ║
║                                                                           ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
