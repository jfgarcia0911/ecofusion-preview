## What Are Action Thresholds?

**Action Threshold** = The pest population level at which you must take control action to prevent economic damage.

**Key Concept:** Some pest presence is acceptable. The goal is MANAGEMENT, not eradication.

---

## Factors Affecting Thresholds

```
LOWER THRESHOLD                    HIGHER THRESHOLD
(Act quickly)                      (Can tolerate more)
═══════════════                    ═══════════════════

✓ High value crop                  ✓ Lower value crop
✓ Near harvest                     ✓ Early in season
✓ Fresh market (appearance)        ✓ Processing use
✓ Fast-reproducing pest            ✓ Slow-reproducing pest
✓ Virus vector (thrips, aphids)    ✓ Non-vector pests
✓ Highly susceptible variety       ✓ Resistant variety
✓ Warm weather (pests thrive)      ✓ Cool weather
✓ Stressed plants                  ✓ Vigorous plants
```

## Greenhouse/Indoor Farm Thresholds

### LEAFY GREENS (Lettuce, Herbs, Greens)

**Market:** Fresh, high aesthetic standards

| Pest/Disease | Threshold | Monitoring Method | Rationale |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Aphids** | 1-2 per plant | Visual inspection, 20% of plants | Zero tolerance - aesthetic, rapid reproduction |
| **Thrips** | 1-2 per blue trap/week | Blue sticky traps | Virus vectors - very low tolerance |
| **Whiteflies** | 2-3 per yellow trap/week | Yellow sticky traps | Establish before visible on plants |
| **Spider mites** | First detection | Visual, hand lens | Spread rapidly, hard to see initially |
| **Fungus gnats** | 10-20 per trap | Yellow traps (horizontal) | Adults nuisance, larvae damage roots |
| **Downy mildew** | First symptoms | Visual (purple fuzz underside) | Spreads in days - act immediately |
| **Powdery mildew** | First symptoms | Visual (white powder) | Cosmetic damage unacceptable |

---

### FRUITING CROPS (Tomatoes, Peppers, Cucumbers)

**Market:** Some cosmetic damage acceptable on fruit

| Pest/Disease | Threshold | Monitoring Method | Rationale |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Aphids** | 5-10 per shoot tip | Visual, count terminals | Some damage acceptable, beneficials can control |
| **Whiteflies** | 5-10 per yellow trap/week | Yellow sticky traps | Higher tolerance than greens |
| **Spider mites** | 5-10 per leaflet | Tap test, hand lens | Early detection critical, some OK on lower leaves |
| **Thrips** | 1-2 per blue trap | Blue sticky traps | TSWV risk - low tolerance |
| **Hornworms/Caterpillars** | ANY present | Visual inspection | Large feeding damage, remove manually |
| **Powdery mildew** | 1-5% leaf area affected | Visual assessment | Tolerate small amounts, treat before spreads |
| **Botrytis** | First symptoms | Visual (gray mold) | Remove affected tissue immediately |
| **Bacterial spot** | First symptoms | Visual (dark spots, halos) | Hard to control - act early |

---

### MICROGREENS & SEEDLINGS

**Market:** Very high quality, short cycle

| Pest/Disease | Threshold | Monitoring Method | Rationale |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Any pest** | Zero tolerance | Daily visual | Short cycle, no time for infestation |
| **Fungus gnats** | 5 per trap | Yellow traps | Larvae damage delicate roots |
| **Damping off** | First symptoms | Visual (collapsed seedlings) | Spreads rapidly in trays |
| **Algae** | When visible | Visual | Attracts fungus gnats, looks poor |

## Sticky Trap Thresholds

### YELLOW TRAP COUNTS

```
Per Trap/Week    Status         Action
══════════════   ══════════     ══════════════════════════════

0-2              Clean          Continue monitoring

3-5              Low            Increase inspection frequency
                                Check plants for source
                                Note location on map

6-10             Moderate       Identify pest species
                                Inspect plants carefully
                                Consider treatment if plant
                                inspection confirms problem

11-20            High           Treatment likely needed
                                Check plants for damage
                                Implement control measures

>20              Very High      Immediate action required
                                Multiple tactics
                                Increase trap density
```

### BLUE TRAP COUNTS (Thrips)

```
Per Trap/Week    Status         Action
══════════════   ══════════     ══════════════════════════════

0-1              Acceptable     Continue monitoring
                                (Even 1 is concern due to virus)

2-5              Concern        Increase monitoring
                                Inspect flowers/buds
                                Prepare for treatment

>5               Action         Treat immediately
                                Remove flowers
                                Apply controls
                                Check for virus symptoms
```

## Disease Thresholds

### FUNGAL DISEASES

**General Rule:** First symptoms = Action threshold

**Why such low tolerance?**
- Fungi produce millions of spores
- Spread rapidly in favorable conditions
- Easier to prevent than cure
- One infected plant can spread to entire crop

**Specific Thresholds:**

**Powdery Mildew**
- Threshold: First white spots visible
- Action: Improve air circulation, reduce humidity, apply fungicide if spreading

**Downy Mildew**
- Threshold: First yellow patches appear
- Action: IMMEDIATE - extremely aggressive disease

**Botrytis (Gray Mold)**
- Threshold: First gray fuzz on any plant tissue
- Action: Remove affected tissue, reduce humidity, improve airflow

**Root Rot (Pythium)**
- Threshold: First wilted plant despite adequate water
- Action: Check roots, adjust watering, treat media, isolate affected plants

---

### BACTERIAL DISEASES

**General Rule:** First symptoms = Action threshold

**Why?**
- No curative treatments available
- Spread by water, workers, tools
- Can contaminate entire facility

**Bacterial Leaf Spot**
- Threshold: First dark spots with yellow halos
- Action: Remove affected leaves/plants, stop overhead watering, sanitize tools

**Soft Rot**
- Threshold: First mushy, foul-smelling tissue
- Action: Remove immediately, improve air circulation, avoid wounds

---

### VIRAL DISEASES

**Threshold:** ZERO - Remove infected plants immediately!

**Why absolute zero tolerance?**
- NO CURE exists for plant viruses
- Spread by insect vectors and mechanical contact
- Entire plant is infected systemically
- Can spread to all plants in facility

**Action When Suspected:**
- Isolate plant immediately
- Send sample for lab confirmation
- If confirmed: Remove and destroy plant
- Intensify vector (aphid/thrips/whitefly) control
- Sanitize all tools
- Check surrounding plants daily

## Setting Your Own Thresholds

### Decision Factors Worksheet

**1. Crop Value**
   - Value per plant: $ _______
   - If high (>$2): Lower threshold
   - If low (<$0.50): Higher threshold acceptable

**2. Time to Harvest**
   - Days remaining: _______
   - Near harvest (<2 weeks): Lower threshold
   - Early season (>4 weeks): Can tolerate more

**3. Market Standards**
   - Fresh market: Very low tolerance
   - Processing: Moderate tolerance
   - Your market: _______________

**4. Pest Biology**
   - Doubling time: _______ days
   - Fast (< 7 days): Lower threshold
   - Slow (> 14 days): Higher threshold OK

**5. Control Options Available**
   - Effective biocontrol: Can wait slightly
   - Limited options: Act earlier
   - Your options: _______________

**6. Environmental Conditions**
   - Current conditions favor pest? Yes / No
   - If yes: Lower threshold
   - If no: Higher threshold possible

## Threshold Adjustment Examples

**Example 1: Aphids on Lettuce**

**Base threshold:** 1-2 per plant

**Adjust for:**
- Hot weather (+faster reproduction) → Lower to 1 per plant
- Beneficial insects present (+natural control) → Keep at 2 per plant
- 3 days to harvest (+no time for treatment) → Zero tolerance
- **Final threshold:** 0-1 per plant

**Example 2: Spider Mites on Tomatoes**

**Base threshold:** 5-10 per leaflet

**Adjust for:**
- Early season (+time to control) → Keep at 10 per leaflet
- Predatory mites released (+biocontrol) → Keep at 10 per leaflet
- Hot, dry weather (+mite-favorable) → Lower to 5 per leaflet
- **Final threshold:** 5 per leaflet
