## Multiple Choice
**1. The first step in designing an IPM program is:** A) Assessment of current situation
**2. Action thresholds should be based on:** C) Crop value and pest damage potential
**3. Staff training is:** D) Essential for program success
**4. An IPM program should include:** D) All of the above (prevention, monitoring, treatment)
**5. Program evaluation should occur:** C) Regularly throughout the season

## True/False
**6. One IPM program works for all facilities.** False
**7. Prevention is the foundation of IPM programs.** True
**8. IPM programs never need adjustment once implemented.** False

## Short Answer
**9. List THREE components of a comprehensive IPM program.**
**10. Why is staff training important for IPM success?**

## Answer Key
1.A | 2.C | 3.D | 4.D | 5.C | 6.F | 7.T | 8.F
9. Prevention/cultural controls, monitoring, action thresholds, multiple control tactics, documentation, evaluation
10. Staff implement daily, need to identify pests, follow protocols, make informed decisions
