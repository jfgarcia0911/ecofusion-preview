## Multiple Choice
**1. What color sticky traps attract whiteflies and aphids?** A) Blue B) Yellow C) Red D) White

**2. How often should sticky traps be checked in an active monitoring program?** A) Daily B) Weekly C) Monthly D) Once per season

**3. Action threshold for aphids on lettuce is typically:** A) 50+ per plant B) 10-20 per plant C) 1-2 per plant D) 100+ per plant

**4. When inspecting plants, you should:** A) Only look at tops B) Check top to bottom, including undersides C) Only check when you see problems D) Just use sticky traps

**5. Scouting frequency should increase:** A) Never B) When pests detected or after treatments C) Only in summer D) Based on moon phases

## True/False
**6. Monitoring is optional in IPM programs.** False
**7. Good records help identify pest patterns over time.** True
**8. You should scout the same plants every time.** False

## Short Answer (2 points each)
**9. List TWO benefits of regular pest monitoring.**
**10. What is the difference between monitoring trap density and mass trapping density?**

## Answer Key
1.B | 2.B | 3.C | 4.B | 5.B | 6.False | 7.True | 8.False
9. Early detection, informed decisions, trend tracking, treatment effectiveness evaluation, threshold-based action, cost savings
10. Monitoring: 1 per 500-1000 sq ft (detect presence); Mass trapping: 1 per 25-50 sq ft (reduce populations)
