## Answer Key

1. **B** - 8 (they're arachnids)
2. **B** - Two spots on their back
3. **A** - Whiteflies are white and rest on leaves; fungus gnats are dark and hover near media
4. **C** - On young shoots and undersides of leaves
5. **B** - Hand lens (10-20x magnification)
6. **True**
7. **False** - Many different species with different control methods
8. **False** - Most require magnification for accurate ID
9. **Acceptable answers:** Powdery is white powder on top surface/downy is purple-gray fuzz underneath; powdery doesn't need free moisture/downy requires wet leaves; powdery is less destructive/downy very destructive; location of growth differs
10. **Acceptable answers:** Accurate ID ensures proper treatment selection, saves money on ineffective treatments, some pests too small to see (russet mites), lab provides expert identification, worth cost for high-value crops
