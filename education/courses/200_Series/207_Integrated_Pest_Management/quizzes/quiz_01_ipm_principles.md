## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. What does IPM stand for?**
- A) Insect Pest Management
- B) Integrated Pest Management
- C) Intensive Pesticide Methods
- D) Indoor Production Management

**2. In the IPM pyramid, which strategy forms the BASE (widest part)?**
- A) Chemical controls
- B) Biological controls
- C) Monitoring and prevention
- D) Physical controls

**3. What is the primary goal of IPM?**
- A) Complete eradication of all pests
- B) Managing pests below economic damage levels using multiple tactics
- C) Using only organic pesticides
- D) Avoiding all chemical use

**4. An action threshold is:**
- A) The point where all plants must be destroyed
- B) The pest population level that triggers a management response
- C) The maximum number of pests allowed by law
- D) The temperature at which pests die

**5. Which best describes the IPM approach compared to traditional pest control?**
- A) Reactive and calendar-based
- B) Proactive and monitoring-based
- C) Single-tactic focused
- D) Eradication-focused

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Integrated Pest Management
2. **C** - Monitoring and prevention
3. **B** - Managing pests below economic damage levels using multiple tactics
4. **B** - The pest population level that triggers a management response
5. **B** - Proactive and monitoring-based
6. **False** - IPM uses pesticides as one tool when needed, but judiciously
7. **True** - Prevention is always more cost-effective
8. **True** - Monitoring and records are foundations of IPM
9. **Acceptable answers include:** Reduced costs long-term, reduced pesticide resistance, safer for workers/environment, sustainable, better quality control, multiple backup strategies, lower chemical residues
10. **Acceptable answers:** Wrong identification leads to wrong treatment/wasted money, different pests require different control methods, enables selection of most effective tactics, prevents unnecessary pesticide use
