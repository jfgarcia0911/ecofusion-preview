# Quick Pest Identification Guide
## Course 207: Integrated Pest Management - Cheatsheet

---

## Common Greenhouse Pests

### APHIDS
**Size:** 1-3mm
**Color:** Green, black, yellow, pink
**Shape:** Pear-shaped, soft-bodied
**Location:** Undersides of young leaves, new growth
**Damage:** Curled leaves, yellowing, sticky honeydew
**Key ID:** Colonies, both winged and wingless forms, cornicles (tail pipes)

---

### WHITEFLIES
**Size:** 1-2mm
**Color:** White (waxy coating)
**Shape:** Tiny moth-like, 4 wings
**Location:** Undersides of leaves
**Damage:** Yellowing, honeydew, virus transmission
**Key ID:** Fly up in cloud when disturbed, white scales on leaf undersides

---

### SPIDER MITES (Two-Spotted)
**Size:** 0.5mm (barely visible)
**Color:** Yellow-green to orange-red
**Shape:** Oval, 8 legs (arachnid!)
**Location:** Undersides of leaves
**Damage:** Yellow stippling, bronzing, webbing
**Key ID:** Fine webbing, two spots on back (need magnification)

---

### THRIPS
**Size:** 1-2mm
**Color:** Yellow, brown, or black
**Shape:** Slender, cigar-shaped
**Location:** Flowers, buds, young leaves
**Damage:** Silver streaks, black fecal dots
**Key ID:** Very fast-moving, fringed wings, rasping damage

---

### FUNGUS GNATS
**Size:** Adults 3mm, larvae 5mm
**Color:** Adults dark gray/black, larvae white with black head
**Shape:** Delicate flies, mosquito-like
**Location:** Adults fly near media, larvae in media
**Damage:** Root damage from larvae
**Key ID:** Dark flies on yellow sticky traps near media surface

---

### MEALYBUGS
**Size:** 2-5mm
**Color:** White/gray
**Shape:** Oval, segmented
**Location:** Leaf axils, stems, undersides
**Damage:** Stunting, honeydew, sooty mold
**Key ID:** White fuzzy/cottony appearance

---

## Common Diseases

### POWDERY MILDEW
**Appearance:** White/gray powder on leaf surfaces
**Location:** Usually upper leaf surface
**Conditions:** Moderate temps (60-80°F), moderate humidity, poor airflow
**Key ID:** Powder rubs off on fingers, does NOT need wet leaves

---

### DOWNY MILDEW
**Appearance:** Yellow angular patches on top, purple/gray fuzz underneath
**Location:** Angular lesions following veins
**Conditions:** Cool nights, warm days, high humidity, leaf wetness
**Key ID:** Fuzzy growth on UNDERSIDE, angular yellow patches

---

### BOTRYTIS (Gray Mold)
**Appearance:** Gray fuzzy mold
**Location:** Flowers, fruit, wounded tissue
**Conditions:** High humidity (>85%), poor circulation, moisture
**Key ID:** Gray spores, affects dying/wounded tissue first

---

### PYTHIUM (Root Rot)
**Appearance:** Brown, slimy roots
**Location:** Root zone
**Conditions:** Waterlogged media, warm temps, poor drainage
**Key ID:** Outer root layer sloughs off, mushy texture, foul odor possible

## Quick Diagnostic Flow Chart

```
SYMPTOM OBSERVED
    ↓
Is it moving?
    ↓ YES                              ↓ NO
    |                                  |
WHERE IS IT?                      WHAT'S THE SYMPTOM?
    |                                  |
On leaves → Check size/color      White powder → Powdery mildew
Flying → Yellow/white?            Gray fuzz → Botrytis
In flowers → Likely thrips        Yellow patches + fuzz → Downy mildew
Near media → Fungus gnats         Brown roots → Root rot
In colonies → Aphids              Mosaic pattern → Virus
Webbing present → Spider mites    Wilting → Check roots!
```

## Beneficial vs. Pest Look-Alikes

| BENEFICIAL | PEST | KEY DIFFERENCE |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Ladybug (3-7 spots, shiny, dome) | Mexican Bean Beetle (16 spots, fuzzy, flat) | Spot count, texture |
| Lacewing larva (alligator-like, fuzzy) | Aphid (pear-shaped, smooth) | Shape, activity |
| Aphid mummy (tan, bloated, exit hole) | Live aphid (green/black, active) | Color, movement |
| Predatory mite (fast, orange/red) | Spider mite (slow, greenish, webbing) | Speed, webbing |

## Trap Guide

**YELLOW STICKY TRAPS attract:**
- Whiteflies
- Winged aphids
- Fungus gnats
- Shore flies
- Leafminers

**BLUE STICKY TRAPS attract:**
- Thrips (primary)
- Leafminers
