- 1A: Organophosphates

## Treatment Decision Tree

```
PEST DETECTED
    ↓
Above action threshold?
    ↓ NO → Continue monitoring
    ↓ YES
    ↓
Try non-chemical first?
    ↓ YES → Cultural/Physical/Biological
    ↓ NO (urgent/high population)
    ↓
Select LEAST-TOXIC effective option:
    ↓
    [1] Insecticidal soap/oil
    [2] Neem oil
    [3] Microbial (Bt, Beauveria)
    [4] Spinosad
    [5] Pyrethrins (last resort)
    [6] Synthetic (emergency only)
    ↓
Check compatibility with biocontrol
    ↓
Apply per label instructions
    ↓
Document application
    ↓
Monitor effectiveness (7-14 days)
    ↓
Effective? → Return to monitoring
Not effective? → Reassess and adjust
```
