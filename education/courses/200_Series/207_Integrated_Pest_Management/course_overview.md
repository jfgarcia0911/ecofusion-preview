## Course Description

Protect your crops using sustainable, science-based pest management strategies. This comprehensive course covers pest identification, biological controls, cultural practices, and IPM program development for controlled environment agriculture.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Identify common greenhouse pests and diseases
2. Implement biological control programs
3. Apply cultural and physical controls effectively
4. Develop comprehensive IPM programs
5. Make informed treatment decisions
6. Maintain IPM documentation and records

## Key Topics

### Common Pests

| Pest | Signs | Primary Biological Controls |
|------|-------|----------------------------|
| Aphids | Honeydew, curled leaves | Ladybugs, lacewings, Aphidius |
| Fungus Gnats | Flying adults, weak seedlings | Hypoaspis, Bt, nematodes |
| Spider Mites | Stippling, webbing | Phytoseiulus, Neoseiulus |
| Thrips | Silver streaks, distorted growth | Amblyseius, Orius |
| Whiteflies | White adults, honeydew | Encarsia, Eretmocerus |

### Common Diseases

| Disease | Type | Prevention Strategies |
|---------|------|----------------------|
| Powdery Mildew | Fungal | Airflow, resistant varieties |
| Botrytis | Fungal | Lower humidity, sanitation |
| Pythium | Oomycete | Drainage, sterile media |
| Downy Mildew | Oomycete | Avoid leaf wetness, airflow |

### IPM Decision Framework

1. **Prevention** - Cultural practices to avoid problems
2. **Monitoring** - Regular scouting and trap placement
3. **Identification** - Accurate pest/disease diagnosis
4. **Threshold** - Action levels for intervention
5. **Selection** - Choose least-toxic effective option
6. **Evaluation** - Assess effectiveness, adjust
