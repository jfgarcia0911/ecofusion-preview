# Activity: Hands-On Scouting Exercise
## Course 207: Integrated Pest Management

---

## Objective

Practice systematic plant inspection and pest identification skills through guided scouting exercise.

---

## Materials Needed

- Hand lens (10-20x magnification)
- Clipboard and scouting forms
- White paper or clipboard
- Camera/smartphone
- Sample plants (or photos)
- Sticky traps
- Pencil/pen

---

## Exercise 1: Plant Inspection Technique (30 minutes)

### Instructions

1. **Select Your Sample Plant**
   - Choose one plant from your facility or use provided specimen
   - Note crop type, location, and overall appearance

2. **Systematic Top-to-Bottom Inspection**
   - Follow the inspection protocol from Module 4
   - Spend 2-3 minutes per plant section
   - Record all observations

### Inspection Checklist

**Growing Point/New Growth:**
- ☐ Color normal? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Distortion present? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Insects visible? (Describe)
- ☐ Signs of feeding? (Describe)

**Upper Leaves:**
- ☐ Leaf color normal? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Check undersides (pests present?)
- ☐ Webbing visible? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Spots or lesions? (Describe)
- ☐ Honeydew present? (Yes/No)

**Mid Leaves:**
- ☐ Overall condition rating (1-5): ____
- ☐ Damage type (if any): __________
- ☐ Pest damage or disease? ________

**Lower/Older Leaves:**
- ☐ Yellowing (normal aging or problem?)
- ☐ Disease signs? (Describe)
- ☐ Early infestation signs?

**Stems:**
- ☐ Lesions or cankers? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Insects present? (Describe)
- ☐ Structural damage?

**Media Surface:**
- ☐ Algae growth? (Yes/No)
- ☐ Fungus gnats flying? (Count)
- ☐ Moisture level appropriate?

**Roots (if accessible):**
- ☐ Color: White/cream (healthy) or brown (concern)
- ☐ Texture: Firm (good) or mushy (bad)
- ☐ Odor: None (good) or foul (concern)
