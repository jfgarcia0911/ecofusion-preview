## Part 5: Control Tactics

### Your Task

For each major pest, create control strategy using multiple tactics.

### Example: APHID MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL

**Prevention (Cultural):**
- Daily debris removal
- Avoid excess nitrogen
- Screen all vents
- Inspect new plants

**Monitoring:**
- Visual inspection 3x/week
- Yellow sticky traps
- Action threshold: 1-2 per plant

**Control Options (in order of preference):**

**First Response (when at threshold):**
1. Water spray to dislodge (if few plants affected)
2. Prune heavily infested parts
3. Release green lacewing eggs (5-10 per plant)

**If Population Increases:**
4. Release aphid parasitoids (Aphidius, 1-3 per aphid)
5. Apply insecticidal soap (per label)

**Last Resort (if above fail):**
6. Selective pesticide (specify product: __________)
   - Mode of action: __________
   - PHI: __________ days
   - REI: __________ hours

**Post-Treatment:**
- Monitor effectiveness 3-5 days later
- Continue beneficials if compatible
- Document in treatment log

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Your plan will be assessed on:

- **Comprehensiveness** (30%): Addresses all IPM components
- **Integration** (25%): Multiple tactics work together
- **Feasibility** (20%): Realistic for stated facility
- **Detail** (15%): Specific, actionable protocols
- **Organization** (10%): Clear, professional format

---

## Tips for Success

✓ Be specific (not just "use beneficial insects" - which ones, how many, when?)
✓ Justify your choices (why these thresholds? why this sequence?)
✓ Make it practical (can you actually implement this?)
✓ Use course materials (reference modules, cheatsheets)
✓ Think prevention first (strongest emphasis on cultural controls)
✓ Plan for monitoring (can't manage what you don't measure!)
