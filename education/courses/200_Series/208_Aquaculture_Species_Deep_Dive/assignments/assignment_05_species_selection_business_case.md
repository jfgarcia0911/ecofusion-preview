# Assignment 5: Species Selection Business Case
## Course 208: Aquaculture Species Deep Dive

| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 14 |

## Purpose
Develop complete business case justifying species selection for aquaponics operation.

## Required Components

### 1. Species Selection Rationale (8 pts)
- Species characteristics summary
- Comparison to alternatives
- Match to climate and resources
- Regulatory compliance status
- Risk assessment

### 2. Production Plan (8 pts)
- Stocking strategy (cohort management)
- Growth timeline
- Feed program and costs
- Harvest schedule
- Production volume projections

### 3. System Integration (6 pts)
- Tank and filtration design
- Plant production integration
- Water quality management
- Climate control requirements

### 4. Market Analysis (4 pts)
- Target markets (live fish, fillets, ornamental)
- Pricing strategy
- Revenue projections
- Competitive analysis

### 5. Financial Projections (4 pts)
- Startup costs (fish stock, tanks, equipment)
- Operating costs (feed, energy, labor)
- Revenue model
- Profitability timeline

**Deliverable:** 10-15 page business case including:
- Executive summary
- Species justification
- Production plan
- System design
- Financial analysis
- Risk mitigation
