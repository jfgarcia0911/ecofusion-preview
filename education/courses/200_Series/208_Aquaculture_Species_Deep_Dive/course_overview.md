## Course Description

Explore the diverse world of aquaculture species suitable for aquaponics. This comprehensive course covers biology, husbandry, and production of multiple fish species and alternative aquatic organisms. Learn to match species to your system design, climate, and market opportunities.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Compare characteristics of different aquaponics fish species
2. Implement species-specific husbandry practices
3. Manage fish reproduction and breeding programs
4. Diversify production with alternative species
5. Match species selection to system design and climate
6. Evaluate market opportunities for different species

## Regulatory Considerations

| Species | Federal Restrictions | Common State Issues |
|---------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Tilapia | Warm states only | Prohibited in many northern states |
| Trout | Food fish approved | Permit requirements vary |
| Channel Catfish | Generally permitted | Some states require permits |
| Largemouth Bass | Generally permitted | May have game fish restrictions |
| Ornamental Koi | Generally permitted | Disease certifications may be needed |

**Critical:** Always verify state and local regulations before selecting species!
