### 6.2 Goldfish Production

#### Biology & Varieties

**Common Goldfish (Carassius auratus)**
- Easiest ornamental to produce
- Hardy, cold-tolerant (40-80°F)
- Omnivorous (will eat anything)
- Breed readily in ponds and tanks
- Multiple color varieties

#### Goldfish Production Parameters

| Parameter | Optimal | Acceptable | Notes |
|-----------|---------|------------|-------|
| **Temperature** | 65-75°F | 40-80°F | Very hardy |
| **pH** | 7.0-8.0 | 6.5-8.5 | Tolerant |
| **DO** | >5 mg/L | >3 mg/L | Less demanding |
| **Stocking** | 0.1 lb/gal | Varies | Depends on size/type |

**Breeding Goldfish:**

```
GOLDFISH BREEDING CYCLE

Spring Trigger:
   Temperature rises to 60-65°F
   Photoperiod lengthens
        │
        ▼
   Male chases female
   "Breeding stars" on male gills
        │
        ▼
   Female lays 1000-5000 eggs
   (Sticky eggs attach to surfaces)
        │
        ▼
   Eggs hatch in 4-7 days (temp dependent)
        │
        ▼
   Fry free-swimming at 2-3 days post-hatch
        │
        ▼
   Feed infusoria, then baby brine shrimp
        │
        ▼
   Grow to 1-2 inches in 2-3 months

KEY: Can breed year-round in controlled temps!
```

### 6.3 Koi Production

**The Premium Ornamental**

Koi (Nishikigoi) = Colored varieties of Cyprinus carpio (common carp)

```
KOI MARKET TIERS

GRADE              SIZE         PRICE        TARGET MARKET
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BUTTERFLY/POND     6-12"        $10-30       Pond owners
(Standard)

STANDARD KOI       8-14"        $30-100      Hobbyists
(Good quality)

PREMIUM KOI        10-18"       $100-500     Serious hobbyists
(Excellent)

SHOW QUALITY       14-24"       $500-5000+   Collectors
(Championship)

GRAND CHAMPION     20-36"       $10,000+     Collectors/breeders
(World class)

KEY INSIGHT: Quality breeding stock is EVERYTHING
            Poor parents = pond fish
            Premium parents = potential show fish
```

#### Koi Production Requirements

**Water Parameters:**

| Parameter | Optimal | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **Temperature** | 65-75°F | Can tolerate 40-85°F |
| **pH** | 7.0-8.0 | Stable is more important than perfect |
| **DO** | >6 mg/L | Essential for growth and color |
| **Ammonia/Nitrite** | 0 mg/L | Sensitive to poor water quality |

**Feeding for Growth & Color:**

```
KOI NUTRITIONAL NEEDS

Life Stage      Protein    Special Ingredients
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Fry             45-50%     High protein growth

Juveniles       35-40%     Balanced growth
(Growing)

Adults          30-35%     Color enhancement:
(Maintaining)              • Spirulina (blue/green)
                           • Astaxanthin (red)
                           • Paprika (orange)
                           • Wheat germ (digestibility)

Temperature-Based Feeding:
• <50°F: No feeding (dormant)
• 50-60°F: Wheat germ diet (digestible)
• 60-70°F: Normal feeding (1-2% BW/day)
• 70-80°F: Peak feeding (2-3% BW/day)
• >80°F: Reduce feeding (stress)
```

#### Koi Breeding & Selection

**The Art of Koi Culling:**

```
KOI SELECTION PROCESS (From 1000 eggs)

Eggs laid:                    1,000 eggs
    │
    ▼
Hatch:                        800 fry (80% hatch rate)
    │
    ▼
First Cull (1 month):        200 keep (remove deformed, weak)
    │                         Remove: 75%
    ▼
Second Cull (2 months):      50 keep (select for pattern/color)
    │                         Remove: 75%
    ▼
Third Cull (4 months):       10-15 keep (quality selection)
    │                         Remove: 70-80%
    ▼
Final Selection (1 year):    2-5 premium fish (show quality)
    │                         Remove: 60-80%
    ▼
Remainder = Pond grade koi

This is NORMAL! Quality koi production requires heavy culling.
Culled fish sold as lower-grade stock.
```

### 6.7 Ornamental Fish Health

**Common Issues:**

| Disease | Symptoms | Treatment | Prevention |
|---------|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Ich** | White spots | Heat + salt | Quarantine |
| **Fungus** | Cotton growth | Salt, methylene blue | Good water quality |
| **Dropsy** | Bloating, scales raised | Usually fatal | Quality food, clean water |
| **Fin Rot** | Deteriorating fins | Antibiotics | Avoid injury, clean water |
| **Parasites** | Flashing, clamped fins | Specific treatments | Quarantine, biosecurity |

**Quarantine Protocol:**
- 30 days minimum for new stock
- Separate systems (no shared water)
- Medicate prophylactically if needed
- Observe daily
