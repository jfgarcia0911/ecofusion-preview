   ╱          ╲
   ════════════ Second Chelipeds

Size at Harvest: 6-10 inches, 50-100g (2-4 oz)
Time to Market: 5-7 months
```

**Physical Characteristics:**
- Blue-gray translucent body
- Long antennae (twice body length)
- Males develop large claws (impressive!)
- Females smaller, more delicate
- Can grow to 12+ inches

#### Prawn Production Parameters

**Water Quality Requirements:**

| Parameter | Optimal | Acceptable | Critical Notes |
|-----------|---------|------------|----------------|
| **Temperature** | 82-86°F | 75-90°F | <70°F = no growth, >95°F = stress |
| **pH** | 7.0-8.5 | 6.5-9.0 | Stable more important than perfect |
| **DO** | >5 mg/L | >4 mg/L | Critical during molting |
| **Hardness** | 50-150 mg/L | 30-200 mg/L | Need calcium for shell |
| **Ammonia** | 0 mg/L | <0.5 mg/L | Sensitive to ammonia |
| **Salinity** | 0 ppt | 0-5 ppt | Fully freshwater (adults) |

#### Prawn Stocking & Management

**Stocking Density:**

```
PRAWN DENSITY GUIDELINES

System Type          Density              Notes
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Backyard/Hobby      5-10 prawns/m²       Conservative
                    (0.5-1 per sq ft)

Commercial          10-20 prawns/m²      Good management
                    (1-2 per sq ft)

Intensive           20-30 prawns/m²      Expert level
                    (2-3 per sq ft)

Example: 100 sq ft tank bottom
─────────────────────────────────
Conservative: 50-100 prawns
Commercial:   100-200 prawns
Intensive:    200-300 prawns

⚠ Density based on BOTTOM AREA, not water volume!
  (Prawns live on bottom, not in water column)
```

**Critical Management Practices:**

1. **Provide Hiding Structures:**
```
PRAWN HABITAT REQUIREMENTS

Water Surface
═══════════════════════════════
         [Open water]

░░░ PVC pipes (horizontally) ░░░
░░░ Shade cloth (vertical)   ░░░  CRITICAL!
░░░ Netting (layered)        ░░░  Prawns hide
░░░ Rocks/caves              ░░░  after molting
═══════════════════════════════
         Tank Bottom

Without hiding places = HIGH CANNIBALISM
With adequate structure = LOW CANNIBALISM
```

2. **Molting Management:**
- Prawns molt every 2-4 weeks
- Vulnerable to cannibalism for 24-48 hours post-molt
- Need calcium in water (hardness 50+ mg/L)
- Add cuttlebone or calcium supplements

3. **Size Grading:**
- Growth rates vary significantly
- Large males dominate (bullying)
- Grade every 4-6 weeks
- Separate by size classes

#### Prawn Feeding

**Nutritional Requirements:**

| Life Stage | Size | Protein % | Feeding Rate |
|------------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **Post-larvae** | <1g | 40-45% | 15-20% BW/day |
| **Juveniles** | 1-10g | 35-40% | 8-12% BW/day |
| **Grow-out** | 10-50g | 30-35% | 5-8% BW/day |
| **Market** | 50-100g | 28-32% | 3-5% BW/day |

**Feed Types:**
- Commercial prawn pellets (best)
- High-quality fish pellets (acceptable)
- Sinking catfish feed (works)
- Supplemental: Vegetables, algae wafers

**Feeding Schedule:**
- Feed in evening (prawns are nocturnal)
- Spread feed across bottom
- Use feeding trays (monitor consumption)
- Remove uneaten feed after 4-6 hours

```
PRAWN PRODUCTION ECONOMICS

   premium niche product.

### 7.3 Freshwater Crayfish (Red Claw Crayfish)

#### Red Claw Crayfish (Cherax quadricarinatus)

**The Australian Alternative:**

```
RED CLAW CRAYFISH PROFILE

Temperature:      70-85°F (wider than prawns)
Growth Time:      6-9 months to 80-100g
Market Size:      3-4 oz (80-100g)
Price:            $8-15/lb live, $12-20/lb tails
FCR:              2.5-3.5 (lower than prawns)
Hardiness:        VERY HARDY (easier than prawns)

ADVANTAGES vs. Prawns:
✓ Wider temperature tolerance
✓ Less cannibalistic (with structure)
✓ Easier to breed
✓ More forgiving water quality
✓ Better survival rates

DISADVANTAGES:
⚠ Slower growth than prawns
⚠ More aggressive (need space)
⚠ Burrow (can damage liners)
```

#### Crayfish Production Parameters

| Parameter | Optimal | Acceptable |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Temperature** | 75-82°F | 65-88°F |
| **pH** | 7.0-8.5 | 6.5-9.0 |
| **DO** | >4 mg/L | >3 mg/L |
| **Hardness** | 50-300 mg/L | 30-400 mg/L |

**Stocking Density:**
- 5-10 per m² (0.5-1 per sq ft)
- Based on bottom area
- Provide 1 hide per 2-3 crayfish

### 7.4 Polyculture: Fish + Crustaceans

**Compatible Combinations:**

```
SUCCESSFUL POLYCULTURE COMBINATIONS

TILAPIA + PRAWNS
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Water Column: Tilapia swim/feed       │
│  ───────────────────────────────       │
│         🐟    🐟    🐟                 │
│  ═══════════════════════════════       │
│  Bottom Zone: Prawns scavenge          │
│  ░░░🦐░░░░🦐░░░░░🦐░░░              │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘

Benefits:
• Use different niches (no competition)
• Prawns clean up waste feed
• Diversified harvest
• Premium + standard products

Considerations:
• Match temperature needs (both like 80-85°F)
• Reduce tilapia density slightly
• Monitor ammonia carefully

CATFISH + CRAYFISH
✓ Compatible (both bottom dwellers but different behavior)
✓ Similar temperature range
⚠ Crayfish may eat catfish fry (if breeding)

TROUT + CRAYFISH
✗ Poor match (incompatible temperatures)
  Trout: 55-65°F
  Crayfish: 70-85°F
```

### 7.5 Breeding Considerations

#### Prawn Breeding (Advanced)

**Challenge:** Prawn larvae require BRACKISH water (12-16 ppt salinity)

```
PRAWN LIFE CYCLE

Adult Prawns (Freshwater)
      │
      ▼
  Mating & Eggs
      │
      ▼
Eggs hatch → LARVAE (need brackish water!)
      │      12-16 ppt salinity
      │      35-40 days
      ▼
POST-LARVAE (back to freshwater)
      │
      ▼
Grow to adults (6-7 months)

IMPLICATION: Most producers BUY post-larvae
             Breeding requires separate hatchery system
```

#### Crayfish Breeding (Easier)

```
CRAYFISH BREEDING

Mature Adults (6+ months old)
      │
      ▼
Mating (male transfers sperm)
      │
      ▼
Female carries 100-500 eggs under tail
      │
      ▼
Hatch in 3-6 weeks (temperature dependent)
      │
      ▼
Juveniles stay with mother 1-2 weeks
      │
      ▼
Independent growth

ADVANTAGE: All happens in freshwater!
           Can breed in production system
⚠ WARNING: Cannibalism of young is common
```

### 7.6 Harvesting & Marketing

**Harvest Methods:**

| Method | Best For | Pros | Cons |
|--------|----------|------|------|
| **Seine net** | Drain down | Complete harvest | Stressful to fish |
| **Baited traps** | Ongoing | Selective, gentle | Slow, labor |
| **Hand nets** | Small scale | Gentle | Very slow |
| **Grading table** | Size sorting | Precise | Requires drain down |

### 7.7 Common Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **High cannibalism** | Inadequate hiding places | Add more structure |
| **Slow growth** | Low temperature | Increase to 80-85°F |
| **Molting problems** | Low hardness | Add calcium supplement |
| **Fungus on eggs** | Poor water quality | Improve filtration, DO |
| **Aggressive males** | Overcrowding | Reduce density, add hides |
