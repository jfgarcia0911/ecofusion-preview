```

        ↓

    • Grocery stores
    • Farmers markets
    • Direct consumers
    • Pet stores (ornamentals)
    • Wholesalers

STEP 2: ANALYZE COMPETITION
        ↓
    Who else is selling?
    • Imported fish (tilapia from China)
    • Wild-caught local
    • Other aquaponics farms
    • Traditional fish farms

STEP 3: DETERMINE PRICING
        ↓
    What will market pay?
    • Survey retail prices
    • Talk to chefs/buyers
    • Understand price sensitivity
    • Calculate your costs

STEP 4: ASSESS DEMAND
        ↓
    How much can you sell?
    • Restaurant capacity
    • Market foot traffic
    • Seasonal variations
    • Growth potential

STEP 5: DEVELOP POSITIONING
        ↓
    Why choose your fish?
    • Local
    • Sustainable
    • Fresh (never frozen)
    • Traceable
    • Organic (if certified)
    • Story/education
```

### 11.3 Pricing Strategies

#### Cost-Plus Pricing

```
COST CALCULATION TEMPLATE

VARIABLE COSTS (per pound of fish):
Fingerlings:           $______
Feed:                  $______
Labor (feeding, monitoring): $______
Utilities (electricity, water): $______
Packaging:             $______
                       ──────
Subtotal Variable:     $______  (A)

FIXED COSTS (per batch, allocated per pound):
Infrastructure depreciation: $______
Insurance:             $______
Facility costs:        $______
Marketing:             $______
                       ──────
Subtotal Fixed:        $______  (B)

TOTAL COST PER POUND: $(A + B) = $______  (C)

PRICING:
Cost (C):              $______
Profit Margin (30%):   $______
WHOLESALE PRICE:       $______

Retail Markup (40%):   $______
RETAIL PRICE:          $______

Example (Tilapia):
Cost: $3.50/lb
Profit (30%): $1.05/lb
Wholesale: $4.55/lb
Retail markup (40%): $1.82/lb
Retail price: $6.37/lb → $6.49/lb (rounded)
```

---

#### Market-Based Pricing

| Species | Import Price | Local Farm Price | Premium Local | Your Opportunity |
|---------|-------------|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Tilapia** | $4-5/lb | $6-8/lb | $8-10/lb | Position at $7-9/lb |
| **Catfish** | $3-4/lb | $5-7/lb | $7-9/lb | Position at $6-8/lb |
| **Trout** | $8-10/lb | $10-14/lb | $14-18/lb | Position at $12-15/lb |
| **Barramundi** | N/A (rare) | $12-15/lb | $15-20/lb | Position at $14-18/lb |

**Pricing Strategy:**
- Don't compete on price with imports (you'll lose)
- Compete on: Fresh, Local, Sustainable, Traceable, Story
- Premium positioning justified by quality

### 11.4 Restaurant Sales

**The Restaurant Partnership Model:**

```
RESTAURANT SALES STRATEGY

STEP 1: IDENTIFY TARGET RESTAURANTS
✓ Farm-to-table focused
✓ Sustainable seafood commitment
✓ Local sourcing values
✓ Menu flexibility
✗ Chain restaurants (corporate purchasing)
✗ Price-focused only

STEP 2: MAKE CONTACT
• Email chef/owner (not general manager)
• Offer samples (critical!)
• Invite to farm tour
• Share your story

STEP 3: SAMPLE DELIVERY
• Bring FRESH whole fish (show quality)
• Include information sheet:
  - Species details
  - Cooking suggestions
  - Your farm story
  - Pricing
• Follow up in 2-3 days

STEP 4: NEGOTIATE TERMS
• Volume commitment (100 lbs/month?)
• Delivery schedule (weekly? bi-weekly?)
• Price (be flexible for first customer)
• Payment terms (typically Net 30)

STEP 5: DELIVER EXCELLENCE
• Consistent quality
• Reliable delivery
• Communication (shortages, abundance)
• Respond to feedback
```

**Restaurant Pricing Formula:**

```
YOUR WHOLESALE PRICE:    $7/lb (example)
Restaurant food cost:     ~30% of menu price
Menu price calculation:   $7 ÷ 0.30 = $23
Typical fish entrée:      $24-28

This works! Restaurant can make margin while
           paying premium for your local fish.
```

### 11.5 Farmers Market Success

**Farmers Market Best Practices:**

| Success Factor | Implementation |
|----------------|----------------|
| **Product Presentation** | Ice bed display, whole fish + fillets, clear pricing |
| **Education** | Signs explaining aquaponics, sustainability, species |
| **Samples** | Cooked fish samples (if allowed), recipe cards |
| **Story** | Talk to customers, farm photos, bring kids' interest |
| **Consistency** | Same market, same spot, every week (build following) |
| **Add-ons** | Herbs, lettuce from your system, value combo |

**Typical Farmers Market Economics:**

```
FARMERS MARKET PROFITABILITY

Sales per market day:     $400-800 (varies widely)
COGS (fish + ice):        $150-300
Booth fee:                $25-50
Labor (6 hours):          $100-150 (if paid)
Transport/setup:          $20-30
                          ──────
Net per day:              $75-400

Annual (25 markets):      $1,875-10,000

ADVANTAGE: Higher prices ($10-15/lb)
CHALLENGE: Weather-dependent, labor-intensive
BEST FOR: Community building, premium pricing
```

### 11.6 Value-Added Products

**Increasing Profit Margins:**

```
VALUE-ADDED PRODUCT IDEAS

BASIC (Easy to add):
─────────────────
• Fillets (vs. whole fish)          +40% price
• Skin-on vs. skin-off              +$1/lb
• Vacuum sealed                      +$2/lb
• Frozen in meal portions           +$3/lb

INTERMEDIATE:
─────────────
• Smoked fish                        +100%+ price
• Marinated fillets                  +80% price
• Fish tacos (prepared)              +200% price
• Fish cakes/burgers                 +150% price

ADVANCED:
─────────
• Fish sauce/fish fertilizer         High margin
• Dried fish                         Shelf-stable
• Canned/jarred products             Requires licensing
• Pet treats (ornamental culls)      Niche market

SYNERGY PRODUCTS (from your aquaponics):
───────────────────────────────────────
• Aquaponic-grown herbs               Bundle with fish
• Aquaponic lettuce salad kit         "Complete meal"
• Growing kits (system replicas)      Education/retail
• Consulting/workshops                Service revenue
```

### 11.7 CSA/Subscription Models

**Community Supported Aquaculture:**

```
CSA MODEL STRUCTURE

CONCEPT:
Customers pay upfront for season (6-12 months)
Receive weekly/monthly fish share
You get working capital, guaranteed sales

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE:
────────────────

Plan:           Monthly Fish Share
Price:          $60/month
What:           5 lbs fish (variety)
Season:         6 months (April-Sept)
Members:        25 families
Revenue:        25 × $60 × 6 = $9,000

Add-on:         Veggie share from aquaponics (+$40/month)
Total:          $100/month × 25 × 6 = $15,000

ADVANTAGES:
✓ Cash flow (pay upfront or monthly)
✓ Guaranteed sales
✓ Customer loyalty
✓ Community building
✓ Feedback loop

CHALLENGES:
⚠ Must deliver consistently
⚠ Need variety (multiple species?)
⚠ Seasonal demand (winter drop-off)
⚠ Marketing/recruitment
```

### 11.8 Marketing & Branding

**Your Unique Value Proposition:**

```
AQUAPONICS MARKETING MESSAGES

SUSTAINABILITY ANGLE:
"Raised in a closed-loop ecosystem where fish and
 plants grow together in harmony. Zero waste,
 90% less water than traditional farming."

LOCAL/FRESH ANGLE:
"Harvested this morning, on your plate tonight.
 Can imported fish from halfway around the
 world say that?"

TRACEABLE/SAFE ANGLE:
"You can visit our farm, see where your fish grew up,
 meet the farmer. Transparent, traceable, trustworthy."

QUALITY ANGLE:
"Hand-fed premium feed, pristine water quality,
 carefully tended. Taste the difference that
 care makes."

EDUCATION ANGLE:
"Every fish purchase supports sustainable
 agriculture education. We teach schools how to
 grow their own food."
```

### 11.9 Scaling Considerations

```
GROWTH PATHWAY

STAGE 1: HOBBY/MICRO (Start here)
─────────────────────────────────
Production:     50-200 lbs/year
Sales:          Farmers market, friends/family
Investment:     $2,000-10,000
Income:         $500-3,000/year (supplemental)

STAGE 2: PART-TIME SMALL FARM
──────────────────────────────
Production:     500-2,000 lbs/year
Sales:          2-3 restaurants, farmers market, CSA
Investment:     $10,000-50,000
Income:         $5,000-20,000/year

STAGE 3: FULL-TIME SMALL COMMERCIAL
────────────────────────────────────
Production:     5,000-20,000 lbs/year
Sales:          10+ restaurants, wholesale, retail
Investment:     $50,000-200,000
Income:         $30,000-80,000/year (1-2 jobs)

STAGE 4: COMMERCIAL OPERATION
──────────────────────────────
Production:     50,000+ lbs/year
Sales:          Distributors, grocery chains, export
Investment:     $500,000-2,000,000
Income:         $100,000-500,000+/year

CRITICAL: Don't skip stages!
          Grow incrementally, prove model first.
```
