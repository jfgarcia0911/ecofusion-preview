### 12.9 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

```
PROJECT MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ UNREALISTIC PROJECTIONS
   "I'll sell 10,000 lbs in year 1 from backyard system"
   ✓ Base on similar operations' actual results

❌ UNDERESTIMATING COSTS
   "I can build this for $500"
   ✓ Add 30% contingency to all cost estimates

❌ IGNORING REGULATIONS
   "I'll just stock tilapia" (in state where illegal)
   ✓ Research and cite actual regulations

❌ NO MARKET RESEARCH
   "Everyone will want my fish"
   ✓ Talk to actual potential customers

❌ OVERLY COMPLEX FIRST SYSTEM
   "I'll do 5 species in polyculture"
   ✓ Start simple, master one species first

❌ ASSUMING IMMEDIATE SALES
   "Day 1: $10,000 revenue"
   ✓ Allow 6-12 months to build market

❌ NO BACKUP PLANS
   "Nothing will go wrong"
   ✓ Plan for equipment failure, disease, market changes
```
