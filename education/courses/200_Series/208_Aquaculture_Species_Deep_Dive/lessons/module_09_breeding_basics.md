### 9.2 Fish Reproduction Fundamentals

#### Types of Reproduction

```
REPRODUCTIVE STRATEGIES

BROADCAST SPAWNERS              SUBSTRATE SPAWNERS
(Scatter eggs in water)         (Attach eggs to surfaces)
───────────────────            ──────────────────────

Species:                        Species:
• Tilapia (mouthbrooder)       • Catfish (cavity nester)
• Goldfish                      • Bass (nest builder)
• Koi                           • Cichlids (some)
• Barramundi

Management:                     Management:
• Eggs easy to collect         • Need spawning substrates
• High numbers (1000s)         • Lower numbers (100s-1000s)
• Moderate parental care       • Strong parental care
```

#### Sexual Dimorphism (Identifying Males vs. Females)

| Species | Male Characteristics | Female Characteristics |
|---------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **Tilapia** | Pointed genital papilla | Rounded, 3 openings |
| **Goldfish** | Breeding stars on gills | Rounder, fuller body |
| **Catfish** | Narrower head | Rounder body when gravid |
| **Koi** | Slender, rough pectoral fins | Plump body, smooth fins |

### 9.3 Breeding Tilapia

**The Easiest Species to Breed**

```
TILAPIA BREEDING OVERVIEW

SETUP:
• Separate breeding tank (100-300 gallons)
• Male to female ratio: 1:3 to 1:5
• Temperature: 80-86°F (triggers spawning)
• Provide flat surfaces (spawning sites)

PROCESS:
Week 0:   Male establishes territory, courts females
Week 1:   Female lays eggs (100-1000+), male fertilizes
Week 2:   Female holds eggs in mouth (not eating!)
Week 3:   Fry released but return to mouth for protection
Week 4:   Fry independent, start feeding
Week 8:   Fingerling size (1-2 inches)

FREQUENCY: Every 4-6 weeks per female!

CHALLENGE: Too prolific! Population control critical
```

**Tilapia Breeding Methods:**

| Method | Description | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------------|------|------|
| **Natural breeding** | Let them spawn freely | Easy, no intervention | Overpopulation, size variation |
| **Egg harvesting** | Collect eggs from female mouth | Control numbers | Stressful, skill required |
| **Fry collection** | Net fry when released | Moderate control | Labor intensive |
| **Separate sexes** | Keep only males | No breeding | Must buy fingerlings |

### 9.4 Breeding Goldfish & Koi

#### Environmental Triggers

```
GOLDFISH/KOI SPAWNING TRIGGERS

NATURAL METHOD (Seasonal):
───────────────────────────
Fall/Winter:  Drop temperature to 50-55°F
              Reduce feeding
              Shorter photoperiod
                  ↓
              Fish become dormant
                  ↓
Spring:       Gradually increase temperature to 65-70°F
              Lengthen photoperiod (14+ hours light)
              Increase feeding (high protein)
                  ↓
              SPAWNING TRIGGERED! (usually early morning)

CONTROLLED METHOD (Year-round):
────────────────────────────────
Use separate breeding tank
Control temperature cycles
Manipulate photoperiod
Condition breeders with high-quality food
```

#### Goldfish/Koi Breeding Process

```
SPAWNING DAY

Early Morning: Male(s) chase female vigorously
               "Breeding rush" behavior
                  ↓
               Female releases eggs while swimming
               Male(s) release milt (sperm) simultaneously
                  ↓
               Eggs stick to spawning mops, plants, surfaces
               (1000-10,000 eggs typical)
                  ↓
CRITICAL: Remove adults immediately!
         (They will eat eggs)
                  ↓
Egg incubation: 4-7 days (temperature dependent)
                Remove white (unfertilized) eggs
                  ↓
Hatching:       Fry attach to surfaces for 24-48 hours
                (absorbing yolk sac)
                  ↓
Free-swimming:  Begin feeding infusoria or liquid fry food
                  ↓
Growth:         3-4 weeks to visible size
                Cull poor quality as they grow
```

### 9.5 Fry Rearing

#### Critical First Weeks

```
FRY DEVELOPMENT STAGES

STAGE 1: Hatching (Day 0-2)
─────────────────────────────
• Attached to surfaces
• No feeding (absorbing yolk)
• Need still water (no current)
• Temperature stable

STAGE 2: Free-swimming (Day 3-7)
─────────────────────────────────
• Begin swimming horizontally
• START FEEDING: Infusoria, liquid fry food
• Feed 4-6 times daily
• Tiny amounts (don't overfeed!)

STAGE 3: Early growth (Week 2-4)
─────────────────────────────────
• Feed baby brine shrimp
• Transition to powdered feed
• Feed 3-4 times daily
• Begin gentle water changes (10% daily)

STAGE 4: Fingerling (Week 4-8)
──────────────────────────────
• Feed fine crumble
• 3 times daily
• Can tolerate normal water flow
• Grade by size
```

#### Fry Foods Progression

| Age | Food | Particle Size | Protein % |
|-----|------|---------------|-----------|
| **0-3 days** | Yolk sac (no feeding) | - | - |
| **3-7 days** | Infusoria, liquid fry food | Microscopic | 50%+ |
| **1-2 weeks** | Baby brine shrimp (BBS) | 0.4mm | 55%+ |
| **2-4 weeks** | Fry powder | 0.1-0.3mm | 50%+ |
| **4-8 weeks** | Fine crumble | 0.5-1mm | 45%+ |
| **8+ weeks** | Small pellet | 1-2mm | 40%+ |

### 9.7 Genetic Selection

**Improving Your Stock Over Time**

```
SELECTIVE BREEDING PROGRAM

Generation 1: Start with quality breeders
              ↓
              Spawn and raise to market size
              ↓
              SELECT best performers:
              • Fastest growth
              • Best body shape
              • Disease resistance
              • Even size
              ↓
Generation 2: Use ONLY selected fish as breeders
              ↓
              Repeat selection process
              ↓
Generation 3: Noticeable improvement
              ↓
Generation 4-5: Significant improvement
              (20-30% better growth possible)

AVOID:
✗ Breeding siblings (inbreeding)
✗ Keeping slow growers
✗ Using diseased fish
✗ Selecting for only one trait
```

### 9.8 Common Breeding Problems

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **No spawning** | Wrong temperature, immature fish, poor nutrition | Check all parameters, wait for maturity |
| **Low hatch rate** | Infertile eggs, poor water quality, fungus | Improve water quality, remove dead eggs |
| **Fry mortality** | Starvation, poor water quality, disease | Feed correctly, maintain pristine water |
| **Cannibalism** | Wrong food size, overcrowding | Appropriate food, reduce density |
| **Deformities** | Genetics, nutrition, temperature swings | Cull affected fish, improve conditions |

### 9.9 Advanced: Hormone-Induced Breeding

**For species that don't breed naturally in captivity**

```
HORMONE PROTOCOL (Example: Barramundi)

CAUTION: Advanced technique, requires expertise!

Preparation:
• Select healthy breeders (3-4+ years old)
• Condition with high-quality food
• Separate by sex
• Check for readiness (females gravid)

Injection:
• LHRHa (hormone) @ 50-100 μg/kg
• Inject intramuscularly
• Time carefully (evening)

Response:
• Ovulation in 36-48 hours
• Strip eggs from female
• Strip milt from male
• Mix gently
• Incubate in special setup

Success Rate: 50-80% with experience

Note: Most aquaponics operators BUY fingerlings
      of species requiring hormones
```
