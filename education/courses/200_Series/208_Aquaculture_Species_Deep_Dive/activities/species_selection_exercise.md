# Activity: Species Selection Exercise
## Course 208: Aquaculture Species Deep Dive

---

## Activity Overview

**Objective:** Apply the species selection methodology to evaluate real-world scenarios and make informed species choices.

**Duration:** 60-90 minutes

**Format:** Individual or small group

**Materials Needed:**
- Species Selection Scorecard (provided below)
- Course modules 1-2 for reference
- Internet access for research
- Calculator

---

## Part 1: Scenario Analysis

You will evaluate three different scenarios and recommend the best fish species for each.

### Scenario A: Colorado Greenhouse Operation

**Location:** Denver, Colorado
**Climate:** Cold winters (-10°F to 40°F), warm summers (65-95°F)
**Facility:** 2000 sq ft heated greenhouse
**Experience:** Beginner (first commercial attempt)
**Budget:** Moderate ($30,000 startup)
**Target Market:** Local restaurants and farmers markets
**Goals:** Year-round production, premium pricing

**Your Task:**
1. Evaluate 3 species options for this scenario
2. Complete the Species Selection Scorecard for each
3. Recommend the BEST species with justification

**Considerations:**
- Heating costs in winter
- Market preferences in Colorado
- Legal status of species in Colorado
- Temperature control requirements
- Beginner-friendliness

---

## Part 4: Written Justification

For your recommended species in each scenario, write a 1-2 paragraph justification addressing:

1. Why this species is the best fit for this specific situation
2. What challenges the operator should anticipate
3. What critical success factors they must manage
4. What alternative species could work if the first choice doesn't pan out

---

## Part 5: Deep Dive Research

Choose ONE of your recommended species and research:

1. **Fingerling Sources:**
   - Find 3 hatcheries that sell this species
   - Compare prices, minimum orders, shipping
   - Note certifications (disease-free, etc.)

2. **Feed Options:**
   - Identify 3 suitable feed brands
   - Compare protein levels, prices, availability
   - Calculate feed cost to produce 100 lbs of fish

3. **Regulations:**
   - Research specific state regulations for this species
   - Identify required permits/licenses
   - Note any special requirements (inspections, etc.)

4. **Market Pricing:**
   - Survey 3 local markets (restaurants, stores, etc.)
   - Document current prices for this species
   - Identify potential price points for your product

## Tips for Success

1. **Be Objective:** Don't let personal preferences override data
2. **Research Thoroughly:** Use actual state regulations, not assumptions
3. **Consider ALL Factors:** Temperature might eliminate otherwise great options
4. **Think Long-Term:** Consider 5-year plan, not just year 1
5. **Be Realistic:** Factor in operator experience level
6. **Document Sources:** Keep track of where you found information
