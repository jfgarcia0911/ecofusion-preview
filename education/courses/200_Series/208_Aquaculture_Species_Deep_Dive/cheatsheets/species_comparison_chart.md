## Beginner Species Ranking

### BEST FOR BEGINNERS:
1. **Goldfish** - Indestructible, cheap, breed easily
2. **Tilapia** (where legal) - Forgiving, fast growth
3. **Channel Catfish** - Hardy, legal everywhere
4. **Blue Tilapia** - More cold-tolerant than Nile

### INTERMEDIATE:
5. **Barramundi** - Need precise temp control
6. **Koi** - Breeding/grading skills needed
7. **Red Claw Crayfish** - Different management approach

### ADVANCED:
8. **Rainbow Trout** - Chilling required, sensitive
9. **Yellow Perch** - Slow growth, precise conditions
10. **Freshwater Prawns** - Complex, expensive setup

## Key Reminders

**Temperature:**
- Most critical factor - can't easily change climate
- Tilapia DIE <50°F
- Trout DIE >75°F
- Always have backup heating/cooling

**Legal:**
- CHECK YOUR STATE FIRST!
- Tilapia restricted in many states
- Some game fish (bass, perch) may require permits
- Penalties for illegal species are severe

**Economics:**
- Trout: Highest price, highest costs
- Tilapia: Good balance of growth & market
- Catfish: Reliable, proven market
- Ornamentals: Highest profit per fish (if skilled)

**Start Small:**
- Prove concept before scaling
- Master one species before adding more
- Polyculture is advanced - not for beginners
