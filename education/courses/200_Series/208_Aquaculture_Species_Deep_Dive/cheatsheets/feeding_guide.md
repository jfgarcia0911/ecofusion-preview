## Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) Quick Reference

```
FCR = Feed Fed (lbs) ÷ Weight Gain (lbs)

LOWER = BETTER (more efficient)

EXCELLENT FCR:
• Rainbow Trout: 1.1-1.3  ★★★★★
• Barramundi: 1.2-1.5     ★★★★

GOOD FCR:
• Tilapia: 1.4-1.6        ★★★
• Catfish: 1.5-1.8        ★★★
• Perch: 1.5-2.0          ★★★

FAIR FCR:
• Prawns: 2.0-2.5         ★★
• Crayfish: 2.5-3.5       ★
• Goldfish: 2.0-3.0       ★★
```

**Calculate Your FCR:**
```
Example:
Started: 100 fish @ 100g = 10 kg
Ended: 100 fish @ 500g = 50 kg
Weight gain: 40 kg
Feed used: 56 kg

FCR = 56 kg ÷ 40 kg = 1.4

Interpretation: Used 1.4 kg feed per 1 kg growth
               (Excellent for tilapia!)
```

## Daily Feeding Calculations

### Method 1: Percentage of Body Weight

```
STEP 1: Calculate total biomass
        Fish count × Average weight = Total biomass
        Example: 500 fish × 300g each = 150 kg

STEP 2: Determine feeding rate
        From table above (e.g., juveniles = 3% BW/day)

STEP 3: Calculate daily feed
        150 kg × 0.03 = 4.5 kg/day

STEP 4: Divide by number of feedings
        4.5 kg ÷ 3 feedings = 1.5 kg per feeding

STEP 5: Adjust based on observation
        Fish not finishing? Reduce next feeding
        Fish still hungry? Increase slightly
```

### Method 2: Visual Satiation

```
THE OBSERVATION METHOD (More Accurate!)

1. Start with calculated amount
2. Feed slowly
3. Watch feeding behavior:

   ACTIVE FEEDING:
   • Fish competing for food    → Continue feeding
   • Rapid consumption          → Good sign

   SLOWING DOWN:
   • Less competition           → Almost done
   • Slower pickup              → Stop soon

   SATIATION:
   • Food sinking uneaten      → STOP!
   • Fish losing interest       → STOP!

4. Record actual amount fed
5. Adjust tomorrow's ration
6. Over time, you'll know exact amounts

ADVANTAGE: Adjusts for temperature, health, water quality automatically
```

## Feed Storage

```
PROPER FEED STORAGE RULES

✓ DO:
  • Store in cool, dry location (<75°F ideal)
  • Use airtight containers (plastic bins with lids)
  • Elevate off ground (prevent moisture)
  • Label with date received
  • First in, first out (FIFO)
  • Use within 6 months of manufacture
  • Check monthly for mold/insects

✗ DON'T:
  • Leave bags open
  • Store in direct sunlight
  • Store in damp locations
  • Mix old and new feed
  • Use feed >12 months old
  • Ignore musty smells
  • Keep moldy feed

SIGNS OF BAD FEED:
🚫 Musty/rancid odor
🚫 Discoloration (yellowing, darkening)
🚫 Mold/fungus growth
🚫 Insect infestation
🚫 Clumping/caking
🚫 Dusty/crumbling

When in doubt, throw it out! Bad feed = sick fish
```

## Feeding Schedule Examples

### Example 1: Backyard Tilapia (300 fish @ 350g avg)

```
Total biomass: 300 × 350g = 105 kg
Feeding rate: 3% body weight/day
Daily feed: 105 kg × 0.03 = 3.15 kg (round to 3.2 kg)

DAILY SCHEDULE:
8:00 AM   1.3 kg (40%)
1:00 PM   1.0 kg (30%)
6:00 PM   0.9 kg (30%)
          ─────
TOTAL:    3.2 kg

Weekly feed needed: 3.2 kg × 7 = 22.4 kg
Monthly: ~95 kg
Cost @ $1/kg: ~$95/month feed cost
```

### Example 2: Commercial Trout (1000 fish @ 200g avg)

```
Total biomass: 1000 × 200g = 200 kg
Feeding rate: 2.5% body weight/day (at 58°F)
Daily feed: 200 kg × 0.025 = 5.0 kg

DAILY SCHEDULE:
7:00 AM   1.7 kg (34%)
12:00 PM  1.6 kg (33%)
5:00 PM   1.7 kg (33%)
          ─────
TOTAL:    5.0 kg

Monthly feed: 5.0 kg × 30 = 150 kg
Cost @ $1.50/kg: ~$225/month feed cost
Expected gain: 150 kg ÷ 1.2 FCR = 125 kg growth
```

## Feeding Problem Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| **Fish not eating** | Water quality, temperature, disease | Test parameters, observe health |
| **Poor growth despite feeding** | Old feed, wrong protein%, disease | Check feed quality, adjust formula |
| **High FCR (>2.0)** | Overfeeding, low temp, poor feed | Reduce ration, check temp, upgrade feed |
| **Uneaten feed accumulating** | Overfeeding, sick fish | Reduce amount, check fish health |
| **Aggressive feeding competition** | Insufficient food points | Spread feed wider, add feeders |
| **Some fish thin, others fat** | Size variation | Grade by size, separate |

## Key Feeding Rules

```
THE 10 COMMANDMENTS OF FISH FEEDING

1. Feed consistent times daily (fish learn schedule)

2. Feed appropriate food size for fish size

3. Never overfeed (causes water quality problems)

4. Remove uneaten feed after 30-60 minutes

5. Adjust feeding rate for temperature

6. Use fresh feed (<6 months old)

7. Store feed properly (cool, dry, sealed)

8. Monitor FCR monthly

9. Reduce feeding when water quality declines

10. STOP feeding 24-48 hours before harvest
```
