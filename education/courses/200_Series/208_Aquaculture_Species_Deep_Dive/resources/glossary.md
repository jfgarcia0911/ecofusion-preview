# Aquaculture Glossary
## Course 208: Aquaculture Species Deep Dive

---

## A

**Aeration** - The process of adding oxygen to water through air stones, venturi, or water turbulence.

**Alkalinity** - The buffering capacity of water; its ability to resist pH changes. Measured in mg/L as CaCO3.

**All-Male Fingerlings** - Tilapia fingerlings that are genetically or hormonally sex-reversed to be all male, preventing reproduction.

**Ammonia (NH3/NH4+)** - Toxic nitrogen compound excreted by fish as primary waste product. More toxic at higher pH.

**Aquaculture** - The farming of aquatic organisms including fish, crustaceans, mollusks, and aquatic plants.

**Aquaponics** - The integration of aquaculture and hydroponics in a symbiotic system where fish waste provides nutrients for plants.

---

## B

**Barbels** - Whisker-like sensory organs on catfish used to locate food.

**Biofilter** - System component housing beneficial bacteria that convert ammonia to nitrite to nitrate (nitrification).

**Biomass** - The total weight of all organisms (usually fish) in a system. Critical for calculating feeding rates and stocking density.

**Biosecurity** - Practices and protocols designed to prevent the introduction and spread of disease organisms.

**Brackish Water** - Water that is saltier than freshwater but not as salty as seawater, typically 0.5-30 ppt salinity.

**Broodstock** - Adult fish maintained specifically for breeding purposes.

---

## C

**Cannibalism** - The eating of smaller individuals by larger ones of the same species. Common in prawns, barramundi, and some fish when size variation exists.

**Carapace** - The hard upper shell of crustaceans (prawns, crayfish).

**Carnivore** - An organism that primarily eats meat/protein. Examples: trout, barramundi, bass.

**Chelipeds** - The claws of crustaceans.

**Coldwater Species** - Fish requiring water temperatures generally below 65°F for optimal growth. Examples: trout, salmon.

**Columnaris** - Bacterial disease (Flavobacterium columnare) causing white/gray cotton-like patches on fish.

**Crustacean** - Arthropods with hard exoskeletons, including prawns, crayfish, shrimp, and lobsters.

**Culling** - The selective removal of undesirable individuals to improve overall stock quality or control population.

---

## D

**Denitrification** - The bacterial conversion of nitrate (NO3) to nitrogen gas (N2), removing nitrogen from the system.

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** - The amount of oxygen dissolved in water, measured in mg/L or ppm. Critical for fish respiration.

**DWC (Deep Water Culture)** - Aquaponics method where plants float on rafts in deep channels of nutrient-rich water.

---

## E

**eFCR (Economic Feed Conversion Ratio)** - Feed cost divided by fish value, showing true economic efficiency.

**Euryhaline** - Organisms that tolerate a wide range of salinity levels. Example: barramundi.

**Exoskeleton** - External skeleton of crustaceans that must be shed (molted) to allow growth.

---

## F

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** - The ratio of feed fed to weight gain. Formula: Feed Given ÷ Weight Gain. Lower is better.

**Feeder Fish** - Low-cost fish (usually goldfish) sold as food for larger fish or pets.

**Fingerling** - Young fish typically 1-3 inches long, ready for stocking into grow-out systems.

**Fry** - Newly hatched fish, from hatching until they reach fingerling size.

---

## G

**Genital Papilla** - External opening used for sexing fish, particularly tilapia.

**GPM (Gallons Per Minute)** - Flow rate measurement for pumps and water circulation.

**Grading** - Sorting fish by size to reduce size variation and competition.

**Gravid** - Carrying eggs; pregnant (in fish context).

**Grow-out** - The phase of production from fingerling to market size.

---

## H

**Hardness** - The concentration of calcium and magnesium in water, measured in mg/L as CaCO3.

**Hatchery** - Facility specializing in breeding fish and producing fingerlings/fry.

**Hermaphrodite** - Organism with both male and female reproductive organs. Barramundi are protandrous hermaphrodites (male first, then female).

---

## I

**Ich (Ichthyophthirius multifiliis)** - Common parasitic disease causing white spots on fish, also called "white spot disease."

**Infusoria** - Collective term for microscopic organisms used as first food for fish fry.

---

## J

**Juvenile** - Young fish in the stage between fingerling and adult, typically 3-6 inches.

---

## K

**KH (Carbonate Hardness)** - Measure of alkalinity/buffering capacity of water.

**Koi** - Ornamental variety of common carp (Cyprinus carpio) bred for color and pattern.

---

## L

**Larva/Larvae** - Early life stage of fish and crustaceans, typically just after hatching.

**Lethal Limit** - Temperature or water parameter at which fish die.

---

## M

**Market Size** - The weight/size at which fish are typically harvested and sold.

**Milt** - Fish sperm.

**Molting** - The shedding of the exoskeleton in crustaceans to allow growth.

**Mouthbrooder** - Fish that incubates eggs in its mouth. Tilapia are mouthbrooders.

---

## N

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)** - Hydroponic/aquaponic method using shallow channels with thin film of flowing water.

**Nishikigoi** - Japanese term for ornamental koi carp.

**Nitrification** - The bacterial conversion of ammonia to nitrite to nitrate.

**Nitrite (NO2)** - Intermediate toxic nitrogen compound in the nitrogen cycle, converted from ammonia.

**Nitrate (NO3)** - Final product of nitrification; primary plant nutrient, relatively safe for fish.

**Nocturnal** - Active at night. Examples: catfish, prawns.

---

## O

**Omnivore** - Organism that eats both plant and animal material. Examples: tilapia, catfish, goldfish.

**Ornamental Fish** - Fish raised for aesthetic value rather than food. Examples: koi, goldfish, tropical fish.

---

## P

**Palatability** - How appealing food is to fish; affects feed consumption.

**Polyculture** - Raising multiple species together in the same system.

**Post-larvae (PL)** - Stage in prawn development after metamorphosis from larvae, ready for freshwater stocking.

**ppm (parts per million)** - Measurement of concentration, equivalent to mg/L for water.

**ppt (parts per thousand)** - Measurement of salinity.

**Protandrous Hermaphrodite** - Organism that starts life as male and becomes female later. Example: barramundi.

**Protein** - Essential macronutrient in fish feed, required for growth. Carnivores need 40-50%, omnivores 28-35%.

**Purging** - Withholding feed before harvest to empty fish digestive systems, improving meat quality.

---

## Q

**Quarantine** - Isolation of new fish for 30+ days to prevent disease introduction to main system.

---

## R

**Rhizosphere** - The narrow zone of soil/media around plant roots.

**ROS (Recirculating Aquaculture System)** - Intensive fish production system that recycles and treats water.

---

## S

**Salinity** - The saltiness of water, measured in ppt (parts per thousand).

**Sexing** - Determining the sex of fish, important for tilapia population control.

**SGR (Specific Growth Rate)** - Percentage of body weight gained per day, formula: [(ln W2 - ln W1) ÷ days] × 100.

**Spawn/Spawning** - The reproductive process in fish; releasing and fertilizing eggs.

**Stocking Density** - The weight or number of fish per unit volume (lb/gallon) or area (fish/m²).

**Substrate** - Material or surface where fish lay eggs or bacteria colonize.

**Substrate Spawner** - Fish that attach eggs to surfaces or cavities. Example: catfish.

---

## T

**TAN (Total Ammonia Nitrogen)** - The sum of NH3 (toxic un-ionized ammonia) and NH4+ (less toxic ionized ammonium).

**Thermal Tolerance** - The range of temperatures a species can survive and grow in.

---

## U

**Un-ionized Ammonia (NH3)** - The toxic form of ammonia, increases with higher pH.

---

## V

**Venturi** - Device that uses water flow to inject air/oxygen into water.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)** - Measure of air's drying power, important for plant health in aquaponics greenhouses.

---

## W

**Warmwater Species** - Fish requiring water temperatures generally above 75°F for optimal growth. Examples: tilapia, catfish, barramundi.

**Water Column** - The vertical space in the water from surface to bottom.

**Withdrawal Period** - Time required after drug/medication treatment before fish can be harvested for food.

---

## Y

**YY Male** - Genetically "super male" tilapia with two Y chromosomes, used to produce all-male offspring.

**Yolk Sac** - Nutrient reserve attached to newly hatched fry, provides nutrition for first few days before external feeding begins.

---

## Common Abbreviations

- **AP** - Aquaponics
- **BW** - Body Weight
- **CFM** - Cubic Feet per Minute (air flow)
- **DO** - Dissolved Oxygen
- **FCR** - Feed Conversion Ratio
- **FDA** - Food and Drug Administration
- **GH** - General Hardness
- **GPH** - Gallons Per Hour
- **GPM** - Gallons Per Minute
- **HACCP** - Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points
- **KH** - Carbonate Hardness (Alkalinity)
- **LPM** - Liters Per Minute
- **NFT** - Nutrient Film Technique
- **ppm** - Parts Per Million
- **ppt** - Parts Per Thousand
- **RAS** - Recirculating Aquaculture System
- **SGR** - Specific Growth Rate
- **TAN** - Total Ammonia Nitrogen
