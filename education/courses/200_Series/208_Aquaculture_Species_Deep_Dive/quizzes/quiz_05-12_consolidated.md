10. Temperature effect on feeding? **Higher temp = more frequent (to optimal)** ✓
11. Overfeeding consequence? **Poor water quality, wasted money** ✓
12. Alternative feeds are? **Supplements, not complete replacement** ✓
13. Feeding frequency for juveniles? **2-4x daily** ✓
14. Why cheap feed can be expensive? **Poor FCR, slower growth** ✓
15. Most critical macronutrient? **Protein** ✓

## Quiz 10: Health Management (15 questions)

1. #1 cause of fish disease? **Poor water quality** ✓
2. Quarantine duration? **30 days minimum** ✓
3. Ich life cycle duration? **3-7 days** ✓
4. Ich treatment? **Salt + heat, formalin, or copper** ✓
5. Columnaris symptoms? **White/gray patches, cotton-like** ✓
6. Streptococcus symptoms? **Erratic swimming, spiraling, bulging eyes** ✓
7. Prevention vs treatment cost? **Prevention 100x cheaper** ✓
8. FDA-approved treatments include? **Salt, formalin, hydrogen peroxide, certain antibiotics** ✓
9. Withdrawal period for oxytetracycline? **21 days** ✓
10. When to cull vs treat? **Advanced disease, low-value fish, no approved treatment** ✓
11. Daily health check best time? **Feeding time** ✓
12. Necropsy purpose? **Learn from mortality, identify cause** ✓
13. Healthy gill color? **Red/pink** ✓
14. Biosecurity means? **Preventing disease introduction** ✓
15. Using illegal drugs on food fish? **Federal offense, serious consequences** ✓

12. Marketing message focus? **Sustainability, local, traceable** ✓
