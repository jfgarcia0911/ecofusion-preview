# Quiz 3: Channel Catfish

## Questions

1. **What is the main advantage of catfish over tilapia in variable climates?** - A) Faster growth B) Better flavor C) Cold tolerance (survives to 55°F) ✓ D) Higher price

2. **Catfish require what type of feed pellets?** - A) Floating B) Sinking or slow-sink ✓ C) Powdered D) Live food only

3. **What is the typical FCR for channel catfish?** - A) 1.0-1.2 B) 1.5-1.8 ✓ C) 2.0-2.5 D) 2.5-3.0

4. **Why are catfish considered hardy?** - A) Tolerate low DO (>2 mg/L survives) ✓ B) Never get sick C) Don't need filtration D) Eat anything

5. **At what time of day should you feed catfish?** - A) Early morning B) Midday C) Evening (nocturnal feeders) ✓ D) Doesn't matter

6. **How long does it take catfish to reach 1 lb market size?** - A) 4-6 months B) 6-9 months C) 8-12 months ✓ D) 12-18 months

7. **What physical feature helps catfish find food?** - A) Large eyes B) Barbels (whiskers) ✓ C) Lateral line D) Sharp teeth

8. **Why don't catfish overpopulate aquaponics systems like tilapia?** - A) They're sterile B) Require nesting cavities to spawn ✓ C) Only breed in saltwater D) Die after spawning

9. **Catfish can survive DO levels as low as:** - A) 1-2 mg/L ✓ B) 3-4 mg/L C) 5-6 mg/L D) 7-8 mg/L

10. **What is the optimal temperature range for catfish growth?** - A) 60-70°F B) 70-75°F C) 75-85°F ✓ D) 85-95°F

11. **In which US region is catfish most popular?** - A) Northeast B) West Coast C) Southeast ✓ D) Midwest

12. **What protein level is recommended for catfish grow-out?** - A) 20-25% B) 28-32% ✓ C) 40-45% D) 50%+
