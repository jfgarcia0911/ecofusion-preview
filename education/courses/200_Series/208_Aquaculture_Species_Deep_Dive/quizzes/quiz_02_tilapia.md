## Questions

### Question 1
**At what temperature do tilapia DIE?**

A) Below 60°F
B) Below 55°F
C) Below 50°F ✓
D) Below 45°F

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Tilapia cannot survive below 50°F. This is a hard limit - death occurs within hours at these temperatures.*

---

### Question 2
**What is the optimal temperature range for tilapia growth?**

A) 70-75°F
B) 75-82°F ✓
C) 82-90°F
D) 65-70°F

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia grow best at 75-82°F (78-82°F is ideal). Growth slows below 70°F and above 85°F.*

---

### Question 3
**Which tilapia species is the MOST cold-tolerant?**

A) Nile tilapia
B) Mozambique tilapia
C) Blue tilapia ✓
D) Red hybrid

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Blue tilapia (Oreochromis aureus) can tolerate temperatures down to 50°F better than other species, though they still cannot survive prolonged cold.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the typical Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) for tilapia?**

A) 1.0-1.2
B) 1.4-1.6 ✓
C) 1.8-2.0
D) 2.2-2.5

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia have an FCR of 1.4-1.6, meaning 1.4-1.6 lbs of feed produces 1 lb of fish weight gain.*

---

### Question 5
**How do you identify a male tilapia?**

A) Larger size than females
B) Brighter colors
C) Pointed genital papilla ✓
D) Longer fins

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Male tilapia have a pointed genital papilla with one opening, while females have a rounded papilla with three openings.*

---

### Question 6
**Why is population control critical in tilapia aquaponics?**

A) They don't breed in captivity
B) They breed too readily, leading to overcrowding and stunting ✓
C) Males fight and kill each other
D) Breeding spreads disease

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia breed every 4-6 weeks, producing hundreds of offspring. Without control, overcrowding leads to stunted growth and unmarketable fish.*

---

### Question 7
**What is the best solution for tilapia population control?**

A) Add predator fish
B) Hand-pick fry weekly
C) Use all-male fingerlings ✓
D) Separate breeding areas

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Purchasing all-male (YY "super male") fingerlings is the most effective solution, with 95-100% effectiveness and low labor.*

---

### Question 8
**How long does it take tilapia to reach market size (1 lb)?**

A) 3-4 months
B) 6-9 months ✓
C) 12-15 months
D) 18-24 months

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Under optimal conditions (78-82°F, good nutrition), tilapia reach 1 lb in 6-9 months from fingerling size.*

---

### Question 9
**What should you do 24-48 hours before harvesting tilapia?**

A) Increase feeding
B) Stop feeding (purge gut contents) ✓
C) Add salt to water
D) Lower water temperature

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Stopping feeding 24-48 hours before harvest purges gut contents, improving meat quality and reducing processing mess.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the recommended protein level for tilapia grow-out feed?**

A) 20-25%
B) 28-32% ✓
C) 40-45%
D) 50-55%

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia grow-out feed should contain 28-32% protein. Higher protein is needed for fry (45-50%); lower protein can be used for adults.*

---

### Question 11
**At what life stage do tilapia typically reach sexual maturity?**

A) 1-2 months
B) 3-4 months ✓
C) 8-9 months
D) 12+ months

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia can start breeding at just 3-4 months of age, well before they reach market size, which is why population control is so critical.*

---

### Question 12
**What is the maximum safe stocking density for backyard tilapia systems?**

A) 0.25 lb/gallon
B) 0.5 lb/gallon ✓
C) 1.0 lb/gallon
D) 1.5 lb/gallon

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: For backyard/hobby systems, 0.5 lb/gal is a safe maximum. Commercial systems with excellent management can go to 1.0 lb/gal.*

---

### Question 13
**Which disease is tilapia MOST susceptible to at high temperatures (>85°F)?**

A) Ich
B) Columnaris
C) Streptococcus ✓
D) Whirling disease

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Streptococcus infections become more common in tilapia when water temperatures exceed 85°F, especially in combination with high density and stress.*

---

### Question 14
**What is the purpose of using Red Hybrid tilapia instead of standard Nile tilapia?**

A) Faster growth
B) Better disease resistance
C) Visual appeal for retail sales ✓
D) Cold tolerance

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Red Hybrid tilapia are bred for their attractive red/pink color which appeals to retail customers. Their growth and hardiness are similar to Nile tilapia.*

---

### Question 15
**How often should tilapia be fed during the grow-out stage?**

A) Once daily
B) 2-3 times daily ✓
C) 4-6 times daily
D) Free choice (continuous)

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Grow-out tilapia should be fed 2-3 times daily at 2-3% body weight total. Fry need more frequent feeding (4-6x daily).*
