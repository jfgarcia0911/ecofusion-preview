## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the single most critical factor in species selection that cannot be easily modified?**

A) Market demand in your area

B) Temperature requirements of the species ✓

C) Feed availability

D) Fingerling cost

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Temperature requirements are non-negotiable. You can find new markets, change feed brands, or source different fingerlings, but you cannot easily or affordably change your climate or maintain extreme temperatures year-round.*

---

### Question 2
**Which species has the best (lowest) Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)?**

A) Tilapia (1.4-1.6)

B) Channel catfish (1.5-1.8)

C) Rainbow trout (1.1-1.3) ✓

D) Yellow perch (1.5-2.0)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Rainbow trout have the best FCR at 1.1-1.3, meaning they convert feed to body weight most efficiently. This partially offsets their higher feed costs.*

---

### Question 3
**Why is tilapia prohibited or restricted in many northern US states?**

A) They are too expensive to raise

B) They compete with native fish species in the wild ✓

C) They require too much heating

D) They have poor market demand

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Tilapia are considered invasive species risks in warm climates. Northern states restrict them to prevent accidental or intentional release into waterways where they could outcompete native species.*

---

### Question 4
**Which factor should receive the HIGHEST weight in the species selection scorecard?**

A) Growth rate (×2)

B) Legal in your state (×5) ✓

C) Market demand (×3)

D) Feed availability (×1)

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Legal status is weighted ×5 because it's a go/no-go factor. You simply cannot raise illegal species regardless of how profitable they might be.*

---

### Question 5
**What is a beginner-friendly characteristic for a first aquaponics species?**

A) Requires precise water quality management

B) Tolerates water quality fluctuations ✓

C) Needs specialized expensive feed

D) Requires controlled breeding conditions

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Beginners should choose hardy species that tolerate less-than-perfect conditions (like tilapia or catfish) while they develop their skills.*

---

### Question 6
**Which of the following represents a coldwater species?**

A) Tilapia (75-85°F optimal)

B) Barramundi (80-86°F optimal)

C) Rainbow trout (55-65°F optimal) ✓

D) Channel catfish (75-85°F optimal)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Rainbow trout are coldwater species requiring temperatures below 65°F for optimal growth. The other species are all warmwater.*

---

### Question 7
**What does FCR stand for and why is it important?**

A) Fish Condition Rating - measures health

B) Feed Conversion Ratio - measures feed efficiency ✓

C) Financial Cost Ratio - measures profitability

D) Facility Compliance Rating - regulatory measure

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) measures how efficiently fish convert feed into body weight. Lower FCR means less feed needed per pound of fish, reducing your biggest operating cost.*

---

### Question 8
**In polyculture, why are tilapia and prawns considered compatible?**

A) They eat the same food

B) They require identical temperatures

C) They occupy different niches (water column vs. bottom) ✓

D) They breed at the same time

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Tilapia swim and feed in the water column while prawns live on the bottom. This niche separation means they don't compete for space or food.*

---

### Question 9
**Which market channel typically offers the LOWEST price per pound?**

A) Direct to consumer at farmers markets

B) Restaurants (wholesale) ✓

C) Online retail sales

D) Farm-direct sales

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Wholesale to restaurants offers lower prices than retail channels (though still potentially profitable). Direct-to-consumer channels (farmers markets, online, farm-direct) command highest prices.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the primary advantage of ornamental fish over food fish?**

A) Easier to raise

B) Higher profit per fish ✓

C) Faster growth rates

D) Lower initial investment

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Ornamental fish (koi, fancy goldfish) can sell for $10-$1000+ per fish compared to $6-18/lb for food fish, offering much higher profit potential per individual.*

---

### Question 11
**Before selecting a species, what research step is MOST critical?**

A) Calculating projected feed costs

B) Verifying species is legal in your state ✓

C) Finding fingerling suppliers

D) Designing your system

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Legal verification must come first. All other planning is wasted if the species is prohibited in your location. Always check state and local regulations before proceeding.*

---

### Question 12
**Which statement about species selection is TRUE?**

A) Choose species you personally like to eat

B) Always select the fastest-growing species

C) Match species to your climate and system first ✓

D) Price is the only factor that matters

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Successful species selection starts with environmental match (temperature, legal status, system compatibility) before considering personal preferences or single factors like price.*
