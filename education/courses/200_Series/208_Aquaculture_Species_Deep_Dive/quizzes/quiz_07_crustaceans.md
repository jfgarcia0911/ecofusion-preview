## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the optimal temperature range for freshwater prawns?**

A) 70-75°F (too cool)

B) 75-80°F (acceptable but suboptimal)

C) 82-86°F ✓

D) 88-92°F (too warm)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Freshwater prawns are tropical species requiring 82-86°F for optimal growth. This is warmer than tilapia (78-82°F) and much warmer than trout. Temperatures below 75°F result in slow growth; below 70°F prawns essentially stop growing.*

---

### Question 2
**Prawn stocking density should be based on:**

A) Water volume in gallons

B) Bottom area in square feet ✓

C) Total system size

D) Filtration capacity only

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Unlike fish (stocked per gallon), prawns are stocked based on BOTTOM AREA (square feet or square meters) because they live and feed on the bottom. Typical density is 1-2 prawns per square foot in commercial systems.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the most critical requirement to reduce prawn cannibalism?**

A) High protein feed

B) Frequent feeding

C) Hiding structures ✓

D) Low stocking density only

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Hiding structures (PVC pipes, shade cloth, netting, rocks) are absolutely critical. Prawns are vulnerable for 24-48 hours after molting and will be cannibalized without hiding places. This is non-negotiable for successful prawn production.*

---

### Question 4
**How often do prawns typically molt?**

A) Once per week

B) Every 2-4 weeks ✓

C) Once per month

D) Every 3 months

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Prawns molt every 2-4 weeks to grow, shedding their hard exoskeleton. During and immediately after molting, they're soft and vulnerable to cannibalism. This is why hiding structures and adequate calcium in water are essential.*

---

### Question 5
**What special water condition do prawn LARVAE (not adults) require?**

A) Very cold water

B) Acidic water (low pH)

C) Brackish water (12-16 ppt salinity) ✓

D) Very high oxygen

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Prawn larvae require brackish (slightly salty) water at 12-16 ppt salinity for 30-40 days before transitioning to freshwater. This makes breeding complex and why most producers buy post-larvae rather than breeding their own.*

---

### Question 6
**What is the optimal temperature range for red claw crayfish?**

A) 60-70°F (too cold)

B) 70-85°F ✓

C) 80-90°F (too warm)

D) 85-95°F (dangerous)

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Red claw crayfish tolerate 70-85°F, a wider range than prawns. This makes them more forgiving and suitable for operations without precise temperature control. They're generally easier than prawns for beginners.*

---

### Question 7
**What is the main challenge with crustacean production?**

A) Expensive fingerlings

B) High feed costs

C) Cannibalism during molting ✓

D) Disease outbreaks

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Cannibalism during molting is the primary challenge. Without proper hiding structures and management, losses can exceed 50%. This is why adequate structure (1 hide per 2-3 individuals) and proper density are critical.*

---

### Question 8
**Can prawns be successfully raised with tilapia in polyculture?**

A) No, incompatible species

B) No, different temperature needs

C) Yes, they occupy different niches ✓

D) Only in very large systems

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Yes! Prawns (bottom dwellers) and tilapia (water column) occupy different niches and don't compete. Both like warm water (80-85°F), making them compatible. Prawns also clean up waste feed, improving system efficiency.*

---

### Question 9
**What is the typical market price for fresh prawns?**

A) $6-8/lb (too low)

B) $8-12/lb (moderate)

C) $12-20/lb ✓

D) $20-30/lb (higher than typical)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Freshwater prawns command premium prices of $12-20/lb, marketed as "local sustainable shrimp." This premium pricing helps offset higher production costs and longer grow-out times compared to fish.*

---

### Question 10
**Why is calcium important for crustaceans?**

A) Improves flavor

B) Shell and exoskeleton formation ✓

C) Increases growth rate only

D) Prevents disease

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Calcium is essential for forming and hardening the exoskeleton after molting. Water hardness should be 50-150 mg/L minimum. Low calcium results in soft shells, failed molts, and deformities. Add cuttlebone or calcium supplements if needed.*
