## Course Structure

### Lessons (12 modules)

All lessons include ASCII diagrams, tables, species profiles, and practical guidance for aquaponics operators.

1. **module_01_species_selection.md** - Species selection criteria, decision matrices, legal considerations
2. **module_02_tilapia.md** - Tilapia production, varieties, population control, husbandry
3. **module_03_catfish.md** - Channel catfish production, cold tolerance, feeding management
4. **module_04_trout.md** - Rainbow trout, coldwater requirements, chilling systems, economics
5. **module_05_barramundi.md** - Barramundi production, premium warmwater species, FCR optimization
6. **module_06_ornamental_fish.md** - Goldfish, koi, ornamental markets, breeding/grading
7. **module_07_crustaceans.md** - Freshwater prawns, crayfish, polyculture strategies
8. **module_08_feeding_nutrition.md** - Feed formulation, FCR optimization, feeding schedules
9. **module_09_breeding_basics.md** - Fish reproduction, fry rearing, selective breeding
10. **module_10_health_management.md** - Disease prevention, diagnosis, treatment protocols
11. **module_11_market_considerations.md** - Market research, pricing, sales channels, CSA models
12. **module_12_species_project.md** - Capstone project, production planning, financial analysis

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:

1. **Evaluate and select** appropriate species based on climate, regulations, and market factors
2. **Implement** species-specific husbandry protocols for 8+ aquaculture species
3. **Calculate and optimize** Feed Conversion Ratios and feeding schedules
4. **Manage** fish health through prevention, diagnosis, and approved treatments
5. **Design** breeding programs and manage fry rearing
6. **Develop** market strategies and pricing for aquaculture products
7. **Create** comprehensive production plans with financial projections

## How to Use This Course

### For Students:

1. **Start with Lessons** - Read modules 1-12 in order
2. **Take Quizzes** - Complete each module quiz (80% to pass)

4. **Complete Activities** - Hands-on practice with calculations and selection
5. **Study Handouts** - Visual references for species identification
6. **Take Final Exam** - Comprehensive assessment (open-book)

1. **Lesson Plans** - Each module is 45-90 minutes of content

3. **Reference Materials** - Cheatsheets can be distributed as study aids
4. **Grading Rubrics** - Provided for activities and project
