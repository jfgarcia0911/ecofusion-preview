# Educational Activity Cards
## Course 214: Community & School Garden Programs

**50+ Quick Reference Garden Activities**

---

## How to Use These Cards

- Each activity includes: age range, duration, materials, steps, and learning connections
- Mix and match activities for your curriculum
- Adapt to your local plants, seasons, and context
- Use as starting points for full lesson development

---

## OBSERVATION & SCIENCE ACTIVITIES

### 1. Plant Part Scavenger Hunt
**Ages:** 5-10 | **Duration:** 20 min | **Season:** Any

**Materials:** Collection bags, magnifying glasses

**Activity:**
1. Students search for examples of plant parts (root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit, seed)
2. Collect one example of each (if possible without harming plant)
3. Sort and compare findings
4. Discuss functions of each part

**Learning:** Plant anatomy, classification, observation skills

---

### 2. Growth Measurement Study
**Ages:** 6-18 | **Duration:** 5 min daily for 4-8 weeks

**Materials:** Rulers, data sheets, pencils

**Activity:**
1. Students measure plant height weekly
2. Record data in journals or spreadsheets
3. Create graphs of growth over time
4. Analyze patterns and make predictions

**Learning:** Measurement, data collection, graphing, plant growth rates

---

### 3. Soil Texture Test
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Soil samples, water, jars, hand lenses

**Activity:**
1. Collect soil from different locations
2. Jar test: Add soil and water, shake, let settle, observe layers
3. Ribbon test: Wet soil, form ribbon between fingers
4. Compare texture (sand, silt, clay percentages)

**Learning:** Soil science, observation, classification

---

### 4. Decomposition Observation
**Ages:** 8-14 | **Duration:** 15 min weekly for 4-6 weeks

**Materials:** Clear container, organic materials, hand lenses

**Activity:**
1. Layer organic materials in clear container
2. Observe and sketch weekly
3. Identify decomposers (if visible)
4. Measure volume reduction
5. Use finished compost in garden

**Learning:** Decomposition, nutrient cycling, decomposers

---

### 5. Germination Race
**Ages:** 5-12 | **Duration:** 20 min setup, 2-3 weeks observation

**Materials:** Fast-sprouting seeds (radish, bean), planting medium, containers

**Activity:**
1. Plant multiple types of seeds
2. Observe and record germination timing
3. Graph results: which sprouted first?
4. Discuss seed size, plant type, conditions

**Learning:** Germination, data collection, plant life cycles

---

## ECOSYSTEMS & ENVIRONMENT

### 6. Beneficial Insect Hunt
**Ages:** 5-12 | **Duration:** 20 min

**Materials:** Insect identification guides, magnifying glasses, journals

**Activity:**
1. Search for insects in garden
2. Observe without harming
3. Identify beneficial vs. pest insects
4. Discuss role in ecosystem
5. Count different species found

**Learning:** Ecosystems, biodiversity, beneficial insects

---

### 7. Food Web Creation
**Ages:** 9-14 | **Duration:** 45 min

**Materials:** String or yarn, organism cards, large space

**Activity:**
1. Identify organisms in garden (plants, insects, birds, decomposers)
2. Students each represent an organism
3. Use string to show "who eats whom"
4. Observe web complexity
5. Remove one organism - what happens?

**Learning:** Food webs, interdependence, ecosystem balance

---

### 8. Pollinator Watch
**Ages:** 6-14 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Observation sheets, stopwatch, pencils

**Activity:**
1. Select flowering plants to observe
2. Count pollinators visiting in 10-minute periods
3. Identify pollinator types (bees, butterflies, etc.)
4. Compare which flowers attract most
5. Discuss pollinator importance

**Learning:** Pollination, plant reproduction, pollinators, data collection

---

### 9. Water Cycle Demonstration
**Ages:** 7-12 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Clear container with lid, soil, small plant, water

**Activity:**
1. Create mini terrarium in sealed container
2. Water plant and seal
3. Observe condensation, precipitation cycle
4. Discuss evaporation, transpiration, condensation
5. Connect to larger water cycle

**Learning:** Water cycle, plant transpiration, weather

---

### 10. Carbon Sequestration Study
**Ages:** 12-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Scales, plant biomass, data sheets

**Activity:**
1. Weigh seedlings at planting
2. Harvest mature plants and weigh
3. Calculate biomass increase
4. Research carbon content of plant matter
5. Calculate carbon sequestered

**Learning:** Photosynthesis, climate change, plant growth, measurement

---

## MATH & MEASUREMENT

### 11. Garden Bed Area Calculation
**Ages:** 8-14 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Measuring tapes, calculators, graph paper

**Activity:**
1. Measure garden bed dimensions
2. Calculate area (length × width)
3. Calculate perimeter
4. Design bed layout on graph paper
5. Determine plant spacing and quantity needed

**Learning:** Area, perimeter, multiplication, planning

---

### 12. Harvest Weighing & Graphing
**Ages:** 6-14 | **Duration:** 20 min per harvest

**Materials:** Scale, data sheets, graph paper

**Activity:**
1. Weigh harvest from each bed/crop
2. Record data over season
3. Create bar graphs comparing crops
4. Calculate totals
5. Compare to store prices

**Learning:** Measurement, data recording, graphing, addition

---

### 13. Plant Spacing Calculations
**Ages:** 9-14 | **Duration:** 40 min

**Materials:** Measuring tools, plant spacing guides, calculators

**Activity:**
1. Research spacing needs for crops
2. Calculate how many plants fit in bed
3. Use division and area concepts
4. Create planting map to scale
5. Plant according to plan

**Learning:** Division, area, planning, following instructions

---

### 14. Seed Germination Percentages
**Ages:** 10-18 | **Duration:** Setup 20 min, 2 weeks observation

**Materials:** Seeds, planting medium, data sheets

**Activity:**
1. Plant 20-50 seeds
2. Count how many germinate
3. Calculate germination percentage
4. Compare seed varieties or age
5. Graph results

**Learning:** Percentages, data collection, seed viability

---

### 15. Recipe Ratio Scaling
**Ages:** 10-18 | **Duration:** 40 min

**Materials:** Recipe, harvest, cooking equipment, calculators

**Activity:**
1. Start with recipe for 4 servings
2. Scale up for class size (e.g., 24)
3. Calculate ingredient quantities needed
4. Measure and prepare
5. Discuss ratios and proportions

**Learning:** Ratios, multiplication, measurement, fractions

---

## LITERACY & LANGUAGE ARTS

### 16. Garden Journals
**Ages:** 6-18 | **Duration:** 10-15 min per session

**Materials:** Notebooks, pencils, colored pencils

**Activity:**
1. Weekly observation entries
2. Drawings with labels
3. Descriptive writing
4. Questions and wonderings
5. Reflections on experiences

**Learning:** Writing, observation, descriptive language, reflection

---

### 17. Plant "Biography"
**Ages:** 8-14 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Research materials, writing supplies

**Activity:**
1. Choose a plant to follow from seed to harvest
2. Research plant origins and history
3. Document plant's "life story"
4. Write narrative from plant's perspective
5. Present to class

**Learning:** Research, narrative writing, plant life cycles

---

### 18. Garden Poetry
**Ages:** 7-18 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Poetry examples, writing supplies

**Activity:**
1. Spend time observing in garden
2. Collect sensory observations
3. Write haiku, acrostic, or free verse
4. Focus on imagery and descriptive language
5. Share and publish

**Learning:** Poetry, imagery, descriptive writing, observation

---

### 19. Vegetable Research & Report
**Ages:** 9-14 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Research resources, writing supplies

**Activity:**
1. Choose a crop to research
2. Investigate origins, nutrition, growing needs
3. Create informational poster or report
4. Include fun facts and recipes
5. Present to class

**Learning:** Research skills, informational writing, presentation

---

### 20. Garden Signs & Labels
**Ages:** 6-12 | **Duration:** 40 min

**Materials:** Craft supplies, markers, stakes or plaques

**Activity:**
1. Create signs for garden areas
2. Label plants with common and scientific names
3. Make informational signs about care
4. Design welcome sign for garden
5. Practice clear writing and communication

**Learning:** Writing, plant names, communication, design

---

## SOCIAL STUDIES & CULTURE

### 21. Three Sisters Garden
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Corn, bean, squash seeds, research materials

**Activity:**
1. Research indigenous agricultural practices
2. Learn traditional stories
3. Plant corn, beans, squash together
4. Observe companion planting benefits
5. Prepare and taste traditional dish

**Learning:** Indigenous agriculture, cultural traditions, companion planting

---

### 22. Global Food Origins Map
**Ages:** 9-14 | **Duration:** 45 min

**Materials:** World map, research materials, pushpins or stickers

**Activity:**
1. Research origins of crops in garden
2. Mark origin locations on world map
3. Discuss plant migration and trade
4. Connect to social studies curriculum
5. Taste and compare global foods

**Learning:** Geography, agriculture history, global connections

---

### 23. Cultural Food Traditions
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Seeds from diverse cultures, research materials, cooking supplies

**Activity:**
1. Students research family food traditions
2. Grow culturally significant crops
3. Share stories and traditions
4. Prepare traditional dishes
5. Celebrate diversity

**Learning:** Cultural awareness, family history, diversity appreciation

---

### 24. Farm to Table Timeline
**Ages:** 9-14 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Research materials, timeline materials

**Activity:**
1. Trace food item from seed to plate
2. Identify all steps and people involved
3. Create visual timeline
4. Discuss local vs. global food systems
5. Calculate food miles

**Learning:** Food systems, timelines, economic connections

---

### 25. Garden Economy
**Ages:** 11-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Production data, pricing research, calculators

**Activity:**
1. Track costs (seeds, soil, water, labor)
2. Calculate harvest value (market prices)
3. Determine profit/loss
4. Discuss economic concepts
5. Make business decisions

**Learning:** Economics, mathematics, business concepts

---

## ARTS INTEGRATION

### 26. Nature Prints
**Ages:** 5-12 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Paint, paper, leaves and flowers

**Activity:**
1. Collect leaves and flowers
2. Paint one side
3. Press onto paper
4. Create patterns and designs
5. Identify plants used

**Learning:** Art techniques, observation, plant identification

---

### 27. Garden Mandala
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** 45 min

**Materials:** Natural materials from garden, flat surface

**Activity:**
1. Collect diverse natural items (flowers, leaves, seeds, stones)
2. Create circular mandala design
3. Focus on symmetry and patterns
4. Photography to preserve
5. Return materials to garden

**Learning:** Art, patterns, symmetry, mindfulness

---

### 28. Plant Dyes
**Ages:** 10-18 | **Duration:** 60 min

**Materials:** Plants (onion skins, beets, marigolds), fabric, pots

**Activity:**
1. Research natural dye plants
2. Prepare dye baths
3. Dye fabric or yarn
4. Observe color changes
5. Discuss plant pigments and chemistry

**Learning:** Chemistry, art, color theory, plant properties

---

### 29. Garden Photography
**Ages:** 10-18 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Cameras or devices, editing software (optional)

**Activity:**
1. Practice photography techniques (macro, angles, lighting)
2. Document garden through seasons
3. Focus on details, patterns, colors
4. Create photo essay or display
5. Discuss visual storytelling

**Learning:** Photography, observation, visual communication

---

### 30. Scarecrow Building
**Ages:** 6-14 | **Duration:** 60 min

**Materials:** Old clothes, straw or newspaper, pole, creative supplies

**Activity:**
1. Design scarecrow character
2. Stuff and assemble
3. Install in garden
4. Write story or biography for scarecrow
5. Monitor effectiveness (fun vs. actual function)

**Learning:** Creative design, problem-solving, writing

---

## NUTRITION & COOKING

### 31. Rainbow Taste Test
**Ages:** 5-12 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Vegetables of each color, cutting board, knife, plates

**Activity:**
1. Harvest or display produce by color
2. Discuss nutrients in each color
3. Taste samples of each color
4. Rate favorites
5. Discuss "eating the rainbow"

**Learning:** Nutrition, colors, willingness to try new foods

---

### 32. Garden Salsa
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** 45 min

**Materials:** Tomatoes, peppers, onions, cilantro, lime, bowls, tortilla chips

**Activity:**
1. Harvest ingredients
2. Wash and prepare safely
3. Chop and mix (age-appropriate knife skills)
4. Taste and adjust seasonings
5. Share and enjoy with chips

**Learning:** Food preparation, kitchen safety, measurement, taste

---

### 33. Herb Taste Comparison
**Ages:** 7-14 | **Duration:** 20 min

**Materials:** Variety of fresh herbs, tasting cups, water

**Activity:**
1. Smell each herb
2. Taste small amounts
3. Describe flavors (vocabulary building)
4. Match to common uses (basil with tomato, mint with dessert)
5. Create herb preference chart

**Learning:** Sensory awareness, vocabulary, culinary uses

---

### 34. Smoothie Science
**Ages:** 8-14 | **Duration:** 30 min

**Materials:** Blender, greens, fruits, liquids, measuring cups

**Activity:**
1. Discuss nutrition in greens
2. Create smoothie recipes
3. Measure ingredients
4. Blend and taste
5. Evaluate color, texture, flavor balance

**Learning:** Nutrition, measurement, experimentation, blending skills

---

### 35. Sun Tea Brewing
**Ages:** 7-14 | **Duration:** 10 min prep, 3-4 hours sun time

**Materials:** Clear jar, water, fresh herbs (mint, lemon balm), sun

**Activity:**
1. Harvest fresh herbs
2. Add to jar with water
3. Set in sun for 3-4 hours
4. Strain and serve over ice
5. Discuss solar energy and infusion

**Learning:** Solar energy, infusion process, herbal properties

---

## SEASON EXTENSION & SPECIAL PROJECTS

### 36. Seed Starting
**Ages:** 6-18 | **Duration:** 30 min setup, weeks of observation

**Materials:** Seed trays, soil mix, seeds, labels, grow lights (optional)

**Activity:**
1. Prepare seed starting mix
2. Plant seeds at proper depth
3. Label clearly
4. Care for seedlings
5. Transplant when ready

**Learning:** Plant propagation, care, patience, planning

---

### 37. Garden Succession Planting
**Ages:** 11-18 | **Duration:** Planning 30 min, implementation ongoing

**Materials:** Planting calendar, seeds, garden space

**Activity:**
1. Research crop maturation times
2. Plan successive plantings
3. Plant in waves (every 2-3 weeks)
4. Track harvest timing
5. Evaluate continuous production

**Learning:** Planning, time management, production systems

---

### 38. Cold Frame Construction
**Ages:** 12-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Lumber, old window or clear plastic, tools, plans

**Activity:**
1. Research cold frame design
2. Measure and cut materials
3. Assemble structure
4. Install in garden
5. Monitor temperature and plants

**Learning:** Construction, season extension, temperature monitoring

---

### 39. Rainwater Harvesting System
**Ages:** 11-18 | **Duration:** Multiple sessions

**Materials:** Rain barrel, downspout connector, stand, tools

**Activity:**
1. Calculate roof catchment area
2. Install rain barrel
3. Connect to downspout
4. Monitor water collection
5. Use for garden irrigation

**Learning:** Water conservation, volume calculation, systems thinking

---

### 40. Vermicomposting
**Ages:** 8-18 | **Duration:** 20 min setup, ongoing observation

**Materials:** Bin, bedding, red wiggler worms, food scraps

**Activity:**
1. Set up worm bin with bedding
2. Add worms
3. Feed appropriate scraps
4. Monitor moisture and temperature
5. Harvest vermicompost for garden

**Learning:** Decomposition, worm biology, waste reduction
