# Garden Layout Templates
## Course 214: Community & School Garden Programs

---

## Using These Templates

**Purpose:** Design your educational garden using these templates and principles

**How to Use:**
1. Assess your available space (measure dimensions)
2. Choose template that best fits your needs
3. Adapt based on your context
4. Create scaled drawing on graph paper
5. Calculate materials needed

**Scale:** Use 1 square = 1 foot on graph paper

**Best For:** Single classroom, 20-30 students, limited space

**Dimensions:** 20' x 20'

## Design Considerations Checklist

### Site Assessment
- [ ] Full sun exposure (6+ hours minimum)
- [ ] Level ground or terracing plan
- [ ] Water access within 100'
- [ ] Drainage adequate (not swampy)
- [ ] Soil quality tested
- [ ] Away from toxic contamination
- [ ] Visible and accessible
- [ ] Secure (fencing if needed)

### Accessibility
- [ ] Pathways minimum 3-4' wide (5' ideal)
- [ ] Firm, stable pathway surfaces
- [ ] Some beds at 24-30" height
- [ ] Knee clearance under taller beds
- [ ] Accessible water sources
- [ ] Shade structure for breaks
- [ ] Seating areas
- [ ] Clear signage

### Educational Function
- [ ] Gathering/teaching space
- [ ] Demonstration area visible to groups
- [ ] Variety of learning zones
- [ ] Safe for age group
- [ ] Storage for materials
- [ ] Weather protection option

### Sustainability
- [ ] Right-sized for maintenance capacity
- [ ] Water conservation features
- [ ] Compost system
- [ ] Tool storage and organization
- [ ] Durable materials
- [ ] Seasonal interest
- [ ] Phased development plan

## Scaling Your Design

**To create custom scaled drawing:**

1. **Measure your space** accurately (length × width)

2. **Choose scale:** Common scales:
   - 1 square = 1 foot (detailed)
   - 1 square = 2 feet (medium)
   - 1/4" = 1 foot (architectural)

3. **Draw to scale on graph paper**
   - Outline total area
   - Mark existing features (buildings, trees, utilities)
   - Add raised beds to scale
   - Draw pathways
   - Include special zones
   - Note dimensions

4. **Calculate areas:**
   - Growing beds: Length × Width per bed
   - Pathways: Total pathway length × width
   - Special areas: Measure each zone

5. **Estimate materials:**
   - Raised beds: Calculate lumber, hardware
   - Soil: Bed area × depth ÷ 12 = cubic feet needed
   - Pathways: Area × depth of material
   - Fencing: Perimeter measurement
