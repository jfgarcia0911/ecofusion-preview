| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 12 |

## Required Components

### 1. Program Overview (5 pts)
- Mission and vision
- Target audience
- Program goals
- Expected outcomes

### 2. Garden Design Plan (6 pts)
- Site layout and features
- Infrastructure needs
- Plant selection
- Accessibility

### 3. Educational Programming (8 pts)
- Curriculum outline
- Age-appropriate activities
- Integration with school standards
- Assessment methods

### 4. Operations Plan (6 pts)
- Staffing model
- Volunteer management
- Maintenance schedule
- Budget

### 5. Sustainability Plan (5 pts)
- Funding sources
- Community partnerships
- Leadership succession
- Long-term viability

**Deliverable:** 15-20 page program proposal suitable for grant applications or administrative approval
