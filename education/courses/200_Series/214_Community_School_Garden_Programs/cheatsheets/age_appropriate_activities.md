# Age-Appropriate Garden Activities
## Course 214: Community & School Garden Programs

---

## Ages 3-5: Preschool/Pre-K

### Developmental Focus
- Sensory exploration
- Large motor skills
- 5-10 minute attention span
- Concrete, hands-on experiences
- Wonder and curiosity

### Best Activities

**Sensory Scavenger Hunt** (10 min)
- Find something soft (lamb's ear leaf)
- Find something bumpy (tree bark)
- Find something smooth (river rock)
- Find something that smells good (herb)
- Listen for sounds (wind chimes, bees)

**Big Seed Planting** (15 min)
- Beans, sunflowers, pumpkins
- Push finger in soil, drop seed, cover
- Water gently with small watering can
- Mark with popsicle stick and drawing

**Worm Exploration** (10 min)
- Observe worms in clear container
- Touch gently with soft hands
- Talk about what worms do
- Return to compost bin

**Mud Kitchen Play** (15 min)
- "Cooking" with soil, water, plants
- Making "soup" and "pies"
- Pouring, stirring, mixing
- Sensory exploration

**Berry Tasting** (10 min)
- Harvest strawberries or blueberries
- Wash hands first
- Describe color, texture, taste
- Count berries

**Garden Watering** (10 min)
- Small watering cans they can carry
- Practice gentle pouring
- Talk about plant needs
- Observe water soaking in

## Ages 6-7: Kindergarten - 2nd Grade

### Developmental Focus
- Beginning reading/writing
- Simple observation and description
- 15-20 minute activities
- Care and responsibility
- Social skills development

### Best Activities

**Plant Life Cycle Journal** (20 min)
- Draw plant at different stages
- Label parts (with help)
- Measure with non-standard units
- Date observations

**Rainbow Harvest** (20 min)
- Collect vegetables/fruits of each color
- Sort by color
- Count and compare quantities
- Create rainbow display

**Seed Dissection** (15 min)
- Open bean seeds that have soaked overnight
- Find baby plant inside
- Draw what they observe
- Plant some to grow

**Garden Measurement** (20 min)
- Measure plant height with hands or blocks
- "My plant is 5 hands tall!"
- Create class graph of heights
- Observe which grew most

**Beneficial Bug Hunt** (15 min)
- Search for helpful insects (ladybugs, bees)
- Gentle observation
- Draw insects seen
- Discuss how they help

**Planting Partners** (20 min)
- Work in pairs to plant seeds
- One digs, one plants, one waters, both label
- Practice taking turns
- Cooperation and communication

**Garden ABC** (20 min)
- Find plants or items for each letter
- A = Apple, B = Bee, C = Carrot
- Draw or write in journal
- Literacy integration

## Ages 8-10: 3rd - 5th Grade

### Developmental Focus
- Scientific thinking emerging
- Reading and writing independently
- 30-45 minute activities
- Interest in how things work
- Group collaboration

### Best Activities

**Plant Growth Experiment** (45 min setup + ongoing)
- Design experiment (vary light, water, or soil)
- Write hypothesis
- Collect data weekly (measurements, observations)
- Graph results
- Draw conclusions

**Compost Investigation** (30 min)
- Layer demonstration (green/brown)
- Temperature monitoring
- Decomposition observation
- Before/after comparisons
- Discussion of nutrient cycling

**Garden Math** (40 min)
- Calculate raised bed area (length × width)
- Determine plant spacing needs
- Count harvest and weigh crops
- Compare to store prices
- Multiply/divide for recipe scaling

**Ecosystem Mapping** (45 min)
- Identify all organisms in garden
- Create food web diagram
- Show energy flow
- Discuss balance and interdependence
- Predict impacts of changes

**Seed to Table** (40 min)
- Plant, grow, harvest, prepare
- Track days from seed to harvest
- Simple cooking (salad, salsa)
- Taste and describe
- Recipe writing

**Garden Design Challenge** (45 min)
- Given space and requirements
- Plan garden layout on graph paper
- Calculate area and spacing
- Present design to class
- Explain choices

**Weather & Growth Study** (Ongoing)
- Daily weather recording
- Plant growth measurements
- Look for correlations
- Graph temperature and growth
- Predict optimal conditions

## Ages 11-13: 6th - 8th Grade

### Developmental Focus
- Abstract reasoning
- Peer-focused
- Justice and fairness oriented
- Scientific method
- Longer projects

### Best Activities

**Scientific Investigation** (Multiple sessions)
- Student-designed experiment
- Control and variables
- Data collection and analysis
- Written lab report
- Presentation to class

**Food Systems Mapping** (2-3 sessions)
- Trace ingredients from farm to table
- Map local food system
- Compare to global system
- Identify environmental/social impacts
- Propose improvements

**Garden Business Plan** (Multiple sessions)
- Market research (what sells?)
- Production planning
- Cost analysis
- Pricing strategy
- Marketing plan
- Farmers market or farm stand

**Pollinator Research** (2 sessions)
- Research pollinator species
- Identify local pollinators
- Design pollinator garden
- Present findings
- Implement design

**Cooking from Harvest** (40 min)
- Harvest planning
- Recipe selection
- Food preparation
- Nutrition analysis
- Taste testing and evaluation

**Teaching Younger Students** (45 min)
- Plan age-appropriate lesson
- Create materials
- Lead activity with elementary class
- Reflect on experience
- Leadership development

**Environmental Justice Project** (Multiple sessions)
- Research food access issues
- Community food assessment
- Develop action plan
- Implementation (donation, advocacy)
- Reflection on impact

## Ages 14-18: High School

### Developmental Focus
- Complex systems thinking
- Future/career orientation
- Autonomy and leadership
- Real-world application
- Community impact

### Best Activities

**Agricultural Science Research** (Semester project)
- Choose research question
- Literature review
- Design experiment
- Collect and analyze data
- Write formal report
- Conference presentation

**Farm Stand/CSA Operation** (Ongoing)
- Business planning
- Production scheduling
- Customer service
- Financial management
- Marketing and sales
- Profit/loss analysis

**Greenhouse Production** (Semester)
- Crop selection
- Environmental control
- Propagation systems
- Production scheduling
- Harvest and sales
- Business metrics

**Internship with Local Farmer** (Semester/Summer)
- Work-based learning
- Skill development
- Career exploration
- Mentorship
- Portfolio building

**Community Service Project** (Multiple weeks)
- Assess community need
- Develop project plan
- Implementation (food bank garden, senior garden)
- Track impact
- Reflection

**Food Justice Documentary** (Multiple weeks)
- Research food systems issues
- Conduct interviews
- Video production
- Public screening
- Advocacy and action

**Aquaponics/Hydroponics System** (Semester)
- System design
- Build from plans
- Monitor and maintain
- Crop production
- Data analysis
- Optimization

## Multi-Age Activities

### Works for K-12 with adaptation

**Garden Art**
- Younger: Nature collages, plant prints
- Older: Garden design, photography, sculpture

**Seed Starting**
- Younger: Large seeds, simple process
- Older: Multiple varieties, transplanting, propagation

**Harvest Celebration**
- Younger: Tasting, games, exploration
- Older: Cooking, presentations, leadership roles

**Composting**
- Younger: Adding materials, observation
- Older: Managing bins, temperature, chemistry

**Garden Journaling**
- Younger: Drawings with labels
- Older: Scientific observations, data, analysis

## Seasonal Activities Calendar

### Spring
- Seed starting (all ages)
- Soil preparation (ages 8+)
- Planting cool crops (all ages)
- Bird nest observation (ages 5-10)
- Growth measurement (all ages)

### Summer
- Watering and care (all ages)
- Harvest activities (all ages)
- Insect observation (all ages)
- Cooking with harvest (ages 7+)
- Garden business (ages 12+)

### Fall
- Fall planting (all ages)
- Harvest celebrations (all ages)
- Seed saving (ages 8+)
- Composting (all ages)
- Garden cleanup (all ages)

### Winter
- Seed catalog planning (ages 8+)
- Indoor seed starting (all ages)
- Garden design (ages 10+)
- Cooking preserved harvest (ages 7+)
- Reflection and evaluation (ages 10+)

## Safety Reminders by Age

**Ages 3-7:**
- Close adult supervision at all times
- Soft hands with plants and creatures
- No tasting without permission
- Proper hand washing
- Tool use with direct supervision

**Ages 8-12:**
- Review safety rules
- Demonstrate proper tool use
- Check for allergies before tasting
- Sun and hydration awareness
- Know emergency procedures

**Ages 13-18:**
- Greater independence with tools
- Leadership in safety protocols
- First aid knowledge
- Mentor younger students in safety
- Risk assessment skills

---

**Remember:** Best activities are:
- Hands-on and engaging
- Age-appropriate in complexity and duration
- Connected to learning goals
- Flexible for diverse learners
- Aligned with developmental stage
- Safe and supervised appropriately
- FUN!
