## Lesson Plan Checklist

Before submitting, verify:

- [ ] Clear, measurable learning objectives
- [ ] Alignment with academic standards
- [ ] Age-appropriate content and duration
- [ ] Complete materials list
- [ ] Detailed activity steps
- [ ] Assessment strategy included
- [ ] Differentiation addressed
- [ ] Safety considerations noted
- [ ] Reflection component included
- [ ] Cross-curricular connections identified

## Evaluation Rubric

| Criteria | Points | Self-Assessment |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Standards Alignment** (15 pts) | Clear connection to grade-level standards | __/15 |
| **Learning Objectives** (15 pts) | Specific, measurable, achievable | __/15 |
| **Backward Design** (15 pts) | Evidence of starting with end goals | __/15 |
| **Activity Design** (20 pts) | Engaging, hands-on, experiential | __/20 |
| **Assessment** (15 pts) | Aligned to objectives, appropriate methods | __/15 |
| **Differentiation** (10 pts) | Addresses diverse learner needs | __/10 |
