# Activity 1: Stakeholder Mapping Exercise
## Course 214: Community & School Garden Programs

---

## Activity Overview

**Purpose:** Practice identifying and analyzing stakeholders for a garden program

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes

**Format:** Individual or small group

**Materials:** Stakeholder mapping templates, example completed maps

---

## Instructions

### Part 1: Context Selection (5 minutes)

Choose ONE context for your stakeholder mapping:

**Option A:** Your real setting where you hope to implement a garden program

**Option B:** One of the following scenarios:
- Elementary school (K-5), 300 students, urban Title I school
- Middle school (6-8), 600 students, suburban district
- Community garden, urban neighborhood with 10,000 residents
- After-school program serving 50 youth ages 8-14

---

**Prompt Questions:**
- Who will participate or be served?
- Who must approve the project?
- Who controls resources (space, budget)?
- Who would help implement?
- Who might oppose or have concerns?
- Who would benefit?
- Who has relevant expertise?
- Who has influence with decision-makers?

## Evaluation Criteria

Your stakeholder mapping will be assessed on:

- **Comprehensiveness** (25%): Did you identify key stakeholders across categories?
- **Analysis** (25%): Are stakeholders appropriately placed in power-interest grid?
- **Strategy** (30%): Are engagement actions specific, realistic, and strategic?
- **Reflection** (20%): Does reflection demonstrate critical thinking?
