## Project Overview

### Final Project: Complete Garden Program Proposal

**Project Purpose:**
Demonstrate your ability to design a viable, comprehensive garden program by creating a complete proposal that could be implemented in a real setting.

**Deliverables:**
1. Written proposal (10-15 pages)
2. Budget and funding plan
3. Garden design/layout
4. Sample curriculum materials
5. Presentation to cohort (optional: 10-15 minutes)

---

## Project Components

### Part 1: Program Overview (2-3 pages)

**1.1 Context & Rationale**
- Organization/school description
- Community context
- Needs assessment summary
- Purpose and goals of garden program

**1.2 Mission & Vision**
- Program mission statement
- Long-term vision (3-5 years)
- Core values

**1.3 Target Audience**
- Primary participants (age, number)
- Secondary audiences
- Diversity and inclusion considerations

**1.4 Program Goals**
- 3-5 SMART goals
- Alignment with organizational mission
- Expected outcomes

```
EXAMPLE SMART GOAL

"By June 2027, 90% of participating 3rd grade students
(n=60) will demonstrate understanding of plant life
cycles by creating accurate, labeled diagrams of seed
to seed progression, as assessed by standardized rubric."
```

---

### Part 2: Stakeholder Engagement Plan (2 pages)

**2.1 Stakeholder Map**
- Key stakeholders identified
- Power/interest analysis
- Engagement strategies for each group

**2.2 Partnership Plan**
- Potential partner organizations
- Value exchange for partnerships
- Partnership development timeline

**2.3 Communication Strategy**
- Communication channels
- Message development
- Annual communication calendar

---

### Part 3: Garden Design (2-3 pages + visual)

**3.1 Site Analysis**
- Location description
- Site assets and constraints
- Sun/shade/water access
- Soil conditions

**3.2 Garden Layout**
- Scale drawing of garden design
- Growing beds specifications
- Pathway system
- Infrastructure elements
- Special learning zones

**3.3 Accessibility Features**
- Universal design elements
- Inclusive features
- Safety considerations

**3.4 Phased Development**
- Year 1 construction/planting
- Future expansion plans

---

### Part 4: Educational Programming (3-4 pages)

**4.1 Curriculum Framework**
- Grade levels or age groups served
- Academic standards addressed
- Integration approach (full integration vs. supplemental)

**4.2 Scope & Sequence**
- Annual calendar
- Seasonal programming
- Topic progression

**4.3 Sample Lessons**
- 2-3 complete lesson plans using course template
- Age-appropriate activities
- Assessment strategies

**4.4 Professional Development**
- Teacher/staff training plan
- Ongoing support systems

---

### Part 5: Operations Plan (2 pages)

**5.1 Staffing**
- Roles and responsibilities
- Time commitments
- Qualifications needed

**5.2 Volunteer Management**
- Recruitment strategy
- Training approach
- Retention plan
- Recognition system

**5.3 Maintenance System**
- Regular tasks calendar
- Responsibility assignments
- Summer/vacation care plan

**5.4 Policies & Procedures**
- Safety protocols
- Usage policies
- Emergency procedures

---

### Part 6: Budget & Funding (2-3 pages)

**6.1 Startup Budget**
- Infrastructure costs
- Initial materials/supplies
- Planning and development
- Total startup cost

**6.2 Annual Operating Budget**
- Personnel
- Materials and supplies
- Programming costs
- Maintenance
- Total annual cost

**6.3 Funding Strategy**
- Year 1-3 funding sources
- Grant opportunities identified
- Earned income potential
- In-kind contributions
- Sustainability plan

**6.4 Budget Narrative**
- Justification for major expenses
- Cost-saving strategies
- Value proposition

---

### Part 7: Evaluation & Sustainability (2 pages)

**7.1 Evaluation Plan**
- Logic model (inputs → impact)
- Metrics to track
- Data collection methods
- Reporting schedule

**7.2 Sustainability Strategy**
- Five pillars assessment
- Key sustainability tactics
- Long-term vision
- Succession planning

**7.3 Continuous Improvement**
- Annual reflection process
- Adaptation mechanisms

---

### Part 8: Timeline & Next Steps (1 page)

**8.1 Implementation Timeline**
- 12-24 month roadmap
- Major milestones
- Critical path items

**8.2 Immediate Next Steps**
- First 3 actions to take
- Responsible parties
- Deadlines

---

### Assessment Rubric

**Program Proposal Rubric (100 points total)**

| Component | Developing (1-2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) | Points |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|--------------|--------|
| **Context & Goals** (12 pts) | Vague or unrealistic | Clear and achievable | Compelling, data-driven, ambitious yet realistic | /12 |
| **Stakeholder Plan** (12 pts) | Limited stakeholders, weak strategies | Key stakeholders, appropriate engagement | Comprehensive, strategic, inclusive | /12 |
| **Garden Design** (16 pts) | Basic, missing elements | Functional, addresses needs | Innovative, accessible, educational | /16 |
| **Curriculum** (20 pts) | Incomplete, not aligned | Age-appropriate, standards-aligned | Integrated, comprehensive, exemplary lessons | /20 |
| **Operations** (12 pts) | Vague or incomplete | Clear roles, reasonable plan | Detailed, realistic, sustainable | /12 |
| **Budget & Funding** (16 pts) | Unrealistic or missing detail | Realistic, sources identified | Detailed, diversified, sustainable strategy | /16 |
| **Evaluation** (8 pts) | Minimal or vague | Appropriate metrics, clear plan | Comprehensive, theory-based, actionable | /8 |
| **Professional Quality** (4 pts) | Multiple errors, poor organization | Well-organized, few errors | Polished, compelling, publication-ready | /4 |
| **TOTAL** | | | | **/100** |

## Presentation Guidelines (Optional)

### Presentation Format

**Length:** 10-15 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A

**Structure:**
1. Introduction (1 min)
   - Your context and motivation

2. Program Overview (2 min)
   - Mission, goals, target audience

3. Garden Design (2 min)
   - Show layout, highlight unique features

4. Educational Approach (3 min)
   - Curriculum integration
   - Sample activity demonstration

5. Implementation Plan (2 min)
   - Timeline, budget, funding

6. Sustainability (2 min)
   - Long-term viability
   - Community ownership

7. Conclusion (1 min)
   - Next steps, call to action

8. Q&A (5 min)

**Visual Aids:**
- 8-12 slides or equivalent
- Images of garden design
- Key data/statistics
- Budget summary
- Timeline visual

## Project Examples & Resources

### Example Project Outlines

**Example 1: Elementary School Garden**
- Urban Title I school, 300 students
- 1,500 sq ft garden, 6 raised beds
- Science integration for grades K-5
- Part-time coordinator + teacher collaboration
- $15,000 startup, $12,000 annual
- Grant + school budget funding

**Example 2: Community Youth Garden**
- Community center serving ages 8-18
- 2,000 sq ft production garden
- After-school + summer programming
- Volunteer-led with coordinator oversight
- Produce donations to food bank
- $10,000 startup, $8,000 annual
- Mix of grants, earned income, donations

**Example 3: High School Agricultural Science**
- Suburban high school, 1,200 students
- 5,000 sq ft garden + greenhouse
- Agriculture CTE pathway
- Farm stand, CSA shares
- Teacher + student leadership
- $30,000 startup, $15,000 annual (net)
- School budget + earned income
