### 9.1 Sustainability Framework

#### Five Pillars of Program Sustainability

```
SUSTAINABILITY PILLARS

         THRIVING PROGRAM
                │
        ┌───────┴────────┐
        │                │
    LEADERSHIP      FINANCIAL
    SUCCESSION      STABILITY
        │                │
        └───────┬────────┘
                │
        ┌───────┴────────┐
        │                │
    COMMUNITY       INSTITUTIONAL
    OWNERSHIP       INTEGRATION
        │                │
        └───────┬────────┘
                │
           PHYSICAL
        INFRASTRUCTURE
```

**Pillar Assessment:**

| Pillar | Low Sustainability | Medium Sustainability | High Sustainability |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Leadership** | One person, no backup | Small team, some documentation | Multiple leaders, succession plan |
| **Financial** | Single funding source | 2-3 sources, year-to-year | Diversified, multi-year commitments |
| **Community** | Limited volunteers | Regular volunteer base | Deep ownership, waiting list |
| **Institutional** | Extra program, vulnerable | Supported but not integrated | Embedded in mission/operations |
| **Infrastructure** | Temporary, minimal | Functional, some limitations | Permanent, well-maintained |

### 9.2 Institutional Integration

#### Embedding Programs in Organizations

**School Integration Strategies:**

**Curriculum Integration:**
- Aligned to academic standards
- Incorporated into lesson plans
- Regular class schedule time
- Teacher professional development
- Student learning assessment

**Budget Integration:**
- Line item in school/district budget
- Staffing allocation (coordinator hours)
- Supplies and materials
- Professional development funds

**Policy Integration:**
- Included in strategic plan
- Garden use policies established
- Maintenance responsibilities defined
- Field trip/outdoor learning policies

**Culture Integration:**
- Garden as school identity element
- Featured in communications/marketing
- Administrator support and advocacy
- Community pride and visibility

### 9.3 Leadership Development & Succession

#### Building Leadership Capacity

**Leadership Team Structure:**

```
DISTRIBUTED LEADERSHIP MODEL

      Program Coordinator
              │
     ┌────────┼────────┐
     │        │        │
 Operations  Education Community
  Manager    Lead      Engagement
     │        │           │
 ┌───┴───┐   │      ┌────┴────┐
 │       │   │      │         │
Maint. Garden Volunteer Family
Team   Coach  Coord   Liaison
```

**Succession Planning Steps:**

1. **Identify Critical Roles**
   - What positions are essential?
   - What knowledge/skills do they require?
   - What happens if person leaves?

2. **Document Processes**
   - Written procedures for all tasks
   - Calendar of responsibilities
   - Contact lists and resources
   - Institutional knowledge captured

3. **Develop Pipeline**
   - Identify potential leaders
   - Provide training and mentoring
   - Create co-leadership opportunities
   - Gradual responsibility increase

4. **Create Transition Plan**
   - Overlap period for knowledge transfer
   - Documentation handoff
   - Introduction to key relationships
   - Follow-up support

**Documentation Essentials:**

```
PROGRAM DOCUMENTATION

Operations Manual:
├── Annual calendar of tasks
├── Maintenance procedures
├── Planting guides
├── Supplier/vendor list
├── Budget templates
└── Emergency procedures

Education Resources:
├── Curriculum library
├── Lesson plans by grade
├── Assessment tools
├── Teacher orientation guide
└── Activity database

Administrative:
├── Organizational structure
├── Policies and procedures
├── Partnerships/MOUs
├── Funding sources
├── Volunteer database
└── Historical records
```

### 9.4 Financial Sustainability

#### Diversified Funding Strategy

**3-Year Funding Transition:**

**Year 1: Grant-Dependent (Foundation Building)**
- Startup grant: 70%
- School/organization budget: 15%
- Fundraising: 10%
- In-kind: 5%

**Year 2: Diversification**
- Grant renewal: 40%
- School/organization budget: 30%
- Earned income: 15%
- Fundraising: 10%
- Donations: 5%

**Year 3: Sustainability**
- Institutional budget: 50%
- Earned income: 25%
- Fundraising: 15%
- Grants: 10%

**Earned Income Opportunities:**

| Revenue Stream | Startup Effort | Annual Potential | Sustainability Value |
|----------------|----------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Plant sales | Medium | $2,000-$8,000 | High - annual event |
| Produce sales | High | $3,000-$15,000 | Medium - labor intensive |
| Workshops/classes | Medium | $2,000-$10,000 | High - scalable |
| Summer camp | High | $10,000-$30,000 | Medium - seasonal, staff-intensive |
| CSA shares | Very High | $5,000-$20,000 | Low - requires significant production |

### 9.5 Community Ownership & Engagement

#### Building Lasting Commitment

**Engagement Ladder:**

```
COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PROGRESSION

    ┌──────────────────┐
    │   CO-OWNERSHIP   │ ← Advisory board, decision-making
    └────────┬─────────┘
    ┌────────┴─────────┐
    │  DEEP ENGAGEMENT │ ← Regular volunteers, donors
    └────────┬─────────┘
    ┌────────┴─────────┐
    │ ACTIVE PARTICIPATION│ ← Event attendance, occasional help
    └────────┬─────────┘
    ┌────────┴─────────┐
    │    AWARENESS     │ ← Know about program
    └──────────────────┘
```

**Moving People Up the Ladder:**

| Current Level | Engagement Action | Goal |
|---------------|-------------------|------|
| Awareness | Invite to tour/event | Participation |
| Participation | Recruit for volunteer role | Deep engagement |
| Deep Engagement | Join advisory committee | Co-ownership |
| Co-ownership | Leadership role, fundraising | Champion/advocate |

**Community Ownership Indicators:**
- Multiple people/families invested in success
- Community advocates to decision-makers
- Donations of time, money, materials
- Problem-solving collaboration
- Pride and identity connection

### 9.6 Program Evaluation

#### Measuring Success & Improving

**Evaluation Framework:**

**Inputs** → **Activities** → **Outputs** → **Outcomes** → **Impact**

**What to Measure:**

| Evaluation Type | Metrics | Data Collection | Frequency |
|-----------------|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Participation** | # students, # sessions, # volunteers | Attendance logs | Ongoing |
| **Activities** | Lessons taught, events held, maintenance | Activity logs | Ongoing |
| **Learning** | Knowledge, skills, attitudes | Pre/post surveys, observations | Per unit/year |
| **Behavior** | Eating habits, environmental actions | Surveys, tracking | Quarterly/annual |
| **Impact** | Academic achievement, health outcomes | School data, assessments | Annual |

**Sample Evaluation Questions:**

**Short-term Outcomes (1 year):**
- Did students increase plant knowledge?
- Do students try new vegetables?
- Can students perform garden skills?
- Do students show care for living things?

**Medium-term Outcomes (2-3 years):**
- Are students applying science concepts?
- Have eating habits changed?
- Do students engage in environmental behaviors?
- Has academic performance improved?

**Long-term Impact (3-5 years):**
- Are participants healthier?
- Do participants demonstrate environmental stewardship?
- Does community have improved food access?
- Has school culture shifted?

**Data Collection Tools:**

```
EVALUATION TOOLKIT

Quantitative:
├── Student pre/post surveys
├── Teacher surveys
├── Parent surveys
├── Attendance tracking
├── Harvest logs
├── Volunteer hours
└── Academic data (if accessible)

Qualitative:
├── Student reflection journals
├── Focus groups
├── Interviews
├── Photos/videos
├── Teacher observations
└── Stories of change

Analysis:
├── Data spreadsheets
├── Graph/chart creation
├── Comparison over time
└── Success stories documented
```

### 9.7 Continuous Improvement

#### Learning and Adapting

**Annual Reflection Process:**

**End of Year (May/June):**
1. Gather evaluation data
2. Conduct stakeholder surveys
3. Hold reflection meetings with team
4. Document successes and challenges
5. Identify improvement priorities

**Summer Planning (July/August):**
1. Review previous year learnings
2. Set goals for coming year
3. Revise curriculum/activities
4. Plan professional development
5. Update documentation

**Back to School (August/September):**
1. Share plan with stakeholders
2. Train staff/volunteers
3. Implement improvements
4. Continue monitoring and adjusting

**Improvement Priorities Framework:**

| Priority Level | Description | Action |
|----------------|-------------|--------|
| **Critical** | Major problem impacting mission | Immediate attention, significant resources |
| **Important** | Limitation on effectiveness | Address within year, moderate resources |
| **Nice to Have** | Enhancement opportunity | Consider if capacity allows |
| **Future** | Aspiration for later | Document for future consideration |

### 9.8 Common Sustainability Challenges

#### Addressing Threats to Longevity

**Challenge: Coordinator Departure**

| Risk | Prevention | Response if Occurs |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Program falters | Document processes, cross-train, co-leadership | Activate succession plan, interim leadership |

**Challenge: Funding Loss**

| Risk | Prevention | Response if Occurs |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Program cuts or closure | Diversify funding, demonstrate value | Scale back strategically, emergency fundraising, institutional advocacy |

**Challenge: Declining Participation**

| Risk | Prevention | Response if Occurs |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Garden underutilized | Continuous engagement, responsive programming | Survey stakeholders, refresh offerings, recruit new participants |

**Challenge: Maintenance Burden**

| Risk | Prevention | Response if Occurs |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Garden deteriorates | Right-size garden, volunteer system, design for low maintenance | Recruit help, simplify plantings, infrastructure improvements |

**Challenge: Administrative Change**

| Risk | Prevention | Response if Occurs |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Loss of support | Document outcomes, multiple champions, policy integration | Educate new administration, demonstrate value, community advocacy |
