### 5.2 Space Planning

#### Calculating Space Needs

**General Guidelines:**

| Garden Type | Minimum Size | Optimal Size | Students Served |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Classroom Garden** | 200 sq ft | 400-600 sq ft | 20-30 students |
| **Small School Garden** | 800 sq ft | 1,500-2,000 sq ft | 100-200 students |
| **Full School Garden** | 2,000 sq ft | 5,000-10,000 sq ft | 300+ students |
| **Community Teaching Garden** | 1,000 sq ft | 2,000-3,000 sq ft | Variable groups |

**Zone Allocation:**

```
SPACE ALLOCATION (Example 2,000 sq ft garden)

Growing Beds ────────── 40% (800 sq ft)
Pathways ──────────────  25% (500 sq ft)
Gathering/Teaching ───── 15% (300 sq ft)
Storage/Compost ─────── 10% (200 sq ft)
Special Features ─────── 10% (200 sq ft)
```

### 5.3 Garden Layouts

#### Layout Patterns

**Spoke Pattern (Circular):**
```
           [Gathering]
               │
      Bed ──  Hub  ── Bed
       │       │       │
      Bed ── [Paths] ─ Bed
       │              │
      Bed ────────── Bed
```
- Central gathering space
- Equal access to all beds
- Efficient use of space
- Visually appealing

**Grid Pattern (Rectangular):**
```
[Entrance]
    │
[Bed][Path][Bed][Path][Bed]
[Path][────][Path][────][Path]
[Bed][Path][Bed][Path][Bed]
[Path][────][Path][────][Path]
[Bed][Path][Bed][Path][Bed]
    │
[Tool Shed]
```
- Simple, organized
- Easy to expand
- Classroom "ownership" beds
- Efficient maintenance

**Outdoor Classroom Layout:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│        [Demonstration Bed]      │
│               │                 │
│    [Seating Area - Amphitheater]│
│         ╱  │  ╲                 │
│    [Bed] [Path] [Bed]           │
│    [Bed] [Path] [Bed]           │
│               │                 │
│    [Exploration Garden]         │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.4 Educational Garden Elements

#### Essential Components

**Growing Beds:**

| Type | Dimensions | Best For | Considerations |
|------|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Raised beds | 4'x8'x12" | Most gardens | Standard, accessible |
| Tall raised beds | 3'x6'x24-36" | Accessibility, limited mobility | Wheelchair accessible |
| In-ground rows | 3'x variable | Larger spaces | More maintenance |
| Container gardens | Variable pots | Limited space, flexibility | Portable, seasonal |
| Vertical gardens | Wall-mounted | Small spaces, visual interest | Creative growing |

**Pathway Design:**

| Surface | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|---------|------|------|----------|
| Mulch (wood chips) | Affordable, natural | Needs replenishing | Most gardens |
| Gravel | Durable, drainage | Can scatter | High-traffic areas |
| Pavers/stepping stones | Permanent, accessible | Higher cost | Main pathways |
| Grass paths | Natural, soft | Maintenance | Low-traffic areas |
| Decomposed granite | Accessible, firm | Cost | ADA compliance |

**Width Requirements:**
- Main pathways: 4-5 feet (wheelchair accessible)
- Secondary paths: 2-3 feet
- Emergency access: 6-8 feet (if required)

### 5.5 Accessibility & Universal Design

#### Creating Inclusive Gardens

**Universal Design Principles:**

| Principle | Garden Application |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Equitable Use** | Multiple height beds, various activity types |
| **Flexibility** | Adjustable tools, different task options |
| **Simple & Intuitive** | Clear signage, logical layout |
| **Perceptible Information** | Visual, tactile, sensory elements |
| **Tolerance for Error** | Forgiving plants, room for mistakes |
| **Low Physical Effort** | Ergonomic tools, convenient water access |
| **Size & Space** | Adequate maneuvering, reach ranges |

**Accessible Features:**

```
ACCESSIBILITY CHECKLIST

Physical Access:
□ Firm, level pathways (48" minimum width)
□ Wheelchair-accessible beds (24-30" height)
□ Knee clearance under raised beds
□ Stable work surfaces at varied heights
□ Accessible water sources (lever handles)

Sensory Access:
□ High-contrast colors for visual impairment
□ Fragrant plants for sensory experience
□ Textured plants and surfaces
□ Wind chimes and other sound elements
□ Braille or tactile signage

Social Access:
□ Gathering spaces accommodate wheelchairs
□ Multiple activity zones for choice
□ Partner/small group work areas
□ Quiet reflection spaces
```

### 5.6 Water Systems

#### Water Access Planning

**Options & Considerations:**

| System | Cost | Convenience | Best For |
|--------|------|-------------|----------|
| **Hose from building** | Low | Moderate | Small gardens near building |
| **Rain barrels** | Low-Moderate | Educational value | Supplemental water |
| **Dedicated spigot** | Moderate-High | High | Medium-large gardens |
| **Drip irrigation** | Moderate-High | Very high | Intensive production |
| **Irrigation timer** | Moderate | High | Summer/vacation coverage |

**Water Conservation Education:**
- Rain barrel demonstrations
- Mulching practices
- Drip vs. overhead comparison
- Water needs by plant type

### 5.7 Storage & Infrastructure

#### Essential Storage

**Tool Storage Needs:**

```
TOOL STORAGE OPTIONS

Small Garden (< 500 sq ft)
└── Weatherproof deck box or small shed
    Stores: 10-15 hand tools, watering cans

Medium Garden (500-2,000 sq ft)
└── 6'x8' storage shed
    Stores: Tools, wheelbarrow, soil, pots

Large Garden (2,000+ sq ft)
└── 8'x12' shed with workbench
    Stores: Full tool inventory, workspace,
    materials, equipment
```

**Storage Considerations:**
- Security (locks, visibility)
- Tool organization (racks, hooks, outlines)
- Student accessibility vs. security
- Workspace for potting, prep
- Material storage (soil, mulch, pots)

### 5.8 Special Learning Zones

#### Themed Garden Areas

**Sensory Garden:**
- Touch: Lamb's ear, succulents, grasses
- Smell: Lavender, mint, rosemary, basil
- Taste: Cherry tomatoes, snap peas, herbs
- Sound: Bamboo, grasses, wind chimes
- Sight: Colorful flowers, varied textures

**Butterfly/Pollinator Garden:**
- Native flowers for nectar
- Host plants for caterpillars
- Shallow water source
- Shelter and sun exposure
- Observation benches

**Three Sisters Garden:**
- Cultural food traditions
- Companion planting demonstration
- Corn, beans, squash together
- Indigenous agricultural practices

**Pizza/Salsa/Smoothie Garden:**
- Ingredient-focused planting
- Connection to food preparation
- Harvest and cooking integration
- Student engagement through relevance

**Compost Learning Station:**
- Three-bin system for demonstration
- Cross-section viewing window
- Temperature monitoring
- Decomposition observation
- Soil science lessons

### 5.9 Seasonal Design

#### Year-Round Learning Spaces

**Four-Season Planning:**

| Season | Growing Focus | Educational Focus | Infrastructure |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Spring** | Cool crops, transplants | Life cycles, planting | Beds ready, tools accessible |
| **Summer** | Warm crops, maintenance | Growth, water, ecosystems | Irrigation, shade structure |
| **Fall** | Cool crops, harvest | Harvest, preservation | Storage, processing space |
| **Winter** | Planning, indoor growing | Planning, seed starting | Greenhouse/indoor space |

**Winter Garden Solutions:**
- Hoop houses or cold frames
- Indoor growing stations (windowsills, grow lights)
- Evergreen plantings for year-round interest
- Winter interest features (structures, bird feeders)
- Classroom planning and seed starting

## Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Universal Design** | Designing spaces usable by people of all abilities without adaptation |
| **Raised Bed** | Above-ground planting area contained by frame |
| **Accessible Pathway** | Route meeting ADA standards for wheelchair access |
| **Outdoor Classroom** | Garden space designed for group instruction |
| **Sensory Garden** | Garden designed to engage multiple senses |
| **Zone** | Distinct area within garden serving specific purpose |
| **Hardscape** | Non-living garden elements (paths, structures, walls) |
| **Infrastructure** | Basic physical systems (water, storage, utilities) |
