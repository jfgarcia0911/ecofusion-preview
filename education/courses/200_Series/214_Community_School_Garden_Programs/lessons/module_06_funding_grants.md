### 6.4 Grant Writing Fundamentals

#### Understanding What Funders Want

**Funder Priorities Checklist:**

```
ALIGNMENT ASSESSMENT

Mission Fit:
□ Does our project align with funder's mission?
□ Does it address their priority areas?
□ Are we an eligible applicant?

Impact Potential:
□ Clear, measurable outcomes?
□ Evidence-based approach?
□ Reaches priority populations?

Organizational Capacity:
□ Track record of success?
□ Qualified leadership?
□ Financial stability?
□ Community support?

Sustainability:
□ Plan for continuation beyond grant?
□ Diversified funding?
□ Realistic timeline?
```

### 6.5 Grant Proposal Components

#### Standard Proposal Structure

**1. Executive Summary (10% of proposal)**
- Brief overview of project
- Funding request amount
- Key outcomes expected

**2. Statement of Need (20%)**
- Problem or opportunity
- Target population
- Supporting data and evidence
- Community input/assessment

**3. Project Description (30%)**
- Goals and objectives (SMART)
- Activities and timeline
- Staffing and roles
- Evidence-based methods

**4. Evaluation Plan (15%)**
- How success will be measured
- Data collection methods
- Timeline for assessment
- Reporting plan

**5. Budget & Budget Narrative (15%)**
- Line-item budget
- Explanation of costs
- Other funding sources
- In-kind contributions

**6. Organizational Capacity (10%)**
- Mission and history
- Relevant experience
- Leadership qualifications
- Financial stability
- Community partnerships

### 6.6 Writing Strong Proposals

#### Narrative Writing Tips

**Compelling Project Description:**

```
EFFECTIVE PROPOSAL FORMULA

1. HOOK
   Start with compelling statistic or story
   "Only 35% of our students can identify where
   food comes from."

2. CONTEXT
   Explain the broader issue and local situation
   "Research shows garden programs increase
   science achievement..."

3. SOLUTION
   Describe your specific approach
   "We will establish a 1,500 sq ft garden serving
   120 third-graders..."

4. IMPLEMENTATION
   Detail activities and timeline
   "In Year 1, we will build infrastructure, train
   teachers..."

5. OUTCOMES
   Paint picture of success
   "By June 2027, 90% of students will demonstrate
   understanding..."

6. SUSTAINABILITY
   Show long-term thinking
   "Funding will transition to school budget and
   earned income..."
```

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

| Mistake | Why It's Problematic | Fix |
|---------|----------------------|-----|
| Vague goals | Can't measure success | Use SMART goal framework |
| Jargon | Confuses reviewers | Plain language, define terms |
| Unrealistic | Undermines credibility | Honest, achievable scope |
| Missing budget narrative | Unclear why costs necessary | Explain each line item |
| No evaluation | Can't demonstrate impact | Include assessment plan |
| Grammar/spelling errors | Unprofessional | Careful proofreading |

### 6.7 Budget Development

#### Sample Garden Program Budget

**Year One Budget Example ($15,000 grant request):**

| Category | Item | Quantity | Cost | Total |
|----------|------|----------|------|-------|
| **Personnel** | Garden Coordinator (10 hrs/wk) | 30 weeks | $25/hr | $7,500 |
| **Infrastructure** | Raised beds (4'x8') | 6 beds | $300 | $1,800 |
| | Pathways (mulch) | 500 sq ft | $2/sq ft | $1,000 |
| | Fencing | 150 ft | $10/ft | $1,500 |
| | Water access/irrigation | Lump sum | - | $1,200 |
| **Materials** | Tools | Set | - | $600 |
| | Soil and amendments | 10 cu yd | $50/yd | $500 |
| | Seeds and plants | Annual | - | $400 |
| **Programming** | Curriculum materials | - | - | $300 |
| | Teacher training | - | - | $200 |
| **Total** | | | | **$15,000** |

**Budget Narrative Example:**

> "Personnel: Garden Coordinator will work 10 hours per week for 30 weeks (school year) at $25/hour, providing direct instruction, teacher support, volunteer coordination, and maintenance oversight. This position is essential for consistent, quality programming.
>
> Infrastructure: Six raised beds will serve grades K-5, with each grade having dedicated growing space. Beds will be constructed from untreated cedar lumber for longevity and safety, with hardware cloth bottoms to prevent burrowing pests..."

### 6.8 Funding Sustainability Plan

#### Multi-Year Funding Strategy

**3-Year Funding Diversification Plan:**

| Year | Primary Funding | % of Budget | Sustainability Actions |
|------|-----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Year 1** | Startup grant | 80% | Demonstrate success, build support |
| **Year 2** | Mix: grant renewal, school budget, fundraising | 40/30/30% | Develop earned income, engage donors |
| **Year 3** | School budget, earned income, fundraising | 50/30/20% | Full institutional integration |

**Diversification Strategy:**

```
FUNDING DIVERSIFICATION

Year 1
├── Grant: $15,000 (80%)
├── PTA: $2,000 (10%)
└── Donations: $2,000 (10%)
    Total: $19,000

Year 2
├── Grant renewal: $10,000 (40%)
├── School budget: $7,500 (30%)
├── Plant sales: $3,000 (12%)
├── PTA: $2,000 (8%)
└── Donations: $2,500 (10%)
    Total: $25,000

Year 3
├── School budget: $15,000 (50%)
├── Earned income: $9,000 (30%)
├── Fundraising/donations: $6,000 (20%)
    Total: $30,000
```

### 6.9 Stewardship & Reporting

#### Maintaining Funder Relationships

**Grant Reporting Requirements:**

| Report Type | Timing | Contents |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| **Progress Report** | Mid-grant | Activities completed, challenges, spending |
| **Final Report** | Grant end | Outcomes achieved, full budget, impact stories |
| **Financial Report** | As required | Detailed expenses, receipts |
| **Photos/Media** | Ongoing | Document activities for reports and updates |

**Stewardship Best Practices:**

```
FUNDER RELATIONSHIP CHECKLIST

□ Thank you letter immediately upon award
□ Invite funder to visit garden
□ Regular updates (quarterly email)
□ Include in newsletters and social media
□ Acknowledge publicly (signage, events)
□ Submit reports on time
□ Be transparent about challenges
□ Share successes and impact stories
□ Maintain relationship beyond grant period
```
