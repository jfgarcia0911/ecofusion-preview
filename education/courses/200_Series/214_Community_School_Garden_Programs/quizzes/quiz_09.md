# Quiz 9: Program Sustainability
## Course 214: Community & School Garden Programs

**Module 9** | 10 Questions | 15 minutes | 80% to pass

## Questions

**1. The Five Pillars of Program Sustainability include:**
- A) Leadership, Financial, Community, Institutional, Infrastructure ✓
- B) Money, tools, plants, water, soil
- C) Teachers, students, parents, admin, custodians
- D) Spring, summer, fall, winter, year-round

**2. Institutional integration means:**
- A) Garden is an isolated extra program
- B) Garden is embedded in curriculum, budget, policy, and culture ✓
- C) Garden is temporary
- D) Only administrators care

**3. Succession planning involves:**
- A) Hoping for the best
- B) Documenting processes and developing leadership pipeline ✓
- C) Keeping all knowledge secret
- D) Relying on one person forever

**4. Program evaluation should measure:**
- A) Nothing - evaluation is unnecessary
- B) Only participant numbers
- C) Inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact ✓
- D) Only financial data

**5. Continuous improvement includes:**
- A) Never changing anything
- B) Annual reflection, goal-setting, and adjustments ✓
- C) Constant major overhauls
- D) Ignoring feedback

**6. High sustainability in leadership means:**
- A) One person, no backup
- B) Small team, some documentation
- C) Multiple leaders, succession plan, documented processes ✓
- D) No leaders at all

**7. Community ownership is indicated by:**
- A) No one cares about the garden
- B) Multiple people invested, advocacy, donations, pride ✓
- C) Only paid staff involved
- D) Locked gates always

**8. A sustainable program timeline shows:**
- A) Year 1: Grants 80%, Year 3: Institutional budget 50%+ ✓
- B) Always 100% grants
- C) Never any funding
- D) Random funding with no plan

**9. Documentation should include:**
- A) Nothing - keep it all in your head
- B) Operations manual, curriculum library, admin records ✓
- C) Only financial records
- D) Personal diary entries

**10. A 5-year strategic plan helps by:**
- A) Gathering dust on a shelf
- B) Guiding decisions and motivating stakeholders toward shared goals ✓
- C) Limiting creativity
- D) Preventing any changes

**Answers: 1-A, 2-B, 3-B, 4-C, 5-B, 6-C, 7-B, 8-A, 9-B, 10-B**
