**1. A diversified funding strategy means:**

- A) Whole Kids Foundation ✓

- A) Earned income
- B) School operational budget

- A) Plant sales ($2,000-$8,000)
- B) Workshops ($2,000-$10,000)

**7. Grant stewardship includes:**
- A) Ignoring funders after receiving money

**8. SMART goals in grant proposals must be:**
- A) Vague and aspirational
- B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound ✓
- C) Simple and easy
- D) Unlimited in scope

**9. What should you do immediately upon receiving a grant?**
- A) Spend all the money quickly
- B) Send thank you letter ✓
- C) Forget about the funder

**10. By Year 3, sustainable programs should aim for institutional budget to cover:**
- A) 10% of costs
- B) 25% of costs
- C) 50% of costs ✓
- D) 0% of costs
