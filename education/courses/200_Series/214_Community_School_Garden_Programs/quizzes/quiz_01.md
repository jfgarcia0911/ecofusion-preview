## Questions

### Question 1
**According to research cited in the module, what was the approximate increase in science test scores for students participating in garden programs?**

A) 5%
B) 12% ✓
C) 20%
D) 25%

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: UC Davis research (2019) found a 12% increase in science test scores among students participating in garden-based learning programs.*

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following is NOT one of the Five Pillars of Program Sustainability mentioned in the module?**

A) Leadership Succession
B) Financial Sustainability
C) Marketing and Branding ✓
D) Community Ownership

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The Five Pillars are: Leadership Succession, Financial Sustainability, Community Ownership, Institutional Integration, and Physical Infrastructure. Marketing is important but not one of the core pillars.*

---

### Question 3
**What type of garden program model features dedicated full-time staff and integration of both kitchen and garden?**

A) Classroom Teacher-Led
B) Volunteer-Based
C) Edible Schoolyard Model ✓
D) After-School Club

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The Edible Schoolyard model (Berkeley, CA) features full-time dedicated staff integrating both garden and kitchen/cooking instruction.*

---

### Question 4
**Research shows garden programs increase vegetable consumption among elementary students by approximately:**

A) 1.5 times
B) 2.5 times ✓
C) 3.5 times
D) 5 times

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Studies show that students in garden programs consume vegetables 2.5 times more often than peers not in garden programs.*

---

### Question 5
**Which academic subject areas can be integrated with garden-based learning? (Select all that apply - choose the BEST answer)**

A) Science only
B) Science and Math only
C) Science, Math, and Language Arts
D) Science, Math, Language Arts, Social Studies, and Arts ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: Gardens provide rich opportunities for integration across all subject areas including science, math, language arts, social studies, and arts.*

---

### Question 6
**What is the national estimated value of a volunteer hour in 2024?**

A) $15.80
B) $23.50
C) $31.80 ✓
D) $45.00

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The national volunteer hour value is estimated at $31.80 in 2024, representing the economic contribution of volunteer time.*

---

### Question 7
**Which of the following is considered a mental health benefit of garden programs?**

A) Increased test scores
B) Better vegetable consumption
C) Reduced stress and improved mood ✓
D) Higher attendance rates

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Garden programs have documented mental health benefits including stress reduction, lower cortisol levels, and improved mood states.*

---

### Question 8
**The FoodCorps service program places AmeriCorps members in:**

A) Community gardens only
B) High-need schools ✓
C) University research farms
D) Commercial farms

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: FoodCorps places AmeriCorps service members in high-need schools to provide garden-based nutrition education and build community partnerships.*

---

### Question 9
**What is the PRIMARY purpose of garden-based learning according to the module?**

A) Producing food for the cafeteria
B) Providing experiential learning that connects concepts to real experience ✓
C) Saving money on landscaping
D) Keeping students busy during recess

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The core value proposition of garden programs is providing hands-on, experiential learning that engages multiple senses and connects abstract concepts to tangible experiences.*

---

### Question 10
**Which is a common challenge for garden programs mentioned in the module?**

A) Too many volunteers
B) Limited time in packed curriculum schedules ✓
C) Excessive funding
D) Too much administrative support

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Limited time due to curriculum demands and packed schedules is identified as a common challenge for implementing garden programs.*
