## Course Description

Design and implement impactful educational garden programs for schools, community organizations, and public spaces. This course covers program development, curriculum integration, community engagement, volunteer management, and sustainable program models.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Design garden programs for various audiences
2. Develop age-appropriate curriculum and activities
3. Engage communities and diverse stakeholders
4. Manage volunteers and resources effectively
5. Ensure long-term program sustainability
6. Measure and communicate program impact

## Program Benefits

### Educational Outcomes
- Science (biology, ecology, nutrition)
- Math (measuring, counting, graphing)
- Language arts (journaling, observation)
- Social studies (food systems, culture)
- Environmental stewardship

### Health & Wellness
- Increased fruit/vegetable consumption
- Physical activity
- Mental health benefits
- Connection to food sources

### Community Impact
- Intergenerational connections
- Cultural exchange
- Food security
- Neighborhood improvement

## Age-Appropriate Programming

### K-2 (Ages 5-7)
| Focus | Activities |
|-------|------------|
| Sensory exploration | Touch, smell, taste garden items |
| Basic plant needs | Water, sun, soil demonstrations |
| Observation | Drawing, simple journals |
| Movement | Planting, watering, harvesting |

### 3-5 (Ages 8-10)
| Focus | Activities |
|-------|------------|
| Plant life cycles | Seed to harvest tracking |
| Ecosystems | Beneficial insects, composting |
| Measurement | Growth tracking, harvest weights |
| Cooking | Simple recipes with harvest |

### 6-8 (Ages 11-13)
| Focus | Activities |
|-------|------------|
| Scientific method | Experiments, variables |
| Environmental issues | Water, soil, climate |
| Food systems | Farm to table, sustainability |
| Leadership | Teaching younger students |

### 9-12 (Ages 14-18)
| Focus | Activities |
|-------|------------|
| Business/economics | Production planning, sales |
| Technology | Automation, data collection |
| Community engagement | Farmers markets, donations |
| Career exploration | Agriculture, nutrition, science |

## Sustainability Framework

### Five Pillars of Program Sustainability

1. **Leadership Succession**
   - Develop multiple leaders
   - Document processes
   - Create transition plans

2. **Financial Sustainability**
   - Diversified funding sources
   - Earned income strategies
   - Reserve fund building

3. **Community Ownership**
   - Stakeholder engagement
   - Volunteer development
   - Partnership building

4. **Institutional Integration**
   - Curriculum alignment
   - Administrative support
   - Policy integration

5. **Physical Infrastructure**
   - Maintenance plans
   - Capital improvement
   - Long-term design
