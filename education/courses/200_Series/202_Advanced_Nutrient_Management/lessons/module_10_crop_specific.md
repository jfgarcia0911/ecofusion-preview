# Module 10: Crop-Specific Nutrition Programs

**Duration:** 1 hour
**Level:** Intermediate

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Design nutrient programs for leafy greens, herbs, and fruiting crops
2. Adjust formulations for different growth stages
3. Optimize nutrition for quality and yield

## 1. Leafy Greens Nutrition

### Lettuce (Lactuca sativa)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  140-180  (moderate)
P:  40-50    (low-moderate)
K:  200-250  (moderate-high)
Ca: 150-180  (high - tipburn prevention)
Mg: 45-55    (moderate)
S:  60-80    (moderate)
Fe: 2-3      (standard)
EC: 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.2
```

**Growth Stage Adjustments:**
- **Weeks 1-2 (Seedling):** EC 0.8-1.0, emphasize P for roots
- **Weeks 3-4 (Rapid Growth):** EC 1.2-1.4, high N for leaves
- **Weeks 5-6 (Heading):** EC 1.4-1.6, increase Ca, maintain K
- **Pre-Harvest:** Reduce EC to 1.2-1.4 to prevent tipburn

**Tipburn Prevention:**
- Keep Ca >150 ppm
- Maintain EC <1.6 mS/cm
- Ensure airflow (transpiration)
- Avoid high humidity
- Consider foliar Ca sprays

### Spinach (Spinacia oleracea)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  160-200  (high)
P:  45-60    (moderate)
K:  240-300  (high)
Ca: 140-180  (moderate-high)
Mg: 50-65    (moderate-high)
EC: 1.4-1.8 mS/cm
pH: 6.0-6.5
```

**Special Considerations:**
- Higher salt tolerance than lettuce
- Nitrate accumulation possible (monitor)
- Iron deficiency common (ensure chelate)
- Bolting sensitive to stress

### Kale (Brassica oleracea)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  180-220  (high)
P:  50-65    (moderate-high)
K:  260-320  (high)
Ca: 160-200  (high)
Mg: 55-70    (moderate-high)
S:  80-100   (higher for flavor)
B:  0.4-0.6  (higher - brassica requirement)
EC: 1.6-2.2 mS/cm
pH: 6.0-6.5
```

**Special Considerations:**
- High S requirement (flavor development)
- High B requirement (brassica family)
- Iron deficiency possible
- Tolerates higher EC

## 2. Herbs Nutrition

### Basil (Ocimum basilicum)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  120-150  (moderate - excess reduces flavor)
P:  40-55    (moderate)
K:  200-240  (moderate-high)
Ca: 140-170  (moderate-high)
Mg: 45-60    (moderate)
EC: 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
pH: 5.5-6.2
```

**Quality Optimization:**
- Lower N = more flavor oils
- Moderate EC for tender leaves
- Adequate Ca prevents tip burn
- Cool temperatures enhance flavor

**Growth Stage Program:**
- **Vegetative:** EC 1.0-1.2, N 140-150 ppm
- **Production:** EC 1.2-1.6, N 120-140 ppm
- **Pre-Harvest:** Reduce N by 20% for flavor

### Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  140-170  (moderate)
P:  45-55    (moderate)
K:  210-260  (high)
Ca: 150-180  (moderate-high)
EC: 1.2-1.8 mS/cm
pH: 6.0-6.5
```

**Special Considerations:**
- Bolts easily under stress
- Keep EC consistent
- Adequate Ca for quality
- Fast-growing (3-4 weeks)

### Mediterranean Herbs (Oregano, Thyme, Rosemary)

**Nutrient Requirements (ppm):**
```
N:  100-140  (low-moderate, excess reduces flavor)
P:  40-50    (moderate)
K:  180-240  (moderate-high)
Ca: 120-160  (moderate)
Mg: 40-55    (moderate)
EC: 1.4-2.2 mS/cm (higher enhances oils)
pH: 6.0-6.8
```

**Flavor Enhancement:**
- Higher EC (mild stress) = more essential oils
- Lower N = concentrated flavor
- Good P for roots
- Allow slight drought stress (media systems)

## 3. Fruiting Crops - Tomatoes

### Tomato Growth Stage Nutrition

**Transplant/Establishment (Weeks 1-2):**
```
N:  150-180 ppm
P:  50-60 ppm
K:  220-260 ppm
Ca: 160-180 ppm
Mg: 50-60 ppm
EC: 1.5-2.0 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.3
```

**Vegetative Growth (Weeks 3-6):**
```
N:  180-220 ppm
P:  55-70 ppm
K:  260-300 ppm
Ca: 180-200 ppm
Mg: 55-65 ppm
EC: 2.0-2.5 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.3
Ratio: N:K = 1:1.4
```

**Flowering/Fruit Set (Weeks 7-8):**
```
N:  160-190 ppm (reduce N)
P:  60-80 ppm (increase P)
K:  300-350 ppm (increase K)
Ca: 200-220 ppm (increase Ca - fruit quality)
Mg: 60-70 ppm
EC: 2.5-3.0 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.3
Ratio: N:K = 1:1.8
```

**Fruiting/Production (Weeks 9+):**
```
N:  140-170 ppm (further reduce)
P:  65-85 ppm
K:  350-400 ppm (high K for fruit quality)
Ca: 200-240 ppm
Mg: 65-75 ppm
EC: 3.0-4.0 mS/cm (standard)
EC: 4.0-5.0 mS/cm (premium flavor)
pH: 5.8-6.3
Ratio: N:K = 1:2.5
```

### Tomato Type Variations

**Cherry Tomatoes:**
- Higher EC tolerated (3.5-5.0 mS/cm)
- Higher K (400-450 ppm)
- Benefits from stress

**Beefsteak/Large Tomatoes:**
- Lower EC (2.5-3.5 mS/cm)
- More balanced N:K
- Consistent conditions

**Roma/Paste Tomatoes:**
- Moderate EC (3.0-4.0 mS/cm)
- Higher Ca (220-250 ppm)
- Good firmness important

### Blossom End Rot Prevention

**Nutritional Strategy:**
- Ca: >180 ppm minimum, >200 ppm ideal
- Avoid high NH₄-N (reduces Ca uptake)
- Balanced K:Ca ratio (maintain <2:1)
- Adequate Mg (Ca-Mg balance)

**Environmental:**
- Consistent irrigation
- Avoid drought stress
- Maintain transpiration
- Moderate humidity

**Foliar Application:**
- Calcium chloride or calcium nitrate
- 0.5-1.0% solution
- Spray young fruits weekly
- Complements root uptake

## 4. Fruiting Crops - Peppers

### Pepper Nutrition Program

**Vegetative Stage:**
```
N:  160-200 ppm
P:  50-65 ppm
K:  240-280 ppm
Ca: 170-200 ppm
Mg: 55-65 ppm
EC: 2.0-2.5 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.3
```

**Flowering/Fruiting:**
```
N:  140-180 ppm (reduce)
P:  60-80 ppm (increase)
K:  300-360 ppm (increase)
Ca: 190-220 ppm
Mg: 60-70 ppm
EC: 2.5-3.5 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.3
```

**Special Considerations:**
- Lower EC tolerance than tomatoes
- More sensitive to high salts
- Ca critical for fruit quality
- Moderate stress acceptable for flavor

### Pepper Type Variations

**Bell Peppers:**
- EC: 2.0-3.0 mS/cm
- High Ca for firmness
- Consistent conditions

**Hot Peppers (Jalapeño, Habanero):**
- EC: 2.5-3.5 mS/cm
- Higher stress = more capsaicin
- Lower N during fruiting

## 6. Specialty Crops

### Strawberries

**Nutrition Program:**
```
N:  100-140 ppm (lower than most)
P:  40-55 ppm
K:  140-180 ppm
Ca: 120-160 ppm
Mg: 40-50 ppm
Fe: 2-3 ppm (critical)
EC: 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
pH: 5.5-6.0 (lower than most)
```

**Special Requirements:**
- Lower pH than most crops
- Iron critical (acid-loving)
- Lower EC
- High P during flowering
- Calcium for fruit firmness

### Microgreens

**Nutrition Program:**
```
N:  80-120 ppm (lower - short cycle)
P:  30-45 ppm
K:  120-180 ppm
Ca: 100-140 ppm
Mg: 35-50 ppm
EC: 0.8-1.4 mS/cm
pH: 5.8-6.2
```

**Special Considerations:**
- Short growth cycle (7-14 days)
- Lower concentration adequate
- Seed nutrients provide early nutrition
- Focus on balanced, complete nutrition

## 7. Aquaponic Crop Selection

### Best Crops for Aquaponics

**Excellent:**
- Lettuce (low nutrient demand)
- Herbs (basil, mint, cilantro)
- Leafy greens (kale, chard)

**Good:**
- Tomatoes (with supplementation)
- Peppers (with supplementation)
- Cucumbers (with supplementation)

**Challenging:**
- Strawberries (low pH requirement)
- Heavy feeders without supplementation

### Supplementation Strategy

**Essential Supplements:**
1. Iron (weekly addition of Fe-DTPA)
2. Potassium (weekly K₂SO₄ or K₂CO₃)
3. Calcium (bi-weekly Ca(OH)₂ or as needed)

**Conditional Supplements:**
4. Magnesium (monthly MgSO₄)
5. Phosphorus (as needed based on testing)

## 8. Crop Rotation and Succession

### Nutrient Considerations

**Following Heavy Feeders (Tomatoes):**
- Solution may be depleted
- Reset before planting
- Full nutrient refresh

**Following Light Feeders (Lettuce):**
- Solution less depleted
- May just need top-up
- Check EC and pH

### Seasonal Adjustments

**Summer (High Transpiration):**
- Lower EC (osmotic stress risk)
- More frequent top-offs
- Higher Ca for quality

**Winter (Low Transpiration):**
- Can use higher EC
- Less frequent top-offs
- Watch for Ca deficiency (low transpiration)

## 9. Troubleshooting Crop-Specific Issues

### Lettuce Tipburn

**Causes:**
- Inadequate Ca
- High EC
- Low transpiration
- Rapid growth

**Solutions:**
- Increase Ca to >150 ppm
- Reduce EC to <1.6 mS/cm
- Improve airflow
- Foliar Ca sprays
- Slow growth slightly (lower N)

### Tomato Fruit Cracking

**Causes:**
- Inconsistent irrigation
- High humidity
- Rapid fruit expansion
- Low Ca

**Solutions:**
- Consistent watering
- Adequate Ca (>200 ppm)
- Reduce humidity
- Avoid stress-then-recovery cycles

### Basil Leaf Yellowing

**Causes:**
- N deficiency
- Mg deficiency
- Iron deficiency
- Root problems

**Diagnosis:**
- Check EC (adequate nutrition?)
- Check pH (availability?)
- Examine roots (disease?)
- Test solution (verify levels)

## 10. Key Takeaways

1. **Crops have unique requirements**
   - Leafy greens: Moderate EC, high Ca
   - Herbs: Lower N for flavor
   - Fruiting crops: High K during fruiting

2. **Growth stage matters**
   - Adjust N:K ratio through lifecycle
   - Reduce N for fruiting
   - Increase K for fruit quality

3. **EC strategy varies**
   - Lettuce: 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
   - Basil: 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
   - Tomatoes: 2.0-5.0 mS/cm (stage-dependent)

4. **Calcium is critical**
   - Tipburn in lettuce
   - Blossom end rot in tomatoes
   - Fruit quality in all crops

5. **Quality vs. yield trade-offs**
   - Higher EC = better flavor, smaller size
   - Lower EC = larger size, faster growth
   - Optimize for market goals

6. **Aquaponics requires adaptation**
   - Choose appropriate crops
   - Supplement key nutrients
   - Accept compromises

7. **Monitor and adjust**
   - No perfect formula
   - Observe plant response
   - Refine based on results
