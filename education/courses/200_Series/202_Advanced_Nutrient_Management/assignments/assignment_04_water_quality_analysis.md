## Purpose

Source water quality significantly impacts nutrient management. High alkalinity, hardness, or contaminants can interfere with pH control, nutrient availability, and plant health. This assignment develops your ability to read water quality reports and make informed management decisions.

---

## Instructions

You've been provided with a water quality report from a potential farm location. Analyze the data, identify issues, and develop a water treatment strategy.

## Tips for Success

- Compare each parameter to optimal ranges for hydroponics
- Consider cumulative effects (multiple issues compound)
- Think about long-term buildup (sodium, chloride accumulate)
- Evaluate treatment options on cost AND effectiveness
- Be realistic about operational complexity
