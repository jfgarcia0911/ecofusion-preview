## Purpose

Visual diagnosis is a critical skill for managing plant nutrition. By the time a lab test returns results, significant crop damage may have occurred. This exercise develops your ability to quickly and accurately identify nutrient problems from plant symptoms alone.

## Key Diagnostic Clues

### Symptom Location

**Old Leaves First (Mobile Nutrients):**
- Nitrogen (N)
- Phosphorus (P)
- Potassium (K)
- Magnesium (Mg)
- Molybdenum (Mo)

**New Leaves First (Immobile Nutrients):**
- Calcium (Ca)
- Iron (Fe)
- Boron (B)
- Copper (Cu)
- Manganese (Mn)
- Zinc (Zn)
- Sulfur (S) - semi-mobile

### Common Patterns

| Symptom | Most Likely Nutrient |
|---------|-------------------|
| General chlorosis, old leaves first | Nitrogen (N) |
| Purple/red tinting, old leaves | Phosphorus (P) |
| Marginal chlorosis/necrosis, old leaves | Potassium (K) |
| Interveinal chlorosis, new leaves | Iron (Fe) or Manganese (Mn) |
| Tip/margin necrosis, new leaves | Calcium (Ca) |
| Interveinal chlorosis, old leaves | Magnesium (Mg) |

## Tips for Success

- Consider symptom location FIRST (mobile vs. immobile)
- Look for patterns (interveinal, margins, tips)
- Consider the whole plant context
- Rule out non-nutritional causes (pests, disease, environmental)
- Be specific in corrective actions (not just "add fertilizer")
