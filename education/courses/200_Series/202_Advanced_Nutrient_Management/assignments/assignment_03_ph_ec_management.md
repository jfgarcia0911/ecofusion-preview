## Reference Data

### pH Adjustment Guide

**Phosphoric Acid (85%):**
- ~1 mL per 10 gallons lowers pH by ~0.5 units
- Most common choice for hydroponics
- Adds phosphorus

**Potassium Hydroxide:**
- Raises pH
- ~1 gram per 10 gallons raises pH by ~0.5 units
- Adds potassium

### EC Interpretation

| EC (mS/cm) | Interpretation | Action |
|-----------|----------------|--------|
| <0.5 | Very low | Add nutrients immediately |
| 0.5-1.0 | Low | Monitor closely, may need nutrients |
| 1.0-2.5 | Optimal range | Depends on crop and stage |
| 2.5-3.5 | High | Monitor for stress, may dilute |
| >3.5 | Very high | Dilute or replace solution |
