## Purpose

Create a complete, professional nutrient management program that you could implement in a real operation. This capstone assignment integrates deficiency diagnosis, formulation, pH/EC management, and troubleshooting into one comprehensive document.

Your Nutrient Management Plan must include:

1. Crop-specific nutrition programs (3-4 crops)
2. Monitoring protocols and schedules
3. Deficiency/toxicity troubleshooting guide
4. Water quality management strategy
5. Record-keeping templates
