## Tips for Success

- Use dimensional analysis for all calculations
- Account for the % of nutrient in each fertilizer
- Convert between ppm, %, and grams correctly
- Always mix calcium separately from sulfates and phosphates initially
- Double-check your math!

---

## Formulation Cheat Sheet

**Conversion factors:**
- 1 ppm = 1 mg/L
- 1 gallon = 3.785 liters
- To find grams needed: (Target ppm × Volume in L × % purity factor)

**Example calculation:**
For 150 ppm N from 15.5% N fertilizer in 100 gallons:
- 100 gal × 3.785 L/gal = 378.5 L
- 150 mg/L × 378.5 L = 56,775 mg = 56.775 g of pure N needed
- Fertilizer is 15.5% N, so: 56.775 ÷ 0.155 = 366 grams of fertilizer
