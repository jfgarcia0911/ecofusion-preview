# Activity 1: Custom Nutrient Formulation Project

**Course 202: Advanced Nutrient Management**
**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Points:** 30 points

---

## Objective

Design and calculate a complete nutrient solution formulation for a specific crop and system type, demonstrating mastery of nutrient chemistry and formulation calculations.

---

## Scenario

You are setting up a new growing system. Your task is to formulate a custom two-part nutrient solution based on the specifications below.

---

## Project Requirements

### Step 1: System & Crop Selection (5 points)

**Choose ONE of the following scenarios:**

**Option A: NFT Lettuce Production**
- System: Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)
- Crop: Butterhead lettuce
- System volume: 500 liters
- Target EC: 1.4 mS/cm
- pH: 5.8-6.2

**Option B: DWC Tomato Production**
- System: Deep Water Culture (DWC)
- Crop: Indeterminate tomatoes (fruiting stage)
- System volume: 1000 liters
- Target EC: 3.2 mS/cm
- pH: 5.8-6.3

**Option C: Aquaponic Basil Production**
- System: Media bed aquaponics
- Crop: Sweet basil
- System volume: 800 liters
- Fish-provided N: 45 ppm, P: 12 ppm, K: 25 ppm, Ca: 40 ppm
- Target EC: 1.3 mS/cm
- pH: 6.6-7.0

**Document your choice and justify why this formulation is appropriate for the crop/system.**

### Step 4: Calculate Salt Amounts (10 points)

**Show all calculations** for determining how much of each salt is needed.

Use the formula:
```
Salt Amount (g/L) = Target Element (ppm) ÷ Element Fraction in Salt
```

**Part A Calculations:**

Example format:
```
Calcium Nitrate for 160 ppm Ca:
- Ca(NO₃)₂·4H₂O = 17% Ca, 11.9% N
- Amount needed: 160 ÷ 0.17 = 941 mg/L = 0.941 g/L
- Also provides: 0.941 × 0.119 = 0.112 g/L N = 112 ppm N
```
