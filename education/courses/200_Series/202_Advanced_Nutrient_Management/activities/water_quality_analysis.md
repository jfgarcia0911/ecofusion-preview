## Part 2: Treatment Cost-Benefit Analysis (10 points)

**Scenario:** You are setting up a 2000 L NFT system for lettuce production. Your source water is Sample A (municipal with high hardness and alkalinity).

**Treatment Options:**

**Option 1: No Treatment**
- Cost: $0
- Issues: High pH management difficulty, excess Ca/Mg

**Option 2: RO System**
- Initial cost: $1,500
- Filter replacement: $150/year
- Water waste: 3:1 (use 2000 L, waste 6000 L)
- Additional water cost: $200/year
- Benefit: Complete control, consistent quality

**Option 3: Blending (50% RO, 50% Tap)**
- Initial cost: $1,700 (RO + blending valve)
- Filter replacement: $150/year
- Water waste: 1.5:1
- Additional water cost: $100/year
- Benefit: Reduced hardness, some buffering

**Questions:** (10 points)

1. **Calculate first-year total cost for each option:**
   - Option 1: $_______
   - Option 2: $_______
   - Option 3: $_______

2. **Calculate 5-year total cost for each option:**
   - Option 1: $_______
   - Option 2: $_______
   - Option 3: $_______

3. **For each option, estimate pH adjustment chemical costs per year:**
   - Option 1 (high alkalinity): $______ (hint: ~$200-300/year)
   - Option 2 (low alkalinity): $______ (hint: ~$50-100/year)
   - Option 3 (moderate): $______ (hint: ~$100-150/year)

4. **Recalculate 5-year costs including pH adjustment chemicals:**
   - Option 1: $_______
   - Option 2: $_______
   - Option 3: $_______

5. **Which option would you recommend and why?** (Consider costs, convenience, crop value)
