## Section 2: pH and EC Management (10 questions)

11. **If nutrient solution pH is 7.8, iron is likely:**
    - A) Very available
    - B) Moderately available
    - C) Largely unavailable (precipitated)
    - D) Completely absorbed
    - **Answer: C** (1 point)

12. **Alkalinity measures:**
    - A) pH level
    - B) Buffering capacity
    - C) Nutrient concentration
    - D) Salt content
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

13. **Target alkalinity for aquaponic systems is:**
    - A) 0-20 ppm as CaCO₃
    - B) 60-120 ppm as CaCO₃
    - C) 500+ ppm
    - D) Alkalinity doesn't matter
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

14. **EC measures:**
    - A) pH
    - B) Only nitrogen
    - C) Total dissolved salts
    - D) Water temperature
    - **Answer: C** (1 point)

15. **Typical EC for lettuce production is:**
    - A) 0.2-0.5 mS/cm
    - B) 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
    - C) 3.0-4.0 mS/cm
    - D) 5.0+ mS/cm
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

16. **Typical EC for tomatoes during fruiting is:**
    - A) 0.5-1.0 mS/cm
    - B) 1.0-2.0 mS/cm
    - C) 3.0-4.0 mS/cm (up to 5.0 for flavor)
    - D) 10+ mS/cm
    - **Answer: C** (1 point)

17. **If EC is steadily rising, the solution indicates:**
    - A) Plants using more nutrients than water
    - B) Plants using more water than nutrients
    - C) Perfect balance
    - D) System failure
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

18. **To lower pH in hydroponics, use:**
    - A) Baking soda
    - B) Potassium hydroxide
    - C) Phosphoric acid
    - D) Calcium carbonate
    - **Answer: C** (1 point)

19. **In aquaponics, pH typically drifts:**
    - A) Upward
    - B) Downward (nitrification produces acid)
    - C) Remains stable
    - D) Randomly
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

20. **Before diagnosing a nutrient deficiency, always check:**
    - A) Plant height
    - B) pH (availability issue?)
    - C) Neighbor's garden
    - D) Moon phase
    - **Answer: B** (1 point)

    - A) Calcium deficiency
    - B) Iron deficiency
    - C) Nitrogen deficiency

    - A) Nitrogen deficiency
