# Module 3 Quiz: Micronutrients Mastery

**Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Questions

1. **Which micronutrient is most commonly deficient in hydroponic and aquaponic systems?**
   - A) Copper
   - B) Boron
   - C) Iron
   - D) Molybdenum
   - **Answer: C**

2. **What is chelation and why is it important?**
   - A) Removal of nutrients
   - B) Binding of metal ions to keep them soluble and available
   - C) A type of plant disease
   - D) pH adjustment
   - **Answer: B**

3. **Which chelate is most stable at high pH (aquaponic systems)?**
   - A) Fe-EDTA
   - B) Fe-Citrate
   - C) Fe-EDDHA
   - D) No chelate needed
   - **Answer: C**

4. **Iron deficiency appears on which leaves?**
   - A) Old leaves
   - B) Middle leaves
   - C) New/young leaves
   - D) All leaves equally
   - **Answer: C**

5. **What is the key visual difference between iron and manganese deficiency?**
   - A) Mn has necrotic spots; Fe does not
   - B) Fe affects old leaves; Mn affects new
   - C) They are identical
   - D) Mn causes purple color
   - **Answer: A**

6. **High phosphorus levels commonly cause deficiency of which micronutrient?**
   - A) Iron
   - B) Boron
   - C) Zinc
   - D) Manganese
   - **Answer: C**

7. **Boron has a very narrow range between sufficiency and toxicity. What is the target range?**
   - A) 10-20 ppm
   - B) 0.3-0.6 ppm
   - C) 100-200 ppm
   - D) 5-10 ppm
   - **Answer: B**

8. **Which micronutrient becomes MORE available at higher pH (opposite of others)?**
   - A) Iron
   - B) Manganese
   - C) Molybdenum
   - D) Zinc
   - **Answer: C**

9. **Copper toxicity is a concern at what concentration in aquaponics?**
   - A) >0.3 ppm (toxic to fish)
   - B) >10 ppm
   - C) >50 ppm
   - D) Copper is never toxic
   - **Answer: A**

10. **What symptom indicates zinc deficiency?**
    - A) Marginal scorch
    - B) Rosetting (short internodes) and small leaves
    - C) Purple leaves
    - D) Root rot
    - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1-C, 2-B, 3-C, 4-C, 5-A, 6-C, 7-B, 8-C, 9-A, 10-B
