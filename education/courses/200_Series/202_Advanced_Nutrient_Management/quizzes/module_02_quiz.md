# Module 2 Quiz: Macronutrients Deep Dive

**Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Questions

1. **Which macronutrient is MOBILE in plants, meaning deficiency symptoms appear on older leaves first?**
   - A) Calcium
   - B) Nitrogen
   - C) Sulfur
   - D) Iron
   - **Answer: B**

2. **What is the primary visual symptom of potassium deficiency?**
   - A) Interveinal chlorosis on new leaves
   - B) Dark purple leaves
   - C) Marginal chlorosis and necrosis (scorched leaf edges)
   - D) Death of growing points
   - **Answer: C**

3. **Why does phosphorus become unavailable at pH levels above 7.5?**
   - A) It evaporates
   - B) It reacts with calcium and magnesium to form insoluble precipitates
   - C) Plants cannot absorb it at high pH
   - D) It converts to nitrogen
   - **Answer: B**

4. **What is the recommended K:Ca:Mg ratio for most crops?**
   - A) 1:1:1
   - B) 2:2:1
   - C) 4:2:1
   - D) 10:5:1
   - **Answer: C**

5. **Which form of nitrogen is preferred by most plants?**
   - A) Ammonium (NH₄⁺)
   - B) Nitrate (NO₃⁻)
   - C) Urea
   - D) Organic nitrogen
   - **Answer: B**

6. **Blossom end rot in tomatoes is caused by deficiency of which nutrient?**
   - A) Nitrogen
   - B) Potassium
   - C) Calcium
   - D) Magnesium
   - **Answer: C**

7. **What distinguishes magnesium deficiency from iron deficiency visually?**
   - A) Mg affects old leaves; Fe affects new leaves
   - B) Mg affects new leaves; Fe affects old leaves
   - C) They look identical
   - D) Mg causes red leaves; Fe causes yellow
   - **Answer: A**

8. **Why does phosphorus deficiency cause purple coloration in plants?**
   - A) P is naturally purple
   - B) Anthocyanin accumulates when P is deficient
   - C) It's actually a magnesium deficiency
   - D) Purple is the normal color
   - **Answer: B**

9. **In aquaponic systems, which macronutrient is most commonly deficient?**
   - A) Nitrogen
   - B) Sulfur
   - C) Potassium
   - D) Phosphorus (though K and Ca also common)
   - **Answer: C**

10. **What is the primary role of sulfur in plants?**
    - A) Photosynthesis
    - B) Component of proteins and amino acids
    - C) Water regulation
    - D) Flower production
    - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-C, 5-B, 6-C, 7-A, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
