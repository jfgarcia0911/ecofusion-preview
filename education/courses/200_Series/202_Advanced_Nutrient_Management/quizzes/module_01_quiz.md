## Questions

### Question 1
Which mechanism of nutrient delivery accounts for 70-90% of nitrogen uptake in plants?

A) Diffusion
B) Root interception
C) Mass flow
D) Active transport

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
What is the primary difference between active and passive transport in nutrient uptake?

A) Active transport is faster than passive transport
B) Active transport requires cellular energy (ATP)
C) Passive transport can move against concentration gradients
D) Passive transport only works for water

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC) is most relevant in which type of growing system?

A) Deep water culture (DWC)
B) Nutrient film technique (NFT)
C) Media-based systems (coco coir, peat)
D) Aeroponics

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is the optimal root zone temperature range for nutrient uptake?

A) 10-15°C (50-59°F)
B) 15-18°C (59-64°F)
C) 18-24°C (64-75°F)
D) 25-30°C (77-86°F)

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
Which nutrient's uptake is most severely reduced at temperatures below 15°C?

A) Nitrogen
B) Potassium
C) Phosphorus
D) Magnesium

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What is the minimum dissolved oxygen (DO) level recommended for optimal nutrient uptake?

A) 1-3 mg/L
B) 3-5 mg/L
C) 5-8 mg/L
D) 8-10 mg/L

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
Root hairs increase the surface area available for nutrient absorption by approximately:

A) 2-3 times
B) 5-20 times
C) 50-100 times
D) 200-500 times

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which of the following antagonistic relationships is most common?

A) High nitrogen reducing potassium uptake
B) High potassium reducing magnesium uptake
C) High calcium reducing nitrogen uptake
D) High phosphorus reducing nitrogen uptake

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
In the rhizosphere, what do root exudates primarily accomplish?

A) Kill beneficial bacteria
B) Increase pH dramatically
C) Mobilize nutrients and support beneficial microbes
D) Prevent all water uptake

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
Why is active transport particularly important in soilless growing systems?

A) Soilless systems have no nutrients
B) Plants in soilless systems don't need nutrients
C) Nutrients are often present at lower concentrations than in plant tissue
D) Passive transport doesn't work in water

**Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Mass flow
2. B - Active transport requires cellular energy (ATP)
3. C - Media-based systems
4. C - 18-24°C (64-75°F)
5. C - Phosphorus
6. C - 5-8 mg/L
7. B - 5-20 times
8. B - High potassium reducing magnesium uptake
9. C - Mobilize nutrients and support beneficial microbes
10. C - Nutrients are often present at lower concentrations than in plant tissue
