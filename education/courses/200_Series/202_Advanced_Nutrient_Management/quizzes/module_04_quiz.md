# Module 4 Quiz: Deficiency Diagnosis

**Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

1. **What is the first diagnostic question when observing symptoms?** A) pH, B) Which leaves affected (old vs. new)?, C) EC, D) Crop type - **Answer: B**

2. **Mobile nutrient deficiencies appear where first?** A) New leaves, B) Roots, C) Old/lower leaves, D) Flowers - **Answer: C**

3. **Immobile nutrient deficiencies appear where first?** A) Old leaves, B) New leaves/growing points, C) Stems, D) Roots - **Answer: B**

4. **How distinguish nitrogen from sulfur deficiency?** A) N affects old; S affects new leaves, B) N affects new; S affects old, C) Identical, D) Color only - **Answer: A**

5. **Classic magnesium deficiency symptom?** A) General yellowing, B) Interveinal chlorosis on OLD leaves, C) Interveinal on NEW leaves, D) Purple stems - **Answer: B**

6. **Calcium deficiency in lettuce appears as?** A) Yellow leaves, B) Tipburn, C) Root rot, D) Slow growth - **Answer: B**

7. **Iron vs. magnesium deficiency difference?** A) Fe on NEW leaves; Mg on OLD, B) Fe on old; Mg on new, C) Only color, D) Same - **Answer: A**

8. **Before adding nutrients, check what first?** A) Height, B) pH (availability), C) Temperature, D) Light - **Answer: B**

9. **Blossom end rot indicates which deficiency?** A) N, B) P, C) Calcium, D) Fe - **Answer: C**

10. **Time for improvement after correcting immobile deficiency?** A) Hours, B) New growth improves in 3-7 days, C) Immediate, D) Months - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-A, 5-B, 6-B, 7-A, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
