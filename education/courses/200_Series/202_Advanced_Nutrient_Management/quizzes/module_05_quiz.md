# Module 5 Quiz: Aquaponics Supplementation

**Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

1. **Which nutrient is ALWAYS needed in aquaponic systems?** A) Nitrogen, B) Iron, C) Phosphorus, D) Sulfur - **Answer: B**

2. **Which iron chelate is best for aquaponics (pH 6.8-7.2)?** A) Fe-EDTA, B) Fe-Citrate, C) Fe-DTPA or Fe-EDDHA, D) No chelate needed - **Answer: C**

3. **Typical iron consumption rate in aquaponics?** A) 0.1-0.5 ppm/week, B) 1-3 ppm/week, C) 5-10 ppm/week, D) No consumption - **Answer: B**

4. **Best potassium source for low-pH aquaponic systems?** A) KCl, B) K₂CO₃ (raises pH), C) KNO₃, D) No K needed - **Answer: B**

5. **When supplementing aquaponics, where should you add nutrients?** A) Directly in fish tank, B) In plant beds/sump (not fish tank), C) Doesn't matter, D) Don't supplement - **Answer: B**

6. **Safe copper level in aquaponic systems?** A) <0.3 ppm (toxic to fish above), B) <5 ppm, C) <50 ppm, D) Unlimited - **Answer: A**

7. **Why is potassium commonly deficient in aquaponics?** A) Fish eat it all, B) Fish feed is low in K relative to plant needs, C) It evaporates, D) pH destroys it - **Answer: B**

8. **How should large supplements be added in aquaponics?** A) All at once, B) Gradually over days, watching fish, C) Never add supplements, D) Only at night - **Answer: B**

9. **Calcium hydroxide serves dual purpose in aquaponics:** A) Adds Ca + raises pH, B) Adds Ca + lowers pH, C) Only adds Ca, D) Toxic to fish - **Answer: A**

10. **Which crops are best suited for aquaponics?** A) Heavy feeders only, B) Leafy greens and herbs (lower nutrient demand), C) Only fruit crops, D) No crops grow well - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-B, 5-B, 6-A, 7-B, 8-B, 9-A, 10-B
