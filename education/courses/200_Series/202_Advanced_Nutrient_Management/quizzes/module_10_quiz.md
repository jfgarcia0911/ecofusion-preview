## Questions

**1. What is the recommended EC range for lettuce production?**
   - a) 0.5-0.8 mS/cm
   - b) 1.0-1.6 mS/cm
   - c) 3.0-4.0 mS/cm
   - d) 5.0+ mS/cm

**2. How should the N:K ratio change for tomatoes during the fruiting stage?**
   - a) Increase nitrogen, decrease potassium
   - b) Shift to higher potassium, reduce nitrogen
   - c) Keep the ratio equal throughout growth
   - d) No changes needed during fruiting

**3. Basil flavor and essential oil content is enhanced by:**
   - a) Very high nitrogen levels
   - b) Moderate EC with lower nitrogen to concentrate oils
   - c) No nutrients at all
   - d) High phosphorus only

**4. Blossom end rot in tomatoes is prevented by:**
   - a) High nitrogen fertilization
   - b) Adequate calcium (>180 ppm) and consistent watering
   - c) Low pH below 5.0
   - d) Very high EC levels

**5. Which crops perform best in basic aquaponic systems?**
   - a) Heavy-feeding fruiting crops
   - b) Leafy greens and herbs
   - c) Only trees and perennials
   - d) Grain crops

**6. Mediterranean herbs like oregano and thyme prefer:**
   - a) Very low EC (0.5-0.8 mS/cm)
   - b) Higher EC (1.4-2.2 mS/cm) for enhanced flavor
   - c) No nutrients at all
   - d) Only nitrogen fertilization

**7. Strawberries require:**
   - a) High pH above 7.5
   - b) Lower pH (5.5-6.0) and moderate EC
   - c) Very high EC above 4.0 mS/cm
   - d) No iron supplementation

**8. During the tomato fruiting stage, nutrient management should:**
   - a) Increase nitrogen to support fruit growth
   - b) Decrease nitrogen and increase potassium
   - c) Stop all feeding
   - d) Only provide water

**9. Lettuce tipburn is prevented by:**
   - a) Reducing calcium levels
   - b) Adequate calcium (>150 ppm), moderate EC, and good airflow
   - c) Very high EC
   - d) Eliminating water

**10. Cherry tomatoes can tolerate:**
   - a) Only very low EC
   - b) Higher EC (3.5-5.0 mS/cm) for improved flavor
   - c) No nutrients
   - d) Only nitrogen

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 1.0-1.6 mS/cm** - Lettuce is a moderate-feeding crop that performs best at lower EC levels than fruiting vegetables.

2. **b) Shift to higher potassium, reduce nitrogen** - Fruiting requires more K for fruit development; excess N promotes foliage at the expense of fruit.

3. **b) Moderate EC with lower nitrogen to concentrate oils** - Slight nutrient stress and lower N concentrate essential oils that give basil its flavor and aroma.

4. **b) Adequate calcium (>180 ppm) and consistent watering** - BER results from calcium deficiency in developing fruit, often due to inconsistent water supply affecting Ca transport.

5. **b) Leafy greens and herbs** - These crops have moderate nutrient demands that match typical aquaponic nutrient availability.

6. **b) Higher EC (1.4-2.2 mS/cm) for enhanced flavor** - Mediterranean herbs evolved in low-nutrient soils; higher EC concentrates flavor compounds.

7. **b) Lower pH (5.5-6.0) and moderate EC** - Strawberries prefer slightly acidic conditions and are sensitive to high salts and chloride.

8. **b) Decrease nitrogen and increase potassium** - This shift supports fruit development, color, flavor, and shelf life rather than vegetative growth.

9. **b) Adequate calcium (>150 ppm), moderate EC, and good airflow** - Tipburn results from poor calcium transport to rapidly growing leaf margins, worsened by high humidity and low airflow.

10. **b) Higher EC (3.5-5.0 mS/cm) for improved flavor** - Cherry tomatoes tolerate and benefit from higher EC, producing more concentrated flavors and higher brix levels.
