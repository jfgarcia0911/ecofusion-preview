## pH Management

### Target pH Ranges

| System Type | Optimal pH | Acceptable Range | Action Required |
|-------------|------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Hydroponics** | 5.8-6.2 | 5.5-6.5 | <5.0 or >7.0 |
| **Aquaponics** | 6.4-7.0 | 6.0-7.2 | <5.8 or >7.5 |
| **Media (Coco)** | 5.8-6.2 | 5.5-6.5 | <5.0 or >7.0 |
| **Rockwool** | 5.5-6.0 | 5.0-6.5 | <4.5 or >7.0 |

### pH Adjusters

**To LOWER pH (pH Down):**

| Product | Pros | Cons | Typical Use |
|---------|------|------|-------------|
| **Phosphoric Acid** (H₃PO₄) | Adds P, stable | Can oversupply P long-term | 1-3 ml per 100 L |
| **Nitric Acid** (HNO₃) | Adds N | Strong, can oversupply N | 0.5-2 ml per 100 L |
| **Sulfuric Acid** (H₂SO₄) | Adds S, cheap | VERY dangerous | Use extreme caution |
| **Citric Acid** | Safe, organic | Weak, feeds microbes | 5-20 g per 100 L |

**To RAISE pH (pH Up):**

| Product | Pros | Cons | Typical Use |
|---------|------|------|-------------|
| **Potassium Hydroxide** (KOH) | Adds K, effective | Caustic | 1-3 ml per 100 L |
| **Potassium Carbonate** (K₂CO₃) | Adds K, buffers | More expensive | 5-15 g per 100 L |
| **Calcium Hydroxide** (Ca(OH)₂) | Adds Ca, cheap | Can cloud, precipitate | Aqua only, small amounts |

### pH Adjustment Protocol

1. **Test current pH** (calibrated meter)
2. **Dilute adjuster** in 1 L water first
3. **Add slowly** while mixing
4. **Wait 15-30 minutes**
5. **Retest** and adjust if needed
6. **Record** amount used

**Never add concentrated acid/base directly to reservoir!**

## EC Management

### Target EC by Crop

| Crop Category | EC Range (mS/cm) | Notes |
|---------------|------------------|-------|
| **Seedlings** | 0.8-1.2 | All crops - delicate roots |
| **Lettuce** | 1.0-1.6 | Low end for tender varieties |
| **Leafy Greens** | 1.4-2.0 | Spinach, kale, chard |
| **Herbs (Tender)** | 1.0-1.6 | Basil, cilantro, parsley |
| **Herbs (Mediterranean)** | 1.4-2.2 | Oregano, thyme, rosemary |
| **Tomatoes (Veg)** | 2.0-2.5 | Vegetative growth |
| **Tomatoes (Fruiting)** | 3.0-5.0 | Increase for flavor |
| **Peppers** | 2.0-3.5 | Lower tolerance than tomatoes |
| **Cucumbers** | 1.8-2.8 | Salt-sensitive |
| **Strawberries** | 1.0-1.6 | Low EC requirement |
| **Aquaponics (All)** | 0.5-1.2 | Limited by fish tolerance |

### EC Conversions

```
EC (mS/cm) × 1000 = μS/cm
EC (mS/cm) × 500-700 = ppm (meter dependent)

Example:
2.0 mS/cm = 2000 μS/cm = 1000-1400 ppm

Know your meter's conversion factor!
```

### EC Trending

**EC RISING over time:**
```
Day 1: 2.0 mS/cm
Day 3: 2.2 mS/cm
Day 5: 2.4 mS/cm → ACTION NEEDED
```
- **Cause:** More water uptake than nutrients
- **Action:** Top off with plain water
- **Or:** Dilute solution if >150% target

**EC FALLING over time:**
```
Day 1: 2.0 mS/cm
Day 3: 1.8 mS/cm
Day 5: 1.6 mS/cm → ACTION NEEDED
```
- **Cause:** More nutrient uptake than water
- **Action:** Top off with nutrient solution
- **Or:** Add stock solution to increase

**EC STABLE:**
```
Day 1-7: 2.0 ± 0.1 mS/cm → IDEAL
```
- **Meaning:** Balanced water and nutrient uptake
- **Action:** Maintain current program

## Alkalinity & Buffering

### Alkalinity Targets

| System | Target Alkalinity (ppm as CaCO₃) | Why |
|--------|----------------------------------|-----|
| **Hydroponics** | 0-40 (low buffering) | Easier pH control |
| **Aquaponics** | 60-120 (moderate) | Stabilizes pH |
| **High** | >150 | pH very stable but hard to adjust |
| **Low** | <30 | pH swings wildly |

### Testing & Adjusting Alkalinity

**To Increase Alkalinity (Aquaponics):**
- Add potassium bicarbonate (KHCO₃)
- Add calcium carbonate (CaCO₃)
- Add sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃) - less preferred

**To Decrease Alkalinity (Rare):**
- Use RO water
- Add acid (reduces carbonates)
- Usually not needed in hydroponics

## Daily Monitoring Checklist

### Morning Routine (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Check pH** - record value
- [ ] **Check EC** - record value
- [ ] **Check temperature** - root zone and air
- [ ] **Visual plant inspection** - any symptoms?
- [ ] **Observe roots** - color, health (if accessible)

### Adjustment Decision Tree

```
START

pH Outside Range?
├─ YES → Adjust pH (see protocols above)
└─ NO → Continue

EC Outside Range?
├─ YES → Adjust EC (add water or nutrients)
└─ NO → Continue

Plant Symptoms?
├─ YES → Diagnose (use deficiency guide)
└─ NO → System looks good!

Record all data
```

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Problem: pH Won't Stabilize

**Symptoms:** pH swings daily, hard to maintain

**Causes:**
- Low alkalinity (no buffering)
- High alkalinity in source water
- Algae growth

**Solutions:**
- Test alkalinity
- Add buffer (aquaponics) or use RO (hydroponics)
- Control algae (shade, reduce P)

### Problem: EC Climbs Rapidly

**Symptoms:** EC increases 0.5+ mS/cm per day

**Causes:**
- High transpiration (hot, low humidity)
- Small reservoir (concentration effect)
- Over-fertilization

**Solutions:**
- Top off with water only
- Increase reservoir size
- Reduce stock solution strength

### Problem: Both pH and EC Unstable

**Symptoms:** Both parameters vary widely

**Causes:**
- Inconsistent watering
- System leaks
- Variable environmental conditions
- Automated systems malfunctioning

**Solutions:**
- Check for leaks
- Stabilize environment
- Regular monitoring schedule
- Verify equipment function

## Emergency Corrections

### pH Crash (<5.0)

1. **Immediate:** Add pH up slowly
2. **Target:** 5.8-6.0 initially (not full target)
3. **Monitor:** Check every 2-4 hours
4. **Gradual:** Bring to target over 12-24 hours

### pH Spike (>7.5)

1. **Check:** Plants and fish (if aqua)
2. **Immediate:** Add pH down slowly
3. **Investigate:** Why did it spike?
4. **Monitor:** Every 2-4 hours until stable

### EC Too High (2x Target or More)

1. **Immediate:** Dilute 50% with water
2. **Check:** Plant stress (wilting?)
3. **Flush:** Media-based systems
4. **Reset:** Consider full solution change

### EC Too Low (<50% Target)

1. **Add:** Stock solution incrementally
2. **Mix:** Thoroughly between additions
3. **Retest:** After each addition
4. **Target:** Bring to low end of range first
