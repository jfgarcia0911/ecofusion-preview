# Grading Rubric
## Course 202: Advanced Nutrient Management

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 202. The total possible points is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Deficiency Diagnosis Practice | 15 | 13.6% | In-class visual symptom identification |
| Assignment 2: Nutrient Solution Formulation | 20 | 18.2% | In-class mixing calculations |
| Assignment 3: pH and EC Management Exercise | 25 | 22.7% | In-class monitoring and adjustment practice |
| Assignment 4: Water Quality Analysis | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home water test interpretation |
| Assignment 5: Comprehensive Nutrient Management Plan | 30 | 27.3% | Complete crop nutrition program |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

---

## Assignment 1: Deficiency Diagnosis Practice (15 points)

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Visual Identification** (5 pts) | Correctly identifies 9-10 symptoms | Identifies 7-8 correctly | Identifies 5-6 correctly | <5 correct identifications |
| **Deficiency vs. Toxicity** (5 pts) | Accurately distinguishes all cases | 1-2 errors | 3-4 errors | >4 errors |
| **Corrective Actions** (5 pts) | Appropriate, specific solutions | Generally correct solutions | Vague or partially correct | Incorrect solutions |

---

## Assignment 2: Nutrient Solution Formulation (20 points)

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Calculation Accuracy** (5 pts) | All calculations correct | 1-2 minor errors | 3-4 errors | >4 errors |
| **Target EC Achievement** (5 pts) | Within ±0.1 mS/cm of target | Within ±0.2 mS/cm | Within ±0.4 mS/cm | >0.4 mS/cm off |
| **Nutrient Ratios** (5 pts) | Optimal ratios for crop | Minor imbalances | Significant imbalances | Poor ratios |
| **Safety & Documentation** (5 pts) | Complete, proper procedures | Minor documentation gaps | Safety concerns noted | Unsafe practices |

---

## Assignment 3: pH and EC Management Exercise (25 points)

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (6-7 pts) | Good (4-5 pts) | Fair (2-3 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **pH Adjustment** (7 pts) | Accurate calculations and methodology | Minor calculation errors | Significant errors | Incorrect approach |
| **EC Optimization** (6 pts) | Appropriate EC for growth stage | Close to optimal | Suboptimal levels | Inappropriate EC |
| **Monitoring Schedule** (6 pts) | Comprehensive, practical schedule | Good schedule with gaps | Basic schedule | Inadequate monitoring |
| **Problem Solving** (6 pts) | Excellent troubleshooting logic | Good problem-solving | Limited problem-solving | Poor troubleshooting |

---

## Assignment 4: Water Quality Analysis (20 points)

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Data Interpretation** (5 pts) | Thorough, accurate analysis | Good analysis with minor gaps | Surface-level analysis | Poor interpretation |
| **Problem Identification** (5 pts) | All issues identified correctly | Most issues identified | Some issues missed | Major issues missed |
| **Treatment Recommendations** (5 pts) | Appropriate, specific solutions | General correct recommendations | Vague recommendations | Incorrect solutions |
| **Documentation** (5 pts) | Professional, complete report | Good report with minor gaps | Basic report | Incomplete report |

---

## Assignment 5: Comprehensive Nutrient Management Plan (30 points)

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (7-8 pts) | Good (5-6 pts) | Fair (3-4 pts) | Poor (0-2 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Crop-Specific Programs** (8 pts) | Detailed programs for all crops | Good programs with minor gaps | Basic programs | Inadequate programs |
| **Monitoring Protocols** (7 pts) | Comprehensive monitoring plan | Good plan with some gaps | Basic plan | Insufficient plan |
| **Troubleshooting Guide** (8 pts) | Thorough, practical guide | Good guide | Basic guide | Inadequate guide |
| **Professional Presentation** (7 pts) | Excellent organization and clarity | Good presentation | Adequate presentation | Poor presentation |
