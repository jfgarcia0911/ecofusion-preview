## Key Success Strategies

### Master the Fundamentals
- Understand nutrient mobility (mobile vs. immobile)
- Learn optimal pH ranges for nutrient availability
- Memorize target EC ranges for major crop categories

### Use Visual References
- Study deficiency photos extensively
- Create your own reference cards
- Practice with real plant observations

### Practice Calculations
- Work through sample formulation problems
- Use dimensional analysis for unit conversions
- Double-check all math before submission

### Think Systematically
- Follow troubleshooting frameworks
- Consider multiple factors simultaneously
- Document your reasoning process

## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Deficiency Diagnosis Practice (15 points)
Identify nutrient deficiencies from 10 plant photos, distinguish deficiency from toxicity, and recommend corrective actions. Tests your visual diagnostic skills.

### Assignment 2: Nutrient Solution Formulation (20 points)
Calculate and mix a complete nutrient solution to achieve target EC and nutrient ratios for a specific crop. Demonstrates formulation competency.

### Assignment 3: pH and EC Management Exercise (25 points)
Develop monitoring schedules, calculate pH adjustments, optimize EC for growth stages, and troubleshoot common problems. Shows system management skills.

### Assignment 4: Water Quality Analysis (20 points)
Interpret a complete water quality report, identify issues, and recommend treatment strategies. Applies analytical thinking to real-world data.

### Assignment 5: Comprehensive Nutrient Management Plan (30 points)
Create a complete nutrition program including crop-specific formulations, monitoring protocols, deficiency guides, and troubleshooting procedures. Integrates all course concepts.
