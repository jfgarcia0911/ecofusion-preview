```
KEY DIAGNOSTIC RULE:
- MOBILE nutrients → Deficiency on OLD leaves (plant moves nutrient to new growth)
- IMMOBILE nutrients → Deficiency on NEW leaves (cannot be relocated)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

```
EFFECTS OF EC:

LOW EC (<1.0)              MODERATE EC (1.5-2.5)        HIGH EC (>3.0)
• Faster growth            • Balanced growth             • Slower growth
• Larger size              • Good quality                • Smaller size
• Bland flavor             • Moderate flavor             • Intense flavor
• Soft texture             • Good texture                • Firm texture
• Short shelf life         • Good shelf life             • Long shelf life
• Risk: Deficiency         • Optimal for most            • Risk: Salt stress

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MANAGEMENT:
• EC rising → Add water only
• EC falling → Add nutrient solution
• EC stable → System balanced (ideal)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```
