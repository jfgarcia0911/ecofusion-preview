## Course Description

Develop a comprehensive business plan for your sustainable agriculture venture. This course covers market analysis, financial projections, operational planning, and funding strategies specific to aquaponics and controlled environment agriculture businesses.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Conduct thorough market research and competitive analysis
2. Develop realistic financial projections and models
3. Create operational and marketing plans
4. Identify funding sources and prepare compelling pitches
5. Complete a professional, investor-ready business plan

## Business Plan Template Outline

### 1. Executive Summary
- Business concept
- Mission and vision
- Products/services
- Target market
- Competitive advantage
- Financial highlights
- Funding request

### 2. Company Description
- Legal structure
- Ownership
- Location
- History (if applicable)

### 3. Market Analysis
- Industry overview
- Target market definition
- Market size and trends
- Customer profiles
- Competitive landscape

### 4. Products & Services
- Product descriptions
- Pricing strategy
- Production capabilities
- Quality standards

### 5. Marketing & Sales
- Marketing strategy
- Sales channels
- Promotional plans
- Customer acquisition

### 6. Operations
- Production process
- Facility requirements
- Equipment needs
- Staffing plan
- Key suppliers

### 7. Management & Organization
- Organization structure
- Key personnel
- Advisory board
- Hiring plan

### 8. Financial Plan
- Startup costs
- Revenue projections
- Expense projections
- Cash flow forecast
- Break-even analysis
- Funding requirements

### 9. Appendices
- Supporting documents
- Market research data
- Technical specifications
- Resumes
