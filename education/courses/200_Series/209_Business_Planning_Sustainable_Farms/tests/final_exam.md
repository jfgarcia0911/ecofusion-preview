## Section B: Short Answer (10 points)

### Question 41 (2 points)
**List and briefly explain three key components of the Business Model Canvas.**

**Model Answer:**
1. **Value Proposition:** The unique value you deliver to customers that solves their problem or meets their need
2. **Customer Segments:** The specific groups of people or organizations you serve, defined by demographics and needs
3. **Revenue Streams:** How you generate income from each customer segment (sales, subscriptions, fees, etc.)

*Grading: 0.67 points for each correct component with brief explanation*

---

### Question 42 (2 points)
**Explain the difference between TAM, SAM, and SOM in market sizing.**

**Model Answer:**
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Total revenue opportunity if you had 100% market share
- **SAM (Serviceable Available Market):** Portion of TAM you can realistically reach given your geography, channel, and product
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** Realistic market share you can capture in the near term

*Grading: 0.67 points for each correct definition*

---

### Question 43 (2 points)
**What are the three main approaches to pricing and when would you use each?**

**Model Answer:**
1. **Cost-Plus:** Add desired margin to costs - use when costs are well-known and market will bear it
2. **Competitive Pricing:** Price relative to competitors - use in commodity markets with clear competition
3. **Value-Based:** Price based on customer perceived value - use when you have clear differentiation and can justify premium

*Grading: 0.67 points for each approach with appropriate use case*

---

### Question 44 (2 points)
**Why is cash flow often more critical than profitability for farm startups?**

**Model Answer:**
Cash flow is critical because you need actual cash to pay bills, employees, and suppliers, even if you're profitable on paper. A business can be profitable but fail due to cash shortages if:
- Customers pay on net-30 or net-60 terms
- Seasonal revenue patterns create gaps
- Large upfront investments are needed
- Revenue projections don't materialize as quickly as expected

*Grading: 1 point for explaining need for cash to pay bills, 1 point for giving specific examples*

---

### Question 45 (2 points)
**What is the difference between debt financing and equity financing, and what is one advantage of each?**

**Model Answer:**

**Debt Financing:**
- Borrowing money that must be repaid with interest
- Advantage: You keep 100% ownership and control

**Equity Financing:**
- Selling ownership stake in exchange for capital
- Advantage: No monthly payments or personal guarantee; investors share risk

*Grading: 0.5 points for each correct definition, 0.5 points for each advantage*
