## PART 1: Customer Interviews (10 points)

### Requirements

**Conduct 5-10 interviews with potential customers**

**Who to Interview:**
- If B2C (CSA, farmers markets): Home consumers
- If B2B (restaurants, retailers): Chefs, buyers, managers
- Mix of: Current customers (if pilot), target customers, adjacent customers

---

### Interview Guide Template

**Opening (2 minutes):**
"Hi, I'm [name] and I'm planning to start [brief description]. I'm conducting research to understand customer needs. This will take about 15 minutes. Can I ask you some questions?"

**Current Behavior (5 minutes):**

1. Where do you currently buy [product category]?

2. How often do you purchase [product]?

3. How much do you typically spend per [week/order]?

4. What do you like about your current supplier/solution?

5. What frustrates you about your current options?

---

**Needs & Preferences (5 minutes):**

6. What characteristics are most important to you in [product]?
   (Rank: Quality, Price, Freshness, Local, Organic, Convenience, etc.)

7. How do you define "quality" for [product]?

8. What would make you switch to a new supplier?

9. How important is [your key differentiator] to you? (1-10 scale)

---

**Pricing (3 minutes):**

10. What do you currently pay for [product]?

11. Would you pay more for [benefit you provide]? How much more?

12. What price would be too high? Too low (suspicious)?

---

**Your Solution (3 minutes):**

13. [Briefly describe your offering] Does this interest you?

14. What concerns would you have about trying this?

15. If available today, would you purchase? Why or why not?

---

**Closing (2 minutes):**

16. Is there anything else about [product category] I should know?

17. Can I follow up with you as I develop this business?

18. Do you know anyone else I should talk to?

**Thank them for their time!**

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

**Interview Tips:**
- Practice your script before real interviews
- Record responses (with permission) or take detailed notes
- Listen more than you talk
- Ask "why" to dig deeper
- Don't pitch - just learn
- Thank people for their time

**Research Tips:**
- Use multiple data sources for TAM
- Be conservative in your estimates
- Document all assumptions
- Look for contradictory data
- Interview diverse customer types

**Report Tips:**
- Use visuals (charts, graphs)
- Be concise and clear
- Back claims with data
- Acknowledge limitations
- Focus on actionable insights

## EXAMPLE: Sample Interview Insight

**Interview #3 - Restaurant Chef**

"I absolutely want to serve local greens, but my current supplier only has them May through October. In winter, I have to use California greens that arrive 5-7 days after harvest. They're OK, but not what my customers expect. If someone could provide truly fresh, local greens year-round, I'd pay 30% more than what I pay for conventional. The consistency and story would be worth it."

**Insight:** Clear gap in winter supply, willingness to pay premium validated, story/provenance matters to restaurants.
