### 8.3 Zoning & Land Use

#### Zoning Compliance

**Common Zoning Categories:**
- Agricultural (Ag) - Most permissive for farming
- Residential - May restrict commercial farming
- Commercial - Allowed but may have specific requirements
- Industrial - Check local ordinances

**Zoning Issues to Research:**
- Is commercial agriculture allowed?
- Greenhouse/structure size limits
- Setback requirements
- Water discharge regulations
- Noise restrictions
- Odor restrictions
- Parking requirements
- Signage limitations

**Process:**
1. Check current zoning designation
2. Review permitted uses
3. Apply for variance if needed
4. Attend zoning board hearing (if required)
5. Get approval in writing

**Timeline:** 1-6 months
**Cost:** $0-$5,000 (for variances)

### 8.5 Food Safety Regulations

#### FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)

**Who Must Comply:**
- Farms with >$25,000/year in produce sales
- Direct-to-consumer sales may be exempt
- Some exemptions for very small farms

**Key Requirements:**

**1. Produce Safety Rule**
- Agricultural water quality testing
- Biological soil amendments (compost)
- Worker health and hygiene
- Equipment and tools sanitation

**2. Preventive Controls**
- Written food safety plan
- Hazard analysis
- Preventive controls
- Monitoring procedures
- Corrective actions
- Verification activities

**Training Requirements:**
- Qualified individual must complete PCQI training
- Farm workers need basic food safety training

**Timeline:** Varies by farm size
**Cost:** $500-$5,000 for compliance

---

#### Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) Certification

**What is GAP?**
- Voluntary food safety audit program
- USDA-verified
- Many wholesale buyers require it

**What's Audited:**
- Worker training and hygiene
- Water quality and testing
- Soil amendments
- Equipment sanitation
- Field sanitation
- Pest control
- Record keeping

**Process:**
1. Complete pre-assessment
2. Implement food safety practices
3. Maintain records (6-12 months)
4. Schedule USDA audit
5. Pass audit, receive certification
6. Annual renewal

**Cost:** $600-$2,000/year
**Timeline:** 3-12 months to prepare

**Benefits:**
- Access to wholesale markets
- Premium pricing potential
- Reduced liability
- Improved operations

### 8.7 Organic Certification (Optional)

#### USDA Organic Certification

**When Required:**
- Only if using "organic" on labels/marketing
- Sales >$5,000/year of organic products

**Process:**
1. Implement organic practices (36 months for land)
2. Develop Organic System Plan
3. Choose certifying agent
4. Submit application and fees
5. Pass inspection
6. Receive certification
7. Annual renewal and inspections

**Requirements:**
- No synthetic pesticides/fertilizers
- Detailed record keeping
- Buffer zones from conventional farms
- Organic seed sources (when available)

**Timeline:** 3+ years (including transition period)
**Cost:** $500-$2,000/year depending on size

**Aquaponics Note:** Organic certification for aquaponics is complex - research current regulations

### 8.8 Labor & Employment Regulations

#### When You Hire Employees

**Federal Requirements:**

| Requirement | Agency | Notes |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| W-4 Forms | IRS | All employees |
| I-9 Verification | USCIS | Verify work eligibility |
| Payroll Taxes | IRS | Withholding, FICA, FUTA |
| Workers' Compensation | State | Usually required for 1+ employees |
| Unemployment Insurance | State | Tax rate varies by state |
| OSHA Compliance | OSHA | Workplace safety |
| Wage & Hour Laws | DOL | Minimum wage, overtime |

**State Requirements:**
- State withholding tax
- State unemployment tax
- State-specific labor laws
- Agricultural exemptions (vary by state)

**Record Keeping:**
- Time sheets
- Payroll records (3+ years)
- Tax filings
- Safety training documentation

**Cost:** Budget $500-$2,000 for setup + ongoing payroll service

### 8.11 Ongoing Compliance

#### Annual Requirements

**Calendar of Compliance:**

```
JANUARY
├─ File previous year tax returns
├─ Renew business license
└─ Review insurance coverage

MARCH
├─ Submit quarterly payroll taxes (if applicable)
├─ Review food safety plan
└─ Update employee handbook

JUNE
├─ Mid-year financial review
├─ Payroll tax filing
└─ Water quality testing

SEPTEMBER
├─ Payroll tax filing
├─ Prepare for annual inspections
└─ Renew certifications

DECEMBER
├─ Issue W-2s (by Jan 31)
├─ Close books for year
└─ Plan for next year compliance
```
