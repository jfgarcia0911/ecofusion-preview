## Capstone Project Requirements

### Project Overview

The capstone project is the culmination of Course 209. You will create a complete, investor-ready business plan for your sustainable farm business and present it to the class.

**Components:**
1. Written Business Plan (40-70 pages)
2. Pitch Deck (15-20 slides)
3. 15-minute Presentation
4. Q&A Response (10 minutes)

**Total Points:** 80 (of 300 course points)

---

### Part 1: Written Business Plan (50 points)

#### Required Sections

```
YOUR COMPLETE BUSINESS PLAN

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (2 pages)
   ├─ Business concept
   ├─ Market opportunity
   ├─ Competitive advantage
   ├─ Financial highlights
   └─ Funding request

II. COMPANY DESCRIPTION (3-5 pages)
    ├─ Mission, vision, values
    ├─ Legal structure
    ├─ Location and facilities
    ├─ Products/services overview
    └─ Competitive advantages

III. MARKET ANALYSIS (8-12 pages)
     ├─ Industry overview
     ├─ Target market definition
     ├─ Customer segments (2-3 personas)
     ├─ Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
     ├─ Market trends
     ├─ Competitive analysis (4-6 competitors)
     └─ Market entry strategy

IV. BUSINESS MODEL (5-8 pages)
    ├─ Revenue streams
    ├─ Pricing strategy
    ├─ Sales channels
    ├─ Customer relationships
    ├─ Key activities
    ├─ Key resources
    ├─ Key partnerships
    └─ Business Model Canvas

V. MARKETING & SALES (6-10 pages)
   ├─ Marketing strategy
   ├─ Sales strategy
   ├─ Customer acquisition plan
   ├─ Distribution channels
   ├─ Brand positioning
   └─ Marketing budget

VI. OPERATIONS PLAN (8-12 pages)
    ├─ Production process
    ├─ Facility requirements
    ├─ Equipment needs
    ├─ Supply chain
    ├─ Quality control
    ├─ Technology/automation
    ├─ Regulatory compliance
    └─ Permits/licenses needed

VII. MANAGEMENT & ORGANIZATION (4-6 pages)
     ├─ Organizational structure
     ├─ Management team
     ├─ Advisory board
     ├─ Staffing plan
     ├─ Professional services
     └─ Human resources plan

VIII. FINANCIAL PLAN (10-15 pages)
      ├─ Startup costs breakdown
      ├─ Funding sources
      ├─ Revenue projections (3-5 years)
      ├─ Operating expense budget
      ├─ Pro forma income statement
      ├─ Cash flow projections
      ├─ Balance sheet projections
      ├─ Break-even analysis
      ├─ Key financial metrics
      ├─ Sensitivity analysis
      └─ Assumptions documented

IX. RISK MANAGEMENT (3-5 pages)
    ├─ Risk identification
    ├─ Risk mitigation strategies
    ├─ Insurance requirements
    ├─ Contingency plans
    └─ Emergency response

X. IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE (2-3 pages)
   ├─ Major milestones
   ├─ Gantt chart
   ├─ Critical path
   └─ Success metrics

XI. APPENDICES
    ├─ Resumes of key team members
    ├─ Letters of support (3-5)
    ├─ Market research data
    ├─ Technical specifications
    ├─ Sample marketing materials
    ├─ Legal documents
    └─ Detailed financial model
```

---

#### Grading Rubric - Written Plan (50 points)

| Criteria | Points | Excellent (Full Points) | Good (80%) | Needs Work (60%) |
|----------|--------|-------------------------|------------|------------------|
| **Executive Summary** | 5 | Compelling, concise, complete | Clear but could be stronger | Missing elements or unclear |
| **Market Analysis** | 10 | Thorough research, data-backed | Good research, some gaps | Weak research, unsupported |
| **Financial Projections** | 12 | Realistic, detailed, defensible | Reasonable but some issues | Unrealistic or incomplete |
| **Operations Plan** | 8 | Detailed, feasible, well-thought | Clear plan, minor gaps | Vague or unrealistic |
| **Competitive Analysis** | 5 | Thorough, positioning clear | Good analysis, positioning okay | Weak competitive understanding |
| **Professional Quality** | 5 | Polished, error-free, well-formatted | Minor errors, good format | Multiple errors, poor format |
| **Completeness** | 5 | All sections thorough | All sections present, some light | Missing sections or very brief |

**Total: 50 points**

### Part 3: Presentation (15 points)

#### Presentation Format

**Time Allocation:**
- Presentation: 15 minutes
- Q&A: 10 minutes
- Feedback: 5 minutes
- **Total: 30 minutes**

**Setup:**
- Present to class (or panel)
- Use pitch deck
- No reading from notes
- Professional dress recommended

**Requirements:**
- Stay within 15 minutes (+/- 1 minute)
- Cover all key points
- Demonstrate knowledge of business
- Answer questions confidently

---

#### Grading Rubric - Presentation (15 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Delivery** | 5 | Confident, clear, engaging, good pace |
| **Content** | 5 | Thorough coverage, demonstrates understanding |
| **Timing** | 2 | Stays within 14-16 minute window |
| **Q&A Handling** | 3 | Thoughtful, direct answers to questions |

**Total: 15 points**

## Peer Review Process

### Peer Feedback (Optional, Extra Credit)

**Assignment:**
Review 2 classmates' business plans

**Requirements:**
- Read complete plan (1-2 hours each)
- Complete feedback form
- Focus on constructive criticism
- Identify strengths and improvement areas

**Feedback Form Sections:**
1. Overall Impression (1-5 stars)
2. Three Strengths
3. Three Areas for Improvement
4. Most Concerning Gap or Risk
5. Most Exciting Opportunity
6. One Question You'd Ask

**Extra Credit:** 5 points per quality review (max 10 points)

---

### Incorporating Feedback

**After Receiving Feedback:**

**1. Analyze All Feedback**
- Instructor comments
- Peer reviews
- Q&A themes
- Common concerns

**2. Prioritize Changes**
- Critical gaps (fix immediately)
- Important improvements (address soon)
- Nice-to-have enhancements (time permitting)

**3. Revise and Resubmit**
- Optional final revision due 1 week after presentation
- Can improve grade by up to 10%
- Shows responsiveness to feedback
