# Business Plan Checklist
## Course 209: Business Planning for Sustainable Farms

---

## PRE-PLANNING ESSENTIALS

**Market Research:**
- [ ] Conducted 15+ customer interviews
- [ ] Identified and profiled 2-3 customer segments
- [ ] Calculated TAM, SAM, SOM
- [ ] Analyzed 4-6 competitors
- [ ] Validated pricing through test sales or surveys
- [ ] Documented market trends and growth data

**Concept Validation:**
- [ ] Secured letters of intent or pre-sales
- [ ] Tested product/service with real customers
- [ ] Confirmed unique value proposition
- [ ] Identified clear competitive advantages

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (2 pages)

- [ ] Compelling opening hook
- [ ] Clear business concept description
- [ ] Market opportunity quantified (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- [ ] Unique value proposition stated
- [ ] Business model explained
- [ ] Team qualifications highlighted
- [ ] Financial highlights (3-5 year projections)
- [ ] Funding request specified (amount, use, structure)
- [ ] Can stand alone (reader can understand without rest of plan)
- [ ] Written LAST after all other sections complete

---

## COMPANY DESCRIPTION (3-5 pages)

- [ ] Mission statement (1-2 sentences)
- [ ] Vision statement (1 paragraph)
- [ ] Core values listed (3-5 values)
- [ ] Company history/progress to date
- [ ] Legal structure specified (LLC, S-Corp, etc.)
- [ ] Ownership breakdown documented
- [ ] Location and facilities described
- [ ] Products/services overview
- [ ] Competitive advantages listed

---

## MARKET ANALYSIS (8-12 pages)

**Industry Overview:**
- [ ] Industry size and growth rate
- [ ] Key trends documented
- [ ] Major players identified
- [ ] Regulatory environment described
- [ ] Technology trends noted

**Target Market:**
- [ ] Demographics specified
- [ ] Psychographics described
- [ ] Geographic scope defined
- [ ] Market size calculated (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- [ ] 2-3 detailed customer personas created

**Competitive Analysis:**
- [ ] 4-6 competitors analyzed
- [ ] Competitive positioning map included
- [ ] Strengths and weaknesses documented
- [ ] Market gaps identified
- [ ] Differentiation strategy clear

---

## BUSINESS MODEL (5-8 pages)

- [ ] Complete Business Model Canvas included
- [ ] Value proposition clearly articulated
- [ ] Revenue streams detailed (2-4 streams)
- [ ] Pricing strategy explained and justified
- [ ] Sales channels specified
- [ ] Customer relationship approaches described
- [ ] Key activities listed
- [ ] Key resources identified
- [ ] Key partnerships documented
- [ ] Cost structure outlined

---

## MARKETING & SALES (6-10 pages)

**Marketing Strategy:**
- [ ] Brand positioning defined
- [ ] Marketing channels specified
- [ ] Customer acquisition strategy detailed
- [ ] Marketing budget allocated
- [ ] Promotional plans outlined
- [ ] Content/social media strategy

**Sales Strategy:**
- [ ] Sales process mapped
- [ ] Sales funnel/conversion rates estimated
- [ ] Sales team structure (if applicable)
- [ ] Sales forecast by channel
- [ ] Customer retention strategy

---

## OPERATIONS PLAN (8-12 pages)

**Production:**
- [ ] Production process detailed step-by-step
- [ ] Capacity calculations documented
- [ ] Quality control procedures specified
- [ ] Technology/equipment described

**Facility:**
- [ ] Location justified
- [ ] Facility specifications detailed
- [ ] Layout/floor plan included
- [ ] Scalability addressed

**Supply Chain:**
- [ ] Key suppliers identified
- [ ] Backup suppliers listed
- [ ] Inventory management approach
- [ ] Distribution/delivery plan

**Compliance:**
- [ ] Required permits/licenses listed
- [ ] Regulatory requirements documented
- [ ] Food safety plan overview
- [ ] Certification needs identified

---

## MANAGEMENT & ORGANIZATION (4-6 pages)

- [ ] Organizational chart included
- [ ] Management team bios (with photos)
- [ ] Relevant experience highlighted
- [ ] Advisory board members listed
- [ ] Gaps in team identified
- [ ] Hiring plan for Year 1-3
- [ ] Professional service providers listed
- [ ] Board composition (if applicable)

---

## FINANCIAL PLAN (10-15 pages)

**Startup Costs:**
- [ ] Complete itemized list
- [ ] One-time costs separated from recurring
- [ ] Working capital requirement calculated
- [ ] Contingency budget included (10-20%)
- [ ] Total capital requirement summed

**Revenue Projections:**
- [ ] Production capacity calculated
- [ ] Ramp-up assumptions documented
- [ ] Revenue by channel/product
- [ ] Monthly for Year 1
- [ ] Quarterly for Years 2-3
- [ ] Annual for Years 4-5
- [ ] Growth assumptions justified

**Operating Expenses:**
- [ ] Complete expense budget
- [ ] Fixed vs. variable costs identified
- [ ] Assumptions documented
- [ ] Benchmarked against industry norms

**Financial Statements:**
- [ ] Pro forma Income Statement (3-5 years)
- [ ] Cash Flow Statement (3-5 years)
- [ ] Balance Sheet (3-5 years)
- [ ] All statements consistent with each other

**Analysis:**
- [ ] Break-even analysis completed
- [ ] Key metrics dashboard included
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis (3 scenarios)
- [ ] Debt service coverage ratio (if loans)

**Funding:**
- [ ] Total funding needed specified
- [ ] Use of funds breakdown
- [ ] Capital stack documented
- [ ] Proposed terms outlined
- [ ] Repayment plan (if debt)
- [ ] ROI projections (if equity)

---

## RISK MANAGEMENT (3-5 pages)

- [ ] Production risks identified
- [ ] Market risks identified
- [ ] Financial risks identified
- [ ] Legal/regulatory risks identified
- [ ] Mitigation strategies for each risk
- [ ] Insurance requirements documented
- [ ] Contingency plans outlined

## IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE (2-3 pages)

- [ ] Major milestones identified

- [ ] Dependencies noted
- [ ] Success metrics defined
- [ ] Critical path identified

## FORMATTING & PRESENTATION

- [ ] Professional cover page with logo
- [ ] Table of contents with page numbers
- [ ] Consistent formatting throughout
- [ ] Page numbers on all pages
- [ ] Professional fonts (11-12 pt)
- [ ] Clear section headings
- [ ] Charts/graphs readable and labeled
- [ ] High-quality images
- [ ] Proper citations for external data
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors

## QUALITY CHECKS

**Before Submission:**
- [ ] Proofread 3+ times
- [ ] Numbers verified and consistent
- [ ] All calculations checked
- [ ] Reviewed by mentor/advisor
- [ ] Reviewed by accountant
- [ ] Reviewed by attorney (if needed)
- [ ] Feedback incorporated
- [ ] Executive summary updated last
- [ ] All sections complete
- [ ] Appendices attached
- [ ] PDF created and tested

**The "Mom Test":**
- [ ] Someone unfamiliar with the business can understand it
- [ ] Jargon explained or eliminated
- [ ] Value proposition clear
- [ ] Opportunity compelling
- [ ] Plan seems realistic and achievable
