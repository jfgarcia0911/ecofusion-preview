## Questions

### Question 1
**What are the four main categories of risk in farm businesses?**

A) Weather, market, financial, and production

B) Production, market, financial, and legal/liability ✓

C) Environmental, social, economic, and operational

D) Natural, human, technological, and political

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The four main risk categories are: Production (weather, pests, disease), Market (price fluctuations, demand), Financial (cash flow, debt), and Legal/Liability (lawsuits, compliance).*

---

### Question 2
**What type of insurance covers injuries to customers or visitors on your farm?**

A) Property insurance

B) Crop insurance

C) General liability insurance ✓

D) Workers' compensation

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: General liability insurance covers third-party bodily injury and property damage, protecting against customer injury lawsuits.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the deductible in an insurance policy?**

A) The maximum the insurance will pay

B) The amount you pay before insurance coverage begins ✓

C) The monthly premium cost

D) The policy expiration date

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The deductible is the out-of-pocket amount you must pay before insurance coverage kicks in. Higher deductibles lower premiums.*

---

### Question 4
**Which insurance is legally required if you have employees?**

A) General liability

B) Property insurance

C) Workers' compensation ✓

D) Product liability

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Workers' compensation insurance is legally required in most states if you have employees, covering work-related injuries and illnesses.*

---

### Question 5
**What does "business interruption insurance" cover?**

A) Lost income during forced closure due to covered event ✓

B) Employee theft

C) Equipment breakdowns

D) Market price declines

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Business interruption insurance covers lost revenue and ongoing expenses when business operations are disrupted by a covered event (fire, flood, etc.).*

---

### Question 6
**What is the best strategy for managing risk?**

A) Buy insurance for everything

B) Avoid all risky activities

C) Diversify using multiple risk management strategies ✓

D) Accept all risks without mitigation

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Effective risk management uses multiple strategies: avoidance, reduction, transfer (insurance), and acceptance, depending on the specific risk.*

---

### Question 7
**What type of crop insurance is available through USDA Risk Management Agency?**

A) Only for corn and soybeans

B) Multi-Peril Crop Insurance (MPCI) and other programs ✓

C) Only for organic farms

D) No federal crop insurance exists

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: USDA RMA offers various programs including Multi-Peril Crop Insurance (MPCI), Whole Farm Revenue Protection, and specialty crop coverage.*

---

### Question 8
**What is an "umbrella policy"?**

A) Weather insurance for greenhouses

B) Additional liability coverage beyond base policies ✓

C) Insurance that covers everything

D) A policy for multiple properties

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Umbrella policies provide additional liability coverage (typically $1M-$5M) beyond the limits of your general liability and auto policies.*

---

### Question 9
**How can you reduce insurance premiums while maintaining coverage?**

A) Cancel all policies

B) Increase deductibles and implement safety measures ✓

C) Only insure half your property

D) Never file claims

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Higher deductibles, safety improvements, risk reduction measures, and bundling policies can reduce premiums while maintaining adequate coverage.*

---

### Question 10
**What should be included in a farm emergency preparedness plan?**

A) Only fire evacuation routes

B) Contact lists, backup systems, emergency procedures, and recovery protocols ✓

C) Just insurance policy numbers

D) Marketing strategies

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Comprehensive emergency plans include emergency contacts, evacuation procedures, backup power/systems, communication protocols, and business continuity/recovery procedures.*
