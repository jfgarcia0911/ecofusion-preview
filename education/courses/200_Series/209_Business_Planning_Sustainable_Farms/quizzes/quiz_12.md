## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the typical recommended length for a comprehensive farm business plan?**

A) 5-10 pages

B) 15-25 pages

C) 25-40 pages ✓

D) 50+ pages

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Comprehensive business plans typically range from 25-40 pages, providing sufficient detail without being overwhelming. Shorter executive summaries (2-3 pages) are also useful.*

---

### Question 2
**Which section should be written LAST, even though it appears first in the plan?**

A) Market analysis

B) Executive summary ✓

C) Financial projections

D) Operations plan

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The executive summary should be written last because it summarizes all other sections. You can't summarize what you haven't written yet.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the primary purpose of a business plan?**

A) To impress investors with lengthy documents

B) To serve as a strategic roadmap and secure funding ✓

C) To fulfill legal requirements

D) To keep on a shelf for reference

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Business plans serve dual purposes: internal roadmap for decision-making and growth, and external tool to secure funding from lenders/investors.*

---

### Question 4
**How many years of financial projections are typically required in a business plan?**

A) 1 year

B) 2 years

C) 3-5 years ✓

D) 10 years

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Most business plans include 3-5 year financial projections, with Year 1 monthly, Years 2-3 quarterly, and Years 4-5 annually.*

---

### Question 5
**What should the Management & Organization section include?**

A) Only the org chart

B) Team bios, roles, advisors, and organizational structure ✓

C) Just owner information

D) Employee salary details

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: This section should demonstrate team competency through bios, define roles/responsibilities, identify advisors, and show organizational structure.*

---

### Question 6
**What is a SWOT analysis?**

A) Standard Working Operational Tasks

B) Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats ✓

C) Sales, Workflow, Outreach, Targets

D) Systems, Workforce, Operations, Timelines

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: SWOT analyzes internal Strengths and Weaknesses, and external Opportunities and Threats to inform strategic planning.*

---

### Question 7
**Which financial statement shows profitability over a period of time?**

A) Balance sheet

B) Cash flow statement

C) Income statement (P&L) ✓

D) Statement of equity

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The Income Statement (Profit & Loss) shows revenue, expenses, and profitability over a specific time period.*

---

### Question 8
**What belongs in the "Use of Funds" section?**

A) How you'll spend investor or loan capital ✓

B) Customer payment methods

C) Expense tracking procedures

D) Accounting software selection

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: "Use of Funds" details exactly how you'll allocate requested capital (equipment, working capital, facilities, etc.) to reassure lenders/investors.*

---

### Question 9
**What is the recommended maximum length for an executive summary?**

A) 1 page

B) 2-3 pages ✓

C) 5 pages

D) 10 pages

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Executive summaries should be concise (2-3 pages maximum) yet comprehensive enough to stand alone and communicate the entire business opportunity.*

---

### Question 10
**What should appendices include?**

A) Nothing, they're not necessary

B) Supporting documents like resumes, permits, letters of intent, detailed financials ✓

C) Only pictures

D) Restatement of main content

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Appendices contain supporting documentation that validates claims in the main plan without cluttering it: team resumes, letters of support, detailed financials, permits, etc.*
