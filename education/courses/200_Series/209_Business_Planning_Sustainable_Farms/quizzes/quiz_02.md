## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the primary difference between the traditional approach and the market-first approach to farm planning?**

A) Traditional approach is more profitable

B) Market-first approach researches demand before deciding what to grow ✓

C) Traditional approach requires more capital

D) There is no significant difference

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The market-first approach prioritizes understanding customer demand and validating the market before investing in production, which leads to higher success rates.*

---

### Question 2
**According to the module, farms that conduct market research have what success rate compared to those that don't?**

A) 68% vs. 32% ✓

B) 50% vs. 50%

C) 80% vs. 60%

D) 45% vs. 30%

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: The module shows farms with market research have a 68% 5-year success rate compared to 32% for those without market research.*

---

### Question 3
**Which of the following is an example of PRIMARY research?**

A) Reading USDA Agricultural Census data

B) Conducting customer interviews ✓

C) Reviewing competitor websites

D) Reading industry reports

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Primary research involves collecting new data directly from sources, such as customer interviews, surveys, or observations. Secondary research uses existing published data.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the Total Addressable Market (TAM)?**

A) The realistic market share you can capture

B) The portion of the market you can reach

C) Total revenue opportunity if you had 100% market share ✓

D) Your first-year sales projection

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: TAM represents the total market opportunity available if you captured every possible customer. SAM is the portion you can reach, and SOM is what you can realistically capture.*

---

### Question 5
**According to the customer persona example "Sustainability Sarah," what is her primary motivation for purchasing?**

A) Lowest price

B) Values alignment (environmental sustainability, health, local) ✓

C) Convenience only

D) Brand prestige

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Sustainability Sarah is characterized by valuing environmental sustainability, health, and supporting the local economy - she prioritizes values over price.*

---

### Question 6
**What is the recommended sample size for customer interviews in qualitative research?**

A) 5-10 interviews

B) 15-30 interviews ✓

C) 50-75 interviews

D) 100+ interviews

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The module recommends 15-30 customer interviews for effective qualitative primary research to gain deep understanding of needs.*

---

### Question 7
**Which of these is a direct competitor?**

A) Frozen vegetables (substitute product)

B) Conventional farms selling different products to different customers

C) Other local hydroponic farms selling same products to same customers ✓

D) Meal kit delivery services

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Direct competitors sell the same products to the same customer segments. Indirect competitors sell different products that satisfy the same need.*

---

### Question 8
**What is the "Jobs-to-be-Done" framework used for?**

A) Hiring employees

B) Understanding what customers are trying to accomplish when they "hire" your product ✓

C) Creating job descriptions

D) Calculating labor costs

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The Jobs-to-be-Done framework helps understand the functional, emotional, and social jobs customers are trying to accomplish when they purchase your product.*

---

### Question 9
**Which validation method involves getting customers to commit to purchasing before you build your facility?**

A) Focus groups

B) Competitive analysis

C) Pre-orders or letters of intent ✓

D) Secondary research

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Pre-orders, deposits, and letters of intent from customers validate demand before major investment and show that customers are willing to pay.*

---

### Question 10
**According to the Market Validation Checklist, how many potential customers should you interview before significant investment?**

A) At least 5

B) At least 10

C) At least 20 ✓

D) At least 50

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The checklist states you should have interviewed at least 20 potential customers before making significant investment in your farm business.*
