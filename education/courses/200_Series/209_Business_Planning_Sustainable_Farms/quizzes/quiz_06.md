## Questions

### Question 1
**What are the three main pricing approaches discussed in the module?**

A) High, medium, and low pricing

B) Cost-plus, competitive, and value-based ✓

C) Fixed, variable, and dynamic

D) Wholesale, retail, and subscription

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The three fundamental pricing approaches are cost-plus (cost + margin), competitive (market-based), and value-based (customer perceived value).*

---

### Question 2
**If production cost is $5.00/lb and you want a 40% margin, what should the selling price be?**

A) $7.00/lb

B) $7.50/lb

C) $8.00/lb

D) $8.33/lb ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: For a 40% margin: Price = Cost ÷ (1 - Margin%) = $5.00 ÷ (1 - 0.40) = $5.00 ÷ 0.60 = $8.33/lb.*

---

### Question 3
**Which sales channel typically commands the highest price per pound?**

A) Wholesale

B) Restaurant

C) CSA subscription

D) Farmers market ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: Farmers markets allow full retail pricing with highest margins (shown as $12/lb in the example), though they have lower volume than other channels.*

---

### Question 4
**What pricing technique uses prices like $9.99 instead of $10.00?**

A) Prestige pricing

B) Charm pricing ✓

C) Bundle pricing

D) Anchor pricing

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Charm pricing uses prices ending in .99 or .95 to make them appear significantly lower, best for price-sensitive segments.*

---

### Question 5
**When should you typically charge premium prices for local produce?**

A) Summer (high competition)

B) Fall (harvest season)

C) Winter (limited local supply) ✓

D) Spring (growing season starts)

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Winter allows for premium pricing (+15-25%) due to high demand for local and limited competition from outdoor farms.*

---

### Question 6
**In value-based pricing, what justifies charging more than conventional alternatives?**

A) Higher production costs

B) Perceived customer value and differentiation ✓

C) Competitor prices

D) Profit goals

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Value-based pricing is determined by the unique value customers perceive (freshness, sustainability, local, pesticide-free) rather than just costs or competitor prices.*

---

### Question 7
**Which is NOT listed as a common pricing mistake?**

A) Pricing too low

B) Pricing based only on costs

C) Testing different price points ✓

D) Fear of premium pricing

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Testing different price points is actually recommended best practice. The mistakes include pricing too low, only using cost-based pricing, not differentiating by channel, and fear of premium pricing.*

---

### Question 8
**What are the two components needed for full cost calculation?**

A) Labor and materials

B) Direct costs and indirect costs ✓

C) Fixed costs and variable costs

D) Wholesale and retail costs

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Full cost calculation includes direct costs (seeds, packaging, direct labor) and allocated indirect costs (facility overhead, utilities, management, marketing).*

### Question 10
**What is the recommended strategy for communicating premium pricing to customers?**
