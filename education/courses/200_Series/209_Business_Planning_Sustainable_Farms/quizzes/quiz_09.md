## Questions

### Question 1
**What are the three main categories of funding sources for farm businesses?**

A) Grants, investors, and crowdfunding

B) Debt, equity, and grants ✓

C) Banks, government, and private

D) Personal, commercial, and nonprofit

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The three fundamental funding categories are: Debt (loans to be repaid), Equity (selling ownership stake), and Grants (non-repayable funds).*

---

### Question 2
**What is the typical interest rate range for USDA Farm Service Agency (FSA) loans?**

A) 1-3%

B) 3-6% ✓

C) 7-10%

D) 10-15%

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: FSA loans typically offer favorable interest rates in the 3-6% range, lower than conventional commercial loans.*

---

### Question 3
**What percentage of equity do angel investors typically expect in exchange for their investment?**

A) 5-10%

B) 10-25% ✓

C) 30-50%

D) 51-75%

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Angel investors typically seek 10-25% equity stakes in early-stage businesses in exchange for their capital and expertise.*

---

### Question 4
**Which funding source does NOT need to be repaid?**

A) Term loans

B) Lines of credit

C) Equipment financing

D) Grants ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: Grants are non-repayable funds awarded for specific purposes, unlike loans which must be repaid with interest.*

---

### Question 5
**What is the main advantage of crowdfunding beyond raising capital?**

A) No reporting requirements

B) Validates market demand and builds customer base ✓

C) Guaranteed success

D) No fees involved

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Crowdfunding validates that customers are willing to pay before you build, and creates a community of early supporters and customers.*

---

### Question 6
**What does LTV stand for in lending terms?**

A) Long-Term Value

B) Loan-to-Value ratio ✓

C) Lifetime Value

D) Lender's Total Valuation

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio compares the loan amount to the asset's value. Lenders typically finance 60-80% LTV, requiring borrowers to provide the rest.*

---

### Question 7
**Which lending source typically has the LOWEST interest rates?**

A) Credit cards

B) Commercial banks

C) USDA/Government programs ✓

D) Alternative lenders

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Government programs like USDA FSA loans offer the lowest interest rates (3-6%) to support agricultural development.*

---

### Question 8
**What is the typical payback period expected for CSA pre-sales funding?**

A) Within the growing season ✓

B) 1-2 years

C) 3-5 years

D) No payback required

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: CSA pre-sales involve customers paying upfront for weekly shares to be delivered throughout the growing season.*

---

### Question 9
**What is "bootstrap financing"?**

A) Government loan program for new farmers

B) Using personal savings and business revenue to fund growth ✓

C) Securing multiple small loans

D) Borrowing from friends and family

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Bootstrapping means self-funding the business using personal savings, reinvested profits, and minimal external capital.*

---

### Question 10
**What document do lenders typically require to evaluate loan applications?**

A) Marketing brochure

B) Employee handbook

C) Business plan with financial projections ✓

D) Product samples

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Lenders require a comprehensive business plan including financial projections, market analysis, and repayment capacity to evaluate creditworthiness.*
