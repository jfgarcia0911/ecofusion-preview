## Questions

### Question 1
**Which revenue model typically provides the highest profit margins but lower volume?**

A) Wholesale to distributors

B) Direct-to-consumer at farmers markets ✓

C) CSA subscriptions

D) Restaurant food service

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Direct-to-consumer channels like farmers markets offer higher margins (retail pricing) but involve smaller transaction volumes compared to wholesale.*

---

### Question 2
**How many sections does the Business Model Canvas contain?**

A) 5

B) 7

C) 9 ✓

D) 12

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The Business Model Canvas has 9 blocks: Customer Segments, Value Proposition, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Activities, Key Resources, Key Partnerships, and Cost Structure.*

---

### Question 3
**In the Business Model Canvas framework, what should be described in a format like: "[Product] helps [Customer Segment] [Benefit] by [Differentiation]"?**

A) Customer Segments

B) Value Proposition ✓

C) Revenue Streams

D) Channels

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The Value Proposition describes the unique value you deliver to customers using this specific framework to ensure clarity.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the recommended revenue diversification strategy?**

A) 100% from one primary source

B) Primary 40-60%, Secondary 25-35%, Tertiary 10-20%, Experimental 5-10% ✓

C) Equal distribution across all channels

D) 90% from primary, 10% experimental

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Diversification reduces risk while maintaining focus. The recommended mix balances a strong primary revenue source with diversified secondary streams.*

---

### Question 5
**Which pricing method calculates: Production Cost per Unit + Desired Margin = Selling Price?**

A) Value-based pricing

B) Competitive pricing

C) Cost-plus pricing ✓

D) Dynamic pricing

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Cost-plus pricing adds a desired markup percentage to the production cost to determine the selling price.*

---

### Question 6
**In the GreenCycle Aquaponics case study, what percentage of revenue came from CSA subscriptions?**

A) 20%

B) 30%

C) 40% ✓

D) 50%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The case study shows GreenCycle generated 40% of revenue from CSA subscriptions (50 members at $120,000/year total).*

---

### Question 7
**What are the two main categories of costs in a Cost Structure?**

A) Direct and indirect

B) Fixed and variable ✓

C) Primary and secondary

D) Essential and optional

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Fixed costs don't vary with production (rent, insurance, base salaries), while variable costs scale with production (materials, hourly labor, packaging).*

---

### Question 8
**Which of the following is NOT one of the channel phases described in the module?**

A) Awareness

B) Evaluation

C) Purchase

D) Manufacturing ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: The five channel phases are: Awareness, Evaluation, Purchase, Delivery, and After-Sales. Manufacturing is a key activity, not a channel phase.*

---

### Question 9
**When should you consider pivoting your business model?**

A) After the first month if not profitable

B) When customer acquisition cost exceeds customer lifetime value ✓

C) Whenever a competitor changes prices

D) Only if you completely run out of money

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A CAC higher than LTV indicates an unsustainable business model. Other pivot indicators include high churn rates, inability to achieve target margins, or major competitive shifts.*

---

### Question 10
**What type of customer relationship involves farm tours, CSA member events, and social media groups?**

A) Personal assistance

B) Automated services

C) Communities ✓

D) Self-service

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Communities involve creating spaces for customers to connect with each other and the farm, building loyalty and engagement.*
