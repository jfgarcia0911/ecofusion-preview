## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the typical recommended length for an elevator pitch?**

A) 15-30 seconds ✓

B) 1-2 minutes

C) 5 minutes

D) 10 minutes

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: An elevator pitch should be 15-30 seconds - long enough to spark interest but short enough to deliver before someone "gets off the elevator."*

---

### Question 2
**How many slides should a standard pitch deck contain?**

A) 5-7 slides

B) 10-15 slides ✓

C) 20-25 slides

D) 30+ slides

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Effective pitch decks typically contain 10-15 slides covering: Problem, Solution, Market, Business Model, Traction, Team, Competition, Financials, Ask.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the "10/20/30 Rule" for presentations?**

A) 10 slides, 20 minutes, 30-point font ✓

B) 10 minutes, 20 slides, 30% profit margin

C) 10 customers, 20 products, 30% growth

D) $10K investment, 20% return, 30 days

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Guy Kawasaki's 10/20/30 Rule: no more than 10 slides, delivered in 20 minutes, with minimum 30-point font for readability.*

---

### Question 4
**When presenting to investors, what should you emphasize FIRST?**

A) Your credentials and experience

B) The problem you're solving ✓

C) Your financial projections

D) Your product features

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Start with the problem - investors need to understand the market pain point before they'll care about your solution.*

---

### Question 5
**What is "traction" in investor terminology?**

A) Equipment for farm vehicles

B) Evidence that your business is gaining momentum (customers, revenue, partnerships) ✓

C) Your marketing strategy

D) Soil health indicators

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Traction is proof your business is working: customer acquisition, revenue growth, partnerships, pre-orders, or other validation metrics.*

---

### Question 6
**What should you do if you don't know the answer to an investor's question?**

A) Make something up

B) Acknowledge you don't know and commit to following up ✓

C) Change the subject

D) End the meeting

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Honesty builds trust. Acknowledge gaps in knowledge and commit to researching and following up promptly.*

---

### Question 7
**What is the "ask" in a pitch presentation?**

A) Asking questions of the investors

B) The specific amount and terms you're seeking ✓

C) Requesting feedback

D) Asking for introductions

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The "ask" clearly states how much capital you're raising and what you're offering in return (equity %, loan terms, etc.).*

---

### Question 8
**Which storytelling element is most effective in pitches?**

A) Technical specifications

B) Detailed financial formulas

C) Personal connection and emotional narrative ✓

D) Legal documentation

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Stories that connect emotionally (your "why," customer impact stories, personal journey) are far more memorable and persuasive than data alone.*

---

### Question 9
**What should be your primary goal when presenting to lenders (vs. equity investors)?**

A) Demonstrate high growth potential

B) Show ability to repay the loan ✓

C) Offer maximum equity

D) Emphasize innovation

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Lenders focus on repayment ability (cash flow, collateral, credit, character), while equity investors focus on growth potential and returns.*

---

### Question 10
**What is a "deal breaker" question you should be prepared to answer?**

A) What's your favorite vegetable?

B) Why should we invest in you instead of competitors? ✓

C) What's your office address?

D) How many employees will you have in 10 years?

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Investors need to understand your competitive advantage and why you're the best investment opportunity. Have a clear, compelling answer ready.*
