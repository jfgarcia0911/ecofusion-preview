## Questions

### Question 1
**Which business structure offers personal liability protection while allowing pass-through taxation?**

A) Sole Proprietorship

B) General Partnership

C) LLC (Limited Liability Company) ✓

D) C Corporation

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: LLCs provide liability protection (separating personal and business assets) while maintaining pass-through taxation, making them popular for small farms.*

---

### Question 2
**What is the main disadvantage of a Sole Proprietorship?**

A) Complex paperwork

B) High taxes

C) Unlimited personal liability ✓

D) Requires multiple owners

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Sole proprietorships offer no separation between personal and business assets, meaning the owner has unlimited personal liability for business debts.*

---

### Question 3
**Which entity type is subject to "double taxation"?**

A) Sole Proprietorship

B) LLC

C) S Corporation

D) C Corporation ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: C Corporations pay corporate income tax, then shareholders pay personal income tax on dividends, resulting in double taxation.*

---

### Question 4
**What is the primary benefit of forming a cooperative?**

A) Easiest to set up

B) Democratic member control and shared resources ✓

C) No taxes required

D) Unlimited growth potential

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Cooperatives are owned and controlled by members (farmers) who share resources, risks, and profits democratically.*

---

### Question 5
**An S Corporation is limited to how many shareholders?**

A) 50

B) 75

C) 100 ✓

D) Unlimited

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: S Corporations can have no more than 100 shareholders, and all must be U.S. citizens or residents.*

---

### Question 6
**Which business structure requires a written operating agreement (though not always legally required, highly recommended)?**

A) Sole Proprietorship

B) LLC ✓

C) Nonprofit

D) Cooperative

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: LLCs should have an operating agreement defining member roles, profit distribution, and management structure, even if not required by state law.*

---

### Question 7
**What form do LLC owners file with their personal taxes for pass-through income?**

A) Form 1040

B) Schedule C ✓

C) Form 1120

D) Form 990

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: LLC income passes through to owners who report it on Schedule C (sole member) or Schedule K-1 (multiple members) with their personal Form 1040.*

---

### Question 8
**Which entity is best for farms planning to seek venture capital or go public eventually?**

A) Sole Proprietorship

B) LLC

C) S Corporation

D) C Corporation ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: C Corporations can issue multiple stock classes and have unlimited shareholders, making them suitable for venture capital and public offerings.*

---

### Question 9
**What is a DBA?**

A) Direct Business Assets

B) Doing Business As (trade name) ✓

C) Dual Business Arrangement

D) Deductible Business Accounting

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: DBA (Doing Business As) is a trade name or fictitious business name that allows you to operate under a different name than your legal entity name.*

---

### Question 10
**For a husband and wife farm team just starting out with limited capital, which structure is typically recommended initially?**

A) C Corporation

B) Cooperative

C) LLC or Sole Proprietorship ✓

D) Nonprofit

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: LLCs or Sole Proprietorships are simpler and less expensive to set up, suitable for small startups. They can convert to other structures as they grow.*
