## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the key difference between a farm operator and a farm entrepreneur?**

A) Farm operators grow more crops than entrepreneurs

B) Farm entrepreneurs focus on building a profitable business while farm operators focus primarily on production ✓

C) Farm operators use more technology

D) There is no meaningful difference

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Agricultural entrepreneurs approach farming as a business, focusing on markets, profitability, and strategic growth, while traditional farm operators focus mainly on production techniques and yields.*

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following is NOT one of the seven essential traits of successful agricultural entrepreneurs?**

A) Vision and passion

B) Calculated risk-taking

C) Avoiding all forms of innovation ✓

D) Financial literacy

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Successful entrepreneurs seek innovation and competitive advantages. Avoiding innovation would limit growth and competitiveness.*

---

### Question 3
**What is the most common timeline for farm startups to reach profitability?**

A) 6-12 months

B) 12-18 months

C) 2-4 years ✓

D) 5-7 years

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Most farm startups take 2-4 years to reach profitability due to ramp-up time, customer acquisition, and operational optimization.*

---

### Question 4
**According to the module, what percentage of farm businesses typically face capital intensity as a major challenge?**

A) 45%

B) 65%

C) 85%

D) 95% ✓

**Correct Answer: D**

*Explanation: Capital intensity is the most significant challenge, affecting 95% of farm startups due to high upfront equipment and infrastructure costs.*

---

### Question 5
**In the GreenAcres Urban Farm success story, what was a key factor in their success?**

A) They built the largest facility possible from day one

B) They secured restaurant contracts before building ✓

C) They focused only on one product

D) They avoided any educational activities

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: GreenAcres took a market-first approach, securing restaurant commitments before building their facility, which validated demand and ensured revenue.*

---

### Question 6
**What was the primary mistake made by FreshTech Farms that led to their failure?**

A) They started too small

B) They hired too many experienced farmers

C) They over-capitalized early without market validation ✓

D) They focused too much on customers

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: FreshTech raised $2M and built a large facility before validating market demand, leading to cash burn and eventual failure.*

---

### Question 7
**A strong vision statement should include all of the following EXCEPT:**

A) Aspirational future state

B) Specific outcomes

C) Detailed daily operations procedures ✓

D) Authentic values

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Vision statements are aspirational and strategic, not operational. Daily procedures belong in operations manuals.*

---

### Question 8
**What is the "lean startup" approach mentioned in the module?**

A) Hiring the fewest employees possible

B) Starting small, testing assumptions, and iterating ✓

C) Using only organic methods

D) Avoiding all business planning

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The lean startup methodology involves starting with minimal investment, testing market assumptions, learning from results, and iterating based on feedback.*

---

### Question 9
**Why is building a support network critical for farm entrepreneurs?**

A) To have people to blame when things go wrong

B) To access mentorship, resources, and different perspectives ✓

C) It's not actually important

D) Only for inexperienced farmers

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Support networks provide mentorship, share resources, offer different perspectives, and provide encouragement during challenges.*

---

### Question 10
**What is a realistic self-assessment score range that indicates "high readiness" to start a farm business?**

A) 30-49 points

B) 50-69 points

C) 70-90 points ✓

D) Any score above 20 points

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The module's entrepreneurial readiness assessment indicates 70-90 points shows high readiness across personal characteristics, business skills, and resources.*
