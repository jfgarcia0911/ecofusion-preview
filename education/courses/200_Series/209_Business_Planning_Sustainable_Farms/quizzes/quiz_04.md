## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the recommended level of detail for Year 1 financial projections?**

A) Annual projections only

B) Quarterly projections

C) Monthly projections ✓

D) Weekly projections

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Year 1 should have monthly projections for the most detail, Years 2-3 quarterly, and Years 4-5 annual projections.*

### Question 2
**In calculating production capacity, if you have 2,960 plant sites and a 35-day growing cycle, how many crops can you grow per year?**

A) 8.4 cycles

B) 10.4 cycles ✓

C) 12 cycles

D) 15 cycles

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: 365 days ÷ 35-day cycle = 10.4 cycles per year. This calculation is fundamental to capacity-based revenue projections.*

### Question 8
**If fixed costs are $170,000/year, selling price is $10/lb, and variable cost is $3/lb, what is the break-even point?**

A) 17,000 lbs

B) 24,286 lbs ✓

C) 30,000 lbs

D) 56,667 lbs

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Break-even = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost) = $170,000 ÷ ($10 - $3) = $170,000 ÷ $7 = 24,286 lbs.*

---

### Question 9
**In scenario analysis, what probability is typically assigned to the "Base Case"?**

A) 25%

B) 33%

C) 50% ✓

D) 75%

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The three-scenario approach assigns 50% probability to Base Case (most likely), 25% to Best Case, and 25% to Worst Case.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the target Current Ratio for healthy liquidity?**

A) Greater than 0.5

B) Greater than 1.0

C) Greater than 1.5 ✓

D) Greater than 2.5

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Current Ratio (Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities) should be greater than 1.5 for healthy liquidity. Below 1.0 indicates liquidity risk.*
