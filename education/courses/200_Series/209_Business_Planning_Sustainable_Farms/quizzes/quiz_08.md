## Questions

### Question 1
**Which federal agency regulates food safety for most agricultural products?**

A) USDA

B) FDA ✓

C) EPA

D) OSHA

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The FDA (Food and Drug Administration) regulates food safety for most produce, while USDA handles meat, poultry, and eggs.*

---

### Question 2
**What does FSMA stand for?**

A) Farm Safety Management Act

B) Food Safety Modernization Act ✓

C) Federal Standards for Modern Agriculture

D) Facility Sanitation and Maintenance Act

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) established comprehensive food safety rules including the Produce Safety Rule.*

---

### Question 3
**Farms grossing less than what amount annually may qualify for FSMA exemptions?**

A) $10,000

B) $25,000 ✓

C) $100,000

D) $500,000

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The qualified exemption applies to farms with average annual produce sales of $25,000 or less during the previous 3 years.*

---

### Question 4
**What is a zoning variance?**

A) A permanent change to zoning laws

B) Permission to use property in a way not normally allowed by zoning ✓

C) A type of building permit

D) An agricultural tax exemption

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A zoning variance is special permission to use property in a manner that deviates from current zoning regulations.*

---

### Question 5
**Which license is required to sell products by weight?**

A) Business license

B) Food handler's permit

C) Weights and measures certification ✓

D) Health department permit

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Weights and measures certification ensures scales are properly calibrated and legal for commercial transactions.*

---

### Question 6
**At what size do most states require farms to obtain water discharge permits?**

A) Any commercial operation

B) Varies by state, often based on animal units or production scale ✓

C) Over 100 employees

D) Over $1 million revenue

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Water discharge permit requirements vary by state and are typically based on factors like number of animal units, production scale, or proximity to waterways.*

---

### Question 7
**What is GAP certification?**

A) General Aquaponics Protocol

B) Good Agricultural Practices ✓

C) Growing Area Permit

D) Government Approval Process

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: GAP (Good Agricultural Practices) certification verifies farms follow food safety best practices and is often required by wholesale buyers.*

---

### Question 8
**How long must farms typically retain food safety records?**

A) 6 months

B) 1 year

C) 2 years ✓

D) 5 years

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Most food safety regulations require maintaining records for at least 2 years for inspection and traceability purposes.*

---

### Question 9
**Which of these typically does NOT require a permit or license?**

A) Selling at farmers markets

B) Processing value-added products

C) Growing vegetables for personal consumption ✓

D) Discharging water into municipal systems

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Growing for personal consumption generally doesn't require permits. Commercial activities like selling at markets, processing, and water discharge typically require permits.*

---

### Question 10
**What is a cottage food law?**

A) Regulations for farm stays and agritourism

B) Rules allowing small-scale food production in home kitchens ✓

C) Zoning for rural residential properties

D) Building codes for farm structures

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Cottage food laws allow entrepreneurs to produce certain low-risk foods in home kitchens without commercial kitchen licensing, with sales limits.*
