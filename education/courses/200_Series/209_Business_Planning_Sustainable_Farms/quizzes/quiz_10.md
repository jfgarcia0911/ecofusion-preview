## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the typical success rate for first-time grant applicants?**

A) 5-10% ✓

B) 25-30%

C) 50-60%

D) 75-85%

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: First-time grant applicants have a low success rate (5-10%), but this improves significantly with experience and revisions.*

---

### Question 2
**Which type of grant typically ranges from $1,500-$25,000 and targets beginning farmers?**

A) USDA NRCS grants

B) SARE grants ✓

C) SBIR grants

D) State economic development grants

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: SARE (Sustainable Agriculture Research and Education) grants are designed for sustainable ag projects and beginning farmers, typically $1,500-$25,000.*

---

### Question 3
**What is a "matching requirement" in grant terms?**

A) You must match grant funds with your own capital or in-kind contributions ✓

B) Two farmers must apply together

C) The grant must match your revenue

D) Your business plan must match the grant priorities

**Correct Answer: A**

*Explanation: Many grants require matching funds, meaning you must contribute a percentage (often 25-50%) of the total project cost through cash or in-kind resources.*

---

### Question 4
**What should you do FIRST when identifying grant opportunities?**

A) Write the application

B) Determine if your project aligns with the grant's priorities ✓

C) Calculate your budget

D) Contact the grant officer

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: First ensure your project matches the grant's mission and priorities. Don't waste time applying for grants that don't align with your project goals.*

---

### Question 5
**How far in advance should you typically start preparing a grant application?**

A) 1-2 weeks before deadline

B) 1 month before deadline

C) 2-3 months before deadline ✓

D) 6 months before deadline

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Quality grant applications require 2-3 months for research, writing, budget development, and gathering supporting documents.*

---

### Question 6
**What is the purpose of a Logic Model in grant applications?**

A) To show accounting practices

B) To demonstrate how activities lead to outcomes ✓

C) To list project expenses

D) To describe the organizational structure

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A Logic Model visually maps how inputs and activities lead to outputs, outcomes, and impacts, showing the project's theory of change.*

---

### Question 7
**Which section of a grant proposal typically receives the most weight in scoring?**

A) Organizational background

B) Budget justification

C) Project approach and methodology ✓

D) Letters of support

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: The project approach/methodology section demonstrating HOW you'll achieve objectives typically receives the highest point value in grant scoring.*

---

### Question 8
**What are "deliverables" in grant terminology?**

A) Equipment and supplies purchased

B) Specific, measurable outputs you'll produce ✓

C) Payment schedules

D) Grant reports

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Deliverables are tangible products, services, or outcomes you commit to producing (e.g., training sessions conducted, farmers reached, reports published).*

---

### Question 9
**What is the biggest mistake grant applicants make?**

A) Requesting too much money

B) Not following instructions and requirements ✓

C) Using technical jargon

D) Submitting too early

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Failure to follow instructions (word limits, format requirements, required attachments) is the #1 reason applications are disqualified.*

---

### Question 10
**What type of evaluation should be included in your grant proposal?**

A) Only financial audit

B) Qualitative and quantitative measures aligned with objectives ✓

C) Customer satisfaction surveys only

D) No evaluation needed

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Strong proposals include both qualitative (testimonials, case studies) and quantitative (numbers, metrics) evaluation methods tied to stated objectives.*
