## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the first critical step after completing your business plan?**

A) Start construction immediately

B) Create an action plan with prioritized tasks and timeline ✓

C) Quit your job

D) Apply for every possible grant

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Before implementation, create a detailed action plan breaking down the business plan into specific, prioritized tasks with timelines and responsibilities.*

---

### Question 2
**What does a "Minimum Viable Product" (MVP) mean for a farm business?**

A) Growing only one crop

B) Starting with smallest possible operation to test assumptions ✓

C) Minimum legal requirements

D) Lowest quality acceptable

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: MVP means starting with the simplest version of your business that allows you to test key assumptions, learn from customers, and iterate before full investment.*

---

### Question 3
**How often should you review and update your business plan?**

A) Once written, never change it

B) Every 5 years

C) Quarterly or when significant changes occur ✓

D) Daily

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Business plans should be living documents, reviewed quarterly and updated when market conditions, assumptions, or business circumstances change significantly.*

---

### Question 4
**What are Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)?**

A) Types of insurance policies

B) Measurable metrics that track progress toward goals ✓

C) Government regulations

D) Types of business licenses

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: KPIs are specific, measurable metrics (revenue, customer acquisition, production volume, etc.) that track whether you're achieving business objectives.*

---

### Question 5
**What is the recommended approach when your actual results differ significantly from projections?**

A) Ignore the difference

B) Immediately abandon the business

C) Analyze causes and adjust strategy or projections ✓

D) Blame external factors

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Variances between projections and actuals require analysis: identify causes, learn lessons, and adjust either strategy (if fixable) or projections (if assumptions were wrong).*

---

### Question 6
**What is a "pivot" in business terminology?**

A) Physical rotation of greenhouse benches

B) Strategic change in business model based on learning ✓

C) Accounting method

D) Marketing technique

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: A pivot is a fundamental change to business model, product, or strategy based on market feedback and learning, while retaining core vision.*

---

### Question 7
**Which stakeholders should you regularly communicate business plan progress to?**

A) Only yourself

B) Only investors

C) Investors, lenders, team members, key partners, and advisors ✓

D) Everyone on social media

**Correct Answer: C**

*Explanation: Regular communication with all stakeholders (investors, lenders, team, partners, advisors) builds trust, accountability, and support.*

---

### Question 8
**What is a "go/no-go decision point"?**

A) Traffic control for deliveries

B) Predetermined milestone where you evaluate whether to continue or change course ✓

C) Location decisions

D) Crop rotation planning

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Go/no-go decision points are predetermined checkpoints (revenue targets, customer acquisition, etc.) where you objectively evaluate whether to proceed, pivot, or stop.*

---

### Question 9
**What financial metric indicates you should consider pivoting or exiting?**

A) One slow month

B) Consistently missing revenue targets and burning through capital with no path to profitability ✓

C) Any competitor entering the market

D) Seasonal fluctuations

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: Persistent underperformance, unsustainable cash burn, and no clear path to profitability are serious red flags requiring strategic reassessment.*

---

### Question 10
**What is the most important lesson from the business planning process?**

A) The plan document itself is what matters most

B) Planning helps you think strategically, but flexibility and learning matter more ✓

C) Projections should never change

D) Avoid all risks

**Correct Answer: B**

*Explanation: The planning process builds strategic thinking, but execution requires flexibility, continuous learning, and adaptation based on real-world feedback.*
