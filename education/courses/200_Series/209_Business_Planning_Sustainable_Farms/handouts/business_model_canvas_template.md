# Business Model Canvas Template
## Course 209: Business Planning for Sustainable Farms

---

## INSTRUCTIONS

The Business Model Canvas is a strategic tool to design and visualize your business model on one page. Complete each of the 9 building blocks below for your farm business.

**How to Use:**
1. Start with Customer Segments (who are you serving?)
2. Define Value Propositions (what value do you create?)
3. Work through each remaining block
4. Iterate and refine as you develop your plan
5. Use sticky notes if working on physical canvas

**Define who you're creating value for**

- Who are your most important customers?
- Are you serving multiple distinct segments?
- What are their demographics? Psychographics?

**Example Segments:**
- Health-conscious home consumers (age 28-55, income $75K+)
- Farm-to-table restaurants (upscale, local-focused)
