## Lesson Content

### 8.1 Why Records Matter

#### Legal Requirements

**FSMA Mandates:**
- Documentation of compliance activities
- Proof of preventive measures
- Evidence of monitoring
- Corrective action records
- Training documentation

**Retention Period:**
- Minimum 2 years for most records
- Some records: Length of crop storage + 6 months
- Training records: Duration of employment + 2 years

#### Business Benefits

**Beyond Compliance:**

```
VALUE OF GOOD RECORDS

LEGAL PROTECTION
├─ Demonstrate due diligence
├─ Defense in litigation
└─ Reduce liability

OPERATIONAL IMPROVEMENT
├─ Identify patterns and problems
├─ Track effectiveness of procedures
└─ Support continuous improvement

MARKET ACCESS
├─ Customer audit readiness
├─ Certification requirements
└─ Premium market qualification

RECALL EFFICIENCY
├─ Rapid traceability
├─ Minimize scope
└─ Protect brand
```

### 8.2 Required Records Overview

#### Complete FSMA Record Set

```
REQUIRED FSMA DOCUMENTATION

1. WATER RECORDS

   ├─ Corrective actions

   ├─ Source and type

   ├─ Worker training rosters

4. HEALTH & HYGIENE RECORDS
   ├─ Illness reports

   ├─ Cleaning logs

   ├─ Written policies and procedures
   ├─ Hazard analysis
   ├─ Standard operating procedures
   └─ Annual review documentation
```

### 8.3 Water Testing Records

#### What to Document

**For Each Water Sample:**

| Required Information | Example |
|---------------------|---------|
| Sample date and time | 6/15/2024, 8:30 AM |
| Sample location/source | Well #2, North greenhouse |
| Weather conditions | Clear, no rain 7 days |
| Sample collector | Maria Sanchez |
| Laboratory | ABC Water Testing, Inc. |
| Test results | E. coli: <1 CFU/100mL |
| Geometric mean (if applicable) | 8.5 CFU/100mL |
| STV (if applicable) | 35 CFU/100mL |
| Interpretation | PASS (GM<126, STV<410) |

#### Water Treatment Logs

**Daily Monitoring Example:**

```
CHLORINATION SYSTEM LOG

Date: _______________

| Time | Free Cl (ppm) | pH | Water Temp | Initials | Notes |
|------|--------------|-----|------------|----------|-------|
| 6am  | 125          | 7.2 | 58°F       | MS       | OK    |
| 10am | 110          | 7.4 | 62°F       | MS       | OK    |
| 2pm  | 95           | 7.5 | 65°F       | MS       | Low-adjusted |
| 6pm  | 130          | 7.3 | 61°F       | JD       | OK    |

Sanitizer Added: 2L at 2:15pm
Corrective Actions: Increased injection rate due to afternoon drop
```

### 8.6 Sanitation and Equipment Records

#### Daily Cleaning Log

```
EQUIPMENT SANITATION LOG

Equipment: _______________ (Harvest bins, packing tables, etc.)

| Date | Pre-Cleaning Inspection | Cleaning Complete | Sanitizer (ppm) | ATP Result | Initials | Notes |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|------------|----------|-------|
| 6/1  | Debris removed         | ✓                 | 110             | 15 RLU     | MS       | Pass  |
| 6/2  | Heavy soil             | ✓                 | 105             | 45 RLU     | MS       | Re-cleaned |

CLEANING PROCEDURE:
1. Remove debris
2. Wash with detergent
3. Rinse
4. Sanitize (100-200 ppm chlorine)
5. Air dry
6. ATP test (weekly)

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS:
Date: _______ Issue: _______ Action: _______ Verified: _______
```

### 8.7 Temperature and Cold Chain Records

#### Cooler Temperature Log

```
COOLER TEMPERATURE MONITORING

Location: _______________ (Walk-in cooler, pre-cool room, etc.)
Target Range: _______________

| Date | Time | Temperature | Action if OOS | Initials | Notes |
|------|------|-------------|---------------|----------|-------|
| 6/1  | 6am  | 36°F        | -             | JD       | OK    |
| 6/1  | 2pm  | 37°F        | -             | JD       | OK    |
| 6/1  | 10pm | 41°F        | Checked unit  | NW       | Warm-reset |

OUT OF SPECIFICATION PROTOCOL:
If temp >40°F:
1. Check thermometer calibration
2. Inspect cooler door seal
3. Check refrigeration unit
4. Call service if needed
5. Transfer product to backup cooler if prolonged
6. Document all actions
```

#### Product Temperature Log

### 8.8 Traceability Records

#### Harvest and Packing Records

```
LOT TRACEABILITY RECORD

LOT CODE: _______________

HARVEST INFORMATION:
├─ Harvest Date: _______________
├─ Field/Greenhouse: _______________
├─ Crop/Variety: _______________
├─ Quantity Harvested: _______________
└─ Harvested By: _______________

PRE-HARVEST CONDITIONS:
├─ Last Irrigation: _______________
├─ Last Soil Amendment: _______________ (date + type)
├─ Weather: _______________
└─ Animal Intrusion Check: ☐ None observed ☐ See incident log

PACKING INFORMATION:
├─ Pack Date: _______________
├─ Pack Location: _______________
├─ Wash Water Chlorine: _______________ ppm
├─ Product Temperature (post-cool): _______________
└─ Packed By: _______________

DISTRIBUTION:
├─ Customer: _______________
├─ Quantity Shipped: _______________
├─ Ship Date: _______________
├─ Temperature at Loading: _______________
└─ Invoice/PO Number: _______________

RECALL READINESS:
Can trace one step back (supplier) ✓
Can trace one step forward (customer) ✓
```

#### One Up, One Back Rule

```
TRACEABILITY PRINCIPLE

ONE BACK (Immediate Supplier)
├─ Seeds: Supplier name, lot number
├─ Amendments: Source, date received
├─ Packaging: Supplier, food-grade cert
└─ Water source: Well/municipal, testing

YOUR OPERATION
├─ Harvest records by lot
├─ Packing records by lot
└─ Internal traceability

ONE FORWARD (Immediate Customer)
├─ Customer name and contact
├─ Lot codes shipped
├─ Quantities and dates
└─ Invoice numbers
```
