### 10.2 Hazard Analysis

#### Process Flow Diagram

**Create Flow Chart of Your Operation:**

```
PRODUCTION FLOW EXAMPLE (Hydroponic Lettuce)

1. SEED PROCUREMENT
   ↓
2. GERMINATION (Propagation area)
   ↓
3. TRANSPLANTING (Growth systems)
   ↓
4. GROWING (4-6 weeks in NFT channels)
   ↓
5. HARVEST (Cut at base, place in bins)
   ↓
6. TRANSPORT TO PACK HOUSE (Clean bins on cart)
   ↓
7. WASHING (Cold water, sanitizer)
   ↓
8. DRYING (Centrifuge spinner)
   ↓
9. INSPECTION & PACKING (Clamshells)
   ↓
10. COOLING (Walk-in cooler, 35-38°F)
    ↓
11. STORAGE (Up to 5 days)
    ↓
12. DISTRIBUTION (Refrigerated transport)
```

#### Hazard Identification by Step

**For Each Process Step, Identify:**

| Process Step | Potential Hazards | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measures |
|--------------|------------------|------------|----------|------------------|
| Seed procurement | Seed contamination | Low | High | Source from reputable suppliers |
| Germination | Water contamination | Medium | High | Use potable water, monitor |
| Growing | Water quality, cross-contamination | Medium | High | Water testing, system sanitation |
| Harvest | Worker illness, equipment contamination | Medium | High | Training, equipment cleaning |
| Washing | Water contamination, temperature | High | High | **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT** |
| Packing | Worker contamination, foreign objects | Medium | Medium | Hygiene, inspection |
| Cooling | Time/temp abuse | Medium | Medium | Temperature monitoring |
| Storage | Cross-contamination, temp abuse | Medium | Medium | Separation, monitoring |

#### Critical Control Points (CCPs)

**Identify CCPs Using Decision Tree:**

```
CCP DECISION TREE

For each hazard:

Q1: Is there a preventive measure?
    NO → Not a CCP (modify process or accept risk)
    YES → Continue

Q2: Is control at this step necessary to prevent/eliminate hazard?
    NO → Not a CCP
    YES → This is a CCP

EXAMPLE: WASHING STEP
├─ Hazard: Microbial contamination
├─ Q1: Preventive measure? YES (sanitized water)
├─ Q2: Necessary at this step? YES (last kill step)
└─ CONCLUSION: Washing is a CCP
```

**Identified CCPs for Example Operation:**
1. **Water sanitization (washing)** - Microbial control
2. **Temperature control (cooling/storage)** - Microbial growth prevention

### 10.3 Standard Operating Procedures

#### SOP Format

**Each SOP Should Include:**

```
SOP TEMPLATE

TITLE: _____________________

PURPOSE: Why this procedure exists

SCOPE: What/who it applies to

RESPONSIBILITY: Who implements/supervises

FREQUENCY: How often performed

MATERIALS/EQUIPMENT: What's needed

PROCEDURE: Step-by-step instructions
1.
2.
3.
...

MONITORING: What to check, how often

RECORDS: What documentation is created

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS: What to do if problems

VERIFICATION: How to verify effectiveness

REVISION HISTORY: Dates and changes
```

#### Example SOP: Water Quality Management

```
SOP-001: AGRICULTURAL WATER QUALITY MANAGEMENT

PURPOSE:
Ensure all water used for growing, harvest, and packing meets
FSMA Produce Safety Rule standards for microbial quality.

SCOPE:
All water sources (municipal supply, recycled system water)
used in greenhouse hydroponic production.

RESPONSIBILITY:
├─ Operations Manager: Overall oversight
├─ Greenhouse Lead: Daily monitoring
└─ Lab: External testing

FREQUENCY:
├─ Testing: Weekly (E. coli), monthly (full panel)
├─ Monitoring: Continuous (sanitizer, pH)
└─ Treatment: Continuous (UV system)

PROCEDURE:

1. INCOMING WATER (Municipal Supply)
   1.1 Confirm annual water quality report from city
   1.2 Test for E. coli upon receipt of report
   1.3 If E. coli detected, do not use until resolved

2. RECIRCULATING SYSTEM WATER
   2.1 Continuous UV treatment (40 mJ/cm² minimum)
   2.2 Monitor UV intensity hourly (sensor display)
   2.3 Weekly E. coli testing (100mL sample to lab)
   2.4 Monthly water quality panel (E. coli, coliforms, HPC)

3. WASH WATER (Post-harvest)
   3.1 Use municipal or UV-treated water
   3.2 Maintain chlorine 75-150 ppm (test hourly)
   3.3 Maintain pH 6.5-7.5 (test every 2 hours)
   3.4 Replace water when turbid or after 4 hours

MONITORING:
├─ UV intensity: Hourly (sensor reading >threshold)
├─ Chlorine: Hourly (test strips)
├─ pH: Every 2 hours (pH meter)
└─ E. coli: Weekly (lab results)

RECORDS:
├─ Water Treatment Log (daily)
├─ Lab Test Results (weekly/monthly)
└─ Corrective Action Log (as needed)

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS:
├─ E. coli detected: Stop use, investigate, re-test
├─ UV low: Replace lamp, verify intensity
├─ Chlorine low: Increase injection, re-test
└─ pH out of range: Adjust with acid/base

VERIFICATION:
├─ Supervisor reviews logs weekly
├─ Lab result review monthly
├─ Annual system audit
└─ Mock recall exercise (annual)

REVISION HISTORY:
V1.0 - 1/15/2024 - Initial SOP
V1.1 - 6/1/2024 - Added pH monitoring frequency
```

#### Recall Decision Tree

**When to Initiate Recall:**

1. **Confirmed contamination** of distributed product
2. **Epidemiological link** to illnesses
3. **Reasonable probability** of adverse health effects
4. **Regulatory request** (FDA, state)
5. **Customer complaint** of foreign object/contamination

**Recall Class:**
- **Class I:** High probability of serious health effects (e.g., E. coli O157:H7)
- **Class II:** Low probability of serious health effects
- **Class III:** Not likely to cause adverse health effects (quality issue)

### 10.8 Implementation Plan

#### Phased Implementation

**If Starting from Scratch:**

```
IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE

PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Month 1-2)
├─ Obtain PSA Grower Training
├─ Complete coverage determination
├─ Conduct facility/operation assessment
├─ Identify critical gaps

├─ Establish record-keeping system
├─ Create training materials
```
