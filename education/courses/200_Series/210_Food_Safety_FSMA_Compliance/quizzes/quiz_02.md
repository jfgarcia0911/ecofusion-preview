## Questions

### Question 1
**What is the sales threshold for full FSMA Produce Safety Rule compliance?**

A) Average annual produce sales >$250,000
B) Average annual produce sales >$500,000
C) Average annual produce sales >$1,000,000
D) All farms regardless of sales

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
**How is average annual produce sales calculated for FSMA coverage?**

A) Last year's sales only
B) 3-year rolling average
C) 5-year rolling average
D) Projected next year sales

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
**A "qualified end-user" includes: (Select all that apply)**

A) Direct sales to consumers
B) Restaurants within 275 miles
C) National grocery chains
D) Retail establishments in the same state

**Correct Answer:** A, B, D

---

### Question 4
**To qualify for the qualified exemption, what percentage of produce must be sold to qualified end-users?**

A) >25%
B) >50%
C) >75%
D) 100%

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
**If you have a qualified exemption, you must:**

A) Post disclosure statement at point of sale
B) Obtain special FDA permit
C) Pay annual exemption fee
D) Submit monthly reports to FDA

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 6
**Which of the following produce is automatically excluded from the PSR? (Select all that apply)**

A) Lettuce
B) Potatoes (white)
C) Tomatoes (cherry)
D) Asparagus

**Correct Answer:** B, D

---

### Question 7
**When did large farms (>$500K sales) have to comply with FSMA PSR?**

A) January 26, 2016
B) January 26, 2018
C) January 26, 2019
D) January 26, 2020

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
**A farm with $450,000 average annual produce sales, with 70% sold at farmers markets and to local restaurants, would be:**

A) Fully exempt
B) Qualified for exemption
C) Subject to full compliance
D) Only subject to state regulations

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
**FDA can withdraw a qualified exemption if:**

A) Sales exceed threshold
B) Farm is linked to active outbreak investigation
C) Farm changes crops grown
D) State requests withdrawal

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
**Produce receiving commercial processing (kill step) is:**

A) Automatically covered by PSR
B) Excluded if documented
C) Subject to different FSMA rule
D) Not regulated by FDA

**Correct Answer:** B
