# Quiz 7: Harvest & Post-Harvest Handling
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: Pre-harvest assessment should check for:
A) Standing water | B) Animal intrusion | C) Equipment cleanliness | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 2: Best time to harvest for food safety is:
A) After rain immediately | B) Early morning when cool | C) During irrigation | D) Late afternoon when warm
**Answer:** B

### Question 3: Harvest bins must be:
A) Smooth, non-porous, cleanable | B) Shared with chemicals to save money | C) Washed monthly | D) Made of wood
**Answer:** A

### Question 4: Equipment sanitation procedure is:
A) Rinse only | B) Wash with detergent, rinse, sanitize, dry | C) Sanitize only | D) Replace daily
**Answer:** B

### Question 5: Packing facility floors should be:
A) Dirt or gravel | B) Smooth, cleanable, sloped to drains | C) Wood planks | D) Carpet
**Answer:** B

### Question 6: Produce wash water must be:
A) Colder than produce | B) Same temperature as produce | C) ≥10°F warmer than produce | D) Temperature irrelevant
**Answer:** C

### Question 7: Chlorine in wash water should be tested:
A) Weekly | B) Daily | C) Hourly | D) Never
**Answer:** C

### Question 8: Rapid cooling is important to:
A) Improve appearance | B) Inhibit pathogen growth | C) Reduce weight | D) Save energy
**Answer:** B

### Question 9: Cooler temperature should be monitored:
A) Monthly | B) Weekly | C) At least twice daily | D) Only when problems occur
**Answer:** C

### Question 10: ATP testing is used to verify:
A) Equipment cleanliness | B) Pathogen presence | C) Chlorine levels | D) Water temperature
**Answer:** A
