## Questions

### Question 1
**How many Americans get sick from foodborne illness annually?**

A) 3 million
B) 12 million
C) 48 million
D) 100 million

**Correct Answer:** C) 48 million
**Explanation:** CDC estimates 1 in 6 Americans (48 million people) get sick from foodborne illness each year, with 128,000 hospitalizations and 3,000 deaths.

---

### Question 2
**What percentage of foodborne illnesses are linked to produce?**

A) 10%
B) 25%
C) 46%
D) 75%

**Correct Answer:** C) 46%
**Explanation:** According to CDC, produce causes 46% of foodborne illnesses, making it the single largest category despite being rarely cooked.

---

### Question 3
**Which pathogen caused the deadly 2011 cantaloupe outbreak that killed 33 people?**

A) E. coli O157:H7
B) Salmonella
C) Listeria monocytogenes
D) Norovirus

**Correct Answer:** C) Listeria monocytogenes
**Explanation:** The 2011 Jensen Farms cantaloupe outbreak caused by Listeria was the deadliest produce outbreak in U.S. history, killing 33 people and causing the company's bankruptcy.

---

### Question 4
**What was the primary root cause of the 2018 romaine lettuce outbreak?**

A) Worker illness
B) Contaminated irrigation water
C) Unsanitized equipment
D) Animal manure

**Correct Answer:** B) Contaminated irrigation water
**Explanation:** The 2018 Yuma romaine outbreak was traced to contaminated irrigation water, likely from proximity to cattle operations, highlighting the critical importance of water quality.

---

### Question 5
**FSMA stands for:**

A) Food Safety Management Act
B) Farm Safety Modernization Act
C) Food Safety Modernization Act
D) Federal Safety and Monitoring Act

**Correct Answer:** C) Food Safety Modernization Act
**Explanation:** The FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) was signed into law in 2011, representing the most significant food safety reform in over 70 years.

---

### Question 6
**What is the primary difference between FSMA and previous food safety regulations?**

A) FSMA is voluntary while previous rules were mandatory
B) FSMA focuses on prevention while previous rules were reactive
C) FSMA only applies to large operations
D) FSMA eliminated all testing requirements

**Correct Answer:** B) FSMA focuses on prevention while previous rules were reactive
**Explanation:** FSMA shifted from reacting to outbreaks to preventing them through science-based preventive controls and mandatory standards.

---

### Question 7
**Which of the following is NOT one of the five FSMA pillars?**

A) Preventive controls
B) Inspection and compliance
C) Mandatory organic certification
D) Partnerships

**Correct Answer:** C) Mandatory organic certification
**Explanation:** The five FSMA pillars are: (1) Preventive controls, (2) Inspection & compliance, (3) Response, (4) Imports, and (5) Partnerships. Organic certification is separate.

---

### Question 8
**Which food safety standard is a USDA voluntary guidance program (not mandatory)?**

A) FSMA Produce Safety Rule
B) Good Agricultural Practices (GAP)
C) Organic Regulations
D) State health codes

**Correct Answer:** B) Good Agricultural Practices (GAP)
**Explanation:** USDA GAP is a voluntary audit program, while FSMA PSR is mandatory for covered farms, organic regulations are mandatory for certified operations, and state codes vary.

---

### Question 9
**What is the average cost of a produce recall?**

A) $50,000
B) $500,000
C) $10 million
D) $100 million

**Correct Answer:** C) $10 million
**Explanation:** The average recall costs approximately $10 million when including product removal, legal costs, brand damage, and lost sales.

---

### Question 10
**Which of the following are signs of a strong food safety culture? (Select all that apply)**

A) Proactive problem identification
B) Fear of reporting errors
C) Open communication
D) "We've always done it this way" mentality
E) Continuous training investment

**Correct Answer:** A, C, E
**Explanation:** Strong food safety culture is characterized by proactive identification, open communication, and continuous training. Fear of reporting and resistance to change are red flags of poor culture.
