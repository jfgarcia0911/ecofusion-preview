# Quiz 6: Worker Health & Hygiene
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: Qualified supervisor must complete:
A) PSA Grower Training or equivalent | B) Online quiz | C) State certification | D) Medical degree
**Answer:** A

### Question 2: Worker training must be delivered:
A) In English only | B) In language worker understands | C) In writing only | D) Annually regardless of comprehension
**Answer:** B

### Question 3: Workers must be trained:
A) Before or at start of work | B) Within 30 days of hire | C) Within first year | D) Only if handling food
**Answer:** A

### Question 4: Non-punitive illness reporting requires:
A) Paid sick leave | B) Clear policy | C) No disciplinary action | D) All of the above help
**Answer:** D

### Question 5: Proper hand washing takes:
A) 5 seconds | B) 10 seconds | C) 20 seconds | D) 1 minute
**Answer:** C

### Question 6: Hand washing required after:
A) Restroom use | B) Eating/smoking | C) Handling trash/animals | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 7: Portable toilets must be:
A) Within 1/4 mile of workers | B) Serviced regularly | C) Secured to prevent tipping | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 8: Acceptable hand wash station must have:
A) Running water (not standing basin) | B) Soap | C) Single-use towels or air dryer | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 9: Gloves:
A) Substitute for hand washing | B) Never required to be changed | C) Must be changed when contaminated | D) Prohibited by FSMA
**Answer:** C

### Question 10: Training records must include:
A) Worker name and date | B) Topics covered | C) Trainer name | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D
