# Quiz 4: Water Quality Management
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: E. coli is used as indicator organism because:
A) It is always pathogenic | B) It indicates fecal contamination | C) It is easy to kill | D) It grows quickly
**Answer:** B

### Question 2: Water samples should be delivered to lab within:
A) 1 hour | B) 6 hours (24 max with ice) | C) 48 hours | D) 1 week
**Answer:** B

### Question 3: Geometric mean acceptance criterion for agricultural water is:
A) ≤26 CFU/100mL | B) ≤126 CFU/100mL | C) ≤410 CFU/100mL | D) ≤1000 CFU/100mL
**Answer:** B

### Question 4: For produce washing, water should be:
A) ≥10°F warmer than produce | B) ≥10°F cooler than produce | C) Same temperature | D) Temperature doesn't matter
**Answer:** A

### Question 5: Target chlorine level for produce wash water is:
A) 5-10 ppm | B) 25-50 ppm | C) 50-200 ppm | D) 500-1000 ppm
**Answer:** C

### Question 6: UV treatment requires:
A) Chemical addition | B) Clear water (low turbidity) | C) Hot water | D) Acidic pH
**Answer:** B

### Question 7: CEA recirculating systems should test water for E. coli:
A) Monthly | B) Weekly | C) Daily | D) After each harvest
**Answer:** B

### Question 8: If water test shows E. coli above standards, immediately:
A) Dilute the water | B) Stop using source | C) Increase chlorine | D) Test again in 1 week
**Answer:** B

### Question 9: The statistical threshold value (STV) must be:
A) ≤126 CFU/100mL | B) ≤235 CFU/100mL | C) ≤410 CFU/100mL | D) ≤1000 CFU/100mL
**Answer:** C

### Question 10: ATP testing measures:
A) Pathogens directly | B) Organic residue/cleanliness | C) Water pH | D) Chlorine levels
**Answer:** B
