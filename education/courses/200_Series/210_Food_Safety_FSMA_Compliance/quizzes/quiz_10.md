# Quiz 10: Food Safety Plan Development
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: Food safety plan should include:
A) Hazard analysis | B) SOPs | C) Training program | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 2: Critical Control Point (CCP) is:
A) Any step in production | B) Step where control is essential to prevent hazard | C) Optional monitoring point | D) Only for large operations
**Answer:** B

### Question 3: Hazard analysis should identify:
A) Biological hazards | B) Chemical hazards | C) Physical hazards | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 4: SOP should include:
A) Purpose and scope | B) Step-by-step procedure | C) Monitoring and records | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 5: Monitoring plan specifies:
A) What to monitor | B) How often | C) Who is responsible | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 6: Verification activities include:
A) Supervisor review of logs | B) ATP testing | C) Mock recalls | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 7: Food safety plan should be reviewed:
A) Never | B) Only when problems occur | C) Annually minimum | D) Every 10 years
**Answer:** C

### Question 8: Training matrix should specify:
A) Who needs training | B) What topics | C) When/frequency | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 9: Recall procedures should include:
A) Team contact list | B) Decision tree | C) Customer notification template | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 10: Implementation should be:
A) All at once overnight | B) Phased with priorities | C) Delayed indefinitely | D) Only paperwork, no practice
**Answer:** B
