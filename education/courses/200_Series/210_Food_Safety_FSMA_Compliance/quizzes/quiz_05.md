# Quiz 5: Soil Amendments & Composting
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: BSAO stands for:
A) Biological Soil Amendment Operation | B) Biological Soil Amendment of Animal Origin | C) Basic Soil Amendment Option | D) Bacterial Source Amendment Origin
**Answer:** B

### Question 2: Windrow composting requires minimum:
A) 3 days at 131°F | B) 7 days at 131°F | C) 15 days at 131-170°F with 5 turns | D) 30 days at any temperature
**Answer:** C

### Question 3: Static pile composting requires:
A) 3 days at 131-170°F | B) 15 days with turning | C) 30 days minimum | D) No specific requirements
**Answer:** A

### Question 4: Compost temperature should be measured:
A) Daily during active composting | B) Weekly | C) Monthly | D) Only at start and finish
**Answer:** A

### Question 5: Ideal compost C:N ratio is:
A) 10:1 | B) 25-30:1 | C) 50:1 | D) 100:1
**Answer:** B

### Question 6: Squeeze test showing few water drops indicates:
A) Too dry | B) Too wet | C) Optimal moisture (50-60%) | D) Ready to apply
**Answer:** C

### Question 7: For CEA operations, safer amendment alternatives include:
A) Raw manure | B) Synthetic nutrients | C) Fresh blood meal | D) Uncomposted chicken litter
**Answer:** B

### Question 8: Compost tea from animal-based compost:
A) No restrictions | B) Treated same as raw manure | C) Only allowed in organic | D) Prohibited by FSMA
**Answer:** B

### Question 9: Required documentation for BSAO application includes:
A) Date and location | B) Amount applied | C) Treatment method | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 10: Records must be retained for minimum:
A) 6 months | B) 1 year | C) 2 years | D) 5 years
**Answer:** C
