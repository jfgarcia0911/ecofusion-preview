# Quiz 3: Produce Safety Rule Deep Dive
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1
**The primary source of produce contamination is:**
A) Worker illness
B) Agricultural water
C) Equipment
D) Pests
**Answer:** B

### Question 2
**For CEA operations, nutrient solution water is classified as:**
A) Growing water (soil-based standard)
B) Harvest/packing water (no detectable E. coli)
C) Irrigation water (relaxed standard)
D) Not regulated
**Answer:** B

### Question 3
**The acceptable standard for harvest/packing water is:**
A) <126 CFU E. coli/100mL
B) <410 CFU E. coli/100mL
C) No detectable E. coli
D) <1000 CFU E. coli/100mL
**Answer:** C

### Question 4
**Raw manure application requires what waiting period if produce touches soil?**
A) 30 days
B) 60 days
C) 90 days
D) 120 days
**Answer:** D

### Question 5
**Properly composted manure requires:**
A) 120 day interval
B) 90 day interval
C) 30 day interval
D) No waiting period
**Answer:** D

### Question 6
**Workers must report which conditions to supervisor? (Select all)**
A) Vomiting
B) Common cold
C) Diarrhea
D) Jaundice
**Answer:** A, C, D

### Question 7
**Hand washing facilities must include:**
A) Running water, soap, drying method
B) Hand sanitizer only
C) Bucket with water
D) Gloves as substitute
**Answer:** A

### Question 8
**Equipment that contacts produce must be:**
A) Cleanable and maintained
B) Sanitized annually
C) Replaced monthly
D) FDA approved
**Answer:** A

### Question 9
**When significant animal intrusion is observed, you must:**
A) Eliminate all wildlife
B) Assess contamination and take corrective action
C) Immediately destroy all crops
D) Notify FDA within 24 hours
**Answer:** B

### Question 10
**Gloves used by workers:**
A) Eliminate need for hand washing
B) Must be changed when contaminated
C) Can be reused if rinsed
D) Are not allowed under PSR
**Answer:** B
