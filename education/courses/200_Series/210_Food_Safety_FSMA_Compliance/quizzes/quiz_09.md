# Quiz 9: Traceability & Recall Preparedness
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: Good lot coding system should be:
A) Unique and meaningful | B) Linked to records | C) Simple to apply | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 2: "One up, one back" traceability means:
A) One step to supplier, one to customer | B) One year forward and back | C) One product type | D) One record type
**Answer:** A

### Question 3: Mock recall exercises should be conducted:
A) Never | B) Only after real recall | C) Minimum annually | D) Every 10 years
**Answer:** C

### Question 4: Mock recall goal is typically:
A) Complete traceback in <1 hour | B) Complete in <4 hours | C) Complete in 1 week | D) No time limit
**Answer:** B

### Question 5: FSMA Section 204 enhanced traceability applies to:
A) All farms | B) High-risk foods including leafy greens | C) Only imports | D) Only large operations
**Answer:** B

### Question 6: FSMA 204 becomes effective:
A) January 2020 | B) January 2023 | C) January 2026 | D) January 2030
**Answer:** C

### Question 7: In a recall, you must contact:
A) Affected customers | B) FDA/regulatory authority | C) Suppliers of inputs | D) A and B
**Answer:** D

### Question 8: Recall coordinator role should be:
A) Assigned in advance | B) Decided during recall | C) Always owner | D) Rotated weekly
**Answer:** A

### Question 9: Lot code example "240615-A" likely indicates:
A) Random number | B) Date (June 15, 2024) and field/location | C) Product weight | D) Customer code
**Answer:** B

### Question 10: Traceability technology for small farms might include:
A) Blockchain only | B) Paper logs + Excel | C) Complex ERP system | D) No records needed
**Answer:** B
