# Quiz 8: Record Keeping & Documentation
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Questions

### Question 1: Most FSMA records must be retained for minimum:
A) 6 months | B) 1 year | C) 2 years | D) 5 years
**Answer:** C

### Question 2: "If it wasn't documented, it didn't happen" means:
A) Documentation is optional | B) Records provide legal proof of compliance | C) Memory is sufficient | D) Records don't matter
**Answer:** B

### Question 3: Water testing records must include:
A) Sample date/location | B) Test results | C) Laboratory name | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 4: Temperature logs should record:
A) Date and time | B) Temperature reading | C) Corrective actions if needed | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 5: Training records must include:
A) Topics covered | B) Attendee signatures | C) Date and trainer | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 6: Soil amendment application records need:
A) Date and location | B) Type and amount | C) Treatment method | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 7: Records should be organized:
A) Randomly in boxes | B) By category with easy retrieval | C) Only digital | D) Only paper
**Answer:** B

### Question 8: Best practice for record backup is:
A) Paper only | B) Digital only | C) Both paper and digital backup | D) No backup needed
**Answer:** C

### Question 9: Inspection-ready binder should include:
A) Current year critical records | B) Farm overview | C) SOPs | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D

### Question 10: Corrective action documentation should include:
A) What happened | B) What was done | C) Who did it and when | D) All of the above
**Answer:** D
