## Course Description

Navigate food safety regulations and implement compliant systems for your operation. This course covers FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) requirements, HACCP principles, and practical food safety program implementation for controlled environment agriculture.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Interpret FSMA Produce Safety Rule requirements
2. Develop food safety plans and HACCP programs
3. Implement effective worker training programs
4. Maintain required documentation and records
5. Prepare for regulatory inspections and third-party audits
6. Achieve food safety certifications (GAP, etc.)

## Regulatory Framework

### FSMA Coverage Determination

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                     FSMA PRODUCE SAFETY RULE COVERAGE                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                              ║
║   ARE YOU A "FARM" UNDER FSMA?                                              ║
║   ────────────────────────────                                              ║
║   □ Growing produce (including aquaponics/hydroponics)                      ║
║   □ Harvesting, packing, or holding produce                                 ║
║                                                                              ║
║   SALES THRESHOLDS (Adjusted annually)                                      ║
║   ──────────────────────────────────                                        ║
║   • >$500,000 average annual sales: FULL COMPLIANCE                         ║
║   • $250,000-$500,000: Modified requirements (Qualified Exemption)          ║
║   • <$250,000: Exempt from federal rule (state rules may apply)             ║
║                                                                              ║
║   QUALIFIED EXEMPTION REQUIREMENTS:                                         ║
║   ─────────────────────────────────                                         ║
║   1. Average annual produce sales <$500,000 (3-year rolling)                ║
║   2. Majority of sales to "qualified end-users"                             ║
║      • Direct to consumers                                                  ║
║      • Restaurants/retail within same state or 275 miles                    ║
║                                                                              ║
║   NOTE: Even if exempt, best practices protect your business and customers  ║
║                                                                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Key Documentation Requirements

| Document Type | Contents | Retention |
|---------------|----------|-----------|
| Water testing records | Test results, corrective actions | 2 years |
| Sanitation logs | Daily cleaning activities | 2 years |
| Temperature logs | Cooler temps, product temps | 2 years |
| Training records | Who, when, what topics | 2 years |
| Traceability records | Lot codes, distribution | 2 years |

## Food Safety Plan Components

### 1. Farm Description
- Business name and contact
- Products grown
- Production methods
- Distribution channels

### 2. Risk Assessment
- Potential hazards identification
- Hazard analysis by production step
- Control measures

### 3. Standard Operating Procedures
- Pre-harvest activities
- Harvest procedures
- Post-harvest handling
- Sanitation procedures

### 4. Monitoring Procedures
- What to monitor
- How often
- Who is responsible
- Documentation

### 5. Corrective Actions
- Actions for deviations
- Responsible persons
- Documentation requirements

### 6. Training Program
- Topics covered
- Training schedule
- Documentation

### 7. Recall Procedures
- Product traceability
- Customer notification
- Product disposition
