# Facility Audit Exercise
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Activity Overview

**Purpose:** Conduct a comprehensive food safety audit of your operation (real or hypothetical) to identify compliance gaps and improvement opportunities.

**Time Required:** 2-3 hours
**Format:** Individual or small group
**Materials Needed:** Camera/smartphone, measuring tape, thermometer, notebook

---

## Instructions

1. Select a facility to audit (your own operation or visit a willing farm)
2. Complete all sections of the audit checklist
3. Take photos of key areas (facilities, equipment, signage)
4. Document findings objectively
5. Develop prioritized corrective action plan
6. Submit audit report
