## Sample Collection Protocol

**Before Sampling:**
- ☐ Obtain sterile bottles from lab
- ☐ Label with location, date, time
- ☐ Prepare cooler with ice
- ☐ Review collection procedure

**During Collection:**
- ☐ Wear clean gloves
- ☐ Don't touch inside of bottle/cap
- ☐ For tap: Run 2-3 minutes first
- ☐ For surface: 6-12" below surface
- ☐ Fill to neck, cap immediately
- ☐ Place in cooler (1-10°C)

**After Collection:**
- ☐ Deliver within 6 hours (24 max with ice)
- ☐ Complete chain of custody
- ☐ Document weather/conditions
- ☐ File receipt confirmation

## Corrective Action Protocol

**If E. coli Detected in Harvest/Packing Water:**

1. ☐ **IMMEDIATE:** Stop using water source
2. ☐ Switch to backup/treated source
3. ☐ Notify qualified supervisor
4. ☐ Document incident (date, time, result)
5. ☐ Investigate cause (inspect system, weather events)
6. ☐ Re-sample to confirm (within 24 hours)
7. ☐ Implement corrective measures (repair, treatment)
8. ☐ Verify effectiveness (re-test until pass)
9. ☐ Resume normal use when safe
10. ☐ File corrective action report

**If Agricultural Water Exceeds GM/STV:**

1. ☐ Discontinue use from source
2. ☐ Re-sample during initial survey (may be temporary spike)
3. ☐ Investigate contributing factors
4. ☐ Implement corrective measures
5. ☐ Re-calculate GM/STV with new data
6. ☐ Resume use when criteria met
