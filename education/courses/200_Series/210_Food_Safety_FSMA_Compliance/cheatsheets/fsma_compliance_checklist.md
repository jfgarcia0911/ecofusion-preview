### Water Quality Standards

**For Harvest/Packing Water (CEA, washing):**
☐ No detectable generic E. coli
☐ Testing schedule established
☐ Lab selected (ISO 17025 preferred)
☐ Sampling procedures documented

**For Agricultural Water (soil-based growing):**
☐ Initial survey completed (minimum 4 samples)
☐ Geometric mean calculated: _______ CFU/100mL (must be ≤126)
☐ STV calculated: _______ CFU/100mL (must be ≤410)
☐ Annual testing schedule established

### Water Treatment Systems

☐ **Treatment system installed** (if needed): ☐ Chlorine ☐ UV ☐ Ozone ☐ Other: _______
☐ **Monitoring procedures established**
   - Chlorine: ☐ Target: 50-200 ppm, Test: Hourly
   - UV: ☐ Intensity monitored continuously
   - pH: ☐ Target: 6.5-7.5, Test: Every 2 hours
☐ **Maintenance schedule created**
☐ **Backup system identified**

### Amendment Inventory

☐ **List all BSAOs used:**
   - ☐ Raw manure (type: ___________)
   - ☐ Composted manure
   - ☐ Blood meal
   - ☐ Fish emulsion
   - ☐ Other: ___________

☐ **List non-animal amendments:**
   - ☐ Plant compost
   - ☐ Cover crop residues
   - ☐ Synthetic nutrients
   - ☐ Other: ___________

### Composting Process (if applicable)

☐ **Composting method selected:**
   - ☐ Windrow (15 days, 131-170°F, 5 turns)
   - ☐ Static pile (3 days, 131-170°F)
   - ☐ In-vessel (3 days, 131-170°F)

☐ **Temperature monitoring system:**
   - ☐ Compost thermometer acquired
   - ☐ Daily log template created
   - ☐ Target temps: 131-170°F

☐ **Process validation:**
   - ☐ Initial pathogen testing completed
   - ☐ Temperature logs maintained
   - ☐ Turning schedule documented

### Application Procedures

☐ **Application intervals understood:**
   - ☐ Raw manure + soil contact = 120 days
   - ☐ Raw manure + no soil contact = 90 days
   - ☐ Treated manure = 0 days (if validated)

☐ **Application records system:**
   - ☐ Date, location, amount logged
   - ☐ Earliest harvest date calculated
   - ☐ 2-year retention established

☐ **Reportable conditions communicated:**
   - ☐ Vomiting
   - ☐ Diarrhea
   - ☐ Jaundice
   - ☐ Infected wounds
   - ☐ Diagnosed illnesses

☐ **Health records system:** (confidential storage)

### Hygiene Facilities

☐ **Restrooms:**
   - ☐ Adequate number for workforce
   - ☐ Within reasonable distance
   - ☐ Toilet paper, waste receptacles
   - ☐ Regular servicing schedule
   - ☐ Cleaning log maintained

☐ **Hand washing stations:**
   - ☐ At or near restrooms
   - ☐ Within 1/4 mile of work areas
   - ☐ Running water (not standing basin)
   - ☐ Soap dispensed
   - ☐ Single-use towels or air dryer
   - ☐ Signage in appropriate languages

☐ **Facility inspection schedule:**
   - ☐ Daily checks: ___________
   - ☐ Refilling supplies
   - ☐ Documentation logs

☐ **Hand washing procedures:**

   - ☐ Signage posted
   - ☐ Monitoring by supervisors

☐ **Glove policy (if used):**

   - ☐ When to change specified
   - ☐ Not substitute for hand washing

☐ **Eating/drinking policy:**
   - ☐ Designated areas identified
   - ☐ Prohibited in production areas

## Part 6: Domesticated & Wild Animals

### Animal Monitoring

☐ **Monitoring procedures established:**
   - ☐ Regular field/facility inspections
   - ☐ Evidence of intrusion documented
   - ☐ Assessment of contamination risk

☐ **Domesticated animals:**
   - ☐ Working animals separated from production
   - ☐ Pets restricted from production areas
   - ☐ Livestock fenced away from crops

### Prevention Measures

☐ **Wildlife deterrents:**
   - ☐ Attractants removed (fallen fruit, garbage)
   - ☐ Borders maintained (mowing, clearing)
   - ☐ Exclusion where practical (netting, fencing)
   - ☐ Water sources protected

☐ **Intrusion response protocol:**
   - ☐ Assessment procedure documented
   - ☐ Corrective actions identified
   - ☐ Product disposition criteria
   - ☐ Documentation requirements

## Part 7: Harvest & Post-Harvest

### Pre-Harvest

☐ **Pre-harvest assessment checklist:**
   - ☐ No standing water
   - ☐ No animal intrusion
   - ☐ Equipment cleaned
   - ☐ Workers trained
   - ☐ Facilities ready

☐ **Harvest timing:**
   - ☐ After manure application intervals
   - ☐ After irrigation drying time
   - ☐ Optimal temperature (early morning)

### Washing Operations (if applicable)

☐ **Wash water quality:**
   - ☐ Testing completed (no E. coli)
   - ☐ Sanitizer system operational
   - ☐ Temperature monitoring (≥10°F above produce)

☐ **Wash water monitoring:**
   - ☐ Chlorine tested hourly
   - ☐ pH tested every 2 hours
   - ☐ Water changed when turbid
   - ☐ Logs maintained

### Cooling & Storage

☐ **Cooling procedures:**
   - ☐ Method selected: ☐ Hydrocool ☐ Forced-air ☐ Room cooling
   - ☐ Target temperatures established
   - ☐ Cooling verified (product core temp)

☐ **Cold storage:**
   - ☐ Coolers maintained at 34-38°F (crop-specific)
   - ☐ Temperature monitored 2x daily minimum
   - ☐ Temperature logs maintained
   - ☐ Corrective action protocol for deviations

### Packaging & Labeling

☐ **Packaging materials:**
   - ☐ Food-grade certified
   - ☐ Stored to prevent contamination
   - ☐ Supplier documentation on file

☐ **Traceability labeling:**
   - ☐ Lot coding system designed
   - ☐ Farm name on packages
   - ☐ Harvest/pack date
   - ☐ Contact information
