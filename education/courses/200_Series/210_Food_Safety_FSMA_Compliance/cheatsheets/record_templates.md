## Record Retention Guide

| Record Type | Retention Period | Storage Method |
|------------|------------------|---------------|
| Water testing results | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Soil amendment applications | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Training records | Duration of employment + 2 years | Personnel files |
| Harvest/distribution records | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Temperature logs | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Sanitation logs | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Corrective actions | 2 years minimum | Binder + digital backup |
| Food Safety Plan | Current version + archives | Master binder |
