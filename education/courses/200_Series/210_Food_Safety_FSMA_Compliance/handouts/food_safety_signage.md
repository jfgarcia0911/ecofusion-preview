# Food Safety Signage
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

---

## Introduction

This document contains printable food safety signs in ASCII format for posting throughout your operation. These signs reinforce critical food safety practices and meet FSMA training and communication requirements.

**Usage Instructions:**
- Print signs and laminate for durability
- Post in clearly visible locations
- Translate to languages spoken by your workforce
- Update as procedures change
- Include in worker orientation

---

## Multilingual Considerations

### Translation Guidelines

To ensure all workers understand food safety requirements:

1. **Identify Languages Needed**
   - Survey your workforce
   - Determine primary languages spoken
   - Consider literacy levels

2. **Professional Translation**
   - Use certified translators
   - Avoid online translation tools for critical safety information
   - Have translations reviewed by native speakers
   - Test comprehension with workers

3. **Common Languages in Agriculture**
   - Spanish
   - Haitian Creole
   - Various Asian languages (Hmong, Vietnamese, etc.)
   - Indigenous languages

4. **Visual Support**
   - Use icons and symbols
   - Include diagrams and pictures
   - Color coding (but don't rely on color alone)
   - Simple language even in English version

### Quick Translation Reference

**Common Food Safety Phrases:**

| English | Spanish | Note |
|---------|---------|------|
| Wash Your Hands | Lávese Las Manos | Most critical phrase |
| Do Not Work When Ill | No Trabaje Si Está Enfermo | Health reporting |
| Clean Equipment | Limpie El Equipo | Sanitation |
| Report to Supervisor | Reportar al Supervisor | Escalation |
| Temperature | Temperatura | Monitoring |
| Gloves Required | Se Requieren Guantes | PPE |

## Custom Signage Tips

### Creating Effective Food Safety Signs

**DO:**
- Use clear, simple language
- Include specific instructions
- Make text large and readable
- Use high contrast colors
- Laminate for durability
- Post at point of use
- Include "why" when possible
- Add contact information

**DON'T:**
- Use complex sentences
- Include unnecessary information
- Use tiny fonts
- Assume literacy
- Post in hard-to-see locations
- Let signs become outdated
- Rely solely on text
- Forget to translate

### Design Specifications

**Recommended:**
- **Font size:** Minimum 18pt for body text, 36pt+ for headers
- **Font type:** Sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, Verdana)
- **Colors:** Black text on white or yellow background
- **Material:** Laminated paper or weatherproof plastic
- **Size:** 8.5" x 11" minimum, larger for important notices
