## Proper Hand Washing Technique

### The 6 Steps (20 Seconds Minimum)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                     │
│  STEP 1: WET                                        │
│  Turn on water and wet hands completely             │
│  Use warm running water                             │
│                                                     │
│  STEP 2: SOAP                                       │
│  Apply enough soap to cover all hand surfaces       │
│  Liquid or bar soap both acceptable                 │
│                                                     │
│  STEP 3: SCRUB (20 seconds)                         │
│  ✓ Palms together                                   │
│  ✓ Between fingers                                  │
│  ✓ Backs of hands                                   │
│  ✓ Under nails                                      │
│  ✓ Thumbs                                           │
│  ✓ Wrists                                           │
│                                                     │
│  STEP 4: RINSE                                      │
│  Rinse thoroughly under running water               │
│  Ensure all soap is removed                         │
│                                                     │
│  STEP 5: DRY                                        │
│  Dry with single-use towel or air dryer             │
│  Pat dry thoroughly                                 │
│                                                     │
│  STEP 6: TURN OFF                                   │
│  Use towel to turn off manual faucet                │
│  Dispose of towel in waste receptacle               │
│                                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## When to Wash Your Hands

### ALWAYS Wash Before:

- ✓ Starting work
- ✓ Handling food or produce
- ✓ Putting on gloves
- ✓ After any break
- ✓ Entering production area

### ALWAYS Wash After:

- ✓ Using the restroom
- ✓ Eating or drinking
- ✓ Smoking
- ✓ Coughing, sneezing, or blowing nose
- ✓ Touching hair, face, or body
- ✓ Handling trash or waste
- ✓ Handling animals or animal waste
- ✓ Touching soil, manure, or compost
- ✓ Handling dirty equipment
- ✓ Touching contaminated surfaces
- ✓ Changing tasks (e.g., harvest to packing)

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ✗ DON'T:
- Wash hands in standing water (bucket/basin)
- Use hot water (too hot to comfortably hold hands under)
- Skip soap
- Rinse quickly (less than 20 seconds)
- Touch faucet with clean hands
- Wipe on clothing
- Share towel
- Think gloves replace hand washing

### ✓ DO:
- Use running water
- Apply soap generously
- Scrub for full 20 seconds (sing "Happy Birthday" twice)
- Rinse completely
- Dry thoroughly
- Use towel for faucet
- Use single-use or personal towel
- Wash hands before AND after glove use

      ↓

## Glove Use Guidelines

### Gloves Are NOT a Substitute for Hand Washing!

**Proper Glove Use:**

1. **BEFORE putting gloves on:** Wash hands
2. **WHILE wearing gloves:**
   - Change when contaminated
   - Change between tasks
   - Change if torn or damaged
   - Don't touch face, phone, etc.
3. **AFTER removing gloves:** Wash hands

### When to Change Gloves:

- ✓ Beginning of shift (after hand washing)
- ✓ After touching face, hair, or body
- ✓ After handling waste or dirty items
- ✓ After coughing or sneezing
- ✓ Between different tasks
- ✓ If torn or damaged
- ✓ At least every 4 hours

## Hand Hygiene Monitoring

### Self-Check:

Ask yourself:
- Did I wet my hands completely?
- Did I use enough soap?
- Did I scrub for 20 seconds?
- Did I clean under my nails?
- Did I rinse all the soap off?
- Did I dry completely?

### Supervisor Check:

Supervisors should observe and provide feedback on:
- Hand washing frequency
- Proper technique
- Duration (full 20 seconds)
- Station functionality
- Supply availability

## Fingernail and Jewelry Guidelines

### Fingernails:

**Required:**
- ✓ Clean
- ✓ Trimmed short
- ✓ No dirt under nails
- ✓ Scrub under nails while washing

**Prohibited:**
- ✗ Artificial nails
- ✗ Nail polish (may chip into food)
- ✗ Long nails (harbor bacteria)

### Jewelry:

**Prohibited on Hands:**
- ✗ Rings (trap bacteria, contaminate food)
- ✗ Bracelets (trap bacteria, can fall into food)
- ✗ Watches (prevent proper washing)

**Exception:** Plain wedding band may be allowed (check your farm policy)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use hand sanitizer instead of washing?**
A: NO. Hand sanitizer does NOT replace hand washing. Sanitizer may be used IN ADDITION to hand washing but never as a substitute.

**Q: What if the water is too cold?**
A: Report to supervisor immediately. Water should be comfortably warm but not hot. Cold water is less effective at removing oils and dirt.

**Q: What if there's no soap?**
A: DO NOT work without soap. Notify supervisor immediately to refill supplies. Water alone is not sufficient.

**Q: Can I dry my hands on my apron or clothing?**
A: NO. This re-contaminates your clean hands. Always use single-use towel or air dryer.

**Q: Do I need to wash hands if I'm wearing gloves?**
A: YES. Always wash before putting gloves on and after taking them off.

**Q: How often should I wash during a shift?**
A: As often as needed! At minimum after every break, restroom use, and task change. Many workers wash hands 20+ times per shift.
