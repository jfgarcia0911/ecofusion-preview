# Assignment 5: Complete Food Safety Plan
## Course 210: Food Safety & FSMA Compliance

| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 12 |

## Required Components

### 1. Farm Description (4 pts)
- Business information
- Products grown
- Production methods
- Distribution channels

### 2. Hazard Analysis (6 pts)
- Identified hazards by production step
- Risk assessment
- Control measures

### 3. Standard Operating Procedures (8 pts)
- Hand washing and hygiene
- Equipment sanitation
- Harvest/packing area cleaning
- Water management
- Waste disposal

### 4. Monitoring & Record Keeping (6 pts)
- Monitoring procedures
- Documentation requirements
- Record retention
- Corrective actions

### 5. Training Program (3 pts)
- Training curriculum
- Training schedule
- Documentation

### 6. Audit Preparation (3 pts)
- Self-assessment checklist
- Common deficiencies
- Continuous improvement

**Deliverable:** 15-20 page food safety plan ready for implementation and audit
