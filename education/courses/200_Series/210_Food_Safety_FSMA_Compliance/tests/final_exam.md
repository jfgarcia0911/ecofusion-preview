## Section 8: Comprehensive Application (Questions 46-50)

### Question 46
**SHORT ANSWER: List the five key PSR requirement areas.**
**Answer:**
1. Agricultural water
2. Biological soil amendments
3. Worker health and hygiene
4. Equipment, tools, and buildings
5. Domesticated and wild animals

### Question 47
**SHORT ANSWER: Explain why water is considered the #1 contamination risk in produce.**
**Answer:** Water can directly contact produce during growing, harvest, and washing. Unlike other inputs, contaminated water spreads pathogens throughout the operation quickly and can internalize contaminants that cannot be washed off. Water serves as a vehicle for fecal contamination from animals, soil, or other sources.

### Question 48
**SHORT ANSWER: Why is traceability important for food safety?**
**Answer:** Traceability enables rapid identification of contaminated product during outbreaks, minimizing the scope of recalls and protecting public health. It allows targeted product removal rather than industry-wide bans, reducing economic impact. Good traceability also demonstrates due diligence and builds customer confidence.

### Question 49
**SCENARIO: A hydroponic farm has $425,000 average annual sales, with 65% to farmers markets and 35% to a regional distributor. Does the farm qualify for exemption? Explain.**
**Answer:** Yes, the farm qualifies for a qualified exemption. Average sales of $425,000 is below the $500,000 threshold. 65% to farmers markets (direct to consumers) counts as qualified end-users, exceeding the 50% requirement. The farm must post disclosure statements at farmers markets and maintain records, but is not subject to full PSR compliance.

### Question 50
**SCENARIO: A farm applies raw chicken manure to a lettuce field on March 1. The lettuce grows on the soil surface. When is the earliest safe harvest date?**
**Answer:** June 29 (120 days after March 1 application). Because lettuce has direct soil contact with the harvestable portion, the 120-day interval for raw manure applies. Properly composting the manure first would eliminate this waiting period.
