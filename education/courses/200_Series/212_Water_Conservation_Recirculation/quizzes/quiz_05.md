**1. What is the standard UV dose for general agricultural sterilization?**

**4. What is the main advantage of ozone over UV sterilization?**

**5. What is a critical safety concern with ozone?**

**6. What is the typical contact time needed for ozone treatment?**

**7. Why does a dirty UV quartz sleeve reduce effectiveness?**

**8. What does ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential) measure in ozone systems?**
- A) Temperature | B) pH | C) Ozone activity/effectiveness | D) Flow rate

**9. Which sterilization method is generally NOT recommended for continuous recirculating systems?**
- A) UV | B) Ozone | C) Chlorine/bleach | D) Hydrogen peroxide (low dose)

**10. What happens to UV lamp output over time?**
