# Quiz 2: Recirculating Systems Design
## Course 212: Water Conservation & Recirculation

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes
**Total Points:** 20
**Passing Score:** 80% (16 points)

---

## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. What is the formula for calculating required flow rate based on system volume and turnover time?**
- A) Flow Rate = Volume × Turnover Time
- B) Flow Rate = Volume ÷ Turnover Time
- C) Flow Rate = Turnover Time ÷ Volume
- D) Flow Rate = Volume + Turnover Time

**2. In a centralized recirculation system, what is the main DISADVANTAGE?**
- A) Higher capital cost
- B) Complex monitoring
- C) Disease can spread system-wide
- D) Requires more pumps

**3. What is Total Dynamic Head (TDH) in pump sizing?**
- A) The height of the water tank
- B) Total of vertical lift + friction losses + fitting losses
- C) Maximum pump capacity
- D) System volume

**4. For proper UV exposure, what is the typical retention time needed in the UV chamber?**
- A) 1-2 seconds
- B) 4-10 seconds
- C) 30-60 seconds
- D) 2-5 minutes

**5. What percentage of system volume should the reservoir/sump typically be?**
- A) 5-10%
- B) 20-30%
- C) 40-50%
- D) 60-70%

**6. What is the primary advantage of a zoned/modular recirculation system?**
- A) Lower capital cost
- B) Disease isolation between zones
- C) Simplified monitoring
- D) No maintenance required

**7. In a distribution manifold, what happens if the header diameter is too small?**
- A) Perfect equal distribution
- B) Unequal flow to outlets
- C) Reduced energy costs
- D) Increased filtration

**8. What is the recommended flow velocity range for gravity drain lines?**
- A) 1-2 ft/s
- B) 2-4 ft/s
- C) 5-7 ft/s
- D) 8-10 ft/s

**9. For a small system (<1,000 sq ft), which configuration is typically recommended?**
- A) Complex multi-zone with redundancy
- B) Single loop, centralized
- C) Distributed individual systems
- D) Hybrid with backup systems

**10. Critical control points in a recirculating system should include sensors AFTER which processes?**
- A) Growing zone only
- B) Filtration and sterilization
- C) Pumps only
- D) Reservoir only

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Flow Rate = Volume ÷ Turnover Time
2. C - Disease can spread system-wide
3. B - Total of vertical lift + friction losses + fitting losses
4. B - 4-10 seconds
5. B - 20-30%
6. B - Disease isolation between zones
7. B - Unequal flow to outlets
8. B - 2-4 ft/s
9. B - Single loop, centralized
10. B - Filtration and sterilization
