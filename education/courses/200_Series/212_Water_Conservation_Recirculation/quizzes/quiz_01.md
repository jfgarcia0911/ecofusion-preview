# Quiz 1: Water in Agriculture
## Course 212: Water Conservation & Recirculation

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes
**Total Points:** 20
**Passing Score:** 80% (16 points)

---

## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. What percentage of global freshwater withdrawals does agriculture account for?**
- A) 40%
- B) 50%
- C) 70%
- D) 90%

**2. In conventional field agriculture, approximately what percentage of applied water actually reaches crops?**
- A) 15-25%
- B) 35-40%
- C) 50-60%
- D) 70-80%

**3. What is the typical recirculation rate for an advanced recirculating hydroponic system?**
- A) 60-70%
- B) 75-85%
- C) 90-95%
- D) 98-100%

**4. Which factor is the PRIMARY environmental driver for water conservation in agriculture?**
- A) Government regulations only
- B) Aquifer depletion and water scarcity
- C) Consumer preferences
- D) Energy costs

**5. What does WUE (Water Use Efficiency) measure?**
- A) Water cost per acre
- B) Crop yield per unit of water applied
- C) Pump efficiency
- D) Evaporation rate

**6. Compared to conventional field agriculture, how much more water-efficient can recirculating hydroponics be?**
- A) 2-5× more efficient
- B) 5-10× more efficient
- C) 10-30× more efficient
- D) 50-100× more efficient

**7. What is the primary challenge of recirculating systems regarding water quality?**
- A) Water becomes too pure
- B) Concentration of both beneficial and harmful substances
- C) Water temperature drops
- D) pH becomes too stable

**8. In the basic recirculation cycle, what typically represents the largest "loss" requiring makeup water?**
- A) Filter backwash
- B) System leaks
- C) Evapotranspiration
- D) Waste discharge

**9. What is a typical target for the Makeup Water Ratio (MWR) in a well-designed recirculating system?**
- A) <5% of system volume daily
- B) <10% of system volume daily
- C) <20% of system volume daily
- D) <30% of system volume daily

**10. Which region typically has the fastest economic payback for recirculating systems?**
- A) Areas with abundant, cheap water
- B) High water-cost regions (drought areas)
- C) Cold climates
- D) Tropical regions

---

## Answer Key

1. C - 70%
2. B - 35-40%
3. C - 90-95%
4. B - Aquifer depletion and water scarcity
5. B - Crop yield per unit of water applied
6. C - 10-30× more efficient (some systems up to 100×)
7. B - Concentration of both beneficial and harmful substances
8. C - Evapotranspiration
9. A - <5% of system volume daily
10. B - High water-cost regions
