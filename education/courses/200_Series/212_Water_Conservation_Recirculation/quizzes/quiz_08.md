# Quiz 8: Advanced Water Treatment
## Course 212: Water Conservation & Recirculation

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes | **Total Points:** 20 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. What percentage of dissolved solids does reverse osmosis typically remove?**
- A) 50-60% | B) 75-85% | C) 95-99% | D) 100%

**2. What is the typical recovery rate for RO systems?**
- A) 10-20% | B) 25-50% | C) 60-75% | D) 90-95%

**3. What must be removed from water BEFORE reverse osmosis to prevent membrane damage?**
- A) Sodium | B) Chlorine | C) Calcium | D) Nitrogen

**4. What is the primary use of activated carbon filters in CEA?**
- A) Remove nutrients | B) Remove chlorine/chloramine | C) Soften water | D) Raise pH

**5. What is the recommended contact time for catalytic carbon to remove chloramine?**
- A) 10 seconds | B) 1 minute | C) 5-10 minutes | D) 30+ minutes

**6. What does water softening add to water?**
- A) Calcium | B) Potassium | C) Sodium | D) Magnesium

**7. Why is water softening generally NOT recommended for CEA?**
- A) Too expensive | B) Adds sodium which accumulates in recirculating systems | C) Removes beneficial nutrients | D) Increases pH too much

**8. What is the best treatment for high TDS/salts in source water?**
- A) Carbon filter | B) Sand filter | C) Reverse osmosis | D) UV sterilization

**9. In what order should treatment processes typically occur?**
- A) UV → Filter → Carbon → RO
- B) RO → Carbon → Filter → UV
- C) Sediment Filter → Carbon → RO → UV
- D) Carbon → UV → RO → Filter

**10. What is the typical operating cost for RO-treated water in commercial systems?**
- A) $0.50-1.00 per 1,000 gallons | B) $2-5 per 1,000 gallons | C) $10-15 per 1,000 gallons | D) $20+ per 1,000 gallons
