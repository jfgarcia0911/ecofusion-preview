# Quiz 6: Water Testing & Monitoring
## Course 212: Water Conservation & Recirculation

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes | **Total Points:** 20 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. How often should pH meters be calibrated in daily-use situations?**
- A) Daily | B) Weekly | C) Monthly | D) Annually

**2. What solutions are used for two-point pH calibration?**
- A) pH 1 and 14 | B) pH 4 and 10 | C) pH 7 and 4 (or 10) | D) Distilled water

**3. What is the typical accuracy of a digital pen pH meter?**
- A) ±0.01 pH | B) ±0.1 pH | C) ±0.5 pH | D) ±1.0 pH

**4. Which test should be performed DAILY in commercial recirculating systems?**
- A) Full nutrient panel | B) pH, EC, Temperature | C) Laboratory analysis | D) Microbial count

**5. What does a rising EC with dropping pH typically indicate?**
- A) System is perfect | B) Nitrate accumulation, excess fertilization | C) Water evaporation | D) Equipment failure

**6. How is TDS (ppm) calculated from EC (mS/cm)?**
- A) EC × 100 | B) EC × 500 (or 700) | C) EC × 1000 | D) EC ÷ 100

**7. What is the recommended testing frequency for major nutrients (N, P, K)?**
- A) Daily | B) Weekly | C) Monthly | D) Quarterly

**8. How should pH probe be stored when not in use?**
- A) Dry | B) In distilled water | C) In storage solution | D) In tap water

**9. When should you send samples for laboratory analysis?**
- A) Never | B) Daily | C) Quarterly or when troubleshooting | D) Only if legally required

**10. What should you do if a test result seems questionable?**
- A) Ignore it | B) Record and continue | C) Recalibrate and retest | D) Shut down system

## Answer Key
1. B | 2. C | 3. B | 4. B | 5. B | 6. B | 7. B | 8. C | 9. C | 10. C
