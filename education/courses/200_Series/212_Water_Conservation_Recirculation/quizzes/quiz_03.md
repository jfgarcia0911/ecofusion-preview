## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. What is the target pH range for most hydroponic crops?**
- A) 4.5-5.5
- B) 5.5-6.5
- C) 6.5-7.5
- D) 7.0-8.0

**2. What does rising EC with normal pH typically indicate?**
- A) pH buffer failure
- B) Salt accumulation from evapotranspiration
- C) Nutrient deficiency
- D) Disease outbreak

**3. What is the minimum dissolved oxygen target for DWC/raft systems?**
- A) 2 mg/L
- B) 4 mg/L
- C) 7 mg/L
- D) 10 mg/L

**4. How does temperature affect dissolved oxygen capacity?**
- A) Higher temperature = higher DO capacity
- B) Temperature has no effect
- C) Higher temperature = lower DO capacity
- D) Only affects pH, not DO

**5. In recirculating systems, which nutrients typically ACCUMULATE and may require periodic flushing?**
- A) Nitrogen and phosphorus
- B) Sodium and chloride
- C) Iron and potassium
- D) Calcium and magnesium

**6. What is the recommended EC management strategy for recirculating systems?**
- A) Never change water
- B) Dump 10-20% weekly when EC gets too high
- C) Add more nutrients constantly
- D) Use distilled water only

**7. What is the primary cause of pH decline in established recirculating systems?**
- A) CO2 absorption
- B) Nitrate accumulation
- C) Temperature changes
- D) Algae growth

**8. Cloudy water (high turbidity) despite filtration typically indicates:**
- A) Perfect water quality
- B) Bacterial bloom or filter failure
- C) High nutrient levels
- D) Low pH

**9. What is the recommended testing frequency for pH and EC in commercial systems?**
- A) Monthly
- B) Weekly
- C) Daily
- D) Hourly

**10. What percentage recirculation is considered optimal for balancing conservation and quality?**
- A) 60-70%
- B) 75-85%
- C) 90-95%
- D) 99-100%
