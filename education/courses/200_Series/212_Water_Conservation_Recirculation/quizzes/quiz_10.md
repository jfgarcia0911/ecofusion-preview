## Multiple Choice (2 points each)

**1. In the capstone project scenario, what was the target recirculation rate?**

**2. When calculating rainwater collection potential, what factors are needed?**

**3. What is the primary purpose of a current state analysis?**

**4. In system design, which component sizing calculation comes FIRST?**
- A) Pump selection | B) Filter sizing | C) System volume determination | D) Monitoring equipment

**5. What should drive storage tank sizing for rainwater harvesting?**
- A) Budget only | B) Rainfall patterns and dry season length | C) Tank availability | D) Random selection

**6. What is included in total capital costs for a water system project?**
- A) Equipment only | B) Equipment + installation | C) Equipment + installation + contingency | D) Equipment cost ×2

**7. A 10-15 year payback period based on water savings alone is:**
- A) Unacceptable, abandon project | B) Acceptable when including full value (quality, sustainability) | C) Excellent | D) Impossible

**8. What is the recommended approach for large projects?**
- A) Do everything at once | B) Phased implementation | C) Wait until perfect plan | D) Start without planning

**9. Which phase should typically be implemented FIRST in a phased approach?**
- A) Rainwater harvesting | B) Automation | C) Recirculation system upgrade | D) Advanced monitoring

**10. What is the most important deliverable in a water conservation project?**
- A) Fancy graphics | B) Comprehensive design with calculations and justification | C) Lowest cost option | D) Largest system possible
