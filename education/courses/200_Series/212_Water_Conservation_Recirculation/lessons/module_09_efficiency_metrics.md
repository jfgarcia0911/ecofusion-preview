## Core Efficiency Metrics

### Water Use Efficiency (WUE)

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║          WATER USE EFFICIENCY CALCULATION                  ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                            ║
║ WUE = Crop Yield (kg or lb) ÷ Water Applied (liters or gal)║
║                                                            ║
║ Example - Lettuce Production:                              ║
║                                                            ║
║ Conventional Field:                                        ║
║ Yield: 1,000 lbs                                           ║
║ Water: 40,000 gallons                                      ║
║ WUE: 1,000 ÷ 40,000 = 0.025 lb/gal                         ║
║                                                            ║
║ Recirculating Hydroponics:                                 ║
║ Yield: 1,000 lbs                                           ║
║ Water: 1,500 gallons                                       ║
║ WUE: 1,000 ÷ 1,500 = 0.67 lb/gal                           ║
║                                                            ║
║ Improvement: 26× more efficient                            ║
║                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Recirculation Rate

```
Recirculation Rate (%) = (Volume Recirculated ÷ Total Volume Used) × 100

Components:
• Volume Recirculated = Flow returned to system after treatment
• Total Volume Used = Recirculated + Fresh makeup + Discharged

Target Rates by System:
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ System Type         Recirculation %     │
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Basic hydro         85-90%              │
│ Advanced hydro      90-95%              │
│ Aquaponics          95-98%              │
│ Closed-loop NFT     95-99%              │
│ Best practice       >95%                │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘

Example Calculation:
Daily system volume cycled: 5,000 gallons
Fresh makeup added: 250 gallons
Discharged (backwash, dumps): 150 gallons

Total used: 5,000 + 250 + 150 = 5,400 gallons
Recirculated: 5,000 gallons
Rate: (5,000 ÷ 5,400) × 100 = 92.6%
```

### Water Productivity

```
Water Productivity ($) = Revenue ÷ Water Used

Agricultural Comparison:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ System              Water Productivity          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Field crops         $0.10 - $2.00 per m³        │
│ Field vegetables    $2.00 - $8.00 per m³        │
│ Greenhouse (basic)  $20 - $50 per m³            │
│ Recirculating CEA   $80 - $200+ per m³          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Example:
Monthly production: $10,000 revenue
Water used: 150,000 liters (39,626 gallons)
Volume in m³: 150 m³
Productivity: $10,000 ÷ 150 = $66.67 per m³
```

## Advanced Optimization Techniques

### Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) Management

```
VPD AND WATER USE

VPD = Vapor Pressure Deficit
Higher VPD = More evapotranspiration = More water use

VPD Calculation:
VPD (kPa) = (1 - RH/100) × SVP

Where:
SVP = Saturated Vapor Pressure at leaf temperature

Managing VPD:
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ High VPD (>1.5 kPa)                    │
│ • Rapid transpiration                  │
│ • High water use                       │
│ • Potential water stress               │
│ Action: Increase humidity, reduce temp │
│                                        │
│ Optimal VPD (0.8-1.2 kPa)              │
│ • Balanced transpiration               │
│ • Efficient water use                  │
│ • Healthy plant growth                 │
│ Maintain: Monitor and adjust           │
│                                        │
│ Low VPD (<0.6 kPa)                     │
│ • Slow transpiration                   │
│ • Disease risk                         │
│ • Nutrient transport issues            │
│ Action: Decrease humidity, increase temp│
└────────────────────────────────────────┘

Water Savings: 10-25% by optimizing VPD
```

### Nutrient Solution Management

**Optimizing EC to Reduce Discharge:**

```
STRATEGIC EC MANAGEMENT

Traditional Approach:
• Start: 1.8 mS/cm
• Accumulates to 2.8 over 2 weeks
• Dump and refill
• Water waste: 100%

Optimized Approach:
• Start: 1.5 mS/cm
• Monitor daily
• Targeted nutrient additions (not full strength)
• Dilute with fresh water when EC hits 2.5
• Extend cycle to 4-6 weeks
• Water waste: 25-50%

Savings: 50-75% reduction in discharge volume
```

## Module Summary

1. **Measurement enables management** - install meters, track metrics, and analyze trends to drive continuous improvement

4. **Low-cost actions yield quick wins** - leak repairs, backwash optimization, and covering water surfaces pay back in months

5. **Reporting demonstrates value** - quantifying and communicating water stewardship builds brand value and market differentiation
