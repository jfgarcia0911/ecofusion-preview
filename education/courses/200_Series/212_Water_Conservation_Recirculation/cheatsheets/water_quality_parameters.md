## Primary Parameters (Test Daily)

### pH

| Parameter | Target Range | Action Level | Correction |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **pH** | 5.5-6.5 | <5.0 or >7.0 | KOH (raise), Phosphoric acid (lower) |

**Effects of Improper pH:**
- <5.0: Nutrient toxicity, root damage
- >7.0: Nutrient lockout (especially Fe, Mn, P)

**Adjustment Rates:**
- KOH: ~1 mL of 45% solution raises 100 gal by 0.1 pH
- Phosphoric acid: ~1 mL of 85% lowers 100 gal by 0.1 pH

---

### Electrical Conductivity (EC)

| Crop Type | Target EC (mS/cm) | Maximum EC |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| **Lettuce/Leafy Greens** | 1.5-2.2 | 2.8 |
| **Herbs** | 1.5-2.5 | 3.0 |
| **Tomatoes** | 2.0-3.5 | 5.0 |
| **Cucumbers** | 1.7-2.5 | 3.5 |

**EC Management:**
- Rising EC → Dilute with fresh water or partial water change
- Dropping EC → Add nutrients
- Target: Keep within range, dump/dilute when exceeds maximum

**Conversion:** EC (mS/cm) × 500 = TDS (ppm) approximate

### Temperature

| System Type | Optimal Range | Critical Limits |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Cool Season Crops** | 60-68°F | <55°F or >75°F |
| **Warm Season Crops** | 68-75°F | <60°F or >80°F |
| **Root Zone** | 65-72°F | <60°F or >78°F |

**Temperature Effects:**
- Every 10°F increase → ~20% decrease in DO capacity
- >80°F → Root disease risk increases dramatically
- <60°F → Slow growth, nutrient uptake reduced

### Dissolved Oxygen (DO)

| System Type | Minimum | Target | Ideal |
|-------------|---------|--------|-------|
| **NFT** | 4 mg/L | 6 mg/L | 8+ mg/L |
| **DWC/Raft** | 5 mg/L | 7 mg/L | 9+ mg/L |
| **Drip/Media** | 3 mg/L | 5 mg/L | 6+ mg/L |
| **Aquaponics** | 5 mg/L | 6 mg/L | 8+ mg/L |

```
Temperature    DO Saturation
50°F           11.3 mg/L
60°F           10.1 mg/L
70°F            9.1 mg/L
80°F            8.2 mg/L
```

## Macronutrients

| Nutrient | Symbol | Target Range (ppm) | Deficiency Signs | Excess Signs |
|----------|--------|-------------------|------------------|--------------|
| **Nitrogen** | N (NO₃) | 100-200 | Yellowing, slow growth | Dark green, excess vegetative |
| **Phosphorus** | P | 30-60 | Purple stems, stunted | Rare; may lock out micronutrients |
| **Potassium** | K | 200-300 | Marginal burn, weak stems | Rare; affects Ca/Mg uptake |
| **Calcium** | Ca | 150-200 | Tip burn, blossom end rot | Rare; may precipitate |
| **Magnesium** | Mg | 40-60 | Interveinal chlorosis | Rare |
| **Sulfur** | S | 50-100 | Similar to N deficiency | Rare |

## Micronutrients

| Nutrient | Target Range (ppm) | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Iron** | 1.0-3.0 | Chelated form (EDDHA for high pH) |
| **Manganese** | 0.5-1.0 | Competes with Fe at high levels |
| **Boron** | 0.3-0.5 | Narrow safe range; toxic >1.0 |
| **Zinc** | 0.1-0.3 | Often adequate in source water |
| **Copper** | 0.05-0.2 | Toxic at higher levels |
| **Molybdenum** | 0.05-0.1 | Needed in tiny amounts |

## Other Important Parameters

### Alkalinity

| Range (ppm CaCO₃) | Status | Action |
|-------------------|--------|--------|
| <20 | Too low | pH unstable, add buffer |
| 50-100 | Ideal | Maintains stable pH |
| >150 | High | pH hard to lower |

### Turbidity

| NTU | Status | Action |
|-----|--------|--------|
| <5 | Excellent | UV effective |
| 5-10 | Good | Monitor filtration |
| 10-20 | Fair | Clean filters |
| >20 | Poor | UV ineffective, clean/upgrade filtration |

## Troubleshooting Quick Guide

**Problem: pH dropping steadily**
- Cause: Nitrate accumulation, low alkalinity
- Solution: Add KOH or K₂CO₃, check alkalinity

**Problem: EC rising with normal feeding**
- Cause: Evapotranspiration concentration
- Solution: Partial water change, reduce nutrient concentration

**Problem: Low DO despite aeration**
- Cause: High temperature or organic matter
- Solution: Cool water, clean filters, increase aeration

**Problem: Cloudy water**
- Cause: Bacterial bloom or filter failure
- Solution: Check UV function, clean filters, verify sterilization

**Problem: Iron deficiency symptoms**
- Cause: pH too high (>6.5) or wrong chelate
- Solution: Lower pH, use appropriate Fe chelate (EDDHA for pH >6.5)
