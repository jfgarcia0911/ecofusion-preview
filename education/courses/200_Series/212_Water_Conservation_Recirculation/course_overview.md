## Course Description

Maximize water efficiency in your production systems. This course covers water conservation strategies, recirculation technology, treatment methods, and sustainable water management. Learn to achieve near-zero discharge operations while maintaining optimal growing conditions.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Analyze water usage patterns in production systems
2. Implement effective conservation strategies
3. Design and operate recirculation systems
4. Select and manage water treatment technologies
5. Develop comprehensive water management plans
6. Calculate water use efficiency metrics

## Water Use Comparison

| System Type | Water Use | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Field Agriculture | 100% (baseline) | Irrigation losses, runoff |
| Drip Irrigation | 50-70% | Significant reduction |
| Hydroponics | 10-20% | Recirculating systems |
| Aquaponics | 5-10% | Highest efficiency |

## Treatment Technologies

| Technology | Purpose | Best For |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Mechanical filtration | Solids removal | All recirculating systems |
| Biofiltration | Nitrogen conversion | Aquaculture, aquaponics |
| UV sterilization | Pathogen control | Disease prevention |
| Ozone | Disinfection, oxidation | High-density systems |
| Reverse osmosis | Mineral removal | Poor source water |
| Activated carbon | Organics, chlorine | Municipal water |
