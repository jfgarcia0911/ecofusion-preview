## Key Water Pathways Explained

### 1. Fresh Water Input (5-10% of system volume daily)

**Sources:**
- Municipal water (most common)
- Rainwater (ideal for CEA)
- Well water (variable quality)

**Treatment Required:**
- Remove chlorine/chloramine
- Adjust pH if extreme
- Reduce EC/TDS if high (>0.5 mS/cm)
- Sterilize if contaminated

**Purpose:**
- Replace evapotranspiration losses
- Maintain system volume
- Provide fresh nutrients

---

### 2. Growing System (Primary water use)

**Functions:**
- Deliver water and nutrients to plant roots
- Support plant physiological processes
- Provide dissolved oxygen for respiration
- Maintain optimal root zone environment

**Water Transformations:**
- Uptake by roots → Transpiration → Evaporation (95%+ of losses)
- Nutrient extraction (crop removes N, P, K, etc.)
- Temperature moderation
- Minor contamination (root exudates, debris)

---

### 3. Filtration System (Quality maintenance)

**Purpose:**
- Remove suspended solids
- Prevent pathogen buildup
- Protect downstream equipment (UV)
- Maintain water clarity

**Water Loss:**
- Backwash discharge: 1-3% of total flow
- Can be recovered via settling/reuse
- Optimize backwash triggers to minimize waste

---

### 4. Sterilization (Pathogen control)

**Purpose:**
- Inactivate bacteria, viruses, fungi
- Prevent disease spread
- Control algae growth
- Enable safe recirculation

**No Water Loss:**
- UV and ozone don't consume water
- Essential for high recirculation rates

---

### 5. Reservoir/Storage (System heart)

**Functions:**
- Buffer volume for stability
- Nutrient and pH adjustment point
- Monitoring location
- Emergency water supply
- Temperature stabilization

**Critical Control Point:**
- Most comprehensive water quality testing here
- Nutrient injection and chemical additions
- System-wide adjustments made at this point

## Typical Daily Water Budget Example

**System:** 10,000 gallon DWC lettuce operation

```
DAILY WATER FLOW:

Circulated through system:           240,000 gallons
  (10,000 gal system × 24 hours/day)

Fresh makeup water added:            400 gallons (4%)
  Sources:
  - Rainwater: 300 gallons
  - Municipal backup: 100 gallons

Water outputs:
  - Evapotranspiration: 350 gallons (87.5%)
  - Backwash: 30 gallons (7.5%)
  - Minor dump: 20 gallons (5%)
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Total output: 400 gallons (100%)

Recirculation rate: (240,000 - 400) ÷ 240,000 = 99.8%

Actual recirculation (after losses):
  (400 - 350) ÷ 400 = 12.5% waste
  Effective conservation: 87.5%
```
