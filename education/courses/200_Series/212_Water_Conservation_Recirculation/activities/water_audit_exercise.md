# Activity: Comprehensive Water Audit Exercise
## Course 212: Water Conservation & Recirculation

---

## Objective

Conduct a complete water audit of a CEA facility to identify water inputs, uses, losses, and conservation opportunities. This hands-on exercise teaches the systematic approach to measuring, analyzing, and optimizing water management.

**Time Required:** 2-4 hours (plus 1 week of monitoring)

**Materials Needed:**
- Water flow meters (or access to water bills)
- Measuring containers for flow rate estimation
- Stopwatch/timer
- Water quality test equipment
- Logbook or spreadsheet for data collection
- Facility site plan or sketch

---

## Phase 1: Preparation and Setup

### Step 1: Facility Assessment

Create a water use inventory by identifying ALL water sources and uses:

```
WATER INPUTS CHECKLIST:
□ Municipal water connection(s) - location(s): __________
□ Well water - capacity: __________ GPM
□ Rainwater collection - catchment area: __________ sq ft
□ Other: __________

WATER USES CHECKLIST:
□ Growing system makeup water
□ Filter backwashing
□ System cleaning and dumping
□ Irrigation (if applicable)
□ Evaporative cooling
□ Facility wash-down
□ Restrooms/staff facilities
□ Other: __________

WATER OUTPUTS CHECKLIST:
□ Evapotranspiration from crops
□ Filter backwash discharge
□ System dumps/drains
□ Wastewater/sewer discharge
□ Leaks (if known)
□ Other losses: __________
```

### Step 2: Measurement Point Installation

Install or identify measurement points:

1. **Primary Input Meter**
   - Location: Main water supply line
   - Purpose: Total facility water consumption
   - If no meter: Use water bill + sub-metering

2. **Sub-meters (if possible)**
   - Growing system makeup
   - Backwash discharge
   - Staff facilities
   - Other major uses

3. **Visual Indicators**
   - System reservoir level indicators
   - Storage tank levels
   - Drains and discharge points

## Phase 3: Water Balance Analysis

### Create a Water Balance Diagram

```
WEEKLY WATER BALANCE

INPUTS (gallons/week)               OUTPUTS (gallons/week)
─────────────────────               ─────────────────────
Municipal:    __________            Backwash:      __________
Rainwater:    __________            System dump:   __________
                                    Evapotrans.:   __________
                                    Leaks/other:   __________
              ──────────                           ──────────
TOTAL IN:     __________            TOTAL OUT:     __________

Balance check: Input should equal Output (±5%)
Difference: __________ (if >5%, investigate measurement accuracy)
```

### Calculate Key Metrics

**1. Water Use Efficiency (WUE)**
```
WUE = Total Water Input ÷ Crop Production

Weekly calculation:
WUE = __________ gal ÷ __________ lbs = __________ gal/lb

Compare to benchmarks:
- Excellent: <1.5 gal/lb
- Good: 1.5-3.0 gal/lb
- Fair: 3.0-5.0 gal/lb
- Poor: >5.0 gal/lb

Your rating: __________
```

**2. Recirculation Rate**
```
Estimate system volume: __________ gallons
Daily circulation volume: __________ gallons
Fresh makeup water: __________ gallons/day

Recirculation Rate = (Circulated - Makeup) ÷ Circulated × 100

Calculation:
Rate = (__________ - __________) ÷ __________ × 100 = __________%

Compare to benchmarks:
- Excellent: >95%
- Good: 90-95%
- Fair: 80-90%
- Poor: <80%

Your rating: __________
```

**3. Water Loss Breakdown**
```
Calculate percentage of each output:

Evapotranspiration:    __________ gal = __________%
Backwash:              __________ gal = __________%
System dumps:          __________ gal = __________%
Leaks/unaccounted:     __________ gal = __________%
                       Total = 100%

Create a pie chart or bar graph to visualize
```

## Deliverables

Submit a water audit report including:

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Current water use and efficiency
   - Key findings
   - Total savings potential
   - Top 3 recommendations

2. **Detailed Data** (2-3 pages)
   - 7-day monitoring logs
   - Weekly summary calculations
   - Water balance diagram
   - Efficiency metrics

3. **Analysis** (2-3 pages)
   - Water loss breakdown (chart/graph)
   - Comparison to benchmarks
   - Opportunity identification
   - Savings calculations for each improvement

4. **Action Plan** (1-2 pages)
   - Prioritized improvements
   - Implementation timeline
   - Budget estimates
   - Expected outcomes

5. **Supporting Documentation**
   - Photos of measurement points
   - Facility water use diagram
   - Equipment specifications (if applicable)

## Reflection Questions

3. What barriers exist to implementing your top recommendations?
4. How could monitoring and data collection be improved for ongoing tracking?
5. What is your revised water conservation goal based on this audit?
