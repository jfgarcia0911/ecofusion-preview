## Questions

**1. What is the primary benefit of automation in CEA operations?**
- A) It eliminates the need for human workers entirely
- B) It makes the operation look more technologically advanced
- C) It improves reliability, reduces labor, and enables data-driven decisions
- D) It guarantees higher profits

**2. In a labor reduction calculation, what is typically considered automatable?**
- A) 100% of all manual tasks
- B) 60-80% of repetitive monitoring and control tasks
- C) Only data recording tasks
- D) Less than 10% of total labor

**3. What is the "payback period" in an ROI calculation?**
- A) The time it takes to pay back a loan
- B) The time required until cumulative savings equal the initial investment
- C) The warranty period of automation equipment
- D) The expected lifespan of sensors

**4. What is considered an "excellent" payback period for automation investments?**
- A) Less than 6 months
- B) 2-5 years
- C) 5-10 years
- D) Any payback is acceptable

**5. What is "hysteresis" in on/off control?**
- A) A type of sensor
- B) The delay between sensor reading and control action
- C) The difference between on and off setpoints to prevent rapid cycling
- D) A failure mode in relays

**6. Which scenario is best suited for DIY (Arduino/Raspberry Pi) automation?**
- A) Critical life-support systems for $50,000 worth of fish
- B) Learning projects and non-critical monitoring with manageable risk
- C) Large commercial greenhouse requiring 99.9% uptime
- D) Systems requiring regulatory compliance and certification

**7. What is a "fail-safe" design?**
- A) A backup controller that takes over if the primary fails
- B) A system designed so that failures default to a safe state
- C) A warranty that covers all failures
- D) An alarm system

**8. In the automation priority framework, what should be automated FIRST?**
- A) Advanced analytics and predictive models
- B) Life-support and critical safety functions
- C) Automated feeding systems
- D) Lighting schedules

**9. What is a common mistake when implementing automation?**
- A) Testing too thoroughly before deployment
- B) Automating before understanding the system's manual behavior
- C) Maintaining manual override capability
- D) Starting with monitoring before full control

**10. If a pH monitoring system prevents ONE crop loss event worth $5,000, and the system cost $500, what is the ROI?**
- A) 10%
- B) 100%
- C) 900%
- D) 5000%

---

## Answer Key

1. **C** - It improves reliability, reduces labor, and enables data-driven decisions
   - *Explanation:* Automation provides multiple benefits including consistency, labor savings, and data collection for optimization, but doesn't eliminate human judgment or guarantee profits.

2. **B** - 60-80% of repetitive monitoring and control tasks
   - *Explanation:* Not all tasks can or should be automated. Typical operations can automate 60-80% of repetitive tasks while maintaining human oversight.

3. **B** - The time required until cumulative savings equal the initial investment
   - *Explanation:* Payback period = Initial Cost ÷ Annual Net Benefit. It measures how long to recover the investment.

4. **A** - Less than 6 months
   - *Explanation:* Payback < 6 months is considered excellent, 6-12 months is good, 12-24 months is fair.

5. **C** - The difference between on and off setpoints to prevent rapid cycling
   - *Explanation:* Hysteresis (deadband) prevents equipment from turning on and off rapidly when the measured value oscillates around the setpoint.

6. **B** - Learning projects and non-critical monitoring with manageable risk
   - *Explanation:* DIY is great for learning and lower-risk applications. Critical, high-value systems benefit from commercial solutions with support and warranties.

7. **B** - A system designed so that failures default to a safe state
   - *Explanation:* Fail-safe means the system fails to a safe condition (e.g., normally-closed valve fails closed to prevent flooding).

8. **B** - Life-support and critical safety functions
   - *Explanation:* Automate high-risk, high-frequency functions first (fish tank temperature, DO, etc.) before convenience features.

9. **B** - Automating before understanding the system's manual behavior
   - *Explanation:* You should operate manually for at least one full cycle to understand normal behavior before automating.

10. **C** - 900%
    - *Explanation:* ROI = (Benefit - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100 = ($5,000 - $500) ÷ $500 × 100 = 900%
