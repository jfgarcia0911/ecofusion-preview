# Automation & Monitoring Glossary
**Course 203: System Automation & Monitoring**

---

## A

**Accuracy** - How close a sensor measurement is to the true value. Often specified as ±value (e.g., ±0.1 pH).

**Actuator** - A device that converts control signals into physical action (relay, valve, motor, etc.).

**ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter)** - Circuit that converts continuous analog voltages to discrete digital values for computer processing.

**Alert** - Notification sent when a monitored parameter exceeds predefined thresholds.

**Analog Signal** - Continuous signal that can have any value within a range (e.g., 0-5V, 4-20mA).

**API (Application Programming Interface)** - Set of protocols that allows software applications to communicate with each other.

**Arduino** - Open-source microcontroller platform popular for DIY automation projects.

**Aspirated Shield** - Ventilated housing for temperature/humidity sensors that prevents direct solar radiation while allowing airflow.

---

## B

**BNC Connector** - Bayonet Neill-Concelman connector commonly used for pH and other water quality sensor probes.

**Baud Rate** - Speed of serial communication, measured in bits per second (common: 9600, 115200).

---

## C

**Calibration** - Process of adjusting a sensor to read accurately by comparing to known standards.

**Capacitive Sensor** - Sensor that measures changes in capacitance (common for soil moisture, non-contact level sensing).

**Cloud** - Internet-based computing where data and applications are stored on remote servers rather than locally.

**CO2 Enrichment** - Adding carbon dioxide to greenhouse air to enhance photosynthesis (typically 800-1200 ppm).

**Controller** - Device that reads sensors and executes control logic to activate outputs.

**Current Loop (4-20mA)** - Industrial analog signal standard where 4mA represents 0% and 20mA represents 100%.

---

## D

**Dashboard** - Visual interface displaying real-time and historical data from automation systems.

**Data Logging** - Recording sensor values over time for analysis and documentation.

**Deadband** - See Hysteresis.

**DHT22** - Popular digital temperature and humidity sensor for Arduino/Raspberry Pi projects.

**Digital Signal** - Discrete signal with distinct states (typically 0 and 1, or OFF and ON).

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)** - Total photosynthetically active photons received per day, measured in mol/m²/day.

**DO (Dissolved Oxygen)** - Concentration of oxygen dissolved in water, critical for fish and root health (mg/L or ppm).

**Dosing Pump** - Pump that delivers precise amounts of liquid (nutrients, pH adjusters) on demand.

**DS18B20** - Popular digital temperature sensor for Arduino projects (1-Wire protocol).

**Drift** - Gradual change in sensor accuracy over time due to aging, fouling, or degradation.

---

## E

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)** - Measure of solution's ability to conduct electricity, indicates total dissolved solids (mS/cm or µS/cm).

**Escalation** - Progressive increase in alert urgency and notification methods if problem persists.

**Ethernet** - Wired networking standard (CAT5e/CAT6 cables) for reliable data communication.

---

## F

**Fail-Safe** - Design where system defaults to a safe state when power or control is lost.

**Float Switch** - Mechanical level sensor using a buoyant float to trigger switch contacts.

**Firmware** - Software embedded in hardware devices (controllers, sensors).

---

## G

**GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output)** - Programmable pins on microcontrollers/computers for sensor and actuator connections.

**Grafana** - Open-source platform for visualizing time-series data with customizable dashboards.

**Greenhouse Controller** - Automation system specifically designed for managing greenhouse climate (temperature, humidity, vents, etc.).

---

## H

**HVAC** - Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems.

**Hysteresis** - Difference between on and off setpoints in control to prevent rapid cycling (also called deadband).

---

## I

**I2C (Inter-Integrated Circuit)** - Digital communication protocol for connecting sensors to microcontrollers (short distances).

**InfluxDB** - Time-series database optimized for sensor data storage and retrieval.

**IP Address** - Numerical identifier for devices on a network (e.g., 192.168.1.100).

---

## J

**JSON (JavaScript Object Notation)** - Text-based data format commonly used in APIs and data exchange.

---

## L

**Ladder Logic** - Visual programming language for PLCs resembling electrical circuit diagrams.

**LoRa (Long Range)** - Wireless communication protocol for long-distance (km), low-power sensor networks.

---

## M

**Modbus** - Industrial communication protocol for connecting sensors and controllers (Modbus RTU over RS485, Modbus TCP over Ethernet).

**MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport)** - Lightweight publish/subscribe messaging protocol for IoT applications.

**Multimeter** - Test instrument for measuring voltage, current, resistance, and continuity.

---

## N

**NDIR (Non-Dispersive Infrared)** - CO2 sensor technology using infrared light absorption.

**Normally Closed (NC)** - Relay or valve contacts that are closed (connected) when not energized.

**Normally Open (NO)** - Relay or valve contacts that are open (disconnected) when not energized.

---

## O

**ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential)** - Measurement of water's oxidizing or reducing power (mV), indicates sanitizer levels.

---

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)** - Light in the 400-700nm wavelength range used by plants for photosynthesis.

**Payback Period** - Time required for an investment to pay for itself through cost savings or additional revenue.

**Peristaltic Pump** - Positive displacement pump using rollers to compress tubing, accurate for dosing.

**pH** - Measure of acidity/alkalinity on scale of 0-14 (7 is neutral, <7 acidic, >7 alkaline).

**PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative)** - Advanced control algorithm that adjusts output based on current error, accumulated error, and rate of change.

**PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)** - Industrial computer designed for automation in rugged environments.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)** - Measure of light intensity for photosynthesis (µmol/m²/s).

**Precision** - Consistency of sensor readings (repeatability), independent of accuracy.

**Pull-up/Pull-down Resistor** - Resistor used to set default state of digital input when switch is open.

**PWM (Pulse Width Modulation)** - Method of controlling analog outputs (LED brightness, motor speed) by rapidly switching on/off.

---

## R

**Raspberry Pi** - Small single-board computer popular for automation projects with networking and data processing.

**RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System)** - Aquaculture system that filters and reuses water.

**Relay** - Electrically operated switch allowing low-voltage control of high-voltage/current loads.

**Resolution** - Smallest change a sensor can detect and report.

**Response Time (T90)** - Time for sensor to reach 90% of final value after a step change.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - Financial metric comparing benefits to costs (ROI % = (Benefit - Cost) / Cost × 100).

**RS485** - Industrial serial communication standard allowing multiple devices on one bus (up to 1200m).

**RTD (Resistance Temperature Detector)** - High-accuracy temperature sensor (Pt100 common).

---

## S

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)** - Software system for monitoring and controlling industrial processes.

**Sensor Drift** - See Drift.

**Setpoint** - Target value for a controlled parameter (e.g., temperature setpoint = 72°F).

**Solenoid Valve** - Electrically operated valve using electromagnetic coil to open/close.

**SQL (Structured Query Language)** - Language for managing and querying databases.

**Static IP** - Manually assigned IP address that doesn't change (vs. DHCP dynamic assignment).

---

## T

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)** - Mass concentration of dissolved substances in water (ppm or mg/L), derived from EC measurement.

**Thermistor** - Temperature sensor using temperature-dependent resistor (inexpensive, common in DIY).

**Thermocouple** - Temperature sensor using junction of dissimilar metals (wide range, less accurate for CEA).

**Time-Series Data** - Data collected at regular time intervals (sensor readings over time).

**Turbidity** - Measure of water clarity/cloudiness (NTU units).

---

## U

**UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)** - Battery backup system providing temporary power during outages.

---

## V

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)** - Device controlling AC motor speed by varying frequency and voltage (energy savings, precise control).

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)** - Measure of atmosphere's drying power based on temperature and humidity (kPa).

---

## W

**Watchdog Timer** - Safety mechanism that resets controller if it becomes unresponsive.

**Wi-Fi** - Wireless networking standard (802.11) for internet and local network connectivity.

---

## Z

**Zigbee** - Wireless mesh networking protocol for low-power sensor networks.

---

## Common Units of Measurement

| Parameter | Common Units | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| **pH** | pH units (0-14) | Logarithmic scale |
| **EC** | mS/cm or µS/cm | 1 mS/cm = 1000 µS/cm |
| **TDS** | ppm or mg/L | Derived from EC |
| **DO** | mg/L or ppm | Also % saturation |
| **Temperature** | °F or °C | °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9 |
| **Light (PAR)** | µmol/m²/s (PPFD) | Instantaneous |
| **Light (DLI)** | mol/m²/day | Accumulated daily |
| **CO2** | ppm (parts per million) | Atmospheric ~420 ppm |
| **Humidity** | % RH (relative humidity) | 0-100% |
| **Pressure** | PSI, kPa, or bar | 1 bar ≈ 14.5 PSI |
| **Flow** | GPM, GPH, or L/min | Gallons/liters per minute/hour |
| **Electrical Current** | mA (milliamps) | 4-20mA common |
| **Voltage** | V (volts) | 5V, 12V, 24V, 120V common |
