## Calculation Verification for Question 10

**Question 10 detailed solution:**
- Target: 250 heads
- Crop loss: 7% → 250 ÷ 0.93 = 269 plants needed
- Transplant success: 93% → 269 ÷ 0.93 = 289 seedlings needed
- Germination: 88% → 289 ÷ 0.88 = 328 seeds
- Buffer: 10% → 328 × 1.10 = 361 seeds

**Note:** Answer C (335) is the closest option provided.
