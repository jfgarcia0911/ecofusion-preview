# Assignment 5: Annual Production Plan
## Course 204: Crop Production Planning

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 10 |

## Purpose
Create comprehensive 12-month production plan integrating all course concepts.

## Components Required

### 1. Executive Summary (4 pts)
- Production goals
- Crop selection rationale
- Key strategies

### 2. Monthly Production Schedule (10 pts)
Complete calendar showing:
- Weekly seeding quantities by crop
- Projected harvest volumes
- Revenue projections
- Seasonal adjustments

### 3. Resource Requirements (6 pts)
- Space allocation
- Labor requirements
- Input needs (seeds, nutrients, packaging)
- Equipment needs

### 4. Quality Management (5 pts)
- Quality standards
- Grading procedures
- Customer specifications

### 5. Risk Management (5 pts)
- Crop failure contingencies
- Market fluctuation strategies
- Weather/seasonal considerations

**Deliverable:** 10-15 page professional production plan with:
- Monthly calendars
- Resource budgets
- Quality protocols
- Risk mitigation strategies
