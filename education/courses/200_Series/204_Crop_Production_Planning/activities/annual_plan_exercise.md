# Activity: Annual Production Plan Exercise
## Course 204: Crop Production Planning

---

## Overview

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Activity Type** | Hands-on Planning Exercise |
| **Duration** | 2-3 hours |
| **Materials** | Planning templates, calculator, crop data |
| **Deliverable** | Complete 12-month production plan |

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Create a comprehensive annual production plan
2. Calculate production capacity and targets
3. Schedule succession plantings
4. Balance production with market demand
5. Identify resource requirements

---
