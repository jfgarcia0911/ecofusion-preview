## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary cause of tipburn in lettuce?

A) Excessive nitrogen
B) Calcium deficiency (often localized transport issue)
C) Too much light
D) Overwatering

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Quality standards should be established before production begins and communicated to customers.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 3
Which quality defect in lettuce is caused by bolting?

A) Yellowing leaves
B) Bitter taste and elongated stem
C) Soft rot
D) Brown edges

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the optimal temperature for post-harvest cooling of leafy greens?

A) 32-36°F
B) 38-42°F
C) 45-50°F
D) 50-55°F

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
True or False: Harvesting lettuce in the early morning when plants are turgid improves post-harvest quality and shelf life.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 6
Which factor is MOST critical for preventing quality loss during packing?

A) Speed of packing
B) Temperature control and gentle handling
C) Type of packaging material
D) Size of packing crew

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the shelf life goal for hydroponic lettuce under proper storage?

A) 3-5 days
B) 7-10 days
C) 10-14 days
D) 14-21 days

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
Which quality grading criterion is used for basil?

A) Head weight
B) Leaf color, aroma, and absence of flowering
C) Root length
D) Stem thickness only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
True or False: Consistent quality is more important than occasional exceptional quality for building customer relationships.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 10
What causes russeting or bronzing on lettuce leaves?

A) Low humidity
B) Ozone exposure or environmental stress
C) High nitrogen
D) Calcium excess

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. B - Calcium deficiency/transport
2. True - Establish standards early
3. B - Bitter taste from bolting
4. A - 32-36°F for cooling
5. True - Morning harvest improves quality
6. B - Temperature and gentle handling
7. C - 10-14 days shelf life
8. B - Color, aroma, no flowering
9. True - Consistency builds trust
10. B - Ozone/environmental stress
