## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended portfolio strategy for most successful CEA operations?

A) 100% specialty crops for maximum profit
B) 70-80% staple crops, 15-25% specialty crops, 5-10% trial crops
C) 50% staple crops, 50% specialty crops
D) Equal distribution across all crop types

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which metric combines both space efficiency and annual turnover to evaluate crop profitability?

A) Gross margin percentage
B) Labor cost per pound
C) Revenue per square foot per year
D) Contribution margin per unit

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
True or False: When selecting crops, market demand should be analyzed before evaluating profitability.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 4
A crop generates $72/sqft/year in revenue with $28/sqft/year in variable costs. What is the contribution margin per square foot?

A) $28
B) $44
C) $72
D) $100

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which growing system is BEST suited for cherry tomatoes and other heavy fruiting crops?

A) NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)
B) Vertical towers
C) Media beds
D) Grow trays

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
True or False: Microgreens typically have the highest revenue per square foot but also the highest labor costs.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 7
In the Crop Evaluation Scorecard, which factor carries the highest weight?

A) Market Demand (25%)
B) Profitability (30%)
C) System Compatibility (15%)
D) Labor Efficiency (20%)

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is a Tier 1 staple crop characteristic?

A) Limited market demand
B) Year-round demand and multiple buyer types
C) Premium pricing with small volume
D) Requires customer education

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Which of the following is NOT one of the five factors in the Strategic Crop Selection Framework?

A) Market Demand
B) Profitability Analysis
C) Marketing Strategy
D) Labor Efficiency

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
True or False: According to the 80/20 rule, approximately 20% of crops generate 80% of revenue in most profitable operations.

**Correct Answer:** True
