## Questions

### Question 1
What is the optimal time of day to transplant seedlings to minimize stress?

A) Early morning
B) Midday
C) Late afternoon
D) Time of day doesn't matter

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
True or False: Seedlings should be hardened off gradually before transplanting to production systems.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 3
What is "transplant shock"?

A) Physical damage to roots during transplanting
B) Temporary growth slowdown as plants adjust to new environment
C) Disease spread during transplanting
D) Nutrient toxicity in new growing location

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
For butterhead lettuce at 8" spacing, how many plants are transplanted per square foot?

A) 1.5 plants
B) 2.25 plants
C) 3.0 plants
D) 4.0 plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What should be done immediately after transplanting to reduce stress?

A) Increase light intensity to maximum
B) Reduce EC slightly and ensure adequate moisture
C) Apply high-nitrogen fertilizer
D) Increase temperature by 10°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
True or False: Transplanting success rates of 90-95% are typical for well-managed operations.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 7
Which spacing is typically used for loose leaf lettuce in NFT or DWC systems?

A) 6" × 6"
B) 8" × 8"
C) 10" × 10"
D) 12" × 12"

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is the primary reason to transplant in the afternoon or evening in hot conditions?

A) Better root growth
B) Reduced evapotranspiration stress during adjustment
C) Higher nutrient uptake
D) Improved germination

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
How long does transplant shock typically last?

A) 6-12 hours
B) 1-2 days
C) 2-4 days
D) 7-10 days

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
True or False: Seedlings with well-developed root systems experience less transplant shock than those with sparse roots.

**Correct Answer:** True
