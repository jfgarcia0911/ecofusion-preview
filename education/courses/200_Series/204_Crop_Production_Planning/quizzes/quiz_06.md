## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary trade-off when optimizing for maximum yield?

A) Higher labor costs
B) Reduced crop quality
C) Longer crop cycles
D) Increased disease pressure

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Tighter plant spacing always results in higher revenue per square foot.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
Which factor has the MOST impact on yield optimization in controlled environment agriculture?

A) Plant genetics
B) Daily light integral (DLI)
C) pH management
D) Irrigation frequency

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the optimal DLI range for maximum lettuce production?

A) 10-12 mol/m²/day
B) 14-17 mol/m²/day
C) 20-24 mol/m²/day
D) 26-30 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
True or False: Increasing plant density by 25% typically increases labor costs by more than 25%.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 6
Which strategy can increase annual turns for leafy greens?

A) Wider plant spacing
B) Lower light intensity
C) Cooler temperatures
D) Optimized environmental conditions reducing crop time

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 7
What is a key indicator that plants are too densely spaced?

A) Faster growth rates
B) Etiolation (stretching) and reduced air circulation
C) Darker leaf color
D) Higher yields per plant

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
For butterhead lettuce, what is the target head weight range for premium quality?

A) 2-4 oz
B) 4-6 oz
C) 6-8 oz
D) 8-10 oz

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
True or False: Variety selection can impact yield by 20-40% even under identical growing conditions.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 10
Which approach typically yields the best financial results?

A) Maximum yield regardless of quality
B) Maximum quality regardless of yield
C) Balanced approach optimizing both yield and quality
D) Focus entirely on reducing production time

**Correct Answer:** C

6. D - Optimized conditions reduce crop time
