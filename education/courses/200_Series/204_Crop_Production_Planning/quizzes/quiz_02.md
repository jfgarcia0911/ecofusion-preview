## Questions

### Question 1
What is succession planting?

A) Planting all crops at once for a single harvest
B) Seeding crops at regular intervals for continuous harvests
C) Planting crops in succession of height
D) Rotating crops through different growing zones

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
You need 200 heads of lettuce per week. With 90% germination, 95% transplant success, 5% crop loss, and 10% buffer, how many seeds should you plant weekly?

A) 200 seeds
B) 256 seeds
C) 222 seeds
D) 180 seeds

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
True or False: A crop with a 6-week cycle will have 6 batches in the system during steady-state succession planting.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 4
Why do crop cycles lengthen in winter even in controlled environments?

A) Only due to colder temperatures
B) Reduced natural light availability (lower DLI)
C) Higher humidity levels
D) Equipment performance decreases

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended seeding buffer for a new operation (less than 1 year)?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-15%
C) 15-20%
D) 20-25%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
True or False: In winter, crop cycles for leafy greens can increase by 25-33% compared to summer cycles.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 7
How many batches of butterhead lettuce (42-day cycle) are typically in propagation during steady-state production?

A) 1 batch
B) 2 batches
C) 4 batches
D) 6 batches

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which production buffer strategy maintains 1-2 extra batches beyond normal rotation?

A) Seeding buffer
B) Extra batch buffer
C) Timing buffer
D) Replacement planting

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is the primary benefit of succession planting for revenue management?

A) Higher total yield
B) Consistent weekly income vs. boom-bust cycle
C) Lower labor costs
D) Reduced seed expenses

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
True or False: Spreadsheet-based production tracking is recommended to start, with specialized software considered after 6-12 months of operation.

**Correct Answer:** True
