## Questions

### Question 1
What are the six universal crop growth stages in correct order?

A) Seedling, Germination, Vegetative, Reproductive, Transition, Maturation
B) Germination, Seedling, Vegetative, Transition, Reproductive, Maturation
C) Germination, Vegetative, Seedling, Flowering, Fruiting, Harvest
D) Seedling, Vegetative, Flowering, Fruiting, Ripening, Harvest

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Lettuce requires a shift from vegetative to reproductive nutrient formulas.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
What N-P-K ratio is appropriate for tomatoes during the flowering and fruiting stages?

A) 3-1-2 (high nitrogen)
B) 2-3-3 to 1-3-4 (lower nitrogen, higher P-K)
C) 1-1-1 (balanced)
D) 5-1-1 (very high nitrogen)

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
At what temperature should butterhead lettuce be grown during production for best quality?

A) 55-60°F
B) 60-68°F
C) 70-75°F
D) 75-80°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What does DIF refer to in crop management?

A) Daily Irrigation Frequency
B) Day-night temperature difference
C) Direct Incoming Flux (light measurement)
D) Disease Infection Factor

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
True or False: Basil plants can be harvested multiple times (4-10 cycles) over 4-6 months.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 7
What EC range is appropriate for tomatoes during the fruiting stage?

A) 0.8-1.2 mS/cm
B) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm
C) 1.8-2.2 mS/cm
D) 2.2-2.8 mS/cm

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 8
When should tomato plants begin receiving pollination assistance?

A) At transplant
B) During vegetative growth
C) When flowering begins
D) When fruits are ripening

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is the optimal temperature for basil production?

A) 60-68°F
B) 65-72°F
C) 70-80°F
D) 80-90°F

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
True or False: Using stage observation combined with size measurements can predict harvest timing within ±1-2 days.

**Correct Answer:** True

4. B - 60-68°F for quality lettuce
5. B - Day-night temperature difference
