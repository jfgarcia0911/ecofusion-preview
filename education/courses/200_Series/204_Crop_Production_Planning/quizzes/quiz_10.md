## Questions

### Question 1
When creating an annual production plan, what should be established FIRST?

A) Seeding schedule
B) Customer commitments and target sales volumes
C) Equipment purchases
D) Staffing plan

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: An annual production plan should be rigid and never adjusted once created.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
What is the recommended approach for planning crop mix in an annual plan?

A) Single crop focus
B) Equal space to all possible crops
C) Core crops (70-80%), rotation crops (15-25%), trials (5-10%)
D) Only trial crops for maximum profit

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
How should seasonal demand fluctuations be addressed in production planning?

A) Ignore them and produce consistently year-round
B) Adjust crop mix and volumes based on known seasonal patterns
C) Shut down in slow seasons
D) Only produce in summer

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
True or False: Buffer capacity should be included in annual production plans to handle unexpected demand or crop losses.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 6
What financial metric is essential to include in a production plan?

A) Stock market performance
B) Projected revenue, costs, and profitability by crop
C) Real estate values
D) Competitor pricing only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
How frequently should production plans be reviewed and adjusted?

A) Once created, never change
B) Every 5 years
C) Monthly or quarterly with formal annual review
D) Daily

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is a key component of risk management in production planning?

A) Insurance policies only
B) Diversification of crops, backup varieties, and contingency plans
C) Growing single crop perfectly
D) Avoiding all trials

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
True or False: Labor planning should be integrated with production schedules to ensure adequate staffing for peak periods.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 10
What is the ultimate goal of a comprehensive production plan?

A) Fill all available space
B) Grow the most crops possible
C) Reliable, profitable production meeting customer needs consistently
D) Win farming awards

**Correct Answer:** C
