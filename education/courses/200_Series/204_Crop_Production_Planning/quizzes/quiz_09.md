## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended starting tool for production planning in a new operation?

A) Enterprise-level farm management software
B) Spreadsheets (Excel or Google Sheets)
C) Custom database application
D) Paper and pencil only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Digital production planning tools should be implemented on day one of operation before processes are established.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
What information should be tracked in a batch tracking system?

A) Crop type only
B) Seed date only
C) Batch ID, crop, seed date, transplant date, harvest date, quantities
D) Harvest date only

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
When should an operation consider moving from spreadsheets to specialized software?

A) Immediately
B) After 1-2 weeks
C) After 6-12 months when processes are established
D) Never, spreadsheets are always sufficient

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
True or False: Production planning software can automatically adjust schedules based on actual vs. planned performance.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 6
Which data point is MOST critical to track for improving future production schedules?

A) Weather conditions
B) Actual vs. planned harvest dates and yields
C) Employee lunch breaks
D) Seed supplier names

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a key benefit of mobile-based farm management apps?

A) Lower cost than spreadsheets
B) Real-time data entry in the growing area
C) No internet connection needed
D) Automatic harvesting

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which planning tool feature is most valuable for multi-crop coordination?

A) Pretty graphics
B) Integrated calendar showing all seeding, transplanting, and harvest tasks
C) Social media integration
D) Weather forecasting

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
True or False: Basic spreadsheet systems can effectively manage production for operations up to 5,000-10,000 square feet.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 10
What should be the primary driver for selecting production planning software?

A) Most expensive option
B) Match to operation complexity, budget, and actual needs
C) Vendor with best advertising
D) Newest technology

**Correct Answer:** B

5. True - Advanced software can auto-adjust
6. B - Actual vs. planned performance
7. B - Real-time field data entry
