## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of conducting variety trials?

A) To fill unused growing space
B) To objectively compare performance across varieties under your conditions
C) To satisfy customer requests
D) To reduce seed costs

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Variety trial results from one location can be directly applied to all other CEA operations without testing.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
How many plants of each variety should be included in a statistically valid trial?

A) 5-10 plants
B) 10-20 plants
C) 20-50 plants
D) 100+ plants

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
Which variety characteristic is MOST important for commercial production?

A) Disease resistance and consistent performance
B) Unique color
C) Fastest growth
D) Lowest seed cost

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
True or False: Variety trials should be conducted during multiple seasons to account for seasonal performance variations.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 6
What metrics should be tracked in a lettuce variety trial?

A) Days to maturity only
B) Days to maturity, head weight, uniformity, quality, disease resistance
C) Seed germination percentage only
D) Customer preference only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
How much space should typically be allocated to variety trials?

A) 1-2% of production space
B) 5-10% of production space
C) 20-25% of production space
D) 50% of production space

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is a key criterion for selecting varieties for CEA production?

A) Outdoor field trial performance
B) Performance in controlled environment conditions (indoor breeding)
C) Oldest available variety
D) Cheapest seed price

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
True or False: Taste testing with customers should be part of the variety evaluation process.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 10
When should a new variety replace an existing core crop?

A) Immediately after one successful trial
B) After consistent superior performance across multiple trials and seasons
C) When seed is on sale
D) Never change core varieties

**Correct Answer:** B
