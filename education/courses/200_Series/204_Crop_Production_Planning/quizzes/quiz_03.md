## Questions

### Question 1
What is the optimal temperature range for lettuce seed germination?

A) 55-60°F
B) 65-70°F
C) 75-80°F
D) 80-85°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
True or False: Seedlings should receive full-strength nutrients immediately after germination.

**Correct Answer:** False

---

### Question 3
What is the typical germination time for lettuce seeds under optimal conditions?

A) 1-2 days
B) 2-3 days
C) 5-7 days
D) 10-14 days

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Which propagation method is MOST common for commercial CEA leafy green production?

A) Direct seeding into production system
B) Plug trays with growing media
C) Hydroponic germination pads
D) Soil blocks

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What EC (electrical conductivity) range is appropriate for seedlings in propagation?

A) 0.2-0.5 mS/cm
B) 0.8-1.2 mS/cm
C) 1.5-2.0 mS/cm
D) 2.0-2.5 mS/cm

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
True or False: Most lettuce varieties are ready to transplant when they have 2-4 true leaves.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 7
What daily light integral (DLI) is appropriate for seedling propagation?

A) 6-10 mol/m²/day
B) 10-14 mol/m²/day
C) 14-18 mol/m²/day
D) 18-22 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
How much propagation space is typically needed to support 1,000 square feet of production space?

A) 50-75 sq ft (5-7.5%)
B) 100-150 sq ft (10-15%)
C) 200-250 sq ft (20-25%)
D) 300-400 sq ft (30-40%)

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Which factor is MOST critical for preventing damping-off disease in seedlings?

A) High humidity
B) Warm temperatures
C) Good air circulation
D) High nutrient levels

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
True or False: Pre-germinating seeds before placing in plugs can improve uniformity and reduce crop time by 2-3 days.

**Correct Answer:** True
