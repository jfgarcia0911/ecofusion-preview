## Course Description

Develop expertise in planning and managing crop production for consistent, high-quality harvests. Master crop selection, scheduling, yield optimization, and quality management to ensure continuous production and satisfied customers year-round.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Select appropriate crops for specific systems and markets
2. Develop production schedules for continuous harvest
3. Optimize yield and quality through cultural practices
4. Manage crop rotation and succession planting
5. Implement quality control programs
6. Use production planning software and tools

## Key Topics

### Crop Selection Criteria
- Market demand analysis
- System compatibility
- Profitability assessment
- Labor requirements
- Space efficiency
- Seasonal considerations

### Production Scheduling
- Succession planting calculations
- Calendar management
- Buffer planning
- Seasonal adjustments
- Multi-crop coordination

### Quality Management
- Quality standards by crop
- Grading criteria
- Consistency protocols
- Customer specification management
- Continuous improvement
