# Module 5: Growth Stage Management
## Course 204: Crop Production Planning

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
1. Identify distinct growth stages for major crop types
2. Adjust environmental conditions by growth stage
3. Modify nutrient formulations for different development phases
4. Time cultural practices to specific growth stages
5. Predict harvest windows based on growth stage progression
6. Optimize quality through stage-specific management

---

## Introduction

All crops progress through distinct developmental stages, each with unique requirements for light, temperature, nutrients, and water. Managing these stages appropriately is the difference between average yields and exceptional production. Understanding growth stages also allows you to accurately predict harvest timing and make informed decisions about resource allocation.

This module teaches you to recognize growth stages and adjust your management practices accordingly for maximum crop performance.

```
│                                                                     │
│  Days 35-42: MATURATION                                             │
│  [mature head]→[HARVEST]                                            │
│  • Maintain conditions                                              │
│  • Monitor size daily                                               │

│  • Harvest at optimal size (variety dependent)                      │
│                                                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Day 7: Vigorous seedlings, 2-3 true leaves
```

## Herbs: Stage Management

### Basil Development

```
BASIL GROWTH AND HARVEST CYCLES
════════════════════════════════

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                     │
│  Weeks 0-3: ESTABLISHMENT                                           │
│  • Germination + seedling development                               │
│  • Temperature: 70-75°F                                             │
│  • Light: 14-16 hrs, 200 μmol/m²/s                                  │
│  • EC: 1.0-1.2 mS/cm                                                │
│  • Focus: Root development, leaf formation                          │
│                                                                     │
│  Weeks 3-7: VEGETATIVE GROWTH                                       │
│  • Rapid leaf and stem development                                  │
│  • Temperature: 70-80°F                                             │
│  • Light: 16-18 hrs, DLI 15-20 mol/m²/day                           │
│  • EC: 1.4-1.8 mS/cm                                                │
│  • Pinch growing tips at 6-8" height                                │
│  • Promotes bushier growth                                          │
│                                                                     │
│  Weeks 7-12: FIRST HARVEST CYCLE                                    │
│  • Cut back 1/3 to 1/2 of plant                                     │
│  • Leave 2-3 sets of leaves on stems                                │
│  • Harvest promotes branching                                       │
│  • Maintain optimal conditions                                      │
│                                                                     │
│  Weeks 12+: SUBSEQUENT HARVESTS                                     │
│  • Harvest every 2-3 weeks                                          │
│  • Prevent flowering (pinch flower buds)                            │
│  • Monitor for declining vigor                                      │
│  • Plan for replanting after 4-6 harvest cycles                     │
│                                                                     │

│                                                                     │

│        ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓    ↓             │
```

## Fruiting Crops: Stage Management

### Tomato Growth Stages

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║              TOMATO (Cherry/Grape) STAGE MANAGEMENT                    ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                        ║
║  STAGE 1: SEEDLING (Weeks 0-4)                                         ║
║  ═════════════════════════════                                         ║
║  Temp: 70-75°F  │  Light: 14-16 hrs  │  EC: 1.0-1.5                   ║
║  • Strong root development critical                                    ║
║  • Compact growth (prevent stretching)                                 ║
║  • 4-6 true leaves before transplant                                   ║
║                                                                        ║
║  STAGE 2: VEGETATIVE (Weeks 4-8)                                       ║
║  ════════════════════════════════                                      ║
║  Temp: 70-78°F  │  Light: 16-18 hrs, DLI 25-30  │  EC: 1.8-2.2        ║
║  • Rapid stem and leaf growth                                          ║
║  • Begin training/trellising at 12-18"                                 ║
║  • High nitrogen for vegetative growth                                 ║
║  • Prune suckers, maintain 1-2 main stems                              ║
║                                                                        ║
║  STAGE 3: FLOWERING (Weeks 8-10)                                       ║
║  ════════════════════════════════                                      ║
║  Temp: 70-78°F day, 60-65°F night  │  EC: 2.0-2.5                     ║
║  • First flower clusters appear                                        ║
║  • Begin pollination (vibration, air movement)                         ║
║  • Shift to bloom formula (lower N, higher P-K)                        ║
║  • Continue pruning and training                                       ║
║                                                                        ║
║  STAGE 4: FRUIT SET & DEVELOPMENT (Weeks 10-12)                        ║
║  ══════════════════════════════════════════════                        ║
║  Temp: 70-75°F day, 62-68°F night  │  EC: 2.2-2.8                     ║
║  • Green fruits forming and sizing                                     ║
║  • High potassium for fruit development                                ║
║  • Manage fruit load (thin if needed)                                  ║
║  • Continue training, remove lower leaves                              ║
║                                                                        ║
║  STAGE 5: RIPENING & HARVEST (Weeks 12+)                               ║
║  ════════════════════════════════════════                              ║
║  Temp: 68-75°F  │  EC: 2.0-2.5  │  Slightly reduce irrigation         ║
║  • Fruits coloring and ripening                                        ║
║  • Harvest ripe fruit every 2-3 days                                   ║
║  • Continuous production for 4-6 months                                ║
║  • Monitor plant health, replace when declining                        ║
║                                                                        ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Critical Interventions by Stage

```
TOMATO CULTURAL PRACTICES TIMELINE
═══════════════════════════════════

Week 4:  TRANSPLANT to production system
Week 5:  Install trellis clips, begin weekly training
Week 6:  FIRST PRUNING - remove lower suckers
Week 7:  Continue training, prune suckers weekly
Week 8:  FLOWERING begins - start pollination routine
Week 9:  Monitor fruit set, adjust pollination
Week 10: FRUIT SET visible - shift to bloom nutrients
Week 11: Continue training, remove lower leaves for air flow
Week 12: FIRST HARVEST (green/breaker stage or ripe)
Week 13+: REGULAR HARVESTS every 2-3 days
         Continue pruning, training, leaf removal
         Monitor for pests/disease
         Replace plants at 24-28 weeks
```

## Harvest Window Prediction

### Monitoring Indicators

```
PREDICTING HARVEST TIMING
═════════════════════════

METHOD 1: CALENDAR-BASED (Baseline)
────────────────────────────────────
Use known DTM (Days to Maturity) for crop/variety
Adjust for:
• Season (add 20-30% in winter)
• Temperature variations
• Light levels

Accuracy: ±5-7 days

METHOD 2: STAGE OBSERVATION (Better)
─────────────────────────────────────
Track developmental milestones:

LETTUCE:
Week 3: Small rosette visible
Week 4: Active leaf development
Week 5: Heading begins (butterhead)
Week 5.5: 75% target size → Harvest in 3-7 days
Week 6: OPTIMAL harvest window

TOMATO:
Week 8: First flowers
Week 10: First fruit set (green)
Week 11: Fruits sizing (golf ball size)
Week 12: First breaker stage → Harvest starting
Weeks 12-24: Continuous harvest

METHOD 3: SIZE/QUALITY MEASUREMENT (Most Accurate)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Measure representative sample daily:

• Head diameter (lettuce)
• Height (greens)
• Fruit size and color (tomatoes)
• Weight (basil bunches)

When 80% of batch reaches target size:
→ Harvest window is 2-5 days away
→ Prepare for harvest operations
→ Alert customers

Accuracy: ±1-2 days
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **All crops progress through distinct stages** - Germination, seedling, vegetative, transition, reproductive, maturation

2. **Each stage has specific requirements** - Temperature, light, nutrients, and water needs change as plants develop

3. **Leafy greens are simpler** - Vegetative growth only, consistent requirements throughout

4. **Fruiting crops are complex** - Multiple stages requiring different nutrient ratios and environmental conditions

5. **Nutrient formulations must shift** - Seedlings need light feeding, vegetative needs high N, fruiting needs high P-K

6. **Temperature affects quality** - Cooler temperatures for lettuce quality, proper DIF for tomato fruit set

7. **Cultural practices are stage-specific** - Pruning, training, and pollination must be timed to growth stages

8. **Harvest prediction improves with observation** - Combining calendar, stage tracking, and measurements provides accuracy
