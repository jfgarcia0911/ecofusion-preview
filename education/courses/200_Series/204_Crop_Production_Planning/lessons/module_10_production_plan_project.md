## Part 4: Operational Protocols

### Standard Operating Procedures

```
PROPAGATION PROTOCOL - Butterhead Lettuce 'Nancy'
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

SEEDING:
• Tray: 128-plug
• Media: Rockwool cubes
• Seeds per cell: 1
• Coverage: Light vermiculite
• Moisture: Pre-soak media, mist after seeding
• Location: Germination shelf with humidity dome
• Temperature: 65-70°F
• Label: Batch ID, date, variety

GERMINATION (Days 0-3):
• Maintain humidity dome
• Check moisture 2×/day
• Target emergence: 90%+ by day 3

SEEDLING PHASE (Days 3-14):
• Remove dome at emergence
• Move to grow lights
• Light: 14-16 hrs, 150-200 μmol/m²/s
• Temperature: 65-68°F
• Fertilizer: Start at day 7, EC 1.0-1.2
• Transplant readiness: 2-3 true leaves, roots visible

TRANSPLANTING PROTOCOL:
────────────────────────
• System: DWC, Bay 2
• Spacing: 8" × 8" (2.25 plants/sqft)
• Timing: Day 14 (optimal window days 12-16)
• Water temp: 65-68°F
• EC: 1.2 (reduced from normal 1.6)
• Procedure: [detailed steps from Module 4]

GROWTH MANAGEMENT:
──────────────────
Days 14-35: Vegetative phase
• Temperature: 62-68°F
• EC: 1.4-1.8
• pH: 5.8-6.2
• Monitor for tipburn, adjust Ca if needed

Days 35-42: Maturation
• Monitor size daily
• Harvest window: When 80% reach 10-12 oz

HARVEST & QUALITY:
──────────────────
• Timing: Morning harvest (cool, turgid)
• Grading: Target 90%+ Grade A
• Post-harvest: Wash, cool to 36°F within 30 min
• Packaging: [customer specifications]
• Shelf life target: 7-10 days

[Create similar SOPs for each crop]

────────────────────────────────

□ Environmental calibration
□ Staff training begins

Weeks 7-8:
□ First trial seedings (small scale)
□ Test propagation protocols
□ Verify environmental controls
□ Refine procedures

MONTH 3: RAMP-UP BEGINS
────────────────────────
Weeks 9-12:
□ Begin 25% production seeding
□ Customer outreach and relationship building
□ Continue refining SOPs
□ Build up rotating batches

MONTH 4: INCREASING PRODUCTION
───────────────────────────────
Weeks 13-16:
□ Increase to 50% production
□ First significant harvests
□ Begin regular customer deliveries
□ Monitor quality closely

MONTH 5: NEAR FULL PRODUCTION
──────────────────────────────
Weeks 17-20:
□ 75-100% production seeding
□ Regular harvest schedule established
□ Customer base growing
□ Cash flow turning positive

MONTH 6: STEADY STATE
─────────────────────
Weeks 21-24:
□ Full production achieved
□ All crops in rotation
□ Consistent weekly deliveries
□ Review performance vs. plan
□ Adjust as needed


KEY MILESTONES & SUCCESS METRICS:
══════════════════════════════════

Month 2:  First successful germination (>90%)
Month 3:  First harvest (quality Grade A >85%)
Month 4:  $5,000 monthly revenue
Month 5:  $7,500 monthly revenue
Month 6:  $9,000+ monthly revenue
         10+ regular customers
         Positive monthly cash flow

RISK MITIGATION:
════════════════

Risk: Poor germination rates
Mitigation: Test seeds before bulk order, maintain backup suppliers

Risk: Customer acquisition slower than expected
Mitigation: Pre-sell during setup, diverse channels, strong marketing

Risk: Technical failures
Mitigation: Redundant systems, backup power, maintenance schedule

Risk: Quality issues
Mitigation: SOPs, quality training, gradual ramp-up allows learning

Risk: Cash flow shortage
Mitigation: Adequate working capital, conservative revenue projections
```
