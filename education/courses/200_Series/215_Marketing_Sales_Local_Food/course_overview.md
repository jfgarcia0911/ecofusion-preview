## Brand Development Framework

### Brand Elements

1. **Brand Name**
   - Memorable and meaningful
   - Easy to spell and pronounce
   - Available (web domain, social handles)

2. **Brand Story**
   - Why you started
   - What makes you different
   - Values and mission

3. **Visual Identity**
   - Logo
   - Color palette
   - Typography
   - Photography style

4. **Brand Voice**
   - Personality traits
   - Tone and language
   - Key messages

5. **Brand Experience**
   - Customer touchpoints
   - Consistent quality
   - Customer service

## Sales Techniques

### Restaurant Sales Process

1. **Research** - Know the restaurant and chef
2. **Approach** - Timing matters (avoid service hours)
3. **Sample** - Bring your best products
4. **Listen** - Understand their needs
5. **Follow up** - Consistent, not pushy
6. **Deliver** - Exceed expectations
7. **Maintain** - Build the relationship

### Retail Sales Approach

1. **Buyer meeting** - Schedule with produce buyer
2. **Pitch preparation** - UPC codes, pricing, shelf life
3. **Packaging** - Retail-ready presentation
4. **Demo offer** - In-store sampling builds sales
5. **Consistent supply** - Reliability is essential
