## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

## How to Use This Template

This marketing calendar helps you plan promotional activities, content themes, and sales campaigns throughout the year. Strategic planning ensures consistent marketing presence and aligns efforts with seasonal opportunities.

**Instructions:**
1. Start with your growing season and product availability
2. Add key dates (holidays, farmers markets, community events)
3. Plan content themes by month
4. Schedule specific marketing tactics
5. Review monthly and adjust based on results

## Tips for Using This Calendar

1. **Fill it in with pencil first** - Marketing plans change!

2. **Plan 90 days ahead minimum** - Gives time to create quality content

3. **Batch content creation** - More efficient to create month's content at once

4. **Review and adjust monthly** - What works changes over time

5. **Track everything** - Can't improve what you don't measure

6. **Be realistic** - Don't over-commit on activities

7. **Align with product availability** - Market what you have!

8. **Plan campaigns around natural peaks** - Work with seasons, not against them

9. **Balance promotional and educational content** - Not all posts should sell

10. **Leave room for spontaneity** - Best marketing often responds to moments
