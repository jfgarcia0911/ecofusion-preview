# Activity: Pricing Strategy Analysis & Development
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 90-120 minutes
**Type:** Individual Project with Excel/Spreadsheet Component
**Deliverables:** Complete pricing strategy for 5-10 products across multiple sales channels

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Calculate true production costs including all overhead
2. Develop channel-specific pricing strategies
3. Conduct competitive market analysis
4. Create value-based pricing justified by unique benefits
5. Build a pricing structure that supports profitability and growth

---

## Introduction

Pricing is both art and science. Price too low, and you work hard for minimal profit. Price too high, and you lose customers to competition. The right pricing strategy balances costs, market conditions, perceived value, and business goals.

This exercise walks you through creating a data-driven pricing strategy for your local food products across different sales channels.

## Common Pricing Mistakes - Avoid These!

❌ **Forgetting to include your labor** - Your time has value!

❌ **Pricing based only on costs** - Ignore value and you leave money on the table

❌ **Same price across all channels** - Different channels = different economics

❌ **Never raising prices** - Costs increase every year; prices must too

❌ **Racing to the bottom** - Lowest price attracts worst customers

❌ **Pricing without knowing costs** - Hope is not a strategy

❌ **Ignoring competitive prices** - Operating in vacuum loses sales

✅ **DO THIS INSTEAD:**
- Calculate true costs including overhead and labor
- Price for value, not just cost recovery
- Use channel-specific pricing
- Review and adjust prices regularly
- Communicate value to justify pricing
- Test price changes before full rollout
