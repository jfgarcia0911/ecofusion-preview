## Part 3: Role-Play Practice (20 minutes)

### Exercise 3.1: Farmers Market Scenarios

**Partner A:** Customer
**Partner B:** Farmer

**Scenario 1: Price-Conscious Shopper**
- Customer: Compares prices, expresses sticker shock
- Farmer: Communicate value, offer entry-level option

**Time:** 3 minutes
**Debrief:** What worked? What could improve?

---

**Scenario 2: Quality Enthusiast**
- Customer: Asks detailed questions about growing methods
- Farmer: Share story, emphasize quality and unique varieties

**Time:** 3 minutes
**Debrief:** Did the pitch resonate? Was value communicated?

---

**Scenario 3: First-Time Visitor**
- Customer: Browsing, unsure what to buy
- Farmer: Engage, educate, suggest, sample

**Time:** 3 minutes
**Debrief:** Did you move from browse to buy?

---

### Exercise 3.2: Restaurant Chef Meeting

**Partner A:** Chef
**Partner B:** Farmer

**Scenario: Initial Sales Meeting**
- Farmer: Deliver 2-minute pitch
- Chef: Ask about pricing, delivery, reliability, current customers
- Farmer: Answer objections, propose trial order

**Time:** 5 minutes
**Debrief:** Was value clear? Did objections get handled well?

---

### Exercise 3.3: Retail Buyer Meeting

**Partner A:** Store Buyer
**Partner B:** Farmer

**Scenario: Negotiating Terms**
- Farmer: Present products, pricing, delivery
- Buyer: Negotiate price, ask about packaging, payment terms
- Farmer: Find win-win agreement

**Time:** 5 minutes
**Debrief:** Was deal mutually beneficial?

## Additional Practice Ideas

**Between Now and Real Sales:**

1. **Mirror Practice**
   - Stand in front of mirror
   - Deliver pitch with confident body language
   - Record video to self-evaluate

2. **Family/Friend Testing**
   - Practice on non-farmers
   - Get honest feedback
   - Refine based on confusion points

3. **Product Knowledge Deep Dive**
   - Know every variety's unique traits
   - Memorize flavor profiles
   - Understand best uses and preparation

4. **Objection Anticipation**
   - List every objection you might hear
   - Write and memorize responses
   - Practice until natural

5. **Success Visualization**
   - Visualize successful sales interactions
   - Imagine confident, warm delivery
   - Rehearse positive outcomes
