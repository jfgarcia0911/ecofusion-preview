# Activity: Customer Persona Development Exercise
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 60-90 minutes
**Type:** Individual Project
**Deliverables:** 3 complete customer personas for your target markets

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Identify your most valuable customer segments
2. Create detailed, research-based buyer personas
3. Develop segment-specific marketing messages
4. Match personas to appropriate sales channels and strategies

---

## Introduction

Customer personas transform abstract market segments into real people you can visualize and market to. Instead of targeting "health-conscious consumers," you'll market to "Sarah, the 34-year-old working mom who wants organic food for her kids but struggles to find time for farmers market trips."

This exercise guides you through creating 3 detailed personas that will inform every marketing decision you make.

## Validation Checklist

Before submitting, verify each persona passes these tests:

☐ **The "Dinner Party Test"** - Could you describe this person at a dinner party and have people nod knowingly? Do they feel REAL?

☐ **The "Different Messages Test"** - Would you market differently to each persona? If not, they're too similar.

☐ **The "Customer Recognition Test"** - Can you think of 2-3 actual customers who match each persona?

☐ **The "Decision-Making Test"** - Can you predict how each persona would respond to a new product or pricing change?

☐ **The "Team Test"** - Could someone else on your team use these personas to make marketing decisions?

## Bonus: Advanced Persona Elements

If you want to go deeper, add these elements:

**Objections & Responses**
- What objections might they have? How do you overcome them?

**Customer Lifetime Value**
- What's a customer in this segment worth over 1 year? 3 years?

**Persona Evolution**
- How might this persona change over time (life stages, economic changes)?

**Micro-Personas**
- Are there important sub-segments within each persona?
