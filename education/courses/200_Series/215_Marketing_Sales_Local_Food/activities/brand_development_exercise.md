# Activity: Farm Brand Development Workshop
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

---

## Overview

**Duration:** 90-120 minutes
**Type:** Individual or Team Project
**Deliverables:** Complete brand strategy document

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
1. Define your farm's unique brand identity and positioning
2. Craft a compelling farm story
3. Develop visual brand elements
4. Create brand guidelines for consistent application

**Color Psychology Guide:**
- Green: Growth, nature, sustainability, health
- Blue: Trust, freshness, water, reliability
- Brown/Earth tones: Natural, authentic, traditional
- Yellow: Optimistic, sunny, abundant
- Red: Passionate, energetic, bold
- Purple: Premium, unique, sophisticated

- Personality: Modern / Traditional / Friendly / Professional

**Body Font:** _________________
- Style: Serif / Sans-serif
- Must be: Readable at small sizes

**Font Pairing Tips:**
- Pair serif headings with sans-serif body (classic)
- Pair bold sans-serif headings with regular sans-serif body (modern)
- Use script sparingly (logos, accents only)

## Part 5: Brand Implementation Plan (10 minutes)

### Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

☐ Finalize brand purpose, promise, positioning statements
☐ Write all three versions of farm story
☐ Select color palette and fonts
☐ Design or refine logo (DIY or hire designer)
☐ Create basic brand guidelines document
☐ Update one key touchpoint (website, business cards, or signage)

### Short-Term Actions (60-90 Days)

☐ Professional photos representing brand style
☐ Update all marketing materials for consistency
☐ Train team on brand story and messaging
☐ Redesign market booth to reflect brand
☐ Create social media templates with brand colors/fonts
☐ Develop email newsletter template

### Long-Term Actions (6-12 Months)

☐ Complete rebrand of all touchpoints
☐ Measure brand awareness and perception
☐ Refine based on customer feedback
☐ Consider trademark for brand name/logo
☐ Expand brand to new products/channels

---

## Deliverable: One-Page Brand Guide

**Create a simple brand guide including:**

1. **Brand Foundation**
   - Purpose statement
   - Promise statement
   - Positioning statement
   - 5 personality traits

2. **Visual Identity**
   - Logo (with spacing rules)
   - Color palette (with codes)
   - Typography choices
   - Photography style notes

3. **Voice & Tone**
   - How we communicate
   - Words we use / avoid
   - Sample messages

4. **Quick Reference**
   - 30-second story
   - Key messages
   - Brand do's and don'ts
