## Understanding the Retail Landscape

### Types of Retail Partners

| Retail Type | Local Food Fit | Pricing | Volume | Requirements |
|-------------|----------------|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Natural Food Co-ops** | Excellent | Premium | Medium | Organic preferred, values alignment |
| **Independent Grocers** | Good | Moderate | Medium-High | Consistent supply, professional packaging |
| **Specialty Stores** | Excellent | Premium | Low-Medium | Unique products, quality focus |
| **Supermarket Chains** | Moderate | Competitive | High | High volume, strict specs, complexity |
| **Farm Stands (others)** | Good | Moderate | Low | Complementary products |

### Retail Economics

**Standard Retail Markup: 40-50%**

```
Your Wholesale Price:    $3.00 (your revenue)
Retail Markup (50%):     $1.50 (store margin)
───────────────────────────────
Retail Price:            $4.50 (customer pays)

Your Production Cost:    $1.50
Your Wholesale Margin:   $1.50 (50%)
```

**Keystone Pricing:** Retail price = 2× wholesale price (50% margin)

## Wholesale Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Step 1: Know your costs
Production cost: $1.50/unit

Step 2: Add minimum margin (30-50%)
Wholesale price = $1.50 / (1 - 0.40) = $2.50

Step 3: Check retail price
Projected retail: $2.50 × 2 = $5.00
Compare to: Farmers market price ($4.50)

If retail < farmers market: Pricing works
If retail > farmers market: Price too high, adjust or reconsider
```

### Channel Pricing Consistency

**Managing Price Channels:**
```
Farmers Market (retail):        $4.50
CSA (discounted retail):        $3.50
Online delivery:                $4.50
Restaurant wholesale:           $2.75
Retail wholesale:               $2.50
───────────────────────────────────────
Average across channels:         $3.55
Weighted gross margin:           58%
```

**Rule:** Wholesale should be 50-70% of your direct retail price

## Packaging and Labeling Requirements

### Retail Packaging Essentials

**Required Elements:**
- Farm name and logo
- Product name and variety
- Weight or count
- Harvest/pack date or "best by" date
- Organic certification (if applicable)
- Contact information
- Barcode/PLU (some stores)

### Labeling Compliance

**FDA Labeling Requirements:**

For fresh produce:
- Farm name and address
- Country of origin (USA)
- Net weight (if packaged)

For value-added products:
- Nutrition facts panel
- Ingredient list
- Allergen warnings
- FDA facility registration (if applicable)

**State/Local Requirements:**
- Cottage food laws (varies by state)
- Liability insurance certificate
- Food handler permit (some states)

### Packaging Options by Budget

| Option | Cost per Unit | Volume | Professional Level |
|--------|---------------|--------|-------------------|
| **Rubber band + sticker** | $0.10 | Low | Basic |
| **Plastic clamshells** | $0.25-0.50 | Medium | Standard |
| **Printed bags** | $0.15-0.30 | Medium-High | Professional |
| **Custom containers** | $0.50-1.50 | High | Premium |

## Securing Retail Accounts

### Outreach and Presentations

**Buyer Contact Strategy:**

1. **Research:** Visit store, understand products and values
2. **Contact:** Email or call produce manager/buyer
3. **Sample:** Bring product samples, farm story, pricing
4. **Terms:** Discuss delivery, pricing, payment
5. **Trial:** Start small (1-2 products, 4-6 week trial)
6. **Grow:** Expand based on sales performance

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: Local [Product] for [Store Name]

Hi [Buyer Name],

I'm [Your Name] from [Farm Name], a local farm growing [products]
just [X miles] from your store. We specialize in [unique selling point].

I notice you carry local products and thought our [product] might be
a great fit for your customers. We currently supply [name retailers/
restaurants if possible].

Could I drop off samples and discuss a potential partnership?
I'm available [days/times] this week.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Contact Info]
```

### The Buyer Meeting

**What Buyers Care About:**
1. **Price** - Competitive wholesale price they can mark up profitably
2. **Quality** - Consistent, attractive product
3. **Reliability** - On-time delivery, consistent availability
4. **Packaging** - Retail-ready, compliant
5. **Support** - Sampling, marketing materials, responsiveness

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What local products sell best for you?"
- "What are your delivery day/time preferences?"
- "What's your payment terms?"
- "Do you need any specific packaging or labeling?"
- "How do you like to receive orders?"

## Logistics and Operations

### Delivery Requirements

**Typical Retail Expectations:**
- Delivery window: 6am-10am (before store opens or early hours)
- Frequency: 1-3x per week based on volume
- Consistency: Same day/time weekly
- Notification: Call/text upon arrival
- Backstock: Deliver to cooler, rotate product

**Delivery Checklist:**
- Invoice with PO number
- Product properly packaged and labeled
- Delivery on time
- Neatly stacked/organized
- Remove packaging waste if requested

### Order Management

**Ordering Systems:**
- Standing orders (automatic weekly)
- Email/call orders (manual, flexible)
- Online portal (professional, automated)

**Lead Time:**
- Standard: 48 hours notice
- Custom/large orders: 1 week notice

### Inventory and Shrink

**Managing Retail Risk:**

**Shrink (Unsold Product):**
- Stores may want credits for unsold product
- Negotiate: "You buy, you own" vs. consignment
- Consignment: Higher risk for you, typical in early relationship
- Buyback: Only for quality issues, not slow sales

**Delivery Frequency vs. Freshness:**
```
Shelf Life: 7-10 days

Option 1: Deliver 2x per week (smaller quantities, fresher)
Option 2: Deliver 1x per week (larger quantities, may need markdowns)

Best: Match delivery frequency to sales rate
```

## Payment Terms and Cash Flow

### Standard Terms

| Term | Meaning | Cash Flow Impact |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Net 7** | Pay within 7 days | Best for farm |
| **Net 15** | Pay within 15 days | Standard |
| **Net 30** | Pay within 30 days | Common but challenging |
| **COD** | Cash on delivery | Rare, but ideal for cash flow |

**Negotiation Tips:**
- Request Net 15 for new accounts
- Offer 2% discount for Net 7 (early payment)
- Require payment before next delivery if overdue
- Consider factoring invoices if cash flow critical

### Managing Receivables

**Systems:**
- Invoice immediately upon delivery
- Track aging (30, 60, 90 days)
- Follow up at 7 days past due
- Stop deliveries at 30 days past due

**Software Options:**
- QuickBooks
- FreshBooks
- Wave (free)
- Excel spreadsheet (simple tracking)

## Growing Retail Accounts

### Performance Monitoring

**Track These Metrics:**
- Sales velocity (units per week)
- Sell-through rate (% sold before spoilage)
- Average order size
- Profitability per account

**Optimization:**
```
If sell-through is high (90%+): Increase volume
If sell-through is low (<70%): Reduce volume or improve marketing
If sales growing: Expand product line
If sales stagnant: In-store demos, merchandising support
```

### In-Store Marketing Support

**Ways to Support Retail Partners:**

1. **Sampling/Demos**
   - In-store tastings (weekends)
   - Recipe cards at display
   - Staff training (product knowledge)

2. **Point-of-Sale Materials**
   - Shelf talkers ("Local! [Farm Name]")
   - Farm story cards
   - Seasonal signage

3. **Social Media**
   - Tag retailer in posts
   - Promote store in your marketing
   - Cross-promote on Instagram/Facebook

4. **Co-Marketing**
   - Feature in store newsletter
   - Joint events (farm tour for store customers)
   - Loyalty program integration

5. **Support retail partners to grow sales** - Sampling, marketing, relationship building
