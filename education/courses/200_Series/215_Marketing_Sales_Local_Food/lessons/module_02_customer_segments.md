# Module 2: Identifying and Targeting Customer Segments
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
1. Identify distinct customer segments for local food products
2. Create detailed buyer personas for targeted marketing
3. Develop segment-specific value propositions
4. Select appropriate sales channels for each segment

---

## Introduction

Not all customers are created equal. Some will buy weekly, pay premium prices, and become loyal advocates. Others will purchase occasionally and remain price-sensitive. Understanding customer segmentation allows you to focus marketing efforts where they'll generate the highest return and craft messages that resonate with specific buyer groups.

This module teaches you how to identify, analyze, and target the right customers for your farm business.

### Behavioral Segmentation

Classifying by purchasing patterns and product usage:

| Segment | Purchase Frequency | Basket Size | Price Sensitivity | Channel Preference |
|---------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Weekly Loyalists** | Weekly | Large ($40-80) | Low | CSA, standing orders, on-farm |
| **Regular Customers** | Bi-weekly | Medium ($20-40) | Moderate | Farmers markets, retail |
| **Seasonal Buyers** | Monthly | Medium ($20-40) | Moderate | Farmers markets |
| **Occasional Shoppers** | Quarterly | Small ($10-20) | High | Farmers markets, impulse |

### Example Personas for Local Food Farm

**Persona 1: "Sustainable Sarah"**

**Demographics:**
- Age: 32
- Location: Urban neighborhood, 12 miles from farm
- Occupation: Non-profit program director
- Education: Master's degree
- Household income: $75,000
- Household: Married, 1 young child

**Psychographics:**
- Values: Environmental sustainability, ethical consumption, community
- Lifestyle: Active, health-conscious, socially engaged
- Hobbies: Hiking, cooking, volunteering
- Media: Instagram, sustainability podcasts, NYT Cooking

**Shopping Behavior:**
- Frequency: Weekly
- Basket size: $45
- Preferred time: Saturday morning farmers market
- Channels: Farmers markets, CSA programs, natural food co-op
- Decision process: Researches farms, values alignment critical

**Goals & Motivations:**
- Feed family healthy, sustainable food
- Reduce environmental footprint
- Support local economy
- Model values for child

**Pain Points:**
- Time-constrained with toddler
- Concerned about pesticide use
- Wants transparency but doesn't always have time to ask questions
- Budget isn't unlimited despite values

**How We Help:**
- Certified sustainable/organic practices with clear signage
- Online ordering with market pickup (saves time)
- Pre-washed, ready-to-use products
- Farm newsletter with growing practices transparency
- "Kids eat free" samples at market

**Marketing Messages:**
- "Know exactly how your food is grown"
- "Supporting sustainable farming, one salad at a time"
- "From our farm to your family in 24 hours"

## Segment-Specific Value Propositions

Once you understand your segments, craft value propositions that speak directly to their needs:

### Value Proposition Framework

```
For [TARGET SEGMENT]
Who [STATEMENT OF NEED OR OPPORTUNITY]
Our [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
Is [PRODUCT CATEGORY]
That [KEY BENEFIT]
Unlike [PRIMARY COMPETITOR]
We [PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATION]
```

### Examples by Segment

**For Committed Locavores:**
"For sustainability-conscious consumers who want to support local farmers and know exactly how their food is grown, our certified-sustainable farm offers complete transparency, farm events, and the highest quality produce grown within 10 miles of your home."

**For Quality Enthusiasts:**
"For food lovers who refuse to compromise on flavor, our farm specializes in heirloom and unique varieties selected for superior taste, harvested at peak ripeness and delivered to market within 24 hours."

**For Convenience Seekers:**
"For busy professionals who want healthy, local food without the farmers market trip, our online ordering and home delivery service brings farm-fresh produce to your door with just a few clicks."

## Matching Segments to Sales Channels

Different customer segments prefer different buying channels:

| Customer Segment | Primary Channel | Secondary Channel | Marketing Focus |
|------------------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Committed Locavores | CSA, On-farm | Farmers markets | Farm story, values, involvement |
| Quality Enthusiasts | Farmers markets | Specialty retail | Unique varieties, taste, quality |
| Convenience Seekers | Delivery, Online | Retail partnerships | Easy ordering, reliability |
| Occasional Buyers | Farmers markets | Special events | Sampling, impulse appeal |

## Customer Research Methods

How do you actually identify and understand your customer segments?

### Research Techniques

**1. Customer Surveys**
- In-person at farmers markets
- Email surveys to CSA members
- Social media polls

**2. Purchase Data Analysis**
- Track buying patterns
- Identify high-value vs. occasional customers
- Note product preferences by customer

**3. Direct Conversations**
- Ask customers why they buy from you
- Learn about pain points and desires
- Gather testimonials and feedback

**4. Social Media Insights**
- Analyze follower demographics
- Monitor engagement patterns
- Read comments and messages

**5. Competitive Shopping**
- Visit other farmers markets
- Observe customer behavior
- Note what messages resonate

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Customer segmentation focuses marketing efforts** - Not all customers are equally valuable or have the same needs

2. **Multiple segmentation frameworks exist** - Use demographic, psychographic, and behavioral lenses

3. **Detailed personas make abstract segments concrete** - Create 3-4 personas representing your target customers

4. **Value propositions must be segment-specific** - Different customers care about different benefits

5. **Match channels to segments** - Different customer types prefer different buying experiences

6. **Research validates assumptions** - Use surveys, data, and conversations to truly understand customers
