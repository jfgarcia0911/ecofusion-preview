## Platform Selection

### Platform Comparison for Farms

| Platform | Best For | Content Type | Time Investment | Audience |
|----------|----------|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Instagram** | Visual storytelling, younger audience | Photos, Stories, Reels | High | 25-45 years |
| **Facebook** | Community building, events | Posts, photos, events | Moderate | 35-65 years |
| **Email** | Direct sales, loyal customers | Newsletters, offers | Moderate | All ages |
| **TikTok** | Viral reach, education | Short videos | High | Under 35 |
| **Pinterest** | Recipes, inspiration | Images, recipes | Low | 25-55 (mostly women) |

**Recommendation:** Start with Instagram + Facebook (cross-post), add email immediately.

## Content Strategy

### Content Pillars for Farms

**70% - Educational/Value**
- Behind-the-scenes farm work
- Growing tips and insights
- Recipe ideas and preparation
- Seasonal produce guides
- Farm updates and harvest forecasts

**20% - Engagement/Community**
- Customer photos and testimonials
- Polls and questions ("What should we grow next year?")
- Farm life and team moments
- User-generated content (repost customer posts)

**10% - Promotional/Sales**
- CSA sign-up reminders
- Farmers market announcements
- Online ordering promotions
- Special offers and discounts

### Content Calendar Template

**Weekly Posting Schedule:**

| Day | Content Type | Example |
|-----|--------------|---------|
| **Monday** | Farm update | "Starting the week harvesting lettuce..." |
| **Tuesday** | Educational tip | "How to store greens for maximum freshness" |
| **Wednesday** | Harvest forecast | "This week's harvest: tomatoes, basil, cukes" |
| **Thursday** | Recipe/cooking | "Simple caprese with our heirlooms" |
| **Friday** | Farmers market reminder | "See you Saturday 8am-1pm!" |
| **Saturday** | Market day live | "We're here! Come say hi" |
| **Sunday** | Behind-the-scenes | "Planting for fall harvests" |

## Content Creation

### Photography Tips

**Lighting:**
- Natural light is best (avoid flash)
- Golden hour (early morning/evening) is ideal
- Overcast days create even, soft light

**Composition:**
- Get close (show details and texture)
- Use rule of thirds
- Include human element (hands, people)
- Show scale and abundance

**Subjects:**
- Harvest baskets (colorful, abundant)
- Close-ups of produce (dew drops, texture)
- Planting and growing process
- Team at work
- Customers enjoying products

### Captions That Convert

**Formula:**
```
Hook (first line)
Story/Value (middle)
Call-to-Action (end)
Hashtags
```

**Example:**
```
Hook: "These tomatoes taste like summer memories 🍅"

Story: We grow 12 heirloom varieties selected for flavor over
shelf life. This Brandywine was picked this morning at peak
ripeness—something you'll never find at a grocery store.

CTA: Available this Saturday at the farmers market, or order
online for Friday pickup. Link in bio!

#localfood #heirloomtomatoes #farmersmarket #farmtotable
#[yourcity]eats #supportlocalfarms
```

## Growing Your Audience

### Organic Growth Tactics

**1. Hashtag Strategy**
- Use 10-20 relevant hashtags
- Mix popular (#farmersmarket) and niche (#aquaponics)
- Include location tags (#[YourCity]food)
- Create branded hashtag (#[YourFarmName])

**2. Engagement**
- Respond to all comments and DMs
- Like and comment on customer posts
- Engage with local food community accounts
- Share and tag partners (restaurants, retailers)

**3. Collaborations**
- Partner with chefs (tag each other)
- Collaborate with food bloggers
- Cross-promote with other farms
- Influencer gifting (give products for posts)

**4. Consistency**
- Post 4-7x per week
- Best times: 7-9am, 12-1pm, 6-8pm
- Use Instagram Stories daily
- Go live from farmers market

## Instagram Specifics

### Features to Use

**Feed Posts:**
- High-quality photos
- Thoughtful captions
- Strategic hashtags
- Posted 3-5x per week

**Stories:**
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Polls and questions
- Countdown to market day
- Quick tips and recipes
- Posted daily

**Reels:**
- Short videos (15-60 sec)
- Trending audio
- Quick tips, time-lapses, harvests
- Highest reach potential
- Posted 2-3x per week

**Highlights:**
- Organize Stories into categories
- "Products," "Recipes," "Farm Tour," "Testimonials"
- Permanent reference for new followers

## Converting Followers to Customers

### Sales Without Being Salesy

**Soft Sells:**
- "We'll be at [market] this Saturday!"
- "Link in bio to order online"
- "DM us to reserve for pickup"
- "Limited quantities this week—see you early!"

**Strong Calls-to-Action:**
- "CSA sign-up closes Friday—link in bio"
- "Order by Wednesday for Friday delivery"
- "First 20 customers get free herbs"

### Link in Bio Strategy

**Options:**
- Direct to online store
- Link tree (multiple links)
- Current promotion or CSA sign-up
- Blog post or recipe

**Update weekly** to match current priority

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Instagram and Facebook are essential for farms** - Visual platforms perfect for showcasing food
2. **Content pillars create balance** - 70% value, 20% engagement, 10% promotion
3. **Consistency beats perfection** - Regular posting with good photos trumps occasional perfect posts
4. **Engagement builds community** - Respond, interact, share customer content
5. **Social drives sales** - With clear CTAs and links
