## Selecting the Right Markets

### Market Evaluation Criteria

- Customer traffic and demographics
- Competition and product gaps
- Vendor fees and distance costs
- Market rules and quality
- Day/time logistics

### Profitability Calculation

```
Break-even Analysis:
Vendor fee: $35
Fuel: $8
Labor (6 hrs × $15): $90
Supplies: $10
Total Cost: $143

Minimum sales (60% margin): $238
Target sales: $400-600
```

## Booth Design Essentials

### Visual Merchandising

**Key Principles:**
- Height variation creates interest
- Color blocking attracts attention
- Abundance displays (even with limited inventory)
- Clear, visible pricing
- Open, inviting layout

### Signage Hierarchy

1. **Farm Identity** (large banner, visible 30+ feet)
2. **Key Messages** ("Organic," "Picked Today")
3. **Product Info** (variety names, prices)

---

## Sales Techniques

### 4-Step Sales Process

1. **ATTRACT** - Greeting, sampling, welcoming body language
2. **ENGAGE** - Ask questions, share story, educate
3. **SUGGEST** - Upsell, bundle, solve customer needs
4. **CLOSE** - Make buying easy, build relationship

### Increasing Transaction Size

- Suggest complementary items
- Bundle pricing (3 for $10)
- Threshold incentives ($30 = free herbs)
- Display recipes showing multiple ingredients

**Target Average Sale: $25-40**

## Building Customer Loyalty

### Customer Stages

- First-time visitor → Create positive impression
- Repeat customer → Build familiarity
- Regular customer → Deepen relationship
- Loyal advocate → Reward and maintain

### Loyalty Programs

- Punch cards (10th purchase free)
- Email list for harvest forecasts
- VIP early shopping access
- Social media community
