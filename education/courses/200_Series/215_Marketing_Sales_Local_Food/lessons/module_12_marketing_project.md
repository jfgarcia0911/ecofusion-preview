## Marketing Goals and Objectives

### SMART Goal Framework

**S**pecific - **M**easurable - **A**chievable - **R**elevant - **T**ime-bound

**Revenue Goals:**
```
Goal: Achieve $100,000 in sales revenue by December 31, 2025

Breakdown by channel:
• Farmers markets: $40,000 (40%)
• CSA program: $35,000 (35%)
• Restaurant sales: $15,000 (15%)
• Online/delivery: $10,000 (10%)
```

**Customer Acquisition Goals:**
```
• Farmers market: 500 unique customers, 100 regular (weekly) customers
• CSA: 50 full-share members
• Restaurant: 5 accounts averaging $60/week each
• Email list: 800 subscribers
• Instagram: 1,500 followers
```

**Retention Goals:**
```
• CSA renewal rate: 70%
• Farmers market repeat purchase rate: 40%
• Customer churn rate: <10% monthly
```

### Content Calendar

**Monthly Themes:**
- January-February: CSA sign-up campaign
- March-April: Spring planting and growth
- May-June: First harvests, farmers market season launch
- July-August: Peak summer bounty
- September-October: Fall crops, harvest celebration
- November-December: Year-end thank you, holiday gifts

**Weekly Content:**
- Monday: Farm update (Instagram, Facebook)
- Tuesday: Educational post (tip, how-to)
- Wednesday: Email newsletter (harvest forecast, availability)
- Thursday: Recipe or cooking inspiration
- Friday: Farmers market reminder
- Saturday: Live from market
- Sunday: Behind-the-scenes, planning ahead

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Marketing plan is your roadmap** - Clear goals, strategies, and tactics keep you focused
2. **Start with research and analysis** - SWOT, market analysis, and competitive positioning inform strategy
3. **Set SMART goals** - Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound objectives
4. **Allocate budget strategically** - 5-10% of revenue across channels based on ROI
5. **Implement with 90-day sprints** - Break annual plan into actionable quarterly projects
6. **Measure and adjust** - Track KPIs, learn what works, optimize continually
