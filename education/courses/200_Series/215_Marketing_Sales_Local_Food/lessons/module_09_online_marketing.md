## Your Farm Website

### Website Essentials

**Must-Have Pages:**
- **Home:** Clear value proposition, current offerings, call-to-action
- **About:** Farm story, values, team, photos
- **Products:** What you grow, availability, how to buy
- **How to Buy:** CSA, markets, delivery, online ordering
- **Contact:** Location, hours, email, phone, social links

**Key Features:**
- Mobile-responsive design (60%+ traffic from phones)
- Fast loading speed (<3 seconds)
- Clear navigation
- Professional photos
- Online ordering integration (if applicable)
- Email signup form

### Website Platforms for Farms

| Platform | Cost | Best For | Ease of Use |
|----------|------|----------|-------------|
| **Wix** | $16-35/month | Visual design, drag-and-drop | Very Easy |

### SEO Basics for Local Food

**Target Keywords:**
- "[City] farmers market"
- "[Product] near me"
- "Local [product] [city]"
- "CSA [city/region]"
- "Farm fresh [product]"

**On-Page SEO:**
- Include location in title tags and headings
- Use keywords naturally in content
- Add alt text to images
- Create blog content (recipes, farm updates)
- Get Google Business listing

## Email Marketing

### Building Your Email List

**Collection Methods:**
- Farmers market signup (tablet/clipboard)
- Website popup or form
- CSA signup
- Free resource download (recipe book, planting calendar)
- In-person checkout ("Want harvest updates?")

**Incentive:** "Get harvest forecast and exclusive discounts!"

### Email Campaign Types

**1. Weekly Harvest Update**
```
Subject: This Week's Harvest: Heirloom Tomatoes + Sweet Corn

What's fresh this week:
🍅 Heirloom tomatoes (peak season!)
🌽 Sweet corn
🥬 Salad greens
🌿 Fresh basil

Available at:
- Saturday Farmers Market (8am-1pm)
- Online ordering (order by Wed, pickup Fri)

Recipe: Caprese Salad with our tomatoes + basil
[Link to recipe]

See you at the market!
[Your Name], [Farm Name]
```

**2. Special Offers**
- CSA early bird discount
- End-of-season sale
- Referral bonus
- Free delivery week

**3. Farm Updates**
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Seasonal transitions
- New products launching
- Farm events and tours

**Best Practices:**
- Send consistently (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Mobile-friendly design
- Clear call-to-action
- Unsubscribe option (required)
- Personal tone (from you, not corporate)

## Content Marketing

### Blog Topics for Farms

**Recipe Content:**
- "5 Ways to Use Fresh Basil"
- "The Perfect Caprese with Heirloom Tomatoes"
- "Summer Grilling Recipes with Farm Fresh Vegetables"

**Educational Content:**
- "What is Aquaponics?" (your farming method)
- "How to Store Lettuce for Maximum Freshness"
- "Seasonal Eating Guide for [Region]"

**Farm Story Content:**
- "A Day in the Life on Our Farm"
- "Why We Grow Heirloom Varieties"
- "Meet the Team: [Name], Head Grower"

**Benefits:**
- SEO (attracts Google traffic)
- Email content (repurpose blog posts)
- Social media content
- Establishes expertise
- Customer education

## Online Advertising (Optional)

### Facebook/Instagram Ads

**When to Use:**
- Promoting CSA sign-up
- New online ordering launch
- Seasonal campaigns (spring, summer)
- Geographic targeting (local area only)

**Budget:**
- Start with $5-10/day
- Target local area (10-20 mile radius)
- Test messaging and images
- Track conversions (sign-ups, orders)

**Ad Example:**
```
Headline: Fresh Lettuce Delivered to Your Door
Body: Order online by Wednesday, delivered Friday.
      Grown with 90% less water than conventional farms.
CTA: Shop Now
Target: 5-mile radius, age 25-55, interests: organic food, farmers markets
```

### Google My Business

**Free and Essential:**
- Appears in local searches
- Shows location, hours, photos
- Customer reviews
- Posts and updates

**Optimize:**
- Complete all fields
- Add high-quality photos (10+)
- Encourage reviews (5-star ratings)
- Post weekly updates
- Respond to reviews

## Analytics and Measurement

### Key Metrics to Track

**Website:**
- Visitors per month
- Conversion rate (% who order/sign up)
- Traffic sources (Google, social, direct)
- Most popular pages

**Email:**
- Open rate (aim for 20-30%)
- Click-through rate (aim for 3-5%)
- List growth rate
- Unsubscribe rate (<1% is healthy)

**Sales:**
- Online order volume
- Average order value
- Customer acquisition cost
- Customer lifetime value

### Tools

**Free:**
- Google Analytics (website tracking)
- Email platform analytics (open/click rates)
- Social media insights (engagement)

**Paid:**
- Google Ads tracking
- Facebook Pixel (conversion tracking)
- CRM systems (customer relationship management)
