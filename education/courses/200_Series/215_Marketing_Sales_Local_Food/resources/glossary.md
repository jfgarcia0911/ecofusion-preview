# Glossary: Marketing & Sales for Local Food
## Course 215

---

## A

**Above the Fold**
Content visible on a webpage without scrolling. Critical for capturing attention and including key calls-to-action.

**Acquisition Cost (CAC - Customer Acquisition Cost)**
The total cost of acquiring a new customer, including marketing, sales, and onboarding expenses.

**Action Threshold**
The point at which a customer takes desired action (purchase, sign-up, etc.) based on marketing messaging.

---

## B

**B2C (Business-to-Consumer)**
Direct sales from business to end consumer, as opposed to wholesale/B2B.

**Bounce Rate**
Percentage of website visitors who leave after viewing only one page. High bounce rates indicate poor engagement.

**Brand**
The complete experience and perception customers have of your farm, including visual identity, messaging, values, and customer interactions.

**Brand Equity**
The commercial value of a brand based on customer perception and loyalty, enabling premium pricing.

**Brand Positioning**
How your brand is perceived relative to competitors; your unique place in the market.

**Buyer Persona**
Semi-fictional representation of your ideal customer based on research and data, including demographics, behaviors, motivations, and pain points.

**Bundle Pricing**
Offering multiple products together at a discount (e.g., "3 for $10" instead of $3.50 each).

---

## C

**Call-to-Action (CTA)**
Directive telling customer what to do next ("Order now," "Visit our market booth," "Sign up for CSA").

**Churn Rate**
Percentage of customers who stop buying within a given period. Lower churn = better retention.

**Charm Pricing**
Pricing strategy using prices ending in .99 or .95 to create perception of value.

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**
Percentage of people who click on a link in email, ad, or social post. Indicates engagement level.

**Competitive Positioning**
Your farm's market position relative to competitors based on differentiation, pricing, and value proposition.

**Consignment**
Arrangement where retailer doesn't pay for product until it sells, with unsold items returned to farmer. Higher risk for farmer.

**Content Marketing**
Creating valuable content (blogs, recipes, farm updates) to attract and engage customers rather than direct advertising.

**Conversion Rate**
Percentage of prospects who take desired action (visitors who buy, email readers who order, etc.).

**Cost-Plus Pricing**
Pricing method that adds desired profit margin to total production costs.

**CSA (Community Supported Agriculture)**
Model where customers purchase shares of farm's harvest upfront, receiving regular boxes of seasonal produce.

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV or LTV)**
Total revenue expected from a customer over entire relationship. Formula: Average purchase × frequency × customer lifespan.

---

## D

**Demographics**
Measurable population characteristics like age, income, education, location, household size.

**Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)**
Sales model selling directly to end consumers, bypassing intermediaries. Includes farmers markets, CSA, on-farm sales.

**Drip Campaign**
Series of automated emails sent on a schedule to nurture leads or onboard customers.

---

## E

**Email Marketing**
Using email to communicate with customers, promote products, and drive sales. Highest ROI of digital marketing channels.

**Engagement Rate**
Percentage of audience interacting with content (likes, comments, shares, clicks). Indicates content effectiveness.

**Evergreen Content**
Content that remains relevant and valuable over time (e.g., "How to Store Lettuce" vs. "This Week's Harvest").

---

## F

**Farmers Market**
Direct retail sales venue where local farmers sell directly to consumers, typically weekly seasonal markets.

**Funnel (Sales/Marketing Funnel)**
Customer journey from awareness to purchase. Stages: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Purchase → Loyalty.

---

## G

**Gross Margin**
Revenue minus cost of goods sold, expressed as percentage. Formula: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100.

---

## H

**Hashtag**
Word or phrase preceded by # used on social media to categorize content and increase discoverability.

**Hyper-Local**
Marketing focused on immediate geographic area (neighborhood, within 5-10 miles).

---

## I

**Inbound Marketing**
Strategy that attracts customers through valuable content and experiences rather than interruption advertising.

**Influencer Marketing**
Partnering with individuals who have engaged audiences to promote products. For farms, local food bloggers and chefs.

---

## K

**Keystone Pricing**
Retail pricing that doubles the wholesale cost (50% markup), standard retail margin.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**
Measurable values indicating success toward goals (e.g., sales per market day, email open rate, customer retention).

---

## L

**Landing Page**
Standalone webpage designed for specific marketing campaign with focused call-to-action.

**Lead**
Potential customer who has shown interest (signed up for email, visited booth, inquired about products).

**Lead Magnet**
Free resource offered in exchange for contact information (e.g., free recipe book for email sign-up).

**Local Food**
Food produced within defined geographic area, typically 50-150 miles from consumer, though definitions vary.

**Locavore**
Consumer committed to eating locally-produced food, often ideologically motivated.

**Loyalty Program**
System rewarding repeat customers with discounts, special access, or free products to encourage continued purchases.

---

## M

**Margin**
Difference between cost and selling price, expressed as percentage of selling price.

**Market Segmentation**
Dividing total market into distinct groups with similar characteristics, needs, or behaviors.

**Markup**
Amount added to cost to determine price, expressed as percentage of cost.

**Multichannel Marketing**
Using multiple sales/marketing channels (farmers markets, CSA, online, retail) to reach customers.

---

## N

**Net 15/Net 30**
Payment terms where buyer pays invoice in 15 or 30 days from delivery. Common in wholesale/restaurant sales.

**Niche Market**
Specific subset of larger market with specialized needs (e.g., certified organic, aquaponics, heirloom-only).

---

## O

**Omnichannel**
Integrated multichannel approach providing seamless customer experience across all channels.

**Open Rate**
Percentage of email recipients who open an email. Target: 20-30% for farm newsletters.

**Organic Reach**
Number of people who see content without paid promotion (vs. paid reach).

**Owned Media**
Marketing channels you control (website, email list, social media accounts) vs. paid or earned media.

---

## P

**Pain Point**
Specific problem or frustration customer experiences that your product/service solves.

**Penetration Pricing**
Pricing below competitors initially to gain market share, then raising prices once established.

**Point of Sale (POS)**
Location and moment where transaction occurs; also refers to payment processing system.

**Positioning Statement**
Formula defining target customer, their need, your category, key benefit, and differentiation from competitors.

**Premium Pricing**
Pricing above competitors based on superior quality, unique value, or strong brand.

**Prestige Pricing**
Using round number prices ($4.00 vs. $3.99) to convey quality and confidence.

**Price Anchoring**
Displaying higher-priced items first to make other prices seem more reasonable.

**Psychographics**
Psychological characteristics including values, attitudes, interests, lifestyle, and personality.

---

## R

**Referral Marketing**
Encouraging existing customers to recommend your farm to others, often with incentives for both parties.

**Retention Rate**
Percentage of customers who continue buying over time. Formula: (Customers at end - new customers) / Customers at start × 100.

**Return on Investment (ROI)**
Measure of profitability. Formula: (Gain from Investment - Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment × 100.

---

## S

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**
Optimizing website and content to rank higher in search results for relevant keywords.

**Segmentation**
See Market Segmentation.

**SMART Goals**
Goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

**Social Proof**
Phenomenon where people follow others' actions. In marketing: testimonials, reviews, user photos, popularity indicators.

**Subscription Model**
Recurring payment model (weekly/monthly) for regular product delivery. Includes CSA and subscription boxes.

**SWOT Analysis**
Strategic planning tool evaluating Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.

---

## T

**Target Market**
Specific group of consumers you aim to reach with products and marketing.

**Touchpoint**
Any point of contact between customer and your farm (market booth, website, email, social media, packaging, etc.).

**Traceability**
Ability to track product from farm to customer, important for food safety and transparency.

---

## U

**Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**
What makes your farm distinctly different and better than competitors.

**Upselling**
Encouraging customer to purchase more or higher-value items than originally intended.

---

## V

**Value-Based Pricing**
Pricing based on perceived value to customer rather than cost or competition.

**Value Proposition**
Clear statement of benefits customer receives from choosing your products over alternatives.

**Viral Content**
Content that spreads rapidly through social sharing due to high engagement.

---

## W

**Wholesale**
Selling products to retailers or restaurants (intermediaries) who resell to consumers, typically at 40-70% of retail price.

**Word-of-Mouth Marketing**
Organic promotion through customer recommendations and conversations. Most trusted form of marketing.

---

## X

**Cross-Selling**
Suggesting complementary products (e.g., selling basil with tomatoes).

---

**CPA:** Cost Per Acquisition

**CRM:** Customer Relationship Management
**CTA:** Call-to-Action
**CTR:** Click-Through Rate
**DM:** Direct Message (social media)
**FAQ:** Frequently Asked Questions
**FOMO:** Fear Of Missing Out (marketing tactic)
**GMV:** Gross Merchandise Value
**KPI:** Key Performance Indicator
**POS:** Point of Sale
**ROI:** Return on Investment
**SEM:** Search Engine Marketing
**SEO:** Search Engine Optimization
**SM:** Social Media
**SMM:** Social Media Marketing
**UGC:** User-Generated Content
**USP:** Unique Selling Proposition
**UI/UX:** User Interface / User Experience
