### Section C: Short Answer

**31.** Acceptable motivations:
- Freshness and quality
- Supporting local economy
- Environmental sustainability
- Food safety and transparency
- Nutrition and health
- Taste and culinary experience
- Connection to food source

**32.** Five persona elements:
- Demographics (age, income, location, household)
- Psychographics (values, lifestyle, interests)
- Shopping behaviors (frequency, channels, basket size)
- Goals and motivations
- Pain points and challenges

**33.** 4-step sales process:
1. ATTRACT - Welcoming greeting, sampling, eye contact
2. ENGAGE - Ask questions, share story, educate
3. SUGGEST - Upsell, bundle, solve needs
4. CLOSE - Make buying easy, thank, build relationship

**34.** Essential website pages:
- Home (value proposition, CTA)
- About (farm story, values, team)
- Products (what you grow)
- How to Buy (CSA, markets, ordering)
- Contact (location, hours, email, phone)

**35.** SWOT components:
- Strengths (internal advantages)
- Weaknesses (internal limitations)
- Opportunities (external possibilities)
- Threats (external challenges)

### Section D: Calculations

**36a.** Cost-plus with 60% margin:
$1.55 / (1 - 0.60) = $1.55 / 0.40 = **$3.875 or ~$3.88**

**36b.** Wholesale (50-70% of $3.50 retail):
$3.50 × 0.50 = $1.75
$3.50 × 0.70 = $2.45
**Wholesale range: $1.75-2.45**

**36c.** Profitability check:
Cost = $1.55
Wholesale = $1.75-2.45
Margin = $0.20-0.90 (13-58%)
**Yes, profitable but lower margin than retail. $2.45 price preferred ($0.90 margin = 58%).**

**37a.** Annual value:
$35 × 20 weeks = **$700 per year**

**37b.** Lifetime Value:
$700 × 3 years = **$2,100**

**37c.** ROI:
Lifetime value: $2,100
Acquisition cost: $150
Net value: $2,100 - $150 = $1,950
ROI = $1,950 / $150 = 13× or **1,300% ROI**
