## Detailed Channel Breakdown

### FARMERS MARKETS

**Pros:**
- ✅ Highest retail prices
- ✅ Direct customer interaction
- ✅ Immediate payment (cash flow)
- ✅ Market research opportunities
- ✅ Brand building and visibility
- ✅ Customer feedback in real-time

**Cons:**
- ❌ Weather dependent
- ❌ Labor intensive (staffing, setup, breakdown)
- ❌ Seasonal in many regions
- ❌ Weekend time commitment
- ❌ Vendor fees
- ❌ Competition from other vendors

**Best For:**
- Startups building customer base
- Premium/specialty products
- Farms near population centers
- Building brand awareness

**Costs:**
- Vendor fees: $20-50/day
- Labor: 6-8 hours × 1-2 people
- Transportation
- Booth equipment: $500-2000 setup
- Samples: $5-10/day

**Time to Profitability:** Immediate (if sales > costs)

**Success Metrics:**
- Average sale: $25-40
- Sales per day: $400-1,200
- Repeat customer rate: 30-40%
- Customer acquisition cost: $5-15

---

### CSA (Community Supported Agriculture)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Guaranteed sales (prepaid)
- ✅ Upfront capital for spring
- ✅ Predictable revenue
- ✅ Loyal, committed customers
- ✅ Shared risk (weather, pests)
- ✅ Lower marketing cost per customer

**Cons:**
- ❌ Recruitment effort each season
- ❌ Packing labor
- ❌ Distribution logistics
- ❌ Customer management (vacations, complaints)
- ❌ Limited flexibility
- ❌ High retention pressure

**Best For:**
- Established farms with production capacity
- Diverse product mix
- Farms seeking cash flow stability
- Strong community connection

**Costs:**
- Marketing/recruitment: $500-2000
- Packing labor: 2-4 hrs/week
- Boxes/containers: $3-8 per reusable
- Delivery (if applicable): Variable
- Communication: $10-30/month (email)

**Time to Profitability:** 3-6 months (recruitment to first payments)

**Success Metrics:**
- Member count: 25-100+
- Renewal rate: 60-80%
- Revenue per member: $600-1,200
- Lifetime value: $1,800-3,600

---

### RESTAURANT SALES

**Pros:**
- ✅ Year-round potential
- ✅ Consistent weekly orders
- ✅ Premium wholesale pricing
- ✅ Prestige/credibility
- ✅ Bulk orders
- ✅ Menu exposure

**Cons:**
- ❌ Net 15-30 payment terms
- ❌ Quality/consistency demands
- ❌ Early delivery times
- ❌ Small order minimums can be inefficient
- ❌ Chef/restaurant turnover
- ❌ Price pressure

**Best For:**
- Consistent production
- Specialty/unique products
- Professional operations
- Weekday delivery capability

**Costs:**
- Delivery vehicle/fuel
- Restaurant-ready packaging
- Invoicing/accounting time
- Samples for initial meetings
- Occasional product replacements

**Time to Profitability:** 1-3 months per account

**Success Metrics:**
- Accounts: 3-10
- Average order: $50-200/week
- Payment days: <45
- Account retention: 70%+

---

### RETAIL PARTNERSHIPS

**Pros:**
- ✅ Volume potential
- ✅ Brand exposure
- ✅ Consistent orders
- ✅ Professional channel
- ✅ Less labor than farmers markets

**Cons:**
- ❌ Lowest wholesale pricing
- ❌ Strict packaging/labeling requirements
- ❌ Net 30 payment common
- ❌ Risk of shrink/unsold product
- ❌ Competition with other suppliers
- ❌ Reduced margin

**Best For:**
- Higher production volume
- Professional packaging capability
- Strong cash flow management
- Consistent product availability

**Costs:**
- Professional packaging/labels: $0.25-0.75/unit
- Delivery
- Shrink/unsold product: 5-15%
- Slotting fees (some stores)
- Invoicing/collections

**Time to Profitability:** 2-4 months

**Success Metrics:**
- Accounts: 2-5
- Sell-through rate: >80%
- Days to payment: <45
- Revenue per account: $500-2000/month

---

### ONLINE/DELIVERY

**Pros:**
- ✅ Convenience premium pricing
- ✅ Flexible ordering window
- ✅ Automated systems possible
- ✅ Customer data collection
- ✅ Subscription potential
- ✅ Weather independent

**Cons:**
- ❌ Delivery costs and logistics
- ❌ Platform fees (if using service)
- ❌ Technology setup
- ❌ Minimum orders needed for efficiency
- ❌ Packaging requirements
- ❌ Customer support demands

**Best For:**
- Urban/suburban locations
- Tech-savvy operations
- Busy professionals as target market
- Supplement to other channels

**Costs:**
- Platform fees: 8-15% or $50-150/month
- Delivery labor/vehicle: $10-25/order
- Packaging: $0.50-1.50/order
- Website/tech: $20-100/month
- Cooler bags/boxes: $5-15 each (reusable)

**Time to Profitability:** 3-6 months

**Success Metrics:**
- Orders per week: 10-50
- Average order: $40-70
- Delivery fee: $5-10
- Subscription rate: 20-30%

---

### ON-FARM SALES

**Pros:**
- ✅ Highest margins (no middleman)
- ✅ Farm visit experience
- ✅ Low marketing cost
- ✅ Flexible hours
- ✅ U-pick potential

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires farm traffic/visibility
- ❌ Limited to farm location
- ❌ Theft risk (honor system)
- ❌ Liability/insurance
- ❌ Facility needs (restroom, parking)
- ❌ Seasonal/weather dependent

**Best For:**
- Roadside farms with traffic
- Rural/agritourism locations
- Weekend/seasonal operations
- Supplemental income

**Costs:**
- Stand/infrastructure: $500-5000
- Signage: $200-1000
- Liability insurance: $500-1500/year
- Point-of-sale system: $0-500
- Restroom facilities (if required)

**Time to Profitability:** Variable

**Success Metrics:**
- Sales per day: $100-500
- Theft/loss: <10%
- Return customers: 40-60%

## Channel Selection Framework

### How to Choose:

**Step 1: Assess Your Capacity**
- Production volume: _____
- Labor available: _____
- Capital for infrastructure: $_____
- Time commitment possible: _____

**Step 2: Match to Customer Segments**
- Who are your primary targets?
- Where do they prefer to shop?
- What channels reach them best?

**Step 3: Calculate Profitability**
- Revenue potential per channel
- Costs specific to channel
- Net margin after all costs
- Time/labor efficiency

**Step 4: Start Small, Test, Expand**
- Begin with 1-2 channels
- Prove profitability
- Refine operations
- Add channels strategically

**Recommended Starter Channel Combinations:**

**Option A: Market Builder**
- Farmers markets (2-3) - 70% revenue
- On-farm sales - 20% revenue
- Email list building for future CSA - 10% effort

**Option B: CSA Focused**
- CSA program - 60% revenue
- Farmers market (1) for visibility - 30% revenue
- Restaurant (1-2 small accounts) - 10% revenue

**Option C: Wholesale Professional**
- Restaurant accounts (5-8) - 50% revenue
- Retail partnerships (2-3) - 40% revenue
- Online ordering - 10% revenue
