## Margin vs. Markup Conversion

| Margin % | Markup % | Cost $1 → Price | Cost $2 → Price |
|----------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 30% | 43% | $1.43 | $2.86 |
| 40% | 67% | $1.67 | $3.33 |
| 50% | 100% | $2.00 | $4.00 |
| 60% | 150% | $2.50 | $5.00 |
| 70% | 233% | $3.33 | $6.67 |

**Formula to convert:**
- Markup % = Margin % / (1 - Margin %)
- Margin % = Markup % / (1 + Markup %)

## Quick Pricing Checks

### Profitability Check
```
Question: Is this price profitable?

✓ Price > Total Cost (includes overhead)
✓ Margin ≥ 30% minimum
✓ Margin ≥ 50% preferred for retail channels
```

### Market Check
```
Question: Will the market accept this price?

✓ Within 10-40% of competitor pricing (if premium)
✓ Value justifies premium (quality, story, unique)
✓ Target customer can afford
✓ Channel expectations met
```

### Reality Check
```
Question: Can I actually achieve this?

✓ Volume exists at this price point
✓ Production costs accurate
✓ Marketing supports premium (if applicable)
✓ Consistent quality to deliver value
```

## Pricing Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Pricing too low out of fear**
→ Undervalues product, unsustainable

❌ **Ignoring true costs**
→ Forgetting labor and overhead

❌ **Competing only on price**
→ Race to bottom, no differentiation

❌ **Never raising prices**
→ Costs increase, margins erode

❌ **Same price across all channels**
→ Different costs and value justify different pricing

✅ **DO THIS INSTEAD:**
- Calculate real costs including ALL labor
- Price for value, not just cost
- Communicate value to justify pricing
- Review and adjust annually
- Different channels = different prices
