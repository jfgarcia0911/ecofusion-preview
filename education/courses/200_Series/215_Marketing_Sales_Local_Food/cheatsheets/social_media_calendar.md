## Monthly Content Planning Template

### Content Pillar Framework

**70% Educational/Value** - Teach, inform, help
**20% Engagement/Community** - Connect, ask, share
**10% Promotional/Sales** - Sell, announce, offer

## Content Ideas by Category

### Educational/Value Content (70%)

**Farm Education:**
- How we grow [crop]
- What is aquaponics/hydroponics?
- Sustainable farming practices explained
- Season extension techniques
- Integrated pest management
- Water conservation methods

**Food Education:**
- How to store [produce] for maximum freshness
- Nutritional benefits of [crop]
- Peak season guide
- Unusual variety spotlights
- "Did you know?" facts about crops

**Cooking/Prep:**
- Simple recipe cards
- Preparation tips ("how to cut a butternut squash")
- Preservation methods (freezing, canning)
- Pairing suggestions
- Kitchen hacks

**Example Posts:**
```
"How to keep salad greens fresh for 10+ days:
1. Don't wash until ready to use
2. Store in breathable container
3. Keep in coldest part of fridge
4. Add paper towel to absorb moisture
Tag someone who needs this tip! 🥬"
```

---

### Engagement/Community Content (20%)

**User-Generated:**
- Repost customer photos (ask permission)
- Share customer recipes
- Testimonials and reviews
- "Customer Spotlight" series

**Interactive:**
- Polls: "What should we grow next year?"
- Questions: "What's your favorite way to use basil?"
- Fill-in-the-blank: "My favorite local food is ____"
- Challenges: "Share your farmers market haul!"

**Farm Life:**
- Team introductions
- Pet/animal content
- Sunrise/sunset farm photos
- Seasonal changes on the farm
- Challenges and real talk

**Example Posts:**
```
"Poll: Which heirloom tomato should we add next season?
💛 Yellow Pear
❤️ Cherokee Purple
🧡 Sungold Cherry
React with your vote!"
```

---

### Promotional/Sales Content (10%)

**Soft Sell:**
- "We'll be at [market] this Saturday 8am-1pm"
- "Ordering open through Wednesday"
- "Limited quantities this week—arrive early"
- "Link in bio to order online"

**Strong Call-to-Action:**
- "CSA sign-up closes Friday—only 10 spots left!"
- "Flash sale: 20% off online orders today only"
- "Pre-order for Saturday pickup"
- "First 20 customers get free herbs"

**Example Posts:**
```
"🍅 Heirloom tomatoes are PEAKING this week!

We picked 200 lbs this morning and they're incredible.
Available:
- Saturday farmers market (while they last)
- Online ordering (closes Wed 5pm)
- Pre-order to guarantee yours

Link in bio to order 👆"
```

## Instagram-Specific Features

### Stories (Daily)

**Morning (7-9am):**
- "Good morning from the farm"
- Harvest updates
- What we're working on today

**Midday (12-2pm):**
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Quick tips
- Polls/questions

**Evening (6-8pm):**
- Recipe inspiration
- Tomorrow's plan
- Call-to-action (order, visit market)

**Story Highlights to Create:**
- 🌱 Products
- 📍 Markets & Pickup
- 🥗 Recipes
- 🚜 Behind the Scenes
- ⭐ Testimonials
- ❓ FAQ
- 🎉 Events

### Reels (2-3x per week)

**Trending Topics:**
- Fast-motion harvesting
- "A day in the life"
- Before/after transformations
- Quick recipe tutorials
- "Things I wish I knew before farming"
- Satisfying harvest videos
- Farm ASMR (rain on greenhouse, etc.)

**Use trending audio** but make it relevant to farming

## Hashtag Strategy

`#[YourFarmName]` - Unique to your farm

- `#[YourCity]Eats`
- `#[YourCity]Food`
- `#[YourCity]FarmersMarket`
- `#[State]Farms`
- `#Local[YourCity]`

- `#FarmFresh`
- `#LocalFood`
- `#FarmToTable`
- `#KnowYourFarmer`
- `#SustainableAgriculture`

- `#EatLocal`
- `#SupportLocalFarms`
- `#RealFood`
- `#FarmLife`

**Total: 15-20 hashtags per post**

Save hashtag groups in your notes app for easy copy/paste

## Content Batching Tips

**Monthly Batching Session (2-4 hours):**
1. Take 20-40 photos in one shoot
2. Write 10-15 captions in advance
3. Save in draft or scheduling tool
4. Schedule posts for month

**Tools to Use:**
- **Free:** Instagram native scheduler, Facebook Creator Studio
- **Paid:** Later, Planoly, Buffer, Hootsuite ($15-50/month)

**Photo Organization:**
- Create folders by content type
- Date photos taken
- Keep best 3-5 per shoot
- Delete blurry/unusable immediately
