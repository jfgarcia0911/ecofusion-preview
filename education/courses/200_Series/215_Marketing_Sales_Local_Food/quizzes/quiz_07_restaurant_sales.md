## Multiple Choice

2. Restaurant wholesale pricing should be: **C) 50-70% of retail price**
3. Typical restaurant payment terms: **B) Net 15-30**
4. What chefs value MOST: **D) Reliability and consistent quality**
5. Restaurant accounts average per week: **B) $200-800**

## True/False
6. Fine dining restaurants are ideal local food partners. **True**
7. Chefs prefer commodity varieties over heirlooms. **False**
8. Restaurant delivery should be early morning before service. **True**

## Short Answer
9. List THREE things to bring to a chef meeting.

1-5 above | 6-8 above
