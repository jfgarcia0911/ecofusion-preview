## Multiple Choice (1 point each)

**1. What is the approximate size of the U.S. local food market?**
- A) $5 billion annually
- B) $10 billion annually
- C) $20 billion annually
- D) $50 billion annually

**2. Which sales channel accounts for the largest share of local food sales?**
- A) CSA programs
- B) Farmers markets
- C) Restaurant sales
- D) Retail partnerships

**3. What percentage of consumers define "local" as within 50 miles?**
- A) 12%
- B) 28%
- C) 42%
- D) 68%

**4. What is the PRIMARY consumer motivation for buying local food?**
- A) Supporting local economy
- B) Environmental sustainability
- C) Freshness and quality
- D) Knowing the farmer

**5. Local food typically commands what price premium over conventional products?**
- A) 5-10%
- B) 10-40%
- C) 50-75%
- D) 100%+

## Answer Key

1. **C** - $20 billion annually
2. **B** - Farmers markets (35% of local food sales)
3. **C** - 42% of consumers
4. **C** - Freshness and quality (87% rate as "very important")
5. **B** - 10-40% premium pricing typical
6. **True** - Market growing steadily in this range
7. **False** - Direct-to-consumer is $11.8B of $20B total (59%)
8. **True** - Committed locavores are 15% of market
9. **Acceptable answers:**
   - Post-pandemic food supply awareness
   - Climate/sustainability consciousness
   - Desire for fresher produce
   - "Know your farmer" movement
   - Restaurant local sourcing trend
   - Technology enabling direct sales
10. **Acceptable answers:**
   - Defines marketing scope and target area
   - Sets customer expectations
   - Determines logistics and delivery zones
   - Guides competitive positioning
