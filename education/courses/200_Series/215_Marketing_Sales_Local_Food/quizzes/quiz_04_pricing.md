# Quiz 4: Pricing Strategies
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes | **Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Multiple Choice
1. Cost-plus pricing formula with 40% desired margin: Price = Cost / ?
- A) 0.40
- B) 0.60
- C) 1.40
- D) 2.40

2. Value-based pricing is primarily determined by:
- A) Your production costs
- B) Competitor pricing
- C) Customer perception of value
- D) Wholesale market rates

3. Which sales channel typically commands the highest price?
- A) Wholesale to retail
- B) Restaurant sales
- C) Farmers markets (direct retail)
- D) CSA programs

4. Charm pricing uses prices ending in:
- A) .00 (round numbers)
- B) .50 (half dollars)
- C) .99 or .95
- D) .25 (quarters)

5. If lettuce costs $1.55 to produce and you want 60% margin, price should be:
- A) $2.48
- B) $3.88
- C) $2.58
- D) $4.65

## True/False
6. Different sales channels should have different pricing. **True/False**
7. Premium pricing requires premium marketing to justify. **True/False**
8. You should never raise prices once set. **True/False**

## Short Answer
9. Calculate: Production cost $2.00, 50% margin needed. What price?

10. Why might a farm charge different prices at farmers markets vs. wholesale?

## Answer Key
1. B ($1.55/0.60=$2.58), 2. C, 3. C, 4. C, 5. B ($1.55/0.40=3.88)
6. True, 7. True, 8. False
9. $4.00 ($2.00/0.50 or $2.00×2)
10. Different costs (labor, fees), different value (direct vs. convenience), market expectations, margin requirements
