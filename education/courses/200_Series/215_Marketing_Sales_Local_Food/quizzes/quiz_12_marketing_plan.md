1. **B** - Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

6. **False** - Should be reviewed and adjusted quarterly
7. **True** - Different channels need different approaches
8. **True** - Data drives optimization and improvement

**9. Acceptable answers:**

- Customer analysis

**10. Acceptable answers:**
- Creates urgency and focus
- Allows for quick adjustments based on results
- More manageable than year-long plans
- Enables quarterly review and optimization
