# Quiz 3: Farm Branding and Identity
## Course 215: Marketing & Sales for Local Food

**Time Allowed:** 15 minutes | **Total Points:** 10 | **Passing Score:** 80%

## Multiple Choice
1. A brand is best defined as:
- A) Your logo and farm name
- B) The complete customer experience and perception
- C) Your packaging design
- D) Your advertising slogan

2. The brand foundation pyramid starts with:
- A) Brand personality
- B) Brand positioning
- C) Brand promise
- D) Brand purpose

3. Which is an example of a brand positioning statement element?
- A) "For health-conscious families who want organic produce"
- B) Your farm address
- C) Product price list
- D) Market schedule

4. Green is commonly associated with which brand meaning?
- A) Passion and energy
- B) Growth, health, and nature
- C) Premium and unique
- D) Trust and reliability

5. Brand consistency means:
- A) Never changing anything
- B) Using the same logo everywhere
- C) Delivering the same experience across all touchpoints
- D) Only using one color

## True/False
6. Your farm story should include challenges you've overcome. **True/False**
7. Packaging is not a branding opportunity. **True/False**
8. Strong brands can command 15-30% price premiums. **True/False**

## Short Answer
9. List THREE brand touchpoints for a farm business.

10. Why is storytelling important for farm branding?

## Answer Key
1. B, 2. D, 3. A, 4. B, 5. C, 6. True, 7. False, 8. True
9. Logo/signage, packaging, booth design, website, social media, customer service, etc.
10. Creates emotional connection, differentiates from competitors, makes brand memorable, builds trust
