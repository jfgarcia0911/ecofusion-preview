# Quiz 8: Retail Partnerships
**Time:** 15 min | **Points:** 10 | **Passing:** 80%

## Multiple Choice
1. Standard retail markup: **B) 40-50%**
2. Keystone pricing means retail price is: **B) 2× wholesale**
3. Wholesale price should be what % of your retail: **C) 50-70%**
4. Required on fresh produce labels: **A) Farm name and contact**
5. Best retail partners for local food: **A) Natural food co-ops**

## True/False
6. Consignment reduces risk for the farm. **False**
7. Net 30 payment terms mean 30 days to pay. **True**
8. Packaging must be retail-ready for store accounts. **True**

## Short Answer
9. Calculate: Your farmers market price is $4.50. What wholesale price to retailer?
10. Why is "shrink" a concern in retail partnerships?

## Answer Key
1-5 above | 6-8 above
9. $2.25-3.15 (50-70% of retail)
10. Unsold product spoilage; farm may bear cost; affects profitability; need to negotiate terms
