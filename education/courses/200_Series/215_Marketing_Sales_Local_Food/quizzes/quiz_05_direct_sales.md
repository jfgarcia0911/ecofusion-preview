# Quiz 5: Direct-to-Consumer Sales
**Time:** 15 min | **Points:** 10 | **Passing:** 80%

## Multiple Choice
1. What does CSA stand for? A) Community Supported Agriculture
2. CSA programs offer farmers: A) Upfront capital | B) Guaranteed sales | C) Shared risk | **D) All of the above**
3. Typical CSA pricing compared to farmers market: A) Same | **B) 20-30% discount** | C) 50% more | D) Double
4. Honor system farm stands typically experience theft loss of: A) <1% | **B) 5-15%** | C) 25-30% | D) 50%+
5. Subscription models benefit farms by providing: **A) Predictable revenue** | B) One-time sales | C) Higher costs | D) More complexity

## True/False
6. CSA members receive the same box contents every week. **False**
7. Online ordering requires 48-hour lead time typically. **True**
8. Delivery models are more profitable than pickup. **False**

## Short Answer
9. List TWO benefits of CSA programs for farmers.
10. What are THREE delivery model options for farms?

## Answer Key
1-5: See above | 6. False (varies by harvest) | 7. True | 8. False (delivery has costs)
9. Guaranteed sales, upfront capital, cash flow, loyal customers, shared risk
10. Fixed route delivery, pickup locations, shipping, farm pickup, CSA drop points
