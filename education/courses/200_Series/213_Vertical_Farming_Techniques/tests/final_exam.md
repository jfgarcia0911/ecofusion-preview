## Section 1: Fundamentals (Questions 1-10)

### Question 1 (2 points)
**Define vertical farming and list its three core characteristics.**

**Model Answer:**
Vertical farming is the practice of growing crops in vertically stacked layers within a controlled environment, typically indoors. Three core characteristics:
1. Vertical stacking of growing layers (2-12+ tiers)
2. Controlled environment with climate, light, and humidity management
3. Soilless cultivation using hydroponics, aeroponics, or aquaponics
