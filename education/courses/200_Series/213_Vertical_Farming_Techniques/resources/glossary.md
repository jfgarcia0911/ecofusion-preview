# Glossary: Vertical Farming Techniques
## Course 213

---

## A

**Aeroponics**: A soilless growing method where plant roots are suspended in air and misted with nutrient solution at regular intervals.

**Airflow**: The movement of air through the growing environment, critical for plant transpiration, CO2 distribution, and disease prevention.

**Automation**: The use of control systems and technology to operate farming equipment with minimal human intervention.

## B

**Batch**: A group of plants that move through the production system together, seeded and harvested at the same time.

## C

**CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture)**: The practice of growing crops in enclosed structures where environmental conditions are precisely managed.

**Climate Control**: The management of temperature, humidity, CO2, and airflow within a growing facility.

**CO2 Enrichment**: The practice of increasing carbon dioxide levels above ambient (400 ppm) to enhance photosynthesis, typically to 800-1200 ppm.

## D

**Deep Water Culture (DWC)**: A hydroponic system where plant roots are suspended in a continuously aerated nutrient solution.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)**: The total amount of photosynthetically active radiation received by plants during a 24-hour period, measured in mol/m²/day.

**Drip Irrigation**: A method of delivering nutrient solution directly to the root zone through emitters.

## E

**EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization - a measure of operating profitability.

**EC (Electrical Conductivity)**: A measurement of the total dissolved salts in nutrient solution, indicating nutrient concentration.

## F

**Fertigation**: The delivery of nutrients to plants through the irrigation system.

**Floor Multiplier**: The ratio of total growing area to floor space, indicating vertical space utilization efficiency.

## G

**Germination Chamber**: A controlled environment specifically designed for seed germination, typically with high humidity and precise temperature.

**Growing Area**: The total square footage of space dedicated to plant production, including all vertical levels.

## H

**HVAC**: Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems used to maintain optimal growing conditions.

**Hydroponics**: A method of growing plants without soil, using mineral nutrient solutions in water.

## I

**Indoor Farming**: Agriculture conducted entirely within enclosed structures, independent of external weather conditions.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: The discount rate that makes the net present value of an investment equal to zero.

## L

**LED (Light Emitting Diode)**: Energy-efficient lighting technology commonly used in vertical farms for its spectral tunability and low heat output.

**Light Recipe**: A specific combination of light spectrum, intensity, and photoperiod optimized for a particular crop or growth stage.

## M

**Mobile Racking**: Rack systems that can be moved laterally on tracks to maximize space utilization by eliminating fixed aisles.

**Multilevel Growing**: The vertical stacking of growing surfaces to multiply production capacity within a given floor area.

## N

**NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)**: A hydroponic system where a thin film of nutrient solution flows over plant roots in sloped channels.

**Nutrient Solution**: A precisely formulated mixture of water and essential mineral elements for plant growth.

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)**: Light in the 400-700 nanometer wavelength range that plants use for photosynthesis.

**Payback Period**: The time required for an investment to generate enough cash flow to recover its initial cost.

**Plant Factory**: A fully enclosed vertical farming facility with complete control over all environmental factors.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)**: The measurement of light intensity reaching the plant canopy, expressed in µmol/m²/s.

**Propagation**: The process of starting new plants from seeds or cuttings before transplanting to production systems.

## R

**Rack System**: The structural framework that supports multiple levels of growing surfaces in a vertical farm.

**Recirculating System**: A hydroponic setup where nutrient solution is collected after passing through the root zone and reused.

**ROI (Return on Investment)**: A measure of profitability calculated as net profit divided by initial investment.

## S

**Seed-to-Sale**: Tracking systems that monitor plants from germination through harvest and delivery to customers.

**Soilless Growing**: Any method of plant cultivation that does not use traditional soil as the growing medium.

**Spectrum**: The wavelength composition of light, particularly important for plant growth and development.

**Stacking Height**: The vertical dimension of a rack system, typically limited by ceiling height and accessibility.

## T

**Tower System**: A vertical growing structure where plants grow from openings in a vertical column.

**Transpiration**: The process by which plants release water vapor through their leaves, driving nutrient uptake.

**Turnover Rate**: The number of complete crop cycles achieved in a given space per year.

## V

**Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD)**: A measure of the drying power of air, calculated from temperature and humidity, that affects plant transpiration.

**Vertical Integration**: Business strategy where a company controls multiple stages of production and distribution.

## W

**Water Use Efficiency**: The ratio of crop yield to water consumed, typically measured in pounds produced per gallon.

## Y

**Yield Density**: The production output per unit area, typically measured in pounds per square foot per year.
