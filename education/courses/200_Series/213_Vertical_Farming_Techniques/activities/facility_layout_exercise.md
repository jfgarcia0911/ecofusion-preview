## Part 2: Rack System Design

### Task 2.1: Select Rack Configuration

Choose your rack system based on crop and production goals:

**Option A: Standard Racks (8 ft wide × 40 ft long)**
- 5 growing levels at 18" spacing
- 1,600 sq ft growing area per rack (8 × 40 × 5 levels)

**Option B: High-Density Racks (4 ft wide × 50 ft long)**
- 6 growing levels at 16" spacing
- 1,200 sq ft growing area per rack (4 × 50 × 6 levels)

**Option C: Mobile Racks (8 ft wide × 30 ft long)**
- 5 growing levels at 18" spacing
- 1,200 sq ft per rack, but 30% more racks fit in space

### Rack Calculation Template

```
RACK CAPACITY CALCULATION

Growing Area Floor Space: _______ sq ft

Selected Rack Type: _______

Rack Footprint: _______ ft × _______ ft = _______ sq ft

Number of Levels: _______

Growing Area per Rack: _______ sq ft

Aisle Width Required: _______ ft

Racks that Fit in Growing Area: _______

TOTAL GROWING AREA: _______ sq ft
(Racks × Area per Rack)

Floor Space Multiplier: _______ X
(Total Growing Area ÷ Floor Space)
```
