## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What is the primary benefit of operating multiple facilities versus a single large facility?**

A) Lower total costs
B) Reduced delivery distances and market diversification
C) Simpler management
D) Less equipment needed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**Economies of scale in vertical farming typically begin to appear at:**

A) 5,000 sq ft
B) 10,000 sq ft
C) 20,000-30,000 sq ft
D) 100,000+ sq ft

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**What is a key advantage of centralized management across multiple facilities?**

A) Individual facility customization
B) Reduced G&A costs through shared services
C) Elimination of technology needs
D) Simplified crop selection

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**Bulk purchasing discounts typically provide what percentage of savings on inputs?**

A) 2-5%
B) 8-12%
C) 15-25%
D) 30-40%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**What is the recommended approach for geographic expansion?**

A) Open all facilities simultaneously
B) Master operations at one site before expanding
C) Start in multiple markets to test demand
D) Build the largest facility possible first

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6 (1 point)
**Which metric is most important when evaluating new market opportunities?**

A) Lowest real estate costs
B) Population density and proximity to target customers
C) Favorable weather
D) Low electricity rates only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7 (1 point)
**Vertical integration in farming refers to:**

A) Building taller facilities
B) Controlling multiple stages of production/distribution
C) Adding more crop tiers
D) Automating all processes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8 (1 point)
**What is a franchise model in vertical farming?**

A) Operating all facilities with company employees
B) Licensing technology and systems to independent operators
C) Selling produce through franchised stores
D) Renting equipment to other farms

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9 (1 point)
**Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are most critical when:**

A) Running a single small facility
B) Scaling to multiple locations to ensure consistency
C) Starting a new business
D) Operating in warm climates

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10 (1 point)
**What is the typical EBITDA margin improvement when scaling from 10,000 to 30,000 sq ft?**

A) 2-5%
B) 5-10%
C) 10-15%
D) 20-30%

**Correct Answer:** C
