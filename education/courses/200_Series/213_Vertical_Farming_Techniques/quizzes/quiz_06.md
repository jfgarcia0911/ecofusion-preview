## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**Which task in vertical farming offers the highest ROI for automation?**

A) Watering
B) Seeding and transplanting
C) Temperature monitoring
D) Packaging

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**What is the primary function of a fertigation system?**

A) Mixing and delivering nutrient solution automatically
B) Measuring plant growth rates
C) Controlling lighting schedules
D) Monitoring air quality

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**IoT sensors in vertical farms typically measure all of the following EXCEPT:**

A) Temperature and humidity
B) pH and EC (electrical conductivity)
C) Employee productivity
D) CO2 levels

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**What is the typical cost range for a seeding robot in commercial vertical farms?**

A) $10,000-25,000
B) $50,000-100,000
C) $150,000-300,000
D) $500,000-1,000,000

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**Machine vision systems in vertical farms are primarily used for:**

A) Security surveillance
B) Plant health monitoring and quality assessment
C) Employee tracking
D) Marketing videos

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6 (1 point)
**What is the recommended frequency for automated pH monitoring in hydroponic systems?**

A) Once per day
B) Every 4-6 hours
C) Continuous (every 15-30 minutes)
D) Once per week

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7 (1 point)
**SCADA systems in vertical farming are used to:**

A) Plant seeds
B) Supervise, control, and acquire data from farm operations
C) Package produce
D) Transport harvested crops

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8 (1 point)
**What labor savings can automated seeding systems typically achieve?**

A) 10-20%
B) 30-50%
C) 60-80%
D) 90-100%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9 (1 point)
**Which automation level is most appropriate for a 5,000 sq ft startup vertical farm?**

A) Fully automated (robotics for all tasks)
B) Semi-automated (climate, irrigation) with manual labor
C) Minimal automation, mostly manual
D) AI-driven autonomous operation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10 (1 point)
**Real-time data logging in vertical farms enables:**

A) Traceability and quality control
B) Predictive maintenance
C) Performance optimization
D) All of the above
