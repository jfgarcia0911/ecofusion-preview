## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**When designing a vertical farm, what should be the first consideration?**

A) Equipment suppliers
B) Market demand and crop selection
C) Building aesthetics
D) Automation technology

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**What is the recommended minimum ceiling height for a 6-tier vertical farm system?**

A) 10-12 feet
B) 12-14 feet
C) 14-16 feet
D) 18-20 feet

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**For a facility design, what percentage of space should be allocated to non-production areas (office, storage, processing)?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-25%
C) 30-40%
D) 45-55%

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**What electrical service capacity is typically required for a 10,000 sq ft vertical farm?**

A) 100-200 amps
B) 200-400 amps
C) 400-800 amps
D) 1,000+ amps

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**When selecting a building for vertical farming, which feature is most critical?**

A) Large windows for natural light
B) Low floor load capacity for cost savings
C) High floor load capacity (100+ lbs/sq ft)
D) Historic architecture

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6 (1 point)

A) 10-20 PSI
B) 30-50 PSI
C) 60-80 PSI
D) 100+ PSI

### Question 7 (1 point)

A) Security
B) Temperature control only
C) Pest and contamination prevention
D) Energy savings

**Correct Answer:** C

### Question 8 (1 point)

B) Start with partial build-out and expand as profitable
C) Wait until full funding to begin
D) Build infrastructure only, no equipment

D) Employee safety only
