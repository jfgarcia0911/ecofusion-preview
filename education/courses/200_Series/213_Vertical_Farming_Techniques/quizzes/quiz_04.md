## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What does PPFD measure in horticultural lighting?**

A) Total light output in lumens
B) Photosynthetic photon flux density (μmol/m²/s)
C) Color temperature in Kelvin
D) Energy consumption in watts

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**What is the typical PPFD range for optimal lettuce production?**

A) 100-150 μmol/m²/s
B) 200-300 μmol/m²/s
C) 400-500 μmol/m²/s
D) 600-800 μmol/m²/s

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**What is LED efficacy measured in?**

A) Lumens per watt
B) PPFD per meter
C) μmol/J (micromoles per joule)
D) Watts per square foot

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**For basil production optimized for flavor, what is the recommended blue light percentage?**

A) 10-15%
B) 20-25%
C) 30-35%
D) 40-50%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**What is the typical photoperiod (light duration) for leafy greens in vertical farms?**

A) 8-10 hours
B) 12-14 hours
C) 16-18 hours
D) 20-24 hours

**Correct Answer:** C

### Question 6 (1 point)
**What is the recommended mounting height for LED fixtures above plant canopy to ensure uniform coverage?**

A) 2-4 inches
B) 6-12 inches
C) 18-24 inches
D) 36-48 inches

**Correct Answer:** B

### Question 7 (1 point)
**High-efficiency LED systems can achieve what efficacy level?**

A) 1.5-1.8 μmol/J
B) 2.0-2.3 μmol/J
C) 2.5-2.8 μmol/J
D) 3.0-3.5 μmol/J

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8 (1 point)
**What is the primary benefit of adding far-red wavelengths (730nm) to LED spectrum?**

A) Increases leaf color intensity
B) Promotes stem elongation and flowering
C) Reduces energy consumption
D) Prevents disease

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9 (1 point)

B) 15-20%

### Question 10 (1 point)
