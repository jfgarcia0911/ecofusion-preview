## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What is the ideal workflow pattern for vertical farm operations?**

A) Random placement based on available space
B) Linear flow from seeding to harvest to packaging
C) Circular pattern with no designated zones
D) Vertical only, no horizontal movement

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**Which zone should be located nearest to the shipping area?**

A) Seeding/propagation
B) Young plant growth
C) Mature plant growth
D) Harvest and post-harvest processing

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**What percentage of facility space should typically be allocated to aisles and circulation?**

A) 5-10%
B) 15-20%
C) 25-35%
D) 40-50%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**Clean room protocols in vertical farms are primarily designed to:**

A) Impress visitors
B) Prevent contamination and disease introduction
C) Reduce energy costs
D) Improve employee comfort

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**What is the recommended frequency for sanitation of work surfaces in harvest areas?**

A) Monthly
B) Weekly
C) Daily
D) After each harvest batch

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 6 (1 point)
**The "First In, First Out" (FIFO) principle is important for:**

A) Employee scheduling
B) Inventory management and product freshness
C) Lighting schedules
D) Equipment maintenance

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7 (1 point)
**What is the typical labor requirement (FTE) for a 10,000 sq ft vertical farm?**

A) 2-3 FTE
B) 4-6 FTE
C) 8-10 FTE
D) 15-20 FTE

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8 (1 point)
**Batch tracking and traceability systems help with:**

A) Food safety and recall management
B) Quality control
C) Regulatory compliance
D) All of the above

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 9 (1 point)
**What is the recommended temperature for post-harvest cold storage of leafy greens?**

A) 28-32°F
B) 34-38°F
C) 40-45°F
D) 50-55°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10 (1 point)
**Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) should be created for:**

A) Only the most complex tasks
B) Critical tasks only
C) All recurring operational tasks
D) Emergency situations only

**Correct Answer:** C
