## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What is the primary principle of aeroponic systems?**

A) Plants float on nutrient-rich water
B) Roots are suspended in air and misted with nutrients
C) Roots grow in flowing channels of nutrient solution
D) Plants grow in vertical towers with drip irrigation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**What is the typical droplet size range for aeroponic misting systems?**

A) 1-5 microns
B) 5-50 microns
C) 50-100 microns
D) 100-200 microns

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**What is the recommended film depth for NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) systems?**

A) 0.5-1 mm
B) 1-3 mm
C) 5-10 mm
D) 10-20 mm

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**Which system type has the LOWEST complexity and maintenance requirements?**

A) Aeroponic
B) NFT
C) DWC (Deep Water Culture)
D) Tower systems

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5 (1 point)

D) $80-120
