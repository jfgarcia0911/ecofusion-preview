## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What is the optimal pH range for most hydroponic nutrient solutions?**

A) 4.5-5.0
B) 5.5-6.5
C) 7.0-7.5
D) 8.0-8.5

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2 (1 point)
**EC (Electrical Conductivity) measures what in hydroponic solutions?**

A) pH levels
B) Temperature
C) Total dissolved solids/nutrient concentration
D) Oxygen levels

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3 (1 point)
**What is the typical EC range for lettuce production (in mS/cm)?**

A) 0.5-1.0
B) 1.2-1.8
C) 2.5-3.0
D) 3.5-4.0

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4 (1 point)
**Which of the following is a macronutrient in hydroponic formulations?**

A) Iron
B) Nitrogen
C) Zinc
D) Boron

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5 (1 point)
**What does the NPK ratio represent?**

A) Nutrient delivery rate
B) Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium concentrations
C) pH balance requirements
D) EC measurement scale

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6 (1 point)
**How often should nutrient solution be completely replaced in most systems?**

A) Daily
B) Weekly
C) Every 2-4 weeks
D) Every 6 months

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7 (1 point)
**What is chelation in nutrient solutions?**

A) Removing excess nutrients
B) Adjusting pH levels
C) Binding micronutrients to keep them available for uptake
D) Filtering out contaminants

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8 (1 point)
**Which micronutrient deficiency causes interveinal chlorosis (yellowing between veins)?**

A) Calcium
B) Iron
C) Nitrogen
D) Phosphorus

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9 (1 point)

A) Tap water (no treatment)
B) Filtered or RO water with <150 ppm starting TDS
C) Distilled water only
D) Rainwater exclusively

### Question 10 (1 point)

B) 65-70°F
