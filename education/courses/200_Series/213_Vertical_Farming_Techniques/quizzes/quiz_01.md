## Questions

### Question 1 (1 point)
**What is the primary characteristic that defines vertical farming?**

A) Growing crops outdoors in vertical towers
B) Growing crops in vertically stacked layers within a controlled environment
C) Using vertical walls for growing vines
D) Stacking containers of soil vertically

**Correct Answer:** B

---
