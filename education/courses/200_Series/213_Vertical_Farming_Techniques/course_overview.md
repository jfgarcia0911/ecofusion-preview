## Course Description

Explore the cutting edge of controlled environment agriculture. This course covers vertical farming systems, technology, economics, and operations for multi-tier production. Learn to evaluate opportunities and design efficient vertical farming systems.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Explain vertical farming systems and technologies
2. Design vertical growing configurations
3. Manage vertical farm operations
4. Analyze vertical farming economics
5. Evaluate vertical farming opportunities
6. Address unique challenges of multi-tier production
