## Required Components

### 1. Facility Specifications (5 pts)
- Building dimensions
- Growing area (total & per level)
- Crop selection
- Production targets

### 2. System Design (10 pts)
- Vertical system type
- Rack/tier configuration
- Growing system (NFT, DWC, etc.)
- Plant density and spacing

### 3. Climate & Lighting (8 pts)
- LED system specifications
- HVAC design
- Humidity control
- Temperature zones

### 4. Operations Plan (4 pts)
- Production workflow
- Labor requirements
- Automation strategy

### 5. Financial Analysis (3 pts)
- Capital budget
- Operating costs
- Revenue projections
- ROI timeline

**Deliverable:** 12-18 page vertical farm design with layout drawings, equipment specs, and financial model
