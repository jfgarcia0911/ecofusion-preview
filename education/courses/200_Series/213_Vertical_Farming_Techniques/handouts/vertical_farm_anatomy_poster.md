## Component Details

### Rack Structure
- Heavy-duty steel or aluminum construction
- Adjustable level heights (typically 16-24" spacing)
- Load capacity: 50-100 lbs per level
- Modular design for easy reconfiguration

### LED Lighting
- Full spectrum with adjustable red/blue ratio
- Efficacy: 2.5-3.0 µmol/J
- Dimming capability (0-100%)
- Waterproof rating for humid environments

### Growing Channels
- NFT: 4-6" wide × 3-4" deep
- DWC: 6-12" deep rafts
- Food-grade materials
- UV-stabilized for longevity

### Irrigation Components
- Nutrient tanks: 500-2,000 gallon capacity
- Pumps: Centrifugal or submersible
- Filtration: 100-200 mesh
- Automated dosing for pH and EC
