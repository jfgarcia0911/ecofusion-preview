# Assignment 5: Complete Lighting System Design
## Course 205: LED Lighting Science

| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 12 |

## Purpose
Design complete lighting system for commercial production facility.

## Components Required

### 1. Facility Specifications (4 pts)
- Growing area dimensions
- Crop types and DLI requirements
- Multi-tier or single-level
- Power availability

### 2. Lighting Design (12 pts)
- Fixture selection and justification
- Layout and spacing plan
- PPFD mapping
- Total system PPF
- Dimming/control strategy

### 3. Electrical Requirements (6 pts)
- Total wattage
- Circuit design
- Wire sizing
- Panel requirements

### 4. Economic Analysis (8 pts)
- Capital costs
- Installation costs
- Operating costs (5-year projection)
- ROI vs. alternatives
- Payback period

**Deliverable:** 10-15 page design document with:
- Lighting layout drawings
- Fixture specifications
- Electrical schematics
- Financial analysis
- Implementation timeline
