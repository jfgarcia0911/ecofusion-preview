# Course 205: LED Lighting Science

## Course Overview

**Level:** Intermediate
**Duration:** 10 hours
**Price:** $299
**Prerequisites:** Basic understanding of controlled environment agriculture

## Description

Master the science and application of LED lighting for controlled environment agriculture. This comprehensive course covers light fundamentals, LED technology, spectrum optimization, PPFD and DLI management, fixture selection, system design, energy efficiency, and practical implementation strategies.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:

1. Understand PAR, PPFD, DLI, and plant-centric lighting metrics
2. Explain LED technology, efficacy, and thermal management
3. Design custom light spectra for specific crops and growth stages
4. Calculate and apply PPFD and DLI requirements
5. Select appropriate LED fixtures based on technical specifications
6. Design complete lighting systems including electrical and controls
7. Optimize energy efficiency and reduce operational costs
8. Apply photoperiod strategies for production scheduling
9. Implement greenhouse supplemental lighting systems
10. Create professional lighting system documentation

## Course Structure

### Lessons (10 modules, ~60 min each)
1. **Light Fundamentals** - PAR, PPFD, DLI, measurement principles
2. **LED Technology** - How LEDs work, drivers, thermal management
3. **Light Spectrum** - Wavelength effects, R:B ratios, morphology control
4. **PPFD and DLI** - Crop requirements, calculations, optimization
5. **Fixture Selection** - Specifications, comparisons, TCO analysis
6. **Light Planning** - System design, layouts, electrical, controls
7. **Energy Efficiency** - Consumption analysis, optimization, rebates
8. **Photoperiod Control** - SDP/LDP responses, flowering, scheduling
9. **Supplemental Lighting** - Greenhouse integration, sensor control
10. **Lighting Project** - Comprehensive capstone design project

## Key Topics Covered

### Technical Foundations
- Electromagnetic spectrum and PAR (400-700nm)
- Light measurement units and conversions
- Inverse square law and uniformity
- LED semiconductor physics and electroluminescence
- Driver technology and thermal management

### Spectrum Science
- McCree curve and photosynthetic efficiency
- Blue, green, red, and far-red wavelength effects
- R:B and R:FR ratios for morphology control
- UV radiation for secondary metabolite production
- Phytochrome system and photoperiodism

### Practical Applications
- PPFD and DLI calculations for all major crops
- Fixture selection based on efficacy and TCO
- Complete lighting system design and documentation
- Energy efficiency optimization strategies
- Greenhouse supplemental lighting integration

### Essential Formulas

```
DLI = PPFD × Hours × 3.6 / 1000
PPFD = (DLI × 1000) / (Hours × 3.6)
Efficacy = PPF / Power (watts)
Uniformity = (Min PPFD / Max PPFD) × 100
R:B Ratio = Red photons / Blue photons
Total PPF = Area × PPFD / Efficiency
```
