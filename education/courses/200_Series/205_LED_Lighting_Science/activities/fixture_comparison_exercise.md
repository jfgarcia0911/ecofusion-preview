**Duration:** 60 minutes **Level:** Intermediate

## Objective
Evaluate and compare LED fixtures using technical specifications to make informed purchasing decisions.

## Part 2: Scenario Analysis (30 min)

### Scenario 1: Lettuce Production (100 m²)
- Target DLI: 15 mol/m²/d
- Photoperiod: 16 hours
- Budget: $20,000
- Required PPFD: 260 μmol/m²/s
- Efficiency: 85%

Calculate for each fixture:
1. Fixtures needed
2. Total capital cost
3. Annual energy cost
4. 5-year TCO

**Which fixture is best value? Why?**

**Which fixture is best for this application? Why?**
