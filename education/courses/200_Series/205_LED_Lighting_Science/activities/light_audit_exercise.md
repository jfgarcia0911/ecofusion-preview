# Light Audit Exercise

**Course:** 205 - LED Lighting Science
**Activity Type:** Hands-On Measurement
**Duration:** 90-120 minutes
**Difficulty:** Intermediate

## Overview

This exercise teaches you to conduct a professional light audit of a growing facility. You will measure PPFD distribution, create a light map, calculate DLI delivery, assess uniformity, and provide recommendations for optimization. This is a critical skill for CEA facility management, troubleshooting, and system design validation.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this exercise, you will be able to:
1. Conduct systematic PPFD measurements across a growing area
2. Create accurate PPFD distribution maps
3. Calculate average PPFD, uniformity, and delivered DLI
4. Identify light distribution problems and their causes
5. Develop actionable recommendations for improvement
6. Document audit findings in a professional report

## Safety Considerations

```
SAFETY PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before Starting:
□ Notify facility personnel of audit activities
□ Identify electrical hazards and avoid contact
□ Use proper ladder safety if working at heights
□ Wear appropriate PPE (gloves, safety glasses if needed)
□ Ensure adequate lighting for workspace navigation
□ Have spotter if using ladders
□ Know location of electrical shut-offs
□ Avoid water/wet areas with electrical equipment

During Measurements:
□ Keep sensor dry and protected
□ Don't stare directly at high-intensity lights
□ Watch for trip hazards (cables, irrigation lines)
□ Take breaks if experiencing eye strain
□ Maintain stable footing on uneven surfaces
```

## Exercise Part 2: PPFD Measurement

### Step 3: Sensor Setup and Calibration Check

```
SENSOR PREPARATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Verify Sensor Function
   □ Power on sensor
   □ Check battery level
   □ Verify display is clear
   □ Test in known light condition

2. Calibration Verification
   □ Last calibration date: _______________
   □ Within 12 months? Yes / No
   □ If no, note in report limitations

3. Sensor Positioning Guidelines
   □ Hold sensor level (parallel to light source)
   □ Position at actual canopy height
   □ Avoid shadows from body or objects
   □ Wait 3-5 seconds for stable reading
   □ Block ambient light if present (greenhouse)

4. Fixture Warm-Up
   □ Ensure fixtures have been on for ≥15 minutes
   □ LED output stabilizes after warm-up
   □ Note time since lights turned on: _______ min
```

### Step 4: Conduct Measurements

```
MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For each grid point:

1. Navigate to marked location
2. Position sensor at canopy height (______ cm above floor)
3. Hold sensor level, parallel to fixtures
4. Avoid casting shadows on sensor
5. Wait for reading to stabilize (3-5 seconds)
6. Record PPFD value in data table below
7. Note any anomalies or observations
8. Move to next grid point
9. Repeat for all points

Tips:
- Measure in systematic pattern (left to right, top to bottom)
- If reading fluctuates, take average of 3 readings
- Note location of any obstructions (posts, equipment)
- Record any fixture failures or visible issues
```

## Exercise Part 3: Data Analysis

### Step 5: Calculate Key Metrics

```
ANALYSIS CALCULATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. AVERAGE PPFD
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Sum all PPFD measurements:             _______ μmol/m²/s
   Divide by number of points:            ÷ _______ points
   Average PPFD:                          = _______ μmol/m²/s


2. MINIMUM AND MAXIMUM VALUES
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Minimum PPFD:                          _______ μmol/m²/s
   Location:                              Point #_____ (X:___, Y:___)

   Maximum PPFD:                          _______ μmol/m²/s
   Location:                              Point #_____ (X:___, Y:___)

   Range (Max - Min):                     _______ μmol/m²/s


3. UNIFORMITY RATIO
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formula: (Minimum PPFD ÷ Maximum PPFD) × 100

   Calculation:
   (_______ ÷ _______) × 100 = _______ %

   Rating:
   □ >90%: Excellent uniformity
   □ 80-90%: Good uniformity (commercial acceptable)
   □ 70-80%: Fair (some growth variation expected)
   □ <70%: Poor (significant issues)

   Your Rating: _______________


4. COEFFICIENT OF VARIATION (Optional)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Use calculator or spreadsheet for standard deviation

   Standard Deviation:                    _______
   Mean PPFD:                             _______
   CV = (StdDev ÷ Mean) × 100:            _______ %

   Target: <10% CV for excellent uniformity


5. DELIVERED DLI
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Formula: DLI = PPFD × Hours × 3.6 ÷ 1000

   Average PPFD:                          _______ μmol/m²/s
   Photoperiod:                           _______ hours

   Calculation:
   _______ × _______ × 3.6 ÷ 1000 = _______ mol/m²/d

   Target DLI for crop:                   _______ mol/m²/d
   Difference (Delivered - Target):       _______ mol/m²/d

   Assessment:
   □ Within ±10% of target (acceptable)
   □ Below target (increase intensity or duration)
   □ Above target (reduce intensity or duration)


6. COVERAGE ADEQUACY
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Determine target PPFD for your crop DLI and photoperiod.

   Target PPFD:                           _______ μmol/m²/s

   Count grid points meeting target (within 10%):
   Points ≥ target:                       _______ of _______ points
   Percentage coverage:                   _______ %

   Assessment:
   □ >90%: Excellent coverage
   □ 70-90%: Acceptable coverage
   □ <70%: Poor coverage, redesign needed

## Exercise Part 4: Problem Identification

### Step 7: Diagnose Distribution Issues

```
PATTERN ANALYSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Common PPFD Distribution Patterns and Causes:

PATTERN 1: High Center, Low Edges
     Low  Med  High Med  Low
     Low  Med  High Med  Low
     Low  Med  High Med  Low

Cause: Fixtures too far apart or mounted too low
Solution: □ Increase mounting height
          □ Add perimeter fixtures
          □ Reduce fixture spacing

PATTERN 2: Alternating Hot/Cold Spots
     Low  High Low  High Low
     High Low  High Low  High
     Low  High Low  High Low

Cause: Fixtures too low, poor overlap
Solution: □ Increase mounting height
          □ Reduce fixture spacing
          □ Use diffusers

PATTERN 3: One Side High, Other Side Low
     High High High Med  Med
     High High High Med  Med
     High High High Med  Med

Cause: Uneven fixture distribution or failure
Solution: □ Check for fixture failures
          □ Rebalance fixture layout
          □ Verify dimming settings

PATTERN 4: Random Hot/Cold Spots
     Med  Low  High Med  Low
     High Med  Low  High Med
     Med  High Med  Low  High

Cause: Obstructions, fixture failures, poor planning
Solution: □ Identify and remove obstructions
          □ Test individual fixtures

### Step 8: Identify Specific Problems

```
PROBLEM CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Check all that apply to your audit:

INTENSITY PROBLEMS
□ Average PPFD below target (>10% under)
□ Average PPFD above target (>10% over)
□ Some areas receive <70% of target PPFD
□ Peak PPFD exceeds crop saturation point

UNIFORMITY PROBLEMS
□ Uniformity ratio <80%
□ Large variations between adjacent points (>30%)
□ Edge falloff (perimeter <70% of center)
□ Hot spots (local peaks >150% of average)
□ Cold spots (local minimums <50% of average)

COVERAGE PROBLEMS
□ Less than 80% of area meets target PPFD
□ Large dead zones with insufficient light
□ Wasted light (areas >130% of target)
□ Poor fixture overlap pattern

FIXTURE PROBLEMS
□ One or more fixtures not operating
□ Uneven output between fixtures
□ Visible dimming inconsistencies
□ Dirty lenses reducing output
□ Heat stress on fixtures (thermal issues)
□ Physical damage or degradation visible

DESIGN PROBLEMS
□ Mounting height inappropriate
□ Fixture spacing too wide
□ Fixture type doesn't match application
□ Insufficient total PPF for area
□ No perimeter lighting to prevent edge falloff
```
