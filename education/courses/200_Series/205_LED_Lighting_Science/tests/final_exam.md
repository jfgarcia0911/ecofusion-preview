## Section 1: Light Fundamentals (Questions 1-10)

1. **PAR wavelength range is:**
   - A) 280-400nm  B) 400-700nm  C) 500-600nm  D) 700-800nm
   - **Answer: B**

2. **Calculate DLI: PPFD=350μmol/m²/s, 16 hours**
   - A) 18.1  B) 20.2  C) 22.4  D) 24.6 mol/m²/d
   - **Answer: B (350×16×3.6/1000=20.16)**

3. **Why can't lux meters measure plant lighting accurately?**
   - A) Too expensive  B) Weighted for human vision  C) Don't work with LEDs  D) Measure wrong spectrum
   - **Answer: B**

4. **Inverse square law: PPFD at 18" is 400μmol/m²/s. At 36" it's:**
   - A) 800  B) 200  C) 100  D) 50 μmol/m²/s
   - **Answer: C (double distance = 1/4 intensity)**

5. **PPF measures:**
   - A) Light at canopy  B) Total fixture output  C) Daily accumulation  D) Human perception
   - **Answer: B**

6. **Light compensation point typically occurs at:**
   - A) 10-25  B) 50-100  C) 200-300  D) 500-700 μmol/m²/s
   - **Answer: B**

7. **Green light's primary value is:**
   - A) Highest efficiency  B) Canopy penetration  C) Brightest to humans  D) Cheapest to produce
   - **Answer: B**

8. **PPFD uniformity target is:**
   - A) >50%  B) >65%  C) >80%  D) >95%
   - **Answer: C**

9. **Calculate PPFD needed: DLI=18mol/m²/d, 18 hours**
   - A) 231  B) 278  C) 309  D) 347 μmol/m²/s
   - **Answer: B (18×1000÷18÷3.6=278)**

10. **Which formula is correct?**
    - A) DLI=PPFD×hours  B) DLI=PPFD×hours×3.6/1000  C) DLI=PPFD/hours  D) DLI=PPFD+hours
    - **Answer: B**

## Section 2: LED Technology (Questions 11-20)

11. **LEDs generate light through:**
    - A) Heating  B) Electroluminescence  C) Phosphorescence  D) Chemiluminescence
    - **Answer: B**

12. **LED wavelength is determined by:**
    - A) Voltage  B) Band gap energy  C) Current  D) Temperature
    - **Answer: B**

13. **Typical white LED electrical-to-PAR efficiency:**
    - A) 10-20%  B) 30-40%  C) 60-70%  D) 80-90%
    - **Answer: B**

14. **LEDs require which driver type?**
    - A) Constant voltage  B) Constant current  C) Variable frequency  D) AC direct
    - **Answer: B**

15. **L70 rating means:**
    - A) 70% LEDs working  B) Hours to 70% output  C) 70°C operation  D) 70% efficiency
    - **Answer: B**

16. **For every 10°C junction temperature increase:**
    - A) Output increases  B) Lifespan decreases 30-50%  C) Efficiency improves  D) No effect
    - **Answer: B**

17. **Good horticultural LED efficacy target:**
    - A) >1.5  B) >2.5  C) >5.0  D) >10.0 μmol/J
    - **Answer: B**

18. **Calculate efficacy: PPF=1800μmol/s, Power=600W**
    - A) 2.0  B) 2.5  C) 3.0  D) 3.5 μmol/J
    - **Answer: C (1800/600=3.0)**

19. **White LED phosphor coating:**
    - A) Increases output  B) Converts blue to broader spectrum  C) Reduces heat  D) Improves efficiency
    - **Answer: B**

20. **Full-spectrum LED advantage over monochromatic:**
    - A) Higher efficacy  B) Lower cost  C) Natural appearance, complete PAR  D) Simpler design
    - **Answer: C**

## Section 3: Spectrum (Questions 21-30)

21. **McCree curve shows highest efficiency at:**
    - A) 450nm  B) 550nm  C) 660nm  D) 730nm
    - **Answer: C**

22. **Blue light effect on morphology:**
    - A) Elongation  B) Compact growth  C) No effect  D) Flowering

23. **Calculate R:B ratio: Red=540, Blue=180μmol/m²/s**
    - A) 1.5  B) 2.0  C) 3.0  D) 4.0

24. **Far-red primary function:**
    - A) Photosynthesis  B) Morphology via phytochrome  C) UV protection  D) Chlorophyll synthesis

25. **For leggy plants, you should:**
    - A) Add far-red  B) Increase blue  C) Increase red  D) Remove green

26. **Recommended R:B for lettuce:**
    - A) 0.5-1.0  B) 2.0-3.0  C) 5.0-7.0  D) 10.0-15.0
    - **Answer: B**

27. **Low R:FR ratio causes:**
    - A) Compactness  B) Shade avoidance (elongation)  C) Flowering delay  D) Root growth
    - **Answer: B**

28. **UV-B at low doses promotes:**
    - A) Faster growth  B) Secondary metabolites  C) Root development  D) Germination
    - **Answer: B**

29. **Cannabis flowering spectrum strategy:**
    - A) High blue  B) High red + UV  C) Equal R:B  D) White only

30. **Phytochrome system responds to:**

41. **Most important fixture comparison metric:**
