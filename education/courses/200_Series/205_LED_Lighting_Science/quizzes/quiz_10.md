# Quiz 10: Lighting Project (Comprehensive)
**Module:** 10 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **First step in lighting system design is:** A)Select fixtures B)Define requirements C)Install wiring D)Order equipment **Answer:B**
2. **Calculate total PPF needed: 100m² area, 300μmol/m²/s target, 85% efficiency** A)25,300 B)30,600 C)35,300 D)40,000μmol/s **Answer:C (100×300÷0.85)**
3. **What should fixture quantity include beyond base calculation?** A)Nothing extra B)10-15% margin for degradation/uniformity C)50% extra D)Double quantity **Answer:B**
4. **Why might initial budget projections be exceeded?** A)Electrical service upgrades often overlooked B)Grower incompetence C)Vendor fraud D)No reason **Answer:A**
5. **What is value engineering?** A)Making it look valuable B)Cost reduction while maintaining performance C)Cheapest option D)Engineering degree **Answer:B**
6. **Phased implementation is used to:** A)Confuse installers B)Meet budget constraints while proving concept C)Delay project D)Reduce quality **Answer:B**
7. **ROI analysis should include:** A)Only capital costs B)Capital + operating costs C)Just energy costs D)Marketing budget **Answer:B**
8. **What makes a lighting system "future-proof"?** A)Never needs upgrading B)Modular design, dimming, zone control C)Most expensive D)Proprietary components **Answer:B**
9. **Installation documentation should include:** A)Plans, electrical diagrams, specs, BOM B)Just a sketch C)Verbal instructions D)Photos only **Answer:A**
10. **What is the most common cause of lighting project failure?** A)LED technology B)Inadequate planning and requirements analysis C)Installer skill D)Plant species **Answer:B**
