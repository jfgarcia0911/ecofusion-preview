# Quiz 4: PPFD and DLI

**Module:** 4 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **Calculate DLI: PPFD = 300 μmol/m²/s, photoperiod = 16 hours**
   - A) 15.2 mol/m²/d  B) 17.3 mol/m²/d  C) 19.4 mol/m²/d  D) 21.6 mol/m²/d
   - **Answer: B (300×16×3.6/1000=17.28)**

2. **What DLI range is optimal for lettuce production?**
   - A) 5-10  B) 12-17  C) 20-30  D) 35-45 mol/m²/d
   - **Answer: B**

3. **Calculate required PPFD: Target DLI = 20 mol/m²/d, photoperiod = 14 hours**
   - A) 298  B) 347  C) 397  D) 463 μmol/m²/s
   - **Answer: C (20×1000÷14÷3.6=397)**

4. **A PPFD map shows min=200, max=350 μmol/m²/s. What is the uniformity?**
   - A) 43%  B) 57%  C) 75%  D) 86%
   - **Answer: B (200/350×100=57%)**

5. **What is the target PPFD uniformity for commercial production?**
   - A) >50%  B) >65%  C) >80%  D) >95%
   - **Answer: C**

6. **Which crop requires the HIGHEST DLI?**
   - A) Lettuce  B) Basil  C) Tomato fruiting  D) Microgreens
   - **Answer: C**

7. **How should DLI be adjusted for CO₂-enriched environments (1000ppm)?**
   - A) Reduce 20%  B) No change  C) Increase 20-30%  D) Increase 50%
   - **Answer: C**

8. **At what point should PPFD measurements be taken?**
   - A) Above fixtures  B) Canopy height  C) Root zone  D) Midway between fixture and plants
   - **Answer: B**

9. **Why reduce DLI for seedlings compared to mature plants?**
   - A) Save energy  B) Prevent photoinhibition in young tissues  C) Promote elongation  D) No reason
   - **Answer: B**

10. **What formula converts PPFD to DLI?**
    - A) PPFD × hours  B) PPFD × hours × 3.6 / 1000  C) PPFD / hours  D) PPFD × 24
    - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1.B  2.B  3.C  4.B  5.C  6.C  7.C  8.B  9.B  10.B
