# Quiz 2: LED Technology

**Course:** 205 - LED Lighting Science
**Module:** 2 - LED Technology
**Total Points:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

1. **How do LEDs generate light?**
   - A) Heating a filament
   - B) Exciting mercury vapor
   - C) Electroluminescence in semiconductors
   - D) Phosphorescent coating
   - **Answer: C**

2. **What determines the wavelength (color) of an LED?**
   - A) Applied voltage
   - B) Semiconductor band gap energy
   - C) Current level
   - D) Temperature
   - **Answer: B**

3. **What is the typical electrical-to-PAR efficiency of modern white LEDs?**
   - A) 10-20%
   - B) 30-40%
   - C) 60-70%
   - D) 80-90%
   - **Answer: B**

4. **Why do white LEDs use phosphor coatings?**
   - A) To increase light output
   - B) To convert blue light to broader spectrum
   - C) To reduce heat generation
   - D) To improve efficiency
   - **Answer: B**

5. **What type of driver is required for most LED fixtures?**
   - A) Constant voltage (CV)
   - B) Constant current (CC)
   - C) Variable frequency
   - D) AC direct drive
   - **Answer: B**

6. **How does junction temperature affect LED lifespan?**
   - A) No effect
   - B) Higher temperature = longer life
   - C) Higher temperature = shorter life
   - D) Only affects light output, not lifespan
   - **Answer: C**

7. **What is a typical efficacy target for good quality horticultural LEDs?**
   - A) >1.5 μmol/J
   - B) >2.5 μmol/J
   - C) >5.0 μmol/J
   - D) >10.0 μmol/J
   - **Answer: B**

8. **What does L70 rating mean?**
   - A) 70% of LEDs still working
   - B) 70% of initial light output
   - C) Operating at 70°C
   - D) 70% efficiency
   - **Answer: B**

9. **Why is thermal management critical for LED performance?**
   - A) Heat increases light output
   - B) Heat reduces efficiency and lifespan
   - C) Heat improves spectrum quality
   - D) Heat is not a concern with LEDs
   - **Answer: B**

10. **What is the main advantage of full-spectrum (white + red) LEDs over monochromatic for CEA?**
    - A) Higher cost
    - B) More complex installation
    - C) Natural appearance and complete PAR coverage
    - D) Lower efficiency
    - **Answer: C**
