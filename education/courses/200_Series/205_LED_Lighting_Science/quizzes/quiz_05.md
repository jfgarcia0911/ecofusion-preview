# Quiz 5: Fixture Selection
**Module:** 5 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **Which metric is MOST important for comparing LED fixture efficiency?** A)Wattage B)Lumens C)Efficacy(μmol/J) D)Price **Answer:C**
2. **Calculate fixtures needed: 200m² area, 300μmol/m²/s target, 1500μmol/s per fixture, 80% efficiency** A)40 B)50 C)60 D)75 **Answer:B (200×300÷0.80÷1500=50)**
3. **What is a red flag when evaluating fixture manufacturers?** A)DLC listing B)PPF clearly stated C)No PPFD map provided D)5-year warranty **Answer:C**
4. **Why does Total Cost of Ownership often favor premium fixtures?** A)Lower energy costs over lifetime B)Better appearance C)Easier to install D)Lower initial cost **Answer:A**
5. **Fixture A: $500, 3.0μmol/J. Fixture B: $400, 2.4μmol/J. For same 1200μmol/s output, which uses less power?** A)Equal B)Fixture A C)Fixture B D)Can't determine **Answer:B (A:400W vs B:500W)**
6. **What IP rating is recommended for humid greenhouse environments?** A)IP20 B)IP44 C)IP65+ D)IP rating doesn't matter **Answer:C**
7. **What does a typical LED warranty cover?** A)Light output degradation B)Any failure C)Accidental damage D)Energy costs **Answer:A**
8. **Why is PPF a better metric than wattage for comparing fixtures?** A)Easier to calculate B)PPF measures actual photon output C)Wattage is outdated D)PPF is cheaper **Answer:B**
9. **What should fixture spacing achieve?** A)Maximum fixtures per area B)>80% uniformity C)Equal watts per sq ft D)Minimum cost **Answer:B**
10. **Calculate $/μmol/s: Fixture $600, PPF 2000μmol/s** A)$0.20 B)$0.30 C)$0.40 D)$0.50 **Answer:B (600/2000=0.30)**
