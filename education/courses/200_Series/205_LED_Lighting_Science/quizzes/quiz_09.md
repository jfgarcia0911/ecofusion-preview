# Quiz 9: Supplemental Lighting
**Module:** 9 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **Calculate supplemental DLI needed: Target 25mol/m²/d, Natural 10mol/m²/d** A)10 B)15 C)20 D)25mol/m²/d **Answer:B (25-10=15)**
2. **What is the main economic advantage of supplemental vs sole-source lighting?** A)Better spectrum B)Easier installation C)Free natural light reduces energy costs D)Lower capital cost only **Answer:C**
3. **What spectrum is recommended for greenhouse supplemental LEDs?** A)Blue only B)Red-enriched white C)Purple (R+B) D)Green only **Answer:B**
4. **Why use sensor-based dimming for supplemental lighting?** A)Required by code B)Adjusts to maintain target PPFD regardless of natural light C)Improves spectrum D)Extends lifespan **Answer:B**
5. **Where should PAR sensors be placed in greenhouses?** A)On roof B)At canopy height C)Below plants D)Outside **Answer:B**
6. **What percentage energy savings can sensor control provide vs fixed schedules?** A)5-10% B)10-20% C)25-40% D)50-70% **Answer:C**
7. **In winter at 40°N latitude, typical natural DLI is:** A)1-5 B)5-15 C)20-30 D)35-45mol/m²/d **Answer:B**
8. **What is the typical PAR transmission through greenhouse glazing?** A)50-60% B)70-90% C)95-98% D)100% **Answer:B**
9. **Photoperiod extension uses supplemental light when?** A)Middle of day B)Cloudy days C)Before/after natural photoperiod D)At night **Answer:C**
10. **What control logic prevents over-lighting on sunny days?** A)Turn off when natural>target B)Fixed schedule C)Increase intensity D)Change spectrum **Answer:A**
