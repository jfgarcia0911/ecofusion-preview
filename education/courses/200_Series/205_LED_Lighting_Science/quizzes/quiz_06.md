# Quiz 6: Light Planning
**Module:** 6 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **Calculate total electrical load: 100 fixtures × 400W each** A)20kW B)30kW C)40kW D)50kW **Answer:C**
2. **At 208V with 20A breakers, how many 400W fixtures per circuit (80% capacity)?** A)4 B)6 C)8 D)10 **Answer:C (20×208×0.8÷400=8.3)**
3. **Why use zone-based control in multi-crop facilities?** A)Saves money B)Different crops need different light levels C)Easier wiring D)Required by code **Answer:B**
4. **What is 0-10V dimming?** A)Voltage protocol for intensity control B)10V maximum output C)10 dimming levels D)0-10% dimming range **Answer:A**
5. **Why include 2× safety factor in structural load calculations?** A)Code requirement B)Account for dynamic loads and safety margin C)Double the cost D)Half the weight **Answer:B**
6. **What documents are essential for installation?** A)Photos only B)Lighting plan, electrical diagrams, specs C)Just specifications D)Verbal instructions **Answer:B**
7. **What is demand charge based on?** A)Total kWh used B)Peak kW draw C)Average power D)Time of day **Answer:B**
8. **Why map PPFD distribution before full installation?** A)Required by law B)Verify uniformity and coverage C)Impress clients D)Not necessary **Answer:B**
9. **What is the benefit of staged fixture startup?** A)Looks professional B)Reduces peak demand charges C)Extends lifespan D)Improves spectrum **Answer:B**
10. **Where should control wiring run relative to power wiring?** A)Same conduit B)Separate conduit (code) C)Above it D)Doesn't matter **Answer:B**
