# Quiz 8: Photoperiod Control
**Module:** 8 **Points:** 10 **Passing:** 70%

1. **Cannabis is classified as what type of plant?** A)Long-day B)Short-day C)Day-neutral D)Intermediate **Answer:B**
2. **What photoperiod triggers cannabis flowering?** A)<13 hours light B)>16 hours light C)Exactly 14 hours D)Any photoperiod **Answer:A**
3. **Lettuce bolts when photoperiod exceeds what threshold?** A)10hrs B)12hrs C)14-16hrs D)20hrs **Answer:C**
4. **What is the molecular basis of photoperiodism?** A)Chlorophyll B)Phytochrome C)Carotenoids D)Auxin **Answer:B**
5. **How does night interruption prevent short-day plant flowering?** A)Adds light energy B)Disrupts darkness measurement C)Changes spectrum D)Increases DLI **Answer:B**
6. **What is critical day length?** A)Maximum photoperiod B)Threshold for photoperiodic response C)Minimum DLI D)Optimal growth duration **Answer:B**
7. **Tomatoes are classified as:** A)Short-day B)Long-day C)Day-neutral D)Varies by variety **Answer:C**
8. **End-of-day far-red treatment does what?** A)Prevents flowering B)Accelerates flowering in some species C)Increases photosynthesis D)Reduces energy **Answer:B**
9. **Why are light leaks critical in cannabis flowering rooms?** A)Waste energy B)Disrupt required darkness period C)Change spectrum D)Not actually critical **Answer:B**
10. **What photoperiod prevents bolting in most lettuce varieties?** A)8hrs B)10hrs C)12-14hrs D)18-24hrs **Answer:C**
