# Quiz 3: Light Spectrum

**Course:** 205 - LED Lighting Science
**Module:** 3 - Light Spectrum
**Total Points:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

1. **According to the McCree curve, which wavelength has the highest photosynthetic efficiency?**
   - A) 450nm (blue)  B) 550nm (green)  C) 660nm (red)  D) 730nm (far-red)
   - **Answer: C**

2. **What effect does blue light (400-500nm) have on plant morphology?**
   - A) Elongation  B) Compact, shorter growth  C) No effect  D) Flowering
   - **Answer: B**

3. **Calculate the Red:Blue ratio: Red photons = 600 μmol/m²/s, Blue photons = 200 μmol/m²/s**
   - A) 1.5  B) 2.0  C) 3.0  D) 4.0
   - **Answer: C (600/200 = 3.0)**

4. **What is the primary function of far-red light (700-800nm)?**
   - A) Photosynthesis  B) Morphological control via phytochrome  C) UV protection  D) Chlorophyll synthesis
   - **Answer: B**

5. **Why does green light penetrate plant canopies better than red or blue?**
   - A) Higher energy  B) Less absorbed by upper leaves  C) Reflects more  D) Travels faster
   - **Answer: B**

6. **A crop is growing too tall and leggy. What spectrum adjustment would help?**
   - A) Increase red content  B) Increase blue content  C) Add far-red  D) Remove green
   - **Answer: B**

7. **What is the recommended R:B ratio for balanced lettuce growth?**
   - A) 0.5-1.0  B) 2.0-3.0  C) 5.0-7.0  D) 10.0-15.0
   - **Answer: B**

8. **What does UV-B radiation (280-315nm) promote at low doses?**
   - A) Faster growth  B) Secondary metabolites and flavonoids  C) Root development  D) Seed germination
   - **Answer: B**

9. **What effect does low R:FR ratio have on plants?**
   - A) Compact growth  B) Shade avoidance response (elongation)  C) Flowering delay  D) Root growth
   - **Answer: B**

10. **For cannabis flowering, what spectrum strategy is recommended?**
    - A) High blue (30%)  B) High red (50-60%) + some UV  C) Equal R:B  D) White light only
    - **Answer: B**

**Answer Key:** 1.C  2.B  3.C  4.B  5.B  6.B  7.B  8.B  9.B  10.B
