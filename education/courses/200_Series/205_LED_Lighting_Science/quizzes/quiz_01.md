### Question 1
What wavelength range defines Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR)?

A) 280-400 nm
B) 400-700 nm
C) 500-600 nm
D) 700-800 nm

**Answer:** B) 400-700 nm

---

### Question 2
Which metric should you use to measure light intensity for plant growth?

A) Lumens
B) Lux
C) PPFD (μmol/m²/s)
D) Candela

**Answer:** C) PPFD (μmol/m²/s)

---

### Question 3
Calculate the DLI for a crop receiving 250 μmol/m²/s for 18 hours daily.

A) 12.2 mol/m²/d
B) 13.5 mol/m²/d
C) 16.2 mol/m²/d
D) 18.0 mol/m²/d

**Answer:** C) 16.2 mol/m²/d
**Calculation:** DLI = 250 × 18 × 3.6 / 1000 = 16.2

---

### Question 4
Why can't you use a lux meter to measure plant lighting?

A) Lux meters are too expensive
B) Lux is weighted for human vision, not plant photosynthesis
C) Lux meters don't work with LED lights
D) Plants don't respond to light measured in lux

**Answer:** B) Lux is weighted for human vision, not plant photosynthesis

---

### Question 5
According to the inverse square law, if light intensity is 400 μmol/m²/s at 12 inches, what would it be at 24 inches?

A) 800 μmol/m²/s
B) 200 μmol/m²/s
C) 100 μmol/m²/s
D) 50 μmol/m²/s

**Answer:** C) 100 μmol/m²/s
**Explanation:** Doubling distance = 1/4 intensity (2² = 4)

---

### Question 6
Which wavelength range has the HIGHEST photosynthetic efficiency according to the McCree curve?

A) Blue (400-500 nm)
B) Green (500-600 nm)
C) Red (600-700 nm)
D) Far-red (700-800 nm)

**Answer:** C) Red (600-700 nm)

---

### Question 7
What is the primary reason green light is still valuable for plant growth despite lower quantum efficiency?

A) It reflects off leaves
B) It penetrates deeper into the canopy
C) It is brighter to human eyes
D) It costs less to produce

**Answer:** B) It penetrates deeper into the canopy

---

### Question 8
What unit measures the total photon output of a light fixture?

A) PPFD (μmol/m²/s)
B) DLI (mol/m²/d)
C) PPF (μmol/s)
D) Lux (lm/m²)

**Answer:** C) PPF (μmol/s)

---

### Question 9
At what PPFD range does the light compensation point typically occur for most plants?

A) 10-25 μmol/m²/s
B) 50-100 μmol/m²/s
C) 200-300 μmol/m²/s
D) 500-700 μmol/m²/s

**Answer:** B) 50-100 μmol/m²/s
**Explanation:** Light compensation point is where photosynthesis equals respiration

---

### Question 10
Which statement about PPFD uniformity is correct?

A) Uniformity doesn't matter if average PPFD is correct
B) Target uniformity should be >80% (min/max ratio)
C) Uniformity of 50% is acceptable for most crops
D) Uniformity only matters for ornamental crops

**Answer:** B) Target uniformity should be >80% (min/max ratio)
