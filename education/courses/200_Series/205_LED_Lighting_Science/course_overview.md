## Course Description

Harness the power of LED technology for optimal plant growth. This course covers light science, LED selection, spectrum optimization, lighting design, and energy management. Learn to design, implement, and optimize lighting systems for any controlled environment application.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Explain photobiology and light metrics (PAR, PPFD, DLI)
2. Select appropriate LED fixtures for different applications
3. Design lighting systems for various crops
4. Optimize light spectra for growth stages
5. Manage energy costs and maximize efficiency
6. Calculate and achieve target DLI

## Key Metrics Reference

### Light Measurements

| Metric | Unit | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| PPF | μmol/s | Total light output from fixture |
| PPFD | μmol/m²/s | Light intensity at a point |
| DLI | mol/m²/day | Total daily light (PPFD × hours ÷ 1,000,000) |
| Efficacy | μmol/J | Efficiency of fixture |

### DLI Targets by Crop

| Crop | Minimum DLI | Optimal DLI |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Lettuce | 10 | 14-17 |
| Herbs (basil) | 12 | 15-20 |
| Tomatoes | 20 | 25-35 |
| Peppers | 18 | 22-30 |
| Microgreens | 10 | 12-16 |

### Spectrum Effects

| Wavelength | Color | Primary Effect |
|------------|-------|----------------|
| 400-450 nm | Blue | Compact growth, leaf development |
| 500-550 nm | Green | Canopy penetration |
| 620-700 nm | Red | Photosynthesis, stem elongation |
| 700-750 nm | Far-red | Flowering, shade response |
