# Glossary: LED Lighting Science

**Course:** 205 - LED Lighting Science

## A

**Action Spectrum**: Graph showing the effectiveness of different wavelengths in driving a specific photobiological response (e.g., photosynthesis, germination).

**Ambient Temperature (Ta)**: The temperature of the air surrounding a fixture or LED component.

**Anode**: The positive terminal of an LED where current enters the device.

## B

**Band Gap Energy**: The energy difference between the valence and conduction bands in a semiconductor, determining the wavelength of emitted light.

**Beam Angle**: The angle between directions where intensity is 50% of maximum, measured in degrees. Typical ranges: 60° (narrow), 90° (medium), 120° (wide).

**Blue Light**: Wavelengths between 400-500nm. Promotes compact growth, stomatal opening, and chlorophyll production.

## C

**Candela (cd)**: Unit of luminous intensity (human-centric, not used for horticultural lighting).

**Cathode**: The negative terminal of an LED where current exits the device.

**CCT (Correlated Color Temperature)**: The apparent color of white light measured in Kelvin (K). Lower values (2700-3000K) appear warm/yellow; higher values (5000-6500K) appear cool/blue.

**Chip-on-Board (COB)**: LED package with multiple chips mounted directly on a substrate, providing high power density.

**Constant Current (CC) Driver**: Power supply that maintains fixed current output while voltage varies, required for most LED fixtures.

**CRI (Color Rendering Index)**: Scale (0-100) measuring how accurately a light source reproduces colors compared to natural light. Higher is better.

**Cryptochrome**: Blue light photoreceptor in plants, regulating processes like stomatal opening and flowering.

## D

**DALI (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface)**: Digital communication protocol for advanced lighting control, enabling individual fixture addressing.

**Day-Neutral Plant (DNP)**: Plant whose flowering is not affected by photoperiod length (e.g., tomato, cucumber, pepper).

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)**: Total photosynthetically active photons received over 24 hours, measured in mol/m²/d. Calculated as PPFD × photoperiod × 3.6 / 1000.

**Dimming**: Ability to reduce light output intensity, typically using 0-10V, PWM, or DALI protocols.

## E

**Efficacy**: Measure of LED efficiency, expressed as photon output per watt of electrical input (μmol/J). Higher efficacy = more efficient.

**Electroluminescence**: Light generation through electrical current passing through a semiconductor material.

**Emerson Enhancement Effect**: Phenomenon where combining red and far-red light produces greater photosynthetic rate than either wavelength alone.

## F

**Far-Red Light**: Wavelengths between 700-800nm. Triggers shade avoidance responses and can accelerate flowering in some species.

**Forward Voltage (Vf)**: Voltage drop across an LED when current flows forward, typically 2-4V per LED chip.

**Full-Spectrum**: Light source emitting across entire visible range (400-700nm) plus potentially UV and far-red.

**FWHM (Full Width Half Maximum)**: Bandwidth of LED emission at 50% of peak intensity, typically 20-30nm for monochromatic LEDs.

## G

**Green Light**: Wavelengths between 500-600nm. Penetrates plant canopies better than red or blue, driving photosynthesis in shaded leaves.

## H

**Heat Sink**: Metal component (usually aluminum) that dissipates heat from LEDs to prevent overheating and maintain performance.

**Horticultural Lighting**: Lighting designed specifically for plant growth, measured in plant-centric metrics (PPFD, PPF) rather than human vision metrics (lumens, lux).

## I

**InGaN (Indium Gallium Nitride)**: Semiconductor material used in blue, green, and UV LEDs.

**Inverse Square Law**: Physical law stating light intensity decreases proportionally to the square of distance: I₂ = I₁ × (D₁/D₂)².

**IP Rating (Ingress Protection)**: Two-digit code indicating protection against solids and liquids (e.g., IP65 = dust-tight, water jet resistant).

## J

**Junction Temperature (Tj)**: Temperature at the LED semiconductor junction, critical for performance and lifespan. Lower is better.

## L

**L70**: Industry standard for LED lifespan, indicating hours until light output decreases to 70% of initial value.

**L90**: Hours until light output decreases to 90% of initial value, stricter than L70.

**LED (Light Emitting Diode)**: Semiconductor device that emits light when current passes through it.

**Long-Day Plant (LDP)**: Plant that flowers when day length exceeds critical threshold (e.g., lettuce, spinach, wheat).

**Lumen (lm)**: Unit of luminous flux weighted for human vision. NOT appropriate for measuring plant lighting.

**Lux (lx)**: Luminous flux per unit area (lumens/m²), weighted for human vision. NOT appropriate for measuring plant lighting.

## M

**McCree Curve**: Graph showing relative photosynthetic efficiency across PAR wavelengths, peaking at ~450nm (blue) and ~660nm (red).

**MCPCB (Metal Core Printed Circuit Board)**: Circuit board with aluminum base for improved thermal conductivity compared to standard FR4 PCB.

## N

**nm (Nanometer)**: Unit for measuring wavelength of light (1 billionth of a meter).

## O

**Optics**: Lenses or reflectors that direct and shape light output from LED fixtures.

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)**: Light in the 400-700nm wavelength range that drives photosynthesis.

**Phosphor**: Material that absorbs short-wavelength light and re-emits at longer wavelengths. Used in white LEDs to convert blue to yellow/orange.

**Photoperiod**: Duration of light exposure in a 24-hour period, measured in hours. Critical for photoperiodic plant responses.

**Photoperiodism**: Plant developmental responses (esp. flowering) to day/night length ratios.

**Photomorphogenesis**: Light regulation of plant growth and development beyond photosynthesis.

**Photon**: Particle of light, the basic unit of electromagnetic radiation.

**Phototropin**: Blue light photoreceptor regulating phototropism and chloroplast movement.

**Phytochrome**: Red/far-red photoreceptor system controlling many developmental processes including flowering and shade avoidance. Exists in two forms: Pr (red-absorbing) and Pfr (far-red-absorbing).

**PPF (Photosynthetic Photon Flux)**: Total number of PAR photons emitted by a light source per second, measured in μmol/s.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)**: Number of PAR photons hitting a surface per unit area per time, measured in μmol/m²/s. Distance-dependent.

**Power Factor (PF)**: Ratio of real power to apparent power, indicating electrical efficiency. Target >0.90 for LED drivers.

**PWM (Pulse Width Modulation)**: Dimming method using rapid on/off cycles at varying duty cycles to control intensity.

## Q

**Quantum**: Discrete quantity of energy; for light, a quantum is a photon.

**Quantum Sensor**: Instrument measuring PPFD (μmol/m²/s) across PAR range (400-700nm).

## R

**R:B Ratio (Red:Blue)**: Ratio of red photons (600-700nm) to blue photons (400-500nm), used to predict morphological responses.

**R:FR Ratio (Red:Far-Red)**: Ratio of red photons (600-700nm) to far-red photons (700-800nm), controlling phytochrome photostationary state.

**Red Light**: Wavelengths between 600-700nm. Most efficient for photosynthesis, promotes flowering in some species.

**Reflector**: Optical component that redirects light through reflection.

## S

**Short-Day Plant (SDP)**: Plant that flowers when night length exceeds critical threshold, or day length is below critical threshold (e.g., cannabis, poinsettia, chrysanthemum).

**SMD (Surface Mount Device)**: LED package style mounted directly to circuit board surface.

**Spectrum**: Distribution of light energy across wavelengths, often shown as a graph of intensity vs. wavelength.

**Spectroradiometer**: Instrument measuring spectral power distribution, providing detailed spectrum analysis.

## T

**Thermal Interface Material (TIM)**: Paste or pad between LED board and heat sink that improves thermal conductivity by filling air gaps.

**Thermal Runaway**: Destructive feedback loop where heat increases current, which increases heat, leading to LED failure.

## U

**Uniformity**: Measure of PPFD consistency across growing area, calculated as (Minimum PPFD / Maximum PPFD) × 100%. Target >80%.

**UV-A (315-400nm)**: Ultraviolet radiation promoting anthocyanins, compact growth, and secondary metabolites.

**UV-B (280-315nm)**: Ultraviolet radiation that can enhance flavonoids and stress responses at low doses but causes damage at high doses.

**UV-C (<280nm)**: Germicidal ultraviolet radiation, harmful to plants and humans. Blocked by Earth's atmosphere.

## V

**Voltage Drop**: Reduction in voltage across a component when current flows through it.

**VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit)**: Difference between actual and saturation vapor pressure, indicating drying power of air. Interacts with lighting to affect plant stress.

## W

**Watt (W)**: Unit of electrical power consumption. In LEDs, input power ≠ light output; efficacy determines conversion efficiency.

**Wavelength (λ)**: Distance between peaks of a light wave, measured in nanometers (nm). Determines color/energy of light.

**White LED**: LED using blue chip with yellow phosphor coating to create broad-spectrum white appearance.

## Y

**YAG:Ce (Yttrium Aluminum Garnet doped with Cerium)**: Common phosphor material in white LEDs, converts blue to yellow light.

## Z

**0-10V Dimming**: Analog dimming protocol where 0V = minimum output (often 5-10%) and 10V = maximum output (100%).

## Quick Reference: Common Units

| Metric | Unit | Used For |
|--------|------|----------|
| PPF | μmol/s | Total fixture output |
| PPFD | μmol/m²/s | Light intensity at surface |
| DLI | mol/m²/d | Daily light accumulation |
| Efficacy | μmol/J | LED efficiency |
| Power | W (watts) | Electrical consumption |
| Wavelength | nm | Color of light |
| Photoperiod | hours | Day length |

## Formulas Summary

```
DLI = PPFD × Hours × 3.6 / 1000
PPFD = (DLI × 1000) / (Hours × 3.6)
Efficacy = PPF / Power
R:B Ratio = Red photons / Blue photons
Uniformity = (Min PPFD / Max PPFD) × 100
```
