# LED Fixture Comparison Guide

**Course:** 205 - LED Lighting Science

## Critical Specifications Checklist

```
ESSENTIAL METRICS TO COMPARE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ PPF (μmol/s)
  Total photon output - most important metric
  Compare at same wattage for fair comparison

□ Efficacy (μmol/J)
  Efficiency = PPF ÷ Input Power
  Target: >2.5 μmol/J minimum
  Premium: >3.0 μmol/J
  Excellent: >3.5 μmol/J

□ Input Power (Watts)
  Actual wall consumption (not "LED watts")
  Includes driver losses

□ Spectrum
  Wavelength distribution chart
  Match to crop requirements
  Check R:B and R:FR ratios

□ PPFD Map
  Distribution at specified height
  Verify uniformity
  Confirm coverage area claims

□ Lifespan
  L70 rating (hours to 70% output)
  Target: >50,000 hours
  Premium: >60,000 hours

□ Warranty
  Minimum: 3 years
  Premium: 5-7 years
  Read terms carefully

□ IP Rating
  IP65: Dust-tight, water resistant (minimum)
  IP66: Heavy water spray protection
  Higher needed for wet environments

□ Dimming Capability
  0-10V (most common)
  PWM (precise control)
  DALI (advanced automation)
  DMX (entertainment-grade control)

□ Certifications
  DLC Listed (efficiency)
  ETL/UL (safety)
  Third-party tested
```

## Normalized Performance Metrics

```
CALCULATING COST-EFFECTIVENESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

METRIC 1: Cost Per Photon ($/μmol/s)
─────────────────────────────────────
Lower is better - capital efficiency

Formula: Purchase Price ÷ PPF

Example:
Fixture A: $500 ÷ 1200 μmol/s = $0.42 per μmol/s
Fixture B: $400 ÷ 1000 μmol/s = $0.40 per μmol/s ← Better value
Fixture C: $650 ÷ 1500 μmol/s = $0.43 per μmol/s


METRIC 2: Photons Per Watt (μmol/J)
────────────────────────────────────
Higher is better - operating efficiency

Formula: PPF ÷ Input Power

Example:
Fixture A: 1200 ÷ 400W = 3.0 μmol/J ← Best efficiency
Fixture B: 1000 ÷ 420W = 2.4 μmol/J
Fixture C: 1500 ÷ 480W = 3.1 μmol/J ← Best efficiency


METRIC 3: Coverage Efficiency (m²/kW)
──────────────────────────────────────
Higher is better - area per power

Formula: Coverage Area @ Target PPFD ÷ (Power ÷ 1000)

Example (300 μmol/m²/s target):
Fixture A: 4 m² ÷ 0.4 kW = 10 m²/kW
Fixture B: 3 m² ÷ 0.42 kW = 7.1 m²/kW
Fixture C: 5 m² ÷ 0.48 kW = 10.4 m²/kW ← Best coverage


METRIC 4: Lifetime Photon Output
─────────────────────────────────
Total useful photons over lifespan

Formula: PPF × L70 Hours × 3600

Example:
Fixture A: 1200 × 60,000 × 3600 = 259 × 10¹² photons
Fixture B: 1000 × 40,000 × 3600 = 144 × 10¹² photons
Fixture C: 1500 × 70,000 × 3600 = 378 × 10¹² photons ← Best


METRIC 5: Total Cost of Ownership (5-Year)
───────────────────────────────────────────
Lower is better - true cost

Components:
  Capital: Purchase price
  Energy: Power × Hours × Days × Years × Rate
  Maintenance: Failures × Replacement cost
  Replacement: End-of-life cost

Example (per fixture, 16hr/day):
                    Fixture A    Fixture B    Fixture C
                    ─────────    ─────────    ─────────
Capital             $500         $400         $650
Energy (5yr)        $2,800       $2,940       $3,360
Maintenance         $50          $160         $30
Replacement         $0           $200         $0
                    ──────       ──────       ──────
TOTAL 5-Year TCO    $3,350       $3,700       $4,040

Annual TCO          $670         $740         $808
```

## Quality Indicators: Green Flags vs Red Flags

```
GREEN FLAGS - TRUSTED MANUFACTURERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✓ Detailed PPFD map provided (multiple heights)
✓ PPF and efficacy clearly stated
✓ Spectroradiometer test report available
✓ Third-party certification (DLC, ETL, UL)
✓ Thermal management testing data
✓ Named LED chip manufacturer (Samsung, Osram, Lumileds, etc.)
✓ Named driver brand (Meanwell, Inventronics, Philips, etc.)
✓ Warranty ≥3 years with clear terms
✓ Established company (>3 years in business)
✓ Transparent technical support contact
✓ Published case studies or grower testimonials
✓ Replacement parts available
✓ Realistic performance claims
✓ Professional website and documentation
✓ Sample/demo units available


RED FLAGS - AVOID THESE MANUFACTURERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✗ Only lumens specified (no PPF data)
✗ "Equivalent to XXX watts HPS" without data
✗ No PPFD map or coverage information
✗ Vague "covers X square feet" claims
✗ Unrealistic efficacy claims (>4.0 μmol/J without proof)
✗ Unknown or unspecified LED chip source
✗ Generic "high quality" or "premium" drivers
✗ Very short warranty (<2 years)
✗ Warranty excludes commercial use
✗ Unrealistic lifespan claims (>100,000 hours)
✗ No technical support contact information
✗ Pressure sales tactics or time-limited "deals"
✗ Too-good-to-be-true pricing
✗ No verifiable grower testimonials
✗ Poor or unprofessional website
✗ Company recently rebranded or renamed
✗ No third-party certifications
✗ Unwilling to provide samples
```

## Coverage Area Validation

```
VERIFYING MANUFACTURER CLAIMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Manufacturer Claim: "Covers ___ ft² at ___ μmol/m²/s"

VALIDATION STEPS:

1. Request PPFD Map
   □ Map at claimed coverage area
   □ Multiple mounting heights shown
   □ Grid spacing ≤12" (30cm)
   □ Measurement method disclosed

2. Verify Uniformity
   Min PPFD: _______ μmol/m²/s
   Max PPFD: _______ μmol/m²/s
   Uniformity: (Min ÷ Max) × 100 = _____%

   Acceptable if >70%

3. Check Average PPFD
   Sum all measurement points
   Divide by number of points
   Average: _______ μmol/m²/s

   Should match claimed PPFD ±10%

4. Calculate Effective Coverage
   Count grid points ≥ target PPFD
   Each point = grid area (sq ft)
   Effective Coverage: _______ ft²

   Compare to claim

5. Validate with Inverse Square Law
   PPFD @ 12" = _______ μmol/m²/s
   PPFD @ 24" should be ≈ _____ ÷ 4 = ______ μmol/m²/s
   Actual PPFD @ 24" = _______ μmol/m²/s

   Close match? Yes / No


EXAMPLE:
Claim: "Covers 16 ft² at 300 μmol/m²/s (24" mounting height)"

Measured Data (5×5 grid):
    250  280  300  280  250
    280  320  350  320  280
    300  350  380  350  300
    280  320  350  320  280
    250  280  300  280  250

Average: 299 μmol/m²/s ✓ (matches claim)
Min: 250, Max: 380
Uniformity: 250/380 = 66% (fair, but acceptable)
Points ≥300: 9 of 25 = 36% of area
Effective coverage @ 300+: ~6 ft² (37% of claim) ✗

VERDICT: Average PPFD claim accurate, but uniformity and
         effective coverage overstated. Usable area is less
         than claimed.
```

---

**Pro Tips for Fixture Selection:**
- Focus on PPF and efficacy - they drive long-term value
- Verify manufacturer claims with independent testing when possible
- Request samples before bulk purchase
- Total cost of ownership matters more than purchase price
- Premium fixtures often pay for themselves through energy savings
- Don't compromise on warranty or support for commercial operations
- Beware of "too good to be true" pricing - quality has a cost
