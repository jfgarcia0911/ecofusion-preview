## Section 2: Facility Design and Equipment

### Layout and Space Planning

**Create a floor plan including:**

```
FACILITY LAYOUT REQUIREMENTS

GROWING AREA (50-60% of space):
□ Rack locations and spacing
□ Aisle width (minimum 3 feet)
□ Lighting above each rack level
□ Electrical outlets positioned
□ Air circulation fans
□ Temperature/humidity monitoring

GERMINATION/BLACKOUT AREA (10-15%):
□ Stacking space for trays
□ Shelving for blackout
□ Access to water
□ Temperature controlled

HARVEST/PACKAGING AREA (15-20%):
□ Stainless steel or food-safe work surface
□ Hand washing station
□ Scale for weighing
□ Packaging material storage
□ Access to cold storage

COLD STORAGE (5-10%):
□ Refrigeration unit (walk-in or reach-in)
□ Organized shelving (FIFO)
□ Temperature monitoring
□ Adequate capacity for peak production

STORAGE/DRY GOODS (5-10%):
□ Seed storage (cool, dry, dark)
□ Growing media storage
□ Packaging supplies
□ Tools and equipment
□ Cleaning supplies

OFFICE/ADMIN (5%):
□ Desk/computer for records
□ Inventory management
□ Customer communication

Draw simple floor plan on grid paper or use free tools:
• Floorplanner.com
• RoomSketcher
• Even hand-drawn with measurements
```

```
4. PACKAGING STANDARDS
   ├─ Container selection by customer

   ├─ Labeling requirements
   ├─ Quality inspection
   └─ Storage protocol

5. SANITATION PROTOCOL
   ├─ Daily cleaning tasks
   ├─ Weekly deep cleaning
   ├─ Tool sanitization
   ├─ Hand washing requirements
   └─ Waste disposal

Write 1-2 page detailed SOP for each critical process
Include photos or diagrams where helpful
```

### Quality Monitoring System

```
QUALITY CONTROL PLAN

Daily Checks:
□ Environmental conditions (temp, humidity) 2× daily
□ Scout 10% of trays for pests/disease
□ Water quality (visual check)
□ Equipment function

Weekly Checks:
□ Germination rate testing (sample batch)
□ Yield tracking by variety
□ Customer feedback review
□ Inventory accuracy check

Monthly Checks:
□ Deep equipment inspection
□ Review and update SOPs
□ Supplier quality evaluation
□ Financial performance analysis

Quality Metrics to Track:
• Germination rate: Target >90%
• Yield per tray by variety
• Grade A percentage: Target >85%
• Waste rate: Target <10%
• Customer complaints: Target <2%
• On-time delivery: Target >95%
```
