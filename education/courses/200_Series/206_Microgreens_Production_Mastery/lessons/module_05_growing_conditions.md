### Light Height and Coverage

**Optimal Positioning:**

```
LIGHT-TO-CANOPY DISTANCE

Too High (>24 inches):
├─ Insufficient intensity
├─ Stretched, leggy growth
├─ Slow development
└─ Wasted light (inefficient)

Optimal (12-18 inches):
├─ Uniform coverage
├─ Adequate intensity
├─ Compact, healthy growth
└─ Energy efficient

Too Low (<8 inches):
├─ Light stress/burning
├─ Uneven temperature
├─ Reduced air circulation
└─ Physical contact damage

ADJUSTING FOR GROWTH:
• Start at 16" during transition
• Monitor plant development
• Raise lights if plants touch
• Lower slightly if plants stretch
```

### Watering Methods Comparison

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                        WATERING TECHNIQUES                                 ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  BOTTOM WATERING (Recommended)                                             ║
║  ═══════════════════════════                                              ║
║  Method: Water in catch tray, wicks up through media                       ║
║                                                                            ║
║  ✓ Advantages:                                                             ║
║    • No water on leaves (reduced disease)                                  ║
║    • Encourages deep root growth                                           ║
║    • More even moisture distribution                                       ║
║    • Less risk of disturbing seedlings                                     ║
║                                                                            ║
║  ✗ Considerations:                                                         ║
║    • Requires perforated growing trays                                     ║
║    • Need catch trays underneath                                           ║
║    • Must monitor to avoid over-watering                                   ║
║    • More time per tray                                                    ║
║                                                                            ║
║  TOP WATERING (Misting)                                                    ║
║  ═══════════════════════                                                  ║
║  Method: Spray or mist from above                                          ║
║                                                                            ║
║  ✓ Advantages:                                                             ║
║    • Quick and easy                                                        ║
║    • Good for dense canopies                                               ║
║    • Can combine with foliar feeding                                       ║
║                                                                            ║
║  ✗ Considerations:                                                         ║
║    • Water on leaves increases disease risk                                ║
║    • Can flatten or damage delicate seedlings                              ║
║    • Uneven distribution possible                                          ║
║    • Best as supplement, not primary method                                ║
║                                                                            ║
║  COMBINATION APPROACH (Best Practice)                                      ║
║  ═══════════════════════════════════                                      ║
║  • Bottom water as primary method                                          ║
║  • Light top misting if edges dry out                                      ║
║  • Adjust based on media, variety, environment                             ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Watering Schedule

**General Guidelines:**

```
DAYS AFTER GERMINATION | WATERING FREQUENCY
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Days 0-3 (Blackout)    | Daily check, water if needed
Days 4-5 (Transition)  | Daily, light watering
Days 6-10 (Growth)     | Every 1-2 days (monitor media)
Days 10-14 (Pre-harvest)| As needed (may be daily)

FACTORS AFFECTING FREQUENCY:
• Growing media (soil holds more than coco)
• Tray size and depth
• Temperature (hotter = more water)
• Humidity (drier = more water)
• Variety (peas need more than broccoli)
• Stage of growth (larger plants need more)
```

### Fan Setup and Placement

```
FAN CONFIGURATION FOR MICROGREENS ROOM

Small Operation (1-2 racks):
├─ 1× oscillating fan (12")
├─ Position: Side of room, aimed across trays
├─ Speed: Low-medium
└─ Runtime: Continuous when lights are on

Medium Operation (3-10 racks):
├─ 2-3× oscillating fans
├─ Position: Opposite ends, overlapping coverage
├─ Speed: Low-medium
└─ Runtime: Continuous

Large Operation (10+ racks):
├─ Multiple fans + ventilation system
├─ Exhaust fan for air exchange
├─ Intake vents or fans
└─ Circulation fans throughout space

AIR MOVEMENT TARGETS:
• Gentle, constant movement of plants
• Not strong enough to damage or dry out
• Leaves should sway slightly
• Avoid direct, strong air streams
```

## Troubleshooting Growing Conditions


## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Light is critical** - 200-400 μmol/m²/s for 14-16 hours daily produces optimal results

2. **Temperature range matters** - maintain 65-72°F; extremes cause poor quality

3. **Bottom watering is preferred** - reduces disease while encouraging root growth

4. **Air circulation prevents disease** - continuous, gentle airflow is essential

5. **Monitor and adjust** - daily observation and data logging enable quick corrections
