## Pre-Soaking Protocols

### Seeds That Require Soaking

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         PRE-SOAK REQUIREMENTS                            │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  REQUIRES SOAKING (8-12 hours)                                           │
│  ═══════════════════════════════                                         │
│  • Sunflower (hulled)                                                    │
│  • Pea shoots                                                            │
│  • Cilantro                                                              │
│  • Beet                                                                  │
│  • Chard                                                                 │
│                                                                          │
│  Reason: Large, hard seed coats need moisture to activate               │
│                                                                          │
│  OPTIONAL SOAKING (Can improve germination)                              │
│  ═══════════════════════════════════════════                            │
│  • Radish                                                                │
│  • Mustard greens                                                        │
│  • Wheatgrass                                                            │
│                                                                          │
│  NO SOAKING NEEDED                                                       │
│  ════════════════                                                        │
│  • Broccoli, kale, cabbage (brassicas)                                   │
│  • Arugula                                                               │
│  • Basil, herbs (most)                                                   │
│  • Amaranth                                                              │
│                                                                          │
│  Reason: Small seeds, thin coats, absorb water quickly                   │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Proper Soaking Procedure

**Step-by-Step Soaking:**

```
1. MEASURE SEEDS
   └─ Calculate exact amount needed for # of trays

2. RINSE SEEDS
   └─ Cold water rinse removes dust and debris

3. SOAK IN CONTAINER
   ├─ Food-grade bucket or bowl
   ├─ Cool water (not hot)
   ├─ Water level: 2-3× seed volume
   └─ Cover with lid or cloth

4. SOAK DURATION
   ├─ Sunflower: 8-12 hours
   ├─ Pea shoots: 8-12 hours
   ├─ Cilantro: 8-12 hours (can go 24 hours)
   └─ Beet: 12-24 hours

5. DRAIN THOROUGHLY
   ├─ Pour through colander/strainer
   ├─ Rinse with fresh water
   ├─ Shake to remove excess water
   └─ Seeds should be damp, not dripping

6. SEED IMMEDIATELY
   └─ Don't let soaked seeds sit >2 hours
```

### Soaking Tips and Troubleshooting

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                          SOAKING BEST PRACTICES                            ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  DO:                                                                       ║
║  • Use clean, food-safe containers                                         ║
║  • Change water halfway through for long soaks (>12 hrs)                   ║
║  • Keep at room temperature (68-72°F)                                      ║
║  • Label containers with variety and time                                  ║
║  • Rinse well before and after soaking                                     ║
║                                                                            ║
║  DON'T:                                                                    ║
║  • Soak in hot water (damages seeds)                                       ║
║  • Over-soak (>24 hours = drowning, fermentation)                          ║
║  • Let soaked seeds dry out before seeding                                 ║
║  • Skip rinsing (debris causes mold)                                       ║
║  • Use same water for multiple batches                                     ║
║                                                                            ║
║  TROUBLESHOOTING:                                                          ║
║  • Foul smell = too long, water too warm                                   ║
║  • Seeds floating = empty/dead seeds (skim off)                            ║
║  • Clumping after soak = didn't drain well                                 ║
║  • Poor germination = water too hot or soak too long                       ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Seeding Procedures

### Tray Preparation

**Media Preparation Steps:**

```
SOIL/COCO COIR (Loose Media):

1. Fill tray to proper depth
   ├─ Sunflower/Peas: 1-1.5 inches
   ├─ Radish/Broccoli: 0.5-1 inch
   └─ Most others: 0.75-1 inch

2. Level and compress lightly
   ├─ Use another tray to press gently
   ├─ Surface should be flat, firm
   └─ No low spots (water pools)

3. Pre-moisten media
   ├─ Bottom water or spray lightly
   ├─ Evenly moist, not saturated
   └─ No dry spots or puddles

COCO MATS/GROW PADS:

1. Place mat in tray
   └─ Ensure full contact with tray bottom

2. Pre-moisten completely
   ├─ Bottom water until saturated
   ├─ Allow to drain excess (5-10 min)
   └─ Surface should be uniformly moist
```

### Seeding Techniques

**Method 1: Broadcast Seeding (Most Common)**

```
Best for: Most varieties, especially small seeds

1. PREPARE SEEDS
   └─ Have measured amount ready (soaked if needed)

2. DISTRIBUTE EVENLY
   ├─ Hold seeds in cupped hand
   ├─ Sprinkle from 6-8 inches above tray
   ├─ Use sweeping motion for coverage
   └─ Aim for uniform distribution

3. ADJUST DISTRIBUTION
   ├─ Shake tray gently to settle seeds
   ├─ Push seeds from dense areas to sparse
   ├─ Use fingers to spread clumps
   └─ Verify coverage matches target density

4. SETTLE SEEDS
   └─ Lightly press seeds into media (optional)
```

**Method 2: Precision Seeding**

```
Best for: Sunflower, pea shoots, larger seeds

1. CREATE GRID PATTERN (optional)
   └─ Lightly score media surface

2. PLACE SEEDS INDIVIDUALLY OR IN ROWS
   ├─ Systematic placement
   ├─ Consistent spacing
   └─ Ensures even density

3. PRESS INTO CONTACT
   └─ Gentle pressure for seed-media contact
```

**Method 3: Shaker Method**

```
Best for: Tiny seeds (broccoli, amaranth)

1. CREATE SEED SHAKER
   ├─ Small container with holes in lid
   └─ Or use commercial seed shaker

2. SHAKE EVENLY OVER TRAY
   ├─ Multiple passes for coverage
   ├─ Easier control than hand broadcast
   └─ More uniform distribution
```

## Blackout Period

### Purpose and Science

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                    WHY BLACKOUT MATTERS                                    ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  PROMOTES ROOT DEVELOPMENT                                                 ║
║  ════════════════════════════                                             ║
║  • Darkness encourages downward root growth                                ║
║  • Roots anchor before stem elongation                                     ║
║  • Stronger, more vigorous plants result                                   ║
║                                                                            ║
║  UNIFORM GERMINATION                                                       ║
║  ══════════════════                                                        ║
║  • All seeds emerge at similar time                                        ║
║  • Reduces variation in harvest                                            ║
║  • More consistent product quality                                         ║
║                                                                            ║
║  PRESSURE/WEIGHT STRENGTHENS STEMS                                         ║
║  ════════════════════════════════════════                                 ║
║  • Stacking with weight creates resistance                                 ║
║  • Stems push against weight, grow stronger                                ║
║  • Prevents weak, spindly growth                                           ║
║                                                                            ║
║  MOISTURE RETENTION                                                        ║
║  ═════════════════                                                         ║
║  • Covered/stacked trays retain humidity                                   ║
║  • Reduces need for frequent watering                                      ║
║  • Maintains consistent moisture                                           ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Blackout Setup Methods

**Method 1: Stacking with Weight**

```
For: Sunflower, pea shoots (preferred method)

┌─────────────────┐
│  Weight Tray    │  ← 5-10 lbs (books, weights)
├─────────────────┤
│  Solid Tray     │  ← Cover (blocks light)
├─────────────────┤
│  Growing Tray 1 │  ← Seeds, germinating
├─────────────────┤
│  Growing Tray 2 │  ← Seeds, germinating
├─────────────────┤
│  Growing Tray 3 │  ← Seeds, germinating
├─────────────────┤
│  Solid Tray     │  ← Base (moisture retention)
└─────────────────┘

Stack: 3-5 trays maximum
Weight: 5-10 lbs distributed on top tray
Location: Dark area or covering
```

**Method 2: Covered (No Weight)**

```
For: Small seeds, delicate varieties

┌─────────────────┐
│  Solid Tray     │  ← Inverted as cover
├─────────────────┤
│  Growing Tray   │  ← Seeds, germinating
├─────────────────┤
│  Catch Tray     │  ← Moisture collection
└─────────────────┘

Location: Dark cupboard, under shelf, or covered area
```

**Method 3: Blackout Dome**

```
For: Small-scale or variety trials

Commercial blackout domes or DIY options:
• Humidity domes (opaque)
• Cardboard boxes
• Black plastic sheets
• Dedicated blackout chamber

Advantage: Easy individual tray management
Disadvantage: More space needed
```

## Moisture Management During Germination

### Initial Watering

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   GERMINATION WATERING STRATEGY                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  IMMEDIATELY AFTER SEEDING                                               │
│  ═══════════════════════════                                             │
│  Method: Bottom watering (preferred)                                     │
│    ├─ Place seeded tray in larger tray with water                        │
│    ├─ Water level: 0.25-0.5 inch deep                                    │
│    ├─ Allow media to wick moisture (10-15 min)                           │
│    └─ Remove when surface is moist                                       │
│                                                                          │
│  Alternative: Top misting                                                │
│    ├─ Fine mist spray                                                    │
│    ├─ Light coverage, don't displace seeds                               │
│    └─ Ensure even moisture                                               │
│                                                                          │
│  DURING BLACKOUT PERIOD                                                  │
│  ═════════════════════                                                   │
│  Daily check:                                                            │
│    ├─ Lift stack to check moisture                                       │
│    ├─ Media should remain moist, not wet                                 │
│    ├─ Bottom water if drying                                             │
│    └─ Avoid over-watering (causes mold)                                  │
│                                                                          │
│  MOISTURE INDICATORS                                                     │
│  ══════════════════                                                      │
│  Perfect: Media is dark, moist to touch, no puddles                      │
│  Too dry: Seeds not germinating, media light colored                     │
│  Too wet: Standing water, moldy smell, seed rot                          │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Germination Troubleshooting

### Common Problems and Solutions

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                     GERMINATION PROBLEM SOLVER                             ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  PROBLEM: Poor germination rate (<80%)                                     ║
║  ════════════════════════════════════                                     ║
║  Causes:                                                                   ║
║  • Old or poor-quality seeds → Test batch, change supplier                 ║
║  • Wrong temperature (too hot/cold) → Check temp, adjust                   ║
║  • Seed dried out → Maintain moisture during blackout                      ║
║  • Seed buried too deep → Surface sow or light cover only                  ║
║  • Soaking issues (if applicable) → Review soak protocol                   ║
║                                                                            ║
║  PROBLEM: Mold growth during germination                                   ║
║  ═══════════════════════════════════                                      ║
║  Causes:                                                                   ║
║  • Over-watering → Reduce water, improve drainage                          ║
║  • Poor air circulation → Unstack daily, add ventilation                   ║
║  • Too warm/humid → Lower temperature, reduce humidity                     ║
║  • Contaminated seeds → Use quality seeds, rinse before use                ║
║  • Over-seeding density → Reduce seed rate                                 ║
║                                                                            ║
║  PROBLEM: Uneven germination                                               ║
║  ══════════════════════════                                               ║
║  Causes:                                                                   ║
║  • Uneven moisture → Level trays, even watering                            ║
║  • Temperature variation → Monitor temp, eliminate drafts                  ║
║  • Uneven seeding density → Practice distribution technique                ║
║  • Media not level → Press and level before seeding                        ║
║                                                                            ║
║  PROBLEM: Weak, stretched stems (etiolation)                               ║
║  ══════════════════════════════════════════                               ║
║  Causes:                                                                   ║
║  • Blackout period too long → Remove earlier                               ║
║  • Insufficient weight → Add more weight (sunflower, peas)                 ║
║  • Over-seeding → Reduce density                                           ║
║                                                                            ║
║  PROBLEM: Seeds not shedding hulls (sunflower)                             ║
║  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════                          ║
║  Causes:                                                                   ║
║  • Inadequate soaking → Extend soak to 12 hours                            ║
║  • Not enough weight/pressure → Increase weight                            ║
║  • Low humidity → Mist lightly, maintain moisture                          ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Seeding density is critical** - follow recommended rates (3-4 oz sunflower, 0.5-1 oz broccoli per 10×20 tray)

2. **Pre-soak large seeds** - sunflower, peas, cilantro need 8-12 hours soaking

3. **Blackout period strengthens plants** - 2-5 days darkness with weight for larger seeds

4. **Moisture balance is essential** - keep moist but not wet during germination

5. **Consistency comes with practice** - document your processes and refine based on results
