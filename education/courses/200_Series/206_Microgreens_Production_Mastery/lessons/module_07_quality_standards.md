## Pest and Disease Management

### Prevention First Approach

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                      INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM)                      ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  LEVEL 1: EXCLUSION (Primary Defense)                                     ║
║  ═════════════════════════════════                                        ║
║  • Screen windows and vents (prevent entry)                                ║
║  • Positive air pressure in growing area                                   ║
║  • Clean room protocols                                                    ║
║  • Quarantine new materials                                                ║
║  • Sealed building envelope                                                ║
║                                                                            ║
║  LEVEL 2: SANITATION (Remove Habitat)                                     ║
║  ════════════════════════════════════                                     ║
║  • Daily cleaning of work areas                                            ║
║  • Remove plant debris immediately                                         ║
║  • Sanitize tools and equipment                                            ║
║  • Proper waste management                                                 ║
║  • Clean storage areas                                                     ║
║                                                                            ║
║  LEVEL 3: ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL                                            ║
║  ══════════════════════════════                                           ║
║  • Optimal temperature (not pest-friendly)                                 ║
║  • Proper humidity (40-60% RH)                                             ║
║  • Good air circulation                                                    ║
║  • Appropriate lighting                                                    ║
║                                                                            ║
║  LEVEL 4: MONITORING & EARLY DETECTION                                     ║
║  ═════════════════════════════════════                                    ║
║  • Daily visual inspection (10% of trays)                                  ║
║  • Yellow sticky traps for monitoring                                      ║
║  • Document and track issues                                               ║
║  • Respond immediately to problems                                         ║
║                                                                            ║
║  LEVEL 5: INTERVENTION (Last Resort)                                       ║
║  ═══════════════════════════════════                                      ║
║  • Removal of affected trays                                               ║
║  • Biological controls (if applicable)                                     ║
║  • Organic pesticides (rarely needed)                                      ║
║  • Never use synthetic chemicals on food crops                             ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Common Problems and Solutions

**MOLD AND FUNGAL DISEASES**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        MOLD MANAGEMENT                                   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  WHITE MOLD (Pythium, Fusarium)                                          │
│  ══════════════════════════════                                          │
│  Appearance: White, fuzzy growth on stems/media                          │
│                                                                          │
│  Prevention:                                                             │

│  • Improve air circulation                                               │
│  • Avoid over-watering                                                   │
│  • Maintain humidity <60%                                                │
│  • Use clean seeds and media                                             │
│                                                                          │
│  Treatment:                                                              │
│  • Remove affected trays immediately                                     │
│  • Increase airflow in area                                              │
│  • Review and adjust protocols                                           │
│  • Don't salvage - compost affected trays                                │
│                                                                          │
│  POWDERY MILDEW                                                          │
│  ══════════════                                                          │
│  Appearance: White powdery coating on leaves                             │
│  Common on: Peas, sunflower (late in cycle)                              │
│                                                                          │
│  Prevention:                                                             │
│  • Harvest before it appears (stay on schedule)                          │
│  • Good air movement                                                     │
│  • Don't overhead water late in day                                      │
│  • Space trays appropriately                                             │
│                                                                          │
│  Treatment:                                                              │
│  • Harvest immediately if close to ready                                 │
│  • Otherwise discard tray                                                │
│  • Clean and sanitize growing area                                       │
│                                                                          │
│  DAMPING OFF                                                             │
│  ═══════════                                                             │
│  Appearance: Seedlings collapse, brown water-soaked stems                │
│                                                                          │
│  Prevention:                                                             │
│  • Use sterile media                                                     │
│  • Don't over-water                                                      │
│  • Ensure good drainage                                                  │
│  • Proper air circulation                                                │
│  • Don't bury seeds too deep                                             │
│                                                                          │
│  Treatment:                                                              │
│  • No cure - remove affected trays                                       │
│  • Review water and sanitation practices                                 │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**INSECT PESTS**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       PEST IDENTIFICATION & CONTROL                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  FUNGUS GNATS                                                            │
│  ════════════                                                            │
│  ID: Small black flies, larvae in media                                  │
│  Damage: Eat roots, cosmetic nuisance                                    │
│                                                                          │
│  Control:                                                                │
│  • Allow media surface to dry between watings                            │
│  • Bottom water only (keeps surface dry)                                 │
│  • Yellow sticky traps capture adults                                    │
│  • Beneficial nematodes in media (if severe)                             │
│  • Remove any standing water                                             │
│                                                                          │
│  APHIDS                                                                  │
│  ══════                                                                  │
│  ID: Tiny green, white, or black soft-bodied insects                     │
│  Damage: Suck plant sap, transmit disease                                │
│                                                                          │
│  Control:                                                                │
│  • Very rare in microgreens (short cycle)                                │
│  • Remove affected trays if found                                        │
│  • Check for entry points                                                │
│  • Insecticidal soap (if needed, early in cycle only)                    │
│                                                                          │
│  THRIPS                                                                  │
│  ══════                                                                  │
│  ID: Tiny slender insects, cause silvery streaks                         │
│  Damage: Feeding damage on leaves                                        │
│                                                                          │
│  Control:                                                                │
│  • Rare in clean indoor environments                                     │
│  • Blue sticky traps for monitoring                                      │
│  • Remove affected trays                                                 │
│  • Seal entry points                                                     │
│                                                                          │
│  SHORE FLIES                                                             │
│  ══════════                                                              │
│  ID: Small flies, similar to fungus gnats but stockier                   │
│  Damage: Minimal but can spread disease                                  │
│                                                                          │
│  Control:                                                                │
│  • Same as fungus gnats                                                  │
│  • Strict sanitation                                                     │
│  • Remove algae (they feed on it)                                        │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Monitoring Protocols

```
DAILY PEST & DISEASE SCOUTING

INSPECT 10% OF TRAYS DAILY
Rotate which trays you check each day

For each inspected tray, examine:

1. CANOPY (Top view)
   □ Overall color and vigor
   □ Any yellowing or discoloration
   □ Signs of insect damage
   □ Mold or fungal growth

2. STEMS (Part canopy, look at base)
   □ Firm and upright
   □ No rotting or water-soaking
   □ No lesions or discoloration

3. MEDIA SURFACE
   □ Moisture level appropriate
   □ No mold or algae growth
   □ No insect larvae visible
   □ Roots healthy (white, not brown)

4. FLYING INSECTS
   □ Check sticky traps
   □ Note any flying pests
   □ Count and identify

DOCUMENTATION:
Log findings daily
Track trends over time
Respond to issues immediately
```

### Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP)

```
SIMPLIFIED HACCP FOR MICROGREENS

CRITICAL CONTROL POINTS:

1. SEED SOURCING
   Hazard: Pathogen contamination
   Control: Use only tested, food-safe seeds
   Monitor: Supplier certificates, test results
   Corrective: Reject uncertified seeds

2. WATER QUALITY
   Hazard: Microbial contamination
   Control: Potable water only
   Monitor: Annual testing minimum
   Corrective: Treatment or source change

3. HAND WASHING
   Hazard: Cross-contamination from workers
   Control: Hand washing before food contact
   Monitor: Supervisor checks, hand wash logs
   Corrective: Retraining, policy enforcement

4. TEMPERATURE CONTROL
   Hazard: Bacterial growth on product
   Control: Cool to <40°F within 1 hour
   Monitor: Temperature logs
   Corrective: Adjust cooling, discard if >2 hrs

5. CROSS-CONTAMINATION
   Hazard: Pathogen transfer
   Control: Cleaning and sanitizing protocols
   Monitor: Sanitation logs, visual inspection
   Corrective: Re-clean, improve procedures
```
