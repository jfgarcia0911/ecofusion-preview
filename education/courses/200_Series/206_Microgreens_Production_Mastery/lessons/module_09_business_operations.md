## Pricing Strategy

### Cost-Plus Pricing Method

```
PRICING CALCULATION WORKSHEET

STEP 1: Calculate Cost per Tray
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Direct Costs (10×20 tray):
Seeds (variety-specific)          $_____ (e.g., $0.98 sunflower)
Growing media                      $_____ (e.g., $0.50)
Packaging container                $_____ (e.g., $0.40)
Label                              $_____ (e.g., $0.10)
Utilities (lights, water, heat)    $_____ (e.g., $0.25)
                                   ──────
TOTAL DIRECT COST per tray       = $_____ (Example: $2.23)

STEP 2: Add Overhead Allocation
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Monthly overhead costs:
Rent/facility                      $_____
Labor (if hired)                   $_____
Insurance                          $_____
Marketing                          $_____
Depreciation/equipment             $_____
Admin/misc                         $_____
                                   ──────
TOTAL MONTHLY OVERHEAD           = $_____

Trays produced per month:          _____ trays

Overhead per tray = Monthly Overhead ÷ Trays Produced
                  = $_____ per tray (e.g., $1.12)

STEP 3: Calculate Total Cost
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Direct Cost per tray:              $_____
Overhead per tray:                 $_____
                                   ──────
TOTAL COST per tray              = $_____ (e.g., $3.35)

Average yield per tray:            _____ oz (e.g., 10 oz)
Cost per ounce = Total Cost ÷ Yield
               = $_____ per oz (e.g., $0.34/oz)

Cost per pound = Cost per oz × 16
               = $_____ per lb (e.g., $5.44/lb)

STEP 4: Set Price with Target Margin
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Target gross margin: _____% (common range: 40-60%)

WHOLESALE PRICE = Cost per lb ÷ (1 - Target Margin)
                = $5.44 ÷ (1 - 0.50) = $10.88/lb

Round to market rate: $28-32/lb (market comparison)

Actual margin achieved = (Price - Cost) ÷ Price
                       = ($30 - $5.44) ÷ $30 = 81.8%

RETAIL PRICE (if applicable):
Wholesale × 1.5-2.0 = Retail
$30 × 1.8 = $54/lb retail (or $3.38 per oz)
```

### When to Scale Checklist

```
READY TO SCALE? ASSESSMENT

Before adding capacity, verify:

DEMAND VALIDATED:
□ Consistent orders from existing customers (>90% fill rate)
□ Turning away new customers due to capacity
□ Waiting list or backlog developing
□ Revenue has been steady >3 months

OPERATIONS STABLE:
□ Production yields consistent and predictable
□ Quality standards reliably met
□ Systems documented and repeatable
□ Problem-solving is routine, not crisis

FINANCIAL HEALTH:
□ Profitable at current scale
□ Positive cash flow
□ Capital available for expansion
□ Projected ROI calculated and acceptable

CAPACITY EXISTS:
□ Labor available (you or employees)
□ Physical space for additional racks
□ Utilities can handle increased load
□ Storage/cooler space adequate

If all checkboxes marked: READY TO SCALE
If <75% marked: Continue optimizing current scale
```
