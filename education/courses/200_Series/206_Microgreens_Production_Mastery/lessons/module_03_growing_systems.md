### Recommended Media by Variety

| Variety | Best Media | Alternative | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------|
| Sunflower | Potting mix (1" depth) | Coco mats | Needs good drainage |
| Pea Shoots | Potting mix (1.5" depth) | Coco mats | Heavy roots, deeper media |
| Radish | Coco mats, potting mix (0.5") | Grow pads | Light feeders |
| Broccoli | Coco mats, fine potting mix | Grow pads | Tiny seeds, smooth surface |
| Arugula | Coco mats preferred | Fine potting mix | Clean harvest important |
| Basil | Potting mix (1" depth) | Coco + nutrients | Longer cycle, needs nutrients |
| Wheatgrass | Potting mix (0.75" depth) | Coco mats | Moderate moisture needs |

### Lighting Installation

```
LIGHTING SETUP BEST PRACTICES

Mounting:
• Attach lights to underside of shelf above
• Height: 12-16 inches above tray surface
• Secure firmly to prevent accidents
• Use adjustable chains/wires if possible

Coverage:
• Overlap light coverage 10-20%
• Ensure uniform distribution
• Avoid hot spots or dark corners
• Use light meter to verify intensity

Electrical:
• Dedicated circuits for safety
• GFCI protection in wet areas
• Timers for automation (required)
• Label circuits clearly
• Consider backup power options
```

## Environmental Control

### Temperature Management

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                    TEMPERATURE REQUIREMENTS                                ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  GERMINATION PHASE                                                         ║
║  ═══════════════════                                                       ║
║  Target: 68-75°F (20-24°C)                                                 ║
║  Critical: Avoid >80°F (stunted growth, disease)                           ║
║  Critical: Avoid <60°F (slow germination)                                  ║
║                                                                            ║
║  GROWING PHASE                                                             ║
║  ══════════════                                                            ║
║  Target: 65-72°F (18-22°C)                                                 ║
║  Ideal: Slight night drop (5-10°F cooler)                                  ║
║  Max: <78°F (higher = leggy, disease prone)                                ║
║  Min: >55°F (lower = slow growth)                                          ║
║                                                                            ║
║  TEMPERATURE CONTROL STRATEGIES                                            ║
║  ═════════════════════════════                                            ║
║  • Locate in climate-controlled building                                   ║
║  • Insulate growing room if needed                                         ║
║  • Use fans to distribute heat/cooling                                     ║
║  • LED lights minimize heat addition                                       ║
║  • Monitor with min/max thermometers                                       ║
║  • Consider portable AC/heat if needed                                     ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Humidity and Air Circulation

**Relative Humidity Targets:**
- Germination: 70-80% RH (high moisture)
- Growing: 50-60% RH (moderate)
- Pre-harvest: 40-50% RH (reduces disease)

**Air Movement:**
```
Circulation fans are essential:
• Prevents stagnant air and disease
• Strengthens stems (prevents lodging)
• Distributes temperature evenly
• Reduces excess humidity

Setup:
• Oscillating fans in growing area
• Low speed, continuous operation
• 1-2 fans per 100 sq ft of growing area
• Position for gentle movement, not direct blast
• Clean fans regularly to prevent dust
```

## Production Capacity Planning

### Calculating Growing Space

```
PRODUCTION CAPACITY CALCULATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: Determine rack capacity
────────────────────────────────
Example: 18"×48" rack, 5 shelves
Trays per shelf: 2×4 = 8 trays (10×20 size)
Total per rack: 8 × 5 = 40 trays

Step 2: Calculate production cycles
────────────────────────────────────
Average crop cycle: 12 days
Cycles per year: 365 ÷ 12 = 30.4 turns

Step 3: Annual capacity per rack
─────────────────────────────────
40 trays × 30.4 turns = 1,216 trays/year per rack

Step 4: Convert to production volume
─────────────────────────────────────
Average yield: 10 oz per tray
Annual production: 1,216 trays × 10 oz = 12,160 oz
Convert: 12,160 ÷ 16 = 760 lbs per year per rack

Step 5: Revenue projection
──────────────────────────
Wholesale price: $30/lb
Annual revenue per rack: 760 × $30 = $22,800

Note: Adjust for variety mix, actual yields, efficiency
Realistic efficiency: 80-85% of theoretical capacity
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Start simple with proven systems** - wire shelving and LED shop lights work well for beginners

2. **Vertical space is your most valuable asset** - 5-shelf racks maximize production per sq ft

3. **Media choice affects profitability** - balance cost, labor, and harvest quality

4. **Lighting is critical** - 200-400 μmol/m²/s for 12-16 hours daily

5. **Plan capacity realistically** - one 18"×48" rack produces ~760 lbs/year at 80% efficiency
