### Quality Assessment Pre-Harvest

**The 5-Point Quality Check:**

```
Before harvesting any tray, verify:

1. APPEARANCE
   □ Uniform height and color
   □ No yellowing or discoloration
   □ No visible mold or disease
   □ Clean, fresh appearance

2. STRUCTURE
   □ Strong, upright stems
   □ No lodging (falling over)
   □ Appropriate density (not too crowded)
   □ Good root development

3. MATURITY
   □ Correct stage for variety
   □ True leaves present or emerging
   □ Not overgrown or leggy

4. CLEANLINESS
   □ No soil splash on leaves
   □ Free from debris
   □ No pest presence

5. TIMING
   □ Within optimal harvest window
   □ Scheduled for customer delivery/use
   □ Adequate time for cooling/packaging

Pass all 5 checks = Ready to harvest
Fail any check = Delay or reject tray
```

### Harvest Methods

**Method 1: Cut and Hold (Most Common)**

```
STEP-BY-STEP HARVEST PROCEDURE

1. PREPARE WORKSPACE
   ├─ Sanitize work surface
   ├─ Wash hands thoroughly
   ├─ Set out collection containers
   └─ Have cutting tool ready

2. PRE-HARVEST MISTING (Optional)
   ├─ Light mist on canopy
   ├─ Helps clean and crisp up
   └─ Allow 10-15 min to dry slightly

3. REMOVE TRAY FROM RACK
   └─ Bring to harvest station

4. CUTTING TECHNIQUE
   ├─ Gather handful of microgreens gently
   ├─ Cut 0.25-0.5" above soil line
   ├─ Use smooth, swift motion
   ├─ Hold stems firmly but gently
   └─ Place in collection container

5. SYSTEMATIC COVERAGE
   ├─ Work across tray methodically
   ├─ Don't skip areas
   ├─ Maintain consistent cut height
   └─ Avoid pulling or tearing

6. POST-HARVEST INSPECTION
   ├─ Remove any soil, hulls, or debris
   ├─ Discard discolored or damaged pieces
   └─ Ensure clean, uniform product
```

**Method 2: Living Tray Sales**

```
For farmers market or specialty sales:

1. NO CUTTING REQUIRED
   └─ Sell entire tray with roots intact

2. TRAY PREPARATION
   ├─ Trim edges if uneven
   ├─ Remove any dead/damaged plants
   ├─ Wipe tray exterior clean
   └─ Make visually appealing

3. PACKAGING
   ├─ Place in protective bag or container
   ├─ Include growing/care instructions
   └─ Label with variety and date

Advantages:
• Ultimate freshness (still growing)
• Premium pricing ($8-15 per tray)
• Minimal harvest labor
• Extends shelf life

Disadvantages:
• Limited market (home consumers mainly)
• Requires customer education
• Tray cost built into price
```

### Washing Protocols

**To Wash or Not to Wash?**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        WASHING DECISION MATRIX                           │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  DO NOT WASH (Preferred for most situations)                             │
│  ═══════════════════════════════════════════                            │
│  When:                                                                   │
│  • Grown on clean media (coco mats, grow pads)                           │
│  • No soil splash on leaves                                              │
│  • Clean production environment                                          │
│  • Direct restaurant sales (they may wash)                               │
│                                                                          │
│  Benefits:                                                               │
│  • Extended shelf life (no added moisture)                               │
│  • Less labor and time                                                   │
│  • Reduced water/waste                                                   │
│  • Maintains crispness better                                            │
│                                                                          │
│  WASH WHEN NECESSARY                                                     │
│  ══════════════════════                                                  │
│  When:                                                                   │
│  • Visible soil or debris on product                                     │
│  • Retail packaging requires it                                          │
│  • Customer specifications require washing                               │
│  • Seed hulls still attached (sunflower)                                 │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Washing Procedure (When Required):**

```
TRIPLE WASH METHOD

1. FIRST WASH
   ├─ Fill clean container with cold water
   ├─ Gently submerge microgreens
   ├─ Swish gently to dislodge debris
   ├─ Remove and drain (don't pour through greens)
   └─ Discard wash water

2. SECOND WASH
   ├─ Fresh cold water
   ├─ Optional: Add food-safe sanitizer
   ├─ Gentle agitation
   └─ Remove and drain

3. RINSE (if sanitizer used)
   ├─ Clean cold water
   ├─ Brief rinse
   └─ Remove to drain

4. DRYING (Critical)
   ├─ Salad spinner (gentle spin)
   ├─ Or spread on clean towels
   ├─ Pat gently to remove excess water
   ├─ Allow 10-15 min to air dry
   └─ Must be DRY before packaging

NOTE: Wet microgreens = rapid spoilage
```

## Cooling and Storage

### Importance of Rapid Cooling

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                      TEMPERATURE AND SHELF LIFE                            ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                            ║
║  Temperature vs. Shelf Life Relationship:                                  ║
║                                                                            ║
║  32-36°F (Optimal)          → 7-14 days shelf life                         ║
║  37-40°F (Good)             → 5-7 days shelf life                          ║
║  41-45°F (Marginal)         → 3-5 days shelf life                          ║
║  46-50°F (Poor)             → 1-3 days shelf life                          ║
║  >50°F (Unacceptable)       → <1 day shelf life                            ║
║                                                                            ║
║  THE 1-HOUR RULE                                                           ║
║  ═══════════════                                                           ║
║  Time from harvest to refrigeration should be <1 hour                      ║
║  • Every extra hour at room temp reduces shelf life                        ║
║  • Respiration continues, using energy                                     ║
║  • Quality degradation accelerates                                         ║
║                                                                            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### Cooling Methods

**Small-Scale:**
```
FORCED-AIR COOLING (DIY)

1. Place harvested greens in perforated containers
2. Set in refrigerator
3. Spread out for air circulation
4. Cool to 34-38°F within 30-60 min
5. Transfer to storage containers

Works for: <20 lbs per batch
```

**Commercial-Scale:**
```
REFRIGERATED COLD ROOM

Dedicated walk-in cooler:
• Temperature: 34-36°F
• Humidity: 95-98% RH
• Forced air circulation
• Immediate cooling post-harvest
• Staging area for packaging

Investment: $3,000-10,000+
Required for: >50 lbs/week production
```

### Storage Best Practices

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    OPTIMAL STORAGE CONDITIONS                            │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                          │
│  TEMPERATURE: 34-38°F (1-3°C)                                            │
│  HUMIDITY: 95-98% RH                                                     │
│  AIRFLOW: Moderate (prevents moisture buildup)                           │
│  LIGHT: Dark (slows deterioration)                                       │
│                                                                          │
│  CONTAINER SELECTION                                                     │
│  ═══════════════════                                                     │
│  Best options:                                                           │
│  • Breathable containers (allow gas exchange)                            │
│  • Modified atmosphere packaging                                         │
│  • Perforated bags or clamshells                                         │
│  • Food-grade plastic containers                                         │
│                                                                          │
│  Avoid:                                                                  │
│  • Sealed airtight (causes condensation)                                 │
│  • Thin bags (inadequate protection)                                     │
│  • Metal containers (can react)                                          │
│                                                                          │
│  STACKING AND ORGANIZATION                                               │
│  ═══════════════════════════                                            │
│  • Stack no more than 3-4 containers high                                │
│  • Allow air circulation between stacks                                  │
│  • FIFO rotation (First In, First Out)                                   │
│  • Label all containers with variety and date                            │
│  • Separate varieties to prevent cross-flavoring                         │
│                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Quality Grading

**Grade Standards:**

```
GRADE A (Premium)
• Vibrant color, uniform
• Strong stems, no lodging
• No yellowing or discoloration
• Clean, no debris
• First true leaves perfect stage
• 10-14 day shelf life potential
Pricing: Full retail/premium

GRADE B (Standard)
• Good color, mostly uniform
• Mostly upright, some variation
• Minimal yellowing
• Clean with minor imperfections
• 7-10 day shelf life
Pricing: Standard wholesale

GRADE C (Acceptable)
• Adequate color
• Some lodging or weakness
• Some yellowing or irregularity
• 5-7 day shelf life
Pricing: Discounted

REJECT (Compost)
• Severe discoloration
• Mold or disease present
• Poor structure
• <5 day shelf life
• Don't sell - damages reputation
```

## Module Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Timing is critical** - harvest within 2-4 day optimal window for peak quality

2. **Food safety is non-negotiable** - proper hygiene, sanitation, and temperature control

3. **Cool immediately** - bring to 34-38°F within 1 hour of harvest for maximum shelf life

4. **Handle gently** - microgreens are delicate; rough handling damages product

5. **Package professionally** - proper containers and labeling reflect quality operation
