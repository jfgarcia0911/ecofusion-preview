# Activity 3: Yield Optimization Experiment
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

---

## Objective

Design and conduct a controlled experiment to test how different growing conditions affect microgreens yield, developing practical skills in optimization and data-driven decision making for commercial production.

---

## Overview

- **Duration:** 10-16 days (full production cycle)
- **Variety:** 1 variety tested across multiple conditions
- **Treatments:** 3-4 different growing conditions
- **Replicates:** 2-3 trays per treatment
- **Deliverables:** Complete data set, yield analysis, and optimization recommendations

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, you will be able to:
1. Design a controlled optimization experiment
2. Test variables that affect microgreens yield
3. Accurately measure and compare fresh weight yields
4. Analyze cost-benefit of different growing strategies
5. Make evidence-based production decisions
6. Optimize growing protocols for maximum profitability

---

## Materials Needed

**Equipment:**
- Growing trays (10x20): 6-12 total
- Growing media (sufficient for all trays)
- Seeds (single variety, sufficient quantity)
- Scale (digital, accurate to 0.1 oz)
- Measuring tools (ruler, measuring cups)
- Light meter (optional but helpful)
- Thermometer and hygrometer
- Camera for documentation

**Documentation:**
- Data collection sheets (provided below)
- Notebook for observations
- Spreadsheet for calculations (optional)

### Statistical Analysis

**Determine if differences are meaningful:**

1. **Calculate percentage differences:**
   ```
   Treatment 1 vs Treatment 2:
   (T2 - T1) ÷ T1 × 100 = _____% difference

   Treatment 2 vs Treatment 3:
   (T3 - T2) ÷ T2 × 100 = _____% difference

   Treatment 3 vs Treatment 4:
   (T4 - T3) ÷ T3 × 100 = _____% difference
   ```

2. **Assess variability:**
   - Low CV (<10%): Very consistent results
   - Medium CV (10-20%): Acceptable variability
   - High CV (>20%): High variability, less reliable

## Tips for Success

**Experimental Control:**
- Change ONLY one variable
- Keep everything else identical
- Use proper replication (minimum 2 per treatment)

**Measurement Precision:**
- Weigh seeds accurately
- Tare scale properly
- Measure at same time of day

**Documentation:**
- Write everything down immediately
- Take photos at same times/angles
- Note any deviations from protocol

**Harvest Consistency:**
- Use same cutting technique
- Harvest at same time of day
- Handle all samples equally
