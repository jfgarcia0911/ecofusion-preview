# Activity 2: Market Research Project
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

---

## Objective
Conduct thorough market research in your local area to validate demand, understand competition, and identify target customers for a microgreens business.

---

## Overview
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **Deliverable:** Comprehensive market research report (5-8 pages)
- **Points:** 100

---

## Research Components

### 1. Market Demographics (15 points)

**Research your local market area:**
- Population and demographics
- Average household income
- Food culture and preferences
- Local/sustainable food awareness
- Restaurant density and types

**Deliverable:** 1-2 page summary with data sources cited

---

### 2. Competitive Analysis (25 points)

**Identify and analyze competitors:**
- Find 5-10 microgreens/local greens producers
- Document their offerings, pricing, sales channels
- Assess their strengths and weaknesses
- Identify market gaps or opportunities

**Create competitive analysis matrix:**

| Competitor | Products | Price Range | Sales Channels | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|----------|-------------|----------------|-----------|------------|
| 1. | | | | | |
| 2. | | | | | |

**Deliverable:** Competitive analysis section (2-3 pages)

---

### 3. Customer Discovery (30 points)

**Interview potential customers (minimum 10):**
- 5 restaurants/chefs
- 3 retail buyers (grocery, co-ops)
- 2 direct consumers (farmers market visitors)

**Questions to ask:**
- Do they currently buy microgreens? From whom?
- What varieties do they use/want?
- What quantities do they need weekly?
- What price points are acceptable?
- What quality standards do they expect?
- How important is local sourcing?
- What would make them switch suppliers?

**Deliverable:** Interview summary with key findings (2-3 pages)

---

### 4. Pricing Research (15 points)

**Collect current market pricing:**
- Visit 3-5 farmers markets
- Call/visit 3-5 grocery stores
- Check online delivery services
- Request quotes as potential buyer from competitors

**Document:**
| Product | Source | Price | Package Size | Price per lb |
|---------|--------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| | | | | |

**Deliverable:** Pricing analysis (1 page)

---

### 5. Demand Validation (15 points)

**Estimate potential demand:**
- Total target customers identified
- Average order size (lbs/week)
- Total market potential (lbs/week)
- Your realistic market share (10-20% year 1)
- Revenue potential calculation

**Example:**
```
Target: 20 restaurants
Avg order: 3 lbs/week each
Total potential: 60 lbs/week
Your target (25%): 15 lbs/week
Revenue @ $30/lb: $450/week = $1,800/month
```

**Deliverable:** Demand projection (1 page)

---

## Final Report Structure

**Executive Summary** (0.5 page)
- Key findings summary
- Market opportunity assessment
- Recommendation: Proceed / Don't proceed

**Market Overview** (1-2 pages)
- Demographics
- Food culture
- Local food trends

**Competitive Landscape** (2-3 pages)
- Competitor analysis
- Market positioning opportunities
- Differentiation strategy

**Customer Insights** (2-3 pages)
- Interview findings
- Customer needs and preferences
- Pricing sensitivity

**Opportunity Analysis** (1 page)
- Market size and potential
- Target customer segments
- Revenue projections

**Conclusion and Recommendations** (0.5 page)
- Go/no-go decision
- Next steps if proceeding
