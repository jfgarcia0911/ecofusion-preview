# Activity 1: Production Trial Exercise
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

---

## Objective

Conduct a hands-on microgreens production trial from seed to harvest, documenting the complete process and analyzing results.

---

## Overview

- **Duration:** 10-14 days (one complete production cycle)
- **Varieties:** Minimum 2 (recommended: Sunflower and Radish)
- **Scale:** 2-4 trays per variety
- **Deliverables:** Completed production log and analysis report

---

## Materials Needed

**Equipment:**
- Growing trays (10×20): 4-8 total
- Growing media (soil or coco mats)
- Seeds (sunflower, radish, or your choice)
- Light source (natural or LED)
- Watering equipment
- Thermometer and hygrometer
- Scale for weighing harvest

**Documentation:**
- Production log template (provided below)
- Camera or phone for photos
- Notebook for observations

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Phase 1: Planning and Setup (Day 0)

**Tasks:**
1. Select 2 varieties to trial
2. Gather all materials and equipment
3. Set up growing space with appropriate lighting
4. Create production log sheet
5. Take "before" photos of setup

**Document:**
- Variety selections and reasoning
- Seed source and lot numbers
- Setup location and conditions
- Initial environmental readings

---

### Phase 2: Seeding (Day 0)

**Tasks:**
1. Prepare growing media to appropriate depth
2. Pre-soak seeds if required (sunflower: yes, radish: no)
3. Seed trays at recommended density:
   - Sunflower: 3-4 oz per 10×20 tray
   - Radish: 1-1.5 oz per 10×20 tray
4. Water thoroughly using bottom watering method
5. Label each tray with variety and date
6. Set up blackout (stack with weight for sunflower)

**Document:**
- Exact seed amount used per tray
- Seeding technique used
- Media type and depth
- Initial moisture level
- Photos of seeded trays

---

### Phase 3: Germination/Blackout (Days 1-4)

**Daily Tasks:**
1. Check moisture level (lift trays to assess)
2. Water if needed (bottom watering)
3. Monitor for germination progress
4. Take daily notes and photos

**Document:**
- Daily moisture checks (dry/moist/wet)
- Date when germination first observed
- Percentage of seeds germinated (estimate)
- Any issues or concerns

**Removal from Blackout:**
- When: Cotyledons emerging, shells lifting (sunflower), or day 2-3 (radish)
- Process: Remove weight, unstack, move to light gradually

---

### Phase 4: Growing (Days 4-10)

**Daily Tasks:**
1. Check environmental conditions (temp, humidity, light)
2. Water as needed (bottom watering preferred)
3. Observe growth rate and health
4. Scout for pests or disease
5. Rotate trays if light is uneven
6. Take photos every 2-3 days

**Document:**
- Daily temperature and humidity readings
- Watering frequency and method
- Height measurements every 2 days
- Color and vigor observations
- Any problems encountered
- Light duration and intensity (if measurable)

---

### Phase 5: Harvest (Days 10-14)

**Tasks:**
1. Assess harvest readiness:
   - First true leaves present or emerging
   - Appropriate height reached
   - Vibrant color
   - Strong stems
2. Harvest using clean scissors or knife
3. Cut 0.25-0.5" above soil line
4. Weigh fresh harvest
5. Inspect quality
6. Take final photos

**Document:**
- Exact harvest date (days from seeding)
- Fresh weight per tray
- Visual quality assessment
- Any defects or issues
- Photos of harvested product
