## Multiple Choice Questions

**1. What is the primary botanical characteristic that defines microgreens?**
- A) Harvested after full maturity
- B) Harvested after cotyledon emergence with first true leaves present or emerging
- C) Whole plant consumed including roots
- D) Grown only in darkness

**2. What is the typical age range for harvesting microgreens?**
- A) 2-7 days
- B) 7-21 days
- C) 21-35 days
- D) 40-70 days

**3. Which statement BEST differentiates microgreens from sprouts?**
- A) Microgreens are grown in soil/media and require light; sprouts are grown in water only
- B) Microgreens are cheaper to produce than sprouts
- C) Sprouts have higher food safety standards than microgreens
- D) Microgreens and sprouts are essentially the same product

**4. What is the typical wholesale price range for microgreens?**
- A) $8-15/lb
- B) $15-22/lb
- C) $25-50/lb
- D) $60-80/lb

**5. For a beginner operation producing 40 trays per week, what is a realistic weekly revenue potential at $30/lb wholesale?**
- A) $300-500
- B) $600-800
- C) $900-1,200
- D) $1,500-2,000

**6. What is the minimum recommended startup investment for a basic microgreens operation?**
- A) $500-1,000
- B) $2,000-5,000
- C) $10,000-15,000
- D) $25,000-50,000

**7. Which market segment typically offers the HIGHEST margins for microgreens?**
- A) Wholesale distribution
- B) Restaurant sales
- C) Farmers markets/direct to consumer
- D) Retail grocery stores

**8. What is a common FAILURE pattern for microgreens operations?**
- A) Starting with proven crop varieties
- B) Growing too many varieties without mastering basics
- C) Securing customers before building capacity
- D) Investing in quality seeds

**9. How much growing space is needed for a starter microgreens operation?**
- A) 10-25 sq ft
- B) 50-100 sq ft
- C) 200-300 sq ft
- D) 500+ sq ft

**10. What is the typical production cycle (seed to harvest) for fast-growing varieties like radish?**
- A) 3-5 days
- B) 7-10 days
- C) 14-18 days
- D) 21-28 days

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Harvested after cotyledon emergence with first true leaves present or emerging
2. **B** - 7-21 days
3. **A** - Microgreens are grown in soil/media and require light; sprouts are grown in water only
4. **C** - $25-50/lb
5. **C** - $900-1,200 (40 trays × 12 oz avg ÷ 16 = 30 lbs × $30 = $900)
6. **B** - $2,000-5,000
7. **C** - Farmers markets/direct to consumer
8. **B** - Growing too many varieties without mastering basics
9. **B** - 50-100 sq ft
10. **B** - 7-10 days
