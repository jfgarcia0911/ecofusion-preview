## Multiple Choice Questions

**1. What is the recommended germination rate target for microgreens seeds?**
- A) >70%
- B) >80%
- C) >90%
- D) 100%

**2. How much seed is typically needed for a 10×20 tray of sunflower microgreens?**
- A) 0.5-1 oz
- B) 1-2 oz
- C) 3-4 oz
- D) 6-8 oz

**3. Which variety typically has the HIGHEST yield per tray?**
- A) Broccoli
- B) Radish
- C) Arugula
- D) Sunflower

**4. What is the primary reason to source seeds from microgreens-specific suppliers rather than feed stores?**
- A) Feed store seeds are more expensive
- B) Feed store seeds may be chemically treated and not food-safe
- C) Microgreens suppliers have faster shipping
- D) Feed stores don't sell enough variety

**5. For a beginner, how many varieties should you start with?**
- A) 1-2 varieties
- B) 3-5 varieties
- C) 10-12 varieties
- D) 15+ varieties

**6. Which seed requires pre-soaking before planting?**
- A) Broccoli
- B) Arugula
- C) Sunflower (hulled)
- D) Mustard greens

**7. What is the optimal storage temperature for microgreens seeds?**
- A) 32-40°F (refrigeration)
- B) 40-50°F (cool room temp)
- C) 60-70°F (room temperature)
- D) 70-80°F (warm)

**8. When buying seeds in bulk (25-50 lb bags), what is the approximate cost savings compared to small packages?**
- A) 5-10%
- B) 15-25%
- C) 35-45%
- D) 60-70%

**9. How long do broccoli seeds typically maintain good germination rates when properly stored?**
- A) 3-6 months
- B) 1 year optimal, 2-3 years acceptable
- C) 5-7 years
- D) 10+ years

**10. Which variety typically commands the HIGHEST price per pound?**
- A) Sunflower
- B) Pea shoots
- C) Radish
- D) Basil

---

## Answer Key

1. **C** - >90%
2. **C** - 3-4 oz
3. **D** - Sunflower (10-12 oz per tray)
4. **B** - Feed store seeds may be chemically treated and not food-safe
5. **B** - 3-5 varieties
6. **C** - Sunflower (hulled)
7. **B** - 40-50°F (cool room temp) or refrigeration for extended storage
8. **C** - 35-45% (example: $8/lb vs $4.50/lb = 44% savings)
9. **B** - 1 year optimal, 2-3 years acceptable
10. **D** - Basil (longer cycle, premium pricing)
