**1. How long should sunflower seeds be soaked before planting?**
- A) No soaking needed | B) 2-4 hours | C) 8-12 hours | D) 24+ hours

**2. What is the purpose of the blackout period?**

**3. How many days of blackout do radish microgreens typically need?**
- A) No blackout | B) 2-3 days | C) 5-7 days | D) 10+ days

**4. What weight should be placed on stacked trays during blackout for sunflower?**

**5. What indicates it's time to remove trays from blackout?**

**6. What is the primary cause of mold during germination?**

**7. If germination rate is below 80%, what should you do?**
- A) Use seeds anyway | B) Increase seeding density or contact supplier | C) Add fertilizer | D) Extend blackout period

**8. What is the correct moisture level during germination?**
- A) Bone dry | B) Moist to touch, no standing water | C) Soaking wet | D) Waterlogged

**9. How often should stacked trays be checked during blackout?**
- A) Every hour | B) Daily | C) Every 3 days | D) Only at the end

**10. What causes weak, stretched stems during germination?**
- A) Too much water | B) Blackout too long, insufficient weight, over-seeding | C) Not enough seeds | D) Wrong media
