# Microgreens Production Glossary
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

---

## A

**Aeration** - The process of providing air circulation to plants and growing media to prevent disease and strengthen stems.

**Amaranth** - A microgreen variety with vibrant red/purple coloring and mild, earthy flavor; 12-16 day cycle.

**Arugula (Rocket)** - Fast-growing microgreen with peppery flavor; 8-10 day cycle; also called rocket.

---

## B

**Blackout Period** - The initial growth phase (2-5 days) when seeded trays are kept in complete darkness to promote root development and uniform germination.

**Bottom Watering** - Watering method where water is applied to a catch tray and wicks up through the media; preferred for microgreens to avoid leaf diseases.

**Brassicas** - Plant family including broccoli, kale, cabbage, mustard; popular microgreen varieties known for mild flavors and health benefits.

**Break-Even Point** - The production volume at which total revenue equals total costs; no profit or loss.

---

## C

**Canopy** - The upper leaf layer of microgreens growing in a tray; should be uniform in height and density.

**Clamshell** - Hinged plastic container commonly used for retail packaging of microgreens.

**Coco Coir (Coco Mats)** - Growing medium made from coconut fiber; popular for clean, professional microgreens production.

**Cold Chain** - Maintaining product at refrigeration temperature (34-38°F) continuously from harvest through delivery.

**Contribution Margin** - Selling price minus variable costs; used to calculate break-even point.

**Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** - Direct costs of producing microgreens including seeds, media, packaging, and utilities.

**Cost-Plus Pricing** - Pricing method: Price = Cost ÷ (1 - Target Margin).

**Cotyledons** - The first leaves to emerge from a seed; also called seed leaves; microgreens are harvested when cotyledons are fully expanded.

---

## D

**Damping Off** - Fungal disease causing seedlings to collapse at soil line; prevented through proper sanitation and moisture management.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)** - Total amount of light received over 24 hours; measured in mol/m²/day; target for microgreens: 10-15.

---

## E

**Etiolation** - Abnormal stem elongation (legginess) caused by insufficient light, often resulting in weak, pale plants.

---

## F

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** - Not applicable to microgreens; referenced term from aquaponics courses.

**FIFO (First In, First Out)** - Inventory rotation system ensuring oldest product is used/sold first to minimize waste.

**Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)** - US federal law establishing preventive food safety standards; may apply to larger microgreens operations.

**Fungus Gnats** - Small black flies whose larvae feed on organic matter and roots; controlled through surface drying and bottom watering.

---

## G

**GAP (Good Agricultural Practices)** - Food safety standards for agricultural production including water quality, worker hygiene, and traceability.

**Germination Rate** - Percentage of seeds that successfully sprout; target >90% for quality seeds.

**Gross Margin** - (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue; target for microgreens: >50%.

**Growing Media** - Material in which microgreens are grown; options include soil, coco coir, peat, and specialized grow mats.

---

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points)** - Food safety management system identifying and controlling potential hazards.

**Harvest Window** - The 2-4 day period when microgreens are at optimal maturity for harvest.

**Hulled Seeds** - Seeds with outer shells removed (e.g., sunflower); required for microgreens to prevent shell contamination.

**Humidity** - Moisture content in air; target for microgreens: 40-60% RH during growing, 70-80% during germination.

---

## I

**IPM (Integrated Pest Management)** - Holistic approach to pest control prioritizing prevention, monitoring, and least-toxic interventions.

---

## L

**LED (Light Emitting Diode)** - Energy-efficient lighting technology commonly used for microgreens production.

**Living Tray** - Microgreens sold still growing in the tray; harvested by customer for maximum freshness.

**Lodging** - Plants falling over or laying flat; caused by overwatering, weak stems, or overmature growth.

**Lot Number** - Unique identifier for each production batch enabling traceability.

---

## M

**Microgreens** - Young vegetable greens harvested 7-21 days after seeding when cotyledons are fully expanded and first true leaves emerge.

**Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP)** - Packaging with altered gas composition (reduced O₂, increased CO₂) to extend shelf life.

---

## N

**Net Margin** - Net Profit ÷ Revenue; measures profitability after all expenses; target: >15%.

**Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)** - Hydroponic method; not commonly used for microgreens but referenced in broader CEA context.

---

## O

**Overhead Costs** - Fixed expenses not directly tied to production volume (rent, insurance, equipment depreciation).

---

## P

**Photoperiod** - Hours of light per 24-hour period; typical for microgreens: 14-16 hours.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)** - Light intensity measurement in μmol/m²/s; target for microgreens: 200-400.

**Potable Water** - Water safe for human consumption; required for irrigating food crops.

**Powdery Mildew** - Fungal disease appearing as white powder on leaves; common on peas and sunflower if humidity is high.

**Pre-Soak** - Soaking large seeds (sunflower, peas, cilantro) in water 8-12 hours before seeding to improve germination.

---

## Q

**Quality Grading** - Classification system for microgreens (Grade A, B, C, Reject) based on appearance, uniformity, and condition.

---

## R

**Rack** - Shelving unit with multiple levels for growing microgreens vertically; typical: 18"×48" with 4-6 shelves.

**Radish Microgreens** - Fast-growing (7-10 days) microgreen with spicy flavor and purple/pink stems.

**Relative Humidity (RH)** - Percentage of moisture in air compared to maximum at that temperature.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** - Measure of profitability; (Gain - Cost) ÷ Cost.

---

## S

**Sanitizer** - Chemical solution (bleach, quaternary ammonia, etc.) used to reduce microbial load on surfaces and equipment.

**Seeding Density** - Amount of seed per tray; variety-specific (e.g., sunflower 3-4 oz, broccoli 0.5-1 oz per 10×20 tray).

**Seeding Rate** - See Seeding Density.

**Shelf Life** - Duration product maintains quality after harvest; microgreens: 5-14 days depending on variety and handling.

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)** - Documented step-by-step process for consistent task execution.

**Sprouts** - Germinated seeds grown in water and consumed whole including roots; different from microgreens.

**Staggered Seeding** - Planting on a regular schedule (e.g., every 3 days) to ensure continuous harvest.

**Sunflower Microgreens** - Popular variety with nutty flavor; requires hulled seeds and pre-soak; 10-14 day cycle.

---

## T

**10×20 Tray** - Standard growing tray size (10 inches × 20 inches); most common in microgreens production.

**Traceability** - Ability to track product from seed source through customer delivery; critical for food safety.

**True Leaves** - Leaves that emerge after cotyledons; microgreens are typically harvested when first true leaves are present or emerging.

**Turns** - Number of production cycles per year; calculated as 365 days ÷ crop cycle days.

---

## V

**Variable Costs** - Expenses that change with production volume (seeds, media, packaging).

**Variety** - Specific type of microgreen (sunflower, pea, radish, etc.); each has unique growing requirements and characteristics.

**Vigor** - Measure of seed health and growth strength; vigorous seeds germinate quickly and uniformly.

---

## W

**Weight (Blackout)** - Placing 5-10 lbs on stacked trays during blackout to create resistance and strengthen stems; used for sunflower and peas.

**Wheatgrass** - Grass-family microgreen grown for juicing; 9-12 day cycle; requires pre-soak.

**Wholesale Pricing** - Price charged to restaurants and retailers; typically $25-50/lb for microgreens.

---

## Y

**Yield** - Harvestable weight of microgreens per tray; varies by variety (sunflower: 10-12 oz, radish: 6-8 oz per 10×20 tray).

- **μmol/m²/s** (micromoles per square meter per second) - Light intensity measurement
- **PPFD** - See above
- **mol/m²/day** - Daily Light Integral measurement
- **°F** - Degrees Fahrenheit (temperature)
- **% RH** - Percent Relative Humidity
- **oz** - Ounces (weight)
- **lb** - Pounds (weight)
- **sq ft** - Square feet (area)
- **cu ft** - Cubic feet (volume)
