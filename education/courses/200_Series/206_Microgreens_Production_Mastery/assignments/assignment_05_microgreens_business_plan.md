# Assignment 5: Microgreens Business Plan
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

| **Type** | Project | **Duration** | 2-4 hrs | **Points** | 30 | **Due** | Module 12 |

## Purpose
Create complete business plan for microgreens production and sales.

## Required Components

### 1. Production Plan (10 pts)
- Facility size and layout
- Equipment list and costs
- Variety mix and production schedule
- Weekly/monthly output projections
- Labor requirements

### 2. Market Strategy (8 pts)
- Target customers (restaurants, retail, farmers markets)
- Pricing strategy
- Sales channels
- Customer acquisition plan
- Competitive analysis

### 3. Financial Projections (8 pts)
- Startup costs
- Monthly operating expenses
- Revenue projections (realistic)
- Break-even analysis
- 12-month cash flow

### 4. Operations (4 pts)
- Standard operating procedures
- Quality control protocols
- Food safety compliance
- Scaling strategy

**Deliverable:** 12-18 page professional business plan with:
- Executive summary
- Production details
- Marketing strategy
- Financial models
- Implementation timeline

**Focus:** Demonstrate understanding of microgreens economics and operations
