71. Shelving/racks, LED lighting, growing trays, media/containers (any 4 from: ventilation, timers, growing media, water system)
72. Rapid cooling slows respiration, preserves quality, extends shelf life by 3-5 days. Every hour delay at room temp reduces shelf life significantly.
73. Stack trays with solid bottom cover, add 5-10 lbs weight on top, keep in dark area for 3-4 days until shells lift and cotyledons emerging. Check moisture daily.
