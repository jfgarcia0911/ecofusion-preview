## Harvest Window Quick Reference

```
OPTIMAL HARVEST TIMING
══════════════════════

VISUAL INDICATORS:
✓ Cotyledons fully expanded and open
✓ First true leaves present or just emerging
✓ Height reached target for variety
✓ Vibrant color (variety-specific)
✓ Strong, upright stems

HARVEST WINDOW:
• Day -1: Slightly early (acceptable, 85-90% yield)
• Day 0-2: OPTIMAL (peak quality and yield)
• Day 3-4: Slightly late (acceptable, quality declining)
• Day 5+: Over-mature (don't harvest, quality poor)

COMMON MISTAKES:
✗ Harvesting too early (low yield)
✗ Waiting too long (leggy, poor quality)
✗ Inconsistent timing across trays
```

## Production Capacity Calculator

```
CALCULATE YOUR WEEKLY OUTPUT
═════════════════════════════

Formula: (Trays × Yield × Cycles per week)

Example: Sunflower
• Yield per tray: 10 oz
• Cycle time: 12 days
• Cycles per week: 7 ÷ 12 = 0.58
• Weekly output per tray: 10 oz × 0.58 = 5.8 oz/week

For 20 lbs/week Sunflower:
• 20 lbs = 320 oz needed per week
• 320 oz ÷ 5.8 oz/tray/week = 55 trays in rotation
• Seed ~12 trays every 3 days to maintain 55-tray rotation

QUICK ESTIMATE:
Trays in Production = (Weekly lbs target × 16) ÷ (yield per tray × 52 ÷ cycle days)
```

## Key Timing Reminders

**DAILY:**
- Check blackout trays (moisture, readiness)
- Scout 10% of growing trays
- Water as needed
- Harvest scheduled trays
- Seed per weekly schedule

**WEEKLY:**
- Deep clean and sanitize
- Review upcoming harvest schedule
- Order seeds/supplies as needed
- Inventory check

**MONTHLY:**
- Review yields and adjust seeding rates
- Evaluate variety performance
- Clean equipment thoroughly
- Review financials
