## Pricing Calculator

```
COST-PLUS PRICING WORKSHEET
════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Direct Costs (per tray)
Seeds:                    $_____
Media:                    $_____
Packaging:                $_____
Label:                    $_____
Utilities (allocated):    $_____
                          ──────
Total Direct Cost:        $_____ (A)

STEP 2: Overhead (per tray)
Monthly overhead ÷ Trays produced = $_____ (B)

STEP 3: Total Cost
(A) + (B) = $_____ per tray
÷ Yield (oz) = $_____ per oz
× 16 = $_____ per lb

STEP 4: Set Price
Cost per lb ÷ (1 - Target Margin %) = Price per lb

EXAMPLE:
Cost: $5.50/lb | Target Margin: 50%
Price = $5.50 ÷ 0.50 = $11.00/lb
Market rate: $28-32/lb → Actual margin: 81%
```

## Production Economics

```
REVENUE PER RACK (Annual)
══════════════════════════

18"×48" rack = 40 trays capacity
Average yield: 10 oz/tray
Cycle time: 12 days average
Annual turns: 30 (365 ÷ 12)

Annual production: 40 trays × 10 oz × 30 turns = 12,000 oz = 750 lbs

Revenue @ $30/lb wholesale: 750 × $30 = $22,500 per rack/year

At 80% efficiency: $18,000 per rack/year realistic
```

## Breakeven Analysis

```
CALCULATE YOUR BREAKEVEN POINT
═══════════════════════════════

Fixed Costs (monthly): $_____
(Rent, insurance, depreciation, base labor)

Variable Costs per lb: $_____
(Seeds, media, packaging, utilities)

Selling Price per lb: $_____

Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost = $_____

Breakeven (lbs) = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin

EXAMPLE:
Fixed: $2,500/month
Variable: $5.50/lb
Price: $30/lb
Contribution Margin: $24.50/lb
Breakeven: 2,500 ÷ 24.50 = 102 lbs/month (25.5 lbs/week)
```

## Cash Flow Planning

```
FIRST YEAR CASH FLOW EXPECTATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════

MONTH 1-2 (Setup):
Revenue: $0
Expenses: High (equipment, setup)
Cash Flow: Negative (investment phase)

MONTH 3-4 (Launch):
Revenue: 20-40% of target
Expenses: Moderate (operating + learning)
Cash Flow: Negative

MONTH 5-6 (Ramp Up):
Revenue: 50-70% of target
Expenses: Moderate
Cash Flow: Approaching break-even

MONTH 7-12 (Established):
Revenue: 80-100% of target
Expenses: Stabilized
Cash Flow: Positive (if well-managed)

CRITICAL: Ensure 6-12 months operating capital reserve
```

## Customer Management

```
CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST vs. LIFETIME VALUE
══════════════════════════════════════════════

Cost to Acquire Customer:
Sample kit materials:        $15
Delivery time/fuel:          $10
Follow-up time:              $10
                             ───
Total Acquisition Cost:      $35

Customer Lifetime Value (Example):
Weekly order:                4 lbs @ $30 = $120/week
Margin per order (60%):      $72/week
Average retention:           12 months
Lifetime gross profit:       $72 × 52 = $3,744

Customer LTV / Acquisition Cost = 107× ROI

KEY: Focus on retention - keeping customers is 5-10× cheaper
     than acquiring new ones
```
