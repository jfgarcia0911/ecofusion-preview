## Course Description

Build expertise in the fastest-growing segment of controlled environment agriculture. This intensive course covers microgreen production from seed to sale, including variety selection, growing techniques, quality management, and profitable business models.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Select and source quality microgreen seeds
2. Implement professional production techniques
3. Achieve consistent quality and yields
4. Develop efficient production workflows
5. Market and sell microgreens profitably
6. Scale operations effectively
