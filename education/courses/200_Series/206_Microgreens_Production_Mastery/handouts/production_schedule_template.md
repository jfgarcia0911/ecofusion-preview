# Weekly Production Schedule Template
## Course 206: Microgreens Production Mastery

---

## Purpose

This production schedule template helps you plan, track, and execute your weekly microgreens production. Use it to maintain consistent supply, manage workflow, and ensure you meet customer demand every week.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. **Start with your weekly sales target** - How many pounds/trays do you need to harvest each week?
2. **Work backwards from harvest** - Count back the days for each variety's growth cycle
3. **Create a staggered seeding schedule** - Seed the right amounts on the right days
4. **Track daily tasks** - Use the checklist to stay organized
5. **Review and adjust** - Refine your schedule based on actual results

---

## Tips for Success

**Consistency:**
- Seed on the same days each week
- Maintain the same schedule every week
- Build a routine and stick to it

**Buffer:**
- Always seed 10-15% more than you need
- Account for losses and quality issues
- Better to have surplus than shortfall

**Flexibility:**
- Track actual yields and adjust seeding rates
- Monitor customer demand trends
- Seasonal variations may require schedule adjustments

**Documentation:**
- Keep completed schedules for reference
- Note any deviations and results
- Use data to improve future planning

**Communication:**
- Share schedule with any helpers
- Confirm orders before seeding
- Notify customers of any supply changes
