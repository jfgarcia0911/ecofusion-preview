## Grading Microgreens by Visual Inspection

### GRADE A (Premium Quality)

```
APPEARANCE:
✓ Vibrant, uniform color throughout
✓ All plants at consistent height (±10%)
✓ Strong, upright stems - no lodging
✓ Cotyledons fully expanded, healthy
✓ First true leaves perfect stage
✓ No yellowing, browning, or discoloration
✓ Clean - no soil, debris, or foreign matter

STRUCTURE:
✓ Dense, even canopy coverage
✓ Stems firm and turgid
✓ No wilting or drooping
✓ Roots well-developed (if living tray)

SHELF LIFE POTENTIAL: 10-14 days
PRICING: Full retail/premium
CUSTOMER: High-end restaurants, specialty retail
```

---

### GRADE B (Standard Quality)

```
APPEARANCE:
○ Good color, mostly uniform
○ Slight height variation (<20%)
○ Mostly upright, some leaning
○ Minor yellowing (<5% of tray)
○ Generally clean with minor imperfections
○ Adequate for commercial use

STRUCTURE:
○ Good canopy coverage
○ Stems generally firm
○ Minimal defects

SHELF LIFE POTENTIAL: 7-10 days
PRICING: Standard wholesale
CUSTOMER: Most restaurants, retail
```

---

### GRADE C (Acceptable/Discount)

```
APPEARANCE:
△ Adequate color but variable
△ Noticeable height variation
△ Some lodging or weakness
△ Yellowing present (5-15%)
△ Minor quality issues throughout

STRUCTURE:
△ Uneven canopy
△ Some soft or weak stems
△ Acceptable but not ideal

SHELF LIFE POTENTIAL: 5-7 days
PRICING: Discounted (20-30% off)
CUSTOMER: Budget-conscious buyers, B2B processing
```

---

### REJECT (Compost)

```
UNACCEPTABLE:
✗ Severe discoloration (>20% affected)
✗ Mold or disease visible
✗ Pest presence
✗ Wilted or rotting
✗ Heavy lodging (>30%)
✗ Foreign contamination

ACTION: Do NOT sell
Protect your reputation - compost and improve systems
```

## Variety-Specific Standards

### SUNFLOWER

**Grade A:**
- Bright green leaves, yellow-green stems
- Height: 3-4 inches
- Hulls completely shed
- Cotyledons fully open
- First true leaves just emerging

**Common Defects:**
- Hulls still attached (harvest early or low humidity)
- Leggy stems (insufficient weight during blackout)
- Yellowing (insufficient light)

---

### PEA SHOOTS

**Grade A:**
- Medium-dark green
- Height: 4-6 inches
- Tendrils forming
- Sweet fresh appearance
- Crisp, turgid stems

**Common Defects:**
- Powdery mildew (white powder on leaves)
- Yellowing at base (overwatering)
- Wilting (under-watering or heat)

---

### RADISH

**Grade A:**
- Vibrant green stems with purple/pink tinge
- Height: 2-3 inches
- Compact, dense growth
- First true leaves serrated, emerging
- Spicy fresh appearance

**Common Defects:**
- Leggy, stretched (low light, warm temps)
- Pale color (insufficient light)
- String stems (over-mature)

---

### BROCCOLI

**Grade A:**
- Deep dark green
- Height: 2-3 inches
- Compact growth habit
- Cotyledons fully formed
- First true leaves present

**Common Defects:**
- Pale/yellow-green (low light)
- Very small/stunted (too dense seeding)
- Leggy (low light)

## Visual Defects Identification

```
DEFECT SEVERITY SCALE
══════════════════════

MINOR (Grade A with note):
• <5% of tray affected
• Cosmetic only
• Acceptable for all markets
• Example: Few plants shorter than average

MODERATE (Grade B):
• 5-15% affected
• Quality reduced but usable
• Discount or standard pricing
• Example: Some yellowing on edges

MAJOR (Grade C):
• 15-30% affected
• Significant quality issues
• Heavy discount required
• Example: Widespread yellowing, lodging

CRITICAL (Reject):
• >30% affected or ANY food safety issue
• Not sellable
• Compost immediately
• Example: Mold presence, disease
```

## Quality Control Checklist

**PRE-HARVEST INSPECTION:**
- [ ] Color vibrant and uniform
- [ ] Height consistent across tray
- [ ] No visible disease or pests
- [ ] Stems strong and upright
- [ ] True leaves at proper stage
- [ ] Clean appearance
- [ ] Meets customer specifications

**If ANY box unchecked:** Evaluate if tray meets Grade B or C standards, or should be rejected
