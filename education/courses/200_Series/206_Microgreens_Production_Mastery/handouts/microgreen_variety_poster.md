## Serving Suggestions

**SUNFLOWER:** "Our most popular! Crunchy, nutty - perfect on sandwiches instead of lettuce, or add to salads for nutrition and texture."

**PEA SHOOTS:** "Sweet and tender - amazing in stir-fry (just 30 seconds!), spring rolls, or fresh salads. Kids love the sweet pea flavor!"

**RADISH:** "Spicy kick! Great for tacos, Asian bowls, or anywhere you want a peppery punch. The purple color is gorgeous too!"

**BROCCOLI:** "Mild and nutritious - 40 times more cancer-fighting sulforaphane than mature broccoli! Great for anyone, especially kids."

**BASIL:** "Intense basil flavor - use sparingly on pizza, pasta, or in pesto. A little goes a long way!"

## Presentation Tips

**For Printing as Poster:**
- Print on 11x17 or larger paper
- Laminate for durability in growing area
- Display at farmer's market booth
- Use as customer handout (front/back)

**For Digital Use:**
- Save as PDF for tablet/phone reference
- Share with customers via email
- Post variety highlights on social media
- Use as training tool for staff
