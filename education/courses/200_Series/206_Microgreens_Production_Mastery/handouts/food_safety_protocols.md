## Daily Food Safety Checklist

### WORKER HYGIENE (Before ANY food contact)

```
REQUIRED - NO EXCEPTIONS:
□ Hands washed with soap and water (20+ seconds)
□ Clean clothing or clean apron
□ Hair covered (cap, hairnet, or bandana)
□ No jewelry on hands or wrists
□ No eating, drinking, or smoking in production areas
□ Report any illness - DO NOT work if sick
□ Cover any cuts or wounds with waterproof bandages
```

### HANDWASHING PROCEDURE

```
PROPER TECHNIQUE:
1. Wet hands with clean running water
2. Apply soap
3. Lather and scrub:
   - Palms and backs of hands
   - Between fingers
   - Under nails
   - Wrists
4. Scrub for at least 20 seconds
5. Rinse thoroughly
6. Dry with clean towel or air dryer

WHEN TO WASH:
• Before starting work
• After using restroom
• After touching face, hair, or body
• After handling waste or cleaning
• After breaks
• Between tasks (e.g., seeding to harvest)
• Whenever hands may be contaminated
```

## Equipment Sanitation

### DAILY CLEANING SCHEDULE

**HARVEST TOOLS (After each use):**
1. Remove debris and rinse with water
2. Wash with hot soapy water
3. Rinse with clean water
4. Sanitize using approved sanitizer
5. Air dry on clean surface

**WORK SURFACES (Before and after use):**
1. Clear all items from surface
2. Sweep/wipe debris
3. Wash with hot soapy water
4. Rinse with clean water
5. Apply sanitizer, let stand per label
6. Air dry or wipe with clean cloth

**CONTAINERS & BINS (After each use):**
1. Empty all contents
2. Rinse with water
3. Wash with hot soapy water
4. Rinse thoroughly
5. Sanitize
6. Store inverted or covered

## Sanitizer Preparation

### BLEACH SOLUTION (Cheap, effective)

```
MIXING INSTRUCTIONS:
1 tablespoon bleach per 1 gallon water
OR
1 teaspoon bleach per 1 quart water

USAGE:
• Mix fresh daily
• Apply to clean surfaces
• Let stand 1-2 minutes
• Air dry (do not rinse)
• Replace solution when dirty

SAFETY:
• Never mix with other chemicals
• Use in ventilated area
• Wear gloves
• Label container clearly
```

### COMMERCIAL SANITIZERS
- Follow manufacturer instructions
- Common types: Quaternary ammonia, hydrogen peroxide
- More expensive but less odor
- Test strips verify concentration

## Temperature Control

### CRITICAL TEMPERATURE GUIDELINES

```
DANGER ZONE: 40-140°F
Bacteria multiply rapidly in this range

MICROGREENS HANDLING:
Cold Storage: 34-38°F (keep continuously)
Maximum time at room temp: <1 hour
Transport: <40°F at all times

TEMPERATURE LOG:
Check and record 2× daily minimum:

Morning Check:
Time: _____ | Cooler Temp: _____°F
Action if >40°F: _______________________

Evening Check:
Time: _____ | Cooler Temp: _____°F
Action if >40°F: _______________________
```

## Water Safety

### WATER QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

```
IRRIGATION WATER:
✓ Must be potable (drinkable) quality
✓ From municipal supply OR tested well water
✓ No surface water (ponds, streams, rain catchment)
✓ Test annually minimum:
  - Total coliform
  - E. coli
  - Other pathogens as required by regulations

TESTING:
• Send samples to certified lab
• Document results
• Keep records minimum 2 years
• Address any contamination immediately

CONTAMINATED WATER PROTOCOL:
1. Stop using water immediately
2. Use bottled or treated water
3. Discard any product irrigated with contaminated water
4. Identify and fix contamination source
5. Re-test before resuming use
```

## Traceability System

### LOT NUMBERING

```
SIMPLE LOT CODE FORMAT:
YYMMDD-V-T

Example: 240615-SUN-A
• 24 = Year (2024)
• 06 = Month (June)
• 15 = Day
• SUN = Variety code (Sunflower)
• A = Batch identifier

LABEL ALL:
• Seed bags (with supplier lot #)
• Seeding trays (with lot code)
• Packaged product (with lot code)

RECORDS TO KEEP:
• Seed purchase records (supplier, lot #, date)
• Planting log (date, variety, lot #, quantity)
• Harvest log (date, lot #, yield, quality)
• Sales records (customer, lot #, quantity, date)

RETENTION: Minimum 2 years (check local regulations)
```

## Recall Procedure

### IN CASE OF CONTAMINATION OR CUSTOMER ILLNESS

```
IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Stop distribution of potentially affected product
2. Identify all lots that may be affected
3. Contact all customers who received product
4. Retrieve product if possible
5. Document everything
6. Contact local health department if required
7. Investigate root cause
8. Implement corrective actions
9. Document lessons learned

TRACEABILITY TEST:
Practice recall drill quarterly:
• Select random lot code
• Trace backward to seed source
• Trace forward to all customers
• Goal: Complete trace in <2 hours
```

## Pest Control

### INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT

```
PREVENTION (Primary defense):
□ Keep facility clean and organized
□ Remove waste daily
□ Seal entry points (screens, door sweeps)
□ Positive air pressure
□ No food or drinks in production area
□ Regular monitoring

MONITORING:
□ Yellow sticky traps for flying insects
□ Daily visual inspection (10% of trays)
□ Document any pest sightings
□ Identify pest species

INTERVENTION (If needed):
□ Remove affected trays immediately
□ Increase sanitation
□ Physical controls (traps, barriers)
□ Biological controls if appropriate
□ Chemical controls: ONLY food-safe, organic approved products
□ NEVER use pesticides not labeled for food crops

PESTICIDE USE (if absolutely necessary):
• Only products approved for food crops
• Follow label directions exactly
• Document all applications
• Respect pre-harvest intervals
• Consider third-party certification requirements
```

## Visitor and Vendor Policy

```
FACILITY ACCESS CONTROL:

AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY in production areas

VISITORS (non-employees):

□ Wash hands before entry
□ Wear hair covering

```

## Personal Items Policy

```
NOT ALLOWED IN PRODUCTION AREAS:
✗ Personal bags, purses, backpacks
✗ Cell phones (except for work purposes)
✗ Food or beverages
✗ Tobacco products
✗ Jewelry (except plain wedding band)
✗ Cosmetics, perfumes, colognes

DESIGNATED BREAK AREA:
• Separate from production
• Personal items stored there
• Food and drinks allowed only in break area
```

---

## Illness Policy

```
EMPLOYEES MUST NOT WORK IF:
✗ Vomiting or diarrhea
✗ Fever
✗ Jaundice (yellow skin/eyes)
✗ Sore throat with fever
✗ Infected cuts or sores on hands
✗ Diagnosed with foodborne illness

RETURN TO WORK:
• 24 hours after symptoms resolve
• Doctor's clearance if required
• No exceptions - protect product and customers
```
