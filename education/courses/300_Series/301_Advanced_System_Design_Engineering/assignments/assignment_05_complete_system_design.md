## Project Objective

Design a complete commercial aquaponics system from concept through operational documentation. This capstone integrates all course concepts into a professional-quality engineering deliverable suitable for construction, permitting, and operation.

---

## Design Brief

**Project:** Commercial Aquaponics Production Facility

**Scale:** Medium commercial operation
- Production area: 3,000-5,000 sq ft
- Target production: 10,000-15,000 lbs/year mixed greens
- Fish production: 3,000-5,000 lbs/year tilapia
- Location: Your choice (specify climate zone)

**Scope:** Complete system design including:
- All tanks and vessels
- Plumbing and hydraulic systems
- Biofilter and water treatment
- Grow systems
- Environmental controls
- Electrical systems
- Automation and monitoring

### 2. Engineering Calculations (20 points)

Submit detailed calculations for:

#### A. Hydraulic Analysis (6 points)
**Required Calculations:**
- Flow rate requirements (turnover calculations)
- Total Dynamic Head (TDH) for each pump
- Pump sizing and selection
- Pipe sizing for all major runs
- Velocity calculations
- Friction loss analysis

**Format:**
```
1. System Flow Requirements
   - Tank volumes: _______
   - Target turnover rate: _______
   - Required flow rate: _______ GPM

2. TDH Calculation - Main Pump
   - Static head: _______ ft
   - Friction head: _______ ft
   - Pressure head: _______ ft
   - Total TDH: _______ ft

3. Pump Selection
   - Selected model: _______
   - Curve analysis: (include pump curve)
   - Operating point: _______ GPM @ _______ ft
```

#### B. Biofilter Sizing (6 points)
**Required Calculations:**
- Daily feed calculation
- TAN load determination
- Required surface area
- Media volume calculation
- Configuration recommendation

**Include:**
- Safety factors applied
- Media type selection and justification
- Hydraulic loading rates
- Expected performance parameters

#### C. Structural & Electrical Analysis (8 points)

**Structural:**
- Floor loading calculations
- Support requirements for tanks
- Point loads on structure
- Foundation requirements

**Electrical:**
- Complete load calculations
- Circuit design
- Panel sizing
- Voltage drop analysis
- One-line diagram

### 3. Specifications (15 points)

#### A. Equipment Specifications (7 points)

Create detailed specifications for major equipment:

**Format for Each Item:**
```
ITEM: Main Circulation Pump
QUANTITY: 2 (duty + standby)
PERFORMANCE: 150 GPM @ 18 ft TDH
CONSTRUCTION:
  - Material: 316 stainless steel wetted parts
  - Motor: 5 HP, 230V, single phase, TEFC
  - Seal: Mechanical, Viton
FEATURES:
  - Variable frequency drive compatible
  - Isolation flanges included
  - Pressure gauges on suction and discharge
INSTALLATION: Horizontal centrifugal, concrete pad
MANUFACTURER: [Acceptable manufacturers list]
```

**Required Equipment Specifications:**
- All pumps (3-4 units)
- Biofilter with media
- Air blowers
- Heaters/chillers
- Control system
- Major instruments

#### B. Material Specifications (4 points)

Specify materials for:
- Piping (by service: circulation, drain, aeration)
- Valves and fittings
- Tanks (material, construction, warranty)
- Electrical components
- Grow beds/rafts
- Plumbing supports and hangers

#### C. Performance Requirements (4 points)

Define operational targets:
- Water quality parameters
- Flow rates and turnover
- Temperature control ranges
- Backup and redundancy requirements
- Alarm setpoints
- Safety interlocks

## Tips for Excellence

1. **Start early** - this is substantial work
2. **Plan your system completely** before detailed design
3. **Be realistic** - design to actual commercial standards
4. **Show your work** - calculations must be traceable
5. **Check against rubric** before submitting
6. **Professional presentation matters** - this showcases your skills
7. **Proof read everything** - typos undermine credibility
8. **Think constructibility** - could someone build from your drawings?
