# Module 6: Grow System Engineering

## Introduction

Grow system engineering encompasses the design of hydroponic subsystems within aquaponic operations—including deep water culture (DWC), nutrient film technique (NFT), vertical towers, and media beds. Each system type has unique hydraulic, structural, and biological requirements that must be engineered for reliable performance.

This module covers the technical design of various grow systems, including flow dynamics, structural support, plumbing manifolds, and drainage. You'll learn to engineer grow systems that deliver consistent results at commercial scale.

**Duration:** 1 hour

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Design deep water culture (DWC) raft systems with proper flow distribution
2. Engineer NFT systems with appropriate slope and channel sizing
3. Design vertical growing towers and their distribution systems
4. Calculate structural loads for grow systems and supports
5. Design flood-and-drain media bed systems
6. Specify materials and components for durability
7. Integrate grow systems with overall aquaponic design

## 1. Deep Water Culture (DWC) Systems

### 1.1 DWC Design Parameters

**Basic Configuration:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ~~~○~~~~~○~~~~~○~~~~~○~~~~~○~~~ Water │ Rafts floating
│ ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│ 8-12" deep
│                                         │
│     ↑↑↑ Aeration   Flow →             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘

Depth: 8-12 inches typical
Flow: 50-100 GPM per 1000 ft² growing area
Aeration: 0.5-1.0 CFM per ft² growing area
Water temperature: 65-72°F (optimum)
DO: >5 mg/L minimum, >6 mg/L optimal
```

**Sizing Calculations:**
```
Channel width: 4 feet (standard raft size)
Channel length: 50-100 feet maximum
Volume per channel: W × L × D × 7.48 gal/ft³

Example:
4 ft wide × 80 ft long × 0.83 ft deep (10 inches)
Volume = 4 × 80 × 0.83 × 7.48 = 1,984 gallons

Number of rafts: Length / Raft Length
= 80 ft / 4 ft = 20 rafts per channel

Growing area: 20 rafts × 16 ft² = 320 ft²
```
