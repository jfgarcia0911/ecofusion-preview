# Module 9: Plumbing Detailed Design

## Introduction

Plumbing design encompasses pipes, fittings, valves, and all water conveyance infrastructure. Proper plumbing ensures reliable flow, prevents leaks, allows maintenance, and meets plumbing codes. This module provides detailed specifications for commercial aquaponic plumbing systems.

**Duration:** 1 hour

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Select appropriate piping materials and schedules
2. Design valve stations for isolation and control
3. Specify unions, flanges, and serviceable connections
4. Apply plumbing codes and standards
5. Design drains and overflow protection
6. Create plumbing isometric drawings
7. Develop maintenance protocols

---

## 1. Pipe Selection

### 1.1 Material Comparison

| Material | Pressure Rating | Cost | Lifespan | Best Use |
|----------|----------------|------|----------|----------|
| PVC Schedule 40 | 280 psi @ 73°F | $ | 50+ years | General purpose |
| PVC Schedule 80 | 400 psi @ 73°F | $$ | 50+ years | High pressure |
| CPVC | 400 psi @ 73°F | $$ | 50 years | Hot water capable |
| HDPE | 160 psi @ 73°F | $$ | 50+ years | Flexibility needed |
| ABS | 180 psi @ 73°F | $ | 50 years | Drainage |

### 1.2 Valve Specification

**Ball Valves:** Quick shutoff, minimal pressure drop
**Gate Valves:** Full-port flow, gradual control
**Check Valves:** Prevent backflow, critical for pump protection

---

## Summary

Proper plumbing design ensures reliable water movement, easy maintenance, and code compliance.
