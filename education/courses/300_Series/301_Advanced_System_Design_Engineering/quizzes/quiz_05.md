# Quiz 5: Environmental Systems Integration

**Course:** 301 - Advanced System Design & Engineering
**Module:** 5 - Environmental Systems Integration

## Questions

### 1. What is the optimal VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit) for vegetative plant growth?
a) 0.4-0.8 kPa
b) 0.8-1.2 kPa
c) 1.2-1.6 kPa
d) 1.6-2.0 kPa

### 2. Calculate daily water transpiration for 100 m² growing area at 3 L/m²/day.
a) 150 L/day
b) 200 L/day
c) 250 L/day
d) 300 L/day

### 3. What is the recommended air exchange rate for a greenhouse?
a) 0.5-1 volume/minute
b) 1-2 volumes/minute
c) 3-4 volumes/minute
d) 5-6 volumes/minute

### 4. What R-value insulation is recommended for temperate climate greenhouses?
a) R-5 to R-10
b) R-10 to R-20
c) R-20 to R-30
d) R-30 to R-40

### 5. Calculate heating load for 1000 ft² greenhouse with ΔT = 40°F and U = 0.8 BTU/ft²·°F·hr.
a) 24,000 BTU/hr
b) 32,000 BTU/hr
c) 40,000 BTU/hr
d) 48,000 BTU/hr

### 6. What is the ideal CO₂ concentration for enhanced plant growth?
a) 400-600 ppm
b) 800-1200 ppm
c) 1400-1800 ppm
d) 2000-2400 ppm

### 7. What percentage of solar radiation should shade cloth block during summer?
a) 10-20%
b) 30-50%
c) 60-70%
d) 80-90%

### 8. What is the recommended temperature differential between day and night?
a) 2-5°F
b) 5-10°F
c) 10-15°F
d) 15-20°F

### 9. Calculate cooling capacity needed for 500 m² at 500 W/m² solar load with 60% efficiency.
a) 125 kW
b) 150 kW
c) 175 kW
d) 200 kW

### 10. What is the optimal relative humidity range for most crops?
a) 30-50%
b) 50-70%
c) 70-85%
d) 85-95%

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 0.8-1.2 kPa**
**Explanation:** Vegetative growth optimal VPD is 0.8-1.2 kPa; seedlings need 0.4-0.8 kPa.

### 2. Answer: **d) 300 L/day**
**Explanation:** 100 m² × 3 L/m²/day = 300 L/day transpiration

### 3. Answer: **b) 1-2 volumes/minute**
**Explanation:** Greenhouses typically require 1-2 complete air exchanges per minute for temperature control.

### 4. Answer: **b) R-10 to R-20**
**Explanation:** Temperate climate greenhouses typically use R-10 to R-20 insulation for end walls and north walls.

### 5. Answer: **b) 32,000 BTU/hr**
**Explanation:** Q = A × U × ΔT = 1000 × 0.8 × 40 = 32,000 BTU/hr

### 6. Answer: **b) 800-1200 ppm**
**Explanation:** CO₂ enrichment to 800-1200 ppm enhances photosynthesis; ambient is ~400 ppm.

### 7. Answer: **b) 30-50%**
**Explanation:** Summer shade cloth typically blocks 30-50% of light to prevent heat stress while maintaining growth.

### 8. Answer: **b) 5-10°F**
**Explanation:** DIF (day-night temperature differential) of 5-10°F promotes healthy plant development.

### 9. Answer: **b) 150 kW**
**Explanation:** Load = 500 m² × 500 W/m² = 250 kW; Cooling = 250 kW × 0.6 = 150 kW

### 10. Answer: **b) 50-70%**
**Explanation:** Most crops thrive at 50-70% RH; too high promotes disease, too low causes stress.
