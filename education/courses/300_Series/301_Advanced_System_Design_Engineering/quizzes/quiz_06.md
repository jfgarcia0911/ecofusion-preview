## Questions

### 1. What is the recommended plant density for NFT channels?
a) 4-8 plants/ft²
b) 8-16 plants/ft²
c) 16-24 plants/ft²
d) 24-32 plants/ft²

### 2. Calculate the flow rate needed for a 40 ft NFT channel.
a) 0.25-0.5 GPM
b) 0.5-1.0 GPM
c) 1.0-1.5 GPM
d) 1.5-2.0 GPM

### 3. What is the optimal slope for NFT channels?
a) 1:50 (2%)
b) 1:100 (1%)
c) 1:200 (0.5%)
d) Level (0%)

### 4. What media depth is recommended for DWC raft systems?
a) 2-4 inches
b) 6-12 inches
c) 12-18 inches
d) 18-24 inches

### 5. How many square feet of growing area does one 4×8 ft raft provide?
a) 16 ft²
b) 24 ft²
c) 32 ft²
d) 40 ft²

### 6. What is the recommended fill/drain cycle time for flood and drain systems?
a) 5-10 minutes
b) 15-30 minutes
c) 45-60 minutes
d) 90-120 minutes

### 7. Calculate Dutch bucket spacing for tomatoes (need 4 ft² per plant).
a) 12" × 12"
b) 18" × 18"
c) 24" × 24"
d) 36" × 36"

### 8. What is the optimal solution depth in NFT channels?
a) 1/8 - 1/4 inch
b) 1/4 - 1/2 inch
c) 1/2 - 1 inch
d) 1 - 2 inches

### 9. What media particle size is best for media bed systems?
a) 1/8 - 1/4 inch
b) 1/4 - 3/4 inch
c) 3/4 - 1 inch
d) 1 - 2 inches

### 10. Calculate vertical tower capacity: 6 ft height, 8 plants/ft.
a) 36 plants
b) 42 plants
c) 48 plants
d) 54 plants

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 8-16 plants/ft²**
**Explanation:** NFT systems typically support 8-16 plants per square foot depending on crop type.

### 2. Answer: **b) 0.5-1.0 GPM**
**Explanation:** NFT channels require 0.5-1.0 GPM (approximately 1 GPM per 50-100 ft).

### 3. Answer: **b) 1:100 (1%)**
**Explanation:** NFT optimal slope is 1:100 (1%) - steep enough for flow, gentle enough for nutrient contact.

### 4. Answer: **b) 6-12 inches**
**Explanation:** DWC raft systems typically operate at 6-12 inch water depth for root zone support.

### 5. Answer: **c) 32 ft²**
**Explanation:** 4 ft × 8 ft = 32 ft² of growing surface.

### 6. Answer: **b) 15-30 minutes**
**Explanation:** Flood and drain cycles typically run 15-30 minutes fill, 30-45 minutes drain.

### 7. Answer: **c) 24" × 24"**
**Explanation:** 24" × 24" = 4 ft² per plant, meeting tomato spacing requirements.

### 8. Answer: **b) 1/4 - 1/2 inch**
**Explanation:** NFT film depth should be 1/4 - 1/2 inch for adequate nutrient delivery without drowning roots.

### 9. Answer: **b) 1/4 - 3/4 inch**
**Explanation:** Media in 1/4 - 3/4 inch range provides good drainage and surface area for bacteria.

### 10. Answer: **c) 48 plants**
**Explanation:** 6 ft × 8 plants/ft = 48 plants per tower.
