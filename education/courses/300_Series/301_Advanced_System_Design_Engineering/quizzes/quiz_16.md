## Questions

### 1. What is the standard scale for detailed component drawings?
a) 1:1 or 1:2
b) 1:10 or 1:20
c) 1:50 or 1:100
d) 1:200 or 1:500

### 2. What drawing view shows the system from above?
a) Elevation
b) Section
c) Plan
d) Isometric

### 3. What line weight is used for object outlines in CAD?
a) 0.13 mm
b) 0.25 mm
c) 0.50 mm
d) 1.00 mm

### 4. What is the purpose of a P&ID (Process & Instrumentation Diagram)?
a) Show physical layout
b) Show process flow and controls
c) Show electrical connections
d) Show structural details

### 5. What symbol represents a centrifugal pump in P&ID?
a) Rectangle
b) Circle
c) Triangle
d) Circle with impeller

### 6. Calculate drawing scale for 40 ft greenhouse on 11×17" paper (landscape).
a) 1/4" = 1'
b) 1/2" = 1'
c) 1" = 1'
d) 2" = 1'

### 7. What file format preserves CAD layers and editability?
a) PDF
b) DWG/DXF
c) JPG
d) PNG

### 8. What dimension tolerance is standard for aquaponic plumbing?
a) ±1/32"
b) ±1/16"
c) ±1/8"
d) ±1/4"

### 9. What is the ISO standard paper size closest to US Letter?
a) A3
b) A4
c) A5
d) B4

### 10. What CAD layer naming convention is most common?
a) Random names
b) Discipline-Type-Status
c) Numbers only
d) Colors only

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **a) 1:1 or 1:2**
**Explanation:** Detailed component drawings use 1:1 (full size) or 1:2 (half size) for clarity.

### 2. Answer: **c) Plan**
**Explanation:** Plan view (top view) shows the system layout from above.

### 3. Answer: **c) 0.50 mm**
**Explanation:** Object outlines typically use 0.50 mm line weight; hidden lines use 0.25 mm.

### 4. Answer: **b) Show process flow and controls**
**Explanation:** P&ID shows process flow, instrumentation, and control systems schematically.

### 5. Answer: **d) Circle with impeller**
**Explanation:** Pump symbol is typically a circle with impeller representation.

### 6. Answer: **a) 1/4" = 1'**
**Explanation:** 40 ft building: at 1/4"=1' scale = 10" on paper (fits 17" width with margins).

### 7. Answer: **b) DWG/DXF**
**Explanation:** DWG and DXF formats preserve layers, dimensions, and full editability.

### 8. Answer: **c) ±1/8"**
**Explanation:** Standard tolerance for plumbing and tank fabrication is ±1/8".

### 9. Answer: **b) A4**
**Explanation:** A4 (210×297 mm) is closest to US Letter (8.5×11"); A4 is slightly narrower and taller.

### 10. Answer: **b) Discipline-Type-Status**
**Explanation:** Standard CAD layers use format like "A-WALL" (Architecture-Wall) or "P-PIPE" (Plumbing-Pipe).
