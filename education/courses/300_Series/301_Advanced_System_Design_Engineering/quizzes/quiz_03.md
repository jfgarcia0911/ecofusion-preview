## Questions

### 1. What is the hydrostatic pressure at the bottom of a 6 ft deep tank?
a) 1.8 psi
b) 2.6 psi
c) 3.5 psi
d) 4.2 psi

### 2. What thickness is required for a rectangular acrylic panel 36" × 48" supporting 4 ft of water?
a) 1/4"
b) 3/8"
c) 1/2"
d) 3/4"

### 3. What is the recommended length-to-diameter ratio for circular fish tanks?
a) 1:1 (equal)
b) 2:1
c) 3:1
d) 4:1

### 4. What is the minimum recommended freeboard for a culture tank?
a) 3 inches
b) 6 inches
c) 12 inches
d) 18 inches

### 5. For biofilter tanks, what is the recommended water depth range?
a) 6-12 inches
b) 12-24 inches
c) 24-48 inches
d) 48-72 inches

### 6. What material is NOT suitable for potable aquaponic systems?
a) HDPE
b) Fiberglass with food-grade gel coat
c) Recycled IBC totes
d) Stainless steel 304

### 7. Calculate the volume of a cylindrical tank: 6 ft diameter × 4 ft height.
a) 113 gallons
b) 565 gallons
c) 848 gallons
d) 1,131 gallons

### 8. What is the recommended turnover rate for fish culture tanks?
a) 0.5-1 times per hour
b) 1-2 times per hour
c) 3-4 times per hour
d) 5-6 times per hour

### 9. In a conical bottom tank, what cone angle provides good solids settling?
a) 15-30 degrees
b) 45-60 degrees
c) 60-75 degrees
d) 75-90 degrees

### 10. What is the design safety factor for structural tank calculations?
a) 1.5
b) 2.0
c) 2.5
d) 3.0

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 2.6 psi**
**Explanation:** Pressure = 0.433 psi/ft × 6 ft = 2.6 psi

### 2. Answer: **d) 3/4"**
**Explanation:** For large acrylic panels under 4 ft of water pressure, 3/4" thickness provides adequate safety factor.

### 3. Answer: **a) 1:1 (equal)**
**Explanation:** Circular tanks are typically designed with depth equal to diameter for optimal water circulation.

### 4. Answer: **c) 12 inches**
**Explanation:** Minimum 12" freeboard prevents splashing and fish jumping out.

### 5. Answer: **c) 24-48 inches**
**Explanation:** Biofilters typically operate at 2-4 ft depth for adequate media volume and hydraulic retention.

### 6. Answer: **c) Recycled IBC totes**
**Explanation:** Recycled IBC totes may have contained chemicals and are not suitable for food production without proper certification.

### 7. Answer: **c) 848 gallons**
**Explanation:**
- Volume = π × r² × h = π × 3² × 4 = 113.1 ft³
- Gallons = 113.1 × 7.48 = 846 gallons ≈ 848 gallons

### 8. Answer: **b) 1-2 times per hour**
**Explanation:** Fish tanks typically require 1-2 turnovers per hour minimum for adequate water quality.

### 9. Answer: **b) 45-60 degrees**
**Explanation:** Cone angles of 45-60 degrees provide good solids settling toward the drain point.

### 10. Answer: **b) 2.0**
**Explanation:** Standard structural safety factor for tank design is 2.0.
