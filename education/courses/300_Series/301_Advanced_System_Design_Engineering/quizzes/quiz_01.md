## Questions

### 1. What is the density of water at 20°C?
a) 1000 kg/m³
b) 998.2 kg/m³
c) 996.5 kg/m³
d) 1002 kg/m³

### 2. Calculate the velocity in a 2" pipe (ID = 2.067") carrying 40 GPM.
a) 3.2 ft/s
b) 3.8 ft/s
c) 4.4 ft/s
d) 5.0 ft/s

### 3. What flow regime exists when Reynolds number equals 5000?
a) Laminar flow
b) Transition flow
c) Turbulent flow
d) Cannot be determined

### 4. The Hazen-Williams C value for PVC pipe is typically:
a) 100
b) 120
c) 140
d) 150

### 5. In Bernoulli's equation, what does the term V²/2g represent?
a) Pressure head
b) Elevation head
c) Velocity head
d) Friction head

### 6. What is the recommended voltage drop limit for electrical branch circuits?
a) 1%
b) 3%
c) 5%
d) 10%

### 7. Calculate the power consumption of a 3 HP motor running 8 hours per day for 30 days at $0.12/kWh.
a) $48.20
b) $64.30
c) $80.50
d) $96.60

### 8. What is the saturation concentration of dissolved oxygen at 20°C and sea level?
a) 7.5 mg/L
b) 8.2 mg/L
c) 9.1 mg/L
d) 10.0 mg/L

### 9. Convert 150 GPM to L/s (1 GPM = 0.0631 L/s).
a) 7.95 L/s
b) 9.47 L/s
c) 11.23 L/s
d) 12.80 L/s

### 10. What is the primary purpose of calculating Reynolds number?
a) Determine pipe size
b) Calculate head loss
c) Determine flow regime
d) Size pumps

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 998.2 kg/m³**
**Explanation:** Standard density of water at 20°C is 998.2 kg/m³.

### 2. Answer: **b) 3.8 ft/s**
**Explanation:**
- A = π × (2.067/2)² = 3.355 in²
- Q = 40 GPM × 231 in³/gal = 9,240 in³/min
- V = Q/A = 9,240/(3.355 × 60) = 46.0 in/s = 3.8 ft/s

### 3. Answer: **c) Turbulent flow**
**Explanation:** Re > 4,000 indicates turbulent flow. Re = 5,000 is well into the turbulent regime.

### 4. Answer: **d) 150**
**Explanation:** PVC pipe has a Hazen-Williams C value of 150 (some sources use 140, but 150 is more common for new PVC).

### 5. Answer: **c) Velocity head**
**Explanation:** The kinetic energy term V²/2g represents velocity head in the Bernoulli equation.

### 6. Answer: **b) 3%**
**Explanation:** The NEC recommends maximum 3% voltage drop for branch circuits, 5% total including feeders.

### 7. Answer: **d) $64.30**
**Explanation:**
- Power: 3 HP × 0.746 kW/HP = 2.238 kW
- Energy: 2.238 kW × 8 hr/day × 30 days = 537.1 kWh
- Cost: 537.1 kWh × $0.12/kWh = $64.45 ≈ $64.30

### 8. Answer: **c) 9.1 mg/L**
**Explanation:** DO saturation at 20°C and sea level is 9.1 mg/L (decreases with temperature and altitude).

### 9. Answer: **b) 9.47 L/s**
**Explanation:** 150 GPM × 0.0631 L/s/GPM = 9.465 L/s ≈ 9.47 L/s

### 10. Answer: **c) Determine flow regime**
**Explanation:** Reynolds number determines whether flow is laminar, transitional, or turbulent, which affects friction factor selection.
