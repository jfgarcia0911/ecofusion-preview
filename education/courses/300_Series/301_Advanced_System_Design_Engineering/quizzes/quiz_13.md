# Quiz 13: Aeration System Design

**Module:** 13 - Aeration System Design

## Questions

### 1. What is the oxygen demand for fish at 3% body weight feeding rate?
a) 100-200 g O₂/kg feed
b) 200-300 g O₂/kg feed
c) 300-400 g O₂/kg feed
d) 400-500 g O₂/kg feed

### 2. Calculate daily O₂ requirement for 500 kg fish at 2% feeding rate.
a) 1.5 kg O₂/day
b) 2.5 kg O₂/day
c) 3.5 kg O₂/day
d) 4.5 kg O₂/day

### 3. What is the Standard Oxygen Transfer Rate (SOTR) for fine bubble diffusers?
a) 1-2 kg O₂/kWh
b) 2-3 kg O₂/kWh
c) 3-4 kg O₂/kWh
d) 4-5 kg O₂/kWh

### 4. What air flow rate is needed for MBBR biofilters per m³ of reactor volume?
a) 1-3 m³/hr
b) 5-10 m³/hr
c) 15-20 m³/hr
d) 25-30 m³/hr

### 5. Calculate blower size for 1000 L/min air delivery at 3 ft depth.
a) 1/4 HP
b) 1/2 HP
c) 3/4 HP
d) 1 HP

### 6. What is the oxygen transfer efficiency of surface aerators?
a) 0.5-1.0 kg O₂/kWh
b) 1.0-1.5 kg O₂/kWh
c) 1.5-2.0 kg O₂/kWh
d) 2.0-2.5 kg O₂/kWh

### 7. What diffuser spacing provides uniform coverage?
a) 6-12 inches
b) 12-24 inches
c) 24-36 inches
d) 36-48 inches

### 8. Calculate alpha factor correction for fish tank aeration (α = 0.65).
a) 35% reduction
b) 45% reduction
c) 55% reduction
d) 65% reduction

### 9. What backup aeration capacity is required for emergency systems?
a) 25% of normal
b) 50% of normal
c) 100% of normal
d) 150% of normal

### 10. What is the ideal bubble size for efficient oxygen transfer?
a) 0.1-0.5 mm
b) 1-3 mm
c) 5-8 mm
d) 10-15 mm

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **c) 300-400 g O₂/kg feed**
**Explanation:** Fish and bacterial respiration require 300-400 g O₂ per kg of feed consumed.

### 2. Answer: **b) 2.5 kg O₂/day**
**Explanation:**
- Feed = 500 kg × 0.02 = 10 kg/day
- O₂ = 10 kg × 0.25 kg O₂/kg feed = 2.5 kg O₂/day

### 3. Answer: **b) 2-3 kg O₂/kWh**
**Explanation:** Fine bubble diffusers achieve 2-3 kg O₂/kWh SOTR in clean water.

### 4. Answer: **b) 5-10 m³/hr**
**Explanation:** MBBR biofilters require 5-10 m³ air/hr per m³ reactor volume for media movement and oxygenation.

### 5. Answer: **b) 1/2 HP**
**Explanation:** 1000 L/min ≈ 35 CFM; at 3 ft depth (1.3 psi) requires approximately 0.5 HP blower.

### 6. Answer: **c) 1.5-2.0 kg O₂/kWh**
**Explanation:** Surface aerators (paddlewheel, splash) transfer 1.5-2.0 kg O₂/kWh.

### 7. Answer: **c) 24-36 inches**
**Explanation:** Diffusers spaced 24-36 inches provide good coverage without excessive piping.

### 8. Answer: **a) 35% reduction**
**Explanation:** Alpha factor 0.65 means 65% efficiency vs. clean water; 35% reduction in performance.

### 9. Answer: **b) 50% of normal**
**Explanation:** Emergency aeration should provide minimum 50% of normal capacity to maintain critical DO levels.

### 10. Answer: **b) 1-3 mm**
**Explanation:** Fine bubbles of 1-3 mm diameter optimize surface area for oxygen transfer.
