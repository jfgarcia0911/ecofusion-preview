# Quiz 14: Lighting System Engineering

**Course:** 301 - Advanced System Design & Engineering
**Module:** 14 - Lighting System Engineering

## Questions

### 1. What is the recommended PPFD for leafy greens?
a) 100-200 μmol/m²/s
b) 200-400 μmol/m²/s
c) 400-600 μmol/m²/s
d) 600-800 μmol/m²/s

### 2. Calculate DLI for 300 μmol/m²/s over 16 hours.
a) 12.8 mol/m²/day
b) 17.3 mol/m²/day
c) 21.6 mol/m²/day
d) 25.9 mol/m²/day

### 3. What is the efficacy of modern LED grow lights?
a) 1.0-1.5 μmol/J
b) 1.5-2.0 μmol/J
c) 2.0-2.8 μmol/J
d) 3.0-3.5 μmol/J

### 4. Calculate power needed for 100 m² at 400 μmol/m²/s using 2.5 μmol/J LEDs.
a) 12 kW
b) 16 kW
c) 20 kW
d) 24 kW

### 5. What is the optimal red:blue ratio for vegetative growth?
a) 1:1
b) 3:1
c) 5:1
d) 10:1

### 6. What mounting height provides uniform coverage for 4×4 ft area?
a) 12 inches
b) 24 inches
c) 36 inches
d) 48 inches

### 7. Calculate monthly electricity cost: 10 kW lights, 16 hr/day, 30 days, $0.12/kWh.
a) $460
b) $576
c) $692
d) $808

### 8. What spectrum is most efficient for photosynthesis?
a) Blue (400-500 nm)
b) Green (500-600 nm)
c) Red (600-700 nm)
d) Far-red (700-800 nm)

### 9. What is the PAR photon flux range?
a) 200-500 nm
b) 300-600 nm
c) 400-700 nm
d) 500-800 nm

### 10. Calculate fixture spacing for 120° beam angle at 24" height.
a) 24 inches
b) 36 inches
c) 48 inches
d) 60 inches

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 200-400 μmol/m²/s**
**Explanation:** Leafy greens thrive at 200-400 μmol/m²/s PPFD; fruiting crops need 400-800+.

### 2. Answer: **b) 17.3 mol/m²/day**
**Explanation:** DLI = PPFD × hours × 3.6 / 1000 = 300 × 16 × 3.6 / 1000 = 17.28 mol/m²/day

### 3. Answer: **c) 2.0-2.8 μmol/J**
**Explanation:** Modern LED grow lights achieve 2.0-2.8 μmol/J efficacy (2022-2025 technology).

### 4. Answer: **b) 16 kW**
**Explanation:**
- Total PPFD = 100 m² × 400 μmol/m²/s = 40,000 μmol/s
- Power = 40,000 ÷ 2.5 = 16,000 W = 16 kW

### 5. Answer: **c) 5:1**
**Explanation:** Optimal red:blue ratio for vegetative growth is approximately 5:1 (varies by species).

### 6. Answer: **b) 24 inches**
**Explanation:** 24" mounting height provides good uniformity for 4×4 ft area with typical LED fixtures.

### 7. Answer: **b) $576**
**Explanation:** 10 kW × 16 hr/day × 30 days × $0.12/kWh = $576

### 8. Answer: **c) Red (600-700 nm)**
**Explanation:** Red spectrum (especially 660 nm) is most efficiently absorbed for photosynthesis.

### 9. Answer: **c) 400-700 nm**
**Explanation:** PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation) spans 400-700 nm wavelengths.

### 10. Answer: **c) 48 inches**
**Explanation:** 120° beam angle at 24" height covers ~48" diameter; fixtures spaced ~48" for overlap.
