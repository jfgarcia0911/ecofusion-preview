## Questions

### 1. What is the recommended turnover rate for solids settling tanks?
a) 1-2 times/hour
b) 3-4 times/hour
c) 6-8 times/hour
d) 10-12 times/hour

### 2. Calculate settling velocity for 100 micron particles (Stokes' Law approximation).
a) 0.5 cm/s
b) 1.0 cm/s
c) 2.0 cm/s
d) 4.0 cm/s

### 3. What mesh size is needed to filter 50 micron particles?
a) 100 mesh
b) 200 mesh
c) 300 mesh
d) 400 mesh

### 4. What is the typical backwash flow rate as percentage of design flow?
a) 50-75%
b) 100-150%
c) 200-300%
d) 400-500%

### 5. Calculate UV dose required for 99.9% bacteria reduction.
a) 10 mJ/cm²
b) 30 mJ/cm²
c) 60 mJ/cm²
d) 100 mJ/cm²

### 6. What is the contact time needed for 10 ppm ozone treatment?
a) 30 seconds
b) 1-2 minutes
c) 5-10 minutes
d) 15-20 minutes

### 7. What percentage of flow should bypass a protein skimmer?
a) 5-10%
b) 25-50%
c) 75-100%
d) 150-200%

### 8. Calculate foam fractionator sizing for 1000 gallons at 2× turnover.
a) 30 GPM capacity
b) 60 GPM capacity
c) 120 GPM capacity
d) 240 GPM capacity

### 9. What particle size do mechanical filters typically remove?
a) 1-10 microns
b) 10-50 microns
c) 50-100 microns
d) 100-500 microns

### 10. What is the minimum contact time for UV disinfection at 100 GPM?
a) 1 second
b) 3 seconds
c) 5 seconds
d) 10 seconds

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 3-4 times/hour**
**Explanation:** Solids settling tanks operate best at 3-4 turnovers/hour for optimal settling.

### 2. Answer: **b) 1.0 cm/s**
**Explanation:** 100 micron particles settle at approximately 1 cm/s under ideal conditions.

### 3. Answer: **c) 300 mesh**
**Explanation:** Mesh number = 25,400 ÷ microns; 300 mesh ≈ 50 micron openings.

### 4. Answer: **c) 200-300%**
**Explanation:** Backwash requires 2-3× normal flow rate to properly clean filter media.

### 5. Answer: **b) 30 mJ/cm²**
**Explanation:** 30 mJ/cm² UV dose achieves 99.9% (3-log) bacteria reduction.

### 6. Answer: **b) 1-2 minutes**
**Explanation:** 10 ppm ozone requires 1-2 minute contact time for effective disinfection.

### 7. Answer: **a) 5-10%**
**Explanation:** Protein skimmers typically process 5-10% of total system flow for efficiency.

### 8. Answer: **a) 30 GPM capacity**
**Explanation:** 1000 gal × 2/hour = 2000 gal/hr ÷ 60 = 33 GPM; skimmer handles 10% = 3.3 GPM (but answer suggests treating partial flow).

**Correction:** Question may be ambiguous; typical answer **a) 30 GPM** for partial flow processing.

### 9. Answer: **c) 50-100 microns**
**Explanation:** Typical mechanical filters (screen/drum) remove particles 50-100+ microns.

### 10. Answer: **b) 3 seconds**
**Explanation:** Minimum 3 second contact time in UV chamber for proper dose delivery.
