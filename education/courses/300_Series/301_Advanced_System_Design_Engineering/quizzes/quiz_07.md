## Questions

### 1. What is the weight of water per gallon?
a) 6.34 lbs
b) 7.48 lbs
c) 8.34 lbs
d) 9.18 lbs

### 2. Calculate total load for a 1000-gallon tank on a support structure.
a) 6,340 lbs
b) 7,480 lbs
c) 8,340 lbs
d) 9,180 lbs

### 3. What is the typical allowable floor load for residential construction?
a) 20 psf
b) 40 psf
c) 80 psf
d) 120 psf

### 4. Calculate floor loading for 500 gallons distributed over 50 ft².
a) 42 psf
b) 63 psf
c) 84 psf
d) 105 psf

### 5. What safety factor should be used for structural calculations?
a) 1.5
b) 2.0
c) 2.5
d) 3.0

### 6. What is the maximum recommended span for 2×4 shelving supporting 50 lbs/ft?
a) 24 inches
b) 36 inches
c) 48 inches
d) 60 inches

### 7. Calculate point load from a 250-gallon tank on four legs.
a) 417 lbs per leg
b) 521 lbs per leg
c) 625 lbs per leg
d) 729 lbs per leg

### 8. What material has the highest strength-to-weight ratio?
a) Wood
b) Steel
c) Aluminum
d) Reinforced concrete

### 9. What is the modulus of elasticity (E) for structural steel?
a) 15 × 10⁶ psi
b) 20 × 10⁶ psi
c) 25 × 10⁶ psi
d) 29 × 10⁶ psi

### 10. Calculate deflection concern: 10 ft span with 1/360 limit.
a) 0.17 inches
b) 0.33 inches
c) 0.50 inches
d) 0.67 inches

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **c) 8.34 lbs**
**Explanation:** Water weighs 8.34 lbs per gallon (62.4 lbs/ft³ ÷ 7.48 gal/ft³).

### 2. Answer: **c) 8,340 lbs**
**Explanation:** 1000 gallons × 8.34 lbs/gal = 8,340 lbs (add tank weight and safety factor in practice).

### 3. Answer: **b) 40 psf**
**Explanation:** Residential floors are typically rated for 40 psf live load; commercial may be 50-100 psf.

### 4. Answer: **c) 84 psf**
**Explanation:** 500 gal × 8.34 lbs/gal = 4,170 lbs; 4,170 ÷ 50 ft² = 83.4 psf ≈ 84 psf

### 5. Answer: **c) 2.5**
**Explanation:** Structural calculations typically use safety factor of 2.5 for aquaponic systems.

### 6. Answer: **b) 36 inches**
**Explanation:** 2×4 shelf at 50 lbs/ft should not exceed 36" span to prevent excessive deflection.

### 7. Answer: **b) 521 lbs per leg**
**Explanation:** 250 gal × 8.34 lbs/gal = 2,085 lbs; 2,085 ÷ 4 legs = 521 lbs per leg

### 8. Answer: **c) Aluminum**
**Explanation:** Aluminum has the highest strength-to-weight ratio, making it ideal for lightweight structures.

### 9. Answer: **d) 29 × 10⁶ psi**
**Explanation:** Structural steel has E = 29 × 10⁶ psi (modulus of elasticity).

### 10. Answer: **b) 0.33 inches**
**Explanation:** Max deflection = Span ÷ 360 = (10 ft × 12 in/ft) ÷ 360 = 0.33 inches
