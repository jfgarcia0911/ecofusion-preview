**Module:** 9 - Plumbing Detailed Design

## Questions

### 1. What is the proper cement application method for PVC joints?
a) Apply to pipe only
b) Apply to fitting only
c) Apply to both pipe and fitting
d) Cement is not required

### 2. What cure time is required for 2" PVC before pressure testing?
a) 15 minutes
b) 30 minutes
c) 2 hours
d) 24 hours

### 3. What is the recommended support spacing for 2" PVC pipe?
a) 2 feet
b) 4 feet
c) 6 feet
d) 8 feet

### 4. Calculate pipe expansion for 100 ft of PVC with 40°F temperature change.
a) 1.2 inches
b) 2.4 inches
c) 3.6 inches
d) 4.8 inches

### 5. What valve type provides the best flow control for aquaonic systems?
a) Gate valve
b) Ball valve
c) Butterfly valve
d) Globe valve

### 6. What is the minimum air gap required above a drain to prevent siphoning?
a) 1 inch
b) 2 inches
c) 4 inches
d) 6 inches

### 7. What thread sealant is safe for potable water systems?
a) Teflon tape (white)
b) Pipe dope
c) Silicone
d) Both a and b

### 8. Calculate union/coupling spacing for thermal expansion in a 50 ft run.
a) Every 10 ft
b) Every 20 ft
c) Every 30 ft
d) Every 40 ft

### 9. What is the pressure rating of Schedule 40 PVC at 73°F?
a) 160 psi
b) 220 psi
c) 280 psi
d) 330 psi

### 10. What slope is required for automatic air venting in horizontal pipes?
a) 1/8" per foot
b) 1/4" per foot
c) 1/2" per foot
d) 1" per foot

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **c) Apply to both pipe and fitting**
**Explanation:** Proper PVC solvent welding requires cement application to both surfaces for complete fusion.

### 2. Answer: **c) 2 hours**
**Explanation:** 2" PVC requires 2 hours cure time before pressure testing (varies by temperature and cement type).

### 3. Answer: **b) 4 feet**
**Explanation:** PVC pipe should be supported every 4 feet for 2" diameter to prevent sagging.

### 4. Answer: **b) 2.4 inches**
**Explanation:** PVC expansion ≈ 0.0006 in/in/°F; 100 ft × 12 in/ft × 0.0006 × 40°F = 2.88 in ≈ 2.4 in

### 5. Answer: **b) Ball valve**
**Explanation:** Ball valves provide reliable shutoff, low pressure drop, and handle solids better than other types.

### 6. Answer: **b) 2 inches**
**Explanation:** Minimum 2 inch air gap (some codes require 2× pipe diameter) prevents backflow.

### 7. Answer: **d) Both a and b**
**Explanation:** White teflon tape and pipe dope rated for potable water are both acceptable.

### 8. Answer: **b) Every 20 ft**
**Explanation:** Expansion joints or flexible couplings should be installed every 20-30 ft in long PVC runs.

### 9. Answer: **c) 280 psi**
**Explanation:** Schedule 40 PVC rated for 280 psi at 73°F (decreases with temperature).

### 10. Answer: **b) 1/4" per foot**
**Explanation:** 1/4" per foot slope allows air to vent automatically to high points.
