# Quiz 15: Material Selection

**Course:** 301 - Advanced System Design & Engineering
**Module:** 15 - Material Selection
**Total Points:** 10 (1 point each)
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10)

---

## Questions

### 1. What stainless steel grade is required for saltwater applications?
a) 304
b) 316
c) 410
d) 430

### 2. What is the UV resistance rating for food-grade HDPE?
a) Poor (1-2 years)
b) Fair (3-5 years)
c) Good (5-10 years)
d) Excellent (10+ years)

### 3. Calculate thermal expansion for 50 ft aluminum pipe with 50°F change.
a) 0.6 inches
b) 0.8 inches
c) 1.0 inches
d) 1.4 inches

### 4. What material is NOT suitable for continuous water contact?
a) PVC
b) CPVC
c) ABS
d) HDPE

### 5. What is the chemical resistance of polypropylene to acids?
a) Poor
b) Fair
c) Good
d) Excellent

### 6. What gasket material is food-safe and chemical resistant?
a) Nitrile rubber
b) EPDM
c) Silicone
d) Neoprene

### 7. Calculate weight of 304 stainless steel plate: 4 ft × 8 ft × 1/4" (density = 0.29 lb/in³).
a) 278 lbs
b) 334 lbs
c) 390 lbs
d) 446 lbs

### 8. What coating protects mild steel in humid environments?
a) Paint only
b) Galvanization
c) Powder coating
d) All of the above

### 9. What is the service temperature limit for PVC Schedule 40?
a) 100°F
b) 140°F
c) 180°F
d) 220°F

### 10. What material provides best insulation R-value per inch?
a) Fiberglass
b) Polystyrene
c) Polyurethane
d) Rock wool

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 316**
**Explanation:** 316 stainless steel contains molybdenum for superior corrosion resistance in saltwater.

### 2. Answer: **d) Excellent (10+ years)**
**Explanation:** UV-stabilized food-grade HDPE has excellent UV resistance lasting 10+ years.

### 3. Answer: **d) 1.4 inches**
**Explanation:** Aluminum expansion = 13 × 10⁻⁶ in/in/°F; 50 ft × 12 × 13×10⁻⁶ × 50 = 1.38 in

### 4. Answer: **c) ABS**
**Explanation:** ABS is not recommended for continuous water contact; use PVC, CPVC, or HDPE instead.

### 5. Answer: **d) Excellent**
**Explanation:** Polypropylene has excellent chemical resistance to most acids and bases.

### 6. Answer: **c) Silicone**
**Explanation:** Silicone gaskets are food-safe, FDA approved, and chemically resistant.

### 7. Answer: **b) 334 lbs**
**Explanation:** Volume = 48 × 96 × 0.25 = 1,152 in³; Weight = 1,152 × 0.29 = 334 lbs

### 8. Answer: **d) All of the above**
**Explanation:** Paint, galvanization, and powder coating all protect steel from corrosion.

### 9. Answer: **b) 140°F**
**Explanation:** PVC Schedule 40 maximum service temperature is 140°F; higher temps reduce pressure rating.

### 10. Answer: **c) Polyurethane**
**Explanation:** Polyurethane foam provides highest R-value per inch (R-6 to R-7 per inch).
