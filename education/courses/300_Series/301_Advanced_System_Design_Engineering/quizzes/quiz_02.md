## Questions

### 1. What are the components of Total Dynamic Head (TDH)?
a) Static head only
b) Static head + friction loss
c) Static head + friction loss + minor losses + pressure head
d) Pump head + system head

### 2. A system has 10 ft static head and K = 0.002. What is the TDH at 50 GPM?
a) 10 ft
b) 12 ft
c) 15 ft
d) 18 ft

### 3. What is the K-value for a standard 90° elbow?
a) 0.4
b) 0.6
c) 0.9
d) 1.8

### 4. Where should a pump operate for best efficiency?
a) At shutoff head (zero flow)
b) At maximum flow
c) At 80-90% of BEP
d) At 50% of maximum head

### 5. What is the minimum impeller clearance for handling solids in aquaponic systems?
a) 1/8" (3 mm)
b) 1/4" (6 mm)
c) 1/2" (12 mm)
d) 1" (25 mm)

### 6. Calculate the velocity in a 3" pipe (ID = 3.068") carrying 100 GPM.
a) 4.2 ft/s
b) 5.6 ft/s
c) 6.8 ft/s
d) 7.2 ft/s

### 7. What is the recommended velocity range for pump discharge lines?
a) 1-3 ft/s
b) 2-5 ft/s
c) 4-8 ft/s
d) 8-12 ft/s

### 8. A gravity drain must flow at what percentage full for proper air circulation?
a) 25%
b) 50%
c) 75%
d) 100%

### 9. What is the minimum recommended slope for gravity drains?
a) 1/8" per foot (1%)
b) 1/4" per foot (2%)
c) 1/2" per foot (4%)
d) 1" per foot (8%)

### 10. In manifold design, what diameter ratio ensures uniform flow distribution?
a) D_manifold = D_branch
b) D_manifold = 1.25 × D_branch
c) D_manifold ≥ 1.5 × D_branch
d) D_manifold ≥ 2.0 × D_branch

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **c) Static head + friction loss + minor losses + pressure head**
**Explanation:** TDH = Hₛ + Hf + Hₘ + Hₚ + Hᵥ (velocity head usually negligible)

### 2. Answer: **b) 12 ft**
**Explanation:**
- TDH = Hₛ + K × Q²
- TDH = 10 + 0.002 × 50²
- TDH = 10 + 0.002 × 2500 = 10 + 5 = 15 ft

**Correction:** Answer is actually **c) 15 ft**

### 3. Answer: **c) 0.9**
**Explanation:** Standard radius 90° elbow has K = 0.9; long radius has K = 0.6

### 4. Answer: **c) At 80-90% of BEP**
**Explanation:** Operating at 80-90% of Best Efficiency Point provides good efficiency with operational margin.

### 5. Answer: **b) 1/4" (6 mm)**
**Explanation:** Minimum 1/4" (6 mm) impeller clearance required for handling solids in aquaponic systems.

### 6. Answer: **b) 5.6 ft/s**
**Explanation:**
- A = π × (3.068/2)² = 7.39 in²
- V = (100 GPM × 231 in³/gal) / (7.39 in² × 60 s/min)
- V = 23,100 / 443.4 = 52.1 in/s = 4.34 ft/s

**Correction:** Calculation gives approximately 4.3 ft/s, closest answer is **a) 4.2 ft/s**

### 7. Answer: **c) 4-8 ft/s**
**Explanation:** Recommended discharge velocity is 4-8 ft/s; suction lines should be 2-5 ft/s.

### 8. Answer: **b) 50%**
**Explanation:** Gravity drains should flow approximately 50% full to allow air circulation and prevent siphoning.

### 9. Answer: **b) 1/4" per foot (2%)**
**Explanation:** Minimum slope is 1/4" per foot; 1/2" per foot is recommended for better flow.

### 10. Answer: **c) D_manifold ≥ 1.5 × D_branch**
**Explanation:** Manifold diameter should be at least 1.5 times branch diameter for uniform distribution.
