## Questions

### 1. What is the typical nitrification rate for moving bed biofilter media?
a) 0.1-0.3 g TAN/m²/day
b) 0.5-1.0 g TAN/m²/day
c) 1.5-2.0 g TAN/m²/day
d) 3.0-5.0 g TAN/m²/day

### 2. Calculate TAN production from 10 kg/day feed at 40% protein.
a) 0.184 kg TAN/day
b) 0.368 kg TAN/day
c) 0.552 kg TAN/day
d) 0.736 kg TAN/day

### 3. What is the surface area of K1 media per cubic meter?
a) 100 m²/m³
b) 300 m²/m³
c) 500 m²/m³
d) 800 m²/m³

### 4. What dissolved oxygen level is required for effective nitrification?
a) >2 mg/L
b) >4 mg/L
c) >6 mg/L
d) >8 mg/L

### 5. What is the optimal pH range for nitrifying bacteria?
a) 5.5-6.5
b) 6.5-7.5
c) 7.0-8.0
d) 8.0-9.0

### 6. How long does it take to establish a mature nitrifying biofilm?
a) 1-2 weeks
b) 3-6 weeks
c) 8-12 weeks
d) 16-20 weeks

### 7. What is the recommended hydraulic retention time (HRT) for a moving bed bioreactor?
a) 5-15 minutes
b) 20-40 minutes
c) 1-2 hours
d) 3-4 hours

### 8. Calculate biofilter volume needed for 0.5 kg TAN/day with media at 500 m²/m³ and 0.8 g TAN/m²/day.
a) 0.8 m³
b) 1.25 m³
c) 1.6 m³
d) 2.0 m³

### 9. What percentage of biofilter volume should be filled with K1 media?
a) 25-30%
b) 40-50%
c) 60-70%
d) 80-90%

### 10. What safety factor should be applied to biofilter sizing calculations?
a) 1.2
b) 1.5
c) 2.0
d) 2.5

---

## Answer Key

### 1. Answer: **b) 0.5-1.0 g TAN/m²/day**
**Explanation:** Typical nitrification rate for moving bed media is 0.5-1.0 g TAN/m²/day under optimal conditions.

### 2. Answer: **b) 0.368 kg TAN/day**
**Explanation:** TAN = Feed × Protein × 0.092 = 10 × 0.40 × 0.092 = 0.368 kg TAN/day

### 3. Answer: **c) 500 m²/m³**
**Explanation:** K1 media provides approximately 500 m² of surface area per cubic meter.

### 4. Answer: **b) >4 mg/L**
**Explanation:** Nitrifying bacteria require minimum 4 mg/L DO; >6 mg/L is optimal.

### 5. Answer: **c) 7.0-8.0**
**Explanation:** Nitrifying bacteria perform best at pH 7.0-8.0; activity decreases significantly below 6.5.

### 6. Answer: **b) 3-6 weeks**
**Explanation:** Establishing a mature nitrifying biofilm typically takes 3-6 weeks under optimal conditions.

### 7. Answer: **b) 20-40 minutes**
**Explanation:** Moving bed bioreactors typically operate with 20-40 minute HRT for adequate contact time.

### 8. Answer: **b) 1.25 m³**
**Explanation:**
- Surface area needed = 0.5 kg/day ÷ 0.8 g/m²/day = 500,000 g ÷ 0.8 = 625 m²
- Volume = 625 m² ÷ 500 m²/m³ = 1.25 m³

### 9. Answer: **b) 40-50%**
**Explanation:** MBBR tanks should be 40-50% filled to allow proper media movement and prevent clogging.

### 10. Answer: **b) 1.5**
**Explanation:** Apply 1.5 safety factor (50% extra capacity) to biofilter sizing for operational margin.
