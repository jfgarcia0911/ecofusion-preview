**Hands-On Learning Activity**

## Objective

Conduct a simplified energy audit of a CEA facility (your own or a case study) to identify energy-saving opportunities.

## Part 2: Baseline Analysis (10 points)

### Task 2.1: Calculate Key Metrics

Using your collected data, calculate:

**a) Annual Energy Consumption**
- Total electricity: __________ kWh/year
- Total natural gas: __________ therms/year
- Total energy cost: $ __________/year

**b) Energy Use Intensity (EUI)**
```
EUI = Annual kWh ÷ Facility sq ft

Your calculation:
EUI = __________ kWh ÷ __________ sq ft = __________ kWh/sq ft/year
```

**c) Energy per Production**
```
Energy Intensity = Annual kWh ÷ Annual Production (lbs)

Your calculation:
Energy/lb = __________ kWh ÷ __________ lbs = __________ kWh/lb
```

**d) Energy Cost per Production**
```
Energy Cost/lb = Total Energy Cost ÷ Annual Production

Your calculation:
Cost/lb = $ __________ ÷ __________ lbs = $ __________/lb
```

**Deliverable:** Completed calculations with work shown **[4 points]**

## Case Study Option

If you don't have access to a real facility, use this case study:

**Midwest Greenhouse**
- Size: 10,000 sq ft glass greenhouse
- Production: 80,000 lbs tomatoes/year
- Age: 15 years
- Operating: Year-round, 24/7

**Annual Energy Data:**
- Electricity: 350,000 kWh, $45,500
- Natural gas: 55,000 therms, $77,000
- Peak demand: 75 kW

**Equipment:**
- Lighting: 30 × 600W HPS fixtures (original, 15 years old)
- Heating: Natural gas boiler, 800 MBH, 78% AFUE
- Cooling: Exhaust fans, evaporative pads
- Pumps: 3 HP irrigation pump, constant speed
- Controls: Manual thermostats, no automation

**Observations:**
- No thermal curtains
- Air leaks visible around doors
- Lights manually controlled
- Thermostat set at constant 70°F
- Pump runs 24/7

Complete the audit exercise using this data.
