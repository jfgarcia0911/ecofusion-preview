# Grading Rubric
## Course 305: Energy Systems for CEA

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 305. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Energy Audit and Assessment | 15 | 13.6% | In-class facility energy analysis |
| Assignment 2: Renewable Energy System Design | 20 | 18.2% | In-class solar/wind integration planning |
| Assignment 3: HVAC Optimization Strategy | 25 | 22.7% | In-class climate control efficiency analysis |
| Assignment 4: Energy Storage Solution Design | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home battery and thermal storage planning |
| Assignment 5: Comprehensive Energy Management Plan | 30 | 27.3% | Complete net-zero energy facility strategy |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

---

## Assignment 1: Energy Audit and Assessment (15 points)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Energy Consumption Analysis** (5 pts) | Comprehensive breakdown of all energy uses with measurements | Good analysis of major energy consumers | Basic energy assessment | Incomplete analysis |
| **Efficiency Opportunities** (5 pts) | Multiple high-value efficiency improvements identified | Several reasonable opportunities found | Limited opportunities identified | No efficiency analysis |
| **Baseline Metrics** (5 pts) | Detailed energy metrics with benchmarking | Good baseline establishment | Basic metrics calculated | Poor baseline data |

**Total:** 15 points

---

## Assignment 2: Renewable Energy System Design (20 points)
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **System Sizing** (5 pts) | Accurate capacity calculations with load profiles | Good sizing methodology | Basic capacity estimates | Poor sizing approach |
| **Technology Selection** (5 pts) | Optimal renewable technology mix with justification | Reasonable technology choices | Limited technology consideration | Inappropriate technology |
| **Grid Integration** (5 pts) | Complete interconnection plan with net metering | Basic grid connection approach | Vague integration plan | No integration planning |
| **Financial Analysis** (5 pts) | Detailed ROI with incentives and payback | Basic financial assessment | Limited financial analysis | No financial evaluation |

**Total:** 20 points

## Assignment 5: Comprehensive Energy Management Plan (30 points)
**Duration:** 2-4 hours | **Type:** Comprehensive project

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (7-8 pts) | Good (5-6 pts) | Fair (3-4 pts) | Poor (0-2 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Energy Strategy** (7 pts) | Compelling vision for net-zero or energy-positive facility | Good energy reduction strategy | Basic efficiency goals | Weak energy planning |

## Grading Scale

| Percentage | Letter Grade | Point Range | Performance Level |
|------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 90-100% | A | 99-110 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |
| 80-89% | B | 88-98 | Good - Meets all requirements |
| 70-79% | C | 77-87 | Fair - Meets most requirements |
| 60-69% | D | 66-76 | Poor - Below expectations |
| Below 60% | F | 0-65 | Failing - Does not meet requirements |

**Note:** A minimum of 80% (88 points) is required to pass this course and receive a Certificate of Completion.

---

## Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% penalty
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% penalty
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval
- Extensions may be granted for extenuating circumstances with advance notice

---

## Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. While collaboration and discussion are encouraged during in-class activities, individual assignments must reflect your own analysis and work. Plagiarism or copying will result in a zero for the assignment and may result in dismissal from the course.

---

## Feedback and Revision

- In-class activities receive immediate feedback
- Take-home assignments graded within 5 business days
- Final projects graded within 7 business days
- Students may request clarification on grading decisions
- Major projects may be revised and resubmitted once if score is below 80%
