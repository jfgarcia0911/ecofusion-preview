## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Energy Audit and Assessment (15 points)

**Purpose:** Understand current energy consumption and identify improvement opportunities.

**What You'll Do:**
- Analyze energy consumption by system (HVAC, lighting, water, automation)
- Calculate energy intensity metrics (kWh per kg of production)
- Identify inefficiencies and waste
- Benchmark against industry standards
- Prioritize improvement opportunities

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Conduct professional energy audits
- Calculate meaningful energy metrics
- Identify high-impact efficiency opportunities

**Deliverable:** Energy audit report with consumption breakdown, metrics, and improvement recommendations

---

### Assignment 2: Renewable Energy System Design (20 points)

**Purpose:** Design renewable energy systems appropriate for CEA facilities.

**What You'll Do:**
- Analyze site conditions (solar irradiance, wind, geothermal potential)
- Size renewable energy systems to meet facility loads
- Select appropriate technologies and equipment
- Design grid interconnection and net metering
- Calculate financial returns and incentives

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Design solar PV systems for agriculture
- Evaluate renewable energy technologies
- Calculate renewable energy economics

**Deliverable:** Renewable energy system design with sizing calculations, equipment specifications, and financial analysis

---

### Assignment 3: HVAC Optimization Strategy (25 points)

**Purpose:** Maximize HVAC efficiency while maintaining optimal growing conditions.

**What You'll Do:**
- Assess current HVAC system performance
- Design heat recovery systems (air-to-air, water-to-water)
- Develop advanced control strategies
- Optimize for variable loads and conditions
- Calculate energy and cost savings

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Optimize HVAC systems for CEA
- Implement heat recovery technologies
- Design intelligent climate controls

**Deliverable:** HVAC optimization plan with heat recovery design, control algorithms, and savings projections

---

### Assignment 4: Energy Storage Solution Design (20 points)

**Purpose:** Design energy storage systems to optimize renewable integration and reduce costs.

**What You'll Do:**
- Select appropriate storage technologies (lithium-ion, thermal, etc.)
- Size storage capacity for facility needs
- Design integration with renewable and grid power
- Optimize for demand charge reduction and time-of-use rates
- Calculate economic returns and payback

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Design battery energy storage systems
- Implement thermal energy storage
- Optimize storage for economic benefit

**Deliverable:** Energy storage system design with capacity calculations, integration plan, and economic analysis

---

### Assignment 5: Comprehensive Energy Management Plan (30 points)

**Purpose:** Create a holistic energy management strategy for sustainable CEA operations.

**What You'll Do:**
- Develop vision for net-zero or energy-positive facility
- Integrate efficiency, renewable, and storage solutions
- Create phased implementation roadmap
- Design energy management and monitoring systems
- Calculate total energy and cost savings
- Address financing and incentive opportunities
- Develop performance metrics and verification plan

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Think strategically about energy management
- Integrate multiple energy solutions
- Plan practical implementation
- Measure and verify energy performance

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive energy management plan (15-25 pages) with technical designs, implementation roadmap, and financial analysis

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need engineering background for this course?**
A: No, but comfort with basic math and calculations is helpful. We provide formulas and examples for all calculations.

**Q: What tools can I use for energy calculations?**
A: Any tools are acceptable - Excel, online calculators, PVWatts, RETScreen, etc. We provide templates and resources.

**Q: Can I focus on a specific facility type?**
A: Yes! Tailor assignments to your facility type - greenhouse, vertical farm, aquaponics, etc.

**Q: What if I don't have access to facility energy data?**
A: We provide typical energy consumption profiles. However, using real data makes assignments more valuable.

**Q: Should I include specific equipment models and costs?**
A: Yes, using real equipment specifications and current pricing makes your work more practical and implementable.

**Q: Can I work with other students?**
A: In-class activities encourage discussion. Take-home assignments and the final project must be your own work.

**Q: How detailed should the energy management plan be?**
A: Aim for implementation-ready detail - specific enough that a contractor could use your plan.

**Q: How is the final grade calculated?**
A: Sum of all assignment points (max 110). You need 88+ points (80%) to pass and receive your certificate.

## Academic Integrity Reminder

All submitted work must be your own original work. While you may discuss approaches with classmates, your calculations, designs, and written submissions must be independently created. Using someone else's work or presenting unattributed content as your own constitutes plagiarism and will result in serious consequences.

When using external sources (equipment specs, utility rates, research data), always provide proper citations.
