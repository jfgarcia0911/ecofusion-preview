## Questions

### Question 1
What is the current Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) rate for solar PV systems?

A) 10%
B) 20%
C) 30%
D) 40%

**Correct Answer: C) 30%**

---

### Question 2
Solar PV system costs (2025) are approximately:

A) $1.00-1.50/W
B) $2.00-3.00/W
C) $4.00-5.00/W
D) $6.00-8.00/W

**Correct Answer: B) $2.00-3.00/W**

---

### Question 3
The "derate factor" in solar calculations accounts for:

A) Only shading losses
B) Only inverter efficiency
C) All system losses (wiring, inverter, soiling, temperature, etc.)
D) Only temperature effects

**Correct Answer: C) All system losses**

---

### Question 4
A 50 kW solar system in a location with 5.0 sun hours/day and 0.80 derate factor produces annually:

A) 60,500 kWh
B) 73,000 kWh
C) 91,250 kWh
D) 105,000 kWh

**Correct Answer: B) 73,000 kWh**
**Calculation: 50 kW × 5.0 hrs × 365 days × 0.80 = 73,000 kWh**

---

### Question 5
Net metering allows:

A) Selling excess solar to utility at retail rate
B) Buying power at wholesale rates only
C) Time-shifted consumption credits only
D) No grid connection

**Correct Answer: A) Selling excess solar to utility at retail rate**

---

### Question 6
What is the typical degradation rate for modern solar panels?

A) 0.1% per year
B) 0.5% per year
C) 1.0% per year
D) 2.0% per year

**Correct Answer: B) 0.5% per year**

---

### Question 7
Solar panels should face which direction in the Northern Hemisphere for optimal production?

A) North
B) South
C) East
D) West

**Correct Answer: B) South**

---

### Question 8
A facility uses 400,000 kWh annually. A 100 kW solar system producing 130,000 kWh/year would offset:

A) 22.5% of consumption
B) 27.5% of consumption
C) 32.5% of consumption
D) 37.5% of consumption

**Correct Answer: C) 32.5% of consumption**
**Calculation: 130,000 ÷ 400,000 = 0.325 = 32.5%**

---

### Question 9
What is the typical warranty period for solar panel production capacity?

A) 5-10 years
B) 10-15 years
C) 15-20 years
D) 25-30 years

**Correct Answer: D) 25-30 years**

---

### Question 10
Which factor has the LEAST impact on solar production?

A) Shading from nearby trees
B) Panel orientation and tilt
C) Temperature (panels are blue vs black)
D) Geographic location

**Correct Answer: C) Temperature (panel color)**

---
