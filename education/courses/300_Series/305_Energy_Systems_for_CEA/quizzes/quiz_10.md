## Questions

### Question 1
Which battery chemistry has the longest cycle life?

A) Lead-acid (flooded)
B) Lead-acid (AGM)
C) Lithium iron phosphate (LFP)
D) Nickel-metal hydride

**Correct Answer: C) Lithium iron phosphate (LFP)**

---

### Question 2
Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries typically last:

A) 1,000-3,000 cycles
B) 3,000-5,000 cycles
C) 7,000+ cycles
D) 15,000+ cycles

**Correct Answer: C) 7,000+ cycles**

---

### Question 3
Battery storage is most economically attractive with:

A) Low electricity rates
B) High demand charges
C) Flat rate structures
D) Rural locations only

**Correct Answer: B) High demand charges**

---

### Question 4
What is "depth of discharge" (DoD)?

A) The physical depth of battery installation
B) The percentage of battery capacity used
C) The rate of energy discharge
D) The temperature of the battery

**Correct Answer: B) The percentage of battery capacity used**

---

### Question 5
A 100 kWh battery with 90% depth of discharge can provide how much usable energy?

A) 70 kWh
B) 80 kWh
C) 90 kWh
D) 100 kWh

**Correct Answer: C) 90 kWh**
**Calculation: 100 kWh × 0.90 DoD = 90 kWh usable**

---

### Question 6
Round-trip efficiency measures:

A) Battery charging speed
B) Energy out ÷ energy in
C) Discharge rate capability
D) Physical space requirements

**Correct Answer: B) Energy out ÷ energy in**

---

### Question 7
Typical round-trip efficiency for lithium-ion batteries:

A) 60-70%
B) 70-80%
C) 85-95%
D) 95-99%

**Correct Answer: C) 85-95%**

---

### Question 8
Battery storage provides which benefit for CEA facilities?

A) Demand charge reduction through peak shaving
B) Backup power during outages
C) Time-of-use rate optimization
D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: D) All of the above**

---

### Question 9
Current cost for lithium-ion battery storage systems (2025):

A) $200-400/kWh
B) $500-800/kWh
C) $1,000-1,500/kWh
D) $2,000-3,000/kWh

**Correct Answer: B) $500-800/kWh**

---

### Question 10
What is the primary factor limiting widespread battery adoption in CEA?

A) Technology reliability
B) High upfront cost relative to savings
C) Space requirements
D) Safety concerns

**Correct Answer: B) High upfront cost relative to savings**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - LFP batteries have longest cycle life
2. C - LFP lasts 7,000+ cycles
3. B - High demand charges improve battery economics
4. B - DoD = percentage of capacity used
5. C - Usable energy = 90 kWh
6. B - Round-trip efficiency = output/input
7. C - Li-ion round-trip efficiency 85-95%
8. D - Batteries provide all listed benefits
9. B - Current cost $500-800/kWh
10. B - High cost is primary barrier
