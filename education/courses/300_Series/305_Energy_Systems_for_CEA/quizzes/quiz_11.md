## Questions

### Question 1
Demand charges are typically based on:

A) Total monthly kWh consumption
B) Average kW demand over the month
C) Peak 15-minute kW demand
D) Daily peak demand average

**Correct Answer: C) Peak 15-minute kW demand**

---

### Question 2
Power factor measures:

A) The ratio of real power to apparent power
B) The total energy consumed
C) The voltage stability
D) The frequency variation

**Correct Answer: A) The ratio of real power to apparent power**

---

### Question 3
What is a "good" power factor?

A) 0.5-0.7
B) 0.7-0.8
C) 0.9-1.0
D) >1.0

**Correct Answer: C) 0.9-1.0**

---

### Question 4
A facility uses 50 kW real power with a power factor of 0.70. What is the apparent power (kVA)?

A) 35 kVA
B) 50 kVA
C) 65 kVA
D) 71 kVA

**Correct Answer: D) 71 kVA**
**Calculation: kVA = kW ÷ PF = 50 ÷ 0.70 = 71.4 kVA**

---

### Question 5
Time-of-use (TOU) rates charge higher prices:

A) During periods of high grid demand
B) During nighttime hours
C) On weekends only
D) Randomly throughout the month

**Correct Answer: A) During periods of high grid demand**

---

### Question 6
Power factor correction typically pays back in:

A) <6 months
B) 1-3 years
C) 5-7 years
D) >10 years

**Correct Answer: B) 1-3 years**

---

### Question 7
Which equipment typically causes poor power factor?

A) LED lighting
B) Resistive heaters
C) Motors and transformers
D) Photovoltaic inverters

**Correct Answer: C) Motors and transformers**

---

### Question 8
A facility has a $15/kW demand charge and reaches 250 kW peak demand. What is the monthly demand charge?

A) $2,500
B) $3,000
C) $3,750
D) $4,500

**Correct Answer: C) $3,750**
**Calculation: 250 kW × $15/kW = $3,750**

---

### Question 9
Load shifting involves:

A) Moving consumption from peak to off-peak periods
B) Reducing total consumption
C) Improving equipment efficiency
D) Installing renewable energy

**Correct Answer: A) Moving consumption from peak to off-peak**

---

### Question 10
Which CEA load is MOST flexible for time-of-use optimization?

A) Critical climate control
B) Irrigation pumping and water treatment
C) Security lighting
D) Monitoring systems

**Correct Answer: B) Irrigation pumping and water treatment**
