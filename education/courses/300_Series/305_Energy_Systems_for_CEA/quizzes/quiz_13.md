## Questions

### Question 1
The Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) for solar is currently:

A) 10%
B) 20%
C) 30%
D) 50%

**Correct Answer: C) 30%**

---

### Question 2
MACRS depreciation for solar PV systems is:

A) 3 years
B) 5 years
C) 7 years
D) 15 years

**Correct Answer: B) 5 years**

---

### Question 3
Which financing option requires NO upfront capital?

A) Cash purchase
B) Conventional loan
C) Energy Service Company (ESCO) performance contract
D) Equipment lease

**Correct Answer: C) ESCO performance contract**

---

### Question 4
A $100,000 solar project with 30% ITC has a net cost of:

A) $30,000
B) $50,000
C) $70,000
D) $90,000

**Correct Answer: C) $70,000**
**Calculation: $100,000 - ($100,000 × 0.30) = $70,000**

---

### Question 5
USDA REAP grants can cover up to what percentage of project costs?

A) 10%
B) 25%
C) 50%
D) 75%

**Correct Answer: B) 25%**

---

### Question 6
Which incentive is typically provided by electric utilities?

A) Federal tax credits
B) State sales tax exemptions
C) Equipment rebates for efficiency upgrades
D) MACRS depreciation

**Correct Answer: C) Equipment rebates for efficiency upgrades**

---

### Question 7
Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy (C-PACE) financing is repaid through:

A) Monthly utility bills
B) Annual tax filings
C) Property tax assessments
D) Equipment lease payments

**Correct Answer: C) Property tax assessments**

---

### Question 8
A project receives a $20,000 utility rebate and 30% ITC on a $100,000 project. What is the net cost?

A) $50,000
B) $56,000
C) $60,000
D) $66,000

**Correct Answer: B) $56,000**
**Calculation: ITC applies to net: ($100,000 - $20,000) × 0.30 = $24,000. Net = $100,000 - $20,000 - $24,000 = $56,000**

---

### Question 9
Which statement about tax credits is TRUE?

A) Credits reduce taxable income
B) Credits directly reduce tax owed
C) Credits are the same as deductions
D) Credits only apply to individuals

**Correct Answer: B) Credits directly reduce tax owed**

---

### Question 10
On-bill financing allows:

A) Lower electricity rates
B) Loan payments through utility bill
C) Free energy audits
D) Tax-free purchases

**Correct Answer: B) Loan payments through utility bill**

---

7. C - C-PACE repaid through property taxes
