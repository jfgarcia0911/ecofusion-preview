## Questions

### Question 1
Solar thermal systems are most cost-effective for which CEA application?

A) Generating electricity
B) Lighting supplementation
C) Water heating and space heating
D) Cooling applications

**Correct Answer: C) Water heating and space heating**

---

### Question 2
What is the typical efficiency of flat-plate solar thermal collectors?

A) 20-30%
B) 40-50%
C) 60-70%
D) 80-90%

**Correct Answer: C) 60-70%**

---

### Question 3
Solar thermal is typically more cost-effective than solar PV when:

A) No thermal load exists
B) Thermal loads are consistent year-round
C) Only summer heating is needed
D) Never cost-effective

**Correct Answer: B) Thermal loads are consistent year-round**

---

### Question 4
A solar thermal system produces 500,000 BTU/day. How many therms is this equivalent to?

A) 3 therms
B) 5 therms
C) 8 therms
D) 12 therms

**Correct Answer: B) 5 therms**
**Calculation: 500,000 BTU ÷ 100,000 BTU/therm = 5 therms**

---

### Question 5
What is the main advantage of evacuated tube collectors over flat-plate collectors?

A) Lower cost
B) Simpler installation
C) Better performance in cold weather
D) Lighter weight

**Correct Answer: C) Better performance in cold weather**

---

### Question 6
Typical payback period for solar thermal systems in aquaponics facilities:

A) 1-3 years
B) 4-8 years
C) 10-15 years
D) 20+ years

**Correct Answer: B) 4-8 years**

---

### Question 7
Which component stores heat in a solar thermal system?

A) Collector
B) Heat exchanger
C) Storage tank
D) Circulation pump

**Correct Answer: C) Storage tank**

---

### Question 8
Solar thermal systems require freeze protection in cold climates through:

A) Drainback systems or antifreeze
B) Continuous pump operation
C) Electric heating elements
D) No protection needed

**Correct Answer: A) Drainback systems or antifreeze**

---

### Question 9
A 200 sq ft solar thermal array in a location with 4.5 sun hours/day and 65% efficiency produces how much heat daily?

A) 210,000 BTU
B) 265,000 BTU
C) 315,000 BTU
D) 380,000 BTU

**Correct Answer: C) 315,000 BTU**
**Calculation: 200 sq ft × 4.5 hrs × 3,413 BTU/kWh × 1 kW/m² × 0.0929 m²/sq ft × 0.65 = ~315,000 BTU**

---

### Question 10
Which CEA thermal application benefits MOST from solar thermal?

A) Greenhouse air heating (seasonal)
B) Aquaponics fish tank heating (year-round)
C) Summer cooling
D) Humidity control

**Correct Answer: B) Aquaponics fish tank heating (year-round)**
