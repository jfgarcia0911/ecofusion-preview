### Question 1
What is the typical cost range for a Level 2 detailed energy audit?

A) $500-1,500
B) $3,000-10,000
C) $10,000-30,000
D) $30,000-50,000

**Correct Answer: B) $3,000-10,000**

### Question 2
Which type of audit is required for performance contracts with guaranteed savings?

A) Level 1 Walk-Through
B) Level 2 Detailed
C) Level 3 Investment-Grade
D) Self-Audit

**Correct Answer: C) Level 3 Investment-Grade**

---

### Question 3
How many months of utility bill data should be gathered before an energy audit?

A) 3-6 months
B) 6-9 months
C) 12-24 months
D) 36 months

**Correct Answer: C) 12-24 months**

---

### Question 4
What does "load factor" measure?

A) The ratio of average demand to peak demand
B) The efficiency of electrical equipment
C) The power factor of motors
D) The capacity of transformers

**Correct Answer: A) The ratio of average demand to peak demand**

---

### Question 5
A facility uses 100,000 kWh per month with a peak demand of 200 kW. What is the load factor?

A) 0.50 (50%)
B) 0.69 (69%)
C) 0.80 (80%)
D) 0.90 (90%)

**Correct Answer: B) 0.69 (69%)**
**Calculation: Average kW = 100,000 kWh ÷ 730 hours = 137 kW. Load Factor = 137 ÷ 200 = 0.69**

---

### Question 6
Which tool measures electrical current without direct contact?

A) Multimeter
B) Clamp meter
C) Thermocouple
D) Lux meter

**Correct Answer: B) Clamp meter**

### Question 7
What is the recommended minimum monitoring duration for continuously operating equipment?

A) 1 hour
B) 24 hours
C) 1 week
D) 1 month

**Correct Answer: B) 24 hours**

### Question 8
Which metric is most useful for comparing energy efficiency between different CEA facilities?

A) Total annual kWh
B) Peak demand (kW)
C) Energy Use Intensity (kWh/sq ft)
D) Power factor

**Correct Answer: C) Energy Use Intensity (kWh/sq ft)**

---

### Question 9
What is the first priority when implementing energy efficiency measures?

A) Renewable energy systems
B) No-cost/low-cost operational improvements
C) Major equipment replacements
D) Building envelope upgrades

**Correct Answer: B) No-cost/low-cost operational improvements**

---

### Question 10
An audit identifies a measure saving $5,000/year with a cost of $12,000. What is the simple payback?

A) 1.4 years
B) 2.4 years
C) 3.4 years
D) 4.4 years

**Correct Answer: B) 2.4 years**
**Calculation: Payback = $12,000 ÷ $5,000/year = 2.4 years**
