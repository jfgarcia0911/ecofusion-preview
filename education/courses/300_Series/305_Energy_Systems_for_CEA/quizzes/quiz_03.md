## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical efficacy range for modern LED grow lights?

A) 1.0-1.5 µmol/J
B) 1.7-2.0 µmol/J
C) 2.5-3.5 µmol/J
D) 4.0-5.0 µmol/J

**Correct Answer: C) 2.5-3.5 µmol/J**

---

### Question 2
Compared to HPS lighting, LEDs typically save what percentage of energy for the same light output?

A) 10-20%
B) 30-50%
C) 60-70%
D) 80-90%

**Correct Answer: B) 30-50%**

---

### Question 3
What is the target Daily Light Integral (DLI) for lettuce production?

A) 8-12 mol/m²/day
B) 14-17 mol/m²/day
C) 20-25 mol/m²/day
D) 30-35 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer: B) 14-17 mol/m²/day**

---

### Question 4
A grow space needs 800 µmol/m²/s PPFD for 16 hours/day. What is the DLI?

A) 36.8 mol/m²/day
B) 42.2 mol/m²/day
C) 46.1 mol/m²/day
D) 52.8 mol/m²/day

**Correct Answer: C) 46.1 mol/m²/day**
**Calculation: DLI = 800 µmol/m²/s × 16 hrs × 3,600 s/hr ÷ 1,000,000 = 46.08 mol/m²/day**

---

### Question 5
Which light spectrum is most energy-efficient for plant growth?

A) Full spectrum white
B) Blue + red only
C) Blue + red + far red
D) All are equally efficient

**Correct Answer: B) Blue + red only**
**Explanation: Plants primarily use blue and red wavelengths for photosynthesis**

---

### Question 6
What is the typical lifespan of LED grow lights?

A) 10,000-20,000 hours
B) 30,000-40,000 hours
C) 50,000-70,000 hours
D) 100,000+ hours

**Correct Answer: C) 50,000-70,000 hours**

---

### Question 7
LED dimming typically saves energy:

A) Proportionally to light reduction (50% dim = 50% savings)
B) Less than proportional (50% dim = 30% savings)
C) More than proportional (50% dim = 60% savings)
D) No savings, just reduced light

**Correct Answer: A) Proportionally to light reduction**

---

### Question 8
You replace 100 × 400W HPS fixtures with 100 × 200W LED fixtures, operating 18 hrs/day, 350 days/year. Annual energy savings?

A) 105,000 kWh
B) 126,000 kWh
C) 147,000 kWh
D) 168,000 kWh

**Correct Answer: B) 126,000 kWh**
**Calculation: (400W - 200W) × 100 × 18 hrs × 350 days = 126,000,000 Wh = 126,000 kWh**

---

### Question 9
What is the most common cause of premature LED failure in CEA?

A) Electrical surges
B) Excessive heat/poor cooling
C) Humidity exposure
D) UV degradation

**Correct Answer: B) Excessive heat/poor cooling**

---

### Question 10
Utility rebates for LED upgrades typically reduce payback by:

A) 10-20%
B) 30-50%
C) 60-80%
D) 90-100%

**Correct Answer: B) 30-50%**
