## Questions

### Question 1
What percentage of operating costs does energy typically represent in CEA facilities?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-15%
C) 15-40%
D) 50-60%

**Correct Answer: C) 15-40%**

---

### Question 2
A 5,000 sq ft indoor farm uses 500,000 kWh annually. What is the Energy Use Intensity (EUI)?

A) 50 kWh/sq ft/year
B) 75 kWh/sq ft/year
C) 100 kWh/sq ft/year
D) 125 kWh/sq ft/year

**Correct Answer: C) 100 kWh/sq ft/year**
**Calculation: 500,000 kWh ÷ 5,000 sq ft = 100 kWh/sq ft/year**

---

### Question 3
Which facility type typically has the highest energy intensity (kWh per pound of produce)?

A) Greenhouse with minimal lighting
B) Greenhouse with supplemental lighting
C) Indoor/Vertical farm
D) Outdoor field production

**Correct Answer: C) Indoor/Vertical farm**
**Explanation: Indoor farms use 5-15 kWh/lb vs. 0.5-2 kWh/lb for minimal lighting greenhouses**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Energy represents 15-40% of operating costs
2. C - EUI = 100 kWh/sq ft/year
3. C - Indoor/vertical farms have highest intensity
