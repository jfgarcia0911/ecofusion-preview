## Questions

### Question 1
What does a lower U-value indicate?

A) Better insulation (less heat transfer)
B) Worse insulation (more heat transfer)
C) Higher light transmission
D) Greater structural strength

**Correct Answer: A) Better insulation (less heat transfer)**

---

### Question 2
Double poly glazing has a typical U-value of:

A) 0.35 Btu/hr-sq ft-°F
B) 0.70 Btu/hr-sq ft-°F
C) 1.15 Btu/hr-sq ft-°F
D) 1.50 Btu/hr-sq ft-°F

**Correct Answer: B) 0.70 Btu/hr-sq ft-°F**

---

### Question 3
What is ACH (Air Changes per Hour)?

A) The rate at which indoor air is replaced
B) The heating capacity in BTUs
C) The cooling efficiency ratio
D) The air conditioning hours per day

**Correct Answer: A) The rate at which indoor air is replaced**

---

### Question 4
A 10,000 sq ft greenhouse with 100,000 cu ft volume has 1.5 ACH. How much air leaks out per hour?

A) 100,000 cu ft
B) 150,000 cu ft
C) 200,000 cu ft
D) 250,000 cu ft

**Correct Answer: B) 150,000 cu ft**
**Calculation: 100,000 cu ft × 1.5 ACH = 150,000 cu ft/hr**

---

### Question 5
Which envelope upgrade typically offers the best return on investment?

A) Triple-pane glazing
B) Air sealing and infiltration reduction
C) Roof insulation upgrade
D) Complete window replacement

**Correct Answer: B) Air sealing and infiltration reduction**

---

### Question 6
Reducing infiltration from 2.0 ACH to 0.8 ACH typically pays back in:

A) <1 year
B) 2-3 years
C) 5-7 years
D) >10 years

**Correct Answer: A) <1 year**

---

### Question 7
What is the typical R-value of standard double poly greenhouse glazing?

A) R-1 to R-2
B) R-3 to R-4
C) R-5 to R-7
D) R-10 to R-15

**Correct Answer: A) R-1 to R-2**

---

### Question 8
Infrared-blocking glazing is most beneficial for:

A) Cold climate heating reduction
B) Hot climate cooling reduction
C) Light transmission improvement
D) Hail protection

**Correct Answer: B) Hot climate cooling reduction**

---

### Question 9
A 20,000 sq ft greenhouse roof with U-value 0.70 experiences a 40°F temperature difference. What is the hourly heat loss?

A) 420,000 BTU/hr
B) 490,000 BTU/hr
C) 560,000 BTU/hr
D) 630,000 BTU/hr

**Correct Answer: C) 560,000 BTU/hr**
**Calculation: Heat Loss = Area × U-value × ΔT = 20,000 × 0.70 × 40 = 560,000 BTU/hr**

---

### Question 10
Which material provides the best insulation value per inch?

A) Fiberglass batts (R-3.5/inch)
B) Cellulose (R-3.7/inch)
C) Spray foam (R-6.5/inch)
D) Rigid foam board (R-5/inch)

**Correct Answer: C) Spray foam (R-6.5/inch)**
