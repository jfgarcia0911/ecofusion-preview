## Questions

### Question 1
Lowering heating setpoint by 1°F typically saves what percentage of heating energy?

A) 1-2%
B) 3-5%
C) 7-10%
D) 12-15%

**Correct Answer: B) 3-5%**

---

### Question 2
What is the typical efficiency range for a modern condensing boiler?

A) 75-80%
B) 80-85%
C) 85-90%
D) 92-98%

**Correct Answer: D) 92-98%**

---

### Question 3
Thermal curtains can reduce nighttime heat loss by approximately:

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50%
D) 70-80%

**Correct Answer: C) 50%**

---

### Question 4
A greenhouse uses 80,000 therms of natural gas annually for heating at $1.20/therm. Installing thermal curtains saves 35% of heating energy. What is the annual savings?

A) $28,000
B) $33,600
C) $42,000
D) $48,000

**Correct Answer: B) $33,600**
**Calculation: 80,000 therms × 0.35 × $1.20 = $33,600**

---

### Question 5
What is the primary advantage of variable speed fans over on/off fans?

A) Lower initial cost
B) Quieter operation
C) Better humidity control
D) Energy savings at partial loads

**Correct Answer: D) Energy savings at partial loads**

---

### Question 6
Heat recovery from lighting can provide what benefit?

A) Extended lamp life
B) Reduced cooling loads only
C) Free heating during cool periods
D) No significant benefit

**Correct Answer: C) Free heating during cool periods**

---

### Question 7
What is the recommended minimum insulation R-value for CEA walls in cold climates?

A) R-5 to R-10
B) R-10 to R-15
C) R-20 to R-30
D) R-40 to R-50

**Correct Answer: C) R-20 to R-30**

---

### Question 8
Demand-controlled ventilation (DCV) adjusts ventilation based on:

A) Time of day
B) Outside temperature
C) CO₂ levels or occupancy
D) Humidity only

**Correct Answer: C) CO₂ levels or occupancy**

---

### Question 9
What is the typical payback period for upgrading from a standard efficiency boiler (85%) to a condensing boiler (95%)?

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-5 years
C) 7-10 years
D) 12-15 years

**Correct Answer: B) 3-5 years**

---

### Question 10
Which HVAC upgrade typically offers the fastest payback?

A) New high-efficiency chiller
B) Air sealing and infiltration reduction
C) Geothermal heat pump
D) Complete ductwork replacement

**Correct Answer: B) Air sealing and infiltration reduction**

---

5. D - Variable speed saves energy at partial loads
6. C - Heat recovery provides free heating
7. C - R-20 to R-30 recommended for cold climates
8. C - DCV responds to CO₂ or occupancy
9. B - Condensing boiler payback is 3-5 years
