## Questions

### Question 1
What is the first step in developing an energy management plan?

A) Install renewable energy
B) Baseline current energy use
C) Replace all equipment
D) Hire an energy manager

**Correct Answer: B) Baseline current energy use**

---

### Question 2
An energy baseline should include data from:

A) 1 month of operation
B) 3 months of operation
C) At least 12 months of operation
D) 5 years of operation

**Correct Answer: C) At least 12 months of operation**

---

### Question 3
What does "low-hanging fruit" refer to in energy management?

A) Agricultural production
B) Easy, quick-win efficiency improvements
C) Renewable energy systems
D) Long-term strategic planning

**Correct Answer: B) Easy, quick-win efficiency improvements**

---

### Question 4
Energy Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) should be:

A) General and flexible
B) Specific and measurable
C) Changed frequently
D) Based on industry averages only

**Correct Answer: B) Specific and measurable**

---

### Question 5
Which metric is MOST useful for tracking CEA energy performance?

A) Total kWh only
B) Total cost only
C) kWh per pound of produce
D) Peak demand only

**Correct Answer: C) kWh per pound of produce**

---

### Question 6
How often should energy data be reviewed?

A) Annually
B) Quarterly
C) Monthly
D) All of the above at different levels of detail

**Correct Answer: D) All of the above at different levels**

---

### Question 7
Continuous commissioning involves:

A) One-time equipment setup
B) Ongoing optimization and adjustment
C) Only new construction
D) Annual equipment replacement

**Correct Answer: B) Ongoing optimization and adjustment**

---

### Question 8
Which is NOT a component of a comprehensive energy plan?

A) Current baseline and benchmarking
B) Efficiency opportunities with costs/savings
C) Implementation timeline
D) Guarantee of 100% energy independence

**Correct Answer: D) Guarantee of 100% energy independence**

---

### Question 9
Staff engagement in energy management is important because:

A) They operate the equipment daily
B) They can identify operational inefficiencies
C) Behavioral changes can save 5-15% energy
D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: D) All of the above**

---

### Question 10
An effective energy management program should include:

A) One-time audit only
B) Continuous monitoring and adjustment
C) Focus on equipment replacement only
D) No staff involvement

**Correct Answer: B) Continuous monitoring and adjustment**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Start by baselining current use
2. C - Need 12+ months for baseline
3. B - Low-hanging fruit = quick wins
4. B - KPIs must be specific and measurable
5. C - kWh/lb most useful CEA metric
6. D - Review at multiple frequencies
7. B - Continuous commissioning = ongoing optimization
8. D - 100% independence rarely realistic
9. D - Staff engagement provides all benefits
10. B - Requires continuous monitoring
