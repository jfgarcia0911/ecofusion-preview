## Questions

### Question 1
What is Total Dynamic Head (TDH) in a pumping system?

A) The vertical height the pump must lift water
B) Static head + pressure head + friction losses
C) The pressure at the pump discharge
D) The flow rate in gallons per minute

**Correct Answer: B) Static head + pressure head + friction losses**

---

### Question 2
According to the pump affinity laws, if pump speed is reduced to 80%, power consumption becomes:

A) 80% of original (0.8¹)
B) 64% of original (0.8²)
C) 51% of original (0.8³)
D) 40% of original

**Correct Answer: C) 51% of original (0.8³)**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical payback period for installing a VFD on a pump in a variable flow application?

A) <6 months
B) 1-3 years
C) 5-7 years
D) >10 years

**Correct Answer: B) 1-3 years**

---

### Question 4
A 10 HP pump operates 24/7 at full speed. With a VFD, average speed will be 70%. What is the new power consumption? (1 HP = 0.746 kW)

A) 2.56 kW
B) 3.68 kW
C) 5.22 kW
D) 7.46 kW

**Correct Answer: A) 2.56 kW**
**Calculation: 10 HP × (0.70)³ = 3.43 HP × 0.746 = 2.56 kW**

---

### Question 5
Increasing pipe diameter in a pumping system results in:

A) Higher friction losses
B) Lower friction losses
C) No change in friction losses
D) Higher energy costs

**Correct Answer: B) Lower friction losses**

---

### Question 6
What is the typical efficiency range for centrifugal pumps?

A) 40-60%
B) 60-75%
C) 70-85%
D) 90-95%

**Correct Answer: C) 70-85%**

---

### Question 7
Which pumping configuration is most energy-efficient for variable flow applications?

A) Single large pump running continuously
B) Multiple smaller pumps with staging
C) Constant speed pump with bypass valve
D) Oversized pump with throttle valve

**Correct Answer: B) Multiple smaller pumps with staging**

---

### Question 8
Pumping system efficiency can be improved by all EXCEPT:

A) Right-sizing pumps to actual needs
B) Reducing pipe friction through larger pipes
C) Installing VFDs for variable loads
D) Maximizing pump speed for better performance

**Correct Answer: D) Maximizing pump speed for better performance**

---

### Question 9
A facility replaces a 5 HP pump running 8,760 hrs/year with a 3 HP pump. At $0.12/kWh, what is the annual savings?

A) $982
B) $1,314
C) $1,568
D) $1,966

**Correct Answer: C) $1,568**
**Calculation: (5-3) HP × 0.746 kW/HP × 8,760 hrs × $0.12 = $1,568**

---

### Question 10
What is the most common cause of excessive pump energy use?

A) Wrong pump type for application
B) Oversized pumps running inefficiently
C) Dirty filters
D) Low voltage supply

**Correct Answer: B) Oversized pumps running inefficiently**
