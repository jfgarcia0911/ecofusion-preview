## Questions

### Question 1
What does CHP stand for?

A) Central Heat Plant
B) Combined Heat and Power
C) Continuous Heating Process
D) Combustion Heat Production

**Correct Answer: B) Combined Heat and Power**

---

### Question 2
CHP systems are also known as:

A) Dual-fuel systems
B) Cogeneration
C) Thermal generators
D) Hybrid heating

**Correct Answer: B) Cogeneration**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical overall efficiency of CHP systems?

A) 40-50%
B) 55-65%
C) 70-80%
D) 85-95%

**Correct Answer: C) 70-80%**

---

### Question 4
CHP systems should be sized based on:

A) Peak electrical demand
B) Average electrical demand
C) Thermal load requirements (heat-to-power ratio)
D) Available fuel supply

**Correct Answer: C) Thermal load requirements**

---

### Question 5
A CHP system generates 100 kW electricity and 200 kW useful heat. What is the heat-to-power ratio?

A) 0.5:1
B) 1:1
C) 2:1
D) 3:1

**Correct Answer: C) 2:1**
**Calculation: 200 kW heat ÷ 100 kW power = 2:1**

---

### Question 6
Which CEA operation is BEST suited for CHP?

A) Seasonal greenhouse (heating only in winter)
B) Year-round indoor farm with consistent loads
C) Small hobby greenhouse
D) Outdoor field operation

**Correct Answer: B) Year-round facility with consistent loads**

---

### Question 7
CHP systems provide which of the following benefits?

A) Electricity generation only
B) Heat generation only
C) Both electricity and useful heat
D) Cooling only

**Correct Answer: C) Both electricity and useful heat**

---

### Question 8
What is the typical payback period for CHP systems in appropriate applications?

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-7 years
C) 10-15 years
D) 20+ years

**Correct Answer: B) 3-7 years**

---

### Question 9
CHP economic viability improves with:

A) Low electricity rates
B) High spark spread (electricity cost - gas cost)
C) Seasonal operation only
D) Low thermal loads

**Correct Answer: B) High spark spread**

---

### Question 10
Which is NOT a typical CHP technology for CEA?

A) Microturbines
B) Reciprocating engines
C) Fuel cells
D) Wind turbines

**Correct Answer: D) Wind turbines**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Combined Heat and Power
2. B - Also called cogeneration
3. C - Overall efficiency 70-80%
4. C - Size based on thermal load/heat-to-power ratio
5. C - Heat-to-power ratio = 2:1
6. B - Best for year-round consistent loads
7. C - Provides both electricity and heat
8. B - Typical payback 3-7 years
9. B - High spark spread improves economics
10. D - Wind turbines not a CHP technology
