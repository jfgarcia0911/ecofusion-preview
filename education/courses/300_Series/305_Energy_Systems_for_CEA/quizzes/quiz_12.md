## Questions

### Question 1
Simple payback period is calculated as:

A) Annual Savings ÷ Investment Cost
B) Investment Cost ÷ Annual Savings
C) NPV ÷ IRR
D) ROI × Years

**Correct Answer: B) Investment Cost ÷ Annual Savings**

---

### Question 2
A project costs $40,000 and saves $8,000 annually. What is the simple payback?

A) 3 years
B) 4 years
C) 5 years
D) 6 years

**Correct Answer: C) 5 years**
**Calculation: $40,000 ÷ $8,000 = 5 years**

---

### Question 3
Net Present Value (NPV) accounts for:

A) Only initial investment
B) Time value of money
C) Only annual savings
D) Equipment lifespan only

**Correct Answer: B) Time value of money**

---

### Question 4
A project with NPV > 0 indicates:

A) The project loses money
B) The project breaks even
C) The project is profitable
D) More analysis needed

**Correct Answer: C) The project is profitable**

---

### Question 5
Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is:

A) The interest rate paid on loans
B) The discount rate where NPV = 0
C) The annual energy savings rate
D) The inflation adjustment factor

**Correct Answer: B) The discount rate where NPV = 0**

---

### Question 6
Which economic metric best accounts for long-term cash flows?

A) Simple payback
B) Return on Investment (ROI)
C) Net Present Value (NPV)
D) Energy savings percentage

**Correct Answer: C) Net Present Value (NPV)**

---

### Question 7
Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) is useful for:

A) Calculating monthly utility bills
B) Comparing different energy generation options
C) Determining equipment efficiency
D) Tracking consumption patterns

**Correct Answer: B) Comparing different energy generation options**

---

### Question 8
An investment of $50,000 returns $12,000 annually for 10 years. Using a 6% discount rate, what is the approximate NPV?

A) $28,000
B) $38,000
C) $48,000
D) $58,000

**Correct Answer: B) $38,000**
**Note: Using NPV formula with 6% discount rate over 10 years**

---

### Question 9
When comparing projects, which should you select?

A) Highest simple payback
B) Lowest NPV
C) Highest NPV with IRR > hurdle rate
D) Longest lifespan

**Correct Answer: C) Highest NPV with IRR > hurdle rate**

---

### Question 10
What is a typical hurdle rate (minimum acceptable IRR) for CEA energy projects?

A) 2-5%
B) 8-15%
C) 20-30%
D) 40-50%

**Correct Answer: B) 8-15%**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Payback = Investment ÷ Annual Savings
2. C - Simple payback = 5 years
3. B - NPV accounts for time value of money
4. C - NPV > 0 means profitable
5. B - IRR is discount rate where NPV = 0
6. C - NPV best for long-term analysis
7. B - LCOE compares generation options
8. B - NPV ≈ $38,000
9. C - Select highest NPV with IRR > hurdle rate
10. B - Typical hurdle rate 8-15%
