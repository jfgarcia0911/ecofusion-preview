## Course Description

Master energy efficiency and renewable energy integration for controlled environment agriculture. This course covers energy auditing, efficiency improvements, renewable energy systems, and economic analysis for CEA facilities. Learn to reduce operating costs while improving sustainability.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Conduct comprehensive energy audits
2. Design energy-efficient systems
3. Evaluate renewable energy options
4. Implement energy management programs
5. Calculate return on energy investments
6. Develop facility energy plans

## Efficiency Opportunities

### Quick Wins (Low Cost, High Impact)

| Measure | Typical Savings | Payback |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| LED lighting upgrade | 30-50% lighting | 2-4 years |
| VFD on pumps | 20-40% pumping | 1-3 years |
| Thermostat setbacks | 5-15% heating | Immediate |
| Plug load management | 5-10% misc | <1 year |
| Maintenance optimization | 5-15% overall | Immediate |

### Major Investments

| Measure | Typical Savings | Payback |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Solar PV | 50-100% electric | 5-10 years |
| Thermal curtains | 20-40% heating | 3-5 years |
| High-efficiency HVAC | 20-30% HVAC | 5-8 years |
| Heat recovery | 10-30% heating | 3-6 years |
| Building insulation | 15-30% heating | 5-10 years |

## ROI Analysis Framework

### Simple Payback
```
Payback (years) = Investment Cost ÷ Annual Savings
```

### Net Present Value
```
NPV = -Investment + Σ(Annual Savings ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)^Year)
```

### Levelized Cost of Energy
```
LCOE = (Capital Cost + O&M) ÷ Lifetime Energy Production
```
