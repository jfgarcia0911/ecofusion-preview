### Problem 1: Energy Intensity Calculation (5 points)

A 8,000 sq ft greenhouse consumes 45,000 kWh in January and produces 12,000 lbs of tomatoes.

Calculate:
a) Monthly EUI (kWh/sq ft) **[2 points]**
b) Energy intensity (kWh/lb) **[2 points]**
c) If electricity costs $0.14/kWh, what is the energy cost per pound? **[1 point]**

**Solution:**
a) EUI = 45,000 kWh ÷ 8,000 sq ft = 5.625 kWh/sq ft/month
b) Energy intensity = 45,000 kWh ÷ 12,000 lbs = 3.75 kWh/lb
c) Energy cost = (45,000 × $0.14) ÷ 12,000 = $0.525/lb

### Problem 2: LED Retrofit ROI (5 points)

Current System: 20 × 600W HPS fixtures, 16 hrs/day, 300 days/year
Proposed System: 20 × 250W LED fixtures (same light output)
Electricity rate: $0.13/kWh
HPS lamp replacement: $60/lamp, replace annually
LED cost: $800/fixture installed
Utility rebate: $50/fixture

Calculate:
a) Annual energy savings (kWh) **[2 points]**
b) Annual cost savings (energy + maintenance) **[2 points]**
c) Simple payback period **[1 point]**

**Solution:**
a) Current: 20 × 600W × 16 × 300 = 57,600 kWh/year
   Proposed: 20 × 250W × 16 × 300 = 24,000 kWh/year
   Savings: 33,600 kWh/year

b) Energy savings: 33,600 × $0.13 = $4,368/year
   Maintenance savings: 20 × $60 = $1,200/year
   Total: $5,568/year

c) Cost: (20 × $800) - (20 × $50) = $16,000 - $1,000 = $15,000
   Payback: $15,000 ÷ $5,568 = 2.7 years

### Problem 3: VFD Pump Savings (5 points)

A 5 HP pump runs 24/7 at constant speed.
With VFD, average speed will be 75% of full.
Current runtime: 8,760 hours/year
Electricity rate: $0.12/kWh

Calculate:
a) Current annual energy consumption (kWh) **[2 points]**
b) Energy consumption with VFD (kWh) **[2 points]**
c) Annual cost savings **[1 point]**

**Solution:**
a) Current: 5 HP × 0.746 kW/HP × 8,760 hrs = 32,695 kWh/year

b) With VFD (affinity law - cubic relationship):
   Power at 75% speed = 5 HP × (0.75)³ = 5 × 0.422 = 2.11 HP
   Energy: 2.11 × 0.746 × 8,760 = 13,794 kWh/year

c) Savings: (32,695 - 13,794) × $0.12 = $2,268/year

### Problem 4: Solar PV System Sizing (5 points)

Design a solar system to offset 40% of annual consumption.
Annual consumption: 300,000 kWh
Location: 4.5 sun hours/day average
Derate factor: 0.82
System cost: $2.50/W installed
Federal ITC: 30%

Calculate:
a) Target annual solar production (kWh) **[1 point]**
b) Required system size (kW DC) **[2 points]**
c) Net cost after ITC **[2 points]**

**Solution:**
a) Target: 300,000 × 0.40 = 120,000 kWh/year

b) Production per kW: 1 kW × 4.5 hrs × 365 days × 0.82 = 1,348 kWh/year/kW
   Required: 120,000 ÷ 1,348 = 89 kW system

c) Gross cost: 89,000W × $2.50 = $222,500
   ITC: $222,500 × 0.30 = $66,750
   Net cost: $222,500 - $66,750 = $155,750
