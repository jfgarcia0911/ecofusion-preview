# Energy Systems Glossary

**Course 305: Energy Systems for CEA**
**Comprehensive Term Reference**

---

## A

**ACH (Air Changes per Hour)**: The number of times the entire volume of air in a space is replaced in one hour. Affects infiltration heat loss.

**Affinity Laws**: Mathematical relationships describing how pump performance changes with speed. Critical for VFD analysis.

**AFUE (Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency)**: Measure of boiler/furnace seasonal efficiency, expressed as percentage.

**Amp (Ampere)**: Unit of electrical current flow.

---

## B

**Ballast**: Device that regulates current to HPS or fluorescent lamps. Can reduce overall system efficiency.

**Baseload**: Minimum continuous electrical or thermal load that runs 24/7.

**Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR)**: Present value of benefits divided by present value of costs. BCR >1.0 indicates good investment.

**Blended Rate**: Average cost per kWh including all charges divided by total consumption.

**Btu (British Thermal Unit)**: Amount of heat required to raise one pound of water by 1°F. Standard unit for thermal energy.

---

## C

**CHP (Combined Heat and Power)**: System that simultaneously generates electricity and captures waste heat for useful purposes. Also called cogeneration.

**CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute)**: Volumetric flow rate of air or gas.

**COP (Coefficient of Performance)**: Ratio of useful heat delivered to energy consumed. Higher is better. Common for heat pumps.

**Curtain (Thermal)**: Automated fabric screen that deploys over glazing to reduce nighttime heat loss.

---

## D

**Demand Charge**: Fee based on peak power consumption (kW) during a billing period, not total energy used.

**Demand Response (DR)**: Programs where customers reduce load during peak periods in exchange for payments or rate discounts.

**Derate Factor**: Efficiency losses in real-world solar PV systems (typically 0.75-0.85). Accounts for temperature, soiling, inverter losses, etc.

**DLI (Daily Light Integral)**: Total photons received per day, measured in mol/m²/day. Key metric for crop lighting requirements.

**DOD (Depth of Discharge)**: Percentage of battery capacity used. Deep cycling reduces battery life.

---

## E

**Efficacy (Lighting)**: Light output per unit of electrical input. Measured in µmol/J for horticultural lighting.

**EUI (Energy Use Intensity)**: Annual energy consumption per unit floor area. Measured in kWh/sq ft/year or kWh/m²/year.

**ESPC (Energy Savings Performance Contract)**: Agreement where contractor guarantees energy savings to pay for improvements.

---

## F

**Friction Loss**: Pressure drop in piping due to fluid friction. Increases with flow rate and decreases with larger pipe diameter.

**Fuel Cell**: Electrochemical device that converts fuel (usually natural gas or hydrogen) directly to electricity and heat.

---

## G

**GPM (Gallons per Minute)**: Volumetric flow rate of liquid.

**Grid Services**: Revenue programs where facilities provide load flexibility to support electrical grid operations.

---

## H

**HDD (Heating Degree Days)**: Measure of how cold a location is. Sum of degrees below 65°F over a year.

**Head (Pump)**: Energy per unit weight of fluid. Measured in feet. Includes static lift, pressure, and friction.

**Heat Recovery**: Capturing waste heat from equipment for useful purposes. Common with CHP, dehumidifiers, refrigeration.

**HPS (High-Pressure Sodium)**: Traditional horticultural lighting technology. Being replaced by LED.

**HVAC**: Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems.

---

## I

**Infiltration**: Uncontrolled air leakage into/out of building. Major source of heat loss.

**Inverter**: Device that converts DC electricity (from solar panels or batteries) to AC for facility use.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: Discount rate at which NPV equals zero. Measure of investment profitability.

**ITC (Investment Tax Credit)**: Federal tax credit for renewable energy systems. Currently 30% for solar PV.

---

## K

**kVA (Kilovolt-Ampere)**: Measure of apparent power. Related to real power (kW) by power factor.

**kW (Kilowatt)**: Unit of power (1,000 watts). Instantaneous rate of energy use.

**kWh (Kilowatt-hour)**: Unit of energy. Power consumed over time. What utilities bill for.

---

## L

**LCOE (Levelized Cost of Energy)**: Average cost per kWh over a system's lifetime. Used to compare energy sources.

**LED (Light-Emitting Diode)**: Modern, efficient lighting technology. Dominant choice for new CEA installations.

**LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)**: Battery chemistry. Safe, long-lived, good for CEA applications.

**Load Factor**: Ratio of average demand to peak demand. Higher is better (more efficient capacity use).

---

## M

**MACRS (Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System)**: IRS depreciation method for business assets. Provides tax benefits for energy equipment.

**MBH**: 1,000 Btu/hr. Unit for boiler capacity.

**Microturbine**: Small gas turbine for CHP applications. 30-500 kW typical.

**M&V (Measurement and Verification)**: Process of documenting and verifying actual energy savings from improvements.

---

## N

**Net Metering**: Utility arrangement crediting customers for excess solar electricity exported to grid.

**NPV (Net Present Value)**: Sum of present values of cash flows. Positive NPV indicates profitable investment.

---

## O

**O&M (Operations and Maintenance)**: Ongoing costs to operate and maintain systems.

---

## P

**PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)**: Light spectrum (400-700nm) used by plants for photosynthesis.

**Payback Period**: Time required for cumulative savings to equal initial investment.

**PF (Power Factor)**: Ratio of real power to apparent power. Low PF increases costs and losses.

**PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)**: Instantaneous PAR at crop canopy. Measured in µmol/m²/s.

**PV (Photovoltaic)**: Solar panels that convert sunlight directly to electricity.

---

## R

**REAP (Rural Energy for America Program)**: USDA grant and loan guarantee program for agricultural renewable energy and efficiency projects.

**ROI (Return on Investment)**: Profitability metric. Annual return as percentage of investment.

---

## S

**SEER (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio)**: Cooling efficiency rating for air conditioners. Higher is better.

**Setpoint**: Target temperature for heating/cooling system.

**SGIP (Self-Generation Incentive Program)**: California program providing rebates for energy storage and generation.

**Spark Spread**: Difference between electricity price and cost of natural gas to generate that electricity. Affects CHP economics.

---

## T

**TDH (Total Dynamic Head)**: Total resistance that pump must overcome. Sum of static, pressure, and friction components.

**Therm**: 100,000 Btu. Standard unit for natural gas billing.

**TOU (Time-of-Use)**: Rate structure where electricity price varies by time of day. Higher during peak demand periods.

---

## U

**U-Value**: Heat transfer coefficient. Inverse of R-value. Lower is better (less heat loss).

---

## V

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)**: Electronic controller that varies motor speed. Enables huge energy savings on pumps and fans with variable loads.

**Volt (V)**: Unit of electrical potential.

---

## W

**Watt (W)**: Unit of power. 1 W = 1 Joule per second.

---

## Units & Conversions

### Energy
- 1 kWh = 3,412 Btu
- 1 therm = 100,000 Btu = 29.3 kWh
- 1 MMBtu = 1,000,000 Btu = 10 therms

### Power
- 1 HP = 0.746 kW = 2,545 Btu/hr
- 1 kW = 1.341 HP = 3,412 Btu/hr
- 1 ton (cooling) = 12,000 Btu/hr = 3.52 kW

### Area
- 1 m² = 10.76 sq ft
- 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft

### Volume
- 1 gallon = 3.785 liters = 0.1337 cubic feet
- 1 cubic foot = 7.48 gallons

### Pressure/Head
- 1 PSI = 2.31 feet of head (water)
- 1 foot of head = 0.433 PSI

### Temperature
- °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32
- °C = (°F - 32) ÷ 1.8

## Typical Ranges (Quick Reference)

### Energy Intensity
- Greenhouse (minimal light): 0.5-2 kWh/lb
- Greenhouse (supplemental): 2-5 kWh/lb
- Indoor/Vertical: 5-15 kWh/lb

### Equipment Efficiency
- LED efficacy: 2.5-3.5 µmol/J
- HPS efficacy: 1.7-2.0 µmol/J
- Condensing boiler: 92-98% AFUE
- Heat pump COP: 2.5-4.0
- Solar PV efficiency: 18-22%

### Financial Metrics (Guidelines)
- Excellent payback: <3 years
- Good payback: 3-7 years
- Target IRR: >15%
- Minimum NPV: >0
