## 13.1 Federal Incentives

### Investment Tax Credit (ITC)

```
FEDERAL SOLAR ITC (2025)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Current Rate: 30% (through 2032)
Eligible Technologies:
  ✓ Solar PV
  ✓ Solar thermal
  ✓ Small wind
  ✓ Fuel cells
  ✓ Microturbines
  ✓ Combined heat & power (CHP)
  ✓ Energy storage (if charged 100% by renewables)

How It Works:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Example: $125,000 solar PV system

  Gross cost:              $125,000
  ITC (30%):                $37,500

  Tax liability reduction: $37,500 in year installed
  Net cost:                 $87,500

Requirements:
  • Must have sufficient tax liability
  • Must own the system (not lease)
  • Must be placed in service by 12/31/2032
  • 5-year recapture period (keep system operating)

Special Rules for Businesses:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Bonus Depreciation: Additional 60% (phasing out)
  Depreciation basis: $87,500 (after ITC)
  Bonus depreciation: $87,500 × 0.60 = $52,500
  Tax benefit (25% rate): $13,125 in Year 1

Plus: MACRS 5-year depreciation on remainder

Total Year 1 tax benefits:
  ITC:                      $37,500
  Bonus depreciation:       $13,125
  Regular depreciation:     $3,500
  ────────────────────────
  Total:                    $54,125

Effective net cost: $125,000 - $54,125 = $70,875 (43% reduction!)
```

### Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System (MACRS)

```
MACRS DEPRECIATION SCHEDULE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5-Year Property (most energy equipment):
  • Solar PV
  • CHP systems
  • HVAC equipment
  • LED lighting
  • Controls

Depreciation Schedule (without bonus):

Year    Rate    Example ($100,000 basis, 25% tax rate)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1       20%     $20,000 depreciation → $5,000 tax savings
2       32%     $32,000 → $8,000
3       19.2%   $19,200 → $4,800
4       11.52%  $11,520 → $2,880
5       11.52%  $11,520 → $2,880
6       5.76%   $5,760 → $1,440
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total   100%    Total tax benefit: $25,000 (PV: ~$22,000)

Note: Basis reduced by 50% of ITC
  If $100,000 system with 30% ITC:
  Depreciation basis = $100,000 - ($30,000 × 0.50) = $85,000
```

## 13.2 State & Utility Programs

### State Incentive Database

```
COMMON STATE INCENTIVES (examples, varies by location)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

California:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • SGIP (Self-Generation Incentive): $200-350/kWh for storage
  • Net metering: 1:1 credit for solar exports
  • Property tax exemption: Solar not added to property value
  • Sales tax exemption: No sales tax on solar equipment

New York:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • NY-Sun: $0.20-0.40/W solar rebate
  • Energy storage incentive: $250-350/kWh
  • Accelerated depreciation (state)
  • Sales tax exemption

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • SMART solar incentive: $0.30-0.40/kWh for 10 years

  • Sales tax exemption

  • Property tax exemption: 100% for renewable systems
  • Local rebates: Vary by utility

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Energy Trust incentives: $0.50-1.50/W solar

  • Property tax exemption
```

### Utility Rebate Programs

```
TYPICAL UTILITY INCENTIVES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lighting Rebates:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LED fixtures: $30-75 per fixture
  LED lamps: $3-8 per lamp
  Controls: $50-150 per control point

  Example: 100 LED fixtures @ $50/fixture = $5,000 rebate

HVAC Rebates:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  High-efficiency boiler: $3,000-8,000
  Heat pump: $500-1,500 per ton
  Programmable thermostat: $50-150
  VFD on HVAC motors: $100-200/HP

  Example: 95% condensing boiler = $5,000 rebate

Motor/Pump Rebates:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  VFD: $100-250 per HP
  Premium efficiency motor: $15-40 per HP

  Example: 5 HP VFD @ $150/HP = $750 rebate

Envelope Rebates:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Insulation: $0.15-0.40 per sq ft
  Air sealing: $200-1,000 per project
  Windows: 10-25% of cost

Custom Program:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For unique projects not covered by prescriptive rebates
  Typical: $0.08-0.15 per kWh saved annually
  Requires: Energy audit, M&V plan

  Example: Custom project saving 50,000 kWh/year
    Rebate: 50,000 × $0.12 = $6,000

Application Process:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Pre-approval (BEFORE purchase, usually required)
  2. Purchase equipment (keep all receipts)
  3. Installation
  4. Verification (utility may inspect)
  5. Submit application with documentation
  6. Receive rebate (4-12 weeks typically)

  TIP: Always get pre-approval before buying!
```

## 13.3 Grant Programs

### USDA Rural Energy for America Program (REAP)

```
USDA REAP GRANTS & LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Eligibility:
  ✓ Agricultural producers (>50% income from ag)
  ✓ Small rural businesses
  ✓ CEA operations generally qualify!

Funding Available:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Grants: Up to 25% of project cost (max $500,000)
Loan Guarantees: Up to 75% of project cost (max $25 million)
Combined: Up to 75% grant + loan guarantee

Eligible Projects:
  ✓ Renewable energy systems (solar, wind, biomass, geothermal)
  ✓ Energy efficiency improvements
  ✓ Renewable energy feasibility studies (up to $100,000)

Example Project:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

50 kW Solar PV System
  Total cost:              $125,000
  REAP grant (25%):         $31,250
  Federal ITC (30%):        $37,500
  Loan guarantee (50%):     $62,500 (financed)

  Cash needed:              $31,250
  Financing:                $62,500 @ 4.5%, 10 years

  Annual debt service:      $7,800
  Annual savings:           $9,000
  Positive cash flow:       $1,200/year from Day 1!

Application Process:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Timeline:
  • Applications due: Usually 1-2 times per year
  • Award notification: 3-6 months
  • Project completion: Within 2 years

Required Documentation:
  □ Technical assessment (energy audit/engineering study)
  □ Financial information (3 years tax returns)
  □ Quotes from contractors
  □ Environmental review
  □ Cost/benefit analysis

Success Tips:
  • Work with experienced grant writer
  • Start early (6+ months before deadline)
  • Demonstrate strong financials
  • Show environmental benefits
  • Include energy efficiency measures
  • Highlight rural job creation

Success Rate: ~30-40% of applications funded
```

## 13.4 Financing Options

### Commercial Loan Structures

```
FINANCING ALTERNATIVES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Option 1: CONVENTIONAL TERM LOAN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Terms:
  • 5-10 year amortization
  • 5-8% interest rate
  • 20-30% down payment
  • Collateral required

Example: $100,000 loan, 7%, 7 years
  Monthly payment: $1,518
  Annual debt service: $18,216
  Total interest: $27,512

Best for: Established businesses, good credit

Option 2: SBA 504 LOAN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Terms:
  • Up to 90% financing (10% down)
  • 10-20 year term
  • Below-market rates (4-6%)
  • For long-term fixed assets

Example: $100,000 project
  Down payment: $10,000
  SBA 504 loan: $40,000 @ 4.5%, 20 years
  Bank loan: $50,000 @ 6%, 10 years

  Annual payment: $7,800
  Total interest: $34,500

Best for: Significant capital projects, longer payback

Option 3: EQUIPMENT FINANCING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Terms:
  • 100% financing (no down payment)
  • 3-7 year term
  • 6-10% rate
  • Equipment is collateral

Example: $75,000 LED retrofit
  $75,000 @ 8%, 5 years
  Monthly: $1,520
  Annual: $18,240

Best for: Equipment upgrades, limited cash

Option 4: ENERGY SERVICE AGREEMENT (ESA) / PPA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Structure:
  • Third party owns equipment
  • You buy energy/savings at fixed rate
  • No upfront cost
  • Term: 7-20 years

Example: Solar PV
  You pay: $0.10/kWh for solar production
  Grid rate: $0.14/kWh
  Immediate savings: $0.04/kWh

  No money down
  Savings from day 1
  After term: Buy system or renew

Best for: No capital available, off balance sheet

Option 5: LEASE (Capital or Operating)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Capital Lease:
  • Ownership transfers at end
  • On balance sheet
  • Can claim depreciation

Operating Lease:
  • Return equipment at end
  • Off balance sheet
  • Simpler

Example: $50,000 system, 7-year operating lease
  Monthly: $750
  Annual: $9,000
  Total: $63,000

Best for: Preserve capital, off balance sheet needs
```

## 13.5 Tax Equity Structures

### Advanced Financing

```
TAX EQUITY PARTNERSHIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Many CEA operations don't have enough tax liability
         to use full ITC/depreciation benefits

Solution: Partner with tax equity investor who has tax appetite

Common Structures:

1. PARTNERSHIP FLIP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Structure:
  • Tax investor: 99% owner (gets tax benefits)
  • CEA operation: 1% owner initially
  • After target return hit: Ownership "flips"
  • Final: CEA 95%, investor 5%

Example: $125,000 solar project

  Tax investor contributes: $50,000 cash
  Gets: 99% of ITC + depreciation
  Tax benefits to investor: ~$40,000
  Investor return: 20% IRR (meets target in ~5 years)
  Then: Ownership flips to 95% CEA / 5% investor

  CEA benefit: System for $50,000 effective cost

2. SALE-LEASEBACK
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Structure:
  • CEA builds system
  • Immediately sells to tax equity investor
  • Leases back from investor
  • Option to buy at end

Example:
  Build: $125,000 system
  Sell to investor: $100,000
  Investor gets: Tax benefits
  CEA leases back: $10,000/year for 10 years
  Buyout option: $10,000 at year 10

  Net cost to CEA: $110,000 vs $125,000

Minimum Size: Usually $500K+ (high transaction costs)
Best for: Large projects, insufficient tax appetite
Complexity: High (requires sophisticated legal/tax structure)
```

## 13.6 On-Bill Financing

### Utility-Backed Programs

```
ON-BILL FINANCING/REPAYMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Concept: Utility finances project, repayment on electric bill

On-Bill Financing (OBF):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Utility provides loan
  • Repayment added to monthly bill
  • Term: 5-10 years typical
  • Rate: Below market (2-5%)

Example: $40,000 efficiency upgrade
  Loan: $40,000 @ 3%, 7 years
  Monthly payment: $524
  Annual savings: $8,000/month = $667/month

  Net bill impact: $667 savings - $524 payment = $143/month savings
  IMMEDIATE positive cash flow!

On-Bill Repayment (OBR):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Third-party lender finances
  • Utility collects payment on bill
  • Utility-backed (payment obligation stays with meter)

Advantages:
  ✓ Simple application
  ✓ Below-market rates
  ✓ Designed for positive cash flow
  ✓ Utility endorsement
  ✓ Streamlined process

Limitations:
  ✗ Project size limits ($10K-$100K typical)
  ✗ Must use utility-approved contractors
  ✗ Eligible measures may be limited
  ✗ Not all utilities offer

Availability: Check with your utility
```

## 13.7 Incentive Stacking Strategy

### Maximizing Total Benefits

```
COMPREHENSIVE INCENTIVE EXAMPLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Project: 75 kW Solar PV System
Location: California
Facility: Commercial greenhouse
Cost: $187,500 ($2.50/W)

Available Incentives:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Federal ITC (30%):
   $187,500 × 0.30 = $56,250

2. USDA REAP Grant (25%):
   $187,500 × 0.25 = $46,875

3. State SGIP (storage, if added 50 kWh):
   50 kWh × $250/kWh = $12,500

4. Utility rebate ($0.30/W):
   75,000W × $0.30 = $22,500

5. Property tax exemption:
   Saves ~$2,800/year for 15 years = $29,000 PV

6. MACRS depreciation (5-year):
   Basis: $187,500 - ($56,250 × 0.50) = $159,375
   Tax benefit (25% rate): $39,844 PV

Incentive Coordination Rules:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CAREFUL: Some incentives affect others!

ITC calculation basis:
  Gross cost:                          $187,500
  Less utility rebate (taxable):       -$22,500
  Less USDA grant:                     -$46,875
  Adjusted ITC basis:                  $118,125
  ITC (30%):                            $35,438 (reduced!)

MACRS basis:
  Adjusted cost:                       $118,125
  Less 50% of ITC:                     -$17,719
  Depreciation basis:                  $100,406
  Tax benefit:                          $25,102 (reduced!)

Corrected Incentive Stack:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Federal ITC:                            $35,438
USDA REAP grant:                        $46,875
Utility rebate:                         $22,500
MACRS depreciation (PV):                $25,102
Property tax exemption (PV):            $29,000
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total benefits (PV):                   $158,915

Net Project Cost:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Gross cost:                            $187,500
Less REAP grant (upfront):             -$46,875
Less utility rebate (upfront):         -$22,500
Cash outlay:                           $118,125

Year 1:
  ITC:                                  $35,438
  MACRS:                                 $5,020
  Tax benefits:                         $40,458

Effective net cost: $118,125 - $40,458 = $77,667
Total incentive capture: 59% of gross cost!

Annual Production: 120,000 kWh
Annual Savings: $16,800
Payback on net cost: 4.6 years
IRR: 21.3%
```

## 13.8 Application Strategy

### Maximizing Success

```
INCENTIVE APPLICATION BEST PRACTICES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pre-Application Phase:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3-6 Months Before:
  □ Research all available programs
  □ Attend utility workshops/webinars
  □ Contact program administrators with questions
  □ Review eligibility requirements
  □ Check application deadlines
  □ Gather required documentation
  □ Get preliminary quotes from contractors

1-3 Months Before:
  □ Complete energy audit (if required)
  □ Finalize equipment specifications
  □ Obtain firm contractor quotes
  □ Prepare financial documentation
  □ Draft application materials
  □ Have expert review application

Application Phase:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  □ Submit complete application (nothing missing!)
  □ Respond promptly to any requests
  □ Follow up on status
  □ Get pre-approval BEFORE purchasing
  □ Keep all receipts/documentation
  □ Photograph installation
  □ Document any changes from original plan

Common Mistakes to Avoid:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ✗ Purchasing before pre-approval
  ✗ Missing application deadlines
  ✗ Incomplete documentation
  ✗ Wrong equipment specifications
  ✗ Contractor not on approved list
  ✗ Not reading fine print
  ✗ Underestimating timeline
  ✗ Forgetting to claim all incentives

Success Tips:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ✓ Start early
  ✓ Be thorough and organized
  ✓ Use approved contractors
  ✓ Over-communicate with program staff
  ✓ Keep meticulous records
  ✓ Follow all requirements exactly
  ✓ Build relationships with program managers
  ✓ Consider hiring grant writer for large projects
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Federal ITC is powerful** - 30% reduces cost dramatically
2. **Stack incentives carefully** - Some reduce others, understand rules
3. **USDA REAP excellent for ag** - 25% grant + loan guarantee
4. **Utility rebates vary widely** - Check all local programs
5. **Financing enables projects** - Don't let cash constraints stop you
6. **Pre-approval is critical** - Never buy before approval
7. **Documentation matters** - Keep everything, photograph installation
