## Economic Analysis Methods

### Simple Payback
```
Payback (years) = Investment Cost ÷ Annual Savings

Example: $20,000 investment, $5,000/year savings
Payback = $20,000 ÷ $5,000 = 4 years
```

**Use when:** Quick screening, simple comparison
**Limitation:** Ignores time value of money

---

### Return on Investment (ROI)
```
ROI = (Total Savings - Investment) ÷ Investment × 100%

Example: $20,000 investment, $5,000/yr × 10 years = $50,000
ROI = ($50,000 - $20,000) ÷ $20,000 × 100% = 150%
```

**Use when:** Comparing investment alternatives
**Limitation:** Ignores timing of cash flows

---

### Net Present Value (NPV)
```
NPV = -Initial Investment + Σ(Annual Savings ÷ (1 + r)^t)

Where:
  r = discount rate (6-10% typical)
  t = year

Example: $20,000 investment, $5,000/year, 6% discount, 10 years
NPV = -$20,000 + $36,800 = $16,800
```

**Use when:** Long-term analysis needed
**Decision:** Accept if NPV > 0

---

### Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
```
IRR = The discount rate where NPV = 0

Decision: Accept if IRR > Hurdle Rate (typically 8-15%)
```

**Use when:** Comparing projects of different sizes/durations

---

### Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)
```
LCOE = (Capital Cost + Lifetime O&M) ÷ Lifetime Energy Production

Example: Solar PV
Capital: $150,000 (after ITC)
O&M (25 years): $25,000
Production: 2,500,000 kWh

LCOE = ($150,000 + $25,000) ÷ 2,500,000 = $0.07/kWh
```

**Use when:** Comparing generation technologies
**Compare to:** Current utility rate

## Financing Impact on ROI

### Cash Purchase
- **Pros:** Simple, no interest, full tax benefits
- **Cons:** Requires capital, opportunity cost
- **Effective ROI:** As calculated above

### Loan Financing (6% interest, 10 years)
- **Pros:** Preserves cash, spreads cost
- **Cons:** Interest expense reduces savings
- **Effective ROI:** Reduced by ~20-30%

**Example:**
- Project: $50,000, saves $10,000/year
- Cash payback: 5 years, ROI: 100% (10 years)
- Loan payback: Monthly $555, Net savings $3,300/year
- Effective payback: 10 years, ROI: 66% (10 years)

### Performance Contract (ESCO)
- **Pros:** Zero upfront cost, guaranteed savings
- **Cons:** Share savings (typically 50-80% to ESCO for 5-10 years)
- **Effective ROI:** Lower initially, full savings after contract

### Lease
- **Pros:** No upfront cost, predictable payments
- **Cons:** No tax benefits, total cost higher
- **Effective ROI:** Typically 30-50% lower than cash

## Quick Decision Guide

### Accept Investment If:
- ✓ Simple payback < 5 years (for most projects)
- ✓ Simple payback < 10 years (for renewables)
- ✓ NPV > 0
- ✓ IRR > hurdle rate (8-15%)
- ✓ Utility rebates available
- ✓ Low-cost financing available

### Proceed with Caution If:
- ⚠ Payback > 10 years
- ⚠ Unproven technology
- ⚠ High maintenance requirements
- ⚠ Uncertain energy prices
- ⚠ Facility may relocate

### Reject Investment If:
- ✗ NPV < 0
- ✗ IRR < risk-free rate
- ✗ Payback > equipment lifespan
- ✗ Better alternatives available
- ✗ Insufficient capital
