# Energy Audit Checklist

**Course 305: Energy Systems for CEA**
**Quick Reference Guide**

---

## Pre-Audit Preparation

### Data Collection (Start 2-4 weeks before audit)

- [ ] **Utility Bills** (12-24 months minimum)
  - Electric bills (all months)
  - Natural gas bills
  - Propane/fuel oil invoices
  - Water/sewer bills

- [ ] **Facility Documentation**
  - Building plans and drawings
  - Equipment specifications and nameplates
  - Control system documentation
  - Previous audit or commissioning reports
  - Maintenance logs

- [ ] **Operational Data**
  - Production records (yields, crop schedules)
  - Operating hours and schedules
  - Staff count and shifts
  - Recent changes or upgrades

- [ ] **Tools & Equipment**
  - Clamp meter (ammeter)
  - Power meter/logger
  - Thermocouple or infrared thermometer
  - Light meter (lux or PPFD meter)
  - Blower door (for infiltration testing)
  - Camera for documentation
  - Clipboard, forms, laptop

## On-Site Audit Checklist

### 1. Lighting Systems

- [ ] Type of fixtures (LED, HPS, fluorescent, MH)
- [ ] Wattage and quantity of each type
- [ ] Operating hours per day
- [ ] Control systems (timers, sensors, manual)
- [ ] Light levels (PPFD or lux measurements)
- [ ] Fixture condition and cleanliness
- [ ] Opportunity: LED upgrade potential
- [ ] Opportunity: Controls optimization

**Key Measurements:**
- PPFD levels at canopy: _______ µmol/m²/s
- Current fixture watts: _______ W
- Operating hours/day: _______ hrs
- Annual kWh = Watts × Qty × Hrs/day × 365 ÷ 1,000

## Key Formulas

**Energy Use Intensity (EUI):**
```
EUI = Annual kWh ÷ Building Area (sq ft)
```

**Energy per Pound:**
```
kWh/lb = Annual kWh ÷ Annual Production (lbs)
```

**Simple Payback:**
```
Payback (years) = Investment Cost ÷ Annual Savings
```

**Load Factor:**
```
Load Factor = Average kW ÷ Peak kW
```

**Potential Savings from Efficiency Measure:**
```
kWh Savings = (Current Watts - New Watts) × Qty × Hours/year ÷ 1,000
$ Savings = kWh Savings × $/kWh
```
