5. Achieve consistent, high-quality plant production

## 1. Quality Standards

### Plant Quality Metrics

| Parameter | Measurement Method | Target | Rejection Criteria |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| **Germination %** | Count emerged/total seeds | >85% | <75% |
| **Rooting %** | Count rooted/total cuttings | >80% | <70% |
| **Height** | Ruler from soil to apex | Variety-specific ±15% | >30% deviation |
| **Stem caliper** | Digital caliper at soil line | 3-5mm (tomato seedling) | <2mm or >7mm |
| **Leaf color** | SPAD meter or visual | SPAD 35-45 (most crops) | <30 or >55 |
| **Root quality** | Visual rating 1-5 scale | 4-5 (extensive white roots) | 1-2 (poor roots) |
| **Uniformity (CV)** | Measure 25 plants, calculate | <15% variation | >25% variation |
| **Freedom from pests** | Visual inspection | Zero tolerance | Any visible pests |
| **Freedom from disease** | Visual inspection | Zero tolerance | Any symptoms |

### Quality Grading System

```
GRADE A (PREMIUM) - 100% price
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
├─ Meets all target specifications
├─ Uniform size and color
├─ No pest or disease symptoms
├─ Well-rooted, healthy root system
├─ Transplant-ready
└─ >85% of production should be Grade A

GRADE B (STANDARD) - 75-85% price
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
├─ Minor deviations from targets
├─ Slight size variation
├─ Will perform adequately after transplant
├─ Cosmetic issues only
└─ 10-15% of production acceptable

GRADE C (CULL) - Discard or donate
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
├─ Significantly outside specifications
├─ Disease or severe pest damage
├─ Poor rooting or stunted growth
├─ Will not produce acceptable crop
└─ <5% cull rate is goal
```

## 2. Testing and Monitoring

### Germination Testing

**Standard Protocol** (AOSA Rules):
1. Sample size: 4 reps of 50-100 seeds
2. Duration: Species-specific (7-21 days)
3. Conditions: Optimal temperature, moisture, light
4. Evaluation: Normal vs. abnormal seedlings
5. Calculation: (Normal seedlings ÷ Total seeds) × 100

**When to Test:**
- Upon receiving new seed lots
- Before large seeding operations
- Every 6-12 months for stored seed
- If germination appears poor

### Disease and Pest Monitoring

**IPM Scouting Schedule:**
- Walk-through inspection: Daily
- Detailed scouting: 2-3× per week
- Yellow sticky cards: Monitor weekly
- Record all findings in log

**Threshold Levels:**
- Disease: Zero tolerance in propagation
- Aphids: 1 per 10 plants = treat
- Fungus gnats: 5 per sticky card = treat
- Thrips: 1 per 10 plants = treat

| KPI | Calculation | Target | Review Frequency |
|-----|-------------|--------|------------------|
| **Success rate** | (Grade A+B) ÷ Total started | >90% | Per lot |
| **Production efficiency** | Actual ÷ Planned output | >95% | Weekly |
| **Labor per 1000 plants** | Hours ÷ (Plants ÷ 1000) | <5 hours | Monthly |

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

### 5 Whys Method for Problem-Solving

**Example: Low Germination (only 65%)**

1. **Why?** Seeds didn't germinate
   - Why? → Medium was too dry

2. **Why was medium too dry?**
   - Why? → Irrigation timer malfunction

3. **Why did timer malfunction?**
   - Why? → Power outage reset timer

4. **Why wasn't this caught?**
   - Why? → No daily check of irrigation system

5. **Why no daily check?**
   - Why? → No checklist/protocol in place

**Root Cause**: Lack of standard operating procedure for daily equipment checks

**Solution**: Create daily checklist including irrigation system verification

## 5. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### Example SOP: Tomato Cutting Propagation

```
SOP #: 006-TC                                        Revision: 2.1
Title: Tomato Cutting Propagation                    Date: 2025-01-15
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE: Standardize tomato cutting propagation for 85%+ success rate

MATERIALS:
├─ Healthy stock plants (30-45 days old)
├─ Sharp pruners/knife (sterilized)
├─ IBA 2,000 ppm gel
├─ 72-cell trays with peat:perlite mix (70:30)
├─ Mist propagation area

PROCEDURE:
1. Take cuttings from stock plants in early morning
   • Select 4-6" terminal shoots
   • Cut just below node at 45° angle
   • Place immediately in water

2. Prepare cuttings
   • Remove lower 2-3 leaves
   • Retain 2-3 leaves at top
   • Dip base 1/2" into rooting hormone

3. Stick cuttings
   • Pre-dibble holes in moistened medium
   • Insert cutting 1" deep
   • Firm medium around stem
   • Space 1 cutting per cell

4. Place under mist
   • Mist: 6 sec every 10 min (daytime)
   • Night: Off or 1× per hour
   • Bottom heat: 70-75°F
   • Ambient: 68-75°F

5. Monitor and care
   • Check daily for wilting, disease
   • Remove any diseased plants immediately
   • Reduce mist when roots visible (10-14 days)

6. Wean and transplant
   • Gradually reduce mist over 3-5 days
   • Transplant when roots 1-2" long (14-21 days)
   • Grade and record data

QUALITY STANDARDS:
├─ >85% rooting success
├─ Roots visible within 14 days
├─ Ready for transplant by day 21
└─ <5% disease/pest issues

SAFETY/NOTES:
• Sterilize tools between plant varieties
• Wash hands before handling cuttings
• Monitor mist system functionality daily
```

## 6. Continuous Improvement

### Kaizen Approach to Propagation

**Small, Continuous Changes:**

1. **Weekly team meeting**
   - Review previous week's data
   - Identify one problem to solve
   - Brainstorm solutions
   - Implement best idea immediately

2. **A/B Testing**
   - Test new methods on 10-20% of production
   - Compare to standard method
   - If better, adopt; if worse, revert
   - Document everything

3. **Benchmark Against Best**
   - Track industry standards
   - Visit other operations
   - Adopt proven best practices
   - Set stretch goals

**Example Improvements:**
- Reduced germination time 2 days by optimizing temperature
- Increased rooting % from 78% to 91% with improved mist timing
- Cut labor 15% by reorganizing workspace layout

## 7. Customer Quality Feedback

### Feedback Collection System

**At Point of Sale:**
- Provide care instructions
- Include contact information
- Offer replacement guarantee

**Follow-up Survey** (2-4 weeks post-purchase):
1. Did plants arrive in good condition? (Yes/No)
2. Were plants true-to-type? (Yes/No)
3. Transplant success rate? (%)
4. How would you rate overall quality? (1-5)
5. Would you order again? (Yes/No)
6. Comments: ___________

**Response to Negative Feedback:**
- Contact within 24 hours
- Investigate root cause
- Offer replacement or refund
- Implement corrective action
- Follow up to ensure satisfaction

## Key Takeaways

1. **Measure what matters** - Define clear quality metrics and track consistently
2. **Grade every batch** - Ensures accountability and identifies problems early
3. **Document everything** - Data drives improvement
4. **Root cause analysis** - Fix problems permanently, not temporarily
5. **SOPs ensure consistency** - Especially important with multiple staff
6. **Customer feedback** is the ultimate quality measure
