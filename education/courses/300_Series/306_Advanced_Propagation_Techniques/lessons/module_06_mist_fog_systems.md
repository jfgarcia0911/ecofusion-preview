### Mist vs. Fog vs. Standard Irrigation

| System Type | Droplet Size | Function | Best For | Cost |
|-------------|--------------|----------|----------|------|
| **Intermittent mist** | 50-100 microns | Leaf moisture, cooling | Softwood cuttings, leafy | Medium |
| **Fog** | <10 microns | Humidity, minimal wetting | Sensitive species, tissue culture | High |
| **Overhead sprinkler** | 500+ microns | Irrigation, coarse mist | Seedlings, hardy plants | Low |
| **Sub-irrigation** | N/A (bottom water) | Root zone only | Rooted cuttings, seedlings | Medium |

### Electronic Leaf Sensor Operation

```
ELECTRONIC LEAF SENSOR SYSTEM
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRINCIPLE:
├─ Artificial "leaf" with moisture-sensitive circuit
├─ When dry, completes circuit → activates mist
├─ When wet, breaks circuit → mist stops
└─ Self-adjusting to environmental conditions

SETUP:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  1. Place sensor at cutting height, in representative location          │
│  2. Ensure sensor receives mist (not in dead zone)                      │
│  3. Set sensitivity dial (how dry before misting)                       │
│  4. Set minimum off-time (prevent too-frequent misting)                 │
│  5. Set maximum on-time (safety - prevents flooding)                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TYPICAL SETTINGS:
├─ Sensitivity: 3-5 (1=very wet, 10=very dry)
├─ Minimum off-time: 3-8 minutes
├─ Maximum on-time: 15-30 seconds
└─ Night mode: Reduced sensitivity or off

ADVANTAGES OVER TIMERS:
✓ Automatically adjusts to weather changes
✓ Responds to actual plant needs
✓ Prevents over/under-misting
✓ Saves water during cloudy/humid periods
✓ Increases mist during hot/dry periods

MAINTENANCE:
├─ Clean sensor weekly (mineral deposits affect accuracy)
├─ Replace sensor pad annually
├─ Check calibration monthly
└─ Have backup timer as failsafe
```

## 5. Disease Management in Mist Systems

### Disease Prevention Strategies

| Strategy | Implementation | Effectiveness | Notes |
|----------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Sanitation** | Clean benches, tools, pots between batches | High | Foundation of disease control |
| **Air circulation** | Fans to promote air movement | High | Prevents Botrytis, other fungi |
| **Reduce mist at night** | Timer or sensor adjustment | Medium | Allows leaves to dry overnight |
| **Preventative fungicides** | Rotate biologicals/chemicals | Medium-High | Use with caution on cuttings |
| **Remove infected plants** | Scout daily, cull immediately | High | Prevents spread |
| **Reduce leaf overlap** | Proper spacing | Medium | Improves air flow |
| **Chlorine in mist water** | 1-2 ppm free chlorine | Medium | Can help but may damage leaves |
| **UV sterilization** | Inline UV treatment of water | Low-Medium | Expensive, limited benefit |

## Key Takeaways

1. **Mist is essential** for softwood cutting success - 90-95% vs 50-60% without
2. **Control system matters** - electronic leaf sensors provide best results
3. **Water quality** directly affects nozzle longevity and plant health
4. **Proper coverage** requires careful nozzle selection and placement
5. **Disease prevention** through sanitation and air movement is critical
6. **System pays for itself** quickly through improved rooting success
