## 2. Environmental Control Strategies

### Temperature Management

**Heating Options:**
- Radiant hot water: Even heat, energy-efficient, $$$
- Forced air: Quick response, less uniform, $$
- Electric heat mats: Zone control, expensive to operate, $$

**Cooling Options:**
- Natural ventilation: Free, weather-dependent
- Evaporative cooling (pad & fan): Effective in dry climates, $$
- Air conditioning: Precise, expensive, $$$

**Recommended**: Combination of radiant heat + evaporative cooling + ventilation

### Humidity Control

```
HUMIDITY MANAGEMENT BY ZONE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GERMINATION ZONE (70-90% RH)
├─ Enclosed area with humidity tent or chamber
├─ Mist system or fogger
├─ Minimal ventilation
└─ Monitor with hygrometer

ROOTING ZONE (85-100% RH for cuttings)
├─ Mist system (primary)
├─ Fog system (advanced)
├─ Enclosed benches with clear covers (low-tech)
└─ Gradual weaning as roots develop

HARDENING ZONE (50-70% RH)
├─ Ambient greenhouse humidity
├─ Ventilation to prevent excess moisture
├─ Fans for air movement
└─ Transitional environment
```

### Light Management

| Growth Stage | Light Level (fc) | DLI (mol/m²/d) | Duration | Source |
|--------------|------------------|----------------|----------|--------|
| Seed germination (light-sensitive) | 100-500 | 2-4 | 12-16 hr | Ambient or fluorescent |
| Seedling/cutting rooting | 500-1,500 | 5-10 | 14-16 hr | Ambient + HPS/LED |
| Active growth | 2,000-5,000 | 12-20 | 14-18 hr | Full greenhouse + supplement |
| Hardening/pre-transplant | 3,000-6,000 | 17-25 | 12-14 hr | Maximum available |

## 4. Production Planning

### Space Utilization Calculation

```
EXAMPLE: Tomato Transplant Production
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GOAL: 5,000 transplants per week, year-round

SPACE CALCULATION:
├─ Germination: 7-10 days in 72-cell trays
│  └─ 5,000 ÷ 72 = 70 trays × 1.2 sq ft = 84 sq ft × 1.5 weeks = 126 sq ft
│
├─ Growing on: 3-4 weeks in 50-cell trays (larger cells)
│  └─ 5,000 ÷ 50 = 100 trays × 1.5 sq ft = 150 sq ft × 4 weeks = 600 sq ft
│
├─ Hardening: 1 week at final spacing
│  └─ Same as above × 1 week = 150 sq ft
│
└─ TOTAL BENCH SPACE: 876 sq ft

With aisles, work areas (35% overhead):
└─ Total greenhouse: 876 ÷ 0.65 = 1,348 sq ft minimum

For 30' × 48' house (1,440 sq ft):
└─ Can produce 5,200 transplants/week = 270,000 per year
```

### Production Scheduling

```
ROLLING SCHEDULE (Continuous Production)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEEK 1:
├─ Monday: Seed 5,000 (Batch A)
├─ Clean and prepare for next week
└─ Harvest/ship previous batches

WEEK 2:
├─ Monday: Seed 5,000 (Batch B)
├─ Batch A moves to growing-on area
└─ Continue routine maintenance

WEEK 3:
├─ Monday: Seed 5,000 (Batch C)
├─ Batch B moves to growing-on
├─ Batch A enters hardening
└─ Quality check all batches

WEEK 4:
├─ Monday: Seed 5,000 (Batch D)
├─ Ship Batch A (completed)
├─ Batch C to growing-on, B to hardening
└─ 4 batches in production at all times

STEADY STATE:
└─ Ship 5,000 plants every Monday
   Consistent workflow, predictable labor
```

## 5. Energy Efficiency

### Cost-Saving Strategies

**Heating:**
- Thermal screens/energy curtains (save 30-50%)
- Zone heating (heat only active areas)
- Insulated north wall
- Radiant vs. forced air (20-30% more efficient)

**Cooling:**
- Natural ventilation maximized
- Shade cloth in summer
- Evaporative cooling (dry climates)
- Night cooling/thermal mass

**Lighting:**
- LED vs. HPS (40-50% energy savings)
- Light sensors/timers
- Maximize natural light with clean glazing

### Energy Budget Example

```
MONTHLY ENERGY COSTS (2,000 sq ft prop house, Northern climate)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WINTER (December-February):
├─ Heating: $1,200-2,000 (major cost)
├─ Supplemental lighting: $300-500
├─ Mist/irrigation pumps: $50-100
├─ Ventilation fans: $50-100
└─ TOTAL: $1,600-2,700/month ($0.80-1.35/sq ft)

SUMMER (June-August):
├─ Heating: $0-200
├─ Cooling (evap or fans): $200-400
├─ Mist/irrigation: $100-200 (higher use)
├─ Lighting: $100-200 (less needed)
└─ TOTAL: $400-1,000/month ($0.20-0.50/sq ft)

ANNUAL AVERAGE: $0.50-0.90/sq ft/month

STRATEGIES TO REDUCE:
├─ Energy curtain: Save $200-400/month in winter
├─ LED lighting: Save $100-200/month
├─ Efficient controls: Save $100-300/month
└─ ROI on efficiency upgrades: 2-4 years
```

## 6. Sanitation and Workflow

### Sanitation Protocols

**Between Batches:**
1. Remove all plants and debris
2. Sweep/vacuum all surfaces
3. Wash benches with detergent
4. Disinfect with quaternary ammonium or bleach (10%)
5. Rinse thoroughly
6. Allow to dry before replanting

**Ongoing:**
- Remove dead/diseased plants daily
- Sanitize tools between uses
- Foot baths at entry points
- Limit visitor access
- Quarantine new plant material

### Efficient Workflow Design

```
PRODUCTION FLOW (minimize handling)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INPUT → PREPARATION → PROPAGATION → GROWING → SHIPPING
   ↓         ↓             ↓            ↓          ↓
Receiving  Seeding/     Germination/ Root/grow  Staging
  dock    cutting prep   rooting    to size     & loading

PRINCIPLES:
├─ Linear flow (no backtracking)
├─ Minimize plant handling
├─ Group similar tasks (batch processing)
├─ Dedicated work zones
└─ Strategic material storage
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Dedicated propagation space** increases efficiency and success rates
2. **Zone-based design** allows optimized conditions for each stage
3. **Environmental control** is critical - invest in good systems early
4. **Space utilization** determines profitability - plan for continuous production
5. **Energy efficiency** significantly impacts operating costs
6. **Workflow design** reduces labor and improves quality
