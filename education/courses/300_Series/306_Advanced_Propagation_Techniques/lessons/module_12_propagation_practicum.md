## 1. Comprehensive Hands-On Projects

### Project A: Multi-Method Propagation Comparison

**Goal**: Compare 3 propagation methods for basil

**Methods to Test:**
1. **Seed germination** (baseline)
2. **Softwood cuttings** (traditional vegetative)
3. **Tissue culture** (advanced, if facilities available)

**Variables to Measure:**
- Time to transplant-ready stage
- Success rate (% of propagules producing quality plants)
- Labor hours per 100 plants
- Material cost per 100 plants
- Total cost per successful plant
- Plant quality (uniformity, vigor)

**Sample Size**: 100 propagules per method (300 total)

**Duration**: 6-8 weeks

**Deliverable**: Complete data analysis with recommendation for commercial production

---

### Project B: Optimization Experiment

**Goal**: Optimize one factor in propagation

**Example Topics:**
- **Rooting hormone concentration** (test 4-5 concentrations)
- **Substrate comparison** (3-4 different mixes)
- **Germination temperature** (test range for crop)
- **Misting frequency** (4-5 schedules)
- **Fertilizer program** (concentration or timing)

**Experimental Design:**
- Control group (standard practice)
- 3-4 treatment groups
- 25-50 propagules per treatment
- Replicate experiment 2-3 times
- Statistical analysis of results

**Deliverable**: Scientific report with protocol, data, analysis, conclusions

## 2. Case Study Analysis

### Case Study 1: Failed Cutting Propagation

**Scenario:**
A commercial propagator attempts 10,000 rosemary cuttings. After 4 weeks:
- Only 35% have roots (target: 80%)
- 25% have died
- 40% show callus but no roots
- Average rooting time: 6 weeks (target: 4 weeks)

**Your Task:**
1. List 5 possible causes for poor results
2. Describe diagnostic steps to identify root cause
3. Propose corrective actions for each potential cause
4. Design an experiment to prevent recurrence
5. Calculate financial impact of failure

---

### Case Study 2: Contamination Crisis in Tissue Culture

**Scenario:**
Tissue culture lab experiences sudden spike in contamination:
- Week 1: 5% contamination (normal)
- Week 2: 15% contamination
- Week 3: 35% contamination
- Week 4: 50% contamination

Cultures show bacterial contamination (cloudy medium, slimy growth)

**Your Task:**
1. Develop systematic investigation plan
2. Identify 10 potential contamination sources
3. Prioritize most likely causes
4. Create corrective action plan
5. Design preventative measures
6. Estimate financial loss and recovery time

---

### Case Study 3: Scaling Production

**Scenario:**
Small propagation business currently produces 5,000 transplants/month wants to scale to 20,000/month.

Current setup:
- 800 sq ft greenhouse
- Manual seeding and watering
- One full-time employee
- $8,000 monthly revenue, $2,500 profit

**Your Task:**
1. Calculate space requirements for 4× increase
2. Identify equipment/infrastructure needs
3. Determine labor requirements
4. Estimate capital investment required
5. Project new financial performance
6. Create 12-month implementation plan
7. Identify risks and mitigation strategies
