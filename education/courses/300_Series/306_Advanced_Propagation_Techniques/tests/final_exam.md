41. (3 points) Physical dormancy = hard seed coat prevents water uptake (legumes); broken by scarification. Physiological dormancy = embryo requires after-ripening (lettuce); broken by stratification or gibberellic acid.

42. (2 points) 10,000 ÷ 0.92 ÷ 0.95 = 11,464 seeds

43. (3 points) Temperature: 70-75°F; Humidity: 90-100%; Light: 500-1,500 fc (moderate)

**Part III (Problem Solving - 20 points):**

Scenario A - Germination Failure:
1. Possible causes: thermoinhibition (temp too high for lettuce), poor seed-medium contact (dry surface), aged seed lot
2. Tests: retest seed germination in lab, check medium temperature, examine seed-medium contact
3. Corrections: reduce temperature to 65-70°F, ensure proper watering, use fresh seed lot
4. Prevention: temperature monitoring, proper seeding technique, regular seed testing

Scenario B - Production Planning:
1. Seeds weekly: 2,000 ÷ 0.90 = 2,222 seeds
2. Space: 2,222 × 5 weeks = 11,110 plants in production ÷ 100 per sq ft = 112 sq ft
3. Week 1: Seed Batch A; Week 2: Seed B, A growing; Week 3: Seed C, A&B growing; Week 4: Seed D, B&C growing, A ships; Week 5+: Rolling schedule
4. Bottleneck: bench space during peak production, germination capacity, or transplanting labor

**Part IV (Applied Knowledge - 10 points):**

Sample design elements:
- Zone 1 (30%): Germination chamber with bottom heat
- Zone 2 (50%): Growing area with supplemental lighting
- Zone 3 (20%): Hardening area with increased ventilation
- Key equipment: mist system (germination), LED lighting (growing), exhaust fans (hardening)
- Capacity: Example for lettuce at 4-week cycles = 1,440 sq ft × 100 plants/sq ft × 13 cycles = 187,200 plants/year
- Critical control: Temperature/humidity control for consistent germination and growth

**Bonus Question (+5 points):**
Tissue-cultured plants grown in vitro have non-functional stomata, thin cuticles, and heterotrophic metabolism. Acclimatization involves gradually exposing to lower humidity, higher light, and normal atmospheric CO₂ while stomata develop functionality, cuticles thicken, and plants shift to autotrophic (photosynthetic) metabolism. This process takes 2-4 weeks with gradual environmental changes.
