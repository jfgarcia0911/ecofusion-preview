## Questions

**1. A Key Performance Indicator (KPI) in propagation is:**

a) A measurable metric of production success
b) A type of fertilizer
c) A disease organism
d) A propagation technique

---

**2. Germination percentage should be calculated as:**

a) All emerged seedlings ÷ seeds sown
b) Normal healthy seedlings ÷ seeds sown
c) Abnormal seedlings ÷ seeds sown
d) Dead seeds ÷ seeds sown

---

**3. An acceptable germination rate for commercial production is generally:**

a) 50-60%
b) 65-75%
c) 80-95%+
d) 100% required

---

**4. Uniformity in seedling production is important because:**

a) It looks better
b) It ensures even maturity and reduces labor
c) It costs less
d) It prevents disease

---

**5. Pour-through testing measures:**

a) Soil temperature
b) Nutrient solution EC and pH
c) Light levels
d) Humidity

---

**6. Visual grading of transplants should assess:**

a) Height and color only
b) Overall vigor, uniformity, root development, pest/disease status
c) Container size
d) Soil moisture only

---

**7. The coefficient of variation (CV) measures:**

a) Temperature changes
b) Uniformity of a population (lower CV = more uniform)
c) Profit margin
d) Disease severity

---

**8. Tracking propagation success rates helps:**

a) Identify problems early
b) Improve production planning
c) Calculate true costs
d) All of the above

---

**9. Common quality defects in transplants include:**

a) Leggy/stretched growth
b) Nutrient deficiencies
c) Root binding
d) All of the above

---

**10. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) in propagation:**

a) Are unnecessary paperwork
b) Ensure consistency and quality across batches
c) Only for large operations
d) Should change frequently

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. a (KPI is measurable metric of success)
2. b (Normal healthy seedlings ÷ seeds sown)
3. c (80-95%+ is commercial standard)
4. b (Even maturity reduces labor, improves efficiency)
5. b (Pour-through tests EC and pH of growing medium)
6. b (Comprehensive assessment of plant quality)
7. b (CV measures uniformity - lower is better)
8. d (All of the above - multiple benefits to tracking)
9. d (All are common quality defects)
10. b (SOPs ensure consistency and quality)
