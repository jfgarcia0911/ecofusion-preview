## Questions

**1. Tissue culture propagation is also known as:**

a) Hydroponics
b) Micropropagation
c) Grafting
d) Seed propagation

---

**2. The main advantage of tissue culture is:**

a) Low cost
b) Rapid multiplication and disease-free plants
c) Simple equipment needs
d) No special training required

---

**3. Stage I of tissue culture involves:**

a) Rooting plantlets
b) Shoot multiplication
c) Establishment and sterilization of explant
d) Acclimatization

---

**4. The growth regulator ratio that promotes shoot multiplication is:**

a) High auxin : low cytokinin
b) Low auxin : high cytokinin
c) No growth regulators
d) Equal auxin and cytokinin

---

**5. Contamination in tissue culture most commonly comes from:**

a) Media ingredients
b) The explant (plant tissue)
c) Airborne spores
d) Culture vessels

---

**6. The laminar flow hood in tissue culture:**

a) Provides sterile filtered air
b) Grows the plants
c) Sterilizes media
d) Controls temperature

---

**7. Agar in tissue culture media serves to:**

a) Provide nutrients
b) Kill bacteria
c) Solidify the medium
d) Promote growth

---

**8. The autoclave sterilizes media and instruments by:**

a) Chemical treatment
b) UV radiation
c) High pressure steam (121°C, 15 PSI)
d) Freezing

---

**9. Acclimatization (Stage IV) is necessary because tissue-cultured plants:**

a) Are toxic
b) Have non-functional stomata and need gradual hardening
c) Grow too fast
d) Have no roots

---

**10. Compared to conventional propagation, tissue culture's main limitation is:**

a) Cannot propagate many species
b) High setup cost and technical expertise required
c) Slow multiplication
d) Plants are weaker

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Micropropagation is another term for tissue culture)
2. b (Rapid multiplication, disease-free status are main advantages)
3. c (Stage I is establishment and sterilization)
4. b (Low auxin : high cytokinin promotes shoots)
5. b (The explant is the most common contamination source)
6. a (Laminar flow provides sterile HEPA-filtered air)
7. c (Agar solidifies the medium like gelatin)
8. c (Autoclave uses high pressure steam at 121°C)
9. b (TC plants have non-functional stomata, thin cuticles, need hardening)
10. b (High cost and expertise are main limitations)
