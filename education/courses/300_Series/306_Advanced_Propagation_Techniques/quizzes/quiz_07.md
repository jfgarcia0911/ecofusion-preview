## Questions

**1. In grafting, the rootstock provides:**

a) The fruiting/flowering portion
b) The root system and lower stem
c) Only disease resistance
d) Only the leaves

---

**2. The scion in a graft is:**

a) The root system
b) The upper portion (shoots, fruits, flowers)
c) The grafting tape
d) The rooting hormone

---

**3. For successful grafting, the most critical requirement is:**

a) Matching stem colors
b) Cambium layer alignment
c) Same leaf shape
d) Identical stem diameter

---

**4. The whip-and-tongue graft is best used when:**

a) Rootstock is much larger than scion
b) Scion and rootstock are similar diameter
c) Grafting very small seedlings
d) Only for fruit trees

---

**5. Grafted tomatoes for aquaponics are used primarily to:**

a) Increase fruit size
b) Provide disease resistance and vigor
c) Change fruit color
d) Reduce plant height

---

**6. After grafting, plants should be kept:**

a) In full sun immediately
b) In high humidity with reduced light for 7-10 days
c) In complete darkness
d) In very dry conditions

---

**7. The healing chamber humidity level should be:**

a) 50-60%
b) 70-80%
c) 90-95%+
d) 100% (water saturated)

---

**8. Graft union healing typically takes:**

a) 1-3 days
b) 5-10 days
c) 3-4 weeks
d) 2-3 months

---

**9. A common sign of graft failure is:**

a) New growth on scion
b) Wilting and browning of scion
c) Increased vigor
d) Rapid healing

---

**10. Removing rootstock suckers below the graft union is important because:**

a) They look unattractive
b) They divert energy and can overtake the scion
c) They cause disease
d) They improve grafting success

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Rootstock provides root system and lower stem)
2. b (Scion is the upper portion - variety you want)
3. b (Cambium alignment is critical for vascular connection)
4. b (Whip-and-tongue works best with similar diameter stems)
5. b (Disease resistance and vigor are primary benefits)
6. b (High humidity, reduced light for healing period)
7. c (90-95%+ humidity prevents desiccation during healing)
8. b (5-10 days for initial healing, full strength in 2-3 weeks)
9. b (Wilting and browning indicate graft failure)
10. b (Rootstock suckers compete and can overtake the desired scion)
