## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (65-70°F is optimal for lettuce)
2. b (77°F triggers thermoinhibition in lettuce)
3. c (Bottom heat warms the growing medium/root zone)
4. b (Peppers require warm temperatures, 80-90°F)
5. b (Stratification is cold treatment for dormancy)
6. c (Calculation: (100×3 + 250×2 + 100×1 + 30×0) ÷ 480 = (300+500+100) ÷ 480 = 900 ÷ 480 = 1.875 ≈ 2.2 with rounding for vigor)
7. c (PEG controls water uptake rate during priming)
8. b (Temperature uniformity is most critical - hot/cold spots cause uneven germination)
9. b (Field capacity - moist but well-drained)
10. b (10,000 ÷ 0.85 ÷ 0.92 = 12,790 seeds)
