## Questions

**1. The primary advantage of vegetative propagation over seed propagation is:**

a) Lower cost
b) Genetic uniformity (clone of parent)
c) Faster propagation
d) No disease transmission

---

**2. Softwood cuttings are taken from:**

a) Dormant winter wood
b) Current season's new growth
c) Two-year-old stems
d) Root tissue

---

**3. The optimal rooting time for most softwood herb cuttings is:**

a) 3-5 days
b) 1-3 weeks
c) 4-6 weeks
d) 8-12 weeks

---

**4. Adventitious roots on cuttings originate from:**

a) Existing root tissue
b) Cambium or pericycle cells
c) Leaf tissue
d) Flower buds

---

**5. Which phase of root formation is most critical for humidity management?**

a) Dedifferentiation (Days 0-3)
b) Root initiation (Days 3-7)
c) Root development (Days 7-14)
d) All phases equally critical

---

**6. Wounding the base of a cutting before applying rooting hormone:**

a) Always reduces rooting success
b) Has no effect
c) Can improve rooting in difficult species by exposing cambium
d) Is only for aesthetic purposes

---

**7. The ideal rooting medium for cuttings should be:**

a) High in nutrients
b) Well-draining but moisture-retentive, sterile, low fertility
c) 100% organic matter
d) Heavy clay-based

---

**8. Stock plants for cutting production should be:**

a) High in nitrogen for maximum growth
b) Moderately fertilized with high light for carbohydrate reserves
c) Kept in low light
d) Allowed to flower freely

---

**9. The C:N ratio (carbon to nitrogen) that favors rooting is:**

a) Low C:N (more nitrogen)
b) High C:N (more carbohydrates)
c) Equal C and N
d) C:N ratio has no effect

---

**10. A basil stock plant produces 12 cuttings every 3 weeks. How many stock plants are needed to produce 2,000 cuttings per week continuously?**

a) 167 plants
b) 250 plants
c) 500 plants
d) 750 plants

**Show calculation:**

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Genetic uniformity - exact clone of parent)
2. b (Softwood = current season's new growth)
3. b (1-3 weeks typical for softwood herbs)
4. b (Adventitious roots form from cambium or pericycle)
5. c (Root development phase when roots emerge - most critical for humidity)
6. c (Wounding exposes cambium, increases hormone uptake in difficult species)
7. b (Well-draining, sterile, low fertility is ideal)
8. b (Moderate N, high light builds carbohydrate reserves)
9. b (High C:N ratio favors rooting)
10. c (Weekly need: 2,000. Per 3-week cycle: 6,000 cuttings. Per plant per cycle: 12 cuttings. Plants needed: 6,000 ÷ 12 = 500 plants)
