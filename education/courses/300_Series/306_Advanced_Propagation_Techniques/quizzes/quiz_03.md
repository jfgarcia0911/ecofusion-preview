**Module:** 3 - Seedling Management

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Select the best answer for multiple choice questions.

## Questions

**1. The cotyledons of a seedling function primarily to:**

a) Absorb water from soil
b) Provide initial food reserves or photosynthesize
c) Anchor the plant
d) Produce hormones

---

**2. "Damping off" disease in seedlings is caused by:**

a) Overwatering only
b) Fungal pathogens (Pythium, Rhizoctonia)
c) Nutrient deficiency
d) Too much light

---

**3. The best prevention for damping off is:**

a) Heavy fungicide application
b) Sterile media, good drainage, air circulation
c) Extra fertilizer
d) Reduced watering

---

**4. When should you begin fertilizing seedlings?**

a) Immediately after sowing
b) When cotyledons emerge
c) When first true leaves appear
d) Not until transplanting

---

**5. Initial fertilizer concentration for young seedlings should be:**

a) 25-50 ppm nitrogen
b) 100-150 ppm nitrogen
c) 200-300 ppm nitrogen
d) 400+ ppm nitrogen

---

**6. DIF (difference between day and night temperature) affects:**

a) Germination rate
b) Stem elongation
c) Root growth only
d) Leaf color

---

**7. Negative DIF (cooler days than nights) results in:**

a) Taller, stretched plants
b) Shorter, more compact plants
c) No effect on height
d) Increased branching

---

**8. "Hardening off" seedlings involves:**

a) Reducing water only
b) Gradually exposing to outdoor conditions
c) Stopping all fertilizer
d) Increasing temperature

---

**9. Leggy, stretched seedlings are typically caused by:**

a) Too much light
b) Insufficient light and/or high temperature
c) Over-fertilization
d) Low humidity

---

**10. The best time to transplant seedlings to larger containers is:**

a) As soon as cotyledons emerge
b) When 2-4 true leaves have developed
c) When roots fill the entire container
d) Just before flowering

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Cotyledons provide stored food or photosynthesize)
2. b (Fungal pathogens cause damping off)
3. b (Prevention through cultural practices is most effective)
4. c (Begin feeding when true leaves appear and photosynthesis begins)
5. a (Start with low concentration, 25-50 ppm N)
6. b (DIF primarily affects stem elongation)
7. b (Negative DIF produces compact plants)
8. b (Hardening off is gradual acclimation to outdoor conditions)
9. b (Insufficient light and/or high temps cause stretching)
10. b (Transplant at 2-4 true leaf stage for best results)
