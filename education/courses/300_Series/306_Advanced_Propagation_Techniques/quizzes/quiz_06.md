**Module:** 6 - Mist and Fog Systems

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Select the best answer for multiple choice questions.

## Questions

**1. The primary purpose of intermittent mist in propagation is to:**

a) Provide water to roots
b) Reduce leaf temperature and prevent desiccation
c) Deliver nutrients
d) Kill pathogens

---

**2. Mist systems should operate:**

a) 24 hours continuously
b) Only during daylight hours when transpiration occurs
c) Only at night
d) Once per day

---

**3. An electronic leaf sensor controls mist by:**

a) Measuring air temperature
b) Detecting moisture level on artificial leaf
c) Timing intervals
d) Measuring light intensity

---

**4. Compared to mist systems, fog systems produce:**

a) Larger water droplets
b) Smaller water droplets (1-10 microns)
c) The same droplet size
d) No water droplets

---

**5. Excessive misting can cause:**

a) Nutrient leaching from leaves
b) Increased disease pressure
c) Reduced rooting
d) All of the above

---

**6. As cuttings develop roots (week 2-3), misting frequency should:**

a) Increase
b) Decrease
c) Stay the same
d) Stop completely immediately

---

**7. The ideal mist duration per cycle is:**

a) 1-2 seconds
b) 5-10 seconds
c) 30-60 seconds
d) 2-3 minutes

---

**8. Mist nozzles should be cleaned:**

a) Once per year
b) Once per month
c) Weekly or when clogged
d) Never needed

---

**9. Water quality for mist systems is critical because:**

a) Minerals can clog nozzles
b) Salts deposit on leaves blocking light
c) pH affects rooting
d) All of the above

---

**10. The main advantage of fog systems over mist systems is:**

a) Lower initial cost
b) Better humidity control with less wetting
c) Simpler to install
d) No maintenance required

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Reduce leaf temperature and prevent desiccation)
2. b (Operate during daylight when transpiration occurs)
3. b (Detects moisture on artificial leaf surface)
4. b (Fog produces much smaller droplets, 1-10 microns)
5. d (All of the above - over-misting has multiple negative effects)
6. b (Decrease as roots develop and plants become self-sufficient)
7. b (5-10 seconds is typical per mist cycle)
8. c (Clean weekly or when clogged to maintain performance)
9. d (All factors - minerals, salts, pH all matter)
10. b (Better humidity with less leaf wetting reduces disease)
