**11. Which treatment method provides the most consistent hormone coverage?**

b) Gel dip

**12. A propagator notices uneven rooting across a batch where powder hormone was used. The most likely cause is:**

a) Old hormone that has degraded
b) Uneven powder application
c) Variable cutting quality
d) Temperature fluctuations

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (IBA)
2. b (stays localized)
3. c (2,000-5,000 ppm for semi-hardwood)
4. 100 mL stock + 400 mL solvent (C1×V1=C2×V2: 10,000×V1=2,000×500; V1=100)
5. c (low concentration for long time)
6. b (too high concentration)
7. c (longer shelf life)
8. b (high auxin : low cytokinin)
9. a (too high concentration)
10. b (10 grams = 10,000 mg = 10,000 ppm in 1 L)
11. c (liquid quick dip)
12. b (uneven powder application)
