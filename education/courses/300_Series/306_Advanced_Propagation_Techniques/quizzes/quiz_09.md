## Questions

**1. The optimal air temperature for most cutting propagation is:**

a) 60-65°F
b) 70-75°F
c) 80-85°F
d) 90-95°F

---

**2. Bottom heat for rooting cuttings should be maintained at:**

a) Same as air temperature
b) 5-10°F warmer than air
c) 5-10°F cooler than air
d) Bottom heat is not beneficial

---

**3. Relative humidity during active rooting phase should be:**

a) 40-50%
b) 60-70%
c) 90-100%
d) Humidity doesn't matter

---

**4. DLI (Daily Light Integral) is measured in:**

a) Lumens
b) Watts
c) mol/m²/day
d) Degrees Celsius

---

**5. Seedlings generally require a DLI of:**

a) 2-4 mol/m²/day
b) 6-12 mol/m²/day
c) 15-20 mol/m²/day
d) 25-30 mol/m²/day

---

**6. LED grow lights for propagation should have:**

a) Only red spectrum
b) Only blue spectrum
c) Full spectrum with emphasis on blue and red
d) Only white light

---

**7. Air circulation in a propagation area helps:**

a) Strengthen stems
b) Reduce disease pressure
c) Even out temperature
d) All of the above

---

**8. The purpose of zoning a propagation facility is to:**

a) Separate different environmental needs
b) Improve workflow efficiency
c) Optimize space utilization
d) All of the above

---

**9. Germination chambers are typically kept:**

a) Cool and dark
b) Warm and dark/low light
c) Cool and bright
d) Warm and bright

---

**10. Bench heating systems for propagation commonly use:**

a) Hot air blowers
b) Radiant ceiling heaters
c) Hot water tubes or heating cables
d) Wood stoves

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (70-75°F is optimal for most propagation)
2. b (Bottom heat should be 5-10°F warmer than air)
3. c (90-100% RH during active rooting prevents desiccation)
4. c (DLI measured in mol/m²/day)
5. b (6-12 mol/m²/day typical for seedlings)
6. c (Full spectrum with blue and red emphasis)
7. d (All of the above - multiple benefits)
8. d (All of the above - zoning serves multiple purposes)
9. b (Warm and dark/low light for germination)
10. c (Hot water tubes or heating cables for bottom heat)
