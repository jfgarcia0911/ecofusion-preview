## Questions

**1. When troubleshooting poor germination, the first factors to check are:**

a) Seed quality, temperature, and moisture
b) Fertilizer levels
c) Light spectrum
d) Container size

---

**2. If cuttings are wilting excessively under mist, the most likely cause is:**

a) Too much misting
b) Insufficient misting
c) Too much light or heat
d) Both b and c

---

**3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) in propagation emphasizes:**

a) Heavy pesticide use
b) Prevention, monitoring, and targeted intervention
c) Ignoring pests until harvest
d) Only organic methods

---

**4. The best time to take softwood cuttings is:**

a) Late afternoon
b) Early morning when turgid
c) Midday in full sun
d) Time of day doesn't matter

---

**5. Which propagation method provides the fastest turnaround for basil production?**

a) Seed propagation
b) Softwood cuttings
c) Grafting
d) Tissue culture

---

**6. To prevent damping off disease, you should:**

a) Use sterile media
b) Provide good air circulation
c) Avoid overwatering
d) All of the above

---

**7. Economic analysis of propagation should include:**

a) Direct material and labor costs only
b) Direct costs plus allocated overhead
c) Only seed costs
d) Equipment costs only

---

**8. The primary reason for hardening off transplants is to:**

a) Make them look better
b) Acclimate to outdoor conditions (light, temperature, wind)
c) Reduce watering needs
d) Increase plant height

---

**9. If a graft union fails to heal after 10 days, possible causes include:**

a) Poor cambium alignment
b) Desiccation during healing
c) Disease at graft site
d) All of the above

---

**10. For year-round propagation business success, the most important factor is:**

a) Expensive equipment
b) Largest greenhouse
c) Consistent quality and reliable delivery
d) Lowest prices

---

## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. a (Seed quality, temperature, moisture are primary factors)
2. d (Both insufficient misting AND too much light/heat cause wilting)
3. b (IPM emphasizes prevention, monitoring, targeted action)
4. b (Early morning when plants are fully turgid)
5. b (Softwood cuttings - faster than seed, practical unlike TC)
6. d (All of the above - integrated prevention approach)
7. b (Full costing includes direct + overhead)
8. b (Acclimation to outdoor conditions)
9. d (All can cause graft failure)
10. c (Consistent quality and reliability build reputation)
