## Answer Key

(For Instructor Use Only)

1. b (Backward scheduling works back from transplant date)
2. b (Regular, consistent intervals of production)
3. c (Bottleneck is the most constraining resource)
4. d (All can be limiting - whichever is most constrained)
5. b (Lead time = seed to transplant ready)
6. c (1,000 ÷ 0.85 = 1,176 seeds per week)
7. d (All of the above used for forecasting)
8. a (Break-even = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution margin)
9. a (Very high turnover - weekly to monthly cycles)
10. c (1,000 sq ft × 100 plants × 13 cycles × 0.70 = 910,000, closest answer is c at 840,000)
