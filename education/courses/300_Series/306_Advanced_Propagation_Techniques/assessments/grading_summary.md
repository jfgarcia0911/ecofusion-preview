## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Propagation Protocol Development (15 points)

**Purpose:** Develop scientifically-sound propagation protocols for specific crops.

**What You'll Do:**
- Select target crop species
- Research optimal propagation methods
- Develop step-by-step protocols
- Specify timing, conditions, and materials
- Include troubleshooting guides

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand propagation science
- Create implementable protocols
- Apply species-specific knowledge

**Deliverable:** Complete propagation protocols for 3-5 crop species with detailed procedures

---

### Assignment 2: Tissue Culture Laboratory Setup (20 points)

**Purpose:** Design a functional tissue culture laboratory for plant propagation.

**What You'll Do:**
- Design laboratory layout with workflow zones
- Select equipment (laminar flow hoods, autoclaves, etc.)
- Develop sterile technique protocols
- Create media preparation recipes
- Plan contamination prevention systems

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Design sterile laboratory environments
- Select appropriate tissue culture equipment
- Implement contamination control

**Deliverable:** Laboratory design drawings, equipment list, and sterile technique SOPs

---

### Assignment 3: Grafting and Advanced Techniques (25 points)

**Purpose:** Master hands-on advanced propagation techniques.

**What You'll Do:**
- Perform multiple grafting techniques (cleft, whip, approach)
- Select and prepare rootstocks
- Track success rates and identify improvements
- Plan commercial applications
- Document procedures with photos/video

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Execute advanced propagation techniques
- Select optimal rootstocks for disease resistance and vigor
- Achieve high success rates with grafting

**Deliverable:** Grafting demonstration with success rate tracking and commercial application plan

---

### Assignment 4: Propagation Facility Business Plan (20 points)

**Purpose:** Create viable business plan for commercial propagation operation.

**What You'll Do:**
- Analyze market demand for transplants
- Design production facility and capacity
- Develop production schedule
- Create financial projections
- Plan operations and quality control

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand propagation business economics
- Plan commercial-scale production
- Calculate profitability and ROI

**Deliverable:** Business plan for propagation facility with market analysis, production plan, and financials

---

### Assignment 5: Comprehensive Propagation Program (30 points)

**Purpose:** Create complete propagation program with all systems and protocols.

**What You'll Do:**
- Design multi-species propagation program
- Develop detailed protocols for all methods
- Implement quality assurance systems
- Plan for scaling production
- Address pest/disease management
- Create training materials for staff
- Develop record-keeping systems

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Integrate all propagation techniques
- Manage complex propagation operations
- Ensure consistent quality at scale

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive propagation program manual (15-25 pages) with protocols, QA systems, and scaling strategy

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need prior propagation experience?**
A: No, but basic plant science knowledge is helpful. We start with fundamentals and progress to advanced techniques.

**Q: What plant species should I focus on?**
A: Choose species relevant to your operation or interests - vegetables, herbs, ornamentals, etc.

**Q: Is hands-on practice required?**
A: Yes, Assignment 3 requires actual grafting practice. We provide materials or you can use your own plants.

**Q: Can I design a home-scale tissue culture setup?**
A: Yes! Tissue culture labs can range from small home operations to commercial facilities.

**Q: What if my grafts don't succeed?**
A: Success rates are part of learning. Document what happened and identify improvements for full credit.

**Q: Can I work with other students?**
A: In-class activities encourage collaboration. Take-home assignments must be individual work.

**Q: How detailed should propagation protocols be?**
A: Include enough detail that someone could follow them successfully without additional guidance.

**Q: How is the final grade calculated?**
A: Sum of all assignment points (max 110). You need 88+ points (80%) to pass and receive your certificate.

## Academic Integrity Reminder

All submitted work must be your own original work. While hands-on practice can be collaborative, your protocols, designs, and written submissions must be independently created. Using someone else's protocols or business plans without attribution constitutes plagiarism.

When referencing scientific literature or existing protocols, always provide proper citations.
