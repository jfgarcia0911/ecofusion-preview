# Grading Rubric
## Course 306: Advanced Propagation Techniques

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 306. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Propagation Protocol Development | 15 | 13.6% | In-class activity creating species-specific protocols |
| Assignment 2: Tissue Culture Laboratory Setup | 20 | 18.2% | In-class lab design and sterile technique planning |
| Assignment 3: Grafting and Advanced Techniques | 25 | 22.7% | In-class hands-on propagation methods |
| Assignment 4: Propagation Facility Business Plan | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home commercial propagation operation plan |
| Assignment 5: Comprehensive Propagation Program | 30 | 27.3% | Complete nursery operations strategy |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

## Assignment 1: Propagation Protocol Development (15 points)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Protocol Completeness** (5 pts) | Detailed step-by-step protocols with timing and conditions | Good protocol outline with key steps | Basic protocol information | Incomplete protocol |
| **Scientific Accuracy** (5 pts) | Scientifically sound methods based on research | Generally accurate approach | Some inaccuracies present | Poor scientific basis |
| **Species Selection** (5 pts) | Appropriate methods for specific crop requirements | Reasonable method selection | Limited species consideration | Inappropriate methods |

**Total:** 15 points

---

## Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% penalty
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% penalty
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval
- Extensions may be granted for extenuating circumstances with advance notice

---

## Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. While collaboration and discussion are encouraged during in-class activities, individual assignments must reflect your own analysis and work. Plagiarism or copying will result in a zero for the assignment and may result in dismissal from the course.

---

## Feedback and Revision

- In-class activities receive immediate feedback
- Take-home assignments graded within 5 business days
- Final projects graded within 7 business days
- Students may request clarification on grading decisions
- Major projects may be revised and resubmitted once if score is below 80%
