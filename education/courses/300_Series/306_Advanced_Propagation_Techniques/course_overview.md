### Viability Testing Methods

| Method | Time Required | Accuracy | Best For |
|--------|---------------|----------|----------|
| Standard germination test | 7-21 days | Highest | All seeds |
| Tetrazolium (TZ) test | 24-48 hours | High | Quick assessment |
| Electrical conductivity | 24 hours | Moderate | Vigor testing |
| Float test | Minutes | Low | Large seeds |
| Cut test | Minutes | Low | Visual check |

## Germination Requirements

### Optimal Conditions by Crop Type

| Crop Category | Temperature | Light | Notes |
|---------------|-------------|-------|-------|
| Lettuce | 65-70°F | Light required | Inhibited above 77°F |
| Tomato | 70-85°F | Not required | Needs warmth |
| Basil | 70-75°F | Light helpful | Consistent moisture |
| Peppers | 80-90°F | Not required | Bottom heat essential |
| Cucumber | 70-95°F | Not required | Fast germination |
| Brassicas | 65-75°F | Not required | Cool germinators |

### Seed Treatments

| Treatment | Purpose | Application |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Priming | Faster germination | Pre-hydration |
| Scarification | Break dormancy | Physical/chemical |
| Stratification | Break dormancy | Cold treatment |
| Inoculation | Add beneficials | Rhizobium, mycorrhizae |
| Coating | Handling, protection | Pelleting, film coat |

## Vegetative Propagation Methods

### Cutting Types & Success Rates

| Cutting Type | Best Season | Rooting Time | Success Rate |
|--------------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| Softwood | Spring-early summer | 2-4 weeks | 80-95% |
| Semi-hardwood | Late summer | 4-8 weeks | 60-80% |
| Hardwood | Winter dormant | 8-16 weeks | 40-70% |
| Leaf | Anytime | 4-8 weeks | 70-90% |
| Root | Early spring | 6-12 weeks | 50-80% |

### Rooting Hormone Guide

| Hormone Type | IBA Concentration | Best For |
|--------------|-------------------|----------|
| Gel (light) | 1,000-2,500 ppm | Softwood, easy rooters |
| Gel (medium) | 3,000-8,000 ppm | Semi-hardwood |
| Gel (strong) | 10,000+ ppm | Hardwood, difficult species |
| Liquid | Variable | Precise control |
| Powder | Variable | Traditional method |

## Commercial Propagation System Design

### Environment Specifications

| Parameter | Seed Germination | Cutting Propagation |
|-----------|------------------|---------------------|
| Air temperature | 68-77°F | 70-75°F |
| Bottom heat | 72-80°F | 70-77°F |
| Relative humidity | 70-80% | 90-100% |
| Light level | 0-500 ft-candles | 500-1,000 ft-candles |
| Photoperiod | 12-16 hours | 12-18 hours |

### Production Calculations

```
Seedlings Needed = (Weekly Sales × Plants per Unit) ÷ Transplant Success Rate

Propagation Capacity = Available Space × Plants per sq ft × Cycles per Year

Example:
Weekly transplants needed: 5,000
Propagation density: 100 plants/sq ft
Growth time: 3 weeks
Space required: (5,000 × 3) ÷ 100 = 150 sq ft propagation area
```
