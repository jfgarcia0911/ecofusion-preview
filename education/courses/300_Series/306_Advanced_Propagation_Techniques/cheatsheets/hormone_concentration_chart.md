## Application Methods

### Quick Dip (Recommended for Commercial Use)

**Advantages:**
- Fast and efficient
- Precise concentration control
- Can treat many cuttings with small volume
- Less waste than powder

**Procedure:**
1. Pour working solution into small container (50-100 mL)
2. Dip cutting base 1/2-1" deep
3. Hold for 5 seconds
4. Allow alcohol to evaporate 30 seconds
5. Stick into medium

**Tips:**
- Replace solution every 100 cuttings or when contaminated
- Use separate container (don't dip into stock bottle)
- Work quickly - cuttings can dry out

### Long Soak

**Advantages:**
- Gentle, low phytotoxicity risk
- Hormone penetrates deeper
- Good for difficult species
- Can treat large batches overnight

**Procedure:**
1. Dilute to appropriate concentration in water
2. Bundle 10-25 cuttings with rubber band
3. Stand upright in solution, bases submerged 1-2"
4. Keep in cool, dark location 12-24 hours
5. Remove, rinse briefly, stick

### Powder/Gel

**Advantages:**
- No mixing required
- Long shelf life
- Simple to use
- Portable

**Procedure:**
1. Moisten base of cutting (for powder) or use dry (gel)
2. Dip base 1/4-1/2" into product
3. Tap to remove excess
4. Stick immediately

**Tips:**
- Pour small amount into separate dish (don't contaminate main supply)
- Remove excess - too much inhibits rooting
- Check expiration date

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **No improvement with hormone** | Concentration too low, hormone degraded | Increase concentration, use fresh hormone |
| **Tissue burning/death at cut** | Concentration too high | Reduce by 25-50%, shorter dip time |
| **Excessive callus, few roots** | Too high concentration or NAA | Switch to IBA, lower concentration |
| **Uneven rooting** | Inconsistent application | Use liquid quick dip for uniformity |
| **Roots only on one side** | Powder application uneven | Dip entire circumference evenly |

## Safety and Storage

**Handling:**
- Wear gloves when handling concentrates
- Work in well-ventilated area (alcohol fumes)
- Wash hands after use

**Storage:**
- Store powder/gel in cool, dry location
- Stock solutions: Dark glass bottle, cool temperature
- Working solutions: Use same day, discard after
- Label all containers with concentration and date

**Shelf Life:**
- Powder: 2-3 years sealed, 1 year opened
- Gel: 1-2 years
- Stock solution (alcohol): 6-12 months
- Working solution: Same day use only

---

## Notes:

- Start with lower end of range; increase if results poor
- Add wounding for difficult species (increases uptake)
- Higher concentrations may inhibit rather than promote
- Environmental factors (humidity, temp) matter as much as hormone
