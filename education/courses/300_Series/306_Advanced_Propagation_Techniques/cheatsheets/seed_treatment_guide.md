## Pre-Treatment Methods

### Scarification (Breaking Hard Seed Coats)

| Method | Procedure | Best For | Safety |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Mechanical** | Rub with sandpaper or file | Large seeds (beans, peas) | Safe |
| **Hot Water** | Pour 170-190°F water over seeds, soak 12-24 hrs | Medium seeds (many legumes) | Caution: hot water |
| **Boiling Water** | Pour boiling water, remove from heat, soak 24 hrs | Very hard coats (some natives) | Caution: boiling water |
| **Acid** | Soak in concentrated sulfuric acid 10-60 min, rinse thoroughly | Commercial scale, very hard seeds | Danger: wear PPE |

### Stratification (Breaking Dormancy)

| Type | Temperature | Duration | Moisture | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Cold Moist** | 34-41°F | 30-120 days | Moist medium | Apple, many perennials |
| **Warm then Cold** | 68-77°F then 34-41°F | 30-90 days each | Moist | Some natives, trillium |
| **Cold Dry** | 32-40°F | Variable | Dry storage | Some herbs |

**Moist Stratification Setup:**
1. Mix seeds with moist (not wet) vermiculite or peat
2. Place in sealed plastic bag
3. Refrigerate at 34-41°F
4. Check weekly, add moisture if needed
5. Sow when specified time complete

### Priming (Speed Germination)

| Method | Solution | Duration | Rinse | Dry Before Sowing |
|--------|----------|----------|-------|-------------------|
| **Hydropriming** | Pure water | 12-24 hrs | No | Yes (1-2 hrs) |
| **Osmopriming** | PEG 6000 or KNO₃ | 1-7 days | Yes, thoroughly | Yes |
| **Solid Matrix** | Vermiculite + water (1:3 w:w) | 3-7 days | No | Slight surface dry |

**Quick Priming Protocol:**
1. Soak seeds in water 12-24 hours
2. Drain and surface dry
3. Sow immediately or dry completely for storage
4. Expect 1-3 days faster germination

## Seed Treatment Decision Tree

```
START: Do seeds germinate >85% without treatment?
  │
  ├─ YES → No treatment needed
  │
  └─ NO → Perform germination test to diagnose
      │
      ├─ Hard, impermeable seed coat?
      │   └─ YES → SCARIFY (mechanical/hot water/acid)
      │
      ├─ Seeds imbibe water but don't grow?
      │   └─ YES → Check for dormancy:
      │       ├─ Need cold? → COLD STRATIFY
      │       ├─ Need warm? → WARM STRATIFY
      │       └─ Light required? → GERMINATE IN LIGHT
      │
      └─ Germination slow but eventually good?
          └─ YES → PRIME to speed up (optional)
```

## Common Crops Quick Reference

| Crop | Treatment | Duration/Method | Notes |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| **Lettuce** | None (or prime) | - | Light required, <77°F |
| **Tomato** | None | - | 70-85°F optimal |
| **Pepper** | None (or prime) | - | Needs warmth, 80-90°F |
| **Basil** | None | - | 70-80°F |
| **Peas** | Scarify (if old) | Nick or soak 12 hrs | Inoculate with rhizobia |
| **Beans** | None (fresh seed) | - | Don't oversoak |
| **Parsley** | Soak | 24 hours | Slow (14-21 days) |
| **Carrot** | Prime (optional) | 24-48 hrs | Very slow without |
| **Spinach** | Cold water soak | 24 hours | Germinate cool |
| **Onion** | None | - | Don't need light |

## Seed Coating Types

| Type | Weight Increase | Purpose | Cost |
|------|----------------|---------|------|
| **Film Coat** | 5-10% | Color code, handle, add chemicals | $ |
| **Encrustment** | 30-100% | Improve shape, precision seeding | $$ |
| **Pelleting** | 200-500% | Round seed, automated seeding | $$$ |

**When to Use Pelleted Seed:**
- Automated vacuum seeders
- Very small seeds (petunia, lettuce)
- Precision spacing required
- Worth extra cost if labor savings significant

## Storage After Treatment

| Treatment | Can Store? | How | Duration |
|-----------|------------|-----|----------|
| Scarified | Yes | Dry, cool | Same as untreated |
| Stratified | Limited | Keep cold, moist until sowing | Sow within 1 week |
| Primed | Limited | Dry completely then store cool | Up to 6 months if dried |
| Coated | Yes | Cool, dry | Check manufacturer specs |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| No germination after hot water | Water too hot, killed embryo | Use 170-190°F, not boiling |
| Rotting during stratification | Too wet, poor air | Use barely moist medium |
| Uneven germination | Inadequate treatment time | Extend stratification period |
| Still hard after scarification | Insufficient scarification | Retreat more aggressively |

---

## Safety Reminders

- **Acid scarification**: Wear goggles, gloves, apron. Add acid to water, never reverse. Neutralize with baking soda before disposal.
- **Hot water**: Use thermometer, don't guess. Too hot kills seed.
- **Treated seed**: Commercial fungicide-treated seed (colored) should not be eaten. Wear gloves when handling.
