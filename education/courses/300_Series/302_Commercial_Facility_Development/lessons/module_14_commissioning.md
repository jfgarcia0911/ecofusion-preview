# Module 14: Commissioning Process

**Duration:** 1 hour
**Learning Objectives:**
- Execute comprehensive facility commissioning
- Conduct crop trials and validation
- Train operations staff effectively
- Document system performance
- Achieve operational readiness

---

## 1. Commissioning Overview

```
COMMISSIONING PHASES

Phase 1: Pre-Functional Verification (Week 1-2)
□ Equipment installation complete
□ All connections made
□ Power and utilities available
□ Pre-startup checklists complete
□ Safety devices functional

Phase 2: Functional Performance Testing (Week 3-5)
□ Individual system testing
□ Integrated system testing
□ Performance verification
□ Deficiency identification and correction

Phase 3: Crop Trials (Week 6-12)
□ First test crops planted
□ System performance under load
□ Crop quality assessment
□ System adjustments and optimization

Phase 4: Training & Documentation (Ongoing)
□ Staff training on all systems
□ SOPs developed and documented
□ As-built documentation compiled
□ Warranty registration completed

Phase 5: Performance Verification (Week 12-16)
□ Systems meeting performance targets
□ Crops growing successfully
□ Staff proficient in operations
□ Ready for full production

Total Time: 3-4 months typical
```

## 2. System Performance Testing

```
FUNCTIONAL PERFORMANCE TEST PLAN

Environmental Control System:
Test Objective: Maintain setpoints under load

Test Procedure:
1. Set environmental targets (temp, humidity, CO2)
2. Run lights at full intensity (maximum heat load)
3. Operate irrigation (add humidity load)
4. Monitor for 72+ hours
5. Record all parameters every 15 minutes

Success Criteria:
□ Temperature: ±2°F of setpoint
□ Humidity: ±5% RH of setpoint
□ CO2: ±100 ppm of setpoint (if enriching)
□ Recovery time after disturbance: <30 minutes
□ No system faults or shutdowns

Irrigation System:
Test Objective: Deliver uniform, controlled water and nutrients

Test Procedure:
1. Program full irrigation schedule
2. Run for one week
3. Measure flow, pressure, pH, EC at multiple points
4. Calculate uniformity coefficient
5. Verify dosing accuracy (compare input to output)

Success Criteria:
□ Uniformity coefficient >90%
□ pH: ±0.2 of target
□ EC: ±10% of target
□ Pressure: ±5 PSI across zones
□ No leaks, clogs, or failures

Lighting System:
Test Objective: Provide uniform, reliable light

Test Procedure:
1. Operate full photoperiod for 2 weeks
2. Measure PAR at multiple points
3. Monitor for failures
4. Test dimming function (if applicable)
5. Verify timer accuracy

Success Criteria:
□ Average PAR: ±10% of spec
□ Uniformity: <15% variation
□ Zero failures
□ Dimming smooth 0-100%
□ Timer accuracy: ±1 minute per day

Water Treatment System:
Test Objective: Produce quality water consistently

Test Procedure:
1. Operate continuously for 1 week
2. Test input and output water daily
3. Measure flow rate
4. Monitor waste ratio
5. Track filter pressure differential

Success Criteria:
□ Output TDS: <50 ppm
□ Flow rate: ≥design flow
□ Waste ratio: 3:1 to 5:1
□ No fouling indicators (rising pressure drop)

Control System:
Test Objective: Accurately monitor and control all systems

Test Procedure:
1. Verify all sensor readings
2. Test all control outputs
3. Simulate alarm conditions
4. Verify data logging
5. Test remote access (if enabled)

Success Criteria:
□ Sensors accurate (±2% for critical parameters)
□ Controls responsive (<1 minute to command)
□ Alarms functional
□ Data logged continuously
□ Remote access secure and functional

Deficiency Resolution:
□ Document all test failures
□ Identify root causes
□ Implement corrections
□ Retest until passing
□ Sign off when acceptable
```

## 3. Crop Trials

```
FIRST CROP TRIAL PROTOCOL

Crop Selection:
□ Choose fast-growing, forgiving crop (lettuce, herbs)
□ Start with 10-25% of capacity
□ Single variety for consistency
□ Known cultivar with established requirements

Trial Setup:
□ Prepare growing areas
□ Calibrate irrigation and fertigation
□ Verify environmental setpoints appropriate
□ Document starting conditions

Monitoring:
Daily:
□ Visual inspection (color, vigor, pests, diseases)
□ Spot-check pH and EC
□ Environmental parameters
□ Photo documentation

Weekly:
□ Measure growth (height, leaf count, etc.)
□ Detailed water quality checks
□ Detailed environmental data review
□ Adjust systems as needed

Data Collection:
□ Germination rate (if starting from seed)
□ Growth rate (height, biomass)
□ Time to harvest
□ Yield per plant
□ Quality assessment (color, flavor, shelf life)
□ Waste/cull rate

System Performance:
□ Irrigation uniformity (do all plants grow equally?)
□ Light uniformity (do all areas produce equally?)
□ Environmental stability (any stress indicators?)
□ Equipment reliability (any failures?)

Issues to Watch:
⚠ Nutrient deficiencies (chlorosis, stunting)
⚠ Nutrient toxicities (tip burn, etc.)
⚠ Environmental stress (stretching, bitterness)
⚠ Pests or diseases (even in controlled environment)
⚠ System failures (outages, leaks, etc.)

Adjustments:
Based on observations:
□ Fine-tune nutrient formulation
□ Adjust pH/EC targets
□ Modify light intensity/photoperiod
□ Tweak temperature/humidity setpoints
□ Change irrigation frequency/duration

Success Criteria:
□ Crops reach harvestable size
□ Quality meets market standards
□ Yield in expected range
□ Few losses/culls (<10%)
□ Systems performed reliably
□ Team capable of managing

Next Steps:
If successful:
→ Scale up to 50% capacity
→ Introduce crop diversity
→ Begin market sales

If issues:
→ Diagnose problems
→ Make necessary changes
→ Repeat trial
→ Don't proceed to full production until resolved
```

## 6. Performance Metrics

```
KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

Production Metrics:
□ Yield (lbs or units per SF per year)
□ Crop quality (% meeting grade standards)
□ Turns per year (harvest cycles)
□ Waste/cull rate (% unsaleable)
□ Time to harvest (days from seed/transplant)

System Performance:
□ Uptime (% of time operational)
□ Environmental setpoint adherence (% of time in range)
□ Irrigation uniformity coefficient
□ Light intensity uniformity
□ Water quality (TDS, pH)

Resource Efficiency:
□ Energy use per unit production (kWh/lb)
□ Water use per unit production (gal/lb)
□ Nutrient use efficiency (lbs nutrients/lbs produce)
□ Labor hours per unit production

Financial Metrics:
□ Revenue per SF per year
□ Cost of goods sold (% of revenue)
□ Gross profit margin
□ Operating expenses (% of revenue)
□ Net profit margin

Targets:

Lettuce Production (example):
□ Yield: 25-35 lbs/SF/year
□ Quality: >95% Grade A
□ Turns: 12-16 per year
□ Waste: <5%
□ Time to harvest: 21-35 days

System Performance:
□ Uptime: >99%
□ Temp in range: >98% of time
□ Humidity in range: >95% of time
□ Irrigation uniformity: >90%
□ Light uniformity: CV <15%

Efficiency:
□ Energy: 5-10 kWh/lb
□ Water: 3-7 gal/lb
□ Labor: 0.15-0.25 hrs/lb

Financial:
□ Revenue: $40-80/SF/year
□ COGS: 30-50% of revenue
□ Gross margin: 50-70%
□ Operating expenses: 30-50% of revenue
□ Net margin: 10-30%

Tracking:
□ Data collection systems
□ Weekly/monthly reports
□ Variance analysis
□ Continuous improvement initiatives
```

## Summary

Successful commissioning requires:

1. **Systematic testing** - all systems individually and integrated
2. **Crop validation** - actual growing trials
3. **Staff training** - operators proficient
4. **SOP development** - procedures documented
5. **Performance verification** - systems meeting targets
6. **Thorough documentation** - as-builts, manuals, reports
7. **Issue resolution** - all deficiencies corrected
8. **Readiness assessment** - production, operations, market
9. **Formal acceptance** - sign-off by all parties
10. **Continuous improvement** - ongoing optimization

Commissioning bridges construction and operations. Take the time to do it right for long-term success.
