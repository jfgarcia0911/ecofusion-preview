## 1. Final Project Overview

### Project Assignment

**Objective:** Create a comprehensive development plan for a commercial CEA facility

**Deliverables:**
1. Executive Summary (2 pages)
2. Site Evaluation & Selection (5-8 pages)
3. Facility Design Overview (5-8 pages)
4. Project Budget (3-5 pages)
5. Project Schedule (2-3 pages)
6. Financing Plan (3-5 pages)
7. Risk Analysis & Mitigation (3-5 pages)
8. Operational Plan (3-5 pages)
9. Financial Projections (3-5 pages)
10. Presentation Slides (15-20 slides)

**Total Report:** 30-50 pages + presentation

**Project Scenarios (Choose One):**

**Scenario A: Urban Vertical Farm**
- Location: Major metro area (population >500K)
- Size: 10,000 SF warehouse conversion
- System: Vertical hydroponic (NFT or DWC)
- Products: Leafy greens and herbs
- Market: High-end restaurants and grocery

**Scenario B: Suburban Greenhouse Operation**
- Location: Suburban area (20-50 miles from city)
- Size: 20,000 SF new greenhouse construction
- System: Hydroponic (NFT or gutters)
- Products: Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers
- Market: Grocery stores and farmers markets

**Scenario C: Rural Aquaponic Facility**
- Location: Rural area (50+ miles from city)
- Size: 15,000 SF building (existing or new)
- System: Aquaponic (DWC with tilapia)
- Products: Leafy greens, herbs, and fish
- Market: Regional distributors and direct sales

## 12. Evaluation Criteria

Your project will be evaluated on:

**Completeness (25 points)**
- All required sections included
- Adequate detail and analysis
- Professional formatting

**Analysis Quality (25 points)**
- Thorough site evaluation
- Realistic budget and schedule
- Sound financial projections
- Thoughtful risk analysis

**Technical Competence (20 points)**
- Appropriate system design
- Correct sizing calculations
- Feasible operational plan
- Industry best practices applied

**Financial Viability (15 points)**
- Realistic revenue assumptions
- Adequate capitalization
- Achievable profitability
- Acceptable return on investment

**Presentation Quality (15 points)**
- Clear and organized
- Effective visual aids
- Professional delivery
- Addresses questions well

**Total: 100 points**

**Grading Scale:**
- 90-100: A (Excellent, investment-ready)
- 80-89: B (Good, needs minor refinement)
- 70-79: C (Acceptable, needs significant work)
- <70: D/F (Not viable, major issues)
