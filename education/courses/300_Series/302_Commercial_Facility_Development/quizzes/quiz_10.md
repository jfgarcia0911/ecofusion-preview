# Quiz 10: Construction Management

**Course:** 302 - Commercial Facility Development
**Module:** 10
**Points:** 3

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. What is the primary role of a construction manager?

A) Perform construction work
B) Coordinate schedule, budget, and quality
C) Design the facility
D) Select equipment

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. How often should you conduct site visits during active construction?

A) Once at project start
B) Monthly
C) Weekly or more frequently
D) Only at completion

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is a "change order"?

A) Modification to original contract scope or price
B) Firing the contractor
C) Changing project managers
D) Ordering new equipment

**Answer:** A

---

### 4. What should be documented during construction site visits?

A) Weather conditions only
B) Progress, issues, decisions, and photos
C) Contractor lunch breaks
D) Visitor names only

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is the purpose of a construction schedule?

A) Determine contractor pay only
B) Coordinate work sequencing and timeline
C) Satisfy permit requirements
D) Marketing purposes

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. Who should attend weekly construction meetings?

A) Owner only
B) Key stakeholders: owner, contractor, architect, consultants
C) Contractor only
D) Neighbors and public

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is "retainage" in construction contracts?

A) Percentage withheld until satisfactory completion
B) Extra payment for good work
C) Contractor profit margin
D) Sales tax on materials

**Answer:** A

---

### 8. When should equipment long-lead items be ordered?

A) After construction is complete
B) Early in construction to avoid delays
C) Only after building inspection
D) During commissioning

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is a punch list?

A) List of contractors to hire
B) Final items needing correction before closeout
C) Equipment to purchase
D) Daily task list

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What should trigger extra scrutiny during construction?

A) Contractors asking questions
B) Significant deviations from schedule or budget
C) Normal material deliveries
D) Regular progress

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Coordinate schedule, budget, and quality
2. C - Weekly or more frequently
3. A - Modification to original contract scope or price
4. B - Progress, issues, decisions, and photos
5. B - Coordinate work sequencing and timeline
6. B - Key stakeholders: owner, contractor, architect, consultants
7. A - Percentage withheld until satisfactory completion
8. B - Early in construction to avoid delays
9. B - Final items needing correction before closeout
10. B - Significant deviations from schedule or budget
