## Instructions

## Questions

### 1. What is the first phase of design development?

A) Construction documents
B) Schematic design
C) Design development
D) Permit submission

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. Who leads the design team for a CEA facility?

A) General contractor
B) Architect or engineer
C) Equipment manufacturer
D) Building owner

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. What is a critical consideration in CEA facility layout?

A) Decorative aesthetics
B) Workflow efficiency and contamination control
C) Maximum window placement
D) Open floor plans for collaboration

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which design element is essential for maintaining climate control?

A) Large windows for natural light
B) Proper insulation and vapor barriers
C) Open doorways between zones
D) Minimal HVAC capacity

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is the purpose of a "design-build" approach?

A) Owner designs, separate contractor builds
B) Single entity responsible for both design and construction
C) Multiple contractors compete during construction
D) Design without construction involvement

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. What should drive facility layout decisions?

A) Architectural aesthetics primarily
B) Operational workflow and efficiency
C) Lowest construction cost only
D) Maximum square footage

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is an important consideration for floor design in CEA facilities?

A) Decorative tile patterns
B) Drainage and slip resistance
C) Carpet for noise reduction
D) Glass flooring for transparency

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. Why are "as-built" drawings important?

A) They show the original design intent
B) They document actual construction as completed
C) They are only needed for permits
D) They are optional documentation

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is a common mistake in CEA facility design?

A) Over-designing structural capacity
B) Insufficient space for equipment access and maintenance
C) Too much electrical capacity
D) Excessive ceiling height

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. When should equipment manufacturers be consulted in design?

A) After construction is complete
B) Early in schematic design phase
C) Only during installation
D) Never - designers know best

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Schematic design
2. B - Architect or engineer
3. B - Workflow efficiency and contamination control
4. B - Proper insulation and vapor barriers
5. B - Single entity responsible for both design and construction
6. B - Operational workflow and efficiency
7. B - Drainage and slip resistance
8. B - They document actual construction as completed
9. B - Insufficient space for equipment access and maintenance
10. B - Early in schematic design phase
