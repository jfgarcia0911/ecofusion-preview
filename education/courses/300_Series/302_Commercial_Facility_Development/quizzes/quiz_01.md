**Module:** 1

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. What is the typical capital cost range per square foot for commercial CEA facilities?

A) $20-$50 per sq ft
B) $50-$100 per sq ft
C) $100-$300+ per sq ft
D) $500-$1000 per sq ft

**Answer:** C

---

### 2. Which project phase typically takes the longest in facility development?

A) Planning
B) Design & Permitting
C) Construction
D) Commissioning

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is a critical success factor that sets CEA projects apart from traditional agriculture?

A) Low capital requirements
B) Simple technical systems
C) Specialized contractors with CEA experience
D) Minimal permitting requirements

**Answer:** C

---

### 4. For a medium-scale CEA facility (5,000-20,000 sq ft), what is the typical development timeline?

A) 6-12 months
B) 12-18 months
C) 18-30 months
D) 24-48 months

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. Which of the following is NOT a unique consideration for CEA facility development?

A) High electrical loads
B) Specialized irrigation systems
C) Agricultural vs. industrial zoning classification
D) Low water usage requirements

**Answer:** D

---

### 6. What is the recommended contingency percentage for new construction CEA projects?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-15%
C) 15-20%
D) 25-30%

**Answer:** C

---

### 7. During which phase should financing be secured?

A) Planning
B) Pre-Development
C) Construction
D) Commissioning

**Answer:** A

---

### 8. What is the primary purpose of the commissioning phase?

A) Obtain building permits
B) System testing and staff training
C) Secure financing
D) Select contractors

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which factor contributes most to timeline variability in CEA facility development?

A) Equipment delivery times
B) Permitting processes
C) Weather delays
D) Labor shortages

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What percentage of total capital cost typically goes to MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-15%
C) 20-25%
D) 30-40%

**Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. C - $100-$300+ per sq ft
2. C - Construction
3. C - Specialized contractors with CEA experience
4. B - 12-18 months
5. D - Low water usage requirements (CEA typically uses significant water)
6. C - 15-20%
7. A - Planning
8. B - System testing and staff training
9. B - Permitting processes
10. C - 20-25%
