## Questions

### 1. What is the first step in the permitting process?

A) Submit building permit application
B) Verify zoning compliance
C) Hire contractors
D) Order equipment

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. How long can the permitting process take in complex jurisdictions?

A) 1-2 weeks
B) 1-2 months
C) 2-6 months or more
D) 1-2 years

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. Which permit is typically required for CEA water systems?

A) Food service permit
B) Plumbing and water discharge permit
C) Pesticide application permit
D) Waste management permit

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What classification challenge do CEA facilities often face?

A) Agricultural vs. industrial zoning
B) Residential vs. commercial
C) Retail vs. wholesale
D) Educational vs. recreational

**Answer:** A

---

### 5. Which professional typically stamps structural drawings for permits?

A) Architect
B) General contractor
C) Licensed structural engineer
D) Equipment manufacturer

**Answer:** C

---

### 6. What is a common requirement for food safety certification?

A) FDA registration
B) USDA organic certification
C) EPA environmental permit
D) OSHA workplace safety approval

**Answer:** A

---

### 7. When should you engage with local planning officials?

A) After construction begins
B) After permits are denied
C) Early in the site selection process
D) Only if required by law

**Answer:** C

---

### 8. What is a "conditional use permit"?

A) Temporary permission for short-term use
B) Special approval for non-standard uses in a zone
C) A permit that expires annually
D) Permission to use residential property

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which building code aspect is critical for CEA facilities?

A) Residential egress requirements
B) Electrical load capacity and safety
C) Retail display regulations
D) Office furniture specifications

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What environmental permit may be required for water discharge?

A) NPDES (National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System)
B) Pesticide application license
C) Air quality permit for emissions
D) Solid waste permit

**Answer:** A

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Verify zoning compliance
2. C - 2-6 months or more
3. B - Plumbing and water discharge permit
4. A - Agricultural vs. industrial zoning
5. C - Licensed structural engineer
6. A - FDA registration
7. C - Early in the site selection process
8. B - Special approval for non-standard uses in a zone
9. B - Electrical load capacity and safety
10. A - NPDES (National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System)
