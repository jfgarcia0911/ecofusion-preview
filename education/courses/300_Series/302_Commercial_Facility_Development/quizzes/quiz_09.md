# Quiz 9: Contractor Selection

**Course:** 302 - Commercial Facility Development
**Module:** 9
**Points:** 3

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. What is the most important factor when selecting a general contractor?

A) Lowest bid price
B) Relevant experience and references
C) Proximity to project site
D) Company size

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. How many contractors should you ideally solicit bids from?

A) 1
B) 2
C) 3-5
D) 10+

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. What should be included in a Request for Proposal (RFP)?

A) Budget constraints only
B) Complete plans, specifications, and project requirements
C) Contractor recommendations
D) Equipment preferences only

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is a performance bond?

A) Insurance protecting against contractor default
B) A payment to motivate contractors
C) A discount for early completion
D) A penalty for late completion

**Answer:** A

---

### 5. Why is CEA-specific experience valuable in contractors?

A) They charge lower rates
B) They understand unique technical requirements
C) They have more employees
D) They work faster

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. What is the purpose of checking contractor references?

A) Verify licensing only
B) Understand past performance and reliability
C) Negotiate lower prices
D) Find design ideas

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What should you verify about a contractor's insurance?

A) They have any insurance at all
B) Adequate general liability and workers' compensation coverage
C) Health insurance for employees
D) Vehicle insurance only

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. What is a "prevailing wage" requirement?

A) Minimum wage for all workers
B) Legally required wage rates for public projects
C) Negotiated contractor rates
D) Average industry compensation

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. When comparing contractor bids, what should you analyze beyond price?

A) Company logo design
B) Scope completeness, timeline, and qualifications
C) Office location only
D) Number of employees

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is a key red flag when evaluating contractors?

A) Asking clarifying questions
B) Requesting deposits significantly higher than industry standard
C) Providing detailed proposals
D) Having insurance coverage

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Relevant experience and references
2. C - 3-5
3. B - Complete plans, specifications, and project requirements
4. A - Insurance protecting against contractor default
5. B - They understand unique technical requirements
6. B - Understand past performance and reliability
7. B - Adequate general liability and workers' compensation coverage
8. B - Legally required wage rates for public projects
9. B - Scope completeness, timeline, and qualifications
10. B - Requesting deposits significantly higher than industry standard
