# Quiz 11: Equipment Procurement

**Course:** 302 - Commercial Facility Development
**Module:** 11
**Points:** 3
**Passing Score:** 80%

---

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. When should equipment selection begin?

A) After construction is complete
B) During design development phase
C) During commissioning
D) After first harvest

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is a "long-lead item" in equipment procurement?

A) Equipment that lasts a long time
B) Equipment requiring extended delivery time
C) The longest piece of equipment
D) Equipment with extended warranties

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. What should you request from equipment vendors?

A) Verbal quotes only
B) Detailed written quotes with specifications
C) General price ranges
D) No documentation needed

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is an important consideration when selecting grow lights?

A) Color preference
B) Efficacy (μmol/J) and spectrum
C) Brand popularity only
D) Packaging design

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. Why is it important to verify equipment lead times?

A) To negotiate better prices
B) To ensure delivery aligns with construction schedule
C) To compare warranties
D) To determine storage needs

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. What should be included in equipment purchase agreements?

A) Price only
B) Specifications, delivery date, warranty, installation support
C) Verbal promises
D) Marketing materials

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is the advantage of buying complete system packages from one vendor?

A) Always cheapest option
B) Integrated design and single-source support
C) Fastest delivery
D) Easier to finance

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. What should you verify about equipment electrical requirements?

A) Equipment is plug-and-play
B) Voltage, phase, and amperage match facility capacity
C) Equipment uses batteries only
D) Extension cords are sufficient

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. Why is equipment training important?

A) Required by law
B) Ensures proper operation and maintenance
C) Increases equipment value
D) Only needed for complex systems

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What documentation should you receive with equipment?

A) Sales receipt only
B) Manuals, warranties, parts lists, O&M guides
C) Shipping labels
D) Advertising brochures

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - During design development phase
2. B - Equipment requiring extended delivery time
3. B - Detailed written quotes with specifications
4. B - Efficacy (μmol/J) and spectrum
5. B - To ensure delivery aligns with construction schedule
6. B - Specifications, delivery date, warranty, installation support
7. B - Integrated design and single-source support
8. B - Voltage, phase, and amperage match facility capacity
9. B - Ensures proper operation and maintenance
10. B - Manuals, warranties, parts lists, O&M guides
