## Questions

### 1. What is the largest utility expense for most CEA facilities?

A) Water
B) Natural gas
C) Electricity
D) Sewage

**Answer:** C

---

### 2. What is a typical electrical demand for a 10,000 sq ft CEA facility?

A) 50-100 kW
B) 200-500 kW
C) 1-2 MW
D) 5-10 MW

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. What is the primary purpose of a utility capacity study?

A) Estimate monthly bills
B) Verify adequate service is available
C) Negotiate lower rates
D) Design backup generators

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which utility upgrade is often required for CEA facilities?

A) Residential water meter
B) Standard 200-amp service
C) Transformer and service entrance upgrade
D) Satellite internet connection

**Answer:** C

---

### 5. What is a demand charge on electrical bills?

A) Charge for total energy used (kWh)
B) Charge based on peak power usage (kW)
C) Monthly service fee
D) Renewable energy surcharge

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. How much water does a typical hydroponic facility use per pound of produce?

A) 1-3 gallons
B) 5-10 gallons
C) 15-20 gallons
D) 25-30 gallons

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is an effective strategy to reduce demand charges?

A) Use electricity only at night
B) Load shedding and demand management
C) Install solar panels
D) Reduce water usage

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. Which backup power option is most common for critical CEA systems?

A) Solar panels with battery storage
B) Manual generator startup
C) Automatic standby generator
D) Uninterruptible power supply (UPS) only

**Answer:** C

---

### 9. What internet speed is recommended for automated CEA facilities?

A) 1-5 Mbps
B) 10-25 Mbps
C) 50-100 Mbps or higher
D) Standard dial-up

**Answer:** C

---

### 10. Who typically pays for utility infrastructure upgrades to serve a new facility?

A) The utility company always
B) The local government
C) The facility owner/tenant
D) Shared equally by all parties

**Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Electricity
2. B - 200-500 kW
3. B - Verify adequate service is available
4. C - Transformer and service entrance upgrade
5. B - Charge based on peak power usage (kW)
6. B - 5-10 gallons
7. B - Load shedding and demand management
8. C - Automatic standby generator
9. C - 50-100 Mbps or higher
10. C - The facility owner/tenant
