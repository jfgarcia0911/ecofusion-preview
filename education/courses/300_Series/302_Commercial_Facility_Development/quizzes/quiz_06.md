## Questions

### 1. What percentage of total capital cost typically goes to land/building acquisition?

A) 10-15%
B) 20-25%
C) 30-40%
D) 50-60%

**Answer:** C

---

### 2. What are "soft costs" in a construction budget?

A) Equipment that can be easily moved
B) Non-construction costs like design, permits, and legal fees
C) Contingency funds
D) Labor costs

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. What percentage should soft costs represent in a total project budget?

A) 3-5%
B) 10-15%
C) 20-25%
D) 30-35%

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. Why is contingency funding important?

A) To pay for equipment upgrades
B) To cover unexpected costs and changes
C) To fund marketing campaigns
D) To hire additional staff

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is a common budgeting mistake in CEA facility development?

A) Hiring too many consultants
B) Underestimating utility connection costs
C) Over-budgeting for equipment
D) Allocating too much for contingency

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. Which budget category includes HVAC, electrical, and plumbing?

A) Growing systems
B) Site work
C) MEP systems
D) Soft costs

**Answer:** C

---

### 7. What is the recommended approach for equipment budgeting?

A) Select cheapest options available
B) Get detailed quotes from multiple vendors
C) Use industry average estimates only
D) Wait until construction is complete

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. What percentage of capital cost is typically allocated to growing systems?

A) 5-10%
B) 15-20%
C) 25-30%
D) 40-50%

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. When should value engineering be conducted?

A) After construction is complete
B) During design phase before final plans
C) Only if over budget
D) Never - it compromises quality

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is included in "site work" costs?

A) Building construction only
B) Equipment installation
C) Grading, utilities, parking, landscaping
D) Interior finishes

**Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. C - 30-40%
2. B - Non-construction costs like design, permits, and legal fees
3. B - 10-15%
4. B - To cover unexpected costs and changes
5. B - Underestimating utility connection costs
6. C - MEP systems
7. B - Get detailed quotes from multiple vendors
8. B - 15-20%
9. B - During design phase before final plans
10. C - Grading, utilities, parking, landscaping
