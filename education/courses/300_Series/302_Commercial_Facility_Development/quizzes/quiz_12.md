## Questions

### 1. What does MEP stand for?

A) Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing
B) Management, Equipment, Production
C) Materials, Energy, Performance
D) Monitoring, Evaluation, Planning

**Answer:** A

---

### 2. Which MEP system typically requires the most coordination in CEA facilities?

A) Plumbing only
B) Electrical only
C) All systems - they're highly integrated
D) Mechanical only

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is critical for HVAC system installation in CEA?

A) Using residential-grade equipment
B) Proper sizing and ductwork design
C) Installing smallest capacity to save money
D) Avoiding insulation

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. Why is electrical system capacity planning important?

A) To minimize electrical costs
B) To accommodate current and future equipment loads
C) To reduce lighting needs
D) To avoid using generators

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is an important consideration for plumbing in hydroponic systems?

A) Using cheapest PVC pipes
B) Proper materials compatible with nutrients and UV resistance
C) Avoiding backflow prevention
D) Minimizing pipe sizes

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. What should be tested during MEP commissioning?

A) Aesthetic appearance only
B) All system functionality, capacity, and integration
C) Equipment brand names
D) Construction timeline

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is the purpose of dedicated electrical circuits for critical equipment?

A) Increase costs unnecessarily
B) Prevent overloading and ensure reliable operation
C) Simplify wiring
D) Reduce voltage

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. Why is proper drainage important in CEA facilities?

A) Aesthetic reasons
B) Prevent water damage and contamination
C) Reduce construction costs
D) Increase floor load capacity

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. What type of lighting control is recommended for CEA?

A) Manual switches only
B) Automated controls with timers and sensors
C) Always-on lighting
D) Natural sunlight only

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is a common MEP installation mistake?

A) Following manufacturer specifications
B) Inadequate equipment access for maintenance
C) Using licensed contractors
D) Proper testing and commissioning

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing
2. C - All systems - they're highly integrated
3. B - Proper sizing and ductwork design
4. B - To accommodate current and future equipment loads
5. B - Proper materials compatible with nutrients and UV resistance
6. B - All system functionality, capacity, and integration
7. B - Prevent overloading and ensure reliable operation
8. B - Prevent water damage and contamination
9. B - Automated controls with timers and sensors
10. B - Inadequate equipment access for maintenance
