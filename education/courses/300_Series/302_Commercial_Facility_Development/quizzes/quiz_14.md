# Quiz 14: Commissioning Process

**Course:** 302 - Commercial Facility Development
**Module:** 14
**Points:** 3
**Passing Score:** 80%

---

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. What is the primary purpose of facility commissioning?

A) Marketing the facility
B) Verifying all systems function properly before operation
C) Obtaining final payment
D) Training sales staff

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. How long does a typical commissioning period last?

A) 1-2 days
B) 1-2 weeks
C) 1-3 months
D) 6-12 months

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. What should be tested during environmental control commissioning?

A) Aesthetic appearance
B) Temperature, humidity, CO2 control, and stability
C) Staff uniforms
D) Product packaging

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. Why are crop trials important during commissioning?

A) Generate revenue immediately
B) Test systems under actual growing conditions
C) Create marketing content
D) Satisfy building inspectors

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What documentation should be developed during commissioning?

A) Marketing brochures
B) Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
C) Financial statements
D) Competitor analysis

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. Who should be trained during commissioning?

A) Customers only
B) All operational staff on systems and procedures
C) Investors only
D) Construction workers

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is a commissioning checklist used for?

A) Grocery shopping
B) Systematically verify all systems and requirements
C) Employee attendance
D) Visitor sign-in

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. What should be done if systems fail during commissioning?

A) Proceed with operations anyway
B) Identify root cause, repair, and retest
C) Hide from investors
D) Blame contractors

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. What inspections typically occur during commissioning?

A) Only informal walkthroughs
B) Final building, electrical, plumbing, and health inspections
C) Social media reviews
D) Competitor visits

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What indicates successful commissioning completion?

A) Construction payment made
B) All systems stable, staff trained, inspections passed
C) Marketing materials printed
D) Website launched

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Verifying all systems function properly before operation
2. C - 1-3 months
3. B - Temperature, humidity, CO2 control, and stability
4. B - Test systems under actual growing conditions
5. B - Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
6. B - All operational staff on systems and procedures
7. B - Systematically verify all systems and requirements
8. B - Identify root cause, repair, and retest
9. B - Final building, electrical, plumbing, and health inspections
10. B - All systems stable, staff trained, inspections passed
