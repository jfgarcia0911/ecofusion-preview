# Quiz 13: Growing System Installation

**Course:** 302 - Commercial Facility Development
**Module:** 13
**Points:** 3

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

## Questions

### 1. When should growing system installation begin?

A) Before building construction
B) After MEP systems are substantially complete
C) During foundation work
D) Before permitting

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is critical when installing aquaponic fish tanks?

A) Decorative appearance
B) Proper leveling and structural support
C) Transparent materials only
D) Minimum size possible

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. Why is system cleanliness important during installation?

A) Aesthetic appearance
B) Prevent contamination and ensure crop health
C) Improve resale value
D) Reduce insurance costs

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What should be verified before filling water systems?

A) Color scheme matches
B) All connections are leak-free and secure
C) Systems look impressive
D) Construction is 100% complete

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is the purpose of a "flush and fill" procedure?

A) Test drainage only
B) Remove construction debris and establish clean water
C) Demonstrate to inspectors
D) Test pumps at maximum capacity

**Answer:** B

---

### 6. Why is lighting height adjustment capability important?

A) Aesthetic flexibility
B) Accommodate different crop heights and light requirements
C) Impress visitors
D) Reduce installation costs

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What should be installed to monitor system performance?

A) Decorative displays
B) Sensors for pH, EC, temperature, and other parameters
C) Mirrors for visual inspection
D) Advertising screens

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. What is important for grow bed installation in media-based systems?

A) Using any available material
B) Proper drainage, level installation, and food-grade materials
C) Decorative appearance
D) Minimum depth

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. Why should you perform test runs before adding crops?

A) Required by law
B) Identify and fix issues without risking crop loss
C) Impress investors
D) Increase costs

**Answer:** B

---

### 10. What documentation should be created during installation?

A) Social media posts only
B) As-built drawings, settings, and configuration details
C) Marketing materials
D) Employee schedules

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - After MEP systems are substantially complete
2. B - Proper leveling and structural support
3. B - Prevent contamination and ensure crop health
4. B - All connections are leak-free and secure
5. B - Remove construction debris and establish clean water
6. B - Accommodate different crop heights and light requirements
7. B - Sensors for pH, EC, temperature, and other parameters
8. B - Proper drainage, level installation, and food-grade materials
9. B - Identify and fix issues without risking crop loss
10. B - As-built drawings, settings, and configuration details
