## Questions

### 1. What is the primary advantage of leasing over purchasing property for CEA facilities?

A) Greater long-term equity
B) Lower upfront capital requirements
C) Complete control over modifications
D) No landlord restrictions

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is a typical lease term for commercial CEA operations?

A) 1-3 years
B) 3-5 years
C) 5-10 years with options
D) 15-20 years

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. Which type of building is often most suitable for conversion to CEA?

A) Office buildings
B) Retail storefronts
C) Industrial warehouses
D) Residential apartments

**Answer:** C

---

### 4. What percentage of annual revenue should real estate costs ideally not exceed?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-15%
C) 15-25%
D) 30-40%

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is a "triple net" (NNN) lease?

A) Tenant pays base rent only
B) Tenant pays rent plus utilities
C) Tenant pays rent, taxes, insurance, and maintenance
D) Landlord pays all expenses

**Answer:** C

---

### 6. When evaluating existing buildings, what electrical capacity should you verify?

A) 100-200 amps
B) 400-600 amps
C) 1000-2000 amps or more
D) Standard residential service

**Answer:** C

---

### 7. What is a key consideration for expansion potential?

A) Proximity to residential areas
B) Adjacent available space or land
C) Historic building designation
D) Proximity to competitors

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. Which zoning classification typically works best for CEA facilities?

A) Residential
B) Commercial retail
C) Industrial or agricultural
D) Mixed-use only

**Answer:** C

---

### 9. What is an important negotiation point in CEA leases?

A) Ability to make structural modifications
B) Furniture and fixtures included
C) Month-to-month terms
D) Residential use provisions

**Answer:** A

---

### 10. What due diligence item is critical before purchasing property?

A) Interior paint colors
B) Title search and survey
C) Previous tenant industry
D) Neighboring business types

**Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Lower upfront capital requirements
2. C - 5-10 years with options
3. C - Industrial warehouses
4. B - 10-15%
5. C - Tenant pays rent, taxes, insurance, and maintenance
6. C - 1000-2000 amps or more
7. B - Adjacent available space or land
8. C - Industrial or agricultural
9. A - Ability to make structural modifications
10. B - Title search and survey
