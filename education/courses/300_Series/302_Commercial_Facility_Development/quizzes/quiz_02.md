## Questions

### 1. What is the most heavily weighted factor in the site selection scoring matrix?

A) Real Estate Cost
B) Market Access
C) Utility Economics
D) Labor Market

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. What percentage variation in operating costs can result from site selection?

A) 5-10%
B) 10-20%
C) 30-50%
D) 60-80%

**Answer:** C

---

### 3. For optimal market access, customers should ideally be within what distance?

A) 10 miles
B) 25 miles
C) 50 miles
D) 100 miles

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is considered an excellent electric rate for CEA operations?

A) Less than $0.08/kWh
B) Less than $0.12/kWh
C) Less than $0.15/kWh
D) Less than $0.20/kWh

**Answer:** A

---

### 5. What minimum ceiling height is ideal for CEA facilities?

A) 10 feet
B) 14 feet
C) 20 feet
D) 30 feet

**Answer:** C

---

### 6. Which climate factor increases operating costs for CEA facilities?

A) Low heating degree days
B) Moderate humidity (40-60%)
C) High heating degree days
D) Excellent solar resources

**Answer:** C

---

### 7. What is a critical component of site due diligence?

A) Interior design consultation
B) Phase I Environmental Assessment
C) Marketing campaign development
D) Recipe development

**Answer:** B

---

### 8. How much time should be allocated for thorough site selection?

A) 2-4 weeks
B) 1-2 months
C) 3-6 months
D) 9-12 months

**Answer:** C

---

### 9. What floor load capacity is considered excellent for CEA facilities?

A) Greater than 50 psf
B) Greater than 80 psf
C) Greater than 100 psf
D) Greater than 150 psf

**Answer:** D

---

### 10. Which infrastructure element is weighted at 5% in the site evaluation matrix?

A) Market Access
B) Utility Economics
C) Infrastructure (roads, internet)
D) Labor Market

**Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Market Access (25%)
2. C - 30-50%
3. B - 25 miles
4. A - Less than $0.08/kWh
5. C - 20 feet
6. C - High heating degree days (increases heating costs)
7. B - Phase I Environmental Assessment
8. C - 3-6 months
9. D - Greater than 150 psf
10. C - Infrastructure (roads, internet)
