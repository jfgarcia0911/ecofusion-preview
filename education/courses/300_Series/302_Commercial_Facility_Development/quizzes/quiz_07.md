## Questions

### 1. What is a typical debt-to-equity ratio for CEA facility financing?

A) 90/10
B) 70/30
C) 50/50
D) 30/70

**Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which type of lender is most likely to finance agricultural projects?

A) Credit card companies
B) Farm Credit System or agricultural lenders
C) Personal loan providers
D) Payday loan services

**Answer:** B

---

### 3. What is the typical interest rate range for CEA project financing?

A) 1-3%
B) 4-8%
C) 10-15%
D) 20-25%

**Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is the purpose of the USDA Rural Development program?

A) Provide grants only
B) Support rural business development with loans and grants
C) Regulate agricultural operations
D) Enforce environmental compliance

**Answer:** B

---

### 5. What tax incentive can reduce costs for energy-efficient equipment?

A) Property tax exemption
B) Sales tax holiday
C) Investment Tax Credit (ITC) or Section 179 deduction
D) Import duty waiver

**Answer:** C

---

### 6. What do lenders typically require as collateral for facility loans?

A) Personal guarantee only
B) Equipment and real estate
C) Future crop production
D) Customer contracts only

**Answer:** B

---

### 7. What is a common requirement in loan covenants?

A) Maintain specific financial ratios
B) Change crop types quarterly
C) Hire specific contractors
D) Avoid hiring employees

**Answer:** A

---

### 8. Which incentive can help offset high electricity costs?

A) Property tax credits
B) Utility company rebates for energy efficiency
C) Sales tax exemptions
D) Import tariff reductions

**Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is the typical loan term for CEA facility financing?

A) 1-3 years
B) 5-7 years
C) 10-20 years
D) 30-40 years

**Answer:** C

---

### 10. What should be included in a financing package for lenders?

A) Business plan and financial projections
B) Employee resumes only
C) Marketing materials only
D) Crop recipes

**Answer:** A

---

6. B - Equipment and real estate

8. B - Utility company rebates for energy efficiency
