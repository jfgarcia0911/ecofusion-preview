## Course Description

Navigate the complex process of developing a commercial controlled environment agriculture facility from site selection through construction and commissioning. This comprehensive course covers real estate, permitting, financing, construction management, and successful facility startup.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Evaluate sites for commercial CEA development
2. Navigate permitting and regulatory processes
3. Develop accurate project budgets and timelines
4. Manage construction and contractor relationships
5. Execute successful facility commissioning
6. Avoid common development pitfalls

## Site Selection Criteria

### Location Scoring Matrix

| Factor | Weight | Scoring Criteria |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Market Access | 25% | Distance to customers, population density |
| Utility Costs | 20% | Electric rates, water costs, gas availability |
| Real Estate Cost | 15% | Purchase/lease $/sq ft |
| Labor Market | 15% | Availability, wage rates |
| Climate | 10% | Heating/cooling loads |
| Regulations | 10% | Zoning, permits, inspections |
| Infrastructure | 5% | Roads, internet, services |

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Phase I Environmental Assessment
- [ ] Utility capacity verification
- [ ] Zoning confirmation
- [ ] Soil/foundation analysis
- [ ] Flood zone determination
- [ ] Title search
- [ ] Survey
