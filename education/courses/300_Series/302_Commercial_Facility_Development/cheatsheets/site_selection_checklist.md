## PHASE 1: INITIAL SCREENING

### Location Requirements
- [ ] Within target market radius (typically <50 miles to customers)
- [ ] Acceptable real estate costs (<$15/sq ft preferred)
- [ ] Adequate electrical service available (verify with utility)
- [ ] Appropriate zoning or variance obtainable
- [ ] Reasonable permitting timeline (<6 months preferred)

### Immediate Disqualifiers
- [ ] Prohibited zoning with no variance path
- [ ] Insufficient electrical capacity (no upgrade possible)
- [ ] Flood zone without mitigation options
- [ ] Contaminated site (Phase I environmental issues)
- [ ] Prohibitive real estate costs (>$25/sq ft)

## PHASE 2: MARKET ACCESS EVALUATION (25 points)

### Customer Proximity (10 points)
- [ ] **10 pts:** Major metro <25 miles, population >500K
- [ ] **7 pts:** Mid-size city <50 miles, population 100K-500K
- [ ] **4 pts:** Small city <100 miles, population 50K-100K
- [ ] **1 pt:** Rural area >100 miles, population <50K

### Market Density (5 points)
- [ ] **5 pts:** >200 restaurants/retailers in area
- [ ] **3 pts:** 100-200 food service establishments
- [ ] **2 pts:** 50-100 establishments
- [ ] **1 pt:** <50 establishments

### Competition Analysis (5 points)
- [ ] **5 pts:** No direct CEA competitors
- [ ] **3 pts:** 1-2 small competitors
- [ ] **2 pts:** 3-4 competitors
- [ ] **1 pt:** Market saturated with CEA producers

### Distribution Access (5 points)
- [ ] **5 pts:** Highway access, 3+ distribution options
- [ ] **3 pts:** Good road access, 1-2 distributors
- [ ] **2 pts:** Moderate access, limited distributors
- [ ] **1 pt:** Poor access, no established distribution

**Market Access Subtotal: _____ / 25**

## DECISION CRITERIA

### Score Interpretation
- **90-100:** Exceptional site - proceed immediately
- **80-89:** Excellent site - strong candidate
- **70-79:** Good site - acceptable with minor concerns
- **60-69:** Marginal site - significant issues to address
- **<60:** Poor site - not recommended

### Additional Considerations
- [ ] Site aligns with business plan and growth strategy
- [ ] Total project cost within budget constraints
- [ ] Timeline acceptable for business needs
- [ ] Risk factors manageable
- [ ] Financing obtainable for this location
