# Course 302: Commercial Facility Development
## Assignment 04: Development Schedule Project

**Type:** Take-Home | **Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Points:** 30

## Objective
Develop a detailed project schedule for commercial facility development including resources, dependencies, and risk management.

## Requirements

### 1. Gantt Chart/Timeline (12 points)
- All major phases broken into tasks
- Realistic duration estimates
- Dependencies clearly shown
- Critical path identified

### 2. Resource Allocation (8 points)
- Key personnel assignments
- Equipment/contractor scheduling
- Budget allocation by phase
- Procurement timeline

### 3. Risk Management (10 points)
- Identify 10+ project risks
- Assess probability and impact
- Mitigation strategies
- Contingency planning

## Deliverables
1. Detailed Gantt chart (Excel/Project/PDF)
2. Resource allocation table
3. Risk register with mitigation plans
4. Executive summary (1 page)

## Grading Rubric
- Schedule completeness & realism: 12 pts
- Resource planning: 8 pts
- Risk identification & mitigation: 10 pts
