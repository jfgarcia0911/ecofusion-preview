## Scenario

You are evaluating three potential sites for a 10,000 sq ft greenhouse aquaponics facility. Use the provided site evaluation matrix to score and rank the locations.

### Evaluation Factors & Weights

| Factor | Weight | Scoring Criteria |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| Market Access | 25% | Distance to customers, population density |
| Utility Costs | 20% | Electric rates, water costs |
| Real Estate Cost | 15% | Purchase/lease $/sq ft |
| Labor Market | 15% | Availability, wage rates |
| Climate | 10% | Heating/cooling loads |
| Regulations | 10% | Zoning, permits |
| Infrastructure | 5% | Roads, internet |

### Site Data

**Site A - Urban Industrial:**
- Market: 2 miles to downtown, 500K population within 10 miles
- Electricity: $0.14/kWh, Water: $4.50/1000 gal
- Lease: $12/sq ft/year
- Labor: Abundant, $16/hr average
- Climate: Moderate, 5,000 HDD
- Zoning: Requires variance
- Infrastructure: Excellent

**Site B - Suburban:**
- Market: 15 miles to city, 200K population
- Electricity: $0.11/kWh, Water: $3.20/1000 gal
- Purchase: $25/sq ft
- Labor: Available, $14/hr average
- Climate: Cold, 7,500 HDD
- Zoning: Agricultural, approved
- Infrastructure: Good

**Site C - Rural:**
- Market: 45 miles to city, 50K local
- Electricity: $0.09/kWh, Water: $2.00/1000 gal (well)
- Purchase: $8/sq ft
- Labor: Limited, $12/hr average
- Climate: Cold, 8,000 HDD
- Zoning: Agricultural, approved
- Infrastructure: Basic
