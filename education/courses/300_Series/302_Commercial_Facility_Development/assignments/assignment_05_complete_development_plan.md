# Course 302: Commercial Facility Development
## Assignment 05: Complete Development Plan (Capstone)

**Type:** Comprehensive Project | **Duration:** 2-4 hours | **Points:** 40

## Objective
Create an investor-ready development plan for a commercial CEA facility from concept through launch.

## Project Scope
Design a complete development plan for a facility of your choice:
- Greenhouse (5,000-20,000 sq ft)
- Vertical farm (2,000-10,000 sq ft)
- Aquaponics (3,000-15,000 sq ft)

## Required Components

### 1. Executive Summary (5 points)
- Project overview and vision
- Key metrics (size, capacity, investment)
- Expected timeline and milestones
- Investment highlight (1-2 pages)

### 2. Site Analysis (8 points)
- Site selection justification
- Site evaluation scoring
- Zoning and regulatory analysis
- Utility infrastructure assessment
- Access and logistics evaluation

### 3. Complete Project Budget (10 points)
- Detailed line-item budget by category
- Capital expenditure breakdown
- Soft costs itemized
- Contingency allocations
- Financing structure
- Cash flow projections (36 months)

### 4. Development Timeline (8 points)
- Comprehensive Gantt chart
- All phases and major tasks
- Critical path analysis
- Resource allocation
- Key milestones and deliverables

### 5. Financing Strategy (5 points)
- Capital sources identified
- Debt/equity structure
- Incentives and grants pursued
- Return on investment projections

### 6. Risk Management Plan (4 points)
- Comprehensive risk register
- Mitigation strategies for top 10 risks
- Contingency plans
- Insurance and legal considerations

## Submission Requirements

### Format
- Professional PDF document
- 15-25 pages total
- Executive summary at front
- Table of contents
- Supporting schedules and exhibits as appendices

### Components Checklist
- [ ] Cover page with project name
