# Activity: Specialty Crop Trial Planning Exercise

**Course 307: Specialty Crop Production**
**Activity Type:** Planning and Analysis Exercise
**Time Required:** 60-90 minutes
**Objective:** Design a specialty crop trial program for market testing

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, you will:
- Select appropriate specialty crops for trial based on market research
- Design a production plan for trial crops
- Create a market testing strategy
- Develop success metrics for evaluating trials
- Plan for scaling successful crops

---

## Scenario

You have a 100 sq ft growing area available for specialty crop trials. Your goal is to identify 3-5 high-potential specialty crops to test for your market before committing significant production space. You have access to basic hydroponic or aquaponic systems (your choice) and want to trial crops over a 6-month period.

**Your Local Market Context** (choose one or create your own):

**Option A - Urban Restaurant Market**
- Location: Mid-size city (200,000+ population)
- 15+ upscale restaurants within delivery range
- Active farmers market scene
- 3-4 specialty grocers
- Strong farm-to-table movement

**Option B - Suburban/Small Town**
- Location: Smaller community (30,000-50,000)
- 3-5 higher-end restaurants
- Weekly farmers market (seasonal)
- 1-2 natural food stores
- Growing interest in local food

**Option C - Tourist/Resort Area**
- Location: Seasonal tourist destination
- 8-12 restaurants (mix of casual and fine dining)
- Strong summer season, quieter winter
- Farmers markets summer only
- Wedding/event venues present

## Instructor Review Checklist

When reviewing student submissions, check for:

□ Market research shows actual customer identification (not generic)
□ Crop selection is justified based on market research
□ Space allocation adds up to 100 sq ft
□ Production timeline is realistic for chosen crops
□ Sample distribution plan is specific and actionable
□ Success metrics are measurable and realistic
□ Contingency plans demonstrate critical thinking
□ Overall plan is coherent and executable

## Next Steps

After completing this activity:

1. **Refine your plan** based on instructor feedback

5. **Set calendar reminders** for key milestones
6. **Create tracking spreadsheet** for recording results
7. **Execute your trial** and document learnings
