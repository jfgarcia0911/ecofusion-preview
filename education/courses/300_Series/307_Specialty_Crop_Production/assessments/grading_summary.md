## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Crop Selection and Market Analysis (15 points)

**Purpose:** Identify specialty crops with strong market potential and profitability.

**What You'll Do:**
- Research specialty crop markets (herbs, edible flowers, microgreens, etc.)
- Analyze local and regional demand
- Calculate potential profitability per square foot
- Assess competition and market gaps
- Select optimal crop portfolio

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand specialty crop markets
- Evaluate crop profitability
- Identify market opportunities

**Deliverable:** Market analysis report with crop selection recommendations and profit projections

---

### Assignment 2: Growing Protocol Development (20 points)

**Purpose:** Create detailed cultivation protocols for specialty crop production.

**What You'll Do:**
- Define optimal growing conditions (light, temperature, humidity)
- Develop nutrient management programs
- Create pest and disease management plans
- Establish quality standards and grading criteria
- Document harvesting procedures

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Master specialty crop cultivation
- Implement quality-focused growing
- Develop comprehensive SOPs

**Deliverable:** Complete growing protocols for 3-5 specialty crops with environmental specs and quality standards

---

### Assignment 3: Harvest and Post-Harvest Planning (25 points)

**Purpose:** Ensure premium quality through optimal harvest and handling.

**What You'll Do:**
- Define harvest maturity indicators
- Develop harvest timing schedules
- Create post-harvest handling procedures
- Design storage and packaging systems
- Implement shelf life extension strategies

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Master harvest timing for quality
- Implement proper post-harvest handling
- Maximize product shelf life and value

**Deliverable:** Harvest and post-harvest management plan with timing guides, handling SOPs, and packaging design

---

### Assignment 4: Specialty Crop Marketing Strategy (20 points)

**Purpose:** Develop marketing strategy to command premium prices.

**What You'll Do:**
- Create brand story and positioning
- Develop pricing strategy with premium justification
- Select sales channels (farmers markets, restaurants, retail)
- Plan customer acquisition and retention
- Design marketing materials and campaigns

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Build premium agricultural brands
- Price specialty crops appropriately
- Execute multi-channel marketing

**Deliverable:** Marketing strategy document with brand positioning, pricing, channel plan, and customer acquisition tactics

---

### Assignment 5: Comprehensive Production Program (30 points)

**Purpose:** Create complete specialty crop production and marketing operation.

**What You'll Do:**
- Develop year-round production schedule
- Implement comprehensive quality assurance
- Create detailed financial projections
- Design integrated marketing execution
- Plan for scaling and expansion
- Address food safety and compliance
- Develop customer relationship management

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Integrate production and marketing
- Manage specialty crop operations at scale
- Build profitable niche agriculture business

**Deliverable:** Complete specialty crop business plan (15-25 pages) with production schedule, quality systems, financials, and marketing execution

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What types of specialty crops should I focus on?**
A: Choose crops with local market demand - culinary herbs, edible flowers, microgreens, baby vegetables, ethnic vegetables, etc.

**Q: How do I research specialty crop markets?**
A: Talk to chefs, visit farmers markets, research food trends, connect with specialty food distributors.

**Q: Can I focus on just one specialty crop?**
A: Yes, but we recommend a portfolio approach for risk management and market diversification.

**Q: How do I justify premium pricing?**
A: Through superior quality, unique varieties, organic certification, local production, and strong branding.

**Q: Do I need food safety certification?**
A: Requirements vary by market. We cover GAP, organic, and other relevant certifications.

**Q: Can I work with other students?**
A: In-class activities encourage discussion. Take-home assignments must be individual work.

**Q: How detailed should growing protocols be?**
A: Include enough detail for consistent, replicable production at commercial scale.

**Q: How is the final grade calculated?**
A: Sum of all assignment points (max 110). You need 88+ points (80%) to pass and receive your certificate.
