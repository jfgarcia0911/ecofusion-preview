## Introduction to Asian Greens

Asian greens represent one of the fastest-growing segments in specialty crop markets, driven by increasing popularity of Asian cuisines, health-conscious consumers, and chef innovation. These crops typically command premium prices while being well-suited to hydroponic and aquaponic production.

### Market Overview

**Growth Trends:**
- Asian cuisine restaurant growth: 8-12% annually
- Home cooking of Asian dishes: 15-20% increase (2020-2025)
- Farmers market demand: 20-25% annual growth in urban markets
- Grocery store Asian produce section expansion: Widespread

**Price Premiums:**
- 100-200% premium over conventional greens
- $8-15/lb wholesale for premium varieties
- $12-22/lb retail pricing
- Strong year-round demand in urban areas

## Asian Greens Crop Profiles

### Bok Choy (Brassica rapa chinensis)

**Overview:**
Bok choy is the most widely recognized Asian green in Western markets, making it an excellent entry crop for specialty production.

**Varieties:**

| Variety | Size | Days to Harvest | Market Application |
|---------|------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Toy Choy** | Micro (4-6") | 21-30 | High-end restaurants, baby vegetables |
| **Shanghai Bok Choy** | Small (6-8") | 30-40 | Premium restaurant, retail |
| **Win-Win Choy** | Medium (8-10") | 40-50 | General market, stir-fry |
| **China Express** | Large (10-14") | 45-55 | Wholesale, Asian markets |

**Growing Requirements:**

```
OPTIMAL CONDITIONS:
Temperature: 60-70°F (15-21°C)
  - Cool season crop
  - Tolerates light frost
  - Bolts above 75°F consistently

pH Range: 6.0-7.0
EC: 1.5-2.5 mS/cm

Light: 12-14 hours daily
  - DLI: 12-17 mol/m²/day
  - Can tolerate partial shade
  - Lower light = slower growth but acceptable

Water: Consistent moisture critical
  - Avoid water stress
  - High humidity preferred (60-80%)
  - Good air circulation prevents disease
```

**Nutrient Requirements:**

| Growth Stage | EC (mS/cm) | N-P-K Ratio | Key Notes |
|--------------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Seedling (0-14 days) | 1.2-1.5 | 1-1-1 | Light feeding |
| Vegetative (15-30 days) | 1.8-2.2 | 3-1-2 | Increase nitrogen |
| Pre-harvest (30+ days) | 2.0-2.5 | 2-1-2 | Maintain quality |

**Spacing and Density:**

```
Baby Bok Choy (Toy Choy):
- 4" × 4" spacing
- 9 plants per square foot
- Yield: 2-3 lbs per sq ft

Standard Bok Choy:
- 6" × 6" spacing
- 4 plants per square foot
- Yield: 2-4 lbs per sq ft

Large Bok Choy:
- 8" × 8" spacing
- 2.25 plants per square foot
- Yield: 3-5 lbs per sq ft
```

**Harvest and Post-Harvest:**
- Harvest when leaves are crisp and firm
- Cut at base, leaving roots in system
- Rinse gently in cold water
- Shake off excess water
- Store at 34-38°F, 95% humidity
- Shelf life: 10-14 days properly stored

**Market Pricing:**
```
Baby bok choy: $12-18/lb (wholesale), $18-28/lb (retail)
Standard bok choy: $8-14/lb (wholesale), $14-22/lb (retail)
```

### Mizuna (Brassica rapa nipposinica)

**Overview:**
Mizuna is a feathery, mild mustard green with attractive appearance and versatile culinary applications. Excellent for salad mixes.

**Characteristics:**
- Deeply serrated, feathery leaves
- Mild, slightly peppery flavor
- White or purple stems (variety dependent)
- Excellent visual appeal in salad mixes
- Fast growing and reliable

**Growing Requirements:**

```
Temperature: 60-70°F (15-21°C)
  - More cold-tolerant than bok choy
  - Can harvest through winter in mild climates
  - Becomes bitter above 75°F

pH: 6.0-7.0
EC: 1.5-2.2 mS/cm

Spacing: 4-6" apart
  - Baby leaf: 2" spacing, 14-21 days
  - Full head: 6" spacing, 28-40 days
  - Dense planting for salad mix

Light Requirements:
  - DLI: 12-15 mol/m²/day
  - Tolerates lower light than most greens
  - Grows well in fall/winter greenhouse
```

**Varieties:**

| Variety | Leaf Color | Stem Color | Days to Harvest | Best Use |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------------|----------|
| **Green Mizuna** | Dark green | White | 21-28 (baby), 40-50 (full) | Salad mix, braising |
| **Purple Mizuna** | Green with purple veins | Purple | 28-35 (baby), 45-55 (full) | Premium salad mix |
| **Kyona Mizuna** | Bright green | White | 21-30 | Quick baby leaf |

**Production Notes:**
- Excellent for cut-and-come-again harvesting
- Can harvest outer leaves, plant continues producing
- Regrowth in 10-14 days after cutting
- 3-4 harvests possible per plant

**Market Applications:**
```
Salad Mix Component: $14-20/lb wholesale
Full Head (6-8"): $10-16/lb wholesale
Baby Leaf: $16-24/lb wholesale
Braising Green: $8-12/lb wholesale
```

### Tatsoi (Brassica rapa rosularis)

**Overview:**
Tatsoi forms attractive rosettes of dark green, spoon-shaped leaves. Excellent cold tolerance and unique appearance.

**Characteristics:**
- Rosette growth pattern (looks like flowering plant)
- Dark green, glossy spoon-shaped leaves
- Mild, slightly mustard-like flavor
- Very cold tolerant
- Excellent visual appeal

**Growing Parameters:**

```
Temperature: 50-70°F (10-21°C)
  - Exceptional cold tolerance
  - Can tolerate temperatures to 32°F
  - Ideal for winter production
  - Bolts quickly above 75°F

pH: 6.0-7.0
EC: 1.8-2.5 mS/cm

Spacing:
  - Baby rosettes (4"): 4" × 4" (9/sq ft)
  - Standard (6-8"): 6" × 6" (4/sq ft)
  - Large rosettes (10-12"): 8" × 8" (2.25/sq ft)

Days to Harvest:
  - Baby: 25-30 days
  - Standard: 35-45 days
  - Large: 45-55 days
```

**Nutrient Management:**

| Stage | EC | Notes |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Establishment | 1.5-1.8 | First 2 weeks |
| Rapid growth | 2.0-2.5 | Weeks 3-5 |
| Pre-harvest | 2.2-2.8 | Final week - intensifies color |

**Unique Selling Points:**
- Rosette form is very attractive on plate
- Excellent in stir-fries (wilts beautifully)
- High vitamin content (marketing point)
- Cold-hardy for year-round production
- Premium pricing for perfect rosettes

**Pricing:**
```
Baby tatsoi rosettes (4"): $16-24/lb
Standard rosettes (6-8"): $12-18/lb
Baby leaf (cut): $14-20/lb
```

### Komatsuna (Brassica rapa perviridis)

**Overview:**
Japanese mustard spinach, mild flavor, extremely fast growing and productive.

**Characteristics:**
- Mild, spinach-like flavor (not peppery)
- Smooth, oval leaves on slender stems
- Excellent for customers who find mustards too spicy
- Very fast growing
- High yielding

**Growing Specifications:**

```
Temperature: 55-75°F (13-24°C)
  - Wider temperature tolerance than other Asian greens
  - Good summer production crop
  - Maintains quality in warmer conditions

pH: 6.0-7.0
EC: 1.5-2.5 mS/cm

Growth Rate: VERY FAST
  - Baby leaf: 18-25 days
  - Full size: 30-40 days
  - One of fastest Asian greens

Spacing:
  - Intensive: 4" × 4" (9/sq ft)
  - Standard: 6" × 6" (4/sq ft)
  - Yield: 2-3 lbs per sq ft
```

**Production Advantages:**
- Faster than most greens (quick cash flow)
- Heat tolerant (summer production)
- High yield per square foot
- Reliable germination and growth
- Fewer pest issues than other brassicas

**Market Positioning:**
```
"Japanese spinach" - easier sell than "mustard greens"
Mild flavor appeals to broader customer base
Excellent for customers new to Asian greens
Good in salad mix and cooking applications

Pricing:
Baby leaf: $12-18/lb wholesale
Full size: $8-14/lb wholesale
```

### Choy Sum (Brassica rapa parachinensis)

**Overview:**
Flowering cabbage shoots, premium crop for Chinese cuisine, higher value than standard greens.

**Characteristics:**
- Harvest includes flowering shoots
- Sweet, mild flavor
- Tender stems and leaves both eaten
- Yellow flowers are edible and decorative
- Essential ingredient in Cantonese cooking

**Growing Requirements:**

```
Temperature: 60-70°F (15-21°C)
  - Cool season crop
  - Harvest when flower buds appear but before opening
  - Timing is critical for quality

pH: 6.0-7.0
EC: 2.0-2.8 mS/cm (higher than most greens)

Days to Harvest: 35-50 days
  - Harvest when flower stems 6-8" long
  - Yellow buds visible but not open
  - Regular harvesting encourages production

Spacing: 6" × 6" (4 plants/sq ft)
```

**Harvest Technique:**
```
PRIMARY HARVEST:
- Cut main flowering stem when 6-8" tall
- Include 2-3 leaves with stem
- Buds should be tight, yellow visible
- This is premium product

SECONDARY HARVEST:
- Side shoots develop after main stem cut
- Harvest side shoots at 4-6"
- Can get 2-3 additional harvests
- Lower price but still profitable

Total yield: 3-5 lbs per sq ft over 8-10 weeks
```

**Market Value:**
```
Primary harvest (with flower buds): $14-22/lb wholesale
Secondary shoots: $10-16/lb wholesale
Strong demand from Chinese restaurants
Educate chefs on proper harvest stage
Very high value per square foot
```

### Gai Lan (Chinese Broccoli)

**Overview:**
Chinese broccoli, thick stems with small florets and leaves, premium price point.

**Characteristics:**
- Thick, tender stems (primary eating part)
- Small white or yellow flowers
- Large, blue-green leaves
- Harvest while stems still tender
- Popular in Chinese and Thai cuisine

**Growing Parameters:**

```
Temperature: 60-75°F (15-24°C)
  - Cool to moderate temperatures
  - More heat tolerant than heading broccoli
  - Stems become tough if too hot

pH: 6.2-7.0
EC: 2.0-2.8 mS/cm

Days to Harvest: 60-70 days (full size)
  - Baby gai lan: 35-45 days (premium product)
  - Harvest when flower buds visible but stems tender

Spacing:
  - Baby gai lan: 4" × 4" (9/sq ft)
  - Standard: 8" × 8" (2.25/sq ft)
  - Needs more space than leafy greens
```

**Production Considerations:**
- Longer growing period than other Asian greens
- Higher nutrient requirements (similar to broccoli)
- Test stem tenderness before harvest
- Premium pricing justifies longer growing time

**Market Positioning:**
```
Premium Asian green - highest price point
Essential for authentic Chinese cooking
Thick tender stems are delicacy
Strong demand from Asian restaurants

Pricing:
Baby gai lan: $16-24/lb wholesale
Standard gai lan: $12-18/lb wholesale
Must compete with imported product
Local, fresh, tender = competitive advantage
```

### Shiso / Perilla (Perilla frutescens)

**Overview:**
Japanese herb with unique flavor, used in sushi and Japanese cuisine, very high value.

**Characteristics:**
- Large, aromatic leaves
- Green or purple varieties
- Distinctive flavor (mint/basil/anise notes)
- Essential in Japanese cuisine
- Ornamental appeal

**Growing Requirements:**

```
Temperature: 70-80°F (21-27°C)
  - WARM season crop (unlike most Asian greens)
  - Not cold tolerant
  - Excellent summer crop

pH: 5.5-6.5
EC: 1.5-2.2 mS/cm

Light: High light requirement
  - DLI: 15-20 mol/m²/day
  - Full sun equivalent
  - Low light = reduced flavor intensity

Days to First Harvest: 60-75 days from seed
Growth Habit: Bushy, branching
  - Pinch tips to encourage branching
  - Continuous harvest once established
```

**Varieties:**

| Type | Leaf Color | Flavor Intensity | Market |
|------|-----------|------------------|--------|
| **Green Shiso** | Bright green | Moderate | Sushi garnish, wraps |
| **Red/Purple Shiso** | Deep purple | Strong | Pickling, color, garnish |
| **Britton Shiso** | Green with purple | Moderate-Strong | Specialty applications |

**Production Strategy:**
```
ESTABLISHMENT (8-10 weeks):
- Start from seed or cuttings
- Slower initial growth than greens
- Investment period before harvest

PRODUCTION (months 3-12):
- Continuous harvest of individual leaves
- Or harvest entire branch tips
- Pinch flowers to prolong leaf production
- Very productive once established

Yield: 0.5-1 lb per plant per month (established)
```

**Market Value:**
```
Premium pricing - very high value:
Fresh leaves: $20-35/lb wholesale
Single perfect leaves: $0.25-0.50 each (restaurant)
Strong demand from:
  - Sushi restaurants (garnish, wrap)
  - Japanese restaurants (various dishes)
  - Fine dining (unique flavor)
  - Cocktail programs (garnish)

Limited competition - few growers
Year-round demand in urban markets
```

### Chinese Cabbage (Napa Cabbage)

**Overview:**
Large, mild cabbage forming cylindrical heads, bulk market crop with steady demand.

**Characteristics:**
- Forms elongated heads
- Mild, sweet flavor
- Crisp texture
- Versatile cooking and fresh use
- Larger scale production suitable

**Growing Specifications:**

```
Temperature: 60-70°F (15-21°C)
  - Cool season crop
  - Heads best at consistent cool temps
  - Bolts readily if temperature fluctuates

pH: 6.0-7.0
EC: 2.0-2.8 mS/cm (higher nutrient needs)

Days to Harvest: 55-70 days
  - Variety dependent
  - Harvest when heads firm

Spacing: 12" × 12" (0.7/sq ft)
  - Needs significant space
  - Not suitable for intensive production
  - Better for larger operations

Head Weight: 2-4 lbs typical
```

**Varieties:**

| Variety | Days | Head Shape | Best Use |
|---------|------|------------|----------|
| **China Flash** | 55-60 | Cylindrical | Quick turnover |
| **Rubicon** | 60-65 | Barrel | Heat tolerance |
| **Monument** | 65-70 | Tall cylinder | Premium markets |
| **Yuki** | 55-60 | Compact | Small space |

**Market Considerations:**
```
ADVANTAGES:
- Steady demand (staple ingredient)
- Longer shelf life (14-21 days)
- Larger order sizes possible
- Less delicate than other greens

DISADVANTAGES:
- Lower price per pound ($4-8/lb wholesale)
- Requires more space
- Longer growing time
- Competes with imported product

## Production Systems for Asian Greens

### Deep Water Culture (DWC)

**Advantages for Asian Greens:**
- Excellent for larger plants (bok choy, gai lan, Chinese cabbage)
- Provides stable root zone environment
- Good for longer-season crops
- Easy to maintain consistent water level

**System Parameters:**
```
Water depth: 8-12"
Plant spacing: 6-8" (variety dependent)
Air stone placement: Every 4-6 sq ft
Water temperature: 65-70°F
EC monitoring: Daily
pH monitoring: Daily
```

**Best Crops for DWC:**
- Bok choy (all sizes)
- Tatsoi
- Gai lan
- Chinese cabbage
- Komatsuna (full size)

---

### Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)

**Advantages:**
- Excellent for baby greens and quick-turn crops
- Efficient use of space
- Easy harvesting
- Lower water volume (faster nutrient adjustment)

**System Parameters:**
```
Channel slope: 1:100 (1% grade)
Flow rate: 0.5-1 gallon per minute
Film depth: 1/4-1/2"
Plant spacing: 4-6" in channels
EC monitoring: 2x daily
```

**Best Crops for NFT:**
- Baby bok choy
- Mizuna (baby and full)
- Tatsoi (baby)
- Komatsuna
- Choy sum (adequate spacing)

---

### Raft/Float System

**Advantages:**
- Large volume buffering
- Stable conditions
- Lower labor for monitoring
- Good for uniform crops

**System Parameters:**
```
Water depth: 6-8"
Raft thickness: 1.5-2"
Hole spacing: 4-8" (crop dependent)
Aeration: Continuous
Water turnover: 2-4x daily
```

**Best Crops:**
- Bok choy (standard size)
- Tatsoi
- Mizuna
- Komatsuna
- Asian greens salad mix

---

### Media Bed (Aquaponics)

**Advantages:**
- Excellent for larger, longer-season crops
- Provides mechanical filtration (aquaponics)
- Good root support for tall plants
- Flexible spacing

**System Parameters:**
```
Media depth: 12"
Plant spacing: Variable by crop
Flood/drain cycle: 15 minutes on, 45 off
Media: Expanded clay, lava rock
Fish-to-plant ratio: 1 lb fish per 5-7 sq ft bed
```

**Best Crops:**
- Gai lan
- Large bok choy
- Chinese cabbage
- Shiso
- Choy sum

## Succession Planting Schedule

### Continuous Harvest Model

**Goal:** Harvest 3-5 varieties weekly for consistent restaurant supply

**4-Week Rotation Example:**

```
WEEK 1 PLANTINGS:
- Baby bok choy (Tray 1): 25 plants

- Tatsoi (Tray 1): 25 plants

WEEK 2 PLANTINGS:
- Komatsuna (Tray 2): 40 plants

WEEK 3 PLANTINGS:
- Mizuna (Tray 3): 30 plants
- Tatsoi (Tray 3): 25 plants
- Choy sum (Tray 1): 20 plants

WEEK 4 PLANTINGS:
- Baby bok choy (Tray 4): 25 plants
- Komatsuna (Tray 4): 40 plants

HARVEST SCHEDULE (once established):
Week 4: Tray 1 baby bok choy, mizuna, tatsoi

## Quality Standards and Grading

### Premium Grade (100% Price)

**Criteria:**
- Uniform size and color
- No insect damage
- No tip burn or chlorosis
- Crisp, turgid leaves
- No bolting or flowering (except choy sum)
- Minimal root damage
- Perfect visual appeal

**Applications:**
- Fine dining restaurants
- High-end retail
- Specialty food stores
- Premium farmers markets

---

### Choice Grade (80-90% Price)

**Criteria:**
- Minor size variation
- Slight tip burn (outer leaves only)
- Very minimal insect damage
- Generally good appearance
- Acceptable for most uses

**Applications:**
- Casual restaurants
- Standard retail
- Food service
- General farmers markets

---

### Standard Grade (60-75% Price)

**Criteria:**
- Visible imperfections
- Size variation
- Some tip burn or damage
- Still edible and safe
- Suitable for cooking

**Applications:**
- Wholesale to processors
- Prepared food operations
- Value-conscious restaurants
- Home use (farmers market discounts)

## Pest and Disease Management

### Common Issues in Asian Greens

#### Aphids
**Impact:** Moderate - cosmetic damage, honeydew
**Control:**
- Yellow sticky traps for monitoring
- Insecticidal soap (OMRI approved)
- Neem oil spray
- Ladybugs (biological control)
- Strong water spray for small infestations

#### Tip Burn (Calcium Deficiency)
**Symptoms:** Brown edges on newest leaves
**Causes:**
- Rapid growth outpacing calcium uptake
- Low calcium in solution
- High temperature stress
**Solutions:**
- Increase calcium concentration
- Ensure adequate EC (2.0-2.5)
- Lower temperature
- Improve air circulation
- Foliar calcium spray

#### Leaf Spot Diseases
**Symptoms:** Brown or black spots on leaves
**Causes:**
- High humidity with poor air flow
- Water on leaves
- Contaminated irrigation water
**Prevention:**
- Good air circulation (fans)
- Avoid wetting foliage
- Proper spacing
- Remove infected leaves immediately
- Hydrogen peroxide solution (1:100)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Asian greens offer excellent profitability** with 100-200% premiums over standard greens and strong market demand.

2. **Diversify varieties** to appeal to different market segments and spread risk across multiple crops with different harvest times.

3. **Temperature management is critical** - most Asian greens are cool-season crops that bolt in heat; shiso is the exception.

4. **Succession planting ensures consistent supply** essential for building reliable restaurant relationships.

5. **Quality grading impacts pricing significantly** - premium grade can command 30-40% higher prices than standard grade.

6. **Market education is important** - many customers are unfamiliar with these crops; provide recipes and cooking instructions.

7. **Baby vegetables command highest premiums** - baby bok choy and baby gai lan can sell for 150-200% of standard size pricing.
