# Module 10: Cucumbers & Squash

## Learning Objectives

- Select specialty cucumber and squash varieties for premium markets
- Manage vining crop production in vertical systems
- Optimize pollination for fruiting crops
- Market specialty cucumbers and squash effectively

---

## Specialty Cucumber Market

**Premium Varieties:**
- Mini/Persian cucumbers: $4-8/lb (vs. $1-2/lb standard)
- Japanese cucumbers: $5-10/lb
- Lemon cucumbers: $6-12/lb
- Specialty pickling varieties: $3-6/lb

**Market Segments:** Restaurants (Persian cukes), retail (mini cukes), farmers markets (heirloom varieties), pickle makers (specialty varieties).

---

## Recommended Varieties

### Mini/Persian Cucumbers
**Characteristics:** 4-6" length, thin skin, seedless or nearly seedless, crisp and sweet
**Varieties:** Socrates, Katrina, Persian Star
**Market:** Highest demand - snacking, lunch boxes, premium salads
**Pricing:** $6-10/lb wholesale, $8-14/lb retail

### Japanese (Kyuri) Cucumbers
**Characteristics:** Long (12-14"), slender, ridged skin, mild flavor
**Varieties:** Suyo Long, Tendergreen
**Market:** Asian restaurants, specialty stores
**Pricing:** $5-9/lb

### Specialty/Heirloom
**Lemon Cucumber:** Round, yellow, mild and sweet - $8-14/lb
**Boothby's Blonde:** Oval, cream-colored, heirloom - $6-12/lb
**Armenian Cucumber:** Very long, ribbed, sweet - $4-8/lb

## Production System

**Vertical Trellising:**
```
Support: Overhead wire or A-frame trellis
Plant spacing: 24" in row
Training: Single leader, remove side shoots
Pruning: Remove lower leaves for air circulation

Advantages:
- Efficient use of space (vertical growing)
- Easier harvest
- Better air circulation (disease prevention)
- Straighter fruit
```

**Hydroponic Systems:**
- Dutch buckets (5 gal) - Best for long-season cukes
- Media beds (aquaponics) - Good support for vines
- NFT - Not recommended (insufficient support)

---

## Growing Parameters

```
Temperature: 75-85°F day, 65-75°F night
pH: 5.8-6.3
EC: 2.0-2.8 mS/cm (vegetative), 2.5-3.0 mS/cm (fruiting)
Light: DLI 17-25 mol/m²/day (high light requirement)

Days to first harvest: 50-60 days
Production period: 8-12 weeks
Yield: 20-30 lbs per plant (variety dependent)
```

---

## Pollination

**Parthenocarpic vs. Standard:**
- Parthenocarpic (seedless): No pollination needed, fruit sets without fertilization
- Standard: Requires pollination (bees or hand pollination)
- **Recommendation:** Use parthenocarpic varieties for controlled environment (no pollination needed)

**If Standard Varieties:**
- Hand pollination: Transfer pollen from male to female flowers with brush
- Female flowers identified by small fruit at base
- Pollinate in morning
- Or introduce bumble bees

---

## Common Issues

**Bitter Fruit:**
- Causes: Heat stress, water stress, over-maturity
- Prevention: Consistent watering, avoid temperature stress, harvest on time
- Variety selection (some more bitter-prone)

**Powdery Mildew:**
- Very common in cucumbers
- Prevention: Air circulation, lower humidity, resistant varieties
- Control: Sulfur spray, milk spray (1:9)

**Blossom End Rot:**
- Less common than tomatoes but occurs
- Prevention: Adequate calcium, consistent moisture

## Specialty Squash

**Summer Squash Varieties:**
- Zephyr (yellow/green bicolor): $5-9/lb
- Round zucchini (Eight Ball): $4-7/lb
- Pattypan (scalloped): $4-8/lb
- Golden zucchini: $3-6/lb
- Costata Romanesco (ribbed): $6-10/lb

**Production Notes:**
```
Space requirement: 4-9 sq ft per plant (bushy varieties less)
Yield: 15-25 lbs per plant
Harvest: Daily when 4-8" long (tenderness, flavor)
Season: 6-10 weeks production

Challenges:
- Space-intensive for yield
- Powdery mildew susceptible
- Borers (soil-based systems)

Best approach: Small plantings of specialty varieties for diversification
```

## Economics (100 sq ft Cucumbers)

**Production:**
```
Plants: 40 (2.5 sq ft per plant, vertical)
Yield per plant: 25 lbs
Total: 1,000 lbs

Revenue (Persian cucumbers @ $7/lb):
$7,000

Costs:
- Transplants: $80
- Nutrients: $250
- Support/trellis: $150
- Labor (8 hrs/wk × $20 × 20 weeks): $3,200
- Packaging: $150
Total: $3,830

Net Profit: $3,170
Profit per sq ft: $31.70/year
ROI: 83%
```

**Analysis:** Profitable with premium varieties and good management. Better than standard varieties but less profitable than greens or cherry tomatoes. Best as diversification crop.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Mini/Persian cucumbers offer best specialty market opportunity
2. Parthenocarpic varieties eliminate pollination challenges
3. Vertical trellising essential for space efficiency
4. Powdery mildew major issue - prevention critical
5. Specialty squash space-intensive - use selectively
6. Fresh market only (short shelf life 5-7 days)
7. Good restaurant crop but moderate overall profitability
