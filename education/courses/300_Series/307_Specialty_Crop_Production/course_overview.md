## Course Description

Diversify your production with high-value specialty crops. This course covers production of specialty greens, edible flowers, medicinal herbs, and unique crops for premium markets. Learn cultivation techniques and marketing strategies to capture premium pricing.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Identify market opportunities for specialty crops
2. Produce a variety of specialty greens and herbs
3. Cultivate edible flowers successfully
4. Grow medicinal and culinary herbs
5. Market specialty crops to premium channels
6. Develop specialty crop business strategies

## Quality & Post-Harvest

### Quality Grading Standards

| Grade | Characteristics | Price Factor |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Premium | Perfect appearance, optimal size | 100% |
| Choice | Minor imperfections, good size | 75-85% |
| Standard | Some damage, size variation | 50-65% |
| Seconds | Cosmetic issues, still edible | 25-40% |

### Shelf Life by Category

| Category | Refrigerated Shelf Life | Optimal Storage |
|----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Microgreens | 7-14 days | 34-38°F, vented |
| Baby leaf | 7-10 days | 34-38°F, high humidity |
| Herbs (cut) | 5-10 days | 40-45°F, water tubes |
| Edible flowers | 3-7 days | 34-38°F, single layer |
| Asian greens | 7-14 days | 34-38°F, perforated |

| Food service distributors | Medium | High | Contract |

### Pricing Strategy

```
Premium Price Formula:
Base Price + Quality Premium + Uniqueness Premium + Service Premium = Final Price

Example - Specialty Basil:
Standard basil: $3.00/oz
+ Variety premium (Thai): $1.00
+ Quality premium (perfect): $0.50
+ Service premium (same-day): $0.50
= Premium price: $5.00/oz
```
