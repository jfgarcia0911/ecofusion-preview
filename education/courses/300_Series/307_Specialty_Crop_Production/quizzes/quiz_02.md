## Questions

### 1. What is the optimal temperature range for growing baby bok choy?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-70°F
c) 70-80°F
d) 80-90°F

### 2. Which Asian green has the shortest days to harvest?
a) Gai Lan (60-70 days)
b) Chinese Cabbage (55-70 days)
c) Bok Choy baby (21-30 days)
d) Komatsuna (25-35 days)

### 3. What is the typical yield per square foot for mizuna?
a) 0.5-1 lb
b) 1.5-2 lbs
c) 2-3 lbs
d) 3-4 lbs

### 4. Which Asian green is most tolerant of cool temperatures (50-70°F range)?
a) Shiso
b) Tatsoi
c) Choy Sum
d) Gai Lan

### 5. What is the recommended EC range for most Asian greens in hydroponic systems?
a) 0.8-1.2 mS/cm
b) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm
c) 1.8-2.4 mS/cm
d) 2.4-3.0 mS/cm

### 6. Shiso (Perilla) requires what temperature range for optimal growth?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-70°F
c) 70-80°F
d) 80-90°F

### 7. What is a key market advantage of Asian greens for specialty growers?
a) They require minimal nutrients
b) Growing demand in ethnic and mainstream markets
c) They can be stored for months
d) They need no temperature control

### 8. Which Asian green is known for its cold tolerance and can handle temperatures as low as 50°F?
a) Shiso
b) Choy Sum
c) Tatsoi
d) Chinese Cabbage

### 9. What is the typical wholesale price range for baby Asian greens in premium markets?
a) $4-8/lb
b) $8-14/lb
c) $14-22/lb
d) $22-30/lb

### 10. For intensive production, what is the recommended spacing for most Asian greens?
a) 2" × 2"
b) 4" × 4"
c) 6" × 6"
d) 8" × 8"

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 60-70°F**
   *Explanation: Baby bok choy thrives in moderate temperatures of 60-70°F, typical of cool-season production.*

2. **c) Bok Choy baby (21-30 days)**
   *Explanation: Baby bok choy has the shortest harvest time at 21-30 days, allowing for rapid turnover and multiple crops.*

3. **b) 1.5-2 lbs**
   *Explanation: Mizuna typically yields 1.5-2 lbs per square foot under optimal growing conditions.*

4. **b) Tatsoi**
   *Explanation: Tatsoi tolerates the widest cool temperature range (50-70°F), making it ideal for cooler production environments.*

5. **b) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: Most Asian greens perform best at moderate EC levels of 1.2-1.8 mS/cm in hydroponic systems.*

6. **c) 70-80°F**
   *Explanation: Shiso (Perilla) is a warm-season crop requiring 70-80°F for optimal growth, unlike most other Asian greens.*

7. **b) Growing demand in ethnic and mainstream markets**
   *Explanation: Asian greens are experiencing increasing demand as ethnic cuisines become mainstream and consumers seek variety.*

8. **c) Tatsoi**
   *Explanation: Tatsoi is exceptionally cold-tolerant and can thrive at temperatures as low as 50°F.*

9. **c) $14-22/lb**
   *Explanation: Baby Asian greens in premium markets (white-tablecloth restaurants) command $14-22/lb wholesale prices.*

10. **c) 6" × 6"**
    *Explanation: Most Asian greens are grown at 6" × 6" spacing for intensive production, balancing plant size with density.*
