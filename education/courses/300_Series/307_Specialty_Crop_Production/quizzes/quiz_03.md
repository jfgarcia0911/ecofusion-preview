## Questions

### 1. Which lettuce type is known for having the highest tolerance to tip burn?
a) Butterhead
b) Romaine
c) Oak leaf
d) Batavian

### 2. What is the optimal pH range for hydroponic lettuce production?
a) 4.5-5.5
b) 5.5-6.5
c) 6.5-7.5
d) 7.5-8.5

### 3. What temperature range is ideal for preventing tip burn in lettuce?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-68°F
c) 68-75°F
d) 75-82°F

### 4. Which specialty lettuce variety commands the highest premium in fine dining markets?
a) Green Oak Leaf
b) Red Butter Lettuce
c) Speckled Romaine varieties
d) Iceberg

### 5. What is the typical DLI (Daily Light Integral) requirement for premium lettuce production?
a) 8-12 mol/m²/day
b) 12-17 mol/m²/day
c) 17-22 mol/m²/day
d) 22-26 mol/m²/day

### 6. What is the recommended EC range for lettuce in NFT systems?
a) 0.8-1.0 mS/cm
b) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm
c) 2.0-2.5 mS/cm
d) 2.5-3.0 mS/cm

### 7. How many days to harvest for most specialty lettuce varieties?
a) 21-28 days
b) 28-35 days
c) 35-45 days
d) 45-60 days

### 8. What is the primary cause of tip burn in lettuce?
a) Excessive nitrogen
b) Calcium deficiency at leaf margins
c) Too much light
d) Fungal infection

### 9. What is the typical wholesale price range for specialty lettuce heads in premium markets?
a) $0.50-1.00/head
b) $1.00-2.00/head
c) $2.00-3.50/head
d) $3.50-5.00/head

### 10. Which growing system is most commonly used for commercial head lettuce production?
a) Deep Water Culture (DWC)
b) Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)
c) Dutch bucket systems
d) Aeroponics

### ---

## Answer Key

1. **d) Batavian**
   *Explanation: Batavian lettuce varieties have superior tip burn resistance compared to other types, making them more reliable in variable conditions.*

2. **b) 5.5-6.5**
   *Explanation: Lettuce performs optimally at pH 5.5-6.5, allowing for proper nutrient availability, especially calcium uptake.*

3. **b) 60-68°F**
   *Explanation: Maintaining temperatures between 60-68°F helps prevent tip burn by ensuring proper calcium transport to leaf margins.*

4. **c) Speckled Romaine varieties**
   *Explanation: Speckled and specialty-colored romaine varieties command premium prices due to their unique appearance and visual appeal.*

5. **b) 12-17 mol/m²/day**
   *Explanation: Premium lettuce production requires 12-17 mol/m²/day DLI for optimal growth, color development, and quality.*

6. **b) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: NFT lettuce systems perform best at moderate EC levels of 1.2-1.8 mS/cm to balance growth rate and quality.*

7. **c) 35-45 days**
   *Explanation: Most specialty lettuce varieties require 35-45 days to reach full head size for premium markets.*

8. **b) Calcium deficiency at leaf margins**
   *Explanation: Tip burn is caused by localized calcium deficiency at rapidly growing leaf margins, often due to transpiration issues or environmental stress.*

9. **c) $2.00-3.50/head**
   *Explanation: Specialty lettuce heads command $2.00-3.50/head wholesale in premium markets, significantly above standard varieties.*

10. **b) Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)**
    *Explanation: NFT is the most common commercial system for head lettuce due to excellent oxygenation, water efficiency, and ease of management.*
