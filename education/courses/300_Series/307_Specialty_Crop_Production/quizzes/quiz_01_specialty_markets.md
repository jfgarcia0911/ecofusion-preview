## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical price premium for specialty crops compared to standard commodity crops?

A) 10-25%
B) 50-100%
C) 150-800%
D) 1000-2000%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
Which sales channel typically offers the HIGHEST price for specialty crops?

A) Food service distributors
B) White-tablecloth fine dining restaurants
C) Wholesale to retail stores
D) Farmers markets

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 3
What is a defining characteristic of a specialty crop?

A) It must be certified organic
B) It can only be sold at farmers markets
C) It commands a higher price than conventional alternatives
D) It must be grown hydroponically

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 4
According to the market opportunity matrix, which crop category typically has the LOWEST production complexity?

A) Edible flowers
B) Asian greens
C) Medicinal herbs
D) Exotic vegetables

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 5
What is the recommended customer diversification strategy to reduce risk?

A) Focus on one large customer for efficiency
B) No single customer should exceed 20% of revenue
C) Only sell through one sales channel
D) Target only restaurants or only retail, not both

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 6
In the premium pricing formula, what factors contribute to the final market price?

A) Base cost only
B) Base cost + quality premium
C) Base cost + quality premium + scarcity premium + service premium
D) Whatever the market will bear

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 7
What is the typical gross profit margin range for a farmers market compared to restaurant wholesale?

A) Farmers markets and restaurant wholesale have similar margins
B) Restaurant wholesale has higher margins
C) Farmers markets have 150-180% of wholesale effective pricing due to direct sales
D) Farmers markets always have lower margins due to table fees

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 8
Which market trend is MOST driving specialty crop demand?

A) Decreasing consumer interest in food quality
B) Chef-driven innovation and unique ingredient sourcing
C) Lower prices for specialty items
D) Government mandates for specialty crops

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 9
What is the recommended approach for market entry with specialty crops?

A) Invest heavily and produce 20+ varieties immediately
B) Start with 3-5 specialty crops, perfect protocols, then scale
C) Focus only on the most expensive crops
D) Wait until you have confirmed buyers before growing anything

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 10
What is a critical success factor for building long-term restaurant relationships?

A) Always offering the lowest price
B) Reliability and consistent quality/availability
C) Changing products frequently to maintain interest
D) Only delivering when convenient for you

**Correct Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Specialty crops typically command 150-800% premiums
2. B - White-tablecloth fine dining offers highest prices
3. C - Higher price point is defining characteristic
4. B - Asian greens have low production complexity
5. B - Diversify with no customer >20% revenue
6. C - All four factors contribute to premium pricing
7. C - Farmers markets enable direct pricing 150-180% of wholesale
8. B - Chef innovation drives specialty demand
9. B - Start small with 3-5 crops, perfect, then scale
10. B - Reliability and consistency build trust
