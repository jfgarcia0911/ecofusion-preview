## Questions

### Question 1
What is the typical planting density for baby leaf production compared to full-size lettuce?

A) Same density (2-4 plants/sq ft)
B) Slightly higher (6-8 plants/sq ft)
C) Much higher (16-25 plants/sq ft)
D) Lower (1-2 plants/sq ft)

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 2
How many crop cycles per year are typical for baby leaf production?

A) 2-4 cycles
B) 4-6 cycles
C) 8-12 cycles
D) 16-20 cycles

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 3
What is the typical days-to-harvest range for baby leaf greens?

A) 5-10 days
B) 14-28 days
C) 35-50 days
D) 60-90 days

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 4
In a well-designed baby leaf salad mix, what percentage should be base mild lettuces?

A) 10-20%
B) 25-35%
C) 40-50%
D) 70-80%

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 5
What is the critical water temperature for washing baby leaf greens?

A) 70-75°F (warm for cleaning)
B) 50-55°F (moderate)
C) 38-42°F (cold)
D) Temperature doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 6
What percentage of fresh weight remains after spinning/drying baby leaf greens?

A) Leaves should be completely bone-dry (0% moisture)
B) Leaves should be surface-dry (no water droplets visible)
C) Leaves should remain quite wet (helps shelf life)
D) It doesn't matter

**Correct Answer: B**

---

### Question 7
What is the wholesale price range for premium baby leaf salad mixes?

A) $3-6/lb
B) $6-10/lb
C) $10-20/lb
D) $25-40/lb

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 8
Which succession planting strategy ensures continuous weekly harvests?

A) Plant everything at once
B) Plant small amounts every 2-4 weeks
C) Plant synchronized batches weekly with different components
D) Plant only when you have orders

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 9
What shelf life should properly handled baby leaf greens achieve?

A) 2-3 days
B) 4-5 days
C) 7-10 days
D) 14-21 days

**Correct Answer: C**

---

### Question 10
What is the primary challenge that limits profitability in small-scale baby leaf production?

A) Low market demand
B) Difficulty growing baby greens
C) High labor intensity relative to output
D) Lack of equipment

**Correct Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Baby leaf requires 16-25 plants/sq ft
2. C - Fast turnover enables 8-12 cycles annually
3. B - Harvest at 14-28 days for baby stage
4. C - 40-50% base provides mild foundation
5. C - Cold water (38-42°F) critical for shelf life
6. B - Surface-dry prevents decay, maintains quality
7. C - Premium baby mixes sell for $10-20/lb wholesale
8. C - Weekly synchronized component planting
9. C - 7-10 days with proper handling
10. C - Labor intensity is primary profitability challenge
