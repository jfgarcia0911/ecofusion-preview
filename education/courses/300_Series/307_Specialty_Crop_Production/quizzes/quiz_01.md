## Questions

### 1. What is the primary characteristic that defines a specialty crop?
a) It requires special growing equipment
b) It commands premium prices above conventional alternatives
c) It can only be grown in greenhouses
d) It has a very long growing season

### 2. According to the Market Opportunity Matrix, which specialty crop category typically commands the highest price premium?
a) Asian greens (100-200%)
b) Specialty herbs (150-400%)
c) Baby leaf salad (100-200%)
d) Edible flowers (400-800%)

### 3. What is the typical volume requirement for white-tablecloth fine dining restaurants?
a) 1-5 lbs/week
b) 5-20 lbs/week
c) 20-50 lbs/week
d) 50-100 lbs/week

### 4. In the Specialty Crop Value Equation, which factors contribute to market value?
a) Demand, scarcity, and quality factors only
b) Demand and quality factors only
c) Scarcity and quality factors only
d) Base price, demand, scarcity, and quality factors

### 5. What is the typical wholesale price range for microgreens according to the course materials?
a) $5-10/lb
b) $10-20/lb
c) $25-50/lb
d) $50-75/lb

### 6. Which sales channel typically offers the highest price premium for specialty crops?
a) Food service distributors
b) Farmers markets
c) White-tablecloth restaurants
d) CSA subscriptions

### 7. What is a key advantage of specialty crops over commodity crops?
a) They require less water
b) They grow faster
c) They have limited commercial availability creating market opportunities
d) They need no pest management

### 8. For farm-to-table casual dining restaurants, what is the typical delivery expectation?
a) Daily delivery
b) 2-3 times per week
c) Weekly delivery
d) Bi-weekly delivery

### 9. Which characteristic is NOT typically associated with specialty crops?
a) Higher price point than conventional alternatives
b) Unique varieties or characteristics
c) Long shelf life suitable for long-distance shipping
d) Specific cultural or culinary applications

### 10. What is the typical production complexity rating for microgreens?
a) Low
b) Moderate
c) High
d) Very High

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) It commands premium prices above conventional alternatives**
   *Explanation: Specialty crops are primarily defined by their ability to command premium prices due to unique characteristics, limited availability, or specialized market appeal.*

2. **d) Edible flowers (400-800%)**
   *Explanation: According to the Market Opportunity Matrix, edible flowers command the highest price premium at 400-800% above conventional crops.*

3. **b) 5-20 lbs/week**
   *Explanation: White-tablecloth fine dining restaurants typically require low-medium volumes of 5-20 lbs per week, focusing on quality over quantity.*

4. **d) Base price, demand, scarcity, and quality factors**
   *Explanation: The complete formula is Market Value = Base Price × (Demand Factor + Scarcity Factor + Quality Factor).*

5. **c) $25-50/lb**
   *Explanation: Microgreens typically command wholesale prices of $25-50/lb, representing a 300-500% premium over standard crops.*

6. **c) White-tablecloth restaurants**
   *Explanation: White-tablecloth fine dining restaurants offer the highest price premium due to their focus on quality and low price sensitivity.*

7. **c) They have limited commercial availability creating market opportunities**
   *Explanation: Limited commercial availability is a key characteristic of specialty crops that allows producers to capture premium pricing.*

8. **b) 2-3 times per week**
   *Explanation: Farm-to-table casual dining restaurants typically expect delivery 2-3 times per week, balancing freshness with operational efficiency.*

9. **c) Long shelf life suitable for long-distance shipping**
   *Explanation: Specialty crops often have SHORT shelf life requiring local production, not long shelf life.*

10. **b) Moderate**
    *Explanation: Microgreens are rated as moderate production complexity - not as simple as basic greens but not as complex as some exotic crops.*
