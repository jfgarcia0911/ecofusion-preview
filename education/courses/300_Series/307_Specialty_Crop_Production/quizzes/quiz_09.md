## Questions

### 1. What is the optimal temperature range for cherry tomato fruit set?
a) 55-65°F
b) 65-75°F
c) 75-85°F
d) 85-95°F

### 2. Which type of cherry tomato growth habit is preferred for hydroponic systems?
a) Determinate
b) Indeterminate
c) Semi-determinate
d) Bush type

### 3. What is the recommended EC range for cherry tomato production during fruiting?
a) 1.5-2.0 mS/cm
b) 2.0-2.5 mS/cm
c) 2.5-3.5 mS/cm
d) 3.5-4.5 mS/cm

### 4. What is the typical wholesale price for specialty cherry tomatoes?
a) $1-2/lb
b) $2-4/lb
c) $4-6/lb
d) $6-10/lb

### 5. How often should cherry tomato plants be pruned and trained?
a) Once at planting
b) Monthly
c) Weekly
d) Daily

### 6. What is the typical time from transplant to first harvest for cherry tomatoes?
a) 30-40 days
b) 45-55 days
c) 60-75 days
d) 80-90 days

### 7. What minimum DLI is required for quality cherry tomato production?
a) 10-15 mol/m²/day
b) 15-20 mol/m²/day
c) 20-25 mol/m²/day
d) 25-30 mol/m²/day

### 8. Which specialty cherry tomato color typically commands the highest premium?
a) Red
b) Yellow/orange
c) Purple/black
d) All colors are priced the same

### 9. What is the optimal pH range for hydroponic cherry tomato production?
a) 4.5-5.5
b) 5.5-6.5
c) 6.5-7.5
d) 7.5-8.5

### 10. How long can a well-managed indeterminate cherry tomato plant produce?
a) 2-3 months
b) 3-6 months
c) 6-10 months
d) 10-12 months

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 65-75°F**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomatoes set fruit optimally at 65-75°F; temperatures above 85°F or below 55°F can reduce fruit set.*

2. **b) Indeterminate**
   *Explanation: Indeterminate varieties continuously grow and produce, making them ideal for extended hydroponic production with vertical training.*

3. **c) 2.5-3.5 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomatoes during fruiting require higher EC (2.5-3.5 mS/cm) to support heavy fruit production and enhance flavor.*

4. **c) $4-6/lb**
   *Explanation: Specialty cherry tomatoes, particularly heirloom and colored varieties, command premium wholesale prices of $4-6/lb.*

5. **c) Weekly**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomato plants require weekly pruning and training to remove suckers, maintain structure, and optimize production.*

6. **c) 60-75 days**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomatoes typically require 60-75 days from transplant to first harvest, though production continues for months after.*

7. **c) 20-25 mol/m²/day**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomatoes need minimum 20-25 mol/m²/day DLI for quality fruit production, color development, and flavor.*

8. **c) Purple/black**
   *Explanation: Purple/black cherry tomatoes like 'Black Cherry' command the highest premiums due to unique appearance and rich flavor.*

9. **b) 5.5-6.5**
   *Explanation: Cherry tomatoes perform optimally at pH 5.5-6.5, ensuring proper nutrient uptake for heavy fruit production.*

10. **c) 6-10 months**
    *Explanation: Well-managed indeterminate cherry tomato plants can produce continuously for 6-10 months in hydroponic systems.*
