## Questions

### 1. What is the typical wholesale price range for wasabi root?
a) $20-40/lb
b) $40-80/lb
c) $100-150/lb
d) $200-300/lb

### 2. What is the unique growing requirement for wasabi?
a) High heat and full sun
b) Cool temperatures (46-68°F) and shade
c) Saline conditions
d) Low humidity

### 3. How long does it typically take to grow wasabi to harvestable size?
a) 3-6 months
b) 6-12 months
c) 12-18 months
d) 18-24 months

### 4. Which exotic crop is known for being saline-tolerant?
a) Mâche
b) Agretti
c) Sorrel
d) Purslane

### 5. What is the typical market for specialty exotic crops?
a) Mass market grocery stores
b) High-end restaurants and gourmet markets
c) Farmers markets only
d) Food service distributors

### 6. What temperature range is optimal for mâche (corn salad)?
a) 35-45°F
b) 45-60°F
c) 60-75°F
d) 75-85°F

### 7. Which exotic crop is a succulent with health food appeal?
a) Watercress
b) Shiso
c) Purslane
d) Mâche

### 8. What is a key requirement for watercress production?
a) Dry conditions
b) Cool flowing water
c) High heat
d) Low light

### 9. What is the typical wholesale price for specialty exotic greens like mâche?
a) $8-12/lb
b) $12-18/lb
c) $20-30/lb
d) $40-50/lb

### 10. Which exotic herb is essential for Japanese cuisine?
a) Sorrel
b) Watercress
c) Shiso
d) Agretti

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) $100-150/lb**
   *Explanation: Wasabi commands exceptional prices of $100-150/lb wholesale due to difficulty of production and limited supply.*

2. **b) Cool temperatures (46-68°F) and shade**
   *Explanation: Wasabi requires cool, shaded conditions mimicking its native mountainous stream habitat in Japan.*

3. **d) 18-24 months**
   *Explanation: Wasabi requires 18-24 months to reach harvestable size, making it a long-term specialty crop requiring patience.*

4. **b) Agretti**
   *Explanation: Agretti (Salsola soda) is naturally saline-tolerant, allowing it to grow in conditions unsuitable for most crops.*

5. **b) High-end restaurants and gourmet markets**
   *Explanation: Exotic specialty crops target high-end restaurants and gourmet markets where chefs value unique ingredients.*

6. **b) 45-60°F**
   *Explanation: Mâche is a cool-season crop thriving at 45-60°F, making it ideal for fall/winter production.*

7. **c) Purslane**
   *Explanation: Purslane is a succulent green with high omega-3 content, appealing to health-conscious consumers.*

8. **b) Cool flowing water**
   *Explanation: Watercress requires cool, flowing water (similar to its natural stream habitat) for optimal production.*

9. **c) $20-30/lb**
   *Explanation: Specialty exotic greens like mâche command premium prices of $20-30/lb due to limited availability and gourmet appeal.*

10. **c) Shiso**
    *Explanation: Shiso (Perilla) is an essential herb in Japanese cuisine, used extensively in sushi and traditional dishes.*
