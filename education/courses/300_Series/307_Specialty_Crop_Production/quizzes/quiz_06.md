## Questions

### 1. What is the typical wholesale price range for edible flowers?
a) $5-10/oz
b) $10-15/oz
c) $15-30/oz
d) $30-45/oz

### 2. What is the PRIMARY food safety requirement for edible flower production?
a) Organic certification required
b) 100% pesticide-free with no exceptions
c) Must be grown in soil
d) Requires special fertilizers

### 3. Which edible flower is known for its peppery flavor?
a) Viola/Pansy
b) Calendula
c) Nasturtium
d) Borage

### 4. What is the optimal storage temperature for edible flowers?
a) 28-32°F
b) 34-38°F
c) 40-45°F
d) 45-50°F

### 5. What is the typical shelf life for properly stored edible flowers?
a) 1-2 days
b) 3-7 days
c) 7-14 days
d) 14-21 days

### 6. Which flower should be harvested "just opened" rather than at full bloom?
a) Calendula
b) Viola/Pansy
c) Nasturtium
d) Dianthus

### 7. What flavor profile does borage flower provide?
a) Peppery
b) Cucumber
c) Citrus
d) Clove/spicy

### 8. Why must edible flowers be labeled as "food-grade, not ornamental"?
a) Marketing preference
b) Legal requirement to distinguish from flowers grown with pesticides
c) Price differentiation
d) Packaging requirements

### 9. Which edible flower has a citrus flavor profile?
a) Chive blossoms
b) Marigold (signet)
c) Dianthus
d) Squash blossoms

### 10. What is the best harvest timing for squash blossoms?
a) Day before opening
b) Day of opening
c) 2-3 days after opening
d) When fully mature

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) $15-30/oz**
   *Explanation: Edible flowers command premium wholesale prices of $15-30/oz due to their specialty nature and careful handling requirements.*

2. **b) 100% pesticide-free with no exceptions**
   *Explanation: Edible flowers must be completely pesticide-free as they are consumed whole; this is a non-negotiable food safety requirement.*

3. **c) Nasturtium**
   *Explanation: Nasturtium flowers have a distinctive peppery flavor, making them popular for salads and as garnishes.*

4. **b) 34-38°F**
   *Explanation: Edible flowers should be stored at 34-38°F in single layers to maintain quality and extend shelf life.*

5. **b) 3-7 days**
   *Explanation: Properly stored edible flowers maintain quality for 3-7 days, requiring careful handling and rapid turnover.*

6. **c) Nasturtium**
   *Explanation: Nasturtium flowers should be harvested "just opened" for optimal appearance and flavor.*

7. **b) Cucumber**
   *Explanation: Borage flowers have a distinctive cucumber flavor, making them unique among edible flowers.*

8. **b) Legal requirement to distinguish from flowers grown with pesticides**
   *Explanation: Labeling as "food-grade" is essential to distinguish edible flowers from ornamental flowers that may contain pesticide residues.*

9. **b) Marigold (signet)**
   *Explanation: Signet marigolds have a citrus flavor profile, adding bright notes to dishes.*

10. **b) Day of opening**
    *Explanation: Squash blossoms are best harvested on the day of opening for peak freshness, texture, and flavor.*
