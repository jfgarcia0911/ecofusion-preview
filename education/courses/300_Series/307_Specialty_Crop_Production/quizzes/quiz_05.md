## Questions

### 1. What is the typical wholesale price range for specialty basil varieties (Thai, Lemon, Purple)?
a) $8-14/lb
b) $12-18/lb
c) $18-32/lb
d) $32-48/lb

### 2. What is the optimal temperature range for basil production?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-70°F
c) 70-85°F
d) 85-95°F

### 3. Which herb variety commands the highest premium price?
a) Italian basil
b) Parsley
c) French tarragon
d) Cilantro

### 4. What is the recommended DLI (Daily Light Integral) for basil production?
a) 8-12 mol/m²/day
b) 12-14 mol/m²/day
c) 15-20 mol/m²/day
d) 20-25 mol/m²/day

### 5. What is a key advantage of perennial herbs like mint and oregano?
a) They produce more per plant than annuals
b) They provide continuous harvest from established plants
c) They require no nutrients
d) They grow faster than annual herbs

### 6. What EC range is recommended for basil to achieve intense flavor?
a) 1.0-1.4 mS/cm
b) 1.4-1.8 mS/cm
c) 1.6-2.4 mS/cm
d) 2.4-3.0 mS/cm

### 7. What is the typical shelf life for cut fresh herbs under proper refrigeration?
a) 1-3 days
b) 3-5 days
c) 5-10 days
d) 10-14 days

### 8. Which basil variety is preferred for Asian cuisine and commands premium pricing?
a) Genovese basil
b) Italian Large Leaf
c) Thai basil
d) Lemon basil

### 9. What is the optimal pH range for most culinary herbs?
a) 4.5-5.5
b) 5.5-6.5
c) 6.5-7.5
d) 7.5-8.5

### 10. For intensive basil production, what plant spacing is recommended?
a) 4" × 4"
b) 6" × 6"
c) 8" × 8"
d) 12" × 12"

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) $18-32/lb**
   *Explanation: Specialty basil varieties (Thai, Lemon, Purple) command premium prices of $18-32/lb, compared to $12-18/lb for standard Italian basil.*

2. **c) 70-85°F**
   *Explanation: Basil is a warm-season crop requiring 70-85°F for optimal growth; temperatures below 50°F cause damage.*

3. **c) French tarragon**
   *Explanation: French tarragon commands the highest premium at $24-40/lb due to its unique flavor and difficulty to produce from seed.*

4. **c) 15-20 mol/m²/day**
   *Explanation: Basil has high light requirements of 15-20 mol/m²/day for optimal growth, flavor development, and essential oil production.*

5. **b) They provide continuous harvest from established plants**
   *Explanation: Perennial herbs like mint and oregano can be harvested continuously from the same plants for months or years, reducing replanting costs.*

6. **c) 1.6-2.4 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: Higher EC levels (1.6-2.4 mS/cm) in basil production result in more intense flavor and essential oil content.*

7. **c) 5-10 days**
   *Explanation: Most cut fresh herbs maintain quality for 5-10 days under proper refrigeration (40-45°F with water tubes).*

8. **c) Thai basil**
   *Explanation: Thai basil, with its distinctive anise flavor and purple stems, is the preferred variety for Asian cuisine and commands $18-28/lb.*

9. **b) 5.5-6.5**
   *Explanation: Most culinary herbs perform optimally at pH 5.5-6.5, ensuring proper nutrient availability.*

10. **b) 6" × 6"**
    *Explanation: Intensive basil production uses 6" × 6" spacing (4 plants/sq ft) to balance plant size, air circulation, and productivity.*
