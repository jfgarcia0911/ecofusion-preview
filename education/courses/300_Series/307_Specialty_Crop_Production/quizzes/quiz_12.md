## Questions

### 1. What is the optimal time of day to harvest specialty greens for maximum quality?
a) Midday (12-2 PM)
b) Early morning after dew dries (before 10 AM)
c) Late afternoon (4-6 PM)
d) Evening (after 7 PM)

### 2. According to the "Hour Rule," how quickly should harvested produce be cooled?
a) Within 30 minutes
b) Within 1 hour
c) Within 4 hours
d) Within 24 hours

### 3. What happens to shelf life if pre-cooling is delayed to 4 hours after harvest?
a) No significant impact (100% shelf life)
b) Reduced to 80-90% shelf life
c) Reduced to 60-70% shelf life
d) Reduced to 40-50% shelf life

### 4. What is the optimal storage temperature for most leafy greens and herbs?
a) 28-32°F
b) 34-38°F
c) 40-45°F
d) 45-50°F

### 5. Which crops should NOT be pre-cooled using cold water immersion?
a) Asian greens and lettuce
b) Basil and delicate edible flowers
c) Root crops
d) Most herbs

### 6. What is the typical refrigerated shelf life for microgreens?
a) 3-5 days
b) 5-7 days
c) 7-14 days
d) 14-21 days

### 7. What percentage of shelf life potential is achieved if produce is pre-cooled within 2 hours?
a) 60-70%
b) 70-80%
c) 80-90%
d) 95-100%

### 8. What is the optimal storage temperature for edible flowers?
a) 28-32°F
b) 34-38°F
c) 40-45°F
d) 45-50°F

### 9. Why is early morning harvest preferred over midday harvest?
a) Cooler air temperatures
b) Maximum plant turgidity and lower field heat
c) Better weather conditions
d) More convenient timing

### 10. What is the shelf life of properly stored cut fresh herbs?
a) 1-3 days
b) 3-5 days
c) 5-10 days
d) 10-14 days

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Early morning after dew dries (before 10 AM)**
   *Explanation: Early morning harvest provides maximum turgidity, cool temperatures, peak essential oils, and 30-50% better shelf life.*

2. **b) Within 1 hour**
   *Explanation: The "Hour Rule" states that produce should be cooled from field temperature to storage temperature within 1 hour for optimal shelf life.*

3. **c) Reduced to 60-70% shelf life**
   *Explanation: Delaying pre-cooling to 4 hours reduces shelf life potential to only 60-70% of maximum.*

4. **b) 34-38°F**
   *Explanation: Most leafy greens and herbs store optimally at 34-38°F, balancing cold storage without freezing damage.*

5. **b) Basil and delicate edible flowers**
   *Explanation: Basil is cold-sensitive and edible flowers are too delicate for water immersion; both require refrigerated air cooling.*

6. **c) 7-14 days**
   *Explanation: Properly handled and stored microgreens maintain quality for 7-14 days at 34-38°F.*

7. **c) 80-90%**
   *Explanation: Pre-cooling within 2 hours of harvest retains 80-90% of potential shelf life, though still less than the optimal 1-hour window.*

8. **b) 34-38°F**
   *Explanation: Edible flowers should be stored at 34-38°F in single layers to maximize their limited 3-7 day shelf life.*

9. **b) Maximum plant turgidity and lower field heat**
   *Explanation: Early morning harvest captures plants at maximum turgidity (from overnight water uptake) with minimal field heat to remove.*

10. **c) 5-10 days**
    *Explanation: Most cut fresh herbs maintain quality for 5-10 days when properly stored at 40-45°F in water tubes.*
