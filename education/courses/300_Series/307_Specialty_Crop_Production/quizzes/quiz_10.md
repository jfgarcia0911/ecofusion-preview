## Questions

### 1. What type of cucumber is best suited for hydroponic production?
a) Pickling cucumbers
b) Slicing cucumbers
c) Parthenocarpic (seedless) cucumbers
d) Lemon cucumbers

### 2. What is the optimal temperature range for cucumber production?
a) 55-65°F
b) 65-75°F
c) 75-85°F
d) 85-95°F

### 3. What EC range is recommended for cucumber production?
a) 1.2-1.8 mS/cm
b) 1.8-2.4 mS/cm
c) 2.4-3.0 mS/cm
d) 3.0-3.6 mS/cm

### 4. What is the typical time from transplant to first harvest for cucumbers?
a) 20-30 days
b) 35-45 days
c) 50-60 days
d) 65-75 days

### 5. Which specialty squash product commands the highest price premium?
a) Large zucchini
b) Baby zucchini (4-6 inches)
c) Summer squash
d) Winter squash

### 6. What is the recommended relative humidity for cucumber production?
a) 40-50%
b) 50-60%
c) 60-70%
d) 70-80%

### 7. How often can cucumbers be harvested during peak production?
a) Weekly
b) Every 3-4 days
c) Daily to every other day
d) Monthly

### 8. What is the optimal pH range for cucumbers and squash?
a) 4.5-5.5
b) 5.5-6.5
c) 6.5-7.5
d) 7.5-8.5

### 9. What training method is most common for hydroponic cucumbers?
a) Ground/sprawling
b) Vertical trellis with single stem
c) Bush/compact
d) No training needed

### 10. What is the typical yield per plant for well-managed hydroponic cucumbers?
a) 5-10 lbs
b) 15-25 lbs
c) 30-50 lbs
d) 60-80 lbs

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) Parthenocarpic (seedless) cucumbers**
   *Explanation: Parthenocarpic varieties don't require pollination, produce uniform seedless fruit, and are ideal for controlled hydroponic environments.*

2. **b) 65-75°F**
   *Explanation: Cucumbers thrive at 65-75°F, balancing growth rate with fruit quality; temperatures above 85°F can reduce fruit set.*

3. **b) 1.8-2.4 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: Cucumbers prefer moderate EC levels of 1.8-2.4 mS/cm to support rapid growth and fruit production.*

4. **b) 35-45 days**
   *Explanation: Cucumbers are relatively fast, reaching first harvest 35-45 days from transplant in optimal hydroponic conditions.*

5. **b) Baby zucchini (4-6 inches)**
   *Explanation: Baby zucchini harvested at 4-6 inches command premium prices ($3-5/lb) compared to standard full-size squash.*

6. **c) 60-70%**
   *Explanation: Cucumbers prefer moderate to high humidity (60-70%) to support rapid growth and prevent stress.*

7. **c) Daily to every other day**
   *Explanation: During peak production, cucumbers require daily or every-other-day harvest to maintain fruit quality and encourage continued production.*

8. **b) 5.5-6.5**
   *Explanation: Cucumbers and squash perform optimally at pH 5.5-6.5 for proper nutrient availability.*

9. **b) Vertical trellis with single stem**
   *Explanation: Vertical single-stem training maximizes space efficiency and light exposure while simplifying harvest in hydroponic systems.*

10. **c) 30-50 lbs**
    *Explanation: Well-managed hydroponic cucumber plants can produce 30-50 lbs over their production cycle (3-4 months).*
