## Questions

### 1. What is the optimal temperature range for pepper fruit set?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-70°F
c) 70-85°F
d) 85-95°F

### 2. Which specialty pepper type typically commands the highest price per pound?
a) Bell peppers
b) Jalapeños
c) Specialty sweet peppers (mini, colored)
d) Banana peppers

### 3. What is the typical days to first harvest for most pepper varieties?
a) 30-45 days
b) 45-60 days
c) 60-80 days
d) 80-100 days

### 4. What EC range is recommended for pepper production?
a) 1.0-1.5 mS/cm
b) 1.5-2.0 mS/cm
c) 2.0-2.5 mS/cm
d) 2.5-3.5 mS/cm

### 5. Which factor is MOST critical for successful pepper pollination in controlled environments?
a) High humidity
b) Air movement for flower vibration
c) Low light
d) Cool temperatures

### 6. What is the optimal pH range for hydroponic pepper production?
a) 4.5-5.5
b) 5.5-6.5
c) 6.5-7.5
d) 7.5-8.5

### 7. Specialty mini sweet peppers typically wholesale for what price range?
a) $1-2/lb
b) $2-3/lb
c) $3-5/lb
d) $5-7/lb

### 8. How long can pepper plants produce fruit in a well-managed hydroponic system?
a) 2-3 months
b) 4-6 months
c) 6-12 months
d) 12-18 months

### 9. What is the recommended DLI for optimal pepper production?
a) 10-15 mol/m²/day
b) 15-20 mol/m²/day
c) 20-30 mol/m²/day
d) 30-40 mol/m²/day

### 10. Which chile pepper is known for extremely high heat levels (100,000+ Scoville)?
a) Jalapeño
b) Serrano
c) Habanero
d) Poblano

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) 70-85°F**
   *Explanation: Peppers set fruit optimally at 70-85°F; temperatures above 90°F or below 60°F can prevent fruit set.*

2. **c) Specialty sweet peppers (mini, colored)**
   *Explanation: Specialty sweet peppers like mini bells and colored varieties command premium prices of $3-5/lb or more wholesale.*

3. **c) 60-80 days**
   *Explanation: Most pepper varieties require 60-80 days from transplant to first harvest, making them a longer-term crop.*

4. **d) 2.5-3.5 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: Peppers are heavy feeders requiring higher EC levels (2.5-3.5 mS/cm) for optimal fruit production and flavor.*

5. **b) Air movement for flower vibration**
   *Explanation: Gentle air movement helps vibrate flowers for pollen release, essential for fruit set in controlled environments without insects.*

6. **b) 5.5-6.5**
   *Explanation: Peppers perform best at pH 5.5-6.5, ensuring optimal nutrient availability for heavy fruit production.*

7. **c) $3-5/lb**
   *Explanation: Specialty mini sweet peppers command premium wholesale prices of $3-5/lb due to their appeal and hand-harvesting requirements.*

8. **c) 6-12 months**
   *Explanation: Well-managed pepper plants can produce continuously for 6-12 months in hydroponic systems, providing excellent ROI.*

9. **c) 20-30 mol/m²/day**
   *Explanation: Peppers are high-light crops requiring 20-30 mol/m²/day DLI for optimal flowering, fruit set, and production.*

10. **c) Habanero**
    *Explanation: Habanero peppers rate 100,000-350,000 Scoville Heat Units, much higher than jalapeños (2,500-8,000 SHU).*
