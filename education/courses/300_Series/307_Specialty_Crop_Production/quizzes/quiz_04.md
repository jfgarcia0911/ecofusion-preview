## Questions

### 1. What is the typical harvest window for baby leaf greens?
a) 7-14 days
b) 14-21 days
c) 21-28 days
d) 28-35 days

### 2. What seeding density is recommended for baby leaf production compared to full-size lettuce?
a) Same density
b) 2-3 times higher
c) 4-6 times higher
d) 10-12 times higher

### 3. What is the optimal leaf size for premium baby leaf salad mixes?
a) 1-2 inches
b) 2-4 inches
c) 4-6 inches
d) 6-8 inches

### 4. Which factor is MOST critical for uniform germination in baby leaf production?
a) Seed depth consistency
b) Temperature control
c) Light intensity
d) Nutrient concentration

### 5. What is the typical wholesale price range for baby leaf salad mixes?
a) $4-8/lb
b) $10-20/lb
c) $20-30/lb
d) $30-40/lb

### 6. How many cuts can typically be achieved from baby leaf greens before replanting?
a) 1 cut only
b) 1-2 cuts
c) 2-3 cuts
d) 3-4 cuts

### 7. What is the recommended temperature for baby leaf production?
a) 50-60°F
b) 60-70°F
c) 70-80°F
d) 80-90°F

### 8. Which growing method is BEST suited for high-volume baby leaf production?
a) Individual net pots
b) Float/raft systems with dense seeding
c) Dutch buckets
d) Vertical towers

### 9. What EC level is optimal for baby leaf greens to balance growth speed and flavor?
a) 0.8-1.2 mS/cm
b) 1.2-1.6 mS/cm
c) 1.6-2.2 mS/cm
d) 2.2-2.8 mS/cm

### 10. Which mix component provides spicy/peppery flavor in baby leaf salads?
a) Baby butterhead lettuce
b) Baby arugula and mustards
c) Baby spinach
d) Baby chard

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) 14-21 days**
   *Explanation: Baby leaf greens are typically harvested at 14-21 days when leaves reach optimal 2-4 inch size for salad mixes.*

2. **c) 4-6 times higher**
   *Explanation: Baby leaf production requires 4-6 times higher seeding density than full-size lettuce to achieve the dense, tender leaf canopy desired.*

3. **b) 2-4 inches**
   *Explanation: Premium baby leaf is harvested at 2-4 inches for optimal tenderness, appearance, and eating quality.*

4. **b) Temperature control**
   *Explanation: Consistent temperature (65-70°F) is most critical for uniform germination, ensuring all seeds emerge simultaneously for even growth.*

5. **b) $10-20/lb**
   *Explanation: Baby leaf salad mixes typically command $10-20/lb wholesale, with premium mixes at the higher end of the range.*

6. **b) 1-2 cuts**
   *Explanation: Baby leaf greens typically allow 1-2 cuts before quality declines and replanting is more efficient than continued cutting.*

7. **b) 60-70°F**
   *Explanation: Baby leaf greens thrive at 60-70°F, balancing rapid growth with quality and preventing bolting.*

8. **b) Float/raft systems with dense seeding**
   *Explanation: Float/raft systems excel for baby leaf production, allowing dense seeding and easy harvesting of entire rafts.*

9. **b) 1.2-1.6 mS/cm**
   *Explanation: EC of 1.2-1.6 mS/cm provides optimal balance between rapid growth and flavor development in baby leaf greens.*

10. **b) Baby arugula and mustards**
    *Explanation: Baby arugula and mustards provide the spicy, peppery notes that add flavor complexity to baby leaf mixes.*
