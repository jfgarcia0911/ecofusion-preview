## Culinary Herbs (Annual)

| Herb | Seed to Harvest | Spacing | Production Period |
|------|----------------|---------|------------------|
| **Basil** | 40-56 | 6-8" × 6-8" | 12-16 weeks |
| **Cilantro** | 50-60 | 4" × 4" | 3-4 weeks (then bolts) |
| **Dill** | 40-55 | 6" × 6" | 4-6 weeks |
| **Parsley** | 60-75 | 6" × 6" | 6-9 months |

**Harvest Frequency:**
- Basil: Every 2-3 weeks (cutting encourages regrowth)
- Cilantro: Succession plant every 2-3 weeks
- Parsley: Every 3-4 weeks (cut-and-come-again)

**Basil from Cuttings:**
- Cutting to transplant-ready: 14-21 days
- Transplant to first harvest: 21-28 days
- Total: 35-49 days (faster than seed)

## Succession Planting Calendar Template

### 4-Week Rotation Example (Baby Leaf Production)

```
WEEK 1:
Monday: Plant Tray A - Base lettuce mix
Wednesday: Plant Tray B - Red lettuce varieties
Friday: Plant Tray C - Mustards and arugula

WEEK 2:
Monday: Plant Tray D - Base lettuce mix
Wednesday: Plant Tray E - Red lettuce varieties
Friday: Plant Tray F - Specialty greens

WEEK 3:
Monday: Plant Tray G - Base lettuce mix
Wednesday: Plant Tray H - Red lettuce varieties
Friday: Plant Tray I - Mustards and arugula

WEEK 4:
Monday: Plant Tray J - Base lettuce mix
Wednesday: Plant Tray K - Red lettuce varieties
Friday: Plant Tray L - Herbs or specialty

WEEK 5 (Start harvesting Week 1 plantings):
Monday: HARVEST Tray A, then replant
Wednesday: HARVEST Tray B, then replant
Friday: HARVEST Tray C, then replant

Continue rotating...
```

**Benefits:**
- Consistent weekly harvests
- Even labor distribution
- Predictable product availability
- Synchronized component maturity

## Seasonal Considerations

### Cool Season Crops (Best in Fall/Winter/Spring)
- Lettuce (all types)
- Asian greens (most)
- Peas
- Cilantro
- Mâche
- Violas/Pansies

**Advantage:** Natural cooling reduces climate control costs

---

### Warm Season Crops (Best in Spring/Summer)
- Basil
- Cucumbers
- Peppers
- Tomatoes
- Nasturtiums
- Summer squash

**Advantage:** Faster growth, higher yields in warm months

---

### Year-Round Production
- Microgreens (controlled environment)
- Baby leaf mixes (climate controlled)
- Perennial herbs (established plants)
- Specialty lettuces (with climate control)

**Requirements:** Adequate climate control and supplemental lighting

## Quick Reference: Crop Rotation by Speed

### Ultra-Fast (1-2 weeks)
- Microgreens only

### Fast (2-4 weeks)
- Baby leaf salad mix
- Baby arugula
- Baby mustards
- Cilantro (harvest once then replant)

### Moderate (4-8 weeks)
- Full-size lettuces
- Asian greens (most)
- Annual herbs (basil, parsley)

### Slow (8-16 weeks)
- Edible flowers
- Fruiting crops (first harvest)
- Perennial herbs (establishment)

### Very Slow (16+ weeks)
- Fruiting crops (full production)
- Rosemary (establishment)
- Wasabi (avoid unless committed)

## Pro Tips for Timing

1. **Always have crops at different stages** - Don't plant everything at once
2. **Account for climate** - Adjust times for your environment
3. **Build in buffer time** - Add 10-20% to expected harvest dates
4. **Track actual performance** - Record your real times and adjust
5. **Weather affects timing** - Cloudy periods slow growth
6. **Variety matters** - Different varieties of same crop vary
7. **Plan for market demand** - Time harvests for peak sales periods
8. **Have backup crops** - If something fails, next succession ready
