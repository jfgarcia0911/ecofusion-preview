## Course Description

Optimize the journey from harvest to customer. This course covers supply chain design, cold chain management, distribution strategies, and logistics optimization for fresh produce. Learn to reduce waste, improve freshness, and maximize customer satisfaction.

---

1. Design efficient supply chain systems

4. Optimize logistics and fulfillment

## Post-Harvest Physiology

### Respiration & Shelf Life

| Crop Category | Respiration Rate | Shelf Life | Optimal Temp |
|---------------|------------------|------------|--------------|
| Leafy greens | High | 7-14 days | 32-36°F |
| Herbs | Very High | 5-10 days | 32-40°F |
| Tomatoes | Low-Medium | 10-21 days | 55-70°F |
| Cucumbers | Low | 10-14 days | 50-55°F |
| Peppers | Low | 14-21 days | 45-50°F |
| Microgreens | Very High | 7-10 days | 34-38°F |

### Quality Loss Factors

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Temperature abuse | Rapid quality loss | Maintain cold chain |
| Physical damage | Bruising, decay | Careful handling |
| Moisture loss | Wilting, weight loss | Proper humidity |
| Ethylene exposure | Premature senescence | Separation, ventilation |
| Microbial growth | Spoilage, safety | Sanitation, cold chain |

## Cold Chain Management

### Temperature Zones

| Zone | Temperature | Products |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Chill (Cold) | 32-36°F | Leafy greens, herbs |
| Cool | 45-55°F | Cucumbers, peppers, tomatoes |
| Ambient | 55-70°F | Tomatoes (ripe), basil |
| Frozen | Below 32°F | Not recommended for fresh |

### Cooling Methods

| Method | Cooling Rate | Best For | Cost |
|--------|-------------|----------|------|
| Room cooling | Slow (hours) | Low respiration crops | Low |
| Forced-air cooling | Fast (1-2 hrs) | Most produce | Medium |
| Hydrocooling | Very fast | Root vegetables | Medium |
| Vacuum cooling | Fastest | Leafy greens | High |
| Package icing | Medium | Fish, specialty items | Medium |

## Food Safety in Distribution

### HACCP Control Points

| CCP | Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring |
|-----|--------|----------------|------------|
| Receiving | Contamination | Visual inspection | Each delivery |
| Cold storage | Temperature | ≤41°F | Continuous |
| Cross-contamination | Pathogen transfer | Separation | Each batch |
| Transport | Temperature abuse | ≤41°F or appropriate | Trip logs |
| Delivery | Time/temp | 4-hour limit | Timestamps |

### Traceability Requirements

```
One-up, One-back Traceability:

Source Information (One-back):
├── Grower/Producer ID
├── Harvest date
├── Lot number
└── Location

Your Operation:
├── Receipt date/time
├── Storage location
├── Processing records
└── Pack date/lot

Customer Information (One-up):
├── Customer name
├── Ship date
├── Quantity
└── Lot delivered
```

## Logistics Optimization

### Delivery Route Planning

| Factor | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| Customer locations | Clustering, zones |
| Order volume | Vehicle capacity |
| Delivery windows | Restaurant schedules |
| Temperature requirements | Mixed load management |
| Traffic patterns | Time of day |
| Fuel efficiency | Route optimization |

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Target | Calculation |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| On-time delivery | >95% | Deliveries on time / Total deliveries |
| Order accuracy | >99% | Correct orders / Total orders |
| Shrink rate | <3% | Lost product / Total shipped |
| Cold chain compliance | 100% | Time in temp / Total time |
| Customer satisfaction | >4.5/5 | Survey scores |
