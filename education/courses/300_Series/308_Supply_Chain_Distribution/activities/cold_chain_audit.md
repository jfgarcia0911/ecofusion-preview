# Activity: Cold Chain Audit

**Course 308:** Supply Chain & Distribution
**Time Required:** 2-3 hours
**Objective:** Assess your current cold chain performance and identify improvement opportunities

---

## Overview

This hands-on activity guides you through a complete cold chain audit of your operation, from harvest to customer delivery. You'll measure actual temperatures, identify gaps, and develop an action plan for improvement.

---

## Materials Needed

- Temperature monitoring devices (thermometers, data loggers)
- Stopwatch or timer
- Notebook or digital device for notes
- Camera for documentation
- This worksheet
