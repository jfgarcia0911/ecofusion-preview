## Questions

### Question 1
What is the main advantage of direct-to-consumer distribution?

A) Lowest costs
B) Easiest logistics
C) Maximum freshness and highest margins
D) Requires no cold storage

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
Which distribution model typically offers the lowest cost per unit delivered?

A) Farmers markets
B) CSA programs
C) Restaurant direct sales
D) Third-party distributor

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 3
What is a CSA program?

A) Cold Storage Agreement
B) Community Supported Agriculture
C) Centralized Sales Association
D) Commercial Shipping Alliance

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the primary challenge of direct-to-restaurant sales?

A) Low prices
B) Irregular delivery schedules and specific demands
C) Too much competition
D) Lack of customer interest

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which distribution channel provides the most direct customer feedback?

A) Wholesale distributors
B) Retail stores
C) Farmers markets
D) Food service distributors

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What is a hybrid distribution model?

A) Using only one sales channel
B) Selling only wholesale
C) Combining multiple distribution channels
D) Eliminating all middlemen

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
What percentage of production is typically recommended for testing a new distribution channel?

A) 5-10%
B) 25-35%
C) 50-60%
D) 75-85%

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 8
Which distribution model requires the most logistics management?

A) Single wholesale account
B) One farmers market
C) Hybrid multi-channel approach
D) CSA only

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is "aggregation" in local food distribution?

A) Selling only large quantities
B) Combining products from multiple farms for distribution
C) Storing products long-term
D) Pricing strategies

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Which factor is LEAST important when selecting a distribution channel?

A) Product shelf life
B) Production volume
C) Farm logo colors
D) Target market preferences

**Correct Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Maximum freshness and highest margins
2. D - Third-party distributor
3. B - Community Supported Agriculture
4. B - Irregular delivery schedules and specific demands
5. C - Farmers markets
6. C - Combining multiple distribution channels
7. A - 5-10%
8. C - Hybrid multi-channel approach
9. B - Combining products from multiple farms for distribution
10. C - Farm logo colors
