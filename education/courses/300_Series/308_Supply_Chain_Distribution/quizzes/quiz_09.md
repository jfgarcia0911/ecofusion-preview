## Questions

### Question 1
What does 3PL stand for?

A) Three Product Line
B) Third-Party Logistics
C) Triple Processing Location
D) Three Point Loading

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the primary benefit of using a 3PL provider?

A) Higher profit margins always
B) Access to established distribution networks and expertise
C) Complete control over all operations
D) Elimination of all costs

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is typically the 3PL fee structure for produce distribution?

A) Flat annual fee only
B) Percentage of sales (15-30%) plus handling fees
C) Free service
D) Equipment rental only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Which factor is MOST important when selecting a 3PL partner?

A) Fancy office location
B) Experience with temperature-sensitive fresh produce
C) Company size only
D) Lowest price regardless of service

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "drop shipping"?

A) Throwing packages
B) Direct shipment from producer to end customer without warehousing
C) Broken deliveries
D) Failed deliveries

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What should be included in a 3PL service level agreement (SLA)?

A) Only payment terms
B) Temperature requirements, delivery windows, quality standards
C) Just contact information
D) Marketing strategies

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the main disadvantage of using 3PL providers?

A) Better efficiency
B) Wider reach
C) Reduced margins and less control over customer experience
D) Professional handling

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
How often should you review 3PL performance?

A) Once per year only
B) Never
C) Weekly or monthly with defined KPIs
D) Only when problems occur

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is "co-packing" in the context of 3PL services?

A) Packing products together
B) Contract packaging services provided by third parties
C) Cooperative farming
D) Cold storage only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
When does it make sense to use a 3PL instead of self-distribution?

A) For very small volumes (<50 lbs/week)
B) When seeking to expand reach beyond self-delivery capacity
C) Never
D) Only for international shipping

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Third-Party Logistics
2. B - Access to established distribution networks and expertise
3. B - Percentage of sales (15-30%) plus handling fees
4. B - Experience with temperature-sensitive fresh produce
5. B - Direct shipment from producer to end customer without warehousing
6. B - Temperature requirements, delivery windows, quality standards
7. C - Reduced margins and less control over customer experience
8. C - Weekly or monthly with defined KPIs
9. B - Contract packaging services provided by third parties
10. B - When seeking to expand reach beyond self-delivery capacity
