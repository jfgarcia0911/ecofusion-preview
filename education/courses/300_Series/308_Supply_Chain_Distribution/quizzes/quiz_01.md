## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between fresh produce supply chains and other product supply chains?

A) Fresh produce is more expensive to transport
B) Fresh produce is a depreciating asset with limited shelf life
C) Fresh produce requires more packaging
D) Fresh produce has fewer distribution channels

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
According to the Q₁₀ rule, how much does respiration rate approximately change for every 18°F temperature increase?

A) Increases 25%
B) Increases 50%
C) Doubles (100% increase)
D) Triples (200% increase)

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
Which of the following is a critical control point (CCP) in a fresh produce supply chain?

A) Employee scheduling
B) Marketing materials
C) Cold storage temperature
D) Office equipment

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is the recommended inventory turnover rate for leafy greens?

A) Once per month
B) Once per week
C) 2-4 times per week
D) 8-10 times per week

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
In the hybrid distribution model example, what percentage of production was allocated to restaurant direct sales?

A) 20%
B) 30%
C) 35%
D) 40%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What does "time compression" mean in supply chain design?

A) Reducing storage temperature
B) Minimizing time from harvest to consumption
C) Shortening employee work hours
D) Speeding up the growing cycle

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Calculate the gross margin: Selling price = $6.00/lb, COGS = $3.50/lb

A) 35.5%
B) 41.7%
C) 58.3%
D) 71.4%

**Correct Answer:** B
*Formula: ($6.00 - $3.50) / $6.00 = 0.417 = 41.7%*

---

### Question 8
Which metric measures how often inventory is sold and replaced?

A) Shrink rate
B) Fill rate
C) Inventory turnover
D) Order accuracy

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9

A) From 12% to 8%
B) From 10% to 5%
C) From 8% to 2.5%
D) From 6% to 3%

**Correct Answer:** C

### Question 10
What is the primary objective of supply chain risk management?

A) Eliminate all risks completely
B) Identify, assess, and mitigate potential disruptions
C) Purchase insurance for all assets
D) Avoid taking any risks

**Correct Answer:** B
