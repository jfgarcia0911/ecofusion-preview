## Questions

### Question 1
What does FIFO stand for in inventory management?

A) First In, First Out
B) Fast In, Fast Out
C) Final Inventory, Final Output
D) Fresh Items, Fresh Orders

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
Why is FIFO critical for fresh produce?

A) It's legally required
B) It ensures oldest product is sold first to minimize waste
C) It makes counting easier
D) It improves packaging

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is inventory turnover?

A) Physical rotation of products
B) Number of times inventory is sold and replaced in a period
C) Employees changing shifts
D) Warehouse reorganization

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Calculate inventory turnover: Weekly sales = 1,500 lbs, Average inventory = 375 lbs

A) 2 times per week
B) 3 times per week
C) 4 times per week
D) 5 times per week

**Correct Answer:** C
*Formula: 1,500 ÷ 375 = 4*

---

### Question 5
What is the ideal inventory level for highly perishable greens?

A) 7-10 days supply
B) 3-5 days supply
C) 1-2 days supply
D) 14 days supply

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What is "shrink" in inventory management?

A) Products getting smaller
B) Inventory loss due to spoilage, damage, or theft
C) Reduced storage space
D) Price reductions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of lot tracking?

A) Parking management
B) Traceability for food safety and quality control
C) Employee scheduling
D) Equipment maintenance

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
When should inventory counts be performed?

A) Once per year
B) Monthly only
C) Daily or weekly, depending on turnover rate
D) Never, use estimates

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is par level inventory?

A) Golf course inventory
B) Minimum stock level that triggers reordering
C) Maximum storage capacity
D) Average of all inventory

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Which inventory management system is best for small farms?

A) Complex ERP system only
B) No system, memory only
C) Simple spreadsheet or basic software
D) Hire full-time inventory manager

**Correct Answer:** C
