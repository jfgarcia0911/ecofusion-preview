## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary driver of quality loss in fresh produce after harvest?

A) Lack of sunlight
B) Continued respiration and metabolism
C) Loss of nutrients
D) Exposure to oxygen

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the Q10 rule in post-harvest physiology?

A) Temperature affects respiration rate by 10% per degree
B) Respiration rate doubles for every 10°C increase
C) Products last 10 days if properly stored
D) Quality decreases 10% per day

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Which produce item has the highest respiration rate?

A) Tomatoes
B) Peppers
C) Leafy greens
D) Root vegetables

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is ethylene's primary effect on fresh produce?

A) Increases shelf life
B) Prevents ripening
C) Accelerates ripening and senescence
D) Improves color

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
At what temperature should leafy greens be stored for optimal shelf life?

A) 25-30°F
B) 32-36°F
C) 40-45°F
D) 50-55°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is transpiration in produce?

A) Movement of nutrients
B) Water loss through plant tissues
C) Gas exchange during respiration
D) Photosynthesis after harvest

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which factor does NOT directly affect respiration rate?

A) Temperature
B) Oxygen concentration
C) Package color
D) Physical damage

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What percentage of water can leafy greens lose before visible wilting occurs?

A) 1-2%
B) 3-5%
C) 10-12%
D) 15-20%

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Why should tomatoes not be stored below 55°F?

A) They freeze easily
B) They develop chilling injury
C) They ripen too slowly
D) They lose all color

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is the ideal relative humidity for storing most leafy greens?

A) 50-60%
B) 65-75%
C) 85-95%
D) 100%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Continued respiration and metabolism
2. B - Respiration rate doubles for every 10°C increase
3. C - Leafy greens
4. C - Accelerates ripening and senescence
5. B - 32-36°F
6. B - Water loss through plant tissues
7. C - Package color
8. B - 3-5%
9. B - They develop chilling injury
10. C - 85-95%
