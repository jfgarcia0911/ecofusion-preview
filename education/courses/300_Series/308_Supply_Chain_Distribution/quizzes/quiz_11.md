## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of temperature data loggers in distribution?

A) Weather forecasting
B) Continuous monitoring and documentation of cold chain compliance
C) Employee tracking
D) Fuel efficiency

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What technology enables real-time tracking of delivery vehicles?

A) Radio only
B) GPS and telematics systems
C) Paper maps
D) Cell phones only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is a QR code used for in produce distribution?

A) Decoration
B) Quick access to product information and traceability data
C) Pricing only
D) Employee identification

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is blockchain technology's potential benefit in food supply chains?

A) Cryptocurrency payments
B) Immutable, transparent record of product journey from farm to customer
C) Video games
D) Social media

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What should temperature data loggers record at minimum?

A) Temperature only
B) Temperature, date/time, and location
C) Color changes
D) Weight

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is an inventory management software's primary function?

A) Email marketing
B) Real-time tracking of stock levels, orders, and sales
C) Photo editing
D) Weather monitoring

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
How often should temperature monitoring alerts be reviewed?

A) Monthly
B) Weekly
C) Immediately upon receipt
D) Annually

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8

A) Finding scenic routes

**Correct Answer:** B

### Question 9

### Question 10
