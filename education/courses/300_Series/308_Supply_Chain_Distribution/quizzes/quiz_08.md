## Questions

### Question 1
What does HACCP stand for?

A) Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points
B) Health And Cleanliness Control Program
C) Harvest Analysis Cold Chain Protocol
D) Handling And Cooling Control Points

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
At what temperature must fresh-cut produce be maintained during distribution?

A) Below 50°F
B) Below 45°F
C) Below 41°F
D) Below 32°F

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What is a Critical Control Point (CCP)?

A) Any step in the process
B) A step where control can be applied to prevent/eliminate a food safety hazard
C) The most expensive operation
D) Where managers work

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
How long can fresh produce be in the "danger zone" (41°F-135°F) before it must be discarded?

A) 1 hour
B) 2 hours
C) 4 hours
D) 6 hours

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
What is the primary purpose of produce traceability?

A) Marketing
B) Pricing strategies
C) Rapid identification and isolation of contaminated products
D) Employee tracking

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
Which of these is NOT a common food safety hazard in distribution?

A) Temperature abuse
B) Cross-contamination
C) Product color
D) Pathogen growth

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
How often should delivery vehicle coolers be cleaned and sanitized?

A) Once per month
B) Once per week
C) Daily or after each delivery run
D) Only when visibly dirty

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What information must be included in lot tracking records?

A) Only the product name
B) Harvest date, lot number, destination, and delivery date
C) Just the price
D) Only customer name

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is the first step in responding to a potential food safety issue?

A) Destroy all product
B) Ignore minor issues
C) Immediately isolate affected product and notify authorities
D) Continue normal operations

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
Which regulation governs food safety for produce growers and distributors?

A) FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)
B) EPA Clean Air Act
C) OSHA regulations only
D) Local health codes only

**Correct Answer:** A

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points
2. C - Below 41°F
3. B - A step where control can be applied to prevent/eliminate a food safety hazard
4. C - 4 hours
5. C - Rapid identification and isolation of contaminated products
6. C - Product color
7. C - Daily or after each delivery run
8. B - Harvest date, lot number, destination, and delivery date
9. C - Immediately isolate affected product and notify authorities
10. A - FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)
