## Questions

### Question 1
What is the "cold chain"?

A) A refrigeration equipment brand
B) Continuous temperature-controlled supply chain
C) A type of cooling system
D) Ice-based cooling method

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
How quickly should leafy greens be cooled after harvest?

A) Within 6 hours
B) Within 4 hours
C) Within 2 hours
D) Within 30 minutes

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 3
What is the most effective cooling method for leafy greens?

A) Room cooling
B) Forced-air cooling
C) Hydrocooling
D) Vacuum cooling

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 4
At what temperature does the "danger zone" for bacterial growth begin?

A) 32°F
B) 41°F
C) 50°F
D) 60°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the primary benefit of pre-cooling produce?

A) Reduces labor costs
B) Removes field heat and slows deterioration
C) Improves appearance
D) Increases weight

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
How often should cold storage temperatures be monitored?

A) Once per day
B) Twice per day
C) Every 4 hours
D) Continuously with data logging

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Question 7
What is the acceptable temperature variance in a cold storage unit?

A) ±1°F
B) ±3°F
C) ±5°F
D) ±10°F

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which cooling method uses water to rapidly cool produce?

A) Forced-air cooling
B) Vacuum cooling
C) Hydrocooling
D) Room cooling

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What happens if produce experiences temperature "cycling"?

A) Improved shelf life
B) Better flavor
C) Condensation and accelerated spoilage
D) No significant effect

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
What is the recommended cold chain break time limit for leafy greens?

A) No more than 5 minutes
B) No more than 15 minutes
C) No more than 30 minutes
D) No more than 1 hour

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Continuous temperature-controlled supply chain
2. D - Within 30 minutes
3. D - Vacuum cooling
4. B - 41°F
5. B - Removes field heat and slows deterioration
6. D - Continuously with data logging
7. B - ±3°F
8. C - Hydrocooling
9. C - Condensation and accelerated spoilage
10. B - No more than 15 minutes
