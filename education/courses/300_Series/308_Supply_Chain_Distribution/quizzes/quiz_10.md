## Questions

### Question 1
What is the biggest challenge in e-commerce fulfillment for fresh produce?

A) Website design
B) Maintaining temperature control during last-mile delivery
C) Payment processing
D) Product photography

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is "last-mile delivery"?

A) The longest delivery route
B) Final step from distribution center to customer's door
C) International shipping
D) Backup delivery option

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
Which packaging is essential for shipping fresh produce via e-commerce?

A) Plain cardboard boxes
B) Insulated boxes with ice packs or gel packs
C) Plastic bags only
D) Newspaper wrapping

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is the recommended maximum transit time for shipping leafy greens via e-commerce?

A) Same day to next day (24 hours)
B) 3-5 days
C) 1 week
D) 2 weeks

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
What should customers be advised to do immediately upon receiving fresh produce?

A) Leave package outside
B) Open and refrigerate immediately
C) Wait 24 hours before opening
D) Return to sender

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a "delivery window" in e-commerce?

A) Time to look outside
B) Specific time frame when customer must be available to receive order
C) Dashboard feature
D) Packaging opening

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which e-commerce model works best for local fresh produce?

A) International overnight shipping
B) Local delivery zones with same-day or next-day service
C) 2-week standard shipping
D) Mail-only service

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is an important feature of an online ordering system for fresh produce?

A) Automatic video games
B) Real-time inventory updates and order cutoff times
C) Social media integration only
D) Music player

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
How should subscription box services manage inventory?

A) Random selection
B) Unlimited inventory
C) Pre-order system with harvest-to-order approach
D) Buy from grocery stores

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
What metric is most important for e-commerce fulfillment success?

A) Website traffic only
B) On-time delivery rate and product quality upon arrival
C) Number of social media followers

**Correct Answer:** B
