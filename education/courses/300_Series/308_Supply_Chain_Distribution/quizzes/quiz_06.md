## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary consideration when selecting delivery vehicles for fresh produce?

A) Vehicle color
B) Temperature control capability
C) Brand name
D) Age of vehicle only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is route optimization?

A) Choosing the fastest roads only
B) Planning delivery routes to minimize time, distance, and cost
C) Delivering to the nearest customer first
D) Always using highways

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the recommended maximum delivery time for refrigerated leafy greens?

A) 8 hours
B) 6 hours
C) 4 hours
D) 2 hours

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
Which routing strategy groups deliveries by geographic area?

A) Linear routing
B) Cluster routing
C) Random routing
D) Alphabetical routing

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is "backhaul" in transportation logistics?

A) Returning empty after delivery
B) Using the return trip to transport goods or materials
C) Reverse driving
D) Emergency backup routes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is the average cost per mile for operating a refrigerated delivery van?

A) $0.25-0.35
B) $0.50-0.75
C) $1.00-1.50
D) $2.00-2.50

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What should be done if a refrigerated vehicle breaks down during delivery?

A) Continue without refrigeration
B) Wait for repairs regardless of time
C) Transfer product to backup vehicle or coolers immediately
D) Return to farm

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
Which delivery time is preferred by most restaurants?

A) Late afternoon
B) During lunch rush
C) Early morning (before service)
D) Late evening

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is the primary benefit of GPS tracking for delivery vehicles?

A) Entertainment for drivers
B) Real-time location monitoring and route optimization
C) Preventing speeding only
D) Music streaming

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
How should mixed-temperature loads be organized in a delivery vehicle?

A) Randomly
B) Warmest items at front, coldest at back
C) All items mixed together
D) Proper separation with temperature zones or barriers

**Correct Answer:** D

---
