## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary goal of supply chain optimization?

A) Hiring more employees
B) Maximizing efficiency while maintaining quality and minimizing costs
C) Buying more equipment
D) Expanding to new markets only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is "lean" supply chain management?

A) Reducing employee meals
B) Eliminating waste and non-value-adding activities
C) Losing weight
D) Downsizing only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the 80/20 rule (Pareto Principle) in supply chain?

A) 80% of revenue comes from 20% of products/customers
B) Work 80 hours, rest 20
C) 80% water, 20% nutrients
D) 80°F temperature, 20% humidity

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 4
What is "just-in-time" inventory management?

A) Always being late
B) Maintaining minimal inventory by receiving goods only when needed
C) Working overtime
D) Rush deliveries only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which metric indicates supply chain efficiency?

A) Number of employees only
B) Order-to-delivery time and cost per unit delivered
C) Office size
D) Vehicle color

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is value stream mapping?

A) Finding valuable customers
B) Visualizing all steps in process to identify waste and improvement opportunities
C) Geographic mapping
D) Pricing strategies

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the first step in supply chain optimization?

A) Buy new technology
B) Hire consultants
C) Measure and analyze current performance
D) Change everything immediately

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
What is continuous improvement (Kaizen)?

A) One-time major change
B) Ongoing incremental improvements in processes
C) Constant criticism
D) Equipment upgrades only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
How should supply chain performance be benchmarked?

A) Against your own past performance and industry standards
B) Random comparison
C) No benchmarking needed
D) Only against largest competitors

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 10
What is the benefit of cross-training employees in supply chain operations?

A) Higher wages
B) Flexibility, reduced bottlenecks, and better coverage during absences
C) Longer work hours
D) Confusion

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Maximizing efficiency while maintaining quality and minimizing costs
2. B - Eliminating waste and non-value-adding activities
3. A - 80% of revenue comes from 20% of products/customers
4. B - Maintaining minimal inventory by receiving goods only when needed
5. B - Order-to-delivery time and cost per unit delivered
6. B - Visualizing all steps in process to identify waste and improvement opportunities
7. C - Measure and analyze current performance
8. B - Ongoing incremental improvements in processes
9. A - Against your own past performance and industry standards
10. B - Flexibility, reduced bottlenecks, and better coverage during absences
