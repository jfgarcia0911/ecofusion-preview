## Questions

### Question 1
What are the primary functions of produce packaging?

A) Branding only
B) Protection, containment, and communication
C) Weight reduction
D) Color enhancement

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which packaging material provides the best moisture retention for leafy greens?

A) Cardboard boxes
B) Paper bags
C) Perforated plastic film
D) Open mesh bags

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What is the purpose of perforation in produce packaging?

A) Aesthetic appeal
B) Gas exchange and respiration management
C) Cost reduction
D) Easier opening

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What does MAP stand for in packaging?

A) Maximum Air Protection
B) Modified Atmosphere Packaging
C) Minimum Air Penetration
D) Multiple Application Package

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which packaging is best for farmers market sales?

A) Bulk boxes
B) Clear clamshells with branding
C) Plain plastic bags
D) No packaging

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is the main disadvantage of using Styrofoam packaging?

A) High cost
B) Poor insulation
C) Environmental impact and non-recyclability
D) Weak structure

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
How should packaging be designed for stacking on pallets?

A) Round shapes
B) Square/rectangular with flat surfaces
C) Irregular shapes
D) Soft materials only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What information is required on produce packaging labels?

A) Only the farm name
B) Product name, weight, farm info, harvest date
C) Just the price
D) Only handling instructions

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
Which sustainable packaging option is gaining popularity?

A) Single-use plastics
B) Styrofoam
C) Compostable plant-based materials
D) Non-recyclable laminates

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
What is the ideal package fill percentage to prevent damage during transport?

A) 50-60%
B) 70-80%
C) 90-95%
D) 100%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

## Answer Key
