## Assessment Descriptions

### Assignment 1: Supply Chain Mapping Exercise (15 points)

**Purpose:** Understand the complete distribution network and identify optimization opportunities.

**What You'll Do:**
- Map all pathways from farm to end customer
- Identify all stakeholders and touchpoints
- Analyze current inefficiencies and bottlenecks
- Calculate costs at each distribution stage
- Identify improvement opportunities

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Visualize complex supply chains
- Identify distribution inefficiencies
- Understand stakeholder roles

**Deliverable:** Visual supply chain map with stakeholder analysis and improvement recommendations

---

### Assignment 2: Logistics Optimization Plan (20 points)

**Purpose:** Design efficient delivery systems minimizing cost while maintaining quality.

**What You'll Do:**
- Plan optimal delivery routes and schedules
- Select appropriate transportation methods
- Calculate delivery costs and efficiency metrics
- Design for multiple distribution channels
- Optimize for fuel efficiency and timing

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Plan efficient delivery routes
- Select cost-effective transportation
- Measure logistics performance

**Deliverable:** Logistics optimization plan with route maps, cost analysis, and performance KPIs

---

### Assignment 3: Cold Chain Management System (25 points)

**Purpose:** Maintain product quality through temperature-controlled distribution.

**What You'll Do:**
- Design cold chain from harvest to customer
- Select refrigeration and packaging solutions
- Implement temperature monitoring systems
- Develop quality verification procedures
- Address food safety compliance

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Manage temperature throughout distribution
- Select appropriate cold chain technologies
- Ensure food safety compliance

**Deliverable:** Cold chain management system with temperature protocols, packaging specs, and quality monitoring

---

### Assignment 4: Distribution Partnership Strategy (20 points)

**Purpose:** Build strategic partnerships for expanded market reach.

**What You'll Do:**
- Identify potential distribution partners
- Develop partnership criteria and selection process
- Create partnership agreements and terms
- Design relationship management systems
- Establish partner performance evaluation

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Select strategic distribution partners
- Structure mutually beneficial agreements
- Manage partner relationships effectively

**Deliverable:** Partnership strategy document with partner criteria, agreement templates, and management systems

---

### Assignment 5: Comprehensive Distribution System (30 points)

**Purpose:** Create complete distribution system from farm to customer.

**What You'll Do:**
- Design end-to-end distribution architecture
- Develop SOPs for all distribution processes
- Integrate technology for tracking and management
- Plan for multiple distribution channels
- Create scalability strategy
- Address regulatory compliance
- Develop contingency plans

**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Design scalable distribution systems
- Integrate distribution technology
- Manage multi-channel distribution
- Plan for growth and contingencies

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive distribution system plan (15-25 pages) with SOPs, technology integration, and scaling strategy

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What distribution channels should I focus on?**
A: Consider farmers markets, restaurants, retail, food hubs, CSA, and direct-to-consumer based on your market.

**Q: How do I calculate delivery costs accurately?**
A: Include fuel, vehicle depreciation, labor, insurance, packaging, and refrigeration costs.

**Q: What technology is recommended for distribution management?**
A: Route optimization software, temperature monitoring, inventory management, and customer tracking systems.

**Q: How do I ensure food safety in distribution?**
A: Follow GAP guidelines, maintain cold chain, use proper packaging, and implement traceability.

**Q: Can I use third-party logistics providers?**
A: Yes, we cover both in-house and outsourced distribution strategies.

**Q: How detailed should the comprehensive system be?**
A: Include implementation-ready detail with specific routes, equipment, procedures, and costs.

**Q: How is the final grade calculated?**
A: Sum of all assignment points (max 110). You need 88+ points (80%) to pass.
