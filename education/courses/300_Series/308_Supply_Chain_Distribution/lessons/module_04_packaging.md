## Packaging Materials

### Plastic Clamshells

**Description:** Hinged plastic containers with snap closure

**Types:**
- PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate): Clear, rigid, recyclable
- OPS (Oriented Polystyrene): Crystal clear, good clarity
- PLA (Polylactic Acid): Compostable, plant-based

```
CLAMSHELL SPECIFICATIONS

Standard Sizes:
├─ 4 oz: Microgreens, baby greens
├─ 5 oz: Spring mix, herbs
├─ 8 oz: Salad greens, cooking greens
├─ 16 oz: Family-size salads
└─ 32 oz: Bulk greens

Features:
✓ Product visibility
✓ Crush protection
✓ Humidity retention (90-95%)
✓ Stackable
✓ Tamper-evident
```

**Pros:**
- Excellent product visibility
- Good protection from handling damage
- Maintains humidity
- Professional appearance
- Shelf-stable (no refrigeration needed for short periods)

**Cons:**
- Higher cost ($0.25-0.75 per unit)
- Plastic waste concern
- Not all are recyclable
- Bulky for transport

**Best for:**
- Retail sales (grocery stores)
- Farmers markets
- Microgreens and delicate herbs
- Premium positioning

**Cost:** $0.25-0.75 per clamshell depending on size and material

### Perforated Bags

**Description:** Plastic bags with micro-perforations for gas exchange

**Types:**
- LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene): Flexible, economical
- HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene): Stronger, more rigid
- Biodegradable options: Higher cost, limited availability

```
BAG PERFORATION DESIGN

Perforation Patterns:
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●     │
│  ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●     │
│                             │
│     Product Area            │
│                             │
│  ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●     │
│  ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●     │
└─────────────────────────────┘

Function:
- Gas exchange (O₂, CO₂, ethylene)
- Moisture balance
- Prevent condensation
- Extend shelf life

Perforation density:
- Low respiration crops: Fewer holes
- High respiration crops: More holes
```

**Pros:**
- Lower cost ($0.05-0.15 per bag)
- Moisture retention
- Modified atmosphere
- Lightweight
- Various sizes available

**Cons:**
- Less protection than clamshells
- Can fog with condensation
- May tear during handling
- Limited product visibility

**Best for:**
- Leafy greens (lettuce, kale, spinach)
- Herbs (cilantro, parsley)
- Wholesale/restaurant sales
- Cost-conscious operations

**Cost:** $0.05-0.15 per bag

### Cardboard Boxes

**Description:** Corrugated fiberboard containers

**Types:**
- Wax-coated: Water-resistant, traditional
- Unwaxed: Recyclable, gaining popularity
- Custom printed: Brand building

```
BOX CONSTRUCTION

Standard Corrugated Box:
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│   12" × 18" × 9" (Standard) │
│                             │
│   Features:                 │
│   - Ventilation holes       │
│   - Hand holes for carrying │
│   - Stackable design        │
│   - Printing area           │
│                             │
│   Capacity: 20-30 lbs       │
└─────────────────────────────┘

Flute Types:
A-flute: Thick, cushioning (1/4")
B-flute: Medium (1/8")
C-flute: Most common (11/64")
E-flute: Thin, printable (1/16")
```

**Pros:**
- Cost-effective ($0.50-2.00 per box)
- Recyclable/compostable
- Good stacking strength
- Customizable printing
- Industry standard

**Cons:**
- Moisture sensitivity (unless waxed)
- Less product visibility
- Can absorb odors
- Bulky when empty

**Best for:**
- Bulk/wholesale distribution
- Transport packaging
- Restaurant delivery
- Secondary packaging

**Cost:** $0.50-2.00 per box depending on size and printing

### Reusable Containers

**Description:** Durable plastic bins and crates for multiple uses

**Types:**
- Collapsible bins: Space-efficient when empty
- Solid wall crates: Maximum strength
- Vented crates: Airflow for field packing

```
REUSABLE CONTAINER SYSTEM

Purchase Model:
┌──────────────────────────┐
│  Initial Investment      │
│  $8-15 per container     │
│                          │
│  Expected Life:          │
│  100-500 uses            │
│                          │
│  Cost per use:           │
│  $0.02-0.15              │
└──────────────────────────┘

Rental/Pool Model:
┌──────────────────────────┐
│  Rental Fee:             │
│  $0.50-1.50 per trip     │
│                          │
│  Deposit:                │
│  $5-10 per container     │
│                          │
│  Return System:          │
│  Coordinated logistics   │
└──────────────────────────┘
```

**Pros:**
- Lowest long-term cost
- Most sustainable option
- Excellent protection
- Stackable and nestable
- Washable/sanitizable

**Cons:**
- High upfront investment
- Requires tracking system
- Customer must return
- Storage space when empty
- Washing/sanitizing labor

**Best for:**
- Restaurant direct sales
- CSA programs
- Local market routes
- High-volume accounts

**Cost:** $8-15 initial, $0.02-0.15 per use

### Specialty Packaging

**Breathable Films:**
- Laser-perforated
- Selective gas transmission
- Premium pricing
- Extended shelf life

**Stand-Up Pouches:**
- Consumer-friendly
- Resealable options
- Good for herbs, microgreens
- $0.30-0.80 per pouch

**Compostable Packaging:**
- PLA clamshells
- Cellulose bags
- Plant-based materials
- 20-50% higher cost

## Packaging by Distribution Channel

### Farmers Market Packaging

**Requirements:**
- Eye-catching presentation
- Easy customer handling
- Impulse purchase appeal
- Brand visibility

**Recommended:**
```
FARMERS MARKET PACKAGING MIX

Option A: Premium Positioning
├─ Clamshells for all products
├─ Custom labels with farm branding
├─ Professional appearance
├─ Price: Premium (+20-30%)
└─ Cost per package: $0.40-0.80

Option B: Value Positioning
├─ Perforated bags for greens
├─ Clamshells for microgreens/herbs
├─ Simple labeling
├─ Price: Market rate
└─ Cost per package: $0.15-0.35

Option C: Bulk/Low-Waste
├─ Customer brings containers
├─ Weigh and sell by pound
├─ Minimal packaging
├─ Price: Discount for no packaging
└─ Cost per package: $0.05 (bag only)
```

**Best Practices:**
- Pre-package popular sizes
- Offer bulk options
- Prominent farm branding
- Clear product labels
- Easy-to-carry designs

### Restaurant Direct Packaging

**Requirements:**
- Bulk sizing for kitchen use
- Minimal packaging waste
- Freshness preservation
- Easy identification

**Recommended:**
```
RESTAURANT PACKAGING STRATEGY

Volume Accounts (>25 lbs/week):
├─ Reusable bins (deposit system)
├─ Product in sealed bags inside bins
├─ Labeled with harvest date and lot
├─ Return empty bins on next delivery
└─ Cost: $0.10-0.20 per pound delivered

Smaller Accounts (<25 lbs/week):
├─ Cardboard boxes
├─ Perforated bags inside
├─ Disposable packaging
├─ No return logistics
└─ Cost: $0.25-0.40 per pound delivered

Premium/White Tablecloth:
├─ Clamshells or small boxes
├─ Individual portion sizes
├─ Premium presentation
├─ Branded packaging
└─ Cost: $0.40-0.75 per unit
```

## Sustainable Packaging Options

### Environmental Impact Comparison

| Material | Production Impact | Recyclability | Compostability | Cost Premium |
|----------|------------------|---------------|----------------|--------------|
| Virgin plastic (PET) | High | Yes (limited) | No | Baseline |
| Recycled PET | Medium | Yes | No | +10-20% |
| PLA (corn-based) | Medium | No | Yes (commercial) | +30-50% |
| Cellulose film | Low | Limited | Yes | +40-60% |
| Paper/cardboard | Low | Yes | Yes | -10 to +20% |
| Reusable plastic | High initial | Yes | No | -50% long-term |

### Compostable Packaging

**Certification Standards:**
- ASTM D6400 (composting standard)
- BPI (Biodegradable Products Institute)
- Cedar Grove (actual composting facility testing)

**Common Materials:**
```
COMPOSTABLE OPTIONS

PLA Clamshells:
├─ Made from corn/sugarcane
├─ Clear, similar to PET
├─ Requires commercial composting (140°F+)
├─ NOT home compostable
├─ Cost: +30-50% vs. conventional
└─ Best for: Retail, premium markets

Cellulose Bags:
├─ Made from wood pulp
├─ Breathable, moisture-resistant
├─ Home compostable (60-90 days)
├─ Lower clarity than plastic
├─ Cost: +40-60% vs. conventional
└─ Best for: Eco-conscious customers

Molded Fiber:
├─ Recycled paper pulp
├─ Egg-carton style
├─ Fully compostable
├─ Limited moisture resistance
├─ Cost: +20-40% vs. conventional
└─ Best for: Dry products, short retail time
```

### Reducing Packaging Waste

**Strategies:**

1. **Right-Sizing**
   - Match package size to product volume
   - Eliminate excess material
   - Reduce shipping weight

2. **Reusable Systems**
   - Customer deposit programs
   - Delivery bin returns
   - Washable containers

3. **Minimal Packaging**
   - Bulk sales
   - Customer-provided containers
   - Band/sticker labels only

4. **Packaging Take-Back**
   - Collect and reuse clean packaging
   - Recycle collected materials
   - Customer incentives for returns

**Example Program:**
```
REUSABLE CLAMSHELL PROGRAM

Customer Purchase:
├─ Initial purchase includes $1 deposit
├─ Customer returns clean clamshell next visit
├─ Receives $1 credit toward next purchase
└─ Or keeps clamshell, no credit

Farm Process:
├─ Collect returned clamshells
├─ Wash and sanitize (commercial dishwasher)
├─ Inspect for damage
├─ Reuse up to 20 times
└─ Recycle damaged units

Economics:
Initial clamshell cost: $0.60
Washing cost per cycle: $0.08
Average reuses: 15 times
Cost per use: $0.10 (vs. $0.60 single-use)
Savings: 83% over single-use
```

### Organic Labeling

**USDA Organic Requirements:**

If USDA Certified Organic:
```
Required Elements:
□ USDA Organic seal (if >95% organic ingredients)
□ Certifier name and address
□ "Certified Organic by [Certifier]"
□ Can state "100% Organic" if applicable

Prohibited:
□ Cannot use term "organic" without certification
□ Cannot imply organic without certification
□ Must follow specific label placement rules
```

### Traceability Labeling

**FSMA Traceability Rule (Foods on Traceability List):**

```
REQUIRED TRACEABILITY DATA

Lot Code System:
└─ Product code + Date + Batch

Example: "LT120825-A"
├─ LT = Lettuce
├─ 120825 = December 8, 2025
└─ A = First batch of day

Information to Track:
□ Harvest location (field ID)
□ Harvest date
□ Pack date
□ Cooling date/time
□ Customer/destination
□ Quantity shipped
□ Lot code on every package
```

## Packaging Cost Analysis

### Cost Breakdown Example

**Mid-Size Farm (2,000 lbs/week, retail packaging):**

```
WEEKLY PACKAGING COSTS

Product Mix:
├─ Lettuce (50%): 1,000 lbs → 125 8oz clamshells
├─ Greens (30%): 600 lbs → 75 8oz clamshells
├─ Herbs (15%): 300 lbs → 60 4oz clamshells
└─ Microgreens (5%): 100 lbs → 25 4oz clamshells

Packaging Costs:
├─ 8oz clamshells: 200 × $0.45 = $90
├─ 4oz clamshells: 85 × $0.35 = $30
├─ Labels: 285 × $0.05 = $14
└─ TOTAL WEEKLY: $134

Annual Packaging Cost: $6,968
Cost per pound: $0.07
Percentage of revenue (avg $5/lb): 1.4%

Alternative: Perforated Bags
├─ All products in bags: 285 × $0.10 = $29/week
├─ Annual savings: $5,460
└─ Trade-off: Lower presentation value
```

### ROI of Premium Packaging

**Case Study:**

```
SCENARIO COMPARISON

Option A: Basic Packaging (Bags)
├─ Cost per unit: $0.10
├─ Selling price: $4.50/lb (market rate)
├─ Sales volume: 1,500 lbs/week
├─ Weekly revenue: $6,750
├─ Packaging cost: $150/week
└─ Net revenue: $6,600/week

Option B: Premium Packaging (Clamshells)
├─ Cost per unit: $0.45
├─ Selling price: $5.50/lb (20% premium)
├─ Sales volume: 1,300 lbs/week (13% reduction)
├─ Weekly revenue: $7,150
├─ Packaging cost: $585/week
└─ Net revenue: $6,565/week

ANALYSIS:
- Premium packaging generates $450 more gross revenue
- But costs $435 more for packaging
- Net gain: Only $15/week
- Conclusion: Premium only works if no volume loss
               OR if margin gain exceeds volume loss
```

## Summary

Effective packaging balances:

1. **Protection**: Keep product fresh and damage-free
2. **Cost**: Optimize for margin and affordability
3. **Sustainability**: Minimize environmental impact
4. **Branding**: Differentiate and build customer loyalty
5. **Compliance**: Meet all regulatory requirements

**Key Takeaways:**
- Match packaging to channel needs
- Consider total cost, not just purchase price
- Sustainable packaging can build customer loyalty
- Test before committing to large orders
- Labels are a marketing opportunity

Right packaging enhances product value, reduces waste, and builds brand recognition in a competitive market.
