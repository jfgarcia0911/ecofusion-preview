## Quick Decision Chart

### By Temperature Zone

```
ZONE 1: COLD (32-36°F)
Use for: Most leafy greens, herbs (except basil),
         brassicas, root vegetables
Humidity: 95-100% for most

ZONE 2: COOL (45-55°F)
Use for: Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, eggplant,
         summer squash, basil
Humidity: 85-95%
CRITICAL: Do not mix with Zone 1 crops

ZONE 3: MODERATE (55-65°F)
Use for: Winter squash, sweet potatoes (after curing)
Humidity: 50-85%
```

### By Chilling Sensitivity

**CHILLING-SENSITIVE (DO NOT store below 45-50°F):**
- Basil
- Tomatoes (all stages)
- Peppers
- Cucumbers
- Eggplant
- Summer squash
- Sweet potatoes

**SYMPTOM OF CHILLING INJURY:**
- Surface pitting
- Internal browning
- Increased decay
- Failure to ripen (tomatoes)
- Water-soaked appearance
- Off-flavors

## Respiration Rate Reference

**EXTREMELY HIGH (>100 mg CO₂/kg·hr @ 41°F):**
- Asparagus, mushrooms, sweet corn
- Management: Sell immediately, aggressive cooling

**VERY HIGH (60-100 mg CO₂/kg·hr):**
- Basil, cilantro, arugula, microgreens
- Management: 5-10 day shelf life max, rapid turnover

**HIGH (40-60 mg CO₂/kg·hr):**
- Spinach, kale, green beans
- Management: 7-14 day shelf life, good cooling critical

**MODERATE (20-40 mg CO₂/kg·hr):**
- Lettuce, broccoli, celery
- Management: 10-21 day shelf life, maintain cold chain

**LOW (10-20 mg CO₂/kg·hr):**
- Peppers, cucumbers, carrots
- Management: 14-30 day shelf life

**VERY LOW (<10 mg CO₂/kg·hr):**
- Onions, garlic, winter squash, potatoes
- Management: 30-180 day shelf life

## Ethylene Sensitivity

**VERY SENSITIVE (avoid ethylene exposure):**
- Lettuce (russet spotting)
- Cucumbers (yellowing)
- Basil (blackening, leaf drop)
- Kale (yellowing)
- Broccoli (yellowing)

**MODERATELY SENSITIVE:**
- Peppers (color change)
- Tomatoes (premature ripening)
- Herbs (yellowing)

**LOW SENSITIVITY:**
- Onions
- Garlic
- Root vegetables

**ETHYLENE PRODUCERS (keep separate):**
- Ripe tomatoes (high)
- Apples, pears (high)
- Melons (moderate)
- Cucumbers (moderate)

## Temperature Abuse Impact

**Every hour at room temperature (70°F) costs approximately 4-6 hours of refrigerated shelf life**

Example: Lettuce left at room temp for 3 hours = Loss of 12-18 hours refrigerated shelf life

**Q₁₀ Rule:** Respiration rate doubles with every 18°F increase

Example:
- Lettuce @ 34°F: 25 mg CO₂/kg·hr (baseline)
- Lettuce @ 52°F: 50 mg CO₂/kg·hr (2x respiration)
- Lettuce @ 70°F: 100 mg CO₂/kg·hr (4x respiration)

**Impact:** 4x faster quality degradation at room temperature

## Emergency Quick Reference

**CROP NOT LISTED?**
1. **Leafy/green crop:** Start with 32-36°F, 95-100% RH
2. **Fruiting vegetable:** Start with 45-55°F, 85-95% RH, check chilling sensitivity
3. **Root vegetable:** Start with 32-36°F, 95-100% RH
4. **If in doubt:** Use 38-40°F as safe middle ground, monitor for chilling injury

**TEMP OUT OF RANGE?**
- Too cold (<32°F): Risk of freezing injury
- Slightly warm (40-45°F): Acceptable short-term (<24 hrs) for most crops
- Above 45°F: Rapid quality loss for cold-requiring crops
- Above 55°F: Only chilling-sensitive crops tolerate
