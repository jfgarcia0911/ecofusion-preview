## Key Distribution Cost Formulas

### 1. Cost Per Pound Delivered

```
Cost Per Pound = Total Distribution Costs ÷ Total Pounds Delivered

Example:
Weekly costs: $800
Pounds delivered: 1,500 lbs
Cost per pound: $800 ÷ 1,500 = $0.53/lb
```

**Benchmark:** Target <$0.50/lb for local distribution

---

### 2. Cost Per Mile

```
Cost Per Mile = Total Vehicle Costs ÷ Total Miles Driven

Example:
Fuel: $60
Vehicle depreciation: $40
Insurance (weekly): $25
Maintenance: $15
Total: $140
Miles driven: 200
Cost per mile: $140 ÷ 200 = $0.70/mile
```

**Benchmark:** $0.50-$0.75/mile for refrigerated van

---

### 3. Cost Per Delivery Stop

```
Cost Per Stop = Total Delivery Costs ÷ Number of Stops

Example:
Total weekly delivery costs: $800
Number of stops: 35
Cost per stop: $800 ÷ 35 = $22.86/stop
```

**Benchmark:** $15-$25/stop for cluster routing

---

### 4. Delivery Cost as Percentage of Revenue

```
Delivery % = (Total Delivery Costs ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

Example:
Delivery costs: $800/week
Revenue: $9,000/week
Delivery %: ($800 ÷ $9,000) × 100 = 8.9%
```

**Benchmark:** 5-10% for profitable operations

## Labor Cost Calculations

### Driver/Delivery Labor

```
Labor Cost Per Delivery = (Hours × Hourly Rate) + Benefits

Example:
Delivery route: 4 hours
Hourly rate: $18/hour
Benefits/taxes (25%): $18 × 1.25 = $22.50/hour
Total labor cost: 4 hours × $22.50 = $90

Deliveries made: 12 stops
Labor cost per stop: $90 ÷ 12 = $7.50/stop
```

## Packaging & Materials Costs

### Cost Per Unit Packaged

```
Packaging Cost = (Container + Label + Materials) ÷ Units

Example - Clamshell Lettuce:
Clamshell: $0.35
Label: $0.05
Elastic band: $0.02
Total: $0.42 per unit

Price: $4.00
Packaging %: ($0.42 ÷ $4.00) × 100 = 10.5%
```

**Benchmark:** Keep packaging costs <15% of retail price

## Route Optimization Calculations

### Delivery Efficiency Metrics

#### Stops Per Hour

```
Stops Per Hour = Total Stops ÷ Total Hours

Example:
Stops: 12
Time: 4 hours
Stops/hour: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 stops/hour
```

**Benchmark:** 3-4 stops/hour for local routes

#### Miles Per Stop

```
Miles Per Stop = Total Miles ÷ Number of Stops

Example:
Route miles: 45 miles
Stops: 12
Miles/stop: 45 ÷ 12 = 3.75 miles/stop
```

**Benchmark:** <5 miles/stop with cluster routing

**After Optimization:**

## Minimum Order Calculations

### Break-Even Minimum Order

```
Minimum Order = Delivery Cost ÷ Target Gross Margin %

Example:
Delivery cost per stop: $22
Target margin: 40%
Minimum order: $22 ÷ 0.40 = $55
```

### Tiered Delivery Fee Structure

| Order Size | Delivery Fee | Calculation |
|------------|--------------|-------------|
| <$50 | $15 | Discourages small orders |
| $50-$99 | $10 | Partial subsidy |
| $100-$199 | $5 | Incentive to grow order |
| $200+ | FREE | Reward for large orders |

**Impact Example:**
- Average order without minimums: $65
- Average order with $100 free delivery: $115 (+77%)
- Delivery cost absorbed by increased margin

```
Fuel Cost = (Miles ÷ MPG) × Price Per Gallon

Example:
```

## Volume Breakpoint Analysis

### When to Upgrade Distribution

#### From Personal Vehicle to Cargo Van

```
Current capacity: 100 lbs/week
Current cost: $0.25/mile × 50 miles = $12.50 + 3 hrs labor = $67
Cost per lb: $67 ÷ 100 = $0.67/lb

Cargo van capacity: 500 lbs/week
Cargo van cost: $0.45/mile × 50 miles = $22.50 + 3 hrs labor = $90
Cost per lb: $90 ÷ 500 = $0.18/lb

BREAKPOINT: ~250 lbs/week (cost per lb equalizes)
```

#### From Self-Delivery to 3PL

```
Self-delivery (refrigerated van):
Fixed costs: $150/week (vehicle)
Variable costs: $0.30/mile + $22.50/hour labor
Weekly volume: 1,500 lbs, 200 miles, 16 hours
Total cost: $150 + $60 + $360 = $570
Cost per lb: $0.38/lb

3PL service:
Fee: 20% of revenue
Average price: $5.00/lb
Revenue: 1,500 lbs × $5 = $7,500
3PL cost: $7,500 × 0.20 = $1,500
Cost per lb: $1.00/lb

DECISION: Self-delivery more cost-effective up to ~3,000 lbs/week
```

## Inventory Carrying Costs

### Cost to Hold Inventory

```
Carrying Cost = (Average Inventory Value × Carrying Rate %) ÷ Turnover

Example:
Average inventory: $2,000
Carrying rate: 25%/year (spoilage, storage, opportunity cost)
Turnover: 52 times/year (weekly)
Annual carrying cost: $2,000 × 0.25 = $500
Weekly cost: $500 ÷ 52 = $9.62/week
```

### Carrying Cost Components

| Cost Type | % of Inventory Value | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| **Spoilage/Shrink** | 10-15% | Highest for perishables |
| **Storage (cold)** | 5-8% | Utilities, maintenance |
| **Opportunity Cost** | 5-10% | Capital tied up |
| **Handling** | 3-5% | Labor to manage |
| **TOTAL** | **23-38%** | Annual carrying rate |

## Delivery Pricing Strategies

### Strategy 1: Flat Fee Per Delivery

```
Flat fee: $25/delivery
Works when: Orders are consistent size
Pros: Simple, predictable
Cons: May subsidize small orders, penalize large
```

### Strategy 2: Percentage of Order

```
Delivery fee: 10% of order value
Minimum: $10
Works when: Variable order sizes
Pros: Scales with order
Cons: Can discourage large orders
```

### Strategy 3: Free Delivery Minimum

```
Free delivery: Orders $150+
Fee below minimum: $20
Works when: Want to encourage larger orders
Pros: Incentivizes growth
Cons: May lose small customers
```

### Strategy 4: Distance-Based

```
Zone 1 (0-5 miles): $10
Zone 2 (5-10 miles): $15
Zone 3 (10-20 miles): $25
Works when: Geographic spread
Pros: Fair cost allocation
Cons: Complex to communicate
```

## Cost Reduction Strategies

1. **Cluster Routing:** Group deliveries by area to reduce miles
2. **Minimum Orders:** Set minimums to ensure delivery profitability
3. **Backhaul:** Find return loads to offset costs
4. **Optimize Packaging:** Balance protection with cost
5. **Right-Size Vehicle:** Don't over-invest in capacity
6. **Aggregate Deliveries:** Combine multiple farms' orders
7. **Technology:** Use route optimization software
8. **Schedule Efficiency:** Deliver when restaurants/retailers receive
9. **Fuel Management:** Monitor and improve MPG
10. **Labor Efficiency:** Cross-train staff for multiple roles
