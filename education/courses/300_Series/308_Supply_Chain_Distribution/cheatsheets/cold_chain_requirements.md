## Temperature Zones Quick Reference

### Zone 1: Cold (32-36°F / 0-2°C)
**Products:** Most leafy greens, herbs (except basil), root vegetables, microgreens
**Priority:** Highest for perishable items
**Equipment:** Walk-in cooler, reach-in refrigerator

### Zone 2: Cool (45-55°F / 7-13°C)
**Products:** Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, eggplant, basil
**Priority:** Critical to prevent chilling injury
**Equipment:** Temperature-controlled room or designated cooler section

### Zone 3: Ambient (55-70°F / 13-21°C)
**Products:** Ripe tomatoes, winter squash, cured onions
**Priority:** Short-term staging only
**Equipment:** Climate-controlled packing area

## Temperature Monitoring Requirements

### Documentation Frequency

| Storage Type | Check Frequency | Recording Method | Alert Threshold |
|--------------|----------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Cold Storage** | Continuous (automated) | Digital data logger | ±3°F variance |
| **Transport** | Every 30 minutes | Manual or GPS logger | Above 41°F |
| **Receiving** | Every delivery | Infrared thermometer | Product >41°F |
| **Packing Area** | Every 2 hours | Wall thermometer | Above 55°F |

### Critical Temperature Alerts

| Alert Level | Temperature | Action Required | Timeline |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Critical** | >45°F (leafy greens) | Immediate response | <15 minutes |
| **Warning** | 38-45°F (leafy greens) | Investigate and adjust | <1 hour |
| **Chilling Risk** | <45°F (basil, tomatoes) | Move to warmer zone | <30 minutes |

## Q10 Rule - Respiration & Temperature

### Shelf Life Impact of Temperature

| Temperature Increase | Respiration Rate Change | Shelf Life Impact |
|---------------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| +10°C (18°F) | 2x (doubles) | Cut in half |
| +20°C (36°F) | 4x (quadruples) | Reduced to 1/4 |
| +30°C (54°F) | 8x | Reduced to 1/8 |

### Practical Example: Lettuce at Different Temperatures

| Storage Temp | Respiration Rate | Expected Shelf Life |
|--------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 32°F (0°C) | 1x (baseline) | 14-21 days |
| 41°F (5°C) | 1.5x | 9-14 days |
| 50°F (10°C) | 2x | 7-10 days |
| 68°F (20°C) | 4x | 3-5 days |
| 86°F (30°C) | 8x | 1-2 days |

## Cold Chain Break Limits

### Maximum Allowable Time Out of Temperature

| Product Category | Max Break Time | Temperature During Break | Recovery Action |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| **Leafy Greens** | 15 minutes | <60°F ambient | Resume cooling immediately |
| **Herbs** | 15 minutes | <60°F ambient | Resume cooling immediately |
| **Microgreens** | 10 minutes | <55°F ambient | Resume cooling immediately |
| **Root Vegetables** | 30 minutes | <70°F ambient | Resume cooling within 1 hour |
| **Tomatoes/Peppers** | 60 minutes | <75°F ambient | Resume appropriate temp |

### Food Safety Danger Zone

**Temperature Range:** 41°F - 135°F (5°C - 57°C)
**Maximum Time Allowed:** 4 hours cumulative
**Action if Exceeded:** Product must be discarded (fresh-cut items)

## Packaging & Cold Chain

### Insulation Requirements for Transport

| Transit Time | Outside Temp | Insulation Needed | Ice Packs |
|--------------|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| <1 hour | <70°F | None (refrigerated vehicle) | No |
| 1-2 hours | <80°F | Insulated totes | Optional |
| 2-4 hours | <85°F | 1" foam coolers | Yes (2-4 packs) |
| 4-8 hours | <90°F | 2" foam coolers | Yes (4-6 packs) |
| 8-24 hours | Any | 2"+ insulated boxes | Yes (6+ packs) |

### Ice Pack Ratios

**General Rule:** 1 lb of ice per 3-4 lbs of produce for 24-hour transit

| Box Size | Product Weight | Ice Packs Needed | Pack Size |
|----------|----------------|------------------|-----------|
| Small (12x12x6") | 5-8 lbs | 2-3 packs | 16 oz each |
| Medium (16x12x9") | 10-15 lbs | 4-5 packs | 24 oz each |
| Large (20x16x12") | 20-30 lbs | 6-8 packs | 32 oz each |

## Emergency Protocols

### Cold Storage Failure Response

**Temperature >45°F for >30 minutes:**

1. **Immediate (0-15 min):**
   - Alert manager and staff
   - Stop new product intake
   - Call refrigeration service
   - Begin emergency cooling (ice, portable units)

2. **Short-term (15-60 min):**
   - Move product to backup cooler
   - Sort by priority (most perishable first)
   - Document temperatures and timeline
   - Contact customers for critical orders

3. **Recovery (1-4 hours):**
   - Quality assessment of all product
   - Discard items >41°F for >4 hours
   - Update inventory
   - Document incident and corrective actions

### Vehicle Refrigeration Failure

**Temperature >45°F during delivery:**

1. Transfer product to insulated coolers with ice
2. Complete deliveries within 2 hours
3. Check product temperatures upon delivery
4. Notify customers of cold chain break
5. Document incident
6. Repair vehicle before next use

## Calibration & Maintenance

### Equipment Calibration Schedule

| Equipment | Calibration Frequency | Method | Tolerance |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|-----------|
| **Digital Thermometers** | Monthly | Ice bath (32°F) | ±1°F |
| **Data Loggers** | Quarterly | Reference thermometer | ±0.5°F |
| **Infrared Thermometers** | Monthly | Ice bath verification | ±2°F |
| **Wall Thermometers** | Quarterly | Digital reference | ±2°F |

### Maintenance Checklist

**Weekly:**
- Clean temperature sensors
- Check door seals
- Verify alarm functionality
- Review temperature logs

**Monthly:**
- Calibrate thermometers
- Clean condenser coils
- Check refrigerant levels
- Test backup systems

**Quarterly:**
- Professional HVAC service
- Replace air filters
- Verify temperature uniformity
- Update documentation
