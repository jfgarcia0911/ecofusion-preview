## Developing Your Excellence Roadmap

### The 3-Year Transformation Plan

**Year 1: Build the Foundation**

**Q1: Assessment & Planning**
```
□ Conduct facility assessment (all modules)
□ Identify critical gaps
□ Form steering committee
□ Develop detailed roadmap

□ Launch communication campaign
```

```
□ Implement 5S program (all areas)
□ Develop core SOPs
□ Establish safety programs (JSAs, training)
□ Launch leader standard work
□ Create visual management boards
□ Begin daily tier meetings
```

**Q3: Stability Systems**
```
□ Implement preventive maintenance program
□ Launch autonomous maintenance
□ Establish quality control processes
□ Deploy basic KPI tracking
□ Conduct operator training
□ First improvement projects
```

**Q4: Standardization**
```
□ Complete SOP documentation

□ Launch quality audits
□ First benchmarking study

```

```
□ Deploy advanced KPIs (balanced scorecard)

□ First kaizen events

```

```
   (Module 7 - Performance Metrics)

   (Module 6 - Continuous Improvement)

   (Module 10 - Maintenance Management)

   (Module 13 - Change Management)

    (Module 12 - Cost Management)
```

```
□ I will model the behaviors I expect

```

```
Improvement: 20%

└─ Project reviews

Strategy & Planning: 10%
```

## Sustaining Excellence

### The Never-Ending Journey

**Excellence is not a destination:**
- Competitors improve
- Markets change
- Technology advances
- Best practices evolve
- Complacency kills excellence

**Sustaining Mechanisms:**

**1. Leadership Commitment**

- Resources protected even in tough times

**2. Systems and Processes**
- Standards updated continuously
- Training program robust
- Metrics visible and reviewed
- Improvement process embedded

**3. Culture and Mindset**
- Never satisfied, always improving
- Benchmark continuously
- Share and learn openly
- Pride in excellence

**4. Recognition and Reinforcement**
- Excellence criteria in performance reviews
- Promotions favor improvement leaders
- Success stories celebrated
- Industry engagement encouraged
