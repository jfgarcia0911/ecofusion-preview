# Grading Summary
## Course 310: Facility Management Excellence

---

## Course Assessment Overview

This document provides a comprehensive summary of all graded assessments for Course 310: Facility Management Excellence. Students should use this as a roadmap to understand expectations and plan their work throughout the course.

---

## Total Points and Grading Scale

| Component | Points Available |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Assignment 1: Facility Audit and Assessment | 15 |
| Assignment 2: Maintenance Management System | 20 |
| Assignment 3: Safety and Compliance Program | 25 |
| Assignment 4: Team Management and Training | 20 |
| Assignment 5: Complete Facility Operations Plan | 30 |
| **TOTAL COURSE POINTS** | **110** |

### Required for Passing
- **Minimum Score:** 88 points (80%)
- **Certificate Awarded:** Certificate of Completion upon achieving 80% or higher

---

## Assessment Schedule

### Week 1-2: Early Course
**Assignment 1: Facility Audit and Assessment**
- **Due:** End of Module 1
- **Points:** 15
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes
- **Focus:** Conduct comprehensive facility evaluation and identify improvement priorities

### Week 2-3: Mid-Course (Part 1)
**Assignment 2: Maintenance Management System**
- **Due:** End of Module 2
- **Points:** 20
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 20-30 minutes
- **Focus:** Design preventive maintenance and asset management systems

### Week 3-4: Mid-Course (Part 2)
**Assignment 3: Safety and Compliance Program**
- **Due:** End of Module 5
- **Points:** 25
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Focus:** Implement comprehensive safety and regulatory compliance systems

### Week 4-5: Late Course
**Assignment 4: Team Management and Training**
- **Due:** End of Module 7
- **Points:** 20
- **Type:** Take-home assignment
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Focus:** Develop team structure, training programs, and performance management

### Week 5-6: Final
**Assignment 5: Complete Facility Operations Plan**
- **Due:** End of Module 16
- **Points:** 30
- **Type:** Comprehensive project
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours
- **Focus:** Create complete facility management system with all integrated operations
