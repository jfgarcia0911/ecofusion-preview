## Questions

### 1. What is the primary principle of "Quality at the Source"?

A) Inspect quality only at final packaging
B) Build quality into processes and catch defects where they occur
C) Outsource quality control to third parties
D) Accept defects as unavoidable

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. In the quality hierarchy, which approach is most effective?

A) Detection (inspection after production)
B) Prevention (designing quality into processes)
C) Correction (fixing defects after discovery)
D) Acceptance (tolerating defects)

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. What does HACCP stand for in food safety management?

A) Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points
B) High Accuracy Crop Control Process
C) Hazardous Chemical Control Protocol
D) Health and Cleanliness Compliance Program

**Answer: A**

---

### 4. What is a Critical Control Point (CCP) in HACCP?

A) Any step in the process
B) A point where control is essential to prevent food safety hazards
C) The most expensive equipment
D) Employee break areas

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is the purpose of statistical process control (SPC)?

A) To eliminate all variation
B) To monitor processes and distinguish between normal and abnormal variation
C) To replace operator judgment
D) To increase complexity

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. In quality grading systems, what does "A-grade" typically indicate?

A) Minimum acceptable quality
B) Premium quality meeting all specifications
C) Rejected product
D) Sample for testing

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is the root cause analysis tool that asks "why" five times?

A) Pareto analysis
B) Fishbone diagram
C) 5 Whys method
D) Control charts

**Answer: C**

---

### 8. What should be done when a process consistently produces out-of-specification product?

A) Continue production and hope it improves
B) Stop production, identify root cause, and correct before resuming
C) Blame operators
D) Lower quality standards

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What is the purpose of lot traceability in CEA operations?

A) To track employee performance
B) To identify and isolate affected product in case of quality issues
C) To increase paperwork
D) To confuse customers

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. Which quality cost is typically highest when prevention is neglected?

A) Prevention costs
B) Appraisal costs
C) Internal failure costs
D) External failure costs (returns, complaints, recalls)

**Answer: D**

---

## Answer Key
