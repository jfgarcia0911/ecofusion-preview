## Questions

### 1. What does PDCA stand for in continuous improvement?

A) Plan, Design, Create, Assess
B) Plan, Do, Check, Act
C) Produce, Deliver, Count, Adjust
D) Prepare, Deploy, Control, Audit

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. In the PDCA cycle, what happens in the "Check" phase?

A) Write checks for expenses
B) Verify results against expected outcomes and analyze data
C) Inspect final products only
D) Check employee attendance

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. What is an A3 report in problem-solving?

A) A size of paper (11x17) used for one-page structured problem-solving
B) A three-page assessment
C) An annual report
D) A type of audit form

**Answer: A**

---

### 4. What should be the focus of improvement projects?

A) Making work more complicated
B) Data-driven problems with measurable impact
C) Random changes for the sake of change
D) Only addressing employee complaints

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. How often should improvement ideas be encouraged from frontline employees?

A) Once per year
B) Only during annual reviews
C) Continuously as part of daily culture
D) Never - management handles improvements

**Answer: C**

---

### 6. What is the purpose of a root cause analysis?

A) To find someone to blame
B) To identify underlying causes rather than just symptoms
C) To analyze plant roots
D) To delay taking action

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. In a fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram, what goes at the "head" of the fish?

A) The root cause
B) The solution
C) The problem or effect being analyzed
D) The person responsible

**Answer: C**

---

### 8. What is a key characteristic of successful improvement teams?

A) Only managers participate
B) Cross-functional membership with frontline involvement
C) Very large teams (20+ people)
D) No time limits on projects

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. After implementing an improvement, what is the most important next step?

A) Move immediately to the next project
B) Standardize the improvement and monitor for sustainability
C) Celebrate and forget about it
D) Keep it a secret

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. What is the "Kaizen event" approach?

A) Annual celebration party
B) Focused, short-term (3-5 day) intensive improvement project
C) Monthly management meeting
D) Emergency response drill

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Plan, Do, Check, Act
2. B - Verify results against expected outcomes and analyze data
3. A - A size of paper (11x17) used for one-page structured problem-solving
4. B - Data-driven problems with measurable impact
5. C - Continuously as part of daily culture
6. B - To identify underlying causes rather than just symptoms
7. C - The problem or effect being analyzed
8. B - Cross-functional membership with frontline involvement
9. B - Standardize the improvement and monitor for sustainability
10. B - Focused, short-term (3-5 day) intensive improvement project
