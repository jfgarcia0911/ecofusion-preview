## Questions

### 1. What percentage of effective communication is typically non-verbal?

A) 10-20%
B) 30-40%
C) 50-65%
D) 70-90%

**Answer: D**

---

### 2. What is "active listening"?

A) Listening while exercising
B) Fully concentrating, understanding, responding, and remembering what is said
C) Waiting for your turn to talk
D) Listening to loud music

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. When communicating process changes to your team, what is most important?

A) Send a single email and expect compliance
B) Explain the "why" behind the change, not just the "what"
C) Implement changes without explanation
D) Only tell supervisors and let them figure it out

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What is the purpose of visual management boards in communication?

A) To decorate the workplace
B) To provide at-a-glance information and shared understanding
C) To replace all verbal communication
D) To monitor employees

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. In shift handoff communication, what is critical to include?

A) Personal opinions only
B) Current status, issues, pending actions, and special attention items
C) Gossip about other shifts
D) Only problems, never successes

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. What is the "read-back" technique in critical communications?

A) Reading yesterday's reports
B) Receiver repeats back information to confirm understanding
C) Reading while walking backward
D) Reviewing old emails

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. When should you communicate bad news to leadership?

A) Never - hide all problems
B) Wait until it becomes a crisis
C) Early and directly with facts and proposed solutions
D) Blame someone else first

**Answer: C**

---

### 8. What is the most effective channel for complex or sensitive information?

A) Group text message
B) Face-to-face or video conversation
C) Office memo
D) Through the grapevine

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. How should technical information be communicated to non-technical stakeholders?

A) Use maximum technical jargon to sound smart
B) Avoid the topic entirely
C) Translate to plain language with relevant context and examples
D) Make them figure it out themselves

**Answer: C**

---

### 10. What is a communication "closed loop"?

A) A circular discussion that goes nowhere
B) Sender transmits, receiver confirms receipt and understanding, sender verifies
C) Keeping information within a small group
D) Repeating the same message endlessly

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. D - 70-90%
2. B - Fully concentrating, understanding, responding, and remembering what is said
3. B - Explain the "why" behind the change, not just the "what"
4. B - To provide at-a-glance information and shared understanding
5. B - Current status, issues, pending actions, and special attention items
6. B - Receiver repeats back information to confirm understanding
7. C - Early and directly with facts and proposed solutions
8. B - Face-to-face or video conversation
9. C - Translate to plain language with relevant context and examples
10. B - Sender transmits, receiver confirms receipt and understanding, sender verifies
