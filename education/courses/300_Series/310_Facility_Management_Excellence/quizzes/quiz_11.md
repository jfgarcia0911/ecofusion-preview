## Questions

### 1. What is the hierarchy of controls for workplace hazards (most to least effective)?

A) PPE, Engineering, Administrative, Elimination
B) Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE
C) Administrative, PPE, Elimination, Engineering
D) Training, Warning signs, PPE, Engineering

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. What does "LTI" stand for in safety metrics?

A) Long-Term Investment
B) Lost Time Injury
C) Laboratory Test Interval
D) Lighting Temperature Index

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. What is a "near miss" in safety management?

A) An accident that almost didn't happen
B) An incident that could have caused injury but didn't
C) A successful safety drill
D) Missing safety training

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. Why should near misses be reported and investigated?

A) To punish employees
B) They provide opportunities to prevent actual injuries
C) To create paperwork
D) They should not be reported

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is "lockout/tagout" (LOTO)?

A) Locking the facility at night
B) Procedures to control hazardous energy during equipment maintenance
C) Tagging damaged products
D) Security access control

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. When should safety training be conducted?

A) Only during initial hire
B) Once every 5 years
C) Initially, during refresher periods, and when procedures change
D) Only after an accident occurs

**Answer: C**

---

### 7. What is the purpose of a Job Safety Analysis (JSA)?

A) To evaluate employee performance
B) To break down job tasks and identify hazards for each step
C) To determine salary levels
D) To schedule work shifts

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. What PPE is typically required when handling concentrated nutrients?

A) No PPE needed
B) Just safety glasses
C) Gloves, safety glasses, and protective clothing
D) Full hazmat suit always

**Answer: C**

---

### 9. What is the first step when an injury occurs in the facility?

A) Complete incident report
B) Provide first aid and ensure scene safety
C) Call the media
D) Blame the employee

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. What does a "safety culture" mean?

A) Having a safety poster
B) Safety is valued, integrated into daily operations, and everyone's responsibility
C) Only the safety manager cares about safety
D) Compliance only when inspectors visit

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE
2. B - Lost Time Injury
3. B - An incident that could have caused injury but didn't
4. B - They provide opportunities to prevent actual injuries
5. B - Procedures to control hazardous energy during equipment maintenance
6. C - Initially, during refresher periods, and when procedures change
7. B - To break down job tasks and identify hazards for each step
8. C - Gloves, safety glasses, and protective clothing
9. B - Provide first aid and ensure scene safety
10. B - Safety is valued, integrated into daily operations, and everyone's responsibility
