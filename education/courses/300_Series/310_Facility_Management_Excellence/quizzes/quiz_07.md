## Questions

### 1. What does KPI stand for?

A) Key Performance Indicator
B) Knowledge Process Integration
C) Keep Producing Indefinitely
D) Kinetic Production Index

**Answer: A**

---

### 2. What are the four perspectives in a Balanced Scorecard?

A) Sales, Marketing, Production, Finance
B) Financial, Customer, Operations, People/Learning
C) Quality, Quantity, Cost, Delivery
D) Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. What is a leading indicator?

A) The most important metric
B) A predictive metric that provides early warning of future performance
C) Last month's results
D) The manager's favorite metric

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. Which is an example of a lagging indicator?

A) Employee training hours completed
B) Preventive maintenance completion rate
C) Monthly revenue or yield achieved
D) Current customer satisfaction survey

**Answer: C**

---

### 5. What is OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)?

A) Only Electrical Equipment
B) A composite metric measuring availability, performance, and quality
C) Organic Environmental Efficiency
D) Operational Expense Evaluation

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. For a CEA facility, which metric measures space productivity?

A) Employees per square foot
B) Pounds harvested per square foot per year
C) Visitors per month
D) Equipment count

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. How should KPIs be displayed in a facility?

A) Hidden in management offices
B) Only in computer databases
C) Visually on dashboards accessible to all relevant staff
D) Never displayed to avoid pressure

**Answer: C**

---

### 8. What is the recommended frequency for reviewing operational KPIs?

A) Once per year
B) Every quarter
C) Daily or weekly for critical metrics
D) Never - set and forget

**Answer: C**

---

### 9. What is a target range for gross margin in CEA operations?

A) 10-20%
B) 40-60%
C) 80-90%
D) 100%+

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. Which safety metric is the ultimate goal for any facility?

A) Acceptable level of minor injuries
B) Zero lost-time injuries (LTI)
C) Reducing incidents by 50%
D) Minimal incident reporting

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Key Performance Indicator
2. B - Financial, Customer, Operations, People/Learning
3. B - A predictive metric that provides early warning of future performance
4. C - Monthly revenue or yield achieved
5. B - A composite metric measuring availability, performance, and quality
6. B - Pounds harvested per square foot per year
7. C - Visually on dashboards accessible to all relevant staff
8. C - Daily or weekly for critical metrics
9. B - 40-60%
10. B - Zero lost-time injuries (LTI)
