## Questions

### 1. What is the primary goal of production planning in CEA facilities?

A) To maximize labor costs
B) To match production output with customer demand efficiently
C) To keep all equipment running 24/7
D) To produce as much as possible regardless of demand

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. What is "takt time" in production management?

A) Time to complete one harvest
B) The rate at which products must be produced to meet customer demand
C) Total operating hours per day
D) Equipment maintenance time

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. In CEA production, what does "cycle time" measure?

A) Marketing cycle duration
B) Time from seed to harvest for one crop batch
C) Employee shift rotation
D) Equipment replacement interval

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What is a key benefit of production leveling (Heijunka)?

A) Increases waste and variability
B) Reduces predictability
C) Smooths workload and reduces peaks/valleys in demand
D) Eliminates the need for planning

**Answer: C**

---

### 5. What is the purpose of a production control board?

A) To discipline underperforming employees
B) To visualize production status, targets, and issues in real-time
C) To replace computer systems
D) To decorate the facility

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. In batch production planning, what should determine batch sizes?

A) Maximum equipment capacity only
B) Balance between customer demand, crop cycles, and labor efficiency
C) Largest possible batches always
D) Random selection

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is a production bottleneck?

A) A type of plant disease
B) The constraint that limits overall system throughput
C) A water storage container
D) A safety feature

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. How should production priorities be established when multiple crops are grown?

A) First-come, first-served basis
B) Based on customer commitments, profitability, and resource availability
C) Always prioritize the fastest-growing crops
D) Random assignment

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What is the purpose of daily production meetings (huddles)?

A) To socialize with coworkers
B) To review yesterday's results, today's plan, and address issues
C) To extend the workday
D) To replace written communication

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. What is "first-time yield" in production management?

A) The first harvest from a new facility
B) Percentage of production that meets quality standards without rework
C) Initial investment returns
D) First quarter financial results

**Answer: B**

---
