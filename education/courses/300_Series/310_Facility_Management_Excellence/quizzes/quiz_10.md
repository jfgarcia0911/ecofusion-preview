## Questions

### 1. What is the primary difference between preventive and reactive maintenance?

A) Cost - reactive is always cheaper
B) Preventive is scheduled before failure; reactive responds after failure
C) No difference - both are the same
D) Preventive is only for new equipment

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. What does TPM stand for in maintenance management?

A) Total Profit Maximization
B) Technical Performance Monitoring
C) Total Productive Maintenance
D) Temporary Preventive Measures

**Answer: C**

---

### 3. What is "autonomous maintenance"?

A) Robots performing maintenance
B) Operators performing basic maintenance on their equipment
C) Unsupervised maintenance work
D) Self-healing equipment

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What is the goal of predictive maintenance?

A) Guessing when equipment will fail
B) Using data and condition monitoring to predict failures before they occur
C) Replacing all equipment annually
D) Ignoring maintenance until failure

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. How should preventive maintenance schedules be determined?

A) Random intervals
B) Only when convenient
C) Based on manufacturer recommendations, operating hours, and historical data
D) Never schedule - wait for breakdowns

**Answer: C**

---

### 6. What is MTBF in maintenance metrics?

A) Main Tank Bacteria Filtration
B) Mean Time Between Failures
C) Monthly Total Budget for Facilities
D) Mechanical Testing Before Function

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is the primary purpose of a CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System)?

A) To replace maintenance technicians
B) To track, schedule, and manage maintenance activities and assets
C) To play computer games
D) To monitor competitors

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. Which maintenance task should be performed daily on CEA pumps?

A) Complete rebuild
B) Visual inspection and verification of operation
C) Replacement
D) Nothing - monthly is sufficient

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What is a critical component of a maintenance spare parts strategy?

A) Order everything possible
B) Keep zero inventory to save money
C) Stock critical parts based on failure risk and lead time
D) Only order after failure occurs

**Answer: C**

---

### 10. What should be done after completing any maintenance work?

A) Move to the next task immediately
B) Document the work, verify operation, and update records
C) Take a break and forget about it
D) Wait for the next failure

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

3. B - Operators performing basic maintenance on their equipment
4. B - Using data and condition monitoring to predict failures before they occur
