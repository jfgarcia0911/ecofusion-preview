## Questions

### 1. What are the main categories of operating costs in a CEA facility?

A) Labor, Energy, Inputs (seeds/nutrients), Packaging, Overhead
B) Marketing, Sales, Distribution only
C) Rent, Utilities, Insurance only
D) Equipment purchases only

**Answer: A**

---

### 2. What is typically the largest operating cost in a vertical farm?

A) Seeds
B) Labor
C) Energy (electricity)
D) Packaging

**Answer: C**

---

### 3. What is the purpose of a cost-per-pound metric?

A) To weigh products
B) To compare efficiency and profitability across different crops and time periods
C) To price products
D) To measure employee weight

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What is a fixed cost?

A) Costs that change with production volume
B) Costs that stay constant regardless of production level
C) Costs that are frozen
D) Repair costs only

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is a variable cost?

A) Costs that never change
B) Costs that vary with production volume
C) Unpredictable costs
D) Optional costs

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. How can energy costs be reduced in CEA facilities?

A) Stop all production
B) LED lighting, efficient HVAC, demand management, renewable energy
C) Run equipment 24/7 without monitoring
D) Ignore energy consumption

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is the break-even point?

A) When equipment breaks
B) When revenue equals total costs (no profit, no loss)
C) Maximum production capacity
D) Minimum price point

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. What does ROI stand for?

A) Return on Investment
B) Rate of Increase
C) Rotation of Inventory
D) Rules of Industry

**Answer: A**

---

### 9. How should facility managers approach cost reduction?

A) Cut all expenses immediately
B) Eliminate quality to save money
C) Systematically identify waste and inefficiency while maintaining quality
D) Reduce staffing to minimum regardless of impact

**Answer: C**

---

### 10. What is the value of benchmarking costs against industry standards?

A) No value - every facility is unique
B) Identifies areas where costs are out of line and improvement opportunities
C) Only for bragging rights
D) To copy competitors exactly

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. A - Labor, Energy, Inputs (seeds/nutrients), Packaging, Overhead
2. C - Energy (electricity)
3. B - To compare efficiency and profitability across different crops and time periods
4. B - Costs that stay constant regardless of production level
5. B - Costs that vary with production volume
6. B - LED lighting, efficient HVAC, demand management, renewable energy
7. B - When revenue equals total costs (no profit, no loss)
8. A - Return on Investment
9. C - Systematically identify waste and inefficiency while maintaining quality
