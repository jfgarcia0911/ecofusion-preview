## Questions

### 1. What is the primary reason most change initiatives fail?

A) Lack of budget
B) Poor technology
C) Resistance from people and inadequate change management
D) Bad timing

**Answer: C**

---

### 2. According to Kotter's change model, what is the first step?

A) Implement immediately
B) Create a sense of urgency
C) Celebrate success
D) Form committees

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. What is change resistance?

A) A type of electrical resistance
B) Natural human reaction to uncertainty and disruption
C) A sign of bad employees
D) Something that only happens in poor organizations

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. How should leaders address change resistance?

A) Ignore it and push forward
B) Fire resistant employees
C) Listen, address concerns, involve people, and communicate clearly
D) Cancel all changes

**Answer: C**

---

### 5. What is a "change champion"?

A) The CEO only
B) An influential person who actively supports and promotes the change
C) A sports award
D) The newest employee

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. Why is communication critical during organizational change?

A) It's not - just implement changes
B) Reduces uncertainty, builds buy-in, and helps people understand the "why"
C) To create more meetings
D) To slow down the process

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is the best approach to implementing major facility changes?

A) Change everything at once
B) Pilot test, learn, adjust, then scale
C) Keep changes secret until launch
D) Let each person do it their own way

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. What role do frontline employees play in successful change?

A) No role - management handles all change
B) Only compliance with directives
C) Critical role providing input, feedback, and implementation
D) Passive observers

**Answer: C**

---

### 9. How should success be measured during change implementation?

A) Only final results matter
B) Track both leading indicators (adoption) and lagging indicators (results)
C) Don't measure - just hope for the best
D) Financial metrics only

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. After implementing a change, what is essential for sustainability?

A) Move to next change immediately
B) Standardize, train, monitor, and reinforce the new way
C) Assume it will stick automatically
D) Stop all communication

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. C - Resistance from people and inadequate change management
2. B - Create a sense of urgency
3. B - Natural human reaction to uncertainty and disruption
4. C - Listen, address concerns, involve people, and communicate clearly
5. B - An influential person who actively supports and promotes the change
6. B - Reduces uncertainty, builds buy-in, and helps people understand the "why"
7. B - Pilot test, learn, adjust, then scale
8. C - Critical role providing input, feedback, and implementation
9. B - Track both leading indicators (adoption) and lagging indicators (results)
10. B - Standardize, train, monitor, and reinforce the new way
