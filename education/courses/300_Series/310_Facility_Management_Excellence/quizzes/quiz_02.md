## Questions

### 1. What is the primary purpose of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)?

A) To create bureaucracy and paperwork
B) To ensure consistent, repeatable processes and outcomes
C) To micromanage employees
D) To comply with government regulations only

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. Which of the following is NOT a required element of a well-written SOP?

A) Purpose and scope
B) Sequential steps
C) Employee disciplinary procedures
D) Quality checks and verification points

**Answer: C**

---

### 3. What is the recommended format for writing SOP steps?

A) Passive voice with complex terminology
B) Active voice, simple language, one action per step
C) Academic writing style
D) Paragraph format with multiple actions combined

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. In visual management, what is the primary purpose of color-coding pipes and zones?

A) To make the facility look attractive
B) For quick identification and mistake-proofing
C) To meet building code requirements
D) To impress visitors

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is a "shadow board" in standard work?

A) A backup board of directors
B) A visual tool showing the proper place for each tool
C) A secondary workspace
D) A scheduling board for night shifts

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. How often should SOPs be reviewed and updated?

A) Once when created, then never
B) Every 5 years
C) Regularly (at least annually) and when processes change
D) Only when problems occur

**Answer: C**

---

### 7. What is the purpose of including troubleshooting sections in SOPs?

A) To blame employees for mistakes
B) To help operators quickly resolve common problems
C) To create more paperwork
D) To replace training programs

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. Which visual management tool provides real-time operational status?

A) Shadow boards
B) Floor markings
C) Andon signals/lights
D) Color-coded pipes

**Answer: C**

---

### 9. What is the best way to verify that operators are following standard work?

A) Install surveillance cameras
B) Conduct regular gemba walks and audits
C) Review paperwork only
D) Wait for problems to occur

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. When creating SOPs for nutrient mixing, which safety information is most critical to include?

A) Employee break schedules
B) PPE requirements and chemical handling procedures
C) Parking lot procedures
D) Office dress code

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - To ensure consistent, repeatable processes and outcomes
2. C - Employee disciplinary procedures
3. B - Active voice, simple language, one action per step
4. B - For quick identification and mistake-proofing
5. B - A visual tool showing the proper place for each tool
6. C - Regularly (at least annually) and when processes change
7. B - To help operators quickly resolve common problems
8. C - Andon signals/lights
9. B - Conduct regular gemba walks and audits
10. B - PPE requirements and chemical handling procedures
