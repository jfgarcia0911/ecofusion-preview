## Questions

### 1. What is the definition of a crisis in facility management?

A) Any minor problem
B) A significant unexpected event threatening operations, safety, or reputation
C) Daily operational challenges
D) Budget overruns

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. What is the most critical phase of crisis management?

A) Recovery
B) Blame assignment
C) Preparation and prevention
D) Media relations

**Answer: C**

---

### 3. What should a crisis response plan include?

A) Only contact numbers
B) Roles, procedures, communication protocols, and decision authorities
C) Just hope and prayers
D) Marketing slogans

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. In a CEA facility, what crisis requires the fastest response time?

A) Pest detection
B) Power failure affecting life support systems
C) Packaging shortage
D) Website down

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is the purpose of crisis drills and simulations?

A) To scare employees
B) To test plans, build muscle memory, and identify gaps
C) To waste time
D) To fulfill compliance requirements only

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. Who should be on the crisis response team?

A) Only the CEO
B) Cross-functional leaders with clear roles and decision authority
C) External consultants only
D) Whoever is available

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What is critical in crisis communication?

A) Hiding information from stakeholders
B) Timely, accurate, transparent information to relevant parties
C) Blaming others publicly
D) Waiting until everything is perfect

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. After a crisis is resolved, what is the most important step?

A) Forget about it and move on
B) Conduct after-action review and update plans based on lessons learned
C) Celebrate without analysis
D) Blame finding mission

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What backup systems are critical for CEA facilities?

A) None needed
B) Power generation, water pumps, environmental controls
C) Only internet backup
D) Backup office supplies

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. How should a facility manager prioritize during a multi-faceted crisis?

A) Random approach
B) Address everything simultaneously
C) Life safety first, then asset protection, then business continuity
D) Financial concerns first always

**Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key

2. C - Preparation and prevention
3. B - Roles, procedures, communication protocols, and decision authorities
4. B - Power failure affecting life support systems
5. B - To test plans, build muscle memory, and identify gaps
6. B - Cross-functional leaders with clear roles and decision authority
7. B - Timely, accurate, transparent information to relevant parties
8. B - Conduct after-action review and update plans based on lessons learned
9. B - Power generation, water pumps, environmental controls
