## Questions

### 1. What is the most important role of a facility manager as a leader?

A) Being the technical expert on everything
B) Developing people and building team capability
C) Making all decisions without input
D) Maximizing personal authority

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. What is "servant leadership"?

A) Treating employees as servants
B) Leading by serving the needs of the team and removing obstacles
C) Always following rather than leading
D) Delegating all responsibilities

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. According to the SBI-I feedback model, what does "SBI-I" stand for?

A) Safety, Budget, Inventory, Inspection
B) Situation, Behavior, Impact, Improvement
C) Systems, Benchmarks, Indicators, Implementation
D) Standard, Basic, Intermediate, Improvement

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. How often should leaders conduct one-on-one meetings with direct reports?

A) Once per year
B) Only when problems occur
C) Regularly (weekly or bi-weekly)
D) Never - only group meetings needed

**Answer: C**

---

### 5. What is the primary purpose of daily huddles or stand-up meetings?

A) To micromanage employees
B) Brief alignment on priorities, issues, and wins (typically 10-15 minutes)
C) To extend the workday
D) To replace all other communication

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. When delegating tasks, what is essential for success?

A) Vague instructions to encourage creativity
B) Clear expectations, resources, authority, and follow-up
C) Delegating only unimportant tasks
D) Doing the work yourself if it's important

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. How should a leader handle conflict between team members?

A) Ignore it and hope it resolves itself
B) Take sides based on personal preference
C) Address it promptly with fact-based discussion and mediation
D) Fire both employees

**Answer: C**

---

### 8. What is "psychological safety" in teams?

A) Physical safety equipment
B) Environment where people feel safe to speak up, ask questions, and admit mistakes
C) Job security guarantees
D) Workplace insurance coverage

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What is the best approach to recognizing employee achievements?

A) Never recognize to avoid favoritism
B) Only annual bonuses
C) Timely, specific, and sincere recognition both publicly and privately
D) Generic "good job" comments

**Answer: C**

---

### 10. As a leader, what should you do when you don't know the answer to a question?

A) Make up an answer to maintain authority
B) Blame others for not informing you
C) Acknowledge it, commit to finding out, and follow up
D) Change the subject

**Answer: C**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Developing people and building team capability
2. B - Leading by serving the needs of the team and removing obstacles
3. B - Situation, Behavior, Impact, Improvement
4. C - Regularly (weekly or bi-weekly)
5. B - Brief alignment on priorities, issues, and wins (typically 10-15 minutes)
6. B - Clear expectations, resources, authority, and follow-up
7. C - Address it promptly with fact-based discussion and mediation
8. B - Environment where people feel safe to speak up, ask questions, and admit mistakes
9. C - Timely, specific, and sincere recognition both publicly and privately
10. C - Acknowledge it, commit to finding out, and follow up
