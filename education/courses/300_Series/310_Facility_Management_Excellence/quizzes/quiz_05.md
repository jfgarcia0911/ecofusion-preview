## Questions

### 1. What is the core objective of Lean methodology?

A) Maximize inventory levels
B) Eliminate waste and maximize customer value
C) Increase process complexity
D) Reduce employee headcount

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. In the "8 Wastes" (DOWNTIME), what does the "D" represent?

A) Delays
B) Defects
C) Distance
D) Documentation

**Answer: B**

---

### 3. Which of the following is an example of "Overproduction" waste in CEA?

A) Growing exactly what customers ordered
B) Growing more crops than current demand requires
C) Maintaining backup inventory
D) Planning future production

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What is a "Gemba walk"?

A) A walking meditation practice
B) Going to the actual place where work is done to observe
C) A facility tour for visitors
D) An exercise break for employees

**Answer: B**

---

### 5. What is the purpose of Value Stream Mapping?

A) To calculate employee salaries
B) To visualize all steps in a process and identify waste
C) To design marketing materials
D) To plan office layouts

**Answer: B**

---

### 6. In Lean, what is "Pull" production?

A) Physically pulling crops out of systems
B) Producing based on actual customer demand rather than forecasts
C) Forcing employees to work harder
D) Pulling weeds from grow beds

**Answer: B**

---

### 7. What does the "5S" methodology help achieve?

A) Five speed settings on equipment
B) Organized, clean, and efficient workspaces
C) Five safety protocols
D) Five sales strategies

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. Which waste occurs when employees' skills and ideas are not utilized?

A) Transportation
B) Motion
C) Non-utilized talent
D) Waiting

**Answer: C**

---

### 9. What is "Kaizen"?

A) A type of Japanese vegetable
B) Continuous improvement involving everyone
C) Emergency response protocol
D) Quality inspection method

**Answer: B**

---

### 10. In CEA, what is an example of "Motion" waste?

A) Crops growing in the system
B) Operators walking excessive distances to retrieve tools
C) Air circulation in grow rooms
D) Water flowing through pipes

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Eliminate waste and maximize customer value
2. B - Defects
3. B - Growing more crops than current demand requires
4. B - Going to the actual place where work is done to observe
5. B - To visualize all steps in a process and identify waste
6. B - Producing based on actual customer demand rather than forecasts
7. B - Organized, clean, and efficient workspaces
8. C - Non-utilized talent
9. B - Continuous improvement involving everyone
10. B - Operators walking excessive distances to retrieve tools
