## Questions

### 1. What is the primary definition of Operations Excellence in CEA facilities?

A) Achieving the highest yields possible
B) Systematic execution of business strategy more consistently and reliably than competition
C) Having the most advanced technology
D) Reducing costs to minimum levels

**Answer: B**

---

### 2. According to the Operations Excellence Pyramid, which level must be established first?

A) Optimization
B) Consistency
C) Foundation
D) Excellence

**Answer: C**

---

### 3. What are the core components of Level 1 (Foundation) in the Excellence Pyramid?

A) Data analytics and continuous improvement
B) Safety, 5S, and basic maintenance
C) Process automation and AI
D) Benchmarking and innovation

**Answer: B**

---

### 4. What happens when you attempt optimization (Level 4) without stability (Level 2)?

A) You achieve faster results
B) You reduce costs immediately
C) Improvements don't stick and processes remain unreliable
D) Equipment performance improves

**Answer: C**

---

### 5. What is the minimum equipment uptime requirement for Level 2 (Stability)?

A) 85%
B) 90%
C) 95%
D) 99%

**Answer: C**

---

### 6. In facility maturity assessment, your overall maturity level is determined by:

A) Your highest scoring area
B) The average of all areas
C) Your lowest scoring area
D) The financial performance metrics

**Answer: C**

---

### 7. Which of the following is an example of Level 3 (Consistency) in CEA operations?

A) Basic equipment cleaning
B) Documented SOPs with visual management systems
C) World-class benchmark performance
D) Data-driven experimentation

**Answer: B**

---

### 8. According to the 80/20 Rule of Excellence, foundation work (Levels 1-2) represents:

A) 20% of the work, 80% of the glory
B) 80% of the work, 20% of the glory
C) 50% of the work, 50% of the glory
D) 100% of the work, no glory

**Answer: B**

---

### 9. What is a key characteristic that distinguishes Operations Excellence from Good Performance?

A) Occasional great results vs. consistent great results
B) Higher costs vs. lower costs
C) More employees vs. fewer employees
D) Larger facilities vs. smaller facilities

**Answer: A**

---

### 10. Which leadership behavior has the greatest impact on driving operations excellence?

A) Sending emails about quality
B) Visible commitment through daily facility walks
C) Delegating all decisions to managers
D) Focusing exclusively on financial reports

**Answer: B**

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Systematic execution of business strategy more consistently and reliably than competition
2. C - Foundation
3. B - Safety, 5S, and basic maintenance
4. C - Improvements don't stick and processes remain unreliable
5. C - 95%
6. C - Your lowest scoring area
7. B - Documented SOPs with visual management systems
8. B - 80% of the work, 20% of the glory
9. A - Occasional great results vs. consistent great results
10. B - Visible commitment through daily facility walks
