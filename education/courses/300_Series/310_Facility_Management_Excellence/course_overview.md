## Lean Principles for CEA

### The 8 Wastes (DOWNTIME)

| Waste | CEA Example | Elimination Strategy |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| **D**efects | Unmarketable produce | Quality at source |
| **O**verproduction | Growing more than demand | Demand planning |
| **W**aiting | Delayed harvests, bottlenecks | Flow optimization |
| **N**on-utilized talent | Under-skilled staff | Training, empowerment |
| **T**ransportation | Excessive material movement | Layout optimization |
| **I**nventory | Excess supplies, unharvested crop | Just-in-time |
| **M**otion | Unnecessary worker movement | Ergonomic design |
| **E**xtra processing | Over-handling, redundant checks | Process simplification |

## Team Leadership

### Leadership Competencies

| Competency | Behaviors | Development |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Vision** | Sets direction, inspires | Strategic planning exercises |
| **Communication** | Clear, frequent, two-way | Presentation, listening skills |
| **Accountability** | Sets expectations, follows up | Goal-setting frameworks |
| **Development** | Coaches, mentors, delegates | Training, feedback skills |
| **Problem-solving** | Systematic, data-driven | Root cause analysis |
| **Teamwork** | Collaborates, builds trust | Team-building activities |

### Performance Feedback Model

```
SBI-I Feedback Model:

S - Situation: "During yesterday's harvest..."
B - Behavior: "...I noticed you double-checked each weight..."
I - Impact: "...which resulted in zero customer complaints."
I - Improvement/Reinforcement: "Please continue this practice."

For constructive feedback:
S - Situation: "When packing orders this morning..."
B - Behavior: "...some boxes were missing labels..."
I - Impact: "...causing a 30-minute shipping delay."
I - Improvement: "Let's create a checklist to prevent this."
```

## Maintenance Management

### Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)

| Pillar | Description | Activities |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Autonomous maintenance | Operators maintain equipment | Daily checks, cleaning, lubrication |
| Planned maintenance | Scheduled preventive work | PM schedules, parts replacement |
| Quality maintenance | Prevent defects | Process capability, calibration |
| Focused improvement | Eliminate losses | Root cause analysis, projects |
| Early equipment management | Design for maintainability | Specifications, vendor selection |
| Training & education | Build skills | Certification, cross-training |
| Safety & environment | Zero accidents | Safety audits, procedures |
| Office TPM | Administrative efficiency | Process improvement |

### PM Schedule Framework

| Equipment | Daily | Weekly | Monthly | Quarterly | Annual |
|-----------|-------|--------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Pumps | Visual | Clean strainers | Check seals | Impeller inspect | Replace seals |
| Lighting | Visual | Clean fixtures | Check output | Ballast test | Replace bulbs |
| HVAC | Filter check | Drain clean | Belt inspect | Coil clean | Full service |
| Sensors | Readings | Calibration check | Full calibration | Replace probes | Recertify |

## Crisis Management

### Crisis Response Framework

| Phase | Actions | Responsibility |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Preparation** | Plans, training, drills | Management |
| **Detection** | Monitoring, alarms, observation | All staff |
| **Assessment** | Severity, scope, resources | Crisis team |
| **Response** | Execute plans, communicate | Crisis team |
| **Recovery** | Restore operations | All staff |
| **Review** | Lessons learned, improvements | Management |

### Common CEA Crises

| Crisis | Response Priority | Prevention |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| Power failure | Fish life support | Backup generators, UPS |
| Water system failure | Fish, crops | Redundant pumps, reserves |
| Disease outbreak | Containment, treatment | Biosecurity, monitoring |
| Equipment failure | Backup, repair | PM program |
| Weather extreme | Protection, backup | Design, monitoring |
| Food safety incident | Recall, investigation | HACCP, traceability |
