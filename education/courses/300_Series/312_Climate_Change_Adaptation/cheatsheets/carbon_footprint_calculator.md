## CEA Carbon Footprint Assessment

Calculate your operation's greenhouse gas emissions and identify reduction opportunities.

---

## 1. Carbon Footprint Formula

```
TOTAL CARBON FOOTPRINT (kg CO2e/year) =
    Scope 1 (Direct) + Scope 2 (Electricity) + Scope 3 (Indirect)

Per-Unit Footprint = Total Footprint / Annual Production (kg or lbs)

Target: <2 kg CO2e/kg produce (for leafy greens)
Excellent: <1 kg CO2e/kg produce
```

## 12. Carbon Certification & Marketing

### Certification Options

**Carbon Neutral Certification:**
- Requirements: Net zero emissions (including offsets)
- Certifiers: Climate Neutral, CarbonFree, SCS Global
- Cost: $1,000-5,000 annually
- Marketing value: Premium positioning

**Low-Carbon Product Label:**
- Requirements: <X kg CO2e/kg (varies by standard)
- Certifiers: Carbon Trust, Climate Declared
- Cost: $2,000-10,000
- Marketing value: Eco-conscious consumers

**Renewable Energy Certification:**
- Requirements: % renewable energy
- Certifiers: Green-e, LEED
- Cost: Variable
- Marketing value: Sustainability leadership

## 14. Quick Reference - Emission Factors

### Fuels
- Natural gas: 5.3 kg CO2e/therm (or 0.053 kg/kWh)
- Propane: 5.68 kg CO2e/gallon
- Diesel: 10.2 kg CO2e/gallon
- Gasoline: 8.9 kg CO2e/gallon
- Heating oil: 10.2 kg CO2e/gallon

### Electricity (varies by location)
- U.S. average: 0.42 kg CO2e/kWh
- Coal states: 0.75-0.85 kg CO2e/kWh
- Clean grids: 0.05-0.15 kg CO2e/kWh

### Transportation
- Small truck: 0.4 kg CO2e/mile
- Large truck: 0.6 kg CO2e/mile
- Personal vehicle: 0.4 kg CO2e/mile

### Materials
- Nitrogen fertilizer: 4.0 kg CO2e/kg
- Hydroponic nutrients: ~2.0 kg CO2e/kg
- Plastic packaging: 0.02 kg CO2e/clamshell
- Water (municipal): 0.0003 kg CO2e/gallon
