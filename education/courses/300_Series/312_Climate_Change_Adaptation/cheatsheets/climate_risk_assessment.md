## Quick Reference: Vulnerability Assessment

### 1. Vulnerability Formula

```
VULNERABILITY = (EXPOSURE × SENSITIVITY) - ADAPTIVE CAPACITY

Score Range:
• 1-25: Low vulnerability (well-prepared)
• 25-50: Moderate vulnerability (some gaps)
• 50-75: High vulnerability (major gaps)
• 75-125: Extreme vulnerability (urgent action needed)
```

## 3. Exposure Assessment (Rate 1-5)

- [ ] **Growing areas:** Outdoor (5) → Protected (3) → Indoor (1)
- [ ] **Fish tanks:** Exposed (5) → Partially protected (3) → Climate controlled (1)
- [ ] **Water supply:** Rain-fed (5) → Surface water (4) → Municipal (2) → Multiple sources (1)
- [ ] **Power supply:** No backup (5) → Partial backup (3) → Full redundancy (1)
- [ ] **Structure:** Standard (3) → Enhanced (2) → Hurricane-rated (1)

---

## 4. Sensitivity Assessment (Rate 1-5)

- [ ] **Crop heat tolerance:** Cool-season only (5) → Mixed (3) → Heat-tolerant (1)
- [ ] **Water dependency:** Rain-fed (5) → Irrigation (3) → Recirculating (1)
- [ ] **Energy interruption:** Critical systems (5) → Important (3) → Minimal impact (1)
- [ ] **Market access:** Single customer (5) → Few customers (3) → Diverse (1)
- [ ] **Financial cushion:** No reserves (5) → 1-3 months (3) → 6+ months (1)

---

## 5. Adaptive Capacity Assessment (Rate 1-5)

- [ ] **Backup power:** None (1) → Partial (3) → Full 72+ hours (5)
- [ ] **Water storage:** None (1) → <7 days (2) → 7-30 days (3) → 30-90 days (5)
- [ ] **Climate control:** None (1) → Passive (2) → Active (4) → Redundant (5)
- [ ] **Crop diversity:** 1-2 crops (1) → 3-5 crops (3) → 6-12+ crops (5)
- [ ] **Market diversity:** 1 channel (1) → 2-3 channels (3) → 4-6+ channels (5)
- [ ] **Financial reserves:** 0 months (1) → 1-3 months (2) → 3-6 months (4) → 6-12+ months (5)
- [ ] **Staff expertise:** Single person (1) → 2-3 people (3) → Cross-trained team (5)

## 6. Regional Climate Risks (Quick Reference)

**Southwest (AZ, NM, NV, S.CA):**
- Primary: Extreme heat, water scarcity, drought
- Priorities: Water management, cooling, solar energy

**Southeast (FL, GA, AL, MS, LA):**
- Primary: Hurricanes, humidity, heavy rainfall
- Priorities: Structural design, dehumidification, flood protection

**Midwest/Great Plains (IL, IN, KS, OK, NE):**
- Primary: Temperature extremes, severe storms, variable precipitation
- Priorities: Structural resilience, energy efficiency, backup power

**Northeast (NY, PA, NJ, New England):**
- Primary: Cold winters, short season, variable weather
- Priorities: Season extension, heating efficiency, insulation

**Northwest (WA, OR, N.CA):**
- Primary: Summer drought, wildfire, low winter sun
- Priorities: Rainwater harvest, wildfire prep, supplemental lighting

**Mountain West (CO, UT, ID, WY, MT):**
- Primary: Temperature extremes, short season, limited water
- Priorities: Solar energy, season extension, water efficiency

## 8. Risk Prioritization Matrix

```
         HIGH IMPACT
             ▲
             │
    LOW      │     HIGH
    COST     │     COST
    ─────────┼─────────
    QUICK    │   MAJOR
    WINS     │  PROJECTS
             │
    DO FIRST │ PLAN & FUND
    ─────────┼─────────
    FILL-INS │ RECONSIDER
             │
    DO IF    │  AVOID
    TIME     │
             ▼
         LOW IMPACT
```

**Priority:**
1. Quick Wins (high impact, low cost): Do immediately
2. Major Projects (high impact, high cost): Plan and fund
3. Fill-Ins (low impact, low cost): Do if time allows
4. Reconsider (low impact, high cost): Avoid or redesign

## 10. Resilience Quick Checklist

### Essential (Do These First)
- [ ] Backup generator with 72+ hours fuel (if aquaponics or critical)
- [ ] Temperature monitoring with alerts
- [ ] Written emergency protocols (power outage, heat wave, flood)
- [ ] 3-month financial reserve
- [ ] At least 2 backup suppliers for critical inputs

### Important (Next Priority)
- [ ] Solar energy (if feasible in your region)
- [ ] Water storage (30-90 days)
- [ ] Crop diversity (6-12 types)
- [ ] Market diversity (3-4 channels)
- [ ] Cross-trained staff

### Advanced (Long-term Resilience)
- [ ] Modular system design
- [ ] Renewable energy >50%
- [ ] 6-12 month financial reserves
- [ ] Grower network participation
- [ ] Climate adaptation plan documented
