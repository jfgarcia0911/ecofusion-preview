## Course Description

Prepare for and adapt to changing climate conditions. This course covers climate impacts on agriculture, resilience strategies, adaptation planning, and the role of CEA in food security. Position your operation for long-term success in a changing world.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Analyze climate change impacts on agriculture
2. Assess vulnerabilities in food systems
3. Develop adaptation strategies
4. Position CEA for climate resilience
5. Communicate climate opportunities
6. Create climate adaptation plans

## CEA as Climate Solution

### Climate Advantages of CEA

| Challenge | Field Agriculture | CEA Solution |
|-----------|------------------|--------------|
| Water scarcity | 100% of crop water | 90-95% water reduction |
| Temperature extremes | Crop loss | Climate controlled |
| Pest/disease pressure | Increasing pesticides | Physical barriers, biocontrol |
| Unpredictable seasons | Shortened seasons | Year-round production |
| Land degradation | Soil loss | Soilless, urban locations |
| Supply chain disruption | Regional production | Local production |

### Carbon Footprint Comparison

| Factor | Field (shipped) | Greenhouse (local) | Vertical Farm |
|--------|----------------|--------------------| --------------|
| Growing emissions | Low | Medium | High (energy) |
| Transport emissions | High | Low | Very Low |
| Water use | High | Low | Very Low |
| Land use | High | Medium | Very Low |
| Pesticide use | High | Low | Minimal |
| **Net assessment** | Variable | Often better | Depends on energy |

## Water Adaptation Strategies

### Water Security Measures

| Strategy | Implementation | Cost | Impact |
|----------|---------------|------|--------|
| Recirculating systems | Closed-loop design | High initial | 90%+ reduction |
| Rainwater harvesting | Collection, storage, treatment | Medium | Reduce municipal |
| Condensate recovery | HVAC system capture | Low | 5-15% of needs |
| Greywater recycling | Treatment, non-food use | Medium | Reduce fresh use |
| Drought-tolerant crops | Species/variety selection | Low | Buffer shortages |
| Water storage | Tanks, reservoirs | Medium-High | Emergency supply |

### Water Efficiency Targets

| System Type | Water Use (gal/lb produce) | Target Improvement |
|-------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| Field agriculture | 20-50 | Baseline |
| Greenhouse (drip) | 5-10 | 75-90% reduction |
| NFT/DWC hydroponic | 2-5 | 90-95% reduction |
| Aquaponics | 1-3 | 95%+ reduction |
| Aeroponics | 0.5-2 | 97%+ reduction |

## Extreme Weather Preparation

### Risk Assessment Matrix

| Event | Probability | Impact | Preparedness |
|-------|------------|--------|--------------|
| Power outage | High | Critical | Generators, UPS |
| Flooding | Medium | High | Elevation, drainage |
| Hurricane/tornado | Regional | Catastrophic | Structure, insurance |
| Extreme heat | Increasing | High | Cooling capacity |
| Extreme cold | Regional | High | Backup heating |
| Ice storm | Regional | Medium | Structural design |

### Emergency Preparedness Checklist

| System | Backup Requirement | Duration Target |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Electricity | Generator + fuel | 72+ hours |
| Aeration (fish) | Battery backup, air pumps | 8+ hours |
| Water circulation | Gravity flow option | 24 hours |
| Climate control | Thermal mass, passive | 4-8 hours |
| Communications | Cell, radio | Continuous |
