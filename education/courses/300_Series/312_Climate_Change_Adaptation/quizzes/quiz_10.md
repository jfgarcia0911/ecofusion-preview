## Questions

**1. Which U.S. region is experiencing the most rapid rate of climate warming?**

a) Southeast
b) Midwest
c) Southwest
d) Alaska/Arctic

**Correct Answer:** d) Alaska/Arctic (2-3x faster than global average)

---

**2. What is the primary climate challenge for CEA in the U.S. Southwest?**

a) Excessive rainfall
b) Water scarcity and extreme heat
c) Flooding
d) Cold temperatures

**Correct Answer:** b) Water scarcity and extreme heat

---

**3. Which adaptation strategy is most important for CEA in coastal regions?**

a) Drought planning
b) Sea level rise and hurricane preparedness
c) Wildfire protection
d) Avalanche safety

**Correct Answer:** b) Sea level rise and hurricane preparedness

---

**4. What climate advantage does the Pacific Northwest have for CEA operations?**

a) Extreme heat
b) Low humidity
c) Mild temperatures and hydroelectric renewable energy
d) No water sources

**Correct Answer:** c) Mild temperatures and hydroelectric renewable energy

---

**5. Which region faces the greatest risk from compound heat and drought events?**

a) Alaska
b) Northeast
c) Great Plains and Southwest
d) Hawaii

**Correct Answer:** c) Great Plains and Southwest

---

**6. What is the primary opportunity for winter CEA production in northern climates?**

a) High cooling costs
b) Free cooling and waste heat utilization from heating systems
c) Better sunlight
d) Warmer temperatures

**Correct Answer:** b) Free cooling and waste heat utilization from heating systems

---

**7. Which regional climate trend benefits year-round greenhouse production in southern states?**

a) Longer freeze-free periods
b) Shorter growing seasons
c) Increased snowfall
d) Colder winters

**Correct Answer:** a) Longer freeze-free periods (but increased summer cooling challenges)

---

**8. What adaptation is critical for CEA in tornado-prone regions (Great Plains, Midwest)?**

a) No special measures needed
b) Reinforced structures and emergency shelter plans
c) Taller buildings
d) Underground-only operations

**Correct Answer:** b) Reinforced structures and emergency shelter plans

---

**9. Which water source is most reliable for CEA in drought-prone regions?**

a) Rivers and streams
b) Municipal/treated water with maximum recirculation
c) Rain collection only
d) No reliable sources

**Correct Answer:** b) Municipal/treated water with maximum recirculation (95%+ recovery)

---

**10. What is the best regional strategy for CEA energy management in areas with unreliable grid power?**

a) No backup needed
b) Increased dependence on grid
c) On-site renewable energy and battery storage
d) Diesel generators only

**Correct Answer:** c) On-site renewable energy and battery storage
