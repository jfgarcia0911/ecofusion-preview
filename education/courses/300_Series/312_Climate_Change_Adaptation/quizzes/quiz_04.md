## Questions

**1. What are the two primary ways CEA addresses climate change?**

a) Cooling and heating
b) Mitigation and adaptation
c) Production and distribution
d) Planting and harvesting

**Correct Answer:** b) Mitigation and adaptation

---

**2. How much can local CEA production reduce transportation-related emissions compared to national supply chains?**

a) 10-20%
b) 25-40%
c) 50-90%
d) 100% (zero emissions)

**Correct Answer:** c) 50-90%

---

**3. What is the primary greenhouse gas eliminated by CEA compared to field agriculture?**

a) Carbon dioxide (CO2)
b) Methane (CH4) and Nitrous oxide (N2O)
c) Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
d) Sulfur dioxide (SO2)

**Correct Answer:** b) Methane (CH4) and Nitrous oxide (N2O)

---

**4. What percentage of renewable energy integration can make CEA carbon-neutral or carbon-negative?**

a) 25-50%
b) 50-75%
c) 75-100%
d) Must be 100% renewable

**Correct Answer:** c) 75-100% (with efficient systems and carbon capture)

---

**5. How much water does CEA typically save compared to conventional field agriculture?**

a) 10-30%
b) 30-50%
c) 50-70%
d) 70-95%

**Correct Answer:** d) 70-95% (through recirculation and precision delivery)

---

**6. Which renewable energy source is most commonly integrated with CEA facilities?**

a) Wind turbines
b) Solar photovoltaic panels
c) Hydroelectric dams
d) Geothermal heat pumps

**Correct Answer:** b) Solar photovoltaic panels

---

**7. What is the main climate adaptation benefit of CEA's controlled environment?**

a) Produces more oxygen
b) Complete protection from extreme weather and climate variability
c) Uses less electricity
d) Employs more workers

**Correct Answer:** b) Complete protection from extreme weather and climate variability

---

**8. How does CEA contribute to carbon sequestration?**

a) Trees planted on rooftops
b) Biochar production from plant waste and soil carbon storage
c) Underground CO2 injection
d) Ocean fertilization

**Correct Answer:** b) Biochar production from plant waste and soil carbon storage

---

**9. What is the typical carbon footprint reduction for leafy greens produced in local CEA vs. shipped conventional produce?**

a) 10-20%
b) 20-40%
c) 40-60%
d) 60-80%

**Correct Answer:** c) 40-60% (varies by energy source and transport distance)

---

**10. Which climate policy framework commonly supports CEA development?**

a) Fossil fuel subsidies
b) Carbon pricing and offset markets
c) Deforestation incentives
d) Coal plant construction

**Correct Answer:** b) Carbon pricing and offset markets
