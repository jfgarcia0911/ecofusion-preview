## Questions

**1. What percentage of the U.S. Southwest population is projected to be under high water stress by 2050?**

a) 50%
b) 75%
c) 85%
d) 95%

**Correct Answer:** d) 95%

---

**2. How much water does CEA save compared to conventional field agriculture?**

a) 20-40%
b) 40-60%
c) 60-80%
d) 70-95%

**Correct Answer:** d) 70-95%

---

**3. What is the primary water conservation technique in hydroponic CEA systems?**

a) Drip irrigation
b) Recirculation and recovery of nutrient solution
c) Rainwater harvesting
d) Flood irrigation

**Correct Answer:** b) Recirculation and recovery of nutrient solution

---

**4. What is the typical water recirculation rate in advanced CEA systems?**

a) 50-60%
b) 70-80%
c) 85-95%
d) 99%+

**Correct Answer:** c) 85-95% (some systems achieve 95%+)

---

**5. Which water source is becoming increasingly important for CEA drought resilience?**

a) Groundwater from deep aquifers
b) Atmospheric water generation and condensate recovery
c) River diversion
d) Desalinated ocean water only

**Correct Answer:** b) Atmospheric water generation and condensate recovery

---

**6. What is the main water loss pathway in CEA operations?**

a) Leakage
b) Evaporation and plant transpiration
c) Cleaning and sanitation
d) Employee consumption

**Correct Answer:** b) Evaporation and plant transpiration

---

**7. How can CEA facilities capture and reuse water vapor from plant transpiration?**

a) Open ventilation
b) Dehumidification systems with condensate collection
c) Fans only
d) Cannot be captured

**Correct Answer:** b) Dehumidification systems with condensate collection

---

**8. What water quality parameter is most critical to monitor in recirculating hydroponic systems?**

a) Temperature only
b) Color
c) Electrical conductivity (EC) and pH
d) Taste

**Correct Answer:** c) Electrical conductivity (EC) and pH

---

**9. Which region is experiencing the most rapid depletion of the High Plains (Ogallala) Aquifer?**

a) Northeast
b) Southwest
c) Great Plains (Kansas, Nebraska, Texas)
d) Pacific Northwest

**Correct Answer:** c) Great Plains (Kansas, Nebraska, Texas)

---

**10. What is greywater recycling in CEA applications?**

a) Using dirty water directly on plants
b) Treating and reusing water from washing, cooling, or other non-contaminated sources
c) Storing water in grey tanks
d) Painting water tanks grey

**Correct Answer:** b) Treating and reusing water from washing, cooling, or other non-contaminated sources
