## Questions

**1. How frequently are 100-year flood events projected to occur by 2050?**

a) Once per 100 years
b) Once per 50 years
c) Once per 20 years
d) Once per 5-10 years

**Correct Answer:** d) Once per 5-10 years

---

**2. What is the first priority in extreme weather emergency preparedness?**

a) Protecting equipment
b) Life safety of staff and visitors
c) Saving the crop
d) Maintaining production schedules

**Correct Answer:** b) Life safety of staff and visitors

---

**3. Which extreme weather event poses the greatest direct threat to CEA infrastructure?**

a) Heat waves
b) Droughts
c) High winds and hurricanes
d) Light rain

**Correct Answer:** c) High winds and hurricanes (structural damage risk)

---

**4. What is the recommended backup power runtime capacity for critical CEA systems?**

a) 2-4 hours
b) 6-12 hours
c) 24-72 hours
d) 1 week

**Correct Answer:** c) 24-72 hours (minimum for most emergencies)

---

**5. Which system should receive priority during power outages?**

a) Marketing computers
b) Office lighting
c) Climate control and irrigation for crop survival
d) Decorative lighting

**Correct Answer:** c) Climate control and irrigation for crop survival

---

**6. How often should emergency preparedness drills be conducted?**

a) Once per decade
b) Once per year
c) Quarterly (every 3 months)
d) Monthly

**Correct Answer:** c) Quarterly (every 3 months) or semi-annually minimum

---

**7. What is a critical component of flood preparedness for CEA facilities?**

a) Ignoring flood zones
b) Elevating critical equipment and electrical systems above flood levels
c) Installing equipment in basements
d) Removing all drainage systems

**Correct Answer:** b) Elevating critical equipment and electrical systems above flood levels

---

**8. What is the purpose of a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) in CEA operations?**

a) Increasing profits
b) Ensuring operations can continue or quickly resume after disruptions
c) Reducing labor costs
d) Eliminating all risks

**Correct Answer:** b) Ensuring operations can continue or quickly resume after disruptions

---

**9. Which insurance coverage is essential for climate-related risks in CEA?**

a) Only basic property insurance
b) Comprehensive coverage including flood, wind, business interruption, and crop loss
c) No insurance needed for CEA
d) Vehicle insurance only

**Correct Answer:** b) Comprehensive coverage including flood, wind, business interruption, and crop loss

---

**10. What is the recommended approach to wind protection for greenhouse structures?**

a) No special measures needed
b) Structural reinforcement, wind-rated glazing, and automated venting systems
c) Opening all windows during storms
d) Removing the roof

**Correct Answer:** b) Structural reinforcement, wind-rated glazing, and automated venting systems
