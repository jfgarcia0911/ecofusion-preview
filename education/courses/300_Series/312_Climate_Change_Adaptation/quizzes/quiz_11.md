## Instructions

## Questions

**1. What is the primary purpose of carbon pricing mechanisms?**

a) Increase government revenue only
b) Incentivize emissions reductions by making pollution costly
c) Punish businesses
d) Eliminate all industries

**Correct Answer:** b) Incentivize emissions reductions by making pollution costly

---

**2. Which federal program provides funding for climate-smart agriculture practices?**

a) Medicare
b) USDA Climate-Smart Agriculture and Forestry Partnership Program
c) Highway Trust Fund
d) Social Security

**Correct Answer:** b) USDA Climate-Smart Agriculture and Forestry Partnership Program

---

**3. What is the Investment Tax Credit (ITC) for solar energy installations?**

a) 5%
b) 15%
c) 30% (as of 2024)
d) 50%

**Correct Answer:** c) 30% (as of 2024, under Inflation Reduction Act)

---

**4. Which policy mechanism allows CEA facilities to sell carbon offsets?**

a) Tax deduction
b) Carbon offset markets and registries (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard)
c) Business licenses
d) Health permits

**Correct Answer:** b) Carbon offset markets and registries (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard)

---

**5. What is the purpose of Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)?**

a) Certify organic production
b) Represent the environmental attributes of renewable energy generation
c) Replace electricity meters
d) License electricians

**Correct Answer:** b) Represent the environmental attributes of renewable energy generation

---

**6. Which state has the most comprehensive climate policy framework supporting CEA?**

a) Wyoming
b) California (with Cap-and-Trade, renewable mandates, water efficiency programs)
c) Alabama
d) Montana

**Correct Answer:** b) California (with Cap-and-Trade, renewable mandates, water efficiency programs)

---

**7. What is net metering in the context of on-site renewable energy?**

a) Measuring fish nets
b) Crediting solar energy producers for excess electricity sent to the grid
c) Internet speed testing
d) Crop yield measurement

**Correct Answer:** b) Crediting solar energy producers for excess electricity sent to the grid

---

**8. Which federal law provides significant funding for climate adaptation and clean energy?**

a) Clean Air Act (1970)
b) Inflation Reduction Act (2022)
c) National Parks Act
d) Food Safety Modernization Act

**Correct Answer:** b) Inflation Reduction Act (2022) - $369 billion for climate/energy

---

**9. What type of zoning policy can support urban CEA development?**

a) Prohibition of all agriculture
b) Agricultural zoning in urban areas and adaptive reuse of industrial buildings
c) Residential-only zoning
d) Manufacturing bans

**Correct Answer:** b) Agricultural zoning in urban areas and adaptive reuse of industrial buildings

---

**10. Which incentive helps CEA facilities reduce electricity costs?**

a) Demand response programs and time-of-use rates
b) Increasing peak consumption
c) Ignoring utility bills
d) Using only fossil fuels

**Correct Answer:** a) Demand response programs and time-of-use rates
