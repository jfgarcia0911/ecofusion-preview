## Questions

**1. How many additional extreme heat days (>95°F) are projected annually by 2050 in many U.S. regions?**

a) 5-10 days
b) 10-20 days
c) 30-60 days
d) 100+ days

**Correct Answer:** c) 30-60 days (varies by region)

---

**2. At what temperature do most leafy greens experience heat stress and reduced quality?**

a) 70-75°F
b) 80-85°F
c) 85-90°F
d) 95-100°F

**Correct Answer:** c) 85-90°F and above

---

**3. What is the most energy-efficient cooling strategy for CEA facilities?**

a) Air conditioning only
b) Evaporative cooling (where humidity permits)
c) Opening all doors and windows
d) Ice blocks

**Correct Answer:** b) Evaporative cooling (where humidity permits) - uses 75% less energy than A/C

---

**4. Which plant response to heat stress reduces crop quality?**

a) Increased growth rate
b) Bolting (premature flowering) and bitterness
c) Better flavor
d) Larger leaves

**Correct Answer:** b) Bolting (premature flowering) and bitterness

---

**5. What is the purpose of thermal curtains or shade cloths in greenhouse cooling?**

a) Decoration
b) Reducing solar heat gain while maintaining light for photosynthesis
c) Increasing temperature
d) Keeping birds out

**Correct Answer:** b) Reducing solar heat gain while maintaining light for photosynthesis

---

**6. How much can reflective or white roofing reduce cooling loads in CEA facilities?**

a) 5-10%
b) 15-25%
c) 30-50%
d) 75-90%

**Correct Answer:** b) 15-25%

---

**7. What is the OSHA-recommended heat index threshold for implementing worker safety measures?**

a) 80°F
b) 91°F (caution level)
c) 100°F
d) 110°F

**Correct Answer:** b) 91°F (caution level) - with escalating precautions at higher levels

---

**8. Which cooling method is most suitable for arid climates?**

a) Humidity addition
b) Evaporative cooling
c) Heating
d) Closing all vents

**Correct Answer:** b) Evaporative cooling (takes advantage of low humidity)

---

**9. What is thermal stratification and how can it be managed in tall CEA structures?**

a) Uniform temperature; no management needed
b) Hot air rises creating temperature gradients; managed with circulation fans
c) Cold spots only; requires heating
d) Not relevant to CEA

**Correct Answer:** b) Hot air rises creating temperature gradients; managed with circulation fans

---

**10. Which crop types are most heat-tolerant for summer CEA production?**

a) Lettuce and spinach
b) Basil, tomatoes, and peppers (warm-season crops)
c) Kale and arugula
d) All crops are equally heat-tolerant

**Correct Answer:** b) Basil, tomatoes, and peppers (warm-season crops)
