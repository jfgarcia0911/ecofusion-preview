## Questions

**1. What is the first step in developing a climate adaptation plan?**

a) Implementing solutions
b) Risk and vulnerability assessment
c) Hiring consultants
d) Buying insurance

**Correct Answer:** b) Risk and vulnerability assessment

---

**2. What time horizon should climate adaptation plans consider?**

a) Next month only
b) 1-2 years
c) 5-10 years minimum, with 20-30 year outlook
d) 100 years only

**Correct Answer:** c) 5-10 years minimum, with 20-30 year outlook

---

**3. Which framework is commonly used for adaptation planning?**

a) SWOT analysis only
b) Climate risk assessment → Prioritization → Strategy development → Implementation → Monitoring
c) Random trial and error
d) No framework needed

**Correct Answer:** b) Climate risk assessment → Prioritization → Strategy development → Implementation → Monitoring

---

**4. What does "no-regret" adaptation mean?**

a) Actions that have benefits regardless of climate outcomes
b) Strategies you'll regret later
c) Doing nothing
d) Most expensive options

**Correct Answer:** a) Actions that have benefits regardless of climate outcomes (e.g., energy efficiency)

---

**5. How often should climate adaptation plans be reviewed and updated?**

a) Never - set and forget
b) Once per decade
c) Annually or biennially
d) Every 50 years

**Correct Answer:** c) Annually or biennially (to incorporate new climate data and lessons learned)

---

**6. What is the purpose of scenario planning in climate adaptation?**

a) Entertainment
b) Preparing for multiple possible climate futures to build robust strategies
c) Avoiding all planning
d) Guaranteeing specific outcomes

**Correct Answer:** b) Preparing for multiple possible climate futures to build robust strategies

---

**7. Which stakeholders should be involved in CEA climate adaptation planning?**

a) Only the owner
b) Management, staff, suppliers, customers, insurers, and community partners
c) No one - keep it secret
d) Competitors only

**Correct Answer:** b) Management, staff, suppliers, customers, insurers, and community partners

---

**8. What is adaptive management in the context of climate planning?**

a) Rigid adherence to original plans
b) Iterative approach with monitoring, learning, and adjusting strategies
c) Avoiding all changes
d) Managing only adaptive employees

**Correct Answer:** b) Iterative approach with monitoring, learning, and adjusting strategies

---

**9. Which climate data source provides localized projections for facility planning?**

a) Global climate models only
b) USDA Plant Hardiness Zones and state/regional climate assessment reports
c) Weather forecasts only
d) No data needed

**Correct Answer:** b) USDA Plant Hardiness Zones and state/regional climate assessment reports

---

**10. What is the relationship between mitigation and adaptation in a comprehensive climate plan?**

a) They are mutually exclusive - choose one
b) They are complementary - both reduce overall climate risk
c) Mitigation is unnecessary if you adapt
d) Adaptation is unnecessary if you mitigate

**Correct Answer:** b) They are complementary - both reduce overall climate risk
