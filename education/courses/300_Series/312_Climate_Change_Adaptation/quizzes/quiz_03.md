## Questions

**1. What are the four pillars of food security?**

a) Production, Distribution, Consumption, Waste
b) Availability, Access, Utilization, Stability
c) Planting, Growing, Harvesting, Storage
d) Supply, Demand, Price, Quality

**Correct Answer:** b) Availability, Access, Utilization, Stability

---

**2. How many people are currently experiencing moderate or severe food insecurity globally?**

a) 500 million
b) 1.2 billion
c) 2.4 billion
d) 4.0 billion

**Correct Answer:** c) 2.4 billion

---

**3. By what percentage are global wheat yields projected to decline by 2050 under a +2°C warming scenario with no adaptation?**

a) -2%
b) -6%
c) -15%
d) -25%

**Correct Answer:** b) -6%

---

**4. What is the projected food production gap by 2050 according to FAO estimates?**

a) -10%
b) -20%
c) -35%
d) -50%

**Correct Answer:** c) -35% (demand outpaces supply)

---

**5. How does elevated CO2 affect crop nutritional quality?**

a) Increases protein and mineral content
b) Decreases protein and mineral content through dilution
c) No significant effect on nutrition
d) Only affects vitamin C levels

**Correct Answer:** b) Decreases protein and mineral content through dilution

---

**6. What percentage of income do extreme poverty households (<$2.15/day) spend on food?**

a) 20-30%
b) 30-40%
c) 40-50%
d) 50-80%

**Correct Answer:** d) 50-80%

---

**7. What is the estimated increase in mycotoxin risk in many regions due to climate change?**

a) +5-10%
b) +15-20%
c) +30-50%
d) +75-100%

**Correct Answer:** c) +30-50%

---

**8. How much higher are CEA yields per acre compared to field agriculture?**

a) 2-5x higher
b) 5-10x higher
c) 10-100x higher
d) 500-1000x higher

**Correct Answer:** c) 10-100x higher (due to vertical stacking and year-round production)

---

**9. Which food security dimension is MOST directly addressed by CEA's weather-independent production?**

a) Availability
b) Access
c) Utilization
d) Stability

**Correct Answer:** d) Stability (consistent year-round production regardless of climate)

---

**10. What is a key limitation of CEA in addressing global food security?**

a) Cannot produce any nutritious crops
b) Not suitable for staple crops like grains and legumes at current economics
c) Requires too much water
d) Produces contaminated food

**Correct Answer:** b) Not suitable for staple crops like grains and legumes at current economics
