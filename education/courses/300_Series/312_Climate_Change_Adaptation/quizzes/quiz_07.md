## Questions

**1. What is the largest energy consumption category in most CEA facilities?**

a) Pumps and irrigation
b) Lighting (in operations without natural light)
c) Climate control (HVAC)
d) Monitoring systems

**Correct Answer:** b) Lighting (in operations without natural light) - typically 40-60% of total energy

---

**2. How much can LED lighting reduce energy consumption compared to traditional HPS (High Pressure Sodium) lights?**

a) 10-20%
b) 20-30%
c) 30-50%
d) 50-70%

**Correct Answer:** c) 30-50% (with better spectrum control)

---

**3. What is the primary benefit of integrating solar PV with CEA operations?**

a) Aesthetic improvement
b) Reduced peak demand charges and renewable energy generation
c) Increased water use
d) Lower labor costs

**Correct Answer:** b) Reduced peak demand charges and renewable energy generation

---

**4. What percentage of energy can efficient climate control systems (heat recovery, thermal mass) save?**

a) 5-10%
b) 15-25%
c) 30-60%
d) 70-80%

**Correct Answer:** c) 30-60%

---

**5. Which energy storage solution is most practical for CEA operations with renewable energy?**

a) Pumped hydro storage
b) Battery storage (lithium-ion or flow batteries)
c) Compressed air
d) Flywheels

**Correct Answer:** b) Battery storage (lithium-ion or flow batteries)

---

**6. What is thermal mass in the context of CEA energy management?**

a) The weight of heating equipment
b) Materials that absorb and store heat to reduce temperature fluctuations
c) The mass of plants grown
d) Insulation thickness

**Correct Answer:** b) Materials that absorb and store heat to reduce temperature fluctuations

---

**7. How can CEA facilities participate in demand response programs?**

a) Increasing energy use during peak times
b) Ignoring utility signals
c) Temporarily reducing non-critical loads during peak demand periods
d) Running 24/7 at maximum capacity

**Correct Answer:** c) Temporarily reducing non-critical loads during peak demand periods

---

**8. What is the typical efficiency improvement from using waste heat recovery in CEA?**

a) 2-5%
b) 10-15%
c) 20-40%
d) 60-80%

**Correct Answer:** c) 20-40% (capturing heat from lights, dehumidifiers, etc.)

---

**9. Which renewable energy source provides the best match for CEA's daytime energy peak?**

a) Wind power
b) Solar photovoltaic
c) Tidal power
d) Biomass

**Correct Answer:** b) Solar photovoltaic (matches lighting demand)

---

**10. What is the carbon intensity goal for sustainable CEA operations?**

a) 500+ g CO2/kWh
b) 200-300 g CO2/kWh
c) 50-100 g CO2/kWh or lower (approaching carbon neutral)
d) No specific goal needed

**Correct Answer:** c) 50-100 g CO2/kWh or lower (approaching carbon neutral)
