## Questions

**1. What is resilience in the context of agricultural systems?**

a) Maximum production efficiency
b) The capacity to absorb disturbance, reorganize, and retain essential function
c) The ability to eliminate all risks
d) Having the lowest operating costs

**Correct Answer:** b) The capacity to absorb disturbance, reorganize, and retain essential function

---

**2. What are the three phases of resilience?**

a) Planning, Implementation, Evaluation
b) Resistance, Recovery, Reorganization
c) Start, Middle, End
d) Prevention, Response, Cleanup

**Correct Answer:** b) Resistance, Recovery, Reorganization

---

**3. Which resilience principle involves having backup systems and redundant capacity?**

a) Diversity
b) Modularity
c) Redundancy
d) Feedback

**Correct Answer:** c) Redundancy

---

**4. How does diversity enhance resilience in CEA operations?**

a) By growing only one crop very well
b) By using multiple crop varieties, systems, and suppliers
c) By reducing staff count
d) By eliminating all backup systems

**Correct Answer:** b) By using multiple crop varieties, systems, and suppliers

---

**5. What does modularity mean in resilient system design?**

a) Using the newest technology
b) Having interconnected systems where failure of one affects all
c) Having independent units that can function separately
d) Maximizing system complexity

**Correct Answer:** c) Having independent units that can function separately

---

**6. Why are feedback loops essential for resilience?**

a) They increase costs
b) They slow down operations
c) They enable early detection of problems and adaptive responses
d) They eliminate the need for monitoring

**Correct Answer:** c) They enable early detection of problems and adaptive responses

---

**7. What is the trade-off between efficiency and resilience?**

a) There is no trade-off
b) Resilience costs slightly more but performs better under stress
c) Efficiency always outperforms resilience
d) Resilience eliminates the need for efficiency

**Correct Answer:** b) Resilience costs slightly more but performs better under stress

---

**8. Which is an example of redundancy in CEA operations?**

a) Using only one climate control unit
b) Having a single supplier for all inputs
c) Backup generators, multiple HVAC units, spare parts inventory
d) Eliminating all backup systems to reduce costs

**Correct Answer:** c) Backup generators, multiple HVAC units, spare parts inventory

---

**9. What is adaptive capacity in resilience thinking?**

a) The ability to maintain status quo indefinitely
b) The ability to learn, adjust, and transform in response to change
c) Resistance to any change
d) Having no contingency plans

**Correct Answer:** b) The ability to learn, adjust, and transform in response to change

---

**10. How does CEA's controlled environment contribute to resilience?**

a) It eliminates the need for monitoring
b) It decouples production from external climate variability
c) It makes systems more complex and fragile
d) It increases dependence on weather

**Correct Answer:** b) It decouples production from external climate variability
