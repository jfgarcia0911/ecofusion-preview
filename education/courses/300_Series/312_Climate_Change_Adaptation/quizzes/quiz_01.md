## Questions

**1. What is the primary difference between weather and climate?**

a) Weather is short-term atmospheric conditions; climate is long-term patterns
b) Weather affects agriculture; climate does not
c) Weather is predictable; climate is random
d) There is no meaningful difference

**Correct Answer:** a) Weather is short-term atmospheric conditions; climate is long-term patterns

---

**2. Which greenhouse gas has the highest global warming potential (100-year timeframe)?**

a) Carbon dioxide (CO2)
b) Methane (CH4)
c) Nitrous oxide (N2O)
d) Water vapor

**Correct Answer:** c) Nitrous oxide (N2O) - 298x more potent than CO2

---

**3. How much has global average temperature increased since 1880?**

a) 0.5°C
b) 1.1°C
c) 2.5°C
d) 5.0°C

**Correct Answer:** b) 1.1°C

---

**4. What percentage of agricultural emissions come from methane (CH4)?**

a) 13%
b) 25%
c) 44%
d) 75%

**Correct Answer:** c) 44%

---

**5. Which of the following is a positive feedback loop that amplifies climate change?**

a) Carbon dioxide fertilization
b) Increased cloud formation
c) Ice-albedo feedback
d) Ocean carbon absorption

**Correct Answer:** c) Ice-albedo feedback

---

**6. What is the current concentration of atmospheric CO2 (approximate)?**

a) 280 ppm (pre-industrial level)
b) 350 ppm
c) 420 ppm
d) 550 ppm

**Correct Answer:** c) 420 ppm

---

**7. According to IPCC scenarios, what is the projected global temperature increase by 2100 under a high-emissions scenario (SSP5-8.5)?**

a) +1.4°C
b) +2.7°C
c) +4.4°C
d) +6.0°C

**Correct Answer:** c) +4.4°C

---

**8. Which region of the U.S. is experiencing the fastest rate of warming?**

a) Southeast
b) Northeast
c) Southwest
d) Arctic/Alaska

**Correct Answer:** d) Arctic/Alaska (2-3x faster than global average)

---

**9. What is the primary advantage of CEA in eliminating N2O and CH4 emissions?**

a) CEA uses no nutrients
b) CEA shifts emissions to CO2 from electricity, which can be decarbonized
c) CEA produces no emissions at all
d) CEA plants absorb all greenhouse gases

**Correct Answer:** b) CEA shifts emissions to CO2 from electricity, which can be decarbonized

---

**10. Which climate data source is best for site-specific planning at the local level?**

a) IPCC global reports
b) PRISM Climate Group (high-resolution spatial data)
c) Historical newspapers
d) Social media weather posts

**Correct Answer:** b) PRISM Climate Group (high-resolution spatial data)
