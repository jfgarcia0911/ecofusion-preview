### Question 1
What is the primary advantage of precision agriculture over traditional farming methods?

A) It requires less initial investment
B) It uses experience and intuition for decision-making
C) It provides real-time, data-driven insights for optimization
D) It eliminates the need for human oversight

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Precision agriculture's core advantage is the ability to make data-driven decisions based on real-time quantitative analysis, allowing for continuous optimization that scales beyond human capacity.

---

### Question 2
In the Data Maturity Model, which level represents systematic automated data collection with centralized storage?

A) Level 1 (Ad-hoc Observation)
B) Level 2 (Manual Documentation)
C) Level 3 (Systematic Collection)
D) Level 5 (Predictive & Prescriptive)

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Level 3 is characterized by automated data logging, basic sensors deployed, centralized database, and simple reports—the foundation for analytics.

### Question 3
According to the case study in Module 1, what was the typical ROI (Return on Investment) achieved in the first year for a medium-scale aquaponics operation implementing precision agriculture?

A) 50-100%
B) 150-200%
C) 250-300%
D) 400-500%
