# Grading Rubric
## Course 304: Precision Agriculture Data Analytics

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 304. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Data Collection System Design | 15 | 13.6% | In-class activity designing sensor networks |
| Assignment 2: Data Visualization Dashboard | 20 | 18.2% | In-class dashboard creation exercise |
| Assignment 3: Predictive Model Development | 25 | 22.7% | In-class machine learning model building |
| Assignment 4: Analytics Implementation Plan | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home system integration planning |
| Assignment 5: Comprehensive Analytics Strategy | 30 | 27.3% | Complete data-driven operations framework |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

---

## Assignment 1: Data Collection System Design (15 points)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Sensor Selection** (5 pts) | Optimal sensor types identified with technical justification | Good sensor choices with basic rationale | Limited sensor variety or poor selection | Inadequate sensor planning |
| **Network Architecture** (5 pts) | Comprehensive network design with communication protocols | Basic network structure outlined | Incomplete network design | No network architecture |
| **Data Collection Strategy** (5 pts) | Detailed collection intervals and storage plan | Reasonable collection approach | Vague collection strategy | No collection planning |

**Total:** 15 points

## Assignment 2: Data Visualization Dashboard (20 points)
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Dashboard Design** (5 pts) | Intuitive layout with all critical KPIs visible | Good design with minor usability issues | Cluttered or incomplete dashboard | Poor dashboard design |

| **User Experience** (5 pts) | Excellent usability for farm operators | Good user interface | Basic functionality | Poor user experience |

**Total:** 20 points

## Assignment 5: Comprehensive Analytics Strategy (30 points)

**Note:** A minimum of 80% (88 points) is required to pass this course and receive a Certificate of Completion.

---

## Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% penalty
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% penalty
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval
- Extensions may be granted for extenuating circumstances with advance notice

---

## Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. While collaboration and discussion are encouraged during in-class activities, individual assignments must reflect your own analysis and work. Plagiarism or copying will result in a zero for the assignment and may result in dismissal from the course.

---

## Feedback and Revision

- In-class activities receive immediate feedback
- Take-home assignments graded within 5 business days
- Final projects graded within 7 business days
- Students may request clarification on grading decisions
- Major projects may be revised and resubmitted once if score is below 80%
