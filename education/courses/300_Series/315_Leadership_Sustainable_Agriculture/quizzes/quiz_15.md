**Points:** 3 points
**Passing Score:** 80%

---

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. A Personal Leadership Development Plan should include:
a) Only technical skills
b) Assessment, goals, strategies, timeline, and measurement
c) Vague aspirations only
d) No specific plans

### 2. 360-degree feedback provides:
a) Only your own perspective
b) Input from supervisors, peers, and direct reports
c) Only criticism
d) No useful information

### 3. SMART goals are:
a) Only about intelligence
b) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
c) Vague and general
d) Impossible to achieve

### 4. Why is self-reflection important for leadership development?
a) It's not important
b) It builds self-awareness and learning from experience
c) It wastes time
d) It only creates doubt

### 5. Accountability partners help development by:
a) Doing the work for you
b) Providing support, feedback, and accountability
c) Criticizing everything
d) Creating competition

### 6. Leadership development requires:
a) One-time training only
b) Continuous learning and deliberate practice
c) No ongoing effort
d) Only reading books

### 7. Seeking stretch assignments means:
a) Avoiding all challenges
b) Taking on opportunities that expand capabilities
c) Only doing what's comfortable
d) Delegating everything

### 8. What is the purpose of identifying your leadership gaps?
a) To feel inadequate
b) To focus development efforts on high-impact areas
c) To give up on leadership
d) To hide weaknesses

### 9. Leadership philosophy statements should:
a) Be copied from famous leaders
b) Reflect your authentic values and beliefs
c) Never be revised
d) Be kept completely private

### 10. Measuring leadership development progress involves:
a) Only subjective feelings
b) Feedback, behavioral changes, and impact metrics
c) No measurement needed
d) Waiting for promotion

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Assessment, goals, strategies, timeline, and measurement**
   *Explanation:* Comprehensive development plans include current assessment, specific goals, development strategies, timeline with milestones, and measurement methods.

2. **b) Input from supervisors, peers, and direct reports**
   *Explanation:* 360-degree feedback gathers input from multiple perspectives - supervisors, peers, direct reports, and self - providing well-rounded view.

3. **b) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound**
   *Explanation:* SMART goals are Specific (clear), Measurable (trackable), Achievable (realistic), Relevant (meaningful), and Time-bound (deadline).

4. **b) It builds self-awareness and learning from experience**
   *Explanation:* Self-reflection builds self-awareness, helps extract learning from experiences, identifies patterns, and guides future behavior.

5. **b) Providing support, feedback, and accountability**
   *Explanation:* Accountability partners provide encouragement, honest feedback, accountability to commitments, and support through challenges.

6. **b) Continuous learning and deliberate practice**
   *Explanation:* Leadership development is ongoing - requiring continuous learning, deliberate practice, feedback, and reflection over time.

7. **b) Taking on opportunities that expand capabilities**
   *Explanation:* Stretch assignments are opportunities slightly beyond current capabilities that challenge you to grow and develop new skills.

8. **b) To focus development efforts on high-impact areas**
   *Explanation:* Identifying gaps helps prioritize development efforts on areas with biggest impact on leadership effectiveness.

9. **b) Reflect your authentic values and beliefs**
   *Explanation:* Leadership philosophy should authentically reflect your values, beliefs about people, and principles - not copied from others.

10. **b) Feedback, behavioral changes, and impact metrics**
    *Explanation:* Progress measurement includes feedback from others, observable behavioral changes, and metrics showing leadership impact.
