# Quiz 12: Networking & Relationships

**Module:** Module 12 - Networking & Relationships
**Points:** 3 points
**Passing Score:** 80%

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

## Questions

### 1. Professional networking is primarily about:
a) Collecting business cards
b) Building genuine relationships of mutual value
c) Only getting what you need from others
d) Attending parties

### 2. Effective networkers focus on:
a) How they can help others
b) Only what others can do for them
c) Avoiding all follow-up
d) Superficial connections

### 3. When meeting someone new professionally, you should:
a) Immediately pitch your business
b) Show genuine interest and listen actively
c) Dominate the conversation
d) Avoid asking questions

### 4. Following up after networking events:
a) Is unnecessary and awkward
b) Should happen within a few days with personalized message
c) Can wait several months
d) Should be generic mass email

### 5. A strong professional network provides:
a) Only job opportunities
b) Knowledge, opportunities, support, and perspective
c) No real value
d) Only social connections

### 6. Maintaining relationships requires:
a) Only contacting people when you need something
b) Regular, genuine engagement and mutual support
c) No ongoing effort
d) Avoiding all communication

### 7. LinkedIn and professional platforms are most valuable when:
a) Used to share valuable content and engage meaningfully
b) Only viewing others' profiles
c) Used exclusively for job searching
d) Collecting connections without interaction

### 8. Mentorship relationships are:
a) One-way with no benefit to the mentor
b) Mutually beneficial with learning on both sides
c) Only for junior professionals
d) Waste of time for leaders

### 9. Strategic relationship-building means:
a) Being fake and manipulative
b) Intentionally cultivating relationships aligned with goals and values
c) Avoiding all planning
d) Only networking within your industry

### 10. The key to networking authentically is:
a) Pretending to be someone you're not
b) Being genuine and finding common ground
c) Only talking about yourself
d) Using memorized scripts

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Building genuine relationships of mutual value**
   *Explanation:* Professional networking is about building authentic relationships where both parties can provide and receive value over time.

2. **a) How they can help others**
   *Explanation:* Effective networkers focus on how they can provide value to others, building relationships based on generosity and mutual support.

3. **b) Show genuine interest and listen actively**
   *Explanation:* When meeting new people, show genuine interest, ask thoughtful questions, listen actively, and look for common ground.

4. **b) Should happen within a few days with personalized message**
   *Explanation:* Follow up within a few days while the conversation is fresh, with a personalized message referencing your discussion.

5. **b) Knowledge, opportunities, support, and perspective**
   *Explanation:* A strong network provides access to knowledge, opportunities, emotional support, diverse perspectives, and resources.

6. **b) Regular, genuine engagement and mutual support**
   *Explanation:* Relationships require ongoing nurturing through regular contact, genuine interest, mutual support, and adding value consistently.

7. **a) Used to share valuable content and engage meaningfully**
   *Explanation:* Professional platforms are most valuable when used to share insights, engage in meaningful discussions, and build genuine connections.

8. **b) Mutually beneficial with learning on both sides**
   *Explanation:* Mentorship relationships benefit both parties - mentees gain guidance while mentors gain fresh perspectives and fulfillment.

9. **b) Intentionally cultivating relationships aligned with goals and values**
   *Explanation:* Strategic networking means being intentional about cultivating relationships that align with your goals, values, and areas where you can add mutual value.

10. **b) Being genuine and finding common ground**
    *Explanation:* Authentic networking requires being genuine, showing real interest, finding common ground, and building connections based on shared values or interests.
