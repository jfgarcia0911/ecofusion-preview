# Quiz 6: Communication Mastery

**Module:** Module 6 - Communication Mastery
**Points:** 3 points

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

---

## Questions

### 1. Effective communication is:
a) One-way transmission of information
b) Two-way exchange ensuring shared understanding
c) Only verbal conversations
d) Sending emails frequently

### 2. Active listening involves:
a) Waiting for your turn to talk
b) Fully concentrating, understanding, and responding thoughtfully
c) Interrupting with your opinions
d) Thinking about other things while someone talks

### 3. Which is NOT a barrier to effective communication?
a) Assumptions and biases
b) Emotional reactions
c) Active listening
d) Physical distractions

### 4. Nonverbal communication includes:
a) Only spoken words
b) Body language, facial expressions, and tone
c) Written memos
d) Email subject lines

### 5. When giving feedback, leaders should:
a) Be vague to avoid hurt feelings
b) Be specific, timely, and behavior-focused
c) Only give negative feedback
d) Avoid feedback entirely

### 6. What is the purpose of asking open-ended questions?
a) To get yes/no answers quickly
b) To encourage reflection and deeper conversation
c) To confuse people
d) To avoid listening

### 7. In conflict situations, effective communicators:
a) Avoid the conversation
b) Seek first to understand, then to be understood
c) Immediately assign blame
d) Send an angry email

### 8. Transparent communication in leadership means:
a) Sharing all confidential information
b) Being honest, open, and sharing appropriate information
c) Never communicating bad news
d) Only communicating when required

### 9. When communicating vision, leaders should:
a) Use jargon and technical language
b) Tell compelling stories and paint vivid pictures
c) Keep it secret until fully formed
d) Only communicate once

### 10. Cross-cultural communication requires:
a) Assuming everyone is the same
b) Awareness of different communication styles and norms
c) Avoiding people from different cultures
d) Only using your own cultural approach

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Two-way exchange ensuring shared understanding**
   *Explanation:* Effective communication is a two-way process of exchanging information and ensuring mutual understanding - not just transmitting information.

2. **b) Fully concentrating, understanding, and responding thoughtfully**
   *Explanation:* Active listening means giving full attention, seeking to understand, asking clarifying questions, and responding appropriately.

3. **c) Active listening**
   *Explanation:* Active listening is a communication enabler, not a barrier. Barriers include assumptions, emotions, noise, and distractions.

4. **b) Body language, facial expressions, and tone**
   *Explanation:* Nonverbal communication includes body language, facial expressions, tone of voice, gestures, and eye contact - often conveying more than words.

5. **b) Be specific, timely, and behavior-focused**
   *Explanation:* Effective feedback is specific (what exactly happened), timely (close to the event), behavior-focused (not personality), and balanced.

6. **b) To encourage reflection and deeper conversation**
   *Explanation:* Open-ended questions (how, what, why) encourage thinking, reflection, and deeper dialogue rather than simple yes/no responses.

7. **b) Seek first to understand, then to be understood**
   *Explanation:* In conflict, effective communicators listen to understand the other perspective before advocating their own position.

8. **b) Being honest, open, and sharing appropriate information**
   *Explanation:* Transparent communication means being honest and open, sharing appropriate information while respecting confidentiality and boundaries.

9. **b) Tell compelling stories and paint vivid pictures**
   *Explanation:* Vision communication should use stories, metaphors, and vivid imagery to make the future state compelling and memorable.

10. **b) Awareness of different communication styles and norms**
    *Explanation:* Cross-cultural communication requires awareness that different cultures have different communication styles, directness levels, and interpretations.
