## Questions

### 1. Conflict in organizations is:
a) Always destructive and should be eliminated
b) Natural and can be productive if managed well
c) A sign of poor leadership
d) Only about personality clashes

### 2. Which conflict resolution style involves high assertiveness and high cooperation?
a) Avoiding
b) Accommodating
c) Competing
d) Collaborating

### 3. The avoiding conflict style is most appropriate when:
a) The issue is important to you
b) The issue is trivial or cooling off period is needed
c) Always - avoid all conflict
d) You want to damage relationships

### 4. Competing conflict style should be used when:
a) Relationships are most important
b) You don't care about the outcome
c) Quick decisive action is needed or principles are at stake
d) You want to avoid responsibility

### 5. What is the key to the collaborating approach?
a) One party wins, other loses
b) Both parties avoid the issue
c) Finding win-win solutions meeting both parties' needs
d) Quick compromise without discussion

### 6. When mediating conflict between team members, leaders should:
a) Take sides immediately
b) Remain neutral and help parties find resolution
c) Ignore the conflict
d) Fire both parties

### 7. Task conflict is:
a) Personal attacks between individuals
b) Disagreement about ideas, approaches, or solutions
c) Always destructive
d) Unrelated to work

### 8. Relationship conflict is:
a) Healthy debate about work approaches
b) Personal friction and interpersonal tension
c) Always productive
d) Easy to resolve

### 9. To prevent destructive conflict, leaders should:
a) Suppress all disagreement
b) Create clear norms for healthy debate
c) Encourage personal attacks
d) Ignore brewing tensions

### 10. The first step in addressing conflict is:
a) Assigning blame
b) Understanding each party's perspective
c) Making a quick decision
d) Avoiding the parties involved

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Natural and can be productive if managed well**
   *Explanation:* Conflict is natural in organizations and, when managed constructively, can lead to better decisions, innovation, and stronger relationships.

2. **d) Collaborating**
   *Explanation:* The collaborating style combines high assertiveness (pursuing own concerns) with high cooperation (addressing others' concerns) to find win-win solutions.

3. **b) The issue is trivial or cooling off period is needed**
   *Explanation:* Avoiding is appropriate when the issue is trivial, costs of confronting outweigh benefits, or when cooling off time is needed.

4. **c) Quick decisive action is needed or principles are at stake**
   *Explanation:* Competing is appropriate in emergencies requiring quick action, when enforcing rules, or when standing up for important principles.

5. **c) Finding win-win solutions meeting both parties' needs**
   *Explanation:* Collaborating seeks to fully satisfy both parties' concerns through creative problem-solving and win-win solutions.

6. **b) Remain neutral and help parties find resolution**
   *Explanation:* When mediating, leaders should remain neutral, ensure both parties are heard, facilitate dialogue, and help them find mutually acceptable solutions.

7. **b) Disagreement about ideas, approaches, or solutions**
   *Explanation:* Task conflict involves disagreement about work content, ideas, and approaches - it can be productive if managed well.

8. **b) Personal friction and interpersonal tension**
   *Explanation:* Relationship conflict involves personal friction, tension, and animosity - generally destructive and should be addressed quickly.

9. **b) Create clear norms for healthy debate**
   *Explanation:* Prevention involves establishing norms for respectful disagreement, building trust, clarifying roles, and addressing issues early.

10. **b) Understanding each party's perspective**
    *Explanation:* Effective conflict resolution begins with understanding all perspectives through active listening before moving toward solutions.
