## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Each question is worth 0.3 points.

## Questions

### 1. What is the primary difference between vision and mission?
a) Vision is the future state; mission is what you do
b) Vision and mission are the same thing
c) Mission is long-term; vision is short-term
d) Vision is for profits; mission is for values

### 2. Which element answers the question "Why do we exist?"
a) Vision
b) Mission
c) Purpose
d) Goals

### 3. A compelling organizational vision should include all EXCEPT:
a) Purpose - why you exist
b) Detailed daily procedures
c) Values - how you operate
d) Goals - measurable targets

### 4. In strategic planning, what is the first phase?
a) Implementation
b) Assessment
c) Direction
d) Review

### 5. Which strategic analysis tool examines Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats?
a) PEST Analysis
b) SWOT Analysis
c) Porter's Five Forces
d) Balanced Scorecard

### 6. What should goals in a strategic plan be?
a) Vague and aspirational
b) Measurable and specific
c) Unchangeable once set
d) Kept confidential from the team

### 7. How often should strategic plans be reviewed and adjusted?
a) Never - stick to the original plan
b) Only when crisis occurs
c) Continuously with regular reviews
d) Every 10 years

### 8. Which is an example of a well-crafted vision statement for a sustainable agriculture operation?
a) "To make money from farming"
b) "Regional leader in sustainable food production by 2030"
c) "We grow lettuce"
d) "Do good things"

### 9. In the Strategic Planning Framework, what comes after setting direction?
a) Assessment
b) Review
c) Strategy development
d) Celebration

### 10. Core values in an organization should:
a) Change frequently based on trends
b) Guide how the organization operates
c) Be kept secret from employees
d) Only apply to leadership

---

## Answer Key

1. **a) Vision is the future state; mission is what you do**
   *Explanation:* Vision describes the desired future state, while mission describes what the organization does to achieve that vision.

2. **c) Purpose**
   *Explanation:* Purpose answers "why do we exist," vision is the future state, and mission is what you do.

3. **b) Detailed daily procedures**
   *Explanation:* Vision includes purpose, values, and goals but not detailed operational procedures - those belong in operational plans.

4. **b) Assessment**
   *Explanation:* The Strategic Planning Framework begins with Assessment (SWOT, market analysis, stakeholders) to understand the current state.

5. **b) SWOT Analysis**
   *Explanation:* SWOT Analysis examines internal Strengths and Weaknesses and external Opportunities and Threats.

6. **b) Measurable and specific**
   *Explanation:* Strategic goals should be measurable, specific, and provide clear targets (e.g., "Feed 10,000 families by 2027").

7. **c) Continuously with regular reviews**
   *Explanation:* The Review phase of strategic planning includes continuous improvement with regular KPI reviews and adjustments.

8. **b) "Regional leader in sustainable food production by 2030"**
   *Explanation:* This vision is specific, inspiring, future-focused, and measurable - characteristics of a well-crafted vision statement.

9. **c) Strategy development**
   *Explanation:* The framework flows: Assessment → Direction → Strategy → Implementation → Review.

10. **b) Guide how the organization operates**
    *Explanation:* Core values guide how the organization operates and should remain stable, applying to all members of the organization.
