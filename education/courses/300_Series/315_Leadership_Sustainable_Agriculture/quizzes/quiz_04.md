## Questions

### 1. According to Tuckman's model, which stage comes first in team development?
a) Performing
b) Norming
c) Forming
d) Storming

### 2. During the "Storming" stage, the leader's primary role is to:
a) Delegate completely
b) Coach and mediate conflict
c) Direct and clarify
d) Celebrate and transition

### 3. What characterizes a high-performing team?
a) Avoiding all conflict
b) Working in isolation
c) Trust, commitment, and accountability
d) Individual competition

### 4. According to Patrick Lencioni's model, what is the foundation of team effectiveness?
a) Results
b) Accountability
c) Trust
d) Commitment

### 5. Productive conflict in teams is about:
a) Personal attacks and blame
b) Debating ideas to find best solutions
c) Avoiding disagreement
d) Winning arguments

### 6. What happens when teams lack commitment?
a) They achieve better results
b) They have clarity and alignment
c) They experience ambiguity and lack of buy-in
d) They work more efficiently

### 7. Peer accountability in teams means:
a) Only the leader holds people accountable
b) Team members hold each other accountable to standards
c) No one is held accountable
d) Accountability is optional

### 8. In team formation, what should be clarified first?
a) Individual bonuses
b) Exit strategy
c) Purpose, goals, and roles
d) Vacation schedules

### 9. Diversity in teams:
a) Should be avoided to reduce conflict
b) Strengthens teams through varied perspectives
c) Only matters for marketing
d) Slows down decision-making without benefits

### 10. The leader's role in the "Performing" stage is to:
a) Micromanage all activities
b) Empower and challenge the team
c) Take over all decision-making
d) Disband the team

---

## Answer Key

1. **c) Forming**
   *Explanation:* Tuckman's team development stages progress: Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → Adjourning.

2. **b) Coach and mediate conflict**
   *Explanation:* During Storming, when conflict and competition emerge, the leader's role is to coach and mediate to help the team work through challenges.

3. **c) Trust, commitment, and accountability**
   *Explanation:* High-performing teams are characterized by trust, healthy conflict, commitment, accountability, and focus on collective results.

4. **c) Trust**
   *Explanation:* Lencioni's pyramid places Trust at the foundation - vulnerability-based trust enables all other aspects of team effectiveness.

5. **b) Debating ideas to find best solutions**
   *Explanation:* Productive conflict involves passionate debate around ideas and solutions, not personal attacks - it leads to better decisions.

6. **c) They experience ambiguity and lack of buy-in**
   *Explanation:* Without commitment to decisions and goals, teams suffer from ambiguity, lack of clarity, and poor buy-in.

7. **b) Team members hold each other accountable to standards**
   *Explanation:* Peer accountability means team members hold each other accountable to high standards, not just relying on the leader.

8. **c) Purpose, goals, and roles**
   *Explanation:* Effective team formation begins with clarity on why the team exists, what it aims to achieve, and who is responsible for what.

9. **b) Strengthens teams through varied perspectives**
   *Explanation:* Diversity brings varied perspectives, experiences, and ideas that strengthen problem-solving and innovation.

10. **b) Empower and challenge the team**
    *Explanation:* In the Performing stage, when teams are highly productive, leaders should empower them and provide challenging opportunities.
