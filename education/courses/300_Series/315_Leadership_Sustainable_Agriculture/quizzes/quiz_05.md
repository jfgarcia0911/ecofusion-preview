## Questions

### 1. Emotional Intelligence (EQ) is defined as:
a) The ability to suppress all emotions
b) The ability to understand and manage emotions in self and others
c) Having a high IQ
d) Being overly emotional

### 2. Which is NOT one of the five components of Emotional Intelligence?
a) Self-awareness
b) Self-regulation
c) Technical skills
d) Empathy

### 3. Self-awareness in emotional intelligence means:
a) Ignoring your emotions
b) Understanding your own emotions and their impact
c) Always staying positive
d) Copying others' emotions

### 4. Self-regulation involves:
a) Letting emotions control behavior
b) Managing and controlling emotional responses
c) Never experiencing negative emotions
d) Expressing every emotion immediately

### 5. What is empathy?
a) Feeling sorry for people
b) Understanding and sharing others' emotional experiences
c) Agreeing with everyone
d) Ignoring others' feelings

### 6. Social skills in EQ include:
a) Avoiding all social interactions
b) Building rapport, influencing, and managing relationships
c) Only technical competencies
d) Working alone always

### 7. How can leaders develop self-awareness?
a) Avoiding feedback
b) Reflection, feedback, and assessment
c) Ignoring emotional reactions
d) Never questioning themselves

### 8. Why is emotional intelligence important for leaders?
a) It's not important for leadership
b) It enables effective relationships and influence
c) It replaces the need for technical skills
d) It only matters in personal life

### 9. A leader with high self-regulation would:
a) React impulsively to stress
b) Stay calm and think before acting under pressure
c) Avoid all stressful situations
d) Blame others for problems

### 10. Intrinsic motivation, a component of EQ, is characterized by:
a) External rewards only
b) Internal drive and commitment to goals
c) Avoiding challenges
d) Lack of ambition

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) The ability to understand and manage emotions in self and others**
   *Explanation:* Emotional Intelligence is the capacity to recognize, understand, and manage emotions in yourself and in others.

2. **c) Technical skills**
   *Explanation:* The five components of EQ are: Self-awareness, Self-regulation, Motivation, Empathy, and Social Skills - not technical skills.

3. **b) Understanding your own emotions and their impact**
   *Explanation:* Self-awareness means recognizing your emotions as they happen and understanding how they affect your behavior and others.

4. **b) Managing and controlling emotional responses**
   *Explanation:* Self-regulation is the ability to manage emotions, stay calm under pressure, think before acting, and maintain integrity.

5. **b) Understanding and sharing others' emotional experiences**
   *Explanation:* Empathy is the ability to understand others' perspectives, sense their emotions, and recognize emotional dynamics.

6. **b) Building rapport, influencing, and managing relationships**
   *Explanation:* Social skills include building rapport, persuading and influencing, leading teams, and managing conflict constructively.

7. **b) Reflection, feedback, and assessment**
   *Explanation:* Self-awareness develops through regular reflection, seeking feedback from others, and using assessment tools.

8. **b) It enables effective relationships and influence**
   *Explanation:* EQ is critical for leadership because it enables building relationships, influencing others, managing teams, and navigating complex social dynamics.

9. **b) Stay calm and think before acting under pressure**
   *Explanation:* High self-regulation allows leaders to stay calm under pressure, think before acting, and adapt to changing situations.

10. **b) Internal drive and commitment to goals**
    *Explanation:* Intrinsic motivation means being driven by internal standards, committed to goals despite obstacles, and optimistic when facing setbacks.
