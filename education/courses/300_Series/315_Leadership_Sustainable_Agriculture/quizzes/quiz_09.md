## Questions

### 1. According to Kotter's model, the first step in leading change is:
a) Declaring victory
b) Creating a sense of urgency
c) Implementing solutions
d) Celebrating success

### 2. The three phases of change in Lewin's model are:
a) Start, Middle, End
b) Plan, Do, Review
c) Unfreeze, Change, Refreeze
d) Think, Act, Reflect

### 3. Why do people typically resist change?
a) They are lazy and unmotivated
b) Fear of unknown, loss of control, or competence concerns
c) Change is always bad
d) They enjoy causing problems

### 4. To overcome resistance to change, leaders should:
a) Force compliance through authority
b) Ignore resistance and push forward
c) Communicate, involve, and support people through transition
d) Fire all resisters

### 5. Quick wins in change processes are important because they:
a) Allow leaders to declare victory early
b) Build momentum and demonstrate feasibility
c) Aren't important at all
d) Replace the need for long-term change

### 6. What is a change coalition?
a) Opposition group fighting change
b) Group of influential people supporting and driving change
c) Consultants brought in from outside
d) Unchanged traditions

### 7. Anchoring change in culture means:
a) Going back to old ways
b) Embedding new practices in systems, values, and norms
c) Stopping all change efforts
d) Hiring all new staff

### 8. During change, transparent communication should:
a) Only share good news
b) Be honest about challenges and progress
c) Be minimized to avoid concern
d) Wait until change is complete

### 9. What role do leaders play during organizational change?
a) Stay distant and uninvolved
b) Model desired behaviors and provide support
c) Only give orders from afar
d) Blame others for difficulties

### 10. The "transition" phase of change focuses on:
a) Maintaining status quo
b) The psychological process people go through adapting
c) Only technical implementation
d) Avoiding all emotions

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Creating a sense of urgency**
   *Explanation:* Kotter's first step is creating urgency - helping people see the compelling need for change and the risks of not changing.

2. **c) Unfreeze, Change, Refreeze**
   *Explanation:* Lewin's model describes three phases: Unfreeze (create readiness), Change (implement new way), Refreeze (sustain and anchor).

3. **b) Fear of unknown, loss of control, or competence concerns**
   *Explanation:* Resistance typically stems from fear of uncertainty, feeling powerless, concerns about competence, past failures, or different vision.

4. **c) Communicate, involve, and support people through transition**
   *Explanation:* Overcoming resistance requires clear communication, involvement in the process, training and support, and addressing concerns respectfully.

5. **b) Build momentum and demonstrate feasibility**
   *Explanation:* Quick wins build credibility, demonstrate that change is working, create momentum, and motivate continued effort.

6. **b) Group of influential people supporting and driving change**
   *Explanation:* A change coalition is a group of influential stakeholders who champion the change and help drive it through the organization.

7. **b) Embedding new practices in systems, values, and norms**
   *Explanation:* Anchoring change means embedding new practices in organizational systems, processes, values, and cultural norms so they persist.

8. **b) Be honest about challenges and progress**
   *Explanation:* Transparent communication during change means being honest about both successes and challenges, maintaining trust and credibility.

9. **b) Model desired behaviors and provide support**
   *Explanation:* Leaders must visibly model new behaviors, provide resources and support, remove obstacles, and stay engaged throughout the change.

10. **b) The psychological process people go through adapting**
    *Explanation:* The transition phase addresses the psychological journey people experience - letting go of the old and embracing the new.
