# Quiz 1: Leadership Foundations

**Course:** 315 - Leadership in Sustainable Agriculture
**Module:** 1 - Leadership Foundations
**Time Limit:** 15 minutes
**Passing Score:** 80% (8/10 correct)

---

## Instructions

Select the best answer for each question. This quiz assesses your understanding of leadership foundations, theories, styles, and their application to sustainable agriculture.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between leadership and management?

A) Leadership focuses on systems and processes; management focuses on people
B) Leadership creates change and direction; management maintains order and efficiency
C) Leadership is for executives; management is for supervisors
D) There is no meaningful difference between the two

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
According to the Integrated Leadership Model, what is the foundation of all effective leadership?

A) Vision
B) Execution
C) Self-leadership
D) Results

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
Which leadership style is most appropriate when a team member has high skill but low motivation?

A) Directing
B) Coaching
C) Supporting
D) Delegating

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
According to Goleman's six leadership styles, which style should be used most sparingly as it can damage climate if overused?

A) Visionary
B) Commanding
C) Democratic
D) Affiliative

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Transformational leadership involves all of the following EXCEPT:

A) Idealized influence (role modeling)
B) Inspirational motivation
C) Close supervision and control
D) Intellectual stimulation

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
In agricultural contexts, which leadership style would be most appropriate when implementing a new sustainable practice that the team is skeptical about?

A) Commanding - tell them to do it
B) Pacesetting - demonstrate excellence yourself
C) Visionary - paint picture of why it matters and where you're going
D) Avoiding - let them decide on their own

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
What is a key principle of Servant Leadership?

A) Leaders should be served first to maintain authority
B) Leaders serve followers first, helping them develop and perform
C) Leaders should avoid getting involved in followers' work
D) Leaders should primarily serve shareholders

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which of the following best describes authentic leadership?

A) Leading from your genuine self, aligning actions with values
B) Having an official leadership credential or certification
C) Leading exactly as your mentor or role model leads
D) Avoiding showing any weakness or uncertainty

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Question 9
What is the most effective approach to developing leadership flexibility?

A) Pick one leadership style and master it completely
B) Always use democratic style to build consensus
C) Develop capability in multiple styles and choose based on situation
D) Let your personality determine your style

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
In the context of sustainable agriculture, why is leadership particularly critical?

A) Agricultural businesses are more complex than other industries
B) You're often pioneering new approaches, challenging norms, and driving transformation
C) Farm workers require more direction than other employees
D) Government regulations require designated leaders

**Correct Answer:** B

---

## Answer Key

1. B - Leadership creates change and direction; management maintains order and efficiency
2. C - Self-leadership
3. C - Supporting
4. B - Commanding
5. C - Close supervision and control
6. C - Visionary - paint picture of why it matters and where you're going
7. B - Leaders serve followers first, helping them develop and perform
8. A - Leading from your genuine self, aligning actions with values
9. C - Develop capability in multiple styles and choose based on situation
10. B - You're often pioneering new approaches, challenging norms, and driving transformation
