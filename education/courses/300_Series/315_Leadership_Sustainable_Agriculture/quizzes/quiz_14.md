## Questions

### 1. The primary goal of mentoring is to:
a) Create dependency on the mentor
b) Develop the mentee's capabilities and confidence
c) Show the mentor's superiority
d) Complete the mentor's work

### 2. Effective mentors focus on:
a) Giving all the answers
b) Asking questions that promote reflection and learning
c) Doing the work for the mentee
d) Criticizing constantly

### 3. What is the difference between coaching and mentoring?
a) There is no difference
b) Coaching is skill-focused and shorter-term; mentoring is broader and longer-term
c) Coaching is for sports only
d) Mentoring is only for executives

### 4. When developing others, leaders should:
a) Clone themselves
b) Help people develop their unique strengths
c) Expect everyone to be the same
d) Avoid giving any feedback

### 5. Delegating for development means:
a) Dumping unwanted tasks
b) Assigning stretch opportunities with support
c) Avoiding all follow-up
d) Only delegating easy tasks

### 6. Constructive feedback should be:
a) Vague and general
b) Specific, timely, and focused on behavior
c) Only negative
d) Delivered publicly to embarrass

### 7. A growth mindset believes:
a) Abilities are fixed and unchangeable
b) Abilities can be developed through effort
c) Talent is everything
d) Learning is impossible after childhood

### 8. Creating a learning culture involves:
a) Punishing all mistakes harshly
b) Encouraging experimentation and learning from failures
c) Avoiding all risks
d) Only rewarding perfection

### 9. Succession planning is important because:
a) It's not important - leaders are irreplaceable
b) It ensures continuity and develops future leaders
c) It only matters in large corporations
d) It creates unnecessary competition

### 10. Developing others benefits the organization by:
a) Creating dependency and weakness
b) Building capabilities, engagement, and leadership pipeline
c) Wasting time and resources
d) Only helping individuals

---

## Answer Key

1. **b) Develop the mentee's capabilities and confidence**
   *Explanation:* Mentoring aims to develop the mentee's skills, confidence, network, and career - helping them reach their potential.

2. **b) Asking questions that promote reflection and learning**
   *Explanation:* Effective mentors ask powerful questions, share experiences when relevant, and help mentees develop their own insights rather than just giving answers.

3. **b) Coaching is skill-focused and shorter-term; mentoring is broader and longer-term**
   *Explanation:* Coaching typically focuses on specific skills/performance for shorter duration; mentoring is broader career/personal development over longer time.

4. **b) Help people develop their unique strengths**
   *Explanation:* Good development helps people identify and develop their unique strengths and potential - not creating clones of the leader.

5. **b) Assigning stretch opportunities with support**
   *Explanation:* Developmental delegation assigns challenging opportunities that stretch capabilities while providing support, resources, and feedback.

6. **b) Specific, timely, and focused on behavior**
   *Explanation:* Constructive feedback is specific (what exactly happened), timely (close to the event), behavior-focused (not personality), and balanced.

7. **b) Abilities can be developed through effort**
   *Explanation:* Growth mindset believes abilities and intelligence can be developed through dedication, learning, and practice - not fixed traits.

8. **b) Encouraging experimentation and learning from failures**
   *Explanation:* Learning cultures encourage experimentation, treat failures as learning opportunities, and value development and improvement.

9. **b) It ensures continuity and develops future leaders**
   *Explanation:* Succession planning ensures organizational continuity, develops leadership pipeline, and reduces risk of knowledge/capability loss.

10. **b) Building capabilities, engagement, and leadership pipeline**
    *Explanation:* Developing others builds organizational capabilities, increases engagement and retention, creates leadership pipeline, and strengthens performance.
