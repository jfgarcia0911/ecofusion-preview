# Leadership Styles Quick Reference Guide

**Course 315: Leadership in Sustainable Agriculture**

---

## Goleman's Six Leadership Styles

### Quick Decision Matrix

| Situation | Best Style | Why |
|-----------|------------|-----|
| Crisis, emergency | Commanding | Clear direction needed immediately |
| New direction needed | Visionary | Inspire toward future possibility |
| Developing team member | Coaching | Build capability through guidance |
| Team conflict, morale issues | Affiliative | Rebuild harmony and connections |
| Need buy-in for decision | Democratic | Consensus builds commitment |
| Quick results, you're the expert | Pacesetting | Model excellence, set high bar |

---

## Style Details

### 1. VISIONARY
**When:** New direction, change, unclear future
**Approach:** "Come with me" - Paint compelling future
**Impact:** Most positive, highly motivating
**Agricult use:** Launching regenerative transition, new business model

**Do:**
- Articulate clear vision
- Connect work to purpose
- Inspire possibilities
- Allow flexibility in how

**Don't:**
- Micromanage execution
- Get stuck in details
- Lose sight of current reality

---

### 2. COACHING
**When:** Long-term development, helping people improve
**Approach:** "Try this" - Questions and guidance
**Impact:** Highly positive, builds capability
**Ag use:** Developing junior growers, training new managers

**Do:**
- Ask powerful questions
- Listen actively
- Provide specific feedback
- Support experimentation

**Don't:**
- Give all answers
- Fix problems for them
- Use when time-critical

---

### 3. AFFILIATIVE
**When:** Building harmony, healing rifts, stressful times
**Approach:** "People first" - Empathy and connection
**Impact:** Positive, creates loyalty
**Ag use:** After conflict, during crisis, building team

**Do:**
- Show genuine care
- Build relationships
- Celebrate people
- Create belonging

**Don't:**
- Avoid tough feedback
- Let performance slide
- Use as only style

---

### 4. DEMOCRATIC
**When:** Need input, building buy-in, team has expertise
**Approach:** "What do you think?" - Consensus building
**Impact:** Positive, increases ownership
**Ag use:** Planning, process improvement, major decisions

**Do:**
- Seek diverse input
- Listen to all voices
- Build consensus
- Share decision-making

**Don't:**
- Use in emergencies
- Let it slow critical decisions
- Avoid ultimate responsibility

---

### 5. PACESETTING
**When:** High-performing team, you have expertise, urgency
**Approach:** "Do as I do" - Model excellence
**Impact:** Often negative if overused
**Ag use:** Harvest push, demonstration, short-term sprint

**Do:**
- Set high standards
- Lead by example
- Move quickly
- Expect excellence

**Don't:**
- Use long-term
- Neglect development
- Expect everyone to keep pace
- Forget to explain why

---

### 6. COMMANDING
**When:** Crisis, clear violation, need to turn around quickly
**Approach:** "Do what I say" - Direct and control
**Impact:** Negative if overused
**Ag use:** Food safety emergency, dangerous situation

**Do:**
- Be clear and direct
- Take control
- Make quick decisions
- Follow up firmly

**Don't:**
- Use as default style
- Forget to explain later
- Damage relationships
- Use for long periods

## Agricultural Application Examples

### Seasonal Cycle
- **Planning Season:** Democratic (get team input)
- **Planting Season:** Visionary + Commanding (inspire + direct)
- **Growing Season:** Coaching (develop skills)
- **Harvest Season:** Pacesetting (model excellence)
- **Off-Season:** Affiliative + Coaching (build team, develop)

### Team Member Development Stages
- **New hire:** Directing → Coaching
- **Developing competence:** Coaching → Democratic
- **Fully competent:** Democratic → Delegating
- **Expert:** Affiliative, Visionary

### Organizational Stages
- **Startup:** Visionary + Pacesetting
- **Growth:** Coaching + Democratic
- **Mature:** Democratic + Affiliative
- **Turnaround:** Commanding + Visionary

## Style Combination Strategies

**High Stress + Change:**
Visionary (where we're going) + Affiliative (we're in this together)

**Development Focus:**
Coaching (build skills) + Democratic (empower input)

**Performance Issues:**
Coaching (help improve) → Commanding (if no improvement)

**Team Building:**
Affiliative (build bonds) + Democratic (share decisions)

**Crisis Management:**
Commanding (immediate action) → Visionary (path forward) → Affiliative (heal)

## Self-Assessment: Your Style Preferences

**Rate yourself 1-5 on each style:**

- Visionary: ____ (Most comfortable when inspiring future)
- Coaching: ____ (Most comfortable when developing others)
- Affiliative: ____ (Most comfortable when building harmony)
- Democratic: ____ (Most comfortable when seeking consensus)
- Pacesetting: ____ (Most comfortable when modeling excellence)
- Commanding: ____ (Most comfortable when directing clearly)

**Highest scores:** Your natural styles - use strengths while watching for overuse

**Lowest scores:** Growth opportunity - practice these intentionally

**Goal:** Develop capability in all six, flexibly choosing based on situation

## Quick Tips

**Before choosing style, ask:**
1. What's the situation? (crisis, development, change, etc.)
2. What does the person/team need right now?
3. What's my goal? (results, development, harmony, innovation)
4. How much time do I have?
5. What style will be most effective here?

**Red flags you're using wrong style:**
- Team confused about direction (need Visionary)
- People not growing (need Coaching)
- Low morale, disconnection (need Affiliative)
- Resistance, lack of buy-in (need Democratic)
- Mediocre performance (maybe need Pacesetting)
- Chaos, lack of clarity (maybe need Commanding)

**Remember:**
- No one "right" style
- Situation determines best approach
- Flexibility is the key skill
- Develop all styles, don't just rely on natural preference
- Match style to person's development level
- Can combine multiple styles
- Overuse of any style becomes problematic

6. Two team members in ongoing conflict
7. New employee learning greenhouse systems

**Answers:**
1. Coaching → Commanding (if no improvement)
2. Visionary
3. Commanding
4. Democratic
5. Pacesetting
