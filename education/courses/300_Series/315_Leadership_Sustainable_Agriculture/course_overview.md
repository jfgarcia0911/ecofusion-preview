## Course Description

Develop the leadership capabilities to drive change in sustainable agriculture. This course covers leadership development, organizational change, industry advocacy, and thought leadership. Learn to build and lead organizations that make a lasting impact on food systems.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Apply leadership frameworks effectively
2. Build and lead high-performing organizations
3. Advocate for sustainable agriculture
4. Develop industry influence and networks
5. Drive systemic change in food systems
6. Create a personal leadership development plan

## Vision & Strategy

### Creating Compelling Vision

| Element | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Why you exist | "Nourish communities sustainably" |
| **Vision** | Future state | "Regional leader in sustainable food" |
| **Mission** | What you do | "Provide fresh, local produce year-round" |
| **Values** | How you operate | "Sustainability, quality, community" |
| **Goals** | Measurable targets | "Feed 10,000 families by 2027" |

### Strategic Planning Framework

| Phase | Activities | Outputs |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Assessment** | SWOT, market analysis, stakeholders | Current state understanding |

| **Strategy** | Goals, objectives, initiatives | Strategic plan |
| **Implementation** | Action plans, accountability, resources | Execution roadmap |
| **Review** | KPIs, reviews, adjustments | Continuous improvement |

## Change Leadership

### Change Management Model

| Phase | Focus | Actions |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Unfreeze** | Create urgency | Communicate need, build coalition |
| **Change** | Implement new way | Vision, empower, quick wins |
| **Refreeze** | Sustain change | Anchor in culture, systems |

### Overcoming Resistance

| Resistance Type | Root Cause | Strategy |
|-----------------|-----------|----------|
| **Fear of unknown** | Uncertainty | Communication, involvement |
| **Loss of control** | Powerlessness | Participation, choice |
| **Competence concerns** | Skill gaps | Training, support |
| **Past failures** | Mistrust | Quick wins, transparency |
| **Different vision** | Disagreement | Dialogue, alignment |

## Sustainability Leadership

### Triple Bottom Line Framework

| Dimension | Focus Areas | Metrics |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **People** | Workers, community, customers | Safety, wages, access |
| **Planet** | Environment, resources | Water, energy, waste, emissions |
| **Profit** | Financial sustainability | Revenue, margins, ROI |

### Sustainability Integration

| Level | Integration | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Compliance | Meet requirements | Permits, regulations |
| Efficiency | Cost savings | Energy reduction, waste |
| Strategic | Competitive advantage | Brand, markets, talent |
| Purpose | Core to mission | Regenerative practices |
| Systemic | Industry transformation | Advocacy, collaboration |
