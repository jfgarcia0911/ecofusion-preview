## Questions

### 1. Which U.S. regulatory framework governs food safety in CEA operations?
A) USDA GAP
B) FDA and FSMA
C) EPA Standards
D) State agriculture departments only

### 2. What is the primary food safety certification required in the European Union?
A) FDA certification
B) ISO 9001
C) HACCP
D) USDA Organic

### 3. Which certification standard is specific to Japan for agricultural products?
A) JGAP and JAS
B) GlobalGAP
C) USDA Organic
D) EU Organic

### 4. What additional certification may be required for CEA products in UAE markets?
A) USDA Organic
B) Kosher certification
C) Halal certification
D) Fair Trade

### 5. Which regulatory area shows the HIGHEST variation between countries?
A) Worker safety
B) Food safety standards
C) Building codes
D) Environmental regulations

### 6. What does "GB Standards" refer to in the Chinese market?
A) Green Building certification
B) Guobiao (national standards) for food safety
C) Greenhouse Business licenses
D) Global Benchmark certification

### 7. Which certification is most commonly required for European export markets?
A) USDA Organic
B) JAS
C) GlobalGAP
D) FDA approval

### 8. What regulatory challenge is unique to international trade in CEA products?
A) Labor laws
B) Phytosanitary and customs requirements
C) Energy regulations
D) Building permits

### 9. Which area has become increasingly strict across all international markets?
A) Marketing regulations
B) Environmental regulations (water, emissions, waste)
C) Packaging design
D) Transportation rules

### 10. What is a common regulatory consideration regarding foreign ownership in emerging markets?
A) Completely unrestricted
B) Variable restrictions and investment incentive programs
C) Always prohibited
D) Only allowed with government partnership

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) FDA and FSMA**
   *Explanation: The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) govern food safety for CEA operations.*

2. **C) HACCP**
   *Explanation: HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) is the primary food safety certification required in the EU.*

3. **A) JGAP and JAS**
   *Explanation: Japan requires JGAP (Japan Good Agricultural Practices) and JAS (Japanese Agricultural Standard) certifications.*

4. **C) Halal certification**
   *Explanation: Halal certification may be required or preferred for food products in UAE and other Middle Eastern markets.*

5. **B) Food safety standards**
   *Explanation: Food safety standards show the highest variation between countries, each with their own specific requirements.*

6. **B) Guobiao (national standards) for food safety**
   *Explanation: GB Standards refers to Guobiao, China's national standards system for food safety and quality.*

7. **C) GlobalGAP**
   *Explanation: GlobalGAP is the most commonly required certification for agricultural products entering European markets.*

8. **B) Phytosanitary and customs requirements**
   *Explanation: Phytosanitary certificates and customs requirements are complex and product-specific challenges unique to international trade.*

9. **B) Environmental regulations (water, emissions, waste)**
   *Explanation: Environmental regulations regarding water use, emissions, and waste management are becoming increasingly strict globally.*

10. **B) Variable restrictions and investment incentive programs**
    *Explanation: Foreign ownership regulations vary widely by country, with some restrictions and others offering incentives to attract investment.*
