## Questions

### 1. Which factor should carry the HIGHEST weight when evaluating international market opportunities?
A) Language similarity
B) Market size and growth rate
C) Tourism attractions
D) Time zone alignment

### 2. What is the recommended minimum timeline from market entry to revenue generation?
A) 3-6 months
B) 6-9 months
C) 12-24 months
D) 36-48 months

### 3. Which market characteristic indicates the best opportunity for technology sales?
A) Saturated market with many competitors
B) Growing market with government support and low competition
C) Declining market
D) Markets with no infrastructure

### 4. What is a critical success factor for international expansion?
A) Ignoring local culture
B) Local partnerships and market-specific expertise
C) Using only home country staff
D) Avoiding adaptation

### 5. Which type of market is best suited for a wholly owned subsidiary entry strategy?
A) High-risk emerging markets
B) Mature markets with stable regulations and infrastructure
C) Markets with strict foreign ownership restrictions
D) All markets equally

### 6. What minimum capital is typically required for consulting or technology sales entry?
A) $50K-100K
B) $500K-2M
C) $5M-10M
D) $20M+

### 7. Which international expansion approach reduces risk through sequential entry?
A) Enter all markets simultaneously
B) Phased approach with pilot markets
C) Avoid international markets
D) Random market selection

### 8. What is a key indicator of infrastructure readiness for CEA?
A) Number of restaurants
B) Reliable power, water, and logistics systems
C) Tourist attractions
D) Mountain ranges

### 9. Which partner characteristic is most important when selecting international partners?
A) Size of company only
B) Aligned values, local expertise, and financial stability
C) Same nationality
D) Office location

### 10. What should be the primary focus of an international market entry strategy?
A) Maximum speed regardless of quality
B) Sustainable competitive advantage and long-term profitability
C) Copying competitors exactly
D) Minimizing all investment

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) Market size and growth rate**
   *Explanation: Market size and growth rate should carry 40% combined weight (20% each) as primary market selection factors.*

2. **C) 12-24 months**
   *Explanation: International market entry typically requires 12-24 months from initial entry to revenue generation.*

3. **B) Growing market with government support and low competition**
   *Explanation: Growing markets with government support and limited competition offer the best opportunities for technology sales.*

4. **B) Local partnerships and market-specific expertise**
   *Explanation: Local partnerships, market knowledge, and cultural understanding are critical success factors for international expansion.*

5. **B) Mature markets with stable regulations and infrastructure**
   *Explanation: Wholly owned subsidiaries work best in mature markets with stable regulations, established infrastructure, and lower risk.*

6. **B) $500K-2M**
   *Explanation: Consulting and technology sales typically require $500K-2M in capital, while production requires $10M+.*

7. **B) Phased approach with pilot markets**
   *Explanation: A phased approach testing pilot markets before full expansion reduces risk and allows learning.*

8. **B) Reliable power, water, and logistics systems**
   *Explanation: Infrastructure readiness is indicated by reliable power, water availability, and functional logistics systems.*

9. **B) Aligned values, local expertise, and financial stability**
   *Explanation: Partner selection should prioritize aligned values, local market expertise, and financial stability.*

10. **B) Sustainable competitive advantage and long-term profitability**
    *Explanation: International strategy should focus on sustainable competitive advantage and long-term profitability, not just speed or cost minimization.*
