## Questions

### 1. Which funding source is specifically designed for equipment sales with government backing?
A) Private equity
B) Export-Import Bank
C) Local commercial banks
D) Strategic investors

### 2. What does IFC stand for in the context of international financing?
A) International Finance Corporation
B) International Farming Cooperative
C) Investment Fund Coalition
D) Industrial Finance Committee

### 3. Which funding source is most appropriate for emerging market projects with development impact?
A) Venture capital
B) Commercial banks
C) Development Finance Institutions (DFIs)
D) Angel investors

### 4. What is the primary advantage of using local banks for in-country operations?
A) Lowest interest rates always
B) No requirements
C) Local market knowledge and relationships
D) Government guarantees

### 5. What financial risk is associated with operating in multiple currencies?
A) Interest rate risk
B) Currency fluctuation risk
C) Commodity price risk
D) Liquidity risk

### 6. What is a common mitigation strategy for currency fluctuation risk?
A) Ignoring exchange rates
B) Hedging and local currency pricing
C) Only accepting USD
D) Avoiding international markets

### 7. Which type of financial instrument is commonly used to mitigate payment risk in international trade?
A) Cash in advance only
B) Letters of credit
C) Personal guarantees
D) Verbal agreements

### 8. What do private equity investors typically require in exchange for growth capital?
A) No involvement
B) Governance rights and equity stake
C) Technology transfer
D) Marketing control only

### 9. Which risk requires political risk insurance for mitigation?
A) Currency fluctuation
B) Government expropriation or policy changes
C) Market competition
D) Weather events

### 10. What strings might be attached to government grants for international expansion?
A) None - free money
B) Local employment, technology transfer, or reporting requirements
C) Only repayment terms
D) Marketing restrictions only

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) Export-Import Bank**
   *Explanation: Export-Import Banks provide government-backed financing specifically for equipment and technology sales to foreign buyers.*

2. **A) International Finance Corporation**
   *Explanation: IFC (International Finance Corporation) is part of the World Bank Group and provides financing for private sector development.*

3. **C) Development Finance Institutions (DFIs)**
   *Explanation: DFIs focus on emerging markets and projects with development impact, offering blended finance and favorable terms.*

4. **C) Local market knowledge and relationships**
   *Explanation: Local banks provide valuable market knowledge, relationships, and understanding of local business conditions.*

5. **B) Currency fluctuation risk**
   *Explanation: Operating in multiple currencies exposes companies to exchange rate fluctuations that can impact profitability.*

6. **B) Hedging and local currency pricing**
   *Explanation: Currency hedging instruments and local currency pricing help mitigate exchange rate fluctuation risks.*

7. **B) Letters of credit**
   *Explanation: Letters of credit provide payment security in international trade by guaranteeing payment through banks.*

8. **B) Governance rights and equity stake**
   *Explanation: Private equity investors typically require governance rights, board seats, and equity ownership in exchange for capital.*

9. **B) Government expropriation or policy changes**
   *Explanation: Political risk insurance protects against government expropriation, policy changes, and political instability.*

10. **B) Local employment, technology transfer, or reporting requirements**
    *Explanation: Government grants often come with requirements for local hiring, technology transfer, reporting, or other conditions.*
