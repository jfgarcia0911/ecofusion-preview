## Questions

### 1. What is the approximate total size of the North American CEA market?
A) $3-5 billion
B) $5-7 billion
C) $8-12 billion
D) $15-20 billion

### 2. Which region within the United States is characterized by premium markets, technology hub influence, and high land costs?
A) Northeast
B) Sunbelt
C) Midwest
D) West Coast

### 3. What is Canada's primary competitive advantage in CEA?
A) Largest market size
B) Cold climate expertise and low energy costs
C) Organic certification leadership
D) Export restrictions

### 4. Approximately what percentage of Mexico's CEA production is exported to the United States?
A) 40%
B) 60%
C) 80%
D) 95%

### 5. Which of the following is a major challenge facing the U.S. vertical farming industry?
A) Lack of consumer demand
B) Energy costs and profitability
C) Excessive government regulation
D) Limited technology availability

### 6. What is the estimated size of the U.S. vertical farming market segment?
A) $500M-1B
B) $1.5-2.5B
C) $3-4B
D) $5-6B

### 7. Which trade agreement provides Canada with favorable access to U.S. markets?
A) NAFTA
B) USMCA
C) TPP
D) CAFTA

### 8. What are the primary crops grown in Mexico's CEA industry for export?
A) Leafy greens and herbs
B) Berries and flowers
C) Tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers
D) Microgreens and specialty crops

### 9. What is a key driver for greenhouse development in the U.S. Northeast region?
A) Year-round demand and high population density
B) Low labor costs
C) Warm climate advantages
D) Government subsidies

### 10. Which Canadian energy sources contribute to their competitive advantage in CEA?
A) Coal and oil
B) Hydroelectric and natural gas
C) Nuclear and solar
D) Wind and biomass

---

## Answer Key

1. **C) $8-12 billion**
   *Explanation: North America's total CEA market is valued at $8-12 billion, representing 25-30% of the global market.*

2. **D) West Coast**
   *Explanation: The West Coast features premium markets (SF, LA, Seattle), technology hub influence, high land costs, and water scarcity as a driver.*

3. **B) Cold climate expertise and low energy costs**
   *Explanation: Canada excels in cold climate R&D and benefits from low-cost hydroelectric and natural gas energy.*

4. **C) 80%**
   *Explanation: Approximately 80% of Mexico's CEA production is exported to the United States.*

5. **B) Energy costs and profitability**
   *Explanation: High energy costs and challenges achieving profitability are major issues facing U.S. vertical farming operations.*

6. **B) $1.5-2.5B**
   *Explanation: The U.S. vertical farming segment is valued at $1.5-2.5 billion, while the total U.S. market is $6-9 billion.*

7. **B) USMCA**
   *Explanation: The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) provides favorable trade access between the three countries.*

8. **C) Tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers**
   *Explanation: Mexico's CEA industry focuses primarily on greenhouse production of tomatoes, peppers, and cucumbers for U.S. export.*

9. **A) Year-round demand and high population density**
   *Explanation: The Northeast's year-round consumer demand and high population density drive greenhouse development despite cold climate challenges.*

10. **B) Hydroelectric and natural gas**
    *Explanation: Canada's access to low-cost hydroelectric power and natural gas provides a significant competitive advantage for energy-intensive CEA operations.*
