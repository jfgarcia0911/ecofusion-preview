## Questions

### 1. What is the approximate size of the African CEA market?
A) $0.5-1 billion
B) $2-3 billion
C) $4-5 billion
D) $6-8 billion

### 2. Which African country has the most developed CEA infrastructure?
A) Nigeria
B) Kenya
C) South Africa
D) Egypt

### 3. What is the primary challenge facing CEA development in Nigeria?
A) Lack of consumer demand
B) Infrastructure and power reliability
C) Cold climate
D) Water abundance

### 4. What is Kenya's current strength in agricultural production?
A) Greenhouse vegetables
B) Vertical farming technology
C) Flower production
D) Grain exports

### 5. Which South American country has the largest CEA market in the region?
A) Chile
B) Colombia
C) Argentina
D) Brazil

### 6. What is Chile's unique advantage for CEA production?
A) Large domestic market
B) Counter-seasonal production for export
C) Lowest labor costs
D) Government subsidies

### 7. What is the approximate size of India's CEA market?
A) $200-400M
B) $500M-1B
C) $2-3B
D) $4-5B

### 8. Which existing agricultural expertise can Colombia leverage for CEA expansion?
A) Coffee production
B) Flower production
C) Grain exports
D) Cattle ranching

### 9. What are common challenges across emerging markets for CEA development?
A) Excessive regulation
B) Infrastructure limitations and financing difficulties
C) Oversaturated markets
D) Lack of agricultural land

### 10. Which Southeast Asian countries are identified as emerging CEA markets?
A) Singapore and Japan
B) Vietnam, Thailand, and Indonesia
C) China and South Korea
D) Malaysia and Philippines only

---

## Answer Key

1. **A) $0.5-1 billion**
   *Explanation: Africa's CEA market is valued at $0.5-1 billion, representing 1-2% of the global market with 14-18% CAGR.*

2. **C) South Africa**
   *Explanation: South Africa has the most developed infrastructure in Africa with a $200-400M market focused on tomatoes and peppers.*

3. **B) Infrastructure and power reliability**
   *Explanation: Nigeria faces significant challenges with infrastructure and power reliability, despite having the largest population in Africa.*

4. **C) Flower production**
   *Explanation: Kenya has established strength in flower production and is exploring opportunities to diversify into food crops.*

5. **D) Brazil**
   *Explanation: Brazil has the largest CEA market in Latin America at $400-700M, driven by urban growth and a growing middle class.*

6. **B) Counter-seasonal production for export**
   *Explanation: Chile's counter-seasonal production capability provides export opportunities to Northern Hemisphere markets.*

7. **B) $500M-1B**
   *Explanation: India's CEA market is valued at $500M-1B with massive potential due to population and urbanization.*

8. **B) Flower production**
   *Explanation: Colombia has established expertise in flower production that can be leveraged for diversification into food crop CEA.*

9. **B) Infrastructure limitations and financing difficulties**
   *Explanation: Emerging markets commonly face infrastructure limitations, financing difficulties, political risk, and currency fluctuations.*

10. **B) Vietnam, Thailand, and Indonesia**
    *Explanation: Vietnam, Thailand, and Indonesia are identified as key emerging CEA markets in Southeast Asia with growing urbanization.*
