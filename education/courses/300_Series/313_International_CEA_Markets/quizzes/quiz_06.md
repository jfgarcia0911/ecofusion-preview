**Course 313: International CEA Markets**

## Questions

### 1. Which region is characterized as "task-oriented" with a "direct" negotiation style?
A) East Asia
B) North America
C) Middle East
D) Latin America

### 2. How would you characterize the decision-making speed in East Asian business culture?
A) Very fast
B) Fast
C) Moderate
D) Slow and deliberate

### 3. Which region places the highest emphasis on relationship building in business?
A) North America
B) Northern Europe
C) Middle East
D) Australia

### 4. What are the preferred crops in Japanese CEA markets?
A) Tomatoes and peppers
B) Greens and strawberries
C) Herbs and microgreens
D) Cucumbers and lettuce

### 5. What quality expectation is most important in Japanese markets?
A) Organic certification
B) Low price
C) Perfect appearance
D) Large size

### 6. Which region prefers minimal, eco-friendly packaging?
A) North America
B) Asia
C) Europe
D) Middle East

### 7. What is the typical negotiation style in Middle Eastern business culture?
A) Fast and direct
B) Relationship-based
C) Consensus-driven
D) Competitive bidding

### 8. How does European consumer preference for crop diversity compare to other regions?
A) Low diversity preference
B) Standard diversity
C) High diversity and organic focus
D) Only local varieties

### 9. What cultural factor should companies consider when entering Latin American markets?
A) Fast decision-making is essential
B) Relationships and personal connections are important
C) Only written contracts matter
D) Price is the only consideration

### 10. Which region emphasizes food safety and traceability most strongly in consumer preferences?
A) Latin America
B) Africa
C) China
D) Middle East

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) North America**
   *Explanation: North American business culture is task-oriented with fast decision-making and direct negotiation styles.*

2. **D) Slow and deliberate**
   *Explanation: East Asian business culture is characterized by slow, deliberate decision-making and indirect negotiation.*

3. **C) Middle East**
   *Explanation: The Middle East places the highest emphasis on relationship building, with very high relationship focus in business.*

4. **B) Greens and strawberries**
   *Explanation: Japanese markets prefer leafy greens and strawberries with perfect appearance and premium quality.*

5. **C) Perfect appearance**
   *Explanation: Japanese consumers have the highest quality expectations globally, particularly for perfect visual appearance.*

6. **C) Europe**
   *Explanation: European consumers prefer minimal, eco-friendly packaging aligned with sustainability values.*

7. **B) Relationship-based**
   *Explanation: Middle Eastern negotiation style is relationship-based with variable decision speeds depending on relationships.*

8. **C) High diversity and organic focus**
   *Explanation: European consumers prefer high crop diversity and organic production more than most other regions.*

9. **B) Relationships and personal connections are important**
   *Explanation: Latin American business culture emphasizes relationships, personal connections, and flexible, personal negotiation styles.*

10. **C) China**
    *Explanation: Chinese consumers emphasize food safety and traceability most strongly due to historical food safety concerns.*
