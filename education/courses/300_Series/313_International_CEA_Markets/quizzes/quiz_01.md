## Questions

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice)
What is the approximate total global CEA market size in 2025?

A) $10-15 billion
B) $20-25 billion
C) $35-45 billion
D) $60-70 billion

**Correct Answer:** C) $35-45 billion

---

### Question 2 (Multiple Choice)
Which region represents the largest CEA market globally?

A) North America
B) Europe
C) Asia-Pacific
D) Middle East

**Correct Answer:** C) Asia-Pacific

---

### Question 3 (Multiple Choice)
What is the primary driver of CEA growth in the Middle East?

A) Export opportunities
B) Low labor costs
C) Food security concerns
D) Organic production mandates

**Correct Answer:** C) Food security concerns

---

### Question 4 (True/False)
The Netherlands is the world's #2 agricultural exporter by value despite its small size.

**Correct Answer:** True

---

### Question 5 (Multiple Choice)
What percentage of global CEA investment went to Asia-Pacific in recent years?

A) 10-15%
B) 20-25%
C) 35-40%
D) 50-55%

**Correct Answer:** C) 35-40%

---

### Question 6 (Multiple Choice)
Which country has the most advanced plant factory technology globally?

A) USA
B) Netherlands
C) Japan
D) Singapore

**Correct Answer:** C) Japan

---

### Question 7 (Short Answer)
Name THREE major challenges facing the global CEA industry.

**Sample Answer:**
1. Profitability/high costs (energy, labor, capital)
2. Energy dependency and costs
3. Market education and consumer acceptance

---

### Question 8 (Multiple Choice)
What is Spain's competitive advantage in CEA production?

A) Advanced technology
B) Low-cost production with favorable climate
C) Government subsidies
D) Export infrastructure to Asia

**Correct Answer:** B) Low-cost production with favorable climate

---

### Question 9 (Multiple Choice)
Which market entry strategy offers the lowest risk and investment?

A) Wholly owned subsidiary
B) Joint venture
C) Licensing/technology transfer
D) Acquisition

**Correct Answer:** C) Licensing/technology transfer

---

### Question 10 (Short Answer)
Explain why consolidation is occurring in the CEA industry.

**Sample Answer:**
Consolidation is occurring because: (1) Smaller players struggling with profitability due to high fixed costs, (2) Scale economies becoming essential (100,000+ sq ft needed), (3) Retail partnerships concentrating among larger players, (4) Institutional investors preferring established operations, and (5) Technology advantages narrowing, favoring operational excellence and scale.
