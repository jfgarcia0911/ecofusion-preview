## Questions

### 1. What is the approximate total size of the European CEA market?
A) $5-8 billion
B) $10-15 billion
C) $18-22 billion
D) $25-30 billion

### 2. Which European country is recognized as the global innovation leader in CEA and the world's #2 agricultural exporter by value?
A) Germany
B) Spain
C) Netherlands
D) France

### 3. Approximately how many hectares of greenhouse production exist in Spain's Almería region?
A) 10,000 ha
B) 20,000 ha
C) 31,000 ha
D) 45,000 ha

### 4. What is the Netherlands' average greenhouse farm size, which contributes to their high efficiency?
A) 10+ ha
B) 25+ ha
C) 50+ ha
D) 100+ ha

### 5. Which European country has the largest organic food market in the EU?
A) France
B) United Kingdom
C) Netherlands
D) Germany

### 6. What was the approximate value of government grants provided by the UK for greenhouse development between 2020-2025?
A) GBP 100M+
B) GBP 250M+
C) GBP 500M+
D) GBP 1B+

### 7. What is Spain's (Almería) primary competitive advantage in European CEA production?
A) Advanced automation technology
B) Low-tech but efficient production with low costs
C) Vertical farming expertise
D) Organic certification leadership

### 8. Which university in the Netherlands is recognized as a top global agricultural research institution?
A) Utrecht University
B) Amsterdam Agricultural Institute
C) Wageningen UR
D) Delft Technical University

### 9. What annual value does the Netherlands export in CEA technology?
A) $200-500M
B) $500M-1B
C) $1-2B
D) $3-5B

### 10. What is driving the UK's increased investment in domestic CEA production?
A) Lower labor costs
B) Climate change benefits
C) Post-Brexit food security concerns
D) EU expansion requirements

---

## Answer Key

1. **B) $10-15 billion**
   *Explanation: Europe's CEA market is valued at $10-15 billion, representing 30-35% of the global market with a CAGR of 10-14%.*

2. **C) Netherlands**
   *Explanation: The Netherlands is the global CEA innovation leader and the world's #2 agricultural exporter by value despite its small size.*

3. **C) 31,000 ha**
   *Explanation: Spain's Almería region has approximately 31,000 hectares under plastic greenhouse production.*

4. **C) 50+ ha**
   *Explanation: The Netherlands averages 50+ hectare farm sizes, which enables high efficiency and economies of scale.*

5. **D) Germany**
   *Explanation: Germany has the largest organic food market in the EU and maintains a strong sustainability focus in CEA.*

6. **C) GBP 500M+**
   *Explanation: The UK government provided over GBP 500 million in grants between 2020-2025 to support domestic food production.*

7. **B) Low-tech but efficient production with low costs**
   *Explanation: Almería's competitive advantage is low-tech greenhouse production with low labor costs, high sunshine hours, and proximity to markets.*

8. **C) Wageningen UR**
   *Explanation: Wageningen University & Research is recognized as a top global agricultural research institution and innovation hub.*

9. **C) $1-2B**
   *Explanation: The Netherlands exports $1-2 billion annually in CEA technology, plus additional value in consulting, genetics, and structures.*

10. **C) Post-Brexit food security concerns**
    *Explanation: Brexit reduced EU imports, creating a food security imperative that drove investment in domestic CEA production capacity.*
