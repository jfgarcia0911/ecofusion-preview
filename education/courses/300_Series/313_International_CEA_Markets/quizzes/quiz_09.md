## Questions

### 1. Which market entry strategy requires the LOWEST investment and risk?
A) Wholly owned subsidiary
B) Joint venture
C) Export through distributors
D) Greenfield development

### 2. Which partnership model provides the HIGHEST level of control?
A) Licensing
B) Distribution agreement
C) Wholly owned subsidiary
D) Strategic alliance

### 3. What is a key characteristic of a 50/50 joint venture?
A) One party has complete control
B) Shared investment, risk, and control
C) No financial investment required
D) Limited to one year duration

### 4. Which entry strategy is fastest to implement?
A) Greenfield development
B) Joint venture
C) Export or acquisition
D) Licensing

### 5. What is a primary advantage of licensing or franchising?
A) Highest profit margins
B) Complete operational control
C) Low investment with technology/brand revenue
D) No quality concerns

### 6. Which partnership model is most appropriate for emerging markets with high political risk?
A) Wholly owned subsidiary
B) Greenfield development
C) Strategic alliance or licensing
D) Majority acquisition

### 7. What level of investment is typically required for a joint venture?
A) Low
B) Medium
C) High
D) Highest

### 8. Which model allows for technology sharing while maintaining some control?
A) Export only
B) Strategic alliance
C) Distribution agreement
D) Arm's length sales

### 9. What is the primary risk of a wholly owned subsidiary in a foreign market?
A) No control over operations
B) Highest investment and risk exposure
C) Limited profit potential
D) Cannot hire local employees

### 10. Which entry strategy is most common for equipment and technology sales?
A) Joint venture
B) Wholly owned subsidiary
C) Distribution partnerships or licensing
D) Greenfield only

---

## Answer Key

1. **C) Export through distributors**
   *Explanation: Export through distributors requires the lowest investment and risk while providing fast market access.*

2. **C) Wholly owned subsidiary**
   *Explanation: A wholly owned subsidiary provides the highest level of control but requires the highest investment and risk.*

3. **B) Shared investment, risk, and control**
   *Explanation: A 50/50 joint venture involves shared investment, shared risk, and shared control between partners.*

4. **C) Export or acquisition**
   *Explanation: Export and acquisition are the fastest entry strategies, while greenfield development is the slowest.*

5. **C) Low investment with technology/brand revenue**
   *Explanation: Licensing/franchising allows revenue from technology and brand with low capital investment.*

6. **C) Strategic alliance or licensing**
   *Explanation: Strategic alliances and licensing minimize investment exposure in high political risk environments.*

7. **B) Medium**
   *Explanation: Joint ventures typically require medium investment levels, shared between partners.*

8. **B) Strategic alliance**
   *Explanation: Strategic alliances allow technology sharing while maintaining control and limiting investment.*

9. **B) Highest investment and risk exposure**
   *Explanation: Wholly owned subsidiaries require the highest investment and expose the company to maximum risk.*

10. **C) Distribution partnerships or licensing**
    *Explanation: Equipment and technology companies typically use distribution partnerships and licensing for international sales.*
