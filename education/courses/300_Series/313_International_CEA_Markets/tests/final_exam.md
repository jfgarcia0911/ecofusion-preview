## Section 6: Strategy and Operations (3 questions)

48. The recommended market entry sequencing for most companies is:
    - A) Simultaneous entry to all markets
    - B) Sequential waterfall approach
    - C) Hybrid (sequential for complex, parallel for simple)
    - D) Random based on opportunities

    **Answer: C**

49. True/False: International market entry typically takes 24-36 months from planning to steady-state operations.

    **Answer: True**

50. SHORT ANSWER: Describe three critical success factors for international CEA expansion and why each is important. (6 points)

**Sample Answer:**
1. **Local Partnerships**: Essential for navigating cultural differences, regulatory requirements, and market access; reduce risk and accelerate learning.

2. **Phased Investment**: Test-and-learn approach reduces risk; prove model at small scale before committing large capital; allows adaptation based on actual market conditions.

3. **Organizational Capabilities**: International expansion requires specific skills (cultural intelligence, international finance, regulatory expertise); building these capabilities is essential for managing multiple markets successfully.
