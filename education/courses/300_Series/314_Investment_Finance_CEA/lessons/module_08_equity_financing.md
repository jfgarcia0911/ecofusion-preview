## 8.1 Equity Financing Overview

### Advantages and Disadvantages

**Advantages:**
- No repayment obligation
- Permanent capital
- Investors provide expertise and networks
- Strengthens balance sheet
- Shares risk with investors
- Enables growth beyond debt capacity

**Disadvantages:**
- Ownership dilution
- Loss of control (board seats, voting rights)
- Expensive (higher cost of capital)
- Reporting and governance requirements
- Alignment challenges
- Exit pressure

### Types of Equity Investors

```
EQUITY INVESTOR LANDSCAPE

FRIENDS & FAMILY
├── Amount: $25K - $500K
├── Stage: Pre-seed, Seed
├── Terms: Often simple (convertible note, SAFE)
└── Advantages: Fast, flexible, supportive

ANGEL INVESTORS
├── Amount: $50K - $500K per investor
├── Stage: Seed, early stage
├── Terms: Convertible notes or preferred equity
├── Advantages: Expertise, networks, patient capital
└── Examples: Tech executives, successful entrepreneurs

ANGEL GROUPS
├── Amount: $250K - $2M per round
├── Stage: Seed through Series A
├── Terms: Preferred equity, standardized
├── Advantages: Professionalization, due diligence
└── Examples: Keiretsu Forum, AgFunder Angels

VENTURE CAPITAL - EARLY STAGE
├── Amount: $2M - $10M
├── Stage: Series A, B
├── Terms: Preferred equity with protections
├── Advantages: Large capital, strategic support
└── Examples: Finistere, S2G Ventures, Lux Capital

VENTURE CAPITAL - GROWTH STAGE
├── Amount: $10M - $100M+
├── Stage: Series B, C, D
├── Terms: Preferred equity, board control potential
├── Advantages: Scale capital, exit preparation
└── Examples: Breakthrough Energy, Softbank

PRIVATE EQUITY
├── Amount: $25M - $500M+
├── Stage: Mature, profitable
├── Terms: Common or preferred, control-oriented
├── Advantages: Operational expertise, buy-and-build
└── Examples: KKR, Paine Schwartz Partners

CORPORATE/STRATEGIC
├── Amount: Variable
├── Stage: Any stage
├── Terms: Equity + commercial agreement
├── Advantages: Strategic value, offtake, distribution
└── Examples: Cargill, Kroger, IKEA

CROWDFUNDING
├── Amount: $50K - $5M
├── Stage: Early stage
├── Terms: Simple equity or revenue share
├── Advantages: Marketing, customer validation
└── Platforms: Republic, StartEngine, Wefunder
```

```

Purpose:
├── Concept validation

└── Business plan development

Investors:
├── Founders
├── Friends & family
└── Incubators/accelerators

Valuation: $1M - $3M post-money
Dilution: 15-30%

SEED ($500K - $3M)
Purpose:
├── Pilot facility
├── Technology validation
├── Initial customers
└── Product-market fit

Investors:
├── Angel investors
├── Angel groups
├── Early-stage VCs
└── AgTech accelerators

Valuation: $3M - $10M post-money
Dilution: 20-35%

SERIES A ($3M - $15M)
Purpose:
├── First commercial facility
├── Scale production
├── Team buildout
└── Path to profitability

Investors:
├── Venture capital firms
├── Strategic investors
└── Prior investors (pro-rata)

Valuation: $10M - $40M post-money
Dilution: 25-40%

SERIES B ($10M - $50M)
Purpose:
├── Facility expansion
├── Geographic expansion
├── Operational optimization
└── Approach profitability

Investors:
├── Growth VCs
├── Private equity (growth)
├── Strategic investors
└── Prior investors

Valuation: $40M - $150M post-money
Dilution: 20-30%

SERIES C+ ($25M - $100M+)
Purpose:
├── Market leadership
├── Multiple facilities
├── M&A opportunities
└── IPO preparation

Investors:
├── Late-stage VCs
├── Private equity
├── Hedge funds
└── Public market crossover

Valuation: $150M - $500M+ post-money
Dilution: 15-25%

EXAMPLE: VERTICAL FARM PROGRESSION

Pre-seed (2020): $250K @ $2M post (12.5% dilution)
├── Founders: 87.5%
└── F&F: 12.5%

Seed (2021): $2M @ $8M post (25% dilution)
├── Founders: 65.6% (87.5% × 75%)
├── F&F: 9.4%
└── Angels: 25%

├── Founders: 49.2%
├── F&F: 7.1%
├── Angels: 18.8%
└── VCs: 25%

Series B (2024): $30M @ $120M post (25% dilution)
├── Founders: 36.9%
├── F&F: 5.3%
├── Angels: 14.1%
├── Series A VCs: 18.8%
└── Series B VCs: 25%

Founder dilution: 87.5% → 36.9% (58% diluted)
But: $0.875 share → $36.9 share of $120M = $44.3M value!
```

## 8.3 Term Sheet Essentials

### Key Term Sheet Components

```
TERM SHEET STRUCTURE

OFFERING TERMS
├── Security Type: Series A Preferred Stock
├── Investment Amount: $8,000,000
├── Pre-money Valuation: $24,000,000
├── Post-money Valuation: $32,000,000
├── Price per Share: $4.00
├── Shares Issued: 2,000,000
└── Investor Ownership: 25%

CAPITALIZATION TABLE (Post-Round)
                        Shares      Ownership   Value
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Common (Founders)       4,500,000   56.3%      $18M
Seed Preferred          1,500,000   18.7%      $6M
Series A Preferred      2,000,000   25.0%      $8M
                        ─────────   ─────      ────
TOTAL                   8,000,000   100.0%     $32M

DIVIDEND PROVISIONS
├── Series A: 8% annual cumulative preferred dividend
├── Paid when and if declared by Board
├── Accrues if not paid
└── Paid before common dividends

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
├── 1x non-participating preferred
├── Upon liquidation, M&A, or change of control:
│   1. Series A gets $8M (1x investment)
│   2. Seed gets $4M (1x investment)
│   3. Remainder to common
└── Alternative: Participate pro-rata as common
    (choose more favorable)

Example Liquidation Scenarios:

Sale for $20M:
├── Series A: $8M (preference)
├── Seed: $4M (preference)
└── Common: $8M (37.5% of equity)

Sale for $80M:
├── Series A: $20M (convert to common, 25% × $80M)
├── Seed: $15M (convert to common, 18.7% × $80M)
└── Common: $45M (56.3% × $80M)

CONVERSION RIGHTS
├── Optional: Any time at holder's election
├── Automatic: At IPO >$50M, >$10/share
├── Conversion ratio: 1:1 (subject to anti-dilution)

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
Type: Broad-based weighted average
├── Protects against down rounds
├── Adjusts conversion price
├── Excludes: Options pool, conversions
└── Formula:

New conversion price = Old price ×
    [(Old shares + New consideration / Old price) /
     (Old shares + New shares issued)]

Example Down Round:
├── Series A invested at $4.00/share
├── Series B raises at $2.00/share (50% down)
├── Weighted average adjustment:
│   New conversion price: ~$3.20 (instead of $4.00)
└── Series A now converts to more shares

VOTING RIGHTS
├── Common: 1 vote per share
├── Preferred: 1 vote per common equivalent
├── Series A total: 2,000,000 votes (25%)
└── Protective provisions (see below)

BOARD COMPOSITION
Total Seats: 5
├── Common (Founder): 2 seats
├── Series A: 1 seat
├── Seed/Angels: 1 seat
└── Independent: 1 seat (mutually agreed)

Board Approval Required For:
├── Annual budget and plan
├── CEO hiring/firing
├── Issuance of new securities
├── Transactions >$500K
├── Debt >$2M
└── Dividends or distributions

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
Require Series A majority vote:
├── Amend certificate/bylaws
├── Authorize new senior/parity securities
├── Acquire or be acquired
├── Liquidate, dissolve, or wind up
├── Pay dividends on junior stock
└── Change size of board

INFORMATION RIGHTS
Company shall provide:
├── Monthly financial statements (20 days)
├── Quarterly financial statements (30 days)
├── Annual audited statements (90 days)
├── Annual budget (30 days before FY)
├── Board materials (5 days before meeting)
└── Rights terminate at IPO or sale

PRO-RATA RIGHTS
├── Right to participate in future rounds
├── Maintain ownership percentage
├── Must invest at least $500K to maintain
└── Lapses if don't participate in next round

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
├── If >67% of equity approves sale
├── All shareholders must sell on same terms
└── Protects against minority blocking exit

REGISTRATION RIGHTS
├── Demand rights: 1-2 demands for IPO
├── Piggyback rights: Include in company IPO
├── S-3 rights: After IPO, 2 per year
└── Company pays registration costs

FOUNDER VESTING
├── 4-year vesting schedule
├── 1-year cliff
├── Monthly vesting thereafter
├── Acceleration: 25% upon change of control
└── Good leaver / bad leaver provisions

NO-SHOP CLAUSE
├── 60-day exclusivity period
├── Cannot solicit other investors
├── Can continue discussions in process
└── Breakup fee if terminated: $100K

CONDITIONS TO CLOSING
├── Due diligence satisfactory
├── Definitive agreements executed
├── Board approval
├── No material adverse changes
└── Legal opinions delivered

EXPIRATION
Term sheet expires if not closed within 90 days
```

## 8.4 Negotiating Equity Terms

### Key Negotiation Points

**Valuation:**

```
VALUATION NEGOTIATION

Initial Ask (Founders): $30M pre-money
Initial Offer (VC): $18M pre-money
Raise Amount: $8M

Founder Position:
├── Recent comparable deals at 2.5-3.0x revenue
├── Company revenue: $12M run-rate
├── Implies $30M-$36M valuation
└── Asking bottom of range

VC Position:
├── Company pre-revenue at last round
├── Execution risk remains
├── Market multiples compressed
├── Discount for illiquidity and risk
└── Offering 1.5x revenue

Compromise Strategies:
1. Meet in middle: $24M pre-money (2.0x revenue)
2. Higher valuation with milestones:
   ├── $22M base
   ├── +$3M if hit $18M revenue in 12 months
   └── +$3M if achieve EBITDA positive
3. Higher valuation with participating preferred
4. Lower valuation with investor-friendly terms

FINAL DEAL:
├── Pre-money: $24M
├── Earn-out potential: +$4M
└── Terms: Standard (1x non-participating)
```

**Liquidation Preference:**

```
PREFERENCE NEGOTIATION

VC Proposal: 2x participating preferred
Impact on $40M sale:
├── VC gets: $16M (2x $8M) + 25% of remainder
│   = $16M + $6M = $22M (55% of proceeds)
└── Founders get: $18M (45%)
    But own 56% of equity!

Founder Counter: 1x non-participating
Impact on $40M sale:
├── VC gets: Greater of $8M or 25%
│   = $10M (converts to common, 25%)
└── Founders get: $22.5M (56.3%)
    Fair economic split

Compromise Options:
1. 1.5x non-participating
2. 1x participating up to 2x return, then convert
3. 1x non-participating + dividend preference
4. Variable based on return:
   ├── <2x return: 1x participating
   └── >2x return: Convert to common

FINAL DEAL:
1x non-participating preferred (standard)
VC gets greater of preference or pro-rata share
```

**Board Control:**

```
BOARD COMPOSITION NEGOTIATION

VC Proposal: 5-person board
├── 2 VC seats
├── 2 founder seats
└── 1 independent (VC chooses)
Result: VCs control 3-2

Founder Counter: 5-person board
├── 3 founder seats
├── 1 VC seat
└── 1 independent (mutually agreed)
Result: Founders control

Compromise: 5-person board
├── 2 founder seats
├── 1 VC seat
├── 1 seed/angel seat
└── 1 independent (requires VC + founder agreement)
Result: Balanced, independent is tie-breaker

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS:
Even without board control, VCs get veto rights on:
├── Major decisions (M&A, financing, dividends)
├── Changes to preferred rights
└── Amendments to governance docs

This protects investors without taking control
```

## 8.5 Dilution and Cap Table Management

### Understanding Dilution

```
DILUTION MECHANICS

Starting Point:
Founders own: 10,000,000 shares (100%)

Series A Investment:
├── Pre-money value: $30M ($3.00/share)
├── Investment: $10M
├── Post-money value: $40M
├── New shares issued: 3,333,333
└── Price per share: $3.00 ($10M / 3.33M shares)

Post-Series A Ownership:
├── Founders: 10M shares / 13.33M = 75%
├── Series A: 3.33M shares / 13.33M = 25%
└── Dilution: 100% → 75% (25% dilution)

SUBSEQUENT ROUNDS:

Series B Investment:
├── Pre-money value: $80M
├── Investment: $20M
├── Post-money value: $100M
├── Dilution: 20%

Post-Series B:
├── Founders: 60% (75% × 80%)
├── Series A: 20% (25% × 80%)
└── Series B: 20%

Option Pool (10% post-Series B):
├── New shares reserved: 10% of post-pool total
├── Pool dilutes all shareholders
└── Typically comes from pre-money value

Post-Pool Ownership:
├── Founders: 54% (60% × 90%)
├── Series A: 18% (20% × 90%)
├── Series B: 18% (20% × 90%)
└── Option Pool: 10%

FOUNDER DILUTION OVER TIME:
├── Start: 100%
├── Post-Seed: 80%
├── Post-Series A: 75% → 60% (with pool)
├── Post-Series B: 54%
├── Post-Series C: 40%
└── At Exit: 35% (after option exercises)

Even with dilution, value grows:
├── Start: $0
├── Seed: $8M valuation × 80% = $6.4M
├── Series A: $40M × 60% = $24M
├── Series B: $100M × 54% = $54M
└── Exit: $300M × 35% = $105M

Dilution is acceptable if value grows faster!
```

### Avoiding Excessive Dilution

**Strategies:**

```
DILUTION MINIMIZATION TACTICS

1. RAISE LESS CAPITAL
├── Extend runway with revenue
├── Bootstrap where possible
├── Debt for equipment/real estate
└── Dilute 20% vs. 30%

Example:
├── Need $10M for growth
├── Option A: $10M equity at $40M pre = 25% dilution
├── Option B: $5M equity + $5M debt = 12.5% dilution
└── Savings: 12.5% ownership retained

2. ACHIEVE MILESTONES BEFORE RAISING
├── Higher valuation = less dilution per dollar
├── Delay Series B until profitable
└── Prove unit economics

Example:
├── Raise Series B now at $60M pre = 33% dilution
├── Wait 12 months, achieve profitability
├── Raise at $120M pre = 20% dilution
└── Savings: 13% ownership retained

3. INSIDER ROUNDS
├── Existing investors invest at higher valuation
├── Avoid new investors taking big stake
├── "Pay-to-play" provisions encourage participation

4. REVENUE FINANCING
├── Sell future revenue for upfront capital
├── Repay 1.3-1.5x from revenue over time
├── No dilution
└── Works when have consistent revenue

5. STRATEGIC INVESTORS AT PREMIUM
├── Corporates pay premium for strategic value
├── 10-20% higher valuation than VC
└── Plus commercial benefits

6. EMPLOYEE OPTIONS
├── Grant options, not stock
├── Vest over time (retention)
├── Exercise only if valuable
└── Lower dilution than direct grants

7. PERFORMANCE-BASED VESTING
├── Founders/management earn shares via milestones
├── Reduces initial dilution
├── Aligns incentives
└── Example: 20% vests if hit $50M revenue
```

## 8.6 Special Equity Structures

### SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

```
SAFE NOTE STRUCTURE

Developed by Y Combinator - simplifies early-stage investing

KEY TERMS:
Investment Amount:      $500,000
Valuation Cap:         $8,000,000
Discount:              20%
No interest rate
No maturity date
Converts at next priced round

CONVERSION SCENARIOS:

Scenario 1: Series A at $12M pre-money
├── Series A price: $1.20/share (assume)
├── SAFE converts at lower of:
│   ├── Cap: $8M → $0.80/share
│   └── Discount: $0.96/share ($1.20 × 0.80)
├── SAFE uses: $0.80/share (better for investor)
├── Shares issued: 625,000 ($500K / $0.80)
└── Ownership: ~5%

Scenario 2: Series A at $6M pre-money
├── Series A price: $0.60/share
├── SAFE converts at:
│   ├── Cap: $8M → $0.80/share (worse)
│   └── Discount: $0.48/share (better)
├── SAFE uses: $0.48/share
├── Shares issued: 1,041,667
└── Ownership: ~14% (rewards early risk)

ADVANTAGES:
├── Fast to close (2-3 pages)
├── Low legal costs ($2K vs. $20K)
├── Founder-friendly (no board seat, voting)
└── Standard terms (less negotiation)

DISADVANTAGES:
├── Dilution uncertain until conversion
├── Multiple SAFEs = complex cap table
├── Valuation cap can be problematic
└── Not all investors familiar/comfortable
```

### Preferred Equity Variations

```
PARTICIPATING vs. NON-PARTICIPATING PREFERRED

NON-PARTICIPATING (Investor-friendly):
Upon exit, choose greater of:
├── Liquidation preference (1x investment)
└── Pro-rata common share

Example ($100M exit, $10M invested, 20% ownership):
├── Preference: $10M
├── Pro-rata: $20M (20% of $100M)
└── Investor takes: $20M (converts to common)

PARTICIPATING (Very investor-friendly):
Upon exit, receive:
├── Liquidation preference (1x investment) PLUS
└── Pro-rata share of remainder

Example (same facts):
├── Preference: $10M
├── Remainder: $90M
├── Pro-rata of remainder: $18M (20% of $90M)
└── Investor gets: $28M (28% of total!)

CAPPED PARTICIPATING:
Participating up to X, then convert

Example (2x cap):
├── Take preference + participation
├── Until total return = 2x investment
├── Then convert to common thereafter

At $100M exit:
├── Participating would give: $28M (2.8x)
├── 2x cap limits to: $20M
└── Convert to common: $20M (same as non-participating here)

Benefit shows in moderate exits:

$40M exit:
├── Non-participating: $10M (preference) or $8M (pro-rata) = $10M
├── Participating: $10M + $6M = $16M (40% of proceeds!)
├── Capped participating: $16M (below 2x cap)
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Equity Stages**: CEA companies typically progress through pre-seed, seed, Series A/B/C, raising larger amounts at higher valuations as they derisk

2. **Valuation vs. Terms**: Sometimes better to take lower valuation with founder-friendly terms than higher valuation with onerous provisions

3. **Liquidation Preferences**: Critical economic term; 1x non-participating preferred is market standard for VC deals

4. **Board Composition**: Balance control between founders and investors; independent directors provide tie-breaker

5. **Dilution Management**: Strategic fundraising timing, capital efficiency, and alternative financing minimize dilution while enabling growth

6. **Cap Table Complexity**: Keep cap table clean; too many small investors creates coordination problems

7. **Alignment Matters**: Choose investors aligned with your vision, timeline, and values; chemistry matters as much as terms

8. **Documentation Thoroughness**: Term sheets lead to definitive agreements; all terms must be clearly documented to avoid future disputes
