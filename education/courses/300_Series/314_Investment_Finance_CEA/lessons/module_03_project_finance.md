## 3.1 Introduction to Project Finance

### What is Project Finance?

**Definition**: Project finance is a method of funding where lenders rely primarily on the project's cash flows for repayment, with project assets serving as collateral.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Non-recourse or limited recourse**: Lenders have limited claims on sponsors beyond project assets
- **Asset-backed**: Security interest in physical assets
- **Cash flow dependent**: Debt serviced from operational revenue
- **Ring-fenced**: Separate legal entity (SPV - Special Purpose Vehicle)
- **Long-term**: Financing matches project life (10-25 years typical)

### Project Finance vs. Corporate Finance

| Aspect | Project Finance | Corporate Finance |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Borrower** | Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) | Operating company |
| **Recourse** | Non/limited recourse | Full recourse |
| **Security** | Project assets and cash flows | Corporate assets, guarantees |
| **Loan sizing** | Based on project cash flows | Based on corporate balance sheet |
| **Risk allocation** | Shared among parties | Primarily on corporate entity |
| **Covenants** | Extensive project-specific | General corporate |
| **Flexibility** | Less flexible | More flexible |
| **Cost of capital** | Higher (more complex) | Lower (simpler) |

### When to Use Project Finance for CEA

**Appropriate Scenarios:**
- Large greenfield development ($10M+)
- Standalone facility with independent cash flows
- Desire to keep debt off corporate balance sheet
- Multiple stakeholders/partners
- Infrastructure or real estate component
- Long-term offtake agreements in place
- Limited corporate balance sheet capacity

**Less Appropriate:**
- Small facilities (<$5M)
- Expansion of existing operations
- Strong corporate balance sheet available
- Short-term or pilot projects
- High technology risk
- No revenue contracts

## 3.3 Project Financial Analysis

### Project Returns Calculation

**Key Return Metrics:**

1. **Net Present Value (NPV)**
   - Discount all future cash flows to present
   - Decision rule: NPV > 0 = Accept

2. **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
   - Discount rate where NPV = 0
   - Compare to hurdle rate (required return)

3. **Payback Period**
   - Time to recover initial investment
   - Simple vs. discounted payback

4. **Profitability Index**
   - PV of cash flows / Initial investment
   - Useful for capital rationing

### Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

**Critical Metric for Project Finance**

Formula: `DSCR = (EBITDA - CapEx - Taxes) / Debt Service`

```
DSCR CALCULATION - YEAR 3 EXAMPLE

EBITDA:                                         $3,234,000
Less: Maintenance CapEx                           (100,000)
Less: Cash Taxes                                  (385,000)
                                                ───────────
Cash Available for Debt Service:                $2,749,000

Debt Service Requirements:
├── Principal Payment                             $600,000
└── Interest Payment (@6.5%)                      $780,000
                                                ───────────
Total Debt Service:                             $1,380,000

DSCR = $2,749,000 / $1,380,000 = 1.99x

INTERPRETATION:
├── DSCR > 1.25x: Strong coverage (typical minimum)
├── DSCR 1.99x: Excellent coverage
├── Cash flow covers debt 2x over
└── Comfortable margin of safety

LENDER REQUIREMENTS:
├── Minimum DSCR: 1.25x (typically)
├── Average over life: 1.40x+
├── Covenant trigger: <1.15x = technical default
└── Cash sweep: DSCR >1.75x = no sweep
```

## 3.5 Project Risk Assessment and Mitigation

### Risk Categories

**1. Development and Construction Risk**

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Cost overruns | High | Medium | Fixed-price contracts, contingency |
| Delays | Medium | Medium | Liquidated damages, schedule buffer |
| Design defects | Medium | Low | Experienced design team, peer review |
| Permitting delays | Medium | Medium | Early engagement, experienced team |
| Weather delays | Low | Medium | Weather contingency in schedule |

**2. Technology and Operations Risk**

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Underperformance | Very High | Medium | Proven technology, pilot testing |
| Crop disease | High | Low | Biosecurity protocols, insurance |
| Equipment failure | Medium | Medium | Warranties, maintenance programs |
| Labor shortage | Medium | Medium | Training programs, automation |
| Energy price spikes | High | Medium | Long-term contracts, on-site generation |

**3. Market and Revenue Risk**

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Price decline | High | Medium | Offtake agreements, diversification |
| Volume risk | High | Medium | Multiple customers, contracts |
| Competition | Medium | High | Differentiation, quality focus |
| Customer concentration | High | Low | Expand customer base |
| Market access | Medium | Low | Distribution partnerships |

**4. Financial Risk**

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Interest rate changes | Medium | Medium | Fixed-rate debt, hedges |
| Refinancing risk | High | Low | Long-term debt, amortization match |
| Covenant violation | High | Low | Conservative projections, cushion |
| Working capital needs | Medium | Medium | Adequate reserves, line of credit |

### Financial Covenants

**Typical Project Finance Covenants:**

1. **Debt Service Coverage Ratio**
   - Minimum: 1.25x
   - Maintenance: 1.40x average
   - Measurement: Quarterly, annual compliance

2. **Debt-to-Equity Ratio**
   - Maximum: 2.5:1
   - Tested: Semi-annually
   - Cure rights: 30 days

3. **Distribution Restrictions**
   - No distributions if DSCR < 1.50x
   - Cash sweep if DSCR < 1.30x
   - Lockbox if DSCR < 1.15x

4. **Reserve Accounts**
   - Debt Service Reserve: 6 months P&I
   - Maintenance Reserve: $200K minimum
   - Replenishment: Monthly

5. **Operating Covenants**
   - Maintain insurance (property, liability, crop)
   - Provide financial statements (quarterly)
   - Annual budget approval required
   - Minimum yield requirements
   - Maintain key contracts

6. **Negative Covenants**
   - No additional debt without consent
   - No asset sales without consent
   - No change in business
   - No distributions beyond limits
   - Maintain single-purpose entity

**Covenant Cure Mechanisms:**

```
COVENANT VIOLATION PROCESS

VIOLATION DETECTED
│
├─► Notification to Lender (5 days)
│
├─► Cure Period Begins (30-60 days typically)
│   ├── Option 1: Improve operations
│   ├── Option 2: Equity injection
│   └── Option 3: Debt prepayment
│
├─► If Not Cured:
│   ├── Event of Default declared
│   ├── Cash sweep initiated
│   ├── Lender approval required for disbursements
│   └── Remediation plan required
│
└─► If Not Resolved:
    ├── Lender remedies available
    ├── Acceleration of debt (potential)
    └── Foreclosure (last resort)
```

## 3.6 Offtake Agreements and Revenue Contracts

### Importance of Offtake Agreements

**For Lenders:**
- Reduces market risk
- Provides revenue certainty
- Supports cash flow projections
- Enables non-recourse financing

**For Sponsors:**
- Lower cost of capital
- Higher leverage available
- Reduced execution risk
- Committed customer base

### Types of Offtake Structures

**1. Fixed-Price Contract**
```
STRUCTURE:
├── Price: Fixed at $2.25/lb
├── Volume: 2M lbs/year minimum
├── Term: 5 years
└── Escalation: 2% annually

ADVANTAGES:
├── Price certainty
├── Simple to model
└── Lender-friendly

DISADVANTAGES:
├── No upside if market improves
├── Below-market if prices rise
└── Inflation risk
```

**2. Market-Price Contract with Floor**
```
STRUCTURE:
├── Price: Market price, min $2.00/lb
├── Volume: 1.5M lbs/year minimum
├── Term: 3 years
└── Index: Regional wholesale index

ADVANTAGES:
├── Upside participation
├── Downside protection
└── Market flexibility

DISADVANTAGES:
├── Revenue uncertainty
├── Modeling complexity
└── Index risk
```

**3. Cost-Plus Contract**
```
STRUCTURE:
├── Price: Verified costs + 15% margin
├── Volume: As needed (minimum 1M lbs)
├── Term: 3 years
└── Cost true-up: Quarterly

ADVANTAGES:
├── Margin protection
├── Covers cost inflation
└── Partnership approach

DISADVANTAGES:
├── Price uncertainty
├── Cost verification burden
└── Less lender comfort
```

### Offtake Agreement Terms

**Key Provisions:**

| Provision | Typical Terms | Importance |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| **Minimum Purchase** | 50-80% of capacity | Critical for financing |
| **Price Mechanism** | Fixed, indexed, or hybrid | Cash flow predictability |
| **Term** | 3-7 years | Match to debt tenor |
| **Quality Standards** | Detailed specifications | Minimize disputes |
| **Delivery Schedule** | Weekly/monthly volumes | Working capital planning |
| **Payment Terms** | Net 15-30 days | Cash flow timing |
| **Force Majeure** | Standard exclusions | Risk allocation |
| **Termination Rights** | Limited (for cause only) | Contract stability |

## Key Takeaways

1. **Project Finance Structure**: Non-recourse financing based on project cash flows and assets, suitable for large standalone CEA facilities

2. **Capital Budgeting**: Comprehensive development budgets including land, construction, equipment, soft costs, and contingencies essential for planning

3. **Return Metrics**: NPV, IRR, payback period, and DSCR are critical metrics for evaluating project viability and securing financing

4. **Debt Sizing**: DSCR minimum of 1.25x required by most lenders, with average coverage >1.40x over project life

5. **Risk Allocation**: Effective risk allocation among sponsors, lenders, contractors, and offtakers critical for project success

6. **Offtake Agreements**: Revenue contracts with creditworthy customers enable higher leverage and lower cost of capital

7. **Development Process**: Systematic stage-gate process with clear decision criteria reduces development risk and improves outcomes
