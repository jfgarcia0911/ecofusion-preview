## 13.3 Financial Due Diligence Deep Dive

### Quality of Earnings Analysis

```
QUALITY OF EARNINGS (QoE) REVIEW

Purpose: Verify reported earnings are sustainable and recurring

KEY AREAS OF FOCUS:

1. REVENUE QUALITY
├── Revenue Recognition Policies
│   ├── When is revenue recognized?
│   ├── Are policies consistent with GAAP?
│   ├── Any aggressive revenue recognition?
│   └── Match with customer contracts

├── Revenue Composition
│   ├── Recurring vs. one-time
│   ├── Organic growth vs. acquisitions
│   ├── Geographic/customer/product mix
│   └── Seasonality patterns

├── Revenue Adjustments
│   ├── Returns and allowances
│   ├── Discounts and rebates
│   ├── Bad debt provisions
│   └── Deferred revenue

Example Red Flags:
✗ Revenue recognized before delivery
✗ Large end-of-quarter revenue spikes
✗ Significant related-party revenue
✗ Revenue reversals in subsequent periods
✗ Accounting policy changes to boost revenue

2. COST ANALYSIS
├── COGS Normalization
│   ├── Identify one-time costs
│   ├── Normalize for capacity utilization
│   ├── Adjust for non-recurring items
│   └── Match costs to revenue period

├── Operating Expense Review
│   ├── Discretionary vs. necessary expenses
│   ├── Owner/related party expenses
│   ├── Deferred maintenance
│   └── Below-market compensation

Normalized EBITDA Calculation:
Reported EBITDA:                    $5,250,000
Adjustments:
├── + One-time severance costs         $285,000
├── + Below-market CEO comp            $150,000
├── + Deferred maintenance             $225,000
├── - Non-recurring grant income      ($175,000)
├── - Related party below-market rent ($100,000)
                                    ───────────
Normalized EBITDA:                  $5,635,000

3. WORKING CAPITAL ANALYSIS
├── AR Quality
│   ├── Aging analysis (>90 days problematic)
│   ├── Collection patterns
│   ├── Bad debt reserves adequacy
│   └── Customer creditworthiness

├── Inventory Analysis
│   ├── Turnover rates
│   ├── Obsolescence risk
│   ├── Valuation method (FIFO vs. LIFO)
│   └── Shrinkage and waste

├── AP Review
│   ├── Payment terms compliance
│   ├── Stretched payables (warning sign)
│   ├── Accrued liabilities adequacy
│   └── Supplier relationships

Example Issue Identified:
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO): 45 days
Industry norm: 25-30 days
Issue: Collections slower than reported
Impact: Additional working capital needed
Adjustment: -$500K to NWC target

4. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE REVIEW
├── Maintenance vs. Growth CapEx
│   ├── Separate recurring from one-time
│   ├── Identify deferred maintenance
│   └── Assess adequacy of historical CapEx

├── Future CapEx Requirements
│   ├── Equipment replacement schedule
│   ├── Capacity expansion needs
│   ├── Technology upgrades
│   └── Facility improvements

Example Finding:
Reported Maintenance CapEx: $300K/year
Industry standard: 3-4% of PP&E gross
PP&E Gross: $25M
Required Maintenance CapEx: $750K-$1M/year
Shortfall: $450K-$700K/year
Impact: Lower sustainable FCF than reported

5. TAX REVIEW
├── Effective Tax Rate Analysis
├── NOL Carryforwards
├── Tax Credits and Incentives
├── Tax Contingencies
└── Deferred Tax Assets/Liabilities

QoE SUMMARY EXAMPLE:

Reported Net Income:                $3,750,000
Reported EBITDA:                    $5,250,000

Adjustments to EBITDA:
├── Normalize one-time items:         +$560,000
├── Below-market expenses:            +$150,000
├── Non-recurring income:            (-$275,000)
                                    ───────────
Adjusted EBITDA:                    $5,685,000

Cash Flow Adjustments:
├── Additional maintenance CapEx:    (-$450,000)
├── Working capital normalization:   (-$500,000)
├── Tax adjustments:                 (-$125,000)
                                    ───────────
Sustainable Cash Flow:              $4,610,000

Valuation Impact:
├── Using 10x EBITDA multiple
├── Reported EBITDA: $5,250K × 10 = $52.5M
├── Adjusted EBITDA: $5,685K × 10 = $56.9M
├── Adjusted for cash needs: $56.9M - $1.075M = $55.8M
└── Net impact: +$3.3M (+6%)

In this case, adjustments actually increased value!
```

## 13.5 Red Flags and Deal Breakers

### Critical Issues in CEA Due Diligence

```
RED FLAG CATEGORIES AND RESPONSES

CATEGORY 1: FINANCIAL RED FLAGS

Red Flag: Revenue Declining
├── Severity: High
├── Cause for concern: Fundamental business issue
├── Investigation: Why? Customer loss? Competition? Quality?
├── Potential response:
│   ├── Walkaway if structural decline
│   ├── Price reduction if temporary/fixable
│   └── Earnout structure tied to turnaround

Red Flag: Gross Margins Compressing
├── Severity: High
├── Analysis: Pricing pressure? Cost increases? Efficiency declining?
├── Impact on valuation: Lower sustainable EBITDA
└── Response: Require cost reduction plan, adjust valuation

Red Flag: Unrecorded Liabilities
├── Severity: Critical (deal breaker)
├── Examples: Environmental cleanup, pending litigation, tax issues
├── Response: Require adjustment to purchase price or escrow
└── May kill deal if material

CATEGORY 2: OPERATIONAL RED FLAGS

Red Flag: Declining Yields
├── Severity: High
├── Root cause: Disease? Equipment degradation? Lost knowledge?
├── Impact: Higher costs, lower revenue potential
└── Response: Forensic analysis, plan to remediate, valuation hit

Red Flag: Single Point of Failure
├── Severity: Medium-High
├── Examples: One master grower, single climate system, one customer
├── Risk: Business disruption
└── Response: Require transition plan, retention agreements

Red Flag: Food Safety Violations
├── Severity: Critical
├── Examples: Pathogen detections, recalls, certification failures
├── Impact: Regulatory risk, customer loss, liability
└── Response: Immediate remediation plan or walk away

CATEGORY 3: LEGAL/COMPLIANCE RED FLAGS

Red Flag: IP Not Owned by Company
├── Severity: Critical (potential deal breaker)
├── Example: Founder retained IP rights
├── Impact: Company doesn't own core technology
└── Response: Require immediate IP assignment or walk

Red Flag: Customer Contracts Not Assignable
├── Severity: Medium-High
├── Impact: Revenue at risk in acquisition
├── Response: Require customer consents as closing condition
└── May need to renegotiate contracts

Red Flag: Environmental Issues
├── Severity: Varies (can be critical)
├── Examples: Contamination, permit violations, PFAS in water
├── Response: Phase II assessment, escrow for remediation
└── May kill deal if expensive/uncertain

CATEGORY 4: MARKET/COMMERCIAL RED FLAGS

Red Flag: Customer Concentration >50%
├── Severity: Medium-High
├── Risk: Loss of top customer devastating
├── Mitigation: Long-term contract, customer diversification plan
└── Response: Value discount for concentration risk

Red Flag: No Contracts with Key Customers
├── Severity: Medium
├── Risk: Customers can leave anytime
├── Response: Require contracts pre-close or price adjustment
└── Include earnout tied to customer retention

DEAL BREAKER EXAMPLES:

1. Undisclosed Material Litigation
Situation: $5M lawsuit not disclosed
Impact: Potential $5M liability + reputational damage
Response: Deal terminated due to non-disclosure

2. Food Safety Crisis
Situation: Active Salmonella investigation
Impact: Facility shutdown risk, customer loss
Response: Deal paused or killed

3. IP Ownership Dispute
Situation: Founder claims ownership of growing protocols
Impact: Can't operate without founder
Response: Deal structure changed or killed

4. Major Customer Loss Post-LOI
Situation: 40% customer cancels during diligence
Impact: Fundamentally changes business
Response: Renegotiate price or walk away

5. Environmental Contamination
Situation: Phase II finds soil contamination
Impact: $2M+ cleanup cost
Response: Price reduction or escrow, or walk away

YELLOW FLAGS (Not Deal Breakers):

✓ Fixable Issues:
├── Organizational gaps (hire key roles)
├── Process deficiencies (implement systems)
├── Customer concentration (mitigate over time)
├── Minor compliance issues (remediate)
└── Equipment maintenance backlog (budget for it)

Approach:
├── Identify issues during DD
├── Quantify remediation costs
├── Adjust purchase price or structure
├── Create post-close action plan
└── May use escrow or indemnification

GREEN FLAGS (Positive Discoveries):

✓ Better than expected:
├── Hidden assets (undervalued real estate)
├── Undisclosed contracts or relationships
├── Better yields than reported
├── Stronger customer relationships
└── Valuable but unrecognized IP

Impact: May justify higher valuation or faster close
```

### Conducting Effective DD

```
INVESTOR DD BEST PRACTICES

PREPARATION (Before Kicking Off DD)
├── Define investment thesis clearly
├── Identify key questions to answer
├── Assemble DD team (internal + external advisors)
├── Create DD checklist (comprehensive)
└── Establish timeline and milestones

EXECUTION PHASE

Week 1: Document Review and Analysis
├── Review all provided materials
├── Identify gaps and questions
├── Prepare preliminary issues list
├── Request additional information
└── Schedule site visit and management meetings

Week 2-3: Deep Dive and Site Visit
├── Detailed financial analysis (QoE)
├── Facility tour and observation
├── Management presentations and Q&A
├── Employee and customer conversations
└── Technical and operational assessment

Week 4-5: Follow-Up and Validation
├── Address all open questions
├── Reference calls (customers, suppliers, employees)
├── Industry expert consultations
├── Finalize findings and issues
└── Prepare investment committee memo

Week 6: Decision and Term Finalization
├── Investment committee presentation
├── Final negotiation of terms based on findings
├── Adjust valuation or structure as needed
├── Proceed to definitive documentation or pass
└── Communicate decision

KEY QUESTIONS TO ANSWER:

Strategic Fit:
□ Does this align with our investment thesis?
□ What is the market opportunity size and growth?
□ What is the competitive position and differentiation?
□ Can this achieve our target returns?

Financial Performance:
□ Are financials accurate and sustainable?
□ What is the quality of earnings?
□ What are the cash flow dynamics?
□ What is the appropriate valuation?

Operational Capabilities:
□ Can operations scale efficiently?
□ What are the technology/IP advantages?
□ How strong is the management team?
□ What are key operational risks?

Market Position:
□ Who are the customers and how loyal?
□ What is the competitive landscape?
□ Are there barriers to entry/competitive moats?
□ What are growth opportunities?

Risk Assessment:
□ What could go wrong?
□ How likely and how severe?
□ Are risks acceptable given potential return?
□ Can risks be mitigated?

Value Creation:
□ How will we help company create value?
□ What is the path to exit?
□ What multiple will it command at exit?
□ Does this achieve our return targets?

DECISION FRAMEWORK:

Go/No-Go Criteria:
├── Strategic alignment: ✓ or ✗
├── Return potential: >3x? >20% IRR?
├── Risk level: Within tolerance?
├── Team quality: Capable of execution?
├── Market opportunity: Large and growing?
└── Competitive position: Defensible?

All must be YES to proceed

Investment Committee Memo Outline:
├── Executive Summary (recommendation)
├── Investment Thesis
├── Company Overview
├── Market Analysis
├── Financial Analysis
├── Operational Assessment
├── Management Team
├── Risks and Mitigation
├── Valuation and Returns Analysis
├── Post-Investment Value Creation Plan
└── Recommendation (Approve/Decline/Defer)
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Thorough Preparation**: Organize comprehensive data room before raising capital; saves time and builds credibility

2. **Quality of Earnings**: Financial due diligence focuses on sustainable, recurring cash flows, not just reported numbers

3. **CEA-Specific Items**: Yield performance, food safety, and facility condition are critical operational DD items for CEA

4. **Red Flags**: Identify and address issues early; some are fixable, some are deal-breakers

5. **Proactive Disclosure**: Disclosing known issues upfront builds trust and prevents surprises that kill deals

6. **DD is Two-Way**: Founders should conduct diligence on investors just as investors diligence companies

7. **External Experts**: Leverage advisors (QoE accountants, technical experts, lawyers) for specialized DD areas

8. **Timeline Management**: DD takes 6-12 weeks typically; rushing leads to missed issues or killed deals
