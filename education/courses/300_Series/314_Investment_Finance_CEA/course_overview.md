## Financial Analysis Framework

### Key Financial Metrics for CEA

| Metric | Formula | Target Range |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | 40-60% |
| EBITDA Margin | EBITDA / Revenue | 15-30% |
| Revenue per sq ft | Annual Revenue / Production sq ft | $50-150 |
| CapEx per sq ft | Total Capital / Production sq ft | $50-500 |
| Yield per sq ft | Annual Production lbs / sq ft | 20-100 |
| Labor cost % | Labor Cost / Revenue | 20-40% |
| Energy cost % | Energy Cost / Revenue | 10-25% |

### Unit Economics Analysis

```
UNIT ECONOMICS - VERTICAL FARM LETTUCE

Revenue per Head:                    $2.00
├── Wholesale price per head
│
Cost of Goods Sold:                 -$1.10 (55%)
├── Seeds/transplants              -$0.08
├── Nutrients                      -$0.05
├── Labor (harvest, pack)          -$0.35
├── Packaging                      -$0.15
├── Energy (allocated)             -$0.30
├── Water (allocated)              -$0.02
└── Other variable                 -$0.15

Gross Profit per Head:              $0.90 (45%)

Fixed Cost Allocation:             -$0.50
├── Rent/mortgage                  -$0.15
├── Labor (fixed)                  -$0.20
├── Insurance, admin               -$0.10
└── Depreciation                   -$0.05

Operating Profit per Head:          $0.40 (20%)

Break-even Volume: Fixed Costs / Gross Profit per Head
Example: $500,000 / $0.90 = 555,556 heads annually
```

## Investment Analysis Methods

### Capital Budgeting Techniques

| Method | Formula | Use Case |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Payback Period** | Initial Investment / Annual Cash Flow | Quick screening |
| **NPV** | Σ(Cash Flow / (1+r)^t) - Initial Investment | Primary decision tool |
| **IRR** | Rate where NPV = 0 | Compare alternatives |
| **MIRR** | Modified IRR with reinvestment rate | More realistic |
| **Profitability Index** | PV of Cash Flows / Initial Investment | Capital rationing |

### NPV Example Calculation

```
CEA Project Investment Analysis

Initial Investment: $2,000,000
Project Life: 10 years
Discount Rate: 12%
Annual Cash Flows (after stabilization):

Year 1:  $150,000 (ramp-up)
Year 2:  $300,000
Year 3:  $400,000
Year 4+: $450,000 (stabilized)

NPV Calculation:
Year 0: -$2,000,000 × 1.000 = -$2,000,000
Year 1:    $150,000 × 0.893 =    $133,950
Year 2:    $300,000 × 0.797 =    $239,100
Year 3:    $400,000 × 0.712 =    $284,800
Year 4:    $450,000 × 0.636 =    $286,200
Year 5:    $450,000 × 0.567 =    $255,150
Year 6:    $450,000 × 0.507 =    $228,150
Year 7:    $450,000 × 0.452 =    $203,400
Year 8:    $450,000 × 0.404 =    $181,800
Year 9:    $450,000 × 0.361 =    $162,450
Year 10:   $450,000 × 0.322 =    $144,900
─────────────────────────────────────────
NPV = $119,900 (Positive = Accept)

IRR = 14.3% (> 12% hurdle = Accept)
Payback = 5.7 years
```

## Valuation Methods

### Valuation Approaches

| Method | Best For | Considerations |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **DCF** | Stable operations | Forecast accuracy critical |
| **Comparable Companies** | Market benchmarks | Finding true comparables |
| **Precedent Transactions** | M&A context | Deal-specific factors |
| **Asset-Based** | Liquidation, early stage | Ignores going concern |
| **Revenue Multiple** | Growth companies | Industry-specific ranges |
| **EBITDA Multiple** | Mature operations | Adjustments needed |

### CEA Valuation Multiples

| Metric | Range | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Revenue multiple | 1-5x | Higher for growth, tech |
| EBITDA multiple | 6-15x | Varies by profitability |
| Price per sq ft | $100-800 | By facility type |
| Price per lb capacity | $5-20 | Annual production capacity |

## Financing Options

### Debt vs. Equity Comparison

| Factor | Debt | Equity |
|--------|------|--------|
| Ownership dilution | None | Yes |
| Required payments | Fixed | Discretionary |
| Cost of capital | Lower | Higher |
| Covenants | Yes | Fewer |
| Upside sharing | None | Yes |
| Risk to company | Default risk | Less risky |
| Availability | Requires collateral | Requires growth story |

### Financing Sources by Stage

| Stage | Typical Sources | Amount Range |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| Concept/Seed | Self, F&F, Angels | $50K-$500K |
| Startup | Angels, Seed VC | $500K-$3M |
| Early growth | Series A VC | $3M-$15M |
| Expansion | Series B/C, PE | $15M-$100M+ |
| Mature | Banks, bonds, PE | $50M+ |

## Deal Structure Elements

### Term Sheet Components

| Element | Description | Typical Range |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Valuation | Pre/post-money | Market-based |
| Investment amount | Capital raised | As needed |
| Security type | Common, preferred, convertible | Preferred typical |
| Board seats | Investor representation | 1-2 seats |
| Liquidation preference | Return priority | 1-2x |
| Anti-dilution | Protection mechanism | Weighted average |
| Vesting | Founder shares | 4-year, 1-year cliff |
| Pro-rata rights | Future investment rights | Common |
