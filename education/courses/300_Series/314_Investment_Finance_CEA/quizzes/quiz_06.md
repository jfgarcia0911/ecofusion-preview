## Questions

### Question 1
Which valuation method is most appropriate for a mature CEA operation with stable cash flows?

A) Asset-based valuation
B) Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
C) Revenue multiple only
D) Liquidation value

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the primary challenge when using comparable company analysis for CEA valuation?

A) Too many comparable companies
B) Difficulty finding true comparables in the emerging CEA sector
C) Comparable companies don't have financial statements
D) The method is too complex

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
In DCF valuation, what is terminal value?

A) The value at project completion
B) The value of assets at the end of useful life
C) Estimated value beyond explicit forecast period
D) Salvage value of equipment

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
According to typical CEA valuation multiples, what is the common range for EBITDA multiples in mature operations?

A) 2-5x
B) 6-15x
C) 16-25x
D) 30-40x

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What does the revenue multiple method value?

A) Company value as a multiple of annual or projected revenue
B) Revenue as a multiple of EBITDA
C) Multiple revenue streams
D) Revenue multiplied by number of facilities

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 6
Which valuation approach is most appropriate for a pre-revenue CEA technology startup?

A) EBITDA multiple
B) Asset-based valuation
C) Comparable company or venture capital method
D) Dividend discount model

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
In precedent transaction analysis, what key factor must be adjusted for when comparing deals?

A) Weather conditions
B) Deal-specific factors (control premium, synergies, market conditions)
C) Number of investors
D) Geographic location only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is an enterprise value?

A) Value of equity only
B) Total firm value including debt and equity, minus cash
C) Market value of all facilities
D) Revenue times growth rate

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
When valuing a CEA facility on a price per square foot basis, what is a typical range?

A) $10-50 per sq ft
B) $50-100 per sq ft
C) $100-800 per sq ft
D) $1,000-2,000 per sq ft

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
Why might a CEA technology company command higher valuation multiples than an operating farm?

A) Higher capital requirements
B) Greater scalability and higher margins
C) More employees
D) Larger facilities

**Answer:** B
