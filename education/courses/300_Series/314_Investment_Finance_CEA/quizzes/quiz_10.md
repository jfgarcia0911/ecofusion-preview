## Instructions

## Questions

### Question 1
What is a term sheet?

A) List of payment terms for customers
B) Non-binding outline of key investment terms and conditions
C) Final legal contract
D) Employment agreement

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is included in the capital stack?

A) Only equity investments
B) Only debt instruments
C) All layers of financing (debt, mezzanine, preferred equity, common equity)
D) Physical equipment only

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
In priority of payment during liquidation, which receives payment first?

A) Common equity
B) Preferred equity
C) Mezzanine debt
D) Senior debt

**Answer:** D

---

### Question 4
What is a drag-along right?

A) Right to force all shareholders to sell if majority agrees
B) Right to drag equipment between facilities
C) Right to delay decisions
D) Right to veto sales

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
What is a tag-along right?

A) Right to add terms to contracts
B) Right to join when major shareholders sell their shares
C) Right to priority distributions
D) Right to board observation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What does a board seat provision in a term sheet specify?

A) Physical seating arrangements
B) Investor representation on company's board of directors
C) Number of employees
D) Meeting frequency only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is an earn-out provision?

A) Additional payment based on future performance milestones
B) Employee bonus structure
C) Revenue sharing agreement
D) Customer loyalty program

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 8
What is the purpose of a lock-up period?

A) Security system requirement
B) Restriction on selling shares for specified time period
C) Construction timeline
D) Customer contract length

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
In a joint venture structure, what is created?

A) New legal entity owned by two or more parties
B) Simple loan agreement
C) Customer partnership
D) Employee co-op

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 10
What is a material adverse change (MAC) clause?

A) Equipment upgrade requirement
B) Provision allowing parties to renegotiate or exit if significant negative changes occur
C) Production quality standard
D) Weather-related provision

**Answer:** B
