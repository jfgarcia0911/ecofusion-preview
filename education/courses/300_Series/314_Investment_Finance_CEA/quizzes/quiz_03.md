## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary characteristic of project finance that distinguishes it from corporate finance?

A) Higher interest rates
B) Financing based on project cash flows rather than corporate balance sheet
C) Shorter loan terms
D) No collateral required

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
In a typical CEA project finance structure, what is the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) minimum threshold lenders usually require?

A) 0.8x
B) 1.0x
C) 1.2-1.3x
D) 2.0x

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What does "non-recourse" or "limited recourse" financing mean in project finance?

A) The loan has no interest charges
B) Lenders can only seek repayment from project assets and cash flows, not parent company
C) The project requires no collateral
D) The loan must be repaid within one year

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Which of the following is typically NOT a key participant in a CEA project finance structure?

A) Equity sponsors
B) Senior lenders
C) Retail customers
D) Engineering/construction contractors

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
What is the purpose of an offtake agreement in project finance?

A) Secure future revenue by contracting product sales in advance
B) Reduce construction costs
C) Eliminate equity requirements
D) Avoid paying taxes

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 6
In the capital stack, which financing layer typically has the highest risk and highest potential return?

A) Senior debt
B) Mezzanine debt
C) Preferred equity
D) Common equity

**Answer:** D

---

### Question 7
What is the typical Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio range for senior debt in CEA project finance?

A) 30-40%
B) 50-65%
C) 75-85%
D) 90-100%

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which covenant restricts a project company's ability to pay dividends before meeting certain conditions?

A) Debt Service Coverage Ratio
B) Leverage Ratio
C) Distribution Lock-up Covenant
D) Current Ratio

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is the construction period risk in CEA project finance?

A) Risk that customers won't buy the produce
B) Risk of cost overruns, delays, or technical failures during building phase
C) Risk of crop disease
D) Risk of weather damage

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Which government program is commonly used to support CEA project financing in the United States?

A) Federal Reserve Lending Program
B) SBA 7(a) Loan Program
C) USDA Rural Development Programs
D) EPA Green Bonds

**Answer:** C
