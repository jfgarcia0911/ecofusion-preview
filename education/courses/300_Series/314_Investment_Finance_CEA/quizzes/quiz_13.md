## Instructions

## Questions

### Question 1
What is due diligence?

A) Employee duty requirements
B) Comprehensive investigation and analysis before investment or acquisition
C) Daily operational checklist
D) Customer satisfaction survey

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which of the following is typically NOT a major category of due diligence?

A) Financial due diligence
B) Legal due diligence
C) Employee personal social media review
D) Technical/operational due diligence

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What is the purpose of financial due diligence?

A) Verify accuracy of financial information and identify risks
B) Set future budgets
C) Hire accounting staff
D) File tax returns

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 4
What is a data room in the context of due diligence?

A) Server room in facility
B) Secure repository of company documents for investor review
C) Meeting room for negotiations
D) Climate-controlled growing area

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is quality of earnings (QoE) analysis?

A) Assessment of product quality
B) Analysis of sustainability and quality of reported earnings
C) Employee performance review
D) Customer satisfaction analysis

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What should operational due diligence for a CEA facility include?

A) Only financial statements
B) Facility inspection, yields, equipment condition, processes, and team capabilities
C) Just legal documents
D) Only customer contracts

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a common red flag discovered during due diligence?

A) High customer satisfaction
B) Revenue concentration with single customer
C) Diverse product mix
D) Experienced management team

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
How long does a typical due diligence process take for a significant CEA investment?

A) 1-3 days
B) 1-2 weeks
C) 6-12 weeks
D) 1-2 years

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What is the purpose of legal due diligence?

A) Verify legal compliance and identify liabilities
B) Draft new contracts
C) Hire attorneys
D) Change company structure

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 10
What should be included in technical due diligence for a CEA technology company?

A) Only patents
B) IP assessment, technology validation, scalability, and competitive positioning
C) Just financial projections
D) Employee count

**Answer:** B
