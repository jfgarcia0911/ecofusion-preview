## Questions

### Question 1
What is an exit strategy?

A) Emergency evacuation plan
B) Plan for investors/owners to realize returns and liquidity
C) Employee termination policy
D) Customer contract cancellation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is a strategic acquisition?

A) Buying equipment strategically
B) Purchase by industry participant for strategic value beyond financial returns
C) Employee stock purchase
D) Asset liquidation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the typical timeframe for achieving an exit in venture-backed CEA companies?

A) 1-2 years
B) 3-4 years
C) 5-10 years
D) 20+ years

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is an IPO (Initial Public Offering)?

A) Internal promotion opportunity
B) Process of offering shares to public investors on stock exchange
C) International purchasing organization
D) Investor portfolio optimization

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What typical return range is expected from a strategic acquisition exit?

A) 1-2x
B) 3-10x
C) 15-20x
D) 50x+

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is a management buyout (MBO)?

A) Company buying out customers
B) Company's management team purchasing the business
C) Majority shareholder buying all assets
D) Equipment purchase by managers

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What makes a CEA company attractive for IPO?

A) Small size and limited operations
B) Scale, profitability, growth, and market leadership
C) Recent founding
D) Single facility operation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is a recapitalization?

A) Purchasing new capital equipment
B) Restructuring company's debt and equity mix
C) Relocating headquarters
D) Rebranding the company

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is a key advantage of a strategic buyer over a financial buyer?

A) Lower purchase price
B) Ability to pay premium for synergies and strategic value
C) Faster closing process
D) Less due diligence

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a typical holding period for private equity investors before exit?

A) 1-2 years
B) 4-7 years
C) 10-15 years
D) 25+ years

**Answer:** B
