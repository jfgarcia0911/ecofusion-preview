## Questions

### Question 1
Which financial statement shows a company's financial position at a specific point in time?

A) Income Statement
B) Balance Sheet
C) Cash Flow Statement
D) Statement of Retained Earnings

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the formula for calculating gross profit?

A) Revenue - Operating Expenses
B) Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold
C) Revenue - All Expenses
D) EBITDA - Depreciation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
In the CEA industry, what is a typical target gross margin range?

A) 20-30%
B) 30-40%
C) 40-60%
D) 60-80%

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What does EBITDA stand for?

A) Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Debt, and Amortization
B) Earnings Before Income, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization
C) Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization
D) Earnings Before Interest, Total Debt, and Assets

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 5
Which section of the cash flow statement includes proceeds from debt or equity issuance?

A) Operating Activities
B) Investing Activities
C) Financing Activities
D) None of the above

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What is working capital?

A) Total Assets minus Total Liabilities
B) Current Assets minus Current Liabilities
C) Cash and Cash Equivalents
D) Total Equity

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
Which ratio measures a company's ability to pay short-term obligations?

A) Debt-to-Equity Ratio
B) Return on Assets
C) Current Ratio
D) Asset Turnover

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 8
Capital expenditures (CapEx) appear in which financial statement section?

A) Operating Activities in Cash Flow Statement
B) Investing Activities in Cash Flow Statement
C) Financing Activities in Cash Flow Statement
D) Operating Expenses in Income Statement

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is the accounting equation that must always balance?

A) Revenue - Expenses = Net Income
B) Assets = Liabilities + Equity
C) Cash In - Cash Out = Net Cash Flow
D) Gross Profit - Operating Expenses = EBITDA

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In a CEA operation, which of the following would typically be classified as a fixed cost component of COGS?

A) Seeds and transplants
B) Packaging materials
C) Harvest labor
D) Facility maintenance salaries

**Answer:** D
