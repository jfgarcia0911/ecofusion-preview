## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended length for an investor pitch deck?

A) 3-5 slides
B) 10-15 slides
C) 30-40 slides
D) 100+ slides

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What should be the opening slide of a pitch deck?

A) Detailed financial projections
B) Problem statement or compelling market opportunity
C) Full team biographies
D) Legal disclaimers

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the purpose of the "traction" slide?

A) Show facility construction progress
B) Demonstrate proof of concept, customer adoption, and momentum
C) Display vehicle fleet
D) Show employee count

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
When investors ask "Why you?", what are they assessing?

A) Personal background
B) Team capability and competitive advantages
C) Location choice
D) Office space

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the "ask" in an investment pitch?

A) Asking questions of investors
B) Specific amount of funding needed and proposed terms
C) Request for advice
D) Asking for customers

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What should the "use of funds" section clearly communicate?

A) Past spending history
B) How investment capital will be specifically deployed
C) Personal expenses
D) Competitor spending

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is an elevator pitch?

A) Sales pitch for vertical farming
B) Concise 30-60 second summary of business and value proposition
C) Technical specification document
D) Detailed business plan

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What makes a compelling market size argument?

A) Vague estimates
B) Bottom-up and top-down analysis showing large, addressable opportunity
C) International statistics only
D) Historical data only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
How should competition be addressed in a pitch?

A) Claim no competition exists
B) Acknowledge competition honestly and articulate differentiation
C) Criticize competitors extensively
D) Ignore the topic

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is the most important thing to remember when pitching to investors?

A) Use complex technical jargon
B) Tell a compelling story that clearly communicates value proposition and opportunity
C) Memorize exact script
D) Focus only on technology details

**Answer:** B
