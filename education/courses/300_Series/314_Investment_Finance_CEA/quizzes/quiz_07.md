## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary advantage of debt financing over equity financing?

A) No repayment required
B) Lower cost of capital and no ownership dilution
C) More flexible terms
D) Unlimited availability

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is senior debt?

A) Debt provided by experienced lenders
B) First-priority debt with highest claim on assets and cash flows
C) Debt for senior management
D) The oldest outstanding loan

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is a typical interest rate range for senior bank debt in CEA projects (in a moderate rate environment)?

A) 1-3%
B) 4-8%
C) 10-15%
D) 20%+

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is mezzanine debt?

A) Debt for middle floors of vertical farms
B) Subordinated debt between senior debt and equity, often with equity features
C) Short-term working capital loans
D) Government-subsidized loans

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
Which of the following is a common financial covenant in debt agreements?

A) Minimum production yield
B) Maximum customer complaints
C) Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio
D) Maximum number of employees

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What happens when a borrower violates a debt covenant?

A) Automatic bankruptcy
B) Lender can declare default and accelerate repayment (or negotiate waiver)
C) Interest rate automatically increases 10%
D) Nothing, covenants are just guidelines

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is the purpose of requiring collateral for a loan?

A) To increase interest income
B) To provide security for lender in case of default
C) To make accounting easier
D) Required by tax law

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is an amortization schedule?

A) List of assets being financed
B) Timeline showing periodic principal and interest payments over loan term
C) List of all company debts
D) Schedule of covenant testing dates

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is refinancing?

A) Paying off debt early
B) Replacing existing debt with new debt, often with better terms
C) Adding more debt
D) Converting debt to equity

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
Why might a CEA operation struggle to obtain traditional bank financing?

A) Banks don't lend to agriculture
B) High capital intensity, limited operating history, and technology risk
C) Interest rates are too low
D) CEA is too profitable

**Answer:** B
