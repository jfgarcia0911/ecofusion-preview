# Quiz 11: Investor Relations

**Course:** 314 - Investment & Finance for CEA
**Module:** 11 - Investor Relations
**Total Points:** 10 (1 point per question)
**Passing Score:** 70%

---

## Instructions

Answer all 10 questions. Select the best answer for each multiple-choice question.

---

## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of investor relations?

A) Selling produce to investors
B) Maintaining transparent communication and managing investor expectations
C) Avoiding investor contact
D) Only required for public companies

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
How frequently should formal investor updates typically be provided?

A) Daily
B) Weekly
C) Quarterly or monthly
D) Only when asked

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 3
What should be included in a regular investor update?

A) Only positive news
B) Financial performance, operational metrics, key developments, and challenges
C) Just financial statements
D) Future projections only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is a board of directors' primary fiduciary duty?

A) Maximize employee satisfaction
B) Act in best interests of shareholders
C) Minimize operational costs
D) Increase production volume

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
How should management communicate bad news to investors?

A) Hide it until resolved
B) Blame external factors
C) Transparently and proactively, with mitigation plans
D) Wait for investors to discover it

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 6
What is a board observer?

A) Someone who watches board meetings without voting rights
B) Full board member
C) Customer representative
D) Government regulator

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 7
What key financial metrics should be tracked in investor dashboards for CEA operations?

A) Only revenue
B) Revenue, EBITDA, cash burn, yields, and key unit economics
C) Just profit and loss
D) Stock price only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
What is the purpose of an annual investor meeting?

A) Required by law for all companies
B) Comprehensive review of performance, strategy, and future plans
C) Social gathering only
D) Product sampling event

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
How should investor questions and concerns be handled?

A) Ignored if inconvenient
B) Delegated to junior staff
C) Addressed promptly and directly by appropriate senior team members
D) Deferred indefinitely

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 10
What is a management rights letter?

A) Employee rights document
B) Agreement granting investors information and consultation rights
C) Customer contract
D) Supplier agreement

**Answer:** B
