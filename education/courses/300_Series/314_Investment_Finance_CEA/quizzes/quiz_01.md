## Questions

### Question 1
What was the primary driver that marked the "2022-2024 Market Correction" phase in CEA investment?

A) Decline in LED technology costs
B) Rising interest rates and focus on profitability
C) New government regulations
D) Reduced consumer demand for produce

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which investor type typically has the longest holding period for CEA investments?

A) Venture Capital (7-10 years)
B) Private Equity (4-7 years)
C) Real Estate/Infrastructure Investors (10-20+ years)
D) Strategic Investors (3-5 years)

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 3

A) 3-5x
B) 5-7x
C) 10x+
D) 2-3x

**Answer:** C
