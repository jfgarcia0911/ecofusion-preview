## Questions

### Question 1
What is the primary disadvantage of equity financing?

A) Higher cost of capital and ownership dilution
B) Required fixed payments
C) Short repayment period
D) Limited availability

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 2
What is the difference between pre-money and post-money valuation?

A) Before vs after revenue generation
B) Before vs after adding new investment amount
C) Private vs public company valuation
D) Domestic vs international valuation

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
If a company has a pre-money valuation of $20M and raises $5M, what is the post-money valuation?

A) $15M
B) $20M
C) $25M
D) $100M

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
What is preferred stock?

A) Stock preferred by management
B) Equity with preferential rights (liquidation preference, dividends)
C) The first stock issued by a company
D) Stock with voting rights only

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What does a 1.5x liquidation preference mean?

A) Preferred shareholders receive 150% voting rights
B) Preferred shareholders get 1.5x their investment back before common shareholders in liquidation
C) Stock price increases 1.5x annually
D) Company must raise 1.5x more capital

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is equity vesting for founders?

A) Immediate full ownership
B) Earning ownership over time, typically 4 years with 1-year cliff
C) Selling shares to investors
D) Converting equity to debt

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a "down round"?

A) Financing at lower valuation than previous round
B) Reduced production period
C) Decrease in revenue
D) Firing of employees

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 8
What are anti-dilution provisions?

A) Protections preventing water dilution in nutrients
B) Protections for investors against future lower-priced equity rounds
C) Limits on number of shares issued
D) Restrictions on selling shares

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is a pro-rata right?

A) Right to proportional board representation
B) Right to maintain ownership percentage in future rounds
C) Right to approve major decisions
D) Right to dividends

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In a typical Series A funding round for a CEA startup, what amount range is common?

A) $50K-$500K
B) $500K-$2M
C) $3M-$15M
D) $50M-$100M

**Answer:** C
