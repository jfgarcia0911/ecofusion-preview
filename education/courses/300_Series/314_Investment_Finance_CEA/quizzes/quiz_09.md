## Questions

### Question 1
What is a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)?

A) Insurance policy for investors
B) Convertible security that converts to equity in future financing round
C) Type of senior debt
D) Safety equipment requirement

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
What is the primary advantage of a convertible note?

A) Never needs to be repaid
B) Delays valuation discussion until later round
C) Provides highest returns
D) No interest charges

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is revenue-based financing?

A) Financing based only on projected revenue
B) Repayment as percentage of monthly revenue until cap reached
C) Equity given in exchange for revenue
D) Revenue used as collateral

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
What is crowdfunding?

A) Raising capital from large group of individuals, typically via online platform
B) Group purchasing of produce
C) Employee investment programs
D) Bank consortium lending

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 5
What is a strategic investment?

A) Investment with detailed strategy
B) Investment by industry participant for strategic value beyond financial return
C) Only institutional investments
D) Long-term debt financing

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What are tax credits and incentives?

A) Reduced pricing for customers
B) Government programs reducing tax liability to encourage desired activities
C) Employee benefits
D) Investor tax deductions

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What is a green bond?

A) Debt instrument specifically for environmental/sustainable projects
B) Equity in sustainable companies
C) Government savings bond
D) Employee stock option

**Answer:** A

---

### Question 8
What is equipment financing?

A) Renting all equipment
B) Loan secured by the equipment being purchased
C) Leasing from manufacturer
D) Government equipment grants

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
What is an advantage of grants compared to other financing?

A) Unlimited amounts available
B) No repayment or equity dilution required
C) Faster to obtain
D) No application process

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What is a disadvantage of revenue-based financing?

A) Requires profitable operations
B) Can be expensive in high-growth scenarios due to percentage of revenue payments
C) Requires collateral
D) Causes ownership dilution

**Answer:** B
