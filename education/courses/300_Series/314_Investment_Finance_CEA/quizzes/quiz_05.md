## Questions

### Question 1
What is Net Present Value (NPV)?

A) Total revenue minus total costs
B) The sum of all future cash flows
C) Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment
D) Internal rate of return multiplied by investment

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
A project with a positive NPV at the required discount rate should generally be:

A) Rejected
B) Accepted
C) Reconsidered
D) Delayed

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
What is the Internal Rate of Return (IRR)?

A) The highest possible return on investment
B) The discount rate that makes NPV equal to zero
C) The average annual profit
D) The ratio of revenue to investment

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
If a CEA project has an IRR of 18% and the company's hurdle rate is 12%, what decision should be made?

A) Reject the project
B) Accept the project
C) IRR cannot be compared to hurdle rate
D) Request more funding

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the payback period?

A) Time required to reach positive EBITDA
B) Time required to recover initial investment from cash flows
C) Total project duration
D) Time to achieve market share goals

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
What is the main limitation of using payback period as the sole investment criterion?

A) It's too complex to calculate
B) It ignores cash flows beyond payback point and time value of money
C) It requires too many assumptions
D) It's only applicable to debt financing

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
What does the Profitability Index (PI) measure?

A) Net profit as a percentage of revenue
B) Present value of cash flows per dollar invested
C) Percentage of profitable months
D) Market share profitability

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
A Profitability Index greater than 1.0 indicates:

A) The project will lose money
B) The project will break even
C) The project creates value (NPV > 0)
D) The payback period is too long

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
Why might Modified Internal Rate of Return (MIRR) be preferred over traditional IRR?

A) It's easier to calculate
B) It assumes reinvestment at a more realistic rate
C) It always gives higher returns
D) It doesn't require a discount rate

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In capital rationing situations (limited capital available), which metric is most useful for ranking projects?

A) Payback Period
B) Total NPV
C) Profitability Index
D) Gross Margin

**Answer:** C
