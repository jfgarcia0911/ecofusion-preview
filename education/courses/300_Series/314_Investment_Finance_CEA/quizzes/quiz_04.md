## Questions

### Question 1
What is the recommended flow structure for a financial model?

A) Outputs → Inputs → Calculations
B) Calculations → Outputs → Inputs
C) Inputs → Calculations → Outputs
D) Random organization for flexibility

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 2
In financial model color coding best practices, what color text typically indicates hard-coded inputs/assumptions?

A) Black
B) Blue
C) Green
D) Red

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
For a vertical farm with 50,000 sq ft of production space across 10 levels, what is the net growing area?

A) 50,000 sq ft
B) 100,000 sq ft
C) 500,000 sq ft
D) 5,000 sq ft

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 4
If a crop has 4.5 units/sq ft density, 4.3 units/sq ft yield per cycle, and 13 cycles per year, what is the annual yield per sq ft?

A) 45.9 units
B) 55.9 units
C) 58.5 units
D) 250.5 units

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
In the revenue model, what does "shrink and waste adjustments" account for?

A) Facility size reductions
B) Production waste and distribution damage
C) Employee reductions
D) Market price decreases

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
Which of the following is typically classified as a variable COGS component?

A) Facility maintenance salaries
B) Property taxes
C) Seeds and propagation materials
D) Equipment depreciation

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 7
What is the standard depreciation method used in most CEA financial models?

A) Declining balance
B) Straight-line
C) Units of production
D) Sum-of-years-digits

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
In the integrated three-statement model, which statement should be built first to ensure proper flow?

A) Balance Sheet
B) Cash Flow Statement
C) Income Statement
D) All simultaneously

**Answer:** C

---

### Question 9
What does a two-way sensitivity analysis examine?

A) Impact of changing one variable
B) Impact of changing two variables simultaneously
C) Impact over two time periods
D) Two different scenario outcomes

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
In break-even analysis, what is the formula for operating break-even volume?

A) Total Costs / Revenue per Unit
B) Fixed Costs / Selling Price
C) Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin per Unit
D) Variable Costs / Gross Margin

**Answer:** C
