# Grading Rubric
## Course 309: Research Methodology Agriculture

---

## Overview

This rubric provides detailed grading criteria for all assignments and assessments in Course 309. The total possible points across all assessments is 110 points, with a passing threshold of 80% (88 points).

---

## Assignment Breakdown

| Assignment | Points | Weight | Description |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Assignment 1: Literature Review and Synthesis | 15 | 13.6% | In-class research analysis and summary |
| Assignment 2: Experimental Design Development | 20 | 18.2% | In-class trial protocol creation |
| Assignment 3: Data Collection and Analysis | 25 | 22.7% | In-class statistical analysis exercise |
| Assignment 4: Research Proposal Development | 20 | 18.2% | Take-home study design and methodology |
| Assignment 5: Complete Research Project | 30 | 27.3% | Full agricultural research study |
| **Total** | **110** | **100%** | |

**Passing Threshold:** 88 points (80%)

---

## Assignment 1: Literature Review and Synthesis (15 points)
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (4-5 pts) | Good (3 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Source Quality** (5 pts) | Excellent peer-reviewed sources with relevance | Good academic sources | Limited quality sources | Poor source selection |
| **Synthesis** (5 pts) | Clear integration of findings with insights | Good synthesis of research | Basic summary | Weak synthesis |
| **Gap Identification** (5 pts) | Clear research gaps and opportunities identified | Some gaps noted | Limited gap analysis | No gap identification |

**Total:** 15 points

---

## Assignment 2: Experimental Design Development (20 points)
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Research Question** (5 pts) | Clear, testable hypothesis with significance | Good research question | Vague question | Poor question formulation |
| **Methodology** (5 pts) | Rigorous experimental design with controls | Good methodology | Basic design | Flawed design |
| **Variable Control** (5 pts) | Comprehensive control of confounding variables | Good variable management | Limited controls | Poor variable control |
| **Statistical Power** (5 pts) | Appropriate sample size and replication | Reasonable design power | Limited replication | Inadequate power |

**Total:** 20 points

---

## Assignment 3: Data Collection and Analysis (25 points)
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes | **Type:** In-class activity

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (6-7 pts) | Good (4-5 pts) | Fair (2-3 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Data Collection Protocols** (7 pts) | Detailed, standardized measurement procedures | Good data collection methods | Basic protocols | Poor measurement design |
| **Statistical Analysis** (6 pts) | Appropriate statistical tests with interpretation | Correct analysis approach | Basic statistics | Incorrect analysis |
| **Data Visualization** (6 pts) | Clear, professional figures and tables | Good visualizations | Basic charts | Poor presentation |
| **Results Interpretation** (6 pts) | Thoughtful interpretation with limitations | Good interpretation | Basic conclusions | Weak interpretation |

**Total:** 25 points

---

## Assignment 4: Research Proposal Development (20 points)
**Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Type:** Take-home assignment

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (5 pts) | Good (3-4 pts) | Fair (2 pts) | Poor (0-1 pts) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Background and Rationale** (5 pts) | Compelling justification with literature support | Good background | Basic rationale | Weak justification |
| **Research Design** (5 pts) | Detailed methodology with clear procedures | Good design outline | Basic design | Poor methodology |
| **Timeline and Resources** (5 pts) | Realistic timeline with resource planning | Good planning | Basic timeline | No planning |
| **Expected Outcomes** (5 pts) | Clear expected results and impact | Good outcome projection | Vague expectations | No outcomes defined |

**Total:** 20 points

---

## Assignment 5: Complete Research Project (30 points)
**Duration:** 2-4 hours | **Type:** Comprehensive project

### Grading Criteria

| Criterion | Excellent (7-8 pts) | Good (5-6 pts) | Fair (3-4 pts) | Poor (0-2 pts) |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Research Quality** (7 pts) | Rigorous methodology with strong execution | Good research execution | Basic study quality | Poor research quality |
| **Data Analysis** (8 pts) | Comprehensive statistical analysis with depth | Good analytical approach | Limited analysis | Inadequate analysis |
| **Scientific Writing** (8 pts) | Clear, professional scientific communication | Good writing quality | Basic writing | Poor communication |
| **Contribution** (7 pts) | Meaningful contribution to agricultural knowledge | Good practical value | Limited contribution | No clear value |

**Total:** 30 points

---

## Grading Scale

| Percentage | Letter Grade | Point Range | Performance Level |
|------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 90-100% | A | 99-110 | Excellent - Exceeds expectations |
| 80-89% | B | 88-98 | Good - Meets all requirements |
| 70-79% | C | 77-87 | Fair - Meets most requirements |
| 60-69% | D | 66-76 | Poor - Below expectations |
| Below 60% | F | 0-65 | Failing - Does not meet requirements |

**Note:** A minimum of 80% (88 points) is required to pass this course and receive a Certificate of Completion.

---

## Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted within 24 hours of deadline: 10% penalty
- Assignments submitted 24-48 hours late: 25% penalty
- Assignments submitted more than 48 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval
- Extensions may be granted for extenuating circumstances with advance notice

---

## Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. While collaboration and discussion are encouraged during in-class activities, individual assignments must reflect your own analysis and work. Plagiarism or copying will result in a zero for the assignment and may result in dismissal from the course.

---

## Feedback and Revision

- In-class activities receive immediate feedback
- Take-home assignments graded within 5 business days
- Final projects graded within 7 business days
- Students may request clarification on grading decisions
- Major projects may be revised and resubmitted once if score is below 80%
