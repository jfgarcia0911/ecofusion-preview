# Fish Health Glossary

**Course 303:** Advanced Fish Production & Health

---

## A

**Acclimation:** Gradual adjustment of fish to new environmental conditions (temperature, pH, salinity).

**Acid-Fast Stain:** Special staining procedure used to identify Mycobacterium (fish TB).

**Acute:** Sudden onset, severe symptoms, short duration.

**ADC (Apparent Digestibility Coefficient):** Measure of nutrient digestibility expressed as percentage.

**Aeration:** Introduction of air into water to increase dissolved oxygen.

**Alkalinity:** Water's ability to resist pH changes; measured as mg/L CaCO₃.

**Anorexia:** Loss of appetite; refusal to eat.

**Ascites:** Accumulation of fluid in the abdominal cavity (swollen belly).

**Autogenous Vaccine:** Custom vaccine made from pathogens isolated from the specific farm.

---

## B

**Biofilm:** Layer of bacteria and other microorganisms attached to surfaces.

**Biofilter:** System component that uses beneficial bacteria to convert ammonia to less toxic compounds.

**Biomass:** Total weight of fish in a system or tank.

**Biosecurity:** Practices and procedures to prevent disease introduction and spread.

**Broodstock:** Adult fish maintained for breeding purposes.

**BW (Body Weight):** Weight of fish, often used in feeding calculations (% BW/day).

---

## C

**Carrying Capacity:** Maximum biomass a system can support sustainably.

**Chronic:** Long-lasting, persistent disease or condition.

**Cloaca:** Common chamber for digestive, urinary, and reproductive systems in fish.

**Coefficient of Variation (CV):** Statistical measure of size variation in a population; SD ÷ Mean × 100.

**Cohort:** Group of fish stocked at the same time.

**Columnaris:** Bacterial disease caused by Flavobacterium columnare.

**Concurrent Infection:** Multiple pathogens infecting fish simultaneously.

---

## D

**Degree-Days:** Cumulative temperature measurement (days × average temperature).

**Depopulation:** Complete removal of all fish from a system.

**Digestible Energy (DE):** Energy available to fish after digestion; measured in kJ/g.

**Dissolved Oxygen (DO):** Amount of oxygen dissolved in water; measured in mg/L or ppm.

---

## E

**Ectoparasite:** Parasite living on external surfaces (skin, gills, fins).

**Edema:** Fluid accumulation in tissues causing swelling.

**Endoparasite:** Parasite living inside the host (intestine, organs).

**Epizootic:** Disease affecting many animals in a population simultaneously (epidemic in animals).

**Erratic Swimming:** Abnormal swimming behavior (spinning, darting, loss of balance).

**Etiology:** Cause or origin of a disease.

**Euthanasia:** Humane killing of fish.

**Exophthalmia:** Bulging eyes (pop-eye); often sign of bacterial infection or gas bubble disease.

**Extensive Aquaculture:** Low-density, low-input production relying on natural food.

---

## F

**Fallow Period:** Time when a system is empty (no fish) between production cycles.

**FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio):** Feed fed ÷ Weight gain; measure of feed efficiency.

**FIFO (First In, First Out):** Inventory rotation using oldest stock first.

**Fingerling:** Young fish, typically 2-10 cm length.

**Flashing:** Rapid turning or rubbing against surfaces; indicates irritation.

**Formalin:** 37% formaldehyde solution; used to treat external parasites and fungi.

**Fry:** Newly hatched or very young fish.

---

## G

**Gasping:** Rapid breathing at water surface; indicates low oxygen or gill damage.

**Gill Necrosis:** Death of gill tissue; causes respiratory failure.

**GnRH (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone):** Hormone used to induce spawning.

**Gram Stain:** Staining method to classify bacteria (Gram-positive or Gram-negative).

**Granuloma:** Nodular inflammation; characteristic of mycobacteriosis.

**Grow-out:** Final production phase to market size.

---

## H

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points):** Food safety management system.

**Heritability (h²):** Proportion of trait variation due to genetics.

**Hemorrhage:** Bleeding; blood outside vessels.

**Heterosis (Hybrid Vigor):** Superior performance of F1 hybrid offspring.

**Hyperplasia:** Increased cell number causing tissue thickening.

---

## I

**Ich (Ichthyophthirius multifiliis):** Common ciliated protozoan parasite causing white spot disease.

**Immunocompetence:** Ability of fish immune system to function properly.

**Incubation Period:** Time from pathogen exposure to first clinical signs.

**Intensive Aquaculture:** High-density, high-input production with complete environmental control.

---

## K

**K Factor (Condition Factor):** (Weight × 100) ÷ Length³; measure of fish body condition.

**KHV (Koi Herpesvirus):** Highly contagious viral disease of carp and koi.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator):** Metric used to evaluate production performance.

---

## L

**Larviculture:** Rearing of larval fish.

**Lesion:** Abnormal tissue damage (ulcer, wound, growth).

**Lethargy:** Reduced activity, sluggish movement.

**Live Haul:** Transport of live fish.

---

## M

**MAS (Motile Aeromonad Septicemia):** Disease caused by Aeromonas bacteria.

**Morbidity:** Disease incidence in a population.

**Moribund:** Near death; severely diseased.

**Mortality:** Death rate; number or percentage of fish dying.

**Mucus:** Protective slime coating on fish skin and gills.

**Mycobacteriosis:** Chronic bacterial disease caused by Mycobacterium (fish tuberculosis).

---

## N

**Necropsy:** Post-mortem examination (autopsy for animals).

**Necrosis:** Tissue death.

**Nematode:** Roundworm parasite.

**NFE (Nitrogen-Free Extract):** Carbohydrate content of feed (calculated by difference).

**Nitrification:** Bacterial conversion of ammonia to nitrite to nitrate.

---

## O

**OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health):** International organization setting animal disease standards.

**Operculum:** Gill cover.

**Opportunistic Pathogen:** Organism causing disease only when fish are stressed or immunocompromised.

**Osmoregulation:** Regulation of water and salt balance in fish.

**OTC (Oxytetracycline):** Antibiotic commonly used in aquaculture.

**Oxygenation:** Addition of oxygen to water.

---

## P

**Pathogen:** Disease-causing organism.

**PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction):** Molecular test to detect specific DNA/RNA (viruses, bacteria).

**P/E Ratio:** Protein-to-energy ratio in feed.

**Petechiae:** Small red spots from minor hemorrhaging.

**Photoperiod:** Light-dark cycle (hours of light per day).

**Piping:** Fish gasping at surface due to low oxygen.

**Prevalence:** Percentage of population affected by disease at one time.

**Probiotic:** Beneficial bacteria added to water or feed.

**Proximate Analysis:** Standard feed analysis (protein, fat, fiber, moisture, ash).

---

## Q

**Quarantine:** Isolation of new fish to prevent disease introduction.

**Quaternary Ammonium Compound:** Type of disinfectant (quats).

---

## R

**RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System):** Closed system reusing water with filtration.

**Reportable Disease:** Disease that must be reported to authorities (OIE-notifiable).

**Rigor Mortis:** Stiffening of fish after death.

---

## S

**Saprolegnia:** Fungal infection (cottony growth on skin).

**Saturation:** Maximum amount of gas (usually oxygen) water can hold at given temperature.

**Septicemia:** Bloodstream infection; bacteria throughout body.

**SGR (Specific Growth Rate):** Daily growth as percentage; ((ln Wf - ln Wi) ÷ days) × 100.

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure):** Written instructions for routine operations.

**Spawning:** Reproduction; release of eggs and sperm.

**SPF (Specific Pathogen Free):** Stock certified free of specific diseases.

**Stocking Density:** Biomass per unit volume; kg/m³.

**Streptococcosis:** Bacterial disease caused by Streptococcus bacteria.

**Stress:** Physiological response to adverse conditions; weakens immunity.

**Strip Spawning:** Manual extraction of eggs and sperm for artificial fertilization.

**Subclinical:** Disease present without obvious signs.

**Supersaturation:** Water holding more gas than saturation level; can cause gas bubble disease.

---

## T

**TAN (Total Ammonia Nitrogen):** Sum of NH₃ + NH₄⁺.

**Tagging:** Marking fish for individual identification.

**TGC (Thermal Growth Coefficient):** Temperature-independent growth measure.

**TGP (Total Gas Pressure):** Sum of all dissolved gases; >105% can cause gas bubble disease.

**Theront:** Free-swimming infectious stage of Ich parasite.

**TiLV (Tilapia Lake Virus):** Emerging viral disease of tilapia.

**Tolerance:** Maximum exposure level an organism can withstand.

**Toxicity:** Harmful effect of a substance.

**Trichodina:** Disc-shaped ciliated protozoan parasite.

**Trophont:** Feeding stage of Ich parasite embedded in fish skin.

---

## U

**Ulcer:** Open sore or lesion on skin.

**Unionized Ammonia (NH₃):** Toxic form of ammonia; increases with pH and temperature.

---

## V

**Vaccination:** Administration of vaccine to stimulate immunity.

**Vector:** Organism transmitting disease (e.g., birds, equipment).

**VHS (Viral Hemorrhagic Septicemia):** OIE-notifiable viral disease of fish.

**Virulence:** Degree of pathogenicity; disease-causing ability.

---

## W

**Water Exchange:** Replacement of system water with fresh water.

**Weaning:** Transition from live food to prepared feed.

**Wet Mount:** Microscope preparation of fresh tissue in liquid.

**Whirling Disease:** Myxozoan parasite disease causing neurological damage in salmonids.

**Withdrawal Period:** Time between last treatment and safe harvest for human consumption.

---

## Z

**Zoonotic:** Disease transmissible from animals to humans (e.g., Streptococcus iniae, Mycobacterium).

---

## Units of Measurement

**Temperature:** °C (Celsius) or °F (Fahrenheit)
**Volume:** L (liters), m³ (cubic meters), gallons
**Weight:** g (grams), kg (kilograms), lbs (pounds)
**Length:** cm (centimeters), m (meters), inches
**Concentration:** mg/L (milligrams per liter) = ppm
                   g/L (grams per liter) = ppt
**Density:** kg/m³ (kilograms per cubic meter)
**Flow:** L/min (liters per minute), GPM (gallons per minute)
**Energy:** kJ/g (kilojoules per gram), kcal/g
