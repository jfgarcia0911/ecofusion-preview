## 1. Broodstock Selection

### Selection Criteria

```
BROODSTOCK SELECTION MATRIX

GENETIC TRAITS:
  ✓ Fast growth rate
  ✓ Good body conformation
  ✓ Disease resistance
  ✓ Stress tolerance
  ✓ Feed conversion efficiency
  ✓ Fillet yield
  ✓ Spawning performance

PHENOTYPIC TRAITS:
  ✓ Normal morphology
  ✓ Good body condition (K factor >1.2)
  ✓ Appropriate size/age
  ✓ Bright coloration (species-specific)
  ✓ Fin integrity
  ✓ Active behavior

HEALTH STATUS:
  ✓ Disease-free certified
  ✓ Parasite-free
  ✓ No deformities
  ✓ Good reproductive history
  ✓ Negative for specific pathogens


SELECTION RATIO:
  Typically 5-10% of population selected as broodstock
  Maintain 1:1 to 1:2 male:female ratio
  Avoid inbreeding (track lineages)
```

### Broodstock Conditioning

```
CONDITIONING PROGRAM (6-8 weeks pre-spawning)

NUTRITION:
  Feed: Enhanced broodstock diet
  Protein: 40-45%
  Lipids: 12-18% (high EFA)
  Vitamins: 2-3× normal levels (especially E, C)
  Carotenoids: For egg pigmentation
  Feed Rate: 2-3% BW/day
  Frequency: 2-3 times daily

PHOTOPERIOD:
  Natural: Follow seasonal cues
  Controlled: Manipulate for year-round production
  Example (Tilapia): 14h light: 10h dark promotes spawning

TEMPERATURE:
  Species-specific optimal range
  Tilapia: 26-30°C
  Trout: 8-12°C
  Gradual changes only

ENVIRONMENT:
  Low stress
  Low density (< 20 kg/m³)
  Excellent water quality
  Minimal handling

SIGNS OF READINESS:
  Females: Enlarged abdomen, swollen vent
  Males: Breeding colors, tubercles (some species)
```

## 2. Breeding Systems

### Natural Spawning

```
NATURAL SPAWNING SYSTEMS

POND SPAWNING:
  Setup:
    - Earthen or lined ponds
    - Substrate (if needed)
    - Vegetation/structure
    - Water depth 0.5-1.5m

  Stocking:
    - Sex ratio (species-specific)
    - Density: Low (< 1 fish/m²)
    - Condition checked

  Management:
    - Monitor spawning activity
    - Collect eggs/fry regularly
    - Separate spawners after breeding

TANK SPAWNING:
  Better control than ponds
  Easier egg/fry collection
  Visual monitoring
  Climate control possible

TILAPIA EXAMPLE:
  - Ratio 1 male: 3-5 females
  - Mouth brooders (collect fry from mouth)
  - Continuous spawning (every 4-6 weeks)
  - Separate fry regularly
```

### Induced Spawning

```
HORMONE-INDUCED SPAWNING

REASONS:
  • Out-of-season production
  • Synchronize spawning
  • Species that won't spawn naturally
  • Controlled genetics (strip spawning)

COMMON HORMONES:

HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin):
  Dose: 500-2,000 IU/kg (female)
        250-500 IU/kg (male)
  Timing: Single injection, 24-48h before stripping

GnRH (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone):
  Dose: 50-100 μg/kg
  Often combined with dopamine antagonist

LHRH-a (Synthetic analogs):
  Dose: 10-50 μg/kg
  More potent than natural GnRH

OVAPRIM (Commercial product):
  Dose: 0.5 mL/kg (female)
  Ready-to-use formulation


INJECTION PROCEDURE:
  1. Anesthetize fish (clove oil, MS-222)
  2. Intramuscular injection (dorsal muscle)
  3. Return to spawning tank
  4. Monitor for spawning response
  5. Strip after latency period (species-specific)
```

### Strip Spawning (Artificial Fertilization)

```
STRIP SPAWNING PROTOCOL

TIMING:
  After hormone injection (species-specific latency):
  Tilapia: 24-36 hours
  Trout: 48-72 hours
  Catfish: 12-24 hours

PROCEDURE:

  1. PREPARATION:
     - Clean, dry bowls
     - Towels
     - Egg collection containers
     - Milt collection supplies

  2. EGG COLLECTION (Female):
     - Anesthetize fish
     - Gently wipe dry
     - Massage abdomen anteriorly toward vent
     - Collect eggs in dry bowl (DRY METHOD critical)
     - Avoid water, blood, feces, urine

  3. MILT COLLECTION (Male):
     - Anesthetize
     - Wipe dry and clean vent area
     - Gentle pressure on abdomen
     - Collect milt in small container
     - Keep cool, use within 30 minutes

  4. FERTILIZATION:
     - Add milt to eggs (small amount sufficient)
     - Gently mix with feather
     - Add activating solution:
       * Freshwater: Clean water
       * Marine: Seawater
     - Mix gently for 30-60 seconds
     - Allow to sit 5-10 minutes

  5. WATER-HARDENING:
     - Rinse eggs
     - Transfer to incubation system
     - Begin water flow

  6. RECOVERY:
     - Return broodstock to tank
     - Monitor for 24-48 hours
     - Provide supportive care
```

## 3. Egg Incubation

### Incubation Systems

```
INCUBATOR TYPES:

JAR INCUBATORS (McDonald/Zoug jars):
  Use: Salmonid eggs
  Capacity: 10,000-50,000 eggs
  Flow: Upwelling water
  Advantages: Good circulation, easy monitoring

TRAY INCUBATORS:
  Use: Adhesive or non-adhesive eggs
  Capacity: Varies
  Flow: Through or over trays
  Advantages: High capacity, stacking

AQUARIUM/TANK INCUBATORS:
  Use: Various species
  Setup: Mesh bottoms, gentle flow
  Advantages: Simple, flexible

SUBSTRATE INCUBATORS:
  Use: Species requiring substrate
  Setup: Gravel, artificial substrate
  Advantages: Natural


INCUBATION CONDITIONS:

  Temperature: Species optimal
    Trout: 8-12°C
    Tilapia: 27-30°C
    Catfish: 26-28°C

  DO: >7 mg/L (critical)

  Flow rate: Gentle but thorough

  Water quality:
    - Low ammonia (<0.01 mg/L)
    - Low CO₂
    - No chlorine
    - Filtered (remove debris)

  Light: Minimal to dark (most species)

  Treatment: Formalin (anti-fungal)
    - 100-200 ppm × 15 min daily
    - Or continuous low dose (1-2 ppm)
```

### Egg Management

```
EGG CARE PROTOCOL:

DAILY TASKS:
  □ Monitor temperature
  □ Check DO
  □ Observe development
  □ Remove dead eggs (white/opaque)
  □ Treat with formalin (if used)
  □ Record observations

DEAD EGG REMOVAL:
  - Critical to prevent fungus spread
  - Use forceps or pipette
  - Remove immediately when detected
  - Increase if high mortality

MONITORING DEVELOPMENT:

  Eyed stage:
    - Eyes visible through chorion
    - Embryo movement
    - Can tolerate handling

  Hatching:
    - Chorion breaks down
    - Larvae emerge
    - Weak larvae removed

HATCHING RATES:
  Target: >70% fertilization
  Good: >80% hatch from fertilized eggs
  Factors affecting:
    - Egg quality
    - Milt quality
    - Fertilization technique
    - Incubation conditions
```

## 4. Genetic Principles

### Basic Genetics

```
SELECTIVE BREEDING BASICS

Heritability (h²):
  Proportion of trait variation due to genetics

  High heritability (h² > 0.3):
    - Growth rate
    - Body conformation
    - Fillet yield
    → Rapid improvement possible

  Medium heritability (h² 0.1-0.3):
    - Disease resistance
    - Stress tolerance
    → Moderate improvement

  Low heritability (h² < 0.1):
    - Reproductive traits
    - Survival
    → Slow improvement


SELECTION INTENSITY:
  Percentage of population selected as parents
  Lower % = Faster genetic gain
  But: Risk of inbreeding


GENETIC GAIN PER GENERATION:
  ΔG = i × h² × σ

  Where:
    ΔG = Genetic gain
    i = Selection intensity
    h² = Heritability
    σ = Phenotypic standard deviation

  Example:
    Select top 10% for growth (i = 1.76)
    h² = 0.3
    σ = 50g

    ΔG = 1.76 × 0.3 × 50 = 26.4g per generation
```

### Breeding Program Design

```
FAMILY-BASED SELECTION

FULL-SIB FAMILIES:
  - Same mother and father
  - Best for tracking genetics
  - Requires individual spawning

HALF-SIB FAMILIES:
  - Same father, different mothers
  - Or same mother, different fathers
  - Less control but easier

TAGGING/TRACKING:
  - PIT tags (expensive but permanent)
  - VIE tags (visible implant elastomer)
  - Family tanks (group identification)

BREEDING SCHEME:
  Generation 0: Select from wild/base
  Generation 1: Breed selected, grow out
  Generation 2: Measure trait, select top 10%
  Generation 3: Breed, continue
  Repeat: 10-20 generations

INBREEDING AVOIDANCE:
  - Maintain effective population size (Ne) >50
  - Rotate males between female groups
  - Track pedigrees
  - Limit contribution of top performers
```

## 5. Monosex and Hybrid Production

### Sex Reversal (Tilapia)

```
HORMONAL SEX REVERSAL

Objective: All-male tilapia (faster growth, prevent reproduction)

Protocol:
  1. Collect fry immediately post-swim-up (9-11 days)
  2. Feed hormone-treated diet:
     - 17α-methyltestosterone: 60 mg/kg feed
  3. Duration: 21-28 days
  4. Feed rate: To satiation, 6-8× daily
  5. After treatment: Regular feed
  6. Result: >95% males

Requirements:
  - Precise timing
  - Fresh hormone feed (<1 month old)
  - Complete consumption
  - Proper water quality

Verification:
  - Gonad examination at 3-4 months
  - Target: >95% phenotypic males
```

### Hybridization

```
HYBRID VIGOR (Heterosis)

Benefits:
  ✓ Faster growth
  ✓ Better survival
  ✓ Stress tolerance
  ✓ Uniformity

Examples:
  • Tilapia: Nile × Blue
  • Catfish: Channel × Blue
  • Striped Bass: Striped × White (stripers)

Drawbacks:
  ✗ Must maintain pure lines
  ✗ Hybrid vigor only in F1
  ✗ Cannot use hybrids as broodstock


F1 HYBRID PRODUCTION:

  Pure Line A × Pure Line B → F1 Hybrid

  F1 shows hybrid vigor
  F2 (F1 × F1) loses vigor
  Must cross pure lines each generation
```

## Summary

Breeding program success requires:
- Quality broodstock (genetic and phenotypic selection)
- Proper conditioning (nutrition, environment)
- Controlled spawning (natural or induced)
- Optimal egg incubation (water quality, temperature)
- Genetic management (avoid inbreeding, track lineages)

Long-term: Selective breeding improves production traits over generations
