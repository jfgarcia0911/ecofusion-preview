## Course Description

Achieve expert-level competency in fish production and health management. This advanced course covers intensive aquaculture techniques, disease diagnostics, treatment protocols, biosecurity, and production optimization for commercial aquaponics operations.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Optimize fish production for commercial operations
2. Diagnose fish diseases using multiple diagnostic methods
3. Develop treatment and biosecurity protocols
4. Implement advanced feeding strategies
5. Manage breeding and reproduction programs
6. Analyze and improve production metrics

## Disease Reference Guide

### Common Bacterial Diseases

| Disease | Pathogen | Symptoms | Treatment |
|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Columnaris | F. columnare | White/gray patches, fin rot | Salt, antibiotics |
| Aeromonas | A. hydrophila | Hemorrhaging, ulcers | Salt, temperature |
| Streptococcus | S. iniae | Erratic swimming, exophthalmia | Antibiotics |
| Edwardsiella | E. tarda/ictaluri | Hole-in-head, septicemia | Antibiotics |

### Common Parasitic Diseases

| Disease | Parasite | Symptoms | Treatment |
|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Ich | Ichthyophthirius | White spots, flashing | Salt, heat |
| Costia | Ichthyobodo | Cloudy skin, excess mucus | Salt, formalin |
| Trichodina | Trichodina spp. | Skin irritation, flashing | Salt, formalin |
| Anchor worm | Lernaea | Visible parasites | Manual removal, treatments |

### Biosecurity Levels

| Level | Components |
|-------|------------|
| Basic | Footbaths, handwashing, quarantine |
| Moderate | + Net dips, dedicated equipment, visitor logs |
| High | + Shower in/out, coveralls, limited access |
| Maximum | + Air filtration, complete isolation, testing |

## Production Optimization

### Feed Conversion Targets

| Species | Target FCR | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Tilapia | 1.4-1.6 | Varies with temperature |
| Catfish | 1.5-1.8 | Omnivorous |
| Trout | 1.1-1.3 | Cold water, high protein |
| Barramundi | 1.2-1.5 | Warm water |

### Growth Modeling

```
Daily Growth Rate (%) = ((Final Weight - Initial Weight) / Days) / Avg Weight × 100

Feed Required = Target Weight Gain × FCR

Stocking Density Calculation:
Final Biomass = Fish Count × Final Weight × Survival Rate
Tank Volume Required = Final Biomass ÷ Max Density
```
