# Course 303: Advanced Fish Production & Health
## Assignment 01: Disease Diagnosis Exercise

**Type:** In-Class | **Duration:** 15-20 min | **Points:** 15

## Objective
Diagnose fish diseases from symptoms and recommend appropriate treatments.

## Case Studies
You will receive 3 case studies with symptoms, water quality data, and visual descriptions.

### Tasks per Case
1. Identify probable pathogen
2. Classify disease type (bacterial/parasitic/viral)
3. Recommend diagnostic tests
4. Prescribe treatment protocol
5. Suggest preventive measures

## Grading
- Accurate diagnosis: 5 pts/case
- Treatment appropriateness: 3 pts/case
- Prevention strategies: 2 pts/case
