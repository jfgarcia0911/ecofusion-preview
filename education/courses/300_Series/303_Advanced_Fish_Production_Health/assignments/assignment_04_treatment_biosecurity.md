# Course 303: Advanced Fish Production & Health
## Assignment 04: Treatment Protocol & Biosecurity Plan

**Type:** Take-Home | **Duration:** 1-2 hours | **Points:** 25

## Objectives
1. Develop comprehensive treatment protocols for common fish diseases
2. Create a facility-wide biosecurity plan

## Part 1: Treatment Protocols (15 points)

### Required Protocols
Develop detailed treatment protocols for:
1. Columnaris (bacterial)
2. Ich (parasitic)
3. Viral disease outbreak response

### Each Protocol Must Include
- Diagnosis confirmation steps
- Treatment options (with dosages)
- Water quality adjustments
- Feeding modifications
- Monitoring schedule
- Success criteria
- Alternative treatments if first fails

## Part 2: Biosecurity Plan (10 points)

### Required Components
1. **Entry/Exit Procedures**
   - Personnel protocols
   - Equipment sanitization
   - Visitor management

2. **Operational Biosecurity**
   - Quarantine protocols
   - Equipment dedication
   - Water source protection
   - Feed storage and handling

3. **Disease Response**
   - Detection and reporting
   - Isolation procedures
   - Treatment decisions
   - Record keeping

4. **Monitoring Program**
   - Regular health checks
   - Water quality testing
   - Disease surveillance
   - Documentation requirements

## Deliverables
1. Treatment protocol document (2-3 pages)
2. Biosecurity plan (2-3 pages)
3. Checklists and forms (appendices)

## Grading
- Protocol comprehensiveness: 8 pts
- Protocol accuracy: 7 pts
- Biosecurity plan thoroughness: 7 pts
- Professional presentation: 3 pts
