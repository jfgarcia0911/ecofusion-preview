# Course 303: Advanced Fish Production & Health
## Assignment 03: Production Optimization Analysis

**Type:** In-Class | **Duration:** 30-45 min | **Points:** 30

## Objective
Analyze production data and develop optimization recommendations.

### Production Data (6-month period)
- Initial stocking: 500 fish @ 50g each
- Final harvest: 425 fish @ 650g each
- Total feed used: 210 kg
- Time period: 180 days
- Water temperature: 26-28°C

## Tasks
1. **Calculate Performance Metrics** (12 pts)
   - Final biomass
   - Survival rate
   - Average daily gain (ADG)
   - Feed conversion ratio (FCR)
   - Specific growth rate (SGR)

2. **Comparative Analysis** (10 pts)
   - Compare to industry benchmarks
   - Identify areas above/below standards
   - Calculate potential improvements

3. **Optimization Recommendations** (8 pts)
   - Feeding strategy adjustments
   - Stocking density optimization
   - Water quality targets
   - Expected performance improvements
