# Course 303: Advanced Fish Production & Health
## Assignment 02: Water Quality Management

**Type:** In-Class | **Duration:** 20-30 min | **Points:** 20

## Scenario
Analyze water quality data from a 2,000-gallon tilapia system experiencing fish stress.

### Given Data
- Ammonia: 2.5 mg/L
- Nitrite: 1.8 mg/L
- Nitrate: 180 mg/L
- pH: 6.2
- DO: 3.5 mg/L
- Temperature: 82°F

## Tasks
1. Identify all parameter issues (5 pts)
2. Rank by severity (3 pts)
3. Explain physiological impacts on fish (6 pts)
4. Develop correction plan (6 pts)
