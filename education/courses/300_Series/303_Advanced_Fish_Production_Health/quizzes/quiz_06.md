## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. (1) Stop feeding
   (2) Test water quality (DO, NH₃, NO₂, pH, temp)
   (3) Examine fish externally
   (4) Isolate affected tanks
   (5) Sample moribund fish for necropsy/wet mount
   (Can vary slightly but should include water quality first)

2. Answer: C - Moribund fish
   Why: Active infection present, pathogens detectable, fish still fresh enough for examination, shows disease progression

3. Organism: Trichodina
   Treatment: Salt bath (1-3 ppt), formalin, or potassium permanganate

4. Answer: B - Retain safranin counterstain (Gram-)

5. Condition: Bacterial septicemia (likely Aeromonas, Streptococcus, or similar)
   Next step: Aseptic bacterial culture from kidney/brain, antibiotic sensitivity testing

6. Answer: B - Viruses
   Advantage: Faster results, detects DNA/RNA directly, more sensitive, can detect non-culturable organisms (any 1)

7. Answer: B - 10% formalin solution
   Thickness: 3-5 mm (thin enough for fixative penetration)
