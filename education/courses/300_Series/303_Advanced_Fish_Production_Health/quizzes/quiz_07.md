## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Causative agent: Flavobacterium columnare
   Signs (any 3): White-gray patches, gill necrosis, fin rot, "saddleback" lesions, mouth fungus appearance
   Gram stain: Gram-negative (long rods)

2. Diagnosis: Motile Aeromonad Septicemia (MAS) / Aeromonas infection
   Treatments (any 2): Oxytetracycline (OTC), florfenicol, salt bath (1-3 ppt), enrofloxacin, improve water quality

3. Safety: Zoonotic disease - can infect humans causing cellulitis, septicemia (especially immunocompromised)
   Prevention: VACCINATION (autogenous or commercial vaccine)
   Gram stain: Gram-positive cocci in chains

4. a) 800 × 50 = 40,000 mg = 40 g
   b) 16 kg feed
   c) 40 g ÷ 16 kg = 2.5 g/kg feed

5. Answer: C
   Action: Depopulate system, disinfect thoroughly, do not restock from infected source

6. Indole: Positive (+)
   H₂S: Positive (+)
