## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Three principles:
   (1) Isolation/Segregation - Keep pathogens out
   (2) Traffic Control - Limit movement of people, equipment, animals
   (3) Sanitation - Clean and disinfect everything

2. Minimum: 30-60 days (30 acceptable, 60 preferred)
   Monitor: (1) Behavior/feeding, (2) Clinical signs/mortality, (3) Water quality, (4) Parasites/disease screening, (5) Periodic sampling (any 3)
   Release: After observation period with no disease signs, negative disease screening, normal behavior/health

3. Chlorine: B (200 ppm for footbaths)
   Virkon S: A (1-2% solution)
   Quat: C (varies by product, follow label, needs contact time)
   Most effective against viruses: Virkon S (broad-spectrum, virucidal)

4. Vectors (any 4):
   - Live fish transfers
   - Contaminated water
   - Equipment sharing
   - Personnel/clothing
   - Feed ingredients
   - Wild birds
   - Effluent discharge
   - Shared vehicles
   - Aquatic vegetation

5. Answer: C - Daily
   Why: Organic matter reduces effectiveness, becomes contaminated with pathogens, diluted by water/mud, loses potency

6. SPF: Specific Pathogen Free
   Advantage: Reduced disease risk, better growth/performance, known health status, safer transfers, reduced treatments
   Challenge: Expensive to maintain, requires strict biosecurity, limited availability, may lack genetic diversity, loss of immunity to field pathogens (any 1)
