## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. First feed: Live food (Artemia nauplii, rotifers, copepods, infusoria)
   Transition: When fry reach 7-15 mm, approximately 10-21 days (species dependent)
   Why not formulated: Mouth size too small, digestive system immature, need live movement for feeding stimulus, require highly digestible nutrition

2. Salinity: 25-35 ppt (seawater)
   Temperature: 25-28°C
   Aeration: Strong (vigorous)
   Hatching time: 18-24 hours

3. Fry = 1,000 × 5 = 5,000 fry
   Reduced density: 1-2 fry/L (or 1,000-2,000 fry)
   Why: Increased oxygen demand, waste production, size variation/competition, space requirements, stress reduction

4. Weaning: Gradual transition from live food to artificial/formulated feed
   Schedule (approximate):
   Days 1-5: 75-100% live, 0-25% formulated
   Days 6-10: 25-50% live, 50-75% formulated
   Days 11+: 0% live, 100% formulated
   (Exact schedule varies by species)

5. Answer: B - Swim bladder inflation
   What happens: "Belly slider" or "pinhead" syndrome, unable to maintain buoyancy, poor survival, cannot feed effectively

6. Why grade (any 2):
   - Reduce cannibalism
   - Eliminate size variation
   - More uniform growth
   - Easier feeding management
   - Better final product
   - Identify poor performers
   Frequency: Every 2-4 weeks, or when CV >25%
