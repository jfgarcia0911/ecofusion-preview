## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Factors (any 3):
   - Market demand/price
   - Target weight specification
   - Feed conversion efficiency
   - Growth rate trajectory
   - Seasonal pricing
   - Tank space/new cohort needs
   - Operating costs

   Too early: Lower total weight, poor FCR at small sizes, less income per fish, underutilized tank space/time, market price penalties for small fish

2. Fasting: 24-48 hours (commonly 24 hours)
   Higher temp: Shorter (12-18 hours, faster gut clearance)
   Why: (1) Reduce fecal contamination, (2) Reduce stress/mortality, (3) Improve product quality, (4) Lower oxygen demand (any 2)

3. Maximum density: 0.3-0.5 kg/L (use 0.4 kg/L for calculation)
   Volume = 500 ÷ 0.4 = 1,250 L (or range 1,000-1,667 L depending on density used)
   Must provide: Oxygen/aeration, temperature control (ice or cooling), avoid overcrowding

4. Stress reduction (any 4):
   - Gradual crowding (not sudden)
   - Anesthetic use (if approved)
   - Minimize handling time
   - Avoid abrasion/scale loss
   - Keep fish wet/moist
   - Cool water temperature
   - Gentle netting techniques
   - Reduce noise/disturbance
   - Dawn/dusk harvest (cooler)
   - Adequate oxygen during crowding

5. a) Fillet = 2,100 kg
   b) Yield = (2,100 ÷ 3,000) × 100 = 70%
   Is this good: NO - this is WHOLE BODY minus some parts. Actual fillet should be 30-35% of whole weight. The calculation shows an error or includes more than just fillet. (Note: 33% would be 990 kg fillet)
