## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. a) 1.5 × 0.054 = 0.081 mg/L
   b) NO - exceeds both chronic and acute safe limits
   c) Immediate 50-100% water exchange, stop feeding, increase aeration

2. a) 150 × 0.04 = 6 kg/day
   b) 6 × 7.14 = 42.84 kg/day
   c) 42.84 kg ÷ 200 m³ = 214 mg/L/day

3. DO = 6.46 mg/L
   Marginal - should maintain >7 mg/L for intensive production

4. Cause: CO₂ accumulation
   Solution: Increase degassing/aeration, reduce feeding temporarily

5. a) 80 mg/L
   b) 75 × 80 ÷ 1,000 = 6 kg sodium bicarbonate

6. Answer: C
   Symptoms: Exophthalmia (pop-eye), bubbles in fins/eyes, disorientation, erratic swimming (any 2)

7. DO: >4 mg/L (ideally >6 mg/L)
   pH: 7.5-8.0
   Temperature: Stable is better
