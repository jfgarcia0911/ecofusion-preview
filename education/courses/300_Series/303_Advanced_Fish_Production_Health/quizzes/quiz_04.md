## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Feed = 5,000 × 0.025 = 125 kg/day
   Per feeding = 125 ÷ 4 = 31.25 kg

2. a) 2,500 kg
   b) FCR = 3,750 ÷ 2,500 = 1.5
   c) Yes, falls within optimal range

3. Answer: A - Fry
   Reason: Small stomach size, high metabolic rate, need frequent small meals

4. Answer: B - Decrease feeding rate
   Explanation: Fish metabolism slows with temperature, reduced appetite and digestive capacity

5. Answer: D - All of the above
   Disadvantage: Cannot control total intake, may lead to overfeeding, difficult to monitor health through feeding behavior (any 1)

6. 2-5 cm: B, 5-10 cm: C, 10-20 cm: A

7. Answer: B - Fat oxidation
   Should use: NO - discard, unsafe and poor nutrition

8. Feed until fish show reduced feeding activity, then stop just before complete satiation
   Better because: Prevents overfeeding, reduces waste, maintains water quality, improves FCR, keeps fish eager for next feeding (any 1-2 points)
