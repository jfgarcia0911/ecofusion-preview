### 1. Stocking Density Calculation (1 point)

You have a circular tank with a radius of 2.5 meters and water depth of 1.5 meters. You want to stock tilapia at 50 kg/m³. If you stock 200g fingerlings, how many fish should you stock?

**Show your work:**

Volume = π × r² × depth = π × (2.5)² × 1.5 = _______ m³

Target biomass = Volume × Density = _______ m³ × 50 kg/m³ = _______ kg

Number of fish = Biomass ÷ Individual weight = _______ kg ÷ 0.2 kg = _______ fish

**Answer:** _______ fish
