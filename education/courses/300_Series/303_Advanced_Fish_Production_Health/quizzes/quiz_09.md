## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Species: Carp and koi (Cyprinus carpio)
   Temperature: 18-27°C (optimal 22-25°C)
   Mortality: 70-100%
   Treatment: NO
   Action: Depopulate, disinfect, PCR confirmation, report to authorities

2. Why "Spring": Optimal outbreak temperature is 10-17°C which occurs in spring
   Reportable: YES
   Reportable means: Must notify state/federal animal health authorities, OIE disease, movement restrictions may apply, potential quarantine

3. Signs (any 3): Exophthalmia, neurological signs (erratic swimming), anorexia, lethargy, high mortality (10-90%), syncytial hepatitis
   Management: Strict biosecurity, quarantine, selective breeding for resistance, proper diagnostics, avoid mixing stocks

4. Differences (any 2):
   - Viral: No bacterial growth on culture; Bacterial: Growth on culture media
   - Viral: No response to antibiotics; Bacterial: May respond to antibiotics
   - Viral: Requires PCR/electron microscopy; Bacterial: Visible under light microscope
   - Viral: Often temperature-dependent; Bacterial: Less temperature specific
   Diagnostic test: PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)

5. Answer: D - All of the above

6. Answer: C - Report immediately (even on suspicion, don't wait for confirmation)
   Why important: Prevents spread to other farms, allows rapid response, legal requirement, protects industry, enables movement restrictions, international trade implications
