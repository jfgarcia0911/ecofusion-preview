## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Causative organism: Ichthyophthirius multifiliis
   Life cycle: (1) Trophont (feeding on fish), (2) Tomont (encysted on bottom, dividing), (3) Theront (free-swimming, infectious)
   Why repeat: Treatment only kills free-swimming theronts, not trophonts on fish or tomonts on bottom. Must treat when new theronts emerge.

2. Trichodina: C
   Ichthyobodo: A
   Dactylogyrus: B
   Gill parasite: Dactylogyrus (gill fluke)

3. Salt = 50 × 3 = 150 kg
   Why salt helps: Disrupts osmoregulation of parasites, reduces stress on fish, some direct toxicity to parasites, helps heal damaged tissue

4. Gyrodactylus: Live-bearing (viviparous)
   Dactylogyrus location: Gills
   Dactylogyrus eyespots: Four

5. Answer: B - Manual removal + organophosphate treatment
   (Manual removal for visible adults, chemical for larvae/juveniles)

6. Safety concern: Formalin depletes dissolved oxygen rapidly
   Required action: Increase aeration significantly, monitor DO continuously
