## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. a) 12,000 mg = 12 g
   b) 30 kg
   c) 12 g ÷ 30 kg = 0.4 g/kg feed
   d) 12 g × 10 = 120 g total

2. Amount = 150 g = 0.15 kg
   Critical step: Perform demand test (add small amount to water sample to determine actual requirement, prevents overdose)

3. OTC: C (21 days)
   Florfenicol: A (15 days)
   Sulfa/Ormet: B (500 degree-days, ~42 days at 12°C)
   AQUI-S: D (3 days - anesthetic, short withdrawal)
   Why important: Food safety, drug residues, legal compliance, consumer protection, export requirements

4. Bath: 5 mg/L for 12 hours (or 10 mg/L for 3 hours)
   In-feed: 25-50 mg/kg body weight

5. Answer: B - Salt 1-3 ppt for 5-7 days
