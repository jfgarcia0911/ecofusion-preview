## Answer Key (For Instructor)

1. Essential records (any 6):
   - Daily water quality
   - Feeding records
   - Mortality records
   - Growth/sampling data
   - Treatment/medication logs
   - Stocking/harvest records
   - Equipment maintenance
   - Supplier/inventory records
   - Financial records
   - Visitor/biosecurity log

2. a) Survival = 92%
   b) Weight gain = 3,940 kg
   c) FCR = 6,100 ÷ 3,940 = 1.55
   d) ADG = 430 ÷ 120 = 3.58 g/day

3. Possible causes (any 3):
   - Feed quality deterioration
   - Water quality problems
   - Subclinical disease
   - Incorrect feeding rate
   - Temperature changes
   - Poor feed storage
   - Overfeeding/waste
   - Stress factors

   Examine: Water quality trends, fish health observations, feed storage conditions, temperature logs, feeding rate adjustments, mortality patterns

4. Normal: <0.1% daily (<0.05% ideal)
   Day 3 rate = 0.19%
   Concerning: YES - nearly 2× normal, increasing trend
   Action: Stop feeding, test water quality immediately, examine moribund fish, increase observation, prepare for outbreak response

5. Days remaining = 16.7 days
   Order now: NO (or "not yet") - 5-day lead time means supply through day 11-12, should order around day 8-10 to maintain safety stock
   (Or YES if safety stock policy requires 10+ days on hand)

6. Answer: C - 3 years (FDA/VFD requirement, may be longer in some jurisdictions)
   Must include (any 3):
   - Date of treatment
   - Drug name and dosage
   - Duration of treatment
   - Amount used
   - Veterinary prescription/VFD
   - Withdrawal period

   - Applicator name
