### 1. Specific Growth Rate (SGR) Calculation (3 points)

Fish data over 30 days:
- Initial weight (Wi): 50 g
- Final weight (Wf): 150 g
- Duration: 30 days

**Calculate SGR:**

a) ln(Wf) = ln(150) = _______

b) ln(Wi) = ln(50) = _______

c) SGR = ((ln(Wf) - ln(Wi)) ÷ days) × 100 = _______% /day

---

### 2. Condition Factor (K) (2 points)

A fish weighs 250 g and measures 25 cm in length.

**Calculate K factor:**

K = (Weight × 100) ÷ Length³ = (250 × 100) ÷ (25)³ = _______

**Interpretation:** (Circle one)
- Thin/poor condition (K < 1.0)
- Normal condition (K = 1.0-2.0)
- Robust/excellent condition (K > 2.0)

### 3. Growth Projection (2 points)

Wf = Wi × e^(SGR × days/100)
Wf = 10 × e^(3 × 60/100) = 10 × e^1.8 = _______g

**Total biomass:** 10,000 × _______ g = _______ kg
