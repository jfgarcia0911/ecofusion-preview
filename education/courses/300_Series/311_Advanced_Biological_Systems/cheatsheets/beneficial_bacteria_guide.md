## Nitrifying Bacteria

### Ammonia-Oxidizing Bacteria (AOB)

| Genus | Function | Optimal Conditions | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Nitrosomonas** | NH3 → NO2⁻ | pH 7.5-8.0, 77-86°F | Most common AOB |
| **Nitrosospira** | NH3 → NO2⁻ | pH 7.0-8.0, 68-86°F | Cold-tolerant strains |
| **Nitrosococcus** | NH3 → NO2⁻ | Marine/brackish | Salt-tolerant |

### Nitrite-Oxidizing Bacteria (NOB)

| Genus | Function | Optimal Conditions | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Nitrobacter** | NO2⁻ → NO3⁻ | pH 7.0-8.0, 77-82°F | Fast-growing |
| **Nitrospira** | NO2⁻ → NO3⁻ | pH 7.0-7.5, 72-82°F | More efficient at low NO2⁻ |
| **Nitrococcus** | NO2⁻ → NO3⁻ | Marine systems | Halophilic |

## Nitrification Quick Reference

```
NITRIFICATION PATHWAY

NH3 (Ammonia)
    │
    │  Nitrosomonas, Nitrosospira
    │  Requires: O2, alkalinity
    ▼
NO2⁻ (Nitrite) ◄── TOXIC to fish at >0.5 mg/L
    │
    │  Nitrobacter, Nitrospira
    │  Requires: O2
    ▼
NO3⁻ (Nitrate) ◄── Plant nutrient, safe for fish <200 mg/L


KEY FACTORS FOR NITRIFICATION:

Parameter          Optimal         Acceptable
────────────────────────────────────────────
Temperature        77-86°F         60-95°F
pH                 7.0-8.0         6.5-8.5
Dissolved O2       >6 mg/L         >4 mg/L
Alkalinity         100-200 mg/L    >50 mg/L
Ammonia loading    Match capacity  Avoid overload
```

## Biofilter Inoculation Guide

### Commercial Products

| Product Type | Application Rate | Colonization Time |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Liquid bacteria | Per label, often 1 oz/100 gal | 2-4 weeks |
| Dry bacteria | Per label | 3-6 weeks |
| Seeded media | Replace 10-20% of media | 1-2 weeks |
| Fish introduction | Gradual stocking | 4-8 weeks |

### Cycling Methods

| Method | Duration | Ammonia Source | Notes |
|--------|----------|----------------|-------|
| Fish-in cycle | 4-8 weeks | Fish waste | Stressful to fish |
| Fishless cycle | 3-6 weeks | Pure ammonia | Preferred method |

## Environmental Requirements

```
BACTERIA ENVIRONMENTAL REQUIREMENTS

                    AOB              NOB              PGPR
                    ─────────────    ─────────────    ─────────────
Temperature (°F)    77-86            77-82            65-85
pH Range            7.5-8.0          7.0-8.0          5.5-7.0
Dissolved O2        >4 mg/L          >4 mg/L          >2 mg/L
Light               None (inhibited) None             Variable
Moisture            100% (aquatic)   100% (aquatic)   Moist
Generation Time     8-36 hours       12-48 hours      20-60 min

STRESS FACTORS:
• Temperature shock: >5°F change
• pH shock: >0.5 unit change
• Chemical exposure: Chlorine, medications
• UV light exposure
• Drying out
```

## Troubleshooting Biological Issues

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Ammonia spike | Insufficient AOB | Reduce feeding, add bacteria |
| Nitrite spike | Insufficient NOB | Ensure O2, patience |
| Both elevated | Biofilter crash | Emergency water change, restart |
| Slow cycling | Low temperature | Increase temp to 80°F |
| Cycling stalled | pH too low | Add buffer, target pH 7.5 |
| Slimy biofilter | Heterotroph overgrowth | Improve mechanical filtration |
