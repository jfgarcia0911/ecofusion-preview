## Questions

### 1. What is the target C:N ratio in biofloc systems?
A) 5:1
B) 10-15:1
C) 25:1
D) 50:1

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is the typical biofloc volume target in a well-managed system?
A) 1-2 mL/L
B) 5-8 mL/L
C) 10-15 mL/L
D) 25-30 mL/L

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is the primary protein content of biofloc?
A) 5-10%
B) 10-20%
C) 20-40%
D) 60-80%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. Why is continuous mixing essential in biofloc systems?
A) To cool the water
B) To keep flocs suspended and prevent settling
C) To remove oxygen
D) To sterilize water

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is the minimum dissolved oxygen level required in biofloc systems?
A) >1 mg/L
B) >3 mg/L
C) >5 mg/L
D) >10 mg/L

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 6. What is a primary benefit of biofloc technology?
A) Eliminates need for aeration
B) Reduced water exchange requirements
C) No monitoring needed
D) Removes need for fish feed

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. Which carbon source is commonly added to maintain C:N ratio?
A) Sugar or molasses
B) Sand
C) Salt
D) Alcohol

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### 8. What is a major challenge of biofloc systems?
A) Too simple to manage
B) Higher energy use for mixing and aeration
C) Fish won't eat
D) Plants die immediately

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. How does biofloc improve biosecurity?
A) By reducing pathogen entry with minimal water exchange
B) By killing all bacteria
C) By sterilizing fish
D) By eliminating plants

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### 10. What happens if biofloc becomes too dense?
A) Better water quality
B) Risk of oxygen depletion and fish stress
C) Improved fish growth
D) System becomes self-cleaning

**Correct Answer:** B
