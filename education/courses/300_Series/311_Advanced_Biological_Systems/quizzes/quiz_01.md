**Module:** 1

## Questions

### 1. What is the primary role of microorganisms in aquaponic systems?
A) To consume oxygen
B) To convert fish waste into plant-available nutrients
C) To produce carbon dioxide
D) To increase water temperature

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which bacterial group is responsible for converting ammonia to nitrite?
A) Nitrobacter
B) Nitrospira
C) Nitrosomonas
D) Bacillus

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is the typical cell density in biofilter communities?
A) 10^3-10^5 cells/cm²
B) 10^5-10^7 cells/cm²
C) 10^8-10^10 cells/cm²
D) 10^12-10^14 cells/cm²

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. Which of the following is NOT a component of the aquaponic microbial ecosystem?
A) Fish gut microbiome
B) Root zone microbiome
C) Atmospheric microbiome
D) Biofilter community

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 5. What is the primary function of heterotrophic bacteria in biofilters?
A) Converting ammonia to nitrite
B) Breaking down organic matter
C) Producing oxygen
D) Fixing atmospheric nitrogen

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. Which organism type is part of the water column microbiome that preys on bacteria?
A) Algae
B) Fungi
C) Protozoa
D) Viruses

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 7. What is the key concept regarding microbial community health in aquaponics?
A) Single species dominance is ideal
B) Only autotrophic bacteria are needed
C) Diversity equals resilience
D) Sterile conditions are best

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 8. Which bacterial phylum is commonly found in fish gut microbiomes?
A) Cyanobacteria
B) Proteobacteria
C) Actinobacteria
D) Spirochaetes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is the typical density of bacteria in fish gut?
A) 10^3-10^5 cells/g
B) 10^6-10^8 cells/g
C) 10^9-10^11 cells/g
D) 10^12-10^14 cells/g

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 10. Which zone of the aquaponic system is particularly vulnerable to pathogens?
A) Fish gills
B) Plant leaves
C) Growing media
D) Air stones

**Correct Answer:** A
