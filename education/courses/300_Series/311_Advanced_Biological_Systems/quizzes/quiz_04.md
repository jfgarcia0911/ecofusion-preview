## Questions

### 1. What is the primary function of Bacillus subtilis in aquaponic systems?
A) Ammonia oxidation
B) Disease suppression and plant growth promotion
C) Nitrite oxidation
D) Nitrogen gas production

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which beneficial bacteria is most known for nitrogen fixation?
A) Nitrosomonas
B) Bacillus
C) Azospirillum
D) Nitrobacter

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. When is the best time to apply mycorrhizal inoculants?
A) During system cycling
B) At transplant
C) Before harvesting
D) After system shutdown

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. What type of beneficial microbe is Trichoderma?
A) Bacteria
B) Virus
C) Fungus
D) Protozoa

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 5. Which mechanism allows PGPR to enhance plant nutrition?
A) Water production
B) Nutrient solubilization
C) Light absorption
D) Temperature regulation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. What does ISR stand for in plant-microbe interactions?
A) Integrated System Response
B) Induced Systemic Resistance
C) Internal Stress Reaction
D) Isolated Species Resilience

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. Where should Pseudomonas fluorescens be applied?
A) Fish tank only
B) Root zone
C) Air stones
D) UV sterilizer

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. Which plant hormone is commonly produced by PGPR?
A) Insulin
B) Auxins
C) Cortisol
D) Thyroxine

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. How do beneficial bacteria prevent pathogen establishment?
A) By producing antibiotics only
B) By raising water temperature
C) Through competitive exclusion
D) By removing all oxygen

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 10. What is the primary benefit of mycorrhizae in aquaponics?
A) Ammonia removal
B) Enhanced phosphorus uptake
C) Water filtration
D) Oxygen production

**Correct Answer:** B
