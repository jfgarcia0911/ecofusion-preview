## Questions

### 1. What is the complete chemical equation for ammonia oxidation by AOB?
A) NH3 + O2 → NO3⁻ + H2O
B) NH3 + 1.5 O2 → NO2⁻ + H2O + H⁺
C) NH4⁺ + 2 O2 → NO3⁻ + 2H⁺
D) NH3 + 0.5 O2 → NO2⁻

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which bacteria is responsible for nitrite oxidation?
A) Nitrosomonas
B) Nitrosospira
C) Nitrobacter
D) Pseudomonas

**Correct Answer:** C

### 3. What is the optimal temperature range for nitrification in aquaponics?
