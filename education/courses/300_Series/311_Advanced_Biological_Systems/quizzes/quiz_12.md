# Quiz 12: Biological Troubleshooting

**Module:** 12
**Questions:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

### 1. What is the most common cause of a nitrification crash?
A) Too many plants
B) Sudden pH drop or temperature shock
C) Excessive aeration
D) Too much light

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is the first action to take when ammonia spikes suddenly?
A) Add more fish
B) Reduce or stop feeding temporarily
C) Increase water temperature to 90°F
D) Remove all plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. What causes a sulfur smell in aquaponic systems?
A) Healthy aerobic bacteria
B) Plant roots growing
C) Anaerobic conditions producing H2S
D) Clean water

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. How can excessive biofilm buildup be prevented?
A) Stop all water flow
B) Balance feeding rates and maintain flow
C) Add antibiotics
D) Remove all media

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. What indicates root rot in plants?
A) White healthy roots
B) Brown, slimy roots and wilting
C) Rapid growth
D) Increased flowering

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. Which pathogen commonly causes root rot?
A) Bacillus
B) Nitrosomonas
C) Pythium
D) Azospirillum

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 7. What is the best solution for algae bloom problems?
A) Add more nutrients
B) Reduce light exposure and cover tanks
C) Increase light to 24 hours
D) Add herbicides

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. How should you respond to brown biofilm in biofilters?
A) Immediately clean everything
B) Monitor - brown biofilm is normal and healthy
C) Add bleach
D) Replace all media

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. What causes white, cotton-like growth on fish?
A) Healthy slime coat
B) Fungal infection (often Saprolegnia)
C) Normal scales
D) Plant roots

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is the relationship between dissolved oxygen and biological problems?
A) No relationship
B) Low DO leads to multiple biological issues
C) High DO causes all problems
D) DO only affects plants

**Correct Answer:** B
