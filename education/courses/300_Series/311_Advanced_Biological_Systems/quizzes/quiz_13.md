# Quiz 13: Advanced Monitoring

**Module:** 13
**Questions:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

### 1. What is the primary indicator of biofilter health?
A) Water color
B) Consistent conversion of ammonia and nitrite to nitrate
C) Filter size
D) Water temperature only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. How often should ammonia and nitrite be tested in a new system?
A) Once per year
B) Once per month
C) Daily during cycling, then weekly
D) Never needed

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. What does a sudden drop in pH indicate?
A) Everything is perfect
B) Possible loss of alkalinity or nitrification issues
C) Too many plants
D) Excessive aeration

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which parameter indicates the nitrogen cycling capacity of a system?
A) Water temperature
B) Nitrate accumulation rate
C) Light intensity
D) Fish color

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. What tool can measure dissolved oxygen continuously?
A) pH probe only
B) DO meter or probe
C) Thermometer
D) Light meter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. What is the ideal nitrate range for most aquaponic systems?
A) 0-10 mg/L
B) 10-30 mg/L
C) 40-100 mg/L
D) 200-300 mg/L

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 7. How can you monitor biofilm development visually?
A) Only with microscopes
B) Check media for brown/tan coating
C) Monitor fish behavior only
D) Cannot be done visually

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. What indicates excessive organic loading in a system?
A) Crystal clear water
B) Cloudy water, foam, declining DO
C) High pH
D) Cold water

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which test indicates system alkalinity?
A) Ammonia test
B) Temperature measurement
C) KH or alkalinity test
D) DO test

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 10. How can microbial activity be assessed indirectly?
A) Only through DNA sequencing
B) By monitoring nutrient conversion rates
C) By fish weight only
D) Cannot be assessed

**Correct Answer:** B
