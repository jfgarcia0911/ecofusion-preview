## Questions

### 1. What is the first step in designing a biological enhancement strategy?
A) Buy products immediately
B) Assess current system biology and identify gaps
C) Remove all existing bacteria
D) Change all water

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which factor is most critical when selecting beneficial bacterial products?
A) Package color
B) Match product to system needs and conditions
C) Highest price
D) Largest bottle

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. How should new biological products be introduced to established systems?
A) All at once in large doses
B) Gradually to avoid shocking the ecosystem
C) Never introduce new products
D) Only during winter

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is the purpose of a control group in biological trials?
A) To complicate the experiment
B) To compare treated vs untreated conditions
C) To waste time
D) Not necessary

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. How long should a biological product trial typically run?
A) 1 day
B) 1 week
C) 4-8 weeks minimum
D) 1 year minimum

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 6. What data should be collected when testing beneficial bacteria?
A) None needed
B) Water parameters, plant growth, system stability
C) Only water temperature
D) Only fish count

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. What indicates successful integration of beneficial microbes?
A) Immediate visible changes
B) Improved system stability and performance over time
C) Water turns green
D) All fish die

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. How should biological interventions be documented?
A) Don't document
B) Record dates, dosages, observations, results
C) Memory only
D) Photos only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is a key consideration when combining multiple biological products?
A) Always safe to mix everything
B) Check compatibility and avoid overdosing
C) Mix as many as possible
D) Never use more than one product

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is the best approach to biological system optimization?
A) Change everything at once
B) Iterative testing and gradual improvements
C) Ignore all monitoring
D) Use only chemical interventions

**Correct Answer:** B
