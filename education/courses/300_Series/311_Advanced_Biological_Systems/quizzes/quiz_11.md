## Questions

### 1. What is the primary role of denitrifying bacteria in aquaponic systems?
A) Convert NH3 to NO2
B) Convert NO2 to NO3
C) Convert NO3 to N2 gas
D) Produce ammonia

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 2. What conditions are required for denitrification to occur?
A) High oxygen and no carbon
B) Anaerobic conditions and carbon source
C) Aerobic conditions only
D) Acidic pH below 4.0

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. Which process removes phosphorus from aquaponic water?
A) Nitrification
B) Plant uptake and bacterial assimilation
C) Evaporation
D) Sedimentation only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. What is a constructed wetland used for in aquaponics?
A) Fish breeding
B) Polishing water and removing excess nutrients
C) Growing fish feed
D) Storing equipment

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. Which bacteria can oxidize sulfur compounds in aquaponic systems?
A) Nitrosomonas
B) Thiobacillus
C) E. coli
D) Lactobacillus

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. What indicates healthy biological water treatment?
A) Green cloudy water
B) Clear water, stable parameters, low ammonia/nitrite
C) Strong sulfur smell
D) No bacteria present

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. How do biofilters improve water clarity?
A) By adding chemicals
B) Bacteria consume organic particles and nutrients
C) By heating water
D) By adding dyes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. What is the purpose of a settling tank in biological treatment?
A) Fish breeding
B) Remove solid waste before biological filtration
C) Heat water
D) Kill bacteria

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which organisms help control algae in natural ways?
A) Only fish
B) Protozoa and zooplankton
C) Plants only
D) Viruses

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is the advantage of multi-stage biological filtration?
A) Saves space
B) Different zones optimize different processes
C) Eliminates need for monitoring
D) Reduces all costs to zero

**Correct Answer:** B
