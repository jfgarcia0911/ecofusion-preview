**Module:** 5
**Questions:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

### 1. What is the rhizosphere?
A) The fish tank zone
B) The soil/media zone around plant roots
C) The water surface
D) The air above plants

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which beneficial organism produces antibiotics that suppress plant pathogens?
A) Azospirillum
B) Nitrosomonas
C) Pseudomonas fluorescens
D) Nitrobacter

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is the primary limitation of mycorrhizae in hydroponic-style aquaponics?
A) Too expensive
B) Limited effectiveness due to high nutrient availability
C) Produces toxins
D) Requires soil only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which PGPR mechanism directly provides nitrogen to plants?
A) Phosphate solubilization
B) Nitrogen fixation
C) Iron chelation
D) Hormone production

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. How do PGPR trigger induced systemic resistance (ISR)?
A) By killing all bacteria
B) By activating plant defense pathways
C) By producing excessive heat
D) By removing oxygen

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. What type of application method is recommended for root zone beneficial bacteria?
A) Foliar spray only
B) Root drench or media mix
C) Air injection
D) Fish feed addition

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. Which pathogen is commonly controlled by Trichoderma?
A) Aphids
B) Root rot (Pythium)
C) Whiteflies
D) Spider mites

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. What is the primary benefit of increased microbial diversity in the root zone?
A) Higher water temperature
B) Better disease suppression and nutrient cycling
C) Reduced plant growth
D) Increased pest populations

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which plant growth hormone group is produced by PGPR?
A) Gibberellins
B) Insulin
C) Testosterone
D) Adrenaline

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### 10. How do beneficial microbes improve nutrient uptake?
A) By blocking root growth
B) By converting unavailable nutrients to available forms
C) By consuming all nutrients
D) By reducing water flow

**Correct Answer:** B
