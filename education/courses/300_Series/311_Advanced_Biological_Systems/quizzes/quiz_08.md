# Quiz 8: Biological Disease Control

**Module:** 8
**Questions:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

### 1. Which fungal pathogen commonly causes root rot in aquaponic systems?
A) Botrytis
B) Pythium
C) Aspergillus
D) Candida

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. What is the primary mechanism by which Trichoderma controls plant diseases?
A) Herbicide production
B) Mycoparasitism and competitive exclusion
C) Photosynthesis inhibition
D) Water consumption

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. Which environmental factor most promotes fungal disease development?
A) Low humidity
B) High air circulation
C) Poor air circulation and high humidity
D) Bright light

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. What is the best time to apply biological disease control agents?
A) After plants are heavily infected
B) Preventively before disease appears
C) Only during winter
D) After harvest

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. Which bacterial species is commonly used for disease suppression in root zones?
A) E. coli
B) Salmonella
C) Bacillus subtilis
D) Vibrio

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 6. What role does dissolved oxygen play in preventing root diseases?
A) No effect on disease
B) High DO suppresses anaerobic pathogens
C) Low DO prevents all disease
D) DO increases pathogen growth

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. Which fish disease can be managed through beneficial bacteria in aquaponics?
A) All diseases equally
B) Secondary bacterial infections
C) Viral diseases only
D) Parasitic infections only

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. What is the effect of beneficial microbes on plant immune systems?
A) They weaken immunity
B) No effect
C) They prime and strengthen plant defenses
D) They eliminate the need for plant defenses

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 9. How do UV sterilizers affect biological disease control programs?
A) They enhance beneficial bacteria
B) They can kill both pathogens and beneficials
C) They only kill pathogens
D) They have no effect on microbes

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is the first line of defense against plant diseases in aquaponics?
A) Fungicides
B) Healthy plants and proper environment
C) Antibiotics
D) Removing all microbes

**Correct Answer:** B
