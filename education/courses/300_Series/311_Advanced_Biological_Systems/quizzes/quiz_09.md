# Quiz 9: Fish Gut Health

**Module:** 9
**Questions:** 10
**Passing Score:** 70%

## Questions

### 1. What is the typical bacterial density in a healthy fish gut?
A) 10^3-10^5 cells/g
B) 10^6-10^8 cells/g
C) 10^9-10^11 cells/g
D) 10^15-10^17 cells/g

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 2. Which bacterial phyla dominate healthy fish gut microbiomes?
A) Cyanobacteria and Spirochaetes
B) Proteobacteria and Firmicutes
C) Actinobacteria only
D) None, fish guts are sterile

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. What is the primary benefit of probiotics in fish feed?
A) Increased water temperature
B) Improved digestion and immune function
C) Fish coloration only
D) Reduced growth rates

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which compound in fish mucus provides antimicrobial protection?
A) Sugar
B) Salt
C) Antimicrobial peptides and antibodies
D) Plastic polymers

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 5. How does stress affect fish gut microbiomes?
A) No effect
B) Improves diversity
C) Disrupts beneficial bacteria and allows pathogens
D) Eliminates all bacteria

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 6. What role do prebiotics play in fish nutrition?
A) Direct nutrition for fish
B) Feed beneficial gut bacteria
C) Kill all bacteria
D) Increase ammonia

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. Which feeding practice promotes healthy fish gut microbiomes?
A) Overfeeding
B) Inconsistent feeding
C) High-quality feed at appropriate rates
D) Feeding only once per week

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 8. What is the connection between fish gut health and system water quality?
A) No connection
B) Healthy gut = better digestion = less waste
C) Sick fish produce less waste
D) Gut health only affects fish, not water

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. Which fish organ is particularly vulnerable to pathogenic bacteria?
A) Heart
B) Gills
C) Eyes
D) Fins

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. How can beneficial bacteria be introduced to fish guts?
A) Water changes only
B) Through probiotic-enriched feed
C) UV sterilization
D) Antibiotics

**Correct Answer:** B
