## Questions

### 1. What is the primary component of the biofilm matrix?
A) Bacterial cells
B) Extracellular polymeric substances (EPS)
C) Water
D) Minerals

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. How long does the initial conditioning stage of biofilm formation typically take?
A) Hours
B) Days
C) Weeks
D) Months

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### 3. What is the typical duration for biofilm maturation?
A) 1-2 days
B) 1-2 weeks
C) 4-8 weeks
D) 6-12 months

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. Which layer of the biofilm is most metabolically active?
A) Attachment layer
B) Base layer
C) Outer active layer
D) Conditioning layer

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 5. What is the primary purpose of EPS in biofilm?
A) Provide nutrients
B) Create structure and protection
C) Increase water flow
D) Generate heat

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. During which stage does initial bacterial colonization occur?
A) Conditioning
B) Attachment
C) Growth
D) Maturation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. What management practice helps prevent excessive biofilm buildup?
A) Reduce all water flow
B) Add more nutrients
C) Balance flow and prevent overfeeding
D) Increase temperature to 100°F

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 8. What happens during the dispersal stage of biofilm development?
A) Initial attachment
B) EPS production
C) Sloughing and renewal
D) Complete die-off

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 9. How do nutrients reach bacteria in mature biofilm?
A) Direct contact only
B) Through channels and pores in EPS matrix
C) By diffusion through solid matrix
D) Nutrients cannot reach interior bacteria

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 10. What is the best practice when starting a new biofilter?
A) Use distilled water
B) Seed with beneficial bacteria
C) Add antibiotics
D) Maintain very high flow rates

**Correct Answer:** B
