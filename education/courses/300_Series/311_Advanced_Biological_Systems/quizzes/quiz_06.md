**Module:** 6

## Questions

### 1. What is the ideal C:N ratio for effective composting?
A) 10:1
B) 25-30:1
C) 50:1
D) 100:1

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 2. Which temperature range indicates active thermophilic composting?
A) 50-70°F
B) 70-90°F
C) 131-150°F
D) 180-200°F

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 3. What is the primary benefit of worm castings in aquaponics?
A) pH reduction
B) Rich in beneficial microbes and plant-available nutrients
C) Antibiotic production
D) System cooling

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which worm species is most commonly used for vermicomposting?
A) Nightcrawlers
B) Red wigglers (Eisenia fetida)
C) Earthworms
D) Grubs

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 5. What is the optimal moisture content for vermicomposting?
A) 10-20%
B) 40-50%
C) 70-80%
D) 95-100%

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 6. How can vermicompost tea benefit aquaponic systems?
A) Kills all bacteria
B) Introduces beneficial microbes and soluble nutrients
C) Increases system salinity
D) Reduces oxygen levels

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 7. What indicates that compost is mature and ready to use?
A) Strong ammonia smell
B) Very hot temperature
C) Earthy smell, dark color, cool temperature
D) Visible food scraps

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 8. What is the primary role of thermophilic bacteria in composting?
A) Cooling the pile
B) Breaking down complex organic matter and killing pathogens
C) Producing oxygen
D) Creating ammonia

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. How often should a traditional compost pile be turned?
A) Never
B) Daily
C) Every 3-7 days
D) Once per year

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 10. What can happen if compost is too wet?
A) Faster decomposition
B) Anaerobic conditions and odors
C) Better microbial diversity
D) Improved plant nutrition

**Correct Answer:** B
