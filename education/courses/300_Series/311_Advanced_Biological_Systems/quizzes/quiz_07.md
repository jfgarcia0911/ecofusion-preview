## Questions

### 1. What is the recommended release rate for ladybugs in greenhouse IPM?
A) 100/1,000 sq ft
B) 500/1,000 sq ft
C) 1,500/1,000 sq ft
D) 5,000/1,000 sq ft

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 2. Which beneficial insect is most effective against spider mites?
A) Ladybugs
B) Predatory mites
C) Lacewings
D) Parasitic wasps

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 3. What is the target pest for parasitic wasps?
A) Fungus gnats
B) Spider mites
C) Aphids and whiteflies
D) Caterpillars

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 4. At what temperature range are most beneficial insects most active?
A) 40-50°F
B) 50-60°F
C) 60-85°F
D) 90-100°F

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 5. Which beneficial organism is applied to control fungus gnats in growing media?
A) Ladybugs
B) Beneficial nematodes
C) Lacewings
D) Predatory mites

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 6. What is the foundation level of the IPM pyramid?
A) Biological pesticides
B) Beneficial insects
C) Monitoring and scouting
D) Chemical pesticides

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 7. How many predatory mites are typically released per infested plant?
A) 1-5
B) 10-20
C) 50-100
D) 200-500

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 8. Which cultural control practice is most important for pest prevention?
A) Increasing humidity to 100%
B) Sanitation and environment management
C) Removing all insects
D) Using only artificial light

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### 9. What is the primary advantage of biological pest control over chemical pesticides?
A) Faster kill rate
B) Cheaper upfront cost
C) No chemical residues and sustainable
D) Works at any temperature

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### 10. When should beneficial insects be released?
A) After pest population explodes
B) Only in winter
C) Preventively or at first sign of pests
D) Never in greenhouses

**Correct Answer:** C
