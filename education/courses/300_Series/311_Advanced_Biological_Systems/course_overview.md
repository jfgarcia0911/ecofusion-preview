## Nitrification Deep Dive

### Nitrification Biochemistry

```
NITRIFICATION PATHWAY:

                    Ammonia-Oxidizing Bacteria (AOB)
NH3 + 1.5 O2 ──────────────────────────────────────► NO2⁻ + H2O + H⁺
(Ammonia)      Nitrosomonas, Nitrosospira           (Nitrite)


                    Nitrite-Oxidizing Bacteria (NOB)
NO2⁻ + 0.5 O2 ─────────────────────────────────────► NO3⁻
(Nitrite)      Nitrobacter, Nitrospira              (Nitrate)


COMPLETE PROCESS:
NH4⁺ → NH3 → NH2OH → NO → NO2⁻ → NO3⁻
       ↑        Hydroxylamine    Nitric
       |                         oxide
 pH dependent
 (higher pH = more NH3)
```

### Nitrification Rate Factors

| Factor | Optimal Range | Impact if Out of Range |
|--------|---------------|----------------------|
| Temperature | 77-86°F | <50°F: very slow, >95°F: die-off |
| pH | 7.0-8.0 | <6.5: inhibition, >8.5: free ammonia toxic |
| Dissolved oxygen | >4 mg/L | <2 mg/L: reduced activity |
| Alkalinity | 100-200 mg/L | Low: pH crashes |
| Surface area | Maximize | Less area = less capacity |
| TAN loading | Match capacity | Overloading = ammonia spikes |

### Biofilter Performance Indicators

| Indicator | Target | Action if Off |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Ammonia | <0.5 mg/L | Reduce feeding, add aeration |
| Nitrite | <0.5 mg/L | Check DO, reduce load |
| Nitrate | 40-100 mg/L | Adjust fish:plant ratio |
| pH stability | Minimal drift | Check alkalinity |

### Biofilm Development Stages

| Stage | Duration | Characteristics | Management |
|-------|----------|-----------------|------------|
| Conditioning | Hours | Surface proteins attach | Clean media, stable conditions |
| Attachment | 1-2 days | Initial colonization | Seed with bacteria |
| Growth | 1-4 weeks | EPS production, community | Maintain flow, nutrients |
| Maturation | 4-8 weeks | Full function, equilibrium | Monitor performance |
| Dispersal | Ongoing | Sloughing, renewal | Balance, prevent excess |

## Biofloc Technology

### Biofloc Principles

| Component | Description | Target |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| C:N ratio | Carbon to nitrogen balance | 10-15:1 |
| Mixing | Keep flocs suspended | Continuous |
| Aeration | Support aerobic flocs | >5 mg/L DO |
| Floc density | Biofloc volume | 10-15 mL/L |
| Protein source | Flocs as supplemental feed | 20-40% protein |

### Biofloc Benefits & Challenges

| Benefits | Challenges |
|----------|------------|
| Reduced water exchange | Higher energy for mixing |
| In-situ protein production | Management complexity |
| Improved water quality | Risk of anaerobic zones |
| Reduced feed costs | Requires monitoring |
| Biosecurity improvement | Variable floc composition |

## Biological Troubleshooting

### Common Biological Problems

| Problem | Symptoms | Causes | Solutions |
|---------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Nitrification crash | Ammonia spike | pH drop, temperature, toxins | Buffer, reduce feeding |
| Biofilm excessive | Poor flow, anaerobic | Overfeeding, low flow | Clean, increase circulation |
| Root rot | Brown roots, wilting | Pythium, low DO | Beneficials, aeration |
| Algae bloom | Green water, coating | Excess light, nutrients | Reduce light, cover tanks |
| Off-odor | Sulfur smell | Anaerobic conditions | Increase aeration, clean |
