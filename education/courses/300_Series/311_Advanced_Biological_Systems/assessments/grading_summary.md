# Grading Summary
## Course 311: Advanced Biological Systems

---

## Course Assessment Overview

This document provides a comprehensive summary of all graded assessments for Course 311: Advanced Biological Systems. Students should use this as a roadmap to understand expectations and plan their work throughout the course.

---

| Component | Points Available |

---

## Assessment Schedule

### Week 1-2: Early Course
**Assignment 1: Ecosystem Analysis and Modeling**
- **Due:** End of Module 1
- **Points:** 15
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes
- **Focus:** Map and analyze agricultural ecosystem components and interactions

### Week 2-3: Mid-Course (Part 1)
**Assignment 2: Beneficial Organism Integration**
- **Due:** End of Module 2
- **Points:** 20
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 20-30 minutes
- **Focus:** Design systems for beneficial insects, pollinators, and biocontrol agents

### Week 3-4: Mid-Course (Part 2)
**Assignment 3: Nutrient Cycling Optimization**
- **Due:** End of Module 5
- **Points:** 25
- **Type:** In-class activity
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Focus:** Optimize biological nutrient cycling and waste-to-resource conversion

### Week 4-5: Late Course
**Assignment 4: Microbiome Management Strategy**
- **Due:** End of Module 7
- **Points:** 20
- **Type:** Take-home assignment
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Focus:** Develop comprehensive soil and plant microbiome management program

### Week 5-6: Final
**Assignment 5: Integrated Biological System Design**
- **Due:** End of Module 16
- **Points:** 30
- **Type:** Comprehensive project
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours
- **Focus:** Create complete regenerative agricultural system with biological integration
