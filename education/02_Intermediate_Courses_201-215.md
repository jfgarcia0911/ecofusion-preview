# Course 201: Commercial Aquaponics Operations

## Course Overview

**Course Description:**
Transform your aquaponics knowledge into commercial-scale expertise. This comprehensive course covers facility design, production planning, economics, operations management, and the real-world challenges of running a profitable aquaponics business.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Upon successful completion, learners will be able to:
1. Design commercial-scale aquaponic facilities
2. Develop production plans for consistent output
3. Calculate and optimize operational economics
4. Implement standard operating procedures
5. Manage staff and production schedules
6. Navigate regulatory requirements for commercial operations

**Duration:** 16 hours total
- Online: 16 self-paced modules (1 hour each)
- Live: 4 sessions × 4 hours
- Hybrid: 8 hours online + 8 hours live intensive

**Prerequisites:** Course 102 (Aquaponics Fundamentals)

**Maximum Class Size:** 20 (online), 12 (live)

**Key Concepts:**

**Commercial Readiness Assessment:**

| Factor | Questions to Consider |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Capital** | Do you have $100K-$500K+ for startup? Access to financing? |
| **Experience** | Have you operated systems for 2+ years? Solved major problems? |
| **Market** | Is there unmet demand? Who are your customers? |
| **Location** | Access to markets? Affordable space? Appropriate climate? |
| **Time** | Can you commit full-time for 3-5 years? |
| **Team** | Do you have or can you build the right team? |
| **Resilience** | Can you weather setbacks and slow growth? |

**Success Factors from Industry Leaders:**
1. Start with proven crops (leafy greens, herbs) before experimenting
2. Secure customers before building capacity
3. Build systems for reliability, not innovation
4. Control costs aggressively in early years
5. Develop multiple revenue streams
6. Invest in automation early
7. Focus on consistent quality over maximum quantity

**Common Failure Patterns:**
- Underestimating capital requirements
- Overbuilding before proving market
- Neglecting the fish side of the operation
- Poor location selection
- Lack of farming experience
- Insufficient quality control
- Marketing/sales neglect

**Key Concepts:**

**Production Capacity Calculation:**

```
Growing Area: 5,000 sq ft
System Type: Deep Water Culture (DWC)
Crop: Butterhead Lettuce

Plant Spacing: 8" × 8" = 64 sq in per plant
Plants per sq ft: 144 ÷ 64 = 2.25 plants/sq ft
Total Plant Sites: 5,000 × 2.25 = 11,250 sites

Growth Cycle: 35 days (seed to harvest in system)
Days in propagation: 14 days
Total cycle: 49 days = 7 weeks

Annual Turns: 52 weeks ÷ 7 weeks = 7.4 turns/year
Annual Production: 11,250 × 7.4 = 83,250 heads/year
Weekly Production: 83,250 ÷ 52 = 1,601 heads/week
```

```
SOP #: HARVEST-001
Title: Leafy Green Harvesting Procedure
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: ________
Author: ________
Approved By: ________

PURPOSE:
To ensure consistent, food-safe harvesting of leafy greens.

SCOPE:
Applies to all harvesting activities for lettuce, leafy greens, and herbs.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
• Harvest Team Lead: Oversee process, quality checks
• Harvest Technicians: Execute harvesting
• QA Manager: Final quality verification

EQUIPMENT NEEDED:
□ Clean harvest totes
□ Sanitized harvest knives
□ Gloves (nitrile, food-safe)
□ Hair nets
□ Scale
□ Temperature logger

PROCEDURE:
1. Verify personal hygiene checklist completed
2. Gather sanitized equipment
3. Check harvest list for varieties and quantities
4. Begin harvest at designated area
5. Cut at base, leaving roots in system
6. Place in clean totes (do not overfill)
7. Move immediately to washing station
8. Log harvest quantities
9. Clean and sanitize equipment
10. Return equipment to storage

QUALITY STANDARDS:
• No damaged or yellow leaves
• Proper size (variety-specific)
• No pest damage
• No roots attached
• Temperature: <45°F within 30 minutes

DOCUMENTATION:
• Harvest Log
• Temperature Log
• Quality Deviation Report (if applicable)
```

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|-----|---------|--------|
| Revenue per sq ft | Total Revenue ÷ Grow Area | $30-60/year |

**Pricing Strategy Framework:**

```
Cost-Plus Pricing:
  Direct Costs (seed, media, labor) = $1.50/head lettuce
  Indirect Costs (overhead allocation) = $0.50/head
  Total Cost = $2.00/head
  Target Margin = 40%
  Price = $2.00 ÷ (1 - 0.40) = $3.33/head

Market-Based Pricing:
  Competitor organic lettuce: $3.50-$4.50/head
  Premium for local/aquaponic: 10-20%
  Target Price: $3.85-$5.40/head

Final Price Strategy:
  Wholesale: $2.75-$3.00/head (volume, convenience)
  Retail/Farmers Market: $4.00-$4.50/head (direct, premium)
  Restaurant: $3.25-$3.75/head (relationship, reliability)
```

## Course Overview
