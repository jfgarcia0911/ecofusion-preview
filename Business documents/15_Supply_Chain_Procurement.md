## Executive Summary

EcoFusion's Supply Chain & Procurement Strategy establishes a comprehensive framework for sourcing, logistics, and distribution that ensures operational reliability, cost efficiency, and alignment with our sustainability mission. This document outlines procurement processes, supplier management, inventory optimization, and distribution strategies designed to support rapid growth while maintaining excellence.

**Supply Chain Vision**: Build the most resilient, sustainable, and efficient agricultural supply chain in urban farming, creating competitive advantage through operational excellence.

**Strategic Objectives**:
- 98% order fill rate across all channels
- 40% reduction in supply chain costs through optimization
- 100% sustainable sourcing by Year 3
- <24-hour farm-to-customer delivery capability

## 6. Cold Chain Management

### 6.1 Temperature Requirements

| Product Category | Storage Temp | Transport Temp | Shelf Life |
|------------------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| Leafy greens | 34-38°F | <40°F | 10-14 days |
| Herbs | 38-42°F | <45°F | 7-14 days |
| Microgreens | 34-38°F | <40°F | 7-10 days |
| Fresh fish | 30-34°F | <35°F | 3-5 days |
| Processed fish | 0-5°F (frozen) | <10°F | 6 months |

### 6.2 Cold Chain Monitoring

**Temperature Monitoring System**:

| Location | Monitoring | Alert Threshold | Response Time |
|----------|------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Cold storage | Continuous IoT sensors | ±2°F from setpoint | 15 minutes |
| Vehicles | GPS + temp sensors | >45°F for produce | 5 minutes |
| Packing area | Wall sensors | >50°F ambient | 10 minutes |
| Delivery handoff | Handheld thermometers | Per product specs | Immediate |

### 6.3 Cold Chain Procedures

**Pre-Cooling Requirements**:
- Vehicles pre-cooled to 35°F before loading
- Products staged in cold storage before loading
- Loading completed within 15 minutes
- Door open time minimized

**Temperature Documentation**:
- Temperature logged at harvest
- Temperature logged at packing
- Temperature logged at loading
- Temperature logged at delivery
- All logs retained 2 years
