## Executive Summary

EcoFusion's Board Governance & Advisory Framework establishes the structures, processes, and standards for effective corporate governance that will guide the company through rapid growth and position it for a successful exit. This framework balances founder autonomy with investor rights and independent oversight, creating accountability while maintaining agility.

**Governance Vision**: Build a best-in-class governance structure that provides strategic guidance, ensures fiduciary responsibility, and positions EcoFusion as an institutional-quality investment opportunity.

## 1. Governance Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Governance Principles

1. **Accountability**: Clear responsibility at all levels of leadership
2. **Transparency**: Open communication with all stakeholders
3. **Integrity**: Ethical conduct in all business dealings
4. **Independence**: Objective oversight free from conflicts
5. **Competence**: Directors with relevant expertise and experience
6. **Mission Alignment**: Governance that reinforces our sustainability mission

### 1.2 Governance Framework

```
GOVERNANCE HIERARCHY
```

## 3. Board Committees

### 3.1 Committee Structure

**Year 1-2 (Informal)**:
- Full board handles all matters
- Informal sub-committees as needed

**Year 3+ (Formal Committees)**:

| Committee | Members | Chair | Meetings |
|-----------|---------|-------|----------|
| Audit Committee | 3 independent | Independent | Quarterly |
| Compensation Committee | 3 (majority independent) | Independent | Quarterly |
| Nominating/Governance | 3 (majority independent) | Independent | Annually |
| ESG Committee | 3 | Any director | Quarterly |

### 3.2 Audit Committee

**Responsibilities**:
- Oversee financial reporting and disclosure
- Select and oversee external auditors
- Review internal controls
- Approve related party transactions
- Oversee risk management
- Whistleblower procedures

**Composition Requirements**:
- All members independent
- At least one "financial expert"
- Minimum 3 members

### 3.3 Compensation Committee

**Responsibilities**:
- Set CEO compensation
- Approve executive compensation
- Oversee equity incentive plans
- Review compensation philosophy
- Approve employment agreements

**Composition Requirements**:
- Majority independent
- No CEO participation in own comp
- Minimum 3 members

### 3.4 Nominating & Governance Committee

**Responsibilities**:
- Identify and recruit director candidates
- Evaluate board composition
- Develop governance guidelines
- Oversee board evaluations
- Succession planning

**Composition Requirements**:
- Majority independent
- Minimum 3 members

### 3.5 ESG Committee

**Responsibilities**:
- Oversee sustainability strategy
- Monitor ESG metrics and reporting
- Review community impact programs
- Ensure B Corp maintenance
- Climate and environmental oversight

**Composition Requirements**:
- At least one ESG expert
- Minimum 3 members

## 4. Advisory Board

### 4.1 Advisory Board Purpose

**Roles**:
- Provide specialized expertise
- Industry connections and introductions
- Strategic input on specific initiatives
- Recruitment pipeline for future directors
- Credibility and validation

**Distinction from Board of Directors**:
- No fiduciary duties
- No formal voting rights
- Informal engagement
- Specific expertise focus
- Flexible tenure

### 4.2 Advisory Board Structure

**Target Composition (6-10 Advisors)**:

| Category | # Advisors | Expertise Areas |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Industry Experts | 2-3 | Agriculture, food service, retail |
| Functional Experts | 2-3 | Finance, marketing, operations |
| Technical Experts | 1-2 | Aquaponics, horticulture, biology |
| Community/Mission | 1-2 | Food security, urban development |
| Investors/Business | 1-2 | M&A, fundraising, scaling |

### 4.3 Target Advisory Board Members

| Finance Advisor | CFO of food company | Recruiting |
| Urban Agriculture | Community food leader | Identified |

## 5. Board Operations

### 5.1 Meeting Calendar

| Meeting Type | Frequency | Duration | Focus |
|--------------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Regular Board | Quarterly | 3-4 hours | Strategy, performance, governance |
| Annual Planning | Annual | Full day | Budget, strategy, goals |
| Committee Meetings | Quarterly | 1-2 hours | Committee-specific |
| Special Meetings | As needed | Variable | Urgent matters |

**Annual Calendar**:

| Month | Meeting | Key Topics |
|-------|---------|------------|
| January | Q4 Board + Annual Planning | Year-end review, annual plan |
| March | Committee meetings | Audit, compensation review |
| April | Q1 Board | Q1 results, operational review |
| June | Committee meetings | Mid-year adjustments |
| July | Q2 Board | H1 results, strategy check |
| September | Committee meetings | Budget prep, succession |
| October | Q3 Board | Q3 results, budget review |
| December | Committee meetings | Year-end prep |

### 5.2 Board Materials

**Standard Board Package (delivered 5 days prior)**:

| Document | Content | Pages |
|----------|---------|-------|
| CEO Report | Strategy update, key issues | 3-5 |
| Financial Report | P&L, balance sheet, cash flow | 5-10 |
| KPI Dashboard | Key metrics, trends | 2-3 |
| Operational Update | Production, sales, operations | 3-5 |
| Committee Reports | Committee activities | 2-3 each |
| Action Items | Decisions required | 1-2 |
| Supporting Materials | Appendices as needed | Variable |

### 5.3 Board Meeting Agenda Template

```
BOARD MEETING AGENDA

1. Call to Order & Attendance (5 min)
2. Approval of Previous Minutes (5 min)
3. CEO Report & Strategic Update (30 min)
   - Key accomplishments
   - Challenges and risks
   - Strategic priorities
4. Financial Review (20 min)
   - CFO presentation
   - Budget vs. actual
   - Cash position
5. Operational Review (20 min)
   - Production metrics
   - Sales and marketing
   - Customer update
6. Committee Reports (15 min each)
   - Audit Committee
   - Compensation Committee
   - Other committees
7. Strategic Discussion Topic (30 min)
   - Deep dive on key issue
8. Action Items & Decisions (20 min)
   - Resolutions requiring approval
9. Executive Session (as needed)
   - Independent directors only
10. Adjournment
```

### 5.4 Board Decision Framework

**Decisions Requiring Board Approval**:

| Category | Threshold | Approval |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Annual budget | All | Board approval |
| Capital expenditure | >$100K | Board approval |
| Debt | >$250K | Board approval |
| Equity issuance | All | Board + shareholder |
| Executive hiring | C-level | Board approval |
| Executive compensation | C-level | Comp Committee |
| Major contracts | >$500K | Board approval |
| Acquisitions | All | Board + shareholder |
| Related party | All | Audit Committee |
| Litigation | >$100K | Board notification |

## 8. Governance Policies

### 8.1 Code of Ethics

**Core Principles**:
1. Act in the company's best interest
2. Avoid conflicts of interest
3. Maintain confidentiality
4. Comply with laws and regulations
5. Promote ethical conduct
6. Report violations promptly

### 8.2 Conflict of Interest Policy

**Disclosure Requirements**:
- Annual disclosure questionnaire
- Immediate disclosure of new conflicts
- Recusal from conflicted decisions
- Board review and approval process

**Related Party Transaction Policy**:
- All related party transactions require Audit Committee approval
- Fair market value requirement
- Disclosure in financial statements
- Annual review of all related party relationships

### 8.3 Confidentiality Policy

**Confidential Information**:
- Financial information and projections
- Strategic plans and initiatives
- Customer and supplier data
- Personnel matters
- Board discussions and materials

**Obligations**:
- No disclosure without authorization
- Secure handling of materials
- Return of materials upon departure
- Survival of obligations post-service

### 8.4 Stock Ownership Guidelines

**Director Stock Ownership (Target)**:

| Level | Ownership Target | Timeline |
|-------|------------------|----------|
| Non-employee directors | 3x annual retainer | 5 years |
| CEO | 5x annual salary | 5 years |
| Other executives | 2x annual salary | 5 years |

## 9. Stakeholder Rights

### 9.1 Shareholder Rights

**Common Stock Rights**:
- Voting rights (1 share = 1 vote)
- Right to receive dividends (if declared)
- Liquidation rights
- Information rights (annual financials)
- Preemptive rights (if applicable)

**Preferred Stock Rights (Typical)**:
- Liquidation preference (1x non-participating typical)
- Anti-dilution protection
- Voting rights
- Board representation
- Information rights (monthly financials)
- Protective provisions

### 9.2 Protective Provisions

**Typical Investor Protective Provisions**:

| Action | Approval Required |
|--------|-------------------|
| Change charter/bylaws | Preferred majority |
| Issue senior securities | Preferred majority |
| Change board size | Preferred majority |
| Dividends | Preferred majority |
| Acquisition/sale | Preferred majority |
| Increase option pool | Preferred majority |
| Related party transactions | Preferred majority |

### 9.3 Information Rights

**Monthly Reporting (to Major Investors)**:
- Monthly financial statements (within 30 days)
- Budget vs. actual comparison
- Key metrics dashboard
- Material developments

**Annual Reporting**:
- Annual audited financials
- Annual budget
- Strategic plan update
- Cap table update

### 9.4 Shareholder Meetings

**Annual Meeting Requirements**:
- Hold within 13 months of prior meeting
- Elect directors
- Approve auditors
- Other shareholder matters

## 10. Governance Evolution

### 10.1 Governance Maturity Model

```
GOVERNANCE MATURITY STAGES

STAGE 4: PUBLIC-READY (Year 5+)
├── Full independent board majority
├── All SOX-compliant committees
├── Comprehensive policies
├── External governance assessment
└── Institutional-quality governance

STAGE 3: INSTITUTIONAL (Year 3-5)
├── Independent directors added
├── Formal committee structure
├── Comprehensive governance policies
├── Regular board evaluations
└── Professional board practices

STAGE 2: PROFESSIONALIZED (Year 1-3)
├── Investor directors added
├── Regular board meetings
├── Board materials and processes
├── Advisory board established
└── Basic governance policies

STAGE 1: FOUNDER-LED (Year 0-1)
├── Founder-controlled board
├── Informal governance
├── Basic corporate documents
└── Minimal formal processes
```

### 10.2 Governance Roadmap

| Year | Key Governance Milestones |
|------|---------------------------|
| Year 1 | Formalize board meeting cadence, establish advisory board |
| Year 2 | Add first independent director, implement board evaluation |
| Year 3 | Establish formal committees, comprehensive policies |
| Year 4 | Achieve 50% independent board, external assessment |
| Year 5 | Full institutional governance, exit/IPO readiness |

### 10.3 Board Evaluation

**Annual Board Evaluation Process**:

| Component | Method | Frequency |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Full board evaluation | Survey + discussion | Annual |
| Committee evaluations | Survey | Annual |
| Individual director evaluation | Chair feedback | Annual |
| Chair/CEO evaluation | Board feedback | Annual |

**Evaluation Topics**:
- Board composition and structure
- Meeting effectiveness
- Strategic contribution
- Risk oversight
- CEO relationship
- Committee functioning
- Individual contributions
