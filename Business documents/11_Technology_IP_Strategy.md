### 2.3 System Integration Architecture

**Proprietary Integration Protocols**:

1. **EcoLink**: Real-time communication protocol for sensor-actuator networks
   - Ultra-low latency (<10ms)
   - Deterministic timing for critical controls
   - Built-in redundancy and failsafe

2. **BioSync**: Synchronization protocol for biological system coordination
   - Coordinates fish feeding with plant nutrient uptake
   - Optimizes energy flows between systems
   - Balances production schedules

3. **NutriFlow**: Nutrient management and tracking protocol
   - Tracks nutrients from source through plant uptake
   - Enables precise attribution and optimization
   - Supports food traceability requirements

### 7.2 Trade Secret Protection Framework

**Classification Levels**:

| Level | Definition | Protection Measures |
|-------|------------|---------------------|
| Maximum | Core competitive advantage, irreplaceable | Physical isolation, need-to-know, encrypted storage |
| High | Significant competitive advantage | Access controls, NDA required, audit logging |
| Medium | Moderate business value | Standard security, confidential marking |
| Standard | General business information | Basic access controls |

**Protection Measures**:

1. **Physical Security**
   - Secure facilities with access control
   - Visitor management and escort requirements
   - Clean desk policies
   - Document destruction procedures

2. **Digital Security**
   - Encryption at rest and in transit
   - Role-based access control
   - Data loss prevention (DLP) systems
   - Activity monitoring and audit logs

3. **Legal Protection**
   - Employee confidentiality agreements
   - Vendor and partner NDAs
   - Exit interview and debriefing procedures
   - Litigation readiness documentation

4. **Operational Security**
   - Need-to-know information sharing
   - Compartmentalization of sensitive projects
   - Regular security training
   - Insider threat monitoring

### 7.3 Trade Secret Documentation

**Trade Secret Register**:
- Unique identifier for each trade secret
- Description and scope
- Development history and contributors
- Protection measures implemented
- Access log and authorization record
- Valuation and review schedule
