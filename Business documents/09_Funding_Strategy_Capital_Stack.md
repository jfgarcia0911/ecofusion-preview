## 3. SERIES A STRUCTURE <a name="series-a"></a>

### Term Sheet Summary

| Term | Proposal |
|------|----------|
| **Security** | Series A Preferred Stock |
| **Amount** | $4,000,000 |
| **Pre-money Valuation** | $6,000,000 |
| **Post-money Valuation** | $10,000,000 |
| **Price per Share** | $10.00 |
| **Shares Issued** | 400,000 |
| **Investor Ownership** | 40% |

### Key Terms

**Liquidation Preference:**
- 1x non-participating
- Senior to Common Stock
- Pari passu among Series A holders

**Dividends:**
- 8% cumulative (optional)
- Payable upon liquidation if declared
- Not mandatory

**Anti-Dilution:**
- Broad-based weighted average
- Standard carve-outs for employee pool

**Conversion:**
- Convertible to Common at 1:1
- Automatic upon qualified IPO ($50M+)
- Optional at holder's discretion

**Voting Rights:**
- Vote with Common on as-converted basis
- Protective provisions for major decisions

**Board Composition:**
- 5 directors total
- 2 investor-appointed
- 2 founder-appointed
- 1 independent (mutual agreement)

**Protective Provisions (Investor Approval Required):**
- Charter/bylaws amendments
- Issuance of senior securities
- Sale of company
- Dividend declarations
- Related party transactions >$100K
- Debt >$500K

**Information Rights:**
- Monthly financial statements
- Annual budget and projections
- Quarterly investor updates
- Annual audit

**Pro-Rata Rights:**
- Right to participate in future rounds
- Maintain percentage ownership

**Drag-Along:**
- Majority of Preferred can drag Common
- In sale >2x return to Preferred

**Registration Rights:**
- Standard demand and piggyback rights
- S-3 registration after eligibility
