## Executive Summary

EcoFusion's Customer Success & Retention Strategy establishes a comprehensive framework for acquiring, delighting, and retaining customers across all segments. By combining exceptional product quality with outstanding service and genuine community engagement, we will build a loyal customer base that drives sustainable growth through repeat purchases and powerful word-of-mouth advocacy.

**Customer Success Vision**: Create the most beloved fresh food brand in the Mid-Atlantic region, where every customer interaction reinforces trust, quality, and shared values around sustainable food systems.

**Retention Target**: Achieve 85%+ customer retention rate by Year 3 and 90%+ by Year 5, with Net Promoter Score (NPS) consistently above 70.

## 11. Retention & Win-Back Strategies

### 11.1 Retention Strategy by Segment

| Segment | Retention Challenge | Strategy | Target |

| Lapsed (30-60 days) | Life changes | Re-engagement campaigns | 50% reactivation |

### 11.2 Proactive Retention Actions

**At-Risk Customer Playbook**:

| Risk Level | Trigger | Action | Timeline |
|------------|---------|--------|----------|
| Early warning | Decreased frequency | Personalized email | Within 7 days |
| Moderate risk | 45 days no purchase | Phone call + offer | Within 3 days |
| High risk | 60 days no purchase | Personal visit/call + significant offer | Immediate |
| Churn imminent | 75 days no purchase | VP outreach + retention offer | Immediate |

**Win-Back Campaign Series**:

| Campaign | Timing | Offer | Message |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|

| "What Happened?" | Day 75 | 20% off + survey | Help us understand |
| "Come Back" | Day 90 | 25% off | Special offer to return |
| "Final Chance" | Day 120 | 30% off | Last offer before removal |

**Win-Back Economics**:
- Target: 20% reactivation rate
- Average reactivated CLV: 70% of original
- Win-back CAC: 50% of new customer CAC
