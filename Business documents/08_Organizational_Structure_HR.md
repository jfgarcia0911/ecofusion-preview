# EcoFusion Organizational Structure & HR Plan

## Building a World-Class Team for Category Leadership
### People Strategy for Fortune 500 Trajectory

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

People are EcoFusion's most valuable asset. This comprehensive human resources plan establishes the organizational structure, talent acquisition strategy, compensation philosophy, and culture framework needed to attract, develop, and retain the exceptional team required for industry leadership.

**HR Vision:** Build a team where passionate people do their best work while transforming the food system

**5-Year People Goals:**
- 50+ full-time employees
- <15% voluntary turnover
- >4.5 Glassdoor rating
- 95%+ positions filled internally for growth roles

## 7. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT <a name="performance"></a>

### Performance Philosophy

- Continuous feedback, not annual events
- Focus on development, not just evaluation
- Tie to clear expectations and goals
- Fair, consistent, documented

### Goal-Setting Framework (OKRs)

| Level | Cadence | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Company | Annual/Quarterly | "Achieve $4M revenue with 90% customer satisfaction" |
| Department | Quarterly | "Reduce fish mortality to <0.5%" |
| Individual | Quarterly | "Complete Six Sigma Green Belt certification" |

### Review Cycle

| Event | Frequency | Duration | Participants |
|-------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Weekly 1:1 | Weekly | 30 min | Manager + Employee |
| Monthly Check-in | Monthly | 45 min | Manager + Employee |
| Quarterly Review | Quarterly | 60 min | Manager + Employee + HR |
| Annual Review | Annual | 90 min | Manager + Employee + HR |

### Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Description | % of Employees |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| 5 - Exceptional | Far exceeds all expectations | 5% |
| 4 - Exceeds | Consistently exceeds expectations | 20% |
| 3 - Meets | Meets expectations | 60% |
| 2 - Developing | Partially meets, improvement needed | 10% |
| 1 - Below | Does not meet expectations | 5% |

### Merit Increase Matrix

| Performance Rating | Below Market | At Market | Above Market |
|-------------------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| 5 - Exceptional | 8-10% | 6-8% | 4-6% |
| 4 - Exceeds | 6-8% | 4-6% | 2-4% |
| 3 - Meets | 4-6% | 2-4% | 0-2% |
| 2 - Developing | 0-2% | 0% | 0% |
| 1 - Below | 0% | 0% | 0% |

| 1-2 | Department-specific training | 20 hours |

|---------------|-------------------|-------|
