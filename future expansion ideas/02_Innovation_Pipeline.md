## HORIZON 1 INNOVATIONS (1-3 Years)

### 1. Autonomous Harvesting Robotics

**Vision**: Fully automated harvesting system that operates 24/7

**Technology Components**:
- Computer vision for plant identification and quality assessment
- Soft robotics for delicate plant handling
- Machine learning for optimal harvest timing
- Continuous operation with minimal human intervention

**Impact**:
| Metric | Current | With Innovation |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Labor cost per lb | $0.45 | $0.15 |
| Harvest hours | 8/day | 24/day |
| Quality consistency | 95% | 99% |
| Waste | 5% | 1% |

**Investment**: $500K development + $200K per facility
**Timeline**: Prototype Year 2, Production Year 3
